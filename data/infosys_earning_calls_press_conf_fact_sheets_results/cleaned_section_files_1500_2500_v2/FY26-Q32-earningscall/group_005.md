### Jayesh Sanghrajka

The announced deal is 100% net new and will start ramping up this year.

### Sandeep Shah

Regarding the second-half guidance, did Infosys expect further lower pass-through of third-party item sales? Third-party item sales were 7.4% in the first half versus 8.2% for the full previous year, while Q3 typically sees seasonal strength.

### Jayesh Sanghrajka

As stated at the beginning of the year, Infosys expected third-party item sales to be lower than the previous year, with the same trend continuing. Management did not expect unusual growth or an unusual elevation in third-party item sales in Q3.

### Sandeep Shah

What explained the urgency to deploy 8,000 net additions on the employee side despite the seasonal softness reflected in the second-half implied guidance?

### Jayesh Sanghrajka

This reflected the demand and supply environment. Infosys was already at 85% utilization and had onboarded 12,000 freshers, indicating visibility into the business.

### Salil Parekh

Infosys had a strong Q2 and very good large deals, including one mega deal announced after the quarter. Management highlighted its leadership in enterprise AI and forward-deployed engineering capability and said the company continued to lead in several areas.

The company increased its full-year revenue-growth guidance to 2%-3% for the financial year.
