(1) The adjusted non-IFRS measures exclude the effect of provisions under the Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025. These provisions increased gratuity liability, arising from past service cost relating to plan amendment, and leave liability by ₹ 1,289 crore, recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income. This also resulted in a lower tax of ₹ 318 crore in FY'26.

## Note on Adjusted Non-IFRS performance measures:

Non-IFRS financial performance measures supplement financial information reported on an IFRS basis. They should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the relevant IFRS measures and should be read together with information presented on a reported IFRS basis. The adjustments are intended to reflect the Company's core performance across periods.
