## NOTES:

- The information is extracted from the audited condensed consolidated Balance Sheet and Statement of Comprehensive Income for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026. These statements were taken on record at the Board meeting held on April 23, 2026.
- Interest income before tax was ₹381 crore and ₹327 crore for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively.
- Reversal of tax provisions was ₹774 crore and ₹101 crore for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively. These reversals resulted from orders received under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, for certain assessment years.
- Reported-currency revenue growth includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant-currency growth compares current-period revenue in respective local currencies, converted to US dollars using prior-period exchange rates, with prior-period reported revenue.
- A Fact Sheet providing the Company's operating metrics is available from Infosys.

## NOTES:

1. The adjusted non-IFRS measures exclude the effect of provisions under the Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025. These provisions increased gratuity liability, arising from past service cost related to a plan amendment, and leave liability by ₹1,289 crore, recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income. They also resulted in lower tax of ₹318 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026.
2. Excluding the effect of Income Tax orders received under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and the Labour Codes provisions notified by the Government of India, EPS growth in rupee terms was 12.1% for the year ended March 31, 2026 and 13.9% for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, both year over year.
3. Free cash flow included cash payments toward the Labour Codes of ₹452 crore for the quarter and ₹902 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026.
4. Non-IFRS financial performance measures supplement financial information reported under IFRS. They should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for the relevant IFRS measures and should be read with the reported IFRS information. Infosys states that the adjustments are intended to reflect the Company's core performance across periods.
