# Guidance for FY26

## Q1 FY26 Financial Performance

For the quarter ended June 30, 2025, Infosys reported:

- Revenue of $4,941 million, up 3.8% year over year and 2.6% sequentially in constant currency.
- Reported revenue of ₹42,279 crores, up 7.5% year over year.
- Operating margin of 20.8%, down 0.3 percentage points year over year and 0.2 percentage points sequentially.
- Basic EPS of ₹16.70, up 8.6% year over year.
- Free cash flow of ₹7,533 crores, down 17.7% year over year, with free-cash-flow conversion at 108.8% of net profit.
- Free cash flow of $884 million, equal to 109.3% of net profit.
- ROE of 30.4%, up 140 basis points.

Large-deal TCV was $3.8 billion, with 55% of the value classified as net new.

CEO and Managing Director Salil Parekh attributed the quarter's performance to Infosys's enterprise AI capabilities, client consolidation decisions, large-deal wins, and client relationships. CFO Jayesh Sanghrajka highlighted sequential growth of 2.6%, operating margin of 20.8%, year-over-year EPS growth of 8.6%, cash-flow conversion above 100% for the fifth consecutive quarter, and the management of currency volatility through hedging.

## Guidance for FY26

- Revenue growth of 1%-3% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%-22%.

## Client wins & Testimonials

1. Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Select Portfolio Servicing, Inc. (SPS), covering hybrid cloud solutions, application portfolio management, IT operations, IaaS, SaaS, security operations, and quality assurance. SPS plans to use Infosys Topaz for AI adoption across business, technology, and enterprise functions.

2. Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with AIB to accelerate digital transformation, modernize technology and data capabilities, and improve operational efficiency and resilience in Irish banking.

3. Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with E.ON to enable AI-powered digital workplace transformation across Europe.

4. Infosys expanded its strategic collaboration with DNB Bank ASA to accelerate digital transformation, modernize IT infrastructure, and use AI and machine learning to provide seamless, personalized, and agile banking services.

5. Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Yorkshire Building Society to accelerate digital transformation, including investments in faster payments and enhanced security, in support of the society's 2030 ambitions.

6. Infosys and Spark New Zealand announced a strategic agreement to transform Spark's technology delivery model through digital innovation. The agreement will allow Spark teams to focus on technology strategy and product roadmaps while using Infosys's global scale, AI, and innovation investments.

7. Infosys collaborated with Perfection Fresh to implement Microsoft Sustainability Manager. The rollout provided real-time visibility of produce across locations, improved operational efficiency and audit transparency, and reduced wastage. The implementation expanded from four sites to all 17 Perfection Fresh locations.

8. Infosys Finacle announced a strategic collaboration with Bank of Sydney to support digital transformation using the Infosys Finacle Digital Banking Suite. The initiative is intended to modernize the bank's core and digital banking technology while addressing changing customer needs, digital developments, and regulatory requirements.

9. Infosys BPM launched AI agents for invoice processing within its Infosys Accounts Payable on Cloud solution. The solution is intended to reduce manual dependencies and improve processing speed, accuracy, efficiency, intelligence, and autonomy.

10. Infosys announced a three-year strategic collaboration with the Lawn Tennis Association to deliver AI-powered innovations, including match insights and immersive fan experiences with real-time insights and interactive features.

11. Infosys and Economist Impact launched The Sustainability Atlas, which uses Infosys generative AI capabilities to bring together sustainability data and insights for policymakers and business leaders.
