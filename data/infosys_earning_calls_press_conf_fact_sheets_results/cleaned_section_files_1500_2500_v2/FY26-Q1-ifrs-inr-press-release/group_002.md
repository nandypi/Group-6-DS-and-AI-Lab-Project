## About Infosys

Infosys has more than 320,000 employees and serves clients in 59 countries. The company has more than four decades of experience managing systems for global enterprises and provides digital transformation services powered by cloud and AI. Infosys describes its focus areas as an AI-first core, digital capabilities at scale, continuous learning, governance, environmental sustainability, and an inclusive workplace.
