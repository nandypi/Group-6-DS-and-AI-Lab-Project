## Press Release

- Infosys Finacle and its customers received four Global Banking and Finance Awards 2025:
  - Innovation Award for Excellence in Margin Finance Innovation, India, with HDFC Bank.
  - Most Innovative Payments Channel Modernization, Colombia, with Bancolombia.
  - Technology Award for Best Core Banking Transformation with Real-Time Eventing, with Emirates NBD Bank.
  - Award for Best Customer Journey Initiative, Australia, with Australian Military Bank.
- Infosys Finacle was recognized as The World's Best Software Provider for Virtual Accounts 2025 and The World's Best Software Provider for Liquidity Management 2025 by the Euromoney Transaction Banking Awards.

## Safe Harbor

The release identifies risks and uncertainties related to future growth and financial or operating performance, the McCamish cybersecurity incident, and the United States H-1B visa program. Named risk areas include business-strategy execution, competition for and retention of talent, wage increases, employee reskilling investments, hybrid working, economic and geopolitical conditions, technological disruption including generative AI, immigration and other regulatory changes, ESG objectives, capital allocation, acquisitions, pending litigation, costs related to the McCamish cybersecurity incident, the US government investigation, the September 19, 2025 H-1B proclamation, and current or future tariffs.
