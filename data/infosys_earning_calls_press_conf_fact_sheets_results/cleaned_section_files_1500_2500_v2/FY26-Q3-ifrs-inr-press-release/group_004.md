## NOTES:

1. On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Labour Codes. These codes consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during employment and post-employment. They also introduce changes including a uniform definition of wages and enhanced benefits relating to leave. The Labour Codes adjustments represent an increase of ₹1,289 crore in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and an increase in leave liability. The combined adjustment is recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.
2. Revenue growth in reported currency includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Infosys calculates constant-currency growth by comparing current-period revenues in respective local currencies, converted to US$ using prior-period exchange rates, with prior-period reported revenues.
3. A Fact Sheet providing the operating metrics of the Company can be downloaded from www.infosys.com.
