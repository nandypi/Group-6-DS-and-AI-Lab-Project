## IFRS -INR Press Release

Quarterly revenue crossed the $5 billion mark, with second consecutive-quarter strong performance.

Infosys reported Q2 FY26 revenues of $5,076 million, representing 2.9% year-over-year growth and 2.2% sequential growth in constant currency. Operating margin was 21.0%. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion, equal to 131.1% of net profit. Large deal TCV was $3.1 billion, with 67% classified as net new. Employee headcount increased by 8,203.

H1 FY26 revenues grew 3.3% year over year in constant currency. H1 operating margin was 20.9%.

Salil Parekh, CEO and MD, said that Infosys had delivered two consecutive quarters of strong growth. He attributed the Q2 deal performance, including 67% net-new TCV, to client demand for AI-related value delivery. He also said that investments over the previous three years had supported an AI-first culture and employee reskilling for a human-plus-AI workplace. He described Infosys Topaz as supporting value creation at scale in transformation programs.

## Guidance for FY26:

- Revenue growth of 2%-3% in constant currency
- Operating margin of 20%-22%

## Key highlights:

### For the quarter ended September 30, 2025

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 2.9% year over year and 2.2% sequentially.
- Reported revenues were ₹44,490 crore, up 8.6% year over year.
- Operating margin was 21.0%, down 0.1 percentage points year over year and up 0.2 percentage points sequentially.
- Basic EPS was ₹17.76, up 13.1% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹9,677 crore, up 38.0% year over year, with conversion at 131.2% of net profit.

### For the six months ended September 30, 2025

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 3.3% year over year.
- Reported revenues were ₹86,769 crore, up 8.1% year over year.
- Operating margin was 20.9%, down 0.2 percentage points year over year.
- Basic EPS was ₹34.47, up 10.9% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹17,210 crore, up 6.5% year over year, with conversion at 120.4% of net profit.

Jayesh Sanghrajka, CFO, described Q2 performance as broad-based, with strong growth, resilient margins, high cash generation, and 13.1% year-over-year EPS growth in rupee terms. He said Infosys continued to make strategic investments while focusing on execution amid high uncertainty. In line with its capital allocation policy, Infosys announced an ₹18,000 crore share buyback and an interim dividend of ₹23 per share, up 9.5% from the previous fiscal year.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys transformed ABN AMRO's lending process through an nCino platform implementation that consolidated multiple legacy systems into a unified platform. The expected benefits included improved operational efficiency, enhanced collateral management, and faster, more transparent customer service.
- Infosys collaborated with Mastercard to provide financial institutions enhanced access to Mastercard Move's money-movement capabilities and support the scaling of cross-border payments.
- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Sunrise to accelerate IT transformation, focusing on data security, operational agility, customer experience, and future AI integration.
- Infosys announced a joint venture with Telstra in Australia by acquiring 75% of the shareholding in Versent Group, a wholly owned subsidiary of Telstra Group. The venture is intended to expand AI-enabled cloud and digital solutions for enterprises and support Versent Group's growth in the region.
- Infosys collaborated with RWE AG on automated digital workplace transformation and operational efficiency, with a focus on technology modernization, sustainability, and workforce enablement.
- Infosys collaborated with HanesBrands Inc. on AI-driven transformation and productivity across digital, business applications, and data operations.
- Infosys collaborated with AGCO Corporation on IT and HR operations transformation, with the aim of creating a more responsive and streamlined operating ecosystem.
- Uniting Financial Services, Australia, subscribed to Infosys Finacle's Digital Banking SaaS suite on AWS cloud. The implementation was completed in less than five months and was intended to modernize core banking and digital capabilities.
- Infosys collaborated with Glion Arena Kobe as its official digital innovation and GX partner. The collaboration covers data aggregation, cloud technologies, new revenue opportunities, and digital experiences for fans and visitors, with a focus on sustainable operations.
