## About Infosys

Infosys has over 320,000 employees and operates in 59 countries. The company has more than four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises. Its services include cloud and AI-enabled digital transformation, an AI-first core, digital capabilities at scale, and ongoing digital-skills development. Infosys also states that it is committed to environmental sustainability, governance, and an inclusive workplace.
