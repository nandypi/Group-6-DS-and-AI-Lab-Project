## Infosys Limited Q4 FY26 Media Conference Call

April 23, 2026

## Salil Parekh

Infosys delivered strong performance in financial year 2026:

- Full-year growth was 3.1% in constant-currency terms.
- Q4 year-on-year growth was 4.1% in constant-currency terms.
- Strong industry growth came from Financial Services, Communications, and Manufacturing.
- Europe was a strong-performing geography.
- Large-deal TCV was $14.9 billion for FY26 and $3.2 billion for Q4 FY26. Full-year large-deal TCV was 28% higher than in the previous year.
- Net-new large deals represented 55% of the full-year total.

Infosys discussed its AI strategy at AI Investor Day, including six areas of opportunity: AI strategy and engineering, data, process, legacy modernization, physical AI, and trust. The Topaz Fabric AI platform and Cobalt cloud platform are operational and being used with clients across these areas.

For FY27, Infosys sees significant opportunities in AI services, alongside competitive intensity and an AI productivity impact. Revenue growth guidance is 1.5%-3.5% year-on-year in constant-currency terms, and operating-margin guidance is 20%-22%. Growth is expected to accelerate in Financial Services and the Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services vertical.

### Guidance, demand environment, acquisitions, and margins

#### Ritu Singh, CNBC TV18

Ritu Singh asked about the timing and guidance impact of the Stratus and Optimum acquisitions and the Australian joint venture, the discretionary-spending environment, margin drivers, hiring, wage hikes, and Salil Parekh's tenure.

#### Salil Parekh

Financial Services is expected to see accelerating growth in FY27. Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services is also expected to deliver more growth. Infosys is seeing strong growth in AI services, and the 55% net-new share of FY26 large deals provides support for future growth.

Management continues to see AI-related compression and competitive intensity. These conditions had not changed unusually from the previous quarter. The FY27 guidance reflects growth in the named industries, growth in AI services, AI-related compression, competitive intensity, and the impact of acquisitions.

#### AI services

#### Ritu Singh, CNBC TV18

Ritu Singh asked whether AI services revenue in Q4 FY26 exceeded the 5.5% of revenue reported for Q3 FY26 and asked about Infosys's target market share in the estimated $300-$400 billion AI services addressable market for 2030.

#### Salil Parekh

AI services growth was strong, but Infosys did not disclose the Q4 revenue figure. AI services represented 5.5% of revenue in Q3 FY26, and the Q4 percentage was higher.

The $300-$400 billion addressable-market estimate was for 2030 and came from an external study. Infosys is targeting a significant market share of that opportunity.

### Revenue guidance and acquisition contribution

#### Jayesh Sanghrajka

FY26 growth of 3.1% was achieved after absorbing:

- A 1% reduction from lower third-party-related revenue.
- A 70-basis-point reduction from a lower on-site mix.

For FY27 guidance:

- Stratus, the acquisition with an insurance company, had already closed and was included in the guidance. Its contribution was approximately 25 basis points, or one-quarter of a percentage point.
- Optimum was not included because regulatory approvals were still pending.
- The Australian-client joint venture was also pending regulatory approvals and was not included in the guidance.

### Operating-margin drivers

#### Jayesh Sanghrajka

Full-year FY26 operating margin was 21%. Q4 operating margin was 20.9%.

Compared with the previous quarter, the margin movement included:

- 50 basis points of impact from acquisition-related amortization.
- A 30-basis-point headwind from a one-off benefit received in Q3.
- A 20-basis-point impact from compensation-related matters.
- A 40-basis-point benefit from currency.
- A 30-basis-point contribution from Maximus performance.

Infosys has maintained a margin-expansion and margin-protection program for several years. Management said the program has supported margin resilience and that the company remains confident in the FY27 operating-margin guidance of 20%-22%.

### Hiring

#### Jayesh Sanghrajka

Infosys hired more than 20,000 freshers from the market in FY26. The company expects to hire at least 20,000 freshers in FY27.

### CEO tenure

#### Salil Parekh

Salil Parekh declined to comment on speculation regarding the extension of his tenure, which was scheduled to end in March 2027.

### Global uncertainties and FY27 demand

#### Mansee Dave, ET Now

Mansee Dave asked about margin sustainability and the effects of geopolitical tensions, global uncertainty, and cautious client spending on FY27 growth.

#### Salil Parekh

Management said the margin-expansion and margin-protection program had helped Infosys remain resilient despite multiple headwinds. The company expects continued pressure but remains confident in the FY27 operating-margin guidance of 20%-22%.

At the beginning of calendar 2026, the global environment and growth outlook in Infosys's strong markets appeared positive. Markets where Infosys was increasing its focus, including Japan, were also growing well.
