## Notes

1. On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Labour Codes. These Labour Codes consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during employment and post-employment. They introduce changes including a uniform definition of wages and enhanced benefits relating to leave. The adjustments represent an increase in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and an increase in leave liability, together amounting to $143 million (₹1,289 crore), recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.
2. Revenue growth in reported currency includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant-currency growth is calculated by comparing current-period revenues in respective local currencies, converted to US$ using prior-period exchange rates, with prior-period reported revenues.
3. Quarter and nine-month figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Accordingly, quarter figures added to figures reported for previous quarters may not always add up to the nine-month figures reported in this statement.
