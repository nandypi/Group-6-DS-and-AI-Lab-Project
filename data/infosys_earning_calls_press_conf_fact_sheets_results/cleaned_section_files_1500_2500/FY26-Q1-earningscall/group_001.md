## Infosys Limited Q1 FY26 Earnings Conference Call

July 23, 2025

## Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

Infosys had a strong start to FY26:

- Revenue grew 2.6% sequentially and 3.8% year-on-year in constant currency.
- Growth was broad-based, with the five largest industry groups and largest geographies growing year-on-year in constant currency.
- Large-deal TCV was $3.8 billion.
- Operating margin was 20.8%.
- Free cash flow was $884 million.

Growth was driven by Infosys' enterprise AI capabilities and continued client consolidation decisions. Infosys had built 300 AI agents across business operations and IT areas. The horizontal and vertical agents were described as supporting faster decisions, improved customer experience, and operational efficiency.

Examples of enterprise AI client work included:

- An oil and gas major using AI agents to improve refinery production quality, orchestrate dynamic retail pricing, and automate contract management for trading.
- A global manufacturing company using AI agents across its supply chain to improve productivity and reduce costs, and to resolve malfunctioning-equipment issues.
- A logistics company using AI agents across customer care, operations and logistics, and finance and accounting.
- A leading North American retailer using physical AI, intelligent automation, and edge-based computing vision to improve the in-store shopping experience, customer satisfaction, loyalty, and operational efficiency.
- A global financial services company using the Infosys Enterprise AI Solution with a fine-tuned large language model to translate code and automate documentation. The solution increased developer productivity by 25% and automated 50% of business-requirement creation and support for the modernization plan.

Following 19 leadership ratings received in FY25, Infosys was additionally positioned as a leader in Gartner's first Generative AI Consulting and Implementation Services quadrant. Management stated that Infosys was the only large India-based technology services company positioned as a leader.

Based on Q1 performance and the current outlook, FY26 constant-currency growth guidance was revised from 0%-3% to 1%-3%. FY26 operating-margin guidance remained unchanged at 20%-22%.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Infosys navigated a quarter of global uncertainty while delivering sequential growth, large-deal wins with strong net-new mix, resilient operating margins, high single-digit EPS growth, and free cash flow exceeding 100% of net profit.

Key Q1 FY26 results:

1. Revenue grew 2.6% sequentially, including 0.4% from acquisitions, and 3.8% year-on-year in constant currency. Sequential growth was achieved despite a 60-basis-point reduction in third-party costs, which declined to 7.3% of revenue.
2. Sequential growth was driven by increased realization under Project Maximus. Volume growth was muted but positive.
3. Manufacturing grew in double digits. Financial Services and EURS grew approximately 5% year-on-year in constant currency.
4. North America grew 2.9% sequentially in constant currency, ahead of the company. Europe grew 12.3% year-on-year in constant currency, more than three times the company average.
5. Operating margin was 20.8%, down 20 basis points sequentially and 30 basis points year-on-year. Sequential margin performance absorbed a compensation increase, higher variable pay, and sales and marketing investments.
6. Utilization excluding trainees increased 30 basis points sequentially to 85.2%. Utilization including trainees increased 80 basis points to 82.7%.
7. EPS grew 8.6% year-on-year in rupee terms and 5.8% in dollar terms.
8. Free cash flow was $884 million, equal to 109% of net profit. This was the fifth consecutive quarter in which free cash flow exceeded 100% of net profit. Management expected FY26 free cash flow to remain above 100% of net profit.
9. Consolidated cash and cash equivalents were $5.27 billion at quarter-end after payment of the FY25 final dividend. Yield on the cash balance was 7.2% in Q1.
10. ROE improved by 140 basis points to 30.4%, attributed to dividend payouts.
11. Infosys signed 28 large deals with total contract value of $3.8 billion, including 55% net new. Multiple vendor-consolidation deals had combined TCV of more than $1 billion, including a mega deal with one of the largest global banks.
12. By vertical, Infosys signed 9 deals in Communications, 6 in EURS, 5 in Manufacturing, 4 in Financial Services, and 2 each in Hi-Tech and Retail.
13. By region, Infosys signed 20 deals in the Americas, 6 in Europe, and 2 in the rest of the world.
14. Headcount at quarter-end was 323,788. Attrition increased marginally to 14.4%.

### Q1 FY26 Operating Margin Movement

Operating margin was 20.8%, a decline of 20 basis points sequentially.

#### Headwinds

- 100 basis points from compensation increases and higher variable pay, partly offset by other salary-related items.
- 30 basis points from currency movement.
- 20 basis points from sales investments.

#### Partly Offset by Tailwinds

- 70 basis points from increased realization due to Project Maximus and seasonality.
- 40 basis points from lower amortization costs on intangibles.
- 20 basis points from lower third-party costs.
