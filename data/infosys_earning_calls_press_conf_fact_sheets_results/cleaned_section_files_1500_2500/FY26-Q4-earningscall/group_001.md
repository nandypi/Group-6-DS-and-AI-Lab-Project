# Infosys Limited Q4 FY26 Earnings Conference Call

April 23, 2026

## FY26 and Q4 Performance

Salil Parekh, CEO and Managing Director, reported FY26 constant-currency revenue growth of 3.1% and Q4 year-on-year constant-currency revenue growth of 4.1%.

FY26 performance highlights:

- FY26 revenue crossed $20 billion and grew 3.1% in constant-currency terms, within the upgraded guidance band provided in January.
- Lower third-party costs reduced third-party costs by 1% as a percentage of revenue.
- Onsite mix declined by 0.7%.
- Acquisitions contributed approximately 70 basis points to full-year growth.
- Communications and Manufacturing grew at more than twice the company average. Europe also grew at more than twice the company average, led by the ramp-up of large-deal wins.
- Financial Services and Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services also grew above the company average in constant-currency terms.
- Full-year volumes were broadly flat. Growth was led by higher realization associated with Project Maximus.
- Adjusted operating margin was stable at 21%. Benefits from currency and Project Maximus were reinvested in talent, AI, sales, and marketing.

Q4 performance highlights:

- Revenue grew 4.1% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
- Revenue declined 1.3% sequentially in constant-currency terms because of seasonality and slower decision-making in March.
- Growth was broad-based across major geographies.
- Communications, Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services, and Life Sciences grew well above the company average year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
- Q4 operating margin was 20.9%, down 0.3% sequentially after adjusting for the Labor Code impact in Q3.
- Onsite mix declined to 22.8% from 23.1% in Q3.

## Operating Metrics and Financial Discipline

- Utilization excluding trainees was 83% in Q4 and 84.4% for FY26.
- Utilization including trainees was 81.1% for FY26, reflecting investment in future capacity.
- Technology interventions and a focus on collections reduced DSO, including unbilled revenue net of unearned revenue, to 78, the lowest level in seven years.
- Reported EPS in Indian rupees grew 23.8% year-on-year in Q4 and 11% for FY26.
- EPS adjusted for income-tax orders and the Labor Code grew 13.9% in Q4 and 12.1% for FY26 in Indian rupee terms.
- Free cash flow adjusted for the Labor Code and income-tax refunds was $882 million in Q4 and $3.5 billion for FY26.
- Adjusted free cash flow as a percentage of net profit was 111% in Q4 and 106% for FY26.

## Large Deals and Client Metrics

Infosys reported strong large-deal wins during FY26. Management cited large deals totaling $14.9 billion for the full year, representing 28% growth over the prior year, and also described the full-year large-deal TCV as approximately $15 billion with a 55% net-new mix. Q4 large-deal TCV was $3.2 billion.

The large-deal pipeline remained strong. During FY26:

- $50 million-plus clients increased by 3.
- $100 million-plus clients increased by 3.
- $400 million-plus clients increased by 2.

## Talent and Workforce

- Year-end headcount exceeded 328,000.
- Voluntary attrition declined by 1.5% to 12.6% for FY26.
- Infosys onboarded more than 20,000 freshers in FY26.
- The company expects to hire a similar number of freshers in FY27, with overall hiring calibrated to growth expectations and attrition trends.

## Operating Margin Movement in Q4

Q4 operating margin declined sequentially by 0.3 percentage points to 20.9%.

Headwinds included:

- 50 basis points from additional amortization of intangible assets related to a past acquisition.
- 30 basis points from normalization of a one-off gain in the previous quarter.
- 20 basis points from compensation-related costs, partly offset by lower variable pay.

These were partially offset by:

- 40 basis points from currency.
- 30 basis points from Project Maximus, comprising value-based selling, lean and automation, and critical portfolio initiatives.

## Client Spending and Demand

Jayesh Sanghrajka, CFO, said client spending remained guarded, with greater focus on cost-optimization engagements than on growth-led transformation programs. Infosys saw increasing momentum in AI-driven initiatives, particularly productivity, automation, and platform-led modernization.

## AI Strategy, Platforms, and Partnerships

Infosys identified a large addressable market for AI services across six areas:

- AI strategy and engineering
- Data
- Process
- Legacy modernization
- Physical AI
- Trust

Infosys cited Topaz Fabric as its AI platform and Cobalt as its cloud platform. The company said these platforms support client transformation across the six AI service areas.

Infosys has strategic collaborations with Anthropic and OpenAI for software development, legacy modernization, and agent building. It also has strategic AI collaborations with Google Gemini, NVIDIA, Microsoft, AWS, Google Cloud, and Intel, among others. More than 30,000 Infosys developers have been deployed on GitHub Copilot.

## Examples of AI and Modernization Work

- For Ralph Lauren, Infosys helped build a conversational and personalized AI tool that converted customer interest into a shopping experience. The work resulted in a 12% increase in revenue and a 50% increase in customer engagement.
- For Hertz, Infosys supported the migration of 3 million lines of COBOL code to a modern microservices environment using AI foundation models. The cost was 60% lower and the timeline was 60% quicker than the non-AI approach.
- For BP, Infosys deployed 50 AI-agent initiatives across trading, supply chain, sustainability, and core operations. The initiatives addressed software development, knowledge automation, legacy modernization, and digital decision support, resulting in 95% payment accuracy, 50% faster contract validation, and an 18% improvement in IT operations efficiency.

## FY27 Outlook

Infosys identified opportunities in AI services while noting continued competitive intensity and the potential productivity impact of AI.

FY27 guidance and outlook:

- Revenue growth: 1.5%-3.5% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
- Operating margin: 20%-22%.
- Growth in Financial Services and Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services is expected to accelerate from FY26 to FY27.
- The company expects the first half of FY27 to be stronger than the second half, consistent with normal seasonality.
