# Condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income under IFRS

## Notes

1. The information is extracted from the audited condensed consolidated Balance Sheet and Statement of Comprehensive Income for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. These statements were taken on record at the Board meeting held on October 16, 2025.
2. A Fact Sheet providing the operating metrics of the Company can be downloaded from the Infosys website.
3. Other income is net of finance cost.
4. Quarter and six-month figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Accordingly, quarterly figures may not always add up to the six-month figures reported in the statement.
