## Reconciliation of Reported IFRS financial measures to Adjusted non-IFRS financial measures for three months ended

The table compares reported IFRS and adjusted non-IFRS operating profit and operating margin for the three months ended March 31, 2026, March 31, 2025, and December 31, 2025. Amounts are in US $ million.

| Particulars | Mar 31, 2026 Operating Profit | Mar 31, 2026 Operating Margin (%) | Mar 31, 2025 Operating Profit | Mar 31, 2025 Operating Margin (%) | Dec 31, 2025 Operating Profit | Dec 31, 2025 Operating Margin (%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Reported IFRS | 1,055 | 20.9 | 992 | 21.0 | 937 | 18.4 |
| Adjustments (1) | - | - | - | - | 143 | 2.8 |
| Adjusted non-IFRS | 1,055 | 20.9 | 992 | 21.0 | 1,080 | 21.2 |

(1) The adjusted non-IFRS measures exclude the effect of provisions under the Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025. These provisions increased gratuity liability, arising from past service cost relating to a plan amendment, and leave liability by $143 million, recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income. This also resulted in a lower tax of $3 5 million in Q3'26.
