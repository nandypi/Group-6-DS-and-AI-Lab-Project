## Infosys BPM and Old National Bank Collaboration

Infosys BPM collaborated with Old National Bank on digital transformation, including process optimization, automation, and emerging AI-driven capabilities.

Jeff Newcom, Chief Operations Officer at Old National Bank, said the relationship had accelerated process optimization and automation. Old National Bank was also exploring AI and agentic AI to advance its capabilities and client delivery without building all capabilities internally.

## About Infosys

Infosys stated that more than 325,000 employees work across 63 countries. The company described its business as AI-first consulting and technology services, with more than four decades of experience managing enterprise systems and operations.
