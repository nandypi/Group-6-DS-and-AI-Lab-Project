# Revenue crosses $20 billion mark with resilient growth of 3.1% in FY 26 in constant currency

Infosys delivered FY26 revenues of $20,158 million, representing 3.1% growth in constant currency. Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.3%, adjusted operating margin was 21.0%, and EPS growth was 11.0% in rupee terms. Free cash flow was $3,733 million. Large-deal total contract value was $14.9 billion, with 55% classified as net new.

Q4 FY26 revenues were $5,040 million, up 4.1% year over year in constant currency. Q4 operating margin was 20.9%.

Salil Parekh, CEO and MD, said FY26 performance reflected resilient growth, strong large-deal wins, and market-share gains in large transformation opportunities. He said Infosys' AI services strategy across six areas was gaining market traction, supported by ecosystem AI partnerships, and highlighted the AI First value framework and Topaz Fabric.

## Guidance for FY27:

- Revenue growth of 1.5%-3.5% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%-22%.

## Key highlights:

### For the quarter ended March 31, 2026

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 4.1% year over year and declined 1.3% quarter over quarter.
- Reported revenues were ₹46,402 crore, up 13.4% year over year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 21.0%.
- Basic EPS was ₹21.01, up 23.8% year over year and 29.9% quarter over quarter.
- Free cash flow was ₹7,711 crore; free-cash-flow conversion was 90.6% of net profit.

### For the year ended March 31, 2026

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 3.1% year over year.
- Reported revenues were ₹178,650 crore, up 9.6% year over year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.3%; adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.
- Basic EPS was ₹71.58, up 11.0% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹33,097 crore; free-cash-flow conversion was 112.3% of net profit.

*For detailed explanations of items 1, 2, and 3, refer to the last page of the release.*

Jayesh Sanghrajka, CFO, said FY26 reflected disciplined execution and financial resilience, with a 21% adjusted operating margin and $3.7 billion of free cash flow. Savings from Project Maximus enabled investment in talent, AI, and sales and marketing. He said the company remained focused on margins and cash generation while navigating an evolving macro environment.

In line with its capital allocation policy, the Board proposed a final dividend of ₹25 per share. Together with the interim dividend and recently concluded buyback, shareholder returns for FY26 exceeded ₹37,500 crore.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys collaborated with Citizens Financial Group to accelerate AI-driven transformation across banking operations, product development, and customer experience. The engagement uses Infosys Topaz Fabric through Citizens' AI-first Innovation Hub to support modern, secure, and personalized banking capabilities.

- Infosys collaborated with ExxonMobil to develop and deploy high-efficiency cooling systems for the growing demands of AI and high-performance-computing workloads. The collaboration applies ExxonMobil's energy and thermal-management expertise and Infosys' AI and digital services to support smarter, more efficient, and resilient digital infrastructure.

- Infosys collaborated with Crocs on comprehensive IT and business-process transformation using AI-powered innovation and advanced automation. The objectives include optimizing operations, reducing costs, scaling responsibly, and supporting digital resilience.

- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Incora to use artificial intelligence to make global supply-chain operations faster, more accurate, and more resilient. The initiative supports modernization of Incora's aerospace and defense supply chain.

- Infosys and the University of Nottingham extended their strategic collaboration to strengthen the digital infrastructure of the University's Student Management System, with a focus on performance, security compliance, reliability, and innovation.

- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with ABN AMRO Bank to support the bank's ambition of achieving sustainable and profitable growth through 2028. The engagement focuses on simplifying and modernizing the IT landscape, responsible AI adoption, operational efficiency, customer value, and reducing complexity and operating costs.

- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Anthropic to support automated workflows, accelerated software delivery, and agentic AI solutions across complex, regulated industries. The collaboration combines Anthropic's Claude Code and AI capabilities with Infosys' domain expertise in telecom, financial services, and manufacturing.

- Infosys and Intel expanded their strategic collaboration to help enterprises move AI pilots into production at scale. The collaboration targets performance optimization and measurable enterprise outcomes using Intel's AI hardware ecosystem and energy-efficient AI solutions across data centers, cloud, and edge environments.

- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Cursor to help enterprises build and scale AI-powered digital solutions. The collaboration includes enabling more than 100,000 Infosys software engineers with agentic coding platforms.

- Infosys and Cognition announced a strategic collaboration to accelerate the AI value journey for global enterprises through agentic and autonomous engineering capabilities. Infosys' Exponential Engineering offering, Topaz Fabric, and Cognition's Devin platform are intended to support developer augmentation and autonomous engineering execution.

- Infosys Finacle and Producers Savings Bank Corporation announced an initiative to modernize the bank's technology landscape in the Philippines by upgrading to the latest version of the Finacle Core Banking Solution. The initiative is intended to enable faster and more personalized customer experiences, real-time banking services, and expanded lending capabilities.
