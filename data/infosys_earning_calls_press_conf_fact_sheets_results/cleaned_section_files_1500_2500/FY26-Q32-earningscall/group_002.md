## Satish H. C.

Infosys has emerged as an enterprise AI services and solutions provider. Eight industry analyst firms ranked Infosys as a global leader in 20 separate AI rankings over the previous 12 months. Infosys is delivering more than 2,500 generative AI and AI projects and more than 200 agentic AI projects for clients.

## The first one is making Infosys AI-first:

Infosys began its AI-first journey in 2023.

About 90% of employees are AI aware and equipped to collaborate with and responsibly use AI tools in their daily work. Within the top technology talent pool, 10% are engaged in innovative AI projects and solution building. AI masters and forward-deployed engineers are working on complex industry challenges for clients.

## On the process front

Infosys is using AI to reimagine its ways of working. AI code assistants are accelerating the development life cycle, and developers have produced more than 25 million lines of code using generative AI.

Infosys has deployed AI agents across internal operations. Its multi-agent invoice automation solution unlocked $50 million in incremental cash flow and directly improved free cash flow conversion.

AI has also been deployed to accelerate compliance processes. Some use cases have delivered more than 20x gains for specific activities, with overall end-to-end process productivity improvements of 40% to 50%.

## Now coming to our industry-leading AI offerings

Infosys has applied its AI capabilities to its own operations and delivers these innovations to clients through Infosys Topaz, a suite of generative and agentic AI-powered services and solutions.

Infosys delivers value through two strategic frameworks:

- `services.ai` builds a foundation for improved business services by accelerating IT capabilities and operations for Infosys and its clients.
- `client.ai` focuses on business transformation and enterprise-wide improvements in revenue growth, efficiency, and productivity.

The integrated services stack is a composable set of AI services and agents contextualized for each client and industry. It integrates human and AI agents to improve the velocity, productivity, and quality of IT services and operations.

For `client.ai`, Infosys has 22 industry blueprints and more than 400 agents tailored to specific verticals. Infosys also uses power vibing to rapidly build proofs of value and iterate business-solution prototypes for client problems.

## In terms of delivering enterprise value to surmount pilot paralysis

The difficulty of extracting value from enterprise AI investments and pilots remains a priority for global enterprises. Infosys identifies five key levers for delivering enterprise-scale value.

### Forward-deployed engineers

Infosys forward-deployed engineers provide specialized engineering talent embedded within client businesses.

For a global logistics leader, forward-deployed engineers co-created a solution using real-time data streams and AI to predict shipment life cycles across regions and operating companies. The platform:

- Delivers 400 million messages daily.
- Operates with sub-minute latency.
- Runs continuously, 24/7.
- Generated $1.5 million in immediate benefits.
- Produces $8 million in annual savings.
- Reduced customer service call volumes by 12%.

### Infosys Topaz data workbench

The Infosys Topaz data workbench provides solutions for data preparation, engineering, and governance.

For a leading industrial manufacturer, Infosys built a unified data fabric powered by more than 100 domain-driven multimodal data products centered on equipment operations. The data fabric covers more than 10 petabytes of structured and unstructured data and powers more than 30 AI companions across business functions, driving more than a 90% boost in precision, performance, and productivity.

### Infosys SLM

Infosys delivers small language models for context engineering in agentic AI solutions. These models are adapted to enterprise-specific needs.

Infosys has built four small language models for:

- Banking
- IT operations
- Cybersecurity
- Enterprises broadly

Infosys also offers these models as services so businesses can securely build their own custom AI models. Clients using Infosys Finacle use the small language model capabilities to launch new contextual banking experiences and innovations.

### Infosys Responsible AI Office

Infosys has established a Responsible AI Office and is among the first companies certified on ISO 42001:2023 for management systems implementing Responsible AI projects.

Its Responsible AI toolkit provides clients with defenses and technical guardrails to address AI-related risks.

### Infosys Polydelivery AI model

Infosys's hybrid or flexible Polydelivery AI model addresses dependence on individual AI providers and helps clients avoid vendor lock-in as they scale AI transformation.

Infosys helped a European bank establish an AI innovation lab to create a pipeline of AI-first business initiatives. The bank has deployed 13 AI and agentic AI solutions, with several more in development. The initiative delivered substantial financial gains and contributed to the bank being recognized as the number-one AI-first bank in its region.

### AI ecosystem, partnerships, and research

Infosys has established strategic alliances with Nvidia, Microsoft, AWS, Intel, Meta, Google Cloud, and other companies.

Infosys is among the first and largest enterprises to deploy GitHub Copilot at scale, with more than 22,000 developers onboard. In collaboration with Google Cloud, Infosys has developed more than 200 enterprise-grade AI agents.

Through the Infosys Innovation Network, the company engages with AI start-ups in AI, cybersecurity, data management, and other emerging domains.

Academic collaborations with Cambridge, Columbia, Cornell, Stanford HAI, and MIT support AI research and enterprise applications.

Infosys contributes to global AI standards, including an initiative with OWASP on large language model security. The company also advises policymakers and collaborates with regulators on emerging standards.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys is partnering with companies building large AI capabilities across multiple layers, including chips, infrastructure, models, and deployment.

On the enterprise side, Infosys expects enterprise modernization to benefit significantly from AI. Before AI, legacy modernization had defined timeframes and returns on investment. As AI tools improve and stabilize, they are expected to improve the economics and duration of modernization work.

Infosys can partner with enterprise AI companies because of its knowledge of clients' large and complex technology landscapes. Infosys can help deploy AI into those environments and make the implementations successful. Enterprise AI companies are developing solutions for areas such as sales and marketing growth, revenue generation, cost reduction through processes, customer service, and modernization.
