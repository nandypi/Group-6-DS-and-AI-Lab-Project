# Infosys Limited Q2 FY26 Financial Results Press Conference Call

October 16, 2025

## Q2 FY26 financial performance and guidance

Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, reported the following Q2 FY26 results:

- Revenue grew 2.2% sequentially and 2.9% year over year in constant-currency terms.
- Operating margin was 21%.
- Large deals totaled $3.1 billion, of which 67% was net new work.
- Infosys added 8,000 employees during the quarter.
- Following the Q2 performance, FY26 revenue growth guidance was raised from 1%-3% to 2%-3% in constant-currency terms.
- FY26 operating margin guidance remained 20%-22%.

Management said the second half of the financial year is typically slower than the first half. The increased lower end of the revenue guidance reflects greater confidence in the outlook, while the upper end assumes stability in the operating environment and the lower end assumes further deterioration.

An additional mega deal worth $1.6 billion was announced after the quarter ended but before the results announcement.

## Industry demand and macroeconomic environment

Infosys reported good performance in Manufacturing and Financial Services during the quarter. Retail continued to face constraints, although management said the pipeline in that sector was strong.

Management described the overall environment as uncertain. Some large markets continue to show growth, but inflation and constrained job creation remain factors. Other markets and industries are experiencing cost constraints. Infosys's presence across multiple industries helped support the quarter's performance.

## Artificial intelligence and client demand

Infosys said client interactions show strong interest in deploying AI across enterprises for both growth and cost-efficiency programs. The company is scaling its team of forward-deployed engineers and working on a broad range of enterprise AI projects, including:

- Sales and marketing use cases focused on growth.
- Process optimization and cost reduction.
- Customer service.
- Code development and software development.
- Knowledge processes.
- Credit risk.

In some cases, Infosys is helping clients transform their entire enterprises around AI. Management said the company is the AI partner of choice for a large number of clients and has partnerships with several large technology companies.

## H-1B visas and U.S. delivery

Salil Parekh said the majority of Infosys employees in the United States do not require Infosys immigration support, and that employees requiring company sponsorship represent a minority of the U.S. workforce. Infosys did not provide specific figures on the number of employees deployed on H-1B visas.

The company said it has built U.S. digital, innovation, technology, and AI centers and hubs, maintains relationships with universities, and operates a training facility in the country. Infosys has contacted clients to review delivery continuity and stated that it expects to support client services without disruption.

## Versent acquisition and M&A

The proposed Versent acquisition had not contributed to Q2 FY26 revenue because it was still undergoing regulatory approvals. Infosys expected the transaction to close in the coming months but did not provide an expected future revenue contribution before closing.

Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer, said Versent's prior-year numbers had been disclosed in earlier stock-exchange filings and could be used to estimate its scale.

Infosys said it was evaluating additional acquisition opportunities and had a good M&A pipeline, but did not specify when transactions would materialize or identify particular regions or capability areas.
