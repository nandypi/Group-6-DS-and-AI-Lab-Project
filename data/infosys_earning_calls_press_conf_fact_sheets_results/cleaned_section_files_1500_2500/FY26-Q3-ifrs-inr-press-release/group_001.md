# Strong Q3 Performance: Sequential Revenue Growth of 0.6% in CC, Large Deal Wins of $4.8 billion

Infosys delivered Q3 FY26 revenues of $5,099 million, representing year-on-year growth of 1.7% and sequential growth of 0.6% in constant currency. Reported IFRS operating margin was 18.4%, while adjusted operating margin increased 0.2 percentage points sequentially to 21.2%. Free cash flow was $915 million and adjusted free cash flow was $965 million, representing 112.8% of adjusted net profit. Large deal TCV was $4.8 billion, with 57% net new. Headcount increased by 5,043.

For the nine months ended December 31, 2025, revenue grew 2.8% year on year in constant currency. Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.0% and adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.

Salil Parekh, CEO and Managing Director, said Q3 performance reflected demand for Infosys' enterprise AI value propositions through Infosys Topaz. He said clients increasingly viewed Infosys as an AI partner based on its expertise, innovation capabilities, and delivery credentials. He also highlighted reskilling and transforming the workforce for an AI-augmented environment.

## Guidance for FY26

- Revenue growth of 3.0%-3.5% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%-22%.

The FY26 operating margin guidance excludes the ₹1,289 crore Labour Codes adjustment recorded in the current quarter.

## Key highlights

### For the quarter ended December 31, 2025

- Constant-currency revenue growth of 1.7% year on year and 0.6% sequentially.
- Reported revenue of ₹45,479 crore, up 8.9% year on year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin of 18.4%; adjusted operating margin of 21.2%.
- Reported IFRS basic EPS of ₹16.17; adjusted basic EPS of ₹18.53.
- Free cash flow of ₹8,176 crore; adjusted free cash flow of ₹8,626 crore.
- Adjusted free cash flow conversion of 113.1% of adjusted net profit.

### For the nine months ended December 31, 2025

- Constant-currency revenue growth of 2.8% year on year.
- Reported revenue of ₹132,248 crore, up 8.3% year on year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin of 20.0%; adjusted operating margin of 21.0%.
- Reported IFRS basic EPS of ₹50.64; adjusted basic EPS of ₹52.99.
- Free cash flow of ₹25,386 crore; adjusted free cash flow of ₹25,836 crore.
- Adjusted free cash flow conversion of 117.8% of adjusted net profit.

Adjusted financial measures exclude provisions arising from Government of India Labour Codes notifications for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025.

Jayesh Sanghrajka, CFO, said Q3 performance included 0.6% sequential revenue growth, 0.2 percentage points of adjusted operating margin expansion, $4.8 billion in large deal wins, and $965 million in adjusted free cash flow. He also said Infosys completed its largest-ever buyback of ₹18,000 crore and paid an interim dividend in line with its capital allocation policy.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Metro Bank to transform finance operations using Workday solutions.
- Infosys unveiled an AI-first global capability center model to accelerate the creation and transformation of global capability centers into AI-powered innovation hubs. Lufthansa Systems is collaborating with Infosys to establish a dedicated global capability center focused on aviation safety, operational efficiency, and customer experience.
- Infosys announced a collaboration with NHS Business Services Authority to deliver a workforce management solution for the NHS in England and Wales. The solution is intended to support workforce attraction, retention, and employee services.
- Infosys collaborated with Telenor Shared Services to modernize HR operations using Oracle Fusion Cloud Human Capital Management, with goals including process streamlining, unified data, and improved employee experience.
- Infosys launched Infosys Topaz Fabric, an agentic services suite and multi-layer AI fabric integrating infrastructure, models, data, applications, and workflows in a composable, agent-ready ecosystem. Nu Skin is collaborating with Infosys to develop Agent Assist capabilities for application and infrastructure support.
- Infosys launched the Infosys Customer Experience Suite for Salesforce to help enterprises scale digital workforces and support agentic transformation. VTT implemented a live SDR Agentforce agent with Infosys and Fluido and is developing two additional Agentforce agents.
- Infosys collaborated with Barry Callebaut on a multi-year, AI-powered digital transformation under the BC Next Level program, focused on customer experience, operational efficiency, connected ecosystems, and innovation.
- Infosys collaborated with Fresenius on ELEVATE, a business transformation initiative using SAP S/4HANA to unify and modernize global business processes and IT systems.
