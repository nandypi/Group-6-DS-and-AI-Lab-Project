# Infosys Limited Q3 FY'26 Earnings Conference Call

January 14, 2026

## Salil Parekh

Infosys had a strong Q3 performance:

- Revenue grew 0.6% sequentially and 1.7% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
- Large-deal TCV was $4.8 billion across 26 deals, with 57% net new.
- Adjusted operating margin was 21.2%.
- Free cash flow was $915 million.

The largest deal included a $1.6 billion contract with the U.K. National Health Service. Infosys will help NHS use AI to streamline operations and improve patient care.

Infosys expanded its Topaz AI capabilities with Topaz Fabric, an agent-services suite designed to help clients manage and implement AI agents across the enterprise.

Infosys also announced a partnership with Cognition to combine Cognition's Devin software agent with Infosys' knowledge of client landscapes and industry expertise. The companies are already working together across clients.

In financial year 2026, industry analysts recognized Infosys as a leader across 12 ratings. Infosys reported the following AI adoption and delivery metrics:

- It works with 90% of its 200 largest clients on AI initiatives.
- It is working on 4,600 AI projects.
- Its teams have generated more than 28 million lines of code using AI.
- It has built more than 500 agents.
- It is scaling its forward-deployed engineer team.

Clients are focusing on fragmented data, legacy application landscapes, and business workflows that are not conducive to AI. Infosys combines knowledge of clients' technology landscapes, data engineering, and process reimagination capabilities to support AI value realization.

Infosys identified six AI-services opportunity pools:

- AI engineering services
- Data for AI
- Agents for operations
- AI software development and legacy modernization
- AI and physical devices
- AI trust and risk services

Infosys plans to share a comprehensive view of its AI approach at an Investor Day later this quarter.

In Financial Services, Infosys saw traction in large deals and discretionary projects. Infosys expects acceleration in Financial Services and Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services in financial year 2027 over financial year 2026, based on deal wins and AI-preferred-partner status with 15 of its largest 25 clients in each of these verticals.

Following its Q3 performance, Infosys revised its financial-year revenue-growth guidance to 3% to 3.5% in constant-currency terms. Full-year operating-margin guidance remained 20% to 22%.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Q3 FY'26 performance reflected continued momentum from the previous two quarters. The quarter included a charge associated with changes in labor codes in India, which affected operating profit, net profit, EPS, and free cash flow. Adjusted figures exclude this impact.

Key Q3 FY'26 and nine-month FY26 metrics and commentary:

1. Revenue was $5.1 billion, up 0.6% sequentially and 1.7% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
2. Reported operating margin was 18.4%; adjusted operating margin was 21.2%.
3. Third-party costs as a percentage of revenue decreased 0.3% sequentially and approximately 2.4% year-on-year in Q3.
4. Nine-month revenue growth was 2.8%, at the higher end of the earlier guided range. Third-party costs were approximately 1% lower than in the same period last year and stood at 7.3%.
5. Financial Services grew 3.9% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
6. Europe led geographic growth, increasing 7.2% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
7. Volumes remained soft during the quarter and the year.
8. Revenue per person increased on a nine-month basis, reflecting value-based selling and productivity gains.
9. Adjusted operating margin increased 20 basis points sequentially to 21.2%.
10. Sales and marketing investment increased by double digits year to date and affected margins by approximately 50 basis points.
11. Utilization excluding trainees declined 1 percentage point sequentially to 84.1%. Utilization including trainees declined 2.2 percentage points to 80%, as Infosys continued creating capacity for future growth opportunities.
12. Nine-month adjusted operating margin was 21%, at the midpoint of the full-year guidance range, after absorbing accelerated sales and marketing investment and lower utilization.
13. On-site mix declined 10 basis points in Q3 and 70 basis points in the first nine months of FY26.
14. Net headcount increased by 5,000 to 337,000 employees. Last-twelve-month attrition declined 2% sequentially and 1.4% year-on-year.
15. Q3 large-deal TCV was $4.8 billion, with 57% net new. Nine-month large-deal TCV was $11.7 billion, exceeding full-year FY25 large-deal TCV. Nine-month net-new deal TCV increased 40% year-on-year. The large-deal pipeline remained healthy.
16. DSO, including net unbilled, declined by five days sequentially to 82 days.
17. Adjusted free cash flow, excluding the labor-code impact, was $965 million, equal to 113% of adjusted net profit. Nine-month adjusted free cash flow conversion was 118%.
18. Adjusted EPS in rupee terms increased 11.5% year-on-year during the first nine months of FY26.
19. Infosys completed its largest-ever buyback, returning ₹18,000 crores to shareholders, and paid an interim dividend for FY26 in line with its capital-allocation policy.

Adjusted operating margin expanded 20 basis points sequentially to 21.2%. The identified margin movements were:

### Tailwinds

- 40 basis points from currency movement.
- 40 basis points from Maximus, comprising primarily value-based selling, critical portfolios, and lean and automation.

### Offsets

- 70 basis points from furloughs and lower working days.
