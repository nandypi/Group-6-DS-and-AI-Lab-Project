## Disclaimer for Persons in countries other than India :

Except for the filing of this Letter of Offer with the SEC, the Letter of Offer has not been filed, registered, or approved outside India. Shareholders resident outside India must comply with applicable local legal and regulatory requirements. Participation is not permitted where the Buyback would violate applicable law or require additional registrations or approvals. Non-resident shareholders tendering Equity Shares are deemed to represent that they are legally authorized to participate.

The Letter of Offer was prepared for compliance with Indian Buyback Regulations, and its disclosures may differ from those required in other jurisdictions. The Company and the Manager to the Buyback are not required to update the information after the date of the Letter of Offer except as required by law.

## 4. TEXT OF THE RESOLUTION PASSED AT THE BOARD MEETING

The Buyback through Tender Offer was authorized and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on September 11, 2025.

### Buyback terms

Subject to shareholder approval by special resolution, applicable regulatory approvals, and exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on certain aspects of U.S. federal securities laws, the Board approved the following:

- Buyback of fully paid-up Equity Shares with a face value of ₹5 each.
- Buyback at ₹1,800 per Equity Share.
- Buyback Offer Size of up to ₹18,000 crore.
- Proposed buyback of 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up shares, representing 2.41% of fully paid-up share capital.
- Buyback Offer Size representing 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate of total paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.
- Buyback from shareholders as of a Record Date to be determined by the Board, on a proportionate basis.

The Buyback Offer Size excludes brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, public announcement publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, applicable securities transaction tax, goods and services tax, stamp duty, and other transaction and incidental costs.

The Buyback Period will begin on the date of declaration of the postal-ballot results for the special resolution and end on the last date on which consideration for the Equity Shares bought back is paid.

The Board or Buyback Committee may, up to one working day before the Record Date, increase the Buyback Price and reduce the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the Buyback Offer Size does not change.

### Eligibility and implementation

All equity shareholders as of the Record Date will be eligible to participate, including holders of American Depositary Shares (ADSs) who cancel ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date. ADS holders may re-deposit any withdrawn Equity Shares that are not tendered or are tendered but not accepted in the Buyback.

To the extent legally permissible, the Company may use the SEBI mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchanges and may approach BSE and/or NSE to facilitate implementation. Subject to the decision of the Board or Buyback Committee, either BSE or NSE will act as the designated stock exchange.

The Buyback will be undertaken through the tender-offer route through Indian stock exchanges and funded from the Company's free reserves, securities premium account, or another source permitted under applicable law. The required amount is intended to be met from the Company's current surplus, cash balances, and/or cash available from internal accruals.

### Small Shareholders

The Company may buy back Equity Shares from existing shareholders on a proportionate basis. At least 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of Equity Shares entitled to Small Shareholders based on their shareholding, whichever is higher, will be reserved for Small Shareholders.

For this purpose, Small Shareholders are shareholders whose shares or other specified securities had a market value of not more than ₹2,00,000, based on the closing price on the recognized stock exchange with the highest trading volume as of the Record Date. If shares tendered under the reservation are fewer than the reserved amount, the balance may be adjusted in the general category in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

### Non-resident shareholders

The Buyback of Equity Shares from non-resident members, including Foreign Corporate Bodies, Foreign Institutional Investors, Foreign Portfolio Investors, members of foreign nationality, and ADS holders with underlying Equity Shares, is subject to applicable requirements under:

- Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and related rules and regulations.
- Income Tax Act, 1961, and related rules and regulations.
- Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014, where applicable.
- Approvals from relevant authorities, including the Reserve Bank of India where required.

### Buyback Committee

A Buyback Committee comprising the Chief Financial Officer, Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer, and Company Secretary was constituted to support implementation of the Buyback.

The Committee was authorized to:

1. Seek all required regulatory approvals.
2. Initiate actions for preparing and amending the general-meeting notice or postal-ballot notice, as applicable, and issue the public announcement, Letter of Offer, and related documents.
3. Open, operate, and close necessary accounts, including an escrow account; issue bank guarantees; obtain banking credit facilities, including fund-based and non-fund-based limits; deposit acceptable securities with appropriate margin with merchant bankers; and enter into related agreements.
