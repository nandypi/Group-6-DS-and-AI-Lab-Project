## BSE LIMITED NATIONAL STOCK EXCHANGE OF INDIA LIMITED NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE

November 18, 2025

## Sub: Submission of Letter of Offer for Buyback of equity shares of Infosys Limited

Further to the Company’s November 6, 2025 intimation, Infosys Limited submitted its Letter of Offer dated November 18, 2025 for the shareholder-approved buyback of up to 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each.

The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route at ₹1,800 per equity share, for an aggregate consideration of up to ₹18,000 crore. The Letter of Offer contains disclosures specified under Regulation 8(i)(a) read with Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended.

The Letter of Offer will be sent electronically to eligible shareholders who have registered their email IDs with the depositories.

## Additional information pursuant to U.S. Law

The communication is for informational purposes only and is neither an offer to purchase nor a solicitation of an offer to sell securities. Offers to purchase or solicitations of offers to sell are made pursuant to a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO, including the Letter of Offer and other documents filed by the Company with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

Security holders are advised to review the Schedule TO, the Letter of Offer, related amendments, and other buyback documents before making a decision regarding the buyback.

## LETTER OF OFFER

## THIS DOCUMENT IS IMPORTANT AND REQUIRES YOUR IMMEDIATE ATTENTION

This Letter of Offer dated November 18, 2025 is being sent to registered equity shareholders and beneficial owners of fully paid-up Infosys equity shares as of the Record Date, November 14, 2025, based on the records made available to the Company by the depositories.

The buyback relates to equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each and is subject to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended.

## INFOSYS LIMITED

Infosys Limited proposes to buy back up to 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each. The offer represents 2.41% of the total number of equity shares in the Company’s paid-up equity share capital on a standalone basis.

The buyback will be made from eligible shareholders as of November 14, 2025, on a proportionate basis, subject to the small shareholder reservation, through the tender offer route. The price is ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash, for an aggregate consideration of up to ₹18,000 crore.

1. The buyback is being undertaken under Article 14 of the Company’s Articles of Association, Sections 68, 69, and 70 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, relevant rules, applicable LODR Regulations, the Buy-Back of Securities Regulations, 2018, and applicable SEBI circulars. It is subject to required approvals, permissions, sanctions, exemptions, conditions, and modifications imposed by the relevant authorities.

2. The buyback is subject to necessary approvals, permissions, consents, exemptions, and sanctions from statutory, regulatory, and governmental authorities, including SEBI and the stock exchanges on which the Company’s equity shares are listed: BSE Limited and The National Stock Exchange of India Limited.

3. Based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, the Buyback Offer Size represents:

| Basis | Buyback Offer Size as a percentage of aggregate paid-up share capital and free reserves |
|---|---:|
| Standalone basis | 24.31% |
| Consolidated basis | 21.68% |

Under Regulation 4(i) of the Buy-Back of Securities Regulations, 2018, the Buyback Offer Size is within the statutory limit of 25% of the aggregate fully paid-up capital and free reserves based on the last standalone and consolidated financial statements.

4. The Letter of Offer is sent electronically to all eligible shareholders as of November 14, 2025, in accordance with the Buy-Back of Securities Regulations, 2018 and applicable circulars or notifications. A physical copy will be provided if requested by an eligible shareholder.

5. The procedure for acceptance is described in “Procedure for Tender/Offer and Settlement” on page 53 of the Letter of Offer. The Form of Acceptance-cum-Acknowledgement, or Tender Form, and Form SH-4 are enclosed with the Letter of Offer.

6. The procedure for acceptance is also described in “Process and Methodology for the Buyback” on page 48. The mode of cash payment is described in “Procedure for Tender/Offer and Settlement – Method of Settlement” on page 58.

7. Copies of the Public Announcement and the Letter of Offer, including the Tender Form and Form SH-4, are expected to be available on the websites of SEBI, Infosys, NSE, BSE, KFin Technologies Limited, and Kotak Mahindra Capital Company Limited.

8. Eligible shareholders are advised to read the Letter of Offer, including “Details of the Statutory Approvals” on page 47 and “Note on Taxation” on page 60, before tendering their equity shares.
