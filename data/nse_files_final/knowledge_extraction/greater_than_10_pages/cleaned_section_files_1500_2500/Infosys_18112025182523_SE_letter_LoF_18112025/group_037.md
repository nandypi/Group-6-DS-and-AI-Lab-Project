# Infosys Limited

## Buyback offer

Infosys Limited’s Letter of Offer dated November 18, 2025 relates to a buyback of up to **10,00,00,000 (Ten Crore) fully paid-up equity shares**, each with a face value of **₹5**, at a buyback offer price of **₹1,800 per equity share**, payable in cash. The aggregate buyback consideration will not exceed **₹18,000 crore**.

Shareholders may indicate their India tax residency status as resident or non-resident, provide their country of residence, and specify the investment route for non-resident shareholders as either the Portfolio Investment Scheme or Foreign Investment Scheme.

## Shareholder declarations and obligations

By tendering shares, the shareholder confirms that:

1. The Letter of Offer dated November 18, 2025 has been read and understood, and the shares are being tendered under its terms and conditions.
2. Infosys is authorised to buy back the offered shares and instruct the registrar to extinguish them.
3. The tendered shares are free from liens, equitable interests, charges, and other encumbrances.
4. No restraint, injunction, or other order restricts the shareholder’s right to tender the shares, and the shareholder is legally entitled to tender them.
5. The buyback offer price will be paid after applicable Income Tax deductions and verification of the validity of the documents. Payment will be made through the secondary market mechanism and in accordance with the SEBI Buyback Regulations and applicable SEBI circulars.
6. The demat account must remain active and unblocked to receive shares returned because of rejection or prorated acceptance. The bank account linked to the demat account must remain active and updated to receive payment for accepted shares.
7. Any buyback consideration received wrongfully must be returned to Infosys.
8. The shareholder is responsible for calculating and paying tax on gains arising from the buyback and filing the applicable Indian tax return, in consultation with custodians, authorised dealers, or tax advisers.
9. The shareholder will indemnify Infosys if a tax demand is raised on the company in relation to gains arising to the shareholder from the buyback.
10. The shareholder will provide information about the taxability or non-taxability of buyback proceeds, Indian tax returns, tax payments, and related evidence when requested.
11. The shareholder will execute further documents and provide assurances required to give effect to the tender. The tender form is also treated as consent for filings under the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and related rules.
12. Invalid or unaccepted shares will be returned to the demat account from which they were tendered, at the shareholder’s own risk.

## Requirements for non-resident shareholders

Non-resident shareholders, including NRIs, OCBs, FPIs, foreign nationals, and FIIs, must provide a consent letter stating:

- The number of equity shares to be transferred.
- The name of the investee company: Infosys Limited.
- The transfer price: the price determined under the Buyback Regulations.

The consent letter must be signed by the shareholder or an authorised agent. If signed by an agent, a power of attorney must also be enclosed.

Non-resident shareholders must obtain and submit any required approvals from the concerned authorities, including approvals from the Reserve Bank of India under FEMA. They must also comply with applicable reporting requirements and rules relating to remittance of funds outside India.
