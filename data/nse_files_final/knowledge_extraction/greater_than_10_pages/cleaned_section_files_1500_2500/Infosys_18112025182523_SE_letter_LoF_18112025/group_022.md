## 20. DETAILS OF STATUTORY APPROVALS

- The Board approved the buyback at its meeting held on September 11, 2025. Shareholders approved it through a special resolution by postal ballot, with results announced on November 6, 2025.
- The buyback is subject to any approvals required under the Companies Act, 2013, the Buyback Regulations, and other applicable rules and regulations.
- Each eligible shareholder is responsible for obtaining any statutory consents and approvals required under applicable law to tender equity shares, including approvals from the RBI and/or SEBI where applicable. Copies of such approvals must be provided to the Registrar to the Buyback.
- Buyback participation by non-resident shareholders is subject to approvals from the relevant authorities, including the RBI where required. Non-resident shareholders must submit required approvals with the Tender Form. The Company may make payment where prior RBI approval is not required and may reject the tender where required approval has not been submitted.
- Non-resident shareholders, excluding overseas corporate bodies, who are permitted to participate under the general permission in India’s consolidated Foreign Direct Investment policy and applicable FEMA regulations are not required to obtain RBI approval.
- By participating in the buyback, eligible shareholders, including non-resident shareholders, undertake to complete all applicable regulatory and statutory filings and compliances, including Form FC-TRS where applicable. Shareholders authorise the Company, where necessary and solely to the extent required, to make such filings on their behalf and undertake to provide the Company with the required assistance.
- Because the Company has securities registered under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and has U.S. resident holders through ADSs and equity shares, the Company obtained exemptive relief from the SEC for certain tender offer procedures due to conflicting Indian and U.S. regulatory requirements.
- As of the date of the Letter of Offer, no other statutory or regulatory approval was required to implement the buyback beyond those described above. Any subsequently applicable approval could affect the buyback timetable, which would be notified to the Indian Stock Exchanges.
- Buyback participation by eligible shareholders resident outside India, including foreign corporate bodies, foreign portfolio investors, non-resident Indians, and members of foreign nationality, is subject to FEMA, the Income Tax Act, 1961, and related rules and regulations, including any required approvals from the RBI or other authorities.
- RBI, FEMA, and other applicable reporting requirements for remittance of funds are to be completed by the eligible shareholder and/or the shareholder’s broker.
- If the completed Tender Form and other documents are not received but the equity shares are credited to the Clearing Corporations’ accounts and a valid bid is present in the exchange bidding system, the bid will be deemed accepted.
- The Company has no outstanding debt from lenders and therefore does not require prior lender consent for breach of any lender covenant.

## 21. DETAILS OF THE REGISTRAR TO THE BUYBACK AND COLLECTION CENTRES

Eligible shareholders wishing to tender physical equity shares may send or hand-deliver the Tender Form, the TRS generated by the exchange bidding system, and relevant documents to the Registrar to the Buyback, marked **“Infosys Limited - Buyback 2025”**. The documents had to be received by the Buyback Window Closing Date, November 26, 2025, by 5:00 p.m. IST.

The Registrar to the Buyback is **KFin Technologies Limited**.

For eligible shareholders holding equity shares in dematerialised form, the Tender Form and TRS do not need to be submitted to the Company, Manager to the Buyback, or Registrar. After confirmation that a lien has been marked in the shareholder’s demat account in favour of the Clearing Corporation and a valid bid has been placed in the exchange bidding system, the bid is deemed accepted.

Eligible shareholders were instructed not to send the Tender Form, TRS, or other relevant documents to the Company or the Manager to the Buyback. Incomplete documents could be rejected.

## 22. PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY FOR THE BUYBACK

- The Company proposed to buy back equity shares for an aggregate amount of ₹18,000 crore at ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash. The buyback covered 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing up to 2.41% of the Company’s fully paid-up equity share capital on a standalone basis.
- The buyback size represented 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves based on the Company’s latest audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.
- The buyback was to be conducted on a proportionate basis through a tender offer under Article 14 of the Articles of Association, Sections 68, 69, and 70 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the relevant rules including the Share Capital Rules, and the Buyback Regulations.
- Shareholders approved the buyback by special resolution through postal ballot, with results announced on November 6, 2025. The buyback remained subject to any required approvals, permissions, or sanctions from statutory, regulatory, or governmental authorities, including the RBI, SEBI, and the Indian Stock Exchanges.
- The buyback was open to all eligible shareholders and beneficial owners holding equity shares in physical or electronic form as of the Record Date, including ADS holders who had submitted their desired number of ADSs to the Depositary and withdrawn the underlying equity shares.
- The Company expressed no opinion on whether eligible shareholders should participate in the buyback and advised them to consult their own advisors.
- The Promoters and Promoter Group held 54,20,29,249 equity shares as of the dates of the Public Announcement and Letter of Offer, representing 13.05% of the existing equity share capital.
- Under the SEBI Buyback Regulations, the Promoters and Promoter Group had the option to participate in a tender-offer buyback. They expressed their intention not to participate through letters dated September 14, 16, 17, 18, and 19, 2025.
- Assuming 100% participation and full acceptance from all other eligible shareholders up to their buyback entitlement, the aggregate Promoter and Promoter Group shareholding after completion of the buyback was expected to increase to 13.37% of the post-buyback total paid-up equity share capital, from 13.05% of the pre-buyback total paid-up equity share capital.
