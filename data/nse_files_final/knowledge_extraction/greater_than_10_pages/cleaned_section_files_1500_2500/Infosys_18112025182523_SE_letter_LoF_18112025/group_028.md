## 23.26 Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form:

### 23.26.1 Required documents

Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares who intend to participate in the Buyback must approach their stock broker with the following documents for verification:

1. Original Equity Share certificate(s).
2. Valid share transfer form(s), Form SH-4, duly completed and signed by all registered Eligible Shareholders in the same order as registered with the Company and witnessed at the appropriate place.
3. Self-attested copy of the Eligible Shareholder’s PAN Card.
4. Tender Form, signed by all Eligible Shareholders where the Equity Shares are held jointly, in the same order in which they hold the Equity Shares.
5. Other relevant documents, where applicable, including:
   - Duly attested power of attorney.
   - Corporate authorization, including board resolution and specimen signature.
   - Notarized copy of the death certificate and succession certificate or probated will where the original Eligible Shareholder is deceased.

If the Eligible Shareholder’s address has changed from the address recorded in the Company’s Register of Members, the shareholder must also submit a self-attested copy of one of the following address proofs:

- Valid Aadhaar Card.
- Voter identity card.
- Passport.

### 23.26.2 Bidding and Trade Reporting Slip

Based on the submitted documents, the stock broker will place the bid on behalf of the Eligible Shareholder through the BSE/NSE Acquisition Window.

After placing the bid, the stock broker will provide a Trade Reporting Slip (TRS) generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS will contain details such as:

- Folio number.
- Equity Share certificate number.
- Distinctive number.
- Number of Equity Shares tendered.

### 23.26.3 Submission of certificates and documents

The stock broker or Eligible Shareholder placing a bid for physical Equity Shares must deliver the original Equity Share certificate(s), the documents specified in Paragraph 23.11.2, and the TRS to the Registrar to the Buyback by registered post, courier, or hand delivery.

The documents must reach the Registrar within 2 days of the stock broker placing the bid. If the bid is placed during the last 2 days of the tendering period, the documents must reach the Registrar on or before the Buyback Window Closing Date.

The envelope must be superscribed:

> Infosys Limited - Buyback 2025

The Registrar will retain one copy of the TRS and provide an acknowledgement to the stock broker or Eligible Shareholder.

### 23.26.4 Verification and confirmation of physical bids

Physical Equity Shares will not be accepted unless the complete set of required documents is submitted. Acceptance is subject to verification under the Buyback Regulations and any further applicable directions.

The Registrar will verify the bids daily. Until confirmation by the Registrar, BSE/NSE will display the bids as “unconfirmed physical bids.” After confirmation, the bids will be treated as “Confirmed Bids.”

### 23.26.5 Shares submitted for dematerialization

Eligible Shareholders whose physical Equity Shares have been submitted for dematerialization should ensure that the dematerialization process is completed in sufficient time to participate in the Buyback before the Buyback Window Closing Date.

### 23.26.6 Shares held on a repatriation basis

Where Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis, a Non-Resident Shareholder must obtain and enclose a letter from the authorized dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval.

If the Non-Resident Shareholder cannot provide this certificate, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The shareholder must then submit a consent letter addressed to the Company permitting payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

### 23.26.7 Consequences of missing documents

If any applicable document is not enclosed with the Tender Form, the Equity Shares tendered by Non-Resident Shareholders under the Buyback may be rejected.

## For Equity Shares held by Eligible Shareholders, being Non-Resident Shareholders of Equity Shares (Read with Section 20 'DETAILS OF THE STATUTORY APPROVALS')

i. Non-Resident Shareholders, excluding FPIs/FIIs, must enclose a copy of the RBI permission received to acquire the Equity Shares, where applicable.

ii. For Equity Shares held on a repatriation basis, the Non-Resident Eligible Shareholder must enclose a letter from the authorized dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval, such as an NRE account.

If the shareholder cannot provide this certificate, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The shareholder must submit a consent letter addressed to the Company permitting payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

iii. Eligible Shareholders who are FIIs or FPIs must enclose a copy of their SEBI registration certificate.

If any applicable document is not enclosed with the Tender Form, the Equity Shares tendered under the Buyback may be rejected.

Participation in the Buyback is at the discretion of each Eligible Shareholder. The Company does not accept responsibility for the shareholder’s decision to participate or not participate, or for loss of share certificates or other documents during transit.

## 23.27 Acceptance of orders

The Registrar to the Buyback will provide details of order acceptance to the Clearing Corporation within the specified timelines.

## 23.28 Method of Settlement

### 23.28.1 Settlement of trades

Buyback trades will be settled in a manner similar to the settlement of trades in the secondary market.

### 23.28.2 Payment for dematerialized Equity Shares

The Company will transfer the Buyback consideration, net of tax deducted at source, to the Clearing Corporation’s bank account through the Company’s Broker in accordance with the secondary market mechanism and prescribed schedule.

For dematerialized Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback, the Clearing Corporation will make direct payment to the Eligible Shareholder’s bank account linked to the demat account.

If bank account details are unavailable, or if the fund transfer instruction is rejected by the RBI or the relevant bank, the amount payable will be transferred to the concerned stock broker’s account for onward transfer to the Eligible Shareholder.

### 23.28.3 Certain client types

For certain client types, including NRIs and foreign clients, where specific RBI or other regulatory requirements apply to funds payout and the client does not settle through a custodian, payment will be made to the respective stock broker’s settlement account for release to the Eligible Shareholder’s account.

Client type details will be collected from the Depositories. Funds relating to bids settled through custodians will be transferred to the custodian’s settlement bank account in accordance with the applicable mechanism prescribed by Indian Stock Exchanges and the Clearing Corporation from time to time.

### 23.28.4 Payment for physical Equity Shares

For Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares, funds payout will be made to the respective stock broker’s settlement account for release to the Eligible Shareholder’s account.

### 23.28.5 Cancellation and transfer of shares

The Company or the Registrar to the Buyback will provide the Clearing Corporation with details of each shareholder’s entitlement for the Tender Offer process.

After receiving these details, the Clearing Corporation will cancel excess or unaccepted blocked shares in the shareholder’s demat account. On the settlement date, all blocked shares relating to accepted bids will be transferred to the Clearing Corporation.

### 23.28.6 Inter-depository settlement

For inter-depository transactions, the Clearing Corporation will cancel excess or unaccepted shares in the target depository.

The source depository cannot release the lien without an IDT message from the target depository. The target depository will send the IDT message either on receiving a cancellation request from the Clearing Corporation or automatically after matching the accepted bid details received from the Company or the Registrar to the Buyback.

After receiving the IDT message, the source depository will cancel or release excess or unaccepted blocked shares in the shareholder’s demat account.

After the Tendering Period and receipt of the demat account details and accepted bid quantity, the source depository will debit the accepted bid shares from the shareholder’s demat account and credit them to the Clearing Corporation’s settlement account in the target depository on the settlement date.

### 23.28.7 Transfer of bought-back Equity Shares

Equity Shares bought back in dematerialized form will be transferred directly to the Company’s demat account opened for the Buyback, referred to as the “Company Demat Account,” if this is indicated by the Company’s Broker.

Alternatively, the Company’s Broker will transfer the shares to the Company Demat Account after receiving them through the clearing and settlement mechanism of the Indian Stock Exchanges.
