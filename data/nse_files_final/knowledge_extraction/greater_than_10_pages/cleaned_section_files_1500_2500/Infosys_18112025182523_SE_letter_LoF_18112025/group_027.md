## 23.25 Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialised form:

### 23.25.1

Eligible Shareholders who wish to tender Equity Shares electronically under the Buyback must do so through their respective stock broker(s), providing details of the Equity Shares they intend to tender.

### 23.25.2

The stock broker(s) must place an order or bid on behalf of the Eligible Shareholder(s) through the BSE/NSE Acquisition Window.

### 23.25.3

A lien will be marked on the tendered shares in the Eligible Shareholder’s demat account. The Depositories will provide details of shares marked as lien to the Clearing Corporations.

### 23.25.4

If the shareholder’s demat account is held with one depository while the clearing member pool and Clearing Corporation account are held with another depository, the shares will be blocked in the shareholder’s demat account at the source depository during the tendering period.

The Eligible Shareholder must initiate inter-depository tender offer (`IDT`) instructions at the source depository to the clearing member or Clearing Corporation account at the target depository. The source depository will transfer the securities from free balance to blocked balance and send an IDT message to the target depository to confirm creation of the lien. The target depository will provide details of the blocked shares to the Clearing Corporation.

### 23.25.5

For custodian participant orders involving dematerialised Equity Shares, early pay-in is mandatory before the custodian confirms the order. The custodian must confirm or reject the order no later than the time specified by the Indian Stock Exchanges on the last day of the tendering period, the Buyback Window Closing Date. Unconfirmed orders will be deemed rejected.

For confirmed custodian participant orders, any modification by the stock broker(s) will revoke the custodian’s confirmation. The revised order must then be resubmitted to the custodian for confirmation.

### 23.25.6

After placing the bid, the stock broker(s) will provide the Eligible Shareholder with a transaction registration slip (`TRS`) generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS will include details such as:

- Bid ID number
- Application number
- DP ID
- Client ID
- Number of Equity Shares tendered

If the completed tender form and other documents are not received, but a lien has been marked on the Equity Shares and a valid bid exists in the exchange bidding system, the Eligible Shareholder’s bid will be deemed accepted.

### 23.25.7

Eligible Shareholders must provide all relevant documents necessary to ensure transferability of the Equity Shares covered by the tender form. These may include:

1. A duly attested power of attorney if someone other than the Eligible Shareholder signed the tender form.
2. A duly attested death certificate and succession certificate or legal heirship certificate if an Eligible Shareholder has died.
3. Certified corporate authorisations, including board and/or general meeting resolutions, in the case of companies.

### 23.25.8

For dematerialised Equity Shares, submission of the tender form and TRS is not required. After the Clearing Corporation receives the dematerialised Equity Shares and a valid bid exists in the exchange bidding system, the Buyback will be deemed accepted for Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialised form.

### 23.25.9

Eligible Shareholders must keep their DP accounts active and unblocked to receive credit for Equity Shares returned because of rejection or the prorated Buyback determined by the Company. They must also keep the bank account linked to the DP account active and updated to receive payment for Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

Excess or unaccepted dematerialised Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders will be returned by the Clearing Corporation. If a security transfer instruction is rejected in the depository system, the securities will be transferred to the shareholder broker’s depository pool account for onward transfer to the Eligible Shareholder.

For custodian participant orders, excess or unaccepted dematerialised shares will be refunded to the respective custodian depository pool account.

### 23.25.10

If the Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis, an Eligible Shareholder who is a Non-Resident Shareholder must obtain and enclose a letter from the authorised dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval.

If the Non-Resident Shareholder cannot produce the certificate, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The Eligible Shareholder must then submit a consent letter addressed to the Company, permitting payment on a non-repatriation basis for the valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
