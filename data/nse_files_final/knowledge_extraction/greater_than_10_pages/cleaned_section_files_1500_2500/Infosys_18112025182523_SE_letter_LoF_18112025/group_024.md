## Clubbing of Entitlement

**22.24** To ensure that the same Eligible Shareholder(s) with multiple demat accounts or folios do not receive a higher entitlement under the Small Shareholder category, the Company will club together the Equity Shares held by shareholders with a common PAN for determining the category and entitlement under the Buyback.

For joint shareholding, the Company will club Equity Shares where the sequence of the PANs of the joint shareholders is identical. Where the PANs of all joint shareholders are unavailable, the Registrar will compare the sequence of the joint holders’ names and club Equity Shares where the names are identical.

For physical shareholders, where the sequence of PANs is identical and the PANs of all joint shareholders are unavailable, the Registrar to the Buyback will compare the sequence of the joint holders’ names and club Equity Shares where the sequence of the PANs and names is identical.

Holdings of institutional investors such as mutual funds, pension funds or trusts, and insurance companies with a common PAN will not be clubbed where the Equity Shares are held for different schemes and have different demat account nomenclature. Such holdings will be considered separately based on information prepared by the Registrar and Transfer Agent from shareholder records received from the Depositories.

Equity Shares held under the categories of “clearing members,” “corporate body margin account,” or “corporate body-broker,” with a common PAN, will not be clubbed where the Equity Shares are assumed to be held on behalf of clients.

## Miscellaneous

**22.25** For the avoidance of doubt:

- **22.25.1** Eligible Shareholders should tender Equity Shares from the demat accounts or folios in which they held the Equity Shares as on the Record Date.
- **22.25.2** Equity Shares accepted from each Eligible Shareholder will not exceed the number of Equity Shares tendered by that shareholder.
- **22.25.3** Equity Shares accepted from each Eligible Shareholder will not exceed the number of Equity Shares held by that shareholder as on the Record Date.
- **22.25.4** Equity Shares tendered above the number held by an Eligible Shareholder as on the Record Date will not be considered for acceptance.
- **22.25.5** In case of practical issues arising from rounding-off or otherwise, the Board or the Buyback Committee authorized by the Board may determine the final allocation, including any excess or shortage of Equity Shares after allocation under the process described in Section 22.

## Participation by ADS Holders

**22.26** ADS holders are not eligible to tender ADSs directly in the Buyback. To participate, they must have withdrawn the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs before the Record Date and become holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date.

To do so, ADS holders needed to:

1. Apply for and obtain a PAN from the Indian Income Tax Department to hold Equity Shares directly.
2. Establish an account with a bank, broker, or other nominee in India to receive Equity Shares in electronic or dematerialized form, and establish a brokerage account in India before the Record Date.
3. Submit the desired number of ADSs to Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, as ADR depositary, for cancellation with proper cancellation instructions sufficiently in advance of the Record Date.
4. Withdraw the underlying Equity Shares and hold them as of the Record Date.

As holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date, they may tender any or all of those withdrawn Equity Shares during the Tendering Period in accordance with the Buyback terms.

**22.27** ADS holders who cancelled ADSs and withdrew the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date will receive an entitlement based on the number of Equity Shares held as of the Record Date.

**22.28** Equity Shares trade on the NSE and BSE and cannot be traded on the NYSE. There is no guarantee that any Equity Shares withdrawn after ADS cancellation will be accepted in the Buyback.

The conversion of ADSs into Equity Shares and the redeposit of withdrawn Equity Shares for the creation of ADSs are subject to the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014 and the terms of the ADSs.

**22.29** If an ADS holder withdrew underlying Equity Shares after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date and before the last date of the Tendering Period, the holder may redeposit any such Equity Shares that are not tendered or are tendered but not accepted, and receive ADSs representing those redeposited Equity Shares, provided the Equity Shares and ADS issuance instructions are delivered to the Depositary’s Custodian on or before 30 days after the expiration of the Tendering Period. The Depositary has agreed to waive the ADS issuance fee for ADSs issued during this 30-day period.

**22.30** Withdrawn Equity Shares for which redeposit does not occur, or instructions are not given, within the 30-day period may be redeposited only if doing so would not exceed the specified maximum number of outstanding ADSs. Equity Shares withdrawn before the ADS postal ballot cut-off date or after the last day of the Tendering Period may also be subject to this maximum. The Company cannot assure that these Equity Shares will be eligible for redeposit. The ADS issuance fee will not be waived in these circumstances.

## 23. PROCEDURE FOR TENDER / OFFER AND SETTLEMENT

**23.1** The Buyback is open to Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical or dematerialized form as on the Record Date. It also includes ADS holders who submitted ADSs for cancellation and withdrew the underlying Equity Shares from the American Depositary Receipt program no later than the Record Date.

The Company will conduct the Buyback through a Tender Offer on a proportionate basis. The Letter of Offer and Tender Form will be sent electronically to Eligible Shareholders who have registered their email addresses with the Depositories or the Company. They will be sent by speed post or courier to Equity Shareholders in the United States who have not registered their email addresses. A physical copy will be provided on request. The procedure in Paragraph 23.11 applies if the Letter of Offer or Tender Form is not received.

**23.2** The Company will not accept Equity Shares:

- Subject to a court or competent-authority restraint order on transfer or sale.
- Whose title is under dispute or is otherwise unclear.
- For which loss of share certificates has been notified and duplicate certificates have not been issued.
- That are locked-in or non-transferable during the applicable lock-in or restriction period, in accordance with Regulation 24(v) of the Buyback Regulations.

**23.3** The Company will accept Equity Shares validly tendered by Eligible Shareholders based on their Buyback Entitlement. Additional Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders, if any, will be accepted in accordance with Paragraph 22.21.

**23.4** Eligible Shareholders must tender Equity Shares from the same demat account in which they held the Equity Shares as on the Record Date. Where Equity Shares were held in multiple demat accounts, applications must be submitted separately for each account.

If the demat account has changed, the Eligible Shareholder must provide sufficient proof to the Registrar. The tendered Equity Shares may be accepted subject to verification and validation by the Registrar. If sufficient proof is not received, the Board or the Buyback Committee authorized by the Board may determine the final allocation.
