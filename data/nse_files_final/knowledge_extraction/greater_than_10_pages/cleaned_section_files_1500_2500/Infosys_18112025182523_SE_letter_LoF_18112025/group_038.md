## INSTRUCTIONS

This Tender Form has to be read along with the Letter of Offer and is subject to the terms and conditions mentioned in the Letter of Offer and this Tender Form.

1. The Buyback will open on November 20, 2025 and close on November 26, 2025.

2. This Tender Form has to be read along with the Letter of Offer and is subject to the terms and conditions mentioned in the Letter of Offer and this Tender Form.

3. Eligible shareholders holding and tendering equity shares in dematerialized form are not required to submit the Tender Form and TRS. They must tender through their respective stock broker by indicating the details of the equity shares they intend to tender under the Buyback, following the procedure in paragraph 23.25 of the Letter of Offer.

4. Equity shares tendered in the Buyback may be rejected if:

   - The tenderer was not an eligible shareholder of the Company as on the Record Date.
   - There is a name and PAN mismatch in the eligible shareholder’s demat account.
   - A restraint order of a court or other competent authority exists in relation to the transfer, disposal, or sale of the equity shares.
   - Loss of share certificates has been notified to the Company.
   - The title to the equity shares is under dispute or is otherwise not clear.
   - Any other restraint subsists.
   - For eligible shareholders holding equity shares in physical form on the Record Date who placed their bid in dematerialized form under paragraph 23.29 of the Letter of Offer, the completed Tender Form and other required documents are not received.

5. Non-resident shareholders should, where applicable, enclose:

   - A copy of the permission received from the RBI to acquire the equity shares held in the Company.
   - Where the equity shares are held on a repatriation basis, a letter from the authorized dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the equity shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval.
   - If the required repatriation certificate cannot be produced, a consent letter addressed to the Company allowing payment for valid shares accepted under the Buyback to be made on a non-repatriation basis.

   If applicable documents are not enclosed with the Tender Form, the equity shares tendered under the Buyback may be rejected.

6. The Company will not accept equity shares offered in the Buyback that are under a court restraint order for transfer or sale.

7. If a registered entity has merged with another entity, the merger has been approved and come into effect, but registration of the successor company as shareholder is incomplete, the entity must submit the following documents with the Tender Form:

   - Approval from the appropriate authority for the merger.
   - The scheme of merger.
   - The requisite form filed with the MCA intimating the merger.

8. For the Clearing Corporation to make a payout exceeding Rs. 50,00,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Crore only), the eligible shareholder must provide a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) number. An eligible shareholder tendering shares valued at more than Rs. 50,00,00,000 must provide the LEI number and supporting documents, such as the LEI registration certificate, to the Registrar by 5:00 PM IST on the Buyback Window Closing Date.

9. Eligible shareholders must keep their DP account active and unblocked to receive equity shares returned because of rejection or because the Buyback is conducted on a proportionate basis under the Buyback Regulations.

10. If the Letter of Offer is not received, eligible shareholders may participate by submitting a signed plain-paper application. For jointly held equity shares, the application must be signed by all eligible shareholders and state:

    - Name and address of the eligible shareholder(s).
    - Number of equity shares held as on the Record Date.
    - Client ID number.
    - DP name and ID.
    - Beneficiary account number.
    - Number of equity shares tendered for the Buyback.

11. Eligible shareholders may tender equity shares covered by their Buyback Entitlement in whole or in part, or may tender shares in excess of their Buyback Entitlement, but may not tender more than their holding as on the Record Date.

12. The tendering procedure for eligible shareholders is set out in Section 23 of the Letter of Offer.

13. All documents sent by eligible shareholders are sent at their own risk. Eligible shareholders should safeguard their interests in this regard.

14. By participating in the Buyback, each eligible shareholder, including a non-resident shareholder, undertakes to complete all regulatory and statutory filings and compliances required under applicable law. Each eligible shareholder also authorizes the Company to make, sign, execute, deliver, acknowledge, and perform applications for regulatory reporting, if required, and undertakes to assist the Company with such reporting.

All capitalized items have the meanings ascribed to them in the Letter of Offer.
