## ALL FUTURE CORRESPONDENCE IN CONNECTION WITH THIS BUYBACK, IF ANY, SHOULD BE ADDRESSED TO REGISTRAR TO THE BUYBACK

The registrar identified for the Infosys Limited Buyback 2025 is KFin Technologies Limited.

## FORM OF TRANSFER DEED Form No. SH-4 - Securities Transfer Form

Pursuant to section 56 of the Companies Act, 2013 and sub-rule (1) of rule 11 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014.

The form records the transfer of securities from the named transferor(s) to the named transferee(s), subject to the conditions under which the securities are held.

**Company:** INFOSYS LIMITED  
**CIN:** L85110KA1981PLC013115  
**Listed stock exchanges:** BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited

## Transferors' Particulars

The form requires the registered folio number, full name, PAN number, and signatures of the transferor(s). It also requires witness confirmation, including the witness’s signature, name, address, and pincode.

## Declaration

The transferee must indicate whether:

- Government approval under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 is not required before the transfer of shares; or
- Government approval is required, has been obtained, and is enclosed with the form.

## Enclosures

1. Certificate of shares, debentures, or other securities.
2. Letter of allotment, if no certificate has been issued.
3. Copy of the PAN card of all transferees for listed companies.
4. Other documents, if applicable.

## On the reverse page of the certificate

The reverse page records:

- Name of the transferor
- Name of the transferee
- Number of shares
- Date of transfer

The transfer is signed by the authorized signatory.
