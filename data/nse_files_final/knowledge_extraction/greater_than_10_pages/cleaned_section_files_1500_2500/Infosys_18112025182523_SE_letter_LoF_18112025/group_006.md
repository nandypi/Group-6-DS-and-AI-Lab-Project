## Certain conventions, currency of presentation, use of financial information and stock market data

## Page Numbers and Paragraph Numbers

Unless otherwise stated, references to page numbers and paragraph numbers in the Letter of Offer refer to the pages of the Letter of Offer.

## Currency and Units of Presentation

References to “Rupee(s),” “₹,” “Rs.,” or “INR” mean Indian Rupees, the official currency of India. Unless otherwise stated, financial information is presented in ₹ crore.

## Financial and Other Data

Unless otherwise stated or required by context, the financial information in the Letter of Offer is drawn from:

- Audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for fiscal years 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- Audited interim condensed financial statements as of September 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis.

The Company’s fiscal year begins on April 1 and ends on March 31 of the following year. It is referred to as “Fiscal,” “Fiscal Year,” or “FY.”

## Stock Market Data

Unless otherwise stated or required by context, stock market data in the Letter of Offer is derived from the websites of the Indian stock exchanges.

## Important Notice to All Shareholders

The Letter of Offer has been prepared to comply with the Buyback Regulations and does not constitute an offer or invitation to sell securities in any jurisdiction where such offer or invitation is unauthorized or unlawful. Information disclosed may differ from information required under laws and regulations outside India.

The Letter of Offer will be sent electronically to Equity Shareholders whose names appear on the Company’s register of members as of the Record Date. Receipt of the Letter of Offer in a jurisdiction where the Tender Offer would be illegal or would require local registration or other action will not constitute an offer in that jurisdiction. Shareholders who tender Equity Shares are deemed to represent that they are authorized under applicable local laws to participate in the Buyback.

## Notice to Shareholders in the United States:

- The Buyback relates to securities of an Indian company and is subject to Indian laws and disclosure requirements, which differ from those in the United States. The Letter of Offer follows Indian format and style.
- Financial information has been prepared under the Act and Indian Accounting Standards notified under the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015. It may not be comparable with financial information prepared under U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles.
- The Buyback will remain open for a fixed period of 5 Working Days under Regulation 9(vi) of the Buyback Regulations, rather than the minimum 20 U.S. business days prescribed by Rules 13e-4 and 14e-1 under the United States Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
- The Buyback will be conducted in the United States pursuant to exemptions granted by the SEC from Rules 13e-4(f)(1)(i), 13e-4(f)(3), 13e-4(f)(8)(i), and 14e-1(a) under the Exchange Act. It will otherwise comply, to the extent applicable, with the Exchange Act, the Act, related rules, and the Buyback Regulations.
- Cash received pursuant to the Buyback may be taxable for U.S. federal income tax purposes and under applicable state, local, foreign, and other tax laws. Eligible Shareholders are advised to consult their independent professional advisors regarding tax consequences.
- U.S. Shareholders may face difficulty enforcing rights or claims under U.S. federal securities laws because the Company is incorporated outside the United States and some or all of its officers and directors may reside outside the United States.
- U.S. Shareholders may be unable to sue the Company or its officers and directors in a non-U.S. court for violations of U.S. securities laws or compel the Company or its affiliates to submit to the jurisdiction or judgment of a U.S. court.
- Neither the SEC nor any U.S. state securities commission has approved or disapproved the Buyback or commented on the adequacy or completeness of the Letter of Offer. Any contrary representation is a criminal offense in the United States.
- The Company expresses no opinion on whether Eligible Shareholders should participate in the Buyback. Eligible Shareholders are advised to consult their own advisors.

The Letter of Offer will be sent to Eligible Shareholders by email. A physical copy will be couriered if requested by a shareholder, if a U.S. shareholder has not registered an email address with the Depositories, or if an ADS holder requests a physical copy.
