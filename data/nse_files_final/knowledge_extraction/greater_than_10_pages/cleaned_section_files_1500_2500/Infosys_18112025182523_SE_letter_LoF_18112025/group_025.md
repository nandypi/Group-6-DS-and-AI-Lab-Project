For the Clearing Corporation to make a payout of more than ₹50,00,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Crore only), the Eligible Shareholder must provide a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) number. An Eligible Shareholder tendering shares valued at more than ₹50,00,00,000 must email the LEI number and supporting documents, such as the LEI registration certificate, to the Registrar at `infosys.buyback2025@kfintech.com` by 5 p.m. on the Buyback Window Closing Date.

- **23.5** As disclosed in Section 22 (Process and Methodology of Buyback), the Equity Shares proposed to be bought back are divided into two categories:
  1. Reserved Category for Small Shareholders
  2. General Category for other Eligible Shareholders

  The Buyback Entitlement of each Eligible Shareholder will be calculated according to the applicable category.

- **23.6** After Equity Shares are accepted based on Buyback Entitlement, any Equity Shares remaining to be bought back in one category will first be accepted, in proportion to the Equity Shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement, from Eligible Shareholders in that category. Thereafter, such shares may be accepted from Eligible Shareholders in another category who tendered shares above their Buyback Entitlement.

- **23.7** Purchases under the Offer will be paid for in Indian rupees, including to holders of Equity Shares resident outside India. Payment will be made within five Working Days of the Buyback Window Closing Date, as required by the Buyback Regulations. The Registrar will process bids after the Offer closes, and the Company expects to begin payments as promptly as practicable and within five Working Days of the Buyback Window Closing Date.

- **23.8** Participation in the Buyback is voluntary. Eligible Shareholders may:

  - participate in part or in full and receive cash for the Equity Shares accepted;
  - not participate and retain the resulting increased percentage shareholding after completion of the Buyback, without additional investment;
  - tender part of their Buyback Entitlement; or
  - tender Additional Equity Shares above their Buyback Entitlement and participate in any shortfall resulting from non-participation by other Eligible Shareholders.

  Acceptance of Equity Shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement will follow the procedure described in the Letter of Offer. If an Eligible Shareholder, or a person claiming to be an Eligible Shareholder, cannot participate for any reason, the Company, the Manager, the Registrar, and their officers will not be liable for such non-participation.

- **23.9** The maximum number of Equity Shares that an Eligible Shareholder may tender cannot exceed the number held on the Record Date. If Equity Shares are held through multiple demat accounts, the tender from each demat account cannot exceed the number held in that account on the Record Date.

- **23.10** The Buyback will be implemented using the “Mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeovers, Buy Back and Delisting” under the applicable SEBI circulars, and in accordance with the Companies Act, the Buyback Regulations, and procedures and terms determined by the Board, including any authorized committee, as permitted by law.

Non-receipt of, or accidental failure to dispatch, the Letter of Offer to an eligible person will not invalidate the Buyback Offer. The Company will accept Equity Shares validly tendered based on the holding and entitlement recorded in its records as of the Record Date.
