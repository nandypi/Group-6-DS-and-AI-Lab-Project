## INDEPENDENTAUDITOR'S REPORTONTHEAUDITOFQUARTERLYSTANDALONE FINANCIALRESULTS

## Opinion

We have audited the accompanying statement of standalone financial results of Infosys Limited, submitted pursuant to the requirements of Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (the “LODR Regulations”).

In our opinion, and based on the information, explanations, and audit evidence obtained:

- The statement is presented in accordance with the requirements of Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- The statement gives a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles under the relevant rules and other accounting principles generally accepted in India, of the net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information of the Company for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Act. The auditors stated that they were independent of the Company in accordance with the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), together with the ethical requirements relevant to the audit of the standalone financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 under the Act and related rules. The auditors also stated that they had fulfilled their other ethical responsibilities in accordance with these requirements and the ICAI Code of Ethics.

The auditors stated that the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for their audit opinion.

## Management'sandBoardofDirectors'Responsibilitiesfor theStatement

The standalone financial results were the responsibility of the Company’s Board of Directors and were approved by them for issuance. The statement was compiled from the related audited interim condensed standalone financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

This responsibility included preparing and presenting standalone financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 that give a true and fair view of net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information in accordance with the recognition and measurement principles laid down in Ind AS 34, prescribed under Section 133 of the Act and related rules, and other accounting principles generally accepted in India, and in compliance with Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
