## Shares transferred to the IEPF

During the financial year, the Company transferred shares to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) because dividends remained unclaimed for seven consecutive years:

- 82,811 shares on October 10, 2025
- 6,34,785 shares on January 09, 2026
- 6,124 shares on March 20, 2026

The IEPF processed and settled claims relating to 1,20,193 shares, returning them to the respective shareholders. As of March 31, 2026, the IEPF held 14,35,117 shares transferred under the applicable Rules.

During the year, the Company transferred ₹3,39,64,248 to the IEPF, representing corporate benefits (dividends) accrued on shares already transferred.

## Share transfer system

SEBI, effective April 01, 2019, barred physical transfer of shares of listed companies and mandated transfers only through demat. Investors may continue to hold shares in physical form, but the Company has requested shareholders to dematerialize their shares.

Shareholders holding shares in dematerialized mode have been requested to register their email address, bank account details, and mobile number with their depository participants. Shareholders holding shares in physical mode have been requested to furnish PAN, nomination, contact details, bank account details, and specimen signature for their corresponding folios.

Pursuant to a SEBI Circular dated July 2, 2025, a special window was provided from July 7, 2025 to January 6, 2026 for re-lodgement of transfer requests lodged before April 1, 2019 and pending due to deficiencies. This facility was extended by a SEBI Circular dated January 30, 2026 until February 4, 2027.

Eligible shareholders may submit the requisite documents within the extended timeline. Securities will be credited only in demat form and will be subject to a one-year lock-in, during which they cannot be transferred, pledged, or encumbered.

## Investor conferences / events held in fiscal 2026

Infosys holds press meets and investor or analyst calls after each quarterly results announcement. The Company also holds its Annual General Meeting and participates in sell-side or broker-arranged investor conferences and roadshows. Details of these events are sent to the stock exchanges and updated on the Company's website.

## Investor grievances and investor contacts

The Company has a Board-level Stakeholders Relationship Committee to examine and redress shareholder and investor complaints. The status of complaints is reported to the entire Board, and the Committee meets as often as required.

Most investor grievances and postal or electronic communications were addressed within seven days of receipt. Exceptions involved cases constrained by disputes or legal impediments.

Share transfers, dividend payments, and other investor-related activities are processed at the office of the Company's Registrar and Transfer Agent, KFin Technologies Limited.

## Company shares-related disputes / litigation

Certain pending civil cases involve rival claims by parties seeking declaration of title and accrued benefits relating to the Company's disputed shares. Infosys Limited and its Registrar and Transfer Agent, KFin Technologies Limited, are pro forma defendants in these matters. The matters are not material in nature.
