### Material topics

- Innovation and Intellectual Property
- Products, platforms and solutions
- Responsible AI

### Performance highlights

- 100+ Client Living Labs
- 7 Applied Research Centers
- 973 patents (granted or pending)
- 2,000+ Infosys Knowledge Institute assets

- iCETS platforms received analyst recognition, including an industry-leading rating.

- 430+ startups are part of the Infosys Innovation Network and have generated 1,300+ client impressions.

Infosys' intellectual capital is supported by collaboration with experts, partners, academia, and other stakeholders to develop products and services. Its key innovation platforms and programs include the Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (iCETS), Client Living Labs, the Infosys Innovation Network, and the Infosys Business Incubator. The Infosys Prize and Aarohan Social Innovation Awards provide platforms for innovators and social entrepreneurs.

### Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (iCETS)

iCETS is Infosys' emerging technology incubation unit. It delivers next-generation platforms and innovation-as-a-service powered by New Emerging and eXploratory Technologies (NEXT).

iCETS uses a structured innovation framework covering Now, Near, and Next:

#### Now

Infosys Topaz Fabric provides composable and interoperable AI agents, tools, services, and models. It is designed to help organizations optimize existing IT processes, automate or eliminate manual tasks, and build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

#### Near

Infosys Living Labs are global innovation hubs where clients, partners, startups, and academia co-create solutions using emerging technologies. They support contextualization, experimentation, iteration, and validation in controlled environments.

Client Living Labs deliver innovation-as-a-service through three programs:

- IncubateNext: scalable, real-world innovation
- ImagineNext: future opportunity discovery
- SenseNext: converting early market and technology signals into action

#### Next

Infosys Applied Research Centers innovate across:

- Advanced AI
- New Interaction Models
- Cybersecurity
- Autonomous Machines
- Humanoids
- Quantum

### Nurturing innovation

The Be The Navigator (BTN) program provides a structured framework for ideation, prototyping, and incubation. A total of 12,435 BTN ideas were submitted, 2,429 implementations were completed, and 1,101 were in progress.

### Infosys Innovation Network (IIN)

The Infosys Innovation Network is a partnership of select startups, universities, hyperscalers, and Infosys. It focuses on emerging technology areas including AI, generative AI, data, fintech, cloud, cybersecurity, and InsurTech.

IIN includes more than 430 startups, which have generated more than 1,300 client impressions.

### Infosys Business Incubator (IBI)

The Infosys Business Incubator develops and scales entrepreneurship capabilities within Infosys through micro-platform and micro-service-line businesses. It engages with new cohorts of businesses each year.

IBI has engaged with more than 1,000 entrepreneurs. Five ventures were selected for incubation from cohort 1 and three from cohort 2.

The Be A Maker program supports the showcasing of creative ideas in a phygital mode. It is mentored by ENGG, Kaleidoscope, and iCETS. More than 200 ideas have been generated and five phygital showcases have been created for clients.

### Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI)

The Infosys Knowledge Institute is the business research and thought leadership arm of Infosys. It uses expertise from Infosys and client collaborations to produce content and insights on the business impact of technology. IKI also develops proprietary data and insights through primary research, including large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis.

Its publications include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals.

IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets since inception.

### Product innovation

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multiagent enterprise workflows across humans, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve's product innovation with Infosys' global scale and is intended for enterprise operations and core business functions.

### IP, patents and trademarks

Infosys develops platforms, products, and tools that form part of its intellectual property portfolio. These assets are available on the Infosys Marketplace and are used as market differentiators or productivity-enhancing tools.

- 973 patents are granted or pending.

Its AI work includes process improvement, engineering, customer service, cybersecurity, and employee productivity. Infosys has built four small language models for banking, IT operations, cybersecurity, and enterprises broadly.

### Advancing climate positivity

In FY26, Infosys continued its transition from carbon neutrality to climate positivity through initiatives covering operational efficiency, clean energy, carbon sequestration, regenerative solutions, value chains, and ecosystems.

### 1. Energy efficiency and low-carbon approach

Infosys incorporates energy efficiency across the life cycle of its buildings. Its campuses use radiant cooling, radiant heating, climate-adaptive cooling, intelligent controls, and climate-responsive design.

A total of 84% of Infosys' office space is certified to the highest green building standards.

### 2. Renewable energy

Infosys has installed 62.42 MWp of solar photovoltaic capacity through rooftop and ground-mounted systems.

The company also procures renewable power through third-party power purchase agreements and green tariff mechanisms, adapting its sourcing strategy to regulatory requirements across geographies.

### 3. Decarbonizing the supply chain

Infosys is engaging high-emission IT hardware suppliers through onsite assessments and senior-level discussions. It supports suppliers in measuring Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions, establishing baselines, and adopting verified Environmental Product Declarations.

### 4. Carbon offsets and regeneration

Infosys addresses unavoidable emissions through community-focused carbon offset programs, including clean cookstove initiatives in Rajasthan and biogas projects in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

Its corporate agroforestry program has planted approximately 14 million saplings across multiple states. These plantations support carbon sequestration, soil and water resilience, and rural livelihoods.

The initiatives have benefited more than 300,000 rural families and created more than 4,800 local jobs.

### Water stewardship

Infosys' water stewardship initiatives focus on reducing freshwater withdrawal, increasing reuse, and improving water availability in operating communities. Infosys is a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate.

All wastewater across its campuses is recycled, supported by rainwater harvesting and demand-side efficiency measures. The Bangalore campus is certified as water positive.

Based on NITI AAYOG guidelines, Infosys achieved Scope 1 certification at nine campuses in India during the year as part of its efforts toward water-positive or water-neutral campuses.
