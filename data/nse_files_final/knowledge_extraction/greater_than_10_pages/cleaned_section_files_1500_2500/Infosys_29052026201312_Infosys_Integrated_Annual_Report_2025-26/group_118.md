a. The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared and paid by the Company during the year, was in accordance with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

b. The interim dividend declared and paid by the Company during the year and until the date of this report was in compliance with Section 123 of the Act.

c. The Board of Directors proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by the members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed dividend was in accordance with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

Based on an examination that included test checks, the Company and its subsidiaries incorporated in India, except for the instances mentioned below, used accounting software systems for maintaining their books of account for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The systems had an audit trail (edit log) facility, which operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions recorded in the systems. No instance of tampering with the audit trail feature was identified during the audit. The Parent Company and the relevant Indian subsidiaries preserved the audit trail in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

The financial statements of five subsidiaries that were not material to the Consolidated Financial Statements of the Group had not been audited under the provisions of the Act as of the date of the report. Therefore, the auditors were unable to comment on the reporting requirement under Rule 11(g) of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014, for those five subsidiaries.

With respect to matters specified in paragraphs 3(xxi) and 4 of the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020 (CARO), based on the information and explanations provided and the Auditor's Reports included in the Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, no qualifications or adverse remarks were reported for companies whose audits under Section 143 of the Act had been completed.

For the following subsidiaries included in the consolidated financial statements, audits under Section 143 of the Act had not yet been completed, and the applicable CARO reports were not available:
