The Board of Directors of the Company, including the Nomination and Remuneration Committee where applicable, approved the amendments to the existing plan at its meeting held on April 23, 2026.

The amendments extend the term of the existing performance-based stock incentive plan to incentivize, retain, and attract key talent across an expanded employee base. The Board also approved changes to the performance parameters to:

- Strengthen alignment with long-term shareholder value creation and investor expectations.
- Reinforce a long-term performance orientation within the organization.
- Align operating performance metrics with relevant financial metrics.

The Amended 2019 Plan is available for shareholder review in the company’s investor filings.

The resolutions under Items 4 and 5 seek shareholder approval to amend the existing plan and authorize the Board to grant RSUs and create, issue, offer, and provide equity shares, American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), or cash from time to time to eligible employees of the Company or its subsidiaries under the Amended 2019 Plan.

Pursuant to Regulations 6 and 14 of the SEBI Regulations and Section 67(3)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 16(2) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, the key details of the Amended 2019 Plan are set out below.

All capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings assigned to them in the 2019 Plan.

## 1.  Brief description of the Amended 2019 Plan

The purpose of the Amended 2019 Plan is to:

- Encourage employees to align individual performance with the Company’s shareholder value creation objectives.
- Attract, retain, and motivate talented and identified employees.
- Reward employee performance with ownership.

The Amended 2019 Plan grants Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) to eligible employees. Subject to applicable law and the plan conditions, eligible employees may receive equity shares, ADRs, or cash upon exercise of the RSUs.

The plan is administered by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), referred to as the Administrator. For secondary acquisition and related administrative matters, administration may also be delegated to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. The Administrator’s decisions, determinations, and interpretations are final and binding on eligible employees and participants.

Each RSU grant is evidenced by an award agreement specifying the applicable terms and conditions.

## 2. Total number of shares or benefits to be granted

The total number of equity shares and ADRs that may be provided upon exercise of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries shall not cumulatively exceed **3,26,63,903 equity shares**.

This represents the balance of unutilized equity shares from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**, or approximately **0.8% of the issued capital** as of the date of the notice.

## 3.  Identification of classes of employees entitled to participate and be beneficiaries in the 2019 Plan

Employees, as defined under the SEBI Regulations, of the Company and its subsidiaries, whether working in India or abroad, may participate in the Amended 2019 Plan subject to eligibility criteria specified in the SEBI Regulations or determined by the Administrator.

Under the SEBI Regulations and the Companies Act, 2013, the following are not eligible to participate:

- Employees who are promoters or members of the promoter group.
- Independent directors.
- Directors who, directly or indirectly, either themselves or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company’s outstanding equity shares.

The appraisal process described in paragraph 8 is considered when determining RSU grants.

## 4. Requirements of vesting and period of vesting

RSUs granted under the Amended 2019 Plan vest based on three performance parameters. The NRC determines the relative weighting of each parameter, subject to the following conditions:

- Each parameter must have a weighting of at least 25%.
- No single parameter may have a weighting of more than 50%.

### i) Long-term Relative Total Shareholder Return

Long-term Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR) is measured using rolling three-year TSR against a peer comparator group of at least eight companies. The Administrator will identify the comparator group from the following 10 industry peers:

- Accenture
- DXC Technologies
- Wipro
- TCS
- Cognizant
- IBM
- Capgemini
- Tech Mahindra
- Atos
- HCL

If any peer company is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company in the comparator group.

The Company will transition in phases to a three-year rolling Relative TSR parameter:

- Year 1: one-year TSR
- Year 2: two-year TSR
- Year 3 onward: full three-year TSR

### ii) Annual TSR

Annual TSR comprises:

**A. Relative TSR against industry peers**

Relative TSR is measured against a comparator group of at least eight companies selected from the following 10 industry peers:

- Accenture
- DXC Technologies
- Wipro
- TCS
- Cognizant
- IBM
- Capgemini
- Tech Mahindra
- Atos
- HCL

If any peer company is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company.

**B. Relative TSR against broader market indices**

Relative TSR is measured against domestic and global market indices comprising **MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50**, as determined by the Administrator.

### iii) Annual operating performance metrics

Annual operating performance is measured using:

- Total revenue growth
- Operating margin

These metrics are used to derive shareholder value. The Administrator determines the performance targets for each metric.

Each of the three performance parameters is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest.

### Vesting based on Relative TSR

For Relative TSR under parameters (i) and (ii):

- At or above the 75th percentile, 100% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- Between the 50th and 75th percentile, RSUs will vest proportionately on a linear scale between 60% and 100%; the balance will lapse.
- Below the 50th percentile, none of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.

### Vesting based on operating performance

For the operating performance metrics under parameter (iii):

- At the minimum or threshold performance level, 75% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- Below the minimum or threshold level, none of the RSUs due for vesting will vest and the RSUs will lapse.
- At the target performance level, 100% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- At the stretch target performance level, 125% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- Above the threshold level and below the target level, RSUs will vest proportionately on a linear scale between 75% and 100%; the balance will lapse.
- Above the target level and below the stretch target level, RSUs will vest proportionately on a linear scale between 100% and 125%.

Although individual performance outcomes may exceed 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter shall not exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter.

Any increased dilution of equity share capital or cost of secondary acquisition of equity shares is expected by the Company to be offset by growth in shareholder value and increased shareholder returns.

All RSUs vest according to the schedule determined by the Administrator from the grant date. RSUs that lapse or are forfeited may be used for future grants.

The Company will disclose in subsequent annual reports:

- The performance parameters and their weightings.
- Target achievement for each performance parameter.
- Actual achievement during the year.
- The basis on which RSU vesting was permitted.
- The number of employees covered.
- The number of RSUs granted.
- The number of employees whose RSUs vested.
- The number of RSUs due for vesting under the vesting schedule.
- The number of RSUs vested after meeting the vesting conditions.

The Company has a Compensation Recovery Policy intended to support its pay-for-performance philosophy. The policy is available on the Company’s investor relations website.

## 5.  Maximum period within which the RSUs shall vest

RSUs shall vest over a minimum period of **1 year** and a maximum period of **3 years** from the grant date, as determined by the Administrator and specified in the award agreement.

No vesting tranche may exceed **50% of the total grants** made under the plan.

When determining an eligible employee’s vesting schedule, the Administrator may consider:

- Alignment with accelerated shareholder value creation.
- Retention of key talent.
- Future potential.
- Tenure.

RSUs vest based on achievement of the performance criteria described in paragraph 4.
