## B)  Login method for e-voting and voting during the meeting for shareholders other than individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode and shareholders holding securities in physical mode

### How to Log-in to NSDL e-voting website?

1. Visit the NSDL e-voting website at [https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/](https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/).
2. Select **Login** under the **Shareholder/Member** section.
3. Enter the User ID, password or OTP, and verification code.
4. Shareholders registered for NSDL e-services through IDeAS may log in through [https://eservices.nsdl.com/](https://eservices.nsdl.com/), select e-voting, and proceed to cast their vote.

User ID details are as follows:

| Manner of holding shares | User ID |
|---|---|
| Demat account with NSDL | 8-character DP ID followed by the 8-digit Client ID. Example: if the DP ID is `IN300***` and the Client ID is `12******`, the User ID is `IN300***12******`. |
| Demat account with CDSL | 16-digit Beneficiary ID. |
| Physical mode | EVEN Number followed by the Folio Number registered with the Company. Example: if the Folio Number is `001***` and the EVEN is `139407`, the User ID is `139407001***`. |

### Password details

- Shareholders already registered for e-voting may use their existing password.
- First-time users must retrieve the initial password communicated to them and use it to access the system and change their password.
- If the email ID is registered in the demat account or with the Company, the initial password is sent by email in a PDF attachment. The PDF password is:
  - the 8-digit Client ID for an NSDL account;
  - the last 8 digits of the Client ID for a CDSL account; or
  - the Folio Number for physical shares.
- If the initial password has not been received or has been forgotten, shareholders may use the **Forgot User Details / Password?** option for demat holdings or the **Physical User Reset Password?** option for physical holdings on the NSDL e-voting website.
- Shareholders may also use OTP-based login to cast their vote.
- After entering the password, select **Agree with Terms and Conditions**, click **Login**, and access the e-voting homepage.

## Step 2: Cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system

### How to cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system?

1. After successful login, the system displays the companies and their corresponding EVENs for which the shareholder is eligible to vote.
2. Select the Company’s EVEN for the relevant remote e-voting period.
3. Select assent or dissent for the resolution.
4. Verify or modify the number of shares for which the vote is being cast.
5. Click **Submit** and then **Confirm** when prompted.
6. The message **Vote cast successfully** is displayed after confirmation.
7. A printout of the votes cast may be taken from the confirmation page.
8. Once a vote on a resolution is confirmed, it cannot be modified.

## General guidelines for shareholders

1. Institutional shareholders, other than individuals, HUFs, NRIs, and similar categories, must provide a scanned copy in PDF or JPG format of the relevant Board Resolution, authority letter, or other authorization document, together with the attested specimen signature of the authorized signatory or signatories. These documents may also be uploaded through the **Upload Board Resolution / Authority Letter** option under the e-voting tab.
2. Shareholders should keep their passwords confidential. NSDL e-voting access is disabled after five unsuccessful password attempts. The password must then be reset using the relevant password-recovery option.
3. Shareholders may refer to the FAQs and e-voting user manual available in the download section of the NSDL e-voting website for assistance.

## Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories for procuring user id and password and registration of e mail ids for e-voting for the resolutions set out in this notice:

1. Shareholders holding shares in physical mode must provide the Folio Number, shareholder name, a scanned copy of the share certificate showing the front and back, a self-attested PAN card copy, and a self-attested Aadhaar card copy.
2. Shareholders holding shares in demat mode must provide the DPID-CLID, or the 16-digit Beneficiary ID, shareholder name, client master or Consolidated Account Statement, a self-attested PAN card copy, and a self-attested Aadhaar card copy.
3. Shareholders may alternatively request their user ID and password through the NSDL e-voting process by providing the required documents.
4. Under the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with Depositories and Depository Participants. Shareholders must keep their mobile number and email ID correctly updated in their demat account to access the e-voting facility.
