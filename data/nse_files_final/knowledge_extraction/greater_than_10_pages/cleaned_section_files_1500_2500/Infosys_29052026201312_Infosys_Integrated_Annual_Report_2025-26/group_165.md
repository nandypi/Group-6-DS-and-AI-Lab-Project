## 6. Exercise price, purchase price or pricing formula

The exercise price for RSUs will equal the par value of the shares.

## 7. Exercise period and process of exercise

The exercise period for RSUs will be up to 90 days, except in cases of death or permanent incapacitation of the employee, when RSUs may be exercised within 365 days. The Administrator may determine the exercise period and process, which will be stated in the relevant Eligible Employee’s award agreement.

RSUs are deemed exercised when the Company or its appointed agent receives written or electronic notice of exercise from the person entitled to exercise them, together with full payment for the relevant equity shares or ADRs and applicable taxes, unless the employee is eligible for and chooses cashless exercise.

Where decided by the Administrator, income tax may be deducted from the employee’s salary, in which case payment for the equity shares or ADRs need not include the tax amount.

RSUs will lapse if the employee does not exercise them within the applicable exercise period.

## 8. The appraisal process for determining the eligibility of employees for the Amended 2019 Plan

Eligibility for RSU grants under the Amended 2019 Plan will be determined by the Administrator based on the employee’s organizational level, performance, future potential, and other criteria determined from time to time. Eligible Employees will be selected based on these criteria and management recommendations.

## 9. Maximum number of shares, to be issued per employee and in aggregate

- No Eligible Employee may be issued more than 50,00,000 equity shares of the Company, subject to adjustments for changes in the Company’s capital structure.
- Grants during any one year covering equity shares or ADRs equal to or exceeding 1% of the Company’s issued capital at the time of grant require shareholder approval through a special resolution at a general meeting.
- The total number of equity shares or ADRs provided to employees of the Company and its subsidiaries upon exercise of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan may not cumulatively exceed 3,26,63,903 equity shares, subject to adjustments for changes in the Company’s capital structure.

## 10. Maximum quantum of benefits to be provided per employee under the scheme(s)

Please see paragraph 9 above.

## 11. Whether the scheme(s) is to be implemented and administered directly by the Company or through a trust

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee will administer the Amended 2019 Plan. For secondary acquisition and related administrative matters, it may delegate administration to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

## 12. Whether the scheme(s) involves fresh issue of shares by the Company or secondary acquisition by the trust or both

Equity shares may be acquired through both fresh issue and secondary acquisition by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust, subject to regulatory approvals and the approvals originally granted by shareholders at the Company’s 38th Annual General Meeting on June 22, 2019.

## 13. The amount of provision of money (by way of grant and / or loan) to be provided for implementation of the scheme(s) by the Company to the trust - and if a loan, its tenure, utilization, repayment terms, etc.

The Company may from time to time provide a grant or loan, or provide a guarantee or security in connection with a loan, to fund the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. The funding will be used to purchase the Company’s equity shares from the secondary market through a recognized stock exchange for the Amended 2019 Plan. The amount provided will not exceed statutory limits.

Where the Company provides a loan, exercise proceeds received from Eligible Employees will be used to repay the loan. Dividends received on equity shares purchased under the Amended 2019 Plan may be used as determined by the Administrator, including for secondary acquisition of shares or repayment of any loan from the Company. The tenure and other terms of any loan will be mutually agreed when the loan is granted.

The required details under Section 67 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 16 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, are as follows:

- **Class of employees:** As mentioned in paragraph 3 above.
- **Trustee in whose favor shares are registered:** Same as paragraph 13(c) below.
- **Trust and trustees:** The trust is the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. Its trustees are Nandini S, Shaji Mathew, Deepak Bhalla, and Gopalakrishnan Ramachandran. All are Indian citizens and employees of Infosys Limited. None of the trustees or their respective relatives is related to the Company’s promoters, promoter group, directors, or key managerial personnel.
- **Interests of key managerial personnel, directors, or promoters:** Directors and key managerial personnel may be deemed interested to the extent RSUs are granted to them and to the extent of their shareholding in the Company.
- **Benefits to employees:** Employees may receive equity shares, ADRs, or cash upon exercise of RSUs, in accordance with the relevant award agreement.
- **Voting rights:** Under the SEBI Regulations, the trustee of a trust governed by those regulations may not vote on shares held by the trust.

## 14. Maximum percentage of secondary acquisition (subject to limits specified under the regulations) that can be made by the trust for the purposes of the scheme(s)

The Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust may acquire equity shares through secondary acquisition as originally approved by shareholders at the Company’s 38th Annual General Meeting on June 22, 2019, within the limits specified under applicable laws.

## 15. A statement to the effect that the Company shall conform to the accounting policies specified in Regulation 15

The Company will comply with the applicable accounting policies prescribed under the SEBI Regulations or other applicable law for accounting for stock options, including the related disclosure requirements.

## 16. The method which the Company shall use to value its options:

The Company will use the Fair Value Method to value RSUs for calculating employee compensation cost.

## 17. Lock-in

Shares issued upon exercise of RSUs will not be subject to a lock-in period.

## Validity period of the Amended 2019 Plan

Grants under the Amended 2019 Plan will be made over a further period of seven years from the date shareholders approve the Amended Plan. The Amended 2019 Plan will remain in effect for a further period of ten years from that approval date. Shareholders may extend this validity period if required.

Because the Amended 2019 Plan provides for the issue of equity shares and ADRs to persons other than the Company’s existing shareholders, shareholder consent is being sought under Section 62 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and Regulation 6 of the SEBI Regulations.

Under the SEBI Regulations, a separate resolution is required for grants of RSUs to employees of subsidiaries. Accordingly, resolutions under Item Nos. 4 and 5 are being placed separately for shareholder approval.

None of the promoters, promoter group members, or their relatives is concerned or interested in these resolutions except to the extent of their shareholding in the Company. Directors, key managerial personnel, and their relatives may be deemed concerned or interested to the extent of RSUs granted to them and their shareholding in the Company.

The Board of Directors recommends that shareholders pass the proposed resolutions under Item Nos. 4 and 5 as special resolutions.
