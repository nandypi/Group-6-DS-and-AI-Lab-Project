## 2. Processes to avoid or manage conflicts of interest involving members of the Board

Yes. Infosys has processes that include:

1. **Declaration of interest:** Board members disclose financial interests, affiliations, or relationships that could influence decision-making at appointment and periodically thereafter, in compliance with statutory requirements.
2. **Code of Conduct and Ethics:** The Company’s formal code defines conflicts of interest and provides guidelines for identifying and managing them.
3. **Recusal procedures:** A Board member with a potential conflict must recuse themselves from relevant discussions and voting.
4. **Approval requirements:** Transactions involving entities in which directors have an interest require prior Board approval. Transactions exceeding prescribed thresholds also require shareholder approval.

### 2a. Procedures for sustainable sourcing

Yes. Infosys has adopted a Responsible Supply Chain & Supplier Diversity Policy and a Green Procurement Policy. Suppliers must sign the Supplier Code of Conduct. Periodic ESG assessments are conducted internally and through a third party.

### 2b. Percentage of inputs sourced sustainably

All procurements follow the principles of sustainable sourcing. Suppliers are required to accept the Supplier Code of Conduct, which is based on the UNGC Principles.

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is not applicable to the entity’s activities. Infosys does not manufacture products and operates as an IT services company.
