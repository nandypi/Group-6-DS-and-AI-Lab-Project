## Letter to the Shareholder Performance overview

## Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

## Dear shareholder,

Last year marked an inflection point as AI began evolving from experimentation to enterprise-scale adoption. Infosys shifted its portfolio, invested in talent, and scaled partnerships to help clients adopt AI.

AI-led programs were deployed across 90% of Infosys’ top 200 clients. Infosys’ AI strategy is built on six pillars of its AI-First value framework:

- Strategy and engineering
- Data
- Process
- Legacy modernization
- Physical AI
- Trust

These capabilities address a US$300 billion opportunity, based on market estimates, and support growth programs, legacy-system modernization, data and process readiness, and responsible AI deployment.

For Q3, revenue from these six areas was 5.5% of total revenue and was growing faster than the company overall. Infosys has partnerships with AI-native companies and a large base of employees trained on their foundation models. The company is re-skilling its over 3,25,000 employees on AI.

Client AI transformation programs include rapid AI adoption for coding and code modernization, as well as building agents across business functions.

Infosys Topaz is the company’s AI suite. Topaz Fabric provides a composable stack of services and agents that enables enterprises to use different foundation models in a token-efficient manner, with the stated aim of maximizing value while minimizing cost and risk.

Infosys reported the following operating and capital-allocation figures:

- Revenue of over US$20 billion
- Adjusted operating margin of 21%
- Free cash flow of US$3.7 billion
- Over US$4 billion returned to shareholders
  - Dividends: US$2.1 billion
  - Share buybacks: US$2 billion
- Total dividend announced: ₹48 per share
- Over 20,000 college graduates recruited during the year

Infosys had large deals worth US$14.9 billion during the year. The company also launched a focused growth program targeting large global companies where Infosys has a smaller share of client spending.

Cloud and data expertise remained a foundation for AI-powered transformation. Infosys stated that its investments in Cobalt and its Data and Analytics portfolio positioned the company to address the expanding market opportunity.

### Social impact and sustainability

Infosys Foundation’s focus areas include skilling and livelihoods, healthcare, and sustainability. During the year, the Infosys Foundation impacted over seven million people in India. Springboard provided free digital learning programs to 10 million people worldwide.
