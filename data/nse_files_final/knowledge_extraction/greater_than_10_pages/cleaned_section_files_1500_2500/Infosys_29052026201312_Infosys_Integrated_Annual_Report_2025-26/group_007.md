## Our business context

Technology continues to reshape businesses across industries worldwide. AI is increasingly being applied to domain-specific capabilities and industry-focused use cases. Clients are addressing the need to rapidly reinvent processes and systems in the AI era, requiring knowledge of emerging technologies, new ways of working, AI ecosystems, business processes, and operational practices.

Infosys supports clients in transforming their businesses through technology, including building digital cores and reimagining business operations to improve agility, efficiency, and resilience while accelerating growth initiatives. Organizations are increasingly focused on responsible business practices, including ESG principles, reconfiguring cost structures, improving resilience and agility, delivering personalized customer and employee experiences, and launching innovative products and services.

Businesses are also using AI to:

- Enhance and personalize customer interactions
- Improve marketing and sales efficiency
- Strengthen code quality
- Increase workforce and organizational productivity

The emergence of AI and its potential for broader automation is expected to accelerate workforce transformation.

The traditional services market remains competitive, driven by rapid change and the entry of specialized technology providers. Infosys identifies its industry expertise, end-to-end service capabilities, scalable digital solutions, established platforms, quality and process excellence, distributed agile global delivery model, experienced leadership team, skilled workforce, and execution track record as key differentiators.

## Infosys services brands

Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Topaz, and Infosys Aster represent focused, domain-led offerings that combine the Company’s capabilities and expertise for enterprise transformation needs across cloud, AI, and marketing.

- **Infosys Cobalt:** A set of services, solutions, and platforms for enterprises to accelerate their cloud journey.
- **Infosys Topaz:** An AI-first set of services, solutions, and platforms using generative and agentic AI technologies.
- **Infosys Aster:** An AI-amplified marketing suite for brand experiences, marketing efficiency, and business growth.
