## 4. Management's Responsibility

Management is responsible for selecting or establishing suitable criteria for preparing the Sustainability Information, including the reporting boundary of the BRSR, applicable laws and regulations, identification of key aspects, stakeholder engagement, content, preparation, and presentation of the Identified Sustainability Information.

This responsibility includes designing, implementing, and maintaining internal controls relevant to preparing the BRSR and measuring Identified Sustainability Information free from material misstatement due to fraud or error.

## 5. Inherent limitations

The absence of a significant body of established practice for evaluating and measuring non-financial information allows for different, but acceptable, measurement techniques and may affect comparability between companies.

## 6. Our Independence and Quality Control

The assurance provider maintained its independence and confirmed compliance with the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), the SEBI Master Circular and related clarifications, and applicable competency and experience requirements.

Quality control was maintained under Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1 through documented policies and procedures covering ethical requirements, professional standards, and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## 7. Our Responsibility

The assurance provider's responsibility was to express a reasonable assurance opinion on the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I, based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained.

The engagement was conducted in accordance with:

- Standard on Sustainability Assurance Engagements (SSAE) 3000, “Assurance Engagements on Sustainability Information”; and
- Standard on Assurance Engagements (SAE) 3410, “Assurance Engagements on Greenhouse Gas Statements”.

The engagement procedures included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Identified Sustainability Information, related disclosures, and assessment criteria.
- Making inquiries of management and personnel responsible for preparing the Report, including the environment, compliance, and human resources teams.
- Evaluating the design and implementation of key systems, processes, and controls for recording, processing, and reporting Identified Sustainability Information at the corporate office and sampled other locations.
- Determining the nature, timing, and extent of further procedures based on assessed risks of material misstatement.
- Testing key assumptions, emission factors, and methodologies used to calculate greenhouse gas emissions.
- Testing the process for collating sustainability information by agreeing or reconciling the Identified Sustainability Information with underlying records on a sample basis.
- Testing consolidation for sampled locations and the corporate office within the reporting boundary to assess data completeness.

The assurance provider stated that the evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the reasonable assurance opinion.

## 8. Exclusions

The assurance scope excluded:

- Company operations outside the reporting boundary set out in Question 13 of Section A: General Disclosures of the BRSR, for the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I.
- Report aspects and qualitative or quantitative data other than the Identified Sustainability Information.
- Data and information outside the financial year ended March 31, 2026.
- Statements describing opinions, beliefs, aspirations, expectations, aims, or future intentions.

## 9. Other information

Management is responsible for information included in the BRSR, the Integrated Annual Report, and the Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) Report other than the Identified Sustainability Information and the independent assurance reports dated May 27, 2026.

The assurance opinion does not cover this other information. The assurance provider was required to read the other information and consider whether it was materially inconsistent with the Identified Sustainability Information or otherwise appeared materially misstated. The assurance provider reported no matters in this regard.

## 10. Reasonable Assurance Opinion

Based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained, the BRSR Core indicators for the year ended March 31, 2026, listed in Appendix I and presented in the BRSR, were prepared in all material respects in accordance with the criteria stated in paragraph 3.

## 11. Restriction on use

The reasonable assurance report was prepared for the Board of Directors at the Company's request to assist with reporting on sustainability performance and activities. It was intended solely for the addressees of the report and the stated purpose.
