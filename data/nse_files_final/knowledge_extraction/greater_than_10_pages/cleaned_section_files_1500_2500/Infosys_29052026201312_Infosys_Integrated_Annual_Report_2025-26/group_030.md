## Energy-efficient IT infrastructure

Infosys has adopted measures to improve the energy efficiency and environmental performance of its internal IT infrastructure:

- **Energy-efficient network infrastructure:** InfosysIT uses network equipment with power-saving features.
- **Public cloud adoption:** Internal IT applications have largely transitioned to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premise data centers. Cloud-based collaboration and secure access solutions support hybrid work.
- **IT Service Management:** The ITSM landscape uses a standardized, AI-enabled service desk hosted on a SaaS platform. This has standardized operational processes, increased automation, and improved support-service user experience. AI-assisted agents support employees with daily tasks and AI-enabled work processes.
- **Data center modernization:** Data center modernization is a key part of InfosysIT’s sustainability and responsible-growth strategy. Density-optimized hyperscale platforms support cloud-scale agility and high-density server virtualization, reducing infrastructure sprawl and improving compute-resource utilization.
- **Data Center Infrastructure Management:** Investments in DCIM provide real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure, supporting data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.
- **Enterprise storage:** InfosysIT provides storage services to delivery units using multi-protocol storage technology, including flash storage, fabric pool, and storage grid solutions. Storage grid technology reduces the physical data center footprint, power consumption, and cooling requirements.
- **Hybrid cloud storage:** Hybrid cloud storage provides data protection and scalability without the need for buffer investments. It reduces on-premise data center space, power consumption, and cooling requirements for certain workloads.
- **Cloud-native development environment:** InfosysIT uses an open-source-based cloud-native development platform built on Hyper Converged Infrastructure (HCI) and compute resources. Resource consolidation through HCI reduces the physical data center footprint and hardware requirements.
