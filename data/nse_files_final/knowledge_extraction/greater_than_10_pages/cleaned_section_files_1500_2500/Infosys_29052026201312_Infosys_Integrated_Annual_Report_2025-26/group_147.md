## 2.22 Employee benefits

## Gratuity and pension

The Group provides gratuity, a defined benefit retirement plan, to eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. The trusts are administered by trustees, and contributions are invested in schemes with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Liabilities under the defined benefit plans are determined through actuarial valuations performed by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its Balance Sheet. Remeasurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The actual return on plan assets, in excess of the return calculated using the discount rate applied to the defined benefit obligation, is recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

## Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. The employee and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to a government-administered pension fund.

The annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust is administered by the Government of India. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on the trust’s investments and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of the Indian subsidiaries participate in a defined contribution provident fund plan. The employees and respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Amounts collected are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no further obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution plan. The Group has no further obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund. The corpus is invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined through actuarial valuation performed by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The valuation is based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the Balance Sheet date.

Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
