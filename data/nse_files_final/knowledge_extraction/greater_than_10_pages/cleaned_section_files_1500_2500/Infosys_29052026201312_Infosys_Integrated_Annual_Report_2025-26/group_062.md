## 10. Health and safety management system

### 10a. Whether an occupational health and safety management system has been implemented by the entity? (Yes / No). If yes, the coverage of such system

Yes. Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) is an integral part of Infosys’s ESG framework. Its Health, Safety and Environmental Management System (HSEMS), known as “Ozone,” is based on management commitment, compliance with applicable legal requirements, and stakeholder expectations.

Ozone supports structured HSE processes and has enabled Infosys to achieve ISO 45001:2018 occupational health and safety certifications at locations where the system has been in place for more than three years. These certifications cover all India locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, in line with the enterprise HSE strategy.

More than 85% of Infosys employees are covered by ISO 45001 certification. HSEMS is implemented globally based on legal requirements and internal benchmarks and is included in the internal audit program for governance and performance monitoring.

Infosys’s HSE Policy describes its approach to managing significant HSE risks and opportunities. Initiatives cover mental and emotional health, ergonomics, safety, lifestyle diseases, and preventive care. Occupational Health Centers operate across all India campuses.
