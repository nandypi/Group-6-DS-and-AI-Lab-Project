## 3. Branding

Brand Infosys is described as a key intangible asset. It was ranked among the world's Top 100 brands in FY26 and recognized as the fastest-growing IT services brand globally over a six-year period. The brand positions Infosys as an enterprise AI partner, supported by capabilities in enterprise AI, cloud, digital technologies, and continuous learning.

Infosys' global marketing and partnership activities include:

- Digital innovation partnerships with the Australian Open, Roland-Garros, the ATP, and The International Tennis Hall of Fame.
- Official digital innovation partnerships with Madison Square Garden properties, including the New York Knicks, New York Rangers, and MSG Arena.
- Official digital innovation partnership with the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship.
- Strategic partnerships with Dow Jones, The Economist Group, Financial Times, and Handelsblatt Media Group.
- Confluence client events across the US, Europe, and APAC.

## 5. Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI)

The Infosys Leadership Institute focuses on:

- Succession planning
- Leadership bench strength
- Development of title holders
- Strengthening inclusivity in leadership

Over the last two years, more than 1,500 senior leaders completed ILI's AI for Business Growth program, delivered in partnership with Kellogg. ILI also provided AI immersion programs covering tools including Infosys Topaz Fabric, as well as executive leadership programs developed with Harvard Business School and MIT.

ILI strengthened succession planning through leadership profiles, personalized development plans, executive education, external coaching, and regular reviews. The Constellation program included a 10-month program with Oxford Saïd Business School. Twelve Constellation projects were executed during the year.

Development programs for title holders included:

- The 10-month Enterprise Leadership Program for AVPs
- The Executive Communication program with The Economist for VPs
- The InFusion program for new title holders

Infosys leaders collectively invested more than 10,000 leadership learning days annually during each of the last two years. ILI recorded a Net Promoter Score of more than 96% for the third consecutive year and was recognized as the top unit for Internal Partner Satisfaction for three consecutive years.

The #IamtheFuture program covered more than 700 leaders through role-specific development journeys focused on leadership readiness, inclusion, and leadership pipeline development. The program was redesigned during the year to differentiate development by career stage and emphasize application, simulations, and contextual learning.

ILI was recognized as the Best Business Enabling Function (small) for 2025-26 at the Infosys Awards for Excellence.

## 6. Infosys Knowledge Institute

The Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI) is the business research and thought leadership arm of Infosys. It uses Infosys expertise, client collaborations, primary research, large-scale surveys, and quantitative analysis to produce research on the business impact of technology.

IKI's publications include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals. Since inception, IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets.

[For more information, visit the Infosys Knowledge Institute.](https://infosys.com/iki)

## 7. ESG vision and ambitions

Infosys launched its ESG Vision 2030 in October 2020. In fiscal 2025, it refreshed the vision to address changing stakeholder priorities.

Infosys reported being carbon neutral across its global operations for the seventh consecutive year. The ESG Vision 2030 Refresh includes ambitions to:

- Become climate positive by 2030 through initiatives across Scope 1, 2, and 3.
- Reduce its carbon footprint and sequester more carbon from the atmosphere than it emits.
- Engage clients on climate action through Infosys solutions.
- Maintain 100% recycling of wastewater.
- Sequester more water than consumed year-on-year.
- Achieve zero waste to landfill.
- Empower more than 18 million people through digital skilling programs.
- Enable employment opportunities for more than 500,000 people.
- Support sustainable communities through technology-for-good initiatives, CSR, employee volunteering, healthcare, and women empowerment.
- Maintain a diverse and inclusive board.
- Strengthen data privacy standards and information security practices.
- Provide employee experiences aligned with recognition as a leading employer in key operating regions.

[For more details, read the Infosys ESG Vision 2030 Refresh.](https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/esg-vision-2030/docs/infosys-esg-vision-2030-refresh.pdf)

## Corporate governance report

Corporate governance covers how Infosys is directed and its relationships with stakeholders. The framework emphasizes trust, transparency, accountability, long-term capital formation, economic growth, and financial stability.

## Our corporate governance philosophy

Infosys' corporate governance framework is based on integrity, transparency, fairness, ethical decision-making, and adherence to stakeholder expectations. It is guided by the company's C-LIFE values:

- Client value
- Leadership by example
- Integrity and transparency
- Fairness
- Excellence

The framework is also guided by the principles of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD).

Infosys' corporate governance is reinforced through its Code of Conduct and Ethics, corporate governance guidelines, committee charters, Board and Management processes, audits, and internal control systems. The report provides an overview of the company's adherence to the seven pillars of its governance framework.
