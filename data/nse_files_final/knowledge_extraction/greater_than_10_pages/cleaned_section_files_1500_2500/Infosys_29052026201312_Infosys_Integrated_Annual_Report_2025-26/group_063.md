## 10b. What are the processes used to identify work-related hazards and assess risks on a routine and non-routine basis by the entity?

Infosys identifies and assesses work-related hazards across routine and non-routine activities. Assessments are conducted proactively for existing, new, and modified activities, processes, and services, and in response to regulatory updates.

Routine risk assessments include periodic workplace and ergonomic evaluations, monitoring utility operations, and reviewing employee commute risks. Non-routine activities, including construction, infrastructure development, preventive maintenance, and facility modifications, undergo specific risk assessments before and during execution.

Incidents and near misses are analyzed quarterly to identify trends and emerging risks. Hazards are prioritized and mitigated using the hierarchy of controls, followed by reassessment of residual risks and updates to the risk control matrix as required. Employees and stakeholders participate in the risk assessment process, and learnings from past projects and current operations are incorporated continuously.

## 10c. Whether you have processes for workers to report the work-related hazards and to remove themselves from such risks. (Y / N)

Yes. Infosys has an incident reporting and management process covering accidents, near misses, unsafe acts, and unsafe conditions. Employees can report health and safety concerns through the Infosys Helpdesk (IHD) or designated email addresses. Contract staff without system access can inform their supervisors, who escalate the issue to the local HSE team.

All incidents undergo root cause analysis. Corrective and preventive measures are implemented to avoid recurrence and potential injuries or losses. Awareness of incident reporting is supported through induction programs, online training modules on Lex, and targeted awareness campaigns and mailers. Learnings and corrective actions are shared across locations.

## 10d. Do the employees/ worker of the entity have access to non-occupational medical and healthcare services?

Yes. Infosys operates Occupational Health Centers (OHCs), including physiotherapy facilities, across its campuses in India. Statutory and applicable norms are followed, and competent medical professionals from accredited partner hospitals manage the OHCs and physiotherapy centers. Periodic health risk assessments monitor health trends and support timely specialist consultations and targeted interventions.

Employee well-being initiatives include workshops, awareness campaigns, regular health check-ups, online chat sessions, counseling services such as Samaritans, and targeted wellness programs. OHC visit details are recorded digitally in accordance with applicable regional data protection and privacy regulations.

Infosys also provides healthcare benefits through hospital tie-ups, giving employees and dependents access to health screenings and treatment for non-occupational illnesses. Employees, including contractual staff, receive comprehensive insurance coverage. Contractual employees are included in targeted programs such as mandatory health screenings and risk-based interventions guided by identified hazards and emerging health trends.

## 12. Describe the measures taken by the entity to ensure a safe and healthy work place.

Infosys’ Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) policies, processes, and practices address the physical, emotional, and social well-being of employees. Its Health and Safety Management system is certified to ISO 45001:2018 for all Indian locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries.

Measures implemented include:

1. **Incident management:** Near misses, potential hazards, and accidents are reported through internal applications or email. Causes are identified and analyzed, and corrective actions are taken to prevent recurrence or injuries and losses.
2. **OH&S Committee:** Employees and other than permanent employees participate in the Infosys OH&S Committee. Employee representation in safety committees is 100%. The Committee supports employee consultation and cooperation between employees and management on workplace OH&S.
3. **Training and awareness:** Training includes awareness sessions, mock drills, classroom sessions, and periodic demonstrations. HSEMS training is part of employee induction, and an HSE awareness module is available on Lex. Contract staff receive job-specific and general induction training followed by refresher training.
4. **Safety interventions:** Safety systems include work permits, training, Lock Out Tag Out (LOTO), safety inspections, audits, operational controls, and monitoring. Policies address women’s safety, lone working, transport, travel, construction, and other areas.
5. **Medical services:** Infosys operates OHCs on its campuses in India. Most OHCs operate around the clock based on business requirements. Ambulance services are available, or arrangements are made for ambulances, at all India-based locations.
6. **Health Risk Assessment (HRA):** HRA is conducted annually based on OHC inputs. Prevalent risks for an IT / ITES company include ergonomics, Musculoskeletal Disorders (MSDs), emotional well-being, workplace operations, utilities, and commuting. Initiatives, interventions, virtual engagement sessions, and process controls address these risks.
7. **Ergonomics programs:** Programs include on-site physiotherapy centers in India, ergonomic expert interventions, and ergonomic infrastructure. Periodic sessions cover back care, Repetitive Stress Injuries, and seating posture for employees globally.
8. **Physical and emotional well-being:** Nutritionists at Infosys locations in India provide counseling and guidance. Gyms are also available for physical and mental fitness.
9. **Programs for mothers:** Pregna Care is a healthcare program for expectant mothers in India.
10. **Working environment:** Infosys monitors indoor air quality, lux, and noise levels and promotes the use of green seal chemicals.
