## Manufactured Capital

Infosys’ Manufactured Capital includes energy-efficient offices, data centers, innovation hubs, digital studios, and technology infrastructure across the globe. Its campuses include green buildings and collaborative technology infrastructure intended to support workplaces for employees, clients, and contractors.

## Intellectual Capital

Infosys’ Intellectual Capital is supported by collaboration with experts, partners, academia, and other stakeholders to develop products and services for customers and communities. Its initiatives include iCETS, the Living Labs, the Infosys Innovation Network (IIN), and the Infosys Business Incubator. The Infosys Prize and Aarohan Social Innovation Awards provide platforms for innovators and social entrepreneurs.

## Social and Relationship Capital

Infosys’ social and relationship initiatives focus on client transformation, social innovation, and employability through community skills training. Its global CSR efforts address education, healthcare, women empowerment, science and research, and environmental sustainability.

## About Infosys Corporate overview

Infosys is a global business consulting and technology services company. More than 3,25,000 people support clients in 59 countries. The company states that it has over four decades of experience managing the systems and workings of global enterprises and uses an AI-first value framework, domain expertise, and an AI-native partner ecosystem.

Established in 1981 with capital of US$250, Infosys states that it has grown to a market capitalization of approximately US$54.8 billion. It pioneered the Global Delivery Model and became the first Indian IT company to be listed on NASDAQ.

Since refreshing its ESG Vision 2030 in 2025, Infosys reports progress on Climate Positive goals, skilling and community initiatives, and workforce inclusion. It also describes governance and ethics accountability across its value chain.

## Our Values

Infosys’ Code of Conduct is based on the C-LIFE values:

### Client value

To surpass client expectations consistently.

### Leadership by example

To set standards in business and transactions and be an exemplar for the industry and the company.

### Integrity and transparency

To be ethical, sincere, and open in all transactions.

### Fairness

To be objective and transaction-oriented, thereby earning trust and respect.

### Excellence

To strive relentlessly, constantly improve the company, its teams, services, and products, and become the best.
