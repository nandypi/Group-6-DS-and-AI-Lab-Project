## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)

In accordance with Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Listing Regulations, the BRSR forms part of this Integrated Annual Report. It describes initiatives undertaken by the Company from an environmental, social, and governance perspective.

SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122 dated July 12, 2023, updated the BRSR format to incorporate BRSR Core, a subset of the BRSR containing specific key performance indicators under nine ESG attributes. These indicators are subject to mandatory reasonable assurance by an independent assurance provider.

Infosys appointed Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP as the assurance provider. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP also assured selected indicators of the ESG Report designed based on the GRI Standards. The ESG Report is published annually based on the GRI Standards 2021.

## 8. Other disclosures and affirmations

Pursuant to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014, the Company affirms that, for the year ended March 31, 2026:

- There were no proceedings filed by or against the Company pending under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, before the National Company Law Tribunal or any other court.
- There was no instance of a one-time settlement with any bank or financial institution.
- There were no significant and material orders passed by regulators, courts, or tribunals that impacted the Company’s going-concern status or future operations.
- The Company complied with the provisions of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, and the rules made thereunder, including applicable maternity-benefit obligations for eligible employees.
