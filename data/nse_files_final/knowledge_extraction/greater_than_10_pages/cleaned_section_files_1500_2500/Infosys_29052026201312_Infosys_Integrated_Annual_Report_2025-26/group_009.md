## Managing financial capital

Infosys has a cash-generating business and access to capital markets globally. Its credit rating supports the ability to raise debt at competitive rates in the future, if needed. Primary sources of funds include cash from operations and income from short- and long-term investments.

Primary sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents and cash flow generated from operations. Infosys remains debt-free and maintains cash for operational and strategic requirements, unforeseen events, and investment returns.

Consolidated cash and investments include:

- Bank deposits
- Debt mutual funds
- Commercial Paper (CP)
- Quoted bonds and securities issued by government and quasigovernment organizations
- Non-convertible debentures
- Certificates of Deposits (CDs)
- Instruments issued by eligible financial institutions with high credit ratings, as guided by the treasury policy

Infosys also invests in the startup ecosystem, primarily through minority equity positions in startup organizations and/or venture capital funds. These investments provide access to innovation that can complement the company’s services and solutions.

## Shareholder value creation

Infosys states that responsible business decisions, governance, integrity, and transparency guide its relationship with investors. The company communicates with investors through quarterly post-result calls, analyst meets, the Annual General Meeting, and one-to-one and group interactions.

## Distribution of value created through Capital Allocation Policy

Effective from financial year 2025, Infosys expects to continue returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. The policy also anticipates progressively increasing annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

The company returned approximately 82.1% of free cash flow for fiscals 2025 and 2026 through dividends and buybacks, in line with the Capital Allocation Policy.

Infosys’ Employee Value Proposition (EVP) focuses on meaningful work, continuous growth, protected well-being, and a sense of belonging. The company describes its employee experience approach as “Experience by Design,” with policies, processes, programs, and systems designed around employees.

## Employee Value Proposition

The Employee Value Proposition comprises the experiences, rewards, benefits, and fulfillment employees receive in return for their commitment and skills. Infosys refers to its employees as “Infoscions.”

## Employee health and wellness

Infosys is committed to a safe, healthy, and incident-free workplace for employees, contractors, and other stakeholders. Its integrated Occupational Health and Safety Management System is aligned with ISO 45001 and includes workplace-hazard identification, risk-mitigation controls, regular audits, safety initiatives, employee engagement programs, and emergency preparedness drills.

The HALE (Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment) framework integrates wellness into the company’s culture and operating model. It covers Physical, Emotional, Social, and Digital Well-being, with Safety as a core component.

## Nurturing workplace inclusivity

Infosys describes inclusion as part of its culture and leadership, learning, and development practices.

As part of its ESG Vision 2030, Infosys aims to achieve 45% female representation in its workforce by 2030. Women represented 39.5% of the total workforce in fiscal 2026.

## Infosys Pulse

Infosys Pulse is an employee feedback platform designed to capture workplace-experience insights. It supports leadership alignment with employee sentiment and data-driven organizational actions.

## InStep

InStep is Infosys’ global internship program. It has:

- More than 220 partner institutions in over 50 countries
- More than 3,500 alumni members and interns from over 50 nationalities

The program has been ranked the World’s #1 Internship Program five times in a row by Vault Firsthand. Infosys states that InStep supports academic partnerships, patents, publications, and localization efforts. The launch of InStep Japan expanded the program’s global reach.

## Flexibility by design and distributed workforce

Infosys has established innovation hubs, nearshore centers, and digital design studios across geographies. Its hybrid work model supports working from home and office locations close to employees’ homes. Recent Indian office locations include Ahmedabad, Guwahati, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam, Noida, Hubballi, Indore, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, and Coimbatore.

The distributed-hybrid model supports self-development, collaboration, and business outcomes. Infosys monitors work-hour patterns for excessive or unsustainable workloads and provides managers with insights to identify workload imbalances, staffing challenges, and employees requiring additional support.

## Leveraging AI to improve employee experience

Infosys is integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) across the talent lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, continuous learning, and offboarding.

InfyMe is a mobile-first, self-service platform integrating more than 200 service touchpoints into one interface. The platform supports employee operations, communication, and collaboration, particularly in the hybrid work model.

## Our NAVI-powered interventions

**Data Assist:** A natural-language AI assistant for querying data related to leave, attendance, allocation, assets, confirmation, and master data.

**Policy Advisor:** An AI assistant that interprets policy documents and personalizes responses to employee queries.

## Moments That Matter (MTM)

Moments That Matter is a celebration platform integrated into employee-life-cycle events. Smart alerts inform employees about upcoming team events, and AI can generate cards and messages for colleagues and teams.

AI-driven Pulse analysis supports continuous listening by identifying employee sentiment and enabling early action related to well-being and engagement.

In learning and development, Infosys uses generative AI for personalized learning paths, real-world scenario simulation, and content creation. AI companions on the Lex learning platform, including Zoiee and SynthAIz, provide tutoring, summarization, and contextual learning support.
