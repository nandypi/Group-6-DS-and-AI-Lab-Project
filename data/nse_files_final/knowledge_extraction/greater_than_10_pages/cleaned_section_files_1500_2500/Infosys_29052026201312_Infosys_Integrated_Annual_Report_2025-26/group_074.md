## PRINCIPLE 9: Businesses should engage with and provide value to their consumers in a responsible manner

## 1. Describe the mechanisms in place to receive and respond to consumer complaints and feedback.

Infosys has multiple channels for clients across diverse industry verticals to submit concerns and feedback. Dedicated teams analyze complaints, develop and implement solutions, communicate progress transparently, and seek client approval for corrective actions.

Infosys also engages proactively with clients to understand expectations, gather feedback, and obtain insight into their future outlook. This information supports strategic planning and continuous improvement so that services align with evolving client needs and expectations.
