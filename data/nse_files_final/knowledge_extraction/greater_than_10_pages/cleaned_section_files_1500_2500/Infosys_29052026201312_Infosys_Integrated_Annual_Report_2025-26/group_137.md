## Description of reserves

## Capital redemption reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

## Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the accumulated earnings of the Group.

## Securities premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as securities premium. The balance has been used for bonus issues and share buybacks.

## Share options outstanding account

The share options outstanding account records the fair value of equity-settled, share-based payment transactions with employees. Amounts are transferred to securities premium when stock options are exercised and to general reserve when options are not exercised by employees.

## Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used to acquire new plant and machinery for the Company’s business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include:

- Currency translation adjustments.
- Remeasurement of net defined benefit liabilities or assets.
- Equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income.
- Changes in the fair value of investments.
- Changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes.

## Currency translation reserve

Exchange differences arising from translating the financial statements of foreign subsidiaries whose functional currency is not the Indian rupee are recognized in other comprehensive income and presented within equity.

## Cash flow hedge reserve

For derivatives designated as cash flow hedging instruments, the effective portion of changes in fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss is transferred to the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecast transaction occurs.

## Capital Allocation Policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

In line with the Capital Allocation Policy, the Board approved a proposal on September 11, 2025, to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ` 5 each for ` 18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting by postal ballot, with results declared on November 6, 2025. The offer covered 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis. The buyback price was ` 1,800 per equity share.

The offer was made to eligible equity shareholders as of the November 14, 2025 record date on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The Company completed the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025, and bought back and extinguished all 10,00,00,000 shares.

The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ` 18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013. As of March 31, 2026, the Company had created a capital redemption reserve of ` 50 crore, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The Company’s capital management objective is to maintain its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. The Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares. As of March 31, 2026, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.
