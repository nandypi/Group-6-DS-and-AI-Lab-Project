## Leadership indicators

1. **Stakeholder consultation and Board engagement**

   Infosys identified its most material issues through a data-driven and consultative exercise. Material topics were shortlisted and prioritized based on their impact on stakeholders and the business. Periodic meetings were held to update the Board’s sub-committees.

2. **Use of stakeholder consultation**

   Yes. Stakeholder consultation was used to identify and manage environmental and social topics. Material topics and linked ambitions are covered through the ESG Vision 2030 and annual ESG performance reports.

3. **Engagement with vulnerable and marginalized stakeholder groups**

   Infosys Foundation supports underprivileged sections of society, creates opportunities, and works toward a more equitable society. Infosys contributes to the Foundation as part of its CSR initiatives. The Foundation engages with communities, including vulnerable and marginalized stakeholder groups, across several focus areas.
