## General guidelines for VC participation

- Infosys’ 45th AGM will be conducted through video conferencing in compliance with applicable legal provisions and circulars.
- The video conferencing facility will open 60 minutes before the scheduled start of the AGM. Participation will be available on a first-come-first-served basis.
- The Company may limit the number of members permitted to ask questions, depending on the time available during the AGM.
- Members may join through desktops, smartphones, laptops, tablets, or similar devices. Desktops or laptops with high-speed internet are recommended.
- Participants using mobile devices, tablets, or mobile hotspots may experience audio or video interruptions caused by network fluctuations. A stable Wi-Fi or LAN connection is recommended.

## Instructions for e-voting

Electronic voting through the NSDL e-voting system consists of two steps, beginning with access to the NSDL e-voting system.

## (A) Login method for e-voting for individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and depository participants. Shareholders are advised to keep their mobile numbers and email addresses updated in their demat accounts.

### NSDL shareholders

NSDL shareholders may access e-voting through:

- The NSDL IDeAS service using their existing User ID and password.
- OTP-based login using their 8-digit DP ID, 8-digit Client ID, PAN, verification code, and OTP sent to their registered email address or mobile number.
- The NSDL e-voting website using their 16-digit NSDL demat account number, password or OTP, and verification code.
- Their depository participant’s demat-account login credentials.

After successful authentication, shareholders can select Infosys or NSDL as the e-voting service provider and access the e-voting page during the remote e-voting period or vote during the virtual meeting, where applicable.

Shareholders who are not registered for NSDL IDeAS may register through the NSDL website.

### CDSL shareholders

Users registered for CDSL Easi or Easiest can log in with their existing user ID and password and access the e-voting page without further authentication.

Shareholders who are not registered for Easi or Easiest may register through the CDSL website. Alternatively, they may access the e-voting page using their demat account number and PAN. Authentication is completed through an OTP sent to the mobile number and email address recorded in the demat account.

### Shareholders logging in through depository participants

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may use their depository participant’s login credentials. After authentication, they can select the e-voting option, choose Infosys or the relevant e-voting service provider, and access the e-voting website.

Members unable to retrieve their User ID or password should use the “Forgot User ID” or “Forgot Password” options available on the relevant e-voting website.

### Technical support

For technical issues relating to login through NSDL or CDSL, shareholders may contact the respective depository’s e-voting helpdesk.

| Login type | Helpdesk details |
|---|---|
| Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode with NSDL | NSDL e-voting helpdesk |
| Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode with CDSL | CDSL e-voting helpdesk |
