## Mondelēz

### Mondelēz International establishes an end-to-end traceability reporting capability with EdgeVerve AI Next

#### Enterprise Wide Traceability at Scale

Mondelēz International operates a large snack supply chain spanning thousands of suppliers, hundreds of manufacturing plants, and a global distribution network. Fragmented legacy systems and siloed data created challenges in food safety, regulatory compliance, and product recalls under regulations such as the FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA).

Infosys worked with Mondelēz teams across quality, manufacturing, distribution, customer service, and IT to identify vulnerabilities in production batch and quality management, product bills of material, raw-material conversion, finished-goods receival, palletizing and labeling, shipping, and downstream data management. These requirements were used to define the Mondelēz fit-to-standard Traceability Platform.

Infosys deployed EdgeVerve AI Next Product Traceability, a cloud-based platform that integrates near-real-time and batch data through AI Next Fabric. A graph data store provides visibility from product origin to delivery. A centralized traceability portal supports top-down and bottom-up tracking workflows, and a regulatory control tower supports incident response.

The platform has been implemented in North America, with Europe planned to begin rollout in the second half of 2026. It:

- Connects more than 50 suppliers
- Captures over 0.5 million events per day
- Integrates more than 220 plants and distribution centers
- Maps more than 150 raw-material-to-finished-goods processes

Prebuilt FSMA compliance reports and one-touch batch and handling-unit traceability support regulatory audits and food safety processes.

## Microsoft

### Microsoft cut root cause analysis turnaround by 40% through Infosys' AI-led mission-critical support

#### Faster resolutions with agentic AI

Microsoft's Support for Mission Critical (SfMC) provides high-reliability support for enterprise customers, with a focus on minimizing downtime and resolving incidents rapidly.

Infosys integrated AI capabilities into the SfMC support architecture using the Microsoft AI technology stack. The deployed solutions included:

- Routing and Entitlement Validation Agent for triaging and routing critical cases to appropriately aligned engineers
- Azure Resiliency Correlation Engine for automated root-cause-analysis recommendations
- Notes Observation and Validation Agent for continuous quality audits across global operations

The transformation produced the following results:

- Critical incident response time improved by 33%, reducing engagement time from 15 minutes to under 10 minutes.
- Root cause analysis preparation time decreased by 40%, from five days to one day.
- More than 90% of critical cases received top-box ratings.
- Low case-handling scores were virtually eliminated.

## ABN AMRO

### ABN AMRO collaborates with Infosys to modernize its enterprise content operations

#### Unlocking €64 Million in Annual Efficiency savings

ABN AMRO is a Netherlands-based bank serving approximately 5 million retail customers and 365,000 business clients. Its enterprise content management ecosystem contains billions of documents, including scanned inputs, emails, and archived records.

Infosys and ABN AMRO assessed the bank's enterprise content management landscape and developed a proof of concept based on the Infosys Document Intelligence platform. They then implemented a generative-AI-powered enterprise content management layer using retrieval-augmented generation, integrated with Microsoft Azure AI and data services.

The solution supports automated classification, contextual information extraction, and faster retrieval of complex documents without requiring downloads from the enterprise content management archive. ABN AMRO estimated annual efficiency savings of €64 million. The transformation also improved employee productivity, reduced manual effort, and strengthened compliance.

## Our capitals

Infosys describes its capitals as an interconnected framework for creating and preserving value over the short, medium, and long term. Its strategy and ESG ambitions direct inputs through these capitals to produce outputs and outcomes for stakeholders.

## Financial Capital

Infosys obtains Financial Capital through funds generated from business operations and financing activities. The company describes its financial position as strong, debt-free, and liquid, supported by growth, cash generation, profitability, and sustained execution.

## Natural Capital

Climate action is a key focus of Infosys' Natural Capital approach. Infosys became carbon neutral in 2020, which it describes as 30 years ahead of the timeline set by the Paris Agreement, and has maintained carbon neutrality for seven consecutive years.

Infosys is evolving from carbon neutrality toward climate positivity through regenerative practices, nature-based solutions, and green innovation intended to restore ecosystems and capture more carbon than it emits.

## Human Capital

Infosys identifies talent development as essential to its continued success. The company has established employee upskilling and reskilling programs, including artificial intelligence training, and remains committed to an inclusive workplace for its diverse workforce.
