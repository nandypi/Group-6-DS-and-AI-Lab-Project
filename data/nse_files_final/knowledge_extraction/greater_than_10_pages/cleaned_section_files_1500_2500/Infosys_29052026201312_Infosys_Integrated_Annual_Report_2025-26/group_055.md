## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report

Infosys states that sustainability and responsible business are integrated into its business decisions, operations, and governance. The company adopted the GRI Framework in 2008 and began publishing a Business Responsibility Report in fiscal 2013. Its BRSR follows the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct and addresses ESG-related principles under Regulation 34(2)(f) of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015, as amended.
