As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, 3,87,949 and 2,88,384 cash-settled options were outstanding, respectively. The carrying value of the liability toward cash-settled share-based payments was `5 crore and `8 crore as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

Model inputs include the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on historical volatility of the Company's publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is based on historical movements in their publicly traded equity shares over an equivalent period. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between entities in the peer group.
