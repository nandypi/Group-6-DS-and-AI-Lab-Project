## 5. Does the entity have a business continuity and disaster management plan? Give details in 100 words/ web link.

Yes. Infosys has a Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) called Phoenix, certified by ISO 22301:2019 Security and Resilience – Business Continuity Management Standard.

Phoenix provides a framework for planning, establishing, implementing, operating, monitoring, reviewing, maintaining, and continually improving business continuity measures across Infosys and its subsidiaries. In addition to the corporate-level Phoenix plan, comprehensive business continuity plans cover:

- Business functions
- Locations
- Client accounts

Integrated into the Enterprise Risk Management Framework, these plans address catastrophes and natural or human-made disasters that could disrupt or severely constrain operations. Risk assessments at the functions, locations, and accounts levels document crisis scenarios, mitigation plans, and related controls. The management system is validated through tests and exercises, and Infosys reports that various incidents have been addressed without significant impacts on business continuity or employee safety.

## Enabling Responsible Value Chain Disclosure and Decarbonization

In fiscal 2026, Infosys initiated a SEBI-aligned supplier engagement program focused on ESG disclosures under the BRSR Core framework. The program collected ESG-related disclosures for fiscal 2025 from the top 2% of upstream suppliers by spend, focusing on high-impact suppliers, including human resource service providers and IT service vendors. These suppliers represented approximately 13% of total spend.

The engagement collected data on supplier climate practices, emissions management, and reporting maturity. Based on the assessment:

- Scope 1 emissions attributed to Infosys: 667 tCO2e
- Scope 2 emissions attributed to Infosys: 219 tCO2e
- Attributed emissions as a proportion of the suppliers’ combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions: approximately 1.6%

Infosys calculated the attributed emissions using a spend-based allocation methodology aligned with the GHG Protocol for value-chain disclosure under SEBI requirements. These emissions were not included in Infosys’s reported Scope 3 inventory.
