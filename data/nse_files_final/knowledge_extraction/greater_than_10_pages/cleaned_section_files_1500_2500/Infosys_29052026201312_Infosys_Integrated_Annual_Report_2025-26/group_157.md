## 2.26 Segment reporting

Ind AS 108, *Operating Segments*, establishes requirements for reporting operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group primarily provides end-to-end business solutions to enable clients to enhance business performance. The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates performance and allocates resources using performance indicators by business segment. The accounting principles used to record segment revenue and expenditure are consistent with those used in the financial statements.

The Group’s business segments are:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment combines the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments include operating segments of businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and individually identifiable operating expenses are allocated to segments. Allocated expenses include costs incurred at offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated segment effort.

Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocable because the underlying assets are used interchangeably between segments. These expenses are disclosed as unallocated and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities are also used interchangeably between segments. Management considers it impracticable to provide segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business segment revenue is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for whom revenue is otherwise recognized. Revenue by geographic location is disclosed in Note 2.18, “Revenue from operations.”
