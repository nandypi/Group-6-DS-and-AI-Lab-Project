An independent assessment or assurance was carried out by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP.

## 10. Briefly describe the waste management practices adopted in your establishments. Describe the strategy adopted by your company to reduce usage of hazardous and toxic chemicals in your products and processes and the practices adopted to manage such wastes.

Infosys’ waste management strategy is based on the 5R principle: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Refurbish / Repurpose, and Recycle. Through waste reduction and recovery, 99% of disposed waste is kept out of landfills.

Infosys is working toward its 2030 goal of zero waste to landfills by adopting TRUE Zero Waste Certification at its India campuses. As of fiscal 2026, 46% of its owned campuses in India had achieved TRUE Zero Waste Certification from Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI).

As an IT company, Infosys generates e-waste. It is moving toward a refurbishment-first approach where feasible and partners with authorized recyclers for materials that cannot be refurbished. Authorized Pollution Control Board recyclers and refurbishers are periodically evaluated for compliance with applicable legal requirements and the degree of resource circularity in their processes.

- (1) Includes sanitary waste.
- (2) Excludes construction and demolition waste from infrastructure development.
- (3) Includes waste generated in India, overseas-owned locations, and e-waste for all locations globally.
- (4) Co-processing was included in other recovery operations this year and was included under other disposal operations until the previous year.
