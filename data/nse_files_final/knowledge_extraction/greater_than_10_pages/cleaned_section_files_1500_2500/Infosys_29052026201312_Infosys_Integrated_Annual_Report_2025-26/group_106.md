For domestic defined benefit plans in India, because a market for high-quality corporate bonds has not developed, government bond yields are used as the discount rate. For most overseas defined benefit plans, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used because a market for high-quality corporate bonds has not developed.

The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined by the Company, considering past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management’s estimate of future salary increases.

The attrition rate is based on management’s estimate using the Company’s past long-term employee turnover trend. Employee tenure considers the past long-term trend in employees’ average remaining service life, reflecting the average estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, assumptions about future mortality experience follow published statistics from the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas defined benefit plans, mortality assumptions consider the latest life expectancy statistics, plan experience, and other relevant data.

The Company assesses these assumptions using its projected long-term growth plans and prevalent industry standards.

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Trustees administer contributions made to the trust. Overseas defined benefit plan assets are primarily invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined according to investment criteria prescribed by regulations applicable to pension funds and insurer managers. The insurers’ investments are diversified and include guaranteed-interest-rate arrangements.

These defined benefit plans expose the Company to the following actuarial risks:

- **Interest rate risk:** The present value of the defined benefit plan liability is generally calculated using a discount rate based on government bond yields. In certain overseas jurisdictions, the rate is based on government bond yields adjusted for a corporate spread. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation generally increases.
- **Life expectancy and investment risk:** The pension fund offers a choice between a lifelong pension and a cash lump sum at retirement. The fund has defined rates for converting the lump sum into a pension. Risk arises if members live longer than implied by these conversion rates or if pension assets do not achieve the investment return implied by the rates.
- **Asset volatility:** A proportion of the pension fund is held in equities, which are expected to outperform corporate bonds over the long term but expose the fund to short-term volatility and risk. The pension fund’s insurer board is responsible for the investment strategy and equity allocation.

Sensitivity for significant actuarial assumptions is computed by varying one actuarial assumption used to value the defined benefit obligation while keeping all other actuarial assumptions constant. In practice, this is not probable, and changes in some assumptions may be correlated.

The Company expects to contribute ` 490 crore to gratuity and ` 48 crore to pension during fiscal 2027.
