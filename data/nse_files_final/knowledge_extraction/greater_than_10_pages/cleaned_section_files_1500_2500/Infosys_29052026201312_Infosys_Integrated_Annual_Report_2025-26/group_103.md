## 2.18 Revenue from operations

## Accounting policy

Infosys derives revenue primarily from IT services, including software development and related services, cloud and infrastructure services, maintenance, consulting, package implementation, and licensing of software products and platforms across its core and digital offerings. Customer contracts may be on a time-and-material, unit-of-work, fixed-price, or fixed-timeframe basis.

Revenue is recognized when the contract has been approved in writing, the parties are committed to their obligations, and the contract is legally enforceable. Revenue is recognized upon transfer of control of the promised products or services to customers, at the transaction price expected in exchange for those products or services. When collectability is uncertain, revenue recognition is postponed until the uncertainty is resolved.

The Company identifies distinct performance obligations and allocates the transaction price based on relative standalone selling prices. The price regularly charged when an item is sold separately is the best evidence of standalone selling price. When that evidence is unavailable, Infosys generally uses an expected-cost-plus-margin approach.

Variable consideration, including rebates, volume discounts, and penalties, is included in the transaction price when it can be reasonably estimated and it is probable that a significant reversal of cumulative revenue will not occur when the uncertainty is resolved.

Revenue recognition methods include:

- Revenue from time-and-material and unit-of-work contracts is recognized as the related services are performed.
- Fixed-price maintenance revenue is recognized ratably on a straight-line basis when services consist of an indefinite number of repetitive acts over a specified period. It is recognized using a percentage-of-completion method when the pattern of benefits and fulfillment costs is uneven because the services are discrete rather than repetitive.
- Revenue from other fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts satisfied over time is recognized using the percentage-of-completion method. Costs or efforts incurred to date are used to measure progress against estimated total costs or efforts.
- Estimates of transaction price and total costs or efforts are monitored throughout the contract. Changes in estimates are recognized in net profit in the period of revision. Provisions for estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable.
- Billing may be based on periodic performance or contractual milestones. Revenue in excess of billing is classified as unbilled revenue, while billing in excess of revenue is classified as a contract liability, referred to as unearned revenue.

For software development, related services, and maintenance services, the performance obligations are generally distinct and are satisfied as services are rendered because customers generally obtain control of the work as it progresses. Where standalone selling prices cannot be determined, the expected-cost-plus-margin approach is used.

Certain cloud and infrastructure services contracts include multiple elements subject to specific accounting guidance, including leasing guidance. When hardware and services are distinct performance obligations, consideration is allocated based on relative standalone selling prices. When they are treated as a single performance obligation, revenue is recognized over the contract period based on the measure of progress specified by the contract.

Revenue from licenses is recognized when:

- The customer obtains a right to use the license: when the license is made available to the customer.
- The customer obtains a right to access the license: over the access period.

Arrangements to deliver software products generally include licensing, implementation, and Annual Technical Services (ATS). When license and implementation are distinct performance obligations, the transaction price is allocated based on relative standalone selling prices. If the license requires substantial customization as part of implementation, the license and implementation fee is treated as a single performance obligation and recognized using the percentage-of-completion method as implementation is performed. Revenue from client training, support, and other services is recognized as the related performance obligations are satisfied. ATS revenue is recognized ratably on a straight-line basis over the service period.

For integrated services arrangements involving subcontractor services or third-party vendor equipment or software, revenue is recorded net of costs when Infosys acts as an agent and gross when it acts as the principal. The Company assesses control, responsibility for fulfilling the promise, inventory risk, pricing discretion, and other factors in determining whether it is acting as principal or agent.

A contract modification that adds distinct performance obligations is accounted for as a separate contract when the additional services are priced at standalone selling price. Otherwise, it is accounted for as termination of the existing contract and creation of a new contract. Modifications that do not add a distinct performance obligation are accounted for as part of the existing contract on a cumulative catch-up basis.

Incremental costs of obtaining a contract are recognized as an asset when Infosys expects to recover them. Eligible non-recurring costs, such as setup, transition, or transformation costs, are recognized as an asset when they relate directly to the contract, generate or enhance resources used to satisfy future performance obligations, and are expected to be recovered.

Capitalized contract costs relating to upfront payments to customers are amortized to revenue. Other capitalized costs are amortized to expenses over the contract life on a systematic basis consistent with the transfer of the related goods or services. Capitalized costs are monitored for impairment, with impairment losses recorded when the present value of projected remaining operating cash flows is insufficient to recover their carrying amount.

Revenue is presented net of indirect taxes in the Statement of Profit and Loss.

## Products & platforms

The Company derives revenue from the sale of products and platforms, including Infosys Applied AI, which applies next-generation artificial intelligence and machine learning.

The percentage of revenue from fixed-price contracts was 58% for each of the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

## Trade receivables and Contract Balances

The timing of revenue recognition, billings, and cash collections results in trade receivables, unbilled revenue, and unearned revenue on the Balance Sheet. Amounts are billed as work progresses under agreed contractual terms, either periodically, such as monthly or quarterly, or upon achievement of contractual milestones.

Trade receivables represent unconditional rights to consideration. Unbilled revenue from time-and-material contracts and fixed-price maintenance contracts is classified as a financial asset when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

For other fixed-price contracts, invoicing is based on contractual milestones. Because the timing of revenue recognition differs from invoicing, unbilled revenue is classified as a non-financial contract asset when the right to consideration depends on completion of contractual milestones.

Invoicing in excess of earnings is classified as unearned revenue. Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are presented net of impairment in the Balance Sheet.

During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Company recognized revenue of ₹5,276 crore and ₹4,404 crore, respectively, from opening unearned revenue balances as of April 1, 2025 and April 1, 2024, respectively.

During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, ₹4,413 crore and ₹4,448 crore, respectively, of unbilled revenue pertaining to other fixed-price and fixed-timeframe contracts as of April 1, 2025 and April 1, 2024 was reclassified to trade receivables upon billing to customers after completion of milestones.
