During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Company designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions for the balance in the cash flow hedge reserve as at March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the Statement of Profit and Loss within three months.

The Company determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at inception and through periodic prospective assessments. These assessments consider whether the hedging instrument is expected to offset changes in the cash flows of the hedged items.

If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal for risk management purposes, but the risk management objective remains unchanged and the hedge continues to qualify for hedge accounting, the hedge relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Any hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in the Statement of Profit or Loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.

## Credit risk

Credit risk is the risk of default by a counterparty resulting in a financial loss. Maximum exposure at the reporting date was primarily from:

| Particulars | March 31, 2026 (₹ crore) | March 31, 2025 (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trade receivables | 30,337 | 26,413 |
| Unbilled revenue | 13,706 | 11,988 |

Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are typically unsecured and primarily relate to customers located in the United States of America and Europe. The Company manages credit risk through credit approvals, credit limits, and continuous monitoring of customer creditworthiness. It uses the expected credit loss model and a provision matrix for trade receivables and unbilled revenue, incorporating available credit reports and related credit information.

Credit risk is influenced mainly by individual customer characteristics and concentration among the largest customers. Customer exposure is diversified, with no single customer contributing more than 10% of outstanding trade receivables and unbilled revenue.

## 2.12 Equity

### Accounting policy

#### Ordinary shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity share capital. Incremental costs directly attributable to the issuance of new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of related tax effects.

## Description of reserves

### Capital redemption reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

### Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the Company's accumulated earnings.

### Securities premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as securities premium. The amounts have been used for bonus issues and share buybacks.

### Share options outstanding account

The share options outstanding account records the fair value of equity-settled share-based payment transactions with employees. Amounts recorded in this account are transferred to securities premium upon exercise of stock options and to general reserve when stock options are not exercised by employees.
