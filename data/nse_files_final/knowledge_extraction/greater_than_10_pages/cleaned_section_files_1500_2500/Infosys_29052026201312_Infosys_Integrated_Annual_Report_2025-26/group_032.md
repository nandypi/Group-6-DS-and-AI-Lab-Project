## 8.  Income tax assets / liabilities

Net profit earned from providing software development and other services outside India is subject to tax in the country where the work is performed. Most taxes paid outside India can be claimed as credit against tax liabilities in India. Refer to Note 2.17 of the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements.

## 9. Other financial liabilities

- Accrued compensation to employees includes provisions for bonus, accrued salaries, incentives, and retention bonus payable to staff.
- Financial liability under option arrangements represents the redemption liability associated with acquisitions to purchase the corresponding minority stake.
- Accrued expenses represent amounts accrued for other operational expenses.
- Compensated absences are both accumulating and non-accumulating. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by actuarial valuation.

## IV. Results of our operations

For details about results of operations, refer to the “Results of our operations and state of affairs” section in the Boards' report.

During fiscal 2026, the Company continued Project Maximus, a margin expansion program structured across five pillars:

1. Value-based selling
2. Efficient pyramid
3. Lean, automation and gen AI
4. Improving key portfolios
5. Optimization of indirect costs

Value-based selling identifies opportunities to align pricing with the value clients receive. Efficient pyramid initiatives reduce effort costs through improved utilization, reduced subcontractor use, and changes to role mix and location mix. Lean, automation and gen AI combines productivity and process improvement using automation technologies, including generative AI. Improving key portfolios focuses on margins in large client engagements and service lines. Savings from the optimization of indirect costs supported investment in talent, AI, and sales and marketing.

## 1.  Revenue

The growth in revenue from fiscal 2025 to fiscal 2026 was as follows:

| Particulars | Standalone 2026 | Standalone 2025 | Standalone % change | Consolidated 2026 | Consolidated 2025 | Consolidated % change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 1,48,819 | 1,36,592 | 9.0% | 1,78,650 | 1,62,990 | 9.6% |

The increase in revenue was primarily attributable to higher realization resulting from role mix, improved pricing and productivity, acquisitions, and currency benefit.

Revenue growth in reported terms includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant currency growth represents revenue growth excluding currency fluctuations. It is calculated by comparing current-period revenues in respective local currencies converted to USD using prior-period exchange rates with prior-period reported revenues.

Consolidated revenue in reported currency terms for fiscal 2026 was ₹1,78,650 crore, representing growth of 9.6%. Consolidated revenue in constant currency terms grew by 3.1%.

Infosys added 443 gross new customers during fiscal 2026, compared with 365 gross new customers during fiscal 2025.

## 10. Other liabilities

- Invoicing in excess of revenue is classified as unearned revenue.
- Withholding and other taxes payable represent local taxes payable in the various countries in which the Company operates.
- Infosys provides a provident fund for eligible employees. This is treated as a defined benefit plan because the Company has an obligation to make good any shortfall between the return on trust investments and the notified interest rate.
- The Company operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers.
- Infosys provides gratuity, a defined benefit retirement plan, to eligible employees in India. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure.
- The plans provide periodic payouts after retirement and/or lump-sum payments under the rules of each fund, including death and disability benefits.
