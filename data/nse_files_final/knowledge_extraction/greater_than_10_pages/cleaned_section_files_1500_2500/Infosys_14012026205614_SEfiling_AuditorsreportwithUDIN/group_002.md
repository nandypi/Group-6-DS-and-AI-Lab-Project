## INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT ON THE AUDIT OF QUARTERLY AND NINE MONTHS ENDED STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the accompanying statement of Standalone Financial Results of Infosys Limited for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025. The statement was submitted pursuant to Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

In the auditor’s opinion, based on the information available and explanations provided:

- The statement is presented in accordance with the requirements of Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- The statement gives a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, of the net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information.
