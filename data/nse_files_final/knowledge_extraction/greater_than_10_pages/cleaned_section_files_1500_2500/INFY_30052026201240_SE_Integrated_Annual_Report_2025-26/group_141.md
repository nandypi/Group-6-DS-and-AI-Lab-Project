In assessing the realizability of deferred income tax assets, Management considers whether some or all of the assets will not be realized. Ultimate realization depends on generating future taxable income during the periods in which the temporary differences become deductible.

Management considers the scheduled reversals of deferred income tax liabilities, projected future taxable income, and tax planning strategies. Based on historical taxable income and projected future taxable income over the periods in which the deferred income tax assets are deductible, Management believes that the Group will realize the benefits of those deductible differences. The amount considered realizable could be reduced in the near term if estimates of future taxable income during the carry-forward period are reduced.

The Company’s Advanced Pricing Arrangement (APA) with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) for US branch income tax expired in March 2021. The Company has applied for renewal of the APA. Currently, US taxable income is based on the Company’s best estimate determined using the expected value method.
