## Philosophy

Infosys is committed to a positive and equal-opportunity workplace free from discrimination and harassment. Its employee-experience approach, described as “Experience by Design,” focuses on consistent policies, processes, programs, and systems with employees at the core.

The Company’s employee initiatives are intended to support purposeful work, high-performing teams, continuous learning, career development, leadership development, workforce productivity, employee engagement, and operational excellence. Infosys has adopted a hybrid work model and maintains recognition and rewards programs alongside diversity, equity, and inclusion measures.

## Resolution hubs

Infosys maintains an open-door policy and a grievance-resolution mechanism comprising resolution hubs. The hubs follow the principles of natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Investigations provide involved parties with an opportunity to present facts and supporting evidence.

## Corner Stones

The Infosys Human Rights Statement, Global Policy on Anti-harassment / Discrimination, and Code of Conduct & Ethics are the cornerstones of this framework.

## Culture

Infosys promotes a speak-up culture in which employees are encouraged to report concerns or suspected violations of the Code, Company policies and procedures, or applicable laws.

## Resolution Hubs at Infosys include

### HEAR (Hearing Employees And Resolving)

HEAR is an independent employee-grievance resolution forum under the Human Resources Department. It handles workplace-related grievances that remain unresolved at the unit HR or manager level and provides multiple layers of redressal, including an appeal mechanism.

The HEAR Office uses a decentralized model with a presence across business units, geographies, affiliates, and subsidiaries. A neutral panel conducts objective reviews, and the forum also reviews anonymous concerns in collaboration with the Whistleblower Office. Compliance audits are used where appropriate to assess adherence to relevant policies.

HEAR also advises departments on policy and process compliance and provides employee-feedback insights to senior management. Its grievance-resolution framework is based on due diligence and experience from panel reviews and investigations.

## The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI)

ASHI is Infosys’ enterprise-wide framework for the prevention, prohibition, and redressal of sexual harassment. It applies a gender-neutral, employee-centric, and globally consistent approach for employees, contractors, and associates, with confidentiality, procedural fairness, non-retaliation, and adherence to natural-justice principles.

### ASHI (Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative )

- The initiative addresses the prevention, prohibition, and redressal of sexual-harassment concerns.
- The Grievance Redressal Body (GRB) is the custodian of the initiative.
- An Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) framework is used for handling and resolving incidents.
- The scope is gender-neutral and covers sexual harassment in the workplace.

In accordance with the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, Internal Committees have been established across all development centers under the applicable regulatory jurisdiction. These committees are responsible for addressing sexual-harassment concerns raised by women employees.

ASHI includes safe reporting channels, continuous awareness and education, enablement initiatives, and monitoring and analytics. The GRB comprises external experts, senior internal leaders, and the Investigative Council, and oversees governance, policy interpretation, and implementation relating to workplace sexual-harassment prevention.

Employees can report concerns through the ASHI web application, the InfyMe app, or by email to GRB@infosys.com. Cases follow a formal process aligned with statutory requirements and Company policies.

## Behavior Matters

The Behavior Matters campaign focuses on workplace behavior, respect, collaboration, productivity, employee well-being, and an environment in which employees feel valued and respected.

## Extending the commitment to contract staff

Infosys extends its safe and inclusive workplace commitment to third-party personnel operating on its campuses. Refresher training is delivered in nine Indian languages. Emergency and safety cards containing essential contact information are provided to employees and contracted staff.

## Whistleblower Policy

Infosys’ Whistleblower Policy is aligned with the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX), 2002, and the Companies Act, 2013, which require listed companies to establish a vigil mechanism for reporting genuine concerns.

The mechanism primarily covers complaints relating to probable discrepancies in accounting, internal accounting controls, or auditing, and permits anonymous reporting by employees. The Office of Integrity and Compliance administers the policy and forum. Complaints concerning deviations from workplace policies or processes are reviewed in coordination with the Employee Relations Department.

## Human rights

Infosys is a signatory to the United Nations Global Compact and supports human-rights principles under the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and the International Labour Organization’s Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work. The Company is also a signatory to the UN Women’s Empowerment Principles.

The Infosys Code of Conduct and Ethics includes “Respecting Each Other” as a fundamental principle. The Company’s Human Rights Statement provides a framework for treating employees with respect and dignity and for preventing human-rights violations or abuses in its business practices.

[Further details are available in the Infosys Code of Conduct.](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-Governance/documents/codeofconduct.pdf)

## Recruitment

As of March 31, 2026, the Group employed 3,28,594 employees, including 3,10,887 professionals involved in service delivery to clients, including trainees.

Infosys recruits across various IT fields and maintains relationships with educational institutions worldwide. Its HackWithInfy online coding competition is used to attract coding talent. Through the RestartWithInfy initiative, Infosys hired 947 women who had taken career breaks into the Infosys ecosystem in fiscal 2026.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys received 58,84,480 employment applications, interviewed 4,50,901 applicants, and extended offers of employment to 87,286 applicants. These statistics exclude subsidiaries.

## Education, training and assessment (ETA)

Infosys’ learning programs include technology training, continuous education, reskilling, upskilling, and learning supported by artificial intelligence. The Foundation Training Program prepares newly onboarded entry-level hires for corporate roles and covers more than 40 technology streams, including generative AI and prompt engineering.

In fiscal 2026, 10,766 fresh graduates completed foundation training at the Global Education Center campus in Mysuru and were released to delivery units.

The Continuous Education Program provides online self-learning, instructor-led virtual training, and in-person classroom training. Bridge programs support employees moving into new career fields through training and internship opportunities. In fiscal 2026, 81.8% of the workforce received training, with average annual learning hours of 113 hours. Cumulatively, 84% of the workforce was AI-aware.

Lex, Infosys’ in-house learning platform, offers more than 40,000 curated courses, including more than 30,000 courses procured from vendor partners. On average, 37,000 employees used Lex on weekdays and approximately 8,000 used it on weekends, with average learning time of 39 minutes.

Infosys Wingspan is a configurable talent-transformation platform used by several global organizations for their talent-transformation programs.

Infosys Springboard offers more than 20,000 courses. Approximately 11 million learners worldwide actively consume content on the platform.
