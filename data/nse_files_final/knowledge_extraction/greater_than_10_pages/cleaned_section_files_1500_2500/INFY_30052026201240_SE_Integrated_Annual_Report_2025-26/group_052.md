## 4. Management's Responsibility

Management is responsible for selecting or establishing suitable criteria for preparing the Sustainability Information, including the BRSR reporting boundary, identification of key aspects, stakeholder engagement, content, preparation, and presentation of the Identified Sustainability Information.

This responsibility includes designing, implementing, and maintaining internal controls relevant to the preparation of the BRSR and measurement of the Identified Sustainability Information, ensuring it is free from material misstatement due to fraud or error.

## 5. Inherent limitations

The absence of an established body of practice for evaluating and measuring non-financial information allows for different, but acceptable, measurement techniques. This can affect comparability between companies.

## 6. Our Independence and Quality Control

The assurance provider maintained its independence and confirmed compliance with the ICAI Code of Ethics, the SEBI Master Circular and related clarifications, as well as the required competencies and experience for the engagement.

The provider applies Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1 and maintains a quality-control system covering ethical requirements, professional standards, and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## 7. Our Responsibility

The assurance provider's responsibility is to express a reasonable assurance opinion on the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I, based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained.

The engagement was conducted in accordance with:

- Standard on Sustainability Assurance Engagements (SSAE) 3000, *Assurance Engagements on Sustainability Information*.
- Standard on Assurance Engagements (SAE) 3410, *Assurance Engagements on Greenhouse Gas Statements*.

These standards require planning and performance of procedures to obtain reasonable assurance that the Identified Sustainability Information included in the BRSR is prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the stated criteria.

The procedures included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Identified Sustainability Information and related disclosures.
- Assessing the suitability of the evaluation and measurement criteria.
- Making inquiries of management and personnel responsible for preparing the report, including the environment, compliance, and human resources teams.
- Evaluating the design and implementation of key systems, processes, and controls for recording, processing, and reporting sustainability information at the corporate office and sampled other locations.
- Determining the nature, timing, and extent of further procedures based on assessed risks of material misstatement.
- Testing key assumptions, emission factors, and methodologies used to calculate greenhouse gas emissions.
- Testing the process for collating sustainability information by agreeing or reconciling it with underlying records on a sample basis.
- Testing consolidation for sampled locations and the corporate office within the reporting boundary to assess completeness of reported data.

The assurance provider concluded that the evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the reasonable assurance opinion.

## 8. Exclusions

The assurance scope excluded:

- Company operations outside the reporting boundary set out in Question 13 of Section A, General Disclosures, of the BRSR, for the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I.
- Report aspects and qualitative or quantitative data other than the Identified Sustainability Information.
- Data and information outside the financial year ended March 31, 2026.
- Statements expressing opinions, beliefs, aspirations, expectations, aims, or future intentions.

## 9. Other information

Management is responsible for other information included in the BRSR, the Integrated Annual Report, and the Environment, Social and Governance Report, other than the Identified Sustainability Information and the independent assurance reports dated May 27, 2026.

The assurance opinion does not cover this other information. The assurance provider's responsibility was to read it and consider whether it was materially inconsistent with the Identified Sustainability Information or appeared to be materially misstated. The assurance provider reported no such matters.

## 10. Reasonable Assurance Opinion

Based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained, the BRSR Core indicators for the year ended March 31, 2026, listed in Appendix I and presented in the BRSR, were prepared in all material respects in accordance with the stated criteria.

## 11. Restriction on use

The reasonable assurance report was prepared for the Company's Board of Directors to assist with reporting on the Company's sustainability performance and activities. It is not intended for use by any other person or for any other purpose without prior written consent.
