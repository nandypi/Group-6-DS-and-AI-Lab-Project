## 5. Describe the internal mechanisms in place to redress grievances related to human rights issues.

Infosys provides employees and contract staff access to Resolution Hubs, a grievance resolution mechanism for workplace concerns, including human rights issues.

Resolution Hubs follow the principles of natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Concerns are investigated through a process that gives all involved parties an opportunity to present facts and material evidence, followed by timely action and closure.

## 8. Mechanism to prevent adverse consequences to the complainant in discrimination and harassment cases

Infosys’ non-retaliation policy protects individuals who make good-faith reports of integrity concerns. The protection also extends to anyone who assists with, cooperates in, or reports an integrity concern or question during an investigation.
