## Governance of Board committees

The Board, in consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, is responsible for assigning and determining the terms of service for committee members.

The Chairman of the Board, in consultation with the Company Secretary and the respective committee chairperson, determines the frequency of committee meetings. Generally, committees meet four times a year. The recommendations of the committees are submitted to the Board for approval. During the year, all recommendations of the committees were approved by the Board.

The quorum for meetings is the higher of two members or one-third of the total number of members of the committee.

## Objectives of the Committee

The primary objectives of the Committee are to assist the Board with oversight of:

1. The accuracy, integrity, and transparency of the Company’s financial statements, with adequate and timely disclosures.
2. Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
3. The professional qualifications and independence of the Company’s independent auditors.
4. The performance of the Company’s independent auditors and internal auditors.
5. Acquisitions and investments made by the Company.

## Audit Committee Charter ('the Charter')

In India, the Company is listed on BSE Limited (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE). It is also listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) in the US.

Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations in India and the Blue Ribbon Committee established by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) in the US mandate that listed companies adopt an appropriate charter. The Committee is guided by the Charter, which was adopted by the Board and is available on the Company’s website:

[Audit Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/audit-committee-charter.pdf)

## Process adopted by the Committee to fulfill its objectives

The Committee:

- Ensures an effective and independent internal audit function that provides assurance regarding the adequacy and effectiveness of the design and operation of internal controls.
- Reviews processes intended to safeguard the Company’s assets, ensure effective and efficient use of resources, and support timely and accurate recording of transactions.
- Meets the independent auditors at the end of each quarter and financial year to discuss key observations relating to the relevant financial statements.
- Provides an independent channel of communication for the Chief Compliance Officer, internal auditors, and independent auditors.
- Invites members of Management and, at its discretion, external experts in legal, financial, and technical matters to provide clarifications, advice, and guidance.
- Provides periodic feedback and reports to the Board.
- Periodically reviews its Charter, structure, processes, and membership, and recommends proposed changes to the Board for approval.
- Meets at least four times a year and ensures that the gap between two meetings does not exceed 120 days.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises four members and fulfills the requirements of:

- Audit Committee Charter
- Sections 149 and 177 of the Companies Act, 2013
- Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations
- NYSE guidelines, as applicable

To carry out its responsibilities efficiently and transparently, the Committee relies on Management’s financial expertise and that of the internal and independent auditors.

Management is responsible for the Company’s internal control over financial reporting and the financial reporting process. The independent auditors are responsible for performing an independent audit of the Company’s financial statements in accordance with Generally Accepted Auditing Principles and for issuing a report based on the audit.
