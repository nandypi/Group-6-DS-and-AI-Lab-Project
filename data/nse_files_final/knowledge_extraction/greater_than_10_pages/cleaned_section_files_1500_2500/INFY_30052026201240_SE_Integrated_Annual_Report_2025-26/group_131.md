For all financial assets other than those subject to lifetime ECL, expected credit losses (ECLs) are measured at an amount equal to the 12-month ECL unless there has been a significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition. In that case, lifetime ECL is recognized.

The Group determines allowances for credit losses based on historical loss experience, adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions. The Group considers current and anticipated future economic conditions relating to the industries it serves and the countries in which it operates.

The ECL, or reversal, required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.
