## Opinion

Based on examination of relevant records, information and explanations provided, and representations from Management, the Company complied with the corporate governance conditions stipulated in regulations 17 to 27 and clauses (b) to (i) of regulation 46(2), as well as paragraphs C and D of Schedule V of the Listing Regulations, during the year ended March 31, 2026.

This compliance statement is not an assurance regarding the Company’s future viability or the efficiency or effectiveness with which Management conducted the Company’s affairs.

## Annexure 5 - Secretarial audit report for the financial year ended March 31, 2026 Form No. MR-3

[Pursuant to Section 204(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule No. 9 of the Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules, 2014]

The secretarial audit of Infosys Limited covered compliance with applicable statutory provisions and adherence to good corporate practices. The audit period was April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026.

Based on verification of the Company’s books, papers, minute books, forms, returns, and other records, and information provided by the Company and its representatives, the auditors reported that:

- The Company complied with the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made thereunder.
- The Company complied with the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956 and related rules.
- The Company complied with the Depositories Act, 1996 and related regulations and bye-laws.
- The Company complied with the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 and related rules and regulations concerning Foreign Direct Investment and Overseas Direct Investment. External Commercial Borrowings were not applicable during the audit period.
- The Company complied with the following regulations and guidelines under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992:
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents) Regulations, 1993 and 2025, concerning the Companies Act and dealings with clients.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-back of Securities) Regulations, 2018.
- The following regulations were not applicable during the audit period:
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities) Regulations, 2021.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.
- The Company complied with applicable Secretarial Standards issued by The Institute of Company Secretaries of India.
- The Company complied with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and amendments thereunder.
- On a test-check basis, the Company generally complied with the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 and related rules, and the Software Technology Parks of India Rules and Regulations.

During the audit period, the Company complied with the applicable provisions of the Acts, rules, regulations, guidelines, and standards.

## We further report that

The Board of Directors was duly constituted with an appropriate balance of Executive, Non-Executive, and Independent Directors. No changes took place in the composition of the Board during the audit period.

Adequate notice was generally given for Board meetings, with agendas and detailed agenda notes sent at least seven days in advance. Two meetings were convened at shorter notice, with the necessary approvals obtained under applicable provisions. A system was in place for seeking and obtaining further information and clarifications on agenda items before meetings.

All decisions at Board and Committee meetings were carried out unanimously, as recorded in the relevant minutes.

The Company had systems and processes commensurate with its size and operations to monitor and ensure compliance with applicable laws, rules, regulations, and guidelines.

During the audit period:

- The Company issued and allotted 8,74,856 equity shares of face value ₹5 each under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan.
- The Company issued and allotted 14,53,412 equity shares of face value ₹5 each under the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019.
- The Company bought back 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each for an aggregate consideration of ₹18,000 crore, at a price of ₹1,800 per share.

The Secretarial Audit report is to be read with Annexure A, which forms an integral part of the report.

## Annexure A

The maintenance of secretarial records is the responsibility of Management. The auditors’ responsibility was to express an opinion on those records based on their audit.

The auditors followed audit practices and processes designed to obtain reasonable assurance about the correctness of the secretarial records. Verification was conducted on a test basis. The auditors did not verify the correctness and appropriateness of the Company’s financial records and books of account.

Where required, Management representations were obtained concerning compliance with laws, rules, regulations, and events. Responsibility for compliance with applicable corporate and other laws, rules, regulations, and standards rests with Management. The examination was limited to test-based verification of procedures.

The Secretarial Audit report is not an assurance regarding the Company’s future viability or the efficacy or effectiveness with which Management conducted the Company’s affairs.

## Annexure 6 - Annual report on CSR activities

[Pursuant to Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, as amended.]

## 1. Brief outline on CSR Policy of the Company:

Infosys’ CSR Policy focuses on sustainable development of society and the environment. CSR activities cover:

- Education and skill development to enable sustained livelihoods.
- Healthcare, including preventive health.
- Environmental sustainability and ecological balance.
- Gender equality and empowerment of women.
- Promotion of national heritage, art, and culture.
- Rural development.
- Disaster relief and rehabilitation.

Infosys undertakes CSR initiatives directly and through Infosys Foundation, which was established in 1996.

## CSR activities

Key activities during the year included:

- Education-to-employment initiatives focused on skilling underserved youth through strategic partnerships and livelihood training.
- Scholarships for students pursuing undergraduate studies in National Institutional Ranking Framework-accredited colleges in Engineering and other STEM fields, including nursing, pharmacy, biochemistry, and biotechnology.
- The Infosys Springboard digital literacy program, providing access to core digital and life skills.
- The Universal Cornea Care Mission at the L.V. Prasad Eye Institute, including model optimization, operationalization, validation, and development of a strategy for replication across the country.
- Construction of a mother and child healthcare center at the Government Hospital, Kunigal.
- Tree plantation initiatives to promote environmental sustainability, maintain ecological balance, and enhance farmer livelihoods and income.
- Co-implementation of the Gear for Gold program to develop sustainable, inclusive, and high-performing sports academies through support for coaching, infrastructure, sports science, governance, and administration.
- Support for the operationalization of an advanced mother and child center through the provision of medical equipment at PGIMER Chandigarh.
