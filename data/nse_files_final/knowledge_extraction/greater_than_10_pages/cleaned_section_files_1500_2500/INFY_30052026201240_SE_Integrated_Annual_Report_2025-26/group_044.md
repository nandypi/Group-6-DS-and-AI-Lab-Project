Nandan M. Nilekani voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company.

Salil Parekh receives remuneration only from Infosys Limited and not from any subsidiary or group company. During fiscal 2026, he exercised 2,72,400 RSUs under the 2015 Plan and 64,690 RSUs under the 2019 Plan.

On the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and with shareholder approval, the Board approved the following grants to Salil Parekh:

- 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on cumulative relative TSR over the relevant period, as determined by the Board.
- 18,132 annual time-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2015 Plan, effective February 1, 2026, vesting over three years.
- 66,366 performance-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on performance criteria specified in the 2019 Plan.

These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement and were issued at par value.

In accordance with the Listing Regulations, no employee, key managerial personnel, senior management member, director, or promoter of a listed entity may enter into an agreement with a shareholder or third party regarding compensation or profit-sharing connected with dealings in the Company’s securities without prior Board approval and shareholder approval by ordinary resolution. No such instances were reported during fiscal 2026.
