## 2.25 Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

As per Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, a company meeting the applicability threshold needs to spend at least 2% of its average net profit for the immediately preceding three financial years on corporate social responsibility activities. The CSR areas include education, skilling and livelihoods, environmental sustainability and ecological balance, healthcare including preventive health, national heritage, art and culture, rural development, and disaster relief and rehabilitation. A CSR committee has been formed by the Company under the Act.
