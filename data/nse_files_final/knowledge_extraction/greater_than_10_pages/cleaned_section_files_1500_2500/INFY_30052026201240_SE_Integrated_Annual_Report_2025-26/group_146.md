For domestic defined benefit plans in India, because a market for high-quality corporate bonds has not developed, the yield on government bonds is used as the discount rate. For most overseas defined benefit plans, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used because a market for high-quality corporate bonds has not developed.

The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined by the Company based on factors including past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management’s estimate of future salary increases.

The attrition rate is based on management’s estimate from the Company’s past long-term employee turnover trends. Employee tenure reflects the past long-term trend in employees’ average remaining service life and the average estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

For domestic defined benefit plans in India, assumptions regarding future mortality experience follow published statistics from the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas plans, mortality assumptions consider the latest life-expectancy statistics, plan experience, and other relevant data.

The Group assesses these assumptions against its projected long-term growth plans and prevailing industry standards.

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to the Infosys BPM Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively. As of March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the trustees administered contributions made to these trusts. Gratuity contributions are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

Overseas defined benefit plan assets have primarily been invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined according to the investment criteria prescribed under relevant pension-fund regulations and by the insurer managers. The insurers’ investments are diversified and provide guaranteed interest-rate arrangements.

## Actuarial risks

The defined benefit plans expose the Group to the following actuarial risks:

- **Interest rate risk:** The present value of the defined benefit plan liability is generally calculated using a discount rate based on government bond yields. In certain overseas jurisdictions, the rate is based on government bond yields adjusted for a corporate spread. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation will tend to increase.
- **Life expectancy and investment risk:** The pension fund offers a choice between a lifelong pension and a cash lump sum on retirement. The fund has defined rates for converting the lump sum into a pension. There is a risk that members live longer than implied by these conversion rates or that pension assets do not achieve the investment return implied by the rates.
- **Asset volatility:** A proportion of the pension fund is invested in equities. These investments are expected to outperform corporate bonds over the long term but create short-term volatility and risk. The pension fund insurer’s board is responsible for the investment strategy. Equity allocation is supported by the fund’s long-term investment horizon and objective of providing a reasonable long-term return on members’ account balances.
