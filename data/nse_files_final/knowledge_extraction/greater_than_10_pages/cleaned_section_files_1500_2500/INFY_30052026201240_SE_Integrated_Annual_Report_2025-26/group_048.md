## Procedure for postal ballot

The postal ballot was carried out under Sections 108 and 110 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the relevant rules, and applicable Ministry of Corporate Affairs circulars.

## Details of special resolution proposed to be transacted through postal ballot

None of the businesses proposed for the ensuing AGM requires approval by special resolution through postal ballot.

## Legal compliance

Infosys states that disclosures and policies relating to legal compliance, including non-compliance, regulatory orders, certifications, and complaints, are provided in the corporate governance report.

## Details of non-compliance

No penalty was imposed by any stock exchange, SEBI, or SEC, and there was no instance of non-compliance with legal requirements or capital-market matters during the last three years.

## Regulatory orders

There were no material regulatory orders pertaining to the Company for fiscal 2026.

## CEO and CFO certification

The CEO and CFO certification required under the Listing Regulations is provided in the Integrated Annual Report.

## Code of Conduct and Ethics

Infosys has adopted a Code of Conduct and Ethics applicable to the Board, executive officers, and all employees of the Company and its subsidiaries. All Board members, executive officers, and senior management affirmed compliance with the Code as of March 31, 2026.

## Establishment of vigil / whistleblower mechanism

Infosys has established a mechanism for directors and employees to report unethical behavior, actual or suspected fraud, and violations of the Code. The mechanism includes safeguards against victimization and provides direct access to the Audit Committee chairperson in exceptional cases. During the year, no person was denied access to the Audit Committee.

## Complaints pertaining to sexual harassment

Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report.

## Prevention of insider trading

Infosys amended its Code of Conduct for Prohibition of Insider Trading on April 23, 2026, and its Code on Fair Disclosures and Investor Relations on April 17, 2025.

The insider-trading code regulates, monitors, and reports trading by designated persons and their immediate relatives. It also covers inquiries into leaks or suspected leaks of unpublished price-sensitive information under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance with discretionary requirements

Infosys implemented the following nonmandatory corporate governance measures:

- Separate positions for the Chairman and the CEO & MD, with reimbursement of expenses incurred in official duties.
- A non-executive chairperson who is not related to the CEO & MD.
- A separate office for the Chairman within the Company premises.
- Unmodified audit opinions and reporting.
- Internal auditor reporting directly to the Audit Committee.

## Other disclosures and affirmations

- Infosys complied with the corporate governance requirements under Regulations 17 to 27 and the website disclosure requirements under Regulation 46(2) of the Listing Regulations.
- The securities of the Company were not suspended from trading during fiscal 2026.
