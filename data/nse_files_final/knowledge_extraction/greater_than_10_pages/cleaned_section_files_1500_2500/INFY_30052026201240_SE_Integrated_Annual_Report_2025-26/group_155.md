## 2.26 Segment reporting

Ind AS 108, *Operating segments*, establishes requirements for reporting operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group primarily provides end-to-end business solutions. The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates performance and allocates resources using business-segment performance indicators. Segment accounting principles are consistent with the Group’s accounting policies.

The Group’s business segments are:

- Financial Services
- Manufacturing
- Retail
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment combines the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments include operating segments in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Segment revenue and identifiable operating expenses are assigned based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Allocated expenses include costs incurred by offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated segment effort.

Depreciation and amortization are not specifically allocated because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are disclosed separately as unallocated expenses and adjusted against total Group income. Business assets and liabilities are also used interchangeably between segments, and meaningful allocation is considered impracticable.

Business-segment revenue is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized. Geographic revenue disclosure is provided in Note 2.18, *Revenue from operations*.
