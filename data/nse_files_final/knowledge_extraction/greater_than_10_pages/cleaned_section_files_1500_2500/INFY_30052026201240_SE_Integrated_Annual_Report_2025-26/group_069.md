## 5. Does the entity have a business continuity and disaster management plan? Give details in 100 words/ web link.

Yes. Infosys has a Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) called Phoenix, certified to ISO 22301:2019, Security and Resilience — Business Continuity Management Standard.

The BCMS provides a framework for planning, establishing, implementing, operating, monitoring, reviewing, maintaining, and continually improving business continuity measures across Infosys and its subsidiaries. In addition to the corporate-level Phoenix plan, comprehensive plans are maintained at three operational levels:

- Business functions
- Locations
- Accounts

The BCMS is integrated into the Enterprise Risk Management Framework and covers catastrophes, natural disasters, human-made disasters, and other events that could disrupt or severely constrain operations. Detailed risk assessments identify crisis scenarios, mitigation plans, and relevant controls at the function, location, and account levels.

The management system is validated through tests and exercises. Infosys states that various incidents have been successfully managed without significant business continuity or employee safety impacts.

## Enabling Responsible Value Chain Disclosure and Decarbonization

In fiscal 2026, Infosys initiated a SEBI-aligned supplier engagement program focused on ESG disclosures under the BRSR Core framework. The program collected ESG-related disclosures for fiscal 2025 from the top 2% of upstream suppliers by spend, focusing on high-impact suppliers including human resource service providers and IT service vendors. These suppliers represented approximately 13% of total spend.

The assessment collected data on supplier climate practices, emissions management, and reporting maturity. Using a spend-based allocation methodology aligned with the GHG Protocol, emissions attributed to Infosys were:

- Scope 1: **667 tCO2e**
- Scope 2: **219 tCO2e**

These emissions represented approximately **1.6%** of the suppliers' combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions. Infosys stated that the allocation was made for value chain disclosure under SEBI requirements and was not included in its reported Scope 3 inventory.

The initiative improved supply-chain emissions visibility and supplier engagement and supported the integration of climate considerations into procurement processes.
