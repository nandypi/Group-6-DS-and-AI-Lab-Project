|---|---|---|---|
| Section 6 c) Parameters for Vesting | Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR) against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from a basket of 10 industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If a peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. Relative TSR is also measured against broader domestic and global market indices comprising Nifty 50, S&P 500, and an MSCI index determined by the Administrator. | Long-term Relative TSR for rolling three-year TSR against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, selected from the same 10 industry peers. The Company will implement a phased transition: one-year TSR measurement in Year 1, two-year TSR measurement in Year 2, and full three-year TSR measurement from Year 3 onward. Annual TSR will also be measured against the peer comparator group and broader market indices comprising MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50, as determined by the Administrator. | The proposed three-year rolling Relative TSR parameter is intended to align management compensation with the long-term interests of shareholders and sustained shareholder value creation. There are no changes to the existing industry peer basket. |
| Section 6 c) Operating performance metrics | Total revenue growth, digital revenue growth, and operating margin. Annual performance targets are determined by the Administrator. | Total revenue growth and operating margin. Performance targets are determined by the Administrator. Relative TSR vesting remains: 100% vesting at or above the 75th percentile; proportional vesting between 60% and 100% from the 50th to the 75th percentile; and no vesting below the 50th percentile. Operating performance vesting is: 75% at minimum or threshold achievement; 0% below threshold; 100% at target achievement; 125% at stretch-target achievement; proportional vesting between 75% and 100% above threshold but below target; and proportional vesting between 100% and 125% above target but below stretch target. | The operating metrics are rationalized around revenue and margin to improve consistency and comparability over time. Sub-segment metrics such as digital revenue growth may become transient and less comparable over the long-term 10-year period. The changes also aim to improve transparency regarding payout philosophy. |
| Aggregate operating performance vesting | Not specified in the supplied text. | Although an individual performance outcome may result in achievement above 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter cannot exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter. | — |

| Section 6(e) | For the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(iv), as relevant, in the event of termination of employment or resignation, vested RSUs held as of the termination or resignation date, where termination is not for misconduct, must be exercised within 90 calendar days of the termination or resignation date. | For the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(II), as relevant, in the event of termination of employment or resignation, vested RSUs held as of the termination or resignation date, where termination is not for misconduct, must be exercised within 90 calendar days of the date of vesting. | The revised language is intended to clarify and ensure consistency in the operation of the Plan’s provisions relating to the exercise period for vested RSUs. |
