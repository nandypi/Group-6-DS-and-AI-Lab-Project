## Standalone Statement of Cash Flows

## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the year is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. Highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

_All amounts are in `crore unless stated otherwise._
