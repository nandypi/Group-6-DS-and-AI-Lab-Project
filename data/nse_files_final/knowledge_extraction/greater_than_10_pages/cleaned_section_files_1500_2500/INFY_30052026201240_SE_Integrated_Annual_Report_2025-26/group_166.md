## B) Login method for e-voting and voting during the meeting for shareholders other than individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode and shareholders holding securities in physical mode

### How to Log-in to NSDL e-voting website?

1. Visit the NSDL e-voting website at <https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/> and select **Login** under **Shareholder/Member**.
2. Enter the User ID, Password or OTP, and verification code. Shareholders registered for NSDL e-services through IDeAS may log in at <https://eservices.nsdl.com/>, select e-voting, and proceed to cast their vote.
3. User IDs are determined as follows:

| Manner of holding shares | User ID |
|---|---|
| Demat account with NSDL | 8-character DP ID followed by the 8-digit Client ID. Example: DP ID `IN300***` and Client ID `12******` produce User ID `IN300***12******`. |
| Demat account with CDSL | 16-digit Beneficiary ID. |
| Physical shares | EVEN Number followed by the Folio Number registered with the Company. Example: EVEN `139407` and Folio Number `001***` produce User ID `139407001***`. |

4. Shareholders already registered for e-voting may use their existing password.
5. First-time users must retrieve the initial password communicated by NSDL. If the email is registered, the password is provided in a PDF attachment. The PDF password is:

   - The 8-digit Client ID for an NSDL account.
   - The last 8 digits of the Client ID for a CDSL account.
   - The Folio Number for physical shares.

6. If the initial password cannot be retrieved or has been forgotten:

   - Select **Forgot User Details / Password?** for NSDL or CDSL demat accounts.
   - Select **Physical User Reset Password?** for physical shares.
   - Use the OTP-based login option.
   - If still unable to access the account, submit a request to NSDL with the demat account number or Folio Number, PAN, name, and registered address.

7. Accept the terms and conditions, select **Login**, and open the e-voting homepage.

## Step 2: Cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system

### How to cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system?

1. After logging in, view the companies and their corresponding EVENs for which voting is available.
2. Select the relevant EVEN for the company during the remote e-voting period.
3. Select assent or dissent, verify or modify the number of shares for which the vote is being cast, and select **Submit**.
4. Select **Confirm** when prompted. The message **Vote cast successfully** will be displayed.
5. A printout of the votes may be taken from the confirmation page.
6. Once a vote on a resolution is confirmed, it cannot be modified.

## General guidelines for shareholders

1. Institutional shareholders must send a scanned PDF or JPG copy of the relevant Board Resolution, authority letter, or other authorization, together with the attested specimen signature of the authorized signatory or signatories, to the Scrutinizer. They may alternatively upload the Board Resolution, Power of Attorney, or authority letter through the **Upload Board Resolution / Authority Letter** option under the e-voting tab.
2. Shareholders should keep their passwords confidential. The login will be disabled after five unsuccessful password attempts; access can then be restored using the applicable password-reset option on the NSDL e-voting website.
3. Shareholders may consult the FAQs and e-voting user manual available in the download section of the NSDL e-voting website or contact NSDL for e-voting assistance.

## Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories for procuring user id and password and registration of e mail ids for e-voting for the resolutions set out in this notice:

1. Shareholders holding physical shares must provide the Folio Number, shareholder name, a scanned copy of the share certificate, self-attested PAN, and self-attested Aadhaar documents to the Company.
2. Shareholders holding demat shares must provide the DPID-CLID or 16-digit Beneficiary ID, name, client master or Consolidated Account Statement, self-attested PAN, and self-attested Aadhaar documents to the Company.
3. Shareholders may alternatively request NSDL to provide the e-voting user ID and password by submitting the required documents.
4. Under the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and Depository Participants. Their mobile number and email ID must be correctly updated in the demat account to access the e-voting facility.
