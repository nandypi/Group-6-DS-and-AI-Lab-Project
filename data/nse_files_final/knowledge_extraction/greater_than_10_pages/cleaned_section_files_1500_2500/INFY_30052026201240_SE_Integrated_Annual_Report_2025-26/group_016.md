## Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)

In October 2020, the Company launched its ESG Vision 2030. Its focus includes leveraging technology to address:

- Climate change
- Water management
- Waste management
- Digital skilling
- Diversity and inclusion
- Employee wellness and experience
- Technology for good
- Community development
- Corporate governance

The ESG Committee was constituted on April 14, 2021, to oversee organization-wide ESG initiatives, priorities, and ESG practices. The ESG Committee reports to the Board and meets every quarter to review progress against the ESG ambitions in the ESG Vision 2030.

## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)

In accordance with Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Listing Regulations, the BRSR forms part of this Integrated Annual Report. It describes the Company’s environmental, social, and governance initiatives.

SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122 dated July 12, 2023, updated the BRSR format to incorporate BRSR Core, a subset of BRSR containing specific key performance indicators under nine ESG attributes. These KPIs are subject to mandatory reasonable assurance by an independent assurance provider.

The Company appointed Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP as the assurance provider. The ESG Report is available at: https://www.infosys.com/sustainability/documents/infosys-esg-report-2025-26.pdf.

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP also assured certain selected ESG Report indicators designed according to the GRI Standards.

## 8. Other disclosures and affirmations

Pursuant to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014, the Company affirms that, for the year ended March 31, 2026:

- There were no proceedings filed by or against the Company pending under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, before the National Company Law Tribunal or any other court.
- There was no instance of a one-time settlement with any bank or financial institution.
- There were no significant or material orders passed by regulators, courts, or tribunals impacting the Company’s going concern status or future operations.
- The Company complied with the provisions of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, and the rules made thereunder, including applicable maternity benefit obligations for eligible employees.

The Company also publishes a comprehensive annual ESG Report based on the GRI Standards 2021.
