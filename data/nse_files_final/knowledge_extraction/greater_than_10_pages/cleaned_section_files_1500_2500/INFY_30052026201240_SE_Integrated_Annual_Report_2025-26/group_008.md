# Managing financial capital

Infosys obtains financial capital through funds generated from business operations and financing activities. Its performance has resulted in high growth, strong cash generation, sustained profitability, and a debt-free, liquid balance sheet.

Over ₹37,500 crore was returned to shareholders for fiscal 2026.

Infosys has a high cash-generating business and access to capital markets across the world. Its credit rating allows it to raise debt at competitive rates in the future, if needed. The primary sources of funds are cash from operations and income from short- and long-term investments.

Primary sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents and cash flow generated from operations. Infosys remained debt-free and maintained adequate cash for operational and strategic requirements, unforeseen events, and sufficient returns.

Consolidated cash and investments include:

- Bank deposits
- Debt mutual funds
- Commercial Paper (CP)
- Quoted bonds
- Securities issued by government and quasi-government organizations
- Non-convertible debentures
- Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
- Instruments issued by eligible financial institutions with high credit ratings, guided by the treasury policy

Infosys also creates financial assets by investing in the startup ecosystem. These investments provide access to innovation that, together with its services and solutions, can deliver benefits to clients. Investments most often comprise minority equity positions in startup organizations and/or venture capital funds.

# Shareholder value creation

Infosys seeks to meet investor expectations through responsible business decisions and governance. Integrity and transparency are priorities in its relationship with investors.

Infosys aims to deliver revenue growth and operating margins through financial management and corporate governance, supporting share value appreciation and shareholder value creation. It maintains investor communication through quarterly post-result calls, analyst meets, the Annual General Meeting, and one-to-one and group interactions.

# Distribution of value created through Capital Allocation Policy

Effective from financial year 2025, Infosys expects to continue returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

The Company returned approximately 82.1% of free cash flow for fiscals 2025 and 2026 through dividends and buybacks, in line with the Capital Allocation Policy.

# Delivering value through business strategy

Infosys’ market-oriented four-pronged strategy enables investment in its global digital footprint. This supports its positioning as a partner for digital transformation and increases its potential to attract larger Total Contract Value (TCV) deals and clients. The strategy is intended to support industry-leading growth and profitability.

Infosys brings its Employee Value Proposition (EVP) to life through meaningful work, continuous growth, protected well-being, and a sense of belonging. It focuses on designing consistent policies, processes, programs, and systems with employees at the core.

## Employee Value Proposition

The Employee Value Proposition is the set of experiences, rewards, benefits, and fulfillment employees receive in return for their commitment and skills. It represents Infosys’ promise to its employees, who are referred to as “Infoscions.”

## Employee health and wellness

Infosys is committed to a safe, healthy, and incident-free workplace for employees, contractors, and other stakeholders. Its integrated Occupational Health and Safety Management System is aligned with ISO 45001 and supports workplace hazard identification, risk mitigation, safety initiatives, audits, employee engagement, and emergency preparedness.

HALE (Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment) integrates well-being into the Company’s culture and operating model. It is based on four pillars:

- Physical well-being
- Emotional well-being
- Social well-being
- Digital well-being

Safety is embedded as a core component of the framework.

## Nurturing workplace inclusivity

Infosys describes inclusion as part of its culture and leadership, learning, and growth practices.

As part of its ESG Vision 2030, Infosys aims to achieve 45% female representation in its workforce by 2030. In fiscal 2026, women represented 39.5% of the total workforce.

## Infosys Pulse

Infosys Pulse is an employee feedback platform designed to capture insights about workplace experience. It helps leadership monitor employee sentiment and translate feedback into organizational actions.

## InStep

InStep is Infosys’ global internship program. It has:

- More than 220 partner institutions in over 50 countries
- More than 3,500 alumni members and interns from over 50 nationalities

The launch of InStep Japan expanded the program’s global reach.

## Flexibility by design and distributed workforce

Infosys established innovation hubs, nearshore centers, and digital design studios across geographies. Its hybrid work model supports working from home and from offices close to employees’ homes. Offices established in India in recent years include Ahmedabad, Guwahati, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam, Noida, Hubballi, Indore, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, and Coimbatore.

Infosys follows a distributed-hybrid model supporting self-development, collaboration, and business outcomes. It monitors work-hour patterns to identify excessive or unsustainable workloads and provides managers with insights to address workload imbalances, staffing challenges, stress, and burnout risks.

## Leveraging AI to improve employee experience

Infosys is integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) across the talent lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, continuous learning, and offboarding.

InfyMe is a mobile-first, self-service platform integrating more than 200 service touchpoints into one interface. It supports employee operations, connection, and collaboration, particularly in the hybrid work model.

### Our NAVI-powered interventions

- **Data Assist:** A natural-language AI assistant for queries related to leave, attendance, allocation, assets, confirmation, and master data.
- **Policy Advisor:** An AI assistant that interprets policy documents and personalizes responses to employee queries.

### Moments That Matter (MTM)

Moments That Matter is a celebration platform integrated into employee lifecycle celebrations. Smart alerts notify employees about upcoming team events, and AI can generate cards and messages for colleagues and teams.

AI-driven Pulse analysis supports continuous listening by identifying employee sentiment and enabling action to improve well-being and engagement.

In learning and development, Infosys is deploying generative AI to provide personalized learning paths, simulate real-world scenarios, and assist with content creation. AI companions on its Lex learning platform, including Zoiee and SynthAIz, provide tutoring, summarization, and contextual learning support.
