## 4. Executive summary along with web-link(s) of Impact Assessment of CSR Projects carried out in pursuance of subrule (3) of rule 8

For fiscal 2026, the Company engaged an external agency to support an independent review and impact assessment of its CSR projects.

### Methodology

A mixed-method approach using quantitative and qualitative research tools was used for primary data collection. The assessment included virtual and on-field interactions with project beneficiaries and other relevant stakeholders. The findings were consolidated into a report for Management’s consideration and assessed using the Inclusiveness, Relevance, Effectiveness, Convergence and Sustainability (IRECS) Framework.

### Summary

During the year, impact assessment was conducted on 12 eligible CSR projects across various states in India. The projects aligned with the ESG framework and supported 13 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals, covering education, healthcare, women empowerment, sustainability, and other themes.

### Theme 1: Education and skill development

Impact: Digital education and access to online learning content had a positive impact on students’ academic performance and reduced dropout rates.
