## 3. Accessibility of workplaces

The premises and offices of Infosys in India are accessible to differently abled employees and workers, in accordance with the requirements of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## 4. Equal opportunity policy

Infosys has an equal opportunity policy in accordance with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. The policy is available at [Infosys Diversity and Inclusion Policy](https://www.infosys.com/careers/discover/culture/documents/diversity-inclusion-policy.pdf).

## 10. Health and safety management system

### 10a. Whether an occupational health and safety management system has been implemented by the entity? (Yes / No). If yes, the coverage of such system?

Yes. Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) is part of Infosys’ ESG framework. Its Health, Safety and Environmental Management System (HSEMS), known as “Ozone,” is based on management commitment, compliance with applicable legal requirements, and stakeholder expectations.

Ozone is globally implemented across locations based on legal requirements and internal benchmarks. It is included in the internal audit program to support governance and performance monitoring.

Infosys has achieved ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health and Safety certification at locations where the system has been in place for more than three years. The certifications cover all India locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, in line with the enterprise HSE strategy. More than 85% of Infosys employees are covered by ISO 45001 certification.

The HSE Policy addresses the proactive management of significant HSE risks and opportunities. Occupational health initiatives cover mental and emotional health, ergonomics, safety, lifestyle diseases, and preventive care. Occupational Health Centers operate across all India campuses.
