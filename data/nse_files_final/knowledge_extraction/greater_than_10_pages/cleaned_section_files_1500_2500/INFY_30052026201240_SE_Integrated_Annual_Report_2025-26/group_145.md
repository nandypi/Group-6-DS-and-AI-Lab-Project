## 2.22 Employee benefits

## Gratuity and pension

The Group provides gratuity through defined benefit retirement plans covering eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plans provide a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys Limited contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. The trustees administer contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group also operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic post-retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies with employee age.

Defined benefit liabilities are determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. These plans expose the Group to longevity, interest rate, and market risks.

The net obligation of a defined benefit plan is recognized in the Balance Sheet as an asset or liability. Remeasurements of the net defined benefit liability/(asset) are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The portion of actual plan asset returns exceeding the yields calculated using the discount rate is also recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

## Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. Employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. The Company contributes part of its obligation to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to a government-administered pension fund.

The annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust is administered by the Government of India. The Company is required to make good any shortfall between the return on the trust's investments and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries receive benefits from a defined contribution provident fund plan. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution superannuation plan. The Group has no obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund whose corpus is invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group's compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed because of unused entitlement accumulated at the Balance Sheet date. Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
