## 5. Has the entity implemented a mechanism for Zero Liquid Discharge? If yes, provide details of its coverage and implementation.

Infosys has implemented in-house sewage treatment plants (STPs) at its India-owned campuses to support Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) practices. All owned campuses in India have STPs, ensuring that sewage is treated and recycled water is used for irrigation, HVAC systems, and flushing.

STPs are available at all owned campuses and leased locations in India except 2. At the remaining locations, wastewater is discharged into municipal sewers for further treatment. Infosys also procures greywater from third parties for landscaping, flushing, and other non-potable purposes, reducing the need to source fresh water for these uses.

## 8. Does the entity have any project related to reducing greenhouse gas emission? If yes, provide details.

Infosys has been carbon neutral since fiscal 2020 across Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions. Its approach includes reducing and avoiding emissions through energy efficiency and renewable energy.

Energy-efficiency measures include super-efficient new buildings, an Energy Performance Index (EPI) below 65 kWh/m²-year, real-time monitoring and optimization through smart building systems, and retrofits in existing buildings. In fiscal 2026, retrofit projects for lighting and air-conditioning systems saved 2.04 lakh kWh and reduced emissions by 145 tCO₂e.

Infosys installed 62.42 MWp of solar PV capacity and avoided 1.24 lakh kWh in fiscal 2026. Through captive solar capacity, power purchase agreements, and a green tariff mechanism, renewable sources accounted for 81.8% of electricity consumption in Infosys’s India operations.
