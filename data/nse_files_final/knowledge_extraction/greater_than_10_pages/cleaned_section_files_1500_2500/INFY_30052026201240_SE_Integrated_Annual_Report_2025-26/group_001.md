## UNLOCK VALUE

## Integrated Annual Report 2025-26

### From possibilities to enterprise AI impact

Infosys describes an enterprise AI approach focused on measurable outcomes in revenue, cost, customer experience, responsiveness, workflow redesign, technical-debt reduction, and operational resilience.

The Infosys AI First Value Framework is built around six value pools and is powered by Infosys Topaz™, its generative and agentic AI suite, and Infosys Topaz Fabric™, its agentic services suite. The approach includes preparing structured and unstructured data for AI use, redesigning core processes around domain-aware agents, progressively modernizing legacy systems through AI-enabled reverse engineering, and applying responsible, secure, ethical, and regulation-aligned AI practices.

Infosys reports that it is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys, with more than 4,600 AI projects underway. It has also developed more than 30 new service offerings across the six value pools.

## Liberty Global

### From AI experiments to enterprise impact: How Liberty Global is improving customer experience with Infosys.

#### Supporting 1000+ Platform Deliveries

Liberty Global, a European connectivity and entertainment provider, partnered with Infosys to operate and continuously evolve connectivity and entertainment platforms serving tens of millions of customers across Europe.

Over the past year, Infosys supported more than 1,000 platform deliveries. AI-enabled solutions included:

- **Super Search:** Serves approximately 8 million TV customers and uses large language models to support conversational content discovery across linear TV, on-demand, and streaming services.
- **Agent Assist and Customer Assist:** Deployed across multiple markets to enable more self-service customer-care journeys, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce pressure on care teams.

The initiatives resulted in 50% fewer outages year on year and 60% fewer customers impacted.

The partnership spans more than two decades, including over a decade as a formal strategic partnership.

## Hertz

### Hertz modernized critical workflows 60% faster with AI-first code engineering.

#### AI First Approach

Hertz used complex COBOL systems on a legacy platform. Infosys supported Hertz's modernization through an AI-first approach using mainframe-to-cloud expertise and an AI stack that included OpenAI, Claude Sonnet, and AWS Bedrock.

Infosys analyzed nearly three million lines of legacy COBOL code and used multimodal large language models to create a cloud modernization blueprint. AI-enabled reverse engineering was used to extract core business logic and reimplement it as domain-centric Java microservices using Infosys iLEAD and GitHub Copilot. AI-enabled testing, reusable components, and enterprise governance supported the modernization effort.

The resulting microservices-based digital core is intended to improve platform agility, scalability, and system resilience, support high transaction volumes, and enable rapid responses to changing business and customer requirements on AWS Cloud.

The engagement is expected to deliver:

- 60% faster modernization timelines
- Greater reuse of cross-channel business capabilities
- Reduced hosting costs
- A scalable foundation for further modernization and automation across additional business domains

## Mondelēz

### Mondelēz International establishes an end-to-end traceability reporting capability with EdgeVerve AI Next.

#### Enterprise Wide Traceability at Scale

Mondelēz International operates a global snack supply chain involving thousands of suppliers, hundreds of manufacturing plants, and a global distribution network.

Fragmented legacy systems and siloed data created challenges in food safety and regulatory compliance, including limited visibility across suppliers, plants, and distribution centers. These issues slowed compliance processes and increased recall risks under regulations such as the FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA).

Infosys worked with Mondelēz teams across quality, manufacturing, distribution, customer service, and IT to identify traceability requirements covering:

- Production batch and quality management
- Product bills of material
- Conversion of raw materials into finished goods
- Finished-goods receipt
- Palletizing and labeling
- Shipping and downstream data management

Infosys deployed EdgeVerve AI Next Product Traceability, a scalable cloud-based platform that integrates near-real-time and batch data through AI Next Fabric. Its graph data store provides visibility from product origin to delivery. A centralized traceability portal supports top-down and bottom-up tracking workflows, while a regulatory control tower supports incident response.

The platform has been implemented in North America. Europe is planned to begin rollout in the second half of 2026. The platform:

- Connects more than 50 suppliers
- Captures over 0.5 million events per day
- Integrates more than 220 plants and distribution centers
- Maps more than 150 raw-material-to-finished-goods processes

Prebuilt FSMA compliance reports and one-touch batch and handling-unit traceability are intended to accelerate regulatory audits and improve food-safety and compliance processes.
