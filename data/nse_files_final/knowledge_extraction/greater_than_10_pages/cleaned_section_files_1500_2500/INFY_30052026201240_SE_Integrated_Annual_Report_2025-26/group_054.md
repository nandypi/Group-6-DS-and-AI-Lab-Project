| S. No. | Material issue identified* | Risk or opportunity | Rationale for identifying the risk or opportunity | Approach to adapt or mitigate the risk | Financial implications |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Environment: Climate change | Risk | Climate change risks are increasingly manifesting as strategic, physical, and transitional risks, including market and compliance risks. If not managed adequately, they could affect operations, reputation, and profitability. | Refer to the Infosys ESG Databook 2026 for details on risk mitigation. | Negative: Increased operating costs in meeting environmental standards in line with evolving regulatory requirements. |
| 2 | Environment: Engaging clients on climate actions through our solutions | Opportunity | Increased revenue through development and/or expansion of services that help customers manage climate change risks; savings from using lower-emission energy sources; and the opportunity to lead global action on climate change through advocacy. |  | Positive: Scope to improve Infosys' competitiveness and capitalize on evolving client preferences by leveraging expertise in sustainability, low-carbon transition, and digital/IT to support clients on their sustainability and low-carbon journey. |
| 3 | Social: Facilitating best-in-class employee experience | Risk | Inability to facilitate a best-in-class employee experience may affect the ability to attract, hire, train, engage, and retain talent. | Employee engagement and support initiatives; holistic measures for employee retention and recognition of key and tenured employees; career and leadership development programs; and use of AI and other technologies to enhance employee experience. | Negative: Impact on employer reputation and increased cost of talent, among other effects. |
| 4 | Social: Enabling digital talent at scale | Opportunity | Digital and AI technologies are increasingly shaping how people live and work. New technologies and solutions can amplify human potential, reinvent the workplace, and enable performance beyond previous capabilities. |  | Positive: Given the shortage of digital- and AI-proficient workers, an opportunity exists to bridge the talent gap by using digital and AI technologies to upskill employees, clients, students, educators, and communities while advancing technological inclusion and creating long-term social and economic impact. |

| 6 | Governance: Ethics and compliance | Risk | Evolving regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and public disclosure requirements add uncertainty to compliance policies. Infosys maintains comprehensive compliance frameworks, policies, controls, and programs; engages with regulatory authorities and compliance experts; conducts compliance awareness programs and training for employees and vendors; performs periodic compliance certification with defined accountability; and maintains monitoring, reporting, and governance including Board oversight. | Negative: Impact on reputation and cost of compliance due to investigations, regulatory inquiries, litigation, fines, and related matters. |
