## Cybersecurity

Infosys employees operate in a hybrid model. The Information Security team monitors global cybersecurity events and maintains compliance with applicable requirements. Infosys is certified against the Information Security Management System (ISMS) Standard ISO 27001:2022 and has been attested for SSAE 18 and ISAE 3402 SOC 1 and SOC 2 by an independent audit firm.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys strengthened its cybersecurity posture through technology, tools, and process improvements. The Company focused on cybersecurity workforce training and reskilling, security culture, AI/ML security, cyber resiliency, and secure supply chain ecosystems. Dedicated courses and resource kits were provided to the developer community.

## Internal financial control and its adequacy

The Board has adopted policies and procedures covering orderly and efficient business conduct, adherence to Company policies, asset safeguarding, fraud prevention and detection, error reporting, accounting-record accuracy and completeness, and timely preparation of reliable financial disclosures.

For more details, refer to the “Internal control systems and their adequacy” section in the Management’s Discussion and Analysis.

The annual return in the prescribed format is available at [Infosys annual returns 2025-26](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/documents/annual-returns-2025-26.pdf).

## Secretarial standards

The Company complies with all applicable secretarial standards issued by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India.

## Listing on stock exchanges

The Company’s shares are listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares (ADSs) are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).

## Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF)

During the year, the Company transferred unclaimed and unencashed dividends of `6,12,63,126` to the IEPF. It also transferred 7,23,720 corresponding shares on which dividends had remained unclaimed for seven consecutive years.

The Company paid `3,39,64,248` in dividends relating to shares held by the IEPF.

Details of benefits arising from shares transferred to the IEPF, year-wise unclaimed or unencashed dividends in unpaid dividend accounts, and shares liable for transfer are provided in the Corporate Governance Report and on the [Infosys IEPF page](https://www.infosys.com/investors/shareholder-services/unclaimed-dividend-shares.html).

## Directors' responsibility statement

The financial statements are prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), the historical cost convention, the accrual basis, the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and SEBI guidelines, except for:

- Certain financial instruments measured at fair value.
- Defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

The Ind AS are prescribed under Section 133 of the Act, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and relevant amendment rules. Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except where adoption of a newly issued accounting standard or revision to an existing standard requires a change.

## Board's report

The directors confirm that:

- For the financial year ended March 31, 2026, applicable accounting standards were followed with no material departures.
- Accounting policies were selected and applied consistently, and judgments and estimates were reasonable and prudent, providing a true and fair view of the Company’s affairs and profit for the period.
- Proper and sufficient care was taken to maintain adequate accounting records, safeguard assets, and prevent and detect fraud and other irregularities.
- The annual accounts were prepared on a going-concern basis.
- Internal financial controls were established, were adequate, and operated effectively.
- Systems were established to ensure compliance with applicable laws and were adequate and operating effectively.

The proposed appointment will be recommended to shareholders at the 46th AGM in 2027 for a first term of five consecutive years, until the conclusion of the 51st AGM in 2032. The appointment is subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements.

## Cost records and cost audit

Maintenance of cost records and the requirement for cost audit under Section 148(1) of the Act are not applicable to the Company’s business activities.

## Reporting of frauds by auditors

During fiscal 2026, the statutory auditor and the secretarial auditor did not report any instance of fraud committed by the Company’s officers or employees.

## 6. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Infosys undertakes CSR initiatives primarily through the Infosys Foundation. Focus areas include:

- Education and skill development for sustained livelihoods
- Healthcare, including preventive healthcare
- Environmental sustainability and ecological balance
- Gender equality and women’s empowerment
- National heritage, art, and culture
- Rural development
- Disaster relief and rehabilitation

Infosys also undertakes CSR initiatives outside India, including in the United States, Australia, and various parts of Europe. Initiatives in the United States are carried out through Infosys Foundation USA. These initiatives are in addition to the statutory requirement.

The Company’s CSR Policy is available [here](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/Documents/corporate-social-responsibility-policy.pdf). The annual report on CSR activities is appended as Annexure 6 to the Board’s Report.
