## Salient features of our Enterprise Risk Management program

Our enterprise risk management (ERM) program uses methods to identify risks, evaluate potential impact, and promote risk awareness across the organization.

## Highlights of fiscal 2026

During fiscal 2026, risk management initiatives were undertaken to support service delivery, transparent stakeholder communication, social responsibility, and employee safety and health. The Risk Office assessed, monitored, and reported on geopolitical scenarios; economic uncertainty; inflation; artificial intelligence, technology disruption, and innovation; talent availability; cybersecurity; data protection and privacy; ESG; contractual liabilities; and a complex and evolving regulatory environment.

The key and emerging risks and the Company’s approaches to mitigate them are described below.

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Geopolitical, macroeconomic or health events | Revenues are concentrated in a few geographies and client industries, and a large part of revenue depends on a limited number of clients. Unfavorable geopolitical, economic, or health events may cause currency volatility and reduced spending on technology products and services, adversely affecting demand, growth, and profitability. Policy changes such as tariffs and trade restrictions may be difficult to predict and assess. Impacted capitals: Financial, Social & Relationship, and Human. | Broad-based growth to reduce concentration in any single region, client, or industry; monitoring of geopolitical and macroeconomic scenarios; additional controls to improve operational agility; currency hedging practices; business continuity and resilience measures in regions affected by geopolitical conflicts; hybrid working model; diversified supply chain; and acquisitions to expand into new areas or regions. Opportunity: clients are seeking cost-takeout projects and vendor consolidation. |

## Risk management report

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Artificial Intelligence (AI) risks | Failure to transform existing services using AI or scale new AI-first service offerings to meet evolving customer requirements could affect win rates, reduce market share, and decrease revenue and profits. Impacted capitals: Financial and Intellectual. | Differentiation through innovation and industry solutions leveraging AI; continued investment to identify, incubate, develop, and launch AI-first offerings; proactive measures to identify and mitigate operational issues; an AI talent transformation strategy; and development of deep AI engineering expertise. |
| Global Capability Centers (GCCs) risks | Customers may insource or establish or expand GCCs for services provided by Infosys, reducing addressable market share and affecting growth and profitability. Impacted capitals: Financial and Intellectual. | Collaboration and co-creation to address GCC requirements; scaling a GCC practice for assisted GCC build, build-operate-transfer, captive carveouts, and other engagement models; and an AI-first model to transform GCCs into innovation hubs. |
| Technology disruption and innovation | Failure to identify, invest in, incubate, and operationalize services and offerings aligned with changes in AI, client preferences, and market expectations could disrupt the value proposition and reduce relevance to customers, affecting revenue and profitability. The speed and nature of technological changes make trends difficult to predict. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, and Intellectual. | An executive-led innovation council; joint innovation showcases with clients, industry forums, alliances, partners, and academia; research and development in consulting and industry-domain knowledge-led solutions; employee reskilling; large-deal-specific investments; alliances or partnerships with emerging technology firms. Opportunity: identify, develop, and deploy offerings leveraging next-generation technologies. |
| Talent supply constraints | As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 3,28,594 employees globally. The Company depends on skilled technology, AI, and management talent. Difficulties attracting, hiring, engaging, motivating, reskilling, and retaining talent; managing leadership succession; reshaping the AI-driven talent model; protecting human rights; or evolving the hybrid work model could affect reputation, project staffing, execution of large and complex programs, and cost structures. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, and Manufactured. | Recruitment and onboarding improvements; employee engagement and support initiatives; retention and recognition efforts; career and leadership development programs; a hybrid operating model balancing client requirements, employee preferences, legal requirements, and information security risks; redesigned career architecture; and a future-ready workforce supported by global learning infrastructure. |
| Cybersecurity | Cyber threats evolve rapidly, creating a risk that threat detection and prevention measures may not identify or prevent new, modified, or evolving threats. Attacks that breach information networks or failure to protect sensitive and confidential stakeholder information in accordance with applicable laws and contractual obligations could adversely affect operations and client satisfaction and result in significant regulatory penalties. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, and Manufactured. | Robust cybersecurity strategy, framework, processes, policies, and controls; multilayered governance with executive and Board oversight; continued investment in cybersecurity technologies; collaboration with cyber-intelligence and forensic consultants; periodic tabletop exercises; an up-to-date cybersecurity crisis plan; cybersecurity awareness training with consequence management; security-by-design training and workshops; strong encryption; data backup and recovery mechanisms; and a Cyber Risk Assessment Framework aligned with ISO 31000, ISO 27001, and ISO 27005. Opportunity: provide cybersecurity services to customers. |
| Data protection and privacy | Failure to protect personal and sensitive stakeholder information in accordance with applicable laws could affect operations or result in significant regulatory penalties. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, and Intellectual. | Robust data privacy framework, policies, processes, and controls; privacy-by-design and data protection training and workshops incorporating geographical nuances; multilayered governance with executive and Board oversight; and continuous monitoring of the regulatory landscape. |
| Cost inflation / Inability to improve margin | Failure to operate with sustainable cost levers could adversely affect operational effectiveness and long-term profitability. Impacted capital: Financial. | Effective operations supported by sustainable cost optimization and margin improvement levers, automation, and a planned capital expenditure program focused on technology adoption and efficiency. |
| Environment, Social, Governance (ESG) ambition | Reputation, access to capital, and longer-term financial stability could be at risk if stated goals under the ESG 2030 Vision are not met. Climate change risks are increasingly manifesting as strategic, physical, transitional, market, and compliance risks; inadequate management could affect reputation and profitability. ESG expectations may continue to change because of evolving stakeholder expectations and regulatory requirements. | ESG 2030 ambitions and execution roadmap with dedicated owners; Board-level governance through a dedicated ESG Committee of the Board; periodic risk assessments; alignment of ESG ambitions with strategy; and regulatory landscape monitoring. Opportunity: provide climate change-related solutions and services to customers. For more details, see the [ESG Vision 2030](https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/esg-vision-2030.html). |
