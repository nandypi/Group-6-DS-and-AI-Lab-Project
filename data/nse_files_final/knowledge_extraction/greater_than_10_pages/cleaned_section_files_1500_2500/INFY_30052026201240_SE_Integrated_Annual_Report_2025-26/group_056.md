## Governance, leadership and oversight

## 7. Statement by director responsible for the business responsibility report, highlighting ESG-related challenges, targets and achievements

Infosys’s ESG Vision 2030 guides its efforts to strengthen governance, advance environmental stewardship, foster an inclusive and diverse workplace, and use technology to create long-term stakeholder value.

Fiscal 2026 priorities included:

- Accelerating climate commitments.
- Enabling large-scale skilling.
- Embedding responsible practices across operations and the value chain.
- Enhancing ESG frameworks and disclosures.
- Using governance mechanisms, performance metrics, and stakeholder engagement to support ESG commitments.

Infosys identified climate transition, rapid technological change, increasing regulatory requirements, transparency and accountability expectations, talent transformation, and macroeconomic conditions as ongoing challenges.

The company stated that it expects continued changes in regulatory landscapes, stakeholder expectations, and climate-related risks. Further information on ESG challenges, targets, performance, and achievements is provided in the ESG Report and the “Approaching Value Creation” and “Delivering Value” chapters of the Integrated Annual Report.
