## 2.16 Provisions

## Accounting policy

A provision is recognized when, as a result of a past event, the Company has a present legal or constructive obligation that is reasonably estimable and an outflow of economic benefits to settle the obligation is probable. Provisions are determined by discounting expected future cash flows at a pre-tax rate reflecting current market assessments of the time value of money and risks specific to the liability. The Company recognizes a reimbursement asset only when it is virtually certain that reimbursement will be received if the Company settles the obligation.
