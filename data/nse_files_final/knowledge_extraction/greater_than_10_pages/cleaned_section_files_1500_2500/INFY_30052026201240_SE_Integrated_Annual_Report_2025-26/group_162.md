The Board of Directors approved amendments to the existing plan at its meeting held on April 23, 2026. The amendments expand the employee base covered by the performance-based stock incentive plan and revise performance parameters to strengthen alignment with long-term shareholder value creation, reinforce long-term performance orientation, and align operating performance metrics with relevant financial metrics.

The Amended 2019 Plan is available for shareholder review at [Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/Documents/expanded-stock-ownership-program2019.pdf).

Shareholder approval is sought to amend the existing plan and authorize the Board to grant RSUs and create, issue, offer, and provide equity shares, American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), or cash from time to time to eligible employees of the Company or its subsidiaries.

All capitalized terms not defined in this section have the meanings assigned to them in the 2019 Plan.

## 1.  Brief description of the Amended 2019 Plan

The purpose of the Amended 2019 Plan is to:

- Encourage employees to align individual performance with the Company's shareholder value creation objectives.
- Attract, retain, and motivate talented and identified employees.
- Reward employee performance with ownership.

The Amended 2019 Plan grants Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) to eligible employees. Subject to applicable law and the plan conditions, eligible employees may receive equity shares, ADRs, or cash upon exercise of the RSUs.

The plan is administered by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), referred to as the Administrator. For secondary acquisition and related administrative matters, administration may also be delegated to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. The Administrator's decisions, determinations, and interpretations are final and binding on eligible employees and participants.

Each RSU grant is evidenced by an award agreement specifying the terms and conditions determined by the Administrator. The performance-linked RSUs are intended to align employee rewards with shareholder interests through continued focus on the Company's sustainable, long-term performance and value creation.

## 2. Total number of shares or benefits to be granted

The total number of equity shares and ADRs provided upon exercise of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan for eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries shall not cumulatively exceed **3,26,63,903 equity shares**. This represents the balance of unutilized equity shares from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**, approximately **0.8% of issued capital** as of the date of the Notice.

## 3. Identification of classes of employees entitled to participate and be beneficiaries in the 2019 Plan

Employees, as defined under the SEBI Regulations, of the Company and its subsidiary companies, whether working in India or abroad, may participate in the Amended 2019 Plan subject to eligibility criteria specified in the SEBI Regulations or determined by the Administrator.

Under the SEBI Regulations and the Companies Act, 2013, the following are not eligible to participate:

- Promoters or members of the promoter group.
- Independent directors.
- Directors who, directly or indirectly, either themselves or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company's outstanding equity shares.

The appraisal process described in paragraph 8 of the plan is considered when determining RSU grants.

## 4. Requirements of vesting and period of vesting

RSUs granted under the Amended 2019 Plan vest based on three performance parameters. The NRC determines their relative weightings, subject to the following conditions:

- Each parameter must have a weighting of at least 25%.
- No single parameter may have a weighting of more than 50%.

The performance parameters are:

1. **Long-term Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR):** Rolling three-year TSR compared with a peer group of at least eight companies. The Administrator will select the comparator group from the following 10 industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL.

   If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company in the comparator group.

   The Company will implement a phased transition to a three-year rolling Relative TSR measure:

   - Year 1: one-year TSR.
   - Year 2: two-year TSR.
   - Year 3 onward: full three-year TSR.

2. **Annual TSR:**

   - **Relative TSR against industry peers:** Relative TSR compared with a peer group of at least eight companies selected from Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company.
   - **Relative TSR against broader market indices:** Relative TSR compared with domestic and global indices comprising MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50, as determined by the Administrator.

3. **Annual operating performance metrics:** Company metrics used to derive shareholder value:

   - Total revenue growth.
   - Operating margin.

The Administrator determines the performance targets for the operating performance metrics. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest.

### Relative TSR vesting

| Company performance | RSUs vesting |
|---|---:|
| 75th percentile and above | 100% of RSUs due for vesting |
| 50th to below 75th percentile | Proportionately on a linear scale between 60% and 100%; balance lapses |
| Below 50th percentile | 0%; RSUs due for vesting lapse |

### Operating performance vesting

| Performance achievement | RSUs vesting |
|---|---:|
| Below minimum threshold | 0%; RSUs due for vesting lapse |
| Minimum threshold | 75% of RSUs due for vesting |
| Target | 100% of RSUs due for vesting |
| Stretch target | 125% of RSUs due for vesting |
| Greater than threshold and less than target | Proportionately on a linear scale between 75% and 100%; balance lapses |
| Greater than target and less than stretch target | Proportionately on a linear scale between 100% and 125% |

Although individual performance outcomes may exceed 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter shall not exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter.

Any increase in dilution of equity share capital or cost of secondary acquisition of equity shares is expected to be offset by growth in shareholder value and increased shareholder returns.

All RSUs vest according to the schedule determined by the Administrator from the grant date. RSUs that lapse or are forfeited remain available for future grants.

In subsequent annual reports, the Company will disclose details of:

- The performance parameters and their weightings.
- Target achievement and actual achievement for each performance parameter.
- The basis on which RSU vesting was permitted.
- The number of employees covered.
- The number of RSUs granted.
- The number of employees whose RSUs vested.
- The number of RSUs due for vesting under the vesting schedule.
- The number of RSUs vested under the vesting schedule after meeting the vesting conditions.

The Company has a Compensation Recovery Policy intended to support its pay-for-performance philosophy. The policy is available at [Compensation Recovery Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporategovernance/documents/compensation-recovery-policy.pdf).

## 5. Maximum period within which the RSUs shall vest

RSUs vest over a minimum period of **1 year** and a maximum period of **3 years** from the grant date, as specified in the award agreement. No vesting tranche may exceed **50% of the total grants** made under the plan.

When determining an eligible employee's vesting schedule, the Administrator considers factors including alignment with accelerated shareholder value creation, retention of key talent, future potential, and tenure.

RSUs vest based on achievement of the performance criteria described in paragraph 4.
