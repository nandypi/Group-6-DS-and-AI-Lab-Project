## 5. Cybersecurity and data privacy framework

Yes. Infosys has a cybersecurity framework called SEED, aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), and supported by supplementary policies, processes, procedures, and standards for enterprise-level information security.

Infosys has a Data Privacy Policy published on the Company's intranet. The policy applies across Infosys operations, including activities involving service providers. Infosys provides privacy notices at the point of data collection for internal and external data subjects.

Policy information: [Information Management](https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/governance/information-management.html).

## 6. Corrective actions

Infosys has reviewed its cybersecurity posture, technology, and threat landscape to further strengthen its cyber defense capabilities. The Company continues to review and strengthen cybersecurity processes and controls across its network in line with industry best practices.

### Consumer satisfaction surveys

Yes. Infosys conducts surveys to gauge customer satisfaction for its major services. The Company regularly interacts with clients across multiple platforms and conducts an annual Client Value Survey and periodic engagement surveys.

The surveys use a structured questionnaire, and feedback is collected through a web survey hosted by an independent organization. Survey results help Infosys understand client expectations and needs and serve as an input into investment decisions.

## CEO and CFO certification

The certification was provided by Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, and Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer, for the year ended March 31, 2026.

Management certified that:

1. They reviewed the Balance Sheet as at March 31, 2026, the Statement of Profit and Loss, the Statement of Changes in Equity, the Statement of Cash Flows, the significant accounting policies and explanatory information, and the Board's report for the year ended March 31, 2026.
2. The statements did not contain any materially untrue statement or omit any material fact necessary to make them, in the circumstances, not misleading.
3. The financial statements and other financial information presented, in all material respects, a true and fair view of the Company's affairs, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows, and complied with applicable accounting standards, laws, and regulations.
4. No transactions were entered into during the year that were fraudulent, illegal, or in violation of the Company's Code of Conduct and Ethics, except as disclosed to the auditors and the Audit Committee.
5. Management was responsible for establishing and maintaining disclosure controls and internal controls over financial reporting and had:
   - Designed disclosure controls to ensure that material information relating to the Company and its consolidated subsidiaries was made known to management during preparation of the report.
   - Designed internal controls over financial reporting to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and preparation of financial statements under Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS).
   - Evaluated the effectiveness of the Company's disclosure controls and procedures.
   - Disclosed any material changes in internal control over financial reporting during the most recent financial year.
6. Based on its evaluation of internal controls over financial reporting, management disclosed to the auditors and Audit Committee, where applicable:
   - Any deficiencies in the design or operation of internal controls that could adversely affect financial reporting, including confirmation that there were no material weaknesses and related corrective actions.
   - Any significant changes in internal controls during the year.
   - All significant changes in accounting policies, if any, as disclosed in the notes to the financial statements.
   - Any significant fraud involving management or employees with a significant role in internal controls over financial reporting.
7. No personnel were denied access to the Audit Committee in matters involving alleged misconduct, and whistleblowers were protected from unfair termination and other unfair or prejudicial employment practices.
8. All Board members and senior management personnel affirmed compliance with the Code of Conduct and Ethics for the year covered by the report.

Salil Parekh's DIN: 01876159. The certification was dated April 23, 2026, in Bengaluru.
