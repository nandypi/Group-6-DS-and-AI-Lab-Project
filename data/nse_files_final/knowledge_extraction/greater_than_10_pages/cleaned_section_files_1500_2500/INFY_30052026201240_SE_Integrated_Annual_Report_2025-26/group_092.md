## Financial risk management

## Financial risk factors

The Company's activities expose it to market risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk. Its primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. The Company uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange-related exposures. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of each customer and concentration of risk among the top few customers.

## Market risk

The Company operates internationally, with a major portion of its business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies.

The Company holds foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates. Changes in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and foreign currencies may adversely affect the Company's operating results.
