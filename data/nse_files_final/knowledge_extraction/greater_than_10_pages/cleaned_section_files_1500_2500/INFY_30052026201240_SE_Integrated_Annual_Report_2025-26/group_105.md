## b. Superannuation

The Company contributed ` 541 crore and ` 493 crore to the Superannuation trust during the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively. These contributions were recognized in the Statement of Profit and Loss under employee benefit expense.

## c. Provident fund

Infosys has an obligation to fund any shortfall in the yield of the trust’s investments relative to the administered interest rates on an annual basis. These administered rates are determined annually, predominantly considering social and economic factors. The actuary valued provident fund liabilities based on guidance issued by the Actuarial Society of India.

The following tables present the funded status of Infosys Limited’s defined benefit provident fund plan and the amounts recognized in the Company’s financial statements as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

The asset allocation for plan assets is determined based on the investment criteria prescribed under the relevant regulations.

The actuarial valuation of provident fund liability exposes the Company to interest rate risk. The defined benefit obligation uses a discount rate based on government bonds. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation will tend to increase.

As at March 31, 2026, the defined benefit obligation would be affected by approximately ` 76 crore and ` 151 crore on account of a 0.25% increase and decrease, respectively, in the expected rate of return on plan assets.

The Company contributed ` 1,321 crore and ` 1,158 crore to the provident fund during the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively. These contributions were recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss under employee benefit expense.

The provident plans apply only to employees drawing a salary in Indian rupees.
