## VI. Internal Financial Control Systems and their adequacy

Infosys has aligned its internal financial control systems with the requirements of the Companies Act, 2013, based on the Internal Control—Integrated Framework (2013) issued by the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO).

The internal financial control systems include the design, implementation, and maintenance of controls operating effectively to ensure orderly and efficient business conduct; adherence to company policies; safeguarding of assets; prevention and detection of fraud and errors; accuracy and completeness of accounting records; and timely preparation of reliable financial information.

Management recognizes that controls and procedures, regardless of how well conceived and operated, can provide only reasonable assurance that their objectives will be met. Based on an evaluation as of the end of the period covered by this Integrated Annual Report, the CEO and CFO concluded that Infosys' disclosure controls and procedures were effective in providing reasonable assurance that required information was recorded, processed, summarized, and reported within specified periods, and that material information relating to Infosys and its consolidated subsidiaries was accumulated and communicated to management for timely disclosure decisions.

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, the statutory auditors of Infosys Limited, audited the financial statements included in this Integrated Annual Report and issued a report on the Company's internal financial controls under Section 143 of the Companies Act, 2013, covering the effectiveness of internal financial controls over the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements as at March 31, 2026.

Infosys Limited appointed Ernst & Young LLP to oversee and conduct internal audits. The internal audit plan is reviewed annually in consultation with the statutory auditors and approved by the Audit Committee.

The CEO and CFO Certification section of the Integrated Annual Report discusses the adequacy of Infosys' internal control systems and procedures.
