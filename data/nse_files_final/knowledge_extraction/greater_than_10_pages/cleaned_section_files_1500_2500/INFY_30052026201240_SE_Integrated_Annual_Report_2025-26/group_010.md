## Lake rejuvenation

Infosys implements lake rejuvenation projects in partnership with local stakeholders. These efforts increased cumulative storage capacity from 6 billion liters to 10.3 billion liters, adding 4.3 billion liters of freshwater capacity.

## Waste management

Infosys’ waste management strategy focuses on reducing, reusing, refurbishing, repurposing, and recycling, with an ambition of zero waste to landfill. The company continues to strengthen waste segregation, onsite processing, and material recovery across its campuses.

Organic waste is treated onsite through biogas plants, composting, and recycling. These processes reduce offsite disposal and associated emissions and replace part of the cooking-gas requirement at campuses.

E-waste includes IT equipment, mobile phones, printers, cartridges, electrical and electronic equipment, lithium-ion batteries, and cables. Infosys extends asset life through hardware upgrades, component replacements, re-imaging, reuse, redeployment, and refurbishment.

Eleven Infosys India campuses have achieved TRUE Zero Waste certification.

## Biodiversity

Infosys is strengthening biodiversity stewardship through native plantation, habitat enrichment, and knowledge sharing through its annual BioConserve event.

In FY26, Infosys launched the BioConserve platform, a digital open repository for resources, best practices, and research on biodiversity conservation.

## ASSURE

Launched in partnership with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements (IIHS) in April 2024, Accelerating Sustainable Super Efficient Real Estate (ASSURE) is a multi-stakeholder platform supporting the replication of climate-resilient, energy-efficient buildings across India. The initiative has a target of 100 million sq. ft. by 2030.

More than 15 million sq. ft. of commercial buildings across India were onboarded as ASSURE lighthouse projects. More than 1,250 students and practitioners were upskilled on high-performance buildings through webinars, in-person workshops, and site visits.

ASSURE brings together policymakers, developers, experts, and industry leaders to advance performance measurement, skills, policy, and financing mechanisms.

Infosys’ Manufactured Capital includes energy-efficient offices, data centers, innovation hubs, digital studios, and technology infrastructure across the globe. The infrastructure is designed with consideration for climate change mitigation, natural-resource use, and environmental preservation.

- 84% of office area certified with the highest green-building rating

Infosys continues to invest in green buildings and intelligent, collaborative workplace technologies.

## Advancing the frontiers of energy performance

Infosys uses progressive design strategies, enhanced building envelopes, intelligent HVAC systems, renewable energy integration, high-performance materials with recycled content, passive design principles, and real-time monitoring to reduce energy intensity and improve occupant well-being.

The company’s campus principles include health and wellness, social connection, sustainability, inclusivity, and technology enablement. Environmental measures include efficient interior solutions, sustainable materials, enhanced lighting and air quality, and intelligent digital systems.

Eighty-four percent of Infosys’ office spaces are certified as green buildings.

## Embedding circularity and innovation in design

Infosys integrates Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) into early-stage decision-making for material selection and design optimization.

Steel and cement account for nearly 70% of embodied carbon in office buildings. Infosys has prioritized secondary steel produced from recycled scrap, with an emission intensity of 1–1.5 tCO2e per ton compared with 2.5–3.5 tCO2e per ton for primary steel. Cement use is optimized through blending with industrial by-products such as fly ash and ground granulated blast-furnace slag.

Infosys is advancing thermal management through radiant heating and free cooling under favorable climatic conditions. It has also standardized the procurement of air-conditioning equipment using refrigerants with very low global warming potential.

The company eliminated plastic and thermocol-based packaging for IT equipment and embedded sustainable-packaging criteria into procurement processes. Supplier partnerships support the use of paper-based, recycled, and biodegradable fiber alternatives, including materials derived from agricultural waste.

## Resource circularity in operations

Infosys’ circular-economy initiatives focus on responsible IT-asset utilization, maximum value extraction across the asset lifecycle, and reduced environmental impact. IT assets are reused, redeployed, and refurbished to extend their useful life, reduce e-waste, and reduce demand for new resources.

IT assets are repurposed and reused across functions and delivery centers. Assets reaching the end of their usable life are handled through responsible end-of-life management.

## Green IT

InfosysIT has embedded sustainable practices across the lifecycle of service design, operations, and disposal of IT assets.

## Data center efficiency

InfosysIT has made data center modernization a key part of its sustainability and responsible-growth strategy. Its data center ecosystem prioritizes efficiency, scalability, and long-term resource optimization.

Density-optimized hyperscale platforms support cloud-scale agility and high-density server virtualization. This reduces infrastructure sprawl and increases compute-resource utilization.

InfosysIT has rationalized and consolidated data centers and server rooms across development centers, reducing redundancy, optimizing space usage, and supporting a more sustainable operating footprint.

Investments in Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) provide integrated, real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure. The tools support data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.

## Infrastructure as code

Infrastructure as code supports continuous deployment, integration, and touchless lifecycle management of infrastructure components. The methodology addresses challenges in traditional infrastructure management related to scale, deployment speed, consistency, and interdependence between teams.

A substantial number of playbooks have been developed to automate platform-related processes.

## AIOps-powered digital operations

InfosysIT uses an AIOps platform with intelligent noise suppression and context-driven notifications. The platform provides unified observability across the infrastructure landscape, visibility into capacity usage and cost efficiency, and analysis of high-volume data from diverse IT sources to support proactive operations.

## Public cloud adoption

Infosys has largely transitioned its internal IT applications to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on onsite data centers. Cloud-based collaboration and secure-access solutions support hybrid work.

The IT Service Management (ITSM) landscape uses a standardized, AI-enabled service desk hosted on a SaaS platform. This has supported standardized operational processes, greater automation, AI-assisted agents, and improved user experience across support services.
