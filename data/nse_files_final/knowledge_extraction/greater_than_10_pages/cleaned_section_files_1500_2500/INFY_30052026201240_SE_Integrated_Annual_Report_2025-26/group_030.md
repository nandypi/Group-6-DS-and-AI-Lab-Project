## 8. Income tax assets / liabilities

Net profit earned from providing software development and other services outside India is subject to tax in the country where the work is performed. Most taxes paid outside India can be claimed as a credit against tax liabilities in India. Refer to Note 2.17 of the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements.

## 9. Other financial liabilities

Liabilities for accrued employee compensation include provisions for bonuses, accrued salaries, incentives, and retention bonuses payable to staff. Financial liabilities under option arrangements represent redemption liabilities related to acquisitions to purchase the corresponding minority stake.

Accrued expenses represent amounts accrued for other operating expenses. Compensated absences are accumulating and non-accumulating. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined through actuarial valuation.

## IV. Results of our operations

For details about results of operations, refer to the “Results of our operations and state of affairs” section in the Board’s Report.

During fiscal 2026, the Company continued Project Maximus, a margin expansion program structured around five pillars:

1. Value-based selling
2. Efficient pyramid
3. Lean, automation and gen AI
4. Improving key portfolios
5. Optimization of indirect costs

Value-based selling focuses on aligning pricing with the value received by clients. Efficient pyramid initiatives aim to reduce effort costs through improved utilization, reduced use of subcontractors, and optimization of role and location mix. Lean, automation and gen AI combines productivity and process improvement using automation technologies, including generative AI. Improving key portfolios targets margin improvement in large client engagements and service lines. Savings from optimization of indirect costs supported investment in talent, AI, sales, and marketing.

## 1. Revenue

The growth in revenue in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025 was as follows:

| Particulars | Standalone 2026 | Standalone 2025 | Standalone % change | Consolidated 2026 | Consolidated 2025 | Consolidated % change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 1,48,819 | 1,36,592 | 9.0% | 1,78,650 | 1,62,990 | 9.6% |

The increase in revenue was primarily attributable to higher realization due to role mix, improved pricing and productivity, acquisitions, and currency benefit.

Revenue growth in reported terms includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant currency growth excludes this impact by comparing current-period revenues in local currencies converted to U.S. dollars using prior-period exchange rates with prior-period reported revenues.

Consolidated revenue in reported currency terms was ₹1,78,650 crore in fiscal 2026, representing growth of 9.6%. Consolidated revenue grew 3.1% in constant currency terms.

The Company added 443 new customers on a gross basis during fiscal 2026, compared with 365 during fiscal 2025.

## 10. Other liabilities

Invoicing in excess of revenue is classified as unearned revenue. Withholding and other taxes payable represent local taxes payable in the countries in which the Company operates.

Infosys provides for provident fund contributions for eligible employees under a defined benefit plan because the Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on trust investments and the notified interest rate.

The Company operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers.

Infosys provides gratuity under a defined benefit retirement plan covering eligible employees in India. The Gratuity Plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure. The plans provide periodic post-retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments under the rules of each fund, including death and disability benefits.

## 11. Provisions

The provision for post-sales client support covers the likely cost of supporting clients under fixed-price and fixed-timeframe contracts.

## 12. Leases

Lease additions mainly comprise computers and buildings taken on lease in certain locations outside India.

For the year ended March 31, 2026, approximately 97.1% of consolidated revenue was from exports and 2.9% was from domestic revenue.
