## Annexure 2 - Particulars of contracts / arrangements made with related parties

This Form pertains to contracts, arrangements, and transactions entered into by the Company with related parties referred to in sub-section (1) of Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013, including certain arm’s-length transactions under the third proviso thereto.

## Details of contracts or arrangements or transactions not at arm's length basis

There were no contracts, arrangements, or transactions entered into during the year ended March 31, 2026, that were not at arm’s length.

## Details of material contracts or arrangement or transactions at arm's length basis

There were no material contracts, arrangements, or transactions entered into during the year ended March 31, 2026.

There were no material contracts, arrangements, or transactions requiring disclosure under the Companies Act, 2013. However, the Company obtained shareholder approval for material related-party transactions in accordance with Regulation 23 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Details are available in the [2025 AGM notice](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/documents/agm-notice2025.pdf).

## Annexure 3 - Particulars of employees

Infosys provides AI-first business consulting and technology services to organizations across 59 countries. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee reviews compensation for the CEO and other Senior Management Personnel (SMP) to align short- and long-term business objectives with achievement of goals.

The remuneration and perquisites provided to employees, including management, comply with Rule 5 of Chapter XIII of the Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules, 2014. Tables 3(a) and 3(b) include the perquisite value of stock incentives at exercise and exclude the value at grant.

The change in remuneration in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025 primarily reflects changes in the perquisite value of stock incentives granted in previous years and exercised during the year, as well as compensation revisions. Changes in the perquisite value also reflect the number of units exercised and changes in share price.

### Notes

- The remuneration details pertain to directors and KMP as required under the Companies Act, 2013. The table also includes the percentage increase in remuneration and the ratio of remuneration to MRE, excluding the perquisite value of stock incentives exercised during the year.
- The details are presented on an accrual basis.
- The percentage increase in remuneration is provided for directors and KMP with remuneration for the full current and previous fiscal years. The ratio of remuneration to MRE is provided for directors and KMP with remuneration for the full current fiscal year.
- Changes in remuneration of independent directors are primarily attributable to foreign exchange fluctuations and changes in the composition of Board committees.
- Remuneration to KMP includes fixed pay, variable pay, retiral benefits, and the perquisite value of stock incentives exercised during the period, determined in accordance with the Income-tax Act, 1961. The value of stock incentives granted during the period is not included. The number of stock incentives granted in fiscal 2026 is shown in the table. Independent Directors are not entitled to stock incentives.

- On the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and with shareholder approval, the Board approved the following grants under the terms of his employment agreement effective July 1, 2022:
  - 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on achievement of specified performance targets.
  - 13,273 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
  - 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.
  - 18,132 annual time-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2015 Plan, effective February 1, 2026, vesting over three years.
  - 66,366 performance-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on performance criteria specified in the 2019 Plan.
- These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement.
