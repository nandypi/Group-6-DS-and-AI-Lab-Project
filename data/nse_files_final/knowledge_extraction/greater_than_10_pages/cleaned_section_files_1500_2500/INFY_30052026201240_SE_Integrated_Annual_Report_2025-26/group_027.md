## Annexure 7 - Conservation of energy, research and development, technology absorption, foreign exchange earnings and outgo.

[Particulars pursuant to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014]

Infosys maintained carbon-neutral operations for the seventh consecutive year since fiscal 2020. The Company is pursuing climate-positive objectives, including restoring more natural capital than it consumes, scaling low-carbon interventions, and developing partnerships related to climate resilience and net-zero initiatives.

## Resource conservation initiatives

Infosys' resource conservation initiatives include energy-efficient buildings, retrofits, automation, operational optimization, water management, wastewater treatment, waste management, renewable energy adoption, and carbon offset projects.

### Energy

In fiscal 2026, Infosys spent approximately ₹1.27 crore on infrastructure retrofits to improve operational efficiency. Initiatives included energy-efficient building design, embodied carbon analysis, operational optimization, and employee engagement.

The ASSURE initiative, launched with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements, focuses on decarbonizing India's building sector through lighthouse projects, capacity-building, policy advocacy, and ecosystem collaboration. During fiscal 2026, more than 15 million square feet of built-up area was onboarded as lighthouse projects.

### Renewable energy

Infosys has total installed solar capacity of 62.4 MWp across its campuses, including a 42.5 MWp captive solar plant in Karnataka. The Company also uses power purchase agreements and green tariffs from DISCOMs. In fiscal 2026, renewable sources met 81.8% of electricity requirements in Infosys' India operations.

### Green buildings

All new developments are designed to achieve the highest level of green building certification. Approximately 84% of Infosys' office space is currently certified to the highest level of green building standards.

### Smarter spaces

Infosys' offices use digital connectivity, ergonomic design, intelligent energy systems, and advanced Building Management Systems to optimize lighting, ventilation, and thermal comfort. These systems support hybrid work models, employee well-being, inclusivity, and operational efficiency.

### Water management

Infosys maintains zero wastewater discharge and is a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate. The Company is committed to recharging more water than it consumes by 2030. Its water management practices include reducing freshwater demand, treating and reusing wastewater, and expanding rainwater harvesting across campuses.

Infosys also undertakes lake rejuvenation initiatives intended to support groundwater recharge, biodiversity, and long-term water security.

### Waste management

Infosys is pursuing a zero-waste-to-landfill goal through waste reduction, segregation, recovery, treatment, and management. The Company achieved 99% waste diversion from landfill. In fiscal 2026, 11 campuses achieved True Zero Waste Certification from the Green Business Certification Institute.

### Carbon offset

Infosys uses community-based carbon offset projects to address unavoidable emissions. Clean-cooking projects implemented across five Indian states have supported health and livelihoods and generated income for rural women.

The Company has also initiated large-scale carbon sequestration projects through agroforestry, benefiting small and marginal farmers through improved livelihoods, soil health, food security, and climate resilience.

### Carbon neutral events

Infosys measures, reduces where feasible, and offsets emissions associated with Company-hosted events through certified carbon offset projects. In fiscal 2026, the Company organized eight carbon-neutral events.

## Health, Safety and Environment

Infosys' Health, Safety, and Environmental Management System covers employees, contractors, and visitors. The Company is certified to ISO 14001:2015 for Environmental Management and ISO 45001:2018 for Occupational Health and Safety. The system is designed to support compliance with applicable laws across the regions where Infosys operates.

## Internal IT - AI-first software engineering

Infosys' internal IT organization supports and manages 200 enterprise applications. Its AI adoption strategy has two areas:

1. **AI for engineering excellence and lifecycle compression:** AI is embedded across the software engineering lifecycle, from requirements and design through development, testing, deployment, and support. A digital twin of the enterprise landscape captures contextual knowledge across applications, platforms, and layers. AI-powered agents use this information to support software delivery and developer productivity.

2. **AI for business and operations:** AI agents and generative AI solutions support enterprise functions such as Finance and HR. They respond to user queries across applications, provide contextual assistance, autonomously respond to helpdesk emails, improve first-contact resolution, and reduce service desk workload.

### Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys rolled out the Topaz Fabric platform across the organization to support experimentation, development, and access to generative AI and agentic solutions.

### Education, Training & Assessment (ETA)

ETA developed an agentic AI-powered learning ecosystem anchored on Lex, Infosys' internal learning platform, and extended through Infosys Springboard, its free public learning platform. Zoiee, Knowledge Assistant, SynthAIz, and AI agents support personalized learning, intelligent recommendations, real-time assistance, content creation, transcription, translation, and video localization.

### AI cloud

Infosys is investing in proprietary, on-premise GPU infrastructure for advanced model training and inferencing. The infrastructure supports the integration of open-source large language models and deployment of specialized small language models while maintaining data sovereignty, data confidentiality, and reduced dependence on external token usage.
