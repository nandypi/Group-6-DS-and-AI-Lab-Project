## Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee

Michael Gibbs was the Chairperson and a cybersecurity expert.

The Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee comprises only independent directors. The listed members as of March 31, 2026 were:

1. Michael Gibbs, Chairperson and cybersecurity expert
2. D. Sundaram
3. Govind Iyer
4. Chitra Nayak
5. Helene Auriel Potier (1)

(1) Helene Auriel Potier was appointed as a member of the Committee effective August 1, 2025.

### Committee governance

The Risk Management Committee constituted the Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee in April 2019. The Sub-committee was voluntarily constituted to focus on cybersecurity-related threats. Its objective is to assess cybersecurity-related risks and the Company's preparedness to mitigate and react to such risks. The Sub-committee meets periodically and recommends its findings, if any, to the Risk Management Committee.

The Sub-committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## Stakeholders Relationship Committee

Michael Gibbs was the Chairperson.

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, the Committee comprised:

1. Michael Gibbs, Chairperson
2. D. Sundaram
3. Bobby Parikh
4. Chitra Nayak

The Board appointed A.G.S. Manikantha, Company Secretary, as the Compliance Officer, as required under the Listing Regulations, and as the Nodal Officer to ensure compliance with the IEPF rules.

### Purpose of the Committee

The Committee assists the Board and the Company in overseeing matters relating to the interests of the Company's stakeholders, including shareholders and other security holders.

### Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objectives of the Committee are to:

1. Consider and resolve security-holder concerns or complaints.
2. Monitor and review the Company's investor service standards.
3. Develop an understanding of shareholders' views through direct interaction, analysts' briefings, or shareholder surveys.
4. Oversee and review the engagement and communication plan with shareholders, ensure that their views and concerns are highlighted to the Board at the appropriate time, and ensure that steps are taken to address those concerns.

### Committee governance

The Committee comprises four independent directors and performs functions required by:

- Section 178 of the Companies Act, 2013, and rules framed thereunder
- Regulation 20 of the Listing Regulations and other applicable regulations and laws
- NYSE guidelines, as applicable
- The Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter

### Committee Charter

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter is available on the Company's website at [https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/stakeholdersrelationship-committee.pdf](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/stakeholdersrelationship-committee.pdf).
