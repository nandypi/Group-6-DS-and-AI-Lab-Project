## Key recommendations of the Committee

Based on discussions with Management and the auditors, and a review of Management representations and the auditors’ report, the Committee recommended that the Board:

- Accept the Company’s quarterly condensed financial statements prepared in accordance with Ind AS as specified under the Companies Act, 2013, and the relevant rules, together with IFRS issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.
- Accept the audited financial statements of Infosys Limited prepared in accordance with Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026, as a true and fair statement of the Company’s financial status.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in accordance with Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026, as a true and fair statement of the Group’s financial status.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, prepared in Indian rupees and U.S. dollars in accordance with IFRS, for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, as a true and fair statement of the Group’s financial status.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, prepared in U.S. dollars in accordance with IFRS, for the year ended March 31, 2026, and include them in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F to be filed with the SEC.
- Appoint Ernst & Young LLP as the Company’s internal auditors for the year ending March 31, 2027, to review various Company operations.
- Issue a letter in line with Recommendation No. 9 of the Blue Ribbon Committee on Audit Committee effectiveness for inclusion in the financial statements prepared in accordance with IFRS in the Annual Report on Form 20-F.
- In line with mandatory auditor rotation under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, recommend the appointment of BSR & Co. LLP as statutory auditors commencing with the audit of the Company’s financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2028, including the audit of the quarterly financial statements for that year. This intent was proposed on April 23, 2026.
- Approve the intent to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as the independent registered public accounting firm for U.S. SEC filings, effective fiscal 2027–28, subject to applicable requirements.

Based on its review and discussions with Management and the independent auditors, the Committee stated that the Company’s financial statements were fairly presented in conformity with Ind AS and IFRS and that there were no significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in the Company’s internal control over financial reporting. The Board accepted all recommendations made by the Audit Committee.

## Objectives of the Committee

The main objectives and responsibilities of the Committee are to:

1. Assist the Board in discharging its responsibilities relating to remuneration of the Company’s directors, Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), and senior management.
2. Evaluate and approve the adequacy of remuneration plans, policies, programs, and succession plans for the Company’s directors, KMP, and senior management; identify individuals for appointment to such positions in accordance with prescribed criteria; and recommend their appointment and removal to the Board.
3. Formulate criteria for determining the qualifications, positive attributes, independence, and performance evaluation of directors on the Board.
4. Administer equity-based plans and schemes approved by shareholders.
5. Oversee the Company’s nomination process for KMP and senior management, and identify, screen, and review individuals qualified to serve as directors, KMP, and senior management in accordance with Board-approved criteria.
6. Recommend the appointment and removal of directors for approval at the annual meeting of shareholders.
7. Evaluate the performance of the Board and review implementation and compliance with the evaluation.
8. Oversee leadership development.
9. Develop and maintain corporate governance policies applicable to the Company.
10. Devise a policy on Board diversity.

The Committee comprises four members and fulfills the requirements of:
