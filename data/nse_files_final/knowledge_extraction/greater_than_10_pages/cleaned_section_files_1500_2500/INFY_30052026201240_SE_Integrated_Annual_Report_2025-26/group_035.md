## 3.  Branding

Brand Infosys was ranked among the world's Top 100 brands in FY26 and recognized as the fastest-growing IT services brand globally over a six-year period. The brand positions Infosys as an enterprise AI partner, supported by its experience managing global enterprise systems and its capabilities in enterprise AI, cloud, and other digital technologies.

Infosys' global marketing reach includes digital-first, multichannel campaigns and partnerships with the Australian Open, Roland-Garros, ATP, The International Tennis Hall of Fame, Madison Square Garden properties, the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship, Dow Jones, The Economist Group, Financial Times, and Handelsblatt Media Group. Infosys also organizes business and industry events, including the Confluence client event series across the US, Europe, and APAC.

## 5.  Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI)

The Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI) focuses on succession planning, leadership bench strength, development of title holders, and strengthening inclusivity in leadership.

ILI's AI for Business Growth program, delivered in partnership with Kellogg, was completed by more than 1,500 senior leaders across Infosys over the last two years. A day-long AI immersion program gave senior leaders hands-on experience with AI tools, including Infosys Topaz Fabric. Executive leadership programs delivered in partnership with Harvard Business School and MIT were conducted as weeklong residential cohorts at the Mysuru campus.

ILI strengthened enterprise-wide succession planning through regular data-led reviews, leadership profiles, personalized development plans, executive education, and external coaching. The Constellation program was refreshed to align with enterprise priorities. As part of the program, leaders participated in a 10-month program with Oxford Saïd Business School and worked in cross-functional teams on strategically critical enterprise projects. Twelve Constellation projects were executed during the year.

ILI's development programs for title holders included:

- The 10-month Enterprise Leadership Program for AVPs
- The Executive Communication program in partnership with The Economist for VPs
- The InFusion program for new title holders

Infosys leaders collectively invested more than 10,000 leadership learning days annually during each of the last two years. ILI recorded a Net Promoter Score of more than 96% for the third consecutive year and was recognized as the top unit for Internal Partner Satisfaction for three consecutive years.

The #IamtheFuture program supported multi-year, role-specific development journeys for leaders. It covered more than 700 leaders and was redesigned during the year to differentiate development experiences across career stages, with emphasis on application, simulations, and contextual learning.

## 6.  Infosys Knowledge Institute

The Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI) is the business research and thought leadership arm of Infosys. It uses the expertise of Infosys employees and client collaborations to develop content and insights on the business impact of technology. IKI also conducts primary research through large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis.

IKI's outputs include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals. Since inception, IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets.

## 7. ESG vision and ambitions

Infosys launched its ESG Vision 2030 in October 2020. In fiscal 2025, at the midpoint of its 2030 journey, the company refreshed its ESG ambitions to address changing stakeholder priorities.

Infosys remained carbon neutral across its global operations for the seventh consecutive year. Under the ESG Vision 2030 Refresh, the company aims to become climate positive by 2030 through initiatives covering Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions. Its ambitions include reducing its carbon footprint, sequestering more carbon than it emits, engaging clients on climate action through its solutions, maintaining 100% wastewater recycling, sequestering more water than it consumes year-on-year, and achieving zero waste to landfill.

Infosys' digital skilling programs aim to empower more than 18 million people and enable employment opportunities for more than 500,000 people. Its sustainability and CSR activities include technology-for-good initiatives, employee volunteering, healthcare, and women's empowerment.

Infosys also aims to maintain a strong employee experience, support a diverse and inclusive board, adopt leading data privacy standards, and strengthen its information security practices.

## Corporate governance report

Corporate governance guides how Infosys is directed and how it manages relationships with stakeholders. The company's governance framework is intended to support trust, transparency, accountability, long-term capital, economic growth, and financial stability.

## Our corporate governance philosophy

Infosys' corporate governance reflects its values, culture, policies, and relationships with stakeholders. The company states that integrity and transparency are central to its governance practices and that its affairs are managed fairly and transparently.

The governance framework is based on the C-LIFE values:

- Client value
- Leadership by example
- Integrity and transparency
- Fairness
- Excellence

It is also guided by the principles of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD). The framework is reinforced through the Code of Conduct and Ethics, corporate governance guidelines, committee charters, Board and Management processes, audits, and internal control systems.

The corporate governance report provides an overview of how Infosys' governance adheres to the seven pillars of its governance framework.
