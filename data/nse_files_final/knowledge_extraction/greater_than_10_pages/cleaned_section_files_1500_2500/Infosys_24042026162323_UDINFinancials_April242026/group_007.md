## Auditor's Responsibility

The auditor’s responsibility is to express an opinion on the Company’s internal financial controls with reference to standalone financial statements.

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting issued by the ICAI and the applicable Standards on Auditing prescribed under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. These standards require the auditor to comply with ethical requirements and plan and perform the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether adequate internal financial controls were established and maintained, and whether such controls operated effectively in all material respects.

The audit procedures included obtaining an understanding of the Company’s internal financial controls, assessing the risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error, and evaluating controls based on the assessed risk. The auditor stated that the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the opinion.

## Meaning of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

A company’s internal financial controls with reference to standalone financial statements are processes designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding:

1. The maintenance of records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and dispositions of assets in reasonable detail.
2. The recording of transactions as necessary to permit preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, and the authorization of receipts and expenditures by the Company’s management and directors.
3. The prevention, or timely detection, of unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposition of the Company’s assets that could have a material effect on the financial statements.

## Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Because of the inherent limitations of internal financial controls over financial reporting, including the possibility of collusion or improper management override of controls, misstatements due to error or fraud may not be prevented or detected. Evaluations of internal financial controls for future periods are also subject to the risk that controls may become inadequate because of changes in conditions or that compliance with policies or procedures may deteriorate.

## Opinion

In the auditor’s opinion, the Company’s internal financial controls with reference to standalone financial statements were operating effectively as at March 31, 2026, based on the criteria for internal financial control with reference to standalone financial statements established by the Company, considering the essential components of internal control issued by the ICAI.

## ANNEXURE 'B' TO THE INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT

*(Referred to in paragraph 2 under the “Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements” section of the auditor’s report to the Members of Infosys Limited of even date.)*

Based on the information and explanations provided by the Company and the books of account and records examined in the normal course of audit, the auditor reported the following:

### i. Property, Plant and Equipment, Right-of-Use Assets and Intangible Assets

- The Company maintained proper records showing full particulars, including quantitative details and location, of property, plant and equipment and relevant details of right-of-use assets.
- The Company maintained proper records showing full particulars of intangible assets.
- The Company had a programme of physical verification of property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets designed to cover all assets once every three years. Assets due for verification during the year were physically verified by management. No material discrepancies were noticed.
- Based on examination of property tax receipts, land lease agreements, registered sale deeds, transfer deeds, and conveyance deeds, the title to self-constructed buildings and other immovable properties disclosed under property, plant and equipment was held in the Company’s name as of the balance sheet date, except for properties where the Company is the lessee and the lease agreements were duly executed in favour of the lessee.
- The Company did not revalue any property, plant and equipment, including right-of-use assets, or intangible assets during the year.
- No proceedings were initiated or pending against the Company as of March 31, 2026, for holding benami property under the Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988, as amended in 2016, and the rules made thereunder.

### ii. Inventory and Working Capital Limits

- The Company did not have any inventory; therefore, reporting under Clause 3(ii)(a) of the Order was not applicable.
- The Company was not sanctioned working capital limits exceeding ₹5 crore in aggregate at any time during the year from banks or financial institutions on the security of current assets. Therefore, reporting under Clause 3(ii)(b) of the Order was not applicable.

### iii. Investments, Loans, Guarantees and Securities

The Company made investments and granted unsecured loans to other parties during the year.

- The Company did not provide loans or advances in the nature of loans, stand guarantees, or provide security to any other entity during the year. Reporting under Clause 3(iii)(a) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- In the auditor’s opinion, the investments made and the terms and conditions of loans granted during the year were, prima facie, not prejudicial to the Company’s interests.
- For loans granted by the Company, schedules for repayment of principal and payment of interest were stipulated. Repayments of principal and receipts of interest were generally regular according to the stipulations.
- No overdue amount relating to loans granted by the Company remained outstanding as of the balance sheet date.
- No loan granted by the Company that fell due during the year was renewed or extended, and no fresh loans were granted to settle overdue amounts of existing loans to the same parties.
- The Company did not grant loans or advances in the nature of loans that were repayable on demand or without specified repayment terms or periods.
- The Company did not make investments in firms or limited liability partnerships during the year.
- The Company did not provide guarantees or security, or grant advances in the nature of loans, secured or unsecured, to companies, firms, limited liability partnerships, or any other parties.

### iv. Compliance with Sections 185 and 186

The Company complied with the provisions of Sections 185 and 186 of the Companies Act, 2013, in respect of loans granted, investments made, and guarantees and securities provided, as applicable.

### v. Deposits

The Company did not accept any deposits or amounts deemed to be deposits. Reporting under Clause 3(v) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

### vi. Cost Records

The maintenance of cost records was not specified by the Central Government under Section 148(1) of the Companies Act, 2013, for the business activities carried out by the Company. Reporting under Clause 3(vi) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

### vii. Statutory Dues

The Company was generally regular in depositing undisputed statutory dues, including Goods and Services Tax, Provident Fund, Employees’ State Insurance, Income Tax, Sales Tax, Service Tax, Customs Duty, Excise Duty, Value Added Tax, Cess, and other material statutory dues applicable to it with the appropriate authorities.

As of March 31, 2026, there were no undisputed amounts payable in respect of these statutory dues that had remained in arrears for more than six months from the date they became payable.
