## 15. The Board has confirmed that it has made a full enquiry into the affairs and prospects of the Company and has formed the opinion:

- Immediately following the Board meeting held on September 11, 2025 and the members' resolution approving the buyback on November 4, 2025, there would be no grounds on which the Company could be found unable to pay its debts.
- Having regard to the Company's prospects, management intentions, and financial resources, the Company would be able to meet its liabilities as they fall due and would not be rendered insolvent within one year from September 11, 2025 or from November 4, 2025.
- The Board considered all liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, as if the Company were being wound up under the Companies Act, 2013 and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.

## 16. Report addressed to the Board by the Company's Auditors on the permissible capital payment and the opinion formed by directors regarding insolvency:

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, the Company's statutory auditors, issued a report dated September 11, 2025 to the Board regarding the proposed buyback.

## REF: IL/2025-26/29

## AUDITOR'S REPORT

The Board of Directors had approved the proposed buyback at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, subject to shareholder approval, under Sections 68, 69, and 70 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Buyback Regulations.

Management was responsible for preparing the Statement of Permissible Capital Payment as at June 30, 2025, including the computation of permissible capital payment and supporting records.

The auditors' responsibility was to provide reasonable assurance that:

- They had inquired into the Company's affairs in relation to its audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
- The permissible capital payment stated in Annexure A had been properly determined under Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulations 4(i) and 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations.
- The Board had formed its solvency opinion on reasonable grounds and that the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from September 11, 2025 or from the date of the shareholders' resolution approving the buyback.

The interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements had been audited by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, which issued an unmodified audit opinion dated July 23, 2025.

## Opinion

Based on its inquiries and examination, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP reported that:

- The auditors had inquired into the Company's affairs in relation to the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025, approved by the Board on July 23, 2025.
- The permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback had been properly determined under the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Buyback Regulations.
- The amounts of share capital and free reserves had been extracted from the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
- The Board had formed its solvency opinion on reasonable grounds and the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from September 11, 2025 or from the date of the shareholders' resolution approving the buyback.
