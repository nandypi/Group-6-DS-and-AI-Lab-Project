## Step 2: Cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-Voting system.

After successful login, shareholders can select the relevant company and electronic voting event, open the voting page, select assent or dissent, verify or modify the number of shares, and submit and confirm the vote. A successful vote generates a confirmation message. Once confirmed, a vote cannot be modified.

## General Guidelines for shareholders

1. Institutional shareholders must send a scanned PDF or JPG copy of the relevant Board Resolution, authority letter, or similar authorization, with the attested specimen signature of the authorized signatory or signatories, to the Scrutinizer by email. They may alternatively upload the Board Resolution, Power of Attorney, or authority letter through the e-Voting tab after login.
2. Shareholders should keep their e-voting passwords confidential. Login is disabled after five unsuccessful password attempts; credentials can be reset using the available password-reset options on the NSDL e-voting website.
3. Shareholders may refer to the NSDL FAQs and e-voting user manual for assistance with the voting process.

## Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories for procuring user id and password and registration of e mail ids for e-voting for the resolutions set out in this notice:

1. Shareholders holding shares in physical mode should provide their folio number, name, scanned copies of the front and back of the share certificate, self-attested PAN, and self-attested Aadhaar documentation to Infosys for obtaining e-voting credentials and registering their email ID.
2. Shareholders holding shares in demat mode should provide their 16-digit DPID-CLID or beneficiary ID, name, client master or consolidated account statement, self-attested PAN, and self-attested Aadhaar documentation. Individual demat shareholders may use the login method specified for individual shareholders in Step 1(A).
3. Shareholders may alternatively request their user ID and password from NSDL by providing the required documents.
4. Under the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and depository participants. Their mobile number and email ID must be correctly updated in the demat account to access e-voting.
