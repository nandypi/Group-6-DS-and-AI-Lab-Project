The Company engaged NSDL to facilitate electronic voting. It also made arrangements with KFin Technologies Limited, its Registrar and Share Transfer Agent, to enable members to register their e-mail addresses.

The Scrutinizer will submit the voting report to the Chairman or a person authorized by the Chairman. The postal ballot result will be announced no later than two working days after the conclusion of e-voting and communicated as provided in the Postal Ballot Notice. If approved by the requisite majority, November 4, 2025, the last date of e-voting, will be the date on which the resolution is deemed to have been passed.

Numerical amounts in this section are presented in crore. One crore equals 10 million, or 10,000,000.

## RESOLUTION

## Item no. 1 - Approval for the Buyback of Equity Shares of the Company

To consider and, if thought fit, pass the following resolution as a Special Resolution:

The members’ consent is sought for Infosys Limited to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ` 5 each from shareholders holding shares on a record date to be determined by the Board of Directors or Buyback Committee. The proposed terms are:

- Buyback price: ` 1,800 per equity share.
- Buyback offer size: ` 18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs such as brokerage, filing fees, advisory and intermediary fees, public announcement and publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, securities transaction tax, goods and services tax, stamp duty, and other incidental expenses.
- Buyback offer size as a percentage of aggregate paid-up share capital and free reserves: 24.31% on a standalone basis and 21.68% on a consolidated basis, based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025.
- Proposed number of shares: 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares.
- Proposed shares as a percentage of fully paid-up share capital: 2.41% on a standalone basis.
- Buyback method: Proportionate basis through the tender-offer route on the Indian stock exchanges.

The Board or Buyback Committee may, up to one working day before the record date, increase the buyback price and decrease the number of shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the buyback offer size does not change.

All equity shareholders holding shares on the record date will be eligible to participate, including holders of American Depositary Shares (ADSs) who cancel ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares before the record date. ADS holders may re-deposit any withdrawn shares that are not tendered or are tendered but not accepted, subject to applicable law.

To the extent legally permissible, the Company will use SEBI’s mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchanges pursuant to tender offers under takeover, buyback, and delisting regulations. The mechanism is subject to applicable SEBI circulars, including those dated April 13, 2015; December 9, 2016; August 13, 2021; and March 8, 2023. The Company will approach BSE Limited and/or the National Stock Exchange of India Limited to facilitate the buyback. One of BSE and NSE will be designated as the stock exchange, as determined by the Board or Buyback Committee.

The buyback will be funded from the Company’s free reserves, securities premium account, or another source permitted under the Buyback Regulations or the Companies Act, 2013. The required funds are intended to come from the Company’s current surplus, cash balances, and/or internal accruals.

The Company may buy back shares from existing shareholders on a proportionate basis. Fifteen percent of the shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of shares to which Small Shareholders are entitled under the Buyback Regulations, whichever is applicable, will be reserved for Small Shareholders. If shares tendered under the reservation are fewer than the reserved amount, the balance may be adjusted to the general category.

Buyback participation by non-resident members, including Foreign Corporate Bodies, erstwhile Overseas Corporate Bodies, Foreign Institutional Investors, Foreign Portfolio Investors, foreign nationals, and ADS holders with underlying equity shares, will be subject to applicable requirements under:

- The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and related rules and regulations.
- The Income Tax Act, 1961, and related rules and regulations.
- The Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014.
- Applicable approvals from relevant authorities, including the Reserve Bank of India where required.

The buyback will remain subject to the minimum public shareholding requirement under Regulation 38 of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015.

Under Regulation 9 of the Buyback Regulations, the Company will open an escrow account. The escrow may comprise cash, bank deposits with a scheduled commercial bank, or a bank guarantee created in favour of the manager to the buyback. If the Company fails to fulfil its obligations under the Buyback Regulations, amounts in the escrow account may be forfeited in accordance with Regulation 9 and deposited in SEBI’s Investor Protection and Education Fund.

The resolution does not give any shareholder a right to tender shares or impose an obligation on the Company, Board, or Buyback Committee to buy back shares. The Company, Board, or Buyback Committee may terminate the buyback process where permitted by law.

The Board is authorized to take actions required to implement the buyback, including appointing merchant bankers, brokers, lawyers, depository participants, escrow agents, bankers, advisors, registrars, scrutinizers, consultants, representatives, intermediaries, agencies, printers, and advertising agencies. The Board may also prepare and issue required documents, open accounts, issue public announcements, extinguish share certificates, file a Certificate of Extinguishment, seek approvals, and submit documents or correspondence to SEBI, the Reserve Bank of India, the Government of India, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, BSE, NSE, the New York Stock Exchange, the Registrar of Companies, depositories, and other relevant authorities.

The Board may alter or modify the terms and conditions, delegate or revoke delegated authority, settle questions or difficulties, and take other necessary actions to give effect to the resolution without seeking further member approval, subject to applicable law.
