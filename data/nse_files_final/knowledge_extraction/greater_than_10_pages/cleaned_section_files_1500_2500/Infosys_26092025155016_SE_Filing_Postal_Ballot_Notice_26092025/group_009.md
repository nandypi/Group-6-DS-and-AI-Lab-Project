## 10. Intention of the Promoters, Promoter Group and persons in control of the Company to tender their Equity Shares in the Buyback:

Under the tender offer route permitted by the Buyback Regulations, the Promoter and Promoter Group may participate in the Buyback. The Promoter and Promoter Group expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback through letters dated September 14, 2025, September 16, 2025, September 17, 2025, September 18, 2025, and September 19, 2025. Accordingly, the disclosures required under paragraphs (viii) of Schedule I of the SEBI Buyback Regulations are not applicable.

## 11. Additional Company Confirmations

The Company confirmed that it had not accepted any deposits before or after the applicability of the Companies Act, 2013. It also confirmed that there were no subsisting defaults relating to:

- Redemption of debentures or payment of interest on debentures
- Redemption of preference shares
- Payment of dividends due to shareholders
- Repayment of term loans or payment of interest to financial institutions or banking companies

## 12. Additional Information for Holders of the Company's American Depositary Shares

### i. Withdrawal of Equity Shares Underlying ADSs: Establishing an Indian Demat account (Subtype DR) and a Brokerage Account.

ADS holders are not eligible to tender ADSs directly in the Buyback. To participate, they must hold the underlying Equity Shares as of the Record Date. They must:

1. Apply for and obtain a Permanent Account Number (PAN) from the Indian Income Tax Department.
2. Establish an Indian Demat account (Subtype DR) and a brokerage account with a bank, broker, or other nominee in India sufficiently before the Record Date.
3. Submit the ADSs to the Depositary for cancellation with proper cancellation instructions and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares.
4. Tender some or all of the withdrawn Equity Shares during the Tendering Period.

The expected Record Date was on or around November 14, 2025, subject to shareholder approval of the Buyback.

Each ADS holder is responsible for establishing its own Indian Demat account (Subtype DR) and brokerage account, providing required documentation, and paying related fees, charges, and expenses. ADS holders may apply for a PAN through online Form 49A. The PAN application process includes physical mailing of documentation to and from the Income Tax Department.

The Company was advised that establishing an Indian Demat account (Subtype DR) and brokerage account, including obtaining a PAN and completing know-your-customer and other documentation, typically takes 21–35 calendar days. The actual timing depends on documentation, verification, and governmental agency response times.

ADS holders who have established the required accounts, submitted ADSs for cancellation, and provided delivery instructions would typically receive the underlying Equity Shares within 2–3 business days after cancellation. Additional time should be allowed to complete the withdrawal and delivery before the Record Date.

Based on the expected Record Date, ADS holders had approximately 49 calendar days from the date the Postal Ballot Notice became publicly available to establish the required accounts, obtain a PAN, and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares.

A Registered ADS Holder may surrender ADSs to the Depositary for cancellation and submit a written Withdrawal Order directing delivery of the underlying Equity Shares to the designated Indian Demat account (Subtype DR) and brokerage account.

A Street Name ADS Holder must request its bank, broker, or other nominee to surrender the ADSs for cancellation and provide the Depositary with the required Withdrawal Order and authorized broker ADS cancellation instruction letter.

ADS holders must provide their email address to the Indian depositories when establishing the Indian Demat account and brokerage account so that, after the Record Date, ADS holders determined to be Eligible Shareholders can receive electronic tender offer materials.

The Depositary agreed to waive the ADS cancellation fee in connection with the Buyback. If Equity Shares that were not tendered, or were tendered but not accepted, are re-deposited more than 30 days after the expiration of the Tendering Period, the ADS cancellation fee would apply.

The Depositary would not assist ADS holders with establishing Indian Demat or brokerage accounts, obtaining a PAN, or related matters beyond delivering the Equity Shares underlying cancelled ADSs to the account designated by the holder. ADS holders opening a new DR Subtype account must complete the required steps in time to receive the shares before the Record Date.

### ii. Re-Deposit of Withdrawn Equity Shares.

Equity Shares trade on the NSE and BSE and cannot be traded on the NYSE. There is no guarantee that any Equity Shares withdrawn following ADS cancellation will be accepted in the Buyback.

The Company had received an intimation from SEBI in 2017 that the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, issued by the Government of India, was in force. The conversion of ADSs into Equity Shares and the re-conversion of Equity Shares into ADSs are subject to the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014 and the terms of the ADSs.

If an ADS holder withdraws the underlying Equity Shares after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date and before the last day of the Tendering Period, the holder may re-deposit Equity Shares that are not tendered or are tendered but not accepted in the Buyback, provided the Equity Shares are delivered to the Depositary's custodian with ADS issuance instructions on or before 30 days after the expiration of the Tendering Period.

The Depositary agreed to waive the ADS issuance fee for ADSs issued against such re-deposited Equity Shares during that 30-day period.

Withdrawn Equity Shares for which re-deposit does not occur, or instructions are not given, within the 30-day period may be re-deposited only if doing so would not exceed the specified maximum number of outstanding ADSs. Equity Shares withdrawn before the ADS postal ballot cut-off date or after the last day of the Tendering Period may also be subject to this maximum. The Company could not assure ADS holders that these Equity Shares would be eligible for re-deposit. In these circumstances, the ADS issuance fee would not be waived.

### iii. Buyback Price and Foreign Exchange Considerations.

The Buyback Price would be paid in Indian rupees. Exchange-rate fluctuations between the Indian rupee and the U.S. dollar would affect the U.S. dollar equivalent of the Buyback Price and the Buyback amount.

### iv. Tax and Regulatory Considerations.

Withdrawal of the Equity Shares underlying ADSs, re-deposit of Equity Shares not tendered or not accepted in the Buyback, and sale of withdrawn Equity Shares in the Buyback may have tax implications in India, the United States, and other jurisdictions. These implications may differ from, and may be substantially more onerous than, those applicable to sales of ADSs on the NYSE.

Tax implications may vary based on factors including:

- The period of holding the securities
- The holder's residential status
- The holder's classification
- The nature of the income earned
