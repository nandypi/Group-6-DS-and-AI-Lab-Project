## Additional information pursuant to U.S. Law

The proposed buyback had not yet been approved by the Company’s shareholders and had not commenced. If approved, offers to purchase or solicitations of offers to sell securities would be made pursuant to a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO, including the letter of offer and related documents, to be filed by the Company with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

## Postal Ballot Notice

### Pursuant to Sections 108 and 110 of the Companies Act, 2013 read with Rules 20 and 22 of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014

The resolution described below was proposed to be passed by members as of the cut-off date through postal ballot, using only the remote e-voting process. An explanatory statement under Section 102 of the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable buyback regulations was annexed to the notice.

## Introduction:

## Buyback of Equity Shares of the Company:

The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, approved, subject to shareholder and other required approvals, the proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each.

The proposed buyback terms were:

| Particular | Details |
|---|---|
| Buyback route | Tender offer |
| Buyback price | ₹1,800 per equity share |
| Buyback offer size | ₹18,000 crore |
| Basis for offer size | 24.31% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves based on the latest audited interim condensed standalone financial statements as at June 30, 2025; 21.68% on a consolidated basis |
| Indicative number of equity shares | 10,00,00,000 |
| Indicative percentage of total paid-up equity share capital | Approximately 2.41% as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis |

The buyback was proposed in accordance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, the Companies Act, 2013, and related rules.

All equity shareholders as of the record date would be eligible to participate. Holders of American Depositary Shares would not be eligible to tender ADSs directly. To participate, ADS holders would need to cancel all or the desired portion of their ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares before the record date so that they became equity shareholders as of that date. Equity shares not tendered or not accepted in the buyback could subsequently be re-deposited for the creation of ADSs.

The record date was to be determined by the Board or Buyback Committee.

The Company obtained exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on certain aspects of the tender-offer procedures because of conflicting regulatory requirements under Indian and U.S. laws.

Shareholder approval for the buyback was required by way of a special resolution through postal ballot or at a general meeting, with electronic voting provided to members.

The Board constituted a Buyback Committee comprising the Chief Financial Officer, the Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer, and the Company Secretary. The committee was authorized to undertake actions required in connection with the buyback.

The Company appointed Hemanth, Holla & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries, as scrutinizer for the e-voting process.

The Company engaged the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) to facilitate electronic voting. The postal-ballot notice and e-voting instructions were sent electronically to members whose email addresses were registered with the Company or their depository participant. Physical copies were to be sent upon request where email addresses were not registered.

The cut-off date for determining members eligible to vote was September 22, 2025. Remote e-voting was scheduled as follows:

| Item | Date and time |
|---|---|
| Commencement of e-voting period | 9:00 a.m. IST on Monday, October 6, 2025 |
| Conclusion of e-voting period | 5:00 p.m. IST on Tuesday, November 4, 2025 |
| Cut-off date for eligibility to vote | Monday, September 22, 2025 |

November 4, 2025, the last date of e-voting, would be the date on which the resolution would be deemed to have been passed if approved by the requisite majority.
