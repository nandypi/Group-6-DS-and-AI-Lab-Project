## SPECIAL NOTE FOR ADS HOLDERS - ACTION REQUIRED TO PARTICIPATE IN THE BUYBACK:

ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. However, ADS holders may participate if they cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares to become equity shareholders of the Company as of the Record Date.

To participate, ADS holders must submit the desired number of ADSs to Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, as ADR depositary (the “Depositary”), for cancellation sufficiently before the Record Date and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares. ADS holders must establish a depository account in India before the Record Date to receive the withdrawn Equity Shares.

Section 12, “Additional Information for Holders of Company's American Depositary Shares,” provides further details, including the process for re-depositing withdrawn Equity Shares that are not tendered or are tendered but not accepted in the Buyback.

## 6. Time limit for completing the Buyback

Subject to regulatory consents and approvals, if any, the Buyback is proposed to be completed within one year from the date the Members pass the special resolution referred to in the Postal Ballot Notice.

## 7. Compliance with Section 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations

As of June 30, 2025, the aggregate paid-up share capital and free reserves were ₹74,042 crore on a standalone basis and ₹83,011 crore on a consolidated basis, based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements available as of the Board meeting held on September 11, 2025.

Under the Companies Act and the Buyback Regulations, funds deployed for the Buyback cannot exceed 25% of the aggregate fully paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the lower of the standalone and consolidated amounts. The maximum permissible amount is ₹18,510 crore.

| Particulars | Amount (₹ in crore) — Standalone | Amount (₹ in crore) — Consolidated |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total paid-up equity capital (A) | 2,077 | 2,074 |
| Free reserves (B) | 71,965 | 80,937 |
| Total paid-up equity capital and free reserves (A) + (B) | 74,042 | 83,011 |
| Maximum amount permissible for Buyback under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations | 18,510 |  |

The Buyback Offer Size of ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only) is less than 25% of the total paid-up capital and free reserves based on both the standalone and consolidated financial statements.

The number of Equity Shares that can be bought back in any financial year cannot exceed 25% of the total Equity Shares in the total paid-up equity capital of the Company for that financial year.

The Buyback proposes to purchase 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares, which does not exceed 25% of the total number of Equity Shares in the existing total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025.
