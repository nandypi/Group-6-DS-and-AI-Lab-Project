## Sub: Postal Ballot Notice - Disclosure under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015

September 26, 2025

Further to the outcome of the meeting of the Board of Directors of Infosys held on September 11, 2025, the company enclosed a postal ballot notice dated September 25, 2025.

The approval of Infosys members was sought for the following special resolution through postal ballot, conducted only by way of remote e-voting:

| Description of Special Resolution |
|---|
| Approval for the Buyback of Equity Shares of the Company |
