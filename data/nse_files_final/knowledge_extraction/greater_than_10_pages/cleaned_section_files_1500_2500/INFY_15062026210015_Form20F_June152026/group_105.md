## 2.21 Segment reporting

IFRS 8, *Operating Segments*, establishes standards for reporting information about operating segments and related disclosures concerning products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group’s operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to enable clients to enhance business performance. The chief operating decision maker (CODM) evaluates performance and allocates resources based on various performance indicators by business segment. Segment revenue and expenditure are recorded using the accounting principles described in the accounting policies.

The Group’s primary business segments are:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment aggregates the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments represent operating segments covering businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are categorized based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Revenue for all other segments represents revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other enterprises in Public Services.

Allocated segment expenses include expenses incurred for services from the Group’s offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized based on the associated efforts of each segment. Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocable because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are separately disclosed as “unallocated” and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Management believes that providing segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities is not currently practicable because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized.

Disclosure of revenue by geographic locations is given in Note 2.11, Revenue from operations.
