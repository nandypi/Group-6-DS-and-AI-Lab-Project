## Compensation

Executive compensation programs link a significant portion of executives' total rewards to long-term corporate performance goals. The Overview of Executive Leadership Compensation is filed as an exhibit to the Annual Report on Form 20-F. The 2015 Plan and 2019 Plan were previously filed as exhibits to the Annual Report on Form 20-F.

Effective April 1, 2019, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommends to the Board the remuneration payable to directors, key managerial personnel (KMPs), and senior management, as required under the Listing Regulations.

## Independent Directors' Compensation

Compensation payable to independent directors is limited to a fixed annual amount determined and approved by the Board. The total amount does not exceed 1% of net profits for the year, calculated under the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

The Board reviews independent directors' performance annually. Factors considered in determining compensation include global board compensation benchmarks, participation in Board and Committee meetings, committee membership or chairmanship, time spent on official duties, responsibilities under Schedule IV of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, Listing Regulations, and other factors considered appropriate by the Board. Independent directors are also reimbursed for expenses incurred in performing their official duties.

#### (1) Salil Parekh Grants

The Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following fiscal 2026 grants on April 17, 2025. The grants were made effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity grant, vesting in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity ESG grant, vesting in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity TSR grant, vesting in line with the employment agreement based on the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

On January 14, 2026, the Board, based on the Committee's recommendations, granted 18,132 annual time-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective February 1, 2026. These RSUs vest in three equal annual installments after completion of each year of service from the grant date, in line with the employment agreement.

On April 17, 2025, the Board, based on the Committee's recommendations and under the 2019 Plan, approved 66,366 annual performance-based RSUs for fiscal 2026, effective May 2, 2025. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.

#### (2) Other Executive Officer Grants

During fiscal 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved time-based RSU and ESOP grants under the 2015 Plan and performance-based RSU grants under the 2019 Plan for executive officers. The grants were made during fiscal 2026.

Time-based RSUs and ESOPs granted under the 2015 Plan vest over three to four years. RSUs granted under the 2019 Plan vest over three years based on achievement of specified performance targets.

#### (3) Performance Bonus Accruals

Performance bonuses include accruals payable under the relevant employment agreements. Actual amounts may differ after completion of performance evaluations, with differences adjusted at the time of payout.

All grants were made under the 2015 Plan and 2019 Plan. The exercise price for RSUs equals the par value of the shares, while the exercise price for ESOPs is the market price on the grant date. Further details are provided in Note 2.17 of Item 18 of the Annual Report.

Compensation paid in various currencies was converted to U.S. dollars using average exchange rates for presentation in the table.
