## Some notable highlights are:

- **Launchpad:** A mobile and web-based self-service platform for global onboarding. Its AI capabilities include OCR-based data extraction and Smart Verify facial recognition, supporting identity validation and reducing manual effort.
- **Infosys Verify:** Integrated with the Talent Management System to support hiring decisions and background verification. Phase 1 of the background-check transformation uses AI-led agentic interventions for document verification report validation, Launchpad document checks, and automated workflows.
- **InfyMe:** A mobile-first self-service platform integrating more than 200 service touchpoints into one interface. It supports employee networking, collaboration, and hybrid work.
- **NAVI:** An intelligent assistant available on the InfyMe homepage that provides access to policies, data, and employee services, including leave details and team insights.

## Our NAVI-powered interventions

- **Data Assist:** A natural-language AI assistant for querying data related to leave, attendance, allocation, assets, confirmation, master data, and related information for employees and their teams.
- **Policy Advisor:** An AI assistant that interprets policy documents and personalizes responses for employees.
- **Moments That Matter (MTM):** A celebration platform covering events across the employee life cycle. Smart alerts notify employees about upcoming events, and AI can generate cards and messages for colleagues and teams.
- **Pulse Analysis:** An AI-driven employee sentiment and continuous-listening capability that supports early action on well-being and engagement. Managers and leaders can use AI tools for data access, proactive nudges, and decision-making insights.
- **Learning and Development:** Generative AI is used to provide personalized learning paths, simulate real-world scenarios, and assist with content creation. AI companions on the Lex learning platform, including Zoiee and SynthAIz, provide tutoring, summarization, and contextual learning support. AI also supports offboarding and retention analysis.

## EMPLOYEE CARE AND CONNECT; CREATING A POSITIVE WORK ENVIRONMENT

### ASHI

ASHI (Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative) supports a harassment-free workplace. The policy prohibits sexual harassment and unlawful harassment involving employees, supervisors, vendors, and clients, and applies to people involved in operating the Group. It includes periodic interventions for employees and contract staff focused on awareness and responsible action.

### HEAR

HEAR (Hearing Employees and Resolving) provides a formal review procedure when employee grievances cannot be resolved through discussions with the immediate supervisor and the supervisor’s manager. It covers managerial issues, process and policy eligibility, workplace discrimination, performance management, and interpersonal issues.

### HALE

The Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment (HALE) program is a non-monetary employee benefit based on four pillars: physical, emotional, and social well-being, and safety. Its interventions address employee health and lifestyle needs, including awareness, stress reduction, workplace safety, well-being, and productivity.

### Infosys Way of Life

The Infosys Way of Life focuses on culture in the hybrid work environment and is anchored in five cultural markers: Care, Values (C-LIFE), Collaboration, Learning, and Inclusion.

### C-LIFE

C-LIFE, as outlined in the Code of Conduct, stands for Client value, Leadership by example, Integrity & Transparency, Fairness, and Excellence. These values guide workplace conduct and company culture.

### Behavior Matters

Behavior Matters encourages employees to demonstrate positive workplace behavior.

### InfyTribes

InfyTribes is a community ecosystem operating across 12+ countries, with 30K+ members and 120K+ followers.
