## Profiles of Directors and Executive Officers

### Nandan M. Nilekani

Nandan M. Nilekani is the Chairman of Infosys Limited, which he co-founded in 1981. He is also the co-founder and Chairman of EkStep Foundation, a not-for-profit organization focused on a learner-centric, technology-based platform for improving basic literacy and numeracy.

In January 2023, he was appointed co-chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion and Development. He served as the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), with the rank of Cabinet Minister, from 2009 to 2014.

Nilekani was born in Bengaluru and holds a bachelor's degree from IIT Bombay. He is the author of *Imagining India* and co-authored *Rebooting India: Realizing a Billion Aspirations* with Viral Shah and *The Art of Bitfulness: Keeping Calm in the Digital World* with Tanuj Bhojwani, released in January 2022.

### Salil Parekh

Salil Parekh is the Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Infosys. He sets and evolves the company's strategic direction and portfolio of offerings and leads the execution through the company's leadership team.

Parekh has more than three decades of global experience in the IT services industry, including digital transformation, business turnarounds, acquisitions, and offshoring capabilities. Before joining Infosys, he was a member of the Management Board at Capgemini and a partner at Ernst & Young LLP.

At Capgemini, Parekh held leadership positions for 25 years across the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, and Asia. He oversaw a business cluster comprising Application Services in North America, the United Kingdom, and Asia; Cloud Infrastructure Services; and Sogeti. He was responsible for the strategy and execution of these businesses, including setting direction and enabling client adoption. He also chaired Capgemini's North America Executive Council and helped develop its North American growth and turnaround strategy.

At Ernst & Young, Parekh was credited with bringing scale and value to the firm's Indian operations. He holds Master of Engineering degrees in Computer Science and Mechanical Engineering from Cornell University and a Bachelor of Technology degree in Aeronautical Engineering from IIT Bombay.

Parekh serves on the board of Advance CT, an organization supporting business and innovation in Connecticut. He also serves on the board of the US-India Strategic Partnership Forum, which promotes the US-India relationship and collaboration across sectors, including technology.

### D. Sundaram

D. Sundaram is the Lead Independent Director of Infosys. He chairs the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and the Risk Management Committee. He is also a member of the Audit Committee, Stakeholder Relationship Committee, and Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee.

His experience includes corporate finance, business performance, operational monitoring, governance, mergers and acquisitions, talent and people management, and strategy.

Sundaram joined Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) as a management trainee in June 1975 and held several positions, including:

- Commercial Officer for Africa and the Middle East at Unilever, London, from 1990 to 1993.
- Senior Vice President for South Asia and the Middle East from 1996 to 1999.
- Chief Finance Officer of HUL from April 1999 to March 2008.
- Vice Chairman and Chief Finance Officer of HUL from April 2008 to July 2009.

He served as Vice Chairman and Managing Director of TVS Capital Funds, a growth capital private equity fund. He currently serves as an independent director on the boards of Crompton Greaves Consumer and Schneider Electric Infrastructure.

Sundaram served as an independent director on the board of State Bank of India from January 2009 to June 2014 and on the board of SBI Capital Markets from 2002 to 2014. He has also served as a member of the boards of governors of the Institute of Financial Management and Research and KREA University, Andhra Pradesh.

Sundaram holds a postgraduate degree in Management Studies, is a Fellow of the Institute of Cost Accountants, and completed Harvard Business School's Advanced Management Program.
