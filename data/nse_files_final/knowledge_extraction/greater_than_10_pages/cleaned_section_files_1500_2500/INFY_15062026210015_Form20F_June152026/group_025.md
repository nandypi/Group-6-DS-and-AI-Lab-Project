## Infosys Cobalt

Infosys Cobalt is a comprehensive suite of services, platforms, and solutions for cloud-powered and AI-native enterprise transformation. It supports organizations in adopting cloud, data, and AI in a cohesive, scalable, and secure manner, with a focus on sustainable growth, operational resilience, architectural consistency, regulatory compliance, and economic discipline.

Infosys Cobalt provides enterprise-grade cloud and data foundations for scaling advanced analytics, enterprise AI, and intelligent automation across business functions. By embedding AI across the transformation lifecycle—from strategy and architecture through modernization and continuous optimization—it supports the development of production-grade AI programs and measurable business outcomes.

A key element of Infosys Cobalt is Fabric for Cloud. It unifies cloud capabilities across the enterprise across the cloud lifecycle, including discovery, architecture, migration, operations, optimization, and governance. It applies intelligent reasoning, pattern recognition, and automation to architecture design, modernization, reliability, cost optimization, and compliance. This supports repeatable cloud operating models across portfolios, geographies, and business units.

Infosys Cobalt uses a consulting-led approach to reimagine business and operating models. Consulting-driven discovery and strategy address operating models, technology landscapes, and value streams, aligning business objectives with cloud and AI execution. The approach integrates cloud, AI, and data into business processes while incorporating security, compliance, governance, and sustainability.

Infosys Cobalt combines cloud, AI, data, and industry expertise to support the development of adaptive and resilient products, services, and operating models across business functions and geographies.

## Infosys Topaz

Infosys Topaz is an AI-augmented set of services and solution assets designed to apply AI and Agentic AI across Infosys service lines. It uses a poly-AI approach, supported by blueprints, playbooks, and the Infosys Topaz Responsible AI framework.

Infosys Topaz supports AI talent transformation, skills development, and competency building aligned with its AI-first value framework. It is used to reimagine work, workplaces, and workforces, with objectives including business agility and improved customer experience.

Infosys is applying an AI-first approach to its own transformation, with Agentic AI used in internal implementations and client solutions. Through Infosys Topaz Fabric, it is developing specialized AI agents intended to improve productivity, client service, business processes, and enterprise transformation.

The Infosys Topaz Center of Excellence has supported the industrialization of AI across client ecosystems and delivered Generative AI engagements. Several agent-based solutions have been deployed in production. Infosys has also strengthened partnerships with AI-native companies, alliances with hyperscalers, and collaborations with industry startups. The company reported a rising number of AI patents filed during the year and was recognized by industry analysts as a leader in AI and Generative AI.

## (3) Products & Platforms

## Infosys Finacle

Finacle is a digital banking platform and SaaS offering. Its next-generation, AI-powered platform is composable and cloud-native, supporting banking engagement, innovation, operations, and transformation.

Finacle’s AI stack includes Model Context Protocol, MLOps, low-code/no-code, and design-to-code capabilities across core banking, digital engagement, lending, payments, cash management, wealth management, treasury, revenue management, and blockchain.

Finacle uses a Data-to-AI approach to support AI adoption at scale, smarter decisions, contextual offerings, and customer experiences. It is used in more than 100 countries and supports more than one billion people and millions of businesses with saving, payment, borrowing, and investment services.

## Infosys Equinox

Infosys Equinox is an AI-first, composable commerce platform designed to provide intelligent, autonomous, and personalized commerce experiences across channels, customers, and AI agents.

The platform uses a MACH X architecture comprising microservices, API-first design, cloud-native capabilities, headless architecture, and extensibility. It embeds domain-trained AI, decisioning, and agent orchestration across the commerce lifecycle, including product discovery, pricing, fulfillment, and customer service.

Its modular design, partner ecosystem, and commerce-as-a-service operating models allow enterprises to begin with selected capabilities, adapt continuously, and scale commerce with governance.

## Infosys Helix

Infosys Helix is a cloud-native, AI-first platform for U.S. healthcare payers. It is a modular and composable suite designed to modernize core administrative functions, including enrollment, claims, and billing, without disrupting care delivery.

The platform uses machine learning and Generative AI in reactive and agentic processes to support accuracy and turnaround times in business transactions. Infosys Helix is offered through Software as a Service (SaaS) and Business Process as a Service (BPaaS) models, covering IT and business operations for payer organizations.

## Infosys McCamish

Infosys McCamish provides platform-based insurance process management solutions and services across insurance products, distribution models, and deployment options.

Core offerings cover:

- Individual life insurance and annuities
- Employer-sponsored benefits and retirement
- Producer management and compensation

Infosys McCamish provides the VPAS Platform through license and SaaS engagements. It also provides end-to-end business process management policy administration services on the VPAS Platform, including SaaS-BPM hybrid engagements.

## Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys Topaz Fabric is an agentic services suite that brings together AI capabilities across infrastructure, AI models, enterprise data, applications, and workflows. It is interoperable with leading AI tools, platforms, and models, allowing enterprises to remain model-agnostic and build on existing AI investments.

Topaz Fabric supports AI adoption, service resilience, and speed of change across greenfield and brownfield transformations. It includes blueprints, design patterns, orchestration capabilities, and runtime layers for agent-native service delivery.

Infosys Topaz Fabric focuses on five areas:

- Multilayer AI capabilities that orchestrate infrastructure, models, data, applications, and workflows.
- Prepackaged journeys and agentic service suites aligned with Infosys service offerings.
- Enterprise application adapters and Media Transfer Protocol (MTP) servers that connect modern and legacy applications to agentic environments.
- An enterprise knowledge fabric combining structured organizational data with organizational knowledge.
- Hybrid intelligence combining frontier large language models with specialized small language models developed for the service ecosystem.

Topaz Fabric is intended to help organizations adopt AI using existing organizational practices, ready-made patterns, connectors, and generic blueprints. It focuses on orchestration rather than replacement and supports legacy applications, modern applications, and AI-native tools without requiring technology lock-in.

Topaz Fabric includes built-in controls, human handoffs, and enterprise-grade processes intended to support accountability and compliance. It embeds AI into user and customer workflows across the applications and platforms they use.

## Infosys Topaz AI Next

Infosys Topaz AI Next, formerly EdgeVerve AI Next, is a unified AI platform for AI-driven orchestration of complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across humans, AI agents, and enterprise systems.

Aligned with Infosys Topaz, the platform combines EdgeVerve’s AI platform capabilities with Infosys’ enterprise-grade AI-first offerings. It provides workflow and decisioning orchestration supported by governance and lifecycle management.

Topaz AI Next is designed to support enterprise transformation by combining EdgeVerve’s product capabilities with Infosys’ global scale across enterprise operations and core business functions.
