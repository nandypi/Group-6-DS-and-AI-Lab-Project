## Overview

Infosys Limited provides AI-first business consulting and technology services to help organizations unlock AI value at scale. With more than four decades of experience managing the systems and operations of global enterprises, Infosys supports business transformation through its AI-first value framework, domain expertise, and ability to coordinate innovations from its AI-native partner ecosystem.

Infosys’s strategy is to act as a navigator for clients as they ideate, plan, and execute their transition to an AI-first future. This includes building strong data foundations and robust cloud capabilities. Infosys also emphasizes safeguards for privacy, ethics, and controls through responsible AI practices, both within the Group and across the industry.

Infosys’s stated strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities in which it operates.

Infosys’s core values are Client Value, Leadership by Example, Integrity and Transparency, Fairness, and Excellence (“C-LIFE”).

Beginning in 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy to strengthen its relevance with clients and drive accelerated value creation:

1. Scale Agile Digital
2. Energize the core
3. Reskill our people
4. Expand localization
