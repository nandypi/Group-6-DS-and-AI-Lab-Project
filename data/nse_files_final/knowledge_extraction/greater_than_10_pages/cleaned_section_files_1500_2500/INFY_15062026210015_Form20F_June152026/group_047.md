## RECRUITMENT

As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 328,594 employees, including 310,887 professionals involved in service delivery to clients, including trainees.

Infosys recruits across IT specializations through relationships with institutions globally. Its HackWithInfy online coding contest supports recruitment of coders, and the InfyTQ platform extends its Mysuru training content to learners across India.

Infosys uses a hybrid model combining virtual and in-person recruitment and has introduced artificial intelligence into its recruitment process. During fiscal 2026, Infosys received 5,884,480 employment applications, interviewed 450,901 applicants, and extended offers of employment to 87,286 applicants. These statistics exclude subsidiaries.

## EDUCATION, TRAINING AND ASSESSMENT (ETA)

Infosys uses artificial intelligence to personalize learning across its education and training offerings. These efforts received external recognition from Brandon Hall and ATD Best.

The Foundation Training Program operates across India and multiple other countries. It includes more than 40 technology streams and incorporates generative artificial intelligence and prompt engineering courses. In FY26, 10,766 fresh graduates completed training at the Global Education Center campus in Mysore and were released to delivery units.

The Continuous Education Program provides reskilling and upskilling through online self-learning, instructor-led virtual training, and in-person classroom training. Bridge programs provide training and internship opportunities for employees moving into new career fields. In FY26, 81.8% of the workforce received training, with average annual employee learning hours of 113.3 hours. Cumulatively, 84% of the workforce was AI-aware.

Lex, Infosys' in-house learning platform, offers more than 40,000 curated courses, including more than 30,000 courses procured from vendor partners. On average, 37,000 employees used Lex on weekdays and approximately 8,000 used it on weekends, with average learning time of 39 minutes.

Infosys Wingspan is a configurable talent transformation platform used by several global organizations for talent transformation programs.

Infosys Springboard offers more than 20,000 courses. Approximately 11 million learners worldwide actively consume its content.

## INFOSYS LEADERSHIP INSTITUTE

The Infosys Leadership Institute focuses on leadership capabilities aligned with organizational strategy and on preparing Infosys and its leaders for an AI-infused future. Its four strategic areas are:

- Succession planning
- Strengthening leadership bench strength
- Leadership capability development
- Inclusive leadership

During the year, the Infosys Leadership Institute delivered more than 10,000 leadership learning days, doubled average learning days per senior leader, and conducted close to 2,000 personalized one-on-one advisory conversations. Its reported experience scores included a Net Promoter Score of more than 96%, and it received top-unit internal partner rankings for three consecutive years.

## Visas

As of March 31, 2026, less than 50% of Infosys employees in the United States held either H-1B or L-1 visas. H-1B visas allow employees to remain in the United States for up to six years as specialty occupation professionals. L-1 visas allow employees to remain for up to five years as specialized knowledge experts or up to seven years as managers and executives.

## Option plans

## 2015 Plan

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of Infosys and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan is 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The instruments generally vest over four years. The plan numbers were further adjusted in connection with the September 2018 bonus issue.

The controlled trust held 8,650,911 shares as of March 31, 2026, and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each date.
