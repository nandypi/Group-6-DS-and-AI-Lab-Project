## RETURN TO OFFICE AND HYBRID MODEL OF WORK

Infosys has operated a hybrid work model for more than five years. Employees may work across campuses, delivery centers, satellite locations, and remote environments, with flexible working hours based on role requirements, team context, and business needs.

Approximately 10% of employees work fully remotely, 11% work entirely from the office, and 79% work in a hybrid manner.

## Structured approach to enabling hybrid work culture:

Infosys organizes its hybrid work model around three pillars: work, workspace, and workforce. The model is aligned across enterprise, unit, and Development Center (DC) levels and with regional regulations.

Key enablers include:

- **Manager-led enablement:** Managers receive data-driven insights to support fair workload distribution, identify employees requiring support, and maintain team effectiveness.
- **Work-hour and wellbeing monitoring:** Monitoring of work patterns helps identify excessive workloads and mitigate burnout risks.
- **Equity in experience:** Infosys focuses on equitable work distribution and consistent employee experiences across work modes.

## Initiatives to enhance our Employee Value Proposition (EVP)

Infosys' Employee Value Proposition focuses on meaningful work, employee learning and career growth, and opportunities for employees to navigate different career paths.

## EMPLOYEE CAREERS & LEARNING AVENUES

- **The Infosys Skill-based Organization:** A skill journey defines, captures, and assesses skills and translates them into employee identity and deployment. The process is linked to the talent lifecycle to support career growth and organizational agility.
- **Infosys Skills Council:** The Council brings together business, technology, and HR leaders to shape the company's skills and capability agenda, establish future skill priorities, align business demand and client expectations with learning investments, and support workforce readiness.
- **Skill Tags Program:** Skill Tags identifies and recognizes proficiency across key technologies through differentiated badges. The program includes curated courses, certifications, and choice-based real digital projects. Infosys reports more than 10,000 niche, 22,000 premium, and 56,000 standard skill tag holders.
- **Skill Squad Coaching Ecosystem:** Launched in July 2025, this internal coaching model uses in-house expertise for peer-to-peer coaching, with platform-enabled tracking, recognition, and rewards.
- **Work profiles:** A Work Profile in Demand and Deployment combines Technology, Domain, Foundational, and Social skills and represents granular skill jobs used by a service line in client work. The concept was launched as a pilot in one service line.
- **Lex:** Infosys' in-house learning platform supports talent development and hybrid learning models.
- **Learning and Career:** This portal supports employees' learning and career needs and integrates with internal systems such as Lex.
- **Internal Marketplace:** The Internal Marketplace matches employees who are acquiring new skills with opportunities for job rotation in areas aligned with their interests and capabilities.
- **Internal Job Movements:** This platform connects employees with internal job opportunities and supports career movements and promotions.
- **Holistic Skill building & Career pathing:** Infosys defines the Technology, Domain, Foundational, and Social skills needed for current and future roles. Career Canvas enables employees to select desired roles and follow skill-based learning paths.
- **Bridge Programs:** These programs help employees develop new skills and move into careers that typically require different qualifications.
- **Accelerate:** Accelerate provides exposure to different roles and practical experience through short-term internal projects. The platform allows job creators to publish independent job modules for job-seeking colleagues.
- **Platinum Club:** This is an exclusive program for highly skilled and high-performing employees, providing diverse career experiences to qualifying participants.
- **Manager and leadership development:** The Global Delivery Talent Effectiveness program includes external sessions, internal leader interactions, mentoring, cross-skilling, best-practice sharing, and collaboration across business segments and units.
- **Capability Quotient (CQ):** CQ measures and builds capability across Technology, Domain, Foundational, and Social dimensions. It supports skill-based career discussions, career development, and internal mobility. The framework was rolled out in a pilot covering 7,500 employees.
- **Quarterly Check-ins & Performance Management Evolution:** Infosys uses an integrated model linking skill identification, proficiency assessment, and quarterly conversations. These check-ins support feedback, progress tracking, expectation alignment, coaching, removal of blockers, current-role fitment, next-role readiness, and career-path upskilling.
- **Sales Capability Building:** Sales Transformation programs include:
  - **ZENITH CLUB:** A high-performer recognition and reward program in Global Sales.
  - **SYNERGY:** A four-week onboarding program for new sales employees.
  - An eight-week training module covering practical applications of AI in business and responsible AI.
  - **ELEVATE:** A talent mobility platform that provides business leaders with candidates across Global Markets.
  - **INFUSION ASCENT:** An in-person development platform for new title holders, featuring Infosys leaders, external trainers, and client leaders.
  - **ASSURED:** A sales leadership program conducted in partnership with Stanford to strengthen leaders' financial acumen.
  - **SAPIENCE:** A program supporting women in sales through leadership interaction, professional learning, relationship building, and skills development.

## Talent management at Infosys: Evolving with AI

Infosys is integrating AI-assisted and AI-augmented processes into talent management while retaining human oversight and accountability. AI-assisted processes support faster, more consistent, and more transparent routine talent operations. AI-augmented systems provide leaders and managers with visibility into skills, potential, and capability-development needs.

Infosys states that human judgment, empathy, and ownership remain central. AI is being used to support performance conversations, talent movement, succession planning, and development journeys, with governance and human oversight.

## EMPLOYEE EXPERIENCE : AI in Employee Experience

Infosys is integrating AI across the talent lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, continuous learning, and offboarding. The company states that its approach emphasizes responsibility, scalability, long-term value creation, trust, transparency, and alignment with its ESG commitments across governance, people, and technology stewardship. AI solutions are intended to streamline processes and support employees at key points in the employee lifecycle.
