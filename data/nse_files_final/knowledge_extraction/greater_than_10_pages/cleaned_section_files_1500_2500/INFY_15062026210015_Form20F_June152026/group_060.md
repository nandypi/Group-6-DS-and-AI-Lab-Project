- Long-term capital gains realized by a resident from the transfer of ADSs are subject to tax at 10%, plus applicable surcharge and health and education cess. For transfers on or after July 23, 2024, the rate is 12.5%, plus applicable surcharge and health and education cess. Short-term capital gains are taxed at graduated rates, with a maximum rate of 30%, plus applicable surcharge and health and education cess.

- Long-term capital gains realized by a non-resident on the sale of equity shares obtained through conversion of ADSs are subject to tax at 10%, plus applicable surcharge and health and education cess. For transfers on or after July 23, 2024, the rate is 12.5%, plus applicable surcharge and health and education cess. Short-term capital gains are taxed at the rate applicable to the seller.

- The Finance Act, 2018 imposed a 10% tax on long-term capital gains exceeding ₹100,000 from the sale of equity shares in a company, units of an equity-oriented fund, or units of a business trust, subject to Securities Transaction Tax (STT) being paid at the time of purchase and sale. These gains do not receive the benefit of indexation. The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 increased the threshold from ₹100,000 to ₹125,000.

| Transfer takes place | Tax rate on long-term capital gains exceeding ₹125,000 |
|---|---:|
| Before July 23, 2024 | 10% |
| On or after July 23, 2024 | 12.5% |

For investments made on or before January 31, 2018, the cost of acquisition is deemed to be the higher of:

1. The actual cost of acquisition; and
2. The lower of:
   - The fair market value of the investment as of January 31, 2018; and
   - The full value of consideration received or accruing from the transfer, namely, the sale price.

For assets listed as of January 31, 2018, fair market value is the highest price quoted on a recognized stock exchange on that date. If the asset was not traded on that stock exchange on January 31, 2018, the highest price on the immediately preceding trading day is used.

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 amended the tax rates for short-term capital gains on equity shares in a company, units of an equity-oriented fund, or units of a business trust, subject to STT being paid at the time of sale.

| Transfer takes place | Tax rate |
|---|---:|
| Before July 23, 2024 | 15% |
| On or after July 23, 2024 | 20% |

Applicable surcharge rates are:

| Total taxable income range | Domestic company | Foreign company |
|---|---:|---:|
| More than ₹10,000,000 but not more than ₹100,000,000 | 7% | 2% |
| More than ₹100,000,000 | 12% | 5% |

Taxes and applicable surcharge are increased by a health and education cess of 4%.

Securities Transaction Tax (STT) is levied on the sale and purchase of equity shares and derivatives on a recognized stock exchange.

| Type of transaction | Rate of STT before April 1, 2026 | Rate of STT on or after April 1, 2026 | Levied on |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sale of equity shares (delivery based) | 0.1% of the transaction value of securities | 0.1% of the transaction value of securities | Both buyer and seller |
| Sale of equity shares (non-delivery based) | 0.025% of the transaction value of securities | 0.025% of the transaction value of securities | Seller |
| Sale of option in securities | 0.0625% of the option premium | 0.15% of the option premium | Seller |
| Sale of option in securities where the option is exercised | 0.125% of the intrinsic value, defined as settlement price minus strike price | 0.15% of the intrinsic value, defined as settlement price minus strike price | Buyer |
| Sale of futures in securities | 0.0125% of transaction value | 0.05% of transaction value | Seller |

Taxes on capital gains arising from these transactions may be offset by applicable credit mechanisms under double tax avoidance agreements.

Capital gains tax is computed by applying the appropriate tax rate to the difference between the sale price and the purchase price of the ADSs or equity shares.

Under the Scheme, the purchase price of equity shares in an Indian listed company received in exchange for ADSs is the market price of the underlying shares on the date the Depositary gives notice to the custodian of delivery of the equity shares in exchange for the corresponding ADSs, or the “stepped-up” basis purchase price. The market price is the prevailing price of the equity shares on the BSE or NSE, as applicable.

There is no corresponding provision under the Income Tax Act, 1961, concerning the stepped-up basis for the purchase price of equity shares. The tax department in India has not, to the company’s knowledge, denied this benefit. If the tax department denies the benefit, the original purchase price of the ADSs would be treated as the purchase price for computing capital gains tax.

Under the Scheme, a non-resident holder’s holding period for determining the applicable Indian capital gains tax rate on equity shares received in exchange for ADSs begins on the date the Depositary gives notice of redemption to the custodian. The Scheme does not address this issue for resident employees; therefore, the start date of the holding period for such employees is unclear.

### Withholding Tax on Capital Gains

Any taxable gain realized by a non-resident on the sale of ADSs or equity shares is subject to withholding by the buyer.

Under Section 196C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, where interest, dividends, or long-term capital gains from the transfer of bonds or global depository receipts referred to in Section 115AC are payable to a non-resident, tax must be deducted when the income is credited or paid, whichever occurs earlier. The applicable rate is 10%, subject to any concessional rate available under the relevant tax treaty, read with the applicable Multilateral Instrument (MLI).

For long-term capital gains from transfers on or after July 23, 2024, tax is deducted at 12.5%, subject to any concessional rate available under the relevant tax treaty, read with the applicable MLI. To claim a concessional treaty rate, non-resident shareholders must provide required tax forms, including a tax residency certificate.

Under Section 196D(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, no withholding tax is required on capital gains of Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), as defined in Section 115AD, arising from the transfer of securities covered by Section 115AD.
