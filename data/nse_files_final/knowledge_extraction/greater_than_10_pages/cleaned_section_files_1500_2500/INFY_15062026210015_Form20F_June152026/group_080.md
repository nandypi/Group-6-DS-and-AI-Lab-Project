## Financial risk management

## Financial risk factors

The Group's activities expose it to market risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk. The primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. The Group uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange exposures. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of each customer and the concentration of risk among the top few customers.

## Market risk

The Group operates internationally, with a major portion of its business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers in various foreign currencies, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies.

The Group holds foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates. Changes in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and foreign currencies can affect the Group's operating results.

## Derivative financial instruments

The Group primarily holds foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates on foreign currency exposures. The counterparty for such contracts is generally a bank. These instruments are valued using quoted prices for similar assets and liabilities in active markets or inputs that are directly or indirectly observable in the marketplace.
