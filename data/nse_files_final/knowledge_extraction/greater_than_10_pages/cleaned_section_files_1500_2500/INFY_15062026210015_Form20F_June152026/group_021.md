## Application Development Capabilities

Infosys’ Application Development offerings help clients build and evolve software designed to be AI-native. They combine engineering expertise, domain and enterprise context, consulting capabilities, reusable intellectual property, and embedded AI across the application lifecycle from discovery to deployment.

The approach includes developer copilots, pre-built Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) agents, AI-native architecture, agentic workflows, and context-aware data foundations. The stated objectives are to accelerate delivery, improve quality, and reduce operational friction.

The offerings are built on a cloud-agnostic, secure-by-design, product-centric foundation. They include:

- AI-native product and platform engineering
- Intelligent business process reengineering
- Industry-specific solutions
- Vertical package rollouts

Infosys has embedded AI throughout its software development lifecycle and uses solutions including GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, and Devin. Its proprietary capabilities include the Infosys Topaz Fabric platform for persona-based SDLC assistance, the Infosys Agent Control Framework for deterministic outcomes and agentic guardrails, and custom AI models and transformers.

The Application Development offerings are supported by an integrated Domain Consulting Group (DCG), which embeds industry context into AI-native solution design and delivery. DCG provides domain-led consulting, thought leadership, AI-assisted solution blueprinting, reusable solution patterns, and reference architectures.

DCG has developed AI-native blueprints across 23 industry segments, aligned with segment-specific priorities, regulatory needs, and value drivers.

## Application Management Capabilities

Infosys’ Application Management services focus on optimizing the cost and efficiency of application operations, strengthening business service resilience, improving business experience, and supporting technological innovation.

The services are being advanced through agentic AI and a productized service delivery model that combines AI and human process blueprints, Infosys Topaz Fabric, and partner tools. The model is intended to improve productivity and support consistent, data-driven service delivery.

Infosys has established a repository of pre-built, ready-to-deploy AI agents using its proprietary agentic framework. These agents orchestrate application operations workflows by integrating automation bots and conversational AI to support self-healing and predictive application operations. The agents are tailored to individual business needs using codified expertise and client enterprise context.

Application Management capabilities include:

- Proactive and predictive monitoring of critical business processes
- Application portfolio analysis
- Technical debt management
- Cloud adoption support
- Security risk mitigation
- Automation and conversational AI for application operations

Infosys also uses strategic partnerships with AI companies and hyperscalers to integrate AI advancements into its tools and service offerings.

## Application Modernization Capabilities

Infosys’ application modernization offerings help enterprises transform legacy IT environments, reduce technical debt, improve resilience, and respond to changing end-user expectations and business priorities while minimizing disruption to ongoing operations.

The offerings use an AI-first, platform-led approach and cover the application modernization lifecycle across:

- Mainframes and other legacy technologies
- Databases
- Monolithic architectures
- Cloud platforms
- Low-code and no-code ecosystems
- Industry-specific solutions across major verticals

The modernization approach focuses on security and supports the transition of data and application estates toward AI- and cloud-driven environments.

Infosys Topaz Fabric supports modernization and development through end-to-end SDLC automation, guided workflows, and AI-enabled tools. By abstracting underlying technology complexity, it is intended to improve developer productivity, provide insights from legacy systems, reduce dependence on niche skills, and enable faster and more predictable delivery. Integration with enterprise application lifecycle management (ALM) tools supports sprint velocity, release predictability, and product quality.

## Infosys Quality Engineering Services

Infosys Quality Engineering (IQE) provides AI-first, autonomous, and outcome-driven quality engineering services for digital systems operating across AI, cloud, and continuous transformation environments.

IQE combines quality engineering, responsible AI practices, scalable platforms, domain context, and ecosystem partnerships. Its stated focus includes accelerating speed-to-value, reducing operational risk, and supporting measurable business outcomes as software systems become increasingly AI-driven and autonomous.

### AI-Native Quality Engineering

IQE uses agent-driven automation to plan, design, execute, optimize, and self-heal testing workflows. This approach is intended to improve productivity, throughput, and delivery predictability across complex digital environments.

### AI Trust, Assurance and Governance

IQE provides assurance for large language models, domain-specific models, and agentic workflows. Its capabilities address risks related to:

- Bias
- Explainability
- Security
- Regulatory compliance
- Model drift

### Data as the Quality Foundation for AI

IQE supports AI data foundations through:

- Data engineering
- Synthetic data generation
- Data ecosystem assurance
- Validation of AI data pipelines
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) data validation
- Vector database validation

These capabilities support the use of high-quality, representative, and compliant data for training, testing, and monitoring AI systems.

### People and Capability

Infosys continues to invest in a reskilled workforce combining domain expertise with capabilities in AI engineering, agentic systems, data, and responsible AI.

IQE is supported by the Infosys AI value framework, strategic alliances, talent transformation initiatives, and platform-led delivery models.

## Data, Analytics &amp; AI

Infosys Data, Analytics & AI (DNA) provides services covering data, advanced analytics, decision intelligence, AI, and governance. Its services use Infosys Topaz solution assets, hyperscalers, and an AI partner ecosystem.

DNA’s stated focus is on enterprise AI in which intelligence is embedded across decisions, workflows, processes, systems, experiences, and interactions. Its agentic AI capabilities extend beyond generating insights to autonomous business orchestration, with domain-aware AI agents operating using enterprise knowledge and context.

DNA helps clients build portfolios of AI initiatives linked to measurable outcomes, including:

- Revenue uplift
- Margin expansion
- Reduction in cost to serve
- Cycle-time compression
- Experience improvement

These initiatives are industrialized through an operating model spanning data, machine learning (ML), large language models (LLMs), agentic AI, AI applications, and governance.

DNA also uses Infosys Topaz Fabric to create an “enterprise brain” by codifying institutional expertise into governed semantic and knowledge layers. These layers connect policies, systems, data, processes, domain semantics, and real-time signals so that AI agents can operate with contextual awareness.

## Key Offerings

### a. Business Value Management

Business Value Management links AI transformation to measurable outcomes and revenue-related metrics. Through a value office model, Infosys:

- Defines value hypotheses
- Builds and prioritizes AI opportunity portfolios
- Sets adoption key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Tracks realized benefits across productivity, cycle time, cost to serve, experience, and revenue influence

Benchmark-driven diagnostics and industry value frameworks are used to align AI investments with business priorities and value realization.

### b. Data for AI

Data for AI establishes an AI-ready data fabric by unifying disparate sources and extracting knowledge from structured and unstructured data. It provides AI-grade engineering for curated, multimodal data products.

Capabilities include:

- Intelligent cataloguing and harmonization
- Domain ontologies
- Knowledge graphs
- Data lineage
- Security and lifecycle governance
- Master data management (MDM)
- Data consumption and decisioning
- Semantic standardization

The resulting AI data fabric supports AI and agentic business systems.

### c. Modernization.ai

Modernization.ai uses AI-augmented engineering to accelerate cloud adoption, reduce technical debt, and simplify complex technology and data environments with minimal disruption.

Its capabilities include:

- Intelligent code understanding
- Automated refactoring
- Cloud-native patterns
- Platform modernization
- Estate rationalization and consolidation

The offering is intended to support a more agile and cost-efficient digital core, scalable data products, and agentic workflows.
