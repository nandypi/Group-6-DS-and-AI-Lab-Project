# Unpublished Price Sensitive Information

Information concerning the company or its securities, directly or indirectly, that is not generally available and that, upon becoming generally available, is likely to materially affect the price of the securities includes, ordinarily but not exclusively:

- Financial results and guidance.
- Dividends.
- Changes in capital structure.
- Mergers, de-mergers, acquisitions, delisting, disposals, and expansion of business.
- Awards or terminations of orders or contracts outside the normal course of business and other similar transactions.
- Changes in key managerial personnel (KMP), as defined under the Companies Act, 2013, other than changes due to superannuation or expiry of term.
- Resignation of a statutory auditor or secretarial auditor.
- Changes in ratings other than ESG ratings.
- Proposed fundraising.
- Agreements, by whatever name called, that affect the management and control of the company.
- Fraud or defaults by the company, its promoter, director, KMP, or subsidiary, or the arrest of a KMP, promoter, or director, whether in India or abroad.
- Resolution plans, restructuring, or one-time settlements relating to loans or borrowings from banks or financial institutions.
- Admission of a winding-up petition filed by any party or creditor.
- Admission by the Tribunal of an application filed by the corporate applicant or financial creditors to initiate the corporate insolvency resolution process against the company as a corporate debtor.
- Approval or rejection of a resolution plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
- Initiation of a forensic audit, by whatever name called, by the company or any other entity to detect misstatement in financials, misappropriation, siphoning, or diversion of funds, and receipt of the final forensic audit report.
- Actions initiated or orders passed in India or abroad by any regulatory, statutory, enforcement, or judicial body against the listed entity, its directors, KMP, senior management, promoter, or subsidiary in relation to the listed entity.
- Outcomes of litigation or disputes that may affect the listed entity.
- Giving guarantees or indemnities, or becoming a surety, by whatever name called, for a third party outside the normal course of business.
- Granting, withdrawal, surrender, cancellation, or suspension of key licenses or regulatory approvals.
- Other matters specified under SEBI regulations or decided by the company from time to time.

**Explanation 1 — For the purpose of fraud and default disclosures:**

- **Fraud** has the same meaning as in Regulation 2(1)(c) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003.
- **Default** has the same meaning as in Clause 6 of Paragraph A of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (the “Listing Regulations”).

**Explanation 2 — Identification of unpublished price sensitive information:**

The guidelines for materiality referred to in Paragraph A of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, as specified by the Board from time to time, and the materiality provisions referred to in Paragraph B of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations apply when identifying the events listed in this clause as unpublished price sensitive information.

Information remains non-public or unpublished until it has been widely disseminated to the public, for example through a filing with the NSE, BSE, NYSE, or SEC, a press conference, or a release, or until it is accessible to the public on a non-discriminatory basis.

Words and expressions used but not defined in this code, and defined in the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, the Depositories Act, 1996, or the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules and regulations made under those laws, have the meanings assigned to them in those legislations.
