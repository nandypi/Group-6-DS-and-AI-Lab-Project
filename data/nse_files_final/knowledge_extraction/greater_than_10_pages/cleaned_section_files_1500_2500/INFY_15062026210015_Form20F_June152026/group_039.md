Michael Gibbs is an Independent Director of Infosys. He is Chairperson of the Stakeholders Relationship Committee and the Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee. He is also a member of the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Corporate Social Responsibility Committee.

Gibbs was formerly Group CIO of BP plc, where he was responsible for the company’s IT strategy and worldwide computing and telecommunications services. At BP, he led an IT-function transformation, reorganized the operating model, improved cybersecurity, supported the adoption of emerging digital technologies, and developed plans to migrate legacy data centers to the cloud. He previously served as CIO for Conoco Refining & Marketing, Europe and Asia, and ConocoPhillips Supply and Trading, Corporate Functions and Global Downstream. He joined BP as VP/CIO, Refining & Marketing, in 2008 and became Group CIO in 2013.

Gibbs has a degree in Management Science from Oklahoma State University, completed the Executive Management Program at Penn State University in 1997, and completed the Concours/Cash CIO Leadership Program in 2004.

Bobby Parikh is an Independent Director of Infosys. He is Chairperson of the Audit Committee and a member of the Stakeholders Relationship Committee and Risk Management Committee.

Parikh is Managing Partner of Bobby Parikh Associates, which provides strategic tax and regulatory advisory services. His experience includes advising Indian and multinational businesses on India’s policy framework, entry strategies, business-model identification, business structuring, mergers and acquisitions, and other reorganizations. He advises on inbound, outbound, and domestic transactions and works with private equity funds, institutional investors, business owners, and managers.

Parikh was co-founder of BMR Advisors, which he helped establish and run for more than 12 years. He was previously Chief Executive Officer of Ernst & Young in India until December 2003 and worked at Arthur Andersen for more than 17 years, including as Country Managing Partner. He led the Financial Services industry practice at Arthur Andersen and Ernst & Young.

Parikh graduated in Commerce from the University of Mumbai and qualified as a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India in 1987.

Chitra Nayak is an Independent Director of Infosys. She is Chairperson of the Environment, Social and Governance Committee and a member of the Risk Management Committee, Stakeholders Relationship Committee, Corporate Social Responsibility Committee, and Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee.

Nayak has more than 25 years of experience in go-to-market, general management, and operations leadership. She is a board member of Vimo, a healthcare-services access technology platform. She previously served on the boards of Invitae, LifeWorks, Forward Air, and Intercom. She advises startups on go-to-market strategies and is an Executive Advisor at BCG.

Nayak was Chief Operating Officer at Comfy and Funding Circle. She previously held leadership roles at Salesforce, including COO, Platform, and Senior Vice President, Global Sales Development. She also worked at AAA, Charles Schwab, and Boston Consulting Group.

Nayak co-founded Neythri.org and the Salesforce Women’s Network initiative. She holds an MBA with Honors from Harvard Business School, an MS in Environmental Engineering from Cornell University, and a B.Tech in Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology.

Govind Iyer is an Independent Director of Infosys. He is Chairperson of the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee and a member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Environment, Social and Governance Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee.

Iyer retired as a Partner at Egon Zehnder. He previously worked at Procter & Gamble, Coca-Cola, and Heinz. He has served on the Wharton Executive Education Advisory Board and the Advisory Board of the Asian Venture Philanthropy Network. He is a founding board member and Chairperson of Social Venture Partners in India, a member of the Board of Trustees of the Rockefeller Foundation, a Governing Board member of Ashoka University, and a board member of Karmayogi Bharat.

Iyer holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree from Regional Engineering College, Trichy, and an MBA from The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania.

Helene Auriol Potier is an Independent Director of Infosys. She is a member of the Environment, Social and Governance Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Cybersecurity Sub-Committee.

Potier has held senior leadership roles in digital technologies and telecommunications across the United States, Europe, Africa, and Asia. She spent 15 years at Nortel Networks Corporation, including as Vice President, Sales, Mobile Division Worldwide, and Vice President, Services & Operations, EMEA. She was CEO of Africa, Mediterranean and CEE at Dell Inc. and held several leadership roles at Microsoft, including CEO of Microsoft Singapore and Managing Director, Artificial Intelligence Europe. From November 2018 to December 2020, she was Executive Vice-President, International Business, at Orange.

Potier previously served as an independent director of Safran SA, Mimecast Limited, Ispen S.A., and Faiveley Transport S.A. She currently serves as an independent non-executive director of Accor S.A., Randstad N.V., and Oddo BHF S.C.A. She chairs the Accor board ESG committee, Randstad Technology Committee, and Oddo BHF board compensation committee.

Potier holds a Master of Science in Engineering from Telecom Paris and an Executive MBA from INSEAD.

Nitin Paranjpe is an Independent Director of Infosys and was appointed Vice-Chairman of Infosys Limited effective April 30, 2026. He is a member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Audit Committee.

Paranjpe is Non-Executive Chairman of Hindustan Unilever Limited and a member of the Supervisory Board of Heineken NV. He previously served on the Unilever Leadership Executive as Chief People and Transformation Officer and Chief Operating Officer. He joined Hindustan Unilever in 1987, held marketing and sales roles, and became Chief Executive Officer, India, and Executive Vice President for Unilever, South Asia, in April 2008. He is also a member of the Board of Indian School of Business.

Paranjpe holds a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering and an MBA in Marketing from Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management in Mumbai.

Diane Jurgens is an Independent Director of Infosys and a strategic board member and technologist with global C-suite and profit-and-loss leadership experience.

Jurgens currently serves on the boards of Standard Chartered and World 50. At Standard Chartered, she is an Independent Non-Executive Director and a member of the Risk and Culture & Sustainability Committees. She was Chief Information Officer of The Walt Disney Company from 2020 to 2023, where she led enterprise technology. She was Chief Technology Officer at BHP from 2015 to 2020, leading work in autonomous systems, cybersecurity, machine learning, and the Industrial Internet of Things.

Jurgens was President and Managing Director of Shanghai OnStar Telematics from 2012 to 2015, CIO for GM International Operations from 2008 to 2012, and CIO for GM China from 2006 to 2008. She holds an MBA from Seattle University and a master’s degree in electrical engineering from the University of Washington.

Inderpreet Sawhney is the Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer of Infosys. She leads the company’s legal and compliance function and has additional responsibility for Privacy.
