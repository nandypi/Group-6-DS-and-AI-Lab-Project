Agentic AI-led business transformation uses AI agents, domain intelligence, and human expertise to orchestrate core processes. The approach embeds autonomous decision-making into workflows so processes can interpret context, act independently within guardrails, and continuously optimize outcomes. It also supports AI-driven products, services, and new monetization models aligned with enterprise strategy.

## e. Unified Operations.ai

Unified Operations.ai replaces fragmented xOps with a single intelligence-driven operating fabric integrating DataOps, MLOps, LLMOps, AgenticOps, FinOps, and observability. Built on an agentic architecture, it uses real-time telemetry, unified knowledge layers, and autonomous resolution workflows to reduce operational complexity, improve reliability, lower total cost of ownership, achieve business experience-level agreements (XLAs), and support business net promoter scores. Operations move from reactive to proactive through zero-touch execution.

## f. Unified Governance.ai

Unified Governance.ai provides governance across data, models, agents, and applications. It supports trust, ethics, privacy, security, and compliance across the AI and agent lifecycle.

The system continuously monitors data quality, model behavior, agent actions, and consumption patterns to help prevent unsafe, biased, or noncompliant outcomes. It establishes governance strategies, operating models, registries, risk frameworks, and policy-driven guardrails for responsible AI deployment.

## Engineering Services

Infosys Engineering Services provides engineering services across the product and platform value chain, from concept and design through build, manufacture, operations, and sustainment. Its capabilities support product innovation, cost, quality, speed, sustainability, regulatory, and compliance requirements.

The services cover AI-driven, software-defined, connected, and sustainable products and platforms across industries. Core capabilities include:

### AI-First Digital Product Engineering

Infosys combines mechanical, electrical, electronics, silicon, embedded systems, networking, and software engineering with AI-driven design, verification, and optimization.

The experience described includes aerospace structures, turbomachinery, medical devices, software products, and cloud-native digital platforms. These services support time-to-market reduction, product-performance improvement, and lifecycle value creation.

### Connected Systems

Infosys connects physical and digital systems using the Internet of Things (IoT), digital thread, digital twin, cloud, and industrial platforms. The services support smart factories, connected assets, and autonomous systems across aerospace maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO), industrial operations, and connected, electric, and software-defined mobility.

### Next-Generation Networks, Media, and Collaboration Platforms

Infosys builds and modernizes network and communications ecosystems by transforming legacy infrastructures into software-defined, cloud-native, and AI-enabled autonomous networks. Capabilities include:

- Software-defined networking (SDN)
- 5G, private networks, and edge platforms
- AI-driven network transformation
- Media and hi-tech platform engineering
- Intelligent unified communications
- Cloud-based contact centers
- Voice AI solutions

### Emerging Technologies and Ecosystem-Led Innovation

Infosys develops capabilities in next-generation mobility, software-defined vehicles (SDVs), semiconductor engineering, private 5G, Industry 4.0, robotics, and Engineering AI.

Innovation is supported by global engineering labs and partnerships with hyperscalers, technology partners, startups, academia, and industry bodies. Strategic acquisitions, including Kaleidoscope Innovation, InSemi, and In-Tech, strengthen capabilities in product design, semiconductor engineering, and next-generation mobility.

## Cloud and Infrastructure Management

Infosys provides cloud, infrastructure, and security management services for modernizing, securing, and operating hybrid and multicloud environments. Its delivery approach uses AI, automation, and platforms to simplify IT estates, improve resilience, optimize costs, and support digital and AI-led business outcomes.

The services address hybrid and multicloud adoption and enterprise comparisons with hyperscalers across cost, agility, reliability, sustainability, and security. Cloud infrastructure is also described as a foundation for AI, automation, zero-trust security, and digital business transformation.

## Core Capabilities

- **Unified Hybrid IT and Cloud Management:** End-to-end management across on-premises environments, private clouds, public clouds, and edge environments, including workload-aware migration, modernization, unified observability, security, and compliance.
- **Infosys Cobalt — Cloud Transformation at Scale:** Cloud services, solutions, platforms, and industry blueprints supporting composable, cloud-native, secure, and sustainable enterprise transformation.
- **Automation-First, AI-Driven Operations:** The Infosys Automation Suite and Infosys Topaz apply AI and generative AI to infrastructure and cloud operations, supporting predictive and self-healing environments, reduced manual effort, and faster provisioning.

## Strategic Investments Strengthening the Portfolio

- **GuideVision:** Infosys' partnership with GuideVision, a ServiceNow Elite Partner, adds enterprise service management and digital operations capabilities through ServiceNow expertise, strategic advisory, large-scale implementations, and proprietary intellectual property such as SnowMirror.
- **Versent Joint Venture with Telstra, Australia:** Infosys' majority stake in Versent strengthens its cloud, infrastructure, and cybersecurity capabilities in Australia and New Zealand. The joint venture combines Versent's local cloud engineering expertise, Telstra's connectivity, and Infosys' global scale, Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Topaz, and security capabilities.

## Cyber Security

Infosys provides platform-centric, AI-powered cybersecurity services designed to strengthen cybersecurity posture, improve cyber resilience, and respond to evolving risks.

Services include:

- Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- Cloud security
- Data protection and privacy
- Infrastructure security
- Vulnerability management
- Offensive security
- Operational technology (OT) security
- Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)
- Cyber consulting and advisory
- Threat detection and response
- Managed security services
- Security for AI

Additional cybersecurity capabilities include:

- **Cyber Defense Centers:** Centers across the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA), and Asia-Pacific (APAC) designed to prevent, detect, assess, and respond to cybersecurity threats and breaches.
- **Engineering and research labs:** Facilities providing threat-hunting capabilities and cybersecurity technology research.
- **Infosys Cyber Next:** A security-as-a-service platform using pre-selected and pre-integrated security technologies. It provides visibility, lifecycle management, automation, and risk-based prioritization.
- **Strategic partnerships:** Partnerships with cybersecurity software providers supporting secure access, cloud infrastructure, endpoints, applications, email, and related areas.
- **Security for AI:** Services focused on responsible AI adoption and security controls for AI environments.

### Acquisition of The Missing Link

The acquisition of The Missing Link, an Australian cybersecurity services specialist, expanded Infosys' cloud and cybersecurity capabilities and its cyber defense presence in the Asia-Pacific market.

The acquisition added Red Team and Blue Team capabilities, a Global Security Operations Centre (GSOC), and expertise in offensive and defensive security, cyber risk assessments, compliance, and managed security services. These capabilities complement Infosys' global cyber defense centers.

### Infosys Cyber Next

Infosys Cyber Next is a cybersecurity service delivery platform designed to support enterprise security operations at scale. It is powered by the Infosys Topaz Fabric suite, where security AI agents correlate multidomain telemetry, reduce false positives, and automate responses.

The platform uses a modern security-mesh-aligned architecture to unify cyber operations across the enterprise. It addresses alert fatigue, tool sprawl, skill shortages, and expanding attack surfaces by centralizing security insights, automating incident-response workflows, and integrating security tools across the enterprise fabric.
