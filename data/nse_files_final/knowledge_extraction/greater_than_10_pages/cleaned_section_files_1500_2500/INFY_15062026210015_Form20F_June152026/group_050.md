The Indian Companies Act, 2013, requires directors to disclose personal interests in transactions discussed by the Board and not participate in meetings when such transactions are considered. Directors must disclose personal interests annually and at the first Board meeting after an interest arises.

Director remuneration may be fixed by the Board in accordance with the Indian Companies Act, 2013 and applicable Government of India provisions. Directors may not vote on their own remuneration, which is recommended by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

The Articles of Association permit the Company to borrow for legitimate corporate purposes. Shareholder approval by special resolution is required when borrowings, together with existing debt, excluding temporary loans from bankers in the ordinary course of business, exceed the aggregate of paid-up capital, securities premium, and free reserves.

## General

The authorized share capital is ₹24,000,000,000 (Rupees two thousand four hundred crore), divided into 4,800,000,000 equity shares with a par value of ₹5 per share.

As of March 31, 2026, 4,055,591,723 equity shares were issued, outstanding, and fully paid. A controlled trust held 8,650,911 equity shares under the 2015 Plan. Equity shares are the Company's only class of share capital. The Company had no convertible debentures or warrants outstanding.

As of March 31, 2026, 12,740,615 RSUs, including ESOPs, were outstanding under the 2015 Plan and 10,422,882 RSUs, including ESOPs, were outstanding under the 2019 Plan.

For purposes of the Annual Report on Form 20-F, “shareholder” means a shareholder registered as a member in the register of members or whose name appears in the beneficiary position maintained by the depositories.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

The Board approved a buyback of equity shares at its meeting on September 11, 2025, under the tender offer route through the Indian stock exchanges. The Buyback Offer Size was ₹18,000 crore at a price not exceeding ₹1,800 per share, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting, with results declared on November 4, 2025. The buyback was offered to all equity shareholders other than ADS holders. The tendering period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025.

During the buyback, the Company purchased and extinguished 100,000,000 equity shares, representing 2.41% of pre-buyback paid-up equity share capital. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, approximately $2,000 million, excluding transaction costs and buyback tax. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

As of March 31, 2026, the Company had created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore, approximately $6 million, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back. The reserve was appropriated from general reserve and retained earnings in accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

## Buyback completed in February 2023

The Board approved a buyback of equity shares at its meeting on October 13, 2022, under the open market route through the Indian stock exchanges. The Maximum Buyback Size was ₹9,300 crore, excluding buyback tax, at a price not exceeding ₹1,850 per share, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting, with results declared on December 3, 2022. The buyback was offered to all equity shareholders other than the Promoters, Promoter Group, and Persons in Control of the Company.

The buyback commenced on December 7, 2022, and was completed on February 13, 2023. The Company purchased and extinguished 60,426,348 equity shares at a volume-weighted average buyback price of ₹1,539.06 per share. The shares represented 1.44% of pre-buyback paid-up equity share capital.

The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹9,300 crore, excluding transaction costs and buyback tax, equivalent to $1,130 million. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

As of March 31, 2023, the Company had created a Capital Redemption Reserve of $3 million equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve and retained earnings in accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

## Dividends

Under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Board recommends dividends for shareholder approval at a general meeting but is not required to recommend a dividend. Interim dividends require only Board approval.

At the AGM, dividends may be declared for shareholders according to their respective rights and interests in profits, but may not exceed the amount recommended by the Board. Dividends are generally declared per equity share and paid in cash in proportion to the paid-up value of the shares within 30 days of shareholder approval.

The Board may declare and pay interim dividends without shareholder approval, subject to the Indian Companies Act, 2013. The Company must inform the stock exchanges where its equity shares and ADSs are listed of the dividend declared per equity share and the record date or book-closure date.

Dividends may be paid only in cash to registered shareholders as of the record date, including by cash, cheque, warrant, or electronic mode.

Unpaid or unclaimed dividends remaining after 30 days from declaration must be transferred to a special bank account. The Company transfers such amounts within seven days after the 30-day period expires. Amounts unclaimed for seven years from the date of transfer are transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF).

Equity shares for which dividends have not been paid or claimed for seven consecutive years or more are also transferred to the IEPF Authority. Shareholders may claim transferred unclaimed dividends and corresponding shares, including benefits accruing on those shares, from the IEPF by following the procedure prescribed under the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (Accounting, Audit, Transfer and Refund) Rules, 2016, as amended. No claim lies against the Company after the transfer.

Dividends may be paid out of current-year profits after providing for depreciation or out of undistributed profits from previous fiscal years after providing for depreciation. Before declaring a dividend, the Company may transfer an appropriate percentage of profits to reserves.

In the event of inadequate or absent profits, dividends may be declared from accumulated profits transferred to free reserves, subject to the following conditions:

- The dividend rate may not exceed the average rate declared in the three financial years immediately preceding that year. This condition does not apply if the Company did not declare dividends in the preceding three financial years.
- The amount drawn from accumulated profits transferred to reserves may not exceed 10% of paid-up capital and free reserves shown in the latest audited financial statements. The amount drawn must first be used to set off losses incurred in the fiscal year in which the dividend is declared.
- Reserves remaining after the withdrawal may not fall below 15% of paid-up capital shown in the latest audited financial statements.
- The Company may not declare a dividend until carried-forward losses and depreciation not provided for in previous years have been set off against the current year's profit.
