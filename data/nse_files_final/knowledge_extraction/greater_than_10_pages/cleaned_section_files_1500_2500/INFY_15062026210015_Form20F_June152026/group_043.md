## Board Leadership Structure

Nandan M. Nilekani has served as Non-Executive, Non-Independent Chairman of the Board since August 24, 2017. Salil Parekh has served as Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director (CEO & MD) since January 2, 2018. The Board appointed D. Sundaram as Lead Independent Director effective March 23, 2023. D. Sundaram also chairs the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and the Risk Management Committee. Nitin Paranjpe, an Independent Director, was appointed Vice Chairman of the Board effective April 30, 2026.

The responsibilities and authority of the Chairman, Vice Chairman, CEO & MD, and Lead Independent Director are described below.

## Chairman

Infosys has separated the roles of Chairman and CEO & MD. The Chairman leads the Board, presides over Board and shareholder meetings, and supports the integrity, effectiveness, composition, and governance of the Board and its committees.

The Chairman works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee on Board and committee composition, director induction and succession, Board evaluation, and feedback to individual directors. The Chairman also facilitates communication among directors.

## Vice Chairman

The Vice Chairman assists the Chairman with governance responsibilities and strategic leadership. The Vice Chairman presides over Board meetings in the Chairman's absence, supports Board agenda development, facilitates communication between the Board and management, and strengthens Board-level oversight of strategy, risk, performance, and corporate governance.

## CEO & MD

The CEO & MD is responsible for executing corporate strategy in consultation with the Board, managing the Company, achieving annual and long-term business targets, and leading and evaluating other executive leaders.

The CEO & MD monitors competitive conditions, industry developments, and standards; identifies opportunities for expansion and acquisitions; and develops relationships with customers and markets. The CEO & MD also acts as a link between the Board and management.

## Lead Independent Director

The Lead Independent Director provides independent leadership for the Board. The general authority and responsibilities of the role are determined by the independent directors, with additional duties assigned by the Board.

The Lead Independent Director provides leadership to the independent directors, liaises on their behalf, and supports Board effectiveness and high-quality governance.

## Board's Role in Risk Oversight

The Board has overall responsibility for risk management oversight. The Risk Management Committee, comprising independent directors, assists the Board in identifying, evaluating, and mitigating strategic, operational, and external environment risks.

The Risk Management Committee monitors and approves the enterprise risk management framework and related practices. It also reviews and approves risk disclosure statements in public documents and other disclosures.

The Board receives periodic presentations on cybersecurity and information security matters. The Audit Committee and Risk Management Committee receive regular cybersecurity updates from management. The Company's cybersecurity policy and risk management framework are presented annually to the Risk Management Committee. Management periodically reviews cybersecurity and other information security matters and oversees adherence to the information security program.

## Board Member Evaluation

The Board monitors and reviews the Board evaluation framework. In consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, it establishes evaluation criteria for the Chairman, the Board, Board committees, and executive, non-executive, and independent directors. Evaluations are conducted through peer evaluation, excluding the director being evaluated.

Independent directors are evaluated on:

- Contribution to and monitoring of corporate governance practices.
- Contribution of international best practices to address business challenges and risks.
- Participation in long-term strategic planning.
- Commitment to Board and committee responsibilities, including participation in meetings.
- Participation in a formal and rigorous annual review of the Board, its committees, and individual directors.

The Board engaged Egon Zehnder, a leadership advisory firm specializing in board matters, to conduct the fiscal 2026 Board evaluation. The evaluation covered Board dynamics, softer aspects of Board functioning, committee effectiveness, information flow to the Board and its committees, succession planning, and committee composition. The methodology included questionnaires and one-on-one discussions.

The fiscal 2026 evaluation was completed during the year. The Board discussed recommendations from the prior year's evaluation, provided individual feedback, and reviewed progress on prior recommendations and recommendations from the current year.

The evaluation process was also based on confirmation from the independent directors that they met the independence criteria under the Companies Act, 2013, the Listing Regulations, and the NYSE listing manual.

## Succession Planning

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee works with the Board on leadership succession planning for Board and senior management appointments. The Company seeks to maintain an appropriate balance of skills and experience while introducing new perspectives and preserving experience and continuity. It also promotes senior management development for future leadership roles.

## Board Committees

As of March 31, 2026, the Board had six committees:

- Audit Committee
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee
- Stakeholders Relationship Committee
- Risk Management Committee
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee
- Environment, Social and Governance Committee

All committees comprised only independent directors, with one member appointed as chairperson. The Board also established a Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee of the Risk Management Committee.

During the year, the Board approved all recommendations made by the committees.

The Board, in consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, assigns committee members and determines their terms of service. The Chairman, in consultation with the Company Secretary and the relevant committee chairperson, determines meeting frequency. Committees generally meet four times a year.

Committee recommendations are submitted to the Board for approval. The quorum is the higher of two members or one-third of the total committee membership.

Committee charters and corporate governance guidelines are available on the Company's website: <https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/Pages/policies.aspx>.

## Audit Committee

The Audit Committee comprised four independent directors. Each was determined by the Board to be independent under applicable NYSE rules and Rule 10A-3 under the Exchange Act:

- Bobby Parikh, Chairperson and Financial Expert
- D. Sundaram, Financial Expert
- Michael Gibbs
- Nitin Paranjpe

The Company Secretary serves as secretary to the Audit Committee.

The Audit Committee assists the Board in overseeing:

1. The accuracy, integrity, and transparency of the Company's financial statements, including adequate and timely disclosures.
2. Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
3. The professional qualifications and independence of the Company's independent auditor.
4. The performance of the Company's independent and internal auditors.
5. Acquisitions and investments made by the Company.

The Audit Committee met ten times during fiscal 2026.

The Audit Committee approved, and the Board adopted, the Related Party Transaction Policy, Code on Fair Disclosure and Investor Relations, and Insider Trading Policy. Required published policies are available on the Company's website: <https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/Pages/policies.aspx>.

Infosys is listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited in India, and on the NYSE in the United States. The Audit Committee operates under a Board-adopted charter available at <https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/audit-committeecharter.pdf>.
