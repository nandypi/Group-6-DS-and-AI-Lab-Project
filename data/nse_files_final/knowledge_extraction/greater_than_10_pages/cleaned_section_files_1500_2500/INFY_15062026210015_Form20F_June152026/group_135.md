## CERTIFICATION OF CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER PURSUANT TO 18 U.S.C. SECTION 1350, AS ADOPTED PURSUANT TO SECTION 906 OF THE SARBANES-OXLEY ACT OF 2002

Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Infosys Limited, certified on June 15, 2026, that the company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2026:

- Fully complies with the requirements of Section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
- Fairly presents, in all material respects, Infosys Limited’s financial condition and results of operations.

## CERTIFICATION OF CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER PURSUANT TO 18 U.S.C. SECTION 1350, AS ADOPTED PURSUANT TO SECTION 906 OF THE SARBANES-OXLEY ACT OF 2002

Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer of Infosys Limited, certified on June 15, 2026, that the company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2026:

- Fully complies with the requirements of Section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
- Fairly presents, in all material respects, Infosys Limited’s financial condition and results of operations.

## CONSENT OF INDEPENDENT REGISTERED PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FIRM

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP consented on June 15, 2026, to the incorporation by reference in Form S-8 Registration Statement Nos. 333-212767 and 333236753 of its reports dated June 15, 2026, relating to:

- The financial statements of Infosys Limited.
- The effectiveness of Infosys Limited’s internal control over financial reporting.

The reports appear in Infosys Limited’s Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2026.
