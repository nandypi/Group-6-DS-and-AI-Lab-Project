## BSE LIMITED NATIONAL STOCK EXCHANGE OF INDIA LIMITED NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE

June 15, 2026

Infosys filed its Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2026 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. The Form 20-F is available on the Infosys website.

The financial statements included in the Form 20-F were prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB).

The Form 20-F is made available online to American Depositary Share (ADS) holders. Physical or email copies are available at no cost to ADS holders on request.

## PRESS RELEASE Infosys Annual Report on Form 20-F for fiscal 2026 Available Online for ADS holders

Bengaluru — June 15, 2026: Infosys announced that it had filed its Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2026 with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

Infosys describes itself as a provider of AI-first business consulting and technology services. It has more than 325,000 employees, operates in 63 countries, and has more than four decades of experience managing the systems and operations of global enterprises.

## INFOSYS LIMITED

Infosys Limited is incorporated in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

### Securities registered pursuant to Section 12(b) of the Securities Exchange Act

| Title of each class | Trading symbol | Exchange |
|---|---|---|
| American Depositary Shares, each representing one equity share with a par value of ₹5 per share | INFY | New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) |

Securities registered pursuant to Section 12(g): None.

Securities subject to a reporting obligation pursuant to Section 15(d): Not applicable.

As of the close of the period covered by the Annual Report, Infosys had 4,055,591,723 outstanding equity shares.

Infosys indicated that it is a well-known seasoned issuer.

Infosys indicated that:

- It is required to file reports pursuant to Section 13 or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
- It filed all reports required under Section 13 or 15(d) during the preceding 12 months and has been subject to those filing requirements for the past 90 days.
- It submitted electronically every Interactive Data file required under Regulation S-T during the preceding 12 months.
- It is a large accelerated filer.
- It is not an emerging growth company.
- Its filing includes the registered public accounting firm's attestation on management's assessment of the effectiveness of internal control over financial reporting under Section 404(b) of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act.
- The financial statements in the filing do not reflect the correction of an error to previously issued financial statements.
- The filing does not include restatements requiring a recovery analysis of incentive-based compensation.
- Its financial statements were prepared using IFRS as issued by the IASB.
- It is not a shell company.
