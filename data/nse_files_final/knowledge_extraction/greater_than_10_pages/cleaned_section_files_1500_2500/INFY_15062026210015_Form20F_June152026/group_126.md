## Annexure 5 Form B

## SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 [Regulation 7 (2) read with Regulation 6(2) - Continual disclosure]

Details of change in holding of securities of a promoter, member of the promoter group, designated person, director of a listed company, immediate relatives of such persons, and other persons mentioned in Regulation 6(2).

| Required disclosure field | Details |
|---|---|
| Person identification | Name, PAN, CIN/DIN, address, and contact details |
| Category | Promoter, member of promoter group, designated person, or other specified person |
| Securities held before acquisition/disposal | Type of security; number and percentage of shareholding |
| Securities acquired/disposed | Type of security; number; value; transaction type, including buy, sale, pledge, revoke, invoke, or other transaction |
| Securities held after acquisition/disposal | Type of security; number and percentage of shareholding |
| Transaction date | From and to dates for allotment advice, acquisition, or disposal |
| Company intimation date | Date of intimation to the company |
| Mode of acquisition/disposal | On-market, public, rights, preferential offer, off-market, inter se transfer, ESOPs, or other applicable mode |
| Trading exchange | Exchange on which the trade was executed |

Note: “Securities” has the meaning defined under Regulation 2(1)(i) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

### Details of trading in derivatives on the securities of the company

| Contract information | Buy | Sell | Exchange |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type of contract and contract specifications | Notional value; number of units multiplied by contract lot size | Notional value; number of units multiplied by contract lot size | Exchange on which the trade was executed |

Note: In the case of options, notional value shall be calculated based on the premium plus the strike price of the options.

## FORM C (Indicative format)

## SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 Regulation 7(3) - Transactions by other connected persons as identified by the Company

Details of trading in securities by other connected persons identified by the company.

| Required disclosure field | Details |
|---|---|
| Person identification | Name, PAN/CIN/DIN, address, and contact details of the other connected person |
| Connection with company | Nature of the person’s connection with the company |
| Securities held before acquisition/disposal | Type of security; number and percentage of shareholding |
| Securities acquired/disposed | Type of security; number; value; transaction type, including buy, sale, pledge, revoke, invoke, or other transaction |
| Securities held after acquisition/disposal | Type of security; number and percentage of shareholding |
| Transaction date | From and to dates for allotment advice, acquisition, or disposal |
| Company intimation date | Date of intimation to the company |
| Mode of acquisition/disposal | On-market, public, rights, preferential offer, off-market, inter se transfer, ESOPs, or other applicable mode |
| Trading exchange | Exchange on which the trade was executed |

## Details of trading in derivatives by other connected persons as identified by the company

| Contract information | Buy | Sell | Exchange |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type of contract and contract specifications | Notional value; number of units multiplied by contract lot size | Notional value; number of units multiplied by contract lot size | Exchange on which the trade was executed |

Note: In the case of options, notional value shall be calculated based on the premium plus the strike price of the options.
