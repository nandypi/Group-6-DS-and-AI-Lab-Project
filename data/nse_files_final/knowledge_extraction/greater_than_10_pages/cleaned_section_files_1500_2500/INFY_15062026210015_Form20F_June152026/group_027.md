## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Infosys relies on patents, copyrights, trademarks, design laws, trade secrets, confidentiality procedures, and contractual provisions to protect its intellectual property. As of March 31, 2026, it had 973 pending or registered patents in India, the EU, the United States, and other key jurisdictions.

Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries had more than 900 trademarks pending or registered across classes covering various goods and services in 51 countries. Employees, independent contractors, and, where possible, vendors are required to enter into confidentiality agreements. These agreements generally cover confidential or proprietary information developed by Infosys or on its behalf, as well as information disclosed to third parties. Clients usually own the intellectual property in software developed for them.

Infosys considers its trade name, trademarks, service marks, and domain names important to its business. The company has registered its key brand, “INFOSYS,” as a trademark in India and the United States and takes steps to protect these names and marks from infringement.

## EFFECT OF GOVERNMENT REGULATION ON OUR BUSINESS

Infosys benefits from certain Government of India tax incentives, including incentives relating to the export of services from special economic zones (SEZs). These incentives have resulted in relatively lower Indian tax liabilities. Changes in applicable laws, regulations, or government policies could affect the availability or extent of these incentives and consequently affect results of operations.

Infosys has also benefited from the liberalization and deregulation of the Indian economy since 1991. Indian laws and regulations affecting the business include requirements in certain circumstances to obtain approval from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and/or the Ministry of Finance to acquire companies incorporated outside India. Subject to certain exceptions, approval from relevant Indian government authorities may also be required to raise capital outside India. The conversion of equity shares into American Depositary Shares (ADSs) is governed by RBI guidelines.

Under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, a company with net worth of at least ₹500 crore, turnover of at least ₹1,000 crore, or net profit of at least ₹5 crore in the immediately preceding financial year must establish a Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee of the board and spend at least 2% of average net profit for the immediately preceding three financial years on CSR. The CSR Committee must have at least three directors, including at least one independent director, and the CSR policy must be approved by the Board. Approximately $70 million was contributed to CSR activities during fiscal 2026.

The ability of Infosys technology professionals to work in the United States, Europe, and other countries depends on obtaining the necessary visas and work permits, as described in Item 3—Risk Factors.

## LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

### Government Investigation

The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) is investigating how Infosys classified certain H-1B visa-recipient employees in immigration documents filed with certain U.S. government authorities. Infosys is discussing the ongoing investigation with the DOJ and continues its own inquiry. The company cannot currently predict the outcome, including whether it could have a material adverse effect on its business and results of operations.

### Others

The Group is subject to legal proceedings and claims arising in the ordinary course of business. Management reasonably expects that these matters, when ultimately concluded and determined, will not have a material adverse effect on the Group’s results of operations or financial condition.

## D. PROPERTY, PLANT AND EQUIPMENT

Infosys’s corporate headquarters campus is located at Electronics City, Bengaluru, India. Infosys Electronics City comprises approximately 4.7 million square feet of land and 6.5 million square feet of operational facilities. The campus includes an Education, Training and Assessment unit, a Management Development Center, and conference facilities.

Infosys also leases approximately 380,000 square feet of facilities in Electronics City, accommodating approximately 5,000 employees.

Capital expenditure on property, plant and equipment was $306 million in fiscal 2026, $263 million in fiscal 2025, and $266 million in fiscal 2024. These capital expenditures were financed from cash generated by operations. As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had contractual capital-expenditure commitments of $141 million.

Infosys’s software development facilities include networked workstations, servers, data communication links, and videoconferencing infrastructure. The company had a presence in 290 locations across 59 countries.
