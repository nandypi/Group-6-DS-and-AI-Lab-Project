## Liquidity and capital resources

Our principal sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents, investments, and cash flow generated from operations. We have no outstanding borrowings and believe that our working capital is sufficient to meet current requirements.

As of March 31, 2026 and 2025, working capital was $5,396 million and $6,347 million, respectively. Working capital as of March 31, 2026 included $2,341 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1,365 million in current investments. Working capital as of March 31, 2025 included $2,861 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1,460 million in current investments.

A sustained reduction in client IT spending, a longer sales cycle, or a continued economic downturn in the geographic locations or business segments in which we operate could reduce revenues and negatively affect liquidity and cash resources.

Cash and cash equivalents comprise deposits with highly rated banks and financial institutions that can be withdrawn at any time without prior notice or penalty on principal. They are primarily held in Indian Rupees. Restricted cash included in cash and cash equivalents was $44 million as of March 31, 2026 and $50 million as of March 31, 2025. The restrictions primarily relate to bank balances held by irrevocable trusts controlled by Infosys.

Investments comprise mutual fund units, target maturity fund units, quoted debt securities including investments in non-convertible debentures, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, and government securities. Certificates of deposit and commercial paper represent marketable securities of banks, non-banking financial companies, and eligible financial institutions for specified periods, with high domestic credit ratings. Investments in non-convertible debentures represent debt instruments issued by government-aided institutions and financial institutions with high credit ratings.

A significant portion of investments, cash, and cash equivalents is fully liquid. Investments are diversified by maturity and counterparty.

## Net cash provided by operating activities:

Operating cash flows decreased by $312 million in fiscal 2026, primarily due to higher income tax refunds received during fiscal 2025 and higher working capital outflows, partially offset by an increase in net profits adjusted for non-cash items.

Trade receivables as a percentage of last-12-month revenues were 18.4% as of March 31, 2026 and 18.9% as of March 31, 2025. Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), based on last-12-month revenues, was 67 days and 69 days, respectively. DSO decreased in fiscal 2026 due to collections.

In fiscal 2026, income tax paid under protest following demands from tax authorities in major tax jurisdictions was $11 million, relating to fiscal 2018, fiscal 2019, and fiscal 2021 through fiscal 2023. Tax refunds received in fiscal 2026 were $408 million, primarily relating to fiscal 2012, fiscal 2015, fiscal 2018, fiscal 2020, and fiscal 2022 through fiscal 2025.

In fiscal 2025, income tax paid under protest following demands from tax authorities in major tax jurisdictions was $90 million, relating to fiscal 2018 through fiscal 2022. Tax refunds received in fiscal 2025 were $821 million, primarily relating to fiscal 2007 through fiscal 2018 and fiscal 2020 through fiscal 2024.

## Net cash provided by / (used) investing activities:

Net cash generated from investing activities was $10 million in fiscal 2026.

Cash used for business acquisitions was $76 million in fiscal 2026, compared with $377 million in fiscal 2025.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys paid $76 million, net of cash acquired, for the acquisitions of MRE Consulting Ltd and The Missing Link. The aggregate purchase consideration included contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $8 million, or $9 million on an undiscounted basis, as of the acquisition date. The contingent consideration is payable over the following two years, subject to achievement of certain financial targets.

The acquisitions also include retention payouts and bonuses of $37 million payable to acquiree employees two to three years from the acquisition date, subject to continuous employment with the group and achievement of certain financial targets.

Net cash provided by or used in investing activities relating to the acquisition of property, plant and equipment and intangibles, net of sale proceeds, was $306 million in fiscal 2026 and $263 million in fiscal 2025. These investments primarily related to software development centers. During fiscal 2026, proceeds from the sale of property, plant and equipment were $31 million, resulting in a gain of $19 million.

Infosys invested $10,871 million and $10,601 million in marketable securities during fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2025, respectively. It redeemed marketable securities of $11,193 million in fiscal 2026 and $10,825 million in fiscal 2025. Marketable securities include mutual funds, target maturity funds, quoted debt securities, certificates of deposit, and commercial paper.

Investments are redeemed to fund share buybacks, business operations, acquisitions, and dividend payments.

## Net cash used in financing activities:

Net cash used in financing activities in fiscal 2026 primarily included $2,133 million in dividend payments, $2,006 million for share buybacks, and $318 million for lease liability payments.

Net cash used in financing activities in fiscal 2025 primarily included $2,416 million in dividend payments, $278 million for lease liability payments, and $118 million for repayment of the loan of in-tech Holding GmbH at the time of acquisition.

## Proposed acquisitions

On August 13, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 75% of the equity share capital of Telstra Purple Pty Ltd, including certain subsidiaries collectively known as the Versent Group. The consideration, including earn-outs and deferred consideration, is up to AUD 233 million, approximately $152 million, excluding retention bonuses and management incentives, and is subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing adjustments.

## Update on acquisition completed after the end of the reporting period

On March 25, 2026, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of Optimum Achieve Holdings Inc., headquartered in the United States, including its subsidiaries such as Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC. The consideration, including earn-outs, is up to $465 million, excluding management incentives and retention bonuses, and is subject to customary closing adjustments. Infosys Nova Holdings LLC completed the acquisition on May 4, 2026.

On March 25, 2026, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the partnership interests of Stratus Global LLC, headquartered in the United States. Stratus Global LLC serves property and casualty insurers and managing general agents (MGAs). The consideration, including earn-outs, is up to $95 million, excluding management incentives and retention bonuses, and is subject to customary closing adjustments. Infosys Nova Holdings LLC completed the acquisition on April 21, 2026.

## Contractual commitments

As of March 31, 2026, contractual commitments for capital expenditure, primarily for infrastructure facilities and computer equipment, totaled $141 million. These commitments included $109 million for domestic purchases and $32 million for overseas purchases. Capital commitments will be financed from cash generated by operations and are expected to be substantially completed within one year.

As of March 31, 2026, purchase obligations totaled $2,443 million. Approximately 54% was expected to be completed within the following year, with the remainder thereafter.

Purchase obligations are enforceable and legally binding agreements to purchase goods or services that specify significant terms, including fixed or minimum purchase quantities, fixed, minimum, or variable price provisions, and the approximate timing of the transaction.
