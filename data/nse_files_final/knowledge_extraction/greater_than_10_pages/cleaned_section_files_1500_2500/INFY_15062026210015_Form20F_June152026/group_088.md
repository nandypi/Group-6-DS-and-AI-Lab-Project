## 2.10 Business combinations

## Accounting Policy:

Business combinations are accounted for using the acquisition method under IFRS 3 (Revised), Business Combinations.

The purchase price in an acquisition is measured at the fair value of assets transferred, equity instruments issued, and liabilities incurred or assumed at the date control is transferred to the Group. The purchase price also includes the fair value of contingent consideration.

Identifiable assets acquired and liabilities and contingent liabilities assumed in a business combination are initially measured at fair value on the acquisition date. Contingent consideration is remeasured at fair value at each reporting date, with changes recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income.

The interest of non-controlling shareholders is initially measured either at fair value or at the non-controlling interests' proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets. The measurement basis is selected on an acquisition-by-acquisition basis. After acquisition, the carrying amount of non-controlling interests equals the amount initially recognized plus the non-controlling interests' share of subsequent changes in the equity of subsidiaries.

Business combinations between entities under common control are outside the scope of IFRS 3 (Revised), Business Combinations, and are accounted for at the carrying value of assets acquired and liabilities assumed.

Payments related to options issued by the Group over non-controlling interests in subsidiaries are accounted for as financial liabilities and initially recognized at the estimated present value of gross obligations. These options are subsequently measured at fair value to reflect the amount payable when the option becomes exercisable. If the option expires unexercised, the liability is derecognized.

## Acquisition during the year ended March 31, 2026

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership interests or voting interests in the following entities:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.**, an energy and business consulting services company headquartered in Texas, U.S., acquired on April 30, 2025. The acquisition is expected to bring newer capabilities in trading and risk management, particularly in the energy sector.

2. **The Missing Link Security Pty. Ltd., The Missing Link Security Limited, and The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd.**, collectively known as **The Missing Link**, a cybersecurity service provider headquartered in Australia, acquired on April 30, 2025. The acquisition is expected to strengthen the Group's cybersecurity capabilities and its presence in the Australian market.

The excess of purchase consideration over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill were the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill amounting to $9 million is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of $96 million includes upfront cash consideration of $88 million and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $8 million at the acquisition date.

The key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were probabilities assigned to the achievement of financial targets and discount rates ranging from 2% to 3%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026, was approximately $9 million.

The acquisitions include retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over two to three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Retention bonuses and management incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the statement of comprehensive income over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was $23 million at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts had been substantially collected.

Transaction costs incurred in connection with a business combination, including finder's fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of $4 million related to the acquisition were included under administrative expenses in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income for the year ended March 31, 2026.
