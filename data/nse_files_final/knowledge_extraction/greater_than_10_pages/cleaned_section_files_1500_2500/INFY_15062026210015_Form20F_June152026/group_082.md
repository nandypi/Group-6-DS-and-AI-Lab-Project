## Liquidity risk

Liquidity risk is the risk that the Group will not be able to settle or meet its obligations on time.

The Group’s principal sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents, investments, and cash flow generated from operations. The Group has no outstanding borrowings and believes that its working capital is sufficient to meet current requirements.

As of March 31, 2026, the Group had working capital of $5,396 million, including cash and cash equivalents of $2,341 million and current investments of $1,365 million. As of March 31, 2025, working capital was $6,347 million, including cash and cash equivalents of $2,861 million and current investments of $1,460 million.
