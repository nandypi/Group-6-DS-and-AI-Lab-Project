## INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT ON THE AUDIT OF QUARTERLY STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS

## TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF INFOSYS LIMITED

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the accompanying statement of standalone financial results of Infosys Limited for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

In the auditor's opinion, the statement:

- Is presented in accordance with Regulation 33 of the Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015, as amended.
- Gives a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles under Ind AS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, and other generally accepted accounting principles in India, of the Company's net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013.

The auditor stated that it was independent of the Company under the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India's Code of Ethics and other applicable ethical requirements. The auditor considered the audit evidence obtained to be sufficient and appropriate as a basis for its opinion.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for the Statement

The standalone financial results were the responsibility of the Company's Board of Directors and were approved by the Board for issuance. The statement was compiled from the related audited interim condensed standalone financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

The Board's responsibilities included:

- Preparing and presenting standalone financial results that give a true and fair view in accordance with Ind AS 34, the Companies Act, 2013, relevant rules, and Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records to safeguard the Company's assets and prevent and detect fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls relevant to the preparation and presentation of financial statements free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.
- Assessing whether the Company can continue as a going concern and whether it intends to liquidate or cease operations.
- Overseeing the Company's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for Audit of the Standalone Financial Results for the Quarter Ended June 30, 2025

The auditor's objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the statement as a whole was free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing its opinion.

The audit responsibilities included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error, designing responsive audit procedures, and obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal controls to design appropriate audit procedures.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by the Board of Directors.
- Evaluating the appropriateness and reasonableness of disclosures required under Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- Assessing the appropriateness of the Board's use of the going-concern basis of accounting and whether material uncertainty existed regarding the Company's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures in the statement.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence to express an opinion on the statement.
