## 2.10.5 Impairment

The Company recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenues that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

For trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component, loss allowances are measured at lifetime ECL. For all other financial assets, expected credit losses are measured at 12-month ECL unless there has been a significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition, in which case lifetime ECL is used.

The Company determines credit loss allowances based on historical loss experience adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions. It considers current and anticipated future economic conditions relating to the industries it serves and the countries in which it operates.

The ECL adjustment, or reversal, required at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the statement of profit and loss.
