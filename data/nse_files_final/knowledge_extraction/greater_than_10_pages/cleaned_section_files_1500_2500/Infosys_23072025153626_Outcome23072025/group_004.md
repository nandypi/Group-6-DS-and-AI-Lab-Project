## Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP

- Danske IT and Support Services India Private Limited was acquired by Infosys Limited on September 1, 2023, and renamed Idunn Information Technology Private Limited effective April 1, 2024.
- InSemi Technology Services Pvt. Ltd. was acquired by Infosys Limited on May 10, 2024.
- Elbrus Labs Private Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of InSemi Technology Services Pvt. Ltd., was acquired by Infosys Limited on May 10, 2024.
- Infosys Services (Thailand) Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, was incorporated on July 26, 2024.
- Infy tech SAS, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Limited, was incorporated on July 3, 2024.
- in-tech Holding GmbH, acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024, merged into in-tech GmbH effective January 1, 2025.
- in-tech GmbH, a subsidiary of in-tech Holding GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.
- in-tech Automotive Engineering SL, a subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.
- ProIT, a subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.
- in-tech Automotive Engineering de R.L. de C.V., a subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024, and liquidated effective May 7, 2025.
- drivetech Fahrversuch GmbH, a subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.
- Friedrich Wagner Holding Inc, a subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024, and is under liquidation.
- in-tech Automotive Engineering LLC, a subsidiary of Friedrich Wagner Holding Inc, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024, and liquidated effective November 30, 2024.
- Friedrich & Wagner Asia Pacific GmbH, a subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024, and merged into in-tech GmbH effective January 1, 2025.
- in-tech engineering s.r.o., a subsidiary of Friedrich & Wagner Asia Pacific GmbH, was acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.
