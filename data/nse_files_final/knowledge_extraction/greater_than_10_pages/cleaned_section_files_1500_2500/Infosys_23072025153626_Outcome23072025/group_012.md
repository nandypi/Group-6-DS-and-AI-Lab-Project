## About Infosys

Infosys provides digital services and consulting to clients in 59 countries. The company has over 320,000 employees and more than four decades of experience managing the systems and operations of global enterprises. Its capabilities include cloud and artificial intelligence, digital transformation, digital skills development, and continuous improvement through its innovation ecosystem.

## Report on the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

### Opinion

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries comprise the condensed consolidated balance sheet as at June 30, 2025; the condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income; the condensed consolidated statement of changes in equity; the condensed consolidated statement of cash flows for the three months ended June 30, 2025; and the related notes, including material accounting policies and explanatory information.

The auditor stated that, based on the information and explanations provided, the interim condensed consolidated financial statements give a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated state of affairs as at June 30, 2025, its consolidated profit and total comprehensive income, its changes in equity, and its cash flows, in conformity with International Accounting Standard 34, “Interim Financial Reporting” (IAS 34), as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

### Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with applicable auditing standards. The auditor stated that it was independent of the Group in accordance with the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and had fulfilled its other ethical responsibilities. The auditor considered the audit evidence obtained to be sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the audit opinion.

### Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The Company’s Board of Directors is responsible for the preparation and presentation of interim condensed consolidated financial statements that give a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated financial position, consolidated financial performance, consolidated total comprehensive income, consolidated changes in equity, and consolidated cash flows in accordance with IAS 34.

The respective Boards of Directors of the entities included in the Group are responsible for maintaining adequate accounting records, safeguarding the Group’s assets, preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities, and designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls that operate effectively to ensure the accuracy and completeness of accounting records.

In preparing the interim condensed consolidated financial statements, the respective Boards of Directors are responsible for assessing the entities’ ability to continue as a going concern and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless they intend to liquidate the entities, cease operations, or have no realistic alternative but to do so.

The respective Boards of Directors are also responsible for overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.
