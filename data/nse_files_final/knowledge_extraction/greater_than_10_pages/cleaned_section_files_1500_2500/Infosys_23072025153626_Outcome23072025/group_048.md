## Trade Receivables and Contract Balances

The timing of revenue recognition, billings, and cash collections results in receivables, unbilled revenue, and unearned revenue on the Group’s consolidated balance sheet. Amounts are billed as work progresses according to contractual terms, either at periodic intervals such as monthly or quarterly, or upon achievement of contractual milestones.

Receivables are rights to consideration that are unconditional. Unbilled revenue comprising revenue in excess of billings from time-and-material contracts and fixed-price maintenance contracts is classified as a financial asset when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

For other fixed-price contracts, invoicing is based on contractual milestones. Because the timing of revenue recognition differs from the timing of invoicing, unbilled revenue for these contracts is classified as a nonfinancial contract asset when the right to consideration depends on completion of contractual milestones.

Invoicing in excess of earnings is classified as unearned revenue.

Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are presented net of impairment in the consolidated balance sheet.

## 2.18 Equity

### Ordinary Shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity. Incremental costs directly attributable to issuing new ordinary shares or share options, and to buybacks, are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of related tax effects.

### Treasury Shares

When an entity within the Group purchases the Company’s ordinary shares, the consideration paid, including directly attributable incremental costs, is presented as a deduction from total equity until the shares are cancelled, sold, or reissued. When treasury shares are subsequently sold or reissued, the amount received is recognized as an increase in equity, and the resulting surplus or deficit is transferred to or from share premium.

### Share premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as share premium. Share-based compensation recognized in net profit in the condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income is also credited to share premium. Amounts have been utilized for bonus issues and share buybacks from the share premium account.

### Other Reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be utilized for acquiring new plant and machinery for the Company’s business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

### Capital Redemption Reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

### Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the reserve is transferred to net profit in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income when the related forecast transaction occurs.

### Other components of equity

Other components of equity include currency translation; remeasurement of net defined benefit liabilities or assets; fair value changes of equity instruments measured through other comprehensive income; and changes in the fair value of investments, net of taxes.

### 2.18.1 Voting

Each equity shareholder is entitled to one vote per share. Equity shares represented by American Depositary Shares (ADSs) carry similar voting and dividend rights as other equity shares. Each ADS represents one underlying equity share.

### 2.18.2 Liquidation

In the event of liquidation, shareholders are entitled to receive the remaining assets after distribution of all preferential amounts. No such preferential amounts currently exist, other than amounts held by irrevocable controlled trusts. Distributions are proportionate to the number of equity shares held. For irrevocable controlled trusts, the corpus is settled in favor of the beneficiaries.

### 2.18.3 Share options

Holders of options issued under the Company’s share option plans have no voting, dividend, or liquidation rights.

### 2.18.4 Share capital and share premium

The Company has one class of shares: equity shares with a par value of ₹5 each. Controlled trusts held 9,098,409 shares as of June 30, 2025, and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025.

### 2.18.5 Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividend and buyback amounts include applicable taxes.

The Company’s capital management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. It may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares. As of June 30, 2025, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## Dividend

Final dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the Company’s Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are distributed after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a defined benefit gratuity retirement plan covering eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure. The Company contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve make contributions to their respective employee gratuity fund trusts. Trustees administer the contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts and/or a lump-sum payment, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Liabilities for defined benefit plans are determined through actuarial valuations performed by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. These plans expose the Group to actuarial risks, including longevity, interest-rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its balance sheet. Remeasurement gains and losses on the net defined benefit liability or asset are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not reclassified to profit or loss in subsequent periods. The actual return on plan assets in excess of the yield calculated using the discount rate applied to the defined benefit obligation is recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income.
