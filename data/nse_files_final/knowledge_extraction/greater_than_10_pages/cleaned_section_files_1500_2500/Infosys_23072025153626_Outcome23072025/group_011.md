## Client wins & Testimonials

1. Infosys announced the extension of its strategic collaboration with Select Portfolio Servicing, Inc. (SPS), to support greater operational efficiency and service quality through a fully managed services offering covering hybrid cloud solutions, application portfolio management, IT operations, IaaS, SaaS, security operations, and quality assurance. SPS will leverage Infosys Topaz for AI adoption across business, technology, and enterprise functions, with stated objectives including improved availability, scalability, performance, resiliency, security, and stability.

2. Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with AIB to accelerate the bank’s digital transformation initiatives, including modernization of technology and data capabilities, operational efficiency, and resilience.

3. Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with E.ON to enable AI-powered digital workplace transformation across Europe.

4. Infosys announced the expansion of its strategic collaboration with DNB Bank ASA (DNB) to accelerate the bank’s digital transformation. The collaboration covers modernization of DNB’s IT infrastructure and use of advanced technologies such as AI and machine learning to support seamless, personalized, and agile customer services.

5. Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Yorkshire Building Society, one of the largest member-owned financial institutions in the UK, to accelerate its digital transformation. The stated transformation plans include combining digital convenience with human interaction, investing in faster payments and enhanced security, and supporting the organization’s 2030 ambitions.

6. Infosys and Spark New Zealand announced a strategic agreement to support the transformation of Spark’s technology delivery model through digital innovation. Infosys has collaborated with Spark for over 16 years. The agreement is intended to allow Spark teams to focus on technology strategy and product roadmaps while leveraging Infosys’s global scale, AI, and innovation investments.

7. Infosys collaborated with Perfection Fresh to support tracking of sustainability efforts through Microsoft Sustainability Manager. The implementation provided real-time visibility of produce across locations, with stated benefits including improved operational efficiency, audit transparency, and reduced wastage. The rollout expanded from the originally planned 4 sites to all 17 locations.

8. Infosys Finacle announced a strategic collaboration with Bank of Sydney (BoS) to support its digital transformation using the Infosys Finacle Digital Banking Suite. The collaboration addresses Bank of Sydney’s goals related to business growth, changing customer needs, digital advancements, regulatory requirements, and transformation of its core and digital banking technology stack.

9. Infosys BPM announced the launch of AI agents for invoice processing within its Infosys Accounts Payable on Cloud solution. Americana Restaurants used the AI-powered solution to improve invoice-processing speed, accuracy, and efficiency, with Agentic AI intended to reduce manual dependencies and increase automation and autonomy.

10. Infosys announced a three-year strategic collaboration with the Lawn Tennis Association (LTA) to deliver AI-powered innovations, including match insights and immersive fan experiences. The collaboration includes real-time insights and interactive experiences for the HSBC Championships.

11. Infosys and Economist Impact announced the launch of The Sustainability Atlas, which brings together sustainability data and insights to help policymakers and business leaders navigate sustainability topics. The Atlas uses Infosys generative AI capabilities.
