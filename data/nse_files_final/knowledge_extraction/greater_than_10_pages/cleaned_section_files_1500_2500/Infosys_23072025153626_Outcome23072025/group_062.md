## Fair value hierarchy

- **Level 1:** Quoted prices, unadjusted, in active markets for identical assets or liabilities.
- **Level 2:** Inputs other than quoted prices included in Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly as prices or indirectly as derived prices.
- **Level 3:** Inputs for assets or liabilities that are not based on observable market data.

## 2.11 EQUITY

## Ordinary Shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity share capital. Incremental costs directly attributable to issuing new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of related tax effects.

## Capital redemption reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserves or retained earnings.

## Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the Company’s accumulated earnings.

## Securities premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as securities premium. The securities premium account has been used for bonus issues and share buybacks.

## Share options outstanding account

The share options outstanding account records the fair value of equity-settled, share-based payment transactions with employees. Amounts are transferred to securities premium upon exercise of stock options and to general reserves when stock options are not exercised by employees.

## Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used to acquire new plant and machinery for the Company’s business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include:

- Remeasurement of net defined benefit liabilities or assets.
- Equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income.
- Changes in the fair value of investments.
- Changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges.

These amounts are presented net of taxes.
