## Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedging reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the reserve is transferred to the condensed standalone Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecasted transaction occurs.

## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

As at June 30, 2025, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements. The Company’s capital management objective is to maintain its ability to continue as a going concern and an optimal capital structure. It may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares.

## 2.11.2 DIVIDEND

Final dividends are recorded as liabilities when approved by shareholders, while interim dividends are recorded as liabilities when declared by the Board of Directors. Income tax consequences of dividends on equity instruments are recognized according to where the entity originally recognized the transactions or events that generated distributable profits.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Dividend remittances outside India are governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and are subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## 2.11.3 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

### Accounting Policy

The Company recognizes share-based payment compensation expense in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion, with a corresponding increase in the share options outstanding account.

### Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan)

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the AGM, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.

The 2019 Plan may cover up to 5,00,00,000 equity shares. Up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. These parameters include relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics. The instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

### 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan)

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.

The 2015 Plan may cover up to 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust for the 2011 Plan as at March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue. Instruments generally vest over four years.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares, while the exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 90,98,409 shares as at June 30, 2025 and 96,55,927 shares as at March 31, 2025 under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 2,00,000 shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities in each period.
