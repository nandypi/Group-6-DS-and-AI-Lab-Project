## 2.20 EARNINGS PER EQUITY SHARE

### Accounting Policy

Basic earnings per equity share is calculated by dividing net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the period.

Diluted earnings per equity share is calculated using the weighted average number of equity shares used for basic earnings per equity share, adjusted for the potential issuance of dilutive equity shares. The adjustment considers proceeds that would have been received if the shares had been issued at fair value, based on the average market value of the outstanding equity shares.

Dilutive potential equity shares are deemed to have been converted at the beginning of the period unless issued later, and are determined independently for each period presented.

The number of equity shares and potentially dilutive equity shares is adjusted retrospectively for share splits and bonus issues, including changes made before the Board of Directors approves the financial statements.

## 2.21 CONTINGENT LIABILITIES AND COMMITMENTS

### Accounting Policy

A contingent liability is either:

- A possible obligation arising from past events whose existence will be confirmed only by the occurrence or non-occurrence of one or more uncertain future events not wholly within the Company's control; or
- A present obligation arising from past events that is not recognized because an outflow of resources is not probable or the amount cannot be measured with sufficient reliability.

### Legal Proceedings

#### Government Investigation

The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) is investigating how the Company classified certain H-1B visa-recipient employees working for one of its clients in immigration documents filed with certain U.S. government authorities. Infosys is discussing the ongoing investigation with the DOJ and has commenced its own inquiry. At this stage, the Company is unable to predict the outcome, including whether it could have a material adverse effect on its business and results of operations.

#### Others

The Company is subject to legal proceedings and claims arising in the ordinary course of business. Management reasonably expects that these matters, when concluded, will not have a material adverse effect on the Company's results of operations or financial condition.
