## 2.12 Income Taxes

### Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income, except for amounts relating to items recognized directly in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts, except when the deferred tax arises from the initial recognition of goodwill or an asset or liability in a transaction that is not a business combination and affects neither accounting nor taxable profit or loss at the transaction date.

Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized as income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment.

Deferred tax assets are recognized to the extent that future taxable profit is probable and deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, where it has a legally enforceable right to set off the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

The interim-period income tax provision is based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions on the exercise of employee share options exceeding the compensation charged to income are credited to equity.

Income tax expense for the three months ended June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2024 includes provisions, net of reversals, of ₹116 crore and ₹60 crore, respectively. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily relate to the adjudication of disputed matters following the filing of tax returns and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

Deferred income tax for the three months ended June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2024 substantially relates to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

The Company's Advanced Pricing Arrangement (APA) with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) for US branch income tax expired in March 2021. The Company has applied for renewal of the APA. US taxable income is currently based on the Company's best estimate determined using the expected value method.

As at June 30, 2025, claims against the Group not acknowledged as debts from the income tax authorities amounted to ₹1,976 crore. As at March 31, 2025, such claims amounted to ₹1,933 crore.

Amounts paid to statutory authorities against tax claims amounted to ₹4,185 crore as at June 30, 2025 and ₹4,199 crore as at March 31, 2025.

The claims primarily represent demands arising from assessment proceedings under the Income Tax Act, 1961. They relate, among other matters, to the disallowance of expenditure on software treated as capital in nature and payments to associated enterprises treated as liable for withholding taxes. These matters are pending before various income tax authorities. Management and its tax advisors expect the Group's position to be upheld on ultimate resolution and do not expect the matters to have a material adverse effect on the Group's financial position and results of operations.

## 2.13 Earnings per equity share

### Accounting Policy

Basic earnings per equity share is computed by dividing net profit attributable to the equity holders of the Group by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the period.

Diluted earnings per equity share is computed using the weighted average number of equity shares used for basic earnings per equity share, adjusted for the weighted average number of equity shares that could have been issued upon conversion of all dilutive potential equity shares. Dilutive potential equity shares are adjusted for the proceeds receivable if the shares had been issued at fair value, defined as the average market value of the outstanding equity shares.

Dilutive potential equity shares are deemed converted at the beginning of the period unless issued at a later date, and are determined independently for each period presented.

The number of equity shares and potentially dilutive equity shares is adjusted retrospectively for all periods presented for share splits and bonus issues, including changes made before the Board of Directors approved the financial statements.

## 2.15 Segment reporting

IFRS 8 Operating Segments establishes requirements for reporting information about operating segments and related disclosures concerning products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group's operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to help clients enhance business performance. The Chief Operating Decision Maker (CODM) evaluates performance and allocates resources based on various performance indicators by business segment. Segment information is presented by business segment, using accounting principles consistently applied to record revenue and expenditure in individual segments.

The Group's business segments primarily comprise:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment combines the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments comprise operating segments of businesses in India, Japan, and China, Infosys Public Services, and other public-services enterprises.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are categorized based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Revenue for all other segments comprises revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other public-services enterprises.

Allocated segment expenses include costs incurred for services provided by the Group's offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized based on the associated efforts of each segment. Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocated because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are disclosed separately as unallocated expenses and adjusted against the Group's total income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group's business are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Management considers it impracticable to provide segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized.

Disclosure of revenue by geographic location is provided in note 2.16, “Revenue from operations.”
