## About Infosys

Infosys is a global provider of digital services and consulting, with more than 320,000 employees serving clients in 59 countries. Its capabilities include cloud and AI-enabled digital transformation, agile digital services, continuous improvement, and digital-skills transfer through its innovation ecosystem.

## Press Release

### Industry-leading Sequential Growth of 2.6% in CC, Driven by Differentiated Value Proposition in Enterprise AI

Infosys reported Q1 revenues of $4,941 million, representing year-over-year growth of 3.8% and sequential growth of 2.6% in constant currency. Operating margin was 20.8%. Free cash flow was $884 million, equal to 109.3% of net profit. Total contract value of large deal wins was $3.8 billion, of which 55% was net new. Return on equity improved by 140 basis points to 30.4%.

### Guidance for FY26:

- Revenue growth of 1%-3% in constant currency
- Operating margin of 20%-22%

### Key highlights:

#### For the quarter ended June 30, 2025

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew by 3.8% year over year and 2.6% quarter over quarter.
- Reported revenues were $4,941 million, representing year-over-year growth of 4.8%.
- Operating margin was 20.8%, a decline of 0.3% year over year and 0.2% quarter over quarter.
- Basic EPS was $0.20, an increase of 5.8% year over year.
- Free cash flow was $884 million, a decline of 19.2% year over year.
- Free cash flow conversion was 109.3% of net profit.
- The company reported that cash-flow conversion was above 100% for the fifth consecutive quarter.
- Currency volatility was managed through the company's hedging strategy.

Large deal total contract value was $3.8 billion, with 55% net new.

## Risks and uncertainties

The release identified risks relating to business-strategy execution, competition for and retention of talent, wage increases, employee reskilling investments, hybrid working, economic and geopolitical conditions, technological disruption including generative AI, immigration and other regulatory changes, ESG commitments, capital allocation, acquisitions, pending litigation, and additional costs or government investigations related to the McCamish cybersecurity incident.
