## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include currency translation, remeasurement of net defined benefit liability or asset, equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income, changes in the fair value of investments, and changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes.

## Currency translation reserve

Exchange differences arising from translating the financial statements of foreign subsidiaries whose functional currency is not the Indian rupee are recognized in other comprehensive income and presented within equity.

## Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the cash flow hedge reserve is transferred to the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecasted transaction occurs.

## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

The Company’s capital management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. To maintain or achieve this structure, the Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares.

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Accordingly, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## Dividend

Final dividends on shares are recorded as a liability on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the Company’s Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, with a corresponding increase to the share options outstanding account.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan) :

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan is 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the 2019 Plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The performance parameters include a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics.

Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares to vest. These instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.
