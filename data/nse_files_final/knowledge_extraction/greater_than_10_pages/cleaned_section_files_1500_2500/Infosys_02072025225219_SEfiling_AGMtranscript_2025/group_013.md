Infosys has made itself an AI-first company, with AI adopted extensively across the organization. AI is now part of almost every client discussion, either as a component of a project or as the central element of an AI-led transformation.

Infosys has delivered 400 generative AI projects and developed more than 200 agents that are working with clients. It has built 10 million lines of code and four small language models covering:

- Financial services
- IT operations
- Cybersecurity

These foundation models help clients apply AI to their own datasets and to Infosys proprietary data. Infosys also has a global partner ecosystem involving AI, SaaS, and cloud-platform providers. It has been recognized as a market leader in nine generative AI studies by market analysts.

Infosys follows a disciplined M&A approach focused on growth areas within its strategic framework, particularly digital capabilities. Recent and past acquisition areas include:

- Cybersecurity
- Energy consulting
- SaaS
- Engineering services

India is a strategic and growing market for Infosys. The company works with Indian government entities, including on income tax and GST initiatives, and with LIC on next-generation digital capabilities. Finacle is used by a majority of banks in India.

For the current year, Infosys provided guidance of 0–3% growth in constant currency. It does not provide growth guidance for the following two years. The company is focused on AI, cloud, cost management, and efficiency to support continued growth.

Infosys regularly evaluates its business plans and investment requirements. New investments are directed toward areas including generative AI, cybersecurity, and engineering services, based on the potential identified in those areas.

Infosys follows a flexible work model combining working from home and working from the office. Office attendance has improved steadily over several quarters and years. Employees are expected to be on campus when required by clients, and in-person work is also used for coaching, teaming, learning, and development. Work-from-home flexibility is provided where appropriate.

The company has opened or expanded offices closer to employees in Nagpur, Hubli, Vizag, Coimbatore, Kolkata, Mumbai, Gandhinagar, and Noida. These offices are smaller than the main centers in Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai but are growing.

Infosys focuses on attracting, retaining, nurturing, training, upskilling, and reskilling employees. Its employee reward programs include incentives, stock programs, retention programs, and profit sharing. Current upskilling efforts include AI, following earlier large-scale digital upskilling during the transition to digital services.

Infosys's subsidiaries are an integral and strategic part of its business plan and are often involved in large client programs. Subsidiary performance is evaluated on a standalone basis and also with regard to downstream performance, offshore performance, and group synergies. Each subsidiary has specific growth and margin plans benchmarked against industry conditions.

For Q1 FY25, utilization was 85.3%. Full-year FY25 utilization was 85.5%. Over the previous five years, utilization excluding trainees ranged from 80% to 88%. Optimal utilization is 84–85%.

Some onsite work is generally associated with lower profitability.

Infosys's ESG commitment includes enabling clients to pursue climate action through:

- AI-powered energy-transition solutions
- Green IT
- Sustainable sourcing
- ESG compliance and reporting
- Smart-building solutions

The company has incorporated smart-building designs across approximately 30 million square feet of office space, certified to the highest level of green certifications.

Infosys manages foreign-exchange exposure through a hedging strategy supported by its treasury team. It hedges various currencies covering a large part of its revenue and balance-sheet exposure. Expenses provide a partial natural hedge in most currencies, supplemented by a combination of forwards and options.

Infosys is actively expanding into Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities to access local talent. Centers have recently been opened in Gandhinagar, Hubli, Coimbatore, Vizag, and Guwahati. These centers use technologies including AI, generative AI, cloud APIs, cybersecurity, and blockchain.

Infosys previously arranged a Mysuru campus visit for shareholders. Any future campus-visit programs will be communicated to shareholders in advance.

Infosys rolled out salary increases during the year, effective in January and April. The timing of the next salary increase will be decided under Project Maximus after considering factors including inflation, attrition, percentages, peer practices, and niche skills.

Project Maximus includes initiatives to move from value-based selling toward a more efficient employee pyramid. These initiatives are intended to improve margins. Infosys improved margins by 50 basis points in the previous year despite various headwinds.
