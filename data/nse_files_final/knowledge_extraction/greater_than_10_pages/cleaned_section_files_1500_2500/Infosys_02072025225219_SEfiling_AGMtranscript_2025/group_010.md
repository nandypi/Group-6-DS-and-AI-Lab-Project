# Shareholder Questions on Governance, EPS, Investor Complaints, and CSR

Shareholders raised the following questions and requests:

- Whether the company would consider resuming bonus payments after 2018–19 and whether such a decision could be made by the board.
- The Company Secretary was asked to explain the resolution of 39 investor complaints during the last quarter, including the reasons for the complaints and proposed solutions.
- Shareholders questioned whether there were shortcomings in corporate governance. The company's BSE code was cited as 500209 and its symbol as INFY.
- The company was asked about its participation in financial services, healthcare, and other sectors, including their contribution to total business.
- Shareholders noted that employee benefits had increased and questioned the related expense.
- Concern was raised about the decline in basic EPS from 65.62 to 61.58, with a request for the reasons for the decrease.
- Shareholders requested that CSR activities address areas of need across India, including water-related initiatives and children's education, with broader regional coverage.
- The company was asked whether it had purchased electoral bonds or made contributions to political parties, and whether any expenditure had been incurred in this direction during the previous two years.
