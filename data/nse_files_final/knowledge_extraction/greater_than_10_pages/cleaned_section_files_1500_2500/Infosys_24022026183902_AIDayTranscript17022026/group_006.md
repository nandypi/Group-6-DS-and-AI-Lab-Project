## Balakrishna D.R

Infosys is developing AI-augmented services by infusing AI into more than 20 traditional services, including application development, testing, modernization, migration, engineering services, and business operations. The company has created detailed playbooks for using AI to transform service delivery.

Infosys works with leading and open-source AI models, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Amazon Nova, DeepSeek, and Llama. It has also created its own coding model. In the Hertz example, a Claude model generated code and an OpenAI model critiqued the output to improve accuracy.

Infosys also works with AI-enabled tools and platforms including GitHub Copilot, Anthropic CLI, Devin from Cognition, Cursor.ai, and Gemini. The company established a GitHub center of excellence two years before the transcript and stated that it remained the number-one global systems integrator for GitHub adoption.

Enterprise AI models often lack knowledge of an organization's context, standards, libraries, and programs. Infosys addresses this by creating MCP registries and enterprise knowledge graphs, which it combines with AI tools and models.

The company has created service-specific agents across the software development lifecycle, including agents for requirements, design, and architecture. It has created 100 such agents for use across its services.

Infosys has also developed tools within its Topaz Fabric to manage large codebases. These tools shrink code and create call graphs showing relationships between programs, helping AI models process enterprise codebases that may contain millions of lines without hallucinating.

Infosys continues to require skilled engineers alongside AI systems. It is training developers to use AI in their daily work and reported that 90% of its developers had been trained on AI. The company also uses specialized roles such as forward deployment engineers and has created centers of excellence for its partnerships.

Infosys stated that its go-to-market approach for large deals increasingly uses working demonstrations rather than presentations.

### Microsoft engagement

Infosys described its relationship with Microsoft as a 360-degree partnership: the companies go to market together, Infosys is a major Microsoft customer, Microsoft is an Infosys customer, and Infosys provides services to Microsoft.

Microsoft is transitioning from enterprise agreements to Master Customer Agreements (MCAs). The stated MCA features include:

- Reduced paperwork compared with enterprise agreements.
- Evergreening of licenses for use over multiple years rather than the two-year timeframe described for enterprise agreements.
- No minimum seat count, compared with a minimum of 250 seats for enterprise agreements.
- Usage monitoring and the ability to adapt usage.
- Usage-based billing and more flexible billing.

Infosys is helping Microsoft build the IT system required to manage MCAs. The engagement uses the AI technologies described above and has delivered 2x developer velocity and a 35% improvement in time to market.

Infosys is also supporting Microsoft's Intelligent Cloud, including Azure and Microsoft Office, which host mission-critical systems for Microsoft customers. AI agents monitor logs, predict issues before they occur, provide triaging intelligence, and route information to the relevant support engineer. Infosys reported a 40% improvement in incident response and a 10x improvement in root-cause-analysis turnaround.

### Danske engagement

Danske's Forward '28 Strategy focuses on modernizing its landscape, improving process efficiency, and creating a digital bank. Infosys is supporting Danske across AI strategy and implementation, including an innovation lab and multiple AI solution workstreams.

Infosys is using Agentic AI for code development and reported generating more than 2 million lines of code. Its engineers validate the generated code, and 97% of the engineers were reported to be using the approach.

Danske wanted to use ChatGPT with enterprise guardrails, so Infosys created an enterprise ChatGPT solution with more than 16,000 users. Infosys also created other solutions for risk and human resources.

## Mohammed Rafee Tarafdar

Infosys Topaz is intended to provide intellectual property and platforms for AI-first and AI-augmented services. The company compares enterprise landscapes to different city maps and describes Topaz as a way to create enterprise “runways” that accelerate AI adoption from pilots to hundreds of projects.

Infosys described five ways in which Topaz supports AI adoption. The first is rapid experimentation and innovation infrastructure. Infosys teams working with clients evaluate developments in AI, identify relevant technologies, build business-relevant proofs of value, and demonstrate potential applications quickly. The company reported operating approximately 39 innovation labs with clients globally.

The second approach is value-centric, end-to-end process redesign. Infosys has more than 25 industry blueprints containing reimagined business and IT workflows. Consulting teams work with clients to understand their problems, use the product discovery and vibing tool from Topaz Fabric to identify solutions, build prototypes, and use exponential engineering to create production-scale applications by the end of the day. This process is used to demonstrate reimagined workflows.

The third approach is an evolvable architecture designed to provide client optionality. Clients can select their preferred model, agent framework, AI platform, AI cloud, and AI-native tools. Infosys stated that it already supports multiple varieties of these technologies in many production deployments.
