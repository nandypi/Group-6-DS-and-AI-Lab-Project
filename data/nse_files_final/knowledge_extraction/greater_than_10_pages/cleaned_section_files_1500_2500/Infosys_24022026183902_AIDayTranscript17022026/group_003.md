## Infosys?

Infosys describes its current AI services opportunity and explains how it is pursuing that opportunity through an AI-first value framework.

Infosys states that it is doing AI work for 90% of its 200 largest clients. The work spans both small and large components of major programs and is not limited to pilots.

The AI value framework consists of six areas:

1. **AI strategy and engineering:** Strategic AI work, including building and orchestrating agents and coordinating agents developed by Infosys, clients, and third parties.
2. **Data for AI:** Preparing enterprise data for AI. Infosys states that large enterprises generally protect their data rather than broadly sharing it with AI foundation-model providers.
3. **Process:** Applying agents to existing business processes, including technology-enabled and non-technology-enabled processes. Customer service is identified as a major area of opportunity.
4. **Legacy modernization:** Moving large legacy organizations from legacy environments to modern environments.
5. **Physical AI:** Embedding AI software into devices.
6. **AI trust:** Cybersecurity, trust, and responsible AI in the development and scaling of IT systems.

Infosys states that these activities represented 5.5% of its revenue in Q3 and were growing at a robust pace.

The six areas are organized internally into 30 offerings and 100 sub-offerings. These offerings are enabled by Infosys engineers, agents built on Topaz, and partnerships with major AI companies. Infosys states that its client discussions use the six-area framework together with the 30 offerings and 100 sub-offerings, with applications including revenue growth, cost optimization, and innovation.

Infosys describes its AI services capabilities as including:

- Understanding of clients’ business and technology landscapes
- Domain knowledge
- Engineering talent and training
- Platforms and intellectual property developed by Infosys
- Agents ready for deployment
- Support for clients building their own platforms
- A partnership ecosystem involving both established and newer large and small partners

Infosys estimates, based on external analysis, that the opportunity across the six AI services areas could reach between $300 billion and $400 billion by 2030. It also acknowledges that AI-driven productivity could compress the IT services market, while stating that the overall opportunity is expected to be large.

The company’s AI services playbook includes:

- **AI-first services:** New services designed around AI.
- **AI-augmented services:** Existing services infused with AI.
- **Foundational components:** Platforms, intellectual property, Topaz Fabric, and deployable agents.
- **Partnership ecosystem:** Partnerships supporting delivery and go-to-market activities.
- **Talent and culture:** Reskilling and recruitment for the AI era.
- **Brand:** Maintaining and strengthening Infosys’s market brand and connections with CEO-level clients.

Infosys states that it is undertaking a substantial reskilling effort and continuing recruitment. It recruited 20,000 college graduates during the current year through March and planned to recruit another 20,000 college graduates in the next financial year.
