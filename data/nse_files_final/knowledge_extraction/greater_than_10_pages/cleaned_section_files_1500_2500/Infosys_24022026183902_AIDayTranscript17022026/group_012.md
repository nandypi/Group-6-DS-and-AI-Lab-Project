# AI Transformation Opportunities and Partnership Ecosystem

Infosys sees significant opportunity in bringing AI into its business process management and IT stack. Its AI Next platform is available to clients seeking a full AI platform.

AI Next has been used by a leading restaurant chain to accelerate vendor invoice processing. In another client example, AI Next played a significant role in accelerating the supply chain.

Infosys also identifies legacy modernization as a major opportunity. Infosys and its partners have developed accelerators to help clients move from legacy architectures to AI architectures more quickly. One example is a mainframe modernization program completed for Hertz.

Physical AI is another area of development, involving the use of AI in robotics and other physical products.

Infosys acknowledges that AI transformation requires complex execution across multiple components. It has invested in building a partnership ecosystem intended to support clients through this transformation.

Infosys also announced a partnership with Anthropic. It identifies trust, scale, and partnerships as three pillars of AI-led transformation.
