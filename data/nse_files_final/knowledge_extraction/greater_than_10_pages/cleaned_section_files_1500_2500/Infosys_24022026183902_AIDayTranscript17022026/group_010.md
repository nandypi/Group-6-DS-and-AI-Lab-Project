Infosys is building enterprise-level agentic AI platforms intended to change client workflows and enable AI agents to work alongside employees. The company has completed more than 15 such implementations.

Infosys is also modernizing clients’ legacy systems using AI. These systems may have been in place for 15, 20, or 30 years. In one large airline implementation, Infosys deployed agentic AI to modernize systems, improve customer experience, and predict whether baggage would be delivered to customers on time.

In a mega deal with BP, Infosys supported enterprise-wide AI adoption across the value chain, including:

- Production and optimization
- Dynamic pricing in retail and convenience stores
- Contract automation for thousands of contracts
- IT operations
- Corporate functions

Infosys identified more than 50 AI and agentic AI initiatives for BP and used Azure Foundry, the OpenAI stack, and GitHub Copilot for developer productivity.

Reported outcomes included:

- 18% year-one improvement in IT operations efficiency
- 50% faster contract validation
- 95% payment accuracy

For the largest oil and gas upstream operator in Australia, Infosys built an enterprise AI platform covering production and operations, contracting, procurement, finance, HR, and IT operations. More than 16 high-value AI use cases were identified.

Amazon Bedrock was used for agentic AI in upstream functions, while Azure OpenAI Foundry was used for corporate functions. The implementation included an enterprise-grade platform and agents supporting:

- Operational intelligence and insights
- Employee experience improvement
- Asset operations

Reported results included 20% to 35% efficiency gains in the upstream value chain and 15% to 20% productivity gains in employee experience.

Infosys described the approach as an outcome-focused Responsible AI framework intended to operate at scale and speed.
