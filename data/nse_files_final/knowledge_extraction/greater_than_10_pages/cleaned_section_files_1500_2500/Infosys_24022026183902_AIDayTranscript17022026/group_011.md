## Ambeshwar Nath

## Infosys'

AI is increasingly embedded in clients' operations, with applications focused on revenue growth, efficiency, and customer value.

In consumer goods, Infosys is seeing AI used for:

- Precision revenue growth management, using near-real-time ingestion of demand, price, promotion, and channel data to identify whether value is being created or lost. This has the potential to improve overall gross profit by 3% to 5%.
- Hyper-personalized marketing and individualized consumer campaigns.
- AI-based planogramming compliance.

In retail, large language models are being applied to loyalty programs. Infosys stated that, as of 2026, 70% of the loyalty programs it engages with will have an AI component. Other applications include camera vision for store operations and agentic commerce.

In logistics, AI is being used for demand sensing, route optimization, and waste reduction, resulting in overall cost reductions of 15% to 30%.

Infosys described its capabilities as combining domain expertise, data expertise, AI technology capabilities, and governance to deliver business outcomes.

### Ralph Lauren

Ralph Lauren is a high-end fashion and apparel company pursuing the intersection of fashion and technology. Its objectives include enabling online styling similar to in-store stylist interactions, delivering hyper-personalization at scale, and connecting recommendations to real-time inventory to reduce the path to purchase.

Infosys and Microsoft jointly worked with Ralph Lauren to:

- Ingest 50 years of Ralph Lauren archives and lookbooks.
- Build a natural language processing capability that allowed consumers to interact with the AI in a conversational manner.
- Provide personalized outfit recommendations for specific occasions.
- Connect recommendations to inventory so that orders could be placed through a more immediate process.

The engagement resulted in:

- A 50% increase in styling-query interactions.
- A 12.2% increase in revenue for Ralph Lauren, with a significant contribution from online and digital capabilities.

### Posti

Posti is described as Finland's logistics leader and a legacy organization with 400 years of history and a legacy of over 40 years. Its operations have expanded from postal services into parcels, supply chain, warehousing, and end-to-end logistics.

As postal volumes decline across the industry, Posti is focused on developing new-age capabilities, reducing legacy-related costs, and shifting toward growth initiatives in the Nordic market.

Infosys is Posti's end-to-end IT services partner and has implemented an AI-first operating model across both run and transform activities. Infosys is running more than 20 AI initiatives across Posti's postal, parcel, and freight businesses, covering the value chain.

Posti is working with multiple AI toolsets, with Infosys acting as the single partner orchestrating them to deliver outcomes. Reported results include:

- 50% of software generation performed by AI tools.
- 35% improvement in productivity.
- 70% improvement in mean time to recover across legacy systems.

## Infosys

Infosys described execution risk, rather than the size of the opportunity, as a potential constraint for IT services companies adopting AI. It has consequently reworked its partner strategy and built an AI partner ecosystem covering the AI stack, including chips, applications, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and models.

Infosys announced a partnership with Anthropic. The company stated that it needs partnerships with model companies and infrastructure companies investing in AI transformation.

Infosys described its role as orchestrating AI outcomes for clients and managing risks arising from both its own work and its partner ecosystem. The partnerships are curated to support specific industry workloads rather than provide only general-purpose capabilities. Infosys is using completed AI programs as referenceable templates for deployment with additional clients.

Examples include:

- With a leading investment-management firm, Infosys helped implement an NVIDIA stack that accelerated investor onboarding by approximately 30%. The firm has more than 5,000 rules and regulations.
- With a leading telecommunications company, Infosys created a digital sales assistant through which digital agents completed 95% of fiber sales instead of human-powered selling.
- Infosys has delivered work to prepare client data for AI by moving it into easily retrievable and curated environments that can generate insights.
