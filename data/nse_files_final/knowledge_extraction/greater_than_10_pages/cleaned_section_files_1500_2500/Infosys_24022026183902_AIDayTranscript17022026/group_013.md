## Shaji Mathew

Infosys’ AI transformation strategy is built on three pillars:

1. A new talent operating model to enable employees to use AI while developing deep engineering and domain expertise.
2. A new career architecture for an ambidextrous organization.
3. Development of a future-ready workforce through AI-focused training and enablement.

### AI talent development

Infosys is developing deep expertise through:

- External hiring, including recruitment of fresh engineers with salaries of up to INR 21 lakh per annum.
- Recruitment from leading engineering colleges, including IITs and NITs, using a differentiated assessment process.
- Increased focus on full-stack engineers.
- Hiring domain experts from the market.
- Internal development through hands-on bridge programs using sandbox environments.

After completing a bridge program, employees are assessed through an assessment center of excellence. The assessment uses a five-point scale and includes AI-led simulations.

Infosys’ Capability Quotient (CQ) assesses employee skills across four dimensions:

- Technology
- Domain
- Foundational skills
- Societal skills

### Business incubator program

Infosys’ business incubator series identifies AI-focused ideas from within the company. The previous program generated 1,000 ideas. Selected ideas receive organizational funding and mentorship to develop entrepreneurial skills and deep AI engineering capabilities, potentially resulting in platforms and systems.

### Career architecture

Infosys is moving from a traditional, unidimensional career architecture to a Y-shaped model.

- One branch consists of employees enabled to use AI across the company.
- The other branch consists of employees who complete bridge programs, pass assessments, and move into specialist roles focused on deep engineering or domain expertise.
- An expert-led organization provides a flatter structure for highly experienced engineering specialists, including distinguished technology engineer roles. These experts act as catalysts for accelerators supporting the broader specialist ecosystem.

The career architecture also addresses the human-plus-AI work model, in which humans and AI agents perform different parts of the work. Infosys is redesigning job roles and introducing roles such as AI strategists and responsible AI engineers.

### Workforce enablement

Infosys describes three broad workforce categories:

- **AI consumers:** Employees across functions who use AI. Approximately 90% of the organization is enabled on AI.
- **AI builders:** Employees who develop AI models and AI agents and require deeper expertise.
- **AI masters:** Employees who work with large language models, develop small language models, and set AI direction and vision.

Forward deployed engineers are embedded in client organizations and work at the intersection of consulting, technology, and client context. They receive specialized enablement and are assessed through a five-point mechanism that includes AI-led simulations and case-study-based assessments.

The Global Education Center in Mysore has revamped its foundation program for campus recruits to include AI-based training from the beginning of their employment.

Infosys is also conducting leadership AI programs:

- An AI immersion program for executive vice presidents at the Mysore campus in collaboration with Harvard Business University.
- A similar program for senior vice presidents at the Mysore campus in collaboration with MIT during the preceding month.

## Sumit Virmani

Infosys uses strategic partnerships as part of its brand and AI strategy. These partnerships involve platforms associated with major global brands and focus on the intersection of passion and executive participation.

Infosys aims to use these platforms not only for brand visibility but also to apply its technology to improve the end-consumer experience. The partnerships are intended to demonstrate Infosys’ AI capabilities to a broad global audience.

Brand Finance ranked Infosys as the fastest-growing brand in its category worldwide for the last six years.

Infosys has also been included in Kantar BrandZ’s Top 100 brands for several consecutive years, across categories and beyond the IT industry.
