## PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

The Public Announcement concerned the attention of equity shareholders and beneficial owners of equity shares of Infosys Limited in relation to a buyback under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended.

The Buyback was proposed on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. It applied to fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each, based on the records made available to the Company by the depositories, with November 14, 2025 identified as the Record Date.

## 1. DETAILS OF THE BUYBACK OFFER AND BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

- Buyback price: ₹1,800 per Equity Share.
- Maximum aggregate consideration: up to ₹18,000 crore.
- Indicative number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back: approximately 2.41% of the total number of Equity Shares in the Company's paid-up equity share capital on a standalone basis.
- Consideration: payable in cash to eligible shareholders.
- Record Date: November 14, 2025.

The Buyback allocation policy stated that the Buyback was expected to create long-term value for shareholders through a reduction in the equity base. The policy also referred to capital allocation through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and regulatory approvals.

## 4. MAXIMUM AMOUNT REQUIRED UNDER THE PAID-UP CAPITAL AND FREE RESERVES & SOURCES OF FUNDS FROM WHICH BUYBACK WILL BE FINANCED

The amount required for the Buyback was stated to aggregate ₹18,000 crore, being less than 25% of the financial statements of the Company as at June 30, 2025 on both a standalone and consolidated basis.

The Buyback was to be funded out of the Company's free reserves and securities premium account. The disclosure stated that funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, would not be used for the Buyback.

The amount required included applicable taxes and related expenses, including goods and services tax, stamp duty, securities transaction tax, brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, and other incidental expenses.

## 5. BUYBACK PRICE AND BASIS OF ARRIVING AT THE BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

The Equity Shares were proposed to be bought back at ₹1,800 per Equity Share.

The Buyback Price was described as being determined after considering:

- Recent volume-weighted average market prices of the Equity Shares on the BSE and NSE, where the Equity Shares were listed.
- The Company's financial parameters.
- The potential impact of the Buyback on earnings per Equity Share.

The disclosure stated that, in accordance with Regulation 5(vi)(a) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board or Buyback Committee could increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, provided there was no change in the total Buyback size.
