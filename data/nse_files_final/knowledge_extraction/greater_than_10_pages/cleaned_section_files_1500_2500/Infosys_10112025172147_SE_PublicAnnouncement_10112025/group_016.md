## 13. PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY TO BE ADOPTED FOR BUYBACK

### 13.1

The buyback is open to all eligible shareholders, including ADS holders who submit the desired number of ADSs to the Depositary and withdraw the underlying equity shares, and beneficial owners of the Company holding equity shares in physical or electronic form as of the Record Date.

### 13.2

The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis, subject to the terms and conditions prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and applicable SEBI circulars. A reservation for small shareholders will be made in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

The purchase and settlement of equity shares will be carried out through the stock-exchange mechanism, with Kotak Securities Limited acting as the Company’s registered broker.

### 13.4

The Company may request the stock exchanges to provide the acquisition window. Details of the acquisition window will be specified by the Indian Stock Exchanges from time to time.

### 13.6

Eligible shareholders whose stock broker is not registered with BSE or NSE as a trading member or stock broker may approach any BSE- or NSE-registered stock broker to place their bids, subject to applicable law.

Eligible shareholders may also approach the Company’s Broker to place bids, subject to completion of know-your-customer requirements and submission of details required by the Company’s Broker.

### 13.8

The cumulative quantity tendered will be made available on the Indian Stock Exchanges’ websites throughout the Tendering Period and will be updated at specific intervals.

### 13.9

The Company will not accept equity shares where the title to the shares is under dispute or otherwise not clear. In accordance with Regulation 24(v) of the Buyback Regulations, the Company will not buy back locked-in equity shares until the lock-in expires or the shares become transferable.

### 13.10

Non-resident shareholders must comply with reporting requirements under FEMA and any other applicable rules, regulations, and guidelines issued by the RBI for remittance of funds.

## 13.11. Procedure to be followed for Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialized form

- Eligible shareholders must tender their equity shares through their respective stock broker(s), indicating the number of equity shares they intend to tender under the buyback.
- The stock broker(s) must place an order or bid on behalf of the eligible shareholder(s).
- Upon placing the bid, the stock broker(s) will provide a Transaction Registration Slip (TRS) generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS will contain details of the order submitted.
- The relevant number of shares will be marked in the eligible shareholder’s demat account for the shares tendered in the Tender Offer. Details will be provided by the depositories to the Clearing Corporations, namely Indian Clearing Corporation Limited or NSE Clearing Limited, as applicable.
- Where the shareholder’s demat account is held with one depository and the account is sourced from the other depository, the shareholder must provide an inter-depository tender offer instruction to transfer the shares from the free balance to the tender offer account. The instruction must be initiated by the eligible shareholder at the source depository.
- For confirmed custodian orders, the custodian stock broker(s) must provide confirmation or modification of orders no later than the date specified by the Indian Stock Exchanges on the last day of the Tendering Period. Orders not confirmed will be rejected, and custodian confirmation will be revoked.
- Eligible shareholders must provide all relevant documents necessary to establish transferability of the equity shares tendered. These documents may include:
  - A duly attested death certificate and succession certificate or legal-heirship certificate where an eligible shareholder has died.
  - Necessary certified corporate authorizations, including board and/or general meeting resolutions, in the case of companies.
- For dematerialized equity shares, submission of a tender form and TRS is not required. After receipt of the dematerialized equity shares by the Clearing Corporation and a valid bid in the exchange bidding system, the buyback will be deemed to have been accepted for settlement.
- Eligible shareholders must keep their DP account active and unblocked to receive credit for accepted buyback shares and must keep the bank account linked to the DP account active and updated to receive the remittance due.
- If the security transfer instruction is rejected in the depository system, or if excess or unaccepted dematerialized equity shares are returned, the relevant securities will be transferred back to the eligible shareholder’s demat account. In the case of custodian participant orders, excess or unaccepted shares will be transferred to the custodian participant’s depository pool account.
