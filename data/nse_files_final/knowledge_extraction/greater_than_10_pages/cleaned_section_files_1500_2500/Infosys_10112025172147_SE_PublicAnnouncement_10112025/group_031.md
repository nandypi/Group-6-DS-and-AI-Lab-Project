## 2. NECESSITY FOR THE BUYBACK AND DETAILS THEREOF

### 2.1 Rationale and cash requirements

The Buyback is being undertaken by the Company after taking into account its strategic and operational cash needs in the medium term, including:

- Expected cash requirements towards working capital, investments, capital expenditure, and other areas.
- Investments required for execution of the Company’s strategy.
- Minimum cash required for contingencies or unforeseen events, and other significant developments requiring cash investments.
- Funds required for acquisitions that the Board may approve.

The Company’s expected cash generation and strong balance sheet position allow it to reward its members while retaining sufficient capital for business requirements. Since 2017, regular and special dividends have enabled the Company to distribute surplus cash to shareholders in a predictable manner.

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had consolidated cash and investments of ₹45,204 crore. This comprises cash and cash equivalents and current and non-current investments, excluding investments in equity and preference shares and others.

### 2.2 Purpose and expected effects of the Buyback

The Buyback is being undertaken for the following reasons:

- To return surplus cash to members in line with the stated Capital Allocation Policy.
- To provide all members on the record date an option to receive cash for their Equity Shares and increase their percentage shareholding in the Company after the Buyback without additional investment.
- To improve return on equity through distribution of cash and improve earnings per share through reduction in the equity base over the long term, thereby potentially increasing members’ value.

The Buyback is being implemented through the Tender Offer route. The number of shares eligible for reservation for Small Shareholders would be the higher of:

- The number of shares entitled as per their shareholding; or
- 15% of the number of shares to be bought back, as per Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations.

The Company believes that a significant number of its public shareholders would be classified as “Small Shareholders.”

### 2.3 Buyback price premiums

As per the stated Capital Allocation Policy, the following premiums were disclosed:

- Premium of 15.80% and 16.99% to the volume-weighted average market price of the Equity Shares on BSE and stock exchanges regarding the proposal of the Buyback being considered at the Board Meeting.
- Premium of 20.78% and 20.67% to the volume-weighted average market price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, during the four-week period ending September 2025.
- Premium of 25.64% and 25.62% over the closing price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, on September 8, 2025, being the date of intimation to the stock exchanges regarding the proposal of the Buyback being considered at the Board Meeting.
