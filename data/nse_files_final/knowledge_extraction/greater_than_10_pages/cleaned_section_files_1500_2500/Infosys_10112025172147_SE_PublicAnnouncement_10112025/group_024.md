## 11.2. Buyback Price and Foreign Exchange Considerations

The Buyback Price will be paid in Indian rupees. Fluctuations in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and the U.S. dollar will affect the U.S. dollar equivalent of the Buyback Price and the Buyback amount. ADS holders are urged to obtain current exchange-rate information before making a decision regarding the Buyback.

### Withdrawal and Re-Deposit of Equity Shares by ADS Holders

ADS holders who withdraw Equity Shares underlying their ADSs should consider the applicable procedures and potential tax implications before requesting withdrawal or re-deposit.

Withdrawal and re-deposit require an Indian demat account, Subtype DR, and a brokerage account. ADS holders should contact the bank, broker, or other nominee with whom they intend to establish or maintain these accounts, and the depositary, to understand the implications of withdrawing and re-depositing Equity Shares.

The Company cannot assure ADS holders that Equity Shares withdrawn during the relevant 30-day period, or withdrawn before the ADS postal-ballot cut-off date or after the last day of the Tendering Period, will be able to be re-deposited against the creation of ADSs. Any applicable creation fee for issuing ADSs representing such Equity Shares may not be waived if the required deposit or instruction does not occur within the specified period.

### a. India

Although there are arguments that the withdrawal of Equity Shares underlying ADSs should not be subject to Indian capital-gains tax, this position is not free from doubt because the law is not clear on the issue. The Company cannot assure ADS holders that tax will not be imposed.

The bank, broker, or other nominee in India may withhold taxes in connection with the re-deposit of Equity Shares. ADS holders should consult their legal, financial, and tax advisors regarding the potential implications of withdrawing and re-depositing Equity Shares.

### b. United States

For U.S. federal income tax purposes, U.S. holders are generally treated as the owners of the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs. Accordingly, withdrawing Equity Shares underlying ADSs and re-depositing Equity Shares that were not tendered in the Buyback, or that were tendered but not accepted in the Buyback, will generally not be subject to U.S. federal income tax consequences relevant to U.S. holders.

Each ADS holder should consult their own tax advisor regarding the U.S. federal, state, and local tax consequences of withdrawing and re-depositing Equity Shares and participating in the Buyback.

Additional information on tax treatment relevant to ADS holders is expected to be set forth in the Letter of Offer distributed in connection with the Buyback. ADS holders requiring additional information should consult their own legal, financial, and tax advisors, including regarding regulatory approvals and tax considerations in India, the United States, and other relevant jurisdictions.
