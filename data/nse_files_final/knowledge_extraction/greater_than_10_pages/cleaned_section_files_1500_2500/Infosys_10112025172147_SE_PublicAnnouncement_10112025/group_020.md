# Buyback of Equity Shares

## 1.1

The Board of Directors approved the buyback of fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each from the Company’s shareholders, subject to applicable approvals, permissions, and sanctions.

The buyback will be implemented through the tender offer route on a proportionate basis, pursuant to a special resolution approved through postal ballot. The proposed buyback is for up to 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares at ₹1,800 per equity share, for an aggregate amount of up to ₹18,000 crore.

Based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025, the buyback represents 2.41% of the total paid-up equity share capital and 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate of fully paid-up equity share capital and free reserves on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.

The buyback will be conducted in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the Company’s Articles of Association, applicable SEBI circulars, and other applicable laws and regulations.

## 1.2

Because the buyback exceeds 10% of the total paid-up equity share capital and free reserves of the Company, shareholder approval through a special resolution was required under the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 5(b) of the Buyback Regulations.

The buyback was approved through the special resolution set out in the postal ballot notice dated September 25, 2025.

## 1.4

The buyback offer size excludes expenses and transaction costs incurred or to be incurred in connection with the buyback, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, and applicable taxes such as securities transaction tax.

## 1.5

The Company is required to register its securities under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended. Because of conflicting Indian and U.S. regulatory requirements applicable to the tender offer, the Company sought and obtained exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on certain aspects of the buyback procedures.

## 1.6

The equity shares are traded under the following codes:

| Stock exchange | Trading code |
|---|---|
| BSE Limited | 500209 |
| National Stock Exchange of India Limited | INFY |

The ISIN of the equity shares is INE009A01021. The Company’s American Depositary Shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

## 1.7

The buyback will be implemented using the mechanism for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange pursuant to tender offers, takeovers, buybacks, and delisting, as set out in:

- SEBI Circular CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015
- SEBI Circular CFD/DCR2/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/DCR-II/CIR/P/15 dated August 13, 2021
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-2/P/CIR/2023/35

These circulars apply as amended from time to time.

## 1.8

The equity shares will be bought back on a proportionate basis from eligible shareholders through the tender offer route under the Buyback Regulations and applicable laws.

The record date for determining eligible shareholders is November 14, 2025. Eligible shareholders include holders of the Company’s ADSs who cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares before the record date.

Tendering will take place through the stock exchange mechanism specified by SEBI. The Company will request BSE and NSE to provide the acquisition window for facilitating participation in the buyback. BSE will be the designated stock exchange.

## 1.9

Participation in the buyback may attract income tax on the consideration received by eligible shareholders.

## 1.10

If an eligible shareholder is a U.S. holder, as defined in the Company’s Form 6-K filed with the SEC, an exchange of equity shares for cash pursuant to the buyback will generally be a taxable transaction for U.S. federal income tax purposes.

Depending on the applicable U.S. federal income tax laws and the U.S. holder’s particular circumstances, the transaction may generally be treated either as a realization of gain or loss from the disposition of equity shares or as a distribution from the Company under U.S. federal income tax laws.

U.S. holders should review the tax considerations in the Company’s Form 6-K and the Letter of Offer and consult their own tax advisers regarding the applicability and effect of U.S. federal, state, local, and non-U.S. tax laws, as well as estate, gift, and inheritance laws.

## 1.11

The buyback of equity shares from non-resident members of the Company, including foreign corporate bodies, erstwhile Overseas Corporate Bodies, Foreign Institutional Investors, Foreign Portfolio Investors, members of foreign nationality, and ADS holders whose underlying equity shares are withdrawn, will be subject to the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, the Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014, and the rules and regulations made under them, as applicable.

Participation may require approvals from the relevant authorities, including the Reserve Bank of India under FEMA, and will also be subject to applicable income-tax laws.

## 1.12

The buyback will be undertaken on a proportionate basis from all eligible shareholders as at the record date.

A reservation of up to 5% of the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of equity shares to which small shareholders are entitled based on their holding, whichever is higher, will apply to small shareholders.

For this purpose, small shareholders are eligible shareholders who hold equity shares of market value not exceeding the higher of:

- ₹2,00,000; or
- the closing price on the Indian stock exchanges registering the higher trading volume on the record date.
