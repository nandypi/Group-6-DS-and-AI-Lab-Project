## NECESSITY FOR THE BUYBACK AND DETAILS THEREOF

The buyback is being undertaken as part of the Company’s capital allocation policy and in view of its expected cash generation and strong balance sheet position.

The Company stated that cash may be required for:

- Working capital, development, technology, and infrastructure.
- Potential acquisitions approved by the Board.
- Investments required to execute the Company’s strategy.
- Minimum cash requirements for contingencies or unforeseen events.
- Other significant developments requiring cash investments.

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had consolidated cash and investments comprising cash and cash equivalents, current preference shares, and other investments of ₹45,204 crore.

The buyback is intended to return surplus cash to members. It is also expected to improve return on equity through cash distribution and improve earnings per share through a reduction in the equity base.

The buyback is being implemented through a tender offer. The Company stated that the route would involve allocating to Small Shareholders the higher of:

- The number of shares entitled to them based on their shareholding; or
- 15% of the number of shares proposed to be bought back, based on the number of public shareholders classified as Small Shareholders.

The buyback gives members the option to tender their equity shares for cash or retain their shares and increase their percentage shareholding in the Company after the buyback.

Under its stated Capital Allocation Policy, effective from financial year 2025, the Company expects to continue returning approximately 5% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period, excluding cash dividends, through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and required approvals. The Company also expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share.

## 4. MAXIMUM AMOUNT BUYBACK PAID UP CAPITAL AND FREE ANDITS PERCENTAGE REQUIRED RESERVES&amp; OF THE TOTAL UNDERTHE SOURCES OF FUNDS FROM WHICH BUYBACK WILL BE FINANCED

The amount required for the buyback will aggregate to ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only), representing 24.31% and 21.6% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves of the Company on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.

The amount is less than 25% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves of the Company, based on the latest audited condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis.

The buyback will be funded out of the Company’s free reserves and securities premium account, or such other sources as may be permitted under the Buyback Regulations or the Companies Act. Borrowed funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, will not be used for the buyback.

The buyback offer size excludes expenses and transaction costs, including public-announcement and publication expenses, applicable taxes, securities transaction tax, brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, goods and services tax, stamp duty, and other incidental and related expenses.

## 5. BUYBACK PRICE AND BASIS OF ARRIVING AT THE BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

The Company proposes to buy back its equity shares at ₹1,800 per share (Rupees One Thousand Eight Hundred only).

The buyback price was considered with reference to market trends, the volume-weighted average market prices of the equity shares on the BSE and NSE, price-earnings ratio, the impact on other financial parameters, and the possible impact of the buyback on earnings per equity share.

Subject to there being no change in the buyback offer size, the Board or Buyback Committee may increase the buyback price and decrease the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back up to one working day before the Record Date.

The buyback price represents the following premiums:

- 15.60% and 16.99% over the volume-weighted average market prices of the equity shares on the BSE and NSE, respectively, during the three-month period ending September 2025.
- 20.78% and 20.67% over the volume-weighted average market prices of the equity shares on the BSE and NSE, respectively, during the two-week period ending September 8, 2025.
- 25.64% and 25.62% over the closing prices of the equity shares on the BSE and NSE, respectively, on September 5, 2025.
