## 14.8. In relation to the physical Equity Shares

- Equity Shares bought back in dematerialized form will be transferred directly to the Company’s demat account, as indicated by the Company’s Broker, or transferred by the Company’s Broker to the Company Demat Account upon receipt of the Equity Shares through the clearing and settlement mechanism of the Indian Stock Exchanges.
- If physical Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders are not accepted, the share certificates will be returned to the relevant Eligible Shareholders by registered post or ordinary post or courier.
- If only part of the physical shares tendered by an Eligible Shareholder is accepted in the Buyback, the RTA will retain the original share certificate and deface it with a stamp or endorsement indicating the number of Equity Shares accepted. For the unaccepted physical Equity Shares, the Company will issue a Letter of Confirmation (“LOC”) instead of a split share certificate, in accordance with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD_RTAMB/P/CIR/2022/8 dated January 25, 2022, as amended.
- The LOC will be dispatched to the address registered with the RTA. The Eligible Shareholder must submit a dematerialization request within the period specified in the LOC. If the dematerialization request is not submitted within that period, the Company will instead transfer the unaccepted Equity Shares to a separate demat account of the Company opened for this purpose. Such transfer will be at the Eligible Shareholder’s sole risk.
- Eligible Shareholders should ensure that their depository account remains maintained until all formalities relating to the Buyback are completed.
- The Stock Broker will issue a contract note for Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback. The Company’s Broker will also issue a contract note to the Company for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
- Eligible Shareholders should consult their respective stockbroker regarding payment of costs, applicable taxes, charges, and expenses, including brokerage, levied by the stockbroker. These costs will be incurred solely by the Eligible Shareholders.
- Equity Shares lying to the credit of the Company Demat Account and Equity Shares held in physical form will be extinguished in accordance with the procedure prescribed in the Buyback Regulations.

## 15. RECORD DATE, OFFER PERIOD AND SHAREHOLDER ENTITLEMENT

### 15.1. Record Date and Tendering Period

The Company has fixed November 14, 2025 as the record date (“Record Date”) for determining the entitlement and the names of Equity Shareholders eligible to participate in the Buyback.

The tendering period for the Buyback will commence from November 20, 2025 and remain open for five working days, until November 26, 2025 (“Tendering Period”).

### 15.2. Letter of Offer

Eligible Shareholders will receive a Letter of Offer along with a tender offer form indicating their entitlement to participate in the Buyback. An Eligible Shareholder may participate and tender shares even if the Letter of Offer and tender form are not received.

The Letter of Offer will be dispatched electronically in accordance with the provisions of the Act within two working days from the Record Date. If an Eligible Shareholder in the U.S. has not registered an email address with the Depositories, or requests a physical form, the physical form will be couriered to the shareholder’s registered postal address available with the Company.

### 15.3. Eligibility of ADS Holders

All Equity Shareholders of the Company as on the Record Date will be eligible to participate in the Buyback. Holders of ADSs of the Company will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback.

To participate, ADS holders must cancel all or the desired portion of their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date. ADS holders may re-deposit any Equity Shares that are not tendered, or that are tendered but not accepted, for the creation of ADSs.

### 15.4. Buyback Categories and Entitlement

The Equity Shares proposed to be bought back will be divided between:

- the reserved category for Small Shareholders; and
- the general category for all other Eligible Shareholders.

The Buyback Entitlement of each Eligible Shareholder will be calculated based on the number of Equity Shares held by the shareholder as on the Record Date and the Buyback ratio applicable to the category to which the shareholder belongs.

The promoters and members of the promoter group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback. Accordingly, the Equity Shares held by them will be excluded for computing the Buyback Entitlement, and Eligible Shareholders may have a higher Buyback Entitlement ratio.

### 15.5. Small Shareholders

A “Small Shareholder” is a shareholder who holds Equity Shares of market value not more than ₹2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs only), based on the closing market price on the stock exchange where the highest trading volume in the Equity Shares was recorded as of the Record Date.

In accordance with the proviso to Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of Equity Shares entitled as per the shareholding of Small Shareholders as on the Record Date, whichever is higher, will be reserved for Small Shareholders.

### 15.6. Treatment of Joint Holdings and Institutional Holdings

In accordance with Regulation 9(ix) of the Buyback Regulations, the Company will aggregate Equity Shares held by Small Shareholders with a common PAN for determining the category and entitlement.

In the case of joint shareholding, Equity Shares held by joint shareholders with a common PAN will be aggregated. Where the PANs of all joint shareholders are not available, the Company will check the sequence of the names of the joint holders and aggregate the Equity Shares held in that sequence.

Equity Shares held by investors such as mutual funds, pension funds, trusts, and insurance companies under different schemes or clients will be considered separately for determining entitlement.

### 15.7. Voluntary Participation and Additional Shares

Participation in the Buyback is voluntary. Eligible Shareholders may:

- participate in part or in full;
- receive cash for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback; or
- choose not to participate and retain their Equity Shares without any additional investment.

Eligible Shareholders may also tender Additional Equity Shares above their Buyback Entitlement and participate in any shortfall created by the non-participation of other Eligible Shareholders.

### 15.8. Acceptance of Shares Tendered Above Entitlement

After accepting Equity Shares tendered on the basis of the applicable entitlement, Equity Shares tendered over and above the entitlement in one category may be accepted from Eligible Shareholders who have tendered above their entitlement in the other category, in accordance with the procedure set out in the Buyback Regulations.

### 15.9. Maximum Tender

The maximum tender by an Eligible Shareholder cannot exceed the number of Equity Shares held by that Eligible Shareholder as on the Record Date.

If an Eligible Shareholder holds Equity Shares through multiple demat accounts, the tender through each demat account cannot exceed the number of Equity Shares held in that demat account as on the Record Date.

### 15.10. Tender Procedure

Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders, whether within their entitlement or as Additional Equity Shares, will be accepted in accordance with the procedure prescribed under the Buyback Regulations.

Settlement of tenders under the Buyback will be conducted through the stock exchange mechanism for acquisition of shares through tender offers.

Detailed instructions for participating in the Buyback, together with the relevant timetable, will be included in the Letter of Offer and tender offer form. The Letter of Offer and tender offer form will be sent to Eligible Shareholders in due course.
