## 14. METHOD OF SETTLEMENT

### 14.1.

Settlement of trades will be carried out through the mechanism prescribed by the Indian Stock Exchanges and the Clearing Corporation.

### 14.2.

The Company will transfer the consideration pertaining to the Buyback, net of tax deducted at source, to the Clearing Corporation through the Company's Broker. The Clearing Corporation will make the direct fund pay-out to the bank account linked to the demat account or bank account of each Eligible Shareholder holding Equity Shares in dematerialized form.

If the fund transfer instruction is rejected by the RBI or the relevant bank, the funds will be transferred to the concerned Stock Brokers' settlement accounts for onward transfer to the respective Eligible Shareholders.

### 14.3.

For certain client types, including NRIs and foreign clients, where RBI or other regulatory requirements apply to settlement accounts, the relevant client-type details will be collected from the Depositories. Funds will be released through the applicable mechanism prescribed by the Indian Stock Exchanges and the Clearing Corporation.

### 14.4.

For Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form, fund pay-out will be made through the respective Stock Brokers' settlement accounts for release to the respective Eligible Shareholders.

### 14.5.

Details regarding shareholders' entitlement for the Tender Offer and blocked shares in demat accounts will be provided to the Clearing Corporations. On the settlement date, the Clearing Corporations will cancel excess or unaccepted shares and transfer the accepted shares to their settlement accounts.

### 14.6.

In an inter-depository transaction, the Clearing Corporations will cancel excess or unaccepted shares in the target depository. After receipt of the requisite details, including demat-account information and accepted bid shares, the target depository will send a release message to remove the lien to the extent of accepted bid shares on the settlement date.

### 14.7.

Equity Shares bought back in dematerialized form will be transferred directly from the clearing and settlement mechanism to the Company's demat account opened for the Buyback (the “Company Demat Account”), as provided by the Company's Broker.

## 14.8. In relation to the physical Equity Shares:

- The Company encourages Eligible Shareholders holding physical shares to dematerialize those shares.
- For physical shares that are not accepted, the share certificate will be returned by post or courier at the Eligible Shareholder's sole risk.
- If only a portion of the physical shares held by an Eligible Shareholder is accepted, the Company will issue a Letter of Confirmation (“LOC”) for the unaccepted shares instead of issuing a split share certificate.
- The LOC will be issued in accordance with the applicable SEBI circular, including SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD_RTAMB/P/CIR/2022/8 dated January 25, 2022, as amended.
- The LOC will be dispatched to the address registered with the Registrar to the Buyback.
- The RTA will retain the original share certificate and issue the LOC for the unaccepted Equity Shares. The LOC will be valid for 120 days from its date of issuance.
- Within the 120-day period, the Equity Shareholder must submit a dematerialization request for the physical Equity Shares. If the request is not submitted within that period, the RTA will credit the physical Equity Shares to a demat account of the Company opened for this purpose.
- The lien marked against unaccepted Equity Shares should be released after the applicable process. Eligible Shareholders should ensure that their depository account remains maintained until all Buyback formalities are completed.

### 14.9.

The Stock Broker will issue a contract note for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback. The Company's Broker will also issue a contract note to the Company for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

### 14.10.

Eligible Shareholders intending to participate in the Buyback through the secondary market transaction should consult their respective Stock Brokers regarding any charges, including brokerage, that may be levied for tendering Equity Shares. Such charges will be incurred solely by the Eligible Shareholders, and the Company will not bear them.
