## TO ALL STOCK EXCHANGES

November 10, 2025

### Sub: Submission of Public Announcement for Buyback of equity shares of Infosys Limited

Further to the intimation dated November 6, 2025, Infosys Limited announced that its shareholders had approved the buyback of up to 10,00,00,000 (10 crore) fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each.

The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route using the stock exchange mechanism, at a price of ₹1,800 per equity share, for an aggregate consideration of up to ₹18,000 crore.

The public announcement dated November 7, 2025, was published on November 10, 2025, with disclosures specified under Regulation 7(i) and Schedule II of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended.

## Additional information pursuant to U.S. Law

The buyback had not yet commenced as of the communication date. The communication was provided for informational purposes and was neither an offer to purchase nor a solicitation of an offer to sell securities.

Any offers to purchase or solicitations of offers to sell would be made pursuant to a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO, including the letter of offer and other tender-offer documents, to be filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

Security holders were advised to read the Schedule TO documents, amendments, and other buyback-related filings in their entirety before making any decision regarding the buyback because they contain the offer's terms and conditions.

## PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

The public announcement concerns the buyback of equity shares of Infosys Limited from eligible shareholders on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route using the stock exchange mechanism under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended.

The buyback covers up to 10,00,00,000 (10 crore) fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each, representing 2.41% of the total number of equity shares in the paid-up equity share capital of the Company on a standalone basis.

The record date for determining eligible shareholders is November 14, 2025. The buyback price is ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash, and the aggregate consideration is up to ₹18,000 crore.

The Board of Directors approved the buyback proposal, subject to shareholder approval and other necessary approvals, permissions, and sanctions. Shareholders approved the proposal by special resolution through postal ballot.

The buyback is being undertaken in accordance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, the Companies Act, 2013, and the rules made under those laws.

All references to rupees or ₹ refer to Indian rupees. Figures may include rounding adjustments, and decimals have been rounded to two decimal points. One crore represents ₹10 million.
