## Opinion

The report considered the state of affairs of the Company in relation to its audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

The permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback, including premium, was determined in accordance with Section 68(2)(b) and Section 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the applicable Buyback Regulations.

## 13.PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY TO BE ADOPTED FOR BUYBACK

### 13.1

The Buyback is open to all Eligible Shareholders, including ADS holders who submit their desired number of ADSs to the Depositary and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares. Eligible Shareholders must hold Equity Shares, in physical or electronic form, as of the Record Date.

### 13.2

The Buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender-offer route prescribed under the Buyback Regulations, subject to the applicable regulations and SEBI circulars. The Buyback will be implemented in accordance with the Companies Act, the rules made thereunder, and the Buyback Regulations, including the reservation for Small Shareholders.

### 13.3

The Company has appointed Kotak Securities Limited as its registered broker to facilitate the tendering of Equity Shares through the stock-exchange mechanism. The Company’s Broker will facilitate purchases and settlements relating to the Buyback.

### 13.4

The details of the Acquisition Window will be specified by the Indian Stock Exchanges from time to time.

### 13.5

During the Tendering Period, Eligible Shareholders must place bids for their Equity Shares through their respective stockbrokers.

### 13.6

If an Eligible Shareholder’s Stock Broker is not registered with the relevant stock exchange, or if the Eligible Shareholder is unable to place a bid using the unique client code (UCC) facility through the registered stockbroker, the Eligible Shareholder may place the bid through another registered stockbroker, subject to completion of applicable know-your-customer requirements.

### 13.7

Eligible Shareholders may place multiple bids and modify their bids during the Tendering Period. Shareholders should consult their respective Stock Brokers regarding the applicable process.

### 13.8

The cumulative quantity tendered will be made available on the Indian Stock Exchanges’ websites, including www.bseindia.com and www.nseindia.com, throughout the Tendering Period and will be updated at specified intervals.

### 13.9

The Company will not accept Equity Shares that are locked-in or otherwise non-transferable until they become transferable. The Company will also not accept Equity Shares in respect of which ownership is under dispute, duplicate share certificates have not been issued, or the shares are otherwise not clear in title.
