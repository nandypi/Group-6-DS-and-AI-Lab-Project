## 11.2. Buyback Price and Foreign Exchange Considerations

The Buyback Price will be paid in Indian rupees. Fluctuations in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and the U.S. dollar will affect the U.S. dollar equivalent of the Buyback Price, depending on the period of holding and the exchange rate applicable at that time. ADS holders should obtain current exchange-rate information before making any decision with respect to the Buyback.

## 11.3. Tax and Regulatory Considerations

### 1. Withdrawal and Re-Deposit of Equity Shares by ADS Holders

The withdrawal of Equity Shares underlying ADSs and the re-deposit of Equity Shares not tendered or not accepted in the Buyback may have various tax implications in India. The tax implications could vary depending on several factors, including the period of holding, the residential status of the holder, and the nature of the income earned.

While there are arguments that the withdrawal of Equity Shares underlying ADSs should not be subject to Indian capital gains tax, there is no assurance that tax will not be imposed or that the bank, broker, or other nominee in India with whom individual ADS holders establish an Indian demat account (Subtype DR) and a brokerage account will not withhold taxes in connection with the re-deposit of such Equity Shares.

ADS holders should consult their own tax advisers regarding the potential implications of withdrawing and re-depositing Equity Shares before requesting the withdrawal or re-deposit of their ADSs.

### a. India

In addition to considering Indian taxation, ADS holders should consider potential taxation under the laws of their respective jurisdictions arising from the withdrawal and re-deposit of their Equity Shares.

### b. United States

For U.S. federal income tax consequences relevant to U.S. holders, as defined in the Company's Form 20-F, of ADSs and/or Equity Shares, please refer to the Company's Form 20-F.

### Tendering Equity Shares in the Buyback

ADS holders should consult their own tax advisers regarding the tax consequences of participating in the Buyback.

## 11.4. Further Information

For tax-related information on participation in the Buyback, refer to:

ADS holders requiring additional information on surrendering their ADSs for cancellation and withdrawing the Equity Shares represented by their ADSs should contact the entity with which they hold their ADSs and request that the Depositary effect the withdrawal of Equity Shares.

ADS holders requiring additional information on re-depositing Equity Shares, or on establishing an Indian demat account (Subtype DR) and a brokerage account, should contact the bank, broker, or other nominee through which they intend to establish such accounts.

ADS holders are advised to consult their own legal and financial advisers regarding any related regulatory approvals and other matters in the relevant jurisdictions.

**Re:** Statutory Auditor's Report in respect of the proposed buyback under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended (the “Buyback Regulations”).

1. This report is issued in accordance with the terms of the engagement letter dated July 10, 2025.

2. The Board of Directors approved a proposal for the buyback of Equity Shares by the Company, subject to shareholder approval, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, pursuant to Sections 68, 69, and 70 of the Companies Act, 2013, as amended (the “Act”), and the Buyback Regulations.

3. The Company's management requested the auditors to provide a report on the accompanying Statement of Permissible Capital Payment (the “Statement”).

### Management's Responsibility

4. The preparation of the Statement in accordance with Section 68(2) of the Act, including determining the permissible capital payment, is the responsibility of the Company's management. This responsibility includes the preparation and maintenance of accounting records and relevant supporting records and documents, preparing and presenting the Statement, and applying an appropriate basis of preparation and making reasonable estimates in the circumstances.

### Auditor's Responsibility

5. The auditors inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025. The amount of permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback, as stated in Annexure A, was determined with reference to Section 68(2)(b) and Section 68(2)(c) of the Act, Regulation 4(1) and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations, and the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements.

6. The interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025 were audited in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Act and other applicable authoritative pronouncements issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). The auditors issued an unmodified audit opinion on those financial statements in their report dated July 23, 2025.

The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, formed the opinion specified in Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations, on reasonable grounds, that the Company would not be rendered insolvent, having regard to its state of affairs, within a period of one year from the date of passing the Board resolution dated September 11, 2025 and for a period of one year immediately following the date of passing of the shareholders' resolution, in terms of the Act and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, each as amended.

7. The auditors examined the Statement in accordance with the Guidance Note on Audit Reports and Certificates for Special Purposes (Revised 2016) issued by the ICAI and the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Act, insofar as applicable. 8. The auditors inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to its audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025, which were approved by the Board of Directors on July 23, 2025.

9. Based on the inquiries conducted and examinations performed, the auditors reported that:

   i. The amount of permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback of Equity Shares, as computed in the Statement attached as Annexure A, was properly determined in accordance with Section 68(2)(b) and Section 68(2)(c) of the Act, Regulation 4(1), and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations.
