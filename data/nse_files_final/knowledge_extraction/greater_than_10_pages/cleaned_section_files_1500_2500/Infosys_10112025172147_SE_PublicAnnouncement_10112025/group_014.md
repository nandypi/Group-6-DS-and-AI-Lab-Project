## INTENTION OF THE PROMOTER AND PROMOTER GROUP AND PERSONS IN CONTROL OF THE COMPANY TO TENDER THEIR EQUITY SHARES IN THE BUYBACK

The Promoter and Promoter Group expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback. Accordingly, their Equity Shares will not be considered for computing the entitlement ratio, which may result in a higher Buyback entitlement ratio for other eligible shareholders.

The Promoter and Promoter Group had the option to participate in the Buyback under the tender offer route. Their intention not to participate was communicated through letters dated September 14, 2025, September 16, 2025, September 17, 2025, September 18, 2025, and September 19, 2025. Accordingly, the disclosures required under paragraphs (vi) to (x) of Schedule I of the Buyback Regulations are not applicable.

## 8. NO DEFAULTS

The Company confirmed that:

- It had not accepted any deposits.
- There were no defaults in the repayment of deposits, redemption of preference shares, payment of dividends to shareholders, or repayment of debentures or interest.
- There were no subsisting defaults in the redemption or repayment of term loans or interest payable to any financial institution or bank.

## 9. CONFIRMATION BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE COMPANY

As required by Regulation 5(v)(b) of the Buyback Regulations and clause (x) of Schedule I, the Board confirmed that it had made a full enquiry into the affairs and prospects of the Company and formed the following opinion:

- For the year immediately following the date of the Board meeting held on November 4, 2025, and the year immediately following the date of passing of the members’ resolution on November 4, 2025, the Company would be able to meet its liabilities as and when they fell due and would not be rendered insolvent within one year from those dates.
- In forming this opinion, the Board considered the Company’s liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, as if the Company were being wound up under the Companies Act and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.

## 10. CONFIRMATIONS FROM THE COMPANY AS PER THE PROVISIONS OF THE BUYBACK REGULATIONS AND THE COMPANIES ACT

- Subject to applicable law, the Company will not issue any Equity Shares or other securities, including through the exercise of stock options or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares, from the date of the public announcement until the date on which payment of consideration is made to Eligible Shareholders who have accepted the Buyback, except for the discharge of subsisting obligations.
- Under Section 68 of the Companies Act, the Company will not make any further issue of shares or other securities of the same kind, including allotment of new shares under Section 62(1)(a) of the Act, within six months after completion of the Buyback, except by way of bonus shares or Equity Shares issued to discharge subsisting obligations, including conversion of warrants or stock option schemes, or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares.
- Subject to applicable law, the Company will not raise further capital for a period of one year from expiry of the Buyback Period.
- The Company will not buy back locked-in Equity Shares or non-transferable Equity Shares during the period of the lock-in or until the Equity Shares become transferable.
- The Company has been in compliance with Sections 92, 123, 127 and 129 of the Companies Act.
- There were no subsisting defaults in repayment of deposits, payment of interest on deposits, redemption of preference shares, payment of dividends, or repayment of term loans or interest payable to any financial institution or bank.
- Funds borrowed from banks and financial institutions will not be used for the Buyback.
- The aggregate amount of the Buyback is ₹1,800 crore, based on the audited standalone and consolidated financial statements of the Company as at June 30, 2025.
- The Company will not make any offer of Buyback within one year from the date of closure of the preceding Buyback.
- The number of Equity Shares proposed to be purchased under the Buyback is 10,00,00,000 (10 crore), which does not exceed 25% of the total number of fully paid-up Equity Shares as at June 30, 2025.
- The Company has not entered into any compromise or arrangement under the Companies Act as at the date of the Buyback.
- The Company will not directly or indirectly purchase its Equity Shares or other specified securities:

  - Through any subsidiary company, including its own subsidiary companies; or
  - Through any investment company or group of investment companies.

- Consideration for the Buyback will be paid only in cash.
- As at June 30, 2025, the ratio of the aggregate of secured and unsecured debts owed by the Company to its paid-up share capital and free reserves was not, and will not be, more than two times.
- The Buyback will not result in delisting of the Equity Shares from the stock exchanges.
- The Buyback offer will not be withdrawn once the public announcement is made.
- The Company will ensure the consequent reduction of its share capital, where applicable, in the manner prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and the Companies Act within the specified timelines.
- The Company will transfer an amount equivalent to the nominal value of the Equity Shares purchased through the Buyback from its free reserves or securities premium account to the capital redemption reserve account. Details of the transfer will be disclosed in the Company’s subsequent audited financial statements.
- The Company does not have any outstanding debt from lenders. Accordingly, it is not required to obtain prior consent from its lenders for breach of any covenant with such lenders.
- The Company will earmark and arrange adequate sources of funds for the Buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

## 11. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION FOR HOLDERS OF THE COMPANY’S AMERICAN DEPOSITARY SHARES

ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. To participate, they must cancel their ADSs and become holders of the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date.

ADS holders who wish to participate must, sufficiently in advance of the Record Date:

- Apply for and obtain a PAN from the Indian Income Tax Department to enable them to directly hold Equity Shares.
- Open an Indian demat account, subtype DR, and a brokerage account in India.
- Submit their desired number of ADSs to Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, as ADR Depositary, for cancellation and withdrawal of the underlying Equity Shares in electronic, dematerialized form.

The withdrawn Equity Shares must be held as of the Record Date to receive entitlement and be tendered during the Tendering Period.

The Company communicated these requirements in the Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025, including section 12 of the explanatory statement attached to the notice.

## 11.1. Participation by ADS Holders

1. ADS holders who cancel ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares so that they become holders of Equity Shares as of the Record Date will be allocated an entitlement based on the number of Equity Shares held as of the Record Date.

2. The Equity Shares trade on the NSE and the BSE and cannot be traded on the NYSE. There is no guarantee that an ADS holder who submits ADSs for cancellation and withdrawal of the underlying Equity Shares will have any or all of those Equity Shares accepted in the Buyback.

3. The Company received an intimation from SEBI in 2017 that the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, issued by the Government of India, was in force. Since the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, had not been amended with respect to conversion into Equity Shares and reconversion of Equity Shares into depository receipts, the same position applied to the Company. Accordingly, reconversion of the withdrawn Equity Shares into ADSs would be subject to the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, and the terms of the ADSs.

4. Withdrawn Equity Shares for which the required redeposit does not occur, or for which instructions are not given within the applicable period, and Equity Shares withdrawn during the Tendering Period, may be redeposited for the creation of ADSs only to the extent that the maximum number of outstanding ADSs is not exceeded. The Company cannot assure ADS holders that such Equity Shares will be accepted in the Buyback or that ADSs representing those Equity Shares will be issued. The ADS issuance fee will not be waived in connection with the issuance of ADSs.

5. If an ADS holder withdraws the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date and before the last date of the Tendering Period, the ADS holder may deliver those Equity Shares to the Custodian for redeposit and receive ADSs representing the redeposited Equity Shares, subject to the Depositary’s requirements and payment of the applicable ADS issuance fee.
