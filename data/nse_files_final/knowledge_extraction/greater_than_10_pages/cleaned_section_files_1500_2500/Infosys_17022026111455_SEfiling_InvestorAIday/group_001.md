## Tech transitions - Why is the AI transition different?

### Technology has seen fundamental shifts over the years

Source: Coatue

### Tech innovations have continuously redefined enterprise operations

#### Computerization

- Replacement of paper-based workflows
- Enterprise systems
- Addition of IT operations

#### Internet Access

- Globalization and digital reach
- Platform-based business models
- Enterprise data

#### Enterprise tech transitions

The presentation identifies client-server, LAN, web computing, and cloud access as stages in enterprise technology transitions.

#### Cloud Access

- Digital scalability
- Modular business architecture and microservices
- Enterprise IT

### AI adoption is faster than the earlier tech transitions

### The shift has multiple dimensions

The presentation states that AI is not merely a technology layer or an adjacency.

#### Business

- Integrated business functions with AI at the core
- AI-embedded workflows

#### Technology

- AI-ready systems
- AI-enabled data platform
- AI-native architecture

#### Mental Model

- Evident-first principle
- Responsible AI

#### Talent

- Scalable AI-augmented workforce
- Adaptive learning and change management

#### Operating Model

- Cross-functional knowledge graph
- Exponential engineering

AI transformation is described as requiring fundamental, root-and-branch change rather than a lift-and-shift approach.

### Modernization of legacy systems cannot be deferred anymore

#### The true cost of delaying modernization

- Financial drain
- Security vulnerabilities
- Innovation paralysis
- 60-80% of IT budgets spent on outdated systems
- Average breach detection exceeding 200 days in legacy environments
- Legacy systems operating on data silos

#### Demand side needs modernization

- Low agility
- Slow rate of change
- Technical debt
- Cost of security

#### Supply side makes it easier

- High rate of change
- Efficient and productive

Accumulated technology debt over decades must be addressed. Modernized environments are associated in the presentation with enhanced security and compliance and easier scalability.

### Build vs Buy: balance moves towards build and reengineering as AI becomes the core

| Build | Buy |
|---|---|
| Customizable | Standardized |
| Proprietary | Vendor dependency |
| Organic and steady | Faster deployment |
| High internal control | External control |
| Continuous investment | Lock-in and renewals |

The presentation states that enterprises prefer a proprietary agentic layer on top of foundational models, moving from customizable to composable solutions.

### AI is evolving at an astonishing speed led by hyper competitive market, large capital access and rapid R&D

The presentation identifies foundation models in 2023 and continuing innovation in 2025 and 2026. Innovation cycles are tightening, and leaderboards remain in constant motion.

AI investment spending rose from $24 billion in 2023 to an estimated $140 billion in 2025.

### The foundational technology is ahead of its diffusion and deployment

The presentation describes a widening gap between AI progress and enterprise value, with AI progress outpacing enterprise readiness.

### Talent demand is pivoting from legacy roles to high-growth AI skills

#### Fastest declining IT jobs

- Front-End Web Developers
- QA Testers
- IT Support Specialists
- Blockchain Developers

The presentation states that 92 million traditional jobs are expected to be displaced.

#### New/upcoming IT jobs

- Data Annotators
- AI Forensic Analysts
- AI Leads
- AI Engineers
- Forward Deployed Engineers

The presentation states that 170 million new jobs are expected to be created.

### Greenfield AI development is easier than brownfield

#### The greenfield-brownfield productivity gap

#### New build environments

- Clean structure and consistent patterns
- Real-time data availability
- Structured environments
- Probabilistic

#### Task level

- 15-50% productivity

#### Legacy environments

- Technical debt
- Data silos
- Undocumented dependencies
- Brownfield = high overhead + rework
- Deterministic

#### Business function level

- Only 1% fully scaled to AI

The presentation distinguishes task-level productivity from organizational productivity.

### AI implementation requires laser focus

AI investments are described as meaningful only when they lead to major productivity gains.

#### What still matters

- First Principles thinking
- Understanding enterprise context
- Agnostic design
- Getting the house in order
- Leadership in effective change
- Strong collaboration
- Intense focus on productivity
- Engineering bent of mind

## The AI Services Opportunity

### Clients trust Infosys for their AI Journey

Infosys provides AI services to 90% of its 200 large clients.

Liberty Global CEO Mike Fries stated that Infosys provides a delivery model, talent, platform, and partnership intended to support progress from AI pilots to enterprise-wide impact.

Danske Bank COO Frans Woelders stated that Danske Bank entered into a strategic partnership with Infosys to support its “Forward '28” strategy and digital and technology transformation using cloud and AI technologies.
