## Unlocking AI Value - Energy, Utilities, Resources & Services

Ashiss Kumar Dash

### Rapid adoption of AI across EURS Industries

Energy, Utilities & Resources industries are described as central to the physical scalability, reliability, sustainability, and material availability of AI:

- Utilities power AI and influence where AI can grow and how quickly. AI data centers are expected to consume 10–12% of global electricity by 2030.
- Oil and gas underwrites global energy supply stability. AI’s computational needs require natural-gas plants and LNG for grid reliability and load balancing.
- Resources provide raw materials required for AI, including copper, lithium, nickel, cobalt, rare earths, and aluminum.

### Circularity in action

- Energy determines AI’s physical scalability.
- Utilities determine AI’s reliability and sustainability.
- Resources determine AI’s material availability.
- Services continue to realize benefits through inference.

### Industry outlook

- Demand and digital intensity are increasing. These sectors are expected to grow, with AI-enabled operators positioned to expand margins.
- AI is becoming an operating system for industrial infrastructure, including predictive operations, autonomous assets, grid automation, subsurface modeling, remote mining, and industrial copilots.
- AI is central to ERP-led business transformation programs.

Infosys is helping EURS clients redeploy operating-expense savings into AI transformations. Its position is described as being supported by domain capabilities developed over decades.

Infosys is described as the AI partner of choice for 15 of its top 25 clients.

## Process AI and Data for AI at Ralph Lauren

### Business context

- Ralph Lauren sought to reimagine online shopping experiences to resemble interactions with stylists in physical stores.
- Styling and outfit curation relied on manual merchandising, limiting personalization at scale.
- Disconnected inventory data limited the conversion of real-time, shoppable recommendations into sales.

### AI First Solutions

Ralph Lauren, Infosys, and Microsoft launched Ask Ralph, a conversational AI-powered shopping experience.

The solution uses conversational and personalization AI to transform high-intent customer queries into curated, shoppable experiences powered by real-time inventory and the Microsoft stack.

- Conversational AI styling assistant, Ask Ralph, enabling natural-language product discovery.
- Personalization at scale through curated recommendations.
- Real-time inventory integration.
- Scaling of high-touch service from luxury physical stores to mobile devices.
