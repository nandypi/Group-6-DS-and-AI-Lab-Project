## B) Login method for e-voting and voting during the meeting for shareholders other than individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode and shareholders holding securities in physical mode

### How to Log-in to NSDL e-voting website?

1. Visit the NSDL e-voting website and select **Login** under **Shareholder/Member**.
2. Enter the User ID, Password or OTP, and verification code. Shareholders registered for NSDL IDeAS may log in through their existing IDeAS credentials and select e-voting.
3. User IDs are based on the manner of holding shares:

| Manner of holding shares | User ID |
|---|---|
| Demat account with NSDL | 8-character DP ID followed by the 8-digit Client ID |
| Demat account with CDSL | 16-digit Beneficiary ID |
| Physical mode | EVEN Number followed by the Folio Number registered with the Company |

4. Existing e-voting users may use their existing password.
5. First-time users must retrieve the initial password communicated to them. If received as a PDF attachment, the PDF password is the 8-digit NSDL Client ID, the last 8 digits of the CDSL Client ID, or the Folio Number for physical shares.
6. If the initial password cannot be retrieved or has not been received:

   - Use **Forgot User Details / Password?** for demat holdings.
   - Use **Physical User Reset Password?** for physical holdings.
   - Shareholders may request assistance from NSDL by providing their demat account number or Folio Number, PAN, name, and registered address.
   - OTP-based login may also be used.

7. Accept the terms and conditions and select **Login**. The e-voting homepage will open.

## Step 2: Cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system

### How to cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system?

1. After logging in, the shareholder will see the companies and corresponding EVENs for which voting is available.
2. Select the Company’s EVEN for which the vote is to be cast during the remote e-voting period.
3. Select the appropriate option, such as assent or dissent.
4. Verify or modify the number of shares for which the vote is being cast.
5. Select **Submit** and then **Confirm** when prompted.
6. A successful vote will display the message **Vote cast successfully**.
7. A printout of the votes cast may be taken from the confirmation page.
8. Once a vote on a resolution is confirmed, it cannot be modified.

## General guidelines for shareholders

1. Institutional shareholders must provide a scanned copy of the relevant Board Resolution, authority letter, or other authorization document, together with the attested specimen signature of the authorized signatories, to the Scrutinizer. These documents may also be uploaded through the **Upload Board Resolution / Authority Letter** option under the e-voting tab.
2. Shareholders should keep their passwords confidential. Login access is disabled after five unsuccessful password attempts; access may then be restored using the relevant password-reset option.
3. Shareholders may refer to the NSDL shareholder FAQs and e-voting user manual for further guidance.

## Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories for procuring user id and password and registration of e mail ids for e-voting for the resolutions set out in this notice:

1. Shareholders holding physical shares must provide their Folio Number, name, a scanned copy of the share certificate, PAN, and Aadhaar for registration and credential procurement.
2. Shareholders holding shares in demat mode must provide their DPID-CLID or 16-digit Beneficiary ID, name, client master or Consolidated Account Statement, PAN, and Aadhaar.
3. Alternatively, shareholders may request NSDL to provide the User ID and password by submitting the required documents.
4. Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may use the login method specified for individual demat shareholders.
5. Under the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with Depositories and Depository Participants. Their mobile number and email ID must be correctly updated in the demat account to access the e-voting facility.
