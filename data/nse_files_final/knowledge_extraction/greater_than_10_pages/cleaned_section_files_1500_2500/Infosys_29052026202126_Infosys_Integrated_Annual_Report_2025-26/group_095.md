During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Company designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions included in the cash flow hedge reserve as at March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the Statement of Profit and Loss within three months.

The Company determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at hedge inception and through periodic prospective assessments. If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal but the risk management objective remains unchanged and hedge accounting continues to qualify, the relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Hedge ineffectiveness is recognized in the Statement of Profit or Loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.

## Credit risk

Credit risk refers to the risk of default by a counterparty resulting in a financial loss. Maximum exposure at the reporting date primarily comprised:

| Particulars | March 31, 2026 (₹ crore) | March 31, 2025 (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trade receivables | 30,337 | 26,413 |
| Unbilled revenue | 13,706 | 11,988 |

Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are typically unsecured and arise primarily from customers located in the United States of America and Europe. The Company manages credit risk through credit approvals, credit limits, and continuous monitoring of customer creditworthiness. It uses the expected credit loss model and a provision matrix for trade receivables and unbilled revenue, incorporating available credit reports and other relevant credit information.

Credit risk is influenced mainly by individual customer characteristics and concentration among the largest customers. Customer exposure is diversified, and no single customer contributed more than 10% of outstanding trade receivables and unbilled revenue.

The gross carrying amount of a financial asset is written off, either partially or fully, when there is no realistic prospect of recovery.

Credit risk on cash and cash equivalents is limited because the Company generally invests in deposits with banks that have high ratings assigned by international and domestic credit rating agencies. Ratings are monitored periodically, using the latest available ratings as at the date of approval of the financial statements.

The Company’s investments primarily include mutual fund units, target maturity fund units, tax-free bonds, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, treasury bills, government securities, non-convertible debentures, and quoted bonds issued by government and quasi-government organizations. Counterparty risk is assessed using criteria including Tier I Capital, Capital Adequacy Ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and deposit base. These risks are monitored under the Group’s risk management program.

## 2.12 Equity

### Accounting policy

#### Ordinary shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity share capital. Incremental costs directly attributable to the issuance of new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of related tax effects.

### Description of reserves

#### Capital redemption reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

#### Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the accumulated earnings of the Company.

#### Securities premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as securities premium. The securities premium account has been used for bonus issues and share buybacks.

#### Share options outstanding account

The share options outstanding account records the fair value of equity-settled share-based payment transactions with employees. Amounts are transferred to securities premium when stock options are exercised and to general reserve when stock options are not exercised by employees.
