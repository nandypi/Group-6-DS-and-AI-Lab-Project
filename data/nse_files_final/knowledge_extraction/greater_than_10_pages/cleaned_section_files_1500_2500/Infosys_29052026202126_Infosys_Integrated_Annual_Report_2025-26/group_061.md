## Leadership Indicators

### 1. Life Cycle Perspective / Assessments

Life Cycle Assessment is in progress. Infosys is an IT consulting and software services organization. Its life cycle practices include green procurement considerations in technical specifications for purchases and end-of-life management to maximize recycling.

Life Cycle Assessment is mandated for new physical infrastructure to support decisions on environmentally friendly products during the construction and operation of buildings.

### 2. Social or Environmental Concerns and Risks

Infosys reported no significant social or environmental concerns or risks arising from the production and disposal of its products and services. Its environmental footprint is detailed in Principle 6 of the BRSR report.

### 3. Recycled or Reused Input Material

Not applicable. Infosys is an IT services company.

### 4. Products and Packaging Collected at End of Life

Not applicable. Infosys does not manufacture products.

### 5. Reclaimed Products and Packaging Materials

Not applicable. Infosys does not manufacture products.
