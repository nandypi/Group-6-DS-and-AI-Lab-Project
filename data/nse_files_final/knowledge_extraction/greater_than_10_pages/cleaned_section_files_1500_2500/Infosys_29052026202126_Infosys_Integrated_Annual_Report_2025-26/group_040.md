## Governance of Board committees

The Board, in consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, is responsible for assigning and determining the terms of service for committee members.

The Chairman of the Board, in consultation with the Company Secretary and the respective committee chairperson, determines the frequency of committee meetings. Generally, all committees meet four times a year. Committee recommendations are submitted to the Board for approval. During the year, all committee recommendations were approved by the Board.

The quorum for meetings is the higher of two members or one-third of the total number of committee members.

## Objectives of the Committee

The primary objectives of the Committee are to assist the Board with oversight of:

1. The accuracy, integrity, and transparency of the Company’s financial statements, with adequate and timely disclosures.
2. Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
3. The professional qualifications and independence of the Company’s independent auditors.
4. The performance of the Company’s independent and internal auditors.
5. Acquisitions and investments made by the Company.

## Audit Committee Charter ('the Charter')

Infosys is listed on BSE Limited, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited, and the New York Stock Exchange in the US. Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations in India and the Blue Ribbon Committee established by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission mandate that listed companies adopt an appropriate charter.

The Committee is guided by the Charter, which was adopted by the Board and is available at [the Audit Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/audit-committee-charter.pdf).

## Process adopted by the Committee to fulfill its objectives

The Committee’s processes include:

- Ensuring an effective and independent internal audit function that provides assurance regarding the adequacy and effectiveness of internal controls.
- Reviewing processes intended to safeguard the Company’s assets, use resources effectively and efficiently, and record transactions accurately and on a timely basis.
- Meeting the independent auditors at the end of each quarter and financial year to discuss key observations relating to the financial statements for the relevant period.
- Providing an independent channel of communication for the Chief Compliance Officer, internal auditors, and independent auditors.
- Inviting members of Management and, at its discretion, external legal, financial, and technical experts to provide clarification, advice, and guidance.
- Providing periodic feedback and reports to the Board.
- Periodically reviewing its Charter, structure, processes, and membership, and recommending proposed changes to the Board for approval.
- Meeting at least four times a year and ensuring that the gap between two meetings does not exceed 120 days.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises four members and fulfills the requirements of:

- Audit Committee Charter
- Sections 149 and 177 of the Companies Act, 2013
- Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations
- NYSE guidelines, as applicable

The Committee relies on Management’s financial expertise and on the expertise of the internal and independent auditors to carry out its responsibilities efficiently and transparently.

Management is responsible for the Company’s internal control over financial reporting and the financial reporting process. The independent auditors are responsible for performing an independent audit of the Company’s financial statements in accordance with Generally Accepted Auditing Principles and issuing a report based on the audit.
