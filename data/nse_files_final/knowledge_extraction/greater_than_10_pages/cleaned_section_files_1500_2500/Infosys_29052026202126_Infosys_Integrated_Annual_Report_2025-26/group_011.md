## Lake rejuvenation

Infosys continues to implement lake rejuvenation projects in partnership with local stakeholders. These efforts increased cumulative storage capacity from 6 bn liters to 10.3 bn liters, adding 4.3 bn liters of freshwater capacity.

## Waste management

Infosys’ waste management strategy is based on reduce, reuse, refurbish, repurpose, and recycle, with an ambition of zero waste to landfill. The company continues to strengthen waste segregation, onsite processing, and material recovery across its campuses.

Organic waste is treated onsite through biogas plants, composting, and recycling. These processes reduce offsite disposal and associated emissions and replace part of the cooking gas requirements on campuses.

E-waste includes IT equipment, mobile phones, printers, cartridges, refrigerators, microwaves, air conditioners, lithium-ion batteries, and cables. Infosys extends asset life through hardware upgrades, component replacements, re-imaging, reuse, redeployment, and refurbishment.

Eleven campuses across Infosys India have achieved TRUE Zero Waste certification.

## Biodiversity

Infosys is strengthening biodiversity stewardship through native plantation, habitat enrichment, and knowledge sharing through its annual BioConserve event.

In FY26, Infosys launched the BioConserve platform, a digital open repository for resources, best practices, and research on biodiversity conservation.

## ASSURE

Launched with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements (IIHS) in April 2024, Accelerating Sustainable Super Efficient Real Estate (ASSURE) is a multi-stakeholder platform focused on replicating climate-resilient, energy-efficient buildings across India. Its target is 100 million sq. ft. by 2030.

In FY26, more than 15 million sq. ft. of commercial buildings across India were onboarded as lighthouse projects. More than 1,250 students and practitioners were upskilled on high-performance buildings through webinars, in-person workshops, and site visits.

## Advancing the frontiers of energy performance

Infosys uses progressive design strategies, enhanced building envelopes, intelligent HVAC systems, renewable energy, high-performance materials with recycled content, passive design principles, and real-time monitoring to reduce energy intensity and improve occupant well-being.

Infosys campuses are being developed around health and wellness, social connection, sustainability, inclusivity, and technology enablement. Environmental measures include efficient interior solutions, sustainable materials, enhanced lighting and air quality, and intelligent digital systems.

Eighty-four percent of Infosys office spaces are certified as green buildings.

## Embedding circularity and innovation in design

Infosys integrates Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) into early-stage decision-making for material selection and design optimization.

Steel and cement account for nearly 70% of embodied carbon in office buildings. Infosys has prioritized secondary steel produced from recycled scrap, with an emission intensity of 1–1.5 tCO2e/t compared with 2.5–3.5 tCO2e/t for primary steel. Cement use has been optimized through blending with industrial by-products such as fly ash and ground granulated blast furnace slag.

Infosys uses radiant heating, free cooling under favorable climatic conditions, and air-conditioning equipment with refrigerants that have very low global warming potential.

The company eliminated plastic and thermocol-based packaging for IT equipment and introduced sustainable packaging criteria into procurement. Alternatives include paper-based, recycled, and biodegradable fibre materials, including materials derived from agricultural waste.

## Resource circularity in operations

Infosys’ Circular Economy initiatives focus on maximizing value from IT assets throughout their lifecycle while minimizing environmental impact. Assets are reused, redeployed, refurbished, and repurposed across functions and delivery centers. Assets that reach the end of their usable life undergo responsible end-of-life management.

## Green IT

Infosys IT has embedded sustainable practices across the service design, operations, and disposal lifecycles of IT assets.

## Data center efficiency

Infosys IT has made data center modernization a key part of its sustainability and responsible growth strategy. Its data center ecosystem uses density-optimized hyperscale platforms, cloud-scale agility, and high-density server virtualization to reduce infrastructure sprawl and improve compute utilization.

Infosys IT has rationalized and consolidated data centers and server rooms across development centers, reducing redundancy and optimizing space usage.

Investments in Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) provide integrated, real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure to support data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.

## Infrastructure as code

Infrastructure as code supports continuous deployment, integration, and touchless lifecycle management of infrastructure components. Infosys has developed playbooks to automate platform-related processes and improve deployment scalability, speed, and consistency.

## AIOps-powered digital operations

Infosys IT uses an AIOps platform with intelligent noise suppression, context-driven notifications, and unified observability across its infrastructure. The platform provides visibility into capacity usage and cost efficiency and processes high volumes of data from diverse IT sources.

## Public cloud adoption

Infosys has largely transitioned its internal IT applications to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premises data centers. Employees use cloud-based collaboration and secure access solutions to support hybrid work.

The IT Service Management (ITSM) landscape uses a standardized, AI-enabled service desk on a software-as-a-service platform. This has improved process standardization, automation, and user experience across support services, including through AI-assisted agents.
