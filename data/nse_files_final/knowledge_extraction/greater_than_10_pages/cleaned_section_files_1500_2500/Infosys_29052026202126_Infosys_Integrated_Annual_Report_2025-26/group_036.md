## VIII. Other details

## 1.  Quality

The Quality function at Infosys has three strategic imperatives:

- Differentiate Infosys’ services through superior performance and quality.
- Optimize client projects and internal functions for greater efficiency and agility.
- De-risk operations through delivery excellence, compliance, and sustainability.

To standardize AI adoption while accounting for different client maturity levels, the Quality team has defined an adaptable AI-first software engineering framework covering AI-augmented and agentic AI engineering. The framework integrates AI-infused processes with Infosys and third-party platforms, tools, and accelerators. For early AI adopters, agentic AI capabilities include adaptable blueprints, pre-built agents with human-in-the-loop orchestration, agent governance, and responsible AI guardrails.

Infosys is evolving its ways of working for AI-led organizations across Agile, DevSecOps, Site Reliability Engineering, and its product-led, platform-driven operating model. Quality consulting supports AI-infused ways of working and culture, AI engineering practices, and AI governance.

The Quality function drives lean practices and automation through frameworks, processes, tools, and platforms deployed across service lines. Its automation maturity model supports projects in progressing from point tools toward cognitive and autonomous operations using AI and generative AI.

Through structured audits and assessment frameworks, Quality focuses on de-risking the organization and has expanded coverage across services, centers, and subsidiaries.

Infosys complies with international management system standards and models, including:

- ISO 9001
- ISO 27001
- ISO 14001
- ISO 45001
- ISO 50001
- ISO 22301
- ISO 20000
- ISO 27701
- AS 9100
- ISO 13485

Infosys has onboarded AI practices from CMMI.AI and was among the first organizations to complete a pilot assessment by the CMMI Institute. Infosys also achieved reasonable assurance under ISO 14068-1, *Climate change management — Transition to net zero — Part 1: Carbon neutrality*. Infosys China and Infosys Brazil received certification for the ISO 27701 Privacy Information Management System standard.

## 2.  Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (ICETS)

ICETS supports enterprise adoption of emerging technologies through a structured, outcome-driven approach focused on research, experimentation, tools, platforms, and expertise.

## A structured innovation journey: Next, Near, and Now

The ICETS innovation framework balances long-term technology development with near-term business impact.

## Next - Applied Research Centers (ARC)

Applied Research Centers research, create, and apply emerging technologies, including:

- Advanced AI
- Cybersecurity
- Robotics
- Autonomous machines
- Quantum computing
- Next-generation interaction models

The centers combine medium- and long-term research with near-term client applications. Their focus includes building in-house expertise and intellectual property for Infosys and its clients.

## Advanced AI

Enterprise AI is increasingly focused on how systems reason, act, and operate autonomously within business environments. AI agents are being embedded into workflows to:

- Plan and execute multi-step processes.
- Collaborate with humans and other agents.
- Adapt dynamically to changing contexts.

ICETS is addressing challenges related to coordination, governance, and cost predictability through:

- AI agent orchestration frameworks.
- Automated model lifecycle transitions from large language models to small language models.
- Agent certification and governance models.
- Runtime observability, auditability, and control.

## New interaction model

Generative AI is enabling expressive avatars and digital humans that support more natural and context-driven interactions. Capabilities include:

- Realistic speech, lip-sync, and facial expressions.
- Emotion-aware, personalized, and context-driven engagement.

## Cybersecurity

ICETS’ cybersecurity capabilities include:

- AI-driven threat detection and response.
- Privacy-preserving techniques, including federated learning and secure inference.
- Multimodal threat analytics and real-time security operations.

## Autonomous Machines

Capabilities include:

- Intelligent systems operating in dynamic, real-world environments.
- Sensor fusion, simulation, and edge intelligence.
- Scalable deployment across logistics, mobility, and infrastructure.

## Humanoids

Capabilities include:

- Human-like robots with advanced perception and dexterity.
- Human-machine collaboration in complex tasks.
- Focus on safety, productivity, and real-world adaptability.

## Quantum

Capabilities include:

- Hybrid quantum-classical computing approaches.
- Optimization and scientific computing use cases.
- Post-quantum cryptography and enterprise readiness.

## Near - Living Labs

Living Labs support co-creation, rapid prototyping, and real-world validation. They enable clients to contextualize, experiment with, iterate on, and de-risk innovations in controlled environments.

## Infosys Living Labs

Infosys Living Labs are present in all major geographies and have hosted hundreds of client visits. The network showcases capabilities across:

- Infosys Topaz
- Infosys Cobalt
- Infosys Aster
- Brand partnerships
- Hyperscaler and other alliances
- Infosys’ innovation network of startups
- Academic collaborations
- CSR initiatives
- Service lines and business verticals

Specialized showcases address themes such as responsible AI, accessibility, cybersecurity, future of retail, payments, banking, and manufacturing.

Infosys Living Labs also provide environments for innovation teams to work with individual clients on specific challenges and opportunities.

## Client Living Labs

Client Living Labs are innovation-as-a-service engagements addressing client- and industry-specific challenges through emerging technologies, design, innovation management expertise, and rapid experimentation.

There are 40 active Client Living Lab engagements. The innovation services are organized around three flagship programs:

- **IncubateNext:** Rapid experimentation through internal experts and external partnerships, applying emerging technologies and design to real-world use cases across domains and geographies.
- **ImagineNext:** Exploration of future possibilities through trend sensing and innovation insights to identify new business opportunities.
- **SenseNext:** Capture of early signals of market and technology shifts and translation of those insights into actionable strategies.

## Now - Platforms and Ecosystems: Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys Topaz Fabric provides reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities to operationalize AI at scale. It consists of composable and interoperable AI agents, tools, services, and models.

The platform supports optimization and automation of existing IT processes and helps enterprises build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

## Consulting

Infosys’ consulting capabilities combine strategic intelligence, AI-powered insights, and structured frameworks. They cover research, product discovery, large deal solutions, value management, and process re-engineering, supporting road-map development, initiative prioritization, and business-outcome realization.

## Engineering

Infosys’ engineering capabilities span:

- Greenfield engineering.
- Application modernization.
- Platform and cloud migration.
- Data and quality engineering.
- Process re-engineering.

AI-driven execution and intelligent automation are applied across the build, migrate, and run phases of software delivery.

## Operations

Infosys’ operations capabilities support IT, business, and network environments through AI-driven monitoring, automation, and real-time intelligence. They include:

- IT operations.
- Business operations.
- Integrated BizTech operations.
- Network operations.
- Cybersecurity security operations centers.

Self-healing capabilities and predictive insights are used to improve service reliability, issue resolution, and enterprise performance.

## Driving measurable business impact

ICETS supports enterprise-wide AI adoption, faster experimentation and deployment, trusted governance, cost efficiency, operational predictability, and future-ready capabilities across industries.

## Looking ahead

ICETS’ stated focus is on execution excellence, ecosystem collaboration, and responsible innovation, supported by research, experimentation, tools, platforms, and expertise for applying emerging technologies in real-world settings.
