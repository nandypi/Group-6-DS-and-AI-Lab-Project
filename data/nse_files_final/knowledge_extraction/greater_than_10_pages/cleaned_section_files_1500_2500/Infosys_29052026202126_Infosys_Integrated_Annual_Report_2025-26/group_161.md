# Shareholder and AGM Information

In the case of Foreign Institutional Investors (FII) and Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI), tax will be deducted under Section 393 of the IT Act 2025 at 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or at the rate provided in the relevant Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), read with the Multilateral Instrument (MLI), whichever is more beneficial, subject to submission of the required documents.

The deadline for uploading dividend tax documents is June 11, 2026. Shareholders can check their tax credit in Form 168 (erstwhile Form 26AS) through their income-tax e-filing account.

Dividends that remain unclaimed for seven years from the date of transfer to the Company's Unpaid Dividend Account will be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). Shares on which dividends remain unclaimed for seven consecutive years will also be transferred to the IEPF in accordance with Section 124 of the Act and applicable IEPF rules.

## E-voting and the 45th AGM

Infosys has provided members with remote e-voting facilities through the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL), in compliance with Section 108 of the Act, Regulation 44 of the LODR Regulations, and the applicable SEBI circular. Members who have voted through remote e-voting before the AGM may participate in the AGM but may not vote again on the same resolutions.

Hemanth, Holla & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries, has been appointed as the Scrutinizer for conducting the e-voting process.

Members holding shares in physical or dematerialized form as of the cut-off date, June 16, 2026, may vote electronically. The remote e-voting period is:

- Commencement: Thursday, June 18, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. IST
- Closing: Monday, June 22, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. IST

Voting rights are proportionate to each member's share of the Company's paid-up equity share capital as of June 16, 2026. A person who is not a member on the cut-off date may treat the Notice as information only.

Members attending the AGM through video conference who have not voted through remote e-voting, and who are otherwise eligible, may vote through the e-voting system during the AGM. A person holding shares in physical mode, or a person who acquired shares after the Notice was sent and became a member by the cut-off date, may obtain login credentials by requesting them from NSDL. Existing NSDL remote e-voting users may use their existing credentials.

The Scrutinizer will submit the report to the Chairman or an authorized person within 48 hours of the conclusion of the AGM. The results and Scrutinizer's report will be communicated to the stock exchanges, NSDL, and the Company's registrar and transfer agent, and displayed on the Company's website.

## Integrated Annual Report and shareholder records

The Integrated Annual Report for 2025-26, the Notice of the 45th AGM, and e-voting instructions are being sent electronically to members whose email addresses are registered with the Company or their depository participant. Members without registered email addresses will receive a letter containing the web link and access path to the Integrated Annual Report.

The Notice and Integrated Annual Report will also be available through the Company's website, the BSE and NSE websites, and the NSDL e-voting website.

Members holding physical shares must furnish the following KYC details for their folios:

1. PAN
2. Contact details, including postal address with PIN and mobile number
3. Bank account details
4. Specimen signature

Effective April 1, 2024, dividends will be paid exclusively through electronic mode to physical shareholders who have completed the mandatory KYC requirements.

| Type of holder | Investor service or requirement | Form or process |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Register or update PAN, email address, bank details, and other KYC details for securities held in physical mode | Form ISR-1 |
| Physical | Update the signature of a securities holder | Form ISR-2 |
| Physical | Submit a nomination under Rule 19(1) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 | Form SH-13 |
| Physical | Declaration to opt out of nomination | Form ISR-3 |
| Physical | Cancel a nomination or change the nominee | Form SH-14, along with ISR-3 where applicable |
| Physical | Request a duplicate certificate or other service requests for shares, debentures, or bonds held in physical mode | Form ISR-4 |
| Demat | Register or update email address and bank account details | Contact the respective depository participant |
| Demat | Update nomination details | Contact the respective depository participant |

Under Section 72 of the Act, members holding physical shares may submit a nomination using Form SH-13. Members holding dematerialized shares may contact their respective depository participants to update nomination details.

The Notice of the 45th AGM and the Integrated Annual Report are being provided electronically in accordance with the applicable circulars. As the AGM will be held through video conference, the route map, proxy form, and attendance slip are not attached to the Notice.
