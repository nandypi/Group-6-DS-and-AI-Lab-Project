The applicable Indian corporate statutory tax rate was 25.17% for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

Income tax expense includes a reversal, net of provisions, of ₹877 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026 and provisions, net of reversals, of ₹132 crore for the year ended March 31, 2025. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily resulted from adjudication of disputed matters after tax-return filings and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, Infosys received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Indian Income Tax Authorities for assessment year 2013–14 and assessment years 2017–18 to 2021–22. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently, Infosys recognized pretax interest income of ₹381 crore, reversed income tax provisions aggregating ₹869 crore with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss, and reduced contingent liabilities by ₹86 crore.

During the year ended March 31, 2025, Infosys received orders under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Indian Income Tax Authorities for assessment years 2016–17 and 2019–20. These orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently, Infosys recognized pretax interest income of ₹327 crore, reversed income tax provisions aggregating ₹183 crore with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss, and reduced contingent liabilities by ₹1,068 crore.

Foreign tax expense primarily relates to income taxes payable in the United States. In prior years, Infosys benefited from Indian tax incentives for software and service exports from units registered under the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005. Eligible SEZ units commencing services on or after April 1, 2005 could deduct:

- 100% of profits or gains from exported services for the first five years from the financial year in which services commenced.
- 50% of such profits or gains for the following five years.
- Up to 50% of such profits or gains for a further five years, subject to creating and using a Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Deferred income tax for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially relates to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

Infosys is subject to a 15% US Branch Profit Tax (BPT) to the extent that its US branch's net profit for the year exceeds the increase in the branch's net assets during the year, as computed under the Internal Revenue Code.

As at March 31, 2026, Infosys' US branch net assets were approximately ₹7,736 crore. The Company recognized a deferred tax liability for Branch Profit Tax of ₹207 crore, net of credits, because it estimates that the branch profits are expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches of ₹19,270 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹16,593 crore as at March 31, 2025. Infosys can control the timing of reversal, and it is probable that the differences will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The Group primarily intends to repatriate earnings from subsidiaries and branches only to the extent that they can be distributed tax-free.

Deferred income tax assets were not recognized on accumulated losses of ₹4,868 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹4,597 crore as at March 31, 2025, as it is probable that future taxable profits will not be available against which the unused tax losses can be utilized in the foreseeable future.
