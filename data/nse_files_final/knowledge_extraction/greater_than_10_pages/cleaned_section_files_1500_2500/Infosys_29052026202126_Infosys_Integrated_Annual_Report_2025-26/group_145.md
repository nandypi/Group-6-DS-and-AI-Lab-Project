## Trade receivables and contract balances

The timing of revenue recognition, billings, and cash collections results in trade receivables, unbilled revenue, and unearned revenue on the Group's Consolidated Balance Sheet. Amounts are billed as work progresses according to contractual terms, either periodically, such as monthly or quarterly, or upon achievement of contractual milestones.

Trade receivables are unconditional rights to consideration. Unbilled revenue from time-and-material and fixed-price maintenance contracts is classified as a financial asset when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

For other fixed-price contracts, invoicing is based on contractual milestones. Because the right to consideration depends on completion of those milestones, related unbilled revenue is classified as a non-financial contract asset. Amounts invoiced in excess of revenue earned are classified as unearned revenue. Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are presented net of impairment in the Consolidated Balance Sheet.

For the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Company recognized revenue of ` 6,608 crore and ` 5,669 crore, respectively, from opening unearned revenue as of April 1, 2025 and April 1, 2024.

For the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, ` 4,839 crore and ` 4,896 crore, respectively, of unbilled revenue related to other fixed-price and fixed-time-frame contracts as of April 1, 2025 and April 1, 2024 was reclassified to trade receivables upon billing after completion of contractual milestones.

## Remaining performance obligation disclosure

The remaining performance obligation disclosure represents the aggregate transaction price yet to be recognized as revenue at the end of the reporting period, together with the expected timing of recognition.

Applying the practical expedient under Ind AS 115, the Group has not disclosed remaining performance obligations for contracts where recognized revenue corresponds directly with the value to the customer of performance completed to date. These typically include time-and-material and unit-of-work contracts.

Remaining performance obligation estimates are subject to change due to terminations, changes in contract scope, periodic revalidations, adjustments for revenue that has not materialized, and currency fluctuations.

The aggregate value of completely or partially unsatisfied performance obligations, excluding contracts meeting the criteria above, was ` 1,30,017 crore as of March 31, 2026. The Group expected to recognize approximately 49.7% within the following year and the remainder thereafter. The corresponding aggregate value was ` 1,04,785 crore as of March 31, 2025.

Customers can generally terminate the contracts, typically subject to an enforceable termination penalty. Generally, customers have not terminated contracts without cause.

## 2.19 Other income, net

## Accounting policy

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts, and exchange gains or losses on the translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities.

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## Foreign currency

## Functional currency

The functional currency of Infosys, its Indian subsidiaries, and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. The functional currencies of foreign subsidiaries are their respective local currencies. The financial statements are presented in Indian rupees, rounded off to crore; one crore equals ten million.

## Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the Balance Sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss within exchange gains or losses on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in Other Comprehensive Income as qualifying cash-flow hedges.

Non-monetary foreign-currency assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate on the date the fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized on settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Foreign-currency-denominated revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate on the transaction date.

For translation of foreign subsidiaries’ financial statements into the presentation currency, assets and liabilities are translated at the Balance Sheet date exchange rate, while revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated at the average exchange rate for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves under other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is fully disposed of, the relevant currency translation reserve is transferred to net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss. Changes in the parent's ownership that do not result in loss of control are recorded through equity.

Other Comprehensive Income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair-value adjustments arising on the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated at the exchange rate in effect at the Balance Sheet date.

## Government grant

The Group recognizes government grants when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Government grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss systematically over the useful life of the asset. Government grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in net profit over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.
