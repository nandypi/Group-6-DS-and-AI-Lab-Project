The Infosys Prize and Aarohan Social Innovation Awards provide platforms for innovators and social entrepreneurs.

## Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (iCETS)

iCETS is Infosys’ emerging-technology incubation unit. It delivers next-generation platforms and innovation-as-a-service powered by New Emerging and eXploratory Technologies (NEXT).

Its innovation framework is structured around three stages: Now, Near, and Next.

## Now

iCETS uses reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities to operationalize AI at enterprise scale. Infosys Topaz Fabric provides composable and interoperable AI agents, tools, services, and models. It is designed to help organizations optimize existing IT processes, automate or eliminate manual tasks, and build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

## Near

Infosys Living Labs are global innovation hubs where clients, partners, startups, and academia co-create solutions using emerging technologies. They support contextualization, experimentation, iteration, and validation in controlled environments.

Client Living Labs deliver innovation-as-a-service through three programs:

- IncubateNext: scalable, real-world innovation
- ImagineNext: future opportunity discovery
- SenseNext: converting early market and technology signals into action

## Infosys Innovation Network (IIN)

The Infosys Innovation Network is a partnership of selected startups, universities, hyperscalers, and Infosys. It focuses on emerging technology areas including AI, generative AI, data, fintech, cloud, cybersecurity, and InsurTech.

IIN includes more than 430 startups, which have generated more than 1,300 client impressions.

## Infosys Business Incubator (IBI)

The Infosys Business Incubator develops and scales entrepreneurship capabilities within Infosys through next-generation micro-platform and micro-service-line businesses. It has engaged with more than 1,000 entrepreneurs.

Five ventures were selected for incubation from cohort 1 and three from cohort 2.

The Be A Maker program supports creative ideas in a phygital format and enables rapid innovation through expert feedback. More than 200 ideas have been generated and five phygital showcases have been created for clients.

## Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI)

The Infosys Knowledge Institute is the business research and thought-leadership arm of Infosys. It works with Infosys experts and clients to produce research and insights on the business impact of technology. Its research includes primary research, large-scale surveys, and quantitative analysis.

IKI’s publications include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology-trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals. IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets since inception.

## Product innovation

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across people, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve product innovation with Infosys’ global scale and addresses enterprise operations and core business functions.

## IP, patents and trademarks

Infosys develops platforms, products, and tools that form its intellectual-property portfolio. These assets are available through the Infosys Marketplace and are used for market differentiation and productivity enhancement.

Infosys has 973 patents granted or pending. More than 900 trademarks are registered or pending across 51 countries. Infosys is regarded by Indian authorities as a “Well Known” trademark.

## #1 rating in AI

Infosys has built four small language models for banking, IT operations, cybersecurity, and enterprises broadly.

Infosys is advancing from carbon neutrality toward climate positivity through its Reduce and Restore strategy, combining operational efficiency, clean energy, carbon sequestration, and nature-based solutions.

## 1. Energy efficiency and lowcarbon approach

Infosys uses energy-efficient, low-carbon infrastructure across the building life cycle. Its campuses use radiant cooling, radiant heating, climate-adaptive cooling, intelligent controls, and climate-responsive design.

A total of 84% of Infosys’ office space is certified to the highest green-building standards.

## 2. Renewable energy

Infosys has installed 62.42 MWp of solar photovoltaic capacity through rooftop and ground-mounted systems. It also procures renewable power through third-party power purchase agreements and green-tariff mechanisms.

## 3. Decarbonizing the supply chain

Infosys works with its supplier ecosystem to decarbonize the value chain, with a focus on high-emission IT-hardware suppliers. Activities include onsite assessments, senior-level discussions, Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions measurement, emissions baselining, and adoption of verified Environmental Product Declarations.

## 4. Carbon offsets and regeneration

Infosys addresses unavoidable emissions through community-focused carbon-offset programs, including clean cookstove initiatives in Rajasthan and biogas projects in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

Its agroforestry program has planted approximately 14 million saplings across multiple states. These plantations support carbon sequestration, soil and water resilience, and rural livelihoods.

The initiatives have benefited more than 300,000 rural families and created more than 4,800 local jobs.

## Water stewardship

Infosys is a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate and focuses on reducing consumption, increasing water reuse, and improving water availability in operating communities.

One hundred percent of wastewater across Infosys campuses is recycled. The program is supported by rainwater harvesting and demand-side efficiency measures.

The Bangalore campus is certified as water positive. Based on NITI AAYOG guidelines, nine Infosys campuses in India achieved Scope 1 certification for water-positive or water-neutral status during the year.
