## Governance, leadership and oversight

## 7. Statement by director responsible for the business responsibility report, highlighting ESG-related challenges, targets and achievements

Infosys’ ESG Vision 2030 guides its governance, environmental stewardship, workplace inclusion and diversity, and use of technology to create long-term stakeholder value.

The company’s ESG priorities include:

- Accelerating climate commitments.
- Enabling large-scale skilling.
- Embedding responsible practices across operations and the value chain.

Infosys identified climate transition imperatives, rapid technological change, increasing regulatory requirements, and higher expectations for transparency and accountability as relevant operating conditions. During fiscal 2026, the company enhanced its ESG frameworks and disclosures, supported by governance mechanisms, performance metrics, and stakeholder engagement.

The company also identified challenges relating to climate transition, talent transformation, and the macroeconomic environment. It stated that regulatory landscapes, stakeholder expectations, and climate-related risks would continue to evolve.

Further information on ESG-related challenges, targets, performance, and achievements is provided in the ESG Report and the “Approaching Value Creation” and “Delivering Value” chapters of the Integrated Annual Report.
