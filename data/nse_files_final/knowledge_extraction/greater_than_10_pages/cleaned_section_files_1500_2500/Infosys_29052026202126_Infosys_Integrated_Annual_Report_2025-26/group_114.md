## 2.25 Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

As per Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, a company meeting the applicability threshold needs to spend at least 2% of its average net profit for the immediately preceding three financial years on CSR activities. The Company has formed a CSR committee as required by the Act.

The funds were primarily utilized for activities specified in Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013, including education, skilling and livelihoods, environmental sustainability and ecological balance, healthcare including preventive healthcare, promotion of national heritage, art and culture, rural development, and disaster relief and rehabilitation.
