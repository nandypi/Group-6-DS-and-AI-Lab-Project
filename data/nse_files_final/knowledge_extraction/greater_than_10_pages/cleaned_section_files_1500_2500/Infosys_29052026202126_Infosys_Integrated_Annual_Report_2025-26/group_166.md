## Additional information on directors recommended for appointment / reappointment as required under Regulation 36 of the LODR Regulations and applicable secretarial standards

### Nandan M. Nilekani — Chairman

Nandan M. Nilekani is the Co-Founder and Chairman of Infosys Technologies Limited. He was the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), serving in the rank of Cabinet Minister from 2009 to 2014. He is also the Co-Founder and Chairman of EkStep Foundation. In January 2023, he was appointed Co-Chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion, and Development.

He was born in Bengaluru and holds a bachelor's degree from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bombay.

- **Age:** 70 years
- **Nature of expertise:** Information technology, leadership, strategy, board service and governance, financial matters, diversity, global business, sales and marketing, cybersecurity, mergers and acquisitions, risk management, and sustainability and ESG
- **Inter-se relationships with directors and KMP:** None

#### Remuneration

Nandan voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company.

#### Key terms and conditions of appointment

As set out in the resolution at Item No. 3 of the Notice, Nandan, whose office as director is liable to retire at the ensuing AGM and who is eligible, seeks reappointment.

#### Board appointment history and meeting attendance

Nandan was first appointed to the Board in 1981. He ceased to be a Board member on July 9, 2009, and was unanimously appointed as a Board member and Chairman effective August 24, 2017. He voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company. Details of his appointment, remuneration, and Board meeting attendance are provided in the Corporate Governance Report of the Integrated Annual Report.
