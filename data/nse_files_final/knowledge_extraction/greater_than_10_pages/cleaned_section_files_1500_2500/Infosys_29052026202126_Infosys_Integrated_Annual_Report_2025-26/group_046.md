## Materially significant related party transactions

There were no materially significant related party transactions that may have potential conflict with the interests of the listed entity as a whole under the Related Party Transactions Policy.

During the year, the Company obtained shareholders’ approval for material related party transactions in accordance with Regulation 23 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Details are available in the [2025 AGM notice](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/documents/agm-notice2025.pdf).

(3) Salil Parekh:

- Receives remuneration only from Infosys Limited and not from any subsidiary or group company.
- Exercised 2,72,400 Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) under the 2015 Plan and 64,690 RSUs under the 2019 Plan during fiscal 2026.
- On the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and with shareholder approval, the Board approved the following grants under the terms of his employment agreement effective July 1, 2022:
  - 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on achievement of certain performance targets.
  - 13,273 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on achievement of environment, social and governance milestones determined by the Board.
  - 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant period, as determined by the Board.
  - 18,132 annual time-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2015 Plan, effective February 1, 2026, vesting over three years.
  - 66,366 performance-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on the Company’s achievement of performance criteria specified in the 2019 Plan.

These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement. The RSUs were issued at par value.

In accordance with the Listing Regulations, no employee, including key managerial personnel, senior management, directors, or promoters of a listed entity, may enter into an agreement for himself or on behalf of another person with any shareholder or third party regarding compensation or profit-sharing connected with dealings in the Company’s securities without prior Board approval and shareholder approval by ordinary resolution. No such instances were reported during fiscal 2026.
