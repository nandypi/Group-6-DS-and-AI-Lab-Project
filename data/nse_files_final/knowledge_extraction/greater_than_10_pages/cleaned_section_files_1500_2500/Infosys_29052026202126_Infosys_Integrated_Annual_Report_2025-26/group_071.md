## 5. Does the entity have a business continuity and disaster management plan? Give details in 100 words/ web link.

Yes. Infosys has a Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) called Phoenix, certified under ISO 22301:2019, Security and Resilience—Business Continuity Management Standard.

Phoenix provides a framework for planning, establishing, implementing, operating, monitoring, reviewing, maintaining, and continually improving business continuity measures across Infosys and its subsidiaries. In addition to the corporate-level Phoenix plan, comprehensive plans are created at three operational levels:

- Business functions
- Locations
- Accounts

The BCMS is integrated into the Enterprise Risk Management Framework. Plans address crisis scenarios, including catastrophes and natural or human-made disasters that could disrupt or severely constrain operations. Detailed risk assessments at the function, location, and account levels are documented with mitigation plans and controls.

The management system is validated through tests and exercises. Infosys reports that various incidents have been addressed without significant impacts on business continuity or employee safety.

## Enabling Responsible Value Chain Disclosure and Decarbonization

In fiscal 2026, Infosys initiated a SEBI-aligned supplier engagement program focused on ESG disclosures under the BRSR Core framework. The program covered the top 2% of upstream suppliers by spend, focusing on high-impact suppliers, including human resource service providers and IT service vendors. These suppliers represented approximately 13% of total spend.

The engagement collected supplier data on climate practices, emissions management, and reporting maturity. Based on the assessment:

- Scope 1 emissions attributed to Infosys: **667 tCO2e**
- Scope 2 emissions attributed to Infosys: **219 tCO2e**
- Attributed emissions as a percentage of the suppliers’ combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions: **approximately 1.6%**

Emissions attributable to Infosys were calculated using a spend-based allocation methodology aligned with the GHG Protocol for value-chain disclosure under SEBI requirements. They were not included in Infosys’s reported Scope 3 inventory.

The exercise improved emissions visibility, supplier engagement, and prioritization of decarbonization opportunities. Infosys also stated that climate considerations are being integrated into procurement processes.
