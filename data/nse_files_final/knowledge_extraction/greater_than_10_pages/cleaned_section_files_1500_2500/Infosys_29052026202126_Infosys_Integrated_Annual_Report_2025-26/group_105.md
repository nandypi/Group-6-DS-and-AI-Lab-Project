## 2.21.2 Provident fund

Eligible Infosys employees receive provident fund benefits under a defined benefit plan. Eligible employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered employees’ salaries.

The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund. The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. Infosys is obligated to make good any shortfall between the trust’s investment returns and the notified interest rate.

## 2.21.3 Superannuation

Certain Infosys employees participate in a defined contribution plan. The Company has no obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund. The trust fund corpus is invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## 2.21.4 Compensated absences

Infosys has a policy covering accumulating and non-accumulating compensated absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined through an actuarial valuation performed by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The valuation is based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the balance sheet date. Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
