## 10. Briefly describe the waste management practices adopted in your establishments.

Infosys’ waste management strategy is based on the 5R principle: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Refurbish / Repurpose, and Recycle. The company reports that 99% of disposed waste is diverted from landfills.

Infosys is working toward a 2030 goal of zero waste to landfills by adopting TRUE Zero Waste Certification at its India campuses. As of fiscal 2026, 46% of its owned campuses in India had achieved TRUE Zero Waste Certification from Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI).

As an IT company, Infosys generates e-waste and is transitioning toward a refurbishment-first model where feasible. Materials that cannot be refurbished are handled through authorized recycling vendors. Authorized Pollution Control Board recyclers and refurbishers are periodically evaluated for compliance with applicable legal requirements and the extent of resource circularity in their processes.
