## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)

In accordance with Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Listing Regulations, the BRSR forms part of this Integrated Annual Report. It describes initiatives undertaken by the Company from an environmental, social, and governance perspective.

SEBI updated the BRSR format through circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122 dated July 12, 2023, to incorporate BRSR Core, a subset of the BRSR containing specific key performance indicators under nine ESG attributes. These indicators are subject to mandatory reasonable assurance by an independent assurance provider.

Infosys appointed Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP as the assurance provider. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP also assured selected indicators of the ESG Report designed based on GRI standards.

The ESG Report, based on the GRI Standard 2021, is available at: [Infosys ESG Report 2025-26](https://www.infosys.com/sustainability/documents/infosys-esg-report-2025-26.pdf).

## 8. Other disclosures and affirmations

Pursuant to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014, the Company affirms that for the year ended March 31, 2026:

- No proceedings filed by or against the Company were pending under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, before the National Company Law Tribunal or any other court.
- There was no instance of a one-time settlement with any bank or financial institution.
- No significant or material orders were passed by regulators, courts, or tribunals that impacted the going-concern status or the Company’s future operations.
- The Company complied with the provisions of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, and the rules made thereunder, including applicable obligations relating to maternity benefits for eligible employees.
