## 2. Notes to the Consolidated financial statements

## 2.1 Business combinations

## Accounting policy

Business combinations are accounted for using the acquisition method under Ind AS 103, *Business Combinations*.

The purchase price is measured at the fair value, at the date control is transferred to the Group, of assets transferred, equity instruments issued, and liabilities incurred or assumed. It includes the fair value of contingent consideration. Identifiable assets acquired, liabilities assumed, and contingent liabilities assumed are initially measured at fair value on the acquisition date.

Contingent consideration is remeasured at fair value at each reporting date, with changes recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

Non-controlling interests are initially measured either at fair value or at the non-controlling interests’ proportionate share of the acquiree’s identifiable net assets. The measurement basis is selected separately for each acquisition. Subsequently, the carrying amount of non-controlling interests includes the initial amount recognized plus their share of subsequent changes in subsidiary equity.

Business combinations between entities under common control are accounted for at the carrying values of the assets acquired and liabilities assumed in the Group’s consolidated financial statements.

Options issued by the Group over non-controlling interests in subsidiaries are accounted for as financial liabilities. They are initially recognized at the estimated present value of the gross obligations and subsequently measured at fair value. If an option expires unexercised, the liability is derecognized.

## Acquisition during the year ended March 31, 2026

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership or voting interests in the following entities:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — Acquired on April 30, 2025. The energy and business consulting services company is headquartered in Texas, USA. The acquisition is expected to add capabilities in trading and risk management, particularly in the energy sector.
2. **The Missing Link** — Comprises The Missing Link Security Pty. Ltd., The Missing Link Security Limited, and The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd. Acquired on April 30, 2025, the cybersecurity service provider is headquartered in Australia. The acquisition is expected to strengthen cybersecurity capabilities and expand the Group’s presence in the Australian market.

Goodwill of ₹79 crore is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of ₹813 crore comprises upfront cash consideration of ₹743 crore and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of ₹70 crore at the acquisition date.

Transaction costs, including finder’s fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of ₹34 crore related to these acquisitions were included in administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended March 31, 2026.
