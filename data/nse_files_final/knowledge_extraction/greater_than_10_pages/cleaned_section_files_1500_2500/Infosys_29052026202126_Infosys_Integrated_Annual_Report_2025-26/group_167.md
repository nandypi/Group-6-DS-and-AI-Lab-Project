## General guidelines for VC participation

Infosys’ 45th AGM will be conducted through video conferencing in accordance with applicable legal provisions and circulars. The Company will provide the facility to attend the meeting through its designated virtual meeting platform.

Members may participate using desktops, smartphones, laptops, or similar devices. A stable Wi-Fi or LAN connection is recommended because mobile devices, tablets, or mobile hotspots may experience audio or video interruptions.

The Company may limit the number of members permitted to ask questions based on the time available during the AGM.

## Instructions for e-voting

The electronic voting process uses the NSDL e-voting system. Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with NSDL, CDSL, or their Depository Participants.

Shareholders are advised to keep their mobile number and email address updated in their demat accounts to access the e-voting facility.

### (A) Login method for e-voting for individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode

NSDL demat shareholders may access e-voting through the NSDL e-Services or e-voting platforms, using their existing credentials or the applicable demat-account authentication process. After authentication, shareholders can select Infosys or NSDL as the e-voting service provider and cast their vote during the remote e-voting period or during the virtual meeting, where applicable.

CDSL demat shareholders may access e-voting through the CDSL Easi/Easiest platform or through the e-voting access available on the CDSL website. After authentication, eligible companies with active voting can be selected and the shareholder can proceed to the e-voting service provider’s platform.

Shareholders holding securities in demat mode through a Depository Participant may log in using their demat-account credentials. After successful authentication, they can select the e-voting option and access Infosys’ e-voting page through NSDL or CDSL.

Shareholders who cannot retrieve their User ID or password are advised to use the “Forgot User ID” and “Forgot Password” options available on the relevant e-voting website.
