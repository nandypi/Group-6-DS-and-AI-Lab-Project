## Board as a trustee

The Board recognizes its primary role as trustee of shareholder capital and seeks to uphold high standards of corporate governance.

## Corporate governance guidelines

Infosys’ corporate governance guidelines are established by the Board and provide a framework for directors and Management to pursue the Company’s objectives for the benefit of stakeholders. The guidelines are framed in conjunction with the Company’s Memorandum & Articles of Association, Board committee charters, and applicable laws, regulations, and guidelines in India, the US, and other applicable jurisdictions.

The guidelines are available at: https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/Documents/corporate-governance-guidelines.pdf.

## Role of the Board of Directors

The primary role of the Board is trusteeship: protecting and enhancing shareholder value. The Board’s responsibilities include:

- Exercising appropriate control to ensure that the Company is managed efficiently and fulfills stakeholder and societal expectations.
- Monitoring the effectiveness of governance practices and making changes as necessary.
- Providing strategic guidance and monitoring Management.
- Exercising independent judgment on corporate affairs.
- Assigning a sufficient number of non-executive directors to matters involving potential conflicts of interest.
- Reviewing and guiding corporate strategy, major action plans, risk policy, annual budgets, and business plans.
- Setting performance objectives, monitoring implementation and corporate performance, and overseeing major capital expenditures, acquisitions, and divestments.

## Independent directors

Infosys follows the definitions of an independent director under the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the New York Stock Exchange listed company manual, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, and applicable US securities laws.

Based on disclosures received from all independent directors and the Board’s assessment, the independent directors fulfill the applicable requirements and are independent of Management.

## Board composition

As of April 23, 2026, the Board comprised ten members:

- One non-executive, non-independent Chairman
- One Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director (CEO & MD)
- Eight independent directors

The Board member profiles in the Integrated Annual Report include nationality, age, appointment and reappointment dates, Board tenure, term-ending dates, shareholding, Board memberships in Indian companies, committee details under Regulation 26 of the Listing Regulations, and core expertise and competencies.

There were no inter se relationships among the Board members. The Company had no pecuniary relationship with any non-executive director and did not provide loans or advances during the year to firms or companies in which directors were interested.

The Board has six committees:

- Audit Committee
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee
- Stakeholders Relationship Committee
- Risk Management Committee
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee
- ESG Committee

All committees comprise only independent directors, with one independent director serving as chairperson. Infosys also has a Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee under the Risk Management Committee, comprising only independent directors.

## Board meetings

### Scheduling and selection of agenda items for Board meetings

Tentative dates for Board meetings in the next fiscal year are decided in advance and published in the Integrated Annual Report. The Chairman and Company Secretary, in consultation with the CEO & MD and lead independent director, propose the agenda for each meeting. The agenda and explanatory notes are circulated to directors in advance. Each Board member may suggest additional agenda items.

The Board meets at least once a quarter to review quarterly results and other agenda items. Additional meetings are held when necessary. Independent directors are expected to attend at least four quarterly Board meetings and the Annual General Meeting (AGM). Video and teleconferencing facilities are provided to enable participation by directors based in different parts of the world.

Board members are expected to prepare for, attend, and participate in Board and applicable committee meetings and ensure that other commitments do not materially interfere with their responsibilities to the Company.

## Meeting of independent directors

Independent directors are required to meet at least once in a financial year without the attendance of non-independent directors and Management under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, and the related rules.

During the year, the independent directors met four times without executive directors and Management. Matters discussed included:

- Company performance and risks
- Flow of information to the Board
- Competition and strategy
- Leadership strengths and weaknesses
- Governance and compliance
- Board changes and succession planning
- Human resources matters
- Performance of executive Board members, committees, and the Chairman

## Responsible leadership

The Board and Management work together to establish standards of responsible leadership.

## Responsibilities of the Board leadership

The Board oversees Management’s functions and protects the long-term interests of stakeholders. The responsibilities and authority of the Chairman, CEO & MD, and lead independent director are described below.

## Chairman

Infosys has separated the roles of Chairman and CEO & MD. The Chairman leads the Board, promotes Board integrity, and supports a culture in which the Board works for the long-term benefit of the Company and its stakeholders.

The Chairman presides over Board and shareholder meetings, facilitates communication among directors, and oversees governance matters relating to the organization, composition, and effectiveness of the Board and its committees, as well as the performance of individual directors.

The Chairman works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee on Board and committee composition, director appointments, succession planning, Board evaluation, and feedback to individual directors.

## CEO & MD

The CEO & MD is responsible for executing corporate strategy in consultation with the Board, managing the Company, achieving annual and long-term business targets, and leading other executive leaders.

The role also includes monitoring competitive conditions and industry developments, identifying opportunities for expansion and acquisition, building customer and market relationships, and implementing the organization’s vision, mission, and overall direction.

## Lead Independent Director

The lead independent director was appointed by the Board to provide independent leadership. The general authority and responsibilities of the role are decided by the independent directors, with additional duties determined by the Board.

The lead independent director provides leadership to the independent directors, liaises on their behalf, and supports the Board’s effectiveness and the quality of governance.
