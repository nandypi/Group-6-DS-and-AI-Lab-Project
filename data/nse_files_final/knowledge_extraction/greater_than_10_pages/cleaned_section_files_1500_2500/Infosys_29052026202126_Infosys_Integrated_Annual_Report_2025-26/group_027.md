## 4. Executive summary along with web-link(s) of Impact Assessment of CSR Projects carried out in pursuance of subrule (3) of rule 8

For fiscal 2026, the Company engaged an external agency to support an independent review and impact assessment of its CSR projects.

### Methodology

The assessment used a mixed-method approach involving quantitative and qualitative research tools, as relevant, for primary data collection. The assessment team conducted virtual and on-field interactions with project beneficiaries and other stakeholders. The findings were consolidated for management consideration using the Inclusiveness, Relevance, Effectiveness, Convergence and Sustainability (IRECS) Framework. The framework assessed implementation efficacy and efficiency in achieving desired project outputs relative to inputs.

### Summary

During the year, an impact assessment was conducted on 12 eligible CSR projects across various states in India. The projects align with the ESG framework and support 13 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals, covering education, healthcare, women empowerment, sustainability and other themes.

### Theme 1: Education and skill development

**Impact:** Digital education and access to online learning content had a positive impact on students’ academic performance and reduced dropout rates.

| 3 | eVidyaloka Trust - Rural digital and STEM education programme | Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Uttar Pradesh | Infosys Foundation |

### Theme 2: Environment sustainability and ecological balance

**Impact:** Improved cookstove projects reported reductions in waste generation, cooking time, indoor air pollution and associated health hazards. Women were empowered through engagement in social activities within their communities.

**Impact:** The projects strengthened the physical, digital and institutional environments supporting art and heritage. Renovated and purpose-built performance spaces improved accessibility for artists and audiences, while outreach programmes expanded visibility, support and participation for underrepresented practitioners.
