## 2.16 Provisions

## Accounting policy

A provision is recognized when, as a result of a past event, the Group has a present legal or constructive obligation that is reasonably estimable and an outflow of economic benefits is probable. Provisions are determined by discounting expected future cash flows at a pre-tax rate reflecting current market assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific to the liability.

The Group recognizes a reimbursement asset only when it is virtually certain that reimbursement will be received if the Group settles the obligation.

## a. Post-sales client support

The Group provides clients with fixed-period post-sales support on fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts. Costs associated with these services are accrued when the related revenues are recorded and included in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss. The Group estimates these costs based on historical experience and periodically reviews the estimates for material changes in assumptions and likelihood of occurrence.

## b. Onerous contracts

Provisions for onerous contracts are recognized when the expected benefits from a contract are lower than the unavoidable costs of meeting future obligations. Estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable, based on estimated efforts or costs to complete the contract.

The provision is measured at the present value of the lower of the expected cost of terminating the contract and the expected net cost of continuing with the contract. Before establishing a provision, the Group recognizes any impairment loss on assets associated with the contract.

## 2.17 Income taxes

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss, except for amounts relating to items recognized directly in equity, which are recognized in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements, subject to applicable exceptions. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized in the period of enactment or substantive enactment. Deferred tax assets are recognized to the extent that future taxable profit is probable and available for utilizing deductible temporary differences and tax losses.

Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when those earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right of set-off and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously. Interim income tax provisions are based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions earned on exercising employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.
