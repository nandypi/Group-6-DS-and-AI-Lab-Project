## Managing financial capital

Infosys has a cash-generating business and access to capital markets across the world. Its credit rating allows it to raise debt at competitive rates in the future, if needed. The primary sources of funds are cash from operations and income from short- and long-term investments.

Primary sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents and cash flow generated from operations. Infosys remains debt-free and maintains adequate cash for operational and strategic requirements, unforeseen events, and investment returns.

Consolidated cash and investments include:

- Bank deposits
- Debt mutual funds
- Commercial Paper (CP)
- Quoted bonds and securities issued by government and quasi-government organizations
- Non-convertible debentures
- Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
- Instruments issued by eligible financial institutions with high credit ratings, guided by the treasury policy

Infosys also creates financial assets by investing in the startup ecosystem. These investments provide access to innovation that, together with Infosys’ services and solutions, can deliver benefits to clients. Investments most often comprise minority equity positions in startup organizations and/or venture capital funds.

## Shareholder value creation

Infosys seeks to fulfill investor expectations through responsible business decisions, governance, integrity, transparency, and disciplined capital allocation. The Company communicates with investors through quarterly post-result calls, analyst meets, the Annual General Meeting, and one-to-one and group interactions.

## Distribution of value created through Capital Allocation Policy

Effective from financial year 2025, Infosys expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. The Company also expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

The Company returned approximately 82.1% of free cash flow for fiscals 2025 and 2026 through dividends and buybacks, in line with the Capital Allocation Policy.

Infosys’ Employee Value Proposition (EVP) is based on meaningful work, continuous growth, protected well-being, and a sense of belonging. The Company focuses on employee experience through policies, processes, programs, and systems designed around employees.

## Employee Value Proposition

The EVP comprises the experiences, rewards, benefits, and fulfillment employees receive for their commitment and skills. Infosys refers to its employees as “Infoscions.”

## Employee health and wellness

Infosys is committed to a safe, healthy, and incident-free workplace for employees, contractors, and other stakeholders. Its integrated Occupational Health and Safety Management System is aligned with ISO 45001 and includes workplace hazard identification, risk mitigation controls, regular audits, safety initiatives, employee engagement programs, and emergency preparedness drills.

The HALE (Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment) framework integrates wellness into the Company’s culture and operating model. It covers Physical, Emotional, Social, and Digital Well-being, with Safety as a core component.

## Nurturing workplace inclusivity

Inclusion is embedded in how Infosys leads, learns, and grows. As part of its ESG Vision 2030, the Company aims to achieve 45% female representation in its workforce by 2030. Women represented 39.5% of the total workforce in fiscal 2026.

## Infosys Pulse

Infosys Pulse is an employee feedback platform that captures insights on workplace experience. It supports leadership alignment with employee sentiment and data-driven organizational actions.

## InStep

InStep is Infosys’ global internship program with:

- More than 220 partner institutions in over 50 countries
- More than 3,500 alumni members and interns from over 50 nationalities

The program supports academic partnerships, patents, publications, and localization efforts. The launch of InStep Japan expanded the program’s global talent initiatives.

## Flexibility by design and distributed workforce

Infosys has established innovation hubs, nearshore centers, and digital design studios across geographies. Its hybrid work model supports working from home and offices near employees’ homes. Offices established in India over the last few years include Ahmedabad, Guwahati, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam, Noida, Hubballi, Indore, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, and Coimbatore.

The distributed-hybrid model supports self-development, collaboration, and business outcomes. The Company monitors work-hour patterns to identify excessive or unsustainable workloads. Managers receive insights to identify workload imbalances, staffing challenges, and employees who may need additional support.

## Leveraging AI to improve employee experience

Infosys is integrating artificial intelligence across the talent lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, continuous learning, and offboarding.

InfyMe is a mobile-first, self-service platform that integrates more than 200 service touchpoints into one interface. It supports teams in operating, connecting, and collaborating, particularly in the hybrid work model.

## Our NAVI-powered interventions

**Data Assist:** A natural-language AI assistant for queries related to leave, attendance, allocation, assets, confirmation, and master data.

**Policy Advisor:** An AI assistant that interprets policy documents and personalizes responses to employee queries.

## Moments That Matter (MTM)

MTM is a celebration platform integrated into employee lifecycle events. Smart alerts inform employees about upcoming events, and AI can generate cards and messages for colleagues and teams.

AI-driven Pulse analysis supports continuous listening by identifying employee sentiment and enabling early action to improve well-being and engagement.

In learning and development, Infosys is deploying generative AI for personalized learning paths, real-world scenario simulations, and content creation. AI companions on the Lex learning platform, including Zoiee and SynthAIz, provide tutoring, summarization, and contextual learning support.
