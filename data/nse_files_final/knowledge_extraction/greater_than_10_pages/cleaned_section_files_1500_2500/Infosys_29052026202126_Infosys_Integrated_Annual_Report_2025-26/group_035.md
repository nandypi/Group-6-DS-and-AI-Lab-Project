## Philosophy

Infosys is committed to a positive and equal-opportunity workplace free of discrimination and harassment. Its employee experience approach, described as “Experience by Design,” focuses on consistent policies, processes, programs, and systems with employees at the center.

Infosys' employee initiatives focus on helping employees find purpose in their work, contribute through high-performing teams, pursue continuous learning, and develop across career stages. Leadership development supports a diverse internal leadership pipeline.

Infosys has adopted a hybrid work model to balance flexibility with business needs. Technology and automation are used to support workforce productivity, employee engagement, and operational excellence. Recognition and rewards, diversity, equity and inclusion, and workplace safety are also part of the employee experience framework.

## Resolution hubs

Infosys maintains an open-door policy and provides forums for employees to raise workplace concerns through a grievance resolution mechanism comprising resolution hubs.

Resolution hubs follow principles of natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Investigations provide the parties involved with an opportunity to present facts and material evidence before action and closure.

## Corner Stones

The Infosys Human Rights Statement, Global Policy on Anti-harassment / Discrimination, and Code of Conduct & Ethics are identified as cornerstones of this philosophy.

## Culture

Infosys promotes a speak-up culture in which employees are encouraged to report concerns or suspected violations of the Code, Company policies and procedures, or laws.

## Resolution Hubs at Infosys include

### HEAR (Hearing Employees And Resolving)

- Formal forum for workplace grievances unresolved at the Unit HR or manager level.
- Multiple layers of grievance redressal, including an appeal mechanism.
- Neutral panel investigates concerns to reach objective outcomes.
- Anonymous concerns are reviewed in collaboration with the Whistleblower Office.

The HR team implemented enablement and preventive initiatives to strengthen awareness, employee responsibility, and an engaged and empathetic work culture.

### HEAR (Hearing Employees and Resolving)

HEAR is an independent employee grievance resolution forum under the Human Resources Department. It reviews grievances concerning workplace-related matters that may impede a positive work environment.

The HEAR Office follows a decentralized model with institutionalized presence across businesses, units, geographies, affiliates, and subsidiaries. It responds to grievances through objective reviews mediated by a neutral panel or through compliance audits assessing adherence to relevant policies.

The HEAR Office also advises departments on policy and process compliance and provides senior management with upward feedback on employee sentiment. Further information is referenced in the ESG Report 2025-2026.

## The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI)

The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI) is an enterprise-wide framework for preventing, prohibiting, and redressing sexual harassment at the workplace. It applies a gender-neutral, employee-centric, and globally consistent approach based on procedural fairness, confidentiality, natural justice, and non-retaliation.

### ASHI (Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative )

- Aims to prevent, prohibit, and redress sexual-harassment-related concerns.
- The Grievance Redressal Body (GRB) is the custodian of the initiative.
- Uses an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) framework for handling and resolving incidents.
- Has a gender-neutral scope covering sexual harassment at the workplace.

In accordance with the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, Internal Committees have been established across development centers under the relevant regulatory jurisdiction. These committees are responsible for redressing sexual-harassment concerns raised by women employees.

ASHI includes:

- Multiple safe reporting channels.
- Continuous awareness, education, and enablement.
- Monitoring and analytics.

The GRB comprises external experts, senior internal leaders, and the Investigative Council. It oversees governance, policy interpretation, and implementation relating to the prevention of workplace sexual harassment.

Employees can report concerns through the ASHI web application, the InfyMe app, or GRB@infosys.com. Cases follow a formal process aligned with statutory requirements and Company policies.

## Behavior Matters

The Behavior Matters campaign focuses on workplace behavior, respect, collaboration, productivity, employee well-being, and a positive work environment.

## Extending the commitment to contract staff

Infosys extends its safe and inclusive workplace practices to third-party personnel operating on its campuses. Refresher training sessions are delivered in nine Indian languages. Emergency and safety cards with essential contact information are provided to employees and contracted staff.

## Whistleblower Policy

Infosys' Whistleblower Policy is aligned with the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX), 2002, and the Companies Act, 2013, including the requirement for listed companies to establish a vigil mechanism for reporting genuine concerns.

The mechanism primarily covers complaints concerning probable discrepancies in accounting, internal accounting controls, or auditing. It also permits anonymous reporting by employees. The Office of Integrity and Compliance administers the policy and forum. Complaints concerning deviations from workplace policies or processes are reviewed in collaboration with the Employee Relations Department.

## Human rights

Infosys Limited is a signatory to the United Nations Global Compact and supports human rights principles under the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and the International Labor Organization's Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work. Infosys is also a signatory to the UN Women's Empowerment Principles.

The Infosys Code of Conduct and Ethics includes “Respecting Each Other” as a fundamental principle. The Human Rights Statement provides a framework for treating employees with respect and dignity and for applying common principles to business practices. Infosys states that it does not condone human rights violations or abuses.

[Further details are available in the Infosys Code of Conduct.](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-Governance/documents/codeofconduct.pdf)

## Recruitment

As of March 31, 2026, the Group employed 3,28,594 employees, including 3,10,887 professionals involved in service delivery to clients, including trainees.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys:

- Received 58,84,480 employment applications.
- Interviewed 4,50,901 applicants.
- Extended offers of employment to 87,286 applicants.
- Hired 947 women through the RestartWithInfy initiative.

The recruitment statistics exclude subsidiaries.

## Education, training and assessment (ETA)

Infosys' Foundation Training Program prepares newly onboarded entry-level hires for corporate roles. The curriculum covers more than 40 technology streams and includes generative AI and prompt-engineering courses. In fiscal 2026, 10,766 fresh graduates completed training at the Global Education Center campus in Mysuru and were released to delivery units.

The Continuous Education Program provides online self-learning, instructor-led virtual training, and in-person classroom training to reskill and upskill employees in digital technologies, methodologies, and generative AI. Bridge programs provide training and internship opportunities for employees moving to new career fields.

In fiscal 2026, 81.8% of the workforce received training, with average annual employee learning hours of 113. Cumulatively, 84% of the workforce was AI-aware.

Lex, Infosys' in-house learning platform, offers more than 40,000 curated courses, including more than 30,000 courses procured from vendor partners. On average, 37,000 employees used Lex on weekdays and approximately 8,000 used it on weekends. Average learning time was 39 minutes.

Infosys Wingspan is a configurable talent transformation platform used by several global organizations for their talent transformation programs.

Infosys Springboard provides more than 20,000 courses. Approximately 11 million learners worldwide actively consume content on the platform.
