## 1. Brief description of the Amended 2019 Plan

The Board approved amendments to the existing plan at its meeting on April 23, 2026. The amendments extend the plan to an expanded employee base and revise performance parameters to:

- Strengthen alignment with long-term shareholder value creation and investor expectations.
- Reinforce a long-term performance orientation.
- Align operating performance metrics with relevant financial metrics.

The Amended 2019 Plan is intended to:

- Encourage employees to align individual performance with the Company’s shareholder value creation objectives.
- Attract, retain, and motivate talented and identified employees.
- Reward employee performance with ownership.

The plan grants Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) to Eligible Employees. Subject to applicable law and the plan’s conditions, Eligible Employees may receive equity shares, American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), or cash upon exercise of the RSUs.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) will administer the plan. For secondary acquisition and related administrative matters, administration may also be delegated to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. Each RSU grant will be documented through an award agreement specifying the applicable terms and conditions.

The plan’s performance-linked RSUs are intended to align employee rewards with sustainable, long-term Company performance and shareholder value creation.

A copy of the Amended 2019 Plan is available at [Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/Documents/expanded-stock-ownership-program2019.pdf).

## 2. Total number of shares or benefits to be granted

The total number of equity shares and ADRs that may be provided upon exercise of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan for Eligible Employees of the Company and its subsidiaries shall not cumulatively exceed **3,26,63,903 equity shares**.

This represents the balance of unutilized equity shares from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**, or approximately **0.8% of issued capital** as of the date of the notice.

The plan authorizes the Board to grant RSUs and to create, issue, offer, and provide equity shares, ADRs, or cash to Eligible Employees of the Company or its subsidiaries.

## 3. Identification of classes of employees entitled to participate and be beneficiaries in the 2019 Plan

Employees, as defined under the SEBI Regulations, of Infosys and its subsidiary companies, whether working in India or abroad, may participate in the Amended 2019 Plan subject to the eligibility criteria specified in the SEBI Regulations or determined by the Administrator.

The following persons are not eligible to participate:

- Employees who are promoters or members of the promoter group.
- Independent directors.
- Directors who, directly or indirectly, either themselves or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than **10% of the Company’s outstanding equity shares**.

The appraisal process described in paragraph 8 will be considered when determining RSU grants.

## 4. Requirements of vesting and period of vesting

RSUs granted under the Amended 2019 Plan will vest based on three performance parameters. The NRC will determine their relative weightings, subject to the following conditions:

- Each parameter must have a weight of at least **25%**.
- No single parameter may have a weight of more than **50%**.

### i) Long-term Relative Total Shareholder Return

Long-term Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR) will be measured using rolling three-year TSR against a peer comparator group of at least **8 companies**. The Administrator will identify the comparator companies from a basket of the following 10 industry peers:

- Accenture
- DXC Technologies
- Wipro
- TCS
- Cognizant
- IBM
- Capgemini
- Tech Mahindra
- Atos
- HCL

If any peer company is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company in the comparator group.

The Company will implement a phased transition to a three-year rolling Relative TSR parameter:

- Year 1: one-year TSR
- Year 2: two-year TSR
- Year 3 onward: full three-year TSR

### ii) Annual TSR

Annual TSR will be assessed using:

**A. Relative TSR against industry peers**

Relative TSR will be measured against a peer comparator group of at least **8 companies**, selected by the Administrator from the following 10 industry peers:

- Accenture
- DXC Technologies
- Wipro
- TCS
- Cognizant
- IBM
- Capgemini
- Tech Mahindra
- Atos
- HCL

If any peer company is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company.

**B. Relative TSR against broader market indices**

Relative TSR will also be measured against broader domestic and global market indices comprising:

- MSCI
- S&P 500
- Nifty 50

The specific indices will be determined by the Administrator.

### iii) Annual operating performance metrics

The Company’s annual operating performance will be measured using metrics that are used to derive shareholder value:

- Total revenue growth
- Operating margin

The Administrator will determine the performance targets for these metrics.

Each of the three performance parameters will be distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest.

### Relative TSR vesting outcomes

Shares will vest based on the Company’s performance under the Relative TSR parameters in paragraphs (i) and (ii):

- At or above the **75th percentile**, **100%** of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- Between the **50th and 75th percentiles**, RSUs will vest proportionately on a linear scale between **60% and 100%**; the balance will lapse.
- Below the **50th percentile**, none of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.

### Operating performance vesting outcomes

Shares will vest based on the Company’s operating performance metrics as follows:

- At the minimum or threshold level, **75%** of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- Below the minimum or threshold level, none of the RSUs due for vesting will vest and the RSUs will lapse.
- At the target level, **100%** of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- At the stretch target level, **125%** of the RSUs due for vesting will vest.
- Between the threshold and target levels, RSUs will vest proportionately on a linear scale between **75% and 100%**.
- Between the target and stretch target levels, RSUs will vest proportionately on a linear scale between **100% and 125%**.

Although individual performance outcomes may result in achievement levels above 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter will not exceed **100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter**.

Any increased dilution of equity share capital or cost of secondary acquisition of equity shares is expected to be offset by growth in shareholder value and increased shareholder returns.

All RSUs will vest according to the schedule determined by the Administrator from the grant date. RSUs that lapse or are forfeited may be used for future grants.

The Company will disclose in subsequent annual reports:

- The performance parameters and their weightings.
- Target achievement for each performance parameter.
- Performance achieved during the year.
- The basis on which RSU vesting was permitted.
- The number of employees covered.
- The number of RSUs granted.
- The number of employees whose RSUs vested.
- The number of RSUs due for vesting under the vesting schedule.
- The number of RSUs vested after meeting the vesting conditions.

The Company has a Compensation Recovery Policy intended to support its pay-for-performance philosophy. The policy is available at [Infosys Compensation Recovery Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporategovernance/documents/compensation-recovery-policy.pdf).

## 5. Maximum period within which the RSUs shall vest

RSUs shall vest between a minimum of **1 year** and a maximum of **3 years** from the grant date, as specified in the award agreement. No vesting tranche may exceed **50% of the total grants** made under the plan.

When determining an Eligible Employee’s vesting schedule, the Administrator will consider factors including:

- Alignment with accelerated shareholder value creation.
- Retention of key talent.
- Future potential.
- Tenure.

RSUs will vest based on achievement of the performance criteria described in paragraph 4.
