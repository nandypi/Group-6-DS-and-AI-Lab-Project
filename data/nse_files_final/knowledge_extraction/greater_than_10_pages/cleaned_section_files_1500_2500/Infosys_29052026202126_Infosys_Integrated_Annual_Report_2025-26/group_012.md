## Client satisfaction

Infosys conducts structured Annual Client Value and Quarterly Engagement surveys to understand relationships with client organizations, identify areas for improvement, and respond to evolving client needs.

The latest annual client survey indicates that clients view Infosys as a trusted long-term partner, differentiated by account management, partnership orientation, consistent delivery, and experienced subject-matter experts. Tenured client relationships reinforce trust and business alignment. Clients increasingly recognize proactive AI, automation, and strategic technology advisory as value drivers and expect forward-looking, business-led AI advisory to support AI roadmaps, high-value use cases, and scalable business outcomes.

## Community

### Digital skilling at scale

Infosys Springboard is Infosys’ flagship digital learning platform. Its digital skilling initiatives have reached more than 15 million people, including employees, clients’ workforces, students, teachers, and communities.

### Infosys Foundation

Infosys Foundation’s programs in education, healthcare, women’s empowerment, and environmental sustainability reached more than 7 million people in the last year.

The Foundation launched the Aarohan Social Innovation Awards in 2018, which has completed four editions.

### Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program

Infosys Foundation launched the Infosys Springboard Livelihood Program in 2024. The program supports the Company’s ESG ambition to create employment opportunities for 5,00,000 people by 2030.

The program works with implementation agencies across rural belts, tribal regions, aspirational districts, urban slums, ITIs, and colleges. It has trained more than 4,10,000 individuals, and more than 2,20,000 individuals have been enabled with job offers across STEM and non-STEM roles.

### Cornea care initiatives

Infosys Foundation partnered with the LV Prasad Eye Institute for the Universal Cornea Care Mission, which covers early detection, treatment and surgery, eye banking, and long-term follow-up.

During FY2026, the initiative:

- Reached 1,74,659 beneficiaries
- Conducted 2,28,334 school screenings
- Supported the harvesting of 15,911 corneas

The initiative is being implemented across Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, and Telangana.

The Foundation also supports Project Cornea — Corneal Blindness Screening and Treatment Initiative, implemented by Vivekananda Netralaya, part of Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama, Dehradun. Launched in August 2025, the initiative covers Uttarakhand’s Garhwal region and adjoining districts of Western Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, and Himachal Pradesh. It has screened 15,270 individuals.

### Infosys Foundation USA

Infosys Foundation USA focuses on inclusive computer science education and digital upskilling. In fiscal 2026, it reached more than 1.26 million students and 16,000 educators. Since its inception in 2015, it has impacted more than 27 million students and 1.4 million educators.

### CSR in EMEA region

Infosys’ CSR charter in Europe is organized around five pillars:

- Digital Inclusion
- Transformational & Innovative Partnerships
- Education in Emergencies
- Employee Engagement
- Social Impact Thought Leadership

### CSR in APAC region

Infosys’ CSR strategy in the APAC region prioritizes education, reskilling, and digital inclusion. The Company collaborates with local partners on programs supporting underserved communities, developing future leaders, and enabling employee volunteering.

### Infosys Science Foundation

The Infosys Science Foundation awards the annual Infosys Prize to recognize science and research in India. Since 2008, the Prize has been awarded to 104 researchers. Each Prize includes a gold medal, a citation, and a purse of US$100,000.

The winners of the Infosys Prize 2025 included:

- A scientist working on a lower-carbon-footprint method of producing ammonia
- A scholar of Prakrit and Kannada translating a seminal early Kannada text
- An economist working on market design problems involving kidney exchange programs

## Suppliers

Infosys states that its sustainable supply chain practices are founded on the United Nations Global Compact principles covering human rights, labor, the environment, and anti-corruption.
