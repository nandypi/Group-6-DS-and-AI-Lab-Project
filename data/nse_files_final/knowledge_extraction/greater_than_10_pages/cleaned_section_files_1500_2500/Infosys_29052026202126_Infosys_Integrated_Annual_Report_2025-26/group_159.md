Item no. 5 - Approval of the proposed amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 ('the Amended 2019 Plan') and grant of stock incentives to the eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries under the Amended 2019 Plan

To consider and, if thought fit, to pass the following resolution as a Special Resolution:

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021 ('SEBI Regulations'), as amended from time to time, and other applicable rules, regulations, circulars, and guidelines, and subject to the required approvals, permissions, sanctions, conditions, and modifications, the shareholders approve the proposed amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019. The amended program is referred to as the 'Amended 2019 Plan', with details provided in the explanatory statement to the Notice.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board, including authorized officials of the Company, is authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives under the Amended 2019 Plan and grant incentives to:

- Persons permanently employed by the Company's subsidiaries, whether working in India or outside India.
- Directors of the Company's subsidiaries.
- Other persons who may become eligible under applicable laws.

Eligible persons are collectively referred to as 'Eligible Employees'. The following persons are excluded:

- Promoters and persons belonging to the promoter group.
- Independent directors.
- Directors who, either themselves or through a relative or any body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company's outstanding equity shares.

The Board may determine the price, number of tranches, terms, and conditions of the incentives in accordance with the Amended 2019 Plan.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the shareholders approve the following amendments and modifications to the existing plan:

- **A)** Amend Section 13 to extend the period during which awards may be granted to Eligible Employees by seven years from the date of shareholder approval. Consequently, the Amended 2019 Plan will remain in effect for a further period of 10 years from the date of shareholder approval.
- **B)** Amend Section 6(c) relating to vesting based on performance parameters.
- **C)** Amend Section 3 to reflect the unutilized share pool available for grant.
- **D)** Make editorial and clarificatory changes and align the plan with the amended SEBI Regulations.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the maximum number of stock incentives granted to Eligible Employees of the Company and its subsidiaries, in aggregate, under the Amended 2019 Plan will be **3,26,63,903 equity shares**. This represents the unutilized balance from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**.

The exercise price and vesting criteria will be decided by the Board and/or the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, referred to as the 'Administrator'. For matters relating to the secondary acquisition of the Company's equity shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust and related administration, authority may also be delegated to the Trust from time to time in accordance with the Amended 2019 Plan.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** in the event of corporate actions, including rights issues, bonus issues, mergers, restructuring, sale of a division, or similar events, the Board and/or the Administrator may take actions permitted under applicable laws to ensure that fair and equitable benefits under the Amended 2019 Plan are passed on to Eligible Employees.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board is authorized to allot the Company's equity shares required for the Amended 2019 Plan.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board and/or the Administrator is authorized to take the necessary steps for listing shares allotted under the Amended 2019 Plan on the stock exchanges, in accordance with applicable laws and the listing requirements of the concerned stock exchanges.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** for implementing the Amended 2019 Plan and giving effect to these resolutions, the Board and/or the Administrator is authorized, on behalf of the Company, to take all necessary or desirable actions and resolve any issues, questions, difficulties, or doubts that may arise.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust and its trustees are authorized to take steps required in relation to the Amended 2019 Plan and its implementation.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board and/or the Administrator may delegate any or all powers conferred under these resolutions to a committee of directors, with authority for further delegation to Company executives or officers and for executing documents required for this purpose.
