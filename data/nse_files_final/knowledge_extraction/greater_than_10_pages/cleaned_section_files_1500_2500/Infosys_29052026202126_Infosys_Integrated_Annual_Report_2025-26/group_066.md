## 5. Describe the internal mechanisms in place to redress grievances related to human rights issues.

Infosys provides employees and contract staff with access to a grievance-resolution mechanism known as Resolution hubs. Employees and contract staff can raise workplace concerns, including human-rights issues.

Resolution hubs follow principles of natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Concerns are investigated through a process that gives all involved parties an opportunity to present facts and material evidence, with timely action and closure.

## 8. Mechanism to prevent adverse consequences to the complainant in discrimination and harassment cases

Infosys's non-retaliation policy protects individuals who make good-faith reports of integrity concerns. Retaliation by a manager, co-worker, or other person is not tolerated. Protection also extends to anyone who assists with, cooperates in, or reports an integrity investigation or concern.
