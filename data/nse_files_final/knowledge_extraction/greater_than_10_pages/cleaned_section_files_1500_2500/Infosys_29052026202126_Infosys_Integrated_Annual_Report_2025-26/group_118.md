The final dividend proposed in the previous year and declared and paid by the Company during the year was in accordance with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

The interim dividend declared and paid by the Company during the year and up to the date of this report complied with Section 123 of the Act.

The Board of Directors proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by the members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed dividend amount was in accordance with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

Except for the instances mentioned below, based on examination including test checks, the Company and its subsidiaries incorporated in India used accounting software systems for maintaining their respective books of account for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. These systems had an audit-trail (edit-log) facility, which operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions recorded in the systems. The auditors did not identify any instance of tampering with the audit-trail feature. The Parent Company and the above-mentioned Indian subsidiaries preserved the audit trail in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

The financial statements of five subsidiaries that were not material to the Group’s Consolidated Financial Statements had not been audited under the provisions of the Act as of the date of the report. Accordingly, the auditors were unable to comment on the reporting requirement under Rule 11(g) of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014, in respect of those five subsidiaries.

With respect to paragraphs 3(xxi) and 4 of the Companies (Auditor’s Report) Order, 2020 (the “Order” or “CARO”), based on the information and explanations provided and the auditors’ reports on the financial statements of the Company and its subsidiaries for the year ended March 31, 2026, the auditors reported that no qualifications or adverse remarks had been made for companies whose audits under Section 143 of the Act had been completed.

For the following subsidiaries included in the consolidated financial statements, whose audits under Section 143 of the Act had not yet been completed, the applicable CARO reports were not available:
