## Manufactured Capital

Infosys’ Manufactured Capital includes energy-efficient offices, data centers, innovation hubs, digital studios, and technology infrastructure across the globe. The company reports investments in green buildings and collaborative technology infrastructure to support productive, safe, and healthy workplaces for employees, clients, and contractors.

## Intellectual Capital

Infosys’ Intellectual Capital is based on agility, flexibility, and innovation. The company works with experts, partners, academia, and other stakeholders to develop products and services for customers and communities.

Its intellectual-capital initiatives include iCETS, Living Labs, the Infosys Innovation Network (IIN), and the Infosys Business Incubator. The Infosys Prize and Aarohan Social Innovation Awards provide platforms for innovators and social entrepreneurs.

## Social and Relationship Capital

Infosys’ Social and Relationship Capital focuses on stakeholder interests, client transformation, social innovation, and employability through community skill training.

Global CSR efforts address education, healthcare, women’s empowerment, science and research, environmental sustainability, and related areas.

## About Infosys Corporate overview

Infosys is a global provider of AI-first business consulting and technology services. More than 3,25,000 employees support businesses in 59 countries.

The company has more than four decades of experience managing the systems and operations of global enterprises. Its capabilities include an AI-first value framework, domain expertise, and orchestration of innovations from its AI-native partner ecosystem.

Established in 1981 with capital of US$250, Infosys reported a market capitalization of approximately US$54.8 billion. The company pioneered the Global Delivery Model and became the first Indian IT company to be listed on NASDAQ.

Since refreshing its ESG Vision 2030 in 2025, Infosys reported progress in climate-positive goals, skilling and community initiatives, workforce inclusion, and governance across its value chain.

## Our Purpose

To amplify human potential and create the next opportunity for people, businesses and communities.

## Our Values

The Company’s Code of Conduct is based on the C-LIFE values:

- **Client value:** To surpass client expectations consistently.
- **Leadership by example:** To set standards in business and transactions and be an exemplar for the industry and the Company.
- **Integrity and transparency:** To be ethical, sincere, and open in all transactions.
- **Fairness:** To be objective and transaction-oriented, thereby earning trust and respect.
- **Excellence:** To strive relentlessly, constantly improve, and develop the Company, teams, services, and products.
