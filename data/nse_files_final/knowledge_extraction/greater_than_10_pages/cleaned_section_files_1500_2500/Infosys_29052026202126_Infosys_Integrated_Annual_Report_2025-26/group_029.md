## Annexure 7 - Conservation of energy, research and development, technology absorption, foreign exchange earnings and outgo.

*[Particulars pursuant to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014]*

Infosys maintained carbon-neutral operations for the seventh consecutive year since fiscal 2020. The Company is pursuing climate-positive objectives, including restoring more natural capital than it consumes and generating environmental and social benefits for ecosystems and communities.

## Resource conservation initiatives

Infosys’ resource conservation initiatives include energy-efficient buildings, retrofits, automation, operational optimization, water management, waste treatment, renewable energy adoption, and carbon offset projects.

### Energy

In fiscal 2026, Infosys spent approximately ₹1.27 crore on infrastructure retrofits to improve operational efficiency. Its energy-conservation initiatives included energy-efficient building design, embodied carbon analysis, continuous operational optimization, and employee engagement.

The ASSURE initiative, launched with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements, aims to accelerate decarbonization of India’s building sector through lighthouse projects, capacity-building, policy advocacy, and ecosystem collaboration. During fiscal 2026, more than 15 million square feet of built-up area was onboarded as lighthouse projects.

### Renewable energy

Infosys has total installed solar capacity of 62.4 MWp across its campuses, including a 42.5 MWp captive solar plant in Karnataka. The Company also uses power purchase agreements and green tariffs from DISCOMs. In fiscal 2026, renewable sources supplied 81.8% of electricity used in Infosys’ India operations.

### Green buildings

All new developments are designed to achieve the highest level of green-building certification. Approximately 84% of Infosys’ office space is currently certified to the highest level of green-building standards.

### Smarter spaces

Infosys’ next-generation offices combine digital connectivity, ergonomic design, and intelligent energy systems. Advanced Building Management Systems optimize lighting, ventilation, and thermal comfort while supporting hybrid work, employee well-being, inclusivity, and operational efficiency.

### Water management

Infosys maintains zero wastewater discharge and is a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate. The Company is committed to recharging more water than it consumes by 2030. Its water-management practices include reducing freshwater demand, treating and reusing wastewater, and expanding rainwater harvesting across campuses.

Infosys also undertakes lake-rejuvenation initiatives intended to support groundwater recharge, biodiversity, and long-term water security.

### Waste management

Infosys is pursuing zero waste to landfill through waste reduction, segregation, treatment, recovery, and circular-economy practices. The Company achieved 99% waste diversion from landfill. During fiscal 2026, 11 campuses achieved True Zero Waste Certification from the Green Business Certification Institute.

### Carbon offset

Infosys addresses unavoidable emissions through community-based carbon offset projects. Clean-cooking projects implemented across five Indian states support health and livelihoods and help generate income for rural women.

The Company has also initiated large-scale carbon-sequestration projects through agroforestry. These projects support small and marginal farmers while contributing to soil health, food security, and climate resilience.

### Carbon neutral events

Infosys measures, reduces where feasible, and offsets emissions associated with Company-hosted events through certified carbon offset projects. In fiscal 2026, the Company organized eight carbon-neutral events.

## Health, Safety and Environment

Infosys’ Health, Safety, and Environmental Management System (HSEMS) applies to employees, contractors, and visitors. The Company is certified to ISO 14001:2015 for Environmental Management and ISO 45001:2018 for Occupational Health and Safety. The HSEMS framework incorporates compliance with applicable laws across the regions where Infosys operates.

## Technology absorption

## AI-first @ Infosys

### Internal IT - AI-first software engineering

Infosys’ internal IT organization supports and manages 200 enterprise applications. Its AI-led transformation strategy has two primary areas:

1. **AI for Engineering Excellence and Lifecycle Compression:** Infosys has created a digital twin of its enterprise landscape that captures contextual knowledge across applications, platforms, and layers. AI-powered agents support requirements, design, development, testing, deployment, and support, with the objectives of improving developer productivity, quality, decision-making, and software-delivery speed.

2. **AI for Business & Operations:** AI agents and generative AI solutions are used to improve operational efficiency in functions such as Finance and HR. These solutions respond to user queries across applications, provide contextual assistance, autonomously respond to helpdesk emails, improve first-contact resolution, and reduce service-desk workload.

### Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys has rolled out the Topaz Fabric platform across the organization to support experimentation, innovation, and the development and distribution of generative AI and agentic solutions.

### Education, Training & Assessment (ETA)

ETA has developed an agentic AI-powered learning ecosystem centered on Lex, Infosys’ internal learning platform, and extended through Infosys Springboard, its free public learning platform. Solutions including Zoiee, Knowledge Assistant, and SynthAIz support personalized learning, intelligent recommendations, real-time assistance, and continuous guidance.

SynthAIz and AI agents also support the creation of multimodal learning content through automated transcription, translation, and video localization.

### AI cloud

Infosys is investing in proprietary, on-premise GPU infrastructure for advanced model training and inferencing. This infrastructure supports the integration of open-source large language models and deployment of specialized small language models while maintaining data sovereignty, data confidentiality, and control over operational costs.
