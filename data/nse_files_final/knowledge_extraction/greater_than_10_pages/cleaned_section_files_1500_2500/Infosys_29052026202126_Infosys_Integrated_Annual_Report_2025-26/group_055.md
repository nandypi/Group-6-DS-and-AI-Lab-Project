## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report

Infosys reports sustainability and responsible business practices as part of its business strategy and governance. The company adopted the GRI Framework in 2008 and began publishing a Business Responsibility Report in fiscal 2013.

The Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) follows the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC) principles and addresses the ESG-related requirements under Regulation 34(2)(f) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

The disclosures are made on a consolidated basis unless otherwise specified. BRSR core indicators received reasonable assurance, and selected BRSR indicators received reasonable or limited assurance from Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP.
