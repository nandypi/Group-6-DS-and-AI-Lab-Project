## 3. Branding

Brand Infosys is a key intangible asset for the Company. It was ranked among the world's Top 100 brands in FY26 and recognized as the fastest-growing IT services brand globally over a six-year period. Infosys positions itself as an enterprise AI partner, supported by capabilities in enterprise AI, cloud, digital technologies, continuous learning, and the management of global enterprise systems.

Infosys’ global marketing reach includes digital-first multichannel campaigns and partnerships with the Australian Open, Roland-Garros, ATP, the International Tennis Hall of Fame, Madison Square Garden properties, the New York Knicks, the New York Rangers, MSG Arena, and the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship. Strategic partnerships also include Dow Jones, The Economist Group, Financial Times, and Handelsblatt Media Group. Infosys organizes the Confluence client event series across the US, Europe, and APAC, as well as business and industry events and CXO roundtables.

## 5.  Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI)

The Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI) focuses on succession planning, leadership bench strength, development of title holders, and inclusivity in leadership.

ILI delivered the AI for Business Growth program in partnership with Kellogg. More than 1,500 senior leaders across Infosys completed the program over the last two years. A separate AI immersion program provided hands-on experience with AI tools, including Infosys Topaz Fabric. Executive leadership programs were also delivered in partnership with Harvard Business School and MIT through weeklong residential cohorts at the Mysuru campus.

ILI strengthened enterprise-wide succession planning through a structured, data-led approach, regular reviews, leadership profiles, personalized development plans, executive education, and external coaching. The Constellation program included a 10-month program with Oxford Saïd Business School. Twelve Constellation projects were executed during the year.

Development programs for title holders included:

- The 10-month Enterprise Leadership Program for AVPs
- The Executive Communication program for VPs, in partnership with The Economist
- The InFusion program for new title holders

Infosys leaders collectively invested more than 10,000 leadership learning days annually during the last two years. ILI recorded a Net Promoter Score of more than 96% for the third consecutive year and was recognized as the top unit on Internal Partner Satisfaction for three consecutive years.

The #IamtheFuture program covered more than 700 leaders through multi-year, role-specific development journeys focused on leadership readiness, inclusion, and leadership-pipeline development. The program was redesigned during the year to differentiate development experiences across career stages, with emphasis on application, simulations, and contextual learning.

## 6.  Infosys Knowledge Institute

The Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI) is the business research and thought leadership arm of Infosys. It uses the intellectual capital of Infosys experts and client collaborations to produce content and insights on the business impact of technology. IKI also conducts primary research, including large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis.

Its publications include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals. Since inception, IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets.

## 7. ESG vision and ambitions

Infosys launched its ESG Vision 2030 in October 2020 to shape and share solutions serving the development of businesses and communities. In fiscal 2025, the Company launched the ESG Vision 2030 Refresh to address changing stakeholder priorities.

Infosys reported being carbon neutral across its global operations for the seventh consecutive year. Its ESG Vision 2030 Refresh includes the ambition to become climate positive by 2030 through initiatives across Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions; reduce its carbon footprint; sequester more carbon than it emits; engage clients on climate action; maintain 100% recycling of wastewater; sequester more water than it consumes year-on-year; and achieve zero waste to landfill.

Additional ambitions include:

- Empowering more than 18 million people through digital skilling programs
- Enabling employment opportunities for more than 500,000 people
- Supporting sustainable communities through technology-for-good initiatives and CSR efforts
- Continuing employee volunteering, healthcare, and women’s empowerment initiatives
- Maintaining a diverse and inclusive board
- Advancing data privacy standards and information security practices

## Corporate governance report

Corporate governance guides how a company is directed and how it relates to stakeholders. Infosys states that its governance framework is intended to support trust, transparency, accountability, long-term capital, economic growth, and financial stability.

## Our corporate governance philosophy

Infosys’ corporate governance framework is based on integrity, transparency, fairness, ethical business decisions, and stakeholder accountability. The Company states that its governance is guided by the C-LIFE values: Client value, Leadership by example, Integrity and transparency, Fairness, and Excellence. It is also guided by the principles of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD).

The governance framework is reinforced through the Company’s Code of Conduct and Ethics, corporate governance guidelines, committee charters, Board and Management processes, audits, and internal control systems. The report provides an overview of how Infosys’ governance adheres to its seven-pillar governance framework.
