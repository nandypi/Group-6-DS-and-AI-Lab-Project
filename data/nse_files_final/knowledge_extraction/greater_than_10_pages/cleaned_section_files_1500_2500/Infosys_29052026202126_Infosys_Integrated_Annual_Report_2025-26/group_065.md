## Leadership indicators

1. Provide the processes for consultation between stakeholders and the Board on economic, environmental, and social topics or, if consultation is delegated, how feedback from such consultations is provided to the Board.

Infosys identified its most material issues through a data-driven and consultative exercise. Material topics were shortlisted and prioritized based on their impact on stakeholders and the business. Periodic meetings were held to update the sub-committees of the Board.

2. Whether stakeholder consultation is used to support the identification and management of environmental and social topics (Yes / No). If so, provide details of instances as to how the inputs received from stakeholders on these topics were incorporated into policies and activities of the entity.

Yes. Infosys shortlisted and prioritized material topics based on their impact on stakeholders and the business. The material topics and linked ambitions are covered in the ESG Vision 2030 and annual ESG performance reports.

3. Provide details of instances of engagement with, and actions taken to, address the concerns of vulnerable / marginalized stakeholder groups.

Infosys Foundation supports underprivileged sections of society, creates opportunities, and works toward a more equitable society. Infosys contributes to the Foundation as part of its CSR initiatives. The Foundation engages with communities, particularly vulnerable and marginalized stakeholder groups, across a variety of focus areas. The percentage of beneficiaries of CSR projects is reported under Principle 8, Q.6 (Leadership Indicators).

## 2. Details of minimum wages paid to employees and workers

Infosys operates in 59 countries, and employees are hired across geographies. Legal minimum wages vary by country and, in some countries, by state. They are determined using parameters such as tenure, role, location, and citizenship status. Infosys has defined processes to ensure that employees are paid according to applicable local regulations and that it complies with local laws.
