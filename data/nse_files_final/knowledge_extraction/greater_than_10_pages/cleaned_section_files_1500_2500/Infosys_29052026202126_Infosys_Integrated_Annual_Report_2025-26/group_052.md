## Salient features of our Enterprise Risk Management program

Infosys’ enterprise risk management program uses methods to identify risks, evaluate potential impact, and promote risk awareness across the organization.

## Highlights of fiscal 2026

During fiscal 2026, Infosys undertook risk management initiatives covering service delivery, stakeholder communication, social responsibility, employee safety and health, and risk culture.

The Risk Office assessed, monitored, and reported on risks related to geopolitical scenarios; economic uncertainty; inflation; artificial intelligence, technology disruption, and innovation; talent availability; cybersecurity; data protection and privacy; ESG; contractual liabilities; and the complex and evolving regulatory environment.

The key and emerging risks described below include the Company’s approach to mitigation and related opportunities.

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Geopolitical, macroeconomic or health events | Revenues are concentrated in a few geographies and client industries, and a large part of revenue depends on a limited number of clients. Unfavorable geopolitical, economic, or health events may cause currency volatility and reduced technology spending, adversely affecting demand, growth, and profitability. Government policy changes, including tariffs and trade restrictions, are dynamic and difficult to predict. Impacted capitals: Financial, Social & Relationship, and Human. | Broad-based growth to reduce concentration in any single region, client, or industry; monitoring of geopolitical and macroeconomic scenarios; additional controls to improve operational agility; currency hedging; business continuity and resilience measures in affected regions; hybrid working; diversified supply chains; acquisitions to expand into new areas or regions. Opportunity: clients seeking cost-takeout projects and vendor consolidation. |

## Risk management report

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Artificial Intelligence (AI) risks | Failure to transform existing services using AI or scale new AI-first offerings could affect win rates, market share, revenue, and profits. Impacted capitals: Financial and Intellectual. | Differentiate services through innovation and industry solutions using AI; continue investments in AI-first offerings; identify and mitigate operational issues; implement an AI talent transformation strategy; build deep AI engineering expertise. |
| Global Capability Centers (GCCs) risks | Customers may insource or establish or expand GCCs for services provided by Infosys, potentially reducing addressable market share, growth, and profitability. Impacted capitals: Financial and Intellectual. | Collaborate and co-create to address GCC requirements; scale a GCC practice supporting assisted GCC builds, build-operate-transfer, captive carveouts, and other engagement models; use an AI-first model to transform GCCs into innovation hubs. |
| Technology disruption and innovation | Failure to identify, invest in, incubate, and operationalize services aligned with AI, client preferences, and market expectations could disrupt Infosys’ value proposition and reduce customer relevance, revenue, and profitability. The speed and nature of technological change make trends difficult to predict. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, and Intellectual. | Executive-led innovation council; innovation showcases with clients, industry forums, alliances, partners, and academia; research and development in consulting and industry-domain solutions; employee reskilling; large-deal investments to drive innovation and productivity; alliances or partnerships with emerging technology firms. Opportunity: identify, develop, and deploy offerings using next-generation technologies. |
| Talent supply constraints | As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 3,28,594 employees globally. The Company depends on skilled technology, AI, and management talent. Inability to attract, hire, engage, motivate, reskill, and retain talent; manage leadership succession; reshape the AI-driven talent model; protect human rights; or adapt the hybrid work model could affect reputation, project staffing, execution of large and complex programs, and cost structures. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, and Manufactured. | Improve recruitment and onboarding cycle times; employee engagement and support initiatives; retention and recognition programs; career and leadership development; a hybrid operating model balancing client requirements, employee preferences, legal requirements, and information security risks; redesigned career architecture; a future-ready workforce supported by global learning infrastructure. |
| Cybersecurity | Cyber threats evolve rapidly. Failure to adapt threat detection and prevention measures, or attacks that breach information networks, could affect operations and client satisfaction. Failure to protect sensitive and confidential stakeholder information in accordance with applicable laws and contractual obligations could result in significant regulatory penalties. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, and Manufactured. | Robust cybersecurity strategy, framework, processes, policies, and controls; executive and Board oversight; continued investment in cybersecurity technologies; collaboration with cyber intelligence and forensic consultants; periodic tabletop exercises; an up-to-date cybersecurity crisis plan; cybersecurity awareness training and consequence management; security-by-design training; strong encryption and data backup and recovery; Cyber Risk Assessment Framework aligned with ISO 31000, ISO 27001, and ISO 27005. Opportunity: cybersecurity services for customers. |
| Data protection and privacy | Failure to protect personal and sensitive stakeholder information in accordance with applicable laws could affect operations or result in significant regulatory penalties. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, and Intellectual. | Robust data privacy framework, policies, processes, and controls; employee training on privacy by design and data protection and privacy awareness, including geographical nuances; executive and Board oversight; continuous monitoring of the regulatory landscape. |
| Cost inflation / Inability to improve margin | Failure to operate effectively with sustainable cost levers could adversely affect long-term profitability. Impacted capital: Financial. | Effective operations with sustainable cost optimization and margin improvement levers; automation and a planned capital expenditure program focused on technology adoption and efficiency. |
| Environment, Social, Governance (ESG) ambition | Reputation, access to capital, and longer-term financial stability could be at risk if Infosys does not meet its ESG 2030 Vision goals. Climate change risks are increasingly manifesting as strategic, physical, transitional, market, and compliance risks; inadequate management could affect reputation and profitability. ESG expectations may continue to change because of stakeholder expectations and regulatory requirements. | ESG 2030 ambitions and execution roadmap with dedicated owners; Board-level governance and oversight through the dedicated ESG Committee of the Board; periodic risk assessments; alignment of ESG ambitions with strategy; regulatory monitoring. Opportunity: climate change-related solutions and services for customers. For more details, see [Infosys ESG Vision 2030](https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/esg-vision-2030.html). |
