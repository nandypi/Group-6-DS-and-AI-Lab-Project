## 2.26 Segment reporting

Ind AS 108, *Operating Segments*, establishes requirements for reporting operating segments and related disclosures on products and services, geographic areas, and major customers. The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates performance and allocates resources based on business-segment performance indicators.

The Group primarily provides end-to-end business solutions to help clients enhance business performance. Accounting principles used to record segment revenue and expenditure are consistent with the Group's accounting policies.

The Group's business segments are:

- Financial Services
- Manufacturing
- Retail
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences
- All other segments

Financial Services combines the Financial Services operating segment and the Finacle operating segment because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments include operating segments in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Segment revenue and identifiable operating expenses are assigned based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Allocated expenses include costs incurred at offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated segment effort.

Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocable because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are disclosed separately as unallocated expenses and adjusted against the Group's total income.

Assets and liabilities are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Management considers meaningful segregation of available asset and liability data impracticable.

Business-segment revenue is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized. Revenue by geographic location is disclosed in Note 2.18, *Revenue from operations*.
