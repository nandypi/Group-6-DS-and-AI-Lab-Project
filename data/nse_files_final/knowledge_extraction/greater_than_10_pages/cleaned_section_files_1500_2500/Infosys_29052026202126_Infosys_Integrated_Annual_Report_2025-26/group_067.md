## 2. Details of the scope and coverage of any human rights due diligence conducted.

Infosys conducts human rights due diligence across its organization and supply chain, considering local laws and international standards. The process assesses potential and actual human rights impacts and includes periodic internal audits covering labor rights, workplace safety, and non-discrimination.

Findings are integrated into company policies and practices. Infosys also gathers feedback from employees through the Pulse survey and from suppliers through the VenSat survey. Training and awareness programs are provided to employees and partners, and identified issues are addressed through established grievance mechanisms.

The premises and offices of the entity are accessible to differently-abled visitors in accordance with the requirements of India’s Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
