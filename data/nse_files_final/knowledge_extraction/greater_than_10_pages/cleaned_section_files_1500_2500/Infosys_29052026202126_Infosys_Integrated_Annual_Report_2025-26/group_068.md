## 5. Has the entity implemented a mechanism for Zero Liquid Discharge? If yes, provide details of its coverage and implementation.

Infosys has implemented in-house sewage treatment plants (STPs) at its India-owned campuses to facilitate Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) practices. One hundred percent of its owned campuses in India have STPs. Sewage is treated, and the resulting recycled water is used for irrigation, HVAC systems and flushing.

STPs are available at all owned campuses and all but 2 leased locations in India. At the remaining locations, wastewater is discharged into municipal sewers for further treatment. Infosys also procures greywater, or recycled water, from third parties for landscaping, flushing and other non-potable purposes.

## 8. Does the entity have any project related to reducing greenhouse gas emission? If yes, provide details.

Infosys has been carbon neutral since fiscal 2020 across Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions. Its approach includes energy efficiency and renewable energy.

Energy-efficiency measures include new buildings with an Energy Performance Index (EPI) of less than 65 kWh/m²-year, real-time monitoring and optimization through smart building systems, and retrofits in existing buildings. In fiscal 2026, retrofit projects for lighting and air-conditioning systems saved 2.04 lakh kWh and reduced emissions by 145 tCO₂e.

Infosys had installed 62.42 MWp of solar PV capacity and avoided 1.24 lakh kWh in fiscal 2026. Through captive solar capacity, power purchase agreements and the green tariff mechanism, 81.8% of electricity consumption in its India operations came from renewable sources.
