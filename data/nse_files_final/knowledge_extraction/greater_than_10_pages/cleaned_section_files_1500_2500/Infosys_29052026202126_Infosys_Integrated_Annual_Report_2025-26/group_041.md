## Key recommendations of the Committee

Based on discussions with Management and the auditors, and its review of Management representations and the auditors' report, the Committee recommended that the Board:

- Accept the Company's quarterly condensed financial statements prepared under Ind AS, as specified under the Companies Act, 2013 and relevant rules, and IFRS issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.
- Accept the audited financial statements of Infosys Limited prepared under Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared under Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in Indian rupees and U.S. dollars under IFRS for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in U.S. dollars under IFRS for the year ended March 31, 2026, and include them in the Company's Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the SEC.
- Appoint Ernst & Young LLP as the Company's internal auditors for the year ending March 31, 2027, to review various Company operations.
- Issue a letter in line with Recommendation No. 9 of the Blue Ribbon Committee on Audit Committee effectiveness for inclusion in the IFRS financial statements in the Annual Report on Form 20-F.
- In line with mandatory auditor rotation under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, recommend BSR & Co. LLP as statutory auditors commencing with the audit of the Company's financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2028, including the quarterly financial statements for that year. This intent was proposed by the Audit Committee on April 23, 2026.
- Approve the intent to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as the independent registered public accounting firm for U.S. SEC filings effective fiscal 2027–28, subject to applicable requirements.

The Committee stated that the Company's financial statements were fairly presented in conformity with Ind AS and IFRS and that there were no significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting. The Committee also stated that it had complied with its responsibilities under the Charter. The Board accepted all recommendations made by the Audit Committee.

## Objectives of the Committee

The main objectives and responsibilities of the Committee are to:

1. Assist the Board with responsibilities relating to remuneration of the Company's directors, Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), and senior management.
2. Evaluate and approve the adequacy of remuneration plans, policies, programmes, and succession plans for the Company's directors, KMP, and senior management; identify individuals for appointment under prescribed criteria; and recommend their appointment and removal to the Board.
3. Formulate criteria for determining directors' qualifications, positive attributes, independence, and performance evaluation.
4. Administer equity-based plans and schemes approved by shareholders.
5. Oversee the Company's nomination process for KMP and senior management and identify, screen, and review individuals qualified to serve as directors, KMP, and senior management under Board-approved criteria.
6. Recommend the appointment and removal of directors for approval at the annual meeting of shareholders.
7. Evaluate the performance of the Board and review implementation and compliance with the evaluation.
8. Oversee leadership development.
9. Develop and maintain corporate governance policies applicable to the Company.
10. Devise a policy on Board diversity.
