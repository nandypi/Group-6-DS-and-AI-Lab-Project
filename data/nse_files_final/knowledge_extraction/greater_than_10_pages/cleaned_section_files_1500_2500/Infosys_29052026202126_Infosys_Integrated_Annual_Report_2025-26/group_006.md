## Letter to the Shareholder Performance overview

## Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

## Dear shareholder,

Last year marked an inflection point as AI began evolving from experimentation to enterprise-scale adoption. Infosys shifted its portfolio, invested in talent, and scaled partnerships to help clients adopt AI at speed and scale. AI-led programs were deployed across 90% of Infosys' top 200 clients.

Infosys' AI strategy is built on six pillars of its AI-First value framework:

- Strategy and engineering
- Data
- Process
- Legacy modernization
- Physical AI
- Trust

These capabilities address a US$300 billion opportunity, based on market estimates. They support growth programs, legacy-system transformation, data and process readiness, and responsible AI deployment.

For Q3, Infosys reported that revenue from these six areas represented 5.5% of total revenue and was growing significantly faster than the company. Infosys has partnerships with AI-native companies and a large base of employees trained on their foundation models. The company is continuously reskilling its over 3,25,000 employees on AI.

Infosys is advising clients on AI-powered transformation programs, including:

- Coding and code modernization
- Building agents across business functions

Infosys Topaz is the company's AI suite. Its Topaz Fabric provides a composable stack of services and agents that enables enterprises to use different foundation models in a token-efficient manner, with the stated aim of maximizing value while minimizing cost and risk.

Cloud and data expertise remain key foundations for AI-powered transformation. Infosys' investments in Cobalt and its Data and Analytics portfolio support its participation in this market.

Infosys' financial and operating performance included:

- Revenue of over US$20 billion
- Adjusted operating margin of 21%
- Free cash flow of US$3.7 billion
- Over US$4 billion returned to shareholders through dividends of US$2.1 billion and share buybacks of US$2 billion
- Total dividend announced of ₹48 per share
- Over 20,000 college graduates recruited

Infosys had large deals worth US$14.9 billion during the year. The company also launched a focused growth program targeting large global companies where it has a smaller share of client spend.

The Infosys Foundation impacted over seven million people in India through its focus on skilling and livelihoods, healthcare, and sustainability. Springboard provided free digital learning programs to 10 million people globally.
