As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, 3,87,949 and 2,88,384 cash-settled options were outstanding, respectively. The carrying value of the liability toward cash-settled share-based payments was ` 5 crore and ` 8 crore as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively.

The fair value of awards was estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model was used for TSR-based options.

The model inputs included the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term was based on historical volatility of the observed market prices of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies was modelled using historical movements in their publicly traded equity share prices over an equivalent period. Correlation coefficients were calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
