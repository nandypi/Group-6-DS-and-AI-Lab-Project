## Item 6: Approval of the request for re-classification of certain members of the 'Promoter and Promoter Group' of Company to 'Public' category

The members are requested to approve the re-classification of the following Applicants from the 'Promoter and Promoter Group' category to the 'Public' category under Regulation 31A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015:

| Sl. No. | Name of the entity | Category |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Shreyas Shibulal | Promoter |
| 2 | Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal | Promoter Group |

The re-classification is subject to the approvals required under applicable regulations. The Board approved the request at its meeting held on April 30, 2026. No-objection letters were received from BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited on May 11, 2026.

The Company's directors and Company Secretary are authorized to complete the required filings, applications, representations, and other actions with the Stock Exchanges and relevant authorities.

## Notes

The Board, at its meeting held on April 23, 2026, recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026. Subject to approval by the members at the AGM, the final dividend will be paid on June 25, 2026.

Dividend paid or distributed by the Company is taxable in the hands of members under the Income-tax Act, 2025. The Company will deduct tax at source when the final dividend is paid.

For resident shareholders, tax will be deducted at source under Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025:

| Shareholder status | TDS rate |
|---|---:|
| Members having a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) | 10% or the rate notified by the Government of India |
| Members not having a PAN or having an invalid PAN | 20% or the rate notified by the Government of India |

Under Section 262 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, PAN holders who are eligible to obtain Aadhaar must link PAN with Aadhaar. If PAN is not linked with Aadhaar, it may be deemed invalid or inoperative, and tax may be deducted at the higher rate under Section 397, currently 20%.

No tax will be deducted on dividends payable to resident individual shareholders if their total dividend received during tax year 2026-27 does not exceed ₹10,000, or where they provide Form 121, subject to the conditions specified in the Income-tax Act, 2025. Resident shareholders may submit other prescribed documents to claim lower or nil withholding. PAN is mandatory when submitting Form 121 or another document for this purpose.

For non-resident shareholders, tax will be withheld under Section 393 and other applicable provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025, at the rates in force. The withholding rate is 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or the rate notified by the Government of India.

Under Section 159 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, non-resident shareholders may claim benefits under the Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), read with the Multilateral Instrument (MLI), between India and their country of tax residence where those provisions are more beneficial. To claim these benefits, they must provide:

- A duly attested copy of the PAN card issued by the Indian Income Tax authorities or, if PAN is unavailable, the information required under sub-rule 2 of Rule 217 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026.
- A duly attested Tax Residency Certificate for tax year 2026-27 issued by the tax authorities of the country of tax residence.
- Form 41 for claiming tax treaty benefits.
- A self-declaration confirming that the shareholder has no permanent establishment in India, in accordance with the applicable tax treaty and the Income-tax Act, 2025.
- A self-declaration confirming beneficial ownership of the equity shares.
- A self-declaration confirming fulfilment of all conditions required to claim benefits under the applicable tax treaty read with the MLI.
- Any other documents prescribed under the Income-tax Act, 2025, if applicable, or a duly attested certificate for lower withholding of tax.
