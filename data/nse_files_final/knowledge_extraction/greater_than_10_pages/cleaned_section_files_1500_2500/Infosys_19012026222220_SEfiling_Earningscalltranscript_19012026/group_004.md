## Salil Parekh

Infosys will remain flexible in its approach to work-from-home arrangements and in how employees interact with the company and its clients.

A change in the U.S. credit-card interest-rate limit would be addressed by the U.S. banking system. Infosys will continue supporting large bank clients with regulatory changes where required. There will be no change in the company's appraisal approach.

The impact of India's labor-code changes through December 2025 has already been recognized in the financial statements. This was a one-time impact relating to employees' years of service. The changed wage code is expected to create an ongoing impact of approximately 15 basis points annually.

# Infosys Limited Q3 FY'26 Earnings Conference Call

January 14, 2026

## Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Chief Financial Officer

## Sandeep Mahindroo

Financial Controller and Head - Investor Relations

## Salil Parekh

Infosys reported the following Q3 FY'26 results:

- Revenue grew 0.6% sequentially and 1.7% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
- Large deals totaled $4.8 billion across 26 deals, with 57% classified as net new.
- Adjusted operating margin was 21.2%.
- Free cash flow was $915 million.

One significant large deal was a $1.6 billion contract with the National Health Service in the U.K. Infosys will support NHS operations and patient care through AI capabilities.

Infosys expanded its Topaz AI capabilities with Topaz Fabric, an agent-services suite designed to help clients manage and implement AI agents across the enterprise.

Infosys also announced a partnership with Cognition to combine Cognition's Devin software agent with Infosys's knowledge of client environments and industry expertise. The companies were already working together across clients.

In financial year 2026, Infosys was recognized as a leader in 12 industry-analyst ratings. The company reported AI work with 90% of its 200 largest clients, 4,600 AI projects, more than 28 million lines of code generated using AI, and more than 500 agents built. Infosys is scaling its forward-deployed engineering team.

Clients are focusing on fragmented data, legacy application landscapes, and business workflows that are not conducive to AI. Infosys combines knowledge of clients' technology landscapes, data-engineering capabilities, and process-reimagination capabilities to support AI value realization at scale.

Infosys identified six AI-services value pools:

- AI engineering services
- Data for AI
- Agents for operations
- AI software development and legacy modernization
- AI and physical devices
- AI trust and risk services

Infosys expects to share a comprehensive view of its approach at an Investor Day later in the quarter.

In Financial Services, Infosys reported traction in large deals and discretionary projects. The company expects acceleration in Financial Services and Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services in financial year 2027 compared with financial year 2026, based on deal wins and AI preferred-partner status with 15 of its largest 25 clients in each of these verticals.

Infosys revised its financial-year revenue-growth guidance to 3%–3.5% in constant-currency terms. Operating-margin guidance remained at 20%–22%.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Q3 FY'26 performance reflected continued momentum from the previous two quarters. The quarter included a charge associated with changes in India's labor codes, affecting operating profit, net profit, EPS, and free cash flow. Adjusted figures exclude this impact.

Q3 revenue was $5.1 billion, up 0.6% sequentially and 1.7% year-on-year in constant-currency terms. Operating margin was 18.4% including the labor-code impact and 21.2% after adjustment.

Additional Q3 and nine-month highlights were:

1. Revenue grew despite seasonality and lower third-party costs. Third-party costs as a percentage of revenue declined 0.3 percentage points sequentially and approximately 2.4 percentage points year-on-year.
2. Revenue growth for the first nine months was 2.8%. Third-party costs were approximately 1% lower than in the same period of the prior year and stood at 7.3% of revenue.
3. Financial Services grew 3.9% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
4. Europe led geographic growth at 7.2% year-on-year in constant-currency terms.
5. Volumes remained soft during the quarter and the year.
