## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Healthcare benefited from the contribution of the NHS deal signed by Infosys. Infosys does not provide deal-by-deal revenue estimates.

Regarding wage hikes, Infosys considers factors including the timing of the previous wage hike, market conditions, and inflation. In the current calendar, wage hikes were rolled out in two stages, with one part effective in January and the other in April. The next cycle has not yet been decided.

## Abhishek Pathak

There is increasing interest in AI work across industries. In Q3, Infosys saw growth in its AI pipeline, the number of AI projects, and AI-work penetration among its 200 largest clients. Activity was observed across financial services, telecommunications, pharmaceuticals and healthcare, energy and utilities, and, to a lesser extent, life sciences. Financial services showed comparatively stronger activity.

The Infosys-Cognition partnership involves joint work for clients using the Devin software agent and Infosys’s knowledge of client environments and industries. One example is legacy modernization, where the combination can make activities that previously required large, long-term commitments more addressable.

Infosys views this as one of six value pools involving newer areas of work. The combination of Devin and Infosys may enable work that would otherwise not have been undertaken.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Fiscal fourth-quarter guidance incorporates signed deals, deals that are ramping up, calendar days, working days, and other usual quarterly factors. The lower end of the guidance range includes a higher amount of uncertainty, while the higher end assumes a better macroeconomic environment.

Subcontractor usage depends on skill gaps, the skills required, geography, and the duration of the requirement. During the quarter, some large deals and other projects began ramping up, requiring Infosys to use subcontractors to fulfill requirements.

## Salil Parekh

### Manufacturing

Manufacturing faced weakness in automotive and in parts of the industrial sector across Europe. Some Manufacturing companies, partly in the United States and partly in Europe, benefited from supplying capabilities for data-center build-outs.

Automotive weakness is expected to continue. Infosys is focused on expanding its automotive client base and has successfully worked with multiple automotive companies. Broader Manufacturing opportunities related to companies supplying capabilities for the growth in AI and data-center activity, rather than data-center services themselves, are expected to support the business.

### Hi-Tech

The Hi-Tech portfolio includes groups of companies with differing performance. The industry continues to face cost pressure, and clients are seeking productivity benefits. Some clients are also assessing future sources of growth.

Companies supplying servers and related products are performing relatively well amid the AI boom. Other companies are evaluating future growth opportunities, with Infosys supporting that work. The Hi-Tech industry continues to face some constraints.

Over the medium to long term, Hi-Tech is viewed as a suitable industry for Infosys. AI is expected to affect different value pools within the industry, while clients remain focused on managing cost pressures. Infosys needs to provide the services clients require in ways that fit their cost structures. No specific timeline was provided.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

The known impact of the changed labor regulation is being recorded in the current quarter. Unless the regulation changes, Infosys does not expect further one-off impacts going forward.

The estimated recurring impact of the changed labor laws is approximately 15 basis points on an ongoing basis.
