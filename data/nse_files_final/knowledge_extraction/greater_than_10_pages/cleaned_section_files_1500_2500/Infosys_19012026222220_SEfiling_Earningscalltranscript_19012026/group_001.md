## Infosys Limited Press Conference Call

January 14, 2026

### Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

Infosys reported the following Q3 performance:

- Revenue grew 0.6% sequentially and 1.7% year-on-year in constant currency.
- Large deals totaled $4.8 billion across 26 deals, with 57% net new.
- Adjusted operating margin was 21.2%.
- Free cash flow was $915 million.

One of the significant large deals was a $1.6 billion contract with the National Health Service in the U.K. The engagement expands Infosys’s healthcare work and involves helping the NHS use AI to streamline operations and improve patient care for U.K. citizens.

Infosys has expanded its Topaz AI capabilities with Topaz Fabric, an agent services suite that helps clients manage and implement AI agents across the enterprise. The company reported strong AI adoption across its client base:

- AI work was being conducted with more than 90% of its 200 largest clients.
- Infosys was working on 4,600 AI projects.
- Its teams had generated more than 28 million lines of code using AI.
- Infosys had built more than 500 agents.
- The company was scaling its forward-deployed engineering team.

Infosys identified six emerging AI-led value pools:

- AI engineering services
- Data for AI
- Agents for operations
- AI software development and legacy modernization
- AI deployed in physical devices
- AI trust and risk services

Management stated that AI adoption could create incremental opportunities while productivity gains could compress some legacy areas. Infosys planned to share a comprehensive view of its approach at an investor day later in the quarter.

Following the quarter’s performance, Infosys revised its financial-year revenue growth guidance to 3%–3.5% in constant currency. Operating margin guidance remained unchanged at 20%–22%.

### Headcount, demand, and industry outlook

Salil Parekh said the increase in headcount reflected confidence in market demand and supported the higher growth guidance. He said Infosys had seen a favorable outlook in Financial Services and Energy, Utilities, Resources, and Services, supported by deal momentum and its position as an AI partner for large clients.

### Jayesh Sanghrajka

Chief Financial Officer

Infosys had onboarded approximately 18,000 freshers against its plan to hire 20,000 during the year. Many were still under training. Including trainees, utilization had declined, reflecting investment in capacity for future demand.

Sanghrajka said quarterly margin had expanded by 20 basis points compared with the previous quarter. The nine-month margin was 21%, the midpoint of the company’s guidance.

The main drivers of the 20-basis-point quarterly margin expansion were:

- 40 basis points from currency
- 50 basis points from Project Maximus, mainly through value-based selling and lean and automation initiatives across multiple projects
- Offsetting effects from furloughs and fewer working days
- Higher variable-pay accrual compared with the previous quarter
- Offsetting one-off benefits

On a nine-month year-on-year basis, sales and marketing investment had increased by 50 basis points. Lower utilization of approximately 1% had also been absorbed in the margin. Sanghrajka said the company was building future capacity and investing in sales and marketing while maintaining a stable margin plan.

### Wage revisions

Infosys had completed one wage cycle in two parts, in January and April. The next wage revision had not yet been decided.

### AI services

Salil Parekh said Infosys was conducting AI services work with more than 90% of its 200 largest clients. The company was also reshaping existing services using AI. For example, it was using agents across several service lines to support growth or productivity.
