## Standalone Statement of Cash Flows

## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the year is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately.

The Company considers highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.
