## Note:

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends distributed outside India are subject to applicable Indian foreign-exchange laws and withholding tax requirements.

- The payout ratio is computed as a percentage of free cash flow prepared under IFRS.
- Buyback transaction costs of ₹58 crore are excluded.
- The final dividend was recommended by the Board of Directors on April 23, 2026. Payment is subject to shareholder approval at the AGM scheduled for June 23, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026, and payment is scheduled for June 25, 2026.
- Infosys' capital allocation policy is to return approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through semiannual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and approvals. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure under the IFRS Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows. Including buybacks, the Company returned 82.1% of cumulative free cash flow for the years ended March 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026.

## 2. Business description

## Strategy

Infosys' strategy is to help clients navigate their AI journeys and unlock business value by enabling them to ideate, plan, and scale their AI initiatives. This includes building strong data foundations and robust cloud capabilities, supported by safeguards for privacy, ethics, and controls through Responsible AI practices.

## Organization

The Company's go-to-market business units and solutions are detailed in the Operating context section of this Integrated Annual Report.

## Infrastructure

During fiscal 2026, physical infrastructure space increased by 1.87 million sq. ft. Total available space as of March 31, 2026, was 58.20 million sq. ft. Infosys had a presence in 59 countries across 290 locations as of that date.

## Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)

Infosys' M&A approach is aimed at strengthening capabilities, deepening industry expertise, and expanding its geographical footprint.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership interests or voting interests in:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — An energy and business consulting services company headquartered in Texas, U.S.; acquired on April 30, 2025.
2. **The Missing Link Group** — A cybersecurity services provider headquartered in Australia; acquired on April 30, 2025.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group entered into definitive agreements to:

1. Form a joint venture with Telstra, an Australian telecommunications and technology company. As part of the transaction, Infosys will acquire a 75% stake in the Versent Group, a wholly owned Telstra Group subsidiary providing cloud and digital transformation services.
2. Acquire **Stratus Global LLC**, a U.S.-based insurance consulting and technology services company focused on Guidewire services. The acquisition was completed as of the date of the report.
3. Acquire **Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc.**, together with its subsidiaries, including **Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC**, a U.S.-based healthcare digital transformation and consulting company.

The Versent Group comprises Telstra Purple Pty Ltd and the integrated operations of Versent, Epicon, Telstra Purple Digital, and associated Cloud Access products. Telstra Purple Digital is carved out from Telstra's Enterprise Tech Services/Telstra Purple portfolio and excludes Alliance Automation Pty Ltd, Aqura Technologies Pty Ltd, and Telstra Broadcast Services Pty Limited.

## Subsidiaries

Infosys and its subsidiaries provide consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services.

- At the beginning of fiscal 2026, the Group had 30 direct subsidiaries and 76 step-down subsidiaries.
- As of March 31, 2026, the Group had 30 direct subsidiaries and 84 step-down subsidiaries.
- The Company does not have any material subsidiary.

During fiscal 2026, the Board of Directors reviewed the affairs of the subsidiaries. The Company prepared consolidated financial statements under Section 129(3) of the Companies Act, 2013, which form part of this Integrated Annual Report.

A statement containing the salient features of the financial statements of the subsidiaries, in prescribed Form AOC-1, is appended as Annexure 1 to the Board's report. It includes the performance, financial position, and changes relating to each subsidiary during fiscal 2026.

## 3. Human resources management

Infosys' people strategy focuses on attracting and developing talent, building internal leadership pipelines, and supporting employee experience through policies, systems, and programs. Its workforce practices include continuous learning, diversity, equity and inclusion, flexible work models, and technology-led enablement.

The Company maintains employee grievance redressal through structured Resolution Hubs, guided by confidentiality, non-retaliation, natural justice, and timely resolution. Internal Committees address the prevention and redressal of sexual harassment. Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report. No complaint cases were pending for more than 90 days.

Other disclosures and information are set out in the Management's discussion and analysis section.
