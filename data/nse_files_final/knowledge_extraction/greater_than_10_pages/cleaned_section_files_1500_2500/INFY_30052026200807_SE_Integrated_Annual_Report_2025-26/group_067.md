**Independent assessment / evaluation / assurance**

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP carried out the external independent assessment, evaluation, or assurance.

**Waste management practices**

Infosys’ waste management strategy is based on the 5R principle: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Refurbish / Repurpose, and Recycle. The company reports that 99% of disposed waste stays out of landfills and is working toward its 2030 goal of zero waste to landfills through TRUE Zero Waste Certification at its India campuses. As of fiscal 2026, 46% of its owned campuses in India had achieved TRUE Zero Waste Certification from Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI).

As an IT company, Infosys generates e-waste and follows a refurbishment-first approach where feasible. Materials that cannot be refurbished are handled through authorized recycling vendors. Authorized Pollution Control Board recyclers and refurbishers are periodically evaluated for compliance with applicable legal requirements and the extent of resource circularity in their processes.

**Waste reporting notes**

1. Includes sanitary waste.
2. Excludes construction and demolition waste from infrastructure development.
3. Includes waste generated in India, overseas-owned locations, and e-waste for all locations globally.
4. Co-processing was included under “Other recovery operations” in the current year; it was included under “Other disposal operations” in the previous year.
