## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objectives and responsibilities of the Committee are:

1. To assist the Board in fulfilling its oversight responsibilities regarding the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of strategic, operational, and external-environment risks.
2. To monitor and approve the enterprise risk management framework and associated practices of the Company.
3. To periodically assess risks to the effective execution of business strategy and review relevant leading indicators.
4. To periodically review the Company’s risk management processes and practices and ensure appropriate measures are taken to achieve a prudent balance between risk and reward in ongoing and new business activities.
5. To evaluate significant risk exposures and assess Management’s actions to mitigate those exposures in a timely manner.
6. To evaluate cybersecurity-related risks and ensure appropriate procedures are in place to mitigate them in a timely manner.
7. To coordinate activities with the Audit Committee where there is any overlap with audit activities.
8. To periodically review and reassess the adequacy of the Risk Management Committee Charter and recommend proposed changes to the Board for approval.
9. To ensure access to internal information necessary to fulfill its oversight role and obtain advice and assistance from internal or external legal, accounting, or other advisors.
10. To appoint, remove, and approve the terms of remuneration of the Chief Risk Officer.
