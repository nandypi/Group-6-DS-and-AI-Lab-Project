## 8. Income tax assets / liabilities

Net profit earned from providing software development and other services outside India is subject to tax in the countries where the work is performed. Most taxes paid outside India can be claimed as credit against tax liabilities in India. Refer to Note 2.17 of the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements.

## 9. Other financial liabilities

Liabilities for accrued compensation to employees include provisions for bonuses, accrued salaries, incentives, and retention bonuses payable to staff. Financial liabilities under option arrangements represent redemption liabilities related to acquisitions to purchase the corresponding minority stake.

Accrued expenses represent amounts accrued for other operational expenses. Compensated absences are both accumulating and non-accumulating. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by actuarial valuation.

## IV. Results of our operations

For details about results of operations, refer to the “Results of our operations and state of affairs” section in the Board’s Report.

During fiscal 2026, the Company continued Project Maximus, a margin expansion program structured across five pillars:

1. Value-based selling
2. Efficient pyramid
3. Lean, automation and generative AI
4. Improving key portfolios
5. Optimization of indirect costs

Value-based selling identifies opportunities to align pricing with the value clients receive. Efficient pyramid initiatives seek to reduce effort costs through improved utilization, reduced use of subcontractors, and changes in role and location mix. Lean, automation and generative AI initiatives combine productivity and process improvements using automation technologies. Improving key portfolios focuses on margins in large client engagements and service lines. Savings from the optimization of indirect costs supported investment in talent, AI, sales, and marketing.

## 1. Revenue

The growth in revenue from fiscal 2025 to fiscal 2026 was as follows:

| Particulars | Standalone 2026 | Standalone 2025 | Standalone % change | Consolidated 2026 | Consolidated 2025 | Consolidated % change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 1,48,819 | 1,36,592 | 9.0% | 1,78,650 | 1,62,990 | 9.6% |

*Amounts in ₹ crore.*

The increase in revenue was primarily attributable to higher realization due to role mix, improved pricing and productivity, acquisitions, and currency benefit.

Reported revenue growth includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant-currency growth excludes this impact by converting current-period revenues in local currencies to U.S. dollars using prior-period exchange rates and comparing the result with prior-period reported revenues.

Consolidated revenue in reported currency terms was ₹1,78,650 crore in fiscal 2026, representing growth of 9.6%. Consolidated revenue grew 3.1% in constant-currency terms.

The Company added 443 new customers on a gross basis during fiscal 2026, compared with 365 during fiscal 2025.

## 10. Other liabilities

Invoicing in excess of revenue is classified as unearned revenue. Withholding and other taxes payable represent local taxes payable in the countries where the Company operates.

Infosys provides for provident fund contributions for eligible employees as a defined benefit plan because the Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on trust investments and the notified interest rate.

The Company operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers.

Infosys provides gratuity, a defined benefit retirement plan, to eligible employees in India. The Gratuity Plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure. The plans provide periodic post-retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments according to the rules of each fund and include death and disability benefits.

## 11. Provisions

The provision for post-sales client support covers the likely cost of providing client support for fixed-price and fixed-timeframe contracts.

## 12. Leases

Lease additions mainly comprise computers and buildings taken on lease in certain locations outside India.

For the year ended March 31, 2026, approximately 97.1% of consolidated revenue was from exports and 2.9% was from domestic revenue.
