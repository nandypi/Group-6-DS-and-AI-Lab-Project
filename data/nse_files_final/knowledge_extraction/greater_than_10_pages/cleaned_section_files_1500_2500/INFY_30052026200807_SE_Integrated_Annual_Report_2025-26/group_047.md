## Outstanding ADSs / GDRs / warrants or any convertible instruments

Infosys ADSs, evidenced by ADRs, are traded in the United States on the NYSE under the ticker symbol `INFY`, with trading denominated in USD. Each ADS represents one equity share. The ADRs began trading on the NYSE on December 12, 2012.

## Registered office and global locations

Infosys's registered office is at Electronics City, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560100, Karnataka, India.

The Company's operations span 290 locations in 59 countries. Infosys has no manufacturing plants and operates development centers and offices in India and overseas.
