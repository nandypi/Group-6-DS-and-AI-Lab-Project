# Defined Benefit Plans

For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, the yield on government bonds is used as the discount rate because a market for high-quality corporate bonds has not developed. For most overseas defined benefit plans, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used for the same reason.

The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined by the Company based on its past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management’s estimate of future salary increases.

The attrition rate is based on management’s estimate using the Company’s past long-term employee turnover trend. Employee tenure reflects the past long-term trend in employees’ average remaining service life and the average estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, assumptions regarding future mortality experience are based on statistics published by the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas defined benefit plans, mortality assumptions use the latest statistics on life expectancy, plan experience, and other relevant data.

The Group assesses these assumptions against its projected long-term growth plans and prevailing industry standards.

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve make contributions to the Infosys BPM Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively. As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the trustees administered the contributions, which were invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

Plan assets for overseas defined benefit plans were primarily invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined according to the investment criteria prescribed under regulations applicable to pension funds and insurer managers. The insurers’ investments are diversified and provide guaranteed interest rate arrangements.

These defined benefit plans expose the Group to the following actuarial risks:

- **Interest rate risk:** The present value of the defined benefit plan liability is generally calculated using a discount rate based on government bond yields. In certain overseas jurisdictions, it is based on government bond yields adjusted for a corporate spread. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation generally increases.
- **Life expectancy and investment risk:** The pension fund offers members a choice between a lifelong pension and a cash lump sum upon retirement. The fund has defined rates for converting the lump sum to a pension. There is a risk that members live longer than implied by these conversion rates or that pension assets do not achieve the investment return implied by the rates.
- **Asset volatility:** A proportion of the pension fund is held in equities. Although equities are expected to outperform corporate bonds over the long term, they create short-term volatility and risk. The pension fund insurer’s board is responsible for the investment strategy and equity allocation.

| Impact from | Gratuity: 1% point increase/decrease (` crore) | Pension: point increase/0.5% decrease (` crore) |
|---|---:|---:|

The sensitivity to significant actuarial assumptions is computed by varying one actuarial assumption used to value the defined benefit obligation while keeping all other actuarial assumptions constant. In practice, this scenario is not probable, and changes in some assumptions may be correlated.

The Group expects to contribute ` 540 crore to gratuity and ` 66 crore to pension during fiscal 2027.
