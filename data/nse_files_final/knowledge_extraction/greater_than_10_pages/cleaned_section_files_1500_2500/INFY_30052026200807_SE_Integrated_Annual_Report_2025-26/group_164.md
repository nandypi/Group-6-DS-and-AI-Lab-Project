## Background and context for item no. 6

Regulation 31A of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, provides a mechanism for reclassification of members from the “Promoters and Promoter Group” category to the “Public” category, subject to specified conditions.

The Company received a joint request letter dated April 30, 2026, from Mr. Shreyas Shibulal and Ms. Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal (collectively, the “Applicants”) seeking reclassification from the “Promoter and Promoter Group” category to the “Public” category under Regulation 31A.

The Applicants’ shareholding as of March 31, 2026, was:

| Sl. No. | Name of outgoing promoter/promoter group member(s) | No. of shares held | % of holding |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Shreyas Shibulal | 1,79,37,000 | 0.44 |
| 2 | Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal | 48,85,500 | 0.12 |
| Total |  | 2,28,22,500 | 0.56 |

The Applicants stated that their classification as “Promoter and Promoter Group” was solely due to their relationship with Mr. S. D. Shibulal, with Mr. Shreyas Shibulal being his son and Ms. Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal being his daughter-in-law. Mr. S. D. Shibulal was a promoter and co-founder of the Company but ceased to be involved with the Company in 2014.

The Applicants stated that they had never worked with the Company, participated in its business, management, or decision-making, or held a position as a key managerial personnel or director. They also stated that they had no right to appoint directors and no ability to control the Company’s management or policy decisions. They received information about the Company only as ordinary public shareholders.

The Applicants undertook that, together with persons related to them, they:

- Do not hold more than 10% of the Company’s total voting rights.
- Do not directly or indirectly exercise control over the Company’s affairs.
- Do not have special rights in relation to the Company through formal or informal arrangements, including shareholder agreements.
- Are not represented on the Board, including through nominee directorship.
- Do not act as key managerial personnel of the Company.
- Are not “wilful defaulters” under Reserve Bank of India guidelines.
- Are not fugitive economic offenders.

The Applicants confirmed that there was no pending regulatory action against them. They also undertook to:

- Continue complying with the conditions relating to voting rights, control, and special rights from the date of reclassification.
- Comply with the conditions relating to Board representation and key managerial personnel status for at least three years from the date of reclassification.

At its meeting held on April 30, 2026, the Board noted that:

- The Applicants were engaged in their respective independent professions or businesses and were not connected, directly or indirectly, with the Company’s operations or decision-making.
- The Applicants did not exercise influence over the Company’s decisions.
- The Applicants had no representation on the Board and were not acting as key managerial personnel.
- The Applicants held less than 1% of the Company’s paid-up equity share capital.
- The Applicants, together with persons related to them, held less than 10% of the Company’s paid-up equity share capital.

The Board approved the Applicants’ request for reclassification from the “Promoter and Promoter Group” category to the “Public” category, subject to approval from the stock exchanges, shareholder approval, and compliance with other applicable requirements.

The Company filed applications with BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited on May 1, 2026. BSE and NSE issued no-objection letters dated May 11, 2026:

- BSE letter no. LIST/COMP/HG/059/2026-27
- NSE letter no. NSE/LIST/COMP/INFY/587/2026-2027

## Further, the Board confirms the following:

- The Company complies with the minimum public shareholding requirement under Regulation 38 of the LODR Regulations.
- Trading in the Company’s equity shares is not suspended by any stock exchange on which the Company is listed.
- The Company has no outstanding dues to SEBI, the stock exchanges, or the depositories.

The Board recommended that the proposed resolution under Item no. 6 be passed as an ordinary resolution. Under Regulation 31A(3)(a)(vi) of the LODR Regulations, the Applicants and persons related to them, as defined under Regulation 31A, may not vote on the resolution.

No director, key managerial personnel, or their relatives had an interest in or concern with the resolution.

## Additional information on directors recommended for appointment / reappointment as required under Regulation 36 of the LODR Regulations and applicable secretarial standards

### Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman

Nandan M. Nilekani is the Co-Founder and Chairman of Infosys Technologies Limited. He was the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), in the rank of a Cabinet Minister, from 2009 to 2014. He has co-founded and chairs EkStep Foundation, a not-for-profit organization focused on a technology-based platform for literacy and numeracy.

In January 2023, he was appointed co-chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion, and Development.

Nandan received his bachelor’s degree from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bombay.

- **Age:** 70 years
- **Nature of expertise:** Information technology, leadership, strategy, board service and governance, financial matters, diversity, global business, sales and marketing, cybersecurity, mergers and acquisitions, risk management, and sustainability and ESG
