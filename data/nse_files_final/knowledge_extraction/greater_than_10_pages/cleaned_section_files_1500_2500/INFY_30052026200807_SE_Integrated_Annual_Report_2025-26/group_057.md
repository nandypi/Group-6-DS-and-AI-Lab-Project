## Section C: Principle-wise performance disclosure

This section provides performance disclosures on integrating the principles and core elements of responsible business conduct into key processes and decisions. Indicators are categorized as “Essential” and “Leadership”; essential indicators are expected to be disclosed by entities required to file the report, while leadership indicators may be disclosed voluntarily.

## PRINCIPLE 1 Businesses should conduct and govern themselves with integrity, and in a manner that is ethical, transparent and accountable.
