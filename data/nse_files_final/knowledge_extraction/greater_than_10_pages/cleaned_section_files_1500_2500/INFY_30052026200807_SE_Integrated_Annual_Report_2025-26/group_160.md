## Explanatory Statement

## Background and context for item nos. 4 and 5

The proposed amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program, 2019 (the “Amended 2019 Plan”) would continue the grant of stock incentives to eligible employees of Infosys and its subsidiaries.

The 2015 Incentive Compensation Plan was approved by shareholders through a postal ballot dated March 31, 2016. The existing Expanded Stock Ownership Program, 2019 (the “2019 Plan”) was approved at the Annual General Meeting held on June 22, 2019, for a period of seven years and is due to expire on June 21, 2026.

The 2019 Plan originally covered approximately 5,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 1.15% of the Company’s then-outstanding equity shares. As of the date of the Notice, 3,26,63,903 equity shares remained available for grant under the existing plan, representing approximately 0.8% of the Company’s outstanding equity share capital.

As of the date of the Notice, 4,44,19,917 RSUs were available for grant under all ESOP plans, representing approximately 1.1% of the Company’s outstanding equity share capital. This includes RSUs that have vested but remain unexercised and RSUs that have not yet vested.

The proposed amendments are intended to:

- Continue the performance-based stock incentive program across an expanded employee base.
- Support the attraction, retention, and incentivization of key talent.
- Align employee interests with long-term shareholder value creation.

## The summary of the proposed amendments is as under:

## 1. Extension of the 2019 Plan tenure:

The 2019 Plan would be extended for a further period of seven (7) years from the date of shareholder approval. Grants under the 2019 Plan would be made over seven (7) years, and the amended plan would remain valid for ten (10) years from the date shareholder approval is received.

## 2. Strengthening alignment with Long-Term Shareholder Value creation and rationalization of Operational Metrics based on the inputs received from investors:

- A three-year rolling Relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) would be introduced as a vesting parameter.
- Operating metrics would be rationalized to maintain focus on revenue and margin and to improve consistency and comparability over time.
- Sub-segment metrics, such as digital revenue growth, may become transient and less comparable as technology and business models evolve.

## 3. Share pool and dilution

The proposed extension would not increase the original shareholder-approved pool of 5,00,00,000 equity shares. The amended plan would operate within the remaining balance of 3,26,63,903 shares, representing approximately 0.8% of the Company’s equity share capital as of May 28, 2026. The proposal would not result in incremental dilution compared with the earlier approval.

## 4. Regulatory amendments

Other amendments would provide clarity and consistency and align the 2019 Plan with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
