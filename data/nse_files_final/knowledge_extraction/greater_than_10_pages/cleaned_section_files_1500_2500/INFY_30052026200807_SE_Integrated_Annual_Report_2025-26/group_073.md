## 5. Does the entity have a framework / policy on cybersecurity and risks related to data privacy? (Yes / No) If yes, provide web-link of the policy.

Yes. Infosys has a cybersecurity framework, SEED, aligned to NIST's CyberSecurity Framework (CSF), with supplementary policies, processes, procedures, and standards for enterprise-level information security objectives. More information is available at https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/governance/information-management.html.

Infosys has a Data Privacy Policy published on the Company's intranet. The policy applies across Infosys operations, including activities involving service providers. Infosys provides privacy notices at the data collection point for internal and external data subjects.

## Consumer satisfaction survey

Did your entity carry out any survey with regard to consumer satisfaction relating to the major products / services of the entity, significant locations of the entity or the entity as a whole? (Yes / No)

Yes. Infosys conducts surveys to gauge customer satisfaction for its major services. In addition to regular client interactions, the Company uses an annual Client Value Survey and periodic engagement surveys. The surveys use a structured questionnaire, and feedback is collected through a web survey hosted by an independent organization. Survey results help Infosys understand client expectations and needs and are inputs into investment decisions.
