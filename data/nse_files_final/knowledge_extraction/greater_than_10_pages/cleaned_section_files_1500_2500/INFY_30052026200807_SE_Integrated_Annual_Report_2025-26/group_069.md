## 5. Does the entity have a business continuity and disaster management plan? Give details in 100 words/ web link.

Yes. Infosys has a Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) called Phoenix, certified by ISO 22301:2019 Security and Resilience – Business Continuity Management Standard.

Phoenix provides a framework for planning, establishing, implementing, operating, monitoring, reviewing, maintaining, and continually improving business continuity measures across Infosys and its subsidiaries. In addition to the corporate-level Phoenix plan, comprehensive plans are maintained at three operational levels:

- Business functions
- Locations
- Accounts

The BCMS is integrated into the Enterprise Risk Management Framework. It covers crisis scenarios involving catastrophes and natural or human-made disasters that could disrupt or severely constrain operations. Risk assessments at the function, location, and account levels are documented with mitigation plans and controls.

The management system is validated through tests and exercises at multiple levels. Infosys reports that various incidents have been handled without significant impacts on business continuity or employee safety.

## Enabling Responsible Value Chain Disclosure and Decarbonization

In fiscal 2026, Infosys initiated a SEBI-aligned supplier engagement program focused on ESG disclosures under the BRSR Core framework. The program collected ESG-related disclosures for fiscal 2025 from the top 2% of upstream suppliers by spend, focusing on high-impact suppliers including human resource service providers and IT service vendors. These suppliers represented approximately 13% of total spend.

The disclosures covered supplier climate practices, emissions management approaches, and reporting maturity. Using a spend-based allocation methodology aligned with the GHG Protocol, Infosys attributed the following supplier emissions to itself:

| Emission category | Emissions attributed to Infosys | Share of suppliers’ combined emissions |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scope 1 | 667 tCO2e | Approximately 1.6% of combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions |
| Scope 2 | 219 tCO2e | Approximately 1.6% of combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions |

The attributed emissions were calculated for value chain disclosure under SEBI requirements and were not included in Infosys’ reported Scope 3 inventory.

The exercise improved emissions visibility, supplier engagement, and prioritization of decarbonization opportunities. Infosys is continuing to collaborate with suppliers and integrate climate considerations into procurement processes.
