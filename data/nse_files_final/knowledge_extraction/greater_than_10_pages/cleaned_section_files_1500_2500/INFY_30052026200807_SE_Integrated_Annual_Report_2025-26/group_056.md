## Governance, leadership and oversight

## 7. Statement by director responsible for the business responsibility report, highlighting ESG-related challenges, targets and achievements

Infosys’ ESG Vision 2030 guides its governance, environmental stewardship, workplace inclusion and diversity, and responsible use of technology. Its ESG priorities include climate commitments, large-scale skilling, and responsible practices across operations and the value chain.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys reported progress toward its ESG commitments through governance mechanisms, performance metrics, and stakeholder engagement. It continued to address climate transition, talent transformation, regulatory requirements, transparency, accountability, and macroeconomic challenges.

Infosys stated that regulatory expectations and climate-related risks would continue to evolve. Further information on ESG challenges, targets, performance, and achievements is provided in the ESG Report and the “Approaching Value Creation” and “Delivering Value” chapters of the Integrated Annual Report.
