## Fairness and excellence

## Board member evaluation

The Board monitors and reviews the Board evaluation framework. In consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, it establishes evaluation criteria for the Chairman, the Board, Board committees, and executive, non-executive, and independent directors through peer evaluation, excluding the director being evaluated.

Independent directors are evaluated based on indicators including:

- Contribution to and monitoring of corporate governance practices.
- Contribution through the introduction of international best practices to address business challenges and risks.
- Active participation in long-term strategic planning.
- Commitment to directors’ obligations and fiduciary responsibilities, including participation in Board and committee meetings.
- Participation in a formal annual Board review intended to improve the effectiveness of the Board, its committees, and individual directors.

The Board engaged Egon Zehnder, a leadership advisory firm specializing in Board matters, to conduct the fiscal 2026 Board evaluation. The evaluation covered Board dynamics, softer aspects, committee effectiveness, and information flow to the Board and its committees. The methodology included questionnaires and one-on-one discussions. Succession planning and committee composition were also considered.

The fiscal 2026 Board evaluation was completed during the year. The Board discussed recommendations from the previous year’s evaluation, provided individual feedback, and reviewed progress on prior recommendations together with recommendations from the current year.

The evaluation process was based on affirmations from the independent directors that they met the independence criteria under the Companies Act, the Listing Regulations, and the NYSE listing manual.

## Board and executive leadership compensation

## Executive leadership compensation

Executive compensation programs provide performance-based rewards, with a significant portion of total executive rewards tied to the achievement of long-term corporate performance goals and aligned with shareholder interests.

As required under the Listing Regulations, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommends to the Board the remuneration payable to directors, key managerial personnel, and senior management. The Company’s Nomination and Remuneration Policy is available on its website.

## Non-executive and non-independent chairman's compensation

Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman, voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company.

## Independent directors' compensation

Compensation payable to independent directors is limited to a fixed annual amount determined and approved by the Board. The total amount does not exceed 1% of net profit for the year, calculated under the Companies Act, 2013. The Board reviews independent directors’ performance annually.

Factors considered when determining independent directors’ compensation include global Board compensation benchmarks, participation in Board and committee meetings, committee membership or chairmanship, time spent on duties, and roles and functions prescribed under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, the Listing Regulations, and other factors considered appropriate by the Board.

Shareholders at the 34th AGM held on June 22, 2015, approved an amount not exceeding 1% of the Company’s annual net profit, calculated under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013, to be paid and distributed among some or all non-executive directors as decided by the Board. The payment applies to the Company’s profits for each year.

## Corporate governance report

The amount payable to independent directors for the year ended March 31, 2026, was ₹19.91 crore. Independent directors were also reimbursed for expenses incurred in performing their official duties. None of the non-executive directors received remuneration amounting to 50% of the total remuneration paid to non-executive directors during the year ended March 31, 2026.

## Indemnification agreements

The Company entered into agreements to indemnify its directors and officers for claims brought against them to the fullest extent permitted under applicable law. The agreements cover certain expenses, judgments, fines, settlement amounts, and public relations consultation expenses, where required, arising from services provided as a director or officer, including actions brought by or in the right of the Company.

## Agreements binding listed entities

Pursuant to Regulation 30A of the Listing Regulations, no agreement was entered into or executed during fiscal 2026 by the shareholders, promoters, promoter group entities, related parties, directors, key managerial personnel, or employees of the Company or its subsidiaries.
