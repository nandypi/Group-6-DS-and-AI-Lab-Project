## Annexure 'A' to the Independent Auditor's Report

*(Referred to in paragraph 1(f) under 'Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements' section of the report to the Members of Infosys Limited of even date.)*

## Report on the Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements under Clause (i) of sub-section 3 of Section 143 of the Companies Act, 2013 (the 'Act')

The auditors audited the internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements of Infosys Limited as of March 31, 2026, in conjunction with their audit of the Company's standalone financial statements for the year ended on that date.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for Internal Financial Controls

The Company's Management and Board of Directors are responsible for establishing and maintaining internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements, based on the internal control over financial reporting criteria established by the Company and considering the essential components of internal control stated in the Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

These responsibilities include designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls for:

- The orderly and efficient conduct of business.
- Adherence to company policies.
- Safeguarding the Company's assets.
- Prevention and detection of frauds and errors.
- Accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Timely preparation of reliable financial information as required under the Act.

## Auditor's Responsibility

The auditors' responsibility was to express an opinion on the Company's internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements based on their audit.

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting issued by the ICAI and the Standards on Auditing prescribed under Section 143(10) of the Act, to the extent applicable. These standards require compliance with ethical requirements and planning and performance of the audit to obtain reasonable assurance that adequate internal financial controls were established and maintained and operated effectively in all material respects.

The audit procedures included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Company's internal financial controls.
- Assessing the risk of a material weakness.
- Testing and evaluating the design and operating effectiveness of internal controls based on the assessed risk.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence to support the audit opinion.

The procedures selected depended on the auditors' judgment, including their assessment of the risks of material misstatement of the financial statements due to fraud or error.

## Meaning of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Internal financial control with reference to standalone financial statements is a process designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding:

1. The reliability of financial reporting.
2. The preparation of financial statements for external purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
3. The maintenance of records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and dispositions of the Company's assets.
4. The recording of transactions necessary to permit preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
5. The authorization of receipts and expenditures by management and directors.
6. The prevention or timely detection of unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposition of the Company's assets that could materially affect the financial statements.

## Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Because of the inherent limitations of internal financial controls over financial reporting, including the possibility of collusion or improper management override of controls, material misstatements due to error or fraud may occur and not be detected.

Any projection of an evaluation of internal financial controls to future periods is subject to the risk that the controls may become inadequate because of changes in conditions or that the degree of compliance with policies and procedures may deteriorate.

## Opinion

In the auditors' opinion, Infosys Limited had, in all material respects, adequate internal financial controls with reference to its standalone financial statements, and those controls were operating effectively as of March 31, 2026.

The opinion was based on the criteria for internal financial control with reference to standalone financial statements established by the Company, considering the essential components of internal control stated in the ICAI Guidance Note.

## Annexure 'B' to the Independent Auditor's Report

*(Referred to in paragraph 2 under 'Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements' section of the report to the Members of Infosys Limited of even date.)*

Based on the information and explanations provided by the Company and the books and records examined in the normal course of audit, the auditors reported the following:

### i. Property, plant and equipment, right-of-use assets and intangible assets

- The Company maintained proper records showing full particulars, including quantitative details and location, of property, plant and equipment, as well as relevant details of right-of-use assets.
- The Company maintained proper records showing full particulars of intangible assets.
- The Company had a physical verification program covering all property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets at least once every three years. Certain assets due for verification during the year were physically verified by management, and no material discrepancies were reported.
- Based on property tax receipts, lease agreements, and registered sale, transfer, or conveyance deeds examined, the titles of self-constructed buildings and other immovable properties disclosed under Property, Plant and Equipment were held in the Company's name as of the balance sheet date, except for properties where the Company was the lessee under duly executed lease agreements.
- The Company did not revalue any property, plant and equipment, including right-of-use assets, or intangible assets during the year.
- No proceedings were initiated or pending against the Company as of March 31, 2026, for holding benami property under the Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988, as amended in 2016, and related rules.

### ii. Inventory and working capital limits

- The Company did not have any inventory; reporting under clause 3(ii)(a) of the Order was not applicable.
- The Company was not sanctioned working capital limits exceeding `5 crore in aggregate at any point during the year from banks or financial institutions on the security of current assets; reporting under clause 3(ii)(b) of the Order was not applicable.

### iii. Investments, loans, guarantees and securities

- The Company made investments in companies and granted unsecured loans to other parties during the year.
- The Company did not provide loans or advances in the nature of loans, guarantees, or security to any other entity during the year; reporting under clause 3(iii)(a) of the Order was not applicable.
- In the auditors' opinion, the investments made and the terms and conditions of loans granted during the year were prima facie not prejudicial to the Company's interests.
- For loans granted by the Company, repayment schedules for principal and payment of interest were stipulated, and repayments and interest receipts were generally regular.
- No overdue amounts relating to loans granted by the Company remained outstanding as of the balance sheet date.
- No loan that fell due during the year was renewed or extended, and no fresh loan was granted to settle overdue amounts from existing loans to the same parties.
- The Company did not grant loans or advances in the nature of loans that were repayable on demand or without specified repayment terms or periods; reporting under clause 3(iii)(f) of the Order was not applicable.
- The Company did not make investments in firms or limited liability partnerships during the year.
- The Company did not provide guarantees or security, or grant secured or unsecured advances in the nature of loans, to companies, firms, limited liability partnerships, or other parties.

### iv. Compliance with Sections 185 and 186

The Company complied with Sections 185 and 186 of the Companies Act, 2013, in respect of loans granted, investments made, and guarantees and securities provided, as applicable.

### v. Deposits

The Company did not accept any deposits or amounts deemed to be deposits. Reporting under clause 3(v) of the Order was not applicable.

### vi. Cost records

The Central Government had not specified the maintenance of cost records under sub-section (1) of Section 148 of the Companies Act, 2013, for the business activities carried out by the Company. Reporting under clause (vi) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
