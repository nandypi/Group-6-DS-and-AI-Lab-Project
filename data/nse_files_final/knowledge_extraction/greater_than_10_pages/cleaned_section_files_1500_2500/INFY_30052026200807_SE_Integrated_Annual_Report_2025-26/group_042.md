## Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee

The Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee (the “Sub-committee”) comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, its members were:

1. Michael Gibbs, Chairperson and cybersecurity expert
2. D. Sundaram
3. Govind Iyer
4. Chitra Nayak
5. Helene Auriel Potier (1)

The Risk Management Committee constituted the Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee in April 2019. The Sub-committee was voluntarily constituted to focus on cybersecurity-related threats. Its objective is to assess cybersecurity-related risks and the Company's preparedness to mitigate and respond to those risks. The Sub-committee meets periodically and recommends its findings, if any, to the Risk Management Committee.

The Sub-committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## Stakeholders Relationship Committee

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee (the “Committee”) comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, its members were:

1. Michael Gibbs, Chairperson
2. D. Sundaram
3. Bobby Parikh
4. Chitra Nayak

A.G.S. Manikantha, Company Secretary, serves as the Compliance Officer under the Listing Regulations and as the Nodal Officer for compliance with the IEPF rules.

## Purpose of the Committee

The Committee assists the Board and the Company in overseeing matters relating to stakeholder interests. “Stakeholder” includes shareholders and other security holders.

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objectives of the Committee are to:

1. Consider and resolve security-holder concerns or complaints.
2. Monitor and review the Company's investor service standards.
3. Develop an understanding of shareholders' views through direct interaction, analysts' briefings, or shareholder surveys.
4. Oversee and review shareholder engagement and communication plans, ensure shareholder views and concerns are brought to the Board at the appropriate time, and monitor steps taken to address those concerns.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises four independent directors and performs functions required under:

- Section 178 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the rules framed thereunder
- Regulation 20 of the Listing Regulations and other applicable regulations and laws
- Applicable NYSE guidelines
- The Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter

## Committee Charter

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter is available on the Company's website at https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/stakeholdersrelationship-committee.pdf.
