# Income Tax

The applicable Indian corporate statutory tax rate was 25.17% for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

Income tax expense included a reversal, net of provisions, of ` 877 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026 and provisions, net of reversals, of ` 132 crore for the year ended March 31, 2025. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily related to the adjudication of disputed matters following tax-return filings and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, Infosys received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, for assessment year 2013-14 and assessment years 2017-18 to 2021-22. The orders confirmed the Company’s position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently:

- Pre-tax interest income of ` 381 crore was recognized.
- Income tax provisions totaling ` 869 crore were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by ` 86 crore following resolution of the disputes.

During the year ended March 31, 2025, Infosys received orders under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, for assessment years 2016-17 and 2019-20. The orders confirmed the Company’s position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently:

- Pre-tax interest income of ` 327 crore was recognized.
- Income tax provisions totaling ` 183 crore were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by ` 1,068 crore following resolution of the disputes.

Foreign tax expense primarily relates to income taxes payable overseas, principally in the United States.

In India, the Group benefited in prior years from tax incentives provided under the Special Economic Zones (SEZs) Act, 2005, for the export of software and services from units registered under the Act. SEZ units that began providing services on or after April 1, 2005 were eligible for:

- A deduction of 100% of profits or gains derived from export services for the first five years from the financial year in which the unit commenced providing services.
- A deduction of 50% of such profits or gains for the following five years.
- A further deduction of up to 50% of such profits or gains for an additional five years, subject to creating a Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve and using the reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Deferred income tax for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially related to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

Infosys is subject to a 15% Branch Profit Tax (BPT) in the United States when the US branch’s net profit for the year exceeds the increase in the branch’s net assets during the year, calculated under the Internal Revenue Code.

As at March 31, 2026, Infosys’ US branch net assets were approximately ` 7,736 crore. The Company recognized a deferred tax liability for BPT of ` 207 crore, net of credits, because it estimated that the branch profits were expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches of ` 19,270 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ` 16,593 crore as at March 31, 2025. The Company can control the timing of reversal of these temporary differences and considers it probable that they will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The Group generally intends to repatriate earnings from subsidiaries and branches only when they can be distributed in a tax-free manner.

Deferred income tax assets were not recognized on accumulated losses of ` 4,868 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ` 4,597 crore as at March 31, 2025, because it was probable that future taxable profit would not be available against which the unused tax losses could be utilized in the foreseeable future.
