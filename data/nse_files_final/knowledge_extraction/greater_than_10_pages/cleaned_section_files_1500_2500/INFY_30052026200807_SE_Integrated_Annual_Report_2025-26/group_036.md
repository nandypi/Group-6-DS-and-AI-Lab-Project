## Board as a trustee

The Board recognizes its primary role as trustee of shareholder capital. It seeks to uphold excellence and integrity in corporate governance standards.

## Corporate governance guidelines

Infosys' corporate governance guidelines establish the framework within which the Board and Management pursue the Company's objectives for the benefit of stakeholders. The guidelines are framed in conjunction with the Company's Memorandum & Articles of Association, Board committee charters, and applicable laws, regulations, and guidelines in India, the US, and other applicable jurisdictions.

## Role of the Board of Directors

The primary role of the Board is trusteeship: protecting and enhancing shareholder value. The Board's responsibilities include:

- Exercising appropriate control to ensure that the Company is managed efficiently and fulfills stakeholder and societal expectations.
- Monitoring the effectiveness of the Company's governance practices and making changes as necessary.
- Providing strategic guidance and monitoring Management.
- Exercising independent judgment on corporate affairs.
- Assigning a sufficient number of non-executive Board members to matters involving potential conflicts of interest.
- Reviewing and guiding corporate strategy, major action plans, risk policy, annual budgets, and business plans.
- Setting performance objectives, monitoring implementation and corporate performance, and overseeing major capital expenditures, acquisitions, and divestments.

## Independent directors

Infosys follows the definitions of an independent director under the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the New York Stock Exchange listed company manual, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, and applicable US securities laws.

Based on disclosures received from all independent directors and the Board's opinion, the independent directors fulfill the applicable conditions and are independent of Management.

## Board composition

The Company recognizes the importance of Board diversity, including diversity of thought, perspective, regional and industry experience, cultural and geographical background, age, ethnicity, race, gender, knowledge, and skills. Relevant expertise includes financial, global business, leadership, technology, mergers and acquisitions, Board service, strategy, sales and marketing, environmental, social and governance (ESG), risk, and cybersecurity expertise.

As of April 23, 2026, the Board comprised ten members:

- One non-executive and non-independent Chairman: Nandan M. Nilekani
- One Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director: Salil Parekh
- Eight independent directors: D. Sundaram, Nitin Paranjpe, Michael Gibbs, Bobby Parikh, Chitra Nayak, Govind Iyer, Helene Auriol Potier, and Diane Enberg Jurgens

Director profiles, including nationality, age, appointment and reappointment dates, Board tenure, term-ending date, shareholding, Board memberships in Indian companies, committee details under Regulation 26 of the Listing Regulations, and core expertise and competencies, are provided in the Infosys Board of Directors section of the Integrated Annual Report.

There are no inter se relationships among Board members. The Company has no pecuniary relationship with non-executive directors and did not provide loans or advances during the year to firms or companies in which directors are interested.

The Board has six committees:

- Audit Committee
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee
- Stakeholders Relationship Committee
- Risk Management Committee
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee
- ESG Committee

All committees comprise only independent directors, with one independent director serving as chairperson. The Company also has a Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee under the Risk Management Committee, comprising only independent directors.

## Board meetings

## Scheduling and selection of agenda items for Board meetings

Tentative dates for Board meetings for the next fiscal year are decided in advance and published in the Integrated Annual Report. The Chairman and Company Secretary, in consultation with the CEO & MD and lead independent director, propose the agenda for each meeting. The agenda and explanatory notes are circulated to directors in advance. Every Board member may suggest additional agenda items.

The Board meets at least once a quarter to review quarterly results and other agenda items. Additional meetings are held when necessary. Independent directors are expected to attend at least four quarterly Board meetings and the Annual General Meeting (AGM). Video and teleconferencing facilities are provided to support participation by directors located in different parts of the world.

Board members are expected to prepare for, attend, and participate in Board and applicable committee meetings, while ensuring that other commitments do not materially interfere with their responsibilities to the Company.

## Meeting of independent directors

The independent directors are required under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, and related rules to hold at least one meeting each financial year without non-independent directors and Management members present. During the year, the independent directors met four times without executive directors and Management.

The meetings covered, among other matters:

- Company performance and risks
- Flow of information to the Board
- Competition and strategy
- Leadership strengths and weaknesses
- Governance and compliance
- Board movements and succession planning
- Human resources matters
- Performance of executive Board members, committees, and the Chairman

## Responsible leadership

The Board and Management work together to establish standards of responsible leadership and corporate governance.

## Responsibilities of the Board leadership

The Board oversees Management's functions and protects the long-term interests of stakeholders. The responsibilities and authority of the Chairman, CEO & MD, and lead independent director are described below.

## Chairman

The roles of Chairman and CEO & MD are separated. The Chairman leads the Board, promotes Board integrity, presides over Board and shareholder meetings, and supports effective communication among directors.

The Chairman oversees the organization, composition, and effectiveness of the Board and its committees, as well as the performance of individual directors. The Chairman works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee on Board and committee composition, director appointments, succession planning, Board evaluation, and feedback to individual directors.

## CEO & MD

The CEO & MD is responsible for executing corporate strategy in consultation with the Board, managing the Company, achieving annual and long-term business targets, and leading and evaluating other executive leaders.

The CEO & MD monitors the internal and external competitive landscape and industry developments and standards; identifies opportunities for expansion and acquisition; builds relationships with customers and markets; and acts as a link between the Board and Management.

## Lead Independent Director

The lead independent director was appointed to provide robust independent leadership of the Board. The general authority and responsibilities of the lead independent director are decided by the independent directors, with additional duties determined by the Board.

The lead independent director provides leadership to the independent directors, liaises on their behalf, and supports the Board's effectiveness in maintaining high-quality governance and effective Board functioning.

## Key Board qualifications, expertise and attributes

The following qualifications, skills, and attributes are considered when nominating candidates to the Board:

| Qualification or attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Financial | Leadership of a financial firm or management of an enterprise's finance function, resulting in proficiency in complex financial management, capital allocation, and financial reporting processes, or experience actively supervising a principal financial officer, principal accounting officer, controller, public accountant, auditor, or person performing similar functions. |
| Diversity | Representation of gender, ethnic, geographic, cultural, or other perspectives that expand the Board's understanding of the needs and viewpoints of customers, partners, employees, governments, and other stakeholders worldwide. |
| Global business | Experience driving business success in markets around the world, with an understanding of diverse business environments, economic conditions, cultures, and regulatory frameworks, and a broad perspective on global market opportunities. |
| Leadership | Extensive leadership experience at a significant enterprise, resulting in a practical understanding of organizations, processes, strategic planning, and risk management; demonstrated strengths in developing talent, planning succession, and driving change and long-term growth. |
| Information Technology | Significant technology background, resulting in knowledge of how to anticipate technological trends, generate disruptive innovation, and extend or create new business models. |
| Mergers and acquisitions | A history of leading growth through acquisitions and other business combinations, with the ability to assess “build or buy” decisions, analyze the fit of a target with the Company's strategy and culture, accurately value transactions, and evaluate operational integration plans. |
| Board service and governance | Service on a public company board that develops insights into maintaining Board and Management accountability, protecting shareholder interests, and observing appropriate governance practices. |
| Sales and marketing | Experience developing strategies to grow sales and market share, build brand awareness and equity, and enhance enterprise reputation. |
| Sustainability and ESG | Experience leading the sustainability and ESG visions of organizations and integrating them into Company strategy. |
| Risk management | Experience identifying and evaluating significant risk exposures to the Company's business strategy and assessing Management's actions to mitigate strategic, legal, compliance, and operational risks. |
| Cybersecurity | Experience assessing and managing cybersecurity-related risks and implementing cybersecurity policies, procedures, and strategies. |

The details of each director's core expertise and competency are provided in the Infosys Board of Directors section of the Integrated Annual Report.
