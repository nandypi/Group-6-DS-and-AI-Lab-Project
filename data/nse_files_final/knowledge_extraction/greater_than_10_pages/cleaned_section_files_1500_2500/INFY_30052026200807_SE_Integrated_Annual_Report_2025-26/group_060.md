## 3. Accessibility of workplaces

Are the premises and offices of Infosys in India accessible to differently abled employees and workers, as required under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016?

Yes. The premises and offices of Infosys in India are accessible to differently abled employees and workers in accordance with the requirements of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## 4. Equal opportunity policy

Does the entity have an equal opportunity policy as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016? If so, provide a web link to the policy.

Yes. Infosys has an equal opportunity policy in accordance with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. The policy is available at [Diversity and Inclusion Policy](https://www.infosys.com/careers/discover/culture/documents/diversity-inclusion-policy.pdf).

## 10. Health and safety management system:

## 10a. Whether an occupational health and safety management system has been implemented by the entity? (Yes / No). If yes, the coverage of such system?

Yes. Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) is an integral part of Infosys’ ESG framework. Its Health, Safety and Environmental Management System (HSEMS), known as “Ozone,” is based on management commitment, compliance with applicable legal requirements, and stakeholder expectations.

Ozone supports structured HSE processes through globally recognized certifications and is implemented across locations based on legal requirements and internal benchmarks. It is included in Infosys’ internal audit program to support consistent governance and performance.

Infosys has achieved ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health and Safety certifications at locations where the system has been in place for more than three years. The certifications cover all India locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, in line with the enterprise HSE strategy. More than 85% of Infosys employees are covered by ISO 45001 certification.

Infosys’ HSE Policy describes its approach to managing significant HSE risks and opportunities. Initiatives cover mental and emotional health, ergonomics, safety, lifestyle diseases, and preventive care. Occupational Health Centers are active across all India campuses.
