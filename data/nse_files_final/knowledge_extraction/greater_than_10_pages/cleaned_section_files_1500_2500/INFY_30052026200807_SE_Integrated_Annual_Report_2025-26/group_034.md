## VIII. Other details

## 1. Quality

The Quality function at Infosys, aligned with the organization’s vision and strategy of “Navigate the Next,” has three strategic imperatives:

- Differentiate Infosys’ services through superior performance and quality.
- Optimize client projects and internal functions for greater efficiency and agility.
- De-risk operations through delivery excellence, compliance, and sustainability.

### AI-first software engineering

To standardize AI adoption across services while adapting to each client’s AI maturity, the Quality team has defined an AI-first software engineering framework that includes AI-augmented and agentic AI engineering.

AI-augmented engineering integrates AI-infused processes with Infosys and third-party AI platforms, tools, and accelerators. Agentic AI engineering supports early AI adopters through:

- Tailorable blueprints.
- Pre-built agents orchestrated with humans in the loop.
- Agent governance.
- Responsible AI guardrails.

Infosys continues to adapt Agile, DevSecOps, Site Reliability Engineering, and its product-led, platform-driven operating model for AI-led organizations. Quality consulting supports clients in adopting AI-infused ways of working and culture, embedding AI engineering practices, and establishing AI governance.

### Automation, audits, and standards

The Quality function has deployed frameworks, process levers, tools, and platforms across service lines to improve productivity, quality, and engineering excellence. Its automation maturity model supports projects in progressing from point tools toward cognitive and autonomous operations using AI and generative AI.

Structured audits and assessment frameworks have expanded coverage across services, centers, and subsidiaries to support operational risk management and long-term sustainability.

Infosys complies with the following international management-system standards and models:

- ISO 9001
- ISO 27001
- ISO 14001
- ISO 45001
- ISO 50001
- ISO 22301
- ISO 20000
- ISO 27701
- AS 9100
- ISO 13485

Infosys has onboarded AI practices from CMMI.AI and was among the first organizations to complete a pilot assessment by the CMMI Institute. Infosys also achieved reasonable assurance on ISO 14068-1, “Climate change management — Transition to net zero — Part 1: Carbon neutrality.” Infosys China and Infosys Brazil received certification for the ISO 27701 Privacy Information Management System standard.

## 2. Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (ICETS)

ICETS supports enterprise AI-led transformation through emerging technologies, structured innovation, experimentation, governance, and scalable execution.

## A structured innovation journey: Next, Near, and Now

ICETS’ innovation framework combines long-term research, near-term experimentation, and enterprise-scale platforms.

## Next — Applied Research Centers (ARC)

Applied Research Centers research, create, and apply emerging technologies, including:

- Advanced AI.
- Cybersecurity.
- Robotics.
- Autonomous machines.
- Quantum computing.
- Next-generation interaction models.

The centers balance medium- and long-term research with near-term client applications. Their focus includes building in-house expertise and intellectual property for Infosys and its clients.

### Advanced AI

Enterprise AI is increasingly focused on how systems reason, act, and operate autonomously. AI agents are being embedded into workflows to:

- Plan and execute multi-step processes.
- Collaborate with humans and other agents.
- Adapt dynamically to changing contexts.

ICETS’ Applied Research Centers address related challenges through:

- AI agent orchestration frameworks.
- Automated model lifecycle transitions from large language models (LLMs) to small language models (SLMs).
- Agent certification and governance models.
- Runtime observability, auditability, and control.

### New interaction model

Generative AI is enabling expressive avatars and digital humans for customer experience, education, and accessibility. Capabilities include:

- Realistic speech, lip-sync, and facial expressions.
- Emotion-aware, personalized, and context-driven engagement.

### Cybersecurity

ICETS’ cybersecurity focus includes:

- AI-driven threat detection and response.
- Privacy-preserving techniques, including federated learning and secure inference.
- Multimodal threat analytics and real-time security operations.

### Autonomous Machines

Autonomous-machine research includes:

- Intelligent systems operating in dynamic, real-world environments.
- Sensor fusion, simulation, and edge intelligence.
- Scalable deployment across logistics, mobility, and infrastructure.

### Humanoids

Humanoid-related capabilities include:

- Human-like robots with advanced perception and dexterity.
- Human-machine collaboration in complex tasks.
- Safety, productivity, and real-world adaptability.

### Quantum

Quantum-related research includes:

- Hybrid quantum-classical computing approaches.
- Optimization and scientific-computing use cases.
- Post-quantum cryptography and enterprise readiness.

## Near — Living Labs

Living Labs support co-creation, rapid prototyping, and real-world validation, allowing clients to contextualize, experiment with, iterate on, and de-risk innovations in controlled environments.

### Infosys Living Labs

Infosys Living Labs are present across all major geographies, and the network continues to expand. The locations have hosted hundreds of client visits and showcase capabilities across:

- Infosys Topaz.
- Infosys Cobalt.
- Infosys Aster.
- Brand partnerships.
- Hyperscaler and other alliances.
- The Infosys Innovation Network of startups.
- Academic collaborations.
- CSR initiatives.
- Infosys service lines and business verticals.

Specialized showcases cover themes such as responsible AI, accessibility, cybersecurity, future of retail, payments, banking, and manufacturing.

Infosys Living Labs also provide environments for innovation teams to work with individual clients on specific challenges and opportunities.

### Client Living Labs

Client Living Labs are innovation-as-a-service engagements addressing client- and industry-specific challenges through emerging technologies, design, innovation-management expertise, and rapid experimentation.

There are 40 active Client Living Lab engagements. The programs include:

- **IncubateNext:** Rapid experimentation through internal experts and external partnerships, applying emerging technologies and design to real-world use cases across domains and geographies.
- **ImagineNext:** Exploration of future possibilities through trend sensing and innovation insights to identify new business opportunities.
- **SenseNext:** Identification of early market and technology signals and translation of those insights into actionable strategies.

## Now — Platforms and Ecosystems: Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys Topaz Fabric provides composable and interoperable AI agents, tools, services, and models, together with reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities for enterprise-scale AI adoption.

The platform supports the optimization, elimination, or automation of manual IT processes and enables enterprises to build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

### Consulting

Consulting combines strategic intelligence, AI-powered insights, and structured frameworks across research, product discovery, large-deal solutions, value management, and process re-engineering. It supports road-map design, initiative prioritization, and business-outcome realization.

### Engineering

Engineering covers:

- Greenfield engineering.
- Application modernization.
- Platform and cloud migration.
- Data and quality engineering.
- Process re-engineering.

AI-driven execution and modern architectures are applied across the build, migrate, and run phases to support scalable and resilient digital systems.

### Operations

Operations combines AI-driven monitoring, automation, and real-time intelligence across:

- IT operations.
- Business operations.
- Integrated BizTech operations.
- Network operations.
- Cybersecurity security operations centers.

Self-healing capabilities and predictive insights are used to improve service reliability, issue resolution, and enterprise performance.

### Driving measurable business impact

ICETS supports enterprise-wide AI adoption, faster experimentation and deployment, trusted governance, cost efficiency, operational predictability, and future-ready capabilities across industries.

### Looking ahead

ICETS’ stated focus is on execution excellence, ecosystem collaboration, responsible innovation, and providing research, experimentation, tools, platforms, and expertise to support the application of emerging technologies in enterprise environments.
