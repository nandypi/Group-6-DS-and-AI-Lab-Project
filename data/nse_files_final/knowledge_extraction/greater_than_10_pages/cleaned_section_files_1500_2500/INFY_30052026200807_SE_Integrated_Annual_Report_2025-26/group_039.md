## Key recommendations of the Committee

Based on discussions with Management and the auditors, and a review of Management representations and the auditors’ report, the Committee recommended that the Board:

- Accept the Company’s quarterly condensed financial statements prepared in accordance with Ind AS under the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable rules, and IFRS issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.
- Accept the audited standalone financial statements of Infosys Limited prepared in accordance with Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026, as a true and fair statement of the Company’s financial status.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in accordance with Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026, as a true and fair statement of the Group’s financial status.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in Indian rupees and U.S. dollars in accordance with IFRS for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
- Accept the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in U.S. dollars in accordance with IFRS for the year ended March 31, 2026, for inclusion in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F to be filed with the SEC.
- Approve the appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as the Company’s internal auditors for the year ending March 31, 2027, to review various Company operations.
- Note that the Committee will issue a letter in line with Recommendation No. 9 of the Blue Ribbon Committee on Audit Committee effectiveness for inclusion in the IFRS financial statements in the Annual Report on Form 20-F.
- Note the Audit Committee’s April 23, 2026 proposal to recommend BSR & Co. LLP as statutory auditors commencing with the audit of the Company’s financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2028, including that year’s quarterly financial statements, in line with mandatory auditor rotation under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013.
- Note the Audit Committee’s approval of the intent to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as the independent registered public accounting firm for U.S. SEC filings, effective fiscal year 2027–28, subject to applicable requirements.

Based on its review and discussions with Management and the independent auditors, the Committee stated that the Company’s financial statements were fairly presented in conformity with Ind AS and IFRS and that there were no significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting. The Board accepted all recommendations made by the Audit Committee.

## Nomination and Remuneration Committee

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, its members were:

4. Nitin Paranjpe

## Objectives of the Committee

The main objectives and responsibilities of the Committee are to:

1. Assist the Board in discharging its responsibilities relating to remuneration of the Company’s directors, Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), and senior management.
2. Evaluate and approve the adequacy of remuneration plans, policies, programs, and succession plans for directors, KMP, and senior management; identify individuals for appointment in accordance with prescribed criteria; and recommend appointments and removals to the Board.
3. Formulate criteria for determining the qualifications, positive attributes, independence, and performance evaluation of directors on the Board.
4. Administer equity-based plans and schemes approved by shareholders.
5. Oversee the Company’s nomination process for KMP and senior management, and identify, screen, and review individuals qualified to serve as directors, KMP, and senior management in line with Board-approved criteria.
6. Recommend the appointment and removal of directors for approval at the annual meeting of shareholders.
7. Evaluate the performance of the Board and review implementation and compliance with the evaluation.
8. Oversee leadership development.
9. Develop and maintain corporate governance policies applicable to the Company.
10. Devise a policy on Board diversity.

## Composition and attendance

The Committee had four members and was 100% independent.

## Committee governance

The Committee fulfills the requirements of:

- The Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter
- Section 178 of the Companies Act, 2013
- Regulation 19 of the Listing Regulations
- Applicable NYSE guidelines

The Committee oversees the processes for recruiting new Board members and recruiting, motivating, and retaining senior management, as well as the Company’s overall approach to human resources management.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.
