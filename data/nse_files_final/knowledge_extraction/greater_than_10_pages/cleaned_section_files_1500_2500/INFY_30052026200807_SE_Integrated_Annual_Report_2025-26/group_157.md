# Item no. 5 - Approval of the proposed amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 ('the Amended 2019 Plan') and grant of stock incentives to the eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries under the Amended 2019 Plan

To consider and, if thought fit, to pass the following resolution as a Special Resolution:

Shareholder approval is sought for amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019, as applicable to eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries, in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021, and other applicable laws, subject to required approvals, permissions, sanctions, conditions, and modifications.

The Board is authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives under the Amended 2019 Plan to:

- Permanent employees of the Company's subsidiaries, whether working in India or outside India.
- Directors of the Company's subsidiaries.
- Other persons who may become eligible under applicable laws.

Promoters, members of the promoter group, independent directors, and directors who directly or through a relative or body corporate hold more than 10% of the Company's outstanding equity shares are excluded.

The Board may grant incentives at such prices, in one or more tranches, and on such terms and conditions as determined under the Amended 2019 Plan.

The proposed amendments and modifications to the existing plan are:

- **A)** Amend Section 13 to extend the period during which awards may be granted to eligible employees by a further seven years from the date of shareholder approval. The Amended 2019 Plan will consequently remain in effect for a further 10 years from that approval date, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.
- **B)** Amend Section 6(c) relating to vesting based on performance parameters.
- **C)** Amend Section 3 to reflect the unutilized share pool available for grant.
- **D)** Make editorial and clarificatory changes and align the plan with the amended SEBI Regulations.

The maximum number of stock incentives granted to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries, in aggregate, under the Amended 2019 Plan will be **3,26,63,903 equity shares**. This represents the unutilized balance from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**.

The exercise price and vesting criteria will be decided by the Board and/or the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, referred to as the Administrator. For secondary acquisition of the Company's equity shares and related administrative matters, administration may also be delegated to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust from time to time in accordance with the Amended 2019 Plan.

In the event of corporate actions, including rights issues, bonus issues, mergers, restructuring, sale of divisions, or similar events, the Board and/or the Administrator may take actions permitted under applicable laws to ensure fair and equitable benefits for eligible employees.

The Board is authorized to:

- Allot equity shares required for the Amended 2019 Plan.
- Take steps to list shares allotted under the plan on the stock exchanges in accordance with applicable laws and stock-exchange requirements.
- Resolve issues, questions, difficulties, or doubts arising in connection with implementing the plan.
- Delegate any or all powers under the resolution to a committee of directors, with authority for further delegation to Company executives or officers.
- Execute documents and take other actions necessary or desirable to give effect to the resolution.

The Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust and its trustees are authorized to take steps required in relation to the Amended 2019 Plan and its implementation.
