The MRE was ₹11,13,024 and ₹10,72,008 in fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2025, respectively. MRE increased by approximately 4% in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025.

## Annexure 4: Independent Auditor's certificate on corporate governance

**REF:IL/2026-27/001**

## CERTIFICATE ON CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

1. This certificate was issued in accordance with the terms of the auditors’ engagement letter reference no. IL/2025-26/15 dated July 10, 2025, and addendum IL/2025-26/73 dated February 6, 2026.
2. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants and statutory auditors of Infosys Limited, examined the Company’s compliance with the corporate governance conditions for the year ended March 31, 2026. The examination covered regulations 17 to 27, clauses (b) to (i) of regulation 46(2), and paragraphs C and D of Schedule V of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Managements' Responsibility

3. Management is responsible for compliance with the corporate governance conditions. This includes designing, implementing, and maintaining internal controls and procedures to ensure compliance with the corporate governance conditions specified in the Listing Regulations.

## Auditor's Responsibility

4. The auditors’ responsibility was limited to examining the procedures adopted and implemented by the Company to ensure compliance with the corporate governance conditions. The examination was neither an audit nor an expression of opinion on the Company’s financial statements.
5. The auditors examined the books of account and other relevant records and documents maintained by the Company to provide reasonable assurance regarding compliance with corporate governance requirements.
6. The examination was conducted in accordance with the ICAI Guidance Note on Certification of Corporate Governance, applicable Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the ICAI Guidance Note on Reports or Certificates for Special Purposes. The examination also required compliance with the ICAI Code of Ethics.
7. The auditors complied with the applicable requirements of Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1, *Quality Control for Firms that Perform Audits and Reviews of Historical Financial Information, and Other Assurance and Related Services Engagements*.
