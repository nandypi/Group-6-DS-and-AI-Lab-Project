## Opinion

Based on an examination of relevant records, information and explanations provided, and representations from Management, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP certified that Infosys Limited complied with the conditions of Corporate Governance stipulated in regulations 17 to 27, clauses (b) to (i) of regulation 46(2), and paragraphs C and D of Schedule V of the Listing Regulations during the year ended March 31, 2026.

This compliance statement does not provide assurance regarding the Company’s future viability or the efficiency or effectiveness with which Management conducted the Company’s affairs.

## INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S CERTIFICATE TO THE MEMBERS OF INFOSYS LIMITED

The certificate was issued by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants, Firm Registration No. 117366W/W-100018, dated April 23, 2026, at Bengaluru.

## Annexure 5 - Secretarial audit report for the financial year ended March 31, 2026 Form No. MR-3

The secretarial audit covered the period from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026. Based on verification of the Company’s books, papers, minute books, forms, returns, and other records, the auditors reported that Infosys had complied with the applicable statutory provisions and had proper Board processes and compliance mechanisms.

## Auditor's responsibility

The secretarial audit was conducted in accordance with the applicable Auditing Standards issued by The Institute of Company Secretaries of India. The audit evaluated compliance with applicable laws and maintenance of records based on test-check verification and information provided by the Company, its officers, agents, and authorized representatives.

The audit covered:

- The Companies Act, 2013, and rules made thereunder.
- The Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, and rules made thereunder.
- The Depositories Act, 1996, and related regulations and bye-laws.
- The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and related rules and regulations concerning Foreign Direct Investment and Overseas Direct Investment. External Commercial Borrowings were not applicable during the audit period.
- The following regulations and guidelines under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992:
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018. Not applicable during the audit period.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities) Regulations, 2021. Not applicable during the audit period.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents) Regulations, 1993, and the 2025 regulations concerning the Companies Act and dealings with clients.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021. Not applicable during the audit period.
  - Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-back of Securities) Regulations, 2018.

The auditors also examined compliance with:

- Secretarial Standards issued by The Institute of Company Secretaries of India.
- Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and amendments thereunder.

On a test-check basis, Infosys generally complied with the following laws applicable specifically to the Company:

- Special Economic Zones Act, 2005, and rules made thereunder.
- Software Technology Parks of India Rules and Regulations.

During the audit period, the Company complied with the provisions of the applicable Acts, Rules, Regulations, Guidelines, and Standards.

## We further report that

The Board of Directors was duly constituted with an appropriate balance of Executive Directors, Non-Executive Directors, and Independent Directors. No changes took place in the composition of the Board during the audit period.

Adequate notice was given for Board meetings, and agendas and detailed agenda notes were generally sent at least seven days in advance. Two meetings were convened at shorter notice with the necessary approvals. A system existed for obtaining additional information and clarifications on agenda items before meetings and for meaningful participation.

All decisions at Board and Committee meetings were carried out unanimously, as recorded in the minutes.

The Company had systems and processes commensurate with its size and operations to monitor and ensure compliance with applicable laws, rules, regulations, and guidelines.

During the audit period:

- Infosys issued and allotted 874,856 equity shares of face value ₹5 each under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan.
- Infosys issued and allotted 1,453,412 equity shares of face value ₹5 each under the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019.
- Infosys bought back 100,000,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each for an aggregate consideration of ₹18,000 crore, at ₹1,800 per share.

The secretarial audit report stated that maintenance of secretarial records and compliance with applicable laws and regulations were the responsibility of Management. The auditors did not verify the correctness or appropriateness of the Company’s financial records and books of account. The audit was conducted on a test basis, and Management representations were obtained where required.

The secretarial audit report was not an assurance of the Company’s future viability or of the efficacy or effectiveness with which Management conducted the Company’s affairs.

## Annexure A

## Annexure 6 - Annual report on CSR activities

Infosys’s CSR Policy focuses on sustainable development of society and the environment. Its stated focus areas include:

- Education and skill development to enable sustained livelihoods.
- Healthcare, including preventive health.
- Environmental sustainability and ecological balance.
- Gender equality and empowerment of women.
- Promotion of national heritage, art, and culture.
- Rural development.
- Disaster relief and rehabilitation.

## CSR activities

Infosys undertakes CSR initiatives directly and through Infosys Foundation, which was established in 1996.

During the year, the Company reported the following activities:

- Continued education-to-employment initiatives focused on skilling underserved youth through strategic partnerships and livelihood training programs.
- Provided scholarships for students pursuing undergraduate studies in National Institutional Ranking Framework-accredited colleges in engineering and other STEM fields, including nursing, pharmacy, biochemistry, and biotechnology.
- Continued the Infosys Springboard digital literacy program, providing access to core digital and life skills.
- Initiated the Universal Cornea Care Mission at L.V. Prasad Eye Institute by optimizing, operationalizing, and validating the model and defining a strategy for replication across India.
- Supported construction of a mother and child healthcare center at Government Hospital, Kunigal.
- Supported tree plantation initiatives intended to promote environmental sustainability, maintain ecological balance, and enhance farmer livelihoods and income.
- Co-implemented the Gear for Gold program to support inclusive and high-performing sports academies through coaching, infrastructure, sports science, governance, and administration.
- Supported the operationalization of an advanced mother and child center at PGIMER Chandigarh by providing medical equipment.
