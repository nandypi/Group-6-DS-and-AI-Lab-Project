## Philosophy

Infosys is committed to a positive and equal-opportunity workplace free of discrimination and harassment. Its employee-experience approach focuses on consistent policies, processes, programs, and systems, with employees at the center.

Strategic initiatives support employee purpose, meaningful contribution through high-performing teams, continuous learning, and career development. Leadership development remains a focus, with the aim of maintaining a diverse internal leadership pipeline.

Infosys has adopted a hybrid work model to balance flexibility with business needs. Technology and automation are used to support workforce productivity, employee engagement, and operational excellence. Recognition and rewards, diversity, equity and inclusion, and workplace safety are also part of the employee framework.

## Resolution hubs

Infosys maintains an open-door policy and provides forums for employees to raise workplace concerns. Its grievance-resolution mechanism is based on natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Investigations provide involved parties with an opportunity to present facts and supporting evidence.

## Corner Stones

The Infosys Human Rights Statement, Global Policy on Anti-harassment / Discrimination, and Code of Conduct & Ethics are the cornerstones of this philosophy.

## Culture

Infosys promotes a speak-up culture in which employees are encouraged to report concerns or suspected violations of the Code, Company policies and procedures, or applicable laws.

## Resolution Hubs at Infosys include

### HEAR (Hearing Employees And Resolving)

- Formal forum for workplace grievances unresolved at the Unit HR or manager levels.
- Multiple layers of grievance redressal, including an appeal mechanism.
- Neutral panels investigate concerns and determine objective outcomes.
- Anonymous concerns are reviewed in collaboration with the Whistleblower Office.

The HEAR Office is an independent employee grievance-resolution hub under the Human Resources Department. It reviews workplace-related grievances that may impede a positive work environment and operates through a decentralized model across businesses, units, geographies, affiliates, and subsidiaries.

The HEAR Office responds through objective reviews mediated by neutral panels or through compliance audits assessing adherence to relevant policies. It also advises departments on policy and process compliance and provides upward feedback to senior management on employee concerns.

## The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI)

The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI) is an enterprise-wide framework for preventing, prohibiting, and redressing sexual harassment in the workplace. It applies a gender-neutral, employee-centric, and globally consistent approach based on procedural fairness, confidentiality, natural justice, and non-retaliation.

### ASHI (Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative )

- The initiative covers prevention, prohibition, and redressal of sexual-harassment concerns.
- The Grievance Redressal Body (GRB) is the custodian of the initiative.
- An Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) framework is used to handle and resolve incidents.
- The scope is gender-neutral and covers sexual harassment in the workplace.
- Internal Committees have been established at development centers under the regulatory jurisdiction of the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act.
- Prevention and early intervention are supported through awareness, education, leadership accountability, and behavioral reinforcement.

The GRB comprises external experts, senior internal leaders, and the Investigative Council. It oversees governance, policy interpretation, and implementation related to preventing sexual harassment.

Employees can report concerns through the ASHI web application, the InfyMe app, or GRB@infosys.com. Cases follow a formal process aligned with statutory requirements and Company policies.

## Behavior Matters

The Behavior Matters campaign promotes workplace behavior that supports respect, collaboration, productivity, employee well-being, and employee engagement.

## Extending the commitment to contract staff

Infosys extends its safe and inclusive workplace commitments to third-party personnel operating on its campuses. Refresher training is delivered in nine Indian languages, and emergency and safety cards with essential contact information are provided to employees and contracted staff.

## Whistleblower Policy

Infosys’ Whistleblower Policy is aligned with the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX), 2002 and the Companies Act, 2013, including requirements for a vigil mechanism at listed companies.

The whistleblower forum receives, retains, and handles complaints concerning potential discrepancies in accounting, internal accounting controls, or auditing. It also supports anonymous reporting by employees. The Office of Integrity and Compliance administers the policy and forum. Workplace policy and process complaints involving employees are reviewed with the Employee Relations Department.

## Human rights

Infosys supports the protection and advancement of human rights in accordance with the United Nations Global Compact, the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and the International Labor Organization’s Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work. The Company is also a signatory to the UN Women’s Empowerment Principles.

The Infosys Code of Conduct and Ethics includes the principle of “Respecting Each Other.” Infosys’ Human Rights Statement provides a framework for treating employees with respect and dignity and for applying common human-rights principles to business practices.

[Further details are available in the Infosys Code of Conduct.](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-Governance/documents/codeofconduct.pdf)

## Recruitment

As of March 31, 2026, the Group employed 3,28,594 employees, including 3,10,887 professionals involved in client service delivery, including trainees.

During fiscal 2026:

- Infosys received 58,84,480 employment applications.
- Infosys interviewed 4,50,901 applicants.
- Infosys extended offers of employment to 87,286 applicants.
- RestartWithInfy hired 947 women into the Infosys ecosystem.

The application, interview, and offer figures exclude subsidiaries.

## Education, training and assessment (ETA)

The Foundation Training Program prepares entry-level hires for corporate and client-service roles. Its curriculum covers over 40 technology streams and includes generative AI and prompt-engineering courses. In fiscal 2026, 10,766 fresh graduates completed training at the Global Education Center campus in Mysuru and were released to delivery units.

The Continuous Education Program provides reskilling and upskilling through online self-learning, instructor-led virtual training, and in-person classroom training. Bridge programs support employees seeking to move into new career fields while remaining within the organization.

In fiscal 2026:

- 81.8% of the workforce received training.
- Average annual employee learning hours were 113 hours.
- 84% of the workforce was AI-aware.

Lex, Infosys’ in-house learning platform, offers more than 40,000 curated courses, including more than 30,000 courses procured from vendor partners. On average, 37,000 employees used Lex on weekdays and approximately 8,000 employees used it on weekends, with average learning time of 39 minutes.

Infosys Wingspan is a configurable talent-transformation platform used by global organizations for talent-transformation programs.

Infosys Springboard offers more than 20,000 courses. Approximately 11 million learners worldwide actively consume its content.
