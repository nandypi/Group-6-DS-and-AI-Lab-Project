## 2.26 Segment reporting

Ind AS 108, Operating Segments, establishes requirements for reporting operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group primarily provides end-to-end business solutions. The Chief Operating Decision Maker (CODM) evaluates performance and allocates resources based on business-segment performance indicators. Segment revenue and expenditure are recorded using the accounting principles described in the Group’s accounting policies.

The Group’s business segments are:

- Financial Services, including Financial Services and Insurance enterprises and the Finacle operating segment.
- Manufacturing.
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics.
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services.
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media.
- Hi-Tech.
- Life Sciences and Healthcare.
- All other segments, including businesses in India, Japan, and China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Financial Services and Finacle are aggregated because they have similar economic characteristics.

Segment revenue and identifiable operating expenses are allocated based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Allocated expenses include costs incurred for services provided by offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated segment effort.

Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocated because the underlying assets are used interchangeably between segments. These expenses are disclosed separately as unallocated expenses and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities are also used interchangeably between segments. Management considers it impracticable to provide meaningful segment-level disclosures for total assets and liabilities because segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business-segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for whom revenue is otherwise recognized. Revenue by geographic location is disclosed in Note 2.18, “Revenue from operations.”
