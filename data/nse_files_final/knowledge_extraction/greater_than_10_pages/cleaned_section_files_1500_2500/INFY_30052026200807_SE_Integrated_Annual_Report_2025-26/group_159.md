## Shareholder and AGM Procedures

### Dividend tax and unclaimed dividends

For FII/FPI shareholders, tax will be deducted under Section 393 of the IT Act 2025 at 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or at the rate provided under the relevant DTAA read with the MLI, whichever is more beneficial, subject to submission of the required documents.

The required dividend tax documents must be uploaded on the shareholder portal by June 11, 2026. Shareholders can check their tax credit in Form 168, formerly Form 26AS, through their income-tax e-filing account.

Dividends not claimed within seven years from transfer to the Company’s Unpaid Dividend Account will be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). Shares for which dividends remain unclaimed for seven consecutive years will also be transferred to the IEPF under Section 124 of the Act and applicable IEPF rules.

### AGM voting

Infosys has provided electronic voting through the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL), in compliance with Section 108 of the Act, Regulation 44 of the LODR Regulations, and the applicable SEBI circular.

Members who cast votes through remote e-voting before the AGM may attend the AGM but cannot vote again on the same resolutions.

Members holding shares in physical or dematerialized form as of June 16, 2026, may vote electronically. The remote e-voting period is:

- **Commencement:** June 18, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. IST
- **End:** June 22, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. IST

Voting rights are proportionate to each member’s share of the Company’s paid-up equity share capital as of June 16, 2026. Persons who are not members on the cut-off date may treat the notice as information only.

Voting will also be available during the AGM for members attending through video conference who have not already voted through remote e-voting and are otherwise eligible to vote.

The Integrated Annual Report for 2025-26, the Notice of the 45th AGM, and e-voting instructions are being sent electronically to members whose email addresses are registered with the Company or their depository participant. The AGM notice and annual report will also be available through the Company’s website, the BSE and NSE websites, and the NSDL e-voting website.

An explanatory statement under Section 102 of the Act for items 4 to 6, together with additional information under Regulation 36 of the LODR Regulations for directors seeking appointment or reappointment, forms part of the AGM notice.

### KYC and investor services

From April 1, 2024, dividends are paid exclusively through electronic mode to physical shareholders who have completed the mandatory KYC requirements.

Physical shareholders must provide the following details for their folios:

- PAN
- Postal address with PIN and mobile number
- Bank account details
- Specimen signature

| Holder type | Investor service or requirement | Form or process |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Register or update PAN, email address, bank details, and other KYC details for securities held in physical mode | Form ISR-1 |
| Physical | Update the signature of the securities holder | Form ISR-2 |
| Physical | Submit a nomination under Rule 19(1) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 | Form SH-13 |
| Physical | Declaration to opt out of nomination | Form ISR-3 |
| Physical | Cancel a nomination or change the nominee | Form SH-14, along with ISR-3 where applicable |
| Physical | Request a duplicate certificate or other service requests for shares, debentures, or bonds held in physical mode | Form ISR-4 |
| Demat | Register an email address and bank account details | Contact the relevant depository participant and follow its process |

Under Section 72 of the Act, physical shareholders may submit nominations using Form SH-13. Demat shareholders may update nominations through their respective depository participants.

### Scrutinizer and voting results

Hemanth, Holla & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries, represented by a member holding Membership No. FCS 6374 and CP No. 6519, has been appointed as scrutinizer for the e-voting process.

The scrutinizer will submit the report to the Chairman or an authorized person within 48 hours of the AGM’s conclusion, after reviewing votes cast during the AGM and through remote e-voting. The voting results and scrutinizer’s report will be communicated to the stock exchanges, NSDL, and the registrar and transfer agent, and displayed on the Company’s website.

Because the AGM will be held through video conference, the notice does not include a route map, proxy form, or attendance slip.
