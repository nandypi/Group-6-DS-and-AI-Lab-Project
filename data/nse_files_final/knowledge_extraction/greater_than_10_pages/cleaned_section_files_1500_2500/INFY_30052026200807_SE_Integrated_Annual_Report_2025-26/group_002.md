## Microsoft

### Microsoft cut root cause analysis turnaround by 40% through Infosys' AI-led mission-critical support

Microsoft's Support for Mission Critical (SfMC) provides high-reliability support for enterprise customers, focusing on minimal downtime and rapid incident resolution.

Microsoft partnered with Infosys to add AI capabilities to the SfMC support architecture. Using the Microsoft AI technology stack, Infosys deployed:

- A Routing and Entitlement Validation Agent to triage and route critical cases to technically aligned engineers.
- The Azure Resiliency Co-relation Engine to accelerate root cause analysis through automated recommendations.
- A Notes Observation and Validation Agent to conduct continuous quality audits across global operations.

Critical incident response times improved by 33%, reducing engagement time from 15 minutes to under 10 minutes. Root cause analysis preparation time decreased by 40%, from five days to one day. More than 90% of critical cases received top boxed ratings, and low case-handling scores were virtually eliminated.

## ABN AMRO

### ABN AMRO collaborates with Infosys to modernize its enterprise content operations

ABN AMRO is a Netherlands-based bank serving around 5 million retail customers and 365,000 business clients. Its enterprise content management ecosystem contains billions of documents, including scanned inputs, emails, and archived records.

Complex document retrieval previously depended on manual processes that could take several minutes per file. Documents also had to be downloaded from the ECM archive, making compliance enforcement more difficult. Increasing regulatory pressure and data volumes limited scalability.

ABN AMRO and Infosys assessed the ECM landscape and conducted a proof of concept using the Infosys Document Intelligence platform. They then implemented a generative AI-powered ECM AI layer using retrieval-augmented generation, integrated with Microsoft Azure AI and data services.

The solution enabled automated classification, contextual information extraction, and faster retrieval of complex documents without downloads. Delivered on a scalable, cloud-native platform with agile, cross-functional teams, it helped ABN AMRO unlock an estimated €64 million in annual efficiency savings, improve employee productivity, reduce manual effort, strengthen compliance, and provide a foundation for expanding intelligent document processing across additional workflows.

## An introduction to the report

Infosys adopted the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) principles for non-financial performance disclosures 18 years ago and became the first IT company to publish sustainability performance in accordance with the GRI G4 comprehensive criteria in 2014.

The Infosys Integrated Annual Report provides quantitative and qualitative disclosures on financial performance, environmental sustainability, social responsibility, stakeholder relationships, strategy, leadership commitment, and organizational culture.

The 2025-26 report was prepared in accordance with:

- The International Integrated Reporting Framework developed by the International Integrated Reporting Council (IIRC).
- The GRI Standards.
- The International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS).
- Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) guidelines for the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR).

The report also maps Infosys' contribution to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through the Infosys ESG report. Financial and statutory data in the statutory sections meet the requirements of the Companies Act, 2013, including applicable rules, and applicable SEBI regulations.

The Auditors' Report for fiscal 2026 from Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants, ICAI Firm Registration Number 117366W/W-100018, contains no qualification, reservation, or adverse remark.

The Secretarial Auditors' Report for fiscal 2026 from Makarand M. Joshi of Makarand M. Joshi & Co., Company Secretaries, contains no qualification, reservation, or adverse remark.

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP provided an Independent Assurance Statement for the BRSR core disclosures. Selected non-financial sustainability disclosures in the report were also assured by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP.

## Our capitals

Infosys describes its capitals as an integrated view of how short-, medium-, and long-term value is created and preserved. The capitals are interdependent and support the organization's strategy, ESG vision, and ambitions.

## Financial Capital

Infosys obtains Financial Capital through funds generated from business operations and financing activities. The company describes its financial position as high-growth, strongly cash-generative, profitable, debt-free, and liquid, with a focus on maintaining a sustainable and profitable financial position.

## Natural Capital

Climate action is a key focus of Infosys' Natural Capital initiatives. Infosys became carbon neutral in 2020, 30 years ahead of the timeline set by the Paris Agreement, and has maintained carbon neutrality for seven consecutive years.

The company is evolving from carbon neutrality toward climate positivity through regenerative practices, nature-based solutions, and green innovation intended to restore ecosystems and capture more carbon than it emits.

## Human Capital

Infosys describes talent development as essential to its continued success. The company has established employee upskilling and reskilling programs, including artificial intelligence training, and remains committed to maintaining an inclusive workplace for its diverse workforce.

## Manufactured Capital

Infosys' Manufactured Capital includes energy-efficient offices, data centers, innovation hubs, digital studios, and technology infrastructure across the globe. The company describes its campuses as including highly rated green buildings and collaborative technology infrastructure designed to provide productive, safe, and healthy workplaces for employees, clients, and contractors.

## Intellectual Capital

Infosys' Intellectual Capital is based on agility, flexibility, and innovation. The company works with experts, partners, academia, and other stakeholders to develop products and services for customers and communities.

Its intellectual-capital initiatives and platforms include iCETS, Living Labs, the Infosys Innovation Network (IIN), and the Infosys Business Incubator. The Infosys Prize and Aarohan Social Innovation Awards provide platforms for innovators and social entrepreneurs.

## Social and Relationship Capital

Infosys' Social and Relationship Capital focuses on stakeholder interests and helping clients transform their businesses in the digital era.

Its social initiatives include social innovation and employability programs, including community skill training. Global CSR efforts address education, healthcare, women's empowerment, science and research, environmental sustainability, and other areas.

## About Infosys Corporate overview

Infosys is a global provider of AI-first business consulting and technology services. More than 325,000 employees support enterprise transformation across 59 countries.

Established in 1981 with capital of US$250, Infosys has more than four decades of experience managing enterprise systems and operations. The company pioneered the Global Delivery Model and became the first Indian IT company to be listed on NASDAQ.

Infosys states that its market capitalization is approximately US$54.8 billion. It describes its services as combining an AI-first value framework, domain expertise, and an AI-native partner ecosystem.

Since refreshing its ESG Vision 2030 in 2025, Infosys states that it has advanced its climate-positive goals, expanded skilling and community initiatives, and strengthened its inclusive workforce. It also describes governance, ethics, and accountability across its value chain.
