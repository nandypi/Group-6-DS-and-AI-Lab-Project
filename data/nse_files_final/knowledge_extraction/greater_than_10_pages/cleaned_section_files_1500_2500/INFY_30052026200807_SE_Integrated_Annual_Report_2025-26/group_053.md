## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report

Infosys states that sustainability and responsible business are embedded in its strategy, operations, decisions, and governance. The company adopted the GRI Framework in 2008 and published its first Business Responsibility Report in fiscal 2013. The BRSR follows the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC) principles and addresses ESG practices and performance under Regulation 34(2)(f) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
