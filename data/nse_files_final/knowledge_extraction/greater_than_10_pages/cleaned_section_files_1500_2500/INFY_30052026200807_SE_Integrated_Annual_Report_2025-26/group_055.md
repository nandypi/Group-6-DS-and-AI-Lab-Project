Details of Infosys's performance on its ESG goals are available in the chapters “Approaching value creation” and “Delivering value” in this Integrated Annual Report and in the ESG Report 2026. The company reports that it is on track to meet the ESG goals and targets outlined in its ESG Vision 2030.
