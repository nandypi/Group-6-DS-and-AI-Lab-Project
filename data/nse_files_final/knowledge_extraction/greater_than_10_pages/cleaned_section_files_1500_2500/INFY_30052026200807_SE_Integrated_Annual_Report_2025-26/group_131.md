For financial assets subject to lifetime expected credit loss (ECL), the Group measures ECL at lifetime amounts. For all other financial assets, ECL is measured at 12-month ECL unless there has been a significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition, in which case lifetime ECL is used.

The Group determines allowances for credit losses using historical loss experience adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions. It considers current and anticipated future economic conditions relating to the industries with which it deals and the countries in which it operates.

The ECL, or reversal of ECL, required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.
