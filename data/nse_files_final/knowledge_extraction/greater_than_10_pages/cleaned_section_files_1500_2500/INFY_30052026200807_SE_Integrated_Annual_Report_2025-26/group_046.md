## Shares transferred to the IEPF

During the financial year, the Company transferred shares to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF) because dividends had remained unclaimed for seven consecutive years:

- 82,811 shares on October 10, 2025
- 6,34,785 shares on January 09, 2026
- 6,124 shares on March 20, 2026

The IEPF processed and settled claims relating to 1,20,193 shares, returning them to the respective shareholders.

As of March 31, 2026, the IEPF held 14,35,117 shares transferred under the applicable Rules. During the year, the Company also transferred ₹3,39,64,248 to the IEPF, representing corporate benefits, including dividends, accrued on shares already transferred.

## Share transfer system

SEBI, effective April 01, 2019, barred physical transfer of shares of listed companies and mandated that transfers be made only through dematerialized form. Investors may continue to hold shares in physical form, and the Company has requested such shareholders to dematerialize their shares.

Shareholders holding dematerialized shares were requested to register their email address, bank account details, and mobile number with their depository participants. Shareholders holding physical shares were requested to provide PAN, nomination, contact details, bank account details, and specimen signature for their corresponding folios.

Pursuant to a SEBI Circular dated July 2, 2025, a special window was provided from July 7, 2025 to January 6, 2026 for re-lodgement of transfer requests submitted before April 1, 2019 and pending because of deficiencies. This facility was extended by a SEBI Circular dated January 30, 2026 until February 4, 2027. Eligible shareholders may submit the required documents within the extended timeline. Securities will be credited only in dematerialized form and will be subject to a one-year lock-in, during which they cannot be transferred, pledged, or encumbered.

## Investor conferences / events held in fiscal 2026

Infosys holds press meetings and investor or analyst calls after each quarterly results announcement. It also holds an Annual General Meeting accessible to shareholders. Details of these events are sent to the stock exchanges and updated on the Company's website.

Infosys also participates in sell-side or broker-arranged investor conferences and roadshows, including one-on-one and group meetings involving Management or Company representatives. Details of this participation are sent to the stock exchanges and updated on the Company's website.

## Investor grievances and investor contacts

The Company has a Board-level Stakeholders Relationship Committee to examine and redress shareholder and investor complaints. The status of complaints is reported to the entire Board, and the Committee meets as often as required to resolve shareholder grievances.

Most investor grievances and postal or electronic communications were addressed within seven days of receipt. Exceptions involved cases constrained by disputes or legal impediments.

Share transfers, dividend payments, and other investor-related activities are processed at the office of the Company's registrar and transfer agent (RTA), KFin Technologies Limited.

## Company shares-related disputes / litigation

Certain civil cases are pending involving rival claims by parties seeking declaration of title and accrued benefits relating to disputed Company shares. Infosys Limited and its RTA, KFin Technologies Limited, have been made pro forma defendants because the disputed shares relate to the Company. The matters are not material in nature.
