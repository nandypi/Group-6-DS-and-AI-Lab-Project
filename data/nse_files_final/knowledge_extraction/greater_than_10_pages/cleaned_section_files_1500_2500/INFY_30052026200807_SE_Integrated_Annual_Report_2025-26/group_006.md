## Our business context

Technology continues to reshape businesses across industries worldwide. AI is increasingly being applied to domain-specific capabilities and industry-focused use cases. Clients are navigating the need to reinvent processes and systems in the AI era, requiring knowledge of emerging technologies, new ways of working, AI ecosystems, business processes, and operational practices.

Infosys supports clients in transforming their businesses through technology, including building digital cores and reimagining business operations to enhance agility, efficiency, and resilience while accelerating growth initiatives. Organizations are also focusing on responsible business practices, including ESG principles, cost-structure reconfiguration, resilience and agility, personalized customer and employee experiences, and new products and services.

Businesses are using AI to enhance and personalize customer interactions, improve marketing and sales efficiency, strengthen code quality, and increase workforce and organizational productivity. The emergence of AI is expected to accelerate workforce transformation.

The traditional services market remains competitive because of rapid change and the entry of specialized technology providers. Infosys identifies its industry expertise, end-to-end service capabilities, scalable digital solutions, established platforms, quality and process excellence, distributed agile global delivery model, experienced leadership team, skilled workforce, and execution track record as differentiating capabilities.

## Infosys services brands

Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Topaz, and Infosys Aster are focused, domain-led offerings that combine Infosys capabilities and expertise across cloud, AI, and marketing.

- Infosys Cobalt consists of services, solutions, and platforms for enterprises to accelerate their cloud journey.
- Infosys Topaz is an AI-first set of services, solutions, and platforms using generative and agentic AI technologies.
- Infosys Aster is an AI-amplified marketing suite focused on brand experiences, marketing efficiency, and business growth.
