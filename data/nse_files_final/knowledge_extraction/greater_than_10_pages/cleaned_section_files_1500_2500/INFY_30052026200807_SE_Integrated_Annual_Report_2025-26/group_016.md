## Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)

In October 2020, the Company launched its ESG Vision 2030. Its focus areas include technology to address climate change, water management and waste management. Social priorities include digital skilling, diversity and inclusion, employee wellness and experience, technology for good, and community development. The Company also identifies corporate governance and stakeholder interests as ESG priorities.

The ESG Committee was constituted on April 14, 2021, to oversee organization-wide ESG initiatives, priorities and practices. The ESG Committee reports to the Board and meets every quarter to review progress against the ESG Vision 2030.

## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)

In accordance with Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Listing Regulations, the BRSR forms part of this Integrated Annual Report. It describes the Company's environmental, social and governance initiatives.

SEBI's circular no. SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122 dated July 12, 2023, updated the BRSR format to include BRSR Core, a subset of the BRSR containing specified key performance indicators under nine ESG attributes. These indicators are subject to mandatory reasonable assurance by an independent assurance provider.

The Company appointed Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP as the assurance provider. The ESG Report for 2025-26 is available at [Infosys ESG Report 2025-26](https://www.infosys.com/sustainability/documents/infosys-esg-report-2025-26.pdf). Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP also assured selected ESG Report indicators designed using GRI standards.

## 8. Other disclosures and affirmations

For the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company affirmed that:

- No proceedings filed by or against the Company were pending under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, before the National Company Law Tribunal or any other court.
- There was no instance of a one-time settlement with any bank or financial institution.
- There were no significant or material orders passed by regulators, courts or tribunals that would affect the Company's going concern status or future operations.
- The Company complied with the provisions of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, and the rules made thereunder, including applicable maternity benefit obligations for eligible employees.

The Company also publishes a comprehensive annual ESG Report based on the GRI Standard 2021.
