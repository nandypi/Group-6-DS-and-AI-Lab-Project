## Annexure 7 - Conservation of energy, research and development, technology absorption, foreign exchange earnings and outgo.

[Particulars pursuant to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014]

Infosys maintained carbon-neutral operations for the seventh consecutive year since fiscal 2020. The Company is pursuing climate-positive initiatives, including systemic interventions, low-carbon innovation, and partnerships intended to support long-term climate resilience and a net-zero future.

## Resource conservation initiatives

Infosys' resource conservation initiatives include energy-efficient buildings, retrofits, automation, operational optimization, water management, wastewater treatment, waste management, renewable energy, and carbon-offset projects.

### Energy

In fiscal 2026, Infosys continued initiatives covering energy-efficient building design, embodied-carbon analysis, operational optimization, and employee engagement. Approximately ₹1.27 crore was spent on infrastructure retrofits to improve operational efficiency.

The ASSURE (Accelerating Sustainable and Super efficient Real Estate) initiative, launched with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements, focuses on decarbonizing India's building sector through lighthouse projects, capacity-building, policy advocacy, and ecosystem collaboration. During fiscal 2026, more than 15 million square feet of built-up area was onboarded as lighthouse projects.

### Renewable energy

Infosys has total installed solar capacity of 62.4 MWp across its campuses, including a 42.5 MWp captive solar plant in Karnataka. The Company also uses power purchase agreements and green tariffs from DISCOMs. In fiscal 2026, renewable sources met 81.8% of electricity requirements in Infosys' India operations.

### Green buildings

All new developments are designed to achieve the highest level of green-building certification. Approximately 84% of Infosys' office space is currently certified to the highest level of green-building standards.

### Smarter spaces

Infosys' next-generation offices use digital connectivity, ergonomic design, intelligent energy systems, and advanced building-management systems to optimize lighting, ventilation, and thermal comfort. These systems support hybrid work models, employee well-being, inclusivity, and operational efficiency.

### Water management

Infosys maintains zero wastewater discharge, is a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate, and is committed to recharging more water than it consumes by 2030. Its water-management strategy includes reducing freshwater demand, increasing wastewater treatment and reuse, and expanding rainwater harvesting across campuses.

Infosys also undertakes lake-rejuvenation initiatives intended to support groundwater recharge, biodiversity, and long-term water security.

### Waste management

Infosys is working toward zero waste to landfill through waste reduction, segregation, recovery, treatment, and management practices. The Company achieved 99% waste diversion from landfill. In fiscal 2026, 11 campuses achieved True Zero Waste Certification from the Green Business Certification Institute.

### Carbon offset

Infosys addresses unavoidable emissions through community-based carbon-offset projects. Clean-cooking projects implemented in five states across India have supported health, livelihoods, and income generation for rural women.

The Company has also initiated large-scale carbon-sequestration projects through agroforestry, benefiting small and marginal farmers through improved livelihoods, soil health, food security, and climate resilience.

### Carbon neutral events

Infosys measures, reduces where feasible, and offsets emissions associated with Company-hosted events through certified carbon-offset projects. In fiscal 2026, the Company organized eight carbon-neutral events.

## Health, Safety and Environment

Infosys' Health, Safety, and Environmental Management System (HSEMS) covers employees, contractors, and visitors. Infosys is certified to ISO 14001:2015 for Environmental Management and ISO 45001:2018 for Occupational Health and Safety. The HSEMS is based on compliance with applicable laws across the regions where Infosys operates.

## Internal IT - AI-first software engineering

Infosys' internal IT organization supports and manages 200 enterprise applications. Its AI-first transformation strategy has two areas of focus:

1. **AI for Engineering Excellence and Lifecycle Compression:** Infosys has embedded AI across requirements, design, development, testing, deployment, and support. A digital twin of the enterprise landscape captures contextual knowledge across applications, platforms, and layers. AI-powered agents use this digital twin to improve developer productivity and decision-making and compress the software-delivery lifecycle.

2. **AI for Business & Operations:** AI agents and generative AI solutions are used to improve the efficiency of functions such as Finance and HR. The agents address queries across applications, provide contextual assistance, autonomously respond to helpdesk emails, improve first-contact resolution, and reduce service-desk workload.

### Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys has deployed the Topaz Fabric platform across the organization to support experimentation, innovation, and the development and sharing of generative AI and agentic solutions.

### Education, Training & Assessment (ETA)

ETA has developed an agentic AI-powered learning ecosystem anchored on Lex, Infosys' internal learning platform, and extended through Infosys Springboard, its free public learning platform. Zoiee, Knowledge Assistant, SynthAIz, and related AI agents support personalized learning, intelligent recommendations, real-time assistance, and continuous guidance.

SynthAIz and AI agents also support the creation of multimodal learning content through automated transcription, translation, and video localization.

### AI cloud

Infosys is investing in proprietary, on-premise GPU infrastructure for advanced model training and inferencing. The infrastructure supports the integration of open-source large language models and deployment of specialized small language models while maintaining data sovereignty, data confidentiality, and control over operational costs.
