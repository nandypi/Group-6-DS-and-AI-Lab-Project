## Dividend

The final dividend on shares is recorded as a liability when approved by shareholders. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability when declared by the Company's Board of Directors. Income tax consequences of dividends on equity-classified financial instruments are recognized according to where the entity originally recognized the transactions or events that generated distributable profits.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Remittances of dividends outside India are governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and are subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards at the grant date. The fair value is recognized as an expense in the Statement of Profit and Loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, with a corresponding increase to the share options outstanding account.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 ("the 2019 Plan")

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the AGM, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan is 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. These parameters combine relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company's operating performance metrics. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. The instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan ("the 2015 Plan")

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan is 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust for the 2011 Plan as at March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were subsequently adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue. The instruments generally vest over four years.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 86,50,911 shares as at March 31, 2026 and 96,55,927 shares as at March 31, 2025 under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 2,00,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities at each respective year-end.
