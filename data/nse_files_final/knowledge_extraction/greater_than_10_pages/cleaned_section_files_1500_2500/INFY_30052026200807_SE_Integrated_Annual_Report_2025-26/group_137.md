## Notes on grants to KMP

### CEO & MD

#### Under the 2015 Plan

The Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following grants for fiscal 2026 on April 17, 2025. The grants were made effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs, representing the annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, representing the annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs will vest based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, representing the annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs will vest based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant period, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employee agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual grant of RSUs with a fair value of ₹3 crore. These RSUs vest over three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the respective grant date. Accordingly, 18,132 annual time-based RSUs were granted effective February 1, 2026 for fiscal 2026.

Although the annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of March 31, 2026, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense under Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*, because the service commencement date precedes the grant date. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.

#### Under the 2019 Plan

On April 17, 2025, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

### Other KMP

#### Under the 2015 Plan

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants to other KMP under the 2015 Plan:

- 237,370 time-based ESOPs. These ESOPs vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price is the market price on the grant date.
- 82,400 time-based RSUs. These RSUs vest over four years.

#### Under the 2019 Plan

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Board approved performance-based grants of 60,600 RSUs to other KMP under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs vest over three years based on achievement of specified performance targets.
