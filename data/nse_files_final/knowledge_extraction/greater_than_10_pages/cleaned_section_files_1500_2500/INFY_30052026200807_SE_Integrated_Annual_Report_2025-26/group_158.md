## Item 6: Approval of the request for re-classification of certain members of the 'Promoter and Promoter Group' of Company to 'Public' category

To consider and, if thought fit, to pass the following resolution as an ordinary resolution:

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to Regulation 31A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the approval of the Board of Directors at its meeting held on April 30, 2026, and the no-objection letters received from BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited on May 11, 2026, and subject to such other approvals as may be necessary, the consent of the members of the Company be and is hereby accorded for the re-classification of the following persons from the ‘Promoter and Promoter Group’ category to the ‘Public’ category:

| Sl. No. | Name of the entity | Category |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shreyas Shibulal | Promoter |
| 2 | Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal | Promoter Group |

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** any Director and the Company Secretary of the Company be and are hereby severally authorized to perform all acts, deeds, matters and things required to give effect to the resolution, including filing documents with the stock exchanges and other relevant authorities, obtaining necessary approvals, and executing related documents and applications.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** a certified copy of the resolution may be provided to the concerned persons or authorities as necessary.

## Notes

1. The AGM will be held through video conferencing in accordance with the applicable Ministry of Corporate Affairs and SEBI circulars. Participation through video conferencing will be counted for quorum purposes under Section 103 of the Companies Act.

2. As the AGM is being held through video conferencing, members will not have the facility to appoint proxies.

3. Corporate members authorizing representatives to participate and vote must provide a certified copy of the board resolution or authorization letter to the scrutinizer.

4. The registers of directors and key managerial personnel and their shareholding, and contracts or arrangements in which directors are interested, will be available electronically for inspection during the AGM. Other documents referred to in the notice will be available electronically from the date of circulation of the notice until the AGM on June 23, 2026.

5. Dividend payments are required to be made through the Electronic Clearing System. Members holding shares in dematerialized form should update address and bank account details with their depository participants. Members holding shares in physical form should update their details with the Company’s Registrar and Transfer Agent.

6. The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026. Subject to shareholder approval at the AGM, the dividend will be paid on June 25, 2026.

7. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, dividend is taxable in the hands of members. The Company will deduct tax at source when paying the final dividend. Members must submit the required documents to enable determination of the applicable withholding tax rate.

## Tax withholding for resident shareholders

| Shareholder category | Tax deduction at source |
|---|---|
| Members having a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) | 10% or the rate notified by the Government of India |
| Members not having PAN or having an invalid PAN | 20% or the rate notified by the Government of India |

Under Section 262 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, a person allotted a PAN who is eligible to obtain Aadhaar must link PAN with Aadhaar. If this is not done, the PAN may be deemed invalid or inoperative and tax may be deducted at the higher rate under Section 397, namely 20%.

No tax will be deducted from dividends payable to resident individual shareholders if their total dividend during tax year 2026–27 does not exceed ₹10,000, or where members provide Form 121, subject to the conditions specified in the Income-tax Act, 2025. Resident shareholders may also submit other prescribed documents to claim lower or nil withholding. PAN is mandatory for members submitting Form 121 or other applicable documents.

## Tax withholding for non-resident shareholders

Tax will be withheld from dividends payable to non-resident shareholders under Section 393 and other applicable provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025, at the rates in force. The withholding rate is 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or the rate notified by the Government of India.

Under Section 159 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, non-resident shareholders may opt for the provisions of the applicable Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), read with the Multilateral Instrument, if any, between India and the shareholder’s country of tax residence, where those provisions are more beneficial.

To claim DTAA benefits, non-resident shareholders must provide:

- A copy of the PAN card allotted by the Indian Income Tax authorities, duly attested by the shareholder or authorized signatory. If PAN is unavailable, the information required under sub-rule 2 of Rule 217 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026.
- A duly attested Tax Residency Certificate for tax year 2026–27 obtained from the revenue or tax authorities of the country of tax residence.
- Form 41 for claiming tax treaty benefits.
- A self-declaration confirming that the shareholder has no permanent establishment in India, in accordance with the applicable tax treaty and the Income-tax Act, 2025.
- A self-declaration of beneficial ownership of the equity shares.
- A self-declaration confirming fulfillment of all applicable tax treaty conditions for claiming benefits under the treaty read with the Multilateral Instrument.
- Any other documents prescribed under the Income-tax Act, 2025, or a duly attested certificate for lower withholding of tax.
