## 4. Management's Responsibility

The Company's management is responsible for selecting or establishing suitable criteria for preparing the Sustainability Information, including the reporting boundary of the BRSR, applicable laws and regulations, identification of key aspects, stakeholder engagement, content, preparation, and presentation of the Identified Sustainability Information.

This responsibility includes the design, implementation, and maintenance of internal controls relevant to preparing the BRSR and measuring Identified Sustainability Information free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.

## 5. Inherent limitations

The absence of a significant body of established practice for evaluating and measuring non-financial information allows for different but acceptable measurement techniques, which can affect comparability between companies.

## 6. Our Independence and Quality Control

The assurance provider maintained its independence and confirmed compliance with the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), the SEBI Master Circular, and related clarifications. The engagement was conducted by personnel with the required competencies and experience.

Quality control was maintained in accordance with Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1, including documented policies and procedures regarding ethical requirements, professional standards, and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## 7. Our Responsibility

The assurance provider's responsibility was to express a reasonable assurance opinion on the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I, based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained.

The engagement was conducted in accordance with the Standard on Sustainability Assurance Engagements (SSAE) 3000, *Assurance Engagements on Sustainability Information*, and Standard on Assurance Engagements (SAE) 3410, *Assurance Engagements on Greenhouse Gas Statements*, issued by the Sustainability Reporting Standards Board of the ICAI.

The standards required planning and performance of the engagement to obtain reasonable assurance that the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I and included in the BRSR was prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable criteria. The engagement included professional judgment and professional skepticism, as well as identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error.

Procedures included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Identified Sustainability Information and related disclosures.
- Obtaining an understanding of the assessment criteria and their suitability for evaluating and measuring the Identified Sustainability Information.
- Making inquiries of management, including the environment, compliance, and human resources teams and personnel responsible for preparing the report.
- Evaluating the design and implementation of key systems, processes, and controls for recording, processing, and reporting Identified Sustainability Information at the corporate office and selected other locations and offices.
- Determining the nature, timing, and extent of further procedures based on the assessed risks of material misstatement.
- Testing key assumptions, emission factors, and methodologies used to calculate greenhouse gas emissions.
- Testing the process for collating sustainability information by agreeing or reconciling the Identified Sustainability Information with underlying records on a sample basis.
- Testing consolidation for selected locations and offices and the corporate office within the reporting boundary to assess the completeness of reported data.

The assurance provider concluded that the evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the reasonable assurance opinion.

## 8. Exclusions

The assurance scope excluded the following, and no opinion was expressed on them:

- Operations of the Company outside the Reporting Boundary set out in Question 13 of Section A: General Disclosures of the BRSR, for the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I.
- Aspects of the Report and qualitative or quantitative data other than the Identified Sustainability Information.
- Data and information outside the defined reporting period, being the financial year ended March 31, 2026.
- Statements describing opinions, beliefs, aspirations, expectations, aims, or future intentions provided by the Company.

## 9. Other information

The Company's management is responsible for other information included in the BRSR, the Integrated Annual Report, and the Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) Report, other than the Identified Sustainability Information and the independent assurance reports dated May 27, 2026.

The opinion on the Identified Sustainability Information does not cover the other information. The assurance provider's responsibility was to read the other information and consider whether it was materially inconsistent with the Identified Sustainability Information or otherwise materially misstated.

The assurance provider reported that it had nothing to report regarding material misstatement of the other information.

## 10. Reasonable Assurance Opinion

Based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained, the BRSR Core indicators for the year ended March 31, 2026, listed in Appendix I and presented in the BRSR, were prepared in all material respects in accordance with the criteria stated in paragraph 3.

## 11. Restriction on use

The Reasonable Assurance report was prepared for the Board of Directors of the Company solely to assist the Company in reporting on its sustainability performance and activities. The report was not intended for use by any other person or for any other purpose without prior written consent.
