## Instructions for participation through VC

The 45th AGM will be convened through video conference in compliance with applicable provisions of the Act and related circulars. The video-conference facility will open 60 minutes before the scheduled start time and will be available on a first-come-first-served basis.

Members may participate using desktops, smartphones, or laptops. A desktop or laptop with a stable, high-speed internet connection is recommended. Participants using mobile devices, tablets, or mobile hotspots may experience audio or video loss due to network fluctuations.

Members may post questions in the “Ask a Question” section after the AGM proceedings commence and may also be enabled to ask questions through video conference. The Company may limit the number of members asking questions depending on the time available.

Members who have not cast their vote through remote e-voting and are otherwise eligible may vote through the e-voting system during the AGM.

Institutional and corporate shareholders are required to upload a Board Resolution or Authorization Letter authorizing their representatives to attend the AGM through video conference.

## Instructions for e-voting

The NSDL e-voting process consists of two steps.

### Step 1: Access to the NSDL e-voting system

#### (A) Login method for e-voting for individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode

Under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/242 dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and depository participants. Shareholders were advised to update their mobile number and email ID in their demat accounts to access the e-voting facility.

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode with NSDL could access e-voting through NSDL’s IDeAS services, OTP-based login, the NSDL e-voting website, or the NSDL mobile application.

Users of CDSL Easi/Easiest could access e-voting through their existing credentials. Shareholders not registered for Easi/Easiest could register through CDSL or access the e-voting page using their demat account number and PAN, with authentication through an OTP sent to their registered mobile number and email address.

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode could also log in through the credentials of their demat account with their Depository Participant registered with NSDL or CDSL. After authentication, they could select the Company or the relevant e-voting service provider to cast their vote.

Members unable to retrieve their User ID or Password were advised to use the “Forgot User ID” and “Forgot Password” options available on the relevant e-voting website.
