## Information Other than the Financial Statements and Auditor's Report Thereon

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible for the other information included in the Management Discussion and Analysis, Board's Report and annexures, Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, Corporate Governance, and Shareholder's Information. This other information does not include the consolidated or standalone financial statements or the auditor's report.

The auditor's opinion on the Consolidated Financial Statements does not cover the other information. As part of the audit, the auditor read the other information and considered whether it was materially inconsistent with the Consolidated Financial Statements, audit knowledge, or otherwise materially misstated. The auditor had nothing to report.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Consolidated Financial Statements

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible under Section 134(5) of the Act for the preparation and presentation of Consolidated Financial Statements that give a true and fair view of the Group's consolidated financial position, financial performance including other comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows, in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in India, including Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.

The respective Boards of Directors or Trustees of entities included in the Group are responsible for:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records.
- Safeguarding Group assets and preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining effective internal financial controls to ensure the accuracy and completeness of accounting records and the preparation of financial statements free from material misstatement.
- Assessing the ability of the respective entities to continue as a going concern, making related disclosures where applicable, and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless liquidation or cessation of operations is intended or unavoidable.
- Overseeing the Group's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Consolidated Financial Statements

The auditor's objectives are to obtain reasonable assurance that the Consolidated Financial Statements are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing an opinion. Reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but does not guarantee that every material misstatement will be detected.

The audit included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement and performing responsive procedures.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal financial controls. Under Section 143(3)(i) of the Act, the auditor also evaluates the adequacy and operating effectiveness of internal financial controls with reference to the Consolidated Financial Statements for the Company and its Indian subsidiary companies.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies, the reasonableness of accounting estimates, and related disclosures.
- Assessing management's use of the going-concern basis and whether any material uncertainty exists that could cast significant doubt on the Group's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and fair presentation of the Consolidated Financial Statements.
- Obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding entities within the Group.

The auditor considered quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating results, and assessing identified misstatements. The auditor communicated with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant findings, internal financial control deficiencies, independence, and other relevant matters. Matters of most significance in the current-period audit were determined to be key audit matters, subject to legal and regulatory disclosure restrictions.

## Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements

As required by Section 143(3) of the Act, the auditor reported that:

- The information and explanations necessary for the audit were obtained.
- Proper books of account and relevant records required by law were maintained by the Group.
- The Consolidated Balance Sheet, Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss including Other Comprehensive Income, Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity, and Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows agree with the relevant books of account.
- The Consolidated Financial Statements comply with the Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.
- Based on directors' written representations as of March 31, 2026, and reports from statutory auditors of Indian subsidiary companies, none of the directors of Group companies incorporated in India was disqualified as of March 31, 2026 under Section 164(2) of the Act.
- The auditor's separate report in Annexure A expresses an unmodified opinion on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of internal financial controls with reference to the Consolidated Financial Statements of the Company and its Indian subsidiary companies.
- Director remuneration paid by the Company during the year complies with Section 197 of the Act.

With respect to Rule 11 of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014, as amended, the auditor reported that:

- The Consolidated Financial Statements disclose the impact of pending litigation on the Group's consolidated financial position. Refer to Note 2.24 to the Consolidated Financial Statements.
- The Group made provisions required under applicable law or accounting standards for material foreseeable losses. The Group did not have any long-term derivative contracts. Refer to Note 2.16 to the Consolidated Financial Statements.
- There was no delay in transferring amounts required to be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund by the Company and its Indian subsidiary companies.
- Management of the Company and its Indian subsidiaries represented that no funds had been advanced, loaned, or invested from borrowed funds, share premium, or other sources to any person or entity outside the Group, including foreign entities, with the understanding that the intermediary would lend or invest in identified ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees or security on their behalf.
- Management of the Company and its Indian subsidiaries represented that no funds had been received from any person or entity, including foreign entities, with the understanding that the Company or its subsidiaries would lend or invest in identified ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees or security on their behalf.
- Based on the audit procedures performed, nothing came to the auditor's notice indicating that these Rule 11(e) representations contained any material misstatement.

As stated in Note 2.12 to the Consolidated Financial Statements.
