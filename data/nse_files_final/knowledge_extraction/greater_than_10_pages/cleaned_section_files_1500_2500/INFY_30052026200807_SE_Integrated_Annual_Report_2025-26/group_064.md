## 5. Describe the internal mechanisms in place to redress grievances related to human rights issues.

Infosys provides employees and contract staff access to Resolution Hubs, a grievance resolution mechanism for workplace matters, including human rights concerns.

Resolution Hubs follow the principles of natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. The investigation process gives all parties an opportunity to present facts and material evidence, with timely action and closure.

## 8. Mechanism to prevent adverse consequences to the complainant in discrimination and harassment cases

Infosys’ non-retaliation policy protects individuals from retaliation for making good-faith reports of integrity concerns, discrimination, or harassment. The protection also extends to anyone who assists with, cooperates in, or reports an integrity-related investigation or concern.
