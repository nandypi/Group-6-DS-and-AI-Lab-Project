10. If the Letter of Offer is not received, an Eligible Shareholder holding Equity Shares may participate by submitting a signed plain-paper application stating:

- Number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date.
- Client ID number.
- DP name and ID.
- Beneficiary account number.
- Number of Equity Shares tendered for the Buyback.

11. Eligible Shareholders may tender Equity Shares up to their Buyback Entitlement, in whole or in part, or tender more than their Buyback Entitlement, provided that the total tendered does not exceed their holding on the Record Date.

13. All documents sent by Eligible Shareholders are sent at their own risk.

14. By participating in the Buyback, each Eligible Shareholder, including a Non-Resident Shareholder, undertakes to complete all applicable regulatory and statutory filings and compliances. # TENDER FORM FOR ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS

# FORM OF ACCEPTANCE-CUM-ACKNOWLEDGEMENT (FOR ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS HOLDING SHARES IN PHYSICAL FORM)

The Letter of Offer concerns the buyback by Infosys Limited of up to 10,00,00,000 (Ten Crore) fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 (Rupees Five only) each, at ₹1,800 (Rupees One Thousand Eight Hundred only) per Equity Share, payable in cash, for an aggregate amount not exceeding ₹18,000 Crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only).

1. The shareholder confirms that they have read and understood the Letter of Offer dated [●] and tenders the Equity Shares on the terms and conditions set out in the Letter of Offer and Tender Form.

2. The shareholder authorizes the Company to buy back the offered Equity Shares and instruct the Registrar to the Buyback to extinguish those shares.

3. The shareholder confirms that the tendered Equity Shares are free from all liens, equitable interests, charges, and encumbrances.

4. The shareholder declares that no restraint, injunction, or other order restricts the right to tender the Equity Shares and that the shareholder is legally entitled to tender them.

5. The Company is not obliged to accept Equity Shares where the loss of share certificates has been notified to the Company.

6. The Company will pay the Buyback Offer Price only after applicable Income Tax deductions and verification of the validity of the documents. Consideration will be paid to the first-named Eligible Shareholder.

7. The shareholder undertakes to return any Buyback consideration wrongfully received.

8. The shareholder authorizes the Company to return the share certificates if none of the Equity Shares are accepted.

9. If only part of the physical shares is accepted, the shareholder authorizes the Company to issue a Letter of Confirmation (LOC) for the unaccepted Equity Shares.

10. The shareholder acknowledges responsibility for tax due on gains arising from the Buyback and agrees to compute and pay applicable taxes in India and file the relevant tax return in consultation with custodians, authorized dealers, or tax advisors.

11. The shareholder undertakes to indemnify the Company against any tax demand raised on the Company on account of gains arising to the shareholder from the Buyback. The shareholder also undertakes to provide details relating to the taxability or non-taxability of the Buyback proceeds, copies of tax returns filed in India, evidence of tax paid, and other relevant information when requested.

12. Applicable to all non-resident shareholders: The shareholder undertakes to execute further documents and provide assurances required to give effect to the tender or offer. The shareholder agrees to comply with decisions taken by the Company to effect the Buyback under the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Buy-back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, and applicable foreign exchange regulations. The form is deemed to be the shareholder’s consent letter for filings under the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and rules made thereunder.

## 16. Details of other documents enclosed

The following documents may be enclosed, where applicable:

- Power of Attorney.
- Corporate authorisation.
- Self-attested copy of the Permanent Account Number (PAN) card.
- Previous RBI approvals for acquiring the Equity Shares tendered in the Buyback.
- Other documents as specified by the shareholder.

## 17. Equity Shareholders Details

The form requires the following shareholder information:

- Full name(s) of the Equity Shareholder.
- Signature(s).
- PAN number.
- Address of the sole or first Equity Shareholder.
- Telephone number or email ID.

A corporate shareholder must affix its rubber stamp and sign under valid authority. The relevant corporate authorisation must be enclosed with the application form.

## 18. Tender Form conditions

This Tender Form must be read along with the Letter of Offer and is subject to the terms and conditions stated in the Letter of Offer and this Tender Form.
