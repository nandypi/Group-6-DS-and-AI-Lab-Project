## ANNEXURE I

## SUMMARY TERM SHEET

This summary term sheet highlights material information about the Letter of Offer. It does not describe all details of the offer. The Company proposes to buy back 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares of face value ₹5 each at ₹1,800 per Equity Share under the Tender Offer route, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations and applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013.

### Who is offering to purchase the Equity Shares?

The Company is offering to purchase 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares of face value ₹5 each. See Sections 6 and 9.

### What will the purchase price for Equity Shares be and what will be the form of payment?

The Company will pay ₹1,800 per Equity Share purchased in the Buyback.

If the Equity Shares tendered by an Eligible Shareholder are accepted, the Company will pay the purchase price in cash promptly after the expiration of the Offer and no later than 5 (five) working days from closure of the Offer Period. See Sections 1 and 23.

### What is the recent market price of the Equity Shares?

On [●], 2025, the date of the Public Announcement, the last reported sale price of the Equity Shares was [●] and [●] per Equity Share on the BSE and NSE, respectively.

The closing market price as of September 8, 2025, the date of intimation of the Board Meeting for considering the Buyback to the Indian stock exchanges, was ₹1,432.65 on the BSE and ₹1,432.90 on the NSE. See Sections 6 and 12.

### Has the Company or its Board of Directors adopted a position on the Buyback?

The Board of Directors approved the Buyback. Neither the Company, its Board of Directors, nor the Managers to the Buyback make any recommendation to shareholders regarding whether to tender or refrain from tendering their Equity Shares.

### How many Equity Shares will the Company purchase in the Buyback?

The Company will purchase up to 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares, representing 2.41% of its total paid-up equity share capital, from Eligible Shareholders on a proportionate basis, subject to the reservation for Small Shareholders.

If more than 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares are tendered, the Company will first purchase shares tendered by Small Shareholders on a proportionate basis in the Reserved Category, followed by shares tendered by other Shareholders on a proportionate basis in the General Category. Any additional Equity Shares will be accepted proportionately as described in Section 23.

### What happens if more than 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares are tendered?

The Company will not accept more than 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares. Shares will be accepted first from Small Shareholders in the Reserved Category and then from other Shareholders in the General Category, in each case on a proportionate basis as described in Section 23.

### How will the Company pay for the Equity Shares?

Assuming that 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares are tendered and purchased at ₹1,800 per Equity Share, the aggregate consideration will be ₹18,000 crore.

The Company anticipates funding the Buyback from cash on hand generated through internal accruals and/or by liquidating financial instruments held by the Company.

### When can an Eligible Shareholder tender his Equity Shares?

Eligible Shareholders may tender their Equity Shares during the Tendering Period, from [●] to [●].

### How long does an Eligible Shareholder have to tender his Equity Shares; can the Offer be extended, amended or terminated?

Eligible Shareholders may tender their Equity Shares during the Tendering Period, from [●] to [●]. The Offer Period cannot be extended, amended, or terminated.

### How will an Eligible Shareholder be notified if the Company extends the Offer Period or amends the terms of the Buyback?

The Offer Period cannot be extended by the Company, and the Company will not amend the terms of the Buyback after the date of the Letter of Offer.
