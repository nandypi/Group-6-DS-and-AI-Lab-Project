## 23.26 Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form:

- **23.26.1** Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares and intending to participate in the Buyback must approach their stock broker with the following documents for verification:

  - Original Equity Share certificate(s).
  - Valid share transfer form(s), Form SH-4, duly completed and signed by all registered Eligible Shareholders in the same order as recorded with the Company and with signatures witnessed where required.
  - Self-attested copy of the Eligible Shareholder’s PAN card.
  - Tender Form, signed by all joint holders in the same order in which they hold the Equity Shares.
  - Other applicable documents, including an attested power of attorney, corporate authorization, board resolution, specimen signature, or notarized death certificate and succession certificate or probated will.
  - If the registered address has changed, self-attested address proof consisting of a valid Aadhaar card, voter identity card, or passport.

- **23.26.2** Based on these documents, the stock broker will place the bid through the BSE/NSE Acquisition Window. After placing the bid, the stock broker will provide the Eligible Shareholder with a transaction registration slip (TRS) generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS will contain details such as the folio number, Equity Share certificate number, distinctive number, and number of Equity Shares tendered.

- **23.26.3** The stock broker or Eligible Shareholder placing the bid must deliver the original Equity Share certificate(s), the documents mentioned in Paragraph 23.11.2, and the TRS to the Registrar to the Buyback by registered post, courier, or hand delivery within two days of bidding by the stock broker. If the bid is placed during the last two days of the tendering period, the documents must reach the Registrar on or before the Buyback Window Closing Date.

  The envelope should be superscribed **“Infosys Limited - Buyback 2025”**. The Registrar will retain one copy of the TRS and provide an acknowledgement to the stock broker or Eligible Shareholder.

- **23.26.4** Physical Equity Shares will not be accepted unless the complete set of documents is submitted. Acceptance is subject to verification under the Buyback Regulations and any further applicable directions. The Registrar will verify bids daily. Until confirmation, BSE/NSE will display them as **“unconfirmed physical bids.”** After confirmation by the Registrar, they will be treated as **“Confirmed Bids.”**

- **23.26.5** Shareholders who have submitted physical Equity Shares for dematerialization should ensure that the dematerialization process is completed in time to participate before the Buyback Window Closing Date.

- **23.26.6** If the Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis, a Non-Resident Shareholder must enclose a letter from the authorized dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval. If the certificate is unavailable, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis, and the shareholder must submit a consent letter allowing the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

- **23.26.7** If applicable documents are not enclosed with the Tender Form, the Equity Shares tendered by Non-Resident Shareholders are liable to be rejected.

## For Equity Shares held by Eligible Shareholders, being Non-Resident Shareholders of Equity Shares (Read with Section 20 'DETAILS OF THE STATUTORY APPROVALS')

- **i.** Non-Resident Shareholders, excluding FPIs/FIIs, must enclose a copy of any applicable permission received from the RBI to acquire the Equity Shares.

- **ii.** For Equity Shares held on a repatriation basis, the Non-Resident Eligible Shareholder must enclose a letter from the authorized dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account, such as an NRE account, as specified by the RBI in its approval. If the certificate is unavailable, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis, and the shareholder must submit a consent letter allowing the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

- **iii.** Eligible Shareholders who are FIIs or FPIs must enclose a copy of their SEBI registration certificate.

If applicable documents are not enclosed with the Tender Form, the Equity Shares tendered under the Buyback are liable to be rejected.

The decision to participate in the Buyback rests with each Eligible Shareholder. The Company will not be responsible for losses of share certificates or other documents during transit.

## 23.27 Acceptance of orders

The Registrar to the Buyback will provide order-acceptance details to the Clearing Corporation within the specified timelines.

## 23.28 Method of Settlement

- **23.28.1** Trades will be settled in a manner similar to secondary-market trade settlement.

- **23.28.2** The Company will transfer the Buyback consideration, net of tax deducted at source, to the Clearing Corporation’s bank account through the Company’s Broker using the secondary-market mechanism and prescribed schedule.

  For dematerialized Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback, the Clearing Corporation will pay the funds directly to the Eligible Shareholder’s bank account linked to the demat account. If bank details are unavailable or the transfer instruction is rejected by the RBI or the relevant bank, the amount will be transferred to the concerned stock broker’s account for onward transfer to the Eligible Shareholder.

- **23.28.3** For certain client types, including NRIs and foreign clients, where specific RBI or other regulatory requirements apply and the client does not settle through a custodian, funds will be paid to the respective stock broker’s settlement account for release to the Eligible Shareholder’s account.

  Client-type details will be obtained from the Depositories. Funds relating to bids settled through custodians will be transferred to the custodian’s settlement bank account in accordance with the applicable mechanism prescribed by Indian Stock Exchanges and the Clearing Corporation.

- **23.28.4** For Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares, funds will be paid to their respective stock broker’s settlement account for release to the Eligible Shareholder’s account.

- **23.28.5** The Company or the Registrar to the Buyback will provide the Clearing Corporation with details of each shareholder’s entitlement for the Tender Offer process. The Clearing Corporation will cancel excess or unaccepted blocked shares in the shareholder’s demat account. On the settlement date, all blocked shares included in accepted bids will be transferred to the Clearing Corporation.

- **23.28.6** For inter-depository transactions, the Clearing Corporation will cancel excess or unaccepted shares in the target depository. The source depository cannot release the lien without an inter-depository transfer message from the target depository.

  The target depository will send the message based on a cancellation request from the Clearing Corporation or automatically after matching the accepted-bid details received from the Company or Registrar. After receiving the message, the source depository will cancel or release excess or unaccepted blocked shares.

  After the tendering period and receipt of the demat account details and accepted bid quantity, the source depository will debit the accepted bid shares from the shareholder’s demat account and credit them to the Clearing Corporation’s settlement account in the target depository on the settlement date.

- **23.28.7** Equity Shares bought back in dematerialized form will be transferred directly to the Company’s demat account opened for the Buyback (the **“Company Demat Account”**) if indicated by the Company’s Broker. Otherwise, the Company’s Broker will transfer the shares to the Company Demat Account after receiving them through the clearing and settlement mechanism of the Indian Stock Exchanges.
