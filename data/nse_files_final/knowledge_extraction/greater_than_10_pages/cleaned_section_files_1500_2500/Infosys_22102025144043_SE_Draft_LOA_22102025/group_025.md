## Record Date and Ratio of Buyback as per the Buyback Entitlement in each Category:

  - **22.7.2 General Category:** All other Eligible Shareholders.

## Fractional Entitlements

- **22.14** If the Buyback Entitlement calculated by applying the above ratios to the Equity Shares held on the Record Date is not a whole number, the fractional entitlement will be ignored when calculating the number of Equity Shares that may be tendered by Eligible Shareholders in either category.

- **22.15** As a result of ignoring fractional entitlements, Small Shareholders holding [●] or fewer Equity Shares on the Record Date will receive a Tender Form showing zero entitlement. They may tender Additional Equity Shares in the Buyback and will receive preference for acceptance of one Equity Share if they have tendered Additional Equity Shares. Subject to the Buyback Regulations, the Company will use its best efforts to accept Equity Shares tendered by such Eligible Shareholders to the extent possible and permissible.

## Basis of Acceptance of Equity Shares validly tendered in the Reserved Category for Small Shareholders

- **22.16** Subject to the Letter of Offer, Equity Shares tendered by Small Shareholders in the Reserved Category will be accepted in the following order:

  - **22.16.1** Acceptance of 100% of the Equity Shares validly tendered by Small Shareholders, up to the lower of their Buyback Entitlement or the number of Equity Shares tendered.

  - **22.16.2** If Equity Shares remain available for purchase after paragraph 22.16.1, Small Shareholders entitled to tender zero Equity Shares because of fractional entitlement, but who tendered Additional Equity Shares, will receive preference. One Equity Share from the Additional Equity Shares tendered by each such Small Shareholder will be bought back.

  - **22.16.3** If Equity Shares remain available after paragraphs 22.16.1 and 22.16.2, Additional Equity Shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement will be accepted in proportion to the Additional Equity Shares tendered, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

    Valid Acceptances per Small Shareholder will equal:

    `Additional Equity Shares validly tendered by the Small Shareholder ÷ Total Additional Equity Shares validly tendered × Total pending Equity Shares to be accepted in the Reserved Category`

    For this calculation, the Additional Equity Shares of a Small Shareholder from whom one Equity Share has been accepted under paragraph 22.16.2 will be reduced by one.

## Adjustment for fractional results in case of proportionate Acceptance, as described above:

- **22.17** If the proportionately calculated number of Additional Equity Shares to be accepted for a Small Shareholder is not a whole number and the fractional portion is greater than or equal to 0.50, it will be rounded up to the next integer.

- **22.18** If the fractional portion is less than 0.50, it will be ignored.

## Basis of Acceptance of Equity Shares validly tendered in the General Category

- **22.19** Subject to the Letter of Offer, Equity Shares tendered by all other Eligible Shareholders in the General Category will be accepted in the following order:

  - **22.19.1** Acceptance of 100% of the Equity Shares validly tendered by other Eligible Shareholders, up to the lower of their Buyback Entitlement or the number of Equity Shares tendered.

  - **22.19.2** If Equity Shares remain available for purchase after paragraph 22.19.1, Additional Equity Shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement will be accepted in proportion to the Additional Equity Shares tendered, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

    Valid Acceptances per shareholder will equal:

    `Additional Equity Shares validly tendered by the Eligible Shareholder ÷ Total Additional Equity Shares validly tendered in the General Category × Total pending Equity Shares to be accepted in the General Category`

## Adjustment for fractional results in case of proportionate acceptance as described above:

- **22.20** If the proportionately calculated number of Additional Equity Shares to be accepted for an Eligible Shareholder is not a whole number and the fractional portion is greater than or equal to 0.50, it will be rounded up to the next integer. If the fractional portion is less than 0.50, it will be ignored.

## Basis of Acceptance of Equity Shares between categories

- **22.21** If Equity Shares remain available for purchase in one category after applying the acceptance methodology for both categories, and additional validly tendered Equity Shares remain unaccepted in the other category, the additional Equity Shares in the over-tendered category will be accepted proportionately.

  Valid Acceptances per Eligible Shareholder will equal:

  `Additional outstanding Equity Shares validly tendered by the Eligible Shareholder in the Over Tendered Category ÷ Total additional outstanding Equity Shares validly tendered in the Over Tendered Category × Total pending Equity Shares to be bought back in the Partially Filled Category`

- **22.22** If the Partially Filled Category is the General Category and the Over Tendered Category is the Reserved Category for Small Shareholders, the Additional Equity Shares tendered by a Small Shareholder from whom one Equity Share has been accepted under paragraph 22.21 will be reduced by one Equity Share for this calculation.

- **22.23** For proportionate acceptance under paragraph 22.21:

  - **22.23.1** If the proportionately calculated number of Additional Equity Shares to be accepted for an Eligible Shareholder is not a whole number and the fractional portion is greater than or equal to 0.50, it will be rounded up to the next integer.

  - **22.23.2** If the fractional portion is less than 0.50, it will be ignored.
