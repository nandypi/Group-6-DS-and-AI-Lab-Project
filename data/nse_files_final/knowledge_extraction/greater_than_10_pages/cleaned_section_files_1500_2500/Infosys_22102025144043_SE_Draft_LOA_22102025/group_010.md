# RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Board hereby confirms that:

1. All equity shares proposed to be bought back are fully paid-up.

2. Subject to applicable law, the Company will not issue shares or other specified securities, including through a bonus issue, until the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations through stock option schemes or conversion of preference shares or debentures into equity shares. Relevant details and potential impacts will be disclosed in the public announcement.

3. Subject to applicable law, the Company will not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations.

4. Under Section 68(8) of the Act, the Company will not make any further issue of the same kind of shares or other securities, including allotment of new shares under Section 62(1)(a) of the Act, within six months after completion of the Buyback. Exceptions include bonus shares and equity shares issued to discharge subsisting obligations such as conversion of warrants, stock option schemes, sweat equity, or conversion of preference shares or debentures into equity shares.

5. The Company will not buy back locked-in or non-transferable equity shares while the lock-in remains in force or until the shares become transferable.

6. The Company will not buy back equity shares through negotiated deals, whether on or off the stock exchanges, spot transactions, or private arrangements.

7. There are no outstanding defaults in repayment of deposits, payment of interest on deposits, redemption of debentures or preference shares, payment of dividends, or repayment of term loans or related interest payable to financial institutions or banking companies.

8. The Company has complied with Sections 92, 123, 127, and 129 of the Act.

9. Funds borrowed from banks and financial institutions will not be used for the Buyback.

10. The aggregate Buyback amount of ₹18,000 crore does not exceed 25% of the Company’s total paid-up capital and free reserves, based on the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at June 30, 2025.

11. The proposed purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares, or ten crore shares, does not exceed 25% of the total number of equity shares in the paid-up equity share capital as at June 30, 2025.

12. Subject to applicable law, the Company will not make another buyback offer within one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period.

13. The Buyback does not contravene Regulation 4(vii) of the Buyback Regulations because the Company has not made a buyback offer within one year from the expiry of the buyback period of the preceding offer.

14. As at the relevant date, no scheme of amalgamation, compromise, or arrangement under the Act is pending.

15. The Company will earmark and arrange adequate sources of funds for the Buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

16. The Company will not directly or indirectly purchase its own equity shares or other specified securities:

   - Through any subsidiary company, including its own subsidiary companies; or
   - Through any investment company or group of investment companies.

17. Consideration for equity shares bought back by the Company will be paid only in cash.

18. The Buyback will not result in delisting of the equity shares from BSE Limited or National Stock Exchange of India Limited.

19. As at the relevant date, and after the Buyback, the ratio of aggregate secured and unsecured debt owed by the Company will not exceed twice its paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone or consolidated financial statements as at June 30, 2025.

20. The Buyback offer will not be withdrawn once the public announcement is made.

21. The Company will transfer an amount equal to the nominal value of the equity shares purchased through the Buyback from free reserves, the securities premium account, or other sources permitted by law to the capital redemption reserve account. Details of the transfer will be disclosed in subsequent audited financial statements.

22. The Company will reduce its share capital following the Buyback. The equity shares bought back will be extinguished and physically destroyed, where applicable, in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and the Act.

23. The Company has no outstanding debt from lenders and therefore is not required to obtain prior lender consent for breach of any lender covenant.

If the Company fails to fulfil its obligations under the Buyback Regulations, the escrow account, in whole or in part, will be forfeited and distributed pro rata among security holders who accepted the offer. Any balance will be used for investor protection in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

# 5. DETAILS OF THE PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

5.1 In accordance with Regulation 7(i) of the Buyback Regulations, the Company made a Public Announcement dated [●], 2025, published in the specified newspapers on [●], 2025.

5.2 The Public Announcement was issued within two Working Days of the declaration of the postal ballot results on [●], 2025. The postal ballot approved the Buyback through a special resolution of the equity shareholders.

5.3 The Company may publish further notices or corrigenda in the specified newspapers.

5.4 On the date of the Public Announcement and within one business day of dispatching the Postal Ballot Notice, the Company placed advertisements. On dispatch of the Letter of Offer, it will place an advertisement in the print edition of *The New York Times* to draw the attention of U.S. shareholders and enable their participation in the Buyback.

5.5 After dispatch of the Letter of Offer, the Company will publish advertisements in the specified English, Hindi, and Kannada newspapers to inform eligible shareholders that dispatch has been completed. The dispatch advertisement will also include a link for shareholders to check their Buyback entitlement.

# 6. DETAILS OF THE BUYBACK

6.1 The Buyback was authorized by a Board resolution dated September 11, 2025. The Board sought shareholder approval through a special resolution and Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025. The postal ballot results were announced on [●], 2025, and the shareholders approved the Buyback by special resolution.
