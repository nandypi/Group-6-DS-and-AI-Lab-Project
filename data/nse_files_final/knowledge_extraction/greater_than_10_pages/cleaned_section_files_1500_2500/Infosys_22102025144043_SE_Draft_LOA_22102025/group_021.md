### 17.6

The Buyback will not result in any benefit to any Director of the Company except to the extent of:

- Cash consideration received from the Company pursuant to participation in the Buyback as an equity shareholder.
- Changes in shareholding based on the Buyback response, resulting from the extinguishment of Equity Shares and the consequent reduction in Equity Share capital after completion of the Buyback.
