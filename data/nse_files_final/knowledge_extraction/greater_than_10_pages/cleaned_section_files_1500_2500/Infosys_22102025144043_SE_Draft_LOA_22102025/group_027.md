An Eligible Shareholder tendering shares valued at more than ₹50,00,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Crore) must provide a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) number and supporting documents, such as the LEI registration certificate, to the Registrar by 5 p.m. on the Buyback Window Closing Date.

- **23.5** As disclosed in Section 22, “Process and Methodology of Buyback,” the Equity Shares proposed to be bought back are divided into two categories:
  1. Reserved Category for Small Shareholders.
  2. General Category for other Eligible Shareholders.

  The Buyback Entitlement of each Eligible Shareholder will be calculated according to the applicable category.

- **23.6** After Equity Shares are accepted based on Buyback Entitlement, any Equity Shares remaining to be bought back in one category will first be accepted, proportionately to the Equity Shares tendered above entitlement, from Eligible Shareholders in that category. Any remaining shares will then be accepted from Eligible Shareholders in another category who have tendered shares above their Buyback Entitlement.

- **23.7** Purchases under the Offer will be paid for in Indian rupees, including to holders of Equity Shares who reside outside India. Payment for accepted shares will be made within five Working Days after the Buyback Window Closing Date, as required by the Buyback Regulations.

- **23.8** Participation in the Buyback is voluntary. Eligible Shareholders may:

  - Participate in whole or in part and receive cash for Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
  - Choose not to participate and retain a potentially higher percentage shareholding after completion of the Buyback, without making an additional investment.
  - Tender only part of their Buyback Entitlement.
  - Tender Additional Equity Shares above their Buyback Entitlement and participate in any shortfall arising from the non-participation of other Eligible Shareholders.

  Acceptance of shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement will follow the procedure described in the Offer documentation. If an Eligible Shareholder, or a person claiming to be an Eligible Shareholder, cannot participate for any reason, the Company, the Manager, the Registrar, and their officers will not be liable for that non-participation.

- **23.9** The maximum number of Equity Shares tendered by an Eligible Shareholder cannot exceed the number held by that shareholder on the Record Date. If shares are held through multiple demat accounts, the number tendered through any one demat account cannot exceed the shares held in that account on the Record Date.

- **23.10** The Buyback will be implemented through the mechanism for acquisition of shares through a stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under the applicable takeover, buyback, and delisting framework, in accordance with the SEBI Circulars, the Companies Act, the Buyback Regulations, and applicable decisions of the Board or its authorized committee.

Failure to receive the Letter of Offer, or accidental omission to dispatch it to an eligible person, will not invalidate the Buyback Offer. The Company will accept Equity Shares validly tendered based on the holding and entitlement recorded in the Company’s records as of the Record Date.
