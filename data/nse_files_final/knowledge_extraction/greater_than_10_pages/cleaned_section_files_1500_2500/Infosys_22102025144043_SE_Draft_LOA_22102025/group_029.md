23.25.7 Eligible Shareholders shall provide all relevant documents necessary to ensure transferability of the Equity Shares covered by the tender form, including:

- A duly attested power of attorney if a person other than the Eligible Shareholder has signed the tender form.
- A duly attested death certificate and succession certificate or legal heirship certificate if the Eligible Shareholder has expired.
- For companies, certified corporate authorizations, including board and/or general meeting resolutions.

23.25.8 For dematerialized Equity Shares, submission of the tender form and TRS is not required. After the Clearing Corporation receives the dematerialized Equity Shares and a valid bid is submitted in the exchange bidding system, the Buyback shall be deemed to have been accepted for Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialized form.

23.25.9 Eligible Shareholders shall keep their DP account active and unblocked to receive credit if Equity Shares are returned due to rejection or the prorated Buyback decided by the Company. They shall also keep the bank account linked to the DP account active and updated to receive payment for Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

Excess or unaccepted dematerialized Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders shall be returned by the Clearing Corporation. If a security transfer instruction is rejected in the depository system, the securities shall be transferred to the shareholder broker’s depository pool account for onward transfer to the Eligible Shareholder. For custodian participant orders, excess or unaccepted dematerialized shares shall be refunded to the respective custodian depository pool account.

23.25.10 If the Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis, the Eligible Shareholder, being a Non-Resident Shareholder, shall obtain and enclose a letter from its authorised dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval.

If the Non-Resident Shareholder cannot provide this certificate, the Equity Shares shall be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The Eligible Shareholder shall then submit a consent letter addressed to the Company, allowing the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
