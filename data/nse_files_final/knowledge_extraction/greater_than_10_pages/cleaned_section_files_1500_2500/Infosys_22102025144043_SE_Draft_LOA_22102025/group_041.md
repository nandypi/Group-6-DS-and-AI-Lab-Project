# Buyback Tender Form Requirements

1. The Buyback will open on [●] and close on [●].

2. The Tender Form must be read along with the Letter of Offer and is subject to the terms and conditions mentioned in the Letter of Offer and the Tender Form.

3. Eligible Shareholders wishing to tender Equity Shares must ensure that the following documents reach the Registrar, as specified in the Letter of Offer, by [●] at 5:00 PM IST:

   - Duly signed Tender Form. In the case of joint holdings, all Equity Shareholders must sign in the same order in which they hold the Equity Shares.
   - Original share certificates.
   - Valid share transfer form(s) (SH-4), duly completed and signed by all registered Shareholders in the same order and according to the specimen signatures registered with the Company or Registrar, and witnessed at the appropriate place.
   - Self-attested PAN Card copies for all Eligible Shareholders in the case of joint holdings.

4. Eligible Shareholders must also provide all relevant additional documents, including:

   - A duly attested power of attorney registered with the Registrar, if a person other than the Eligible Shareholder has signed the Tender Form.
   - Necessary corporate authorisations, such as board resolutions, in the case of companies.
   - If the shareholder's address has changed from the address recorded in the Register of Members, a self-attested address proof consisting of a valid Aadhaar Card, Voter Identity Card, or Passport.

5. Non-Resident Shareholders must, where applicable:

   - Enclose a copy of the permission received from the RBI to acquire the Equity Shares held in the Company.
   - If the Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis, obtain and enclose a letter from the authorised dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified in the RBI approval.
   - If unable to provide this certificate, the shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The shareholder must then submit a consent letter addressed to the Company permitting payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid shares accepted under the Buyback.

   If applicable documents are not enclosed with the Tender Form, the tendered Equity Shares may be rejected.

6. If a registered entity has merged with another entity, the merger has been approved and has taken effect, but the successor company is not yet registered as the shareholder, the entity must submit the following documents with the Tender Form:

   - Approval from the appropriate authority for the merger.
   - The scheme of merger.
   - The requisite form filed with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) intimating the merger.

7. For the Clearing Corporation to make a payout exceeding Rs. 50,00,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Crores only), the Eligible Shareholder must provide a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) number. An Eligible Shareholder tendering shares with a value exceeding Rs. 50,00,00,000 must provide the LEI number and supporting documents, such as the LEI registration certificate, to the Registrar by email at [●] no later than 5:00 p.m. IST on the Buyback Window Closing Date.

8. If an Eligible Shareholder does not receive the Letter of Offer, the shareholder may participate in the Buyback by submitting a signed plain-paper application stating:

   - Name and address of the Eligible Shareholder or all Eligible Shareholders in the case of joint holdings.
   - Number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date.
   - Client ID number.
   - DP name and ID.
   - Beneficiary account number.
   - Number of Equity Shares tendered for the Buyback.

9. Eligible Shareholders may tender Equity Shares in whole or in part up to their entitlement, or in excess of their entitlement, but not exceeding their holding on the Record Date.

10. Documents sent by the Stock Broker, custodian, or Eligible Shareholder will be sent at their own risk. They are advised to adequately safeguard their interests in this regard.

11. The procedure for tendering Equity Shares is set out in Section 23 of the Letter of Offer.

12. All documents specified in the Tender Form must be enclosed with a valid Tender Form. Otherwise, the tendered Equity Shares may be rejected, including on the following grounds:

   - Share certificates of a company other than the Company are enclosed.
   - Transmission of the Equity Shares is incomplete and the Equity Shares are not registered in the name of the Eligible Shareholders.
   - The Eligible Shareholder has tendered the Equity Shares but the Registrar has not received the physical share certificate.
   - The signatures in the Tender Form and Form SH-4 do not match the specimen signatures recorded with the Company or Registrar.
   - The shareholder was not an Eligible Shareholder of the Company on the Record Date.
   - There is a name mismatch in the shareholder's share certificate.
   - Self-attested PAN Cards of the shareholder and all joint holders have not been submitted.
   - The Eligible Shareholder has made a duplicate bid or order.
   - A court or other competent authority has issued a restraint order relating to transfer, disposal, or sale; loss of share certificates has been notified to the Company; title to the Equity Shares is disputed or unclear; or another restraint applies.
   - The required documents are not received by the Registrar by [●] at 5:00 PM IST.

13. The Company will not accept Equity Shares offered in the Buyback that are subject to a court restraint order relating to the transfer or sale of those Equity Shares.

14. By participating in the Buyback, each Eligible Shareholder, including each Non-Resident Shareholder, undertakes to complete all regulatory and statutory filings and compliances required under applicable law. Each Eligible Shareholder authorises the Company to make, sign, execute, deliver, acknowledge, and perform applications and regulatory reporting, including the FC-TRS form if necessary, and undertakes to assist the Company with such reporting when required.

15. Non-Resident Shareholders must obtain and submit all necessary approvals, where required, from the relevant authorities, including RBI approvals under the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, as amended, and related rules and regulations. They must also comply with applicable FEMA reporting requirements and other rules, regulations, and guidelines relating to remittance of funds outside India.
