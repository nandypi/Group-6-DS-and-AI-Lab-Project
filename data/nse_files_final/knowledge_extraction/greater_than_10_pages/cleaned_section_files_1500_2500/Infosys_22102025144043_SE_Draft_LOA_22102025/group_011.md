| Number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back | Up to 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares, representing up to 2.41% of the total number of Equity Shares in the existing total paid-up Equity Share capital of the Company on a standalone basis. |
| Buyback Offer Price | ₹1,800 (Rupees One Thousand Eight Hundred only) per Equity Share. |
| Applicable regulations and provisions | The Buyback is being undertaken under Chapter III of the Buyback Regulations through the tender offer route and in accordance with other applicable provisions of the Buyback Regulations, SEBI Circulars, LODR Regulations, and Sections 68, 69, 70 and other applicable provisions, if any, of the Act and relevant rules, including the Share Capital Rules and the Management Rules, to the extent applicable. |
| Methodology for the Buyback | Equity Shares will be bought back on a proportionate basis from all Eligible Shareholders, including holders of ADSs of the Company who cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date, so that they become equity shareholders of the Company on the Record Date. The Buyback will be conducted through the Tender Offer route under the Buyback Regulations and facilitated through the stock exchange mechanism specified by SEBI in the SEBI Circulars. The Company will request BSE and NSE to provide the acquisition window for tendering Equity Shares. For the purposes of this Buyback, [●] will |
| Buyback Offer Size | Aggregate consideration of up to ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only), representing up to 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate of fully paid-up Equity Share capital and free reserves based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis and consolidated basis, respectively. The Buyback Offer Size is within the prescribed limit of 25% under the Act and Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations. |
| Expenses excluded from Buyback Offer Size | The Buyback Offer Size excludes expenses and transaction costs incurred or to be incurred for the Buyback, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, public announcement and publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, applicable securities transaction tax, goods and services tax, stamp duty, and other incidental and related expenses. |
| Other Information | Under applicable Indian laws, holders of ADSs will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. To participate, ADS holders must withdraw the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs before the Record Date and become holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date. The Company stated in the Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025 that ADS holders had approximately 49 calendar days from the date of the notice to take the required steps. ADS holders were advised to read paragraphs 22.26–22.30 of the Letter of Offer, “Participation by ADS Holders,” for additional details. |
