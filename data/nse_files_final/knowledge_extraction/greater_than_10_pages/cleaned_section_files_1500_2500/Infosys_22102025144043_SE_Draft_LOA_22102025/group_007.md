## Certain conventions, currency of presentation, use of financial information and stock market data

## Page Numbers and Paragraph Numbers

Unless otherwise stated, references to page numbers and paragraph numbers in the Letter of Offer refer to pages of the Letter of Offer.

## Currency and Units of Presentation

References to “Rupee(s),” “₹,” “Rs.,” or “INR” mean Indian Rupees, the official currency of India. Financial information is presented in ₹ crore unless otherwise stated.

## Financial and Other Data

Unless stated otherwise or required by context, the financial information in the Letter of Offer is based on:

- Audited standalone and consolidated financial statements for fiscal years 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- Audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at September 30, 2025.

The fiscal year begins on April 1 and ends on March 31 of the following year.

## Stock Market Data

Unless stated otherwise or required by context, stock market data in the Letter of Offer is derived from the websites of the Indian stock exchanges.

## 3. DISCLAIMER CLAUSE

- Submission of the Letter of Offer to SEBI is required under the Buyback Regulations and does not constitute SEBI clearance or approval. SEBI does not accept responsibility for the Company’s financial ability to meet its buyback commitments or for the correctness of statements or opinions in the Letter of Offer.
- The Manager to the Buyback, Kotak Mahindra Capital Company Limited, certified to SEBI that the disclosures in the Letter of Offer were generally adequate and conformed to the Act and the Buyback Regulations. Its due diligence certificate stated that:
  1. The Public Announcement and Letter of Offer conform to the relevant documents and materials.
  2. Legal requirements connected with the buyback, including the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended, were complied with.
  3. The disclosures were, to the manager’s knowledge, true, fair, and adequate for Eligible Shareholders to make an informed decision.
  4. Funds used for the buyback would comply with the Companies Act, 2013, as amended.
- Filing the Letter of Offer with SEBI does not release the Company from liabilities under applicable law or from obtaining required statutory or other clearances.
- The Board of Directors and the Company are responsible for ensuring that material information has not been suppressed or misrepresented.
- **ASSUMPTION:** Funds borrowed from banks or financial institutions, if any, will not be used for the buyback.

## Important Notice to All Shareholders

The Letter of Offer is prepared for compliance with the Buyback Regulations and does not constitute an offer or solicitation in any jurisdiction where it is unauthorized or unlawful. Shareholders may participate only if permitted under applicable local laws, rules, regulations, and statutes.

## Notice to Shareholders in the United States:

- The buyback concerns securities of an Indian company and is subject to Indian laws and disclosure requirements. The financial information is prepared under the Companies Act, 2013, and Indian Accounting Standards notified under the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and may not be comparable with financial information prepared under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles.
- The buyback will remain open for a fixed period of 5 Working Days under Regulation 9(vi) of the Buyback Regulations, rather than the minimum period of 20 U.S. business days under Rules 13e-4 and 14e-1 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
- The buyback will be conducted in the United States pursuant to exemptions granted by the SEC from Rules 13e-4(f)(1)(i), 13e-4(f)(3), 13e-4(f)(8)(i), and 14e-1(a) under the Exchange Act, and otherwise in accordance with applicable U.S. and Indian requirements. Details of the exemptive relief are referenced in the section titled “Details of Statutory Approvals” on page 46 of the Letter of Offer.
- Cash received by an Eligible Shareholder pursuant to the buyback may be taxable for U.S. federal income tax purposes and under applicable state, local, foreign, and other tax laws.
- U.S. shareholders may face difficulty enforcing rights or claims under U.S. federal securities laws because the Company is incorporated outside the United States and some or all of its directors and officers may reside outside the United States.
- Neither the SEC nor any U.S. state securities commission has approved or disapproved the buyback or commented on the adequacy or completeness of the Letter of Offer.
- The Company expresses no opinion on whether Eligible Shareholders should participate in the buyback.
