## Infosys Limited Buyback Tender Form

The Letter of Offer relates to the buyback by Infosys Limited of up to 10,00,00,000 (10 crore) fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each at a buyback offer price of ₹1,800 per share. The aggregate buyback consideration will not exceed ₹18,000 crore, payable in cash.

1. I / We, having read and understood the Letter of Offer dated [●], tender / offer my / our equity shares in response to the buyback on the terms and conditions set out in the Letter of Offer and this Tender Form.

2. I / We authorise the Company to buy back the equity shares offered and to instruct the Registrar to the Buyback to extinguish those shares.

3. I / We affirm that the equity shares offered for buyback are free from all liens, equitable interests, charges, and encumbrances.

4. I / We declare that there are no restraints, injunctions, or other orders restricting my / our right to tender the equity shares, and that I / we are legally entitled to tender them.

5. The Company will pay the Buyback Offer Price after applicable Income Tax deductions and verification of the validity of the documents. Payment will be made through the secondary market mechanism and in accordance with the SEBI Buyback Regulations and applicable SEBI circulars.

6. Shareholders must keep the relevant DP account active and unblocked to receive shares returned because of rejection or pro-rata acceptance. The bank account linked to the DP account must also remain active and updated to receive payment for shares accepted in the buyback.

7. Shareholders must return to the Company any buyback consideration wrongfully received.

8. Shareholders are responsible for discharging tax due on gains arising from the buyback. They must compute the applicable gains, pay applicable taxes in India, whether by tax deducted at source or otherwise, and file tax returns in consultation with their custodians, authorised dealers, or tax advisers.

9. Non-resident shareholders, including NRIs, OCBs, FPIs, foreign nationals, and FIIs, must enclose a consent letter stating:

   - the number of equity shares to be transferred;
   - the investee company name, Infosys Limited; and
   - the transfer price, being the price determined in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

   The consent letter must be signed by the shareholder or the shareholder’s duly appointed agent. If signed by an agent, a power of attorney must also be enclosed.

10. Shareholders undertake to indemnify the Company if a tax demand is raised on the Company in relation to gains arising to the shareholder from the buyback. Shareholders must provide relevant information regarding the taxability or non-taxability of the buyback proceeds, including tax returns filed in India and evidence of tax paid, when requested.

11. Shareholders undertake to execute further documents and provide further assurances required to give effect to the tender. The buyback will be conducted in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, and applicable foreign exchange regulations. This form is deemed to be the shareholder’s consent letter for filings under the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA), and related rules.

12. Invalid or unaccepted equity shares will be returned to the demat account from which the shares were tendered, at the shareholder’s own risk.

13. Applicable to all non-resident shareholders: non-resident shareholders must obtain and submit all necessary approvals, including approvals from the Reserve Bank of India under FEMA, where required. They must also comply with applicable reporting requirements and other rules, regulations, and guidelines relating to remittance of funds outside India.

## INSTRUCTIONS

This Tender Form must be read with the Letter of Offer and is subject to the terms and conditions stated in both documents.

1. The buyback will open on [●] and close on [●].

2. For Eligible Shareholders holding and tendering equity shares in dematerialized form, submission of the Tender Form and TRS is not required. Such shareholders must tender through their respective Stock Broker by indicating the number of equity shares they intend to tender. The procedure is set out in Paragraph 23.25 of the Letter of Offer.

3. Equity shares tendered in the buyback may be rejected if:

   - the tenderer was not an Eligible Shareholder as on the Record Date;
   - there is a name and PAN mismatch in the shareholder’s demat account;
   - a court or other competent authority has imposed a restraint order on transfer, disposal, or sale;
   - loss of share certificates has been notified to the Company;
   - title to the equity shares is disputed or otherwise unclear;
   - another restraint affecting the equity shares exists; or
   - the completed Tender Form and other required documents are not received from Eligible Shareholders who held physical shares on the Record Date and placed their bid in dematerialized form in accordance with Paragraph 23.29 of the Letter of Offer.

4. Non-resident shareholders must, where applicable, enclose permission received from the RBI to acquire the equity shares held in the Company.

5. If equity shares are held on a repatriation basis, the non-resident shareholder must obtain and enclose a letter from the authorised dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI.

6. If the non-resident shareholder cannot provide this certificate, the shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The shareholder must then submit a consent letter allowing the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid shares accepted under the buyback.

7. If applicable required documents are not enclosed with the Tender Form, the equity shares tendered may be rejected.

8. The Company will not accept equity shares subject to a court restraint order relating to their transfer or sale.
