25. To perform all acts, deeds, matters, and things considered necessary, expedient, usual, or proper for the Buyback.

The Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer, and Company Secretary were authorized to represent the Company before the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, SEBI, the SEC, the stock exchanges on which the Company’s equity shares are listed, the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), and other agencies connected with the Buyback. They were also authorized to sign and submit forms, letters, documents, and other papers, including certified copies of Board resolutions relating to the Buyback.

These officers were authorized severally to perform acts necessary or incidental to signing and filing forms, paying fees, and completing other actions required for the Buyback.

The Buyback Committee was authorized to implement the Buyback, including:

- Appointing merchant bankers, brokers, lawyers, depository participants, escrow agents, bankers, advisors, registrars, scrutinizers, consultants, representatives, intermediaries, agencies, printers, and advertising agencies.
- Completing incidental documentation and applying to appropriate authorities for required approvals.
- Preparing and issuing Buyback documents, including the public announcement.
- Opening accounts and arranging for extinguishment of share certificates and filing of the Certificate of Extinguishment.
- Submitting undertakings, agreements, papers, documents, and correspondence to SEBI, the Reserve Bank of India, the Government of India, the SEC, BSE Limited, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited, NYSE, the Registrar of Companies, depositories, and other authorities.

Subject to applicable law and approval of the Buyback by the members, the Buyback Committee was authorized to alter or modify the terms and conditions, delegate or sub-delegate its authority to Company officers or representatives, revoke or substitute such delegations, issue directions, and settle questions, difficulties, or doubts relating to the Buyback without further Board approval.

The quorum for a meeting of the Buyback Committee is any two members. The Committee may regulate its own proceedings, meet as often as required, or act through circular resolutions. The Committee must report the status and progress of Buyback-related actions to the Board or its Committee from time to time.

The resolutions do not confer any right on a member to offer shares or impose any obligation on the Company or the Board to buy back shares. They also do not limit the Company’s or the Board’s power to terminate any Buyback process where permitted by law.

The Board confirmed that it had made a full enquiry into the affairs and prospects of the Company and formed the following opinions:

1. Immediately following the Board meeting held on September 11, 2025 and the date of the members’ resolution approving the Buyback, there would be no grounds on which the Company could be found unable to pay its debts.

2. Considering the Company’s prospects for the year immediately following the September 11, 2025 Board meeting and the year immediately following the members’ resolution, the Board’s intended management of the business, and the financial resources expected to be available, the Company would be able to meet its liabilities as they fell due and would not become insolvent within one year from either date.

3. In forming this opinion, the Board considered the Company’s liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, as if the Company were being wound up under the applicable provisions of the Act and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.
