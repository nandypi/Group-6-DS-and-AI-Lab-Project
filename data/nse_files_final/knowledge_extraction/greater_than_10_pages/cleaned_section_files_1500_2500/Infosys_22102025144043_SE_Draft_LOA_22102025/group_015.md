## 12. BUYBACK PRICE AND BASIS OF DETERMINING THE PRICE OF THE BUYBACK

- 12.1 The Equity Shares of the Company are proposed to be bought back at a price of ₹1,800 (Rupees One Thousand Eight Hundred only) per Equity Share (the “Buyback Price”). The Buyback Price was determined after considering factors including the volume-weighted average market prices of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, the price-earnings ratio, the impact on other financial parameters, and the possible impact of the Buyback on earnings per Equity Share.

  In accordance with Regulation 5(via) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board/Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, provided there is no change in the Buyback Offer Size, until one working day before the Record Date fixed for the Buyback.

The Buyback Price represents:

- A premium of 15.60% and 16.99% to the volume-weighted average market price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, during the three-month period ending September 8, 2025.
- A premium of 20.78% and 20.67% to the volume-weighted average market price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, during the two-week period ending September 8, 2025.
- A premium of 25.64% and 25.62% over the closing price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, as on September 8, 2025.

September 8, 2025 was the date of the intimation to the stock exchanges regarding the proposal for the Buyback to be considered at a Board Meeting.

## 13. SOURCES OF FUNDS FOR THE BUYBACK

- 13.1 Assuming full acceptance, ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only), excluding transaction costs, would be utilised by the Company for the Buyback.
- 13.2 The funds for implementing the proposed Buyback will be sourced from the free reserves of the Company, including securities premium, or another source permitted by the Buyback Regulations or the Act.
- 13.3 The Buyback is not likely to cause any material impact on the earnings of the Company, except for the cost of financing the Buyback. This cost would result from a temporary reduction in treasury income that the Company could otherwise have earned on the funds deployed until distribution to shareholders under the Capital Allocation Policy.
- 13.4 The Company will transfer from its free reserves an amount equal to the nominal value of the equity shares bought back to the Capital Redemption Reserve Account. Details of the transfer will be disclosed in subsequent audited financial statements.
- 13.5 Borrowed funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, will not be used for the Buyback.
