## Postal Ballot Notice

Infosys Limited issued the Postal Ballot Notice on April 24, 2026 pursuant to Sections 108 and 110 of the Companies Act, 2013, applicable rules, Regulation 44 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and Secretarial Standard on General Meetings (SS-2).

The notice seeks shareholder approval for the following special resolutions:

| Sr. No. | Particulars |
|---|---|
| 1. | Appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200) as an Independent Director for a term of three years from April 22, 2026 to April 21, 2029. |
| 2. | Re-appointment of Helene Auriol Potier (DIN: 10166891) as an Independent Director for a second term of five years from May 26, 2026 to May 25, 2031. |

The resolutions are to be voted on through remote e-voting only. The Postal Ballot Notice and e-voting instructions are being sent electronically to members whose email addresses are registered with the Company or Depository Participant.

The Company has engaged National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) to facilitate e-voting. Hemanth, Holla & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries (Membership No. FCS 6374; CP No. 6519), has been appointed as the scrutinizer for conducting the Postal Ballot and e-voting process.

### E-voting Period

| Particular | Date and time |
|---|---|
| Commencement of e-voting period | 9:00 a.m. IST on Saturday, April 25, 2026 |
| Conclusion of e-voting period | 5:00 p.m. IST on Sunday, May 24, 2026 |
| Cut-off date for eligibility to vote | Friday, April 17, 2026 |

The e-voting facility will be disabled by NSDL immediately after 5:00 p.m. IST on Sunday, May 24, 2026.

The scrutinizer will submit the report to the Chairman of the Company or a person authorized by the Chairman. The voting result will be announced not later than two working days after the conclusion of e-voting. If approved by the requisite majority, May 24, 2026 will be the date on which the resolutions are deemed to have been passed.

## Resolutions

### Item no. 1: Appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200), as an Independent Director for a term of three years effective from April 22, 2026 to April 21, 2029

The resolution proposes the appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens as an Independent Director of Infosys Limited for three years up to April 21, 2029. She was appointed as an Additional Director in the capacity of an Independent Director with effect from April 22, 2026.

The resolution states that she meets the independence criteria under Section 149(6) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the applicable rules, as well as Regulation 16(1)(b) of the LODR Regulations. The Company has received a written notice from a member under Section 160(1) of the Act. She will not be liable to retire by rotation.

The Board is authorized to delegate the powers necessary to implement the resolution.
