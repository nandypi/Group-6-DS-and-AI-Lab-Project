## Explanatory statement pursuant to Sections 102(1) and 110 of the Companies Act, 2013

## Item no. 1 - Appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200) as an Independent Director of the Company

Pursuant to Section 161 of the Companies Act, 2013, and other applicable provisions, the Board approved the appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200) as an Additional Director in the capacity of Independent Director of the Company for a term of three years, effective April 22, 2026, to April 21, 2029, both days inclusive, subject to shareholder approval through a special resolution.

The Company received the following statutory disclosures and declarations:

- Consent in writing to act as director in Form DIR-2, pursuant to Rule 8 of the Companies (Appointment and Qualifications of Directors) Rules, 2014.
- Intimation in Form DIR-8 confirming that she is not disqualified under Section 164(2) of the Companies Act, 2013.
- Declaration confirming that she meets the independence criteria under Section 149(6) of the Act and the LODR Regulations.
- Declaration pursuant to BSE Circular No. LIST/COMP/14/2018-19 dated June 20, 2018, and NSE Circular No. NSE/CML/2018/24 dated June 20, 2018, confirming that she has not been debarred from holding office as a director by SEBI or any other authority.
- Confirmation that she is not aware of any circumstance or situation that exists or may reasonably be anticipated to impair or impact her ability to discharge her duties as an Independent Director.
- Written notice from a member proposing her candidature under Section 160(1) of the Act.
- Confirmation of compliance with Rules 6(1) and 6(2) of the Appointment Rules regarding registration with the data bank of Independent Directors maintained by the Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs.
- Confirmation that she had not been a partner of a firm that had transactions during the last three financial years with Infosys Limited or its subsidiaries amounting to 10% or more of its gross turnover.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) finalized the desired attributes for the selection of Independent Directors, including experience, expertise, and independence, and recommended Diane’s candidature based on those attributes.

In the opinion of the Board, Diane fulfils the conditions for independence specified in the Act, the Rules made thereunder, the LODR Regulations, and other applicable laws and regulations. The Board noted that her background and experience align with the capabilities identified by the NRC and that she is eligible for appointment as an Independent Director.

The Board considered the appointment justified because:

- She has international experience in technology, sustainability, general management, business operations, and P&L leadership.
- She has expertise in business operations, risk management, strategy, governance, diversity, environment, sustainability, and cybersecurity.
- She has held key leadership roles at companies across multiple jurisdictions.

The resolution seeks shareholder approval for Diane’s appointment as an Independent Director from April 22, 2026, to April 21, 2029, both days inclusive, pursuant to Sections 149, 152, and other applicable provisions of the Act and the Rules made thereunder. She will not be liable to retire by rotation.

The profile, specific areas of expertise, and other relevant information required under the LODR Regulations and SS-2 are provided in the additional information section of the Notice.

No director, key managerial personnel, or their relatives, except Diane, has any financial or other interest or concern in passing the proposed resolution.

The Board, based on the recommendation of the NRC, recommends the special resolution for shareholder approval.

## Item no. 2 - Re-appointment of Helene Auriol Potier (DIN: 10166891) as an Independent Director of the Company

Helene Auriol Potier (DIN: 10166891) was appointed as an Independent Director of the Company effective May 26, 2023, pursuant to Section 149 of the Act and the Companies (Appointment and Qualifications of Directors) Rules, 2014. Shareholders approved the appointment at the Annual General Meeting held on June 28, 2023. Her first term ends on May 25, 2026.

Under Section 149 of the Act, an Independent Director may hold office for two terms of up to five consecutive years each. Helene fulfils the requirements of an Independent Director under Section 149(6) of the Act and Regulation 16(1)(b) of the LODR Regulations.

After considering Helene’s performance evaluation during her first term, as well as her knowledge, expertise, experience, contribution, and time commitment, the NRC recommended her re-appointment for a second term of five years. The NRC considered her diverse skills, leadership capabilities, and expertise in go-to-market, general management, sustainability, and corporate governance.

Based on the NRC’s recommendation, the Board recommended Helene’s re-appointment as an Independent Director for a second term of five years, effective May 26, 2026, to May 25, 2031, both days inclusive. She will not be liable to retire by rotation.

The Company received the following statutory disclosures and declarations:

- Consent in writing to act as director in Form DIR-2, pursuant to Rule 8 of the Appointment Rules.
- Intimation in Form DIR-8 confirming that she is not disqualified under Section 164(2) of the Act.
- Declaration confirming that she meets the independence criteria under Section 149(6) of the Act and the LODR Regulations.
- Declaration pursuant to BSE Circular No. LIST/COMP/14/2018-19 dated June 20, 2018, and NSE Circular No. NSE/CML/2018/24 dated June 20, 2018, confirming that she has not been debarred from holding office as a director by SEBI or any other authority.
- Confirmation that she is not aware of any circumstance or situation that exists or may reasonably be anticipated to impair or impact her ability to discharge her duties as an Independent Director.
- Written notice from a member proposing her candidature under Section 160(1) of the Act.
- Confirmation of compliance with Rules 6(1) and 6(2) of the Appointment Rules regarding registration with the data bank of Independent Directors maintained by the Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs.
- Confirmation that she had not been a partner of a firm that had transactions during the last three financial years with Infosys Limited or its subsidiaries amounting to 10% or more of its gross turnover.

In the opinion of the Board, and based on its evaluation, Helene fulfils the conditions specified in the Act, the Rules made thereunder, and the LODR Regulations for her re-appointment as an Independent Director. The Board considers her independent of the Company’s management.

The resolution seeks shareholder approval for Helene’s re-appointment as an Independent Director for a second term of five years, effective May 26, 2026, to May 25, 2031, both days inclusive, pursuant to Sections 149, 152, and other applicable provisions of the Act and the Rules made thereunder.

The profile, specific areas of expertise, and other relevant information required under the LODR Regulations and SS-2 are provided in the additional information section of the Notice.

No director, key managerial personnel, or their relatives, except Helene, has any financial or other interest or concern in passing the proposed resolution.

The Board, based on the recommendation of the NRC, recommends the special resolution for shareholder approval.

## Additional information on directors recommended for appointment/reappointment as required under Regulation 36 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and applicable secretarial standards

### Diane Enberg Jurgens — Independent Director

Diane Enberg Jurgens has global C-suite experience in technology, P&L leadership, and business transformation across multiple industries. She currently serves on the boards of Standard Chartered and World 50.

At Standard Chartered, she serves as an Independent Non-Executive Director and is a member of the Risk and Culture & Sustainability Committees. She was Chief Information Officer of The Walt Disney Company from 2020 to 2023, where she led enterprise technology and represented Disney on the US-India Strategic Partnership Forum.

She was Chief Technology Officer at BHP from 2015 to 2020, leading work in autonomous systems, cybersecurity, machine learning, and the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT). From 2012 to 2015, she was President and Managing Director of Shanghai OnStar Telematics. Before that, she served as CIO for GM International Operations from 2008 to 2012 and CIO for GM China from 2006 to 2008.

Diane holds an MBA from Seattle University and a master’s degree in electrical engineering from the University of Washington. She serves on the University of Washington College of Engineering Dean’s Advisory Board. She received the Shanghai government’s Magnolia Award and was recognized among the Top 10 Women in Technology.

- **DIN:** 11585200
- **Areas of expertise:** Information technology, sustainability and ESG leadership, cybersecurity, business operations, and P&L management.
