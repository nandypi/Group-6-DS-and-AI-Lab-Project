## Jayesh Sanghrajka

The announced deal is 100% net new and will begin ramping up during the year.

Management expected third-party item sales to be lower than the prior year, with a similar trend continuing. It did not expect unusual growth or elevation in third-party item sales in Q3.

The planned 8,000 net employee additions reflected the demand and supply environment. Infosys was already at 85% utilization and had onboarded 12,000 freshers, indicating business visibility.

## Salil Parekh

Management described Q2 as strong, with very good large deals and one mega deal announced after the quarter.

Management highlighted Infosys’ leadership in enterprise AI and its forward-deployed engineering capability, along with the growth of these areas.

Full-year revenue growth guidance was increased to 2%–3%. Management expected a strong Q3 and Q4.
