## Moderator

The call covered Infosys’ Q2 FY26 performance and subsequent analyst questions.

## Sandeep Mahindroo

The call included remarks from CEO and MD Salil Parekh, CFO Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Delivery Officer Satish H.C., and other leadership team members.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys reported strong Q2 performance, with market-share gains. Revenue grew 2.2% sequentially and 2.9% year-on-year in constant currency. Four of its five large industry verticals and three of its four geographies grew year-on-year in constant currency.

Operating margin expanded by 20 basis points sequentially. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion. Large-deal TCV was $3.1 billion, with 67% net new. Infosys also announced a $1.6 billion mega deal after the quarter ended and before the results announcement.

Infosys added 8,000 employees during the quarter. Client discussions showed a focus on deploying AI across enterprises for growth and cost efficiency. The company continued to scale its team of forward-deployed engineers.

Infosys described an engagement with an apparel company using generative AI and AI Ops technologies to modernize core operations, simplify IT, and increase value from data. For a telecom client, Infosys is infusing advanced intelligence across operations to accelerate innovation and deliver digital experiences.

Infosys also announced a joint venture involving Versent in Australia.

Following Q2 performance, Infosys revised its financial-year revenue-growth guidance to 2%–3% in constant currency. Operating-margin guidance remained 20%–22%.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Infosys reported its second consecutive quarter of strong growth momentum, accompanied by higher margins. The company continued investing in technology, people, and its sales engine.

Key Q2 and H1 FY26 performance details:

1. Q2 revenue exceeded $5 billion, and H1 revenue exceeded $10 billion. Q2 revenue grew 2.2% sequentially, including a 20-basis-point contribution from acquisitions in constant currency.
2. Q2 growth followed 2.6% sequential growth in Q1. H1 revenue therefore grew 3.3%.
3. Volumes remained soft, with most revenue growth driven by higher realization.
4. Financial Services and Manufacturing grew more than 5% year-on-year in constant currency in both Q2 and H1.
5. Europe grew more than 5% year-on-year in constant currency.
6. H1 gross margin was 30.8%, flat year-on-year after absorbing compensation headwinds. This reflected progress in Project Maximus.
7. Q2 operating margin increased 20 basis points sequentially to 21%. H1 operating margin was 20.9%, compared with 21.1% in H1 FY25. Sales and marketing costs grew 12.8% year-on-year in H1.
8. Utilization excluding trainees remained stable at 85%.
9. Onsite mix declined by 40 basis points during Q2 and by 60 basis points during H1.
10. Infosys hired more than 12,000 freshers during the last six months. Total headcount was 332,000, an increase of more than 8,000 during Q2. Attrition was 14.3%.
11. DSO declined by two days year-on-year to 71 days. DSO including net unbilled declined by five days year-on-year to 87 days.
12. Q2 free cash flow was $1.1 billion, equal to 131% of net profit and above 100% for the sixth consecutive quarter, bolstered by tax refunds. H1 free-cash-flow conversion was 120%.
13. Q2 large-deal TCV was $3.1 billion, with 67% net new. H1 deal wins were $6.9 billion, with net new at more than 60%. These figures did not include the mega-deal announcement involving NHS.
14. Q2 EPS grew 13% year-on-year in rupee terms to Rs. 17.6.

Q2 operating margin was 21%, an increase of 20 basis points sequentially. The major components of the sequential margin change were:

**Tailwinds**

- 60 basis points from currency movement.
- 30 basis points from Project Maximus, resulting from increased RPP through value-based selling and lean automation, partly offset by increased subcontracting and lower onsite utilization.

**Offset**

- 70 basis points from higher post-sale customer support costs and other expenses.
