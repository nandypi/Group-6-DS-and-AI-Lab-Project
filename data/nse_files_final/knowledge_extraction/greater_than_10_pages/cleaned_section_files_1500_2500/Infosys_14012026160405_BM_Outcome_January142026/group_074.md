## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan):

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan was 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were subsequently adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs is equal to the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options is the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 8,984,436 shares as of December 31, 2025 and 96,55,927 shares as of March 31, 2025 under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 2,00,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each date.

## Under the 2015 plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following fiscal 2026 grants under the 2015 Plan. The grants were effective May 2, 2025:

- 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore, under the annual performance equity grant. The RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹2 crore, under the annual performance equity ESG grant. The RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of environment, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹5 crore, under the annual performance equity TSR grant. The RSUs will vest based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of December 31, 2025. Because the service commencement date preceded the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the nine months ended December 31, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved time-based grants of 237,370 ESOPs to other KMP under the 2015 Plan. The stock options will vest over four years and will be exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price will be the market price on the grant date.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The model inputs include the share price on the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on the historical volatility of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is based on historical movements in the market prices of their publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
