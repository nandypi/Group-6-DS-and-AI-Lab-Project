## Basis for Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP conducted an audit of the interim condensed standalone financial statements in accordance with applicable auditing standards and stated that it was independent of the Company under the ethical requirements of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). The auditor stated that sufficient and appropriate audit evidence had been obtained as a basis for the audit opinion.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Interim Condensed Standalone Financial Statements

The Board of Directors is responsible for preparing and presenting interim condensed standalone financial statements that give a true and fair view of the Company’s financial position, financial performance, total comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows in accordance with Ind AS 34 and other generally accepted accounting principles in India.

The Board’s responsibilities include:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records.
- Safeguarding the Company’s assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls.
- Assessing the appropriateness of using the going-concern basis of accounting.
- Overseeing the Company’s financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Interim Condensed Standalone Financial Statements

The auditor’s objectives are to obtain reasonable assurance that the interim condensed standalone financial statements are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor’s report containing the auditor’s opinion.

The audit responsibilities include:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement.
- Designing and performing audit procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal financial controls for audit planning.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates.
- Assessing the appropriateness of management’s use of the going-concern basis of accounting.
- Evaluating whether any material uncertainty exists that could cast significant doubt on the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the interim condensed standalone financial statements.

## Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP

The auditor defined materiality as the magnitude of misstatements that, individually or in aggregate, could reasonably influence the economic decisions of users of the financial statements. Quantitative and qualitative factors are considered when planning the audit, evaluating audit results, and assessing identified misstatements.

The auditor also communicates with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, significant deficiencies in internal control, auditor independence, and related safeguards where applicable.

The audit report is dated January 14, 2026, and was issued by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants, Firm Registration No. 117366W/W-100018.
