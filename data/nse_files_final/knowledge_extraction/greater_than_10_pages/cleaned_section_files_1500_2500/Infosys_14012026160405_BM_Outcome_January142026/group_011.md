## b) Buyback of equity shares

The shareholders approved the Board-recommended buyback through e-voting by postal ballot. The results were declared on November 6, 2025.

The buyback comprised the purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800 per equity share.

The offer was made to all eligible equity shareholders as of the record date, November 14, 2025, on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. This included shareholders who became equity shareholders by cancelling American Depository Shares and withdrawing the underlying equity shares.

The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The Company concluded the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025. All 100,000,000 equity shares were bought back and extinguished.

The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs. It was funded from free reserves, including the securities premium, in accordance with Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

In accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, through an appropriation from the general reserve during the quarter ended December 31, 2025.

## c) Impact of Labour Codes

On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Code on Social Security, 2020; and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020. These Labour Codes consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during and after employment.

The Labour Codes introduce changes including a uniform definition of wages and enhanced leave-related benefits. The Company assessed the financial implications, resulting in an increase in the gratuity liability arising from past service cost and an increase in leave liability of ₹1,146 crore.

The Company presented this incremental amount as “Impact of Labour Codes” under “Exceptional Item” in the Condensed Standalone Statement of Profit and Loss for the three months and nine months ended December 31, 2025, because the impact of the new legislation is non-recurring.

The Company continues to monitor developments relating to the Labour Codes and will evaluate any impact on the measurement of employee benefit liabilities.

## d) Update on stock grants

On January 14, 2026, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved annual time-based stock incentives in the form of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) for Salil Parekh, CEO and MD.

The RSUs had a market value of ₹3 crore as of the grant date and were approved under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan, in accordance with the terms of his employment agreement. The RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement.

The RSUs will be granted effective February 1, 2026. The number of RSUs will be calculated based on the market price at the close of trading on the day immediately preceding the grant date. The exercise price will equal the par value of the share.

## 2. Segment Reporting

The Company publishes standalone financial statements along with consolidated financial statements. In accordance with Ind AS 108, Operating Segments, segment information is disclosed in the audited interim consolidated financial statements.

Accordingly, segment information is provided in the audited consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025.

## IFRS

### Strong Q3 Performance: Sequential Revenue Growth of 0.6% in CC, Large Deal Wins of $4.8 billion, Revenue Guidance for FY26 Revised to 3.0%-3.5%

Infosys reported Q3 revenue of $5,099 million, representing year-on-year growth of 1.7% and sequential growth of 0.6% in constant currency.

Reported IFRS operating margin was 18.4%. Adjusted operating margin increased 0.2 percentage points sequentially to 21.2%.

Free cash flow was $915 million. Adjusted free cash flow was $965 million, representing 112.8% of adjusted net profit.

Total contract value of large deal wins was $4.8 billion, with net new work comprising 57%. Headcount increased by 5,043.

Revenue for the nine months ended December 31, 2025 grew 2.8% year on year in constant currency. Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.0%, and adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.

Management referenced enterprise AI capabilities through Infosys Topaz, client AI partnerships, delivery capabilities, and employee reskilling.

## Guidance for FY26:

- Revenue growth of 3.0%-3.5% in constant currency
- Operating margin of 20%-22%
