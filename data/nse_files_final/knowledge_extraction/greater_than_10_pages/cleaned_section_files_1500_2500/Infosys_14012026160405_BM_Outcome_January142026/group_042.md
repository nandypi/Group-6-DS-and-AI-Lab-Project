## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the period is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately. The Group considers highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited provides consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services that support clients' digital transformation. Infosys, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts, is referred to as the “Group.”

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares, representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group's interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Company's Board of Directors on January 14, 2026.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared in compliance with IAS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

The statements use the historical cost convention and accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and the defined benefit liability/(asset), which is recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the Company's Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2025.

Accounting policies were applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard was initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard required a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information used in preparation of the interim condensed consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from source figures and rounded to the nearest digits. Consequently, quarter figures may not always add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.
