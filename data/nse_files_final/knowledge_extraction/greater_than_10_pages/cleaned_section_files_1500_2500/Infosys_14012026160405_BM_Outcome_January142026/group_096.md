## 2.14 PROVISIONS

### Accounting policy

A provision is recognized when, as a result of a past event, the Group has a present legal or constructive obligation that is reasonably estimable and an outflow of economic benefits to settle the obligation is probable. Provisions are determined by discounting expected future cash flows at a pre-tax rate reflecting current market assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific to the liability.

The Group recognizes a reimbursement asset only when it is virtually certain that the reimbursement will be received if the Group settles the obligation.

### a. Post sales client support

The Group provides clients with fixed-period post-sales support on fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts. Costs associated with these services are accrued when the related revenues are recorded and included in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss. The Group estimates these costs based on historical experience and periodically reviews the estimates for material changes in assumptions and likelihood of occurrence.

### b. Onerous contracts

Provisions for onerous contracts are recognized when the expected benefits from a contract are lower than the unavoidable costs of meeting future obligations. Provisions for estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable, based on estimated efforts or costs to complete the contract.

The provision is measured at the present value of the lower of the expected cost of terminating the contract and the expected net cost of continuing with it. Before establishing a provision, the Group recognizes any impairment loss on assets associated with the contract.

### McCamish Cybersecurity incident

In November 2023, certain systems of Infosys McCamish Systems LLC (“McCamish”), a subsidiary of Infosys BPM Limited, were encrypted by ransomware, resulting in the non-availability of certain applications and systems. McCamish initiated its incident response and engaged cybersecurity and other specialists to investigate, remediate, and restore the impacted applications and systems.

By December 31, 2023, McCamish had substantially remediated and restored the affected applications and systems. Third-party investigations assessed whether company or customer data had been subject to unauthorized access or exfiltration. McCamish identified corporate customers and individuals whose information was subject to unauthorized access and exfiltration.

Between March 6, 2024 and July 25, 2024, six actions were filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia against McCamish. The actions were consolidated, and a consolidated class action complaint was filed on November 7, 2024, on behalf of U.S. residents whose personally identifiable information was compromised in the incident, including individuals who received an incident notice.

On December 20, 2024, the Court granted a joint motion to stay proceedings pending mediation. On March 13, 2025, McCamish and the plaintiffs reached an in-principle agreement for a proposed settlement covering the class action lawsuits against McCamish and seven class action lawsuits arising from the incident that were filed against McCamish’s customers.

Under the settlement terms, McCamish agreed to pay $17.5 million, approximately ₹150 crore, into a settlement fund. On December 18, 2025, the Court granted final approval of the settlement. If the settlement is not appealed within 30 days, it will become effective and resolve the allegations in the class action lawsuits without admission of liability.

During the three months ended March 31, 2025, McCamish recorded an accrual of $17.5 million, approximately ₹150 crore, for the settlement and recognized an insurance reimbursement receivable of $17 million, approximately ₹145 crore. The reimbursement was offset against the settlement expense in the Statement of Comprehensive Income. McCamish may incur additional indeterminable costs, including indemnities or damages and claims.

### Government Investigation

The U.S. Department of Justice (“DOJ”) is investigating how the Company classified certain H-1B visa-recipient employees working for one of its clients in immigration documents filed with certain U.S. government authorities. The Company is discussing the matter with the DOJ and continues its own inquiry. The outcome, including whether it could have a material adverse effect on the Company’s business and results of operations, cannot currently be predicted.

### Others

The Group is subject to legal proceedings and claims arising in the ordinary course of business. Management reasonably expects that these matters, when concluded, may not have a material adverse effect on the Group’s results of operations or financial condition.

## 2.15 INCOME TAXES

### Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss, except for items recognized directly in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts, subject to applicable exceptions. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized in income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment.

A deferred tax asset is recognized when it is probable that future taxable profit will be available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to offset the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

The interim income tax provision is based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions on the exercise of employee share options that exceed compensation charged to income are credited to equity.
