## Notes

1. On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Labour Codes, which consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during employment and post-employment. The provisions include a uniform definition of wages and enhanced leave-related benefits. The adjustments represent an increase in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and an increase in leave liability totaling $143 million (₹1,289 crore), recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.
2. Revenue growth in reported currency includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant currency growth is calculated by comparing current-period revenues in local currencies converted to US dollars using prior-period exchange rates with prior-period reported revenues.
3. Quarter and nine-month figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Accordingly, quarterly figures added to figures reported for previous quarters may not always equal the nine-month figures reported in this statement.

## Consolidated Financial Results

### Auditor’s Opinion

The consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025, were audited and submitted pursuant to Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

The auditor stated that the results:

- Include the financial results of the subsidiaries listed in the report’s annexure.
- Are presented in accordance with Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- Present the consolidated net profit, consolidated other comprehensive income, and other financial information in accordance with Ind AS 34, applicable rules, and generally accepted accounting principles in India.

### Basis for Opinion and Financial Reporting Responsibilities

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the applicable Act. The auditor stated that it was independent of the Group under the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India Code of Ethics and that sufficient and appropriate audit evidence was obtained.

The Consolidated Financial Results were the responsibility of the Company’s Board of Directors and were compiled from the audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the three months and nine months ended December 31, 2025. The boards of the companies in the Group are responsible for:

- Preparing and presenting the financial results in accordance with applicable accounting standards and regulations.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records and safeguarding Group assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies and making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining effective internal financial controls.
- Assessing the ability of the respective entities to continue as going concerns.
- Overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.

### Auditor’s Responsibilities

The auditor’s objective was to obtain reasonable assurance that the Consolidated Financial Results were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an audit report containing the opinion.

As part of the audit, the auditor:

- Identified and assessed risks of material misstatement and performed procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtained an understanding of internal control relevant to the audit.
- Evaluated the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates.
- Evaluated the appropriateness and reasonableness of disclosures made under Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
