The aggregate depreciation expense is included in cost of sales in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income. Repairs and maintenance costs are recognized in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income when incurred.

Consequent to the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Amendment Rules, 2021, the Company was required to transfer its CSR capital assets installed before January 2021. The Company incorporated a subsidiary, Infosys Green Forum (IGF), under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, for this purpose. During the year ended March 31, 2022, the Company completed the transfer of assets after obtaining the required regulatory approvals, as applicable.

During fiscal 2024, IGF’s application for regularization of its provisional registration was rejected, and its registration was cancelled by an order dated March 26, 2024, issued by the Income Tax Commissioner (Exemption). IGF has filed an appeal before the Income Tax Tribunal against the order.

The Group had contractual commitments for capital expenditure, primarily comprising commitments for infrastructure facilities and computer equipment, amounting to ₹1,137 crore as at December 31, 2025, compared with ₹935 crore as at March 31, 2025.
