## Strong Q3 Performance: Sequential Revenue Growth of 0.6% in CC, Large Deal Wins of $4.8 billion Revenue Guidance for FY 26 revised to 3.0%-3.5%

For the quarter ended December 31, 2025, Infosys reported revenues of $5,099 million, representing year-over-year growth of 1.7% and sequential growth of 0.6% in constant currency. Reported IFRS operating margin was 18.4%, while adjusted operating margin was 21.2%. Free cash flow was $915 million and adjusted free cash flow was $965 million, representing 112.8% of adjusted net profit. Large deal total contract value was $4.8 billion, with 57% net new. Headcount increased by 5,043.

For the nine months ended December 31, 2025, revenue grew 2.8% year over year in constant currency. Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.0% and adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.

Infosys stated that enterprise AI capabilities through Infosys Topaz were contributing to client engagements and that the company was reskilling and transforming its workforce for an AI-augmented operating environment.

## Guidance for FY26

- Revenue growth of 3.0%-3.5% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%-22%.²

## Key Highlights

### For the Quarter Ended December 31, 2025

- Revenues grew 1.7% year over year and 0.6% sequentially in constant-currency terms.
- Reported IFRS revenues were $5,099 million, with year-over-year growth of 3.2%.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 18.4%; adjusted operating margin was 21.2%.
- Reported IFRS basic EPS was $0.18; adjusted basic EPS was $0.21.
- Free cash flow was $915 million; adjusted free cash flow was $965 million.
- Adjusted free cash flow conversion was 112.8% of adjusted net profit.

### For the Nine Months Ended December 31, 2025

- Revenues grew 2.8% year over year in constant-currency terms.
- Reported IFRS revenues were $15,117 million, with year-over-year growth of 3.9%.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.0%; adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.
- Reported IFRS basic EPS was $0.58; adjusted basic EPS was $0.60.
- Free cash flow was $2,900 million; adjusted free cash flow was $2,950 million.
- Adjusted free cash flow conversion was 117.8% of adjusted net profit.

¹ Adjusted financial measures are non-IFRS financial measures that exclude the impact of provisions arising from the Government of India's Labour Code notifications for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025.

² Operating margin guidance for FY26 excludes the $143 million Labour Code adjustment in the current quarter.
