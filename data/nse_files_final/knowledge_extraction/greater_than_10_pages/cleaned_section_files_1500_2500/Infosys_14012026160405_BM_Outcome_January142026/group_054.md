## 2.12 Income Taxes

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income, except for amounts relating to items recognized directly in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements, except when the deferred tax arises from the initial recognition of goodwill or an asset or liability in a transaction that is not a business combination and affects neither accounting nor taxable profit or loss at the time of the transaction.

Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized as income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment.

Deferred tax assets are recognized when it is probable that future taxable profit will be available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to offset the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

Income tax provisions for interim periods are based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions earned on the exercise of employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.

Income tax expense for the three months ended December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024 includes a reversal, net of provisions, of ₹77 crore and provisions, net of reversals, of ₹106 crore, respectively. For the nine months ended December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024, income tax expense includes provisions, net of reversals, of ₹38 crore and ₹249 crore, respectively. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily relate to the adjudication of disputed matters following the filing of tax returns and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

Deferred income tax for the three and nine months ended December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024 substantially relates to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

The Company’s Advanced Pricing Arrangement with the Internal Revenue Service for US branch income tax expired in March 2021. The Company has applied for renewal, and US taxable income is currently based on its best estimate determined using the expected value method.

As at December 31, 2025, claims against the Group not acknowledged as debts from income tax authorities amounted to ₹2,054 crore. As at March 31, 2025, such claims amounted to ₹1,933 crore.

Amounts paid to statutory authorities against tax claims were ₹1,693 crore as at December 31, 2025 and ₹4,199 crore as at March 31, 2025.

The claims primarily represent demands arising from assessment proceedings under the Income Tax Act, 1961. They relate to issues including the disallowance of software expenditure as capital in nature and payments to associated enterprises treated as liable for withholding taxes. These matters are pending before various income tax authorities. Management and its tax advisors expect the Group’s position to be upheld on ultimate resolution and do not expect a material adverse effect on the Group’s financial position and results of operations.

## 2.13 Earnings per equity share

## Accounting Policy

Basic earnings per equity share is computed by dividing net profit attributable to the equity holders of the Group by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the period.

Diluted earnings per equity share is computed using the weighted average number of equity shares considered for basic earnings per equity share, adjusted for the weighted average number of equity shares that could have been issued upon conversion of all dilutive potential equity shares. The adjustment reflects proceeds receivable had the shares been issued at fair value, based on the average market value of outstanding equity shares.

Dilutive potential equity shares are deemed converted at the beginning of the period unless issued at a later date and are determined independently for each period presented.

The number of equity shares and potentially dilutive equity shares is adjusted retrospectively for share splits and bonus share issues, including changes effected before approval of the financial statements by the Board of Directors.
