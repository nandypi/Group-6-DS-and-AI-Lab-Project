## Apurva Prasad

Apurva Prasad asked whether AI compression, AI embedded in services, or contract structures were affecting the direction of the onsite delivery mix.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Management attributed the onsite mix changes to multiple factors:

- The overall operating environment.
- Visa situations in some countries.
- Infosys's initiative to deliver more work from offshore.
- Lower discretionary spending, which generally requires a higher onsite presence.

Third-party costs were expected to remain in a similar range in FY27. The FY26 exit was expected to result in approximately 40–50 basis points of lower onsite mix, with the trend expected to continue to some extent. Management said the FY27 exit onsite mix was difficult to project.

## Salil Parekh

Management's closing comments highlighted the following:

- FY26 growth of 3.1%.
- FY26 margin of 21%.
- Large deals totaling close to $15 billion.
- Growth guidance for the coming year, including a mix of growth drivers and compression.
- Management stated that the underlying growth was larger on a like-for-like basis after adjusting for one-off technical factors.
- Infosys's AI services approach and strategy were resonating well with clients.
- All six areas were represented in the pipeline.
- Partnerships included AI foundation model companies and other AI tool companies.
