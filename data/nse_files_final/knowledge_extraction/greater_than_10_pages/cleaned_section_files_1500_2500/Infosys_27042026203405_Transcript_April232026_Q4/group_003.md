## Shilpa Phadnis

How does Infosys's deal pipeline lend itself to AI deflation, and how will the company offset productivity demands from clients?

## Salil Parekh

Infosys identified three growth areas:

1. AI services, which are growing well.
2. Large deals, including the net-new portion that contributes to revenue growth in subsequent years.
3. Expansion into clients where Infosys currently has a smaller presence but strong relationships.

Infosys is investing in AI and in programs targeting large companies where it has a smaller presence. At the same time, clients are seeking AI-driven productivity improvements. The Topaz Fabric provides visibility into what is currently achievable and what may become achievable in the future. Technology and operations deals are applying these capabilities immediately.

In many pipeline deals, productivity improvements are accompanied by adjacent scope expansion or consolidation, supported by Infosys's credibility and client relationships. Like-for-like growth in the previous year was higher than 3.1%. Management stated that the growth portion of the business is currently larger than the compression caused by productivity improvements.

## Rishi Basu

The next question is from Padmini Dhruvaraj of The New Indian Express.

## Padmini Dhruvaraj

How is the BFSI sector adopting AI agents, and are tighter regulations creating difficulties? Is AI compressing the traditional IT-services model, and which revenue streams are at risk of being cannibalized?

## Salil Parekh

Financial-services clients are adopting AI quickly. Examples include:

- AI agents for bank KYC and AML processes, which are generating work for Infosys.
- AI applications in credit.
- AI-enabled development of new capabilities.
- Use of foundation models for legacy modernization, including migration from older-generation technology to current-generation technology.

Infosys has partnered with foundation-model companies and other AI leaders, which management said is supporting its position in financial services. Growth in the financial-services business is accelerating, despite compression from client productivity requirements and services that can be more easily delivered using AI.

Compression is occurring in some services while other services are growing. Areas where foundation models and AI tools are particularly efficient include some technology-services work and BPM. Overall growth reflects the balance between these areas.

## Rishi Basu

The next question is from Shristi Achar of The Economic Times.

## Shristi Achar

Was the sequential contraction in total contract value (TCV) caused by reduced discretionary spending or AI-led deflation? Also, what is causing regulatory delays in closing the Versent acquisition?

## Salil Parekh

For large deals, Infosys reported $3.2 billion during the quarter. Large-deal results can vary from quarter to quarter, and management said it did not observe an unusual change in large-deal value.

Annual large-deal value was $14.9 billion, higher than the previous year. Large deals are defined as deals with a value above $50 million.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

The previous quarter included a mega deal with a UK client, which increased the overall figure. Excluding that deal, the quarterly numbers were comparable. Mega deals are lumpy and do not occur every quarter.

Infosys reported approximately $15 billion of large deals for the year, with 55% net new and a 28% year-over-year increase.

The delay in the Versent acquisition is related to regulatory approvals. Infosys has received questions or queries from regulators and has responded to them. The approval process can be unpredictable.

## Shristi Achar

What direct impact are geopolitical conflicts having on client decision-making and potential delays?

## Salil Parekh

Clients are assessing how geopolitical developments unfold. Management said the current situation appeared to be stabilizing. If underlying conditions in Western economies remain resilient, technology spending could receive support.

Within the United States, Infosys is performing very well in certain pockets and industries. The company is also doing well in newer geographies such as Japan.
