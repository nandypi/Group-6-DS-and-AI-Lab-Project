## Trade Receivables and Contract Balances

The timing of revenue recognition, billings, and cash collections results in receivables, unbilled revenue, and unearned revenue on the Group's Consolidated Balance Sheet. Amounts are billed as work progresses according to contractual terms, either at periodic intervals, such as monthly or quarterly, or upon achievement of contractual milestones.

Trade receivables are unconditional rights to consideration. Unbilled revenue from time-and-material contracts and fixed-price maintenance contracts is classified as a financial asset when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

For other fixed-price contracts, invoicing is based on contractual milestones. Because the timing of revenue recognition differs from the timing of invoicing, unbilled revenue for these contracts is classified as a non-financial contract asset when the right to consideration depends on completion of contractual milestones.

Invoicing in excess of earnings is classified as unearned revenue. Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are presented net of impairment in the consolidated balance sheet.

## 2.18 Equity

### Ordinary Shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity. Incremental costs directly attributable to issuing new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of related tax effects.

### Treasury Shares

When an entity within the Group purchases the Company's ordinary shares, the consideration paid, including directly attributable incremental costs, is presented as a deduction from total equity until the shares are cancelled, sold, or reissued. When treasury shares are subsequently sold or reissued, the amount received is recognized as an increase in equity, and the resulting surplus or deficit is transferred to or from share premium.

### Share Premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value are classified as share premium. Share-based compensation recognized in net profit in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income is also credited to share premium. Amounts have been utilized for bonus issues and share buybacks from the share premium account.

The Company has one class of shares, referred to as equity shares, with a par value of ₹5 per share.

### Other Reserves

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve was created from the profit of the eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used by the Company to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

### Capital Redemption Reserve

Under Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

### Cash Flow Hedge Reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the reserve is transferred to net profit in the interim condensed consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income when the related forecast transaction occurs.

### Other Components of Equity

Other components of equity include currency translation; remeasurement of net defined benefit liabilities or assets; fair value changes of equity instruments measured through other comprehensive income; and changes in the fair value of investments, net of taxes.

## 2.18.1 Voting

Each holder of equity shares is entitled to one vote per share. Equity shares represented by American Depositary Shares (ADSs) carry similar voting and dividend rights as other equity shares. Each ADS represents one underlying equity share.

## 2.18.2 Liquidation

In the event of liquidation, shareholders are entitled to receive the Company's remaining assets after distribution of all preferential amounts. No such preferential amounts currently exist other than amounts held by irrevocable controlled trusts. Distribution is proportional to the number of equity shares held. For irrevocable controlled trusts, the corpus would be settled in favor of the beneficiaries.

## 2.18.3 Share Options

Holders of options issued under the Company's share option plans have no voting, dividend, or liquidation rights.

## 2.18.4 Share Capital and Share Premium

The Company has one class of shares, referred to as equity shares, with a par value of ₹5 per share. Controlled trusts held 9,091,403 shares as of September 30, 2025 and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025.

## 2.18.5 Capital Allocation Policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, based on the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Update on Buyback Announced in September 2025

At its meeting on September 11, 2025, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 per share for ₹18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

If approved, the buyback would comprise the purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company's total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis. The offer price would be ₹1,800 per equity share.

The buyback is proposed to be made from all eligible equity shareholders, including those who become equity shareholders as of the record date by cancelling ADSs and withdrawing the underlying equity shares. The purchase would be made on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. The record date is to be determined by the Board or Buyback Committee.

The Company sent notice to shareholders as of September 26, 2025 seeking approval through postal ballot. Voting was expected to end on November 4, 2025.

The Company's capital management objective is to maintain its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. The Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares.

As of September 30, 2025, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.
