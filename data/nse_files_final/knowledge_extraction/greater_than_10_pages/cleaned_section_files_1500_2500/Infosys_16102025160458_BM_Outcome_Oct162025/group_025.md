## 2.3.5 Impairment

The Group recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenue that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

- Trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component are measured at lifetime ECL.
- Other financial assets are measured at 12-month ECL unless credit risk has significantly increased since initial recognition, in which case lifetime ECL is used.
- Allowances are based on historical loss experience adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions, including conditions in the industries and countries in which the Group operates.
- The ECL adjustment required at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.
