## Fair value hierarchy

- **Level 1:** Quoted prices (unadjusted) in active markets for identical assets or liabilities.
- **Level 2:** Inputs other than quoted prices included within Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly as prices or indirectly as inputs derived from prices.
- **Level 3:** Inputs for assets or liabilities that are not based on observable market data.

A one percentage point change in the unobservable inputs used in the fair valuation of Level 3 assets and liabilities does not have a significant impact on their value.

The majority of the Group’s investments are fair valued based on Level 1 or Level 2 inputs. These investments primarily include liquid mutual fund units, target maturity fund units, quoted debt securities, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, and quoted bonds issued by government and quasi-government organizations. The Group considers counterparty risks using multiple criteria, including Tier I capital, capital adequacy ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and deposit base of banks and financial institutions. These risks are monitored regularly under the Group’s risk management program.
