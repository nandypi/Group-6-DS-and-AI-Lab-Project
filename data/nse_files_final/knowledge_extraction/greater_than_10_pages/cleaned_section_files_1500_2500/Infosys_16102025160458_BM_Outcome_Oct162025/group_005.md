## INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT ON THE AUDIT OF QUARTERLY AND HALF YEARLY STANDALONE FINANCIAL RESULTS

## TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF INFOSYS LIMITED

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the accompanying statement of standalone financial results of Infosys Limited for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025, under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

In the auditors' opinion, and based on the information and explanations provided:

- The statement was presented in accordance with Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- The statement gave a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles laid down in Indian Accounting Standard 34, “Interim Financial Reporting” (“Ind AS 34”), prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, together with the relevant rules and other accounting principles generally accepted in India, of the net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information of

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013.

The auditors stated that they were independent of the Company in accordance with the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, together with the ethical requirements relevant to the audit of the standalone financial results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025. They also stated that they had fulfilled their other ethical responsibilities and believed that the audit evidence obtained provided a basis for their opinion.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for the Statement

The standalone financial results were the responsibility of the Company's Board of Directors and were approved by them for issuance. The statement was compiled from the related audited interim condensed standalone financial statements for the three months and six months ended September 30, 2025.

The Board's responsibilities included:

- Preparing and presenting statements that give a true and fair view of net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information in accordance with Ind AS 34 and applicable accounting principles.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls that operated effectively to ensure the accuracy and completeness of accounting records and the preparation and presentation of statements free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.
- Assessing the Company's ability to continue as a going concern and disclosing relevant matters, unless the Company intended to liquidate, cease operations, or had no realistic alternative but to do so.
- Overseeing the Company's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for audit of the Standalone Financial Results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025

The auditors' objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the statement as a whole was free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing their opinion. Reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but does not guarantee that an audit conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing will always detect a material misstatement.

As part of the audit, the auditors stated that they exercised professional judgment and maintained professional skepticism. Their responsibilities included:

- Identifying and assessing the risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error, designing and performing responsive audit procedures, and obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtaining an understanding of internal control relevant to the audit to design appropriate audit procedures, without expressing an opinion on the effectiveness of those controls.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by the Board of Directors.
- Evaluating the appropriateness and reasonableness of disclosures made by the Board of Directors under Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- Concluding on the appropriateness of the Board's use of the going-concern basis of accounting and whether a material uncertainty existed that could cast significant doubt on the Company's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Drawing attention to related disclosures or modifying the audit opinion if a material uncertainty existed and the disclosures were inadequate. The auditors noted that future events or conditions could cause the Company to cease to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the statement and whether it represented the underlying transactions and events fairly.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence to express an opinion on the statement.
