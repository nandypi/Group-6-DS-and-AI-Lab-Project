## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the period is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Cash flows from operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately.

The Group considers highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (“the Company” or “Infosys”) provides consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services to help clients execute digital transformation strategies.

Infosys, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts, is referred to as the “Group.” The company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India, with its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Its primary listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares, representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the company’s Board of Directors on October 16, 2025.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared in compliance with IAS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

They were prepared under the historical cost convention and on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of defined benefit obligations less the fair value of plan assets.

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2025.

Accounting policies were applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard was initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard required a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information used in preparing the interim condensed consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from source data and rounded to the nearest digits. As a result, quarterly figures may not always add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.

## 1.3 Basis of consolidation

Infosys consolidates entities that it owns or controls. The interim condensed consolidated financial statements comprise the financial statements of the company, its controlled trusts, and its subsidiaries.

Control exists when the parent has power over an entity, is exposed or has rights to variable returns from its involvement with the entity, and can affect those returns through its power over the entity. Power is demonstrated through existing rights that provide the ability to direct relevant activities that significantly affect the entity’s returns.

Subsidiaries are consolidated from the date control commences until the date control ceases. Group companies’ financial statements are consolidated on a line-by-line basis. Intra-group balances and transactions, including unrealized gains or losses, are eliminated on consolidation.

The consolidated financial statements apply uniform accounting policies across the Group. Non-controlling interests represent the portion of the net profit or loss and net assets of subsidiaries that are not directly or indirectly owned or controlled by the company.

## 1.4 Use of estimates and judgments

Preparing the interim condensed consolidated financial statements in conformity with IFRS requires management to make estimates, judgments, and assumptions. These affect the application of accounting policies, reported amounts of assets and liabilities, disclosures of contingent assets and liabilities, and reported revenues and expenses.

Accounting policies requiring critical accounting estimates involve complex and subjective judgments and assumptions, as disclosed in Note 1.5. Accounting estimates may change from period to period, and actual results may differ from those estimates. Changes in estimates and judgments are reflected in the period in which management becomes aware of changed circumstances. If material, their effects are disclosed in the notes to the interim condensed consolidated financial statements.
