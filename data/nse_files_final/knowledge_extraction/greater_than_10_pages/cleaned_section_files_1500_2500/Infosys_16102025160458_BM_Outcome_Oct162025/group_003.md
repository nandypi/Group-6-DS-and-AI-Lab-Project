## CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF INFOSYS LIMITED

## Opinion

The consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025 were audited and submitted pursuant to Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

In the auditor’s opinion, the statement:

- Includes the financial results of the subsidiaries listed in the annexure.
- Is presented in accordance with Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- Gives a true and fair view of the consolidated net profit and consolidated other comprehensive income in conformity with Ind AS 34, “Interim Financial Reporting,” prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, and other generally accepted accounting principles in India.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. The auditors stated that they were independent of the Group in accordance with the ICAI Code of Ethics and applicable ethical requirements, and that sufficient and appropriate audit evidence was obtained to provide a basis for the audit opinion.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for the Statement

The consolidated financial results were the responsibility of Infosys Limited’s Board of Directors and were approved by them for issuance. The statement was compiled from the audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the three months and six months ended September 30, 2025.

The Board of Directors was responsible for preparing and presenting the results in accordance with Ind AS 34, Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, applicable rules, generally accepted accounting principles in India, and Regulation 33 of the Listing Regulations. The respective boards of the companies included in the Group were also responsible for maintaining accounting records, safeguarding assets, preventing and detecting fraud and irregularities, applying appropriate accounting policies, making reasonable estimates, and maintaining effective internal financial controls.

The respective boards were responsible for preparing the financial results using the going-concern basis of accounting unless they intended to liquidate their entities, cease operations, or had no realistic alternative but to do so. They were also responsible for overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for audit of the Consolidated Financial Results for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025

The auditors’ objective was to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the consolidated financial results were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor’s report containing their opinion.

The audit included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement and performing procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal controls for designing appropriate audit procedures.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by the Board of Directors.
- Evaluating the appropriateness and reasonableness of disclosures required under Regulation 33 of the LODR Regulations.
- Assessing the appropriateness of using the going-concern basis of accounting and whether any material uncertainty existed regarding the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the statement.
- Performing procedures in accordance with the SEBI circular issued under Regulation 33(8) of the LODR Regulations, to the extent applicable.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group.

Materiality was considered based on the magnitude of misstatements that could influence the economic decisions of reasonably knowledgeable users. The auditors communicated with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, internal control deficiencies, independence requirements, and related matters.
