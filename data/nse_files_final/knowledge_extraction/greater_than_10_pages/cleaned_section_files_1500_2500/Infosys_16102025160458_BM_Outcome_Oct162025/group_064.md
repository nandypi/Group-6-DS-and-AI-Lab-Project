## Condensed Standalone Statement of Cash Flows

### Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the period is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. Highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

## Overview and Notes to the Interim Condensed Standalone Financial Statements

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (“the Company” or “Infosys”) provides consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services to enable clients to execute digital transformation strategies.

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary equity listings are on BSE Ltd. and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares representing equity shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The interim condensed standalone financial statements were approved for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on October 16, 2025.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed standalone financial statements were prepared in compliance with Indian Accounting Standard (Ind AS) 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, under the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

The statements were prepared in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, and guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India. They do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the standalone financial statements and related notes in the Company’s Annual Report for the year ended March 31, 2025.

The Ind AS are prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and relevant amendment rules issued thereafter.

Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting policy is required. Material accounting policy information used in preparing the interim condensed standalone financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Accordingly, quarterly figures added to figures reported for previous quarters may not always equal the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.
