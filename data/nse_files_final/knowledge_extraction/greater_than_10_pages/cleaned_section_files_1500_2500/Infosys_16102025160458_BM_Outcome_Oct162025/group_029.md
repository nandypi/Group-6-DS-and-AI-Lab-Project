*During the three months and six months ended September 30, 2024, certain assets that were not in use, with gross book values of $12 million and $27 million, respectively, were retired. The net book value of these assets was nil.*

The aggregate depreciation expense is included in cost of sales in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income. Repairs and maintenance costs are recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income when incurred.

Consequent to the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Amendment Rules, 2021 (the “Rules”), the Company was required to transfer its CSR capital assets installed prior to January 2021. For this purpose, the Company incorporated a subsidiary, Infosys Green Forum (IGF), under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013.

During the year ended March 31, 2022, the Company completed the transfer of the assets after obtaining the required approvals from regulatory authorities, as applicable. During fiscal 2024, IGF’s application for regularization of its provisional registration was rejected, and its registration was cancelled by an order dated March 26, 2024, issued by the Income Tax Commissioner (Exemption). IGF has filed an appeal before the Income Tax Tribunal against the order.

The Group had contractual commitments for capital expenditure, primarily comprising commitments for infrastructure facilities and computer equipment, of $126 million as at September 30, 2025 and $109 million as at March 31, 2025.
