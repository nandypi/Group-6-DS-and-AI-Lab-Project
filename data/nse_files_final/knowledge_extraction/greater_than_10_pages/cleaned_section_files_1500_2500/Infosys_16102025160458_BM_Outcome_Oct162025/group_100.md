## 2.20 EARNINGS PER EQUITY SHARE

### Accounting policy

Basic earnings per equity share is computed by dividing the net profit attributable to the equity holders of the Group by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the period.

Diluted earnings per equity share is computed by dividing the net profit attributable to the equity holders of the Group by the weighted average number of equity shares considered for deriving basic earnings per equity share, together with the weighted average number of equity shares that could have been issued upon conversion of all dilutive potential equity shares.

Dilutive potential equity shares are adjusted for the proceeds receivable had the shares been issued at fair value, based on the average market value of the outstanding equity shares. Dilutive potential equity shares are deemed converted at the beginning of the period unless issued at a later date and are determined independently for each period presented.

The number of equity shares and potentially dilutive equity shares are adjusted retrospectively for all periods presented for share splits and bonus share issues, including changes effected before approval of the financial statements by the Board of Directors.

## 2.21 CONTINGENT LIABILITIES AND COMMITMENTS

### Accounting policy

A contingent liability is:

- A possible obligation arising from past events whose existence will be confirmed only by the occurrence or non-occurrence of one or more uncertain future events not wholly within the entity’s control; or
- A present obligation arising from past events that is not recognized because an outflow of resources embodying economic benefits is not probable, or because the amount cannot be measured with sufficient reliability.

### 2.21.2 Legal Proceedings

#### McCamish Cybersecurity incident

In November 2023, certain systems of Infosys McCamish Systems LLC (“McCamish”), a subsidiary of Infosys BPM Limited and wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, were encrypted by ransomware. This resulted in the non-availability of certain applications and systems.

By December 31, 2023, McCamish had substantially remediated and restored the affected applications and systems with the assistance of external specialists. A third-party cybersecurity firm investigated whether company or customer data had been subject to unauthorized access or exfiltration. McCamish also engaged a third-party eDiscovery vendor to assess the extent and nature of the data exposure. Corporate customers and individuals whose information was subject to unauthorized access and exfiltration were identified.

From March 6, 2024 through July 25, 2024, six actions were filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia against McCamish. The actions arose from the cybersecurity incident initially disclosed on November 3, 2023. The actions were consolidated, and a consolidated class action complaint was filed on November 7, 2024.

On December 20, 2024, the Court granted the parties’ joint motion to stay proceedings pending mediation. On March 13, 2025, McCamish and the plaintiffs reached an in-principle agreement covering the proposed settlement of the class action lawsuits against McCamish and seven class action lawsuits arising from the incident that had been filed against McCamish’s customers.

On May 9, 2025, McCamish and the plaintiffs entered into a definitive settlement agreement, and the plaintiffs moved for preliminary approval. Under the settlement terms, McCamish agreed to pay $17.5 million, approximately ₹150 crore, into a settlement fund. On July 16, 2025, the Court granted preliminary approval. The settlement remains subject to final court approval and, if approved, will resolve all allegations in the class action lawsuits without admission of liability.

During the three months ended March 31, 2025, McCamish recorded an accrual of $17.5 million, approximately ₹150 crore, for the settlement and recognized an insurance reimbursement receivable of $17 million, approximately ₹145 crore. The reimbursement receivable was offset against the settlement expense in the Statement of Comprehensive Income. McCamish may incur additional indeterminable costs, including costs arising from indemnities or damages or claims.

#### Government Investigation

The U.S. Department of Justice (“DOJ”) is investigating how the Company classified certain H-1B visa-recipient employees working for one of its clients in immigration documents filed with certain U.S. government authorities. The Company is discussing the matter with the DOJ and has commenced its own inquiry. At this stage, the Company is unable to predict the outcome, including whether it could have a material adverse effect on the Company’s business and results of operations.

#### Others

Apart from the matters described above, the Group is subject to legal proceedings and claims arising in the ordinary course of business. Management expects that these matters, when ultimately concluded, may not have a material adverse effect on the Group’s results of operations or financial condition.
