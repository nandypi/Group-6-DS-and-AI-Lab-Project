## Notes on segment information

## Business segments

Based on the “management approach” required by Ind-AS 108—Operating Segments, the Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates the Group’s performance and allocates resources based on an analysis of various performance indicators by business segment. Accordingly, information has been presented across these business segments. The accounting principles used in preparing the financial statements are consistently applied to record revenue and expenditure in individual segments.

## Segmental capital employed

It is currently not practicable to provide segment disclosures relating to total assets and liabilities because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.
