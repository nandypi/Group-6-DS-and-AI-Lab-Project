## 2.10.5 Impairment

The Company recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenues that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

- Trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component are measured at lifetime ECL.
- All other financial assets are measured at 12-month ECL unless credit risk has significantly increased since initial recognition; in that case, lifetime ECL is recognized.
- Credit loss allowances are based on historical loss experience, adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions in the industries and countries in which the Company operates.
- The adjustment required at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the statement of profit and loss.
