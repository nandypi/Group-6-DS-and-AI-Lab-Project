## 2.15 Segment reporting

IFRS 8 Operating Segments establishes standards for reporting operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group primarily provides end-to-end business solutions to help clients enhance business performance. The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates performance and allocates resources using business-segment performance indicators. The accounting principles used to record segment revenue and expenditure are consistent with the Group’s accounting policies.

The Group’s business segments are:

- Financial Services, including Financial Services and Insurance and Finacle
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments, including businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and other Public Services enterprises

The Financial Services reportable segment combines the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because they have similar economic characteristics.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are assigned to segments based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Allocated expenses include offshore software development center costs and on-site expenses categorized according to the associated segment effort.

Certain costs, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocated because the underlying assets are used interchangeably between segments. These costs are disclosed as unallocated expenses and deducted from total segment income.

Assets and liabilities are also used interchangeably between segments. Management therefore does not provide segment-level assets and liabilities because meaningful segregation of the available data is not practicable.

Business-segment revenue is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized. Geographic revenue disclosures are included in note 2.16, “Revenue from operations.”
