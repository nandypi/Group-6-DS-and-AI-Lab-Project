## Opinion

The auditors audited the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the period ended September 30, 2025. The statements included the condensed consolidated balance sheet as at September 30, 2025; the condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income for the three and six months ended on that date; the condensed consolidated statement of changes in equity; the condensed consolidated statement of cash flows for the six months ended on that date; and related notes and accounting policies.

In the auditors’ opinion, the interim condensed consolidated financial statements give a true and fair view, in accordance with IAS 34 as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board, of:

- The consolidated state of affairs of the Group as at September 30, 2025.
- The consolidated profit and consolidated total comprehensive income for the three and six months ended on that date.
- The consolidated changes in equity and consolidated cash flows for the six months ended on that date.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The auditors stated that they were independent of the Group under the ICAI Code of Ethics and had fulfilled their other ethical responsibilities. They stated that the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for their opinion on the interim condensed consolidated financial statements.
