## Dividend

The final dividend on shares is recorded as a liability on the date of approval by shareholders. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the Company’s Board of Directors.

Income tax consequences of dividends on financial instruments classified as equity are recognized according to where the entity originally recognized the past transactions or events that generated distributable profits.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are distributed after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## 2.19 Break-up of expenses and other income, net

### 2.19.1 Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides gratuity through a defined benefit retirement plan covering eligible employees, primarily those of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve make contributions to their respective gratuity fund trusts. Trustees administer the contributions, which are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to the employee’s age.

Liabilities under defined benefit plans are determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. These plans expose the Group to actuarial risks, including longevity, interest-rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in the balance sheet. Re-measurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not reclassified to profit or loss in subsequent periods. The portion of actual returns on plan assets exceeding the yields calculated using the discount rate is recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.

### 2.19.2 Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution plan. The Group has no further obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

### 2.19.3 Provident fund

Eligible Infosys employees receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. Employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. The Company contributes part of these amounts to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust is administered by the Government of India. The Company is required to make good any shortfall between the returns from trust investments and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries receive benefits from provident funds treated as defined contribution plans. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no further obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

### 2.19.4 Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated-absences policy covers accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the balance sheet date.

Expenses for non-accumulating compensated absences are recognized in the period in which the absences occur.

### 2.19.5 Other income, net

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, and exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts and on the translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities.

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

### 2.19.6 Foreign Currency

#### Functional currency and presentation currency

The functional currency of Infosys, its Indian subsidiaries, and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. The functional currencies of foreign subsidiaries are their respective local currencies.

The financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars, rounded to the nearest million, to facilitate comparison of Infosys’ performance and financial position with similar companies domiciled in other geographic locations.

#### Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency using exchange rates in effect at the balance sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income and reported within exchange gains or losses on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in other comprehensive income as qualifying cash-flow hedges.

Non-monetary foreign-currency assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated using the exchange rate on the date the fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated using the exchange rate on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized upon settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Revenue, expense, and cash-flow items denominated in foreign currencies are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate on the transaction date.

For translation of foreign subsidiaries’ financial statements into the presentation currency, assets and liabilities are translated using the exchange rate at the balance sheet date, while revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated using the average exchange rate for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves under other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is fully disposed of, the relevant currency translation amount is transferred to net profit in the statement of comprehensive income. Changes in the parent’s ownership that do not result in loss of control are recorded through equity.

Other comprehensive income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, such as equities classified as financial instruments and measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair value adjustments arising from the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of the foreign entity and translated using the exchange rate at the balance sheet date.

### 2.19.7 Government grants

The Group recognizes government grants only when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Government grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the statement of comprehensive income on a systematic and rational basis over the useful life of the asset.

Government grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in the statement of comprehensive income over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.
