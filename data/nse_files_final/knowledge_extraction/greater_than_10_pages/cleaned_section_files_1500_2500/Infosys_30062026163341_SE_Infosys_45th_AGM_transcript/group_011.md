## Hiranand Kotwani

The shareholder asked how Infosys plans to sustain growth in a difficult market environment, particularly through expansion beyond the United States. He asked about the company’s presence in the rest of the world, the impact of the troubled Middle East region, and how Europe and other markets could contribute to sustained growth.

## Pankaj Manjani

The shareholder asked whether Infosys had assessed the potential impact of automation and AI adoption on its employee base and whether management expected any material workforce reduction in the near to medium term.

He also asked what measures Infosys had taken to train and reskill employees for AI-enabled roles, and how the company planned to help its workforce adapt to technological change while maintaining long-term employability.

## Sachin Singhal

The shareholder questioned why share prices often decline after a company conducts a buyback and whether this is caused by market conditions or other factors.

He referred to participating in an Infosys buyback at approximately Rs 1,800, with around half of his shares accepted in the buyback. He stated that the remaining shares incurred losses that offset the benefit from the buyback. He also raised a taxation concern, stating that investors may owe tax on buyback profits while still holding shares that have declined in value.

He referred to the company’s share price reaching a 52-week low of Rs 1,029 and requested that management consider measures to support shareholders in the future. He also expressed the expectation that the company would continue growing and provide dividends to shareholders and investors.

## Suneet Bhat

The shareholder referred to Infosys’ FY2025–26 annual report and expressed satisfaction with the company’s performance despite a challenging global macroeconomic environment. He mentioned consistent revenue growth, a strong pipeline, and the integration of advanced AI and digital capabilities.

He also referred to the company’s capital allocation policy and final dividend, and praised its focus on corporate governance and ESG standards. He expressed support for the AGM resolutions, including adoption of the audited financial statements and board reappointments.
