## The 45th Annual General Meeting

June 23, 2026

## OTHER REPRESENTATIVES

- Statutory auditors: representatives of Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants, including Vikas Bagaria and Amit Ved, Partners.
- Internal auditors: representatives of Ernst & Young LLP, including Arun Krishnamurthy, Rahul Kumar, Abhishek Sabu, Deepak Jodhani, and Sudhakar R.
- Secretarial auditors: representatives of Makarand M. Joshi & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries, including Vaibhav Dandawate, Abhishek Gupta, and Pranali Jain.
- Scrutinizers: representatives of Hemanth, Holla & Co., Company Secretaries, including B. Hemanth and Swathi Holla.

## QUORUM OF THE MEETING

A total of 326 members representing 4,46,34,277 shares attended the meeting.

## Welcome address - Nandan M. Nilekani

The 45th Annual General Meeting was held through video conference in accordance with circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and SEBI. Members participating through video conference were counted for quorum purposes under the applicable MCA circular and Section 103 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The requisite quorum was present, and the meeting was called to order.
