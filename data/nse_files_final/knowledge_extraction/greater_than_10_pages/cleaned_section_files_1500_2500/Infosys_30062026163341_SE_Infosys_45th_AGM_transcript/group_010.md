# Shareholder Questions on AI, Share Price, Leadership Continuity and CSR

A minority shareholder raised concerns about the potential impact of artificial intelligence on IT services companies such as Infosys and asked whether AI could threaten the company’s future. The shareholder asked what challenges Infosys expected to face and what plan management had to address them.

The shareholder stated that Infosys’s share price had been above ₹2,000 around the previous October and was below ₹1,030 at the time of the AGM. The shareholder said the company’s performance did not appear poor enough to explain the decline and asked what steps management would take to improve the share price.

The shareholder requested continued service from the company chairman for at least the next five years, along with continued service from Salil Parekh as CEO and Managing Director and Manikantha as Company Secretary. The shareholder also expressed support for continued leadership service after the company’s 50th year.

The shareholder thanked the company for its CSR activities and referred to work discussed by Mr. Parekh to improve water levels. The shareholder said water levels in Jamnagar were also low and requested that some CSR funds be spent there. The shareholder and their wife also requested permission to visit the company.

The shareholder said they had sold approximately 10% of their Infosys holding one or two weeks earlier, could not sleep for two nights afterward, and then repurchased the shares. The shareholder concluded by thanking the company for the opportunity to speak at the 45th AGM and wishing it a successful year through March 2027.
