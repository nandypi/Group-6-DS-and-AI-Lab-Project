## Liquidity and capital resources

The principal sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents, investments, and cash flow generated from operations. Infosys had no outstanding borrowings. Working capital was $6,347 million as of March 31, 2025, compared with $6,071 million as of March 31, 2024.

Working capital as of March 31, 2025, included $2,861 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1,460 million in current investments. Working capital as of March 31, 2024, included $1,773 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1,548 million in current investments.

A sustained reduction in client IT spending, a longer sales cycle, or a continued economic downturn in any geographic location or business segment could reduce revenue and negatively affect liquidity and cash resources.

Cash and cash equivalents consist of deposits with banks and financial institutions with high credit ratings that can be withdrawn at any time without prior notice or penalty on principal. They are primarily held in Indian rupees and included restricted cash of $50 million as of March 31, 2025, and $42 million as of March 31, 2024. The restrictions primarily relate to bank balances held by irrevocable trusts controlled by Infosys.

Investments comprise liquid mutual fund units, target maturity fund units, quoted debt securities, including investments in non-convertible debentures, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, and government securities. Certificates of deposit and commercial paper represent marketable securities issued by banks, non-banking financial companies, and eligible financial institutions for specified periods. Investments in non-convertible debentures represent debt instruments issued by government-aided institutions and financial institutions. A significant portion of the investments, cash, and cash equivalents was fully liquid. Investments were diversified by maturity and counterparty.

## Net cash provided by operating activities:

Operating cash flow increased by $1,203 million in fiscal 2025, primarily due to higher net profits adjusted for non-cash items, improved working capital management, and lower net income tax payments resulting from higher tax refunds.

Trade receivables as a percentage of the last 12 months’ revenue were 18.9% as of March 31, 2025, and 19.6% as of March 31, 2024. Days Sales Outstanding based on the last 12 months’ revenue was 69 days and 71 days, respectively. The decrease in Days Sales Outstanding in fiscal 2025 was attributed to collections.

In fiscal 2025, income tax paid under protest following demands from tax authorities in major tax jurisdictions was $90 million, relating to fiscal 2018 through fiscal 2022. Tax refunds received in fiscal 2025 were $821 million, relating primarily to fiscal 2007 through fiscal 2018 and fiscal 2020 through fiscal 2024.

In fiscal 2024, income tax paid under protest was $161 million, relating to fiscal 2012 through fiscal 2013, fiscal 2016 through fiscal 2018, and fiscal 2020 through fiscal 2022. Tax refunds received in fiscal 2024 were $208 million, relating primarily to fiscal 2008 through fiscal 2009, fiscal 2012 through fiscal 2014, and fiscal 2016 through fiscal 2023.

## Net cash used in investing activities:

Net cash used in investing activities for business acquisitions was $377 million in fiscal 2025. There were no acquisitions in fiscal 2024.

During fiscal 2025, Infosys paid $377 million, net of cash acquired, for the acquisitions of in-tech and Insemi. The aggregate purchase consideration included contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $4 million at the acquisition date. The contingent consideration is payable over the following two years, subject to achievement of certain financial targets. The acquisitions also included retention payouts and employee bonuses of $33 million, payable two to five years after the acquisition dates, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of certain financial targets.

Net cash used in investing activities for additional property, plant, equipment, and intangibles for software development centers was $263 million in fiscal 2025 and $266 million in fiscal 2024.

Infosys invested $10,601 million in marketable securities in fiscal 2025 and $10,455 million in fiscal 2024. It redeemed marketable securities of $10,825 million and $9,914 million, respectively. These investments were redeemed to fund business operations, acquisitions, dividend payments, and share buybacks in line with the capital allocation policy. Marketable securities included liquid mutual funds, target maturity funds, quoted debt securities, certificates of deposit, and commercial paper.

## Net cash used in financing activities:

Net cash used in financing activities in fiscal 2025 primarily included $2,416 million of dividend payments, $278 million of lease liability payments, and $118 million of loan repayment for in-tech Holding GmbH at the time of acquisition.

Net cash used in financing activities in fiscal 2024 primarily included $1,777 million of dividend payments and $245 million of lease liability payments.

## Acquisitions

On April 17, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte Ltd., a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of The Missing Link, a cybersecurity services provider headquartered in Australia. Consideration, including earn-outs, was up to AUD 98 million, approximately $62 million, excluding management incentives and retention bonuses and subject to customary closing adjustments. The acquisition was completed on April 30, 2025.

On April 17, 2025, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the partnership interests of MRE Consulting Ltd, an energy consulting company headquartered in the United States. Consideration, including earn-outs, was up to $36 million, excluding management incentives and retention bonuses and subject to customary closing adjustments. Infosys Nova Holdings LLC simultaneously incorporated Infosys Energy Consulting Services LLC to consummate the transaction. The acquisition was completed on April 30, 2025.

## Contractual commitments

As of March 31, 2025, Infosys had capital expenditure commitments, primarily for infrastructure facilities and computer equipment, totaling $109 million. These included $96 million of domestic commitments and $13 million of overseas commitments. Capital commitments will be financed from cash generated by operations, and Infosys expects most outstanding contractual commitments as of March 31, 2025, to be completed within one year.

As of March 31, 2025, Infosys had purchase obligations of $2,173 million. Approximately 57% was expected to be completed within the following year, with the remainder thereafter.

A purchase obligation is an enforceable and legally binding agreement to purchase goods or services that specifies significant terms, including fixed or minimum quantities, fixed, minimum, or variable prices, and the approximate timing of the transaction.

## Capital allocation policy and related payouts

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

At its meeting on April 17, 2025, the Board recommended a final dividend of ₹22 per equity share, approximately $0.26 per equity share, for the financial year ended March 31, 2025. Shareholders approved the dividend at the Company’s AGM held on June 25, 2025. The dividend would result in a net cash outflow of approximately $1,066 million, excluding dividends on treasury shares.

*Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS.*

Dividend and buyback amounts include applicable taxes.
