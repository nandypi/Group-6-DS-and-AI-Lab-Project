## Acquisitions during the year ended March 31, 2023

During fiscal 2023, the Group completed two business combinations to complement its digital offerings by acquiring 100% voting interests in:

1. oddity GmbH, oddity group services GmbH, oddity space GmbH, oddity jungle GmbH, oddity code GmbH, and oddity waves GmbH, collectively known as oddity, a Germany-based group of digital marketing, experience, and commerce agencies, on April 20, 2022; and
2. BASE life science A/S, a consulting and technology firm in the life science industry in Europe, on September 1, 2022.

These acquisitions were expected to strengthen the Group’s creative, branding, and experience design capabilities; augment its life sciences expertise; scale its digital transformation capabilities through cloud-based industry solutions; and expand its presence across Europe.

Goodwill is not tax-deductible. Goodwill pertaining to these business combinations was allocated to operating segments as described in Note 2.9.

The purchase consideration of $122 million included cash consideration of $116 million and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $6 million at the acquisition date.

The key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration at the acquisition date were the probabilities assigned to achievement of financial targets and a discount rate of 12.5%. As of March 31, 2024, the contingent consideration had been fully paid.

The acquisitions included employee retention bonuses payable over three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Performance and retention bonuses are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was $14 million at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2024, the amounts had been fully collected.

Transaction costs incurred in connection with the business combinations, including finder’s fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of $1 million related to the acquisitions were included under administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the year ended March 31, 2023.
