## 2.10 Business combinations

## Accounting Policy:

Business combinations are accounted for using the acquisition method under IFRS 3 (Revised), Business Combinations.

The purchase price in an acquisition is measured at the fair value of assets transferred, equity instruments issued, and liabilities incurred or assumed at the date control is transferred to the Group. The purchase price also includes the fair value of contingent consideration.

Identifiable assets acquired and liabilities and contingent liabilities assumed in a business combination are initially measured at fair value on the acquisition date. Contingent consideration is remeasured at fair value at each reporting date, with changes recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income.

Non-controlling interests are initially measured either at fair value or at the non-controlling interests’ proportionate share of the acquiree’s identifiable net assets. The measurement basis is selected separately for each acquisition. After acquisition, the carrying amount of non-controlling interests includes the initial amount recognized plus the non-controlling interests’ share of subsequent changes in the equity of subsidiaries.

Payments related to options issued by the Group over non-controlling interests in subsidiaries are accounted for as financial liabilities and initially recognized at the estimated present value of the gross obligations. These options are subsequently measured at fair value to reflect the amount payable when the option becomes exercisable. If an option expires unexercised, the liability is derecognized.

Business combinations between entities under common control are outside the scope of IFRS 3 (Revised), Business Combinations, and are accounted for at the carrying value of assets acquired and liabilities assumed.

## Acquisitions during the year ended March 31, 2025

## InSemi

On May 10, 2024, Infosys Ltd acquired 100% of the voting interests in InSemi Technology Services Private Limited, a semiconductor design services company headquartered in India. The acquisition is expected to strengthen Infosys’ expertise in the semiconductor ecosystem and Engineering R&D services.

The purchase price was allocated to assets acquired and liabilities assumed based on their fair values at the acquisition date:

(*) Includes cash and cash equivalents acquired of $5 million.

The excess of purchase consideration over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill were the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill is not tax-deductible.

The $24 million purchase consideration comprised $20 million in cash and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $4 million at the acquisition date.

The key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration at the acquisition date were the probabilities assigned to achievement of financial targets and a discount rate of 5.9%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2025 was $4 million.

The acquisition also included retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Bonuses and incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was $4 million at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2025, the amounts had been substantially collected.

Transaction costs incurred in connection with the acquisition, including finder’s fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of less than $1 million related to the acquisition were included under administrative expenses in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income for the year ended March 31, 2025.
