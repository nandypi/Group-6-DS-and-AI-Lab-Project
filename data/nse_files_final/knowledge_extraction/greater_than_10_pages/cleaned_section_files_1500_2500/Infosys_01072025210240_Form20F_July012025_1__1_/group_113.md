## e. Pay for Performance

Consistent with the Company's executive compensation philosophy, a significant portion of executives' pay is performance-based and “at-risk.”

## i. Performance Bonus

Executive officer performance bonuses are linked to the achievement of company goals, including revenue growth and operating margin, as well as applicable individual goals. Performance assessments may also consider the Company's business targets, overall business and market factors, and other relevant contributors identified by the CEO and NRC. Final payout rates are reviewed and approved by the NRC.

## ii. Stock Incentives

Stock- or equity-based incentives are generally granted or vested based on achievement against Company and/or individual performance parameters. Stock incentives may not be granted if Company performance is below a specific threshold determined by the NRC from time to time.

For performance-based equity grants, the number of shares that vest is based on the Company's and/or individual's achievement of specified performance parameters.

## f. Additional Executive Compensation Practices and Employment Agreements

The Company provides executives with health and wellness benefits and perquisites. These benefits are generally not a significant portion of executive compensation.

The NRC seeks to protect the Company's interests through executive obligations, including non-disclosure, non-compete, and non-solicitation agreements.

The Company has adopted a recoupment policy under which incentive compensation paid or payable to executives, including bonus pay and equity awards, is subject to forfeiture, cancellation, recoupment, or clawback in accordance with applicable laws, government regulations, and stock exchange requirements.

The current employment agreement with Executive Director Mr. Salil Parekh is available at [Executive Employment Agreement](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reportsfilings/documents/ceo-executive-employment-agreement2022.pdf).

Details of executive compensation for the relevant fiscal year are available in the Annual Report.
