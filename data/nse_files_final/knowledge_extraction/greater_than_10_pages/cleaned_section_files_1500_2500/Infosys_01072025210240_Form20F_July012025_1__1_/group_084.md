## 2.15 Equity

### Ordinary Shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity. Incremental costs directly attributable to the issuance of new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of any tax effects.

### Treasury Shares

When any entity within the Group purchases the company’s ordinary shares, the consideration paid, including directly attributable incremental costs, is presented as a deduction from total equity until the shares are cancelled, sold, or reissued. When treasury shares are subsequently sold or reissued, the amount received is recognized as an increase in equity, and the resulting surplus or deficit is transferred to or from share premium.

### Share capital and share premium

The amount received in excess of par value is classified as share premium. Share-based compensation recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income is also credited to share premium. Amounts have been utilized for bonus issues and share buybacks from the share premium account.

The Company has only one class of shares, referred to as equity shares, with a par value of ₹5 each.

### Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the accumulated earnings of the Group.

### Other Reserves

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of the eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be utilized by the Company for acquiring new plant and machinery for its business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

### Capital Redemption Reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, as an appropriation from general reserve or retained earnings.

### Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the cash flow hedge reserve is transferred to net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income when the related forecasted transaction occurs.

### Other components of equity

Other components of equity include currency translation, remeasurement of the net defined benefit liability or asset, fair value changes of equity instruments measured through other comprehensive income, and changes in the fair value of investments, net of taxes.

## 2.15.1 Voting

Each holder of equity shares is entitled to one vote per share. Equity shares represented by American Depositary Shares (ADSs) carry similar voting and dividend rights as other equity shares. Each ADS represents one underlying equity share.

## 2.15.2 Liquidation

In the event of liquidation, shareholders are entitled to receive the remaining assets of the Company after distribution of all preferential amounts. No such preferential amounts currently exist other than amounts held by irrevocable controlled trusts. Distribution will be in proportion to the number of equity shares held. For irrevocable controlled trusts, the corpus would be settled in favor of the beneficiaries.

## 2.15.3 Share options

Holders of options issued under the Company’s share option plans have no voting, dividend, or liquidation rights.

## 2.15.4 Share capital and share premium

The Company has only one class of shares, referred to as equity shares, with a par value of ₹5 each. Controlled trusts held 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, and 10,916,829 shares as of March 31, 2024.

## Capital Allocation Policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in February 2023

In line with the capital allocation policy, the Board approved a buyback at its meeting on October 13, 2022. The buyback was conducted through the open-market route on Indian stock exchanges for up to ₹9,300 crore, excluding buyback tax, at a price not exceeding ₹1,850 per share, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the buyback through e-voting on the postal ballot, with results declared on December 3, 2022. The offer was available to all equity shareholders other than the Promoters, Promoter Group, and Persons in Control of the Company.

The buyback commenced on December 7, 2022, and was completed on February 13, 2023. During the buyback period, the Company purchased and extinguished 60,426,348 equity shares from the stock exchange at a volume-weighted average buyback price of ₹1,539.06 per share. The shares represented 1.44% of the Company’s pre-buyback paid-up equity share capital.

The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹9,300 crore, excluding transaction costs and buyback tax, equivalent to $1,130 million. The Company funded the buyback from its free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

In accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, as of March 31, 2023, the Company created a capital redemption reserve of $3 million, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, as an appropriation from general reserve and retained earnings.

## Dividends

Final dividends on shares are recorded as a liability on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the Company’s Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.
