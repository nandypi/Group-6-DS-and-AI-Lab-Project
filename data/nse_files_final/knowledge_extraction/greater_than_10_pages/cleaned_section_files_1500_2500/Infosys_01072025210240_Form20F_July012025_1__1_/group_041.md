## Recruitment

As of March 31, 2025, the Group employed 323,578 employees, including 306,599 professionals involved in service delivery to clients, including trainees.

Infosys recruits across IT specializations through relationships with institutions globally and initiatives such as HackWithInfy, an online coding contest. The company also uses the InfyTQ learning platform for training and recruitment.

Infosys uses a hybrid recruitment model combining virtual and in-person recruitment and has introduced artificial intelligence into its recruitment process.

During fiscal 2025, Infosys received 4,457,748 employment applications, interviewed 430,080 applicants and extended offers of employment to 83,207 applicants. These statistics exclude subsidiaries.

## Education, Training and Assessment (ETA)

The Foundation Training Program operates across India and other countries for newly onboarded entry-level hires. Its curriculum covers more than 45 technology streams and includes generative AI and prompt engineering courses.

The Continuous Education Program reskills and upskills existing employees to support staffing for client projects and develop expertise in next-generation digital technologies and methodologies, including generative AI. Training includes online self-learning, instructor-led virtual training and in-person classroom training. Bridge programs provide training and internship opportunities for employees changing career fields.

Lex, Infosys' in-house learning platform, offers more than 36,000 curated courses, including more than 30,000 courses procured from vendor partners. Around 20,000 employees used Lex on weekdays and around 5,000 used it on weekends, with average learning time of 40 minutes.

Infosys Wingspan is a configurable talent transformation platform used by several global organizations for talent transformation programs.

Infosys Springboard provides more than 20,000 courses. Approximately 9.2 million learners globally actively consume its content.

## Leadership development

The Infosys Leadership Institute partnered with Kellogg to provide its leaders with a certification program on AI applications for growth. In fiscal 2025, more than 1,000 senior leaders completed the program, including approximately 250 women leaders.

The Infosys Leadership Institute broadened its succession program to include key roles in business and enabling functions. It also created personalized development programs for incumbents and successors in critical organizational positions. The Constellation program was expanded to include more high-potential emerging leaders. Constellation leaders also began a 10-month curated program with Oxford University and worked in cross-functional teams on strategic organizational projects.

Other leadership programs included a 10-month Enterprise Leadership program with multiple universities, including Kellogg, Tuck School of Business and National University of Singapore Business School, and a six-month Executive Presence & Presentation program with The Economist.

The Infosys Leadership Institute completed more than 2,000 one-to-one leadership advisory discussions. More than 97% of leaders completed a significant development intervention, representing more than 10,500 leadership learning days.

The IamtheFuture program had more than 200 women leaders complete a leadership development program. Approximately 100 women leaders completed a Stanford program on executive communication, together with one-to-one coaching from an external communication firm. Career conversations were completed for critical and high-potential women leaders across segments and delivery, and more than 200 women leaders completed development coaching conversations with internal leadership advisors.

## Visas

As of March 31, 2025, less than 50% of Infosys employees in the United States held either H-1B or L-1 visas. H-1B visas allow employees to remain in the United States for up to six years as specialty occupation professionals. L-1 visas allow employees to remain in the United States for up to five years as specialized-knowledge experts or up to seven years as managers and executives.
