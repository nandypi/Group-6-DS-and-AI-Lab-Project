Aggregate depreciation expense is included in cost of sales in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income. Repairs and maintenance costs are recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income when incurred.

Consequent to the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Amendment Rules, 2021 (the “Rules”), the Company was required to transfer its CSR capital assets installed prior to January 2021. The Company incorporated a subsidiary, Infosys Green Forum (IGF), under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. During the year ended March 31, 2022, the Company completed the transfer of assets after obtaining the required regulatory approvals, as applicable.

During fiscal 2024, IGF’s application for regularization of its provisional registration was rejected, and its registration was cancelled by order dated March 26, 2024, issued by the Income Tax Commissioner (Exemption). IGF filed an appeal before the Income Tax Tribunal against the order.

The Group had contractual commitments for capital expenditure, primarily comprising infrastructure facilities and computer equipment, aggregating to $109 million as of March 31, 2025, and $94 million as of March 31, 2024.
