The following table summarizes equity-settled RSUs and stock options outstanding as of March 31, 2024.

| Award plan | Award type | Range of exercise prices per share ($) | No. of shares arising from options | Weighted average remaining contractual life | Weighted average exercise price ($) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2019 Plan | RSUs | 0–0.06 | 8,023,855 | 1.42 | 0.06 |
| 2015 Plan | RSUs | 0–0.06 | 8,076,058 | 1.77 | 0.06 |
| 2015 Plan | Stock options | 5–8 | 82,050 | 1.10 | 6.61 |

The awards relate to American Depositary Shares (ADS) and Indian Equity Shares (IES). No 2019 Plan stock options were outstanding in the $5–8 exercise-price range.

As of March 31, 2025, and March 31, 2024, 288,384 and 291,795 cash-settled options were outstanding, respectively. The carrying value of the liability for cash-settled share-based payments was $2 million as of both March 31, 2025, and March 31, 2024.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for total shareholder return (TSR)-based options.

The valuation inputs include the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility for the Company is based on the historical volatility of its publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparable companies is based on historical movements in their publicly traded equity shares over an equivalent period. Correlation coefficients are calculated between each peer entity and the relevant indices, or between entities in the peer group.
