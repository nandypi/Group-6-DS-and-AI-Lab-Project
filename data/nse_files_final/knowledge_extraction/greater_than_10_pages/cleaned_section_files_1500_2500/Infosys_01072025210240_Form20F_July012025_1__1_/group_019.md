## Cloud and Infrastructure Management Capabilities

Infosys provides cloud and infrastructure services designed to help clients simplify and evolve their IT infrastructure. Its offerings address hybrid IT management and workload migration to cloud environments.

Clients are increasingly migrating workloads to hybrid environments and benchmarking internal IT infrastructure against private and public cloud infrastructure based on performance, cost, agility, and reliability. Infosys addresses this demand through solutions and methodologies for hybrid IT management and cloud workload migration.

Infosys’ industrialized service delivery and unified hybrid IT management approach use automation, cloud, analytics, and mobility to simplify and improve responsiveness in IT environments. Its automation assets, analytics-driven operations, and rapid environment deployment solutions are intended to reduce manual effort, improve asset utilization, and accelerate time to market.

Infosys has invested in platforms and solutions for hybrid IT management and the industrialization of services, including:

- **Infosys Polycloud Platform:** Enables enterprises to build vendor-agnostic solutions across cloud providers and port solutions between providers. The platform includes the Smart Catalog, Telemetry Hub, and an overarching governance and security framework for multiple user groups.
- **Infosys Automation Suite:** Combines process standardization, predictive analytics, workflow automation, and IT operations analytics to reduce manual effort.
