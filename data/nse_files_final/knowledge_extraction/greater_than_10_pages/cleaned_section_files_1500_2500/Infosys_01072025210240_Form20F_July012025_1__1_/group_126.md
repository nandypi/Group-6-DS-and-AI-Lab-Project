## Political Activities

Infosys may communicate its position on important issues to elected representatives and other government officials. Company funds or assets must not be used for political campaigns or political activities without prior written approval from the Board. Infosys does not seek reimbursement for political contributions, use company resources for personal political activities, or represent that it supports a candidate, political cause, or government decision.

## Lobbying

Meetings with government officials and elected representatives may be regulated. Employees must not claim to represent Infosys at such meetings unless specifically authorized by the Company. Such meetings must be conducted with integrity and in line with Infosys’ C-LIFE values.

## Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing

Money laundering includes directly or indirectly participating in activities involving the proceeds of crime, including concealing, possessing, acquiring, or using such proceeds, or presenting them as legitimate property.

Terrorism financing occurs when money or other resources are made available to commit terrorist acts or support terrorist organizations. Suspected activity should be reported through the applicable company reporting channel.

## Immigration

Employees may be required to travel internationally to support business requirements. During such travel, employees must comply with applicable immigration laws and company policies.

## PROTECTING COMPANY ASSETS

Confidential information is a valuable company asset. Directors, employees, and agents must protect all non-public information, including personal information obtained in the course of business. Confidential information must be used only for company business purposes. Personal information must be processed according to company policies, procedures, guidelines, and applicable privacy laws and regulations.

Upon joining Infosys, employees sign a Confidentiality and Nondisclosure Agreement describing their confidentiality obligations. Confidential, private, and sensitive information must be used responsibly and protected against prohibited, arbitrary, or careless disclosure.

## Company Confidential Information

Unless Infosys gives specific consent, preferably in writing, or there is a legal or professional right or duty to disclose, employees must not disclose confidential company information. Confidential or proprietary information about clients, Infosys, or other parties obtained through employment or affiliation with Infosys must not be used for personal advantage or the benefit of third parties.

Infosys’ Information Security Policy requires employees to safeguard Infosys and third-party confidential information while maintaining security, availability, integrity, and confidentiality. The Acceptable Usage Policy defines legitimate uses of Infosys IT resources and requires employees to protect systems against inappropriate or unauthorized access and cybersecurity breaches.

Infosys’ Privacy Policy covers personal information belonging to employees, clients, agents, consultants, suppliers, and contractors. It requires compliance with applicable privacy laws and regulations, including requirements for cross-border data transfers between group entities. Unless authorized and contractually agreed, Infosys protects the confidentiality of personal information, applies appropriate technical and organizational measures, and requires employees to follow mandated processes for its responsible use, disclosure, storage, retention, and other processing.

## Improper Opportunities

Information received through employment must not be used for personal benefit, passed to friends or family members, or used to compete indirectly with Infosys. These restrictions continue after an employee leaves the organization.

## Company Intellectual Property

Infosys’ intellectual property includes copyrights, patents, trademarks, service marks, trade secrets, design rights, logos, brands, and know-how. Employees must protect these assets and promptly disclose works, inventions, or developments created during their employment so that the Company can obtain legal protection where appropriate.

## Speaking to the Media

Infosys has designated its Corporate Communications team to handle communications with the media and to help ensure that information about the Company is accurate. The Chief Executive Officer, Chief Operating Officer, Chief Financial Officer, and Investor Relations Department are official Company spokespeople for financial matters.

Press and financial analyst inquiries must be referred to the Corporate Communications team. Employees must not discuss Infosys services or business online unless authorized, or create the impression that they are speaking or posting on behalf of the Company.

## Providing Information to the Media

Only authorized Company representatives may provide information to the media.

## Physical Access Control

Infosys maintains physical access control procedures to protect communications, Company communication equipment, and Company assets from theft, misuse, and destruction. Employees must comply with the security policies applicable to their location and must not undermine access controls.

## Information Security Policy

The Information Security Policy is intended to protect confidential information from misuse and ensure that only accurate information about Infosys is disclosed.

## Use of Company Assets

Infosys assets must not be used for individual profit or for unlawful, unauthorized, personal, or unethical purposes. Information technology, intellectual property, facilities, equipment, machines, software, and cash may be used only for business purposes and in accordance with applicable policies.

Computers, printers, copiers, and other assets may be used for minor and incidental personal purposes only when such use is minimal, creates no significant incremental cost, does not interfere with work duties, and does not violate laws or Infosys policies. Infosys resources must not be used for personal political activities.

Employees must protect computer hardware, software, data, facilities, and confidential client or Infosys information from destruction, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. Appropriate safeguards include ID badges, passwords, security codes, physical security measures, computer cable locks, and not leaving computers unattended in cars.

Copyrighted materials such as books, music, software, and magazines must not be reproduced, distributed, or altered without permission from the copyright owner or an authorized agent. Software used for Infosys business must be properly licensed and used according to its license. Use of unlicensed software may constitute copyright infringement and may result in disciplinary action.

## Expense Claims

Supervisors, managers, and employees are responsible for complying with Infosys business expense and reimbursement policies. Business-related expense claims must be authorized by the employee’s manager before the expenses are incurred. Personal expenses are not reimbursed by Infosys.
