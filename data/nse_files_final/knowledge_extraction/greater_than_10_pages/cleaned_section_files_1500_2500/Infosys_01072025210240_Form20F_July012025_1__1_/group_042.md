## Note

Among the directors, D. Sundaram, Michael Gibbs, Chitra Nayak, Govind Iyer, Helene Auriol Potier, and Nitin Paranjpe do not hold shares or ADRs of the Company.

## Option plans

## 2015 Plan

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan is 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. Plan numbers were adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue. Awards generally vest over four years.

The controlled trust held 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, and 10,916,829 shares as of March 31, 2024, under the 2015 Plan. Of these, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities in each year.

## 2019 Plan

At its meeting on May 15, 2019, the Board approved the 2019 Plan, subject to shareholder approval, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

On June 22, 2019, following approval by shareholders at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan is 50,000,000 equity shares. Up to 45,000,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

RSUs granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on the achievement of annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. These parameters may include relative total shareholder return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected domestic and global market indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics. Each performance parameter is separately used to calculate the number of shares that vest.

The equity awards granted under the 2015 Plan generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

The RSUs granted under the 2019 Plan generally vest over three years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

Employee stock compensation expense recorded in the statement of comprehensive income was $95 million in fiscal 2025, $79 million in fiscal 2024, and $64 million in fiscal 2023. This expense related to the CEO, other executive officers, and other employees.
