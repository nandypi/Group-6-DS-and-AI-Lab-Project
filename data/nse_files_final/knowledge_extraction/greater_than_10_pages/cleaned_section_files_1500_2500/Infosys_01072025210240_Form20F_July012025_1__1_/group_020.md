Our acquisition of GuideVision, one of the largest ServiceNow Elite Partners in Europe, expanded Infosys’ ServiceNow capabilities. GuideVision provides strategic advisory, consulting, implementation, training, and support services for the ServiceNow platform, including SnowMirror, a proprietary smart data replication tool for ServiceNow.

## Infosys Cobalt ®

Infosys Cobalt ® is a suite of cloud services, solutions, and platforms for enterprise transformation. It combines cloud, AI, and data technologies and is designed with security, pervasive AI adoption, and sustainability in mind.

### Strategic business outcomes

Infosys Cobalt ® supports the development of products and services, revenue generation, customer experience, compliance capabilities, operational excellence, and continuous innovation through cloud, AI, and data technologies.

### Customized industry cloud solutions

Infosys Cobalt ® industry cloud solutions include reference architectures, implementation blueprints, and security-compliant frameworks tailored to industry segments. They are intended to help enterprises use existing IT environments while improving business processes and stakeholder experiences.

### Cloud for AI, innovation and efficiency

Infosys Cobalt ® supports composable architectures, AI and data integration, and the transition toward autonomous operations. It is used to redesign products, services, and core business processes for changing market demands.

## Infosys AI and Automation Capabilities

Infosys AI offerings, powered by Infosys Topaz ®, provide services, solutions, and roadmaps for discovering and scaling enterprise AI adoption.

Infosys is using an AI-first approach for internal transformation and client solutions, including Agentic AI. Through the Infosys Agentic Foundry, it is developing specialized agents for:

- Knowledge discovery and deep research
- Financial compliance auditing
- Coding
- Knowledge retrieval
- Personalized learning

Infosys launched its first Small Language Model (SLM) and integrated it into multiple products and services. BankingSLM is integrated into platforms such as Finacle and powers AI assistants for business operations, system support, and application customization.

Infosys has created 25 AI-enabled service playbooks and 23 AI industry blueprints. Its Topaz ® Center of Excellence works with hyperscalers, startups, industry bodies, and academia on emerging AI trends. More than 275,000 employees have been trained in AI at different proficiency levels.

Infosys’ open-source Responsible AI toolkit has been adopted by the Karnataka Government for startups and recognized by the OECD.AI Policy Observatory. Infosys is collaborating with the Linux Foundation on networking and with universities to establish AI Labs in the United States and the United Kingdom focused on AI models and Responsible AI.

Infosys Topaz ® includes:

- A platform-based poly-AI approach
- Industry blueprints
- AI-enabled playbooks
- Specialized domain-adaptation models, including Narrow Transformer
- The Infosys Topaz ® Responsible AI framework
- An ecosystem of partners across the AI value chain

Infosys has delivered more than 400 generative AI projects using Infosys Topaz ® and its generative AI- and agentic AI-powered services and solutions.

## Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (iCETS)

iCETS incubates emerging technology solutions for Infosys’ clients and business units. It provides next-generation platforms and innovation-as-a-service through four dimensions.

### InspireNext

InspireNext helps identify new business and technology opportunities. Its Listening Post as a Service (LPaaS) has helped more than 65 clients detect early signals of evolving phenomena through innovation centers worldwide.

LPaaS insights support the development of Centers of Excellence in areas including AI, generative AI, quantum computing, cybersecurity, and mixed reality. Example solutions include an AI benchmarking platform and logistics optimization using quantum technologies.

### ServiceNext

ServiceNext uses newly developed capabilities and intellectual property to differentiate or create client offerings. Examples include:

- LEAP for AI-led maintenance projects
- CyberNext for cybersecurity management
- iQE for quality engineering services
- iDSS and iEDPS for data readiness for AI

Infosys reports more than 1,000 client deployments of its intellectual property across its portfolio. Be The Navigator (BTN) is a structured program for client-led grassroots innovation.

### ConnectNext

ConnectNext connects Infosys with an external ecosystem of hyperscalers, startups, universities, and product companies. The Infosys Innovation Network supports startups and collaborations in areas including generative AI, data readiness, immersive experiences, space, and semiconductors.

### IncubateNext

IncubateNext provides rapid innovation and experimentation capabilities using Infosys experts, intellectual property, and external partnerships. Infosys Living Labs has helped more than 100 clients adopt technologies including AI, generative AI, mixed reality, and customer experience solutions.

Infosys Innovation Hubs provide access to more than 190 technology experiences to help clients evaluate and apply innovations to business opportunities.

Infosys has also established Applied Research Centers (ARC) focused on technology and trends research in applied AI, new interaction models, autonomous machines, advanced cybersecurity, and other emerging technology areas.

## Business Process Management Capabilities

Infosys BPM provides end-to-end Business Process Management (BPM) services. Its services include generative business services, AI solutions, agentic AI solutions, SaaS-oriented offerings, skill enhancement, reskilling, complex problem-solving, judgment-based decision-making, and domain expertise.

Infosys BPM services include:

- Finance & Accounting (F&A)
- Sourcing & Procurement (S&P)
- Sales & Fulfilment (S&F)
- Customer Service (CS)
- Human Resource Outsourcing (HRO)
- Legal Process Management (LPM)
- Digital Interactive Services (DIS)
- Digital Transformation Services (DTS)
- Analytics (AT)
- Business Transformation Services
- Annotation Services for AI and machine learning
- Learning Services
- Master Data Management
- Geospatial Data Services
- Business Process as a Service (BPaaS)
- Global Capability Centers (GCC)
- Financial Crime Compliance (FCC)
- Trust & Safety (T&S)
- Industry solutions

Infosys BPM serves industries including banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI), manufacturing, hi-tech, retail, services, utilities, resources and energy (SURE), and telecommunications.

Its platform-based digital capabilities include:

- McCamish NGIN, a policy administration and insurance platform
- Stater, a vertical utilities platform in the mortgage sector
- A Japan-based sourcing and procurement joint venture with Hitachi, Panasonic, Mitsubishi, and Pasona
- APOC (Account Payables on Cloud), an enterprise capabilities platform
