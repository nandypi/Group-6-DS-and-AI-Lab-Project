## Profiles of Directors and Executive Officers as of March 31, 2025

Nandan M. Nilekani is the Chairman of Infosys Limited, which he co-founded in 1981. He is also associated with EkStep, a not-for-profit effort focused on a learner-centric, technology-based platform for basic literacy and numeracy. In January 2023, he was appointed co-chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion and Development. He previously served as Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India in the rank of a Cabinet Minister. Nilekani holds a bachelor’s degree from IIT Bombay. He is the author of *Imagining India* and co-authored *Rebooting India: Realizing a Billion Aspirations* with Viral Shah and *The Art of Bitfulness: Keeping Calm in the Digital World* with Tanuj Bhojwani, released in January 2022.

Salil Parekh is the Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Infosys. He sets and evolves the company’s strategic direction and portfolio of offerings and leads execution through the leadership team. He has more than three decades of global experience in the IT services industry, including digital transformation, business turnarounds, acquisitions, and cloud and AI services.

Before joining Infosys, Parekh was a member of the Group Executive Board at Capgemini, where he held leadership positions for 25 years. His responsibilities included Application Services in North America, the United Kingdom, and Asia; Cloud Infrastructure Services; and Sogeti. He also chaired Capgemini’s North America Executive Council and led the North America growth and turnaround strategy, including the development of offshoring capabilities. Earlier, he was a Partner at Ernst & Young and was involved in scaling its Indian operations. Parekh holds Master of Engineering degrees in Computer Science and Mechanical Engineering from Cornell University and a Bachelor of Technology degree in Aeronautical Engineering from IIT Bombay.

D. Sundaram is the Lead Independent Director of Infosys. He chairs the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and the Risk Management Committee and serves on the Audit Committee, Stakeholder Relationship Committee, and Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee.

Sundaram’s experience includes corporate finance, business performance, operations monitoring, governance, mergers and acquisitions, talent and people management, strategy, information technology, cybersecurity, sustainability, and ESG. He joined Hindustan Unilever Limited as a management trainee in June 1975 and held several positions, including Commercial Officer for Africa and the Middle East in London from 1990 to 1993 and Senior Vice President for South Asia and the Middle East from 1996 to 1999. He was Chief Finance Officer of HUL from April 1999 to March 2008 and Vice Chairman and CFO from April 2008 to July 2009. He currently serves as Chairman and Independent Director of Crompton Greaves Consumer Electricals Limited.

Michael Gibbs is an Independent Director of Infosys. He chairs the Stakeholders Relationship Committee and Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee and serves on the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Corporate Social Responsibility Committee.

Gibbs was formerly Group CIO of BP plc, where he was responsible for setting and implementing BP’s IT strategy and providing computing and telecommunications technology services worldwide. He led the transformation of BP’s IT function, including changes to its operating model, cybersecurity improvements, adoption of emerging digital technologies, and plans to migrate legacy data centers to the cloud. He previously served as CIO for businesses including Conoco Refining & Marketing in Europe and Asia and ConocoPhillips Supply and Trading, Corporate Functions, and Global Downstream. He holds a degree in Management Science from Oklahoma State University and completed the Executive Management Program at Penn State University in 1997 and the Concours/Cash CIO Leadership Program in 2004.

Bobby Parikh is an Independent Director of Infosys. He chairs the Audit Committee and serves on the Stakeholders Relationship Committee and Risk Management Committee.

Parikh is the Managing Partner of Bobby Parikh Associates, a firm focused on strategic tax and regulatory advisory services. His experience includes advising on transactions and business reorganizations that are inbound, outbound, or wholly domestic. He works with private equity funds, institutional investors, and business owners and managers on transaction-related tax and regulatory matters. He also engages with regulators and policy formulators on new regulations and policies.

Parikh co-founded BMR Advisors, which he helped establish and run for more than 12 years. Before forming BMR Advisors, he was Chief Executive Officer of Ernst & Young in India until December 2003. He previously worked at Arthur Andersen for more than 17 years and served as its Country Managing Partner until the Andersen practice combined with Ernst & Young in June 2002. He currently serves as an Independent Director of Biocon Limited and as a Non-Executive Non-Independent Director of Indostar Capital Finance Limited. He holds a degree in Commerce from the University of Mumbai and qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 1987.

Chitra Nayak is an Independent Director of Infosys. She chairs the Environment, Social and Governance Committee and serves on the Risk Management Committee, Stakeholder Relationship Committee, and Corporate Social Responsibility Committee.

Nayak has more than 25 years of experience in go-to-market, general management, and operations leadership. She serves on the boards of UrbanFootprint and GetInsured/Vimo and is a Senior Advisor at the Boston Consulting Group and a venture partner at 1414 Ventures. She was formerly Chief Operating Officer at Comfy, where she oversaw go-to-market strategy, and Chief Operating Officer at Funding Circle. She also held leadership roles at Salesforce for eight years, including COO, Platform, and Senior Vice President, Global Sales Development. Earlier, she worked at AAA, Charles Schwab, and the Boston Consulting Group.

Nayak co-founded Neythri.org, which supports South Asian professional women, and co-founded the Salesforce Women’s Network initiative. She holds an MBA with Honors from Harvard Business School, an MS in Environmental Engineering from Cornell University, and a B.Tech. in Engineering from IIT.

Govind Iyer is an Independent Director of Infosys. He chairs the Corporate Social Responsibility Committee and serves on the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Environment, Social and Governance Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee.

Iyer retired as a Partner at Egon Zehnder, a global leadership advisory firm. Before joining Egon Zehnder, he worked at Procter & Gamble, Coca-Cola, and Heinz. He has served on the Wharton Executive Education Advisory Board and the Advisory Board of the Asian Venture Philanthropy Network. He is a founding board member and Chairperson of Social Venture Partners in India and has served as a board member of GIVE India. He serves on the Board of Karmayogi Bharat, a special purpose vehicle of the Department of Personnel and Training that supports capacity building in the civil service through the iGOT Karmayogi platform.

Iyer holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree from Regional Engineering College, Trichy, and an MBA from The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania.
