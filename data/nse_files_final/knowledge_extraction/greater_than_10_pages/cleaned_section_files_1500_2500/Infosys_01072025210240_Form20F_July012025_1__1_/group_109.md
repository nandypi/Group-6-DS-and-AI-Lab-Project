## Holding the ADSs

## How will you hold your ADSs?

You may hold ADSs either directly or indirectly through a broker or other financial institution.

If held directly, you may hold:

1. An American Depositary Receipt (ADR), which is a certificate evidencing a specific number of ADSs registered in your name; or
2. ADSs in the Direct Registration System (DRS).

ADSs will be issued through DRS unless certificated ADRs are specifically requested. If you hold ADSs indirectly, you must rely on the procedures of your broker or financial institution to assert the rights of ADS holders described in this section.

## Dividends and Other Distributions

## How will you receive dividends and other distributions on the shares?

The depositary has agreed to pay ADS holders the cash dividends or other distributions it or the custodian receives on equity shares or other deposited securities, after deducting applicable fees and expenses. Distributions will be made in proportion to the number of equity shares represented by the ADSs as of the applicable record date, which will be set by the depositary as close as practicable to the record date for the equity shares.

- **Cash.** The depositary will convert, or cause to be converted, cash dividends and other cash distributions, or net proceeds from the sale of equity shares, rights, securities, or other entitlements, into U.S. dollars if conversion and transfer to the United States are reasonably possible. It will then promptly distribute the amount received. If conversion or transfer is not possible or lawful, or required government approval or a license cannot be obtained at a reasonable cost within a reasonable period, the depositary may distribute foreign currency only to ADS holders to whom it is possible to do so. Unconverted foreign currency will be held for the accounts of unpaid ADS holders, will not be invested, and will not accrue interest.

- **Deductions and rounding.** Before making a distribution, the depositary will deduct applicable taxes, governmental charges, and its fees and expenses. It will distribute only whole U.S. dollars and cents and will round fractional cents down to the nearest whole cent.

- **Shares.** For equity shares distributed as a dividend or free distribution, the depositary will either distribute additional ADSs representing those shares or arrange for existing ADSs to represent rights and interests in the additional shares, to the extent reasonably practicable and permitted by law. Applicable fees, charges, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges will be deducted. The depositary will distribute only whole ADSs and may sell shares that would otherwise require delivery of a fractional ADS. It may also sell enough distributed shares to pay related fees, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges.

- **Elective distributions in cash or shares.** If equity shareholders are offered a choice between receiving dividends in cash or shares, the depositary may determine whether the election will be made available to ADS holders after consultation with the company and receipt of timely notice under the deposit agreement. The company must instruct the depositary to make the election available and provide satisfactory evidence that doing so is lawful. The depositary may determine that the election is not legal or reasonably practicable. In that case, it will distribute cash or additional ADSs based on the determination made for equity shareholders who do not elect. The depositary is not required to provide a method for ADS holders to receive shares rather than ADSs, and ADS holders may not receive elective distributions on the same terms as equity shareholders.

- **Rights to purchase additional shares.** If equity shareholders are offered rights to subscribe for additional shares, the depositary will consult with the company after receiving timely notice under the deposit agreement. The company must determine whether making the rights available to ADS holders is lawful and reasonably practicable, instruct the depositary to do so, and provide satisfactory evidence of legality.

  If the rights cannot be made available but can lawfully and reasonably be sold, the depositary will endeavor to sell them and distribute the net proceeds in the same manner as cash. Rights that are neither distributed nor sold will lapse, and ADS holders will receive no value for them.

  If rights are made available, the depositary will establish procedures for their distribution and exercise upon payment of applicable fees, charges, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges. The depositary is not required to provide a method for exercising rights to subscribe for equity shares rather than ADSs.

  U.S. securities laws may restrict the transfer and cancellation of ADSs representing shares purchased upon exercise of rights. ADS holders may not be able to trade those ADSs freely in the United States. In that case, the depositary may deliver restricted depositary shares with the same terms as the ADSs, subject to changes required to implement the restrictions.

  ADS holders may not be given the opportunity to exercise rights on the same terms and conditions as equity shareholders.

- **Other distributions.** Subject to timely notice from the company, and if the depositary determines that the distribution is lawful, reasonably practicable, feasible, and permitted under the deposit agreement, it will distribute other property received on deposited securities using a method it considers equitable and practicable. ADS holders must pay applicable fees, charges, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges.

  If these conditions are not met, the depositary may sell the distributed property and distribute the net proceeds as cash. If it cannot sell the property, it may dispose of it in a reasonably practicable manner or return it to the company. In either case, ADS holders may have no rights to or arising from the property.
