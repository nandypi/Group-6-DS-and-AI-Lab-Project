## Infosys Knowledge Institute('IKI')

Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI) harnesses the intellectual capital of Infosys experts and client collaborations to produce content and insights on the business impact of technology for enterprises. IKI also develops proprietary data and insights through primary research, including large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis.

IKI publishes Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the Tech Compass technology trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals. IKI has collaborated with 800 clients and created more than 1,500 assets since inception.

## (2) Enterprise Solutions

## SAP

The Infosys SAP Practice provides end-to-end consulting, implementation, and support services. Its offerings support S/4HANA cloud adoption, SAP Signavio and LeanIX process excellence, RISE with SAP and GROW with SAP, SAP Business Data Cloud, Business AI, and SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP).

Infosys is a RISE with SAP Validated Partner and provides migration support, cost-optimization services, intelligent automation, integration, and clean-core solutions.

## Oracle

The Infosys Oracle Practice has been a strategic Oracle partner for more than 28 years. It provides strategic advisory services, business transformation, industry solutions, infrastructure modernization, and AI-first implementation and maintenance services.

The practice covers Oracle ERP, Supply Chain, Customer Experience, Human Capital Management, Oracle Cloud Applications, and Oracle technology services.

## Enterprise Application Integration ('EAIS')

The Enterprise Application Integration Services (EAIS) Practice provides offerings in AI, cloud, and digital transformation, including:

- Agentic process automation
- Domain-led process solutions
- Low-code/no-code application development
- Cloud-native development
- Cloud-first integration
- Digital process automation
- Digital supply chain solutions

EAIS offerings are intended to support connected enterprises through cloud integration, application development, process automation, low-code/no-code development, and digital supply chain capabilities.

### Cloud First Integration

The Cloud First Integration practice creates a digital backbone for API value networks. Its capabilities include digital and cloud integration, API management, open APIs, connected marketplaces, event streaming, and integration-platform-as-a-service (iPaaS), with a focus on AI, cloud, data platforms, digital adoption, legacy simplification, and technology displacement.

### Cloud Native Development

The Cloud Native Development practice designs, builds, and runs applications such as microservices on cloud and containerized platforms.

### Digital Process Automation

The Digital Process Automation practice provides agentic process automation, process mining, process optimization, and process automation. Technologies and platforms include Pega, UiPath, Blue Prism, Automation Anywhere, Power Automate, Celonis, and ARIS.

### Low Code/No Code App Development

The Low Code/No Code App Development practice develops modern intelligent digital applications using platforms including Appian, Camunda, and Unqork.

### Digital Supply Chain

The Digital Supply Chain practice provides end-to-end digital supply chain solutions for planning, fulfillment, spend, and asset lifecycle management across the value chain. Areas of focus include order management systems, warehouse management systems, planning, procurement, and enterprise asset management.

## Microsoft Business Applications Services Practice

The Infosys Microsoft Practice provides industry-focused, AI-led, end-to-end services across advisory, consulting, blueprinting, remediation, implementation, rollouts, application maintenance, and support.

The practice has developed industry solutions, horizontal offerings, and accelerators supporting predictability, innovation, business resilience, and measurable outcomes.

Its “Total Human Experience” framework covers:

- Customer Experience (CX)
- Employee Experience (EX)
- Operational Experience (OX)

The framework is supported by a “Platform of Possibilities” digital realization framework intended to improve human experience, adoption, and measurable business value.

The Microsoft Practice combines Microsoft Agents and Copilots, Microsoft Business Applications and the Dynamics 365 suite, Modern Work and the Microsoft 365 suite, Microsoft Power Platform, Dataverse, and Azure-based services.

## Enterprise Cloud Application Solutions ('ECAS')

The ECAS Practice has been a Salesforce partner for more than a decade. It provides consulting, implementation, integration, and support services across customer experience platforms, including:

- Sales Cloud
- Service Cloud
- Marketing Cloud
- App Cloud
- Experience Cloud
- Heroku
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Data Cloud
- Agentforce
- Industry Clouds
- AppExchange technologies

Infosys is a Summit partner of Salesforce and has invested in Salesforce Centers of Excellence. It also collaborates with Salesforce independent software vendor (ISV) partners and has capabilities in Conga, CloudSense, Copado, ServiceMax, and Certinia.

The practice has vertical capabilities in Health Cloud, Education Cloud, Communications Cloud, Financial Services Cloud, Automotive Cloud, Public Sector Cloud, Manufacturing Cloud, and Consumer Goods Cloud. It has developed native Salesforce solutions for life sciences, consumer packaged goods (CPG), retail, manufacturing, and high-tech micro-verticals.

The acquisitions of Fluido and Simplus, Salesforce advisors and consulting partners in the Nordics and the Americas, strengthened Infosys’ Salesforce enterprise cloud services, cloud consulting, implementation, and training capabilities.

Infosys’ Digital Experience offering covers consulting and advisory, digital transformation, implementation and integration, management and operations, and business services.

### Studio-as-a-Service

### Digital Marketing Services

Digital Marketing services use data-driven insights to support personalized experiences and content, customer relationships, brand experience, always-on marketing, and marketing-spend optimization.

## Application Development Capabilities

Infosys Modern Application Development offerings create custom software solutions using technical expertise, domain experience, consulting capabilities, intellectual property assets, and methodologies.

The offerings include:

- Cloud-native development
- Industry-specific vertical package rollouts
- Re-engineering of existing business processes
- Product and platform engineering
- Custom industry solutions

Infosys uses an AI-first, cloud-agnostic, agile, and product-centric approach to develop secure, modular, easy-to-deploy, and scalable solutions.

AI is integrated throughout the software development lifecycle. Infosys uses its iLEAD platform for persona-based software development lifecycle assistance, the Infosys Applied AI platform for foundational AI enablement, custom AI models and transformers, and technologies such as GitHub Copilot. Infosys is among the top global system integrators in GitHub Copilot adoption.

The overall application development and maintenance (ADM) services are supported by a Domain Consulting Group (DCG) comprising functional and product experts from multiple industry segments. DCG provides domain-based consulting, thought leadership, domain-centric solution blueprinting, industry product and package adoption support, and development of Infosys industry-specific intellectual property assets.

DCG has created AI-first industry blueprints for 23 industry segments, aligned with segment-specific challenges, priorities, and opportunities.
