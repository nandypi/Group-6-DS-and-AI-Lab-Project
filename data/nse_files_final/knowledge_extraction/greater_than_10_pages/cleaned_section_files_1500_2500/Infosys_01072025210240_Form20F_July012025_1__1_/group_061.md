## How the Critical Audit Matter Was Addressed in the Audit

Audit procedures related to estimates of total expected costs or efforts to complete fixed-price contracts included the following:

- Tested the effectiveness of controls relating to:
  - Recording efforts or costs incurred and estimating efforts or costs required to complete the remaining contract performance obligations.
  - Access and application controls for time recording, allocation, and budgeting systems that prevent unauthorized changes to recorded efforts incurred.
- Selected a sample of fixed-price contracts measured using the percentage-of-completion method and:
  - Evaluated management’s ability to reasonably estimate progress toward satisfying performance obligations by comparing actual efforts or costs incurred with prior-year estimates for fulfilled performance obligations.
  - Compared efforts or costs incurred to the Group’s estimates of costs or efforts incurred to date, identifying significant variations and evaluating whether those variations were appropriately considered in estimating remaining costs or efforts to complete the contracts.
  - Tested whether estimates were consistent with delivery milestones, customer acceptances, and customer sign-offs to identify possible delays in achieving milestones that could require changes to estimated costs or efforts to complete the remaining performance obligations.

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Bengaluru, India, signed the audit report on July 1, 2025. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP has served as the Company’s auditor since fiscal 2018.
