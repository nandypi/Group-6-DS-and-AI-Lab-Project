Cost of sales decreased by 0.4 percentage points as a percentage of revenue in fiscal 2025 compared with fiscal 2024. This was mainly due to a reduction in cost of efforts—employee and technical subcontractor costs—as a percentage of revenue, driven by reduced onsite mix and improved utilization, partly offset by increased third-party costs. Third-party items bought for service delivery to clients include software and hardware integral to overall client service delivery.
