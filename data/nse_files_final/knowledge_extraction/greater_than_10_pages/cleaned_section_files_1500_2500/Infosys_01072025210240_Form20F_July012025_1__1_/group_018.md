## Application Management Capabilities

Application Management services assist clients in optimizing the cost of application operations, enhancing business service resilience, improving business experience, and supporting technological innovation. Infosys applies generative AI and agentic AI to application operations to improve efficiency and service quality and support autonomous operations.

Infosys uses an industrialized, IP-based service delivery model that leverages the Infosys Live Enterprise Automation Platform (LEAP) and partner tools to improve productivity and support consistent, data-driven service delivery.

Infosys has developed a repository of pre-built, ready-to-deploy AI agents using its proprietary agentic framework. These agents orchestrate application operations workflows through automation bots and conversational AI to support self-healing and predictive application operations.

Infosys supports business availability through proactive monitoring of critical business processes. Its tool-based approach to application portfolio analysis and technical debt management supports cloud adoption and helps mitigate security risks. Generative AI is used throughout the application maintenance lifecycle, together with Infosys intellectual property and partner tools.

## Application Modernization Capabilities

Infosys' AI-first application modernization offerings help enterprises modernize legacy IT systems, data landscapes, and applications while minimizing disruption. The offerings cover industry-specific modernization across major verticals and technology modernization spanning cloud, mainframe, databases, and low-code/no-code platforms.

The Infosys Live Enterprise Application Development Platform (iLEAD), part of Infosys Cobalt®, is designed to simplify and accelerate application modernization and development. It draws on experience from more than 10,000 modernization programs for more than 600 clients and supports hyperautomation through standards and best practices.

iLEAD provides guided workflows and AI-enabled tools that abstract underlying technology complexity, improve developer productivity, unlock information from legacy systems, simplify decision-making, and reduce dependency on niche skills. It also integrates with disparate application lifecycle management (ALM) tools to provide data-driven insights intended to improve sprint velocity, release predictability, and product quality.

## Infosys Quality Engineering Services

Infosys Quality Engineering provides domain-specific services, solutions, and platforms for improving the quality and reliability of digital transformations, accelerating test cycles, and adapting to changing quality requirements. Its AI-first approach integrates intelligent, automated, and scalable quality engineering practices throughout the software lifecycle.

## People

Infosys' quality engineering professionals have expertise in testing, automation, AI/ML, and continuous delivery. They collaborate with development, operations, and business teams.

## Process

Infosys has evolved from traditional, phase-gated quality assurance to agile, DevOps-driven practices. Its approach embeds quality throughout the lifecycle and emphasizes shift-left testing and continuous integration and testing to provide faster feedback, reduce defects, and improve efficiency.

## Technology

Infosys uses AI/ML-based predictive analytics, advanced test automation frameworks, and cloud-native solutions. Its platform-driven approach integrates with CI/CD pipelines and provides real-time insights for continuous improvement.

## Three-Horizon Service Model

Infosys monitors market trends and client needs to identify investment areas and address emerging quality engineering challenges.

- Horizon 1: Mainstream quality engineering services addressing current enterprise needs.
- Horizon 2: Services gaining significant traction and maturity.
- Horizon 3: Emerging and early-stage services focused on future growth.

## Strategic Partnerships

Infosys' four-tiered Quality Engineering Partnership (QEP) model includes:

- QEP1: Productivity improvement partners, including Tricentis, UiPath, and Micro Focus, supporting automation across the quality engineering lifecycle.
- QEP2: Domain and functionality package providers, including SAP, Salesforce, and Adobe, supporting industry expertise.
- QEP3: Cloud service providers, including AWS, Azure, and GCP, supporting cloud-native digital stacks.
- QEP4: AI solution providers and startups, including OpenAI and Nvidia, supporting AI-first offerings.

## Data and Analytics

Infosys' Data Analytics and AI (DNA) practice develops data analytics and AI solutions to help customers use data and AI for business transformation. Through Infosys Topaz®, the practice aims to help enterprises accelerate growth, improve efficiency at scale, and build connected ecosystems.

Infosys helps customers prepare enterprise data for AI by establishing data foundations and platforms. Its services cover the data lifecycle, including defining data and AI strategies, implementing enterprise information architectures, acquiring data from disparate sources, and delivering insights through multiple channels, including self-service options.

The practice combines customer data journeys with AI strategies to support AI-first business transformation. Its capabilities include AI and analytics models, data science, mathematical modeling, robotics, machine learning, and business process automation.

Infosys also provides systems for managing large data volumes and enabling real-time AI-led insights through high-speed data curation and knowledge cataloging. Its AI Brain and Smart Data Fabric capabilities are intended to accelerate time-to-market and support the development of new business models, products, and services.

The practice service offerings include:

- **AI Strategy and Value Office:** Helps enterprises establish business-focused AI strategies, executive governance, processes, and outcome-focused value offices. It also provides guidance on technology and architecture choices and supports the development of data, analytics, and business intelligence competency centers. The Value Office framework is used to conceptualize, define, measure, and manage value generated by AI initiatives.
- **Getting Enterprises Data Ready for AI:** Uses platforms and accelerators to help enterprises catalog and fingerprint data, apply governance guardrails throughout the data and AI lifecycle, and democratize knowledge.
- **Data Modernization and Transformation:** Supports the strategy, assessment, definition, and implementation of end-to-end enterprise information architectures across cloud and hybrid environments. The offering uses an AI-first approach to support business transformation, IT productivity, and data consumption through native cloud platforms and surrounding ecosystems.
- **AI-Powered Business Transformation:** Helps enterprises reimagine business processes with AI-augmented capabilities and pursue measurable business outcomes.
- **Building AI Economy:** Helps enterprises build AI-driven products and services delivered through partner networks.
- **Data Monetization:** Supports the discovery and realization of intelligence-led opportunities specific to individual businesses.
- **Data and AI Governance:** Provides frameworks and expertise for trust, data security, privacy compliance, and ethical AI development.
- **Vendor Consolidation and GCC:** Driven by Infosys' Data & AI Estate Management offering, this service aims to consolidate vendors, reduce services spending, and create a self-funding model to optimize, manage, and transform an enterprise's data and AI landscape. The model includes total-cost-of-ownership reduction through AI-fueled hyperproductivity, vertical consolidation, right-shoring, cloud-driven modernization, and license and application rationalization.
- **Data Operations:** Supports agile, easy-to-manage operations for boundaryless data landscapes through extensive automation.
- **Data Integration and Extract, Transform and Load (ETL):** Provides end-to-end services for building enterprise data warehouses, data marts, and data stores. Services include data engineering, data models, industry-specific models, and data provisioning layers using ETL tools.
- **Master Data Management (MDM), Data Quality and Governance:** Defines and implements MDM capabilities using tools and custom technologies, together with industry-specific data quality and governance services.
- **Business Intelligence and Reporting:** Provides reporting, dashboards, and analytics to support self-service business intelligence and consumption across multiple digital platforms.
- **Enterprise Performance Management:** Delivers enterprise performance management solutions covering key performance indicators, profitability analysis, financial consolidation, and planning.
