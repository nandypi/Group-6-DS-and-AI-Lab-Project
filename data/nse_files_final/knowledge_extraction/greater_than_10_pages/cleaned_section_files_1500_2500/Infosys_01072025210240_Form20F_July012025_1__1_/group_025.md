## Results for Fiscal 2025 compared to Fiscal 2024

During fiscal 2025, the company continued Project Maximus, a margin expansion program structured across five pillars:

- **Value-Based Selling:** Identifying opportunities to align pricing with the value clients receive from the services provided.
- **Efficient Pyramid:** Improving utilization, reducing subcontractors, and optimizing role and location mix to reduce effort costs.
- **Lean, Automation & Gen AI:** Combining productivity and process improvements with automation technologies, including generative AI.
- **Improving Critical Portfolios:** Improving margins on large client engagements and service lines.
- **Reducing Indirect Costs:** Optimizing general and administrative expenses.

## Revenues

Revenues are generated principally from services provided on a time-and-material, unit-of-work, fixed-price, or fixed-timeframe basis. Many client contracts, including fixed-price and fixed-timeframe contracts, can be terminated by clients with or without cause, generally with notice periods of 0 to 90 days. Revenue is generally collected as contractual milestones are completed; therefore, terminated contracts are subject to collection for the portion completed through termination.

The company monitors contract progress and change orders based on contract characteristics and circumstances, including the ability of both the company and the client to perform, extraordinary conditions that may lead to termination, and historical client performance. Fixed-price and fixed-timeframe projects expose the company to cost overruns and inflation risks. Operating results may be adversely affected by inaccurate estimates of completion costs and dates, including wage inflation and currency exchange rates. The company revises project completion estimates periodically; such revisions have not, to date, had a material adverse effect on operating results or financial condition.

The company experiences pricing pressure from clients, including expectations of volume discounts, requests for fixed-price or fixed-timeframe arrangements, and requests for reduced rates. Fixed-price arrangements are generally used where engagement specifications are complete.

The following table sets forth revenue growth in fiscal 2025 from fiscal 2024:

|  | Fiscal 2025 | Fiscal 2024 | Change | Percentage Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Revenue growth in fiscal 2025 was primarily attributable to increased realization from Value-Based Selling, productivity benefits from Lean & Automation, acquisition and revenue mix. These factors were partially offset by declining volumes and currency impact.

### Constant Currency

Constant currency is a non-IFRS measure. Reported-currency revenue growth includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant-currency revenue growth excludes these fluctuations by converting current-period revenues in local currencies into U.S. dollars using prior-period exchange rates and comparing the result with prior-period reported revenues.

Reported-currency revenue for fiscal 2025 was $19,277 million, representing growth of 3.9% compared with fiscal 2024. Constant-currency revenue growth was 4.2% compared with fiscal 2024.

During fiscal 2025, the U.S. dollar depreciated by 1.6% against the United Kingdom pound sterling and appreciated by 1.5% against the Australian dollar and by 0.9% against the euro.

Overall segment profitability increased primarily due to foreign currency translation benefits, Project Maximus benefits from Value-Based Selling and Lean & Automation, improved utilization, and an insurance claim related to fiscal 2024. Financial Services profitability increased mainly due to improved margins in large deals and the insurance claim related to fiscal 2024.

Retail profitability increased mainly due to higher pricing realization and lower costs of third-party items purchased for service delivery as part of deals.

Communication profitability decreased mainly due to the ramp-up of large deals and higher costs of technical subcontractors. Large deals typically have lower margins during the initial deal period.

Life Sciences profitability decreased mainly due to lower realization, higher costs of third-party items purchased for service delivery as part of deals, and a higher onsite mix.

## Onsite and Offshore Revenues

The company assumes full project management responsibility for projects undertaken. Projects are divided into components executed simultaneously at client sites and Development Centers located outside India (“onsite”), and at Global Development Centers in India (“offshore”).

The proportion of work performed at company facilities and client sites varies by period. Onsite work generally has higher billing rates and higher compensation and other expenses. It generates higher revenue per capita but lower gross margins as a percentage of revenue than work performed at company facilities in India. Consequently, total revenues, cost of sales, and gross profit may fluctuate in absolute terms and as a percentage of revenue.

## Revenue Composition and Utilization

Revenues from software services, including BPM services, represented 95.3% of total revenues in fiscal 2025 and 94.5% in fiscal 2024. Products and platforms represented 4.7% and 5.5% of total revenues in fiscal 2025 and fiscal 2024, respectively.

Revenue from fixed-price contracts was approximately 54% in fiscal 2025 and 53% in fiscal 2024.

Revenues and gross profits are affected by employee utilization. Employee utilization for IT services is defined as billed person-months as a proportion of available person-months, excluding sales, administrative, and support personnel. Utilization is managed by monitoring project requirements and timetables. Staffing varies according to project size, complexity, duration, and requirements. Unanticipated termination of a significant project may reduce utilization. Technology professionals are also not utilized while enrolled in training programs, including training for new employees.
