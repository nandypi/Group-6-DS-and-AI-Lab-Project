## Distinct-phased approach adopted in transitioning to hybrid work culture

Infosys adopted a phased, multi-pronged approach to transition to a hybrid work model across work, workspace, and workforce. The transition was managed centrally and at individual unit and Development Center (DC) levels, with regional implementation aligned to the norms and policies of the countries in which Infosys operates.

Key enablers included:

- **Phased approach:** In Phase 1, employees were encouraged to work from DC locations two days per week. Phase 2 moved toward office attendance for 10 days per month, with units and managers having flexibility to determine the approach.
- **Employee voice:** Employee polling was used throughout the return-to-office process to refine hybrid-work frameworks across locations.
- **Work from any office:** Employees can work from any Infosys DC in India. Five new hubs provide additional office options for the distributed workforce.
- **Workspace hoteling:** Employees and teams can book workspace on campus.
- **Equality of experience:** Infosys designed comparable employee touchpoints for people working from home, the office, or client sites.
- **Engagement programs:** Segmented employee engagement programs were conducted in hybrid mode at office campuses.
- **Physical onboarding:** New joiners participate in physical onboarding programs intended to support cultural assimilation.

## Initiatives to enhance our Employee Value Proposition (EVP)

Infosys' Employee Value Proposition focuses on meaningful work, career learning and growth, employee participation in shaping the organization's future, and career opportunities supported by its culture and employee networks.

## EMPLOYEE CAREERS & LEARNING AVENUES

- **Lex:** Infosys' in-house learning platform supports talent development through hybrid learning models.
- **Learning and Career:** A one-stop portal for employees' learning and career needs, integrated with internal systems such as Lex.
- **Internal Marketplace:** Matches employees' skills and capabilities with opportunities for job rotation in selected work areas.
- **Internal Job Movements (IJP):** Connects employees with internal job opportunities, career movements, and promotions.
- **Holistic Skill Building and Career Pathing:** Defines foundational, social, and role-specific skills. Career Canvas allows employees to select target roles and follow skill-based learning paths for current and future roles.
- **Bridge:** Helps employees develop new skills and move into careers that typically require different qualifications.
- **Accelerate:** Provides exposure to roles and practical experience through short-term internal projects. The platform allows job creators to publish independent job modules for job-seeking colleagues.
- **Performance management:** Uses regular manager-team check-ins, career conversations, and Integrated Development Plans (IDPs) to support engagement and development.
- **Faster and predictable careers:** Identifies employees eligible for promotion or progression in the coming quarters, enabling earlier engagement with key talent.
- **Digital Specialist:** An aspirational career track for high performers working with niche technologies on digital transformation projects. Growth is capability-driven rather than tenure-dependent.
- **Digital Quotient (DQ):** Tracks employees' digital skills. Employees with higher Digital Quotient have greater access to new opportunities and projects.
- **Platinum Club:** A program for top-performing employees that provides diverse career experiences.
- **Manager and leadership development:** The Global Delivery Talent Effectiveness program includes external sessions, internal leader interactions, mentoring, cross-skilling, best-practice sharing, and collaboration across business segments and units.
- **Sales Capability Building:** Sales transformation initiatives include:
  - **ZENITH CLUB:** Recognizes and rewards consistent high performance in Global Sales.
  - **SYNERGY:** A four-week onboarding program for new sales employees.
  - **AI First:** More than 500 sales personnel were certified on AI applications for growth through an eight-week program from Kellogg School of Business, covering practical business applications and responsible AI.
  - **ELEVATE:** A talent mobility platform intended to provide Global Markets leaders with qualified candidates.
  - **INFUSION ASCENT:** An in-person platform for new title holders, with training from Infosys leaders, external trainers, and client leaders.
  - **ASSURED:** A sales leadership program conducted in partnership with Stanford, focused on financial acumen.
  - **SAPIENCE:** A platform supporting women in sales through leadership interaction, professional learning, relationship building, and skills development.

## EMPLOYEE EXPERIENCE

Infosys uses employee feedback, technology, and automation across the employee life cycle to support onboarding, engagement, learning, careers, performance management, and workforce efficiency.

- **Launchpad:** A mobile and web-based self-service platform for globally guided onboarding of new hires in the hybrid workplace.
- **Infosys Immerse:** A virtual environment used in onboarding for essential messages, cultural immersion, and connections among new joiners. It includes avatars, learning content, virtual engagement spaces, chat, and audio and video capabilities.
- **Infosys Verify:** A background-check tool integrated with the Talent Management System to address global background-check requirements and varied requests.
- **AI-FIRST:** HR uses AI across the employee life cycle to improve productivity and internal systems. Navi, a generative-AI-powered employee assistant, allows employees to query policies in natural language and receive responses across HR and other employee-facing areas.
- **Moments That Matter (MTM):** A celebration platform designed to support significant moments throughout the employee life cycle. Smart alerts notify employees about upcoming moments, and AI can generate cards and messages for colleagues and teams.
- **People analytics:** People analytics has supported data-driven initiatives since fiscal 2024.
- **InfyMe:** The platform continued to add services supporting team operations, communication, and collaboration in the hybrid working model.
- **Intelligent automation:** Automated nudges guide managers and employees on learning, careers, role changes, retention, and performance management.
- **Digitization:** Technology and automation are used to improve workforce efficiency, engagement, transformation, and innovation. Robotic Process Automation reduces manual work and improves product quality.
- **Quickstart:** An orientation program that provides new employees with information and support during their initial days and helps them integrate into their roles and teams through global- and unit-level initiatives.
- **Global Delivery Talent Effectiveness Team:** Develops learning interventions for delivery managers and leaders, including client leadership sessions and internal talks focused on capability development, collaboration, and best-practice sharing.
- **Business of People COE:** The Business of People Center of Excellence provides customized HR solutions for large transformation deals. Its subject-matter experts develop and deliver HR strategies aligned with business practices, support Build Operate Transfer programs and people transition programs, and provide HR support and client engagement during site visits.
