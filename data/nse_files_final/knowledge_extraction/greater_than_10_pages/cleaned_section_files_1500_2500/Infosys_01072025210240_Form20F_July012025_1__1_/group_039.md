## REWARDS & RECOGNITION

Infosys’ performance-driven recognition framework, RISE, stands for Real, Instant, Specific, and Excite. It brings recognition practices across units together under a standardized umbrella and integrated digital platform.

RISE initiatives include:

- Gracias, an employee appreciation portal.
- Insta Awards, which enables managers to recognize teams instantly.
- Unit & Integrated Account R&R awards, which promote and sustain high-performance work culture.
- Wow Awards, discretionary awards selected by leaders to acknowledge outstanding contributions.
- PM Elite+, quarterly awards recognizing the best-managed projects and top project management talent.
- Infosys Awards For Excellence (AFE), with winners selected after multiple levels of evaluations and audits.
- DC-level biannual or annual awards ceremonies recognizing talent and contributions at each DC.
- Sales Excellence Awards, an annual sales recognition program presented during the annual sales strategy meet, Connect.
- Stellar Awards, presented quarterly to sales employees with winners recognized at the segment level.
- Segment-level annual awards for individuals and teams with outstanding performance, announced during annual segment strategy meetings.

## EMPLOYEE CARE & CONNECT

Infosys states that it is an equal opportunity employer committed to a healthy work environment free from harassment and discrimination. Resolution Hubs address employee concerns through investigations conducted by neutral panels.

- **ASHI (Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative):** Infosys’ policy prohibits sexual harassment and unlawful harassment involving employees, supervisors, vendors, and clients. The policy applies to people involved in the operation of the Group and covers conduct prohibited by applicable federal, state, or local law, ordinance, or regulation. Periodic interventions educate employees and contract staff.
- **HEAR (Hearing Employees and Resolving):** A formal grievance review process for matters that cannot be resolved through discussions with an employee’s immediate supervisor and the supervisor’s manager. It covers managerial issues, process or policy eligibility, workplace discrimination, performance management, and interpersonal issues.
- **HALE (Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment):** A nonmonetary employee benefit based on four pillars: physical, emotional, and social well-being, and safety. The program provides health and lifestyle interventions developed with extended teams, business units, and external partners.
- **Manager Code:** Provides managers with capabilities to help teams build technical, business, people, and digital skills. Seven Manager Codes serve as behavioral principles for managers. Manager Hub provides manager information, toolkits, and resources.
- **Infosys Power of Wellness:** A platform providing curated wellbeing services for managers and their families.
- **MeetUp Campaign:** A targeted communication and digital-nudge program intended to improve meeting experiences.
- **Infosys Great Manager Program:** A self-paced, e-learning program comprising short management courses from eCornell. It develops business acumen, digital mindset, people leadership, and operational excellence.
- **Internal Recognition Programs:** Programs including People Champions, Best Managers, and Managers with Great Teams recognize managers’ work and contributions.

## CREATING A POSITIVE WORK ENVIRONMENT

- **Infosys Way of Life:** A workplace-culture initiative focused on collaboration, innovation, and inclusion in a hybrid work environment. It is anchored in five cultural markers: Care, Values (C-LIFE), Collaboration, Learning, and Inclusion.
- **C-LIFE:** As outlined in the Code of Conduct, C-LIFE stands for Client value, Leadership by example, Integrity & Transparency, Fairness, and Excellence.
- **Behavior Matters:** A campaign encouraging positive workplace behavior.
- **InfyTribes:** An employee community ecosystem built around shared passions and goals. Its clubs include cricket, music, quiz, dance, and Toastmasters. InfyTribes operates across 12 countries, with more than 20,000 members and more than 100,000 followers.
- **INFYusion 2024:** A three-day sports and cultural event bringing together finalists from Infosys DCs.
- **InfyCares:** An employee-volunteering initiative. Activities included seedball making, bookmark creation, solar-lamp assembly, and Educational Jenga. InfyCares recorded 1.30 lakh volunteering hours, with 30,000 volunteers participating in more than 1,000 events across DCs.
- **Alumni Meets:** Infosys held 10 alumni meets in different cities, attended by more than 8,000 alumni.
- **Petit Infoscion Day:** An annual event for employees’ children. Participation increased by 33%, engaging more than 100,000 employees across 20 DCs.
- **Orbit Next:** A program designed to support women’s progression into leadership roles in technology and business through mentorship, workshops, networking, and technical, business, and leadership development.
- **Restart With Infosys:** A program supporting women returning to the workforce after a career break through personalized mentorship and reintegration support. More than 900 women professionals joined Infosys through the program over two years.
