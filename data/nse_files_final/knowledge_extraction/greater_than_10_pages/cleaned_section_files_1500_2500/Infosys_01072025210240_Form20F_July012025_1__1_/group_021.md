## (3) Products & Platforms

### EdgeVerve AI Next

EdgeVerve AI Next is an enterprise transformation platform designed to support AI-led digital transformation. Its Unified AI Platform is designed to use generative AI and agentic AI across people, processes, data, and technology. It combines AI-driven insights, cloud-native architecture, and automation for applied AI, business services, enterprise transformation, and supply chain optimization.

### Finacle

Finacle is a digital banking solutions suite with cloud-native and SaaS offerings. Its solutions address core banking, lending, digital engagement, payments, cash management, wealth management, treasury, analytics, AI, pricing, and blockchain requirements. Banks in more than 100 countries use Finacle to serve more than one billion people.

### McCamish

Infosys McCamish provides platform-based insurance process management solutions and services across insurance products, distribution models, and deployment options.

Its offerings include:

- Individual life insurance and annuities
- Employer-sponsored benefits and retirement
- Producer management and compensation
- VPAS platform licensing, SaaS engagements, and end-to-end BPM policy administration services
- PMACS producer and distribution management
- NGIN cloud-native life insurance and annuity policy administration
- NBUW support for acquisitions, suitability analysis, and underwriting decisions
- Customer and agent portals
- API integration with smart devices and IoT components, including smart video and chatbots

The VPAS platform supports servicing insurance and annuity products across the industry value chain on a single platform. PMACS supports compensation plans, calculations, and distribution management in financial services.

### Panaya

Panaya is a SaaS-based company certified by SAP, Oracle, and Salesforce.com. It provides Smart Testing and Change Intelligence for ERP, CRM, and cloud business applications, including AI-powered test automation, test management, and impact analysis.

Since its founding in 2006, more than 3,000 companies across 62 countries, including one-third of the Fortune 500, have used Panaya for testing and change management in enterprise business applications.

### Infosys Equinox

Infosys Equinox is a composable, human-centric digital commerce and marketing platform for personalized omnichannel shopping experiences. Its MACH-X architecture is microservices-based, API-first, cloud-native, headless, and extensible. The platform includes a partner ecosystem, app store, and commerce-as-a-service operating model.

### Infosys Helix

Infosys Helix is a platform-powered, AI-first suite for analyzing and customizing healthcare data for payers, providers, members, and governments. It includes:

- Provider Lifecycle Management Platform
- Payer On Cloud Platform
- Payer B2B Platform

### Infosys Stater

Infosys Stater operates across the mortgage and consumer loan value chain, with capabilities in digital origination, servicing, and collections. Its digital transformation accelerators include dynamic workflow, API layers, robotic process automation, and analytics.

## SALES AND MARKETING OVERVIEW

Infosys has organized its sales and marketing functions into teams across 59 countries, focused on AI, cloud, and other digital solutions for specific industries and geographies.

The company has more than 320,000 employees, referred to as Infoscions. Its localization investments in talent and innovation centers support client transformation activities. Infosys also states that it is committed to being a well-governed and environmentally sustainable partner, with diverse talent and an inclusive workplace.

## COMPETITION

Infosys identifies intense competition in traditional services, rapid marketplace changes, and emerging players in niche technology areas. The company has invested in proprietary software platforms and products, including:

- Infosys Cobalt, a set of services, solutions, and platforms for enterprise cloud adoption
- Infosys Topaz, a set of AI-first services, solutions, and platforms using generative AI technology
- Infosys Aster, an AI-amplified marketing suite

Infosys identifies potential competition from niche SaaS companies, cloud platform companies, and the insourcing of technology services by clients’ technology departments.
