## Notes:

1. The adjusted non-IFRS measures exclude the effect of provisions resulting from the Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025. These provisions increased the gratuity liability, arising from past service cost relating to a plan amendment, and leave liability by $143 million in the year ended March 31, 2026. They also resulted in a lower tax charge of $35 million.
2. Excluding the effect of Income Tax orders received under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and the Labour Code provisions, EPS growth in rupee terms was 12.1% for the year ended March 31, 2026 and 13.9% for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, both year over year.
3. Free cash flow includes cash payments toward the Labour Codes of $49 million for the quarter and $99 million for the year ended March 31, 2026.
4. Infosys uses non-IFRS financial performance measures to supplement financial information reported under IFRS. These measures should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for the relevant IFRS measures and should be read with the reported IFRS information. Infosys states that the adjustments are intended to reflect core performance across periods.

## Report on the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

## Opinion

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries comprise the condensed consolidated balance sheet as at March 31, 2026; the condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income for the three months and year ended on that date; the condensed consolidated statement of changes in equity; the condensed consolidated statement of cash flows for the year ended on that date; and the related notes.

The auditor stated that the financial statements give a true and fair view, in conformity with International Accounting Standard 34, "Interim Financial Reporting" (IAS 34), as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board, of:

- The consolidated state of affairs of the Group as at March 31, 2026.
- The consolidated profit and consolidated other comprehensive income for the three months and year ended on that date.
- The consolidated changes in equity and consolidated cash flows for the year ended on that date.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The auditor stated that it was independent of the Group under the applicable Code of Ethics and had fulfilled its other ethical responsibilities. The auditor considered the audit evidence obtained sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the audit opinion.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible for preparing and presenting interim condensed consolidated financial statements that provide a true and fair view of the Group's consolidated financial position, financial performance, other comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows in accordance with IAS 34.

The respective Boards of Directors of the entities included in the Group are responsible for:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records and safeguarding Group assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls that operate effectively to ensure the accuracy and completeness of accounting records and the preparation of financial statements free from material misstatement.
- Assessing the ability of the entities to continue as going concerns, disclosing applicable going-concern matters, and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless liquidation or cessation of operations is intended or there is no realistic alternative.
- Overseeing the Group's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The auditor's objective is to obtain reasonable assurance that the interim condensed consolidated financial statements are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing an opinion. The auditor noted that reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but is not a guarantee that an audit will always detect a material misstatement.

The auditor's responsibilities include:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error and designing responsive audit procedures.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal financial controls to design appropriate audit procedures, without expressing an opinion on their effectiveness.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies, the reasonableness of accounting estimates, and related disclosures.
- Evaluating management's use of the going-concern basis of accounting and whether a material uncertainty exists that could cast significant doubt on the Group's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the financial statements.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group and directing, supervising, and performing the audit of those entities.
