## Financial risk factors

The Company's activities expose it to market, credit, and liquidity risks. Its primary focus is to anticipate financial-market uncertainty and minimize potential adverse effects on financial performance.

The Company's primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. It uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange exposures. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of each customer and the concentration of risk among the top few customers.

## Market risk

The Company operates internationally, with a major portion of its business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers in various foreign currencies, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies.

The Company holds foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates. Exchange rates between the Indian rupee and foreign currencies have changed substantially in recent years and may fluctuate substantially in the future. The Company's results of operations are affected when the rupee appreciates or depreciates against these currencies.

During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Company designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions for the balance in the cash flow hedge reserve as at March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the statement of profit and loss within three months.

The Company determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at inception and through periodic prospective assessments, including whether the hedging instrument is expected to offset changes in the cash flows of the hedged items.

If the hedge ratio used for risk management is no longer optimal, but the risk management objective remains unchanged and the hedge continues to qualify for hedge accounting, the hedge relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Any hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in the statement of profit or loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.
