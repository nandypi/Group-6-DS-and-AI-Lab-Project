The applicable Indian corporate statutory tax rate was 25.17% for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

Income tax expense includes a net reversal of provisions of ₹877 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026 and net provisions of ₹132 crore for the year ended March 31, 2025. These amounts primarily relate to adjudication of disputed matters, filing of tax returns, and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, Infosys received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Indian Income Tax Authorities for assessment year 2013–14 and assessment years 2017–18 to 2021–22. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently, the Company recognized pre-tax interest income of ₹381 crore and reversed income tax provisions aggregating ₹869 crore, with the corresponding credit recorded in the Statement of Profit and Loss. Resolution of these disputes also reduced contingent liabilities by ₹86 crore.

During the year ended March 31, 2025, Infosys received orders under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Indian Income Tax Authorities for assessment years 2016–17 and 2019–20. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently, the Company recognized pre-tax interest income of ₹327 crore and reversed income tax provisions aggregating ₹183 crore, with the corresponding credit recorded in the Statement of Profit and Loss. Resolution of these disputes also reduced contingent liabilities by ₹1,068 crore.

Foreign tax expense primarily relates to income taxes payable overseas, principally in the United States.

In India, the Group benefited in prior years from tax incentives for exports of software and services by units registered under the Special Economic Zones (SEZs) Act, 2005. SEZ units that began providing services on or after April 1, 2005 were eligible for:

- A deduction of 100% of profits or gains from exported services for the first five years from the financial year in which the unit commenced providing services.
- A deduction of 50% of such profits or gains for the following five years.
- A further deduction of up to 50% of such profits or gains for an additional five years, subject to creating and using a Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes, in accordance with the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Deferred income tax for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially relates to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

Infosys is subject to a 15% U.S. Branch Profit Tax to the extent that its U.S. branch's net profit during the year exceeds the increase in the branch's net assets during the year, calculated under the U.S. Internal Revenue Code. As at March 31, 2026, the U.S. branch's net assets were approximately ₹7,736 crore. The Company recognized a deferred tax liability for Branch Profit Tax of ₹207 crore, net of credits, because it expects these branch profits to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches amounting to ₹19,270 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹16,593 crore as at March 31, 2025. The Company can control the timing of reversal and considers it probable that the temporary differences will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The Group generally intends to repatriate earnings from subsidiaries and branches only to the extent that they can be distributed tax-free.

Deferred income tax assets were not recognized on accumulated losses of ₹4,868 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹4,597 crore as at March 31, 2025 because it is probable that future taxable profit will not be available against which the unused tax losses can be utilized in the foreseeable future.
