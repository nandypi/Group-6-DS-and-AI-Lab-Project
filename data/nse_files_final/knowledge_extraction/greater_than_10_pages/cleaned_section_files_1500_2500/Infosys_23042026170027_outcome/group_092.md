## Accounting Policy

Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. Highly liquid investments readily convertible into known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

## Overview and Notes to the Standalone Financial Statements

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (“the Company” or “Infosys”) provides AI-first business consulting and technology services. Its capabilities include business transformation, domain expertise, and orchestration of innovations from its AI-native partner ecosystem.

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary equity listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares representing equity shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The standalone financial statements were approved for issue by the Board of Directors on April 23, 2026.

*Net of tax.*

1. The Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve was created from the profit of eligible SEZ units under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used by the Company to acquire new plant and machinery for its business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

2. Profit or loss on transfer of business between entities under common control was taken to reserve.
