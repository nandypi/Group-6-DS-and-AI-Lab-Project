## d) Appointment of Independent Director

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200) on April 17, 2026, as an Additional & Independent Director effective April 22, 2026, for a period of three years, subject to shareholder approval.

## e) Impact of Labour Codes

On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Code on Social Security, 2020; and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (the “Labour Codes”). The Labour Codes consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during and after employment. They include changes such as a uniform definition of wages and enhanced leave benefits.

The Group assessed the financial implications of these changes, which resulted in an increase of approximately ₹1,289 crore in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and in leave liability. As the impact of the new legislation was considered non-recurring, the Group presented the incremental amount as “Impact of Labour Codes” under “Exceptional Item” in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the quarter ended December 31, 2025, and the year ended March 31, 2026.

The Group continues to monitor developments relating to the Labour Codes and will evaluate any impact on the measurement of employee-benefit liabilities.

## f) Update on orders received from the Indian Income Tax Department

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment year 2013–14 and assessment years 2017–18 to 2021–22. These orders confirmed the Company’s position on the tax treatment of certain matters.

As a result:

- Pre-tax interest income of approximately ₹381 crore was recognized and included in other income.
- Income tax provisions aggregating approximately ₹869 crore were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by an aggregate amount of approximately ₹86 crore upon resolution of the disputes.

## h) Update on acquisitions

1. On March 25, 2026, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of Optimum Achieve Holdings Inc., a healthcare digital transformation and consulting firm headquartered in the USA, along with its subsidiaries, including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC. The consideration, including earn-outs, is up to $465 million (approximately ₹4,410 crore), excluding management incentives and retention bonuses, and is subject to customary closing adjustments.

2. On March 25, 2026, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the partnership interests of Stratus Global LLC, an insurance technology partner serving property and casualty insurers and managing general agents (MGAs), headquartered in the USA. The consideration, including earn-outs, is up to $95 million (approximately ₹901 crore), excluding management incentives and retention bonuses, and is subject to customary closing adjustments. As of the date of these results, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC had completed the acquisition of Stratus Global LLC.

## i) Update on stock grants

### i) Grants to CEO & MD

On April 23, 2026, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following annual grants to Salil Parekh, CEO and MD, under his employment agreement approved by shareholders:

- An annual performance-based stock incentive, the Annual Performance Equity Grant, in the form of RSUs covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹34.75 crore as of the grant date under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (2015 Plan). The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of performance targets determined by the Board.
- An Annual Performance Equity ESG Grant in the form of RSUs covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹2 crore as of the grant date under the 2015 Plan. The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of specified environmental, social and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- An Annual Performance Equity TSR Grant in the form of RSUs covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹5 crore as of the grant date under the 2015 Plan. The RSUs will vest on or after March 31, 2027, subject to the Company’s performance on cumulative relative TSR over the two-year cumulative period, as determined by the Board.
- A 2019 Annual Performance Equity Grant in the form of RSUs covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹10 crore as of the grant date under the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program–2019 (2019 Plan). The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of performance criteria specified in the 2019 Plan.

The above RSUs will be granted effective May 2, 2026. The number of RSUs will be calculated based on the market price at the close of trading on the date immediately preceding the grant date.

### ii) Grants to other employees

On April 23, 2026, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved:

- A grant of 27,193 RSUs under the 2015 Plan to eligible employees.
- A grant of performance-based stock incentives (PSUs) under the 2019 Plan to eligible employees, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹1.90 crore as of the grant date. The number of PSUs will be calculated based on the market price at the close of trading on the date immediately preceding the grant date.

Grants made under the 2015 Plan will vest equally over two to three years. Grants made under the 2019 Plan will vest over two years, subject to achievement of performance parameters defined in the 2019 Plan. The RSUs and PSUs will be granted effective May 2, 2026, and the exercise price will equal the par value of the share.

## 2. Information on dividends for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026

For fiscal year 2026, the Board recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share of par value ₹5 each. Payment is subject to shareholder approval at the Company’s Annual General Meeting scheduled for June 23, 2026. The record date for payment of the final dividend is June 10, 2026, and the dividend will be paid on June 25, 2026.

At its meeting held on October 16, 2025, the Board declared an interim dividend of approximately ₹23 per equity share. The record date was October 27, 2025, and the dividend was paid on November 7, 2025. The interim dividend declared in the previous year was ₹21 per equity share.
