## 2.26 SEGMENT REPORTING

Ind AS 108, Operating segments, establishes standards for reporting information about operating segments, products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group’s operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to enable clients to enhance business performance. The Chief Operating Decision Maker (CODM) evaluates performance and allocates resources based on performance indicators by business segment. Segment information is prepared using accounting principles applied consistently with the Group’s accounting policies.

The Group’s business segments are:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment combines the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. “All other segments” includes operating segments of businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are categorized based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Revenue for “All other segments” includes revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other enterprises in Public Services.

Allocated expenses include costs incurred in offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized based on the associated segment effort. Depreciation and amortization are not specifically allocated because the underlying assets are used interchangeably between segments. These expenses are disclosed separately as “unallocated” and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are also not identified with individual reportable segments because they are used interchangeably. Management considers meaningful segregation of the available data for total assets and liabilities impracticable.

Business segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized. Revenue by geographic location is disclosed in note 2.18, “Revenue from operations.”

## Report on the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

### Opinion

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries comprise the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet as at March 31, 2026; the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss, including Other Comprehensive Income, for the three months and year ended on that date; the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity; the Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows for the year ended on that date; and related notes, including material accounting policies and explanatory information.

In the auditor’s opinion, the interim condensed consolidated financial statements give a true and fair view, in conformity with Ind AS 34, “Interim Financial Reporting,” prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, together with the relevant rules and generally accepted accounting principles in India, of:

- the consolidated state of affairs of the Group as at March 31, 2026;
- the Group’s consolidated profit and consolidated other comprehensive income for the three months and year ended on that date;
- the Group’s consolidated changes in equity; and
- the Group’s consolidated cash flows for the year ended on that date.
