As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, 3,87,949 and 2,88,384 cash-settled options were outstanding, respectively. The carrying value of the liability towards cash-settled share-based payments was ₹5 crore and ₹8 crore as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively.

The fair value of the awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes Model for time- and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The valuation inputs include the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on the historical volatility of the observed market prices of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is modelled using historical movements in the market prices of their publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
