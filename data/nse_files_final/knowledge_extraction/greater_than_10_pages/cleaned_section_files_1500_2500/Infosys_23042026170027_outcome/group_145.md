## 2.23 SEGMENT REPORTING

The Company publishes this financial statement along with the interim condensed consolidated financial statements. In accordance with Ind AS 108, Operating Segments, the Company has disclosed segment information in the interim condensed consolidated financial statements.

## TO THE MEMBERS OF INFOSYS LIMITED

## Report on the Audit of the Consolidated Financial Statements

### Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the year ended March 31, 2026. The audited statements comprise:

- The Consolidated Balance Sheet as at March 31, 2026.
- The Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss, including Other Comprehensive Income, for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- The Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity.
- The Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows.
- Notes to the financial statements, including material accounting policies and explanatory information.

In the auditor’s opinion, the consolidated financial statements provide the information required under the Companies Act, 2013, and give a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated state of affairs as at March 31, 2026, together with its consolidated profit, other comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows for the year ended on that date. The statements were prepared in conformity with the Indian Accounting Standards prescribed under Section 133 of the Act and other generally accepted accounting principles in India.

### Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013.

The auditors stated that:

- They were independent of the Group in accordance with the ICAI Code of Ethics and applicable ethical requirements under the Act and related rules.
- They fulfilled their other ethical responsibilities under those requirements.
- The audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the audit opinion.

### Key Audit Matters

Key audit matters were matters that, in the auditors’ professional judgment, were of most significance in the audit of the consolidated financial statements for the current period. These matters were considered in the context of the consolidated financial statements as a whole and in forming the audit opinion; no separate opinion was provided on them.

#### 1. Revenue recognition

The Group’s customer contracts include multiple products and services. Revenue is derived from:

- Software development and related services.
- Maintenance.
- Consulting and package implementation.
- Licensing of software products and platforms across the Group’s core and digital offerings.
- Business process management services.

The Group assesses the services promised in each contract and identifies distinct performance obligations. Identifying distinct performance obligations, including the deliverables and whether the customer can benefit independently from them, involves significant judgment.

Certain integrated service arrangements include subcontractor services or third-party vendor equipment or software. Revenue from third-party vendor products or services is recorded:

- Net of costs when the Group acts as an agent between the customer and the vendor.
- Gross when the Group acts as principal for the transaction.

The Group first evaluates whether it obtains control of the specified goods or services before they are transferred to the customer. It also considers whether it is primarily responsible for fulfilling the promise to provide the specified goods or services, inventory risk, pricing discretion, and other factors to determine whether it controls the products or services and therefore acts as principal or agent.
