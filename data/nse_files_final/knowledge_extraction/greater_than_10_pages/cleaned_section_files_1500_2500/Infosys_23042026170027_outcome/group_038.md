# The estimated useful life is around 1 year to 7 years

The excess of the purchase consideration paid over the fair value of assets acquired has been attributed to goodwill. The primary items that generated this goodwill are the value of the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualify as an intangible asset.

Goodwill amounting to $9 million is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of $96 million includes upfront cash consideration of $88 million and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $8 million as of the acquisition date.

At the acquisition date, the key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were the probabilities assigned to achievement of financial targets and discount rates ranging from 2% to 3%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026, was approximately $9 million.

The acquisitions also include retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over two to three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Retention bonuses and management incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Statement of Comprehensive Income over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was $23 million as of the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts had been substantially collected.

Transaction costs incurred in connection with a business combination, including finder’s fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of $4 million related to the acquisition were included in administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the year ended March 31, 2026.

## 2.11 Employees' Stock Option Plans (ESOP)

## Accounting Policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense relating to share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards at the grant date. The estimated fair value of awards is recognized as an expense in net profit in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, as if the award consisted of multiple awards, with a corresponding increase to share premium.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan)

On June 22, 2019, pursuant to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan may not exceed 50,000,000 equity shares. To implement the 2019 Plan, up to 45,000,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The performance parameters are based on a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, broader domestic and global market indices, and company operating performance metrics. These parameters are distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest. The instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan)

On March 31, 2016, pursuant to shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan may not exceed 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue. The instruments generally vest over four years.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options is the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 8,650,911 shares as of March 31, 2026, and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each respective date.

## Under the 2015 plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants were effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Under an employee agreement approved by the shareholders, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ₹3 crore. The grant vests over time in three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the respective grant date. Accordingly, an annual time-based grant of 18,132 RSUs was made effective February 1, 2026, for fiscal 2026.

Although the annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027, had not been granted as of March 31, 2026, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense under IFRS 2, *Share-based Payment*, because the service commencement date preceded the grant date. For this purpose, the grant date under IFRS 2 was July 1, 2022.
