## Accounting policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the year is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. The Group considers highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.

## Overview and notes to the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (the “Company” or “Infosys”) provides AI-first business consulting and technology services. Its capabilities include managing systems and operations for global enterprises, business transformation, domain expertise, and orchestration of innovations from an AI-native partner ecosystem.

Infosys, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts, is referred to as the “Group.”

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary equity listings are on BSE Ltd. and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares (ADSs), representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).

The Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on April 23, 2026.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements are prepared in compliance with Indian Accounting Standard (Ind AS) 34, Interim Financial Reporting; the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value; the requirements of the Companies Act, 2013; and guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

The statements do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read in conjunction with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the Company’s Annual Report for the year ended March 31, 2025.

The Ind AS are prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and relevant amendment rules issued thereafter.

Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except when a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard requires a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information used in preparing the audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Accordingly, quarter figures added to figures reported for previous quarters may not always equal the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.
