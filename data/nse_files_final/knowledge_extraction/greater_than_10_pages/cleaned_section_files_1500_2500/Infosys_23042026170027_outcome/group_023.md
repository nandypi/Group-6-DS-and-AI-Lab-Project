## About Infosys

Infosys is a global provider of digital services and consulting. More than 325,000 employees serve clients in 63 countries. The company has more than four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises, with capabilities spanning cloud, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, digital skills transfer, and innovation.

Infosys states that it is committed to corporate governance, environmental sustainability, and an inclusive workplace supporting diverse talent.
