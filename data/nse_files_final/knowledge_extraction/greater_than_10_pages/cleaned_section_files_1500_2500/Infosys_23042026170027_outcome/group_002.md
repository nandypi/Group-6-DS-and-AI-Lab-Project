## Annexure I

### Disclosure pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure) Regulations, 2015 read with SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024

| Sl. No. | Particulars | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1. | Name of the Auditor | M/s B S R & Co. LLP |
| 2. | Reason for change viz appointment, reappointment, resignation, removal, death or otherwise | The term of the current statutory auditors, M/s Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 117366W/W-100018), will expire at the conclusion of the ensuing 46th AGM. The Board approved the announcement of the Company’s intention to recommend the appointment of M/s B S R & Co. LLP (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022) as the statutory auditors of the Company. |
| 3. | Date of appointment/re-appointment/cessation (as applicable) and term of appointment/re-appointment | The proposed appointment is for a first term of five consecutive years, commencing from the conclusion of the 46th AGM and continuing until the conclusion of the 51st AGM. The appointment is subject to shareholder approval at the 46th AGM, to be held in FY 2027–28, and fulfilment of all applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations. |
| 4. | Brief Profile (in case of appointment) | B S R & Co. was constituted on March 27, 1990, as a partnership firm with Firm Registration No. 101248W. It was converted into B S R & Co. LLP on October 14, 2013, with Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022. B S R & Co. LLP is a member entity of BSR & Affiliates, a network registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. It operates through 14 locations across India, has over 4,000 personnel and 170 partners, and serves as statutory auditor to several companies listed on Indian stock exchanges, including companies in the IT and ITES sectors. |
| 5. | Disclosure of relationships between Directors | Not applicable. |
