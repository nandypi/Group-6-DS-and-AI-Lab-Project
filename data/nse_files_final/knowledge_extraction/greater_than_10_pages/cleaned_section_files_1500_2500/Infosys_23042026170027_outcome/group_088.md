## Deloitte Haskins &amp; Sells LLP

- The investments made and the terms and conditions of loans granted during the year were, in the auditors’ opinion, prima facie, not prejudicial to the Company’s interests.
- For loans granted by the Company:
  - Repayment schedules for principal and payment of interest were stipulated.
  - Repayments of principal and receipts of interest were generally regular as stipulated.
  - No overdue amounts remained outstanding at the balance sheet date.
  - No loan that fell due during the year was renewed or extended, and no fresh loans were granted to settle overdue amounts on existing loans to the same parties.
  - The Company did not grant loans or advances in the nature of loans repayable on demand or without specifying repayment terms or periods.
- The Company did not make investments in firms or limited liability partnerships during the year.
- The Company did not provide guarantees or securities, or grant secured or unsecured advances in the nature of loans, to companies, firms, limited liability partnerships, or any other parties.
- The Company complied with Sections 185 and 186 of the Companies Act, 2013, in respect of loans granted, investments made, and guarantees and securities provided, as applicable.
- The Company did not accept any deposits or amounts deemed to be deposits.
- Cost records were not required to be maintained under Section 148(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 for the Company’s business activities.

### Borrowings and fundraising

- The Company did not take any loans or other borrowings during the year.
- The Company had not been declared a wilful defaulter by any bank, financial institution, government, or government authority.
- The Company did not take any term loan during the year, and there were no outstanding term loans at the beginning of the year.
- Based on an overall examination of the financial statements, short-term funds were not, prima facie, used for long-term purposes during the year.
- The Company did not take funds from any entity or person on account of, or to meet the obligations of, its subsidiaries.
- The Company did not raise any loans during the year.
- The Company did not raise money through an initial public offering, further public offering, or debt instruments during the year.
- The Company did not make any preferential allotment or private placement of shares or fully, partly, or optionally convertible debentures during the year.

### Fraud reporting

No fraud by the Company, and no material fraud on the Company, was noticed or reported during the year.
