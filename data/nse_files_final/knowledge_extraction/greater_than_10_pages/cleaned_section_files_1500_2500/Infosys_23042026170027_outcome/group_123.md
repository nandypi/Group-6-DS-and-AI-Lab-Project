## 2.25 CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY (CSR)

Under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, a company meeting the applicability threshold is required to spend at least 2% of its average net profit for the immediately preceding three financial years on CSR activities. A CSR committee has been formed in accordance with the Act.
