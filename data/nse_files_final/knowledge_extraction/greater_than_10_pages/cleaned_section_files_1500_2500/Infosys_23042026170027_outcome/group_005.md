(1) The adjusted non-IFRS measures exclude the effect of provisions related to the Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025. These provisions increased gratuity liability, arising from past service cost relating to a plan amendment, and leave liability by ₹1,289 crore in FY'26. This amount was recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income and resulted in a lower tax expense of ₹318 crore in FY'26.

## Note on Adjusted Non-IFRS performance measures:

Infosys uses non-IFRS financial performance measures to supplement financial information reported under IFRS. These measures should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for the relevant IFRS measures and should be read together with information presented on a reported IFRS basis.

## INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT ON AUDIT OF QUARTERLY AND ANNUAL CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.

In the auditor’s opinion, the Statement:

- Includes the financial results of the subsidiaries listed in the report’s Annexure.
- Is presented in accordance with the requirements of Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
- Gives a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles of Ind AS 34, “Interim Financial Reporting,” prescribed under section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, and other generally accepted accounting principles in India, of the Group’s consolidated net profit, consolidated other comprehensive income, and other financial information for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
