## Under the 2015 Plan:

The Board, on April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants were effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs, described as the annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, described as the annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs will vest based on achievement of environment, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, described as the annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs will vest based on the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employment agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ₹3 crore. The grant vests over three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the grant date. Accordingly, 18,132 annual time-based RSUs were granted effective February 1, 2026 for fiscal 2026.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of March 31, 2026. Because the service commencement date preceded the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants to other KMPs under the 2015 Plan:

- 237,370 time-based ESOPs. These ESOPs will vest over four years and will be exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price will be the market price on the grant date.
- 82,400 time-based RSUs, which will vest over four years.

## Under the 2019 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of 60,600 RSUs to other KMPs under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs will vest over three years based on achievement of specified performance targets.
