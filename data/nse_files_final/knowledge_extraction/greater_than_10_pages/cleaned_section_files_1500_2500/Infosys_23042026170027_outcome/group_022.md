# Press Release

- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with ABN AMRO Bank through 2028 to support the bank’s ambition of achieving sustainable and profitable growth. The collaboration includes simplifying and modernizing the bank’s IT landscape, adopting AI, improving operational efficiency and customer value, and reducing complexity and operating costs.

- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Anthropic focused on automated workflows, accelerated software delivery, and agentic AI solutions for complex, regulated industries. The collaboration draws on Infosys’s industry expertise in telecom, financial services, and manufacturing. Infosys developers are using Claude Code to accelerate software development and create AI agents for industries requiring precision, compliance, and domain knowledge.

- Infosys and Intel expanded their strategic collaboration to help enterprises move AI initiatives from pilots to production at scale. The collaboration focuses on performance optimization and measurable enterprise outcomes, including open and energy-efficient AI solutions deployable across data centers, cloud environments, and the edge.

- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Cursor to help enterprises build and scale AI-powered digital solutions. The collaboration is intended to accelerate enterprise AI adoption and enable more than 100,000 Infosys software engineers with agentic coding platforms.

- Infosys and Cognition announced a strategic collaboration focused on agentic and autonomous engineering capabilities for global enterprises. Infosys’s Exponential Engineering offering, Topaz Fabric, and Cognition’s Devin platform are intended to support capabilities ranging from real-time developer augmentation to autonomous engineering execution.

- Infosys Finacle and Producers Savings Bank Corporation announced an initiative to modernize the bank’s technology landscape in the Philippines through an upgrade to the latest version of the Finacle Core Banking Solution. The initiative is intended to support faster and more personalized customer experiences, real-time banking services, expanded digital capabilities, and the scaling of the bank’s lending business. Producers Savings Bank and Infosys Finacle have had a decade-long association.

- Infosys BPM collaborated with Old National Bank on digital transformation activities covering process optimization, automation, and AI-driven and agentic AI capabilities.
