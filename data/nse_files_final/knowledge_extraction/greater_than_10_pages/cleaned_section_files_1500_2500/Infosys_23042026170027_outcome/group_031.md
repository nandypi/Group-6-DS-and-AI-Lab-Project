## 2.3.5 Impairment

The Group recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenue that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

For trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component, loss allowances are measured at an amount equal to lifetime ECL. For all other financial assets, expected credit losses are measured at 12-month ECL unless there has been a significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition, in which case lifetime ECL is used.

The Group determines credit loss allowances based on historical loss experience adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions, including conditions relating to the industries in which it operates and the countries where it operates.

The ECL or reversal required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.
