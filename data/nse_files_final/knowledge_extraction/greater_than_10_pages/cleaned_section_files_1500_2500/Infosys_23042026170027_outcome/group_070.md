## 2.12 Income Taxes

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the interim Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income, except for amounts relating to items recognized directly in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts, subject to applicable exceptions. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using enacted or substantively enacted tax rates and laws expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized in income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment. Deferred tax assets are recognized when future taxable profit is probable against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized.

Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when those earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future. The Group offsets current and deferred tax assets and liabilities when it has a legally enforceable right of set-off and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

Interim-period income tax provision is based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions on exercise of employee share options, when exceeding the related compensation charged to income, are credited to equity.

The applicable Indian corporate statutory tax rate for each of the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 was 25.17%.

Income tax expense for the three months ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 included reversals, net of provisions, of ₹882 crore and ₹117 crore, respectively. Income tax expense for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 included a reversal, net of provisions, of ₹877 crore and provisions, net of reversals, of ₹132 crore, respectively. These provisions and reversals relating to prior periods were primarily due to adjudication of disputed matters following tax-return filings and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment year 2013-14 and assessment years 2017-18 to 2021-22. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently, pre-tax interest income of ₹381 crore was recognized, and income tax provisions aggregating ₹869 crore were reversed with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss. Contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹86 crore following resolution of the disputes.

During the quarter and year ended March 31, 2025, the Company received orders under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment years 2016-17 and 2019-20. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently, pre-tax interest income of ₹327 crore was recognized, and income tax provisions aggregating ₹183 crore were reversed with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss. Contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹1,068 crore following resolution of the disputes.

Foreign tax expense relates to income taxes payable overseas, principally in the United States.

In India, the Company benefited in prior years from income tax incentives for exports of software and services from units registered under the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005. Eligible SEZ units that began providing services on or after April 1, 2005 were entitled to a deduction of 100% of profits or gains from exported services for the first five years from the financial year in which service provision commenced, and 50% for the following five years. Up to 50% was also available for a further five years, subject to creating and using a Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Refer to “Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve” under Note 2.18, Equity.

Deferred income tax for the three months and years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially related to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

Infosys is subject to a 15% U.S. Branch Profit Tax (BPT) to the extent that its U.S. branch's net profit during the year exceeds the increase in the branch's net assets during the year, computed under the Internal Revenue Code. As at March 31, 2026, Infosys's U.S. branch net assets were approximately ₹7,736 crore. The Company had a deferred tax liability for branch profit tax of ₹207 crore, net of credits, because it expected these branch profits to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches amounting to ₹19,270 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹16,593 crore as at March 31, 2025. The Company can control the timing of reversal of these temporary differences, and it is probable that they will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The Group primarily intends to repatriate earnings from subsidiaries and branches only to the extent they can be distributed tax-free.

Deferred income tax assets were not recognized on accumulated losses of ₹4,868 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹4,597 crore as at March 31, 2025 because future taxable profit was not expected to be available against which the unused tax losses could be utilized in the foreseeable future.
