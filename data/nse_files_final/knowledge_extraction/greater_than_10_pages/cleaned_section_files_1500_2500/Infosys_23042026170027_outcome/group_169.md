As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, 3,87,949 and 2,88,384 cash-settled options were outstanding, respectively. The carrying value of the liability toward cash-settled share-based payments was ₹15 crore and ₹18 crore as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively.

The fair value of the awards was estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time- and non-market-performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model was used for TSR-based options.

The valuation inputs included the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility was based on historical volatility of the observed market prices of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term of the options. Expected volatility for comparative companies was modelled using historical movements in their publicly traded equity share prices over a similar period. Correlation coefficients were calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
