## 2.19.4 Impact of Labour Codes

On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Code on Social Security, 2020; and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020. Collectively, these Labour Codes consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during and after employment.

The Labour Codes introduce changes including a uniform definition of wages and enhanced leave benefits. The Group assessed the financial implications, resulting in:

- An increase in gratuity liability arising from past service cost.
- An increase in leave liability of ₹1,289 crore, recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the year ended March 31, 2026.

The Group continues to monitor developments relating to the Labour Codes and will evaluate any effects on the measurement of employee benefit liabilities.

## 2.20 Employee Benefits

### Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a defined benefit gratuity retirement plan covering eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to the Infosys BPM Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively. The trusts are administered by trustees, and contributions are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

The Group also operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Liabilities under these defined benefit plans are determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its Balance Sheet. Remeasurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.

### Provident fund

Eligible Infosys employees receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. The employee and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on trust investments and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries receive benefits from a defined contribution provident fund. The employee and the respective company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no further obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

### Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in defined contribution superannuation plans. The Group has no further obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

### Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed because of unused accumulated entitlement. Expense for non-accumulating absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
