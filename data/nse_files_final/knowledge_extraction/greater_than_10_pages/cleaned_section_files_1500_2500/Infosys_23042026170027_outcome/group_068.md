## Under the 2019 plan:

On April 17, 2025, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved performance-based grants of RSUs amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest based on the achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following grants to other KMP under the 2015 Plan:

- 237,370 time-based ESOPs, vesting over four years and exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price will be the market price on the date of grant.
- 82,400 time-based RSUs, vesting over four years.

## Under the 2019 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved performance-based grants of 60,600 RSUs to other KMP under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs vest over three years based on the achievement of specified performance targets.
