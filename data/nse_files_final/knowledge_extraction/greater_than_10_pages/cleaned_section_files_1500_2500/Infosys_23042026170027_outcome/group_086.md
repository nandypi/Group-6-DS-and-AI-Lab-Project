## Deloitte Haskins &amp; Sells LLP

- **Internal financial controls:** The auditor referred to a separate report in “Annexure A” on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of the Company’s internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements. The report expressed an unmodified opinion.
- **Director remuneration:** In the auditor’s opinion, the remuneration paid by the Company to its directors during the year complied with Section 197 of the Companies Act, as amended.
- **Pending litigation:** The Company disclosed the impact of pending litigations on its financial position in the standalone financial statements. Refer to Note 2.23.
- **Material foreseeable losses and derivatives:** The Company made provisions required under applicable law or accounting standards for material foreseeable losses. Refer to Note 2.16. The Company did not have any long-term derivative contracts.
- **Investor Education and Protection Fund:** There was no delay in transferring amounts required to be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund.
- **Management representations on fund flows:**
  - Management represented that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, no funds had been advanced, loaned, or invested by the Company—whether from borrowed funds, share premium, or any other source—to any person or entity, including foreign entities (“Intermediaries”), with the understanding that the Intermediary would directly or indirectly lend or invest in identified persons or entities (“Ultimate Beneficiaries”), or provide guarantees, security, or similar support on behalf of the Ultimate Beneficiaries.
  - Management represented that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, no funds had been received by the Company from any person or entity, including foreign entities (“Funding Parties”), with the understanding that the Company would directly or indirectly lend or invest in identified persons or entities (“Ultimate Beneficiaries”), or provide guarantees, security, or similar support on behalf of the Ultimate Beneficiaries.
  - Based on audit procedures considered reasonable and appropriate, nothing came to the auditor’s notice indicating that these representations under Rule 11(e), as provided above, contained any material misstatement.
- **Dividends:** As stated in Note 2.12.3 to the standalone financial statements:
  - The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared and paid during the year, complied with Section 123 of the Companies Act, as applicable.
  - The interim dividend declared and paid during the year and up to the date of the auditor’s report complied with Section 123 of the Companies Act.
  - The Board of Directors proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed dividend complied with Section 123 of the Companies Act, as applicable.
- **Accounting software audit trail:** For the financial year ended March 31, 2026, the Company used accounting software with an audit-trail, or edit-log, facility. The facility operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions, and the auditors did not identify any instance of tampering. The audit trail was preserved in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.
- **Companies (Auditor’s Report) Order, 2020:** In accordance with Section 143(11) of the Companies Act, the auditors provided in “Annexure B” a statement on the matters specified in paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Order.
