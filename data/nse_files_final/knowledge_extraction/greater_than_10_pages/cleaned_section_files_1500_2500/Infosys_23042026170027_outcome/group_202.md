## Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP):

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value of awards is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan) :

On June 22, 2019, pursuant to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan shall not exceed 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the 2019 Plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on achievement of defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The performance parameters include a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics.

Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. These instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan) :

On March 31, 2016, pursuant to shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan shall not exceed 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. These instruments generally vest over four years. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares, while the exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 86,50,911 shares as of March 31, 2026, and 96,55,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each date.

## Under the 2015 Plan:

The Board, on April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants were made effective May 2, 2025:

- 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹2 crore, under the annual performance equity ESG grant. These RSUs will vest based on achievement of environment, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹5 crore, under the annual performance equity TSR grant. These RSUs will vest based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employee agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ₹3 crore. The grant vests in three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the grant date. Accordingly, an annual time-based grant of 18,132 RSUs was made effective February 1, 2026, for fiscal 2026.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of March 31, 2026. Since the service commencement date precedes the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*. For this purpose, the grant date under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved time-based grants of 237,370 ESOPs to other KMPs under the 2015 Plan. These ESOPs will vest over four years and will be exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price will be the market price on the grant date.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Board also approved 82,400 time-based RSUs to other KMPs under the 2015 Plan. The time-based RSUs will vest over four years.

## Under the 2019 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of 60,600 RSUs to other KMPs under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs will vest over three years based on achievement of specified performance targets.
