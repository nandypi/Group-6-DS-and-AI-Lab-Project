## Under the 2015 plan:

The Board, on April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following fiscal 2026 grants. The grants were effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs will vest based on achievement of environment, social and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs will vest based on the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant period, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employee agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual grant of RSUs with a fair value of ₹3 crore. These RSUs vest over time in three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the grant date. Accordingly, 18,132 annual time-based RSUs were granted effective February 1, 2026 for fiscal 2026.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of March 31, 2026. Since the service commencement date precedes the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 is July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 plan:

The Board, on April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The grants were effective May 2, 2025, and 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted. These RSUs will vest based on achievement of specified performance targets.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants to other KMPs under the 2015 Plan:

- 237,370 time-based ESOPs. These ESOPs will vest over four years and will be exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price will be the market price on the grant date.
- 82,400 time-based RSUs. These RSUs will vest over four years.

## Under the 2019 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of 60,600 RSUs to other KMPs under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs will vest over three years based on achievement of specified performance targets.

The fair value of the awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The model inputs include the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on historical volatility of the observed market prices of the Company's publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is modeled using historical movements in the market prices of their publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
