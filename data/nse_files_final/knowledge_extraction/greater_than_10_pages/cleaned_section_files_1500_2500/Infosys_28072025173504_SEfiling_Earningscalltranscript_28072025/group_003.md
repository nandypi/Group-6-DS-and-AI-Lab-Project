# Infosys Limited Q1 FY26 Earnings Conference Call

July 23, 2025

## Salil Parekh

Infosys has used a rigorous process for hiring college graduates at Mysore for the last 20 years. Graduates undergo focused foundational training on campus and must meet internal assessment standards. Each employee receives three attempts to meet those standards; employees who do not meet them after the third attempt do not continue with the company.

Employees are provided opportunities for training and learning projects before being deployed to client projects when they are ready.

Infosys has a flexible approach to where employees work. The number of campus employees has increased every quarter for the last several quarters.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

The digital-versus-capabilities metrics published in the ESG report are used for ESG reporting and are not necessarily used for internal IJP decisions.

Infosys has not stopped or deferred lateral hiring.

## Avik Das — The Business Standard

The questions covered Manufacturing growth, the outlook for North America and Financial Services, and potential margin pressure.

## Salil Parekh

Financial Services is showing good traction across all businesses and geographies. North America, Infosys’s largest market, remains in a strong position across industries despite changes in the economic environment.

Outside Logistics and Consumer Products, Infosys is seeing good activity in areas including Energy, Utilities, and parts of Telecommunications. North America is also showing traction in enterprise AI and consolidation wins involving large deals.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Manufacturing remains soft in some areas, particularly auto, industrial, and Europe. Infosys benefited from consolidation and from large deals won in the past, but continues to see concerns about client spending in the identified areas of Manufacturing.

The quarter’s margin declined by 20 basis points. The puts and takes were:

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---:|
| Compensation and variable pay | -100 basis points |
| Currency | -30 basis points |
| Sales investment | -20 basis points |
| Project Maximus benefits | Positive offset |
| Pricing increase and seasonality benefit | +70 basis points |
| Prior-quarter one-off impact | +40 basis points |
| Lower third-party costs | +20 basis points |

Project Maximus remains ongoing. Infosys delivered 50 basis points of margin expansion in the previous year despite multiple headwinds and continues to expect benefits from the project.

Potential offsets include lower growth within the 1%–3% growth range, fixed costs in growth areas, the full-year impact of compensation changes, and transition costs for large deals before revenue begins. Infosys continues to aspire to increase margins from current levels.

## Sanjana B. — The Hindu BusinessLine

The questions covered project changes arising from geopolitical uncertainty, third-party costs and revenues in FY26, and the effect of AI and automation on hiring.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys has not seen changes in specific clients or projects because of changes in the economic outlook in the United States and Europe. Clients are placing greater emphasis on cost and efficiency, with some industry-level impact in Logistics, Consumer Products, and parts of Manufacturing such as auto.

Client cost and consolidation decisions have also created opportunities that have supported growth.

Infosys has a recruitment plan for FY26 covering both college and lateral hiring and will continue with that plan. AI is providing productivity benefits and increasing work with clients, particularly in enterprise AI. Cloud transitions and data-and-analytics foundations are being accelerated, and these programs are fundamental to successful enterprise AI adoption.

## Ayanti Bera — Financial Express

The question covered the reasons for increasing the lower end of FY26 revenue growth guidance.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

The previous guidance assumed heightened uncertainty at the lower end and a steady to marginally improving environment at the upper end. After one quarter of strong performance and strong deal wins, Infosys increased the lower end of its revenue growth guidance from 0% to 1%.

The upper end remains unchanged because of potential uncertainty related to tariffs and the broader macroeconomic environment.
