# Infosys Limited Q1 FY26 Financial Results Press Conference Call

July 23, 2025

## Opening Remarks

Infosys reported a strong start to FY26:

- Revenue grew 2.6% sequentially and 3.8% year-on-year in constant currency.
- Growth was broad-based across the five large industry groups and large geographies.
- Large deals totaled $3.8 billion.
- Growth was driven by enterprise AI and continued client selection of Infosys for consolidation projects.
- Infosys has built 300 AI agents across business operations and IT, and these agents have been deployed with clients.
- Horizontal and vertical AI agents are being used to support faster decisions, improved customer experience, and higher operational efficiency.
- FY26 constant-currency growth guidance was revised from 0%–3% to 1%–3%.
- FY26 margin guidance remained unchanged at 20%–22%.

## Growth Drivers and Guidance

Management said the lower end of the FY26 guidance range was increased because the company was progressing through the year and narrowing its guidance range. The economic environment had become more stable in some areas but was not fully settled.

Management cited continued traction from consolidation work, enterprise AI agents, and changing economic conditions in Europe and the United States as factors supporting growth.

Acquisitions contributed approximately 40 basis points to the 2.6% constant-currency growth reported in Q1. Management expected a similar contribution from inorganic growth for the full year. Excluding acquisitions, constant-currency growth in the quarter was more than 2%, or approximately 2.2%.

Management described active AI-agent projects across areas including supply chains and customer experience. These projects were intended to provide productivity benefits and improve customer service. Clients were also selecting Infosys for consolidation projects based on its delivery capabilities, stability, and AI-related ideas for business improvement.

## Macroeconomic Conditions

Management reported some economic impact in:

- Logistics
- Consumer products
- Manufacturing

These effects were offset in part by increased demand for AI-agent work and client consolidation projects.

## Geography

Europe had performed strongly for several quarters, supported by investments made in the region several years earlier. Management said the investment was working across sectors.

Europe was growing faster than the Americas, although the United States remained Infosys's largest geography.

## Headcount, Utilization, and Hiring

Overall headcount remained constant during the quarter. Utilization reached 85%, described as a peak level.

Infosys delivered 2.6% growth despite flat headcount. The growth included:

- Pricing or RPP increases
- A seasonality benefit
- Approximately 40 basis points from acquisitions
- Volume increases managed within the existing headcount through utilization

Management said that further volume increases would require hiring because utilization was already at a peak. Hiring would continue in line with the plans announced at the beginning of the financial year.

## Wage Hikes and Margin Impact

The first part of the wage hike became effective in January, and the second part was rolled out effective April. The impact of the wage hike and higher variable pay paid to employees was already reflected in the quarter's margin.

The combined impact of wage hikes and higher variable pay was 100 basis points in the quarter. Management said the timing of the next wage hike would be decided later.
