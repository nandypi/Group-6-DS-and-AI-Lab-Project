## Sandeep Shah

### Large-deal pipeline and vendor consolidation

Infosys typically does not provide forward commentary on large-deal values. Management said the large-deal pipeline is in a good position, with opportunities related to vendor consolidation, enterprise AI, and mega deals.

### Discretionary spending

Management does not have a specific view on when discretionary spending will recover. Clients remain comfortable working with Infosys on enterprise AI programs, cloud, data analytics, enterprise applications, and consolidation programs. Cost and efficiency remain significant client priorities.

### EBIT margin outlook

The first quarter represented one-fourth of the year and included the rollout of a compensation increase, which created a significant headwind. Management identified Project Maximus and value-based selling as potential tailwinds, while lower third-party equipment costs could also support margins.

Mega deals that ramp up may create headwinds because transition activities incur costs before generating revenue. Management said its aspiration remains to improve margins from the current level.

## Vibhor Singhal

### Macro conditions and client spending

Management said client interest exists across industries in using enterprise AI for productivity and new business models. This requires increased focus on cloud, data infrastructure, and enterprise applications operating in cloud environments.

Clients also remain focused on cost and efficiency, including through consolidation programs. Management reviews the business outlook each quarter and updates its view based on changes in overall activity.

### Enterprise AI cycle

Management said each major technology shift has affected enterprise decision-making differently and did not characterize the current AI cycle as similar or different from earlier technology cycles.

Infosys is seeing substantial client interest in enterprise AI. Management considers cloud and data capabilities foundational to adoption and said Infosys is well prepared in these areas and in enterprise AI. The enterprise technology landscape is larger and more complex than it was a decade ago, which may require more extensive change.

## Apurva Prasad

### Macro uncertainty and AI productivity

Management attributed the outlook for the remaining part of the year primarily to macroeconomic uncertainty rather than structural AI-related productivity passbacks. Tariff-related and geopolitical uncertainties continue, and client behavior has not changed materially. Many clients remain in a wait-and-watch mode regarding discretionary spending.

Enterprise AI adoption is occurring across multiple areas rather than being concentrated in one geography or vertical. In Financial Services, management said Infosys has become the AI strategic partner for half of its largest clients.
