## Salil Parekh

Thank you, Nandan. Good evening, shareholders, and welcome to the Annual General Meeting. Over the next few minutes, I will share with you some of the highlights of the financial year and what we are looking at as we go ahead.

Financial year 2026 was a strong year for us. First, we had revenue of over US$20 billion. Constant currency growth was 3.1%, with an operating margin of 21%. We had very strong free cash flow. Employees are now over 325,000 across the world. One of the key elements was that we recruited 20,000 college graduates across India and in some other parts of the world as well, as we continue to scale in this new environment and do more and more work within AI. Our clients with revenues of over US$100 million were at 41 as we closed the year.

If you look at the financial performance, we have shared this in rupees. In many areas, we had a positive movement from the last year. In terms of cash flow, we had a positive movement, which was reflected after adjusting for some of the tax refunds that we got in the previous financial year.

We have now returned over ₹55,000 crore over the past two years through the capital return policy, both through dividends and the buyback that we completed. This continues with the approach we have for our capital return policy, across a block of five years, where we return 85% of our free cash flow. Our balance sheet remains extremely strong. It is debt-free. There is no debt. In fact, it is very liquid, with a strong balance of cash and cash investments, which we continue to maintain even as we provide capital return to shareholders and continue with acquisitions.

One of the most critical areas we have focused on in this past year has been to launch our AI approach within the market. Internally, we have been working on this for a number of years. We have built internally something we refer to as Topaz and Fabric, which allows us to make very strong AI capabilities available to our clients in partnership with AI companies.

This is the approach that talks about how we go to market with all of that AI capability. We have seen today that there are six broad areas in which clients are looking to use AI to improve their business for growth, improve their business in terms of quality and service levels, or improve their business in terms of efficiency and cost.

The first area refers to AI engineering and strategy, where we build agents across different platforms and tools. The second is data, which is the critical element of putting AI together. The third is process, where a lot of new work is being done to first make the process more efficient and then use agents to make that process work better. The fourth is modernization of technology using agents. This is one of the largest areas today where our clients are looking to Infosys to help them. The fifth is physical AI, where you look at AI in manufacturing and put software into manufacturing elements where the products are, whether in medical, automotive, or other areas. The sixth is AI trust, where we look at how to make sure AI is secure and responsible.

When we look at this as a collective group, we see that this is approximately US$300 billion of addressable market overall in those six areas, from today to 2030. Over the next five years, that gives us a real focus on how we drive growth there. In this slide, we show the different clients that we are working with in each of those six buckets today. These are actual projects. These are not proof of concept. They are large-scale programs where clients are trusting Infosys to deliver this sort of work for them today.

As an overall journey, we are helping our clients move from where they are today to the future, which is very much AI-driven, because we have a deep understanding of their environment. What is in a client environment? What is in a large enterprise environment? How is the technology set up? How does it interact with the business? What is the architecture landscape? What is the data landscape? That domain knowledge is the value we are bringing, which is why we are seeing a strong connection that our clients are having with us to make AI successful.

We also have extremely good engineering talent. We have shifted a lot of our training onto AI training, and more and more of our employees are ready and certified on the different foundation models, coding tools, and compute platforms that are available for AI. We have built our own platform and IP, Topaz Fabric, which allows us to work with any AI foundation model for the benefit of the client.

In doing that, we have built a very strong partner ecosystem. All of the big AI foundation model companies and compute companies are working with Infosys very closely to make it available for clients based on what the situation is, which industry, what type of work, and which foundation models become relevant for that. We have a very strong partnership approach to driving that.

In Q3 of last year, we shared that approximately 5.5% of our revenue was in AI services, which is approximately US$1 billion annualized. This is growing at a quite fast pace. We had given that when we shared it in Q3, so we reconfirm that this is how we are driving it, and we continue to see good growth in AI services revenue.

On thought leadership, meaning how the analyst community is looking at Infosys, there are 16 areas across these six buckets where Infosys has a leadership rating for AI.

There are two big foundational elements of AI. The first is cloud. Here we have built Cobalt over many years, and we have a very good capability, partnership, and ability to use the cloud compute platform to make sure that AI is working well and migration is working well. A lot of the different foundation models are working on multiple cloud platforms, and that is where the strength comes from Infosys having had that skill and capability for a long time.

The second is data. There is data within the enterprise, structured and unstructured. How can we make all of that available for the AI model? The value for a large enterprise is to take its own data and populate it in the model, so that it becomes more intelligent for the company and the enterprise to benefit. We then ensure that the data is secured and trusted, so that it is not something that the enterprise may feel cannot be depended upon. They can continue with the differentiation coming from their own knowledge, internal data, and knowledge that comes together. Enterprises are also bringing customer and supplier data in addition to their own, which is enhancing how the models are working within large enterprises.

Inside the Company, we have been focused over the last several years on our own efficiency, because we want to maintain, and as you saw in the previous financial year, increase our operating margin. Despite the environment outside, we want to have a very strong operating approach. We have driven cost efficiency with automation, the pyramid, and productivity. We are using AI in many places, for example in finance, marketing, all of our internal functions, and of course in coding as well, which is giving us productivity benefits, improving certain portfolio elements, and reducing indirect costs to make sure that we have the foundation for good profitability.

The strength of Infosys comes from a leading delivery organization where we have excellent training and well-driven teams that make sure that what we commit to our clients, we are able to deliver, now more and more in multi-service offerings and complex programs where there is a lot of trust from our clients, and now in these six new areas of AI where we put even more attention and focus.

On the brand, we have done quite well. The brand is recognized more as associated with AI, top three most valuable brand globally, one of the fastest-growing IT services brands, and Top 100 of any brands in the world in the last financial year. There is good perception with AI, for Topaz, and for cloud, with Cobalt.

We also took some time on acquisitions. We had finished a few in the financial year, and a couple of them got done just after the financial year but before today. One was on the healthcare side and one was on the insurance side. We want to make sure that in areas where we see a good opportunity and where we can scale up, we are doing more of those types of acquisitions, which we are then able to integrate into our business quite well.

We wanted to share a little about how we are working across all the communities we are part of. We have created a lot of impact within India with contributions the Company is making: 200 projects and 7 million people. With Springboard, a training online capability that we provide for free, over 10 million people worldwide have benefited. One example is where we have worked to make sure that water capacity in lakes within different parts of India is enhanced by providing sustainable solutions. All of this comes within the area of supporting communities in the CSR work we are doing.

One of the biggest differences in the way we are approaching the market is how we work internally as One Infosys, whether with service lines, industries, or geographies, making sure that the best capability of Infosys is available to every client and all of the teams inside continue to work well together.

In conclusion, it was a strong execution in the financial year. We have a good AI approach and strategy, and are already starting execution. We already see approximately annualized US$1 billion of AI services revenue that we shared in Q3, growing at a very good pace, with very strong AI partnerships. The foundation model companies are also approaching us, and we are working strongly with them. We have a good stable organization, One Infosys, and continued trust of our clients.

With that, I will conclude. Thank you once again for all your faith and trust in us, and we look forward to the coming year being more and more AI-driven into the future. Thank you.
