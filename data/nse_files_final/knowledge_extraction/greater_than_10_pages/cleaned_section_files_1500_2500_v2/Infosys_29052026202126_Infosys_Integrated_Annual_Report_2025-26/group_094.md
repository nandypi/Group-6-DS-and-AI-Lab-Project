## Financial risk management

## Financial risk factors

The Company's activities expose it to market risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk. Its primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. The Company uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange-related exposures. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of each customer and the concentration of risk among the top few customers.

## Market risk

The Company operates internationally, with a major portion of its business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers in various foreign currencies, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies.

The Company holds foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates. Results of operations may be adversely affected as the Indian rupee appreciates or depreciates against these currencies.

## Derivative financial instruments

The Company primarily holds foreign currency forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates on foreign currency exposures. The counterparty for these contracts is generally a bank. The contracts are valued using quoted prices for similar assets and liabilities in active markets or inputs that are directly or indirectly observable in the marketplace.
