This form discloses contracts, arrangements, and transactions entered into with related parties under Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013, including certain arm’s-length transactions covered by the third proviso to that section.

## Details of material contracts or arrangement or transactions at arm's length basis

There were no material contracts, arrangements, or transactions entered into during the year ended March 31, 2026.

There were no material contracts, arrangements, or transactions requiring disclosure under the Companies Act, 2013. The Company obtained shareholder approval for material related-party transactions in accordance with Regulation 23 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. ## Annexure 3 - Particulars of employees

Infosys provides AI-first business consulting and technology services across 59 countries. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee reviews compensation for the CEO and other Senior Management Personnel to align short-term and long-term business objectives with performance goals.

The remuneration details comply with Rule 5 of Chapter XIII of the Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules, 2014. Tables 3(a) and 3(b) include the perquisite value of stock incentives when exercised, but not their value at grant.

Changes in remuneration in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025 primarily reflect changes in the perquisite value of stock incentives granted in previous years and exercised during fiscal 2026, as well as compensation revisions. The perquisite value also reflects the number of units exercised and changes in share price.

### Notes

- The remuneration details relate to directors and KMP and are provided on an accrual basis.
- The table includes the percentage increase in remuneration and the ratio of remuneration to MRE, both including and excluding the perquisite value of stock incentives exercised during the year.
- The percentage increase compares remuneration for the full current and previous fiscal years. The ratio to MRE uses remuneration for the full current fiscal year.
- Changes in the remuneration of independent directors are primarily attributable to foreign exchange fluctuations and changes in the composition of Board committees.
- (1) Remuneration to KMP includes fixed pay, variable pay, retiral benefits, and the perquisite value of stock incentives exercised during the period, determined under the Income-tax Act, 1961.
