## 3. Accessibility of workplaces

Are the premises and offices of the entity accessible to differently abled employees and workers, as per the requirements of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016?

Yes. Infosys premises and offices in India are accessible to differently abled employees and workers in accordance with the requirements of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## 4. Equal opportunity policy

Does the entity have an equal opportunity policy as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016?

Yes. Infosys has an equal opportunity policy under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. The policy is available at [Diversity and Inclusion Policy](https://www.infosys.com/careers/discover/culture/documents/diversity-inclusion-policy.pdf).

Infosys is committed to providing a safe and positive work environment, as enshrined in its Code of Conduct. Employees and contract staff have access to a grievance resolution mechanism known as Resolution Hubs, where they can raise workplace matters or concerns.

For more information, refer to the Resolution Hubs discussion in the Management Discussion and Analysis section of this Integrated Annual Report.

## 10. Health and safety management system

### 10a. Whether an occupational health and safety management system has been implemented by the entity? (Yes / No). If yes, the coverage of such system?

Yes. Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) is part of Infosys's ESG framework. Its Health, Safety and Environmental Management System (HSEMS), known as “Ozone,” is based on management commitment, compliance with applicable legal requirements, and stakeholder expectations.

Ozone is implemented globally based on legal requirements and internal benchmarks and is included in the internal audit program to support consistent governance and performance.

Infosys has achieved ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health and Safety certification at locations where the system has been in place for more than three years. These certifications cover all India locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, in line with the enterprise HSE strategy. More than 85% of Infosys employees are covered by ISO 45001 certification.

Infosys's HSE Policy addresses the proactive management of significant HSE risks and opportunities. Occupational health initiatives cover mental and emotional health, ergonomics, safety, lifestyle diseases, and preventive care. Occupational Health Centers operate across all India campuses.
