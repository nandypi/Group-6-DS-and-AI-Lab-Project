## Our Strategy Approaching value creation

Infosys’ strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities in which it operates.

Clients and prospective clients are responding to transformative opportunities enabled by software, cloud computing, and AI. Infosys aims to act as a navigator for clients’ AI journeys by helping them ideate, plan, and scale AI initiatives through strong data foundations and cloud capabilities. The company also emphasizes safeguards for privacy, ethics, and controls through Responsible AI practices.

In 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy:

1. Scale Agile Digital
2. Energize the Core
3. Reskill our People
4. Expand Localization

The current AI transition is described as transformational rather than incremental. AI-native systems use probabilistic inference, continuous learning, and agent-based execution.

- **Technology:** Enterprises are shifting from application-centric stacks to platforms incorporating intelligent agents, orchestration layers, and adaptive learning components.
- **Business:** Operating models are evolving from linear, role-based execution toward AI-driven operating loops that continuously sense, decide, and act.
- **Talent and organization:** Work is shifting from code creation toward orchestration, system supervision, domain interpretation, and outcome validation. This requires new roles, large-scale reskilling, and changes in workforce composition.

## Infosys AI vision

Infosys aspires to be a leading partner for unlocking AI value and delivering business outcomes in revenue growth, cost optimization, and innovation.

Its two-pronged AI strategy consists of:

- **AI-first services:** Capturing new demand and driving growth through services designed with AI as the core execution layer.
- **AI-augmented services:** Reinventing existing services by integrating AI into established systems and workflows to improve efficiency, personalization, and outcomes while extending the value of existing investments.

Infosys has developed more than 25 industry-focused AI playbooks covering repeatable use cases, domain context, and execution patterns. These are intended to help enterprises scale AI adoption across growth, efficiency, and innovation, from experimentation to enterprise-wide deployment.

## AI First Value Framework

Infosys introduced its AI-first value framework to help enterprises unlock AI value at scale through its generative and agentic AI suite, Infosys Topaz™. The company estimates, based on market estimates, an incremental AI-first services opportunity of USD 300–400 billion by 2030.

The framework defines six interconnected value pools:

- **AI Strategy & Engineering:** Designing and implementing AI strategies, platforms, and architectures, including AI agents, proprietary platforms, third-party tools, and purpose-built infrastructure.
- **Data for AI:** Preparing structured and unstructured enterprise data for AI model readiness through AI-ready data platforms and AI-grade data engineering, including fingerprinting and synthetic data services.
- **Process AI:** Transforming business processes by integrating AI agents and human expertise and redesigning end-to-end workflows.
- **Agentic Legacy Modernization:** Using AI agents to reverse-engineer existing systems, understand their intent, and progressively modernize them while reducing disruption and technical debt.
- **Physical AI:** Embedding AI into physical products and devices using sensor data, digital twins, robotics, autonomous systems, and edge intelligence.
- **AI Trust:** Applying responsible, secure, and ethical AI practices across the lifecycle, including risk assessments, policy design, security testing, and governance.

Infosys reports that it is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys.

## Platforms and IP

Infosys is using its composable and open agentic services suite, Infosys Topaz Fabric™, and its collaboration with AI disruptors to deliver AI-augmented and AI-first services.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is an enterprise-grade AI services layer within Infosys Topaz. It embeds enterprise context across data, applications, processes, and systems, integrating models, data pipelines, agents, and platforms across diverse technology environments. It is designed to help organizations reimagine business processes while building on existing investments and retaining strategic autonomy.

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across humans, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve product innovation with Infosys’ global scale to transform enterprise operations and core business functions.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is designed for clients with existing AI investments seeking a composable and modular agentic AI platform. Infosys Topaz AI Next is intended for clients seeking an end-to-end, vertically integrated AI platform to reimagine core business processes.

Both platforms institutionalize enterprise knowledge and process semantics and are designed to support continuous value realization from experimentation through large-scale production deployment. Responsible AI, security, governance, and assurance are embedded by design.
