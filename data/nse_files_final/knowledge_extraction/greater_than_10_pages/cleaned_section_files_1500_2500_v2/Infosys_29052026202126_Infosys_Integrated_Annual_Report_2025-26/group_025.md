## Annexure 4: Independent Auditor's certificate on corporate governance

## CERTIFICATE ON CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants and statutory auditors of Infosys Limited, examined the Company's compliance with the corporate governance conditions stipulated in Regulations 17 to 27, clauses (b) to (i) of Regulation 46(2), and paragraphs C and D of Schedule V of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, for the year ended March 31, 2026.

### Managements' Responsibility

The Company's management is responsible for complying with the corporate governance conditions, including designing, implementing, and maintaining internal controls and procedures to ensure compliance with the Listing Regulations.

### Auditor's Responsibility

The auditors' responsibility was limited to examining the procedures adopted and implemented by the Company to ensure compliance with the corporate governance conditions. The examination was neither an audit nor an expression of opinion on the Company's financial statements.

The auditors examined the Company's books of account and other relevant records and documents to provide reasonable assurance on compliance with corporate governance requirements.

The examination was conducted in accordance with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India's Guidance Note on Certification of Corporate Governance, applicable Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Guidance Note on Reports or Certificates for Special Purposes. The auditors also complied with the applicable ethical requirements under the ICAI Code of Ethics.

The auditors complied with the applicable requirements of Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1, *Quality Control for Firms that Perform Audits and Reviews of Historical Financial Information, and Other Assurance and Related Services Engagements*.
