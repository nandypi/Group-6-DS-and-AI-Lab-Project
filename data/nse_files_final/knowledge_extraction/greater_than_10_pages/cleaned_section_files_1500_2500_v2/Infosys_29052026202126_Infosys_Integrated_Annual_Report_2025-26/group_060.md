## 2. Does the entity have processes in place to avoid/manage conflicts of interest involving members of the Board?

Yes. Infosys has established processes to avoid and manage conflicts of interest involving Board members:

1. **Declaration of interest:** Board members must disclose financial interests, affiliations, or relationships that could influence decision-making at appointment and periodically thereafter, in compliance with statutory requirements.
2. **Code of Conduct and Ethics:** The Company has a formal code defining conflicts of interest and providing guidelines for identifying and managing them.
3. **Recusal procedures:** A Board member with a potential conflict must recuse themselves from discussions and voting on the relevant agenda item.
4. **Approval requirements:** Transactions involving entities in which Directors have an interest require prior Board approval. Transactions exceeding prescribed thresholds also require shareholder approval.

### 2a. Does the entity have procedures in place for sustainable sourcing?

Yes. Infosys has adopted a Responsible Supply Chain & Supplier Diversity Policy and a Green Procurement Policy. Suppliers must sign the Supplier Code of Conduct, and periodic ESG assessments are conducted internally and through a third party.

### 2b. If yes, what percentage of inputs were sourced sustainably?

All procurements follow the principles of sustainable sourcing. Suppliers are required to accept the Supplier Code of Conduct, which is based on the UNGC Principles.
