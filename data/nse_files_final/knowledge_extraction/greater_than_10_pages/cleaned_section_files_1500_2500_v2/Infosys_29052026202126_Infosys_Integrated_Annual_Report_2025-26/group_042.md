## Corporate Social Responsibility Committee

The Corporate Social Responsibility Committee comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, the Committee comprised:

1. Govind Iyer, Chairperson
2. Chitra Nayak
3. Michael Gibbs

## Our CSR philosophy

Infosys’ Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) includes community development, institution-building, and sustainability-related initiatives, in addition to philanthropy.

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objective of the Committee is to assist the Board in fulfilling its corporate social responsibility. The Committee is responsible for:

1. Identifying areas of CSR activity.
2. Recommending expenditure on identified CSR activities.
3. Evaluating CSR proposals received from the Company.
4. Implementing and monitoring the CSR Policy.
5. Formulating a CSR annual action plan and recommending it to the Board.
6. Reviewing the Company’s initiatives and programs.
7. Coordinating with Infosys Foundation and agencies such as registered trusts, societies, or Section 8 companies to implement programs and execute initiatives under the Company’s CSR Policy.
8. Reviewing impact assessment reports issued by independent agencies on the Company’s projects.
9. Reviewing CSR reporting and disclosures required under applicable statutes.
10. Reviewing certificates issued for utilization of CSR funds earmarked for specific themes or projects.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises three independent directors and fulfills the requirements of:

- Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013
- CSR Committee Charter

The CSR Committee is responsible for identifying CSR activities, programs, and initiatives under defined guidelines and overseeing the activities and functioning of Infosys Foundation, Infosys Foundation USA, and other initiatives undertaken by the Company, including in Australia and various parts of Europe. The Foundations report to the Committee on the progress of deployed initiatives and assist with periodic disclosures.

The CSR Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## CSR report

The CSR report for the year ended March 31, 2026, as required under the Companies Act, 2013, is attached as Annexure 6 to the Board’s report.

During fiscal 2026, the Company:

- Spent ₹558.04 crore on various CSR projects in India.
- Had an unspent balance of ₹19.00 crore for ongoing projects, to be transferred to the unspent CSR account and spent in accordance with the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, and amendments thereto.
- Spent an additional ₹9.21 crore on ongoing projects from fiscals 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- Spent ₹43.03 crore outside India, including in the US, Australia, and various parts of Europe. This expenditure was over and above the statutory CSR requirement in India.

The Committee periodically reviewed and approved the budget and disbursement of funds and ensured allocation of at least 2% of the average net profits made during the three immediately preceding financial years for CSR activities in India.

## ESG Committee

The ESG Committee was constituted with effect from April 14, 2021. It comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, the Committee comprised:

1. Chitra Nayak, Chairperson
2. Govind Iyer
3. Helene Auriol Potier

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The ESG Committee is responsible for:

1. Guiding the creation and periodic refresh of the Company’s ESG Vision and ambitions and incorporating updates to its ESG vision and goals.
2. Reviewing the operation and working of the ESG Council. The Committee may form subcommittees and delegate authority as appropriate.
3. Ensuring that the Company implements appropriate measures to advance its ESG objectives, with access to internal information necessary to fulfill its duties.
4. Overseeing periodic ESG disclosures and communications, including annual report sections, ESG reports, and Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) filings.
5. Obtaining advice and assistance from internal or external experts and advisors.
6. Reporting periodically to the Board on matters reviewed and decisions or recommendations made.
