## 14. Assessment for the year for health and safety:

Infosys’ Health, Safety and Environmental Management System (HSEMS) is certified to ISO 45001:2018 and is audited annually by certifying bodies. The HSEMS covers all operational activities and employees working for or on behalf of the Company, including deputees at client sites. Infosys’ Corporate Certification Audits and Assessments Team (CCAT) conducts periodic assessments across Infosys locations annually.

|---|---:|
| Health and safety practices | 100% |
| Working conditions | 100% |

## 15. Provide details of any corrective action taken or underway to address safety-related incidents (if any) and on significant risks / concerns arising from assessments of health and safety practices and working conditions.

There were no significant risks or concerns arising from assessments of health and safety practices and working conditions. A few minor safety incidents related to operational controls occurred and were closed with detailed corrective measures. Infosys periodically monitors all controls and makes course corrections as appropriate.

## PRINCIPLE 4: Businesses should respect the interests of and be responsive to all its stakeholders

### 1. Describe the processes for identifying key stakeholder groups of the entity

Infosys identifies and prioritizes stakeholders based on the Company’s impact on them and the ability of stakeholder groups to influence the functioning of the Company. As part of its GRI-based materiality assessment, Infosys has identified six key stakeholder groups:

- Investors/shareholders
- Clients
- Employees and sub-contractors
- Suppliers/partners
- Government/regulators
- The community

The ESG Committee of the Board approves the continued relevance of material topics annually.
