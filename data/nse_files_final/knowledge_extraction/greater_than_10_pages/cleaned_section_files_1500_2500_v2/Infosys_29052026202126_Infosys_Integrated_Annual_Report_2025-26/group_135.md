## Financial risk management

### Financial risk factors

The Group’s activities expose it to market risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk. Its primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. The Group uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange exposures. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of customers and concentration among the largest customers.

### Market risk

The Group operates internationally, with business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers, and intercompany transactions denominated in foreign currencies. The Group uses foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates.

The Group’s results are affected by changes in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and foreign currencies.

During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Group designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions for the cash flow hedge balance as at March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss within three months.

The Group determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and the hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at inception and through periodic prospective assessments.

If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal but the risk management objective remains unchanged and the hedge continues to qualify for hedge accounting, the hedge relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Any hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.
