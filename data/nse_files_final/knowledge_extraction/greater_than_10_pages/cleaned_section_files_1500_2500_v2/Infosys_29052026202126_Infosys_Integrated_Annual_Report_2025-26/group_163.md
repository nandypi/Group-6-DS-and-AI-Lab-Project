# Section 6 c) Parameters for Vesting

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section 6 c) Parameters for Vesting | i. Relative Total Shareholder Return (“Relative TSR”) against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from a basket of 10 industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. ii. Relative TSR against broader market domestic and global indices comprising Nifty 50, S&P 500, and an MSCI index to be determined by the Administrator. | i. Long-term Relative TSR for rolling three-year TSR against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from the same basket of 10 industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. The Company will implement a phased transition to a three-year rolling Relative TSR parameter, with vesting based on one-year TSR in Year 1, two-year TSR in Year 2, and a full three-year TSR measure from Year 3 onwards. ii. Annual TSR: A. Relative TSR against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from the same basket of 10 industry peers. B. Relative TSR against broader domestic and global market indices comprising MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50, as determined by the Administrator. | The changes are intended to strengthen alignment with long-term shareholder value creation and rationalize operational metrics. The introduction of three-year rolling Relative TSR reflects shareholder feedback regarding alignment of management compensation with shareholders’ long-term interests. There are no changes to the basket of existing industry peers. |

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating performance metrics | Operating lead performance metrics used to derive shareholder value: total revenue growth, digital revenue growth, and operating margin. Annual performance targets are determined by the Administrator. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares to vest. | Annual operating performance metrics used to derive shareholder value: total revenue growth and operating margin. Performance targets are determined by the Administrator. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares to vest. Relative TSR vesting: at or above the 75th percentile, 100% of the Restricted Stock Units due for vesting will vest; between the 50th and 75th percentiles, vesting is on a linear scale from 60% to 100%; below the 50th percentile, no Restricted Stock Units due for vesting will vest. Operating performance vesting: at minimum threshold performance, 75% vests; below threshold, none vests; at target performance, 100% vests; at stretch target performance, 125% vests; between threshold and target, vesting is on a linear scale from 75% to 100%; between target and stretch target, vesting is on a linear scale from 100% to 125%. | The proposed changes focus on revenue and margin for continued consistency and comparability. Sub-segment metrics such as digital revenue growth may become transient and less comparable over the long term of 10 years. The revised structure is also intended to increase transparency regarding the payout philosophy. |

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregate operating-performance vesting limit | — | While an individual performance outcome may result in achievement above 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter shall not exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter. | — |

## Section 6(e)

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exercise period after termination or resignation | Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Exercise Period for the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(iv) above, as relevant, shall be as follows: in the event of termination of employment or resignation, Restricted Stock Units vested as of the termination or resignation date, where termination is not for misconduct, shall be exercised within 90 calendar days of the date of termination or resignation. | Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Exercise Period for the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(II) above, as relevant, shall be as follows: in the event of termination of employment or resignation, Restricted Stock Units vested as of the termination or resignation date, where termination is not for misconduct, shall be exercised within 90 calendar days of the date of vesting. | The changes clarify and ensure consistency in the operation of provisions relating to the exercise period for vested RSUs. The revised language clarifies that the exercise period begins on the date the relevant RSUs vest. |
