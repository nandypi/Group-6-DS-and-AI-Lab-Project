## 4. Management's Responsibility

The Company's management is responsible for selecting or establishing suitable criteria for preparing the Sustainability Information, including the BRSR reporting boundary, taking into account applicable laws and regulations related to sustainability reporting.

Management is also responsible for identifying key aspects, engaging with stakeholders, and preparing and presenting the Identified Sustainability Information in accordance with the Criteria. This includes designing, implementing, and maintaining internal controls relevant to the preparation of the BRSR and the measurement of Identified Sustainability Information free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.

## 5. Inherent limitations

The absence of a significant body of established practice for evaluating and measuring non-financial information permits different, but acceptable, measurement techniques. This can affect comparability between companies.

## 6. Our Independence and Quality Control

The assurance provider maintained its independence and confirmed compliance with the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), the SEBI Master Circular and related clarifications, and applicable competency and experience requirements.

The assurance provider applied Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1, *Quality Control for Firms that Perform Audits and Reviews of Historical Financial Information, and Other Assurance and Related Services Engagements*, and maintained a quality-control system covering ethical requirements, professional standards, and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## 7. Our Responsibility

The assurance provider's responsibility was to express a reasonable assurance opinion on the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I, based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained.

The engagement was conducted in accordance with:

- Standard on Sustainability Assurance Engagements (SSAE) 3000, *Assurance Engagements on Sustainability Information*; and
- Standard on Assurance Engagements (SAE) 3410, *Assurance Engagements on Greenhouse Gas Statements*.

Both standards were issued by the Sustainability Reporting Standards Board (SRSB) of the ICAI.

The standards require planning and performance of procedures to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I and included in the BRSR was prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the Criteria.

The assurance engagement included professional judgment and professional skepticism, along with identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error. Procedures included inquiries, observation of processes, inspection of documents, evaluation of quantification methods and reporting policies, analytical procedures, and agreeing or reconciling information with underlying records.

The procedures included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Identified Sustainability Information and related disclosures.
- Obtaining an understanding of the assessment criteria and evaluating their suitability for measuring the Identified Sustainability Information.
- Making inquiries of management, including the environment, compliance, and human resources teams, and personnel responsible for preparing the Report.
- Evaluating the design and implementation of key systems, processes, and controls for recording, processing, and reporting the Identified Sustainability Information at the corporate office and selected other locations.
- Determining the nature, timing, and extent of further procedures based on the risks of material misstatement.
- Testing key assumptions, emission factors, and methodologies used to calculate greenhouse gas emissions.
- Testing the process for collating sustainability information by agreeing or reconciling the Identified Sustainability Information with underlying records on a sample basis.
- Testing the consolidation for selected locations and the corporate office within the reporting boundary to assess the completeness of reported data.

The assurance provider stated that the evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the reasonable assurance opinion.

## 8. Exclusions

The assurance scope excluded the following, and no opinion was expressed on them:

- Company operations outside the Reporting Boundary set out in Question 13 of Section A: General Disclosures of the BRSR, for the Identified Sustainability Information listed in Appendix I.
- Report aspects and qualitative or quantitative data other than the Identified Sustainability Information.
- Data and information outside the defined reporting period, being the financial year ended March 31, 2026.
- Statements expressing opinion, belief, aspiration, expectation, aim, or future intentions.

## 9. Other information

Management is responsible for the other information in the BRSR, the Integrated Annual Report (IAR), and the Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) Report, other than the Identified Sustainability Information and the independent assurance reports dated May 27, 2026.

The opinion on the Identified Sustainability Information does not cover the other information, and no assurance was expressed on it. The assurance provider was required to read the other information and consider whether it was materially inconsistent with the Identified Sustainability Information or otherwise appeared to be materially misstated.

The assurance provider reported that it had nothing to report regarding any material misstatement identified in the other information.

## 10. Reasonable Assurance Opinion

Based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained, the BRSR Core indicators for the year ended March 31, 2026, listed in Appendix I and presented in the BRSR, were prepared in all material respects in accordance with the Criteria stated in paragraph 3.

## 11. Restriction on use

The Reasonable Assurance report was prepared for the Board of Directors of the Company, at the Company's request, solely to assist in reporting on the Company's sustainability performance and activities. The report was not intended for use by any other person or for any other purpose without prior written consent.
