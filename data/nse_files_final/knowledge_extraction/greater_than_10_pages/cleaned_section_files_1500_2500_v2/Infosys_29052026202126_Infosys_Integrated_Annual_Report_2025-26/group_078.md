## Annexure 'A' to the Independent Auditor's Report

*(Referred to in paragraph 1(f) under 'Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements' section of the report to the Members of Infosys Limited.)*

## Report on the Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements under Clause (i) of sub-section 3 of Section 143 of the Companies Act, 2013

The auditors audited the internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements of Infosys Limited as of March 31, 2026, in conjunction with the audit of the Company's standalone financial statements for the year ended on that date.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for Internal Financial Controls

Management and the Board of Directors are responsible for establishing and maintaining adequate internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements, based on criteria established by the Company and the essential components of internal control stated in the Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

These responsibilities include:

- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls.
- Ensuring the orderly and efficient conduct of business and adherence to Company policies.
- Safeguarding assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and errors.
- Maintaining accurate and complete accounting records.
- Preparing reliable financial information on a timely basis as required under the Companies Act, 2013.

## Auditor's Responsibility

The auditors' responsibility was to express an opinion on the Company's internal financial controls with reference to the standalone financial statements. The audit was conducted in accordance with the Guidance Note issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and the applicable Standards on Auditing prescribed under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013.

The audit included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Company's internal financial controls.
- Assessing the risk of a material weakness.
- Testing and evaluating the design and operating effectiveness of controls based on assessed risks.
- Performing procedures selected according to the auditors' judgment, including consideration of risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error.

The auditors stated that the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for their opinion.

## Meaning of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Internal financial control with reference to standalone financial statements is a process designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding:

1. The reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of financial statements for external purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
2. The maintenance of records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and dispositions of company assets.
3. The recording of transactions necessary to permit preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
4. The authorization of receipts and expenditures by management and directors.
5. The prevention or timely detection of unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposition of company assets that could have a material effect on the financial statements.

## Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Because of the inherent limitations of internal financial controls over financial reporting, including the possibility of collusion or improper management override of controls, material misstatements due to error or fraud may occur and not be detected.

Evaluations of internal financial controls for future periods are also subject to the risk that controls may become inadequate because of changes in conditions or that compliance with policies and procedures may deteriorate.

## Opinion

In the auditors' opinion, Infosys Limited had, in all material respects, adequate internal financial controls with reference to its standalone financial statements, and those controls were operating effectively as of March 31, 2026. The opinion was based on criteria established by the Company and the essential components of internal control stated in the Guidance Note issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

## Annexure 'B' to the Independent Auditor's Report

*(Referred to in paragraph 2 under 'Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements' section of the report to the Members of Infosys Limited.)*

Based on the information and explanations provided by the Company and the books and records examined during the audit, the auditors reported the following:

### i. Property, plant and equipment, right-of-use assets, and intangible assets

- Infosys maintained proper records containing full particulars, including quantitative details and the location of property, plant and equipment and relevant details of right-of-use assets.
- Infosys maintained proper records containing full particulars of intangible assets.
- The Company had a program to physically verify property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets so that all assets would be verified once every three years. The auditors considered the program reasonable given the Company's size and the nature of its assets.
- Assets due for verification during the year were physically verified by management. No material discrepancies were reported.
- Based on property tax receipts, lease agreements, and registered title documents examined, the title to self-constructed buildings and the title deeds of other immovable properties disclosed under Property, Plant and Equipment were held in the Company's name as of the balance sheet date, except properties where the Company was the lessee and the lease agreements were duly executed in its favor.
- Infosys did not revalue any property, plant and equipment, including right-of-use assets, or intangible assets during the year.
- No proceedings were initiated during the year or pending as of March 31, 2026 against Infosys for holding benami property under the Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988, as amended in 2016, and the related rules.

### ii. Inventory and working capital limits

- Infosys did not have any inventory; therefore, reporting under clause 3(ii)(a) of the applicable Order was not applicable.
- Infosys was not sanctioned working capital limits exceeding ₹5 crore in aggregate at any time during the year from banks or financial institutions based on the security of current assets. Therefore, reporting under clause 3(ii)(b) of the applicable Order was not applicable.

### iii. Investments and loans

- Infosys made investments in companies and granted unsecured loans to other parties during the year.
- The Company did not provide loans or advances in the nature of loans, guarantees, or security to any other entity during the year; therefore, reporting under clause 3(iii)(a) was not applicable.
- In the auditors' opinion, the investments made and the terms and conditions of loans granted during the year were, prima facie, not prejudicial to the Company's interests.
- For loans granted by the Company, schedules for repayment of principal and payment of interest were stipulated. Repayments of principal and receipts of interest were generally regular according to those schedules.
- No overdue amounts relating to loans granted by the Company remained outstanding as of the balance sheet date.
- No loan that fell due during the year was renewed or extended, and no fresh loan was granted to settle overdue amounts on existing loans to the same parties.
- Infosys did not grant loans or advances in the nature of loans that were repayable on demand or without specifying repayment terms or periods; therefore, reporting under clause 3(iii)(f) was not applicable.
- Infosys did not make investments in firms or limited liability partnerships during the year.
- Infosys did not provide guarantees or security, or grant secured or unsecured advances in the nature of loans, to companies, firms, limited liability partnerships, or other parties.

### iv. Loans, investments, guarantees, and securities

Infosys complied with Sections 185 and 186 of the Companies Act, 2013, in respect of loans granted, investments made, and guarantees and securities provided, as applicable.

### v. Deposits

Infosys did not accept any deposits or amounts deemed to be deposits. Therefore, reporting under clause 3(v) of the applicable Order was not applicable.

### vi. Cost records

The Central Government had not specified maintenance of cost records under Section 148(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 for the business activities carried out by Infosys. Therefore, reporting under clause 3(vi) of the applicable Order was not applicable.
