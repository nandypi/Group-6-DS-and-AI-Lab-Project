### Integrated Annual Report 2025-26

## From possibilities to enterprise AI impact

Infosys describes a shift from enterprise AI experimentation and pilots toward measurable outcomes in revenue, costs, customer experience, responsiveness, and operating models.

The company identifies several requirements for scaling AI:

- Preparing structured and unstructured data as a trusted strategic asset.
- Redesigning core processes around domain-aware agents working alongside people.
- Using AI agents to reverse-engineer and progressively modernize legacy systems.
- Extending intelligence into physical devices, sensors, robotics, autonomous systems, digital twins, and edge intelligence.
- Building AI systems that are responsible, secure, ethical, and aligned with regulations across jurisdictions.

The Infosys AI First Value Framework comprises six value pools and is powered by Infosys Topaz™, its generative and agentic AI suite, and Infosys Topaz Fabric™, its agentic services suite.

Infosys states that it is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys, with more than 4,600 AI projects underway. It has developed more than 30 new service offerings across the six value pools.

## Liberty Global

### From AI experiments to enterprise impact: How Liberty Global is improving customer experience with Infosys.

Liberty Global, a connectivity and entertainment provider in Europe, partnered with Infosys to operate and evolve platforms serving tens of millions of customers across its European footprint.

Over the previous year, Infosys supported more than 1,000 platform deliveries. AI-enabled solutions included:

- **Super Search**, serving approximately 8 million TV customers and using large language models for conversational content discovery across linear TV, on-demand, and streaming services.
- **Agent Assist** and **Customer Assist**, deployed across multiple markets to enable more self-service journeys, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce pressure on customer-care teams.

The initiatives resulted in 50% fewer outages year on year and 60% fewer customers impacted.

The Infosys–Liberty Global partnership spans more than two decades, including more than a decade as a formal strategic partnership.

## Hertz

### Hertz modernized critical workflows 60% faster with AI-first code engineering.

Hertz used complex COBOL systems on a legacy platform and worked with Infosys on an AI-first modernization approach.

Infosys analyzed nearly three million lines of legacy COBOL code and used multimodal large language models to develop a cloud modernization blueprint. The company applied AI-powered reverse engineering to extract business logic and reimplement it as domain-centric Java microservices using Infosys iLEAD and GitHub Copilot.

The modernization approach also used:

- Mainframe-to-cloud expertise
- OpenAI, Claude Sonnet, and AWS Bedrock
- AI-enabled testing
- Reusable components
- Enterprise governance

The resulting microservices-based digital core is intended to improve platform agility, scalability, and system resilience on AWS Cloud. Reported or expected outcomes include:

- 60% faster modernization timelines
- Greater reuse of cross-channel business capabilities
- Reduced hosting costs
- A scalable foundation for further modernization and automation across additional business domains
