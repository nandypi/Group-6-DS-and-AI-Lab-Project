## Shares transferred to the IEPF

## Share transfer system

SEBI, effective April 01, 2019, barred physical transfer of shares of listed companies and mandated transfers only through demat. Investors may continue to hold shares in physical form.

Shareholders holding physical shares have been requested to dematerialize them and furnish PAN, nomination, contact details, bank account details, and specimen signatures for their corresponding folios. Shareholders holding shares in dematerialized mode have been requested to register their email address, bank account details, and mobile number with their depository participants.

Pursuant to a SEBI Circular dated July 2, 2025, a special window was provided from July 7, 2025 to January 6, 2026 for re-lodgement of transfer requests lodged before April 1, 2019 and pending due to deficiencies. This facility was extended by a SEBI Circular dated January 30, 2026 until February 4, 2027.

Eligible shareholders may submit the requisite documents within the extended timeline. Securities will be credited only in demat form and will be subject to a one-year lock-in, during which they cannot be transferred, pledged, or encumbered.

## Investor conferences / events held in fiscal 2026

Infosys holds press meets and investor or analyst calls after every quarterly results announcement. The Company also holds its Annual General Meeting and participates in sell-side or broker-arranged investor conferences and roadshows, including one-on-one and group meetings with investors. Details of these events are sent to the stock exchanges and updated on the Company's website.

## Investor grievances and investor contacts

The Company has a Board-level Stakeholders Relationship Committee to examine and redress complaints by shareholders and investors. The status of complaints is reported to the entire Board, and the Committee meets as often as required to resolve shareholder grievances.

Most investor grievances and postal or electronic communications were addressed within seven days of receipt. Exceptions involved cases constrained by disputes or legal impediments.

Share transfers, dividend payments, and other investor-related activities are processed at the office of the Company's registrar and transfer agent, KFin Technologies Limited.

## Company shares-related disputes / litigation

Certain pending civil cases involve rival claims by parties seeking declarations of title and accrued benefits relating to the Company's disputed shares. Infosys Limited and its registrar and transfer agent, KFin Technologies Limited, are pro forma defendants in these matters. The matters are not material in nature.
