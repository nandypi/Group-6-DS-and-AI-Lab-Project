## Energy-efficient IT infrastructure

Infosys has adopted measures to make its network and computer workloads more energy-efficient and environmentally friendly.

- **Energy-efficient network infrastructure:** Infosys uses network equipment with power-saving features.
- **Public cloud adoption:** Internal IT applications have largely transitioned to the public cloud, providing scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premises data centers. Cloud-based collaboration and secure-access solutions support hybrid work.
- **IT service management:** The IT service management landscape uses a standardized, AI-enabled service desk hosted on a SaaS platform. This has standardized operational processes, increased automation, and improved support-service user experience. AI-assisted agents support employees with daily tasks and AI-enabled ways of working.
- **Data center modernization:** Data center modernization is a pillar of the sustainability and responsible-growth strategy. Infosys uses density-optimized hyperscale platforms, cloud-scale agility, and high-density server virtualization to reduce infrastructure sprawl and improve compute-resource utilization. Investments in Data Center Infrastructure Management capabilities provide real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure, supporting data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.
- **Enterprise storage:** Internal IT provides storage services to delivery units through multiprotocol storage technology, including flash storage, fabric pool, and storage grid solutions. Storage grid technology reduces the physical data-center footprint, power consumption, and cooling requirements. Hybrid cloud storage is used for selected workloads, providing built-in data protection and scalability while reducing the need for buffer investments, on-premises space, power consumption, and cooling.
- **Cloud-native development environment:** The internal IT team uses an open-source-based cloud-native development platform built on Hyper Converged Infrastructure and compute resources. Consolidating resources through HCI reduces the physical data-center footprint and hardware requirements.
