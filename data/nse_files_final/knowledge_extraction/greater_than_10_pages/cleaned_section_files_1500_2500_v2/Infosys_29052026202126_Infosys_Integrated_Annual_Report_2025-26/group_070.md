### 4. Resource-efficiency and emissions-reduction initiatives

| S.No. | Initiative undertaken | Details of the initiative | Outcome of the initiative |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy efficiency improvement in buildings | Radiant heating uses existing radiant panels to heat surfaces that radiate heat into rooms, reducing the need for conventional high-intensity heating. In favorable climates, free cooling uses naturally cool water generated by the cooling tower. The cooled water is circulated through a plate heat exchanger and supplied to a dedicated free-cooling coil in air-handling units (AHUs), reducing mechanical cooling. | Designed for one office location, the system has the potential to reduce energy consumption by approximately 70% compared with traditional resistance heating. The strategy contributed to an approximately 15% reduction in the building's annual HVAC load and is expected to reduce energy consumption and Scope 2 emissions. |
| 2 | Supply chain decarbonization initiatives | Climate action and sustainable packaging requirements were integrated into IT hardware procurement. Suppliers were required to disclose verified Product Carbon Footprint (PCF), climate commitments, and detailed packaging-material information alongside technical specifications. | For laptop procurement in fiscal 2026, the lowest-scoring supplier was excluded and purchase volumes were allocated proportionately to higher-performing suppliers. This resulted in avoided emissions of 8,955 tCO₂e against a standard product specification. |
| 3 | Supply chain decarbonization initiatives | Green material specifications were introduced to support the Net Zero journey. The specifications address embodied carbon, energy and water efficiency, responsible material sourcing, elimination of hazardous substances, circular-economy practices, and improved indoor environmental quality. | Standardized sustainable specifications were established for materials including steel, concrete, glazing, furniture, HVAC systems, electrical equipment, IT hardware, and landscaping elements. |
| 4 | Smart water metering | Meters were installed in water pipes at distribution and consumption points to record flow rates in real time. | Smart water metering systems installed on most campuses helped identify undetected leaks, reduce unaccounted water, identify water-saving opportunities, and provide insights for new designs. |
