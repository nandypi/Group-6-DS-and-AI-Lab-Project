## Risk management report

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Contractual liabilities | Risk of clients demanding more favorable terms, including onerous liability clauses, and inability to adhere to contractual obligations may lead to litigation, fines, and adverse effects on reputation, revenue, and profitability. Impacted capitals: Social & Relationship and Financial. | Dedicated in-house team engages clients on contractual terms. A contract legal playbook and risk framework identify high-risk clauses and appropriate controls. A multi-layered governance process supports contract approval. Dedicated teams adhere to, monitor, and audit contractual obligations. Contractual risks are subject to Board-level monitoring, reporting, and governance. |
| Complex and evolving regulatory environment | Failure to comply with the complex regulatory landscape, including immigration, wages, tax, and sanctions requirements, could result in investigations, regulatory inquiries, litigation, fines, and negative client sentiment. Evolving regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and public disclosure requirements add uncertainty to compliance policies. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, Social & Relationship, and Natural. | A dedicated in-house compliance team works with external partners to monitor regulatory changes. The compliance framework, policies, controls, and programs identify changes, assess their impact, and implement actions. Infosys regularly engages with regulatory authorities and compliance experts. Contract awareness programs and training are provided to employees and vendors. Periodic compliance certification defines accountability and responsibility. Monitoring, reporting, governance, and Board oversight are provided. |

## Cybersecurity risk management

Cyber risks are managed through multi-layered controls using a defense-in-depth approach, covering cybersecurity strategy, policies, processes, and preventive, detective, and corrective controls.

The cybersecurity strategy focuses on:

- Transparency and experience
- Continual improvement and compliance
- Cyber resilience
- Building and maintaining a positive cybersecurity culture

The enterprise Information Security Council (ISC), a high-level working group with representation from various departments and business units, governs and oversees Infosys's Information Security Management System (ISMS). The ISC establishes, directs, monitors, and executes the information security program and reports key risks to the executive leadership through the Operational Risk Council.

## INDEPENDENT PRACTITIONER'S REASONABLE ASSURANCE REPORT ON IDENTIFIED SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION IN INFOSYS LIMITED'S BUSINESS RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

## To the Board of Directors of INFOSYS LIMITED

The reasonable assurance engagement for Infosys Limited was undertaken under an engagement letter dated January 16, 2026, covering the Identified Sustainability Information, also referred to as the BRSR Core indicators. This information is included in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report within the Integrated Annual Report for the year ended March 31, 2026. The engagement was conducted by a multidisciplinary team including assurance practitioners, environmental engineers, and specialists.

## 2. Identified Sustainability Information

The assurance scope consisted of the BRSR Core indicators listed in Appendix I to the report. The BRSR reporting boundary was as disclosed in Question 13 of Section A: General Disclosure of the BRSR, with applicable exceptions disclosed in notes under the respective BRSR questions.

The engagement covered information for the year ended March 31, 2026, unless otherwise stated. No procedures were performed on other elements of the BRSR, and no opinion was expressed on those elements.

## 3. Criteria

The criteria used by Infosys to prepare the Identified Sustainability Information were:

- Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
- Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting Requirements for listed entities under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026.
