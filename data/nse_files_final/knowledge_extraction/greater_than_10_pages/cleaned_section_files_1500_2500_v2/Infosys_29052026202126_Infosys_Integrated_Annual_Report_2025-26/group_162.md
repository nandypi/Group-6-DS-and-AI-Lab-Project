## Explanatory Statement

## Background and context for item nos. 4 and 5

The proposed amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program – 2019 (the “Amended 2019 Plan”) would support the grant of stock incentives to eligible employees of Infosys and its subsidiaries.

Infosys began rewarding employees through stock ownership programs in 1994. The 2015 Incentive Compensation Plan (the “2015 Plan”) was approved by shareholders through a postal ballot dated March 31, 2016. Stock incentives continue to be granted to eligible employees under that plan, with vesting criteria that are largely time-based.

The Expanded Stock Ownership Program, 2019 (the “2019 Plan”) was approved at the Annual General Meeting held on June 22, 2019, for a period of seven years and is due to expire on June 21, 2026. The plan originally covered approximately 5,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 1.15% of the Company’s then-outstanding equity shares.

As of the date of the Notice:

- 3,26,63,903 equity shares remained available for grant under the existing plan, representing approximately 0.8% of the Company’s outstanding equity shares.
- 4,44,19,917 Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) were available for grant under all ESOP plans, representing approximately 1.1% of the Company’s outstanding equity share capital. This included vested but unexercised RSUs and RSUs that had not yet vested.

The Board approved amendments intended to:

- Continue the performance-based stock incentive program across an expanded employee base.
- Attract, retain, and incentivize key talent.
- Maintain alignment between employee interests and long-term shareholder value creation.

## The summary of the proposed amendments is as under:

### 1. Extension of the 2019 Plan tenure

The 2019 Plan would be extended for a further period of seven (7) years from the date of shareholder approval. Grants would be made over seven (7) years, and the amended plan would remain valid for ten (10) years from the date shareholder approval is received.

### 2. Strengthening alignment with Long-Term Shareholder Value creation and rationalization of Operational Metrics based on the inputs received from investors

The proposed amendments would:

- Introduce a three-year rolling Relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) as a vesting parameter.
- Rationalize operating metrics to maintain focus on revenue and margin, supporting consistency and comparability over time.
- Remove reliance on potentially transient or less comparable sub-segment metrics, such as digital revenue growth.

### 3. Share pool and dilution

The proposed extension would not increase the originally approved share pool of 5,00,00,000 equity shares. The Company would continue to operate within the existing approved pool, with no further dilution of equity share capital compared with the earlier approval.

As of the date of the Notice, 3,26,63,903 equity shares remained available for grant. These represented the unutilized balance from the original shareholder-approved pool and approximately 0.8% of the Company’s equity share capital as of May 28, 2026.

### 4. Regulatory and other amendments

Other amendments would provide clarity and consistency and align the 2019 Plan with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
