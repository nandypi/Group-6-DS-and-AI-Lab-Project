The Group determines the allowance for credit losses based on historical loss experience, adjusted to reflect current and estimated future economic conditions. The Group considers current and anticipated future economic conditions relating to the industries with which it deals and the countries where it operates.

The amount of ECL, or reversal, required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.
