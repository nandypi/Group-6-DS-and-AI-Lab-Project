## 10b. What are the processes used to identify work-related hazards and assess risks on a routine and non-routine basis by the entity?

Infosys identifies and assesses work-related hazards for existing, new, and modified activities, processes, and services, including in response to regulatory updates.

Routine risk assessments include:

- Periodic workplace and ergonomic evaluations.
- Monitoring utility operations.
- Reviewing employee commute risks.

Non-routine tasks, including construction, infrastructure development, preventive maintenance, and facility modifications, undergo specific risk assessments before and during execution.

Incidents and near misses are analyzed quarterly to identify trends and emerging risks. Hazards are prioritized and mitigated using the hierarchy of controls, followed by reassessment of residual risks and updates to the risk control matrix as required. Employees and stakeholders participate in the risk assessment process, and learnings from past projects and current operations are incorporated on an ongoing basis.

## 10c. Whether you have processes for workers to report the work-related hazards and to remove themselves from such risks. (Y / N)

Yes. Infosys has an incident reporting and management process covering accidents, near misses, unsafe acts, and unsafe conditions. Employees can report health and safety concerns through the Infosys Helpdesk (IHD) or designated email addresses. Contract staff without system access report concerns to their supervisors, who escalate them to the local HSE team.

All incidents undergo root cause analysis, and corrective and preventive measures are implemented to prevent recurrence and potential injuries or losses. Incident-reporting awareness is supported through induction programs, Lex online training modules, awareness campaigns, and mailers. Learnings and corrective actions are shared across locations.

## 10d. Do the employees/ worker of the entity have access to non-occupational medical and healthcare services?

Yes. Infosys operates Occupational Health Centers (OHCs), including physiotherapy facilities, across its campuses in India. Statutory and applicable norms are followed, and competent medical professionals from accredited partner hospitals manage the OHCs and physiotherapy centers. Periodic health risk assessments monitor health trends and support specialist consultations and targeted interventions.

Employee well-being initiatives include workshops, awareness campaigns, regular health check-ups, online chat sessions, counseling services such as Samaritans, and targeted wellness programs. OHC visit details are recorded digitally in accordance with regional data protection and privacy regulations.

Infosys also provides healthcare benefits through hospital tie-ups, giving employees and their dependents access to health screenings and treatment for non-occupational illnesses. Employees, including contractual staff, receive insurance coverage. Contractual employees are also covered by targeted programs such as mandatory health screenings and risk-based interventions informed by identified hazards and emerging health trends.

## 12. Describe the measures taken by the entity to ensure a safe and healthy work place.

Infosys’ occupational health and safety policies, processes, and practices address the physical, emotional, and social well-being of employees. Its Health and Safety Management system is certified to ISO 45001:2018 for all Indian locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries.

Measures include:

1. **Incident management:** Incidents, near misses, potential hazards, and accidents are reported through internal applications or email. Causes are identified and analyzed, and corrective actions are taken to prevent recurrence and related injuries or losses.
2. **OH&S Committee:** Employees and other than permanent employees participate in the Infosys OH&S Committee. Employee representation in safety committees is 100%.
3. **Training and awareness:** Training includes awareness programs, mock drills, classroom sessions, and periodic demonstrations. HSEMS training forms part of employee induction. An HSE awareness module is available on Lex. Contract staff receive job-specific and general induction training, followed by refresher training.
4. **Safety interventions:** Safety systems include work permits, training, Lock Out Tag Out (LOTO), safety inspections, audits, operational controls, and monitoring. Policies address women’s safety, lone working, transport, travel, construction, and other areas.
5. **Medical services:** Infosys has established OHCs at its campuses in India. Most OHCs operate around the clock based on business requirements. Ambulance services are available, or arrangements are made for ambulances, at all India-based locations.
6. **Health Risk Assessment (HRA):** HRA is conducted annually using inputs from OHCs. Prevalent risks for the IT/ITES business include ergonomics, musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs), emotional well-being, workplace conditions, utility operations, and commuting.
7. **Ergonomics programs:** Programs include on-site physiotherapy centers in India, ergonomic-expert interventions, ergonomic infrastructure, and periodic sessions on back care, repetitive stress injuries, and seating posture for employees globally.
8. **Physical and emotional well-being:** Nutritionists at Infosys locations in India provide counseling and guidance. Gyms are available to support physical and mental fitness.
9. **Programs for mothers:** Pregna Care is a healthcare program for expectant mothers in India.
10. **Working environment:** Infosys monitors indoor air quality, lux levels, and noise levels, and promotes the use of green seal chemicals.
