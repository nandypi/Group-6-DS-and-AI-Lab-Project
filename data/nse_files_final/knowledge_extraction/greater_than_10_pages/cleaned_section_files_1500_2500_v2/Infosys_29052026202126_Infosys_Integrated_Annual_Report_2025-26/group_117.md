## Information Other than the Financial Statements and Auditor's Report Thereon

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible for the preparation of the other information included in the Management Discussion and Analysis, Board's Report and its annexures, Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, Corporate Governance, and Shareholder's Information. This other information excludes the Consolidated Financial Statements, standalone financial statements, and the auditor's reports thereon.

The auditor's opinion on the Consolidated Financial Statements does not cover the other information. As part of the audit, the auditor read the other information and considered whether it was materially inconsistent with the Consolidated Financial Statements, audit knowledge, or otherwise materially misstated. The auditor reported that there was nothing to report.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Consolidated Financial Statements

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible under Section 134(5) of the Act for the preparation and presentation of Consolidated Financial Statements that give a true and fair view of the Group's consolidated financial position, financial performance including other comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows, in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in India and Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.

The respective Boards of Directors or Trustees of entities included in the Group are responsible for:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records.
- Safeguarding Group assets and preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining effective internal financial controls.
- Assessing the ability of the respective entities to continue as going concerns and making related disclosures.
- Overseeing the Group's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Consolidated Financial Statements

The auditor's objective is to obtain reasonable assurance that the Consolidated Financial Statements are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing the auditor's opinion. Reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but does not guarantee that every material misstatement will be detected.

The audit included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement and designing responsive audit procedures.
- Obtaining an understanding of internal financial controls relevant to the audit.
- Expressing an opinion under Section 143(3)(i) of the Act on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of internal financial controls relating to the Consolidated Financial Statements for the Company and its subsidiaries incorporated in India.
- Evaluating accounting policies, accounting estimates, and related disclosures.
- Assessing management's use of the going concern basis and whether any material uncertainty exists that could cast significant doubt on the Group's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the Consolidated Financial Statements.
- Obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group.

Materiality refers to the magnitude of misstatements that, individually or in aggregate, could reasonably be expected to influence the economic decisions of users of the Consolidated Financial Statements. The auditor considered quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating results, and assessing identified misstatements.

The auditor communicated with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, deficiencies in internal financial controls, auditor independence, and other relevant matters. Key audit matters were determined from matters communicated with those charged with governance.

## Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements

As required by Section 143(3) of the Act, the auditor reported that:

- All information and explanations considered necessary for the audit were sought and obtained.
- Proper books of account and relevant records required by law were maintained by the Group.
- The Consolidated Balance Sheet, Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss including Other Comprehensive Income, Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity, and Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows agreed with the relevant books of account.
- The Consolidated Financial Statements complied with the Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.
- Based on written representations received from directors as of March 31, 2026, and reports of statutory auditors of Indian subsidiaries, none of the directors of Group companies incorporated in India was disqualified as of March 31, 2026, under Section 164(2) of the Act.
- The auditor's separate report in Annexure A expressed an unmodified opinion on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of internal financial controls relating to the Consolidated Financial Statements of the Company and its Indian subsidiaries.
- Remuneration paid by the Company to its directors during the year complied with Section 197 of the Act.

With respect to Rule 11 of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014, as amended:

- The Consolidated Financial Statements disclosed the impact of pending litigations on the consolidated financial position of the Group. Refer to Note 2.24 to the Consolidated Financial Statements.
- The Group made provisions required under applicable law or accounting standards for material foreseeable losses. Refer to Note 2.16 to the Consolidated Financial Statements.
- The Group did not have any long-term derivative contracts.
- There was no delay in transferring amounts required to be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund by the Company and its Indian subsidiaries.
- Management represented that no funds had been advanced, loaned, or invested by the Company or its Indian subsidiaries through intermediaries, including foreign entities, with the understanding that such intermediaries would provide funds, guarantees, or securities to ultimate beneficiaries outside the Group.
- Management represented that no funds had been received by the Company or its Indian subsidiaries from funding parties, including foreign entities, with the understanding that the Company or its subsidiaries would provide funds, investments, guarantees, or securities to ultimate beneficiaries.
- Based on the audit procedures performed, nothing came to the auditor's attention indicating that these representations contained any material misstatement.
