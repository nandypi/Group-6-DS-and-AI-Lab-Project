## Corporate

Infosys was incorporated in Pune in 1981 as Infosys Consultants Private Limited under the Companies Act, 1956. Its corporate headquarters were relocated to Bengaluru in 1983.

The Company changed its name to Infosys Technologies Private Limited in April 1992 and to Infosys Technologies Limited in June 1992, when it became a public limited company. It completed its initial public offering in February 1993 and was listed on Indian stock exchanges in June 1993. Trading opened at ₹145 per share compared with the IPO price of ₹95 per share.

In October 1994, Infosys made a private placement of 5,50,000 shares at ₹450 per share to foreign institutional investors, financial institutions, and body corporates.

On March 11, 1999, Infosys listed on NASDAQ and issued 20,70,000 American Depositary Shares (ADSs), equivalent to 10,35,000 equity shares with a par value of ₹10 each, at US$34 per ADS.

The share data referenced in this section is unadjusted for stock splits and bonus shares. Infosys issued secondary-sponsored American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) of US$294 million in July 2003, US$1.1 billion in June 2005, and US$1.6 billion in November 2006.

During fiscal 2012, the Company changed its name from Infosys Technologies Limited to Infosys Limited.

During fiscal 2013, Infosys delisted its ADSs from NASDAQ and listed them on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), Euronext London, and Euronext Paris. During fiscal 2019, the Company voluntarily delisted from Euronext London and Euronext Paris due to low trading volume.

Infosys equity shares and ADSs are listed on the NSE and BSE in India and on the NYSE, respectively, under the symbol `INFY`.

## Bonus issues and stock split

The Company’s stock split in 2000 was in the ratio of 2:1. The section notes that one share held in 1986 before the bonus issues, if continuously held, would represent 16,384 shares after the bonus issues and stock split.

## Unclaimed dividend

Shares on which dividends have not been claimed or encashed for seven consecutive years or more will also be transferred to the IEPF. The Company states that it has not transferred or held any equity shares in a demat suspense account or unclaimed suspense account.
