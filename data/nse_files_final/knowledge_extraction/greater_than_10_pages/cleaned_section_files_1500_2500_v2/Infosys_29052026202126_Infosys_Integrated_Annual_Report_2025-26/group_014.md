## Capital Allocation Policy

Effective from financial year 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends and/or share buybacks and special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as per the Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows prepared under IFRS. Dividend and buyback amounts include applicable taxes.

In line with the Capital Allocation Policy, the Board approved a proposal on September 11, 2025, to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ` 5 each, aggregating up to ` 18,000 crore. Shareholders approved the proposal via postal ballot on November 4, 2025.

The buyback involved the purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company’s standalone paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, at ` 1,800 per share. The offer was made to eligible shareholders as of the November 14, 2025 record date on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route, including shareholders who became eligible through ADS cancellation.

The tender period was open from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The buyback was completed on December 4, 2025, with all shares extinguished. It resulted in a cash outflow of ` 18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs, funded from free reserves including securities premium, in accordance with Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

As required under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ` 50 crore as of March 31, 2026, equivalent to the nominal value of the shares bought back, by appropriating from general reserves.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company paid an interim dividend of ` 23 per equity share and announced a final dividend of ` 25 per share, subject to shareholders’ approval at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. Including the final dividend declared above, the Company returned approximately ` 55,523 crore, representing 82.1% of free cash flow for fiscal 2025 and fiscal 2026 through dividends and buybacks.

[The Capital Allocation Policy is available on the Company’s website.](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/capital-allocation-policy.pdf)

## Liquidity

The principal sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents, investments, and cash flow generated from operations. The Company states that it continues to be debt-free and maintains sufficient cash for strategic and operational requirements.

As of March 31, 2026, working capital was ` 40,794 crore on a standalone basis and ` 51,167 crore on a consolidated basis.

Cash and investments were ` 28,662 crore on a standalone basis and ` 43,075 crore on a consolidated basis as of March 31, 2026, compared with ` 35,916 crore and ` 47,549 crore, respectively, as of March 31, 2025.

Cash and investments include deposits with banks assigned high credit ratings by international and domestic credit-rating agencies. Liquid assets also include debt mutual fund units, tax-free bonds, Certificates of Deposit, Commercial Paper, quoted bonds and securities issued by government and quasi-government organizations, and non-convertible debentures.

The Company considers counterparty risk using criteria including Tier-I capital, capital adequacy ratio, credit rating, profitability, Non-Performing Asset levels, and deposit base of banks and financial institutions.

Details of these investments are disclosed under the “non-current and current investments” sections of the standalone and consolidated financial statements in the Integrated Annual Report.
