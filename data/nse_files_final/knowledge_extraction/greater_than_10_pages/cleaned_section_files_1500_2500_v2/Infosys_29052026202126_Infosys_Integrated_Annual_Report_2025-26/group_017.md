## Cybersecurity

Infosys employees operate in a hybrid model. The Information Security team monitors global cybersecurity events and supports compliance with cybersecurity requirements.

Infosys is certified against the Information Security Management System (ISMS) Standard ISO 27001:2022. It has also been attested for SSAE 18 and ISAE 3402 SOC 1 and SOC 2 by an independent audit firm.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys strengthened its cybersecurity posture through technology, tools, and processes. Key focus areas included:

- Continuous cybersecurity workforce training and reskilling.
- AI/ML security.
- Cyber resiliency.
- Secure supply chain ecosystems.
- Dedicated cybersecurity courses and resource kits for developers.

## Internal financial control and its adequacy

The Board has adopted policies and procedures covering:

- Orderly and efficient conduct of business.
- Adherence to the Company's policies.
- Safeguarding of assets.
- Prevention and detection of fraud.
- Error-reporting mechanisms.
- Accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Timely preparation of reliable financial disclosures.

Further details are provided in the “Internal control systems and their adequacy” section of the Management's Discussion and Analysis.

## Directors' responsibility statement

The financial statements are prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), the provisions of the applicable legislation, and guidelines issued by SEBI.

The financial statements are prepared under the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for:

- Certain financial instruments, which are measured at fair value.
- Defined benefit liabilities or assets, which are recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

Ind AS is prescribed under Section 133 of the Act, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and relevant amendment rules. Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except when a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing standard requires a change in accounting policy.

## Board's report

## 6. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

The Company undertakes CSR initiatives primarily through the Infosys Foundation. Its areas of intervention include:

- Education and skill development to enable sustained livelihoods.
- Healthcare, including preventive healthcare.
- Environmental sustainability and ecological balance.
- Gender equality and empowerment of women.
- Promotion of national heritage, art, and culture.
- Rural development.
- Disaster relief and rehabilitation.

Infosys also undertakes CSR initiatives outside India, including in the US, Australia, and various parts of Europe. Initiatives in the US are carried out through Infosys Foundation USA and are over and above the statutory requirement.

The Company's CSR Policy is available at [Infosys CSR Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/Documents/corporate-social-responsibility-policy.pdf). The annual report on CSR activities is appended as Annexure 6 to the Board's report.

## Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)

In October 2020, the Company launched its ESG Vision 2030.

Its environmental focus includes:

- Climate change.
- Water management.
- Waste management.

Its social focus includes:

- Digital skilling.
- Diversity and inclusion.
- Employee wellness and experience.
- Technology for good.
- Community development.

The Company also focuses on stakeholder interests, core values, and corporate governance.

The ESG Committee was constituted on April 14, 2021, to oversee organization-wide ESG initiatives, priorities, and leading ESG practices. The Committee reports to the Board and meets every quarter to review progress against the ambitions in the ESG Vision 2030.
