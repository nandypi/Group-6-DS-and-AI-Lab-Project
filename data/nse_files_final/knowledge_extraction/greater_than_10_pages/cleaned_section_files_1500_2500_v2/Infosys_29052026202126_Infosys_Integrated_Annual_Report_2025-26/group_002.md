## Mondelēz

### Mondelēz International establishes an end-to-end traceability reporting capability with EdgeVerve AI Next

#### Enterprise-Wide Traceability at Scale

Mondelēz International operates a global snack supply chain spanning thousands of suppliers, hundreds of manufacturing plants, and a global distribution network. Fragmented legacy systems and siloed data created challenges in food safety, regulatory compliance, and product traceability.

Infosys collaborated with Mondelēz teams across quality, manufacturing, distribution, customer service, and IT to identify vulnerabilities across:

- Production batch and quality management
- Product bills of material
- Conversion of raw materials to finished goods
- Finished-goods product receival
- Palletizing and labeling
- Shipping and downstream data management

These requirements were refined into Mondelēz International’s fit-to-standard Traceability Platform requirements.

Infosys deployed EdgeVerve AI Next Product Traceability, a cloud-based platform that integrates near-real-time and batch data through AI Next Fabric. A graph data store provides visibility from product origin through delivery. A centralized traceability portal supports top-down and bottom-up tracking workflows, while a regulatory control tower supports incident response.

The platform has been implemented in North America, with Europe planned to begin rollout in the second half of 2026. It:

- Connects more than 50 suppliers
- Captures more than 0.5 million events per day
- Integrates more than 220 plants and distribution centers
- Maps more than 150 raw-material-to-finished-goods processes

Prebuilt FSMA compliance reports and one-touch batch and handling-unit traceability support regulatory audits and food safety compliance.

## Microsoft

### Microsoft cut root cause analysis turnaround by 40% through Infosys’ AI-led mission-critical support

#### Faster Resolutions with Agentic AI

Microsoft’s Support for Mission-critical (SfMC) service provides high-reliability support for enterprise customers, with a focus on minimizing downtime and resolving incidents quickly.

Infosys partnered with Microsoft to add AI capabilities to the SfMC support architecture. Using the Microsoft AI technology stack, Infosys deployed AI and agentic AI solutions including:

- **Routing and Entitlement Validation Agent:** Triages and routes critical cases to technically aligned engineers.
- **Azure Resiliency Correlation Engine:** Provides automated recommendations to accelerate root cause analysis.
- **Notes Observation and Validation Agent:** Conducts continuous quality audits across global operations.

The transformation produced the following results:

- Critical incident response times improved by 33%, reducing engagement time from 15 minutes to less than 10 minutes.
- Root cause analysis preparation time decreased by 40%, from five days to one day.
- More than 90% of critical cases received top boxed ratings.
- Low case-handling scores were virtually eliminated.

## ABN AMRO

### ABN AMRO collaborates with Infosys to modernize its enterprise content operations

#### Unlocking €64 Million in Annual Efficiency Savings

ABN AMRO is a Netherlands-based bank serving approximately 5 million retail customers and 365,000 business clients. Its enterprise content management ecosystem contains billions of documents, including scanned inputs, emails, and archived records.

Simple document cases could be partially automated, but complex retrieval often required manual processing lasting several minutes per file. Documents also had to be downloaded from the ECM archive, making compliance enforcement difficult as regulatory pressure and data volumes increased.

ABN AMRO partnered with Infosys to modernize its enterprise content operations. The engagement included:

1. An assessment of the ECM landscape.
2. A proof of concept based on the Infosys Document Intelligence platform.
3. Implementation of a generative AI-powered ECM AI layer using retrieval-augmented generation.
4. Integration with Microsoft Azure AI and data services.

The cloud-native platform enabled automated classification, contextual information extraction, and faster retrieval of complex documents without downloads. ABN AMRO estimated annual efficiency savings of €64 million. The transformation also reduced manual effort, improved employee productivity, strengthened compliance, and established a foundation for expanding intelligent document processing across additional workflows.
