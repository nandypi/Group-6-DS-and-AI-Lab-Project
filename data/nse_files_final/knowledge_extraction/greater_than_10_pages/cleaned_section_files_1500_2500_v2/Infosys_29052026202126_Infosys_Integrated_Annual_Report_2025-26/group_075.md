## 5. Cybersecurity and data privacy framework

Yes. Infosys has a cybersecurity framework called SEED, aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), and supported by supplementary policies, processes, procedures, and standards.

Infosys has a Data Privacy Policy published on the Company's intranet. The policy applies across Infosys operations, including activities involving service providers. Infosys provides privacy notices at the point of data collection for internal and external data subjects.

## 6. Corrective actions relating to cybersecurity and data privacy

Infosys has reviewed its cybersecurity posture, technology, and threat landscape to further strengthen its cyber defense capabilities. The Company continues to review and strengthen cybersecurity processes and controls across its network in line with industry best practices.

### Consumer satisfaction survey

Yes. Infosys conducts surveys to gauge customer satisfaction for its major services. The Company interacts with clients regularly across multiple platforms and conducts an annual Client Value Survey and periodic engagement surveys.

The surveys use a structured questionnaire, with feedback collected through a web survey hosted by an independent organization. Survey results help Infosys understand client expectations and needs and serve as an input when making investment decisions.

## CEO and CFO certification

The CEO and Managing Director, Salil Parekh, and the Chief Financial Officer, Jayesh Sanghrajka, certified the following for the year ended March 31, 2026:

1. They reviewed the Balance Sheet as at March 31, 2026; the Statement of Profit and Loss; the Statement of Changes in Equity; the Statement of Cash Flows; the significant accounting policies and explanatory information; and the Board's report for the year ended March 31, 2026.
2. The statements do not contain any materially untrue statement or omit any material fact necessary to ensure that the statements are not misleading for the period covered.
3. The financial statements and other financial information present, in all material respects, a true and fair view of the Company's affairs, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows, and comply with applicable accounting standards, laws, and regulations.
4. No transactions were entered into during the year that were fraudulent, illegal, or violated the Company's Code of Conduct and Ethics, except as disclosed to the auditors and the Audit Committee.
5. Management is responsible for establishing and maintaining disclosure controls and internal control over financial reporting. Management:
   - Designed disclosure controls to ensure that material information relating to the Company and its consolidated subsidiaries was made known during preparation of the report.
   - Designed internal control over financial reporting to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and preparation of financial statements for external purposes in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS).
   - Evaluated the effectiveness of the Company's disclosure controls and procedures.
   - Disclosed changes in internal control over financial reporting that materially affected, or were reasonably likely to materially affect, those controls.
6. Based on the most recent evaluation of internal control over financial reporting, management disclosed to the auditors and the Audit Committee, wherever applicable:
   - Deficiencies in the design or operation of internal controls that could adversely affect financial reporting, and confirmation that there were no material weaknesses, including related corrective actions.
   - Significant changes in internal controls during the year.
   - Significant changes in accounting policies, if any, which were disclosed in the notes to the financial statements.
   - Significant fraud involving management or employees with a significant role in the internal control system over financial reporting.
7. No personnel were denied access to the Audit Committee regarding alleged misconduct, and whistleblowers were protected from unfair termination and other unfair or prejudicial employment practices.
8. All Board members and senior management personnel affirmed compliance with the Code of Conduct and Ethics for the year covered by the report.
