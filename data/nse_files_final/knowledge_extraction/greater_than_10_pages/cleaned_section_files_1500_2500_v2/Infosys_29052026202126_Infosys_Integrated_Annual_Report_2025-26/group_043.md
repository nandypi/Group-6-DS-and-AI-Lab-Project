## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objectives and responsibilities of the Committee are to:

1. Assist the Board in fulfilling its oversight responsibilities regarding the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of strategic, operational, and external environment risks.
2. Monitor and approve the enterprise risk management framework and associated practices of the Company.
3. Periodically assess risks to the effective execution of business strategy and review key leading indicators.
4. Periodically review the Company's risk management processes and practices and ensure appropriate measures are taken to achieve a prudent balance between risk and reward in ongoing and new business activities.
5. Evaluate significant risk exposures and assess Management's timely mitigation actions.
6. Evaluate cybersecurity-related risks and ensure appropriate procedures are in place to mitigate them.
7. Coordinate activities with the Audit Committee where there is overlap with audit activities.
8. Periodically review and reassess the adequacy of the Risk Management Committee Charter and recommend proposed changes to the Board for approval.
9. Ensure access to internal information necessary to fulfil its oversight role and obtain advice and assistance from internal or external legal, accounting, or other advisors.
10. Appoint, remove, and approve the remuneration terms of the Chief Risk Officer.
