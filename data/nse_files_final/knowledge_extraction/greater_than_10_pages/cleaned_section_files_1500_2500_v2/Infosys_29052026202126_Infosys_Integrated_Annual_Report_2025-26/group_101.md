## a. Post-sales client support

The Company provides fixed-period post-sales support on fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts. Costs associated with this support are accrued when the related revenue is recognized and included in the Statement of Profit and Loss. The Company estimates these costs based on historical experience and periodically reviews the estimates for material changes in assumptions and likelihood of occurrence.

## b. Onerous contracts

Provisions for onerous contracts are recognized when the expected benefits from a contract are lower than the unavoidable costs of meeting future obligations. Estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable, based on estimated efforts or costs to complete the contract.

The provision is measured at the present value of the lower of:

- the expected cost of terminating the contract; and
- the expected net cost of continuing with the contract.

Before establishing a provision, the Company recognizes any impairment loss on assets associated with the contract.

## 2.17 Income taxes

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss, except when it relates to items recognized directly in equity, in which case it is recognized in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced to the extent that it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date and expected to apply to taxable income in the years when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized as income or expense in the period that includes the enactment or substantive enactment date.

A deferred income tax asset is recognized to the extent that future taxable profit is probable and available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Company offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to set off the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

Interim-period income tax provisions are based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions earned on the exercise of employee share options, in excess of compensation charged to income, are credited to equity.

The applicable Indian corporate statutory tax rate was 25.17% for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

Income tax expense for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 included a reversal, net of provisions, of ₹779 crore and provisions, net of reversals, of ₹97 crore, respectively. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily related to adjudication of disputed matters following tax-return filings and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment year 2013–14 and assessment years 2017–18 to 2021–22. The orders confirmed the Company’s position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently:

- pre-tax interest income of ₹381 crore was recognized;
- income tax provisions aggregating ₹869 crore were reversed with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss; and
- contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹86 crore following resolution of the disputes.

During the quarter ended March 31, 2025, the Company received orders under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment years 2016–17 and 2019–20. The orders confirmed the Company’s position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently:

- pre-tax interest income of ₹327 crore was recognized;
- income tax provisions aggregating ₹183 crore were reversed with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss; and
- contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹1,068 crore following resolution of the disputes.

Foreign tax expense relates to income taxes payable overseas, principally in the United States.

In India, the Company benefited in prior years from income tax incentives for software and services exports from units registered under the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005. SEZ units that began providing services on or after April 1, 2005, were eligible for:

- a deduction of 100% of profits or gains from export of services for the first five years from the financial year in which the unit began providing services;
- a deduction of 50% for the following five years; and
- a further deduction of up to 50% for an additional five years, subject to creating a Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve and using the reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Deferred income tax for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially related to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

Infosys is subject to a 15% Branch Profit Tax (BPT) in the United States when the U.S. branch’s net profit exceeds the increase in the branch’s net assets during the year, calculated under the Internal Revenue Code. As at March 31, 2026, the U.S. branch’s net assets were approximately ₹7,736 crore. The Company recognized a deferred tax liability for branch profit tax of ₹207 crore, net of credits, because it estimates that these branch profits will be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches amounting to ₹19,270 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹16,593 crore as at March 31, 2025. The Company can control the timing of reversal of these temporary differences and considers it probable that they will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The Company mainly intends to repatriate earnings from subsidiaries and branches only to the extent that they can be distributed in a tax-free manner.

Deferred income tax assets were not recognized on accumulated losses of ₹1,310 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹1,466 crore as at March 31, 2025, because future taxable profit is not considered probable in the foreseeable future. Most of the accumulated losses as at March 31, 2026 are expected to expire between financial years 2028 and 2033.
