## Fairness and excellence

The Board evaluation and director compensation processes are described below.

## Board member evaluation

The Board monitors and reviews the Board evaluation framework. In consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, it establishes evaluation criteria for the Chairman, the Board, Board committees, and executive, non-executive, and independent directors through peer evaluation, excluding the director being evaluated.

Independent directors are evaluated on:

- Contribution to and monitoring of corporate governance practices.
- Contribution through the introduction of international best practices to address business challenges and risks.
- Participation in long-term strategic planning.
- Commitment to director obligations and fiduciary responsibilities, including participation in Board and committee meetings.
- Participation in a formal annual Board review to improve the effectiveness of the Board, its committees, and individual directors.

The Board engaged Egon Zehnder, a leadership advisory firm, to conduct the Board evaluation for fiscal 2026. The process considered Board dynamics, softer aspects, committee effectiveness, information flow to the Board and its committees, succession planning, and committee composition. It used questionnaires and one-on-one discussions.

The fiscal 2026 evaluation was completed during the year. The Board discussed recommendations from the prior year, provided individual feedback, and reviewed progress on prior recommendations and recommendations arising from the current evaluation.

The evaluation process was also based on confirmation from the independent directors that they met the independence criteria under the Companies Act, the Listing Regulations, and the NYSE listing manual.

## Board and executive leadership compensation

### Executive leadership compensation

Executive compensation programs link a significant portion of total executive rewards to long-term corporate performance goals and shareholder interests.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommends remuneration for directors, key managerial personnel (KMP), and senior management to the Board, as required under the Listing Regulations. The Company maintains a Nomination and Remuneration Policy.

### Non-executive and non-independent chairman's compensation

Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman, voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company.

### Independent directors' compensation

Compensation payable to independent directors is limited to a fixed amount per year determined and approved by the Board. The total does not exceed 1% of net profit for the year, calculated under the Companies Act, 2013. The Board reviews independent director performance annually.

The Board considers global board compensation benchmarks, individual participation in Board and committee meetings, committee membership or chairmanship, time spent on other duties, and roles and functions prescribed under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, the Listing Regulations, and other factors it considers appropriate.

Shareholders at the 34th AGM held on June 22, 2015, approved payment of up to 1% of the Company's annual net profit, calculated under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013, to some or all non-executive directors, in a manner determined by the Board. The payment applies to the Company's profits for each year.

## Corporate governance report

The amount payable to independent directors for the year ended March 31, 2026 was ₹19.91 crore. Independent directors were also reimbursed for expenses incurred in performing their official duties. None of the non-executive directors received remuneration amounting to 50% of the total remuneration paid to non-executive directors during the year ended March 31, 2026.

## Indemnification agreements

The Company has entered into agreements to indemnify its directors and officers for claims brought against them to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law. The agreements cover certain expenses, judgments, fines, settlement amounts, and public relations consultation expenses incurred in actions or proceedings arising from their services as directors or officers, including actions brought by or in the right of the Company.

## Agreements binding listed entities

Pursuant to Regulation 30A of the Listing Regulations, no agreement was entered into or executed during fiscal 2026 by the Company's shareholders, promoters, promoter group entities, related parties, directors, key managerial personnel, or employees, or by those of its subsidiaries.
