## Outstanding ADSs / GDRs / warrants or any convertible instruments

Infosys’s ADSs, evidenced by ADRs, are traded on the NYSE under the ticker symbol `INFY` in US dollars. Each ADS represents one equity share. ADRs evidencing ADSs began trading on the NYSE on December 12, 2012.

As of March 31, 2026, there were 1,34,734 record holders of ADRs evidencing 30,50,54,064 ADSs. Infosys had no outstanding GDRs, warrants, or convertible instruments as of that date.

## Registered office and global locations

Infosys’s operations are spread across 290 locations in 59 countries. The Company does not have manufacturing plants, but has development centers and offices in India and overseas.
