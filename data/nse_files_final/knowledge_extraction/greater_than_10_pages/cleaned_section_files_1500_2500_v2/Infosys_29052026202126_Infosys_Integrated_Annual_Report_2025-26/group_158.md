## Notice of the 45 th Annual General Meeting

Notice is given that the 45 th Annual General Meeting of the members of Infosys Limited will be held on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. IST through video conference or other audio-visual means.

The Company will provide electronic voting facilities for all resolutions set forth in the Notice, in accordance with Section 108 of the Companies Act, 2013, the related rules, and Regulation 44 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

## Ordinary business

## Item no. 1 - Adoption of financial statements

To consider and adopt the audited financial statements, including the consolidated financial statements, of the Company for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, together with the reports of the Board of Directors and auditors.

## Item no. 2 - Declaration of dividend

To declare a final dividend of ₹25/- per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

## Item no. 3 - Appointment of Nandan M. Nilekani as a director, liable to retire by rotation

To appoint Nandan M. Nilekani (DIN: 00041245) as a director in place of himself. He retires by rotation, is eligible for reappointment, and has sought reappointment.

Based on the terms of appointment, executive directors and the non-executive, non-independent chairman are subject to retirement by rotation. Nandan M. Nilekani was initially appointed on August 24, 2017, and was last appointed on June 26, 2024. Based on his performance evaluation and the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board recommends his reappointment.

The proposed ordinary resolution seeks approval under Section 152 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, to reappoint Nandan M. Nilekani as a director liable to retire by rotation.

## Special business

## Item no. 4 - Approval of the proposed amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 ('Amended 2019 Plan') and grant of stock incentives to the eligible employees of the Company under the Amended 2019 Plan

Shareholder approval is sought as a Special Resolution to amend the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 and to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives under the Amended 2019 Plan, subject to applicable laws and required approvals.

The Board, including the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and other authorized committees, would be authorized to grant stock incentives to eligible employees at such prices, in one or more tranches, and on such terms and conditions as determined under the Amended 2019 Plan.

Eligible recipients may include persons in the permanent employment of the Company working in India or outside India, directors of the Company, and other persons permitted to receive benefits under applicable laws. Promoters and members of the promoter group, independent directors, and directors who, either directly or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company’s outstanding equity shares are excluded.

The proposed amendments and modifications include:

- Amend Section 13 to extend the period during which awards may be granted to eligible employees by a further seven years from the date of shareholder approval. The Amended 2019 Plan would consequently remain in effect for a further period of 10 years from the date of shareholder approval.
- Amend Section 6(c) concerning vesting based on performance parameters.
- Amend Section 3 to reflect the unutilized share pool available for grant.
- Make editorial and clarificatory changes and align the plan with the amended SEBI Regulations.
