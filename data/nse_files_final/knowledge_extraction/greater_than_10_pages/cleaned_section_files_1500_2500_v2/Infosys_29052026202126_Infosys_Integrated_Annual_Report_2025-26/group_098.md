## 2.12.4 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) Accounting policy

The Company recognizes compensation expense relating to share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, as if the award were, in substance, multiple awards, with a corresponding increase to the share options outstanding account.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan):

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan shall not exceed 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The parameters are based on a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics. Each parameter is distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. These instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan):

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan shall not exceed 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as at March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 86,50,911 shares as at March 31, 2026 and 96,55,927 shares as at March 31, 2025 under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 2,00,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities at each reporting date.

## CEO & MD Under the 2015 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants were effective May 2, 2025:

- 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ` 34.75 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ` 2 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ` 5 crore. These RSUs will vest based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employee agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ` 3 crore. The grant vests over three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the respective grant date. Accordingly, an annual time-based grant of 18,132 RSUs was made effective February 1, 2026, for fiscal 2026.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as at March 31, 2026. Since the service commencement date precedes the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.
