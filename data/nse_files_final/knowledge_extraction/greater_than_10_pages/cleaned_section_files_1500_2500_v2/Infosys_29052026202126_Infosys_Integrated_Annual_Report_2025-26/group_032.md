## 8. Income tax assets / liabilities

Net profit earned from providing software development and other services outside India is subject to tax in the country where the work is performed. Most taxes paid in countries other than India can be claimed as credits against tax liabilities in India. Refer to Note 2.17 of the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements.

## 9. Other financial liabilities

- Accrued compensation to employees includes provisions for bonuses, accrued salaries, incentives, and retention bonuses payable to staff.
- Financial liabilities under option arrangements represent redemption liabilities related to acquisitions to purchase the corresponding minority stake.
- Accrued expenses represent amounts accrued for other operational expenses.
- Compensated absences are both accumulating and non-accumulating. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by actuarial valuation.

## IV. Results of our operations

For details about results of operations, refer to the “Results of our operations and state of affairs” section in the Boards' report.

During fiscal 2026, the Company continued Project Maximus, a margin expansion program structured across five pillars:

1. Value-based selling
2. Efficient pyramid
3. Lean, automation, and generative AI
4. Improving key portfolios
5. Optimization of indirect costs

Value-based selling focuses on identifying opportunities to align pricing with the value clients receive from services. Efficient pyramid initiatives seek to reduce effort costs through improved utilization, reduced subcontractor usage, and changes to role mix and location mix. Lean, automation, and generative AI combine productivity and process improvement using automation technologies. Improving key portfolios focuses on margins on large client engagements and service lines. Savings from the optimization of indirect costs were used to invest in talent, AI, and sales and marketing.

### 1. Revenue

The growth in revenue from fiscal 2025 to fiscal 2026 was as follows:

| Particulars | Standalone 2026 | Standalone 2025 | Standalone % change | Consolidated 2026 | Consolidated 2025 | Consolidated % change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 1,48,819 | 1,36,592 | 9.0% | 1,78,650 | 1,62,990 | 9.6% |

The increase in revenue was primarily attributable to increased realization due to role mix, improved pricing and productivity, acquisitions, and currency benefit.

Revenue growth in reported terms includes the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant-currency growth represents revenue growth excluding the impact of currency fluctuations. Constant-currency growth is calculated by comparing current-period revenues in respective local currencies converted to USD using prior-period exchange rates with prior-period reported revenues.

Consolidated revenue in reported currency terms was ₹1,78,650 crore in fiscal 2026, representing growth of 9.6%. Consolidated revenue in constant-currency terms grew by 3.1%.

Infosys added 443 new customers on a gross basis during fiscal 2026, compared with 365 during fiscal 2025.

## 10. Other liabilities

- Invoicing in excess of revenues is classified as unearned revenue.
- Withholding and other taxes payable represent local taxes payable in various countries where the Company operates.
- Infosys provides a provident fund for eligible employees. This is treated as a defined benefit plan because the Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return from trust investments and the notified interest rate.
- The Company operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers.
- Infosys provides gratuity, a defined benefit retirement plan, for eligible employees in India. The Gratuity Plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment. The amount is based on the employee's salary and tenure of employment.
- The plans provide periodic payouts after retirement and/or lump-sum payments according to the rules of each fund, including death and disability benefits.

## 11. Provisions

Provision for post-sales client support covers the expected cost of providing client support under fixed-price and fixed-timeframe contracts.

## 12. Leases

Lease additions mainly comprise computers and buildings taken on lease at certain locations outside India.

On a consolidated basis, for the year ended March 31, 2026, approximately 97.1% of revenue was from exports and 2.9% was from domestic operations.
