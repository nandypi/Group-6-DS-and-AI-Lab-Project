## Note

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends distributed outside India are subject to applicable Indian foreign-exchange laws and withholding tax requirements.

- Payout ratio is computed as a percentage of free cash flow prepared under IFRS.
- Buyback transaction costs of ₹58 crore are excluded.
- The final dividend was recommended by the Board of Directors on April 23, 2026. Payment is subject to shareholder approval at the AGM scheduled for June 23, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026, and the scheduled payment date is June 25, 2026.
- The current Capital Allocation Policy is to pay approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semiannual dividends, share buybacks, and special dividends, subject to applicable laws and approvals. Including buybacks, the Company returned 82.1% of cumulative free cash flow for the years ended March 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026.

## 2. Business description

## Strategy

Infosys' strategy is to help clients navigate their AI journeys and unlock business value. The Company helps clients ideate, plan, and scale their AI initiatives by building data foundations and cloud capabilities. It also promotes Responsible AI practices addressing privacy, ethics, and controls.

## Organization

Infosys' go-to-market business units and solutions are detailed in the Operating context section of this Integrated Annual Report.

## Infrastructure

During fiscal 2026, Infosys increased its physical infrastructure space by a net 1.87 million sq. ft. Total available space stood at 58.20 million sq. ft. as of March 31, 2026. Infosys had a presence in 59 countries across 290 locations as of that date.

## Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)

Infosys' M&A approach is intended to strengthen capabilities, deepen industry expertise, and expand its geographical footprint.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership or voting interests in:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — an energy and business consulting services company headquartered in Texas, U.S.; acquired on April 30, 2025.
2. **The Missing Link Group** — a cybersecurity services provider headquartered in Australia; acquired on April 30, 2025.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group entered into definitive agreements to:

1. Form a joint venture with Telstra and acquire a 75% stake in the Versent Group, an Australian digital transformation solutions provider and wholly owned subsidiary of the Telstra Group, providing cloud and digital transformation services.
2. Acquire Stratus Global LLC, a U.S.-based insurance consulting and technology services company focused on Guidewire services. The acquisition was completed as of the date of the report.
3. Acquire Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc., including its subsidiaries and Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC, a U.S.-based healthcare digital transformation and consulting company.

The Versent Group comprises Telstra Purple Pty Ltd and the integrated operations of Versent, Epicon, Telstra Purple Digital, and associated Cloud Access products. Telstra Purple Digital is a carve-out from Telstra's Enterprise Tech Services/Telstra Purple portfolio and excludes Alliance Automation Pty Ltd, Aqura Technologies Pty Ltd, and Telstra Broadcast Services Pty Limited.

## 3. Human resources management

Infosys' people strategy focuses on attracting and developing talent, building internal leadership pipelines, and supporting employee experience through policies, systems, and programs. Its workforce practices include continuous learning, diversity, equity and inclusion, flexible work models, and technology-led enablement.

The Company has structured employee grievance redressal through Resolution Hubs. The framework emphasizes natural justice, confidentiality, non-retaliation, and timely resolution. Internal Committees address the prevention and redressal of sexual harassment.

Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report. No complaint cases were pending for more than 90 days.

Other disclosures and information are set out in the Management's discussion and analysis section.
