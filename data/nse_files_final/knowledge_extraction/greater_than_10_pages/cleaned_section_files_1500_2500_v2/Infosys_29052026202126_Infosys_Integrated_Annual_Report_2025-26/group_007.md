## Approaching value creation

## Our business context

Technology continues to reshape businesses across industries worldwide. AI is increasingly being applied to domain-specific capabilities and industry-focused use cases. Clients are addressing the need to reinvent processes and systems in the AI era through emerging technologies, new ways of working, AI ecosystems, business processes, and operational practices.

Infosys supports clients in transforming their businesses through technology, including building a digital core and reimagining business operations to improve agility, efficiency, and resilience and to support growth initiatives. During the year, organizations focused on reconfiguring cost structures, improving resilience and agility, delivering personalized customer and employee experiences, and launching new products and services. Responsible business practices, including ESG adoption, continued to gain momentum.

Businesses are using AI to:

- Enhance and personalize customer interactions.
- Improve marketing and sales efficiency.
- Strengthen code quality.
- Improve workforce and organizational productivity.

The emergence of AI and broader automation is expected to accelerate workforce transformation. The traditional services market remains competitive because of rapid change and the entry of specialized technology providers.

Infosys identifies its industry expertise, end-to-end service capabilities, scalable digital solutions, established platforms, quality and process excellence, distributed agile global delivery model, experienced leadership team, skilled workforce, and execution track record as key differentiators.
