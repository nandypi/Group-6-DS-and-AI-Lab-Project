## Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee

The Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee comprises only independent directors. As on March 31, 2026, it comprised:

1. Michael Gibbs, Chairperson and cybersecurity expert

## Committee governance

The Risk Management Committee constituted the Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee in April 2019. The Sub-committee was voluntarily constituted to focus on cybersecurity-related threats.

Its objective is to assess cybersecurity-related risks and the preparedness of the Company to mitigate and react to those risks. The Sub-committee meets periodically and recommends its findings, if any, to the Risk Management Committee.

The Sub-committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## Stakeholders Relationship Committee

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee comprises only independent directors. As on March 31, 2026, it comprised:

1. Michael Gibbs, Chairperson
2. D. Sundaram
3. Bobby Parikh
4. Chitra Nayak

The Board appointed A.G.S. Manikantha, Company Secretary, as the Compliance Officer required under the Listing Regulations and as the Nodal Officer responsible for ensuring compliance with the IEPF rules.

## Purpose of the Committee

The Committee assists the Board and the Company in overseeing matters concerning the interests of stakeholders, including shareholders and other security holders.

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objectives of the Committee are to:

1. Consider and resolve security holders' concerns or complaints.
2. Monitor and review the Company's investor service standards.
3. Develop an understanding of shareholders' views through direct interaction, analysts' briefings, or shareholder surveys.
4. Oversee and review the shareholder engagement and communication plan, ensure that shareholders' views and concerns are appropriately highlighted to the Board, and monitor actions taken to address those concerns.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises four independent directors and performs functions required by:

- Section 178 of the Companies Act, 2013, and rules framed thereunder
- Regulation 20 of the Listing Regulations and other applicable regulations and laws
- NYSE guidelines, as applicable
- The Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter
