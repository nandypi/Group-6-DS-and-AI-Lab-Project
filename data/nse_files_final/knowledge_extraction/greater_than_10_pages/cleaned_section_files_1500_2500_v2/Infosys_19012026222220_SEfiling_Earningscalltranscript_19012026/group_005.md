1. On a 9-month basis, RPP increased, reflecting continued momentum of value-based selling and productivity increases.

2. Adjusted operating margins increased by 20 basis points sequentially to 21.2%.

3. Sales and marketing investments increased by double digits year to date, impacting margins by approximately 50 basis points.

4. Utilization, excluding trainees, declined by 1% sequentially to 84.1%. Including trainees, utilization declined by 2.2% to 80% as the company continued to create capacity for future growth opportunities.

5. Adjusted margins for the first nine months were at 21%, at the midpoint of guidance, after absorbing accelerated investment in sales and marketing and lower utilization.

6. On-site mix reduced by 10 basis points in Q3 and by 70 basis points over the first nine months of FY'26.

7. Net headcount increased by 5,000 to 337,000 employees. LTM attrition declined by 2% sequentially and by 1.4% year over year, reflecting market conditions and the company's focus on employee retention and upskilling.

8. Large deal TCV was $4.8 billion in Q3, with 57% net new. Total large deal TCV for the first nine months was $11.7 billion, exceeding full-year FY'25 large deal TCV. Net new deal TCV for the first nine months increased by 40% compared with the same period last year. The large deal pipeline remained healthy.

9. Deployment of AI agents in the order-to-receivable cycle contributed to a sequential decline of 5 days in DSO, including net unbilled, to 82 days.

10. Adjusted free cash flow, adjusted for labor codes, was $965 million, or 113% of adjusted net profit. Adjusted free cash flow conversion for the first nine months was 118%.

11. Adjusted EPS in rupee terms grew by 11.5% for the first nine months of FY'26.

12. During the quarter, Infosys completed its largest-ever buyback, returning INR 18,000 crores to shareholders. The company also paid an interim dividend for FY'26 in line with its capital allocation policy.
