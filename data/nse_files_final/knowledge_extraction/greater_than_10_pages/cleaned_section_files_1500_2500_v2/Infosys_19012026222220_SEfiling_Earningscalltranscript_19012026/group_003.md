## Large Deals and Margins

Infosys signed large deals worth **$4.8 billion during the quarter**. Large-deal signings have also increased on a nine-month basis compared with the prior year.

Large deals generally carry slightly lower margins than the company average. Management said the portfolio can make up for this through new work that comes in at better margins. Infosys has not observed a change in this trend.

AI-related productivity requirements are increasing, while clients are also structuring deals around cost optimization and cost takeout. These elements are increasingly bundled into large deals.

## Manufacturing, Europe, and Discretionary Spending

Europe has been performing well for Infosys for several quarters. Manufacturing has also seen broad-based activity and good traction.

Within Manufacturing, demand is benefiting from AI-related growth among companies providing power solutions, engine capacity, and generating capacity. Infosys is also seeing traction in manufacturing engineering services.

In Financial Services, Infosys is seeing a good set of deals and expects AI traction in the industry to support better outcomes in the next financial year than in the current financial year. Financial Services is performing well in the current year.

Energy and Utilities is also seeing a good set of deals across the industry vertical. Infosys said it was not seeing deterioration in other industries.

The macroeconomic environment includes expectations of possible interest-rate cuts, particularly in the United States. Infosys is also expanding programs serving smaller sets of clients, which are growing well. Management identified these factors, along with activity across six AI-related areas, as support for growth over the next year and beyond.

## Cognition Partnership and AI Software Agents

Infosys is working with Cognition, an AI startup whose software agent is designed for software development. Infosys is partnering with Cognition to build agent capacity and enable the agents to operate in client environments.

Infosys contributes an understanding of clients’ technology landscapes and industry constraints and opportunities. Management said combining this expertise with Cognition’s software agent can be valuable for clients.

Management expects the use of AI software engineers and agents to extend across essentially every industry and client over time, depending on each client’s technology landscape and objectives.

In legacy modernization, combining software agents with Infosys’s expertise and client knowledge can improve project economics. This may enable projects that were previously not undertaken, in addition to changing how existing work is performed.

The value created by AI-enabled delivery will determine billing. Some work will continue to use traditional billing models, while billing models may evolve as the AI market develops. Management said there is no immediate change to billing.

## Hiring and Specialized AI Talent

Infosys expects to continue hiring for new areas of AI-enabled demand. The company had completed **18,000 campus hires** to date during the current year and planned to make **20,000 campus hires**. Management said it expected to continue at a similar level in the next year.

Infosys has launched a structure for highly skilled software engineers working in AI. These specialized engineers will receive higher or substantially higher compensation levels.

The company expects different types of employees, with different levels of training, to work jointly with AI agents. Infosys said it intends to remain competitive in recruiting specialized AI talent.

## AI Acquisitions and Partnerships

Infosys does not currently see many AI services companies available for acquisition. It is partnering with companies that build AI agents, models, foundational tools, coding capabilities, customer-service solutions, and related technologies.

Infosys expects to consider acquisitions as larger AI services companies emerge. The company is also evaluating acquisitions that may include AI-related capabilities alongside other areas.

## AI-First GCC Model and Partnerships

Infosys has launched an AI-specific approach for global capability centers (GCCs). The company has active work with some clients and several other clients in the pipeline for large-scale, AI-specific capability building in GCCs.

This activity is in addition to Infosys’s regular GCC work, which management said was progressing well.

Infosys expects to continue forming partnerships with smaller companies that have strong capabilities in foundation models, coding, agent development, and customer service. These are areas in which clients are showing significant interest. The company expects both continued collaboration and strategic partnership announcements.
