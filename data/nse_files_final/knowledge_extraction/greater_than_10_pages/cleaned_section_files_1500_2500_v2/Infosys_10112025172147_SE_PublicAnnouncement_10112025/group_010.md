## PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

### For the attention of equity shareholders and beneficial owners of equity shares of Infosys Limited

Infosys Limited proposes to buy back up to **10,00,00,000 (10 crore) fully paid-up equity shares** of face value **₹5 each**, representing **2.41%** of the total paid-up equity share capital of the Company on a standalone basis.

The buyback will be made from all eligible shareholders as of **November 14, 2025**, the record date, on a proportionate basis, subject to the small shareholder reservation. It will be conducted through the tender offer route using the stock exchange mechanism at a price of **₹1,800 per equity share**, payable in cash.

The aggregate consideration for the buyback will not exceed **₹18,000 crore**.

This Public Announcement is made pursuant to Regulation 7(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended, and contains the disclosures specified in Schedule II read with Schedule I of the Buyback Regulations.

All references to rupees, ₹, or Rs. refer to Indian rupees. One crore represents 10 million, or ₹10,000,000. Certain figures may not add exactly because of rounding adjustments.

## 1. DETAILS OF THE BUYBACK OFFER AND BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 amended the tax treatment of share buybacks with effect from October 1, 2024. Buyback consideration is treated as dividend in the hands of shareholders, and the Company is required to deduct tax at source. No deduction is allowed against such dividend income when computing income from other sources.

The cost of acquisition of shares bought back by the Company is treated as a capital loss in the hands of the shareholder, subject to the provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961.

For resident shareholders, tax is deducted at the prescribed rate under Section 194 of the Income-tax Act. Resident shareholders may provide a lower or nil tax deduction certificate issued by the Income-tax Department under Section 197.

For non-resident shareholders, tax is withheld under Section 195 of the Income-tax Act, subject to applicable provisions. Non-resident shareholders may claim benefits under an applicable Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement under Section 90, including agreements read with the Multilateral Instrument, if they satisfy the relevant conditions and provide the required documents to the Company on or before the close of the tendering period.

Eligible shareholders will receive a letter of offer containing further details on taxation. Shareholders are advised to consult their own legal, financial, and tax advisers before participating in the buyback.

### 1.2

As the buyback exceeds 10% of the total paid-up equity share capital and free reserves of the Company, shareholders approved the buyback through a special resolution under the postal ballot notice dated September 25, 2025. The results of the resolution were announced on November 6, 2025.

The buyback is being undertaken through the tender offer route in accordance with Chapter III of the Buyback Regulations, applicable SEBI circulars, the Companies Act, 2013, and related rules and regulations, including Sections 68, 69, and 70 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, and other applicable provisions.

The Company proposes to buy back up to **10,00,00,000 (10 crore) fully paid-up equity shares**, representing **2.41%** of the Company’s paid-up equity share capital on a standalone basis, at **₹1,800 per equity share**, for an aggregate consideration of up to **₹18,000 crore**.

The buyback is within the prescribed limit of 25%, based on the relevant equity share capital and free reserves reflected in the Company’s financial information as of June 30, 2025.
