## 13.PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY TO BE ADOPTED FOR BUYBACK

### 13.1. Eligibility and statutory conditions

- The buyback is open to all Eligible Shareholders, including ADS holders who withdraw the underlying equity shares and hold those equity shares as of the Record Date. Beneficial owners may hold the equity shares in physical or electronic form.
- The aggregate buyback amount is ₹18,000 crore, based on the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as of June 30, 2025. This amount does not exceed 25% of the Company’s total paid-up capital and free reserves.
- Infosys has not made an offer of buyback within the one-year period preceding the expiry of the previous buyback period.
- The Company will not make another buyback offer during the applicable buyback period.
- The proposed purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares—10 crore shares—does not exceed 25% of the total number of equity shares in the paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025.
- The buyback will not result in the delisting of Infosys equity shares.
- The buyback offer will not be withdrawn once the public announcement has been made.
- The Company will transfer an amount equal to the nominal value of the equity shares purchased through the buyback from its free reserves or securities premium account to the capital redemption reserve account, in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and the applicable law.
- As of June 30, 2025, the ratio of the aggregate secured and unsecured debt owed by the Company was, and after the buyback will be, no more than twice the paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements.
- The Company does not have any outstanding debt from lenders and is therefore not required to obtain lender consent.

### 13.2. Buyback route

The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis, subject to the reservation for Small Shareholders prescribed under the Buyback Regulations, through the “Tender Offer” route and the mechanism for acquisition of shares through the stock exchanges, in accordance with applicable SEBI circulars, the Buyback Regulations, and the applicable rules.

### 13.3. Company’s broker

Infosys has appointed Kotak Securities Limited as the registered broker to facilitate the tendering of equity shares through the stock-exchange mechanism for the buyback and to handle purchases and settlements on behalf of the Company.

### 13.4. Tendering of equity shares

During the Tendering Period, Eligible Shareholders may place orders for equity shares held in dematerialized or physical form through their stock brokers.

Eligible Shareholders who wish to tender equity shares in electronic form must provide their stock broker with details of the equity shares they intend to tender.

### 13.5. Bid modifications and multiple bids

The Buyback Regulations do not restrict Eligible Shareholders from placing multiple bids or modifying their bids. Modification or cancellation of orders and multiple bids from a single Eligible Shareholder will be clubbed and considered as one bid for acceptance purposes. Eligible Shareholders should consult their respective stock brokers.

### 13.6. Stock-broker access

If an Eligible Shareholder is unable to register using the quick Unique Client Code facility through a stock broker registered with the BSE or NSE, the shareholder may approach the Company’s Broker to place bids, subject to completion of know-your-customer requirements and submission of the details required by the Company’s Broker.

### 13.7. Tendered quantity disclosure

The cumulative quantity tendered will be made available on the Indian Stock Exchanges’ websites throughout the Tendering Period and will be updated at specific intervals.

### 13.8. Restrictions on shares accepted for buyback

The Company will not accept equity shares offered in the buyback where:

- the shares are locked-in and the lock-in period has not expired;
- the shares are non-transferable;
- duplicate share certificates have not been issued because of loss of share certificates or another reason, and the request is still under process under applicable law; or
- title to the shares is under dispute or is otherwise not clear.

## 11.1. Participation by ADS Holders:

- ADS holders who cancel ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares sufficiently before the Record Date may become holders of those equity shares as of the Record Date. Their entitlement will depend on the number of equity shares held as of the Record Date.
- ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the buyback.
- As stated in the Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025, ADS holders wishing to participate must:
  - apply for and obtain a Permanent Account Number from the Indian Income Tax Department;
  - establish an Indian demat account, including a Subtype DR account, with a bank, broker, or nominee; and
  - arrange for cancellation of the ADSs and withdrawal of the underlying equity shares through Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, the depositary, sufficiently in advance of the Record Date.
- After becoming holders of the underlying equity shares as of the Record Date, ADS holders may tender some or all of those equity shares in accordance with the buyback terms when the Tendering Period begins.
- Infosys equity shares trade on the NSE and BSE and cannot be traded on the NYSE.
- There is no guarantee that any or all equity shares withdrawn by an ADS holder will be accepted in the buyback.
- Remittance of funds by Eligible Shareholders or the stock brokers through which they place orders or bids must comply with applicable Reserve Bank of India requirements.
