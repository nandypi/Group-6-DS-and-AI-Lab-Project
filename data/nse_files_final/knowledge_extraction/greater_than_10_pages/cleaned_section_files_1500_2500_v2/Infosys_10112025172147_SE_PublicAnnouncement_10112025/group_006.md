## Management's Responsibility

Management is responsible for preparing the Statement in accordance with Section 68(2) of the Companies Act, 2013, including maintaining accounting records and relevant supporting documents and implementing and maintaining internal controls relevant to the amount of permissible capital payment.

## Auditor's Responsibity:

The auditors:

- Inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
- Considered the Board of Directors’ opinion that, having regard to the Company’s state of affairs, the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from the date of the Board resolution and the shareholders’ resolution relating to the proposed buyback.
- Examined the amount of permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback of equity shares, as stated in Annexure A, in accordance with Section 68(2)(b) and Section 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the applicable Buyback Regulations.
- Audited the interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements, on which an unmodified audit opinion was issued in the report dated July 23, 2025.
- Conducted the examination in accordance with the Guidance Note on Audit Reports and Certificates for Special Purposes, as applicable, and complied with the ethical requirements of the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

## Opinion

Based on the inquiries conducted and examination performed, the auditors reported that:

- The amount of permissible capital payment towards the proposed buyback of equity shares, as computed in Annexure A, is in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013 and the applicable Buyback Regulations.
- The amounts of share capital and free reserves were extracted from the Company’s audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

## Restriction on Use

The Board of Directors, at its meetings held on September 10, 2025 and September 11, 2025, formed the opinion specified in Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations that, having regard to the Company’s state of affairs, the Company would not be rendered insolvent under the Companies Act, 2013 and/or the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended, within one year from the relevant Board and shareholders’ resolutions relating to the proposed buyback.

This report was issued at the request of the Company solely for explanatory statements and related submissions concerning the proposed buyback of equity shares. It was not intended for any other purpose without the auditors’ prior written consent.
