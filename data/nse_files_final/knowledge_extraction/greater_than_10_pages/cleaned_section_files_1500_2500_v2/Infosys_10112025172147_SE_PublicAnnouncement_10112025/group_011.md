# Buyback of Equity Shares

## 1.1

The Board of Directors approved, subject to applicable approvals, permissions and sanctions, a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each through a postal-ballot special resolution.

The proposed buyback would be conducted through the tender-offer route under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended. The buyback would be made at a price of ₹1,800 per equity share, for an aggregate offer size of ₹800 crore.

The offer size represents 24.31% of the aggregate of the total paid-up share capital and free reserves of the Company on a standalone basis and 21.68% on a consolidated basis, based on the financial statements as at June 30, 2025.

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission approved the Company’s request for exemptive relief relating to certain aspects of U.S. federal securities laws applicable to the buyback.

The buyback would be undertaken from shareholders on a proportionate basis, with the record date to be determined by the Board or Buyback Committee.

## 1.3

Under Regulation 5(viia) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board or Buyback Committee may increase the buyback price and decrease the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, provided that there is no change in the buyback offer size.

## 1.4

The buyback offer size does not include expenses or transaction costs incurred or to be incurred in connection with the buyback. These include applicable taxes, such as securities transaction tax, goods and services tax and stamp duty, as well as incidental and related expenses.

## 1.5

Because the Company’s securities are registered under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and because of its shareholding by residents through American Depositary Shares and equity shares, the Company obtained exemptive relief from the SEC relating to certain aspects of the tender-offer procedures. The relief addressed potentially conflicting regulatory requirements under Indian law and U.S. federal buyback laws.

## 1.6

The equity shares trade under the code 500209 on BSE Limited and INFY on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. The ISIN of the equity shares is INE009A01021.

The Company’s American Depositary Shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

## 1.7

The buyback would be implemented using the mechanism for acquisition of shares through the stock-exchange mechanism for tender offers under the applicable SEBI circulars, including:

- SEBI Circular CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015.
- SEBI Circular CFD/DCR2/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016.
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/DCR-I/CIR/P/615 dated August 13, 2021.
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-2/P/CIR/2023/35 dated March 8, 2023.

The buyback would also be subject to other applicable circulars, notifications and amendments.

## 1.8

The equity shares would be bought back on a proportionate basis from shareholders holding equity shares before the record date, including holders of American Depositary Shares who become eligible shareholders through cancellation of their ADSs and withdrawal of the underlying equity shares.

The record date for determining eligible shareholders was November 14, 2025. The buyback would be conducted through the tender-offer route prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and applicable laws.

The Company would request the relevant stock exchanges to facilitate the cancellation of ADSs and withdrawal of the underlying equity shares for this purpose.

## 1.9

Participation in the buyback would attract income tax on the consideration received by eligible shareholders.

## 1.10

U.S. holders, as defined in the Form 6-K furnished by the Company to the SEC, should review the tax considerations set forth in the Company’s Form 6-K and the Letter of Offer distributed in connection with the buyback.

Depending on the U.S. holder’s particular circumstances, the exchange of equity shares for cash pursuant to the buyback may generally be treated as a disposition of equity shares or as a distribution from the Company for U.S. federal income tax purposes. U.S. holders should consult their own tax advisers regarding U.S. federal income tax law and any applicable state, local or other tax consequences.

## 1.11

The buyback of equity shares from non-resident members, including foreign corporate bodies, American Depositary Share holders with underlying equity shares, foreign institutional investors, foreign portfolio investors and members of foreign nationality, would be subject to applicable provisions of:

- The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and related rules and regulations.
- The Income-tax Act, 1961, and related rules and regulations.
- The Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014, as applicable.
- Requirements and approvals of the Reserve Bank of India and other relevant authorities, where necessary.

Withdrawal of equity shares, if any, by such non-resident shareholders would also be subject to applicable FEMA requirements and related approvals.

## 1.12

The buyback would be undertaken on a proportionate basis from all eligible shareholders as at the record date.

A reservation of up to 15% of the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of equity shares entitled to eligible shareholders who hold equity shares with a market value of not more than ₹2 lakh based on the closing price on the Indian stock exchanges registering the highest trading volume as at November 4, 2025, whichever is higher, would apply to small shareholders.

## 1.13

A copy of the public announcement would be made available on the websites of the Company, the Manager to the Buyback, the Registrar to the Buyback, SEBI, the SEC, BSE and the National Stock Exchange of India.
