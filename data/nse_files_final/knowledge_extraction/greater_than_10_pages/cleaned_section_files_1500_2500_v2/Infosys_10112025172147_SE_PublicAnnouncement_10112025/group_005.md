## 7. INTENTION OF THE PROMOTER AND PROMOTER GROUP AND PERSONS IN CONTROL OF THE COMPANY TO TENDER THEIR EQUITY SHARES IN THE BUYBACK

The Promoter and Promoter Group expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback. Accordingly, the Equity Shares held by members of the Promoter and Promoter Group shall not be considered for computing the entitlement ratio, in accordance with the proviso to Regulation 4(iv)(a) of the SEBI Buyback Regulations.

The Promoter and Promoter Group had the option to participate in the Buyback. Their intention not to participate was communicated through letters dated September 14, 2025, September 16, 2025, September 17, 2025, and September 18, 2025.

## 8. NO DEFAULTS

The Company confirmed that there were no defaults subsisting in the repayment of deposits, interest, redemption of debentures, or repayment of term loans or interest payable thereon, as applicable. It also confirmed that it had not accepted deposits or defaulted on repayment obligations to financial institutions or banking companies.

## 9. CONFIRMATION BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE COMPANY

As required by clause (x) of Schedule I and Regulation 5(iv)(b) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board confirmed that it had made a full inquiry into the affairs and prospects of the Company and formed the following opinion:

- Immediately following the date of the Board meeting held on September 11, 2025, and the date of the members' resolution approving the Buyback, being November 4, 2025, the Company would be able to meet its liabilities as and when they fell due.
- Having regard to the Board's intentions with respect to the management of the Company's business during the year following the date of the Board meeting and the date of passing of the members' resolution, and to the amount and character of the financial resources available to the Company during that year, the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from those dates.
- In forming this opinion, the Board took into account all liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, as if the Company were being wound up under the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, as amended, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.

## 10. CONFIRMATIONS FROM THE COMPANY AS PER THE PROVISIONS OF THE BUYBACK REGULATIONS AND THE COMPANIES ACT

- All Equity Shares proposed to be bought back are fully paid-up.
- Subject to applicable law, the Company shall not raise further capital for a period of one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period.
- Subject to applicable law, the Company shall not issue any shares or other specified securities, including by way of bonus issue, until the expiry of the Buyback Period, except in discharge of subsisting obligations through stock option schemes or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares. As of the date of the Public Announcement, 524,185 RSUs were vested and could be converted into Equity Shares during the Buyback Period.
- Under Section 68(8) of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company shall not make any further issue of the same kind of shares or other securities for six months after completion of the Buyback, except by way of bonus shares or equity shares issued in discharge of subsisting obligations, including conversion of warrants, stock options, preference shares, or debentures into Equity Shares.
- The Company shall not buy back locked-in Equity Shares or non-transferable Equity Shares until the lock-in expires or the Equity Shares become transferable.
- The Company shall not buy back its Equity Shares from any person through negotiated deals, whether on or off the stock exchanges, through spot transactions, or through any private arrangement.
- The Company has complied with Sections 92, 123, 127, and 129 of the Companies Act, 2013.
- There were no defaults subsisting in repayment of deposits, interest, redemption of debentures, redemption of preference shares, or payment of dividend, or repayment of term loans or interest payable thereon, as applicable.
- Funds borrowed from banks and financial institutions shall not be used for the Buyback.
- Withdrawal of Equity Shares against the creation of ADSs shall be in accordance with the Depository Scheme, 2014, and the terms of the ADSs. Conversion of ADSs into Equity Shares and vice versa is available to the Company.
- Equity Shares withdrawn by an ADS holder may be re-deposited against creation of ADSs only within the applicable 30-day period and subject to the maximum number of outstanding ADSs. The Company cannot assure ADS holders that any ADS issuance fee would be waived in connection with such re-deposit.
- If an ADS holder withdraws Equity Shares early or on the last date of the Tendering Period, the holder may be able to re-deposit those Equity Shares for creation of ADSs and receive ADSs representing the re-deposited Equity Shares if the Equity Shares and ADS issuance instructions are delivered to the Depositary's Custodian within the applicable 30-day period.

## 11.2. Buyback Price and Foreign Exchange Considerations:

The Buyback Price will be paid in Indian rupees. Fluctuations in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and the U.S. dollar will affect the U.S.-dollar equivalent of the Buyback Price and Buyback amount. ADS holders were advised to obtain current exchange-rate information before making any decision with respect to the Buyback.

## 11.3. Tax and Regulatory Considerations:

The withdrawal of Equity Shares underlying ADSs and their re-deposit against the creation of ADSs, as well as the sale of Equity Shares not tendered or not accepted in the Buyback, may have tax implications in India, the United States, and other jurisdictions. The implications may vary depending on factors including the period of holding, securities held, residential status of the holder, tax classification of the holder, and the nature of income earned.

### a. India

There were arguments that the withdrawal of Equity Shares underlying ADSs should not be subject to Indian capital gains tax. However, the Company could not assure ADS holders that tax would not be imposed or that a bank, broker, or other nominee in India would not withhold taxes in connection with the re-deposit of Equity Shares.

ADS holders considering establishing an Indian Demat account (Subtype DR) and a brokerage account with a bank, broker, or other nominee in India were advised to consult their legal and tax advisers regarding the potential implications of withdrawing and re-depositing Equity Shares.

#### i. Withdrawal and Re-Deposit of Equity Shares by ADS Holders:

ADS holders requiring additional information on surrendering ADSs for cancellation and withdrawal of Equity Shares were advised to consult their legal, financial, and tax advisers before surrendering ADSs, requesting the Depositary to effect withdrawal of Equity Shares, or participating in the Buyback.

### b. United States

For U.S. federal income tax purposes, a U.S. holder will generally be treated as the owner of the Equity Shares underlying the holder's ADSs. Important information on the U.S. federal income tax consequences relevant to U.S. holders tendering Equity Shares in the Buyback was set forth in a Form 6-K furnished by the Company to the SEC on or about the date of the Postal Ballot Notice and would be set forth in the Company's Form 20-F.

Each ADS holder should consult their own tax adviser regarding the U.S. federal, state, and local tax consequences of withdrawing and re-depositing Equity Shares, as well as the tax consequences of participating in the Buyback.

In addition to Indian and U.S. tax considerations, ADS holders should consider potential taxation under the laws of their country of residence and other jurisdictions in which they may be subject to taxation arising from the withdrawal and re-deposit of Equity Shares.
