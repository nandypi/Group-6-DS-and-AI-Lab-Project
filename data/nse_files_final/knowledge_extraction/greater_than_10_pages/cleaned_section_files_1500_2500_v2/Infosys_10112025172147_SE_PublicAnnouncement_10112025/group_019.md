# PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

## For the Attention of Equity Shareholders/Beneficial Owners of Equity Shares of Infosys Limited

Infosys Limited announced a buyback of equity shares on a proportionate basis through the tender-offer route under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended.

The Public Announcement was made pursuant to Regulation 7(i) of the Buyback Regulations and contains the disclosures required under those regulations.

## 1. DETAILS OF THE BUYBACK OFFER AND BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

Infosys Limited proposed to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each at ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash.

The buyback represents 2.41% of the company’s equity share capital, based on the equity shares outstanding as of November 14, 2025, the record date. The buyback will be conducted through the tender-offer route on a proportionate basis, subject to a reservation for small shareholders.

The aggregate consideration for the buyback is up to ₹18,000 crore.

### Taxation

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 introduced amendments relating to the taxation of share buybacks with effect from October 1, 2024.

Consideration received by shareholders for shares bought back by a domestic company is treated as dividend in the hands of the shareholders, and the shareholder may treat the cost of the bought-back shares as a capital loss, subject to applicable provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961 and related rules.

The company is required to deduct tax at source from consideration payable to resident shareholders at the applicable prescribed rate. Non-resident shareholders are subject to tax deduction at source under Section 195 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, generally at the prescribed rate of 20% plus applicable surcharge and cess, unless a lower rate is available under applicable provisions, including an applicable Double Tax Avoidance Agreement.

Non-resident shareholders seeking treaty benefits must provide the required documents to the company before the close of the tendering period and satisfy applicable conditions, including those relating to the General Anti-Avoidance Rule and the Multilateral Instrument.

Shareholders were advised to consult their own legal, financial, and tax advisers before participating in the buyback.
