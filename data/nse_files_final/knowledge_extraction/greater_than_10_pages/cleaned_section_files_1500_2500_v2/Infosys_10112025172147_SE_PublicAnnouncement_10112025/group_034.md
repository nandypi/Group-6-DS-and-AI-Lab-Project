## a. India

The buyback price will be paid in Indian rupees. ADS holders are urged to obtain current exchange-rate information between the Indian rupee and the U.S. dollar before making any decision regarding the buyback.

The withdrawal and re-deposit of the equity shares underlying ADSs may have tax implications in India. The tax treatment may depend on factors including the holder's income-tax residency status and classification.

ADS holders should consult their own legal, financial, and tax advisers regarding the potential implications of withdrawing and re-depositing equity shares before making such a request. ADS holders requiring additional information about an Indian demat account (Subtype DR) and a brokerage account should contact the bank or broker through which they hold their ADSs.

## b. United States

ADSs are generally not accepted in the buyback. ADS holders should consider the U.S. federal income-tax consequences of participating in the buyback, withdrawing the underlying equity shares, and re-depositing equity shares that are not tendered.

Each ADS holder should consult their own tax adviser regarding the U.S. federal, state, and local tax consequences of withdrawing and re-depositing equity shares and participating in the buyback.

Important information on the tax treatment relevant to ADS holders will be set forth in the Letter of Offer distributed in connection with the buyback and in a Form 6-K furnished by the Company to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission around the date of the Postal Ballot Notice.

## ii. Tendering Equity Shares in the Buyback

ADS holders should contact the bank, broker, or nominee through which they hold their ADSs when requesting that the depositary withdraw the underlying equity shares and/or participate in the buyback.

## 12. REPORT BY THE COMPANY'S STATUTORY AUDITOR

The section contains the report dated September 1, 2025, issued by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, the Company's statutory auditor, on the permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback and the opinion formed by the Board of Directors regarding insolvency.

### AUDITOR'S REPORT REF: IL/2025-26/29

#### Re: Statutory Auditor's Report in respect of proposed buyback

The report was issued in accordance with the engagement letter dated July 10, 2025.

The Board of Directors approved a proposal for the buyback of equity shares, subject to shareholder approval at the meeting held in September 2025, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013, as amended, and the Buyback Regulations.

### Management's Responsibility

Management is responsible for preparing the statement in accordance with Section 68(2) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the applicable Buyback Regulations, maintaining relevant internal controls, and making estimates that provide an appropriate basis for preparation.

### Auditor's Responsibility

Pursuant to the requirements of the Buyback Regulations, the auditors:

1. Inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
2. Examined the amount of permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback in accordance with Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the applicable Buyback Regulations.
3. Considered the interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025, which had been audited under the applicable standards and authoritative pronouncements issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.
4. Considered the opinion formed by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, as specified in Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations, regarding whether the Company would become insolvent within one year from the date of passing the Board resolution for the proposed buyback.

The auditors conducted their examination in accordance with the Guidance Note on Audit Reports and Certificates for Special Purposes (Revised 2016) issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and the applicable Standards on Auditing. The Guidance Note requires compliance with the applicable ethical requirements and performance of procedures to obtain reasonable assurance that the statement is free from material misstatement.

The auditors also complied with the applicable requirements of the Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1, *Quality Control for Firms that Perform Audits and Reviews of Historical Financial Information, and Other Assurance and Related Services Engagements*.
