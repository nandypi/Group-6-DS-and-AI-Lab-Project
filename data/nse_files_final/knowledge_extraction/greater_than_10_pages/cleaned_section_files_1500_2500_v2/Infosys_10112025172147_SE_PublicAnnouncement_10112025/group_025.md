## 11.4. Further Information.

## 12. REPORT BY THE COMPANY'S STATUTORY AUDITOR

The report dated September 11, 2025, issued by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, the statutory auditor of Infosys Limited, to the Board of Directors is reproduced below.

### Auditor's Report

The report relates to the proposed buyback of equity shares by Infosys Limited under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended (the “Buyback Regulations”).

The auditor was requested by the Company's management to report on:

1. The amount of permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback.
2. Whether the Board of Directors formed its solvency opinion on reasonable grounds and whether the Company would be rendered insolvent within one year from the date of the Board resolution or the shareholders' resolution approving the proposed buyback.

### Management's Responsibility

Management is responsible for preparing the statement of permissible capital payment in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, the Buyback Regulations, and the relevant audited financial statements. This includes maintaining appropriate accounting records, internal controls, and supporting documentation.

### Auditor's Responsibility

The auditor inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

The auditor's responsibility was to provide reasonable assurance that:

- The amount of permissible capital payment stated in Annexure A had been properly determined under Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, and Regulations 4(i) and 5(1)(b) of the Buyback Regulations.
- The Board's solvency opinion was formed on reasonable grounds, considering the Company's state of affairs and the proposed buyback.

The examination was conducted in accordance with the applicable Guidance Note on Audit Reports and Certificates for Special Purposes (Revised 2016), Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the ethical requirements of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

### Opinion

Based on the inquiries conducted and the examination performed, the auditor reported that:

- The amount of permissible capital payment towards the proposed buyback, as computed in the statement attached as Annexure A, was properly determined in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, and the Buyback Regulations, based on the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
- The interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements had been approved by the Company's Board of Directors on July 23, 2025.
- The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 1, 2025, formed the solvency opinion specified in Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations on reasonable grounds. The report states that, having regard to the Company's state of affairs, the Company would not be rendered insolvent within the applicable one-year period in connection with the proposed buyback.

The report was issued at the Company's request solely for inclusion in the explanatory statement accompanying the notice for the special resolution, the public announcement, and other documents relating to the proposed buyback. It was also intended for submission to relevant authorities and the merchant bankers in connection with the buyback and was not issued for any other purpose without the auditor's prior written consent.

## 13. PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY TO BE ADOPTED FOR BUYBACK

### 13.1.

The buyback is open to all eligible shareholders, including ADS holders who submit the required number of ADSs to the Depositary and withdraw the underlying equity shares. Shareholders must hold equity shares as of the record date, in physical or electronic form.

### 13.2.

The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis, subject to the reservation for small shareholders under the Buyback Regulations, through the tender-offer route.

The buyback will be implemented in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013, the Buyback Regulations, and applicable SEBI circulars.

### 13.3.

Infosys has appointed Kotak Securities Limited as the registered broker to facilitate the tendering of equity shares through the stock-exchange mechanism and to facilitate purchases and settlements on behalf of the Company.
