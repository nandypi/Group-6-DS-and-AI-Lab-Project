## 2. NECESSITY FOR THE BUYBACK AND DETAILS THEREOF

The Buyback is being undertaken after considering the Company’s strategic and operational cash requirements in the medium term and the need to return surplus funds to members in an effective and efficient manner, in line with its capital allocation policy.

The Company’s cash requirements include:

- Investments required to execute the Company’s strategy.
- Minimum cash required for contingencies or unforeseen events.
- Other significant developments requiring cash investments.
- Funds required for acquisitions that the Board may approve.
- Working capital investments and capital expenditure on technology and infrastructure.

The Company’s expected cash generation and strong balance sheet position allow it to reward members while retaining regular and special dividends. The Company has undertaken multiple buybacks, which, along with dividends, have enabled it to distribute surplus cash to shareholders in a predictable and efficient manner.

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had consolidated cash and investments comprising cash and cash equivalents, current and non-current investments excluding investments in equity and preference shares, and other investments of ₹45,04 crore.

The Buyback is being undertaken for the following reasons:

- Return surplus cash to members in line with the stated Capital Allocation Policy.
- Improve return on equity through cash distribution and improve earnings per share by reducing the equity base, thereby increasing members’ value.
- Provide members with the option, as of the Record Date, to sell their Equity Shares and receive cash or retain their Equity Shares and obtain a resultant increase in their percentage shareholding after the Buyback, without additional investment.
- Benefit Small Shareholders through a reservation of the higher of: (a) the number of shares entitled according to their shareholding; or (b) 15% of the number of shares proposed to be bought back, as per Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations.

### 2.5. Capital Allocation Policy

Effective from financial year 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends and share buybacks or special dividends, subject to applicable laws.

The Company expects to progressively increase its annual Dividend Per Share and revise its payout ratio, excluding special dividends if any.

In line with the Capital Allocation Policy, the Buyback is expected to create long-term value for shareholders through a reduction in the equity base.

## MAXIMUM NUMBER OF EQUITY SHARES THAT THE COMPANY PROPOSES TO BUYBACK

The indicative number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back at the Buyback Price is 10 crore Equity Shares, comprising approximately 2.41% of the total paid-up equity share capital.

## 4. MAXIMUM AMOUNT REQUIRED UNDER THE BUYBACK AND ITS PERCENTAGE OF THE TOTAL PAID-UP CAPITAL AND FREE RESERVES

### 4.1. Maximum Amount Required

The amount of funds required for the Buyback will aggregate ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only), based on the latest audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements of the Company as at June 30, 2025. This amount is less than 25% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.

### 4.2. Expenses and Transaction Costs

The Buyback Offer Size does not include expenses or transaction costs incurred or to be incurred for the Buyback, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, public announcement publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, and applicable taxes such as securities transaction tax.

### 4.3. Sources of Funds

The proposed Buyback will be financed from the Company’s free reserves and securities premium account. No borrowed funds from banks or financial institutions will be used for the Buyback.

## 5. BUYBACK PRICE AND BASIS OF ARRIVING AT THE BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

The Equity Shares are proposed to be bought back through the tender offer route on BSE and NSE, where the Equity Shares are listed, at a price of ₹1,800 per Equity Share.

In accordance with Regulation 5 of the Buyback Regulations, the Board or Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and the Buyback Offer Size, subject to applicable conditions, including trends in the volume-weighted average market prices of the Equity Shares and the possible impact of the Buyback on earnings per Equity Share. Any such increase may be made up to one working day before the Record Date.

In line with recent market practice, the Buyback Price represents:

- A premium of 20.78% and 20.67% over the volume-weighted average market price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, during the two-week period ending September 8, 2025, being the date of intimation to the stock exchanges regarding the proposal being considered at the Board Meeting.
- A premium of 25.64% and 25.62% over the closing price of the Equity Shares on BSE and NSE, respectively, as of September 8, 2025, being the date of intimation to the stock exchanges regarding the proposal being considered at the Board Meeting.
