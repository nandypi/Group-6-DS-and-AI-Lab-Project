## 15. RECORDDATE,OFFERPERIODANDSHAREHOLDER ENTITLEMENT

### Eligibility and Letter of Offer

All equity shareholders of the Company as of the Record Date will be eligible to participate in the Buyback.

Shareholders will receive a Letter of Offer together with a tender offer form indicating their entitlement to participate in the Buyback.

The Letter of Offer will be dispatched electronically in accordance with applicable requirements. A physical copy may be provided to shareholders or ADS holders upon request, including to an eligible U.S. ADS holder who has not registered an email address with the Depositories, subject to the procedures stated in the Letter of Offer.

Holders of ADSs representing the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date will not be eligible to tender ADSs directly in the Buyback. To participate, ADS holders must cancel all or the desired portion of their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares so that they become equity shareholders as of the Record Date. ADS holders may re-deposit the Equity Shares if the tendered shares are not accepted in the Buyback.

### Shareholder categories and entitlement

The Equity Shares proposed to be bought back by the Company will be divided into two categories:

1. Small Shareholders.
2. All other Eligible Shareholders.

As defined in Regulation 2(1)(n) of the Buyback Regulations, a Small Shareholder is a shareholder whose market value of Equity Shares, based on the closing price of the Equity Shares on the stock exchange with the highest trading volume as of the Record Date, is not more than ₹2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs Only).

In accordance with the proviso to Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back by the Company, or the number of Equity Shares entitled as per the shareholding of Small Shareholders as of the Record Date, whichever is higher, will be reserved for Small Shareholders.

The entitlement of each Eligible Shareholder will be calculated based on the number of Equity Shares held by that shareholder as of the Record Date and the Buyback ratio applicable to the relevant category.

The promoters and members of the promoter group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback. Their non-participation and the resulting higher Buyback entitlement ratio for public shareholders would benefit shareholders classified as Small Shareholders.

For determining the category and entitlement of each Eligible Shareholder:

- Equity Shares held through multiple demat accounts or folios under identical PANs will be clubbed to prevent a shareholder from receiving a higher entitlement.
- Where PANs of joint shareholders are not available, the sequence of the names of the joint shareholders will be checked.
- Joint holdings with identical PAN and name sequences will be clubbed.
- For physical shareholders, joint holdings with identical PAN and name sequences will also be clubbed.
- Shares held under the category of clearing members, corporate body margin accounts, or corporate body-broker accounts will be considered separately where they relate to different schemes, investors, or clients, including mutual funds, pension funds, trusts, and insurance investors.
- Shares held in separate demat accounts with different account nomenclature for different schemes or clients will be considered separately.

### Participation and acceptance

Participation in the Buyback is voluntary. Eligible Shareholders may:

- Tender all or part of their Buyback entitlement.
- Tender additional Equity Shares over and above their entitlement.
- Participate in the shortfall created by the non-participation of other Eligible Shareholders.
- Choose not to participate and retain their Equity Shares.

Eligible Shareholders whose shares are accepted under the Buyback will receive cash in lieu of those Equity Shares. Shareholders who do not participate may experience a resultant increase in their percentage shareholding after completion of the Buyback, without any additional investment.

After accepting the Equity Shares tendered against the entitlement, the Company will accept Equity Shares tendered over and above the entitlement, if any, from Eligible Shareholders in that category. Thereafter, Equity Shares may be accepted from the entitlement available in the other category.

The maximum tender by an Eligible Shareholder cannot exceed the number of Equity Shares held by that shareholder as of the Record Date. The source text further states that, where an Eligible Shareholder holds Equity Shares through multiple demat accounts, tender through a demat account cannot exceed the stated limit; the sentence is incomplete in the supplied section.

Equity Shares tendered in the Buyback will be accepted in accordance with the mechanism for acquisition of securities through the Stock Exchange mechanism under the Buyback Regulations. Settlement of tenders will be carried out through the stock exchange pursuant to the applicable SEBI circulars. Eligible Shareholders will receive a Letter of Entitlement for participating in the Buyback.

## 16. COMPLIANCEOFFICER

Detailed instructions for participating in the Buyback, the relevant timetable, and the tender form will be provided in the Letter of Offer. A physical copy may be couriered to a shareholder’s or ADS holder’s registered postal address available with the Company upon request to the Registrar.
