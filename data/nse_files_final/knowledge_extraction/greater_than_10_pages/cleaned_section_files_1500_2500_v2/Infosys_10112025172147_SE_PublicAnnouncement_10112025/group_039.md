## Additional information pursuant to U.S. Law

The Company’s buyback had not yet commenced as of the date of this communication. The buyback may not commence, may not be completed on the anticipated timetable, or may not be completed at all. Its processes, results, outcomes, expected benefits, and costs are subject to risks and uncertainties.

The Company expects to file a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), including the letter of offer and related documents, and any amendments. Security holders should carefully read these documents in their entirety before making any decision regarding the buyback.

This communication is provided for informational purposes only and is neither an offer to purchase nor a solicitation to sell securities. Any offer to purchase will be made only pursuant to the buyback documents filed with the SEC.

All statements other than statements of historical fact may be considered forward-looking statements. Such statements may be identified by terms including “anticipate,” “believe,” “estimate,” “expect,” and “intend.” They are based on management’s beliefs and assumptions using information currently available to management and are subject to known and unknown risks, uncertainties, and other factors that could cause actual results or outcomes to differ materially from those expressed or implied. Additional factors are discussed in the “Risk Factors” section of the relevant documents.
