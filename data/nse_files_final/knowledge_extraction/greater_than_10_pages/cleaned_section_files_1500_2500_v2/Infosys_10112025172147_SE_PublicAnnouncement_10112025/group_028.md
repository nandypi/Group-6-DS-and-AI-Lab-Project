# 15. RECORD DATE, OFFER PERIOD AND SHAREHOLDER ENTITLEMENT

### 15.1 Record Date and Tendering Period

November 14, 2025 has been fixed as the record date for determining the equity shareholders eligible to participate in the Buyback.

The tendering period for the Buyback will commence on November 20, 2025 and continue until November 26, 2025.

### 15.2 Letter of Offer

Eligible Shareholders will receive a Letter of Offer and tender offer form in due course, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations and applicable circulars or notifications. Participation and tendering may take place even if an Eligible Shareholder does not receive the Letter of Offer or tender form.

### 15.3 Eligibility of Equity Shareholders and ADS Holders

All equity shareholders of the Company as of the Record Date will be eligible to participate in the Buyback.

Holders of ADSs will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. ADS holders must cancel all or the desired portion of their ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares so that they become equity shareholders of the Company as of the Record Date. Such ADS holders may then tender the underlying equity shares in the Buyback. ADSs re-deposited against shares tendered in the Buyback, or shares tendered but not accepted in the Buyback, may be subject to the applicable procedures.

### 15.4 Dispatch of the Letter of Offer

The Letter of Offer will be dispatched through electronic mode in accordance with applicable requirements, within two working days from the Record Date, to the registered email addresses available with the Depositories.

A physical copy will be couriered to shareholders’ or ADS holders’ registered postal addresses available with the Company where required, including where a U.S.-based Eligible Shareholder has not received the electronic Letter of Offer and requests a physical copy.

### 15.6 Definition of Small Shareholder

Under Regulation 2(1)(n) of the Buyback Regulations, a “Small Shareholder” is a shareholder holding equity shares with a market value of not more than ₹2,00,000, based on the closing price of the shares on the stock exchange where the highest trading volume in the equity shares was recorded as of the Record Date.

### 15.7 Reservation for Small Shareholders

In accordance with the proviso to Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, 15% of the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of equity shares entitled as per the shareholding of Small Shareholders as of the Record Date, whichever is higher, will be reserved for Small Shareholders.

The equity shares bought back and accepted in the reserved category will be determined according to the applicable Buyback Regulations.

### 15.8 Calculation of Buyback Entitlement

Based on the holding as of the Record Date, the Company will determine each Eligible Shareholder’s entitlement to tender equity shares in the Buyback in the category to which the shareholder belongs (“Buyback Entitlement”). The entitlement will be calculated based on the number of equity shares held by the respective shareholder as of the Record Date.

The equity shares held by members of the promoter and promoter group will not be considered for computing the entitlement ratio in accordance with the proviso to Regulation 4(iv)(a) of the Buyback Regulations. The promoter and promoter group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback.

The categories for determining entitlement comprise:

1. The reserved category for Small Shareholders.
2. The general category for all other Eligible Shareholders.

### 15.9 Clubbing of Holdings

In accordance with Regulation 9(ix) of the Buyback Regulations, the Company will club together equity shares held by shareholders whose demat accounts or folios do not receive a higher entitlement under the Small Shareholder category.

In cases of joint shareholding where the PANs of the joint shareholders are not available, the Registrar to the Buyback will check the sequence of the names of the joint holders and club together the equity shares held in cases where the names of the joint shareholders are identical.

Where PANs are available and the PANs or the sequence of PANs and names of investors are different, the equity shares will be considered separately for determining the category and entitlement.

Equity shares held by different schemes or through demat accounts with different nomenclature will be considered separately where the shareholder records received from the Depositories show different demat account details. Equity shares held by mutual funds, pension funds, trusts, and insurance clients will be treated in accordance with the relevant shareholder records.

### 15.10 Acceptance of Tendered Shares

Equity shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders within their entitlement will be accepted in the relevant category. Equity shares tendered above the entitlement in one category will be accepted from Eligible Shareholders in that category and thereafter from Eligible Shareholders in the other category, in proportion to the equity shares tendered.

### 15.11 Voluntary Participation

Participation in the Buyback is voluntary. Eligible Shareholders may choose not to participate and may retain their equity shares, resulting in an increase in their percentage shareholding after completion of the Buyback without any additional investment.

Eligible Shareholders may tender part or all of their Buyback Entitlement and may also tender additional equity shares, meaning equity shares above their Buyback Entitlement, subject to the procedure set out for the Buyback.

If an Eligible Shareholder does not participate for any reason, the Company, the Manager, the Registrar to the Buyback, and their officers will not be liable for such non-participation.

### 15.12 Maximum Tender

The maximum tender by an Eligible Shareholder cannot exceed the number of equity shares held by that Eligible Shareholder as of the Record Date.

Where an Eligible Shareholder holds equity shares through multiple demat accounts, the aggregate tender through those demat accounts cannot exceed the number of equity shares held by that Eligible Shareholder as of the Record Date.

### 15.13 Tendering Mechanism

The equity shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders within their entitlement, as well as additional equity shares tendered, will be accepted in accordance with the procedure prescribed in the Buyback Regulations.

Settlement of the tender will be carried out using the “Mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender Offers.”

### 15.14 Participation Instructions

Detailed instructions for participating in the Buyback and tendering equity shares will be provided in the Letter of Offer and tender form.
