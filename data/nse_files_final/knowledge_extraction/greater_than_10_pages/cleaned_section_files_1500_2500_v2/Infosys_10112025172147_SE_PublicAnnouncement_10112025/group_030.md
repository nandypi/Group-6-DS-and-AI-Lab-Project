# Buyback of Equity Shares

## Taxation

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 amended the taxation of buyback income. Buyback consideration is subject to applicable income-tax provisions in the hands of shareholders, and the Company is not required to pay buyback tax on such consideration.

The cost of acquisition of shares bought back by the Company is treated as a capital loss for the shareholder under the applicable provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (ITA).

The Company is required to deduct tax at source at the prescribed rate of 10% under Section 194 of the ITA from consideration payable to resident shareholders for shares bought back. Resident shareholders may submit documents or certificates issued under Section 197 or another applicable provision of the ITA to seek deduction at a lower or nil rate, subject to the applicable requirements.

Non-resident shareholders may be eligible for benefits under an applicable Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), read with the Multilateral Instrument (MLI), between India and the shareholder’s country of tax residence, subject to satisfying the applicable conditions. The General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) may also apply. Non-resident shareholders should provide the required documentation to claim any applicable DTAA benefit.

Buyback consideration payable to non-resident shareholders may be subject to tax deduction at source at the standard prescribed rate unless a lower rate is authorized under the ITA or an applicable DTAA. Eligible shareholders are advised to consult their legal, financial, and tax advisers regarding the tax consequences of participating in the Buyback.

The Buyback will take place through the settlement mechanism of the Stock Exchanges. Securities transaction tax will apply at 0.10% of the value of the transaction.

## Buyback Terms and Approvals

The Board of Directors of the Company and the Buyback Committee approved the proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each from shareholders, subject to applicable approvals, permissions, and sanctions.

The Buyback was approved through a special resolution passed by shareholders through postal ballot. The results of the postal ballot were announced on November 6, 2025.

The Company proposes to buy back up to 10,00,00,000 (10 crore) equity shares, representing 2.41% of the total equity shares in issue on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash. The aggregate amount will be up to ₹18,000 crore.

The Buyback will be conducted through the tender offer route in accordance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, as amended, the Companies Act, 2013, and other applicable rules and regulations.

The Buyback Offer Size represents 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate of the total paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the Company’s interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively. The Buyback is within the prescribed limit of 25% under the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations.

The Board or Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the Buyback Offer Size does not change.

The Buyback Offer Size excludes transaction costs, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, public announcement and publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, and applicable taxes such as securities transaction tax.

## Regulatory Matters

Because the Company’s securities are registered under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act and are held by U.S. residents, the Company obtained exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) concerning certain aspects of the tender offer procedures applicable to the Buyback under U.S. federal securities laws.

The Buyback will be implemented through the mechanism prescribed under applicable SEBI circulars, including:

- SEBI Circular CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015.
- SEBI Circular CFD/DCR2/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016.
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/DCR-II/CIR/P/2018/131 dated August 16, 2018.
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-2/P/CIR/2023/35 dated March 28, 2023.

The Buyback is also subject to applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, and the Articles of Association of the Company.

## Securities and Eligibility

The equity shares are traded under code 500209 on BSE Limited and INFY on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. The ISIN of the equity shares is INE009A01021.

The Company’s American Depositary Shares (ADSs) are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The equity shares will be bought back on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route from shareholders holding equity shares as on the Record Date, November 14, 2025.

Holders of ADSs may participate if they cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares before the Record Date, so that they become equity shareholders of the Company on the Record Date.

Participation by non-resident shareholders, including foreign corporate bodies, former overseas corporate bodies, foreign portfolio investors, members of foreign nationality, and ADS holders holding underlying equity shares after withdrawal, is subject to applicable laws and regulations. These may include the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, rules and regulations framed under it, the Income-tax Act, 1961, the Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014, and requirements or approvals of the Reserve Bank of India and other relevant authorities.

## Small Shareholders

The Buyback will be undertaken on a proportionate basis from eligible shareholders as on the Record Date.

A reservation of up to 15% of the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of equity shares entitled under the applicable provisions, will be available for small shareholders.

For this purpose, a small shareholder is a shareholder holding equity shares with a market value of not more than ₹2 lakh, determined using the applicable closing price on the Indian Stock Exchange with the highest trading volume as on the Record Date.
