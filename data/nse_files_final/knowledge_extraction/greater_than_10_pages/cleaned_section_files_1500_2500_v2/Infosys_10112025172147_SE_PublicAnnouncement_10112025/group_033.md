## 7. INTENTION OF THEPROMOTERANDPROMOTER GROUP AND PERSONS IN CONTROL OF THE COMPANY TO TENDER THEIR EQUITY SHARES IN THE BUYBACK

The Promoter and Promoter Group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback under the tender offer route, through letters dated September 14, 2025.

## 8.NO DEFAULTS

The Company confirms that it has not:

- Accepted deposits or defaulted in repayment of deposits or payment of interest on deposits.
- Defaulted in payment of interest or redemption of debentures.
- Defaulted in redemption of preference shares or payment of dividends due to shareholders.
- Defaulted in repayment of term loans or payment of interest to any financial institution or banking company.

Because the Promoter and Promoter Group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback, the Equity Shares held by them will not be considered for computing Buyback entitlement in accordance with the proviso to Regulation 4(iv)(a) of the SEBI Buyback Regulations. Accordingly, Eligible Shareholders will have a higher Buyback Entitlement ratio.

## 9. CONFIRMATION BYTHEBOARD OFDIRECTORSOF THE COMPANY

The Board confirmed that:

- The Company will be able to meet its liabilities as and when they fall due and will not be rendered insolvent within one year from the date of the Board meeting held on September 11, 2025, and from the date of passing of the members’ resolution approving the Buyback, being November 4, 2025.
- In forming this opinion, the Board took into account the Company’s liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, the prospects of the Company for the year immediately following the Board meeting and the members’ resolution, the Board’s intentions regarding management of the Company’s business during that year, the amount and character of the financial resources available to the Company during that year, the provisions of the Companies Act, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.

All Equity Shares eligible for the Buyback are fully paid-up.

## 10.CONFIRMATIONSFROMTHECOMPANYASPERTHE PROVISIONSOFTHEBUYBACK REGULATIONS AND THE COMPANIES ACT

Subject to applicable law and the exceptions stated below, the Company confirms that:

- It will not issue any debentures, Equity Shares, or other specified securities, including by way of bonus issue, until the expiry of the Buyback Period, except for subsisting obligations through stock option schemes or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares.
- It will not buy back locked-in Equity Shares or non-transferable Equity Shares until the lock-in expires or the Equity Shares become transferable.
- It has complied with Sections 92, 123, 127, and 129 of the Companies Act.
- Funds borrowed from banks and financial institutions will not be used for the Buyback.
- The Buyback of 10,00,00,000 (ten crore) Equity Shares does not exceed 25% of the paid-up share capital as on June 30, 2025.
- The aggregate amount of the Buyback is ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only), based on the latest audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements of the Company as on June 30, 2025, and does not exceed 25% of the total paid-up capital and free reserves of the Company.
- It will not make any offer of Buyback within one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period or the preceding Buyback offer, in accordance with Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations.
- It will earmark and arrange adequate sources of funds for the Buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.
- There is no pending scheme of amalgamation, compromise, or arrangement under the Companies Act as on the date of the announcement.
- It will not directly or indirectly purchase its own Equity Shares or other specified securities:
  - Through any subsidiary company, including its own subsidiary companies; or
  - Through any investment company or group of investment companies.
- Consideration for Equity Shares bought back by the Company will be paid only in cash.
- The Equity Shares will not be delisted from BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
- The Buyback offer will not be withdrawn once the public announcement has been made.
- The nominal value of the Equity Shares purchased through the Buyback will be transferred to the capital redemption reserve account. Details of the transfer will be disclosed in subsequent audited financial statements.
- It will ensure the consequent reduction of share capital after the Buyback. The Equity Shares bought back will be extinguished and physically destroyed within the period prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and the Companies Act.
- The Company does not have any outstanding debt from lenders as of the date of the announcement.

The Company will not, for six months after completion of the Buyback, issue Equity Shares or securities of the kind specified in Section 68(8) of the Companies Act, except by way of bonus shares or Equity Shares issued to discharge subsisting obligations such as conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares.

The Company will not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period, except in discharge of subsisting obligations.

## 11. ADDITIONALINFORMATIONFOR HOLDERSOFTHE COMPANY'SAMERICAN DEPOSITARY SHARES

ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. To participate, ADS holders must, sufficiently in advance of the Record Date:

- Withdraw the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs and become holders of the Company’s Equity Shares as of the Record Date.
- Establish an Indian demat account of the appropriate type and a brokerage account in India.
- Arrange for the withdrawn Equity Shares to be held in electronic or dematerialized form.
- Set up the Equity Shares for cancellation and provide proper cancellation instructions.
- Arrange for the Equity Shares to be delivered to a broker in India to effect transactions in the Equity Shares.

## 11.1. Participation by ADS Holders:

ADS holders who cancel ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares so that they become holders of Equity Shares as of the Record Date will be allotted an entitlement based on the number of Equity Shares held as of the Record Date.

There is no guarantee that any Equity Shares tendered by an ADS holder will be accepted in the Buyback.

The Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, had not been amended with respect to conversion or reconversion between ADSs and Equity Shares. Accordingly, re-deposit of withdrawn Equity Shares and reconversion into depository receipts will be subject to the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, and the terms of the ADSs.

If an ADS holder withdraws the Equity Shares underlying the ADSs after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date and before the start of the Tendering Period, the ADS holder may tender those Equity Shares in the Buyback. Any Equity Shares not accepted in the Buyback may be re-deposited for the creation of ADSs if the holder delivers them to the Custodian for the Depositary, together with ADS issuance instructions, within the specified 30-day period.

The Depositary has agreed to waive the ADS issuance fee that would otherwise be payable for the issuance of ADSs representing such Equity Shares.

## 11.2. Buyback Price and Foreign Exchange Considerations:

Withdrawn Equity Shares for which the relevant requirements are completed after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date and after the last day of the Tendering Period may be re-deposited for the creation of ADSs only to the extent of the specified maximum number of ADSs. The Company cannot assure ADS holders that the ADS issuance fee will be waived for the issuance of ADSs representing such Equity Shares.
