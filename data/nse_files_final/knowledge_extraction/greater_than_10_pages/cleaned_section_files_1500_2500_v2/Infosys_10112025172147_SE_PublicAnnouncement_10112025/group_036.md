## 13.11.Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialized form:

- Eligible Shareholders should approach their respective Stock Broker(s) and indicate the details of the Equity Shares they intend to tender.
- A lien shall be marked in the demat account of the Eligible Shareholder for the Equity Shares intended to be tendered. Details of the shares marked as lien will be provided by the Depositories to the Clearing Corporations, as applicable.
- The Stock Broker(s) must place an order or bid on behalf of the Eligible Shareholder(s) wishing to tender Equity Shares in the Buyback using the Acquisition Window of BSE/NSE.
- During the Tendering Period, the Eligible Shareholder must initiate the required depository transaction instruction through the depository participant or clearing member. This instruction transfers the securities from the free balance in the depository account to the Clearing Corporation account, and the source depository will block the securities.
- Upon placing the bid, the Stock Broker(s) must provide a Transaction Registration Slip (TRS) generated by the exchange bidding system to the Eligible Shareholder. The TRS will contain details of the order submitted, including the order ID and the number of Equity Shares tendered and accepted, as applicable.
- For custodian participant orders for dematerialized Equity Shares, early pay-in is mandatory before confirmation of the order by the custodian. The custodian must confirm the order by the applicable window closing date. For unconfirmed orders, the Stock Broker(s) will revoke the custodian confirmation and send the revised order to the custodian again within the prescribed time.
- Eligible Shareholders must provide all relevant documents necessary to ensure transferability of the Equity Shares in respect of the tender form. These documents may include, but are not limited to:
  - A duly attested death certificate and succession certificate or legal heirship certificate where an Eligible Shareholder has expired.
  - A duly attested power of attorney where a person other than the Eligible Shareholder has signed the tender form.
  - For companies, the necessary certified corporate authorizations, including board and/or general meeting resolutions.
- Eligible Shareholders must keep their DP account active and unblocked and maintain sufficient securities and bank-account details in the depository system. Shareholders must also ensure that the bank account linked to the DP account remains active and updated so that the Clearing Corporation can remit the consideration due for Equity Shares accepted in the Buyback. Equity Shares tendered in excess of the accepted quantity, or rejected or unaccepted Equity Shares, will be returned or credited as applicable.
- In the case of dematerialized Equity Shares, once the Equity Shares have been accepted by the Clearing Corporation and the bid has been submitted in the exchange bidding system, the Buyback will be deemed to have been accepted. Submission of a tender form and TRS is not required.
- In the case of custodian participant orders, any Equity Shares to be returned will be refunded to the respective custodian depository pool account.

## 13.12. Procedure to be followed by the Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form:

- Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares who intend to participate in the Buyback must approach their respective Stock Broker(s) with:
  - The tender form, duly filled in, signed, and attested as applicable.
  - A self-attested copy of the Eligible Shareholder’s PAN card.
  - Valid share transfer form(s), namely Form SH-4, duly filled in and signed by all registered Eligible Shareholders in the same order in which they hold the Equity Shares.
  - Any other relevant documents required for verification, including a duly attested death certificate and succession certificate or probated will, where applicable.
  - If the Eligible Shareholder’s address has changed from the address registered with the Company, a self-attested copy of address proof, such as an Aadhaar card, voter identity card, or passport.
- Based on these documents, the Stock Broker will place a bid on behalf of the Eligible Shareholder using the Acquisition Window of BSE/NSE. The bid will include the required details, including the share certificate number, distinctive number, and number of Equity Shares.
- A Stock Broker or Eligible Shareholder who places a bid on the last day of the Tendering Period must ensure that one copy of the TRS for physical Equity Shares, together with the original share certificate(s) and the required documents, reaches the Registrar on or before the Buyback Window Closing Date. The Registrar will retain the documents and provide an acknowledgement. Where bids are placed before the last day, the Stock Broker or Eligible Shareholder should obtain acknowledgement of the bid from the Stock Broker or Eligible Shareholder, as applicable.
- Eligible Shareholders who have submitted physical Equity Shares for dematerialization may participate in the Buyback before the Buyback Window Closing Date.
- Physical Equity Shares will not be accepted unless the complete set of required documents is submitted, subject to any further directions issued in this regard. The Registrar will verify the bids based on the submitted documents and initially display them as unconfirmed physical bids. After verification, the bids will be treated as confirmed bids.
- Where the Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis, a Non-Resident Eligible Shareholder should obtain and enclose a letter from its authorized dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account. If the certificate cannot be produced, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis, allowing the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
