## 14. METHOD OF SETTLEMENT

The Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders who are Non-Resident Shareholders may be rejected if the required documents are not enclosed with the tender form.

Settlement of trades will follow the applicable secondary-market mechanism.

### 14.2

The Company will transfer the consideration payable for the Buyback, net of tax deducted at source, to the Clearing Corporation through the prescribed secondary-market mechanism. The Clearing Corporation will transfer the funds to the respective Eligible Shareholders’ bank accounts using bank-account details available from the depositories.

If the funds cannot be transferred to an Eligible Shareholder’s bank account, the amount payable will be transferred to the concerned Stock Broker’s settlement account for onward transfer to the Eligible Shareholder.

### 14.3

For certain client types, including NRIs and foreign clients, specific RBI and other regulatory requirements may apply. Relevant details will be collected from the depositories, and funds will be released in accordance with the applicable mechanism prescribed by the Indian Stock Exchanges and the Clearing Corporation.

### 14.5

Details of each shareholder’s entitlement for the Tender Offer will be provided as part of the settlement process. On the settlement date, all blocked shares relating to accepted bids will be transferred to the Clearing Corporations.

### 14.6

In the case of inter-depository settlement, the Clearing Corporations will cancel or release excess or unaccepted blocked shares in the target depository after matching the bid-acceptance details.

The target depository may release the lien only after receiving an IDT message from the source depository. The source depository will cancel or release the excess or unaccepted blocked shares in the shareholder’s demat account based on the communication or message received.

### 14.7

Equity Shares bought back in dematerialized form will be transferred directly to the Company’s demat account through the Clearing Corporation and settlement mechanism, as indicated by the Company or the Company’s Broker.

Unaccepted physical Equity Shares will be returned to the relevant Eligible Shareholders by registered post, ordinary post, or courier, as applicable, at the Eligible Shareholder’s sole risk. Eligible Shareholders should ensure that their depository account remains maintained until all Buyback formalities are completed.

## 14.8: In relation to the physical Equity Shares:

The Company encourages Eligible Shareholders holding physical shares to dematerialize them.

If physical Equity Shares are tendered and accepted, the relevant physical share certificates will be handled in accordance with the applicable SEBI requirements.

If only a portion of the physical shares represented by a Letter of Confirmation (LOC) is accepted in the Buyback, the Company will not issue a split share certificate for the unaccepted shares. Instead, a new consolidated Letter of Confirmation will be issued for the accepted shares, and the unaccepted physical shares will be dealt with through the Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) in accordance with the applicable procedure.

The RTA will retain the original share certificate and issue the necessary Letter of Confirmation. The Eligible Shareholder must submit a dematerialization request within 120 days from the date of issue. If the dematerialization request is not submitted within this period, the RTA will credit the physical Equity Shares to a separate demat account of the Company opened for this purpose. The lien marked against the unaccepted Equity Shares will remain valid for 120 days from the date of issue.

### 14.9

The Stock Broker will issue a contract note for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback. The Company’s Broker will also issue a contract note to the Company for those Equity Shares.

### 14.10

Eligible Shareholders participating in the Buyback will bear all costs, applicable taxes, charges, and expenses, including brokerage, levied by their Stock Broker in connection with the sale of Equity Shares in the Buyback through the secondary market.

The Company will not be responsible for such costs.
