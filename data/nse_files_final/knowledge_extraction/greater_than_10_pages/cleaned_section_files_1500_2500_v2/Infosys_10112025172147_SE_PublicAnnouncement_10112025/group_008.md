## 13.11.Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Sharesin dematerialized form:

- Stock brokers will place orders or bids on behalf of eligible shareholders wishing to tender dematerialized Equity Shares in the Buyback through the Acquisition Window of BSE/NSE.
- A lien will be marked on the demat account of the eligible shareholder for the shares tendered, as applicable, through the National Securities Depository Limited and Central Depository Services Limited to the Clearing Corporations.
- Upon placing the bid, the stock broker will provide a Transaction Registration Slip (TRS) generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS will contain details of the order submitted, including the Bid ID, application number, DP ID, client ID, and number of Equity Shares.
- For dematerialized Equity Shares, submission of a tender form and TRS is not required if a valid bid is entered in the exchange bidding system. After receipt of the dematerialized Equity Shares by the Clearing Corporation, the Buyback will be deemed to have been accepted for the Equity Shares accepted under the prorated Buyback decided by the Company.
- Excess dematerialized Equity Shares or unaccepted dematerialized Equity Shares, if any, will be returned by the Clearing Corporation. If the security transfer is not completed, the shares will be transferred back to the shareholder.
- For custodian participant orders, the custodian must confirm or reject the order. If the custodian rejects an order, the stock broker may revoke the custodian confirmation and submit the revised order to the custodian again for confirmation. Orders not confirmed by the custodian by the last day of the Tendering Period will be deemed rejected.
- Eligible shareholders must provide relevant documents relating to the Equity Shares tendered, as applicable, including:
  - A duly attested death certificate and succession certificate, legal heirship certificate, or probated will if an eligible shareholder has died.
  - For companies, the necessary certified corporate authorizations, including board and/or general meeting resolutions.
  - A duly attested power of attorney or other relevant corporate authorization, where applicable.
- Non-resident shareholders holding Equity Shares on a repatriation basis should obtain and enclose a letter from their authorized dealer or bank confirming that the Equity Shares were acquired on a repatriation basis. If the shareholder cannot provide the certificate, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis, and the eligible shareholder must submit a consent letter addressed to the Company allowing the Company to make the required remittance for Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
- Eligible shareholders holding physical Equity Shares must follow the procedure set out in Section 13.12.

## 13.12.Procedure to be followed by the Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form:

- Eligible shareholders holding physical Equity Shares and intending to tender them must approach their stock broker with the required documents for verification.
- Required documents include:
  - The original Equity Share certificate or certificates.
  - Valid share transfer form or forms, Form SH-4, duly filled in and signed by all registered shareholders in the same order in which they hold the Equity Shares, including the signatures registered with the Company.
  - A duly witnessed transfer authorization in favour of the transferee, where applicable.
  - Proof of address, such as an Aadhaar card, voter identity card, or passport, if the shareholder’s address has changed from the address registered in the Company’s Register of Members.
  - Other relevant documents, including a duly attested power of attorney, corporate authorization, death certificate, succession certificate, legal heirship certificate, or probated will, where applicable.
- Based on the submitted documents, the concerned stock broker will place the bid on behalf of eligible shareholders holding physical Equity Shares and intending to tender them through the Acquisition Window of BSE/NSE. The bid will include details such as the folio number, Equity Share certificate number, distinctive number, and number of Equity Shares tendered.
- The stock broker or eligible shareholder placing a bid must ensure that the complete set of documents reaches the Registrar to the Buyback on or before the Buyback Window Closing Date. The stock broker will provide an acknowledgement of the documents received.
- Physical Equity Shares will not be accepted unless the complete set of documents is submitted and the tender is verified in accordance with the Buyback Regulations and applicable directions. Until confirmation by the Registrar to the Buyback, such bids will be displayed as unconfirmed physical bids. Once confirmed, the bids will be treated as confirmed bids.
- If physical Equity Shares are submitted for dematerialization, the eligible shareholder should ensure that the dematerialization process is completed well in time so that the shareholder can participate in the Buyback before the Buyback Window Closing Date.
- Non-resident shareholders holding Equity Shares on a repatriation basis should obtain and enclose a letter from their authorized dealer or bank confirming that the Equity Shares were acquired on a repatriation basis. If the certificate cannot be provided, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis, and the eligible shareholder must submit a consent letter addressed to the Company allowing the Company to make the required remittance for Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
- Physical Equity Shares tendered without the applicable documents by eligible shareholders, including non-resident shareholders, are liable to be rejected.

## 14.METHOD OFSETTLEMENT

### 14.1.

The settlement of trades will be carried out in a manner similar to the settlement of trades in the secondary market.

### 14.2.

For dematerialized Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback, the Clearing Corporation will make a direct funds pay-out to the respective eligible shareholders’ bank accounts linked to their demat accounts, based on the applicable settlement schedule. The Company will transfer the consideration pertaining to the accepted Equity Shares to the Clearing Corporation.

If the fund transfer instructions for an eligible shareholder are rejected by the relevant bank for any reason, the amount payable will be transferred to the concerned stock broker’s settlement account for onward transfer to the eligible shareholder.

### 14.3.

For certain client types, including non-resident and foreign clients, where specific legal or regulatory requirements apply to funds pay-out and the eligible shareholder does not opt to settle through a custodian, the funds pay-out will be made to the respective stock broker’s settlement accounts for release to the eligible shareholder.

For bids settled through custodians, funds will be transferred to the custodian’s settlement bank account in accordance with the applicable mechanism prescribed from time to time. The relevant bank details will be collected from the Depositories.

### 14.4.

For eligible shareholders holding physical Equity Shares, the funds pay-out will be made to the respective stock brokers’ settlement accounts for release to the respective eligible shareholders.

### 14.5.

Details relating to the eligible shareholder’s entitlement for the accepted bid will be transferred to the Clearing Corporations. The Clearing Corporations will cancel the excess or unaccepted shares blocked in the shareholder’s demat account and release the remaining blocked shares in accordance with the settlement date.

### 14.6.

In an inter-depository transfer, the target depository will transfer the accepted bid shares from the shareholder’s demat account to the Clearing Corporation’s settlement account on the settlement date based on the communication or message received from the source depository, the Company, or the Registrar to the Buyback.

The target depository will cancel or release excess or unaccepted blocked shares after receiving the requisite details, including the demat debit or cancellation request. If the target depository does not receive a release message, it will not be able to release the lien.
