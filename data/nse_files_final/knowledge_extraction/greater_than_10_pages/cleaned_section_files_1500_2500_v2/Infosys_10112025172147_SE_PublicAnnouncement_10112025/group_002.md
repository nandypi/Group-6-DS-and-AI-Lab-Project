## Buyback of Equity Shares

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 amended the tax treatment of buybacks with effect from October 1, 2024, shifting the tax liability to shareholders. The Company is not required to pay buyback tax on distributed income but is required to deduct income tax at source.

Buyback consideration is treated as dividend in the hands of shareholders. No deduction is allowed against such dividend when computing income from other sources. The cost of acquisition of shares bought back by the Company is treated as a capital loss.

The Company is required to deduct tax at source at the standard prescribed rate of 10% under Section 194 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (ITA) for consideration payable to resident shareholders. Resident shareholders may submit a lower tax deduction certificate or other applicable documentation where permitted under the ITA.

For consideration payable to non-resident shareholders, tax is generally deducted at the prescribed rate of 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, under the ITA. Non-resident shareholders may claim benefits under an applicable Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), read with the Multilateral Instrument (MLI), subject to satisfying the applicable requirements and providing the requisite documents to the Company on or before the close of the Tendering Period.

Eligible Shareholders will receive a letter of offer containing further details on taxation. Shareholders are advised to consult their own legal, financial, and tax advisers regarding the tax consequences applicable to their circumstances.

### 1.2

Since the Buyback is more than 10% of the total paid-up equity share capital and free reserves of the Company, the Board sought shareholder approval under Section 68(2)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013, as amended, and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations.

The shareholders approved the Buyback through a Special Resolution by postal ballot. The Postal Ballot Notice was dated September 25, 2025, and the results were announced on November 6, 2025.

The Company proposed to buy back up to 10 crore fully paid-up equity shares at a price of ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash, for an aggregate amount not exceeding ₹18,000 crore.

The Buyback represents 24.31% of the aggregate fully paid-up equity share capital and free reserves based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements of the Company as at June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, and 21.68% on a consolidated basis. The Buyback is within the prescribed limit of 25%.

The Buyback will be undertaken through the tender offer route in accordance with the Buyback Regulations, the SEBI Circulars, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, the Company’s Articles of Association, and other applicable provisions.

### 1.3

In accordance with Regulation 5(vi)(a) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board or Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, provided that there is no change in the Buyback Offer Size.

### 1.4

The Buyback Offer Size does not include transaction costs, such as brokerage fees, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, applicable taxes including securities transaction tax, and public announcement-related expenses.

### 1.5

Given that the Company’s securities are registered under Section 12 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and that U.S. residents hold the Company’s ADSs and equity shares, the Company was required to seek exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on certain aspects of the tender offer procedures because of conflicting regulatory requirements. This relief was obtained after the Board’s approval of the Buyback offer.

### 1.6

The Equity Shares are traded under trading code 500209 on BSE Limited (BSE) and INFY on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE). The American Depositary Shares (ADSs) of the Company are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

### 1.7

The Buyback will be implemented using the mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchanges pursuant to tender offers under the SEBI circulars issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India, including:

- SEBI Circular CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015;
- SEBI Circular CFD/DCR2/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016;
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/DCR-I/CIR/P/2021/615 dated August 13, 2021; and
- SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-2/P/CIR/2023/35 dated March 8, 2023.

The Buyback will also comply with other applicable circulars, notifications, and amendments.

### 1.8

The Equity Shares will be bought back on a proportionate basis from all shareholders holding Equity Shares as of the Record Date, November 14, 2025, through the tender offer route prescribed under the Buyback Regulations.

This includes holders of the Company’s ADSs who cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date. NSE will provide the acquisition window for facilitating the tendering of Equity Shares under the Buyback. BSE will be the designated stock exchange.

### 1.9

Participation in the Buyback by Eligible Shareholders will be subject to the applicable tax consequences.

### 1.10

If an Eligible Shareholder is a U.S. holder as defined in the Company’s Form 6-K furnished to the SEC, participation in the Buyback will generally be a taxable transaction for U.S. federal income tax purposes.

Depending on the applicable U.S. federal income tax laws, a U.S. holder participating in the Buyback may generally be treated as recognizing gain or loss from the disposition of Equity Shares or as receiving a distribution from the Company. U.S. holders should review the U.S. tax considerations set forth in the Company’s Form 6-K and Letter of Offer and consult their own tax advisers regarding the applicability and effect of U.S. federal, state, and local tax laws, as well as the tax laws of any applicable non-U.S. jurisdiction and estate, gift, and inheritance laws.

### 1.11

The Buyback of Equity Shares from non-resident members, including holders of ADSs whose underlying Equity Shares are withdrawn, will be subject to the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA), and the rules and regulations framed thereunder, as applicable.

Participation by non-resident shareholders, including foreign corporate bodies, erstwhile Overseas Corporate Bodies, Foreign Institutional Investors, and other foreign investors, will be subject to applicable approvals and requirements of the Reserve Bank of India and other concerned authorities.

### 1.12

The Buyback will be undertaken on a proportionate basis from all Eligible Shareholders as of the Record Date, November 14, 2025.

A reservation of up to 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of Equity Shares to which Small Shareholders are entitled, whichever is higher, will be made for Small Shareholders. Small Shareholders are Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares with a market value of not more than ₹2 lakh as of the Record Date, based on the highest trading volume on the Indian stock exchanges registering the closing price on the Record Date.
