## 13.12. Procedure to be followed by the Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form:

- Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares and intending to participate in the Buyback must approach their respective stock broker(s) and submit the required documents, including the original Equity Share certificate(s), duly completed and signed tender form, valid share transfer form(s) (Form SH-4), and other applicable documents.
- If the Equity Shares are held in joint names, the tender form and related documents must be signed by all joint holders, as applicable.
- If the address of the Eligible Shareholder has changed from the address registered in the Company's Register of Members, a self-attested copy of the new address proof must be submitted along with other relevant documents.
- If the original Eligible Shareholder has deceased, applicable documents may include an attested power of attorney, corporate authorization, death certificate, succession certificate, or probated will, as applicable.
- The Stock Broker will submit the bid through the Acquisition Window provided by BSE/NSE. The bid should include details such as the folio number, Equity Share certificate number, distinctive number, and number of Equity Shares tendered.
- The Stock Broker or Eligible Shareholder placing a bid for physical Equity Shares must deliver the original Equity Share certificate(s) and applicable documents to the Registrar to the Buyback at the office specified in Paragraph 17, within two days of the closing of the Tendering Period. During the last two days of the Tendering Period, documents should be sent by courier or hand delivery so that they reach the Registrar on or before the Buyback Closing Date.
- The Stock Broker or Eligible Shareholder should ensure that the documents reach the Registrar on or before the Buyback Closing Date. One copy of the TRS will be retained by the Registrar to the Buyback.
- Physical Equity Shares will be subject to verification under the Buyback Regulations. The Buyback will verify bids based on the documents submitted and may request further information or documents.
- Physical Equity Shares will not be accepted unless the complete set of applicable documents is submitted. Until the Registrar to the Buyback confirms the bids, they will be treated as “Confirmed Bids.”
- If the Equity Shares are held on a repatriation basis by an Eligible Shareholder who is a Non-Resident Shareholder, the shareholder should obtain and enclose a letter from the authorised dealer bank confirming that payment for acquiring the Equity Shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the Reserve Bank of India. If the Non-Resident Shareholder cannot provide the confirmation, the Equity Shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The letter should allow the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.
- If an Eligible Shareholder has submitted physical Equity Shares for dematerialization, the shareholder should ensure that the dematerialization process is completed in time to participate in the Buyback before the Buyback Window Closing Date.
- If any of the above-stated documents, as applicable, are not submitted, the relevant bid may not be accepted.

## 14. METHOD OF SETTLEMENT

### 14.1.

The settlement of trade(s) will be carried out in a manner similar to the settlement of trade(s) in the secondary market.

### 14.2.

The Company will transfer the consideration for the Buyback, net of tax deducted at source, to the Clearing Corporation through the Company's Broker for onward transfer to the respective Eligible Shareholder's bank account linked to the demat account.

If the fund transfer instruction is rejected by the RBI or the relevant bank for any reason, the amount payable to the Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialized form will be transferred to the concerned Stock Broker's settlement account for release to the respective Eligible Shareholder.

### 14.3.

For certain client types, including NRIs and foreign clients, where specific bank or regulatory requirements apply, settlement payouts relating to the bids will be made through custodians. For clients who do not opt to settle through a custodian, the settlement account details will be collected from the Depositories and the funds will be transferred to the respective Eligible Shareholder's bank account.

### 14.5.

Details relating to the shareholder's entitlement for the Tender Offer will be transferred to the Clearing Corporations. The Clearing Corporations will cancel the excess or unaccepted blocked shares in the shareholder's demat account. On the settlement date, all blocked shares relating to accepted bids will be processed based on the relevant settlement details.

### 14.6.

In the case of inter-depository transactions, the target depository will debit the securities from the shareholder's demat account and credit them to the Clearing Corporation based on the communication or message received from the source depository. The source depository will provide details of accepted bid shares received from the Company or the Registrar to the Buyback.

The target depository will cancel or release excess or unaccepted block shares in the shareholder's demat account after completion of the Tendering Period and receipt of the requisite details, including the demat account details and accepted bid quantity.

### 14.7.

The Equity Shares bought back in dematerialized form and accepted under the Buyback will be transferred directly from the Company's Broker to the Company's Demat Account through the clearing and settlement mechanism.

## 14.8. In relation to the physical Equity Shares:

- The Company encourages Eligible Shareholders holding physical shares to dematerialize their physical shares.
- If physical shares are not accepted, the share certificate will be returned to the Eligible Shareholder by registered post or courier at the Eligible Shareholder's sole risk.
- If only a portion of the physical shares is accepted in the Buyback, the Company will issue a Letter of Confirmation (LOC) for the unaccepted shares instead of issuing a split share certificate, in accordance with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD_RTAMB/P/CIR/2022/8 dated January 25, 2022, as amended.
- The LOC will be valid for 120 days from the date of issuance. The Eligible Shareholder must complete the required formalities within this period, including maintaining a depository account and submitting the dematerialization request.
- The LOC will be dispatched to the address registered with the RTA. The RTA will retain the original share certificate and deface it with a stamp of “Confirmation Issued” on the face or reverse of the certificate.
- If the Eligible Shareholder fails to submit the dematerialization request within the applicable period, the LOC will be dispatched by registered post or courier at the Eligible Shareholder's sole risk, and the unaccepted physical Equity Shares will be returned after completion of the Buyback formalities.

### 14.9.

The Stock Broker will issue a contract note for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback. The Company's Broker will also issue a contract note to the Company for the Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback.

### 14.10.

Eligible Shareholders who intend to participate in the Buyback must bear all applicable taxes, charges, and expenses, including brokerage levied by the Stock Broker. Eligible Shareholders should consult their respective stock brokers regarding payment of these costs. The Company will not bear these costs.

### 14.11.

The Equity Shares lying to the credit of the Company Demat Account and the Equity Shares bought back and accepted in physical form will be extinguished in the manner and following the procedure prescribed in the Buyback Regulations.

## 15.1.

As required under the Buyback Regulations, the Company has fixed November 14, 2025 as the record date (“Record Date”) for determining the entitlement and the names of the equity shareholders eligible to participate in the Buyback.

The tender period for the Buyback offer will commence from November 20, 2025, not later than four working days after the Record Date, and will remain open for a period of five working days.
