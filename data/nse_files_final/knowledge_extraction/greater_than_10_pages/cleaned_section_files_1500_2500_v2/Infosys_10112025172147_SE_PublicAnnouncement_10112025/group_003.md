## 2. NECESSITY FOR THE BUYBACK AND DETAILS THEREOF

The Buyback is being undertaken after considering the Company's medium-term strategic and operational cash requirements and the need to return surplus funds to members in line with its capital allocation policy. Factors considered include:

- Investments required to execute the Company's strategy.
- Expected cash requirements for working capital, capital expenditure, technology, and infrastructure.
- Funds required for acquisitions approved by the Board.
- Other significant developments requiring cash investments.
- Minimum cash required for contingencies or unforeseen events.

The Company states that its expected cash generation and strong balance sheet position allow it to reward members while retaining sufficient capital for business requirements. Since 2017, the Company has undertaken multiple buybacks.

The Buyback is intended to:

- Return surplus cash to members in line with the Company's stated capital allocation policy.
- Improve return on equity through an increase in members' value.
- Distribute cash and improve earnings per share over the long term by reducing the equity base.
- Give members the option to increase their percentage shareholding after the Buyback without additional investment, or to sell their Equity Shares and receive cash.

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had consolidated cash and non-current investments, excluding investments in equity instruments and comprising cash and cash equivalents, current preference shares, and others, of ₹45,204 crore.

Under its capital allocation policy, effective from financial year 2025, the Company expects to continue returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period, excluding special dividends, through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share while reducing the equity base.

The Buyback is being implemented through a tender offer. The source states that the number of shares allocated to Small Shareholders would be the higher of:

- The number of shares entitled based on their shareholding; or
- 15% of the number of shares proposed to be bought back under Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations.

## MAXIMUM NUMBER OF EQUITY SHARES THAT THE COMPANY PROPOSES TO BUYBACK

The source states that the indicative number of Equity Shares to be bought back would comprise approximately 2.41% of the total Equity Shares on a standalone basis.

## 4. MAXIMUM AMOUNT OF FUNDS REQUIRED FOR THE BUYBACK AND SOURCES OF FUNDS FROM WHICH BUYBACK WILL BE FINANCED

The amount required for the Buyback will aggregate to ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only). This represents 24.31% and 6.8% of the aggregate paid-up capital and free reserves of the Company, based on the financial statements as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis respectively, and is less than 25% on both bases.

The Buyback will be financed out of the Company's free reserves and securities premium account, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations and the Act. Borrowed funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, will not be used for the Buyback.

The Buyback Offer Size excludes brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, goods and services tax, stamp duty, applicable taxes such as securities transaction tax, publication, printing and dispatch expenses, and other incidental transaction costs incurred or to be incurred for the Buyback.

## 5. BUYBACK PRICE AND BASIS OF ARRIVING AT THE BUYBACK OFFER PRICE

The Equity Shares are proposed to be bought back at a price of ₹1,800 per Equity Share.

The Board or Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of Equity Shares bought back, in accordance with Regulation 5(vi)(a) of the Buyback Regulations. Factors considered include the volume-weighted average market prices of the Equity Shares on the BSE and NSE, price-to-earnings ratio, other financial parameters, and the potential impact of the Buyback on earnings per Equity Share.

The Buyback Price represents:

- A premium of 20.78% and 20.67% to the volume-weighted average market price of the Equity Shares on the BSE and NSE, respectively, during the two-week period ending September 8, 2025, being the date of the intimation to the stock exchanges regarding the proposal of the Buyback being considered at the Board Meeting.
- A premium of 25.64% and 25.62% over the closing price of the Equity Shares on the BSE and NSE, respectively.
