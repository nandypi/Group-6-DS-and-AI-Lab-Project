## INTENTION OF THE PROMOTERANDPROMOTER COMPANY TO TENDER THEIR EQUITY SHARES IN GROUP AND PERSONS IN CONTROL OF THE THE BUYBACK

- The promoters and promoter group have expressed their intention not to participate in the buyback. Accordingly, equity shares held by members of the promoter and promoter group will not be considered when computing the entitlement ratio. Eligible shareholders may therefore receive a higher buyback entitlement ratio.
- The source states that the number of equity shares proposed to be purchased under the buyback is `1000000`, described in the source text as “Ten crore.” The source text also states that the aggregate amount of the buyback is `1 crore`; the surrounding numerical text is unclear.
- The Company shall not make any buyback offer within one year from the expiry of the buyback period.

## NO DEFAULTS

The Company confirms that:

- It has not accepted any deposits.
- There are no subsisting defaults in the redemption of debentures or payment of interest.
- There are no defaults in the redemption of preference shares.
- There are no defaults in repayment of loans or payment of interest to financial institutions or banking companies.

## 9. CONFIRMATION BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE COMPANY

The Board of Directors confirms that it has made a full enquiry into the affairs and prospects of the Company and has formed the following opinions:

- Having regard to the Board meeting held on September 4, 2025, the Board meeting approving the buyback on November 4, 2025, and the members’ resolution dated November 4, 2025, the Company will be able to meet its liabilities and will not be rendered insolvent within one year from the date of the members’ resolution.
- Immediately following the date of the Board meeting approving the buyback, the Company will be able to pay its debts.
- In forming these opinions, the Board considered all liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, as if the Company were being wound up under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.
- All equity shares proposed to be bought back are fully paid-up.

## 10. CONFIRMATIONS FROM THE COMPANY AS PER THE COMPANIES ACT AND THE PROVISIONS OF THE BUYBACK REGULATIONS

The Company confirms that:

- Subject to applicable law, it shall not issue shares or other specified securities during the buyback period and for the period ending six months after completion of the buyback, except for the discharge of subsisting obligations, including conversion of warrants, stock option schemes, sweat equity, preference shares, or debentures into equity shares.
- It shall not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the buyback period, except in discharge of subsisting obligations.
- It shall not buy back locked-in or non-transferable equity shares until the lock-in expires or the shares become transferable.
- It shall not purchase its equity shares through negotiated deals, whether on or off the stock exchanges, spot transactions, or private arrangements.
- Funds borrowed from banks and financial institutions will not be used for the buyback.
- There is no pending scheme of amalgamation, compromise, or arrangement under the Companies Act as of the date of the public announcement.
- The buyback will not contravene Regulation 4(vi) of the Buyback Regulations.
- It shall earmark and arrange adequate funds for the buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.
- It shall not directly or indirectly purchase its own equity shares or other specified securities through any subsidiary, including its own subsidiary companies, or through any investment company or group of investment companies.
- Payment for the buyback will be made only in cash.
- The ratio of the aggregate secured and unsecured debt owed by the Company will not exceed twice its paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone or consolidated financial statements as of June 30, 2025.
- The buyback offer shall not be withdrawn after the public announcement is made.
- The Company shall ensure the consequent reduction of share capital. Equity shares bought back shall be extinguished and physically destroyed in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and the Companies Act.
- It shall transfer, from free reserves or securities premium account, an amount equal to the nominal value of the equity shares purchased through the buyback to the capital redemption premium account, or to another source permitted under applicable law, as disclosed in subsequent audited financial statements.
- The Company does not have outstanding debt owed to lenders that requires their consent for the buyback.

## 11. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION FOR HOLDERS OF THE COMPANY'S AMERICAN DEPOSITARY SHARES

- ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs directly in the buyback.
- To participate, ADS holders must withdraw the underlying equity shares before the record date and become holders of the equity shares. They must:
  - Apply for and obtain a Permanent Account Number from the Indian Income Tax Department.
  - Establish an account with a bank, broker, or nominee in India.
  - Establish an Indian demat account with the relevant subtype and a brokerage account sufficiently in advance of the record date.
  - Submit the desired number of ADSs for cancellation to Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, acting as depositary.
  - Tender some or all of the withdrawn equity shares during the tendering period.
- Equity shares trade on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited and BSE Limited and cannot be traded on the New York Stock Exchange.
- An ADS holder who cancels ADSs and withdraws the underlying equity shares before the record date may receive a buyback entitlement based on the number of equity shares held as of the record date.
- If an ADS holder withdraws the underlying equity shares after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date but before the last date of the tendering period, the withdrawn equity shares will not be accepted in the buyback. Equity shares not tendered may be re-deposited to create ADSs, subject to the applicable depositary procedures.
- The depositary has agreed to waive the ADS issuance fee that would otherwise be payable for issuing ADSs representing equity shares re-deposited during the 30-day period, together with the required ADS issuance instructions.
