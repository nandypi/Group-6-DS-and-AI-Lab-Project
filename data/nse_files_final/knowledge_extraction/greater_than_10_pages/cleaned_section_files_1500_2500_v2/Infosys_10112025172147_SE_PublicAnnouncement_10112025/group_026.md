## 13.4. Acquisition Window and Tendering Orders

The Company shall provide a separate acquisition window for the Buyback on the designated stock exchanges, BSE and NSE. Details of the acquisition window will be specified by the Indian stock exchanges.

During the Tendering Period, Eligible Shareholders must place orders to sell Equity Shares through registered stock brokers during normal secondary-market trading hours. Stock brokers may enter orders for Equity Shares held in dematerialized or physical form.

Eligible Shareholders who wish to tender Equity Shares in electronic form must do so through their respective Stock Broker(s), providing details of the Equity Shares intended to be tendered.

The cumulative quantity tendered will be made available on the Indian stock exchange websites at specific intervals during the Tendering Period.

The Buyback Regulations do not restrict Eligible Shareholders from placing multiple bids or modifying bids. Eligible Shareholders should consult their respective Stock Brokers regarding modification or cancellation of multiple bids from a single demat account.

The Company will not accept Equity Shares that are:

- Under restriction or otherwise non-transferable.
- Subject to a request for duplicate share certificates that has not been completed.
- Locked-in under applicable law.
- Subject to a dispute or where title is not clear.

Reporting requirements for Non-Resident Shareholders under FEMA and applicable requirements of the RBI, including remittance of funds, must be complied with by the Eligible Shareholders and/or the Stock Brokers through whom the orders or bids are placed.

## 13.11. Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialized form

1. Stock Broker(s) will place orders on behalf of Eligible Shareholders who wish to tender Equity Shares on BSE or NSE.

2. The lien will be marked in the demat account of the Eligible Shareholder for the Equity Shares tendered. Details of the Equity Shares marked as lien will be provided by the depositories to the Clearing Corporations, as applicable. The depositories include National Securities Depository Limited and Central Depository Services (India) Limited. The Clearing Corporations include the NSE Clearing Limited and BSE Clearing Limited, as applicable.

3. Where the shareholder’s demat account and the broker’s clearing account are held with different depositories, the shareholder must initiate a Depository Tender Offer instruction with the source depository during the Tendering Period. The source depository will block the tendered shares in the shareholder’s demat account for confirming creation of the lien and transfer the details to the clearing member or Clearing Corporation.

4. For custodian participant orders involving dematerialized Equity Shares, any order modification by the Stock Broker(s) will revoke the custodian confirmation. The revised order will be sent to the custodian again for confirmation. The custodian must confirm or reject the order by the last day of the Tendering Period, referred to as the Buyback Window Closing Date. Unconfirmed orders will be deemed rejected.

5. After placing the bid, the Stock Broker(s) will provide the Eligible Shareholder with a Transaction Registration Slip (TRS) generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS will contain details of the order submitted.

6. For dematerialized Equity Shares, submission of a tender form and TRS is not required. After receipt of the dematerialized Equity Shares by the Clearing Corporation and a valid bid in the exchange bidding system, the Buyback will be deemed to have been accepted for the relevant Equity Shares.

7. Eligible Shareholders must provide documents necessary to ensure transferability of the Equity Shares tendered. These documents may include:

   - A duly attested power of attorney where a person other than the Eligible Shareholder has signed the tender form.
   - A duly attested death certificate and succession certificate or probate where the registered shareholder is deceased.
   - For companies, certified corporate authorizations, including board and/or general meeting resolutions.

8. Eligible Shareholders must keep their DP accounts active and unblocked to receive credit for Equity Shares returned because of rejection or excess shares tendered, as well as any remittance due for accepted Equity Shares.

9. If a security transfer instruction is rejected in the depository system, or if the demat account is otherwise unable to receive the Equity Shares, the unaccepted dematerialized Equity Shares will be transferred to the shareholder’s broker’s depository pool account for onward transfer to the shareholder’s depository pool account.

10. In the case of custodian participant orders, any rejected shares will be refunded to the respective custodian.

## 13.12. Procedure to be followed by the Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical form

1. Eligible Shareholders holding physical Equity Shares must approach their respective Stock Broker(s) with the complete set of documents required for verification. The documents may include:

   - A valid share transfer form, Form SH-4, duly completed and signed by all registered Eligible Shareholders in the same order as in the Register of Members, including signatures registered with the Company and signatures witnessed at the appropriate place.
   - The original Equity Share certificate(s).
   - The tender form, duly signed by all Eligible Shareholders.
   - A certified corporate authorization, including a board resolution and specimen signature, where applicable.
   - A notarized copy of a death certificate and succession certificate or probate where the original Eligible Shareholder is deceased, as applicable.
   - Address proof, such as an Aadhaar card, voter identity card, or passport, where the registered address has changed.

2. A Stock Broker will provide a TRS generated by the exchange bidding system to the Eligible Shareholder. The TRS will contain details such as the folio number, distinctive number, and number of Equity Shares tendered.

3. Physical Equity Shares will not be accepted unless the complete set of required documents is submitted. BSE/NSE will display physical bids as unconfirmed until the documents are submitted. The Company will verify the bids based on the documents submitted on a daily basis and mark valid bids as confirmed.

4. The Stock Broker or Eligible Shareholder placing a bid for physical Equity Shares must deliver the original Equity Share certificate(s) and required documents, together with the TRS generated by the stock exchange bidding system, to the Registrar for the Buyback at the address specified in the Buyback documents.

5. The envelope should be superscribed with:

   `Infosys Limited—Buyback 2025`

6. The original certificates and documents must be delivered during the last two days of the Tendering Period, by the Buyback Window Closing Date. One copy of the TRS will be retained by the Registrar to the Buyback.

7. Where an Eligible Shareholder has submitted Equity Shares for dematerialization and intends to participate in the Buyback, the dematerialization process must be completed in sufficient time before the Buyback Window Closing Date.

8. If the required documents are not enclosed with the tender form, the Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders, including Non-Resident Shareholders, may be rejected.

9. A Non-Resident Shareholder holding Equity Shares on a repatriation basis must obtain and enclose a letter from its authorized dealer confirming that the Equity Shares were acquired on a repatriation basis. The letter must allow the Company to make payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback if the required documentation is not available.

10. A Non-Resident Shareholder holding Equity Shares on a non-repatriation basis must submit the required consent or confirmation from its authorized dealer that the Equity Shares were acquired on a non-repatriation basis.
