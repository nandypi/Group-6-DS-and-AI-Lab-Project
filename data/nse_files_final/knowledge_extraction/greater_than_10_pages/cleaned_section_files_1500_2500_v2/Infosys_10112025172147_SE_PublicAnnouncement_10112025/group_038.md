## 15. RECORD DATE, OFFER PERIOD AND SHAREHOLDER ENTITLEMENT

### 15.1 Record Date and Tender Period

As required under the Buyback Regulations, November 14, 2025 was fixed as the record date for determining the equity shareholders eligible to participate in the buyback.

The tender period for the buyback offer will remain open for 5 days.

### 15.2 Letter of Offer

Eligible shareholders may participate and tender shares in the buyback in accordance with the Letter of Offer and tender offer form, which will indicate each eligible shareholder’s entitlement.

The Letter of Offer will be dispatched through electronic mode in accordance with applicable law. A physical copy will be sent where required, including to eligible shareholders in the United States who have not registered an email address with the depositories and to ADS holders where applicable.

### 15.3 Eligibility of Equity Shareholders and ADS Holders

All equity shareholders of the Company as on the record date will be eligible to participate in the buyback.

ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs directly in the buyback. To participate, an ADS holder must cancel all or the desired portion of the ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares before the record date. The underlying equity shares must be held by the ADS holder as an equity shareholder as on the record date. ADSs corresponding to equity shares tendered in the buyback, or accepted in the buyback, cannot be re-deposited.

### 15.4 Dispatch of the Letter of Offer

The Letter of Offer will be dispatched in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Buyback Regulations and the Companies Act.

### 15.5 Buyback Categories and Entitlement

The equity shares proposed to be bought back by the Company will be divided into two categories. The entitlement of each category will be calculated according to the equity shares held by shareholders in that category.

### 15.6 Small Shareholder Definition

A “Small Shareholder” is a shareholder who holds equity shares having a market value of not more than ₹2,00,000, based on the closing price of the shares on the stock exchange having the highest trading volume in respect of the equity shares.

### 15.7 Reservation for Small Shareholders

In accordance with the proviso to Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, 15% of the number of equity shares reserved for the small shareholders, or the number of equity shares entitled to be tendered by small shareholders based on their shareholding as on the record date, whichever is higher, will be reserved for small shareholders.

### 15.8 Calculation of Buyback Entitlement

The Company will determine each eligible shareholder’s entitlement to tender equity shares based on:

- the equity shares held by the shareholder on the record date; and
- the buyback entitlement ratio applicable to the category in which the shareholder belongs.

The Company’s promoter and promoter group have expressed their intention not to participate in the buyback. The equity shares held by members of the promoter and promoter group will be considered for determining entitlement in accordance with the proviso to Regulation 4(iv)(a) of the Buyback Regulations. To that extent, eligible shareholders may have a higher buyback entitlement ratio.

### 15.9 PAN and Common Holdings

In accordance with Regulation 9(ix) of the Buyback Regulations, the Registrar to the buyback will verify the PAN of shareholders to determine whether they fall within the Small Shareholder or General category.

Equity shares held by shareholders with a common PAN will generally be clubbed together for determining entitlement. Joint shareholdings will be considered based on the sequence of PANs and names available in the beneficial position data as on the record date.

Equity shares held through different schemes or under different demat account nomenclature will not be clubbed where the holdings are considered to be held on behalf of investors such as mutual funds, pension funds, trusts, and insurance clients.

### 15.10 Acceptance of Shares Tendered

Equity shares tendered by eligible shareholders will first be considered for acceptance according to their entitlement. Eligible shareholders who tender shares above their entitlement may participate in any shortfall resulting from the non-participation of other eligible shareholders.

### 15.11 Voluntary Participation

Participation in the buyback is voluntary. Eligible shareholders may:

- tender their shares and receive cash for the equity shares accepted under the buyback;
- choose not to participate and increase their percentage shareholding after completion of the buyback, without additional investment;
- tender only part of their buyback entitlement; or
- tender additional equity shares above their entitlement.

The acceptance of additional equity shares will be subject to the buyback procedure and the Buyback Regulations.

### 15.12 Maximum Tender

The maximum number of equity shares that an eligible shareholder may tender in the buyback cannot exceed the number of equity shares held by that eligible shareholder as on the record date.

If an eligible shareholder holds equity shares through multiple demat accounts, the tender through a demat account cannot exceed the shareholder’s buyback entitlement.

### 15.13 Settlement

The equity shares tendered by eligible shareholders, including shares tendered above their entitlement, will be settled through the mechanism for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under the applicable Takeovers, Buy-Back and Delisting Regulations.

### 15.14 Further Details

Further details regarding the buyback, including the Letter of Offer, tender offer form, and relevant timetable, will be communicated to eligible shareholders in due course.
