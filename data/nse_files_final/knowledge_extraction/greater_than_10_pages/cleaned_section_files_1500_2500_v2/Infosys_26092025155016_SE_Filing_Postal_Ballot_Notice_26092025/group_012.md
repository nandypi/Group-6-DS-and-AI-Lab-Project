## 18. General obligations of the Company as per the provisions of the Buyback Regulations and the Act:

- All equity shares included in the Buyback are fully paid-up.
- Subject to applicable law, the Company shall not issue any shares or other specified securities, including by way of bonus issue, until the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations through conversion of warrants, stock option schemes, sweat equity, or conversion of preference shares or debentures into equity shares. Relevant details and the potential impact of these obligations shall be disclosed in the public announcement.
- Subject to applicable law, the Company shall not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations.
- Under Section 68(8) of the Act, the Company shall not make any further issue of the same kind of shares or other securities, including allotment of new shares under Section 62(1)(a) of the Act, within six months after completion of the Buyback. Exceptions include bonus shares and equity shares issued to discharge subsisting obligations.
- The special resolution approving the Buyback will be valid for a maximum period of one year from the date it is passed, or for any extended period permitted under the Act, the Buyback Regulations, or by the appropriate authorities. The exact Buyback timetable will be decided by the Board or its duly constituted committee within these time limits.
- Equity Shares bought back by the Company will be compulsorily cancelled and will not be held for re-issuance.
- The Company shall not buy back equity shares through negotiated deals, whether on or off stock exchanges, through spot transactions, or through private arrangements.
- The Company has not accepted any deposits either before or after the Companies Act, 2013. There are no subsisting defaults in:
  - redemption of debentures or payment of interest;
  - redemption of preference shares;
  - payment of dividends due to shareholders; or
  - repayment of term loans or payment of interest to financial institutions or banking companies.
- No scheme of amalgamation, compromise, or arrangement under the Act is pending as of the date of the notice.
- The Company shall earmark and arrange adequate sources of funds for the Buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.
- As of June 30, 2025, the ratio of aggregate secured and unsecured debt to paid-up share capital and free reserves is not, and will not be, more than twice the paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone or consolidated financial statements.
- The Buyback offer shall not be withdrawn once the public announcement has been made.
- The Company shall not buy back locked-in or non-transferable equity shares until the lock-in ends or the shares become transferable.

The audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, the Postal Ballot Notice and Explanatory Statement, the Memorandum and Articles of Association, the relevant Board resolution for the Buyback, and the Auditors’ Report dated September 11, 2025, are available for electronic inspection by members until the last date of e-voting.

No director, key managerial personnel, or their relatives is interested in or concerned with the resolution, except to the extent of their shareholding. The Board recommends approval of the resolution set out in Item No. 1.

Under Section 68(2)(b) of the Act, because the Buyback exceeds 10% of the total paid-up equity capital and free reserves of the Company, member consent through a special resolution is required.

## Remote e-voting:

- The Company is providing remote e-voting to enable members to vote electronically on the resolution set out in the Postal Ballot Notice.
- The remote e-voting period begins on October 6, 2025, at 09:00 hours IST and ends on November 4, 2025, at 17:00 hours IST. Voting will not be permitted after the closing time, and the NSDL e-voting module will be disabled after expiry of the period.
- Once a member has voted on the resolution, the vote cannot subsequently be changed.
- Voting rights are proportional to each member’s shareholding in the total issued and paid-up equity share capital as of the cut-off date, September 22, 2025.
- Members holding shares in physical or dematerialized form as of September 22, 2025, including members who may not receive the notice because their email address is not registered with the RTA or depository participant, may vote electronically through remote e-voting.

## How do I vote electronically using NSDL e-Voting system?

The NSDL e-Voting process consists of two steps.

## Step 1: Access to NSDL e-Voting system

## A) Login method for e-Voting for Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and depository participants. Shareholders are advised to update their mobile numbers and email addresses in their demat accounts.

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode with NSDL may access e-Voting through:

1. OTP-based login at the NSDL e-Services e-voting login page. Shareholders must enter their DP ID, Client ID, PAN, verification code, and OTP.
2. Existing IDeAS users may log in through the NSDL e-Services website, select **Access to e-Voting** under value-added services, and select the Company or NSDL as the e-voting service provider.
3. Unregistered IDeAS users may register through the NSDL e-Services website.
4. The NSDL e-Voting website at [www.evoting.nsdl.com](https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/) may be accessed using the demat account number, password or OTP, and verification code. After authentication, shareholders can select the Company or NSDL to cast their votes.
5. Shareholders and members may also use the NSDL Speede mobile application.
