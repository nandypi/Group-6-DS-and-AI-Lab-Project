## Explanatory statement pursuant to Sections 102(1) and 110 of the Companies Act, 2013

## Item No. 1 - Approval for the Buyback of Equity Shares of the Company

The Board approved the Company’s capital allocation policy at its meeting held on April 18, 2024, after considering its medium-term strategic and operational cash requirements.

Effective from financial year 2025, the Company expects to return approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. Dividend and buyback amounts include applicable taxes.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under International Financial Reporting Standards issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

At its meeting held on September 11, 2025, the Board approved the proposal to recommend a buyback for an aggregate amount of ₹18,000 crore. Because the Company has securities registered under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and has U.S. resident shareholders through ADSs and equity shares, the Company obtained exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for certain tender-offer procedures affected by differences between Indian and U.S. regulatory requirements.

As the buyback exceeds 10% of the Company’s total paid-up equity capital and free reserves, member approval by special resolution is required under Section 68(2)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013. Approval is being sought through postal ballot under Section 110 of the Act and Rule 22(16)(g) of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014.

## 1. Necessity for the Buyback

The buyback is being undertaken after considering the Company’s medium-term strategic and operational cash requirements and the return of surplus funds to members. Relevant financial parameters and internal factors include:

1. Expected working-capital requirements and capital expenditure on technology and infrastructure.
2. Investments required to execute the Company’s strategy.
3. Funds required for acquisitions that the Board may approve.
4. Minimum cash required for contingencies or unforeseen events.
5. Other significant developments requiring cash investments.

The Company stated that its expected cash generation and balance-sheet position allow it to return surplus cash while retaining sufficient capital for business requirements. Since 2017, the Company has undertaken multiple buybacks, together with regular and special dividends.

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had consolidated cash and investments of ₹45,204 crore. This comprised cash and cash equivalents, current and non-current investments excluding investments in equity and preference shares, and other investments.

The buyback is intended to:

- Return surplus cash to members in line with the capital allocation policy.
- Improve return on equity through cash distribution and improve earnings per share over the long term by reducing the equity base.
- Give members as of the Record Date the option to tender their equity shares for cash or retain them and increase their percentage shareholding after the buyback without additional investment.
- Reserve shares for Small Shareholders under the Tender Offer route. The reserved allocation will be the higher of their entitlement based on shareholding or 15% of the total number of shares proposed to be bought back, in accordance with Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations.

Under the capital allocation policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend. The proposed buyback is expected to reduce the equity base in line with the policy.

## 2. Maximum amount required under the Buyback and its percentage of the total paid up capital and free reserves

The funds required for the buyback will aggregate to ₹18,000 crore. This represents 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively. Both percentages are below 25%, based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025.

The Buyback Offer Size excludes expenses and transaction costs, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, public-announcement publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, securities transaction tax, goods and services tax, stamp duty, and other incidental and related expenses.

The proposed buyback will be funded from the Company’s free reserves and securities premium account, or another source permitted under the Buyback Regulations or the Act. Borrowed funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, will not be used.

## 3. Price at which the Equity Shares are proposed to be bought back and the basis of arriving at such price

The proposed Buyback Price is ₹1,800 per equity share.

The price was determined after considering factors including volume-weighted average market prices on BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited, price-earnings ratios, the effect on other financial parameters, and the possible effect on earnings per equity share.

Under Regulation 5(via) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board or Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and reduce the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the Buyback Offer Size does not change, until one working day before the Record Date.

The Buyback Price represents the following premiums:

- 15.60% over the BSE volume-weighted average market price and 16.99% over the NSE volume-weighted average market price during the three-month period ending September 8, 2025.
- 20.78% over the BSE volume-weighted average market price and 20.67% over the NSE volume-weighted average market price during the two-week period ending September 8, 2025.
- 25.64% over the BSE closing price and 25.62% over the NSE closing price as of September 8, 2025.

## 4. Maximum number of Equity Shares that the Company proposes to buyback

At the Buyback Price and Buyback Offer Size, the indicative number of equity shares to be bought back is 10,00,00,000, or ten crore equity shares. This represents approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis.

Until one working day before the Record Date, the Board or Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and reduce the number of equity shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the Buyback Offer Size does not change.

## 5.  Method to be adopted for Buyback as referred to in Regulation 4(iv)(a) of the Buyback Regulations

The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis through the Tender Offer route, subject to the reservation for Small Shareholders and the requirements of the Buyback Regulations. It will also follow the mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchanges pursuant to tender offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delistings, as prescribed by SEBI circulars.

The buyback will be implemented under the Companies Act, applicable rules, the Buyback Regulations, and the terms and conditions determined by the Company.

The Company will announce a Record Date to identify shareholders eligible to participate. The Record Date is expected to be on or around November 14, 2025. Shareholders holding equity shares as of the Record Date will receive a Letter of Offer and tender/offer form stating their entitlement.

The equity shares to be bought back will be divided into two categories:

- Reserved category for Small Shareholders.
- General category for all other shareholders.
