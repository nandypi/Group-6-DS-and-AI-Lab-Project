## Notes

1. The explanatory statement pursuant to Section 102 read with Section 110 of the Act, stating all material facts and the reasons for the proposal, is annexed to the Postal Ballot Notice.

2. The Postal Ballot Notice was sent to members whose names appeared on the register of members or the list of beneficial owners received from NSDL and CDSL, and whose email addresses were registered with the Company or their depository participant, as of September 22, 2025 (the “Cut-off Date”). Persons who were not members as of the Cut-off Date were to treat the Notice as informational only.

   Voting on the proposed resolution was restricted to electronic voting. Physical copies of the Notice, Postal Ballot Form, and prepaid business reply envelope were not being sent. Members could communicate their assent or dissent only through the remote e-voting system.

3. For ADS holders, the Depositary established September 22, 2025 as the “ADS postal ballot cut-off date” for determining registered ADS holders entitled to receive the postal ballot materials and provide voting instructions. The Depositary was expected to use the Notice to create its own voting instruction form for registered holders of American Depositary Receipts evidencing ADSs. Mailing agents for participants with The Depository Trust Company were instructed to mail the Notice to beneficial ADS holders holding through DTC in “street name.”

4. If passed by the requisite majority, the resolution would be deemed to have been passed on November 4, 2025, the last date specified for voting. A resolution passed through postal ballot is deemed to have been passed as if it had been passed at a general meeting of the members.

5. The Board appointed Hemanth, Holla & Co. (Membership No. FCS 6374; CP No. 6519), Practicing Company Secretaries, as scrutinizer for conducting the e-voting process. The scrutinizer was to submit a report to the Chairman or a person authorized by the Chairman after completing scrutiny of the e-voting. Voting results were to be announced no later than two working days after the conclusion of e-voting and communicated to the stock exchanges and the registrar and transfer agent.

6. Members holding shares in electronic mode who had not registered their email addresses were requested to register them with their respective depository participant. Members holding shares in physical mode were requested to update their email addresses with the Company’s registrar and transfer agent, KFin Technologies Limited.

7. SEBI mandated the submission of permanent account number (PAN), KYC details, and nomination details by holders of physical securities, and the linking of PAN with Aadhaar, through circulars dated March 16, 2023 and November 17, 2023. Physical security holders were requested to submit these details to KFin Technologies Limited. Members holding shares in electronic form were requested to submit their PAN to their depository participant.

### Investor service forms

| Type of holder | Investor service | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Register or update PAN, email address, bank details, and other KYC details for securities held in physical mode | Form ISR-1 |
| Physical | Update the signature of a securities holder | Form ISR-2 |
| Physical | Make a nomination under Rule 19(1) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debenture) Rules, 2014 | Form SH-13 |
| Physical | Declaration to opt out of nomination | Form ISR-3 |
| Physical | Cancel a nomination, together with ISR-3, or change the nominee | Form SH-14 |
| Physical | Request a duplicate certificate or other service requests for shares, debentures, or bonds held in physical form | ISR-4 |
| Demat | Register an email address and bank account details in the demat account through the depository participant’s process | Contact the depository participant |

8. The Postal Ballot Notice was also placed on the Company’s website and the NSDL website.
