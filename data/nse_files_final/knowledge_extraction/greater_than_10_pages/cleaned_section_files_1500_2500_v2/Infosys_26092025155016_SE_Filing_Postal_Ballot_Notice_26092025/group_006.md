# Buyback Participation and Entitlement

As defined in Regulation 2(i)(n) of the Buyback Regulations, a “small shareholder” is a shareholder whose Equity Shares have a market value of not more than ` 2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs only), based on the closing price on the recognised stock exchange with the highest trading volume as of the Record Date.

In accordance with the proviso to Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of Equity Shares entitled to small shareholders based on their shareholding as of the Record Date, whichever is higher, will be reserved for small shareholders.

Based on holdings as of the Record Date, the Company will determine each Eligible Shareholder’s entitlement to tender shares in the Buyback. The entitlement will be calculated using the number of Equity Shares held by the shareholder as of the Record Date and the Buyback ratio applicable to the shareholder’s category.

The promoters and members of the promoter group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback. Accordingly, their Equity Shares will not be considered when computing the entitlement ratio, in accordance with the proviso to Regulation 4(iv)(a) of the SEBI Buyback Regulations. This will result in a higher Buyback Entitlement ratio for Eligible Shareholders.

To prevent shareholders with multiple demat accounts or folios from receiving a higher entitlement under the Small Shareholder category, the Company will club Equity Shares held by shareholders with a common PAN when determining their category and Buyback entitlement.

For joint shareholding, Equity Shares will be clubbed where the sequence of PANs of the joint shareholders is identical. For physical shareholders, Equity Shares will similarly be clubbed where the sequence of PANs is identical. Where PAN is unavailable, physical joint holdings will be clubbed when the sequence of the joint holders’ names is identical.

Institutional investors such as mutual funds, pension funds or trusts, and insurance companies with a common PAN will not be clubbed when their Equity Shares are held under different schemes and different demat account nomenclature, based on information prepared by the Registrar and Transfer Agent from shareholder records received from the Depositories.

Shareholder participation in the Buyback is voluntary. Eligible Shareholders may:

- Participate and receive cash for the shares accepted under the Buyback.
- Choose not to participate.
- Accept part or all of their entitlement.
- Tender additional shares above their entitlement and participate in any shortfall arising from non-participation by other shareholders.

The maximum number of Equity Shares tendered by an Eligible Shareholder cannot exceed the number of Equity Shares held as of the Record Date.

Equity Shares tendered within the entitlement, as well as any additional shares tendered, will be accepted in accordance with the procedure prescribed in the Buyback Regulations. Settlement will use the “Mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to tender offers under Buyback pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeovers, Buyback and Delisting” notified under applicable SEBI circulars.

## Non-resident shareholders

The Buyback of Equity Shares from non-resident shareholders, including Foreign Corporate Bodies, erstwhile Overseas Corporate Bodies, Foreign Institutional Investors, Foreign Portfolio Investors, members of foreign nationality, and ADS holders with underlying Equity Shares following withdrawal of those shares, will be subject to applicable requirements under:

- The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, and related rules and regulations.
- The Income Tax Act, 1961, and related rules and regulations.
- The Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014.

Participation will also be subject to approvals required from relevant authorities, including approvals from the Reserve Bank of India under FEMA. Non-resident shareholders must obtain any approvals required for their participation.

## Eligibility of ADS holders

All equity shareholders as of the Record Date will be eligible to participate in the Buyback. Holders of the Company’s American Depositary Shares will not be eligible to tender ADSs directly.

To participate, ADS holders must cancel all or the desired portion of their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date, so that they become equity shareholders of the Company as of the Record Date. ADS holders may re-deposit any underlying Equity Shares that are not tendered, or that are tendered but not accepted, for the creation of ADSs.

Detailed participation instructions and the relevant timetable will be included in the Letter of Offer to be sent to Eligible Shareholders.
