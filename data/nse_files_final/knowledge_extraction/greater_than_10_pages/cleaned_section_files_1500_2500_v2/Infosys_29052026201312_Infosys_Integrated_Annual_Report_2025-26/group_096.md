## Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used to acquire new plant and machinery for the Company's business under Section 10AA(2) of the Act.

## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include:

- Remeasurement of the net defined benefit liability or asset.
- Equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income.
- Changes in the fair value of investments.
- Changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes.

## Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the cash flow hedge reserve is transferred to the Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecasted transaction occurs.

## In the period of five years immediately preceding March 31, 2026:

### Buyback

The Company purchased and extinguished a total of 21,62,33,685 fully paid-up equity shares of face value `5/-` each from the stock exchange during the five years preceding March 31, 2026. The Company has one class of equity shares.

### Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to return approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividend and buyback amounts include applicable taxes.

### Buyback completed in December 2025

At its meeting on September 11, 2025, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of `5/-` each for `18,000 crore`, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot. Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting, with results declared on November 6, 2025.

The buyback comprised 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company's total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis. The buyback price was `1,800` per equity share. It was offered to eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who became eligible by cancelling ADSs and withdrawing the underlying equity shares, as of the November 14, 2025 record date. The buyback was conducted proportionately through the tender-offer route.

The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The Company completed the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025, buying back and extinguishing all 10,00,00,000 equity shares. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of `18,000 crore`, excluding transaction costs, and was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of `50 crore` as of March 31, 2026, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back and appropriated from the general reserve.

The Company's capital-management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. The Company may adjust dividends, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares to maintain or achieve this structure.

As of March 31, 2026, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## 2.12.3 Dividend

Final dividends are recorded as liabilities on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as liabilities on the date of declaration by the Company's Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Remittances of dividends outside India are governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and applicable withholding-tax rates.
