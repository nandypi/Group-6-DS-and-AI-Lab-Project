## Corporate Social Responsibility Committee

The Corporate Social Responsibility Committee comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, it comprised:

1. Govind Iyer, Chairperson
2. Chitra Nayak
3. Michael Gibbs

## Our CSR philosophy

Infosys's CSR encompasses community development, institution-building, philanthropy, and sustainability-related initiatives.

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objective of the Committee is to assist the Board in fulfilling its corporate social responsibility obligations. Its responsibilities include:

1. Identifying areas of CSR activity.
2. Recommending expenditure on identified CSR activities.
3. Evaluating CSR proposals received from the Company.
4. Implementing and monitoring the CSR Policy.
5. Formulating a CSR annual action plan and recommending it to the Board.
6. Reviewing the Company's CSR initiatives and programs.
7. Coordinating with Infosys Foundation and agencies such as registered trusts, societies, or Section 8 companies to implement programs and initiatives under the CSR Policy.
8. Reviewing impact assessment reports issued by independent agencies for the Company's projects.
9. Reviewing CSR reporting and disclosures required under applicable statutes.
10. Reviewing certificates issued for utilization of CSR funds earmarked for specific themes or projects.

## Committee governance

The CSR Committee comprises three independent directors and fulfills the requirements of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the CSR Committee Charter.

The Committee identifies CSR activities, programs, and initiatives in accordance with defined guidelines and oversees the activities and functioning of Infosys Foundation, Infosys Foundation USA, and other initiatives undertaken by the Company, including in Australia and various parts of Europe. The Foundations report to the Committee on the progress of deployed initiatives and assist with periodic disclosures.

The CSR Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## Committee Policy and Charter

With the approval of the Board, the Committee adopted the CSR Policy as required under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013. The Committee Charter and CSR Policy are available on the Company's website.

## CSR report

The CSR report for the year ended March 31, 2026, as required under the Companies Act, 2013, is attached as Annexure 6 to the Board's report.

The Committee periodically reviewed and approved the budget and disbursement of funds. It ensures that at least 2% of the average net profits of the Company for the three immediately preceding financial years is allocated to CSR activities in India.

During fiscal 2026:

- The Company spent ₹558.04 crore on various CSR projects.
- ₹19.00 crore remained unspent for various ongoing projects and will be transferred to the unspent CSR account and spent in accordance with the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, and amendments thereunder.
- The Company spent an additional ₹9.21 crore on ongoing projects from fiscals 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- CSR spending in the US, Australia, and various parts of Europe was over and above the statutory requirement in India.
- Aggregate CSR spending outside India was ₹43.03 crore.

## ESG Committee

The ESG Committee was constituted with effect from April 14, 2021. It comprises only independent directors. As of March 31, 2026, it comprised:

1. Chitra Nayak, Chairperson
2. Govind Iyer
3. Helene Auriol Potier

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The ESG Committee's responsibilities include:

1. Guiding the creation and periodic refresh of the Company's ESG Vision and Ambitions and monitoring ESG objectives and goals.
2. Reviewing the operation and work of the ESG Council and forming or delegating authority to subcommittees when appropriate.
3. Ensuring that the Company implements appropriate measures to advance its ESG objectives and providing access to internal information necessary to fulfill its duties.
4. Overseeing periodic ESG disclosures and communications, including annual report sections, ESG reports, and Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting filings.
5. Obtaining advice and assistance from internal or external experts and advisors.
6. Reporting periodically to the Board on matters reviewed and decisions or recommendations made.
