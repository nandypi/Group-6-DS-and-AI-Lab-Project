## Key recommendations of the Committee

Based on its discussions with Management and the auditors, and its review of Management representations and the auditors’ report, the Committee recommended the following to the Board:

- Acceptance of the Company’s quarterly condensed financial statements prepared in accordance with Ind AS under the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable rules, and IFRS issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.
- Acceptance of the audited financial statements of Infosys Limited prepared in accordance with Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- Acceptance of the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries prepared in accordance with Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- Acceptance of the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, prepared in Indian rupees and US dollars in accordance with IFRS, for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
- Acceptance of the audited consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, prepared in US dollars in accordance with IFRS, for the year ended March 31, 2026, for inclusion in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the SEC.
- Appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as the Company’s internal auditors for the year ending March 31, 2027, to review various Company operations.
- Issuance of a letter in line with Recommendation No. 9 of the Blue Ribbon Committee on Audit Committee effectiveness for inclusion with the IFRS financial statements in the Annual Report on Form 20-F.
- In line with mandatory auditor rotation under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Audit Committee proposed on April 23, 2026, the appointment of BSR & Co. LLP as statutory auditors commencing with the audit of the Company’s financial statements for the year ended March 31, 2028, including the quarterly financial statements for that year.
- Approval of the intent to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as the independent registered public accounting firm for U.S. SEC filings, effective fiscal 2027-28, subject to applicable requirements.

Based on its review and discussions with Management and the independent auditors, the Committee stated that the Company’s financial statements were fairly presented in conformity with Ind AS and IFRS and that there were no significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting. The Committee stated that it had complied with its responsibilities under its Charter. The Board accepted all recommendations made by the Audit Committee.

## Objectives of the Committee

The main objectives and responsibilities of the Committee are to:

1. Assist the Board in discharging its responsibilities relating to remuneration of the Company’s Directors, Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), and senior management.
2. Evaluate and approve the adequacy of remuneration plans, policies, programmes, and succession plans for the Company’s Directors, KMP, and senior management; identify individuals for appointment to these positions according to prescribed criteria; and recommend their appointment and removal to the Board.
3. Formulate criteria for determining the qualifications, positive attributes, independence, and performance evaluation of directors on the Board.
4. Administer equity-based plans and schemes approved by shareholders.
5. Oversee the Company’s nomination process for KMP and senior management and identify, screen, and review individuals qualified to serve as directors, KMP, and senior management in accordance with Board-approved criteria.
6. Recommend the appointment and removal of directors for approval at the annual meeting of shareholders.
7. Evaluate the performance of the Board and review the implementation of and compliance with the evaluation.
8. Oversee leadership development.
9. Develop and maintain corporate governance policies applicable to the Company.
10. Devise a policy on Board diversity.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises four members and fulfills the requirements of:

- Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter
- Section 178 of the Companies Act, 2013
- Regulation 19 of the Listing Regulations
- NYSE guidelines, as applicable

The Committee oversees the processes by which the Company recruits new Board members and recruits, motivates, and retains senior management, as well as the Company’s overall approach to human resources management.
