## Procedure for postal ballot

The postal ballot was carried out under Sections 108 and 110 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, the related rules, and applicable Ministry of Corporate Affairs circulars.

## Details of special resolution proposed to be transacted through postal ballot

None of the businesses proposed to be transacted at the ensuing AGM requires passing a special resolution through postal ballot.

## Legal compliance

Infosys states that disclosures and policies relating to legal compliance, including non-compliance, regulatory orders, certifications, and complaints, are provided in the corporate governance report.

## Details of non-compliance

No penalty was imposed by any stock exchange, SEBI, or the SEC, and there was no instance of non-compliance with legal requirements or capital-market matters during the last three years.

## Regulatory orders

There were no material regulatory orders pertaining to the Company for fiscal 2026.

## CEO and CFO certification

The CEO and CFO certification required under the Listing Regulations is provided in the Integrated Annual Report.

## Code of Conduct and Ethics

Infosys has adopted a Code of Conduct and Ethics under the Listing Regulations and the Companies Act, 2013. The Code applies to the Board, executive officers, and all employees of Infosys and its subsidiaries.

All Board members, executive officers, and senior management affirmed compliance with the Code as of March 31, 2026. The related declaration forms part of the CEO and CFO certification.

## Establishment of vigil / whistleblower mechanism

Infosys has established a mechanism for directors and employees to report concerns relating to unethical behavior, actual or suspected fraud, and violations of the Code. The mechanism includes safeguards against victimization and provides direct access to the Audit Committee chairperson in exceptional cases.

No person was denied access to the Audit Committee during the year.

## Complaints pertaining to sexual harassment

Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report.

## Prevention of insider trading

Infosys amended its Code of Conduct for Prohibition of Insider Trading on April 23, 2026, and its Code on Fair Disclosures and Investor Relations on April 17, 2025.

The policies and procedures regulate, monitor, and report trading by designated persons and their immediate relatives. They also address matters under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, including inquiries into leaks or suspected leaks of unpublished price-sensitive information.

## Compliance with discretionary requirements

Infosys implemented the following nonmandatory corporate governance measures:

- Separate positions for the Chairman and the CEO & MD, with reimbursement of expenses incurred in the performance of official duties.
- A non-executive chairperson who is not related to the CEO & MD.
- A separate office within the Company premises for the Chairman.
- Unmodified audit opinions and reporting.
- Internal auditor reporting directly to the Audit Committee.

## Other disclosures and affirmations

- Infosys complied with the corporate governance requirements under Regulations 17 to 27 and the website disclosure requirements under Regulation 46(2) of the Listing Regulations.
- Infosys securities were not suspended from trading during fiscal 2026.

## Certificate of non-disqualification of directors

Makarand M. Joshi of Makarand M. Joshi & Co., Company Secretaries, issued the certificate required under the Listing Regulations. The certificate confirms that none of the directors on the Company’s Board had been debarred or disqualified from being appointed or continuing as a director of a company by SEBI, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, or any other statutory authority as of March 31, 2026.
