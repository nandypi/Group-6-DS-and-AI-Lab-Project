## Our Strategy Approaching value creation

Infosys’ strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities in which it operates.

Clients are seeking to reimagine core offerings, processes, and systems in response to advances in software, computing technology, and AI. Infosys’ strategy is to help clients ideate, plan, and scale their AI journeys by building strong data foundations and cloud capabilities, while applying safeguards for privacy, ethics, controls, and Responsible AI.

In 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy:

1. Scale Agile Digital
2. Energize the Core
3. Reskill our People
4. Expand Localization

The current AI transition is described as transformational rather than incremental. AI-native systems use probabilistic inference, continuous learning, and agent-based execution.

- **Technology:** Enterprises are moving from application-centric stacks toward platforms incorporating intelligent agents, orchestration layers, and adaptive learning components.
- **Business:** Operating models are evolving from linear, role-based execution toward AI-driven operating loops that continuously sense, decide, and act.
- **Talent and organization:** Work is shifting from code creation toward orchestration, system supervision, domain interpretation, and outcome validation, requiring new roles, large-scale reskilling, and changes in workforce composition.

## Infosys AI vision

Infosys aspires to be a partner that helps clients unlock AI value and deliver business outcomes in revenue growth, cost optimization, and innovation.

Its two-pronged AI strategy consists of:

- **AI-first services:** Services designed with AI as the core execution layer, enabling new business models, products, and revenue streams.
- **AI-augmented services:** Existing offerings enhanced by integrating AI into established systems and workflows to improve efficiency, personalization, and outcomes while extending the value of existing investments.

These services are supported by platforms and intellectual property, ecosystem partnerships, talent, and the Infosys brand.

Infosys has developed over 25 industry-focused AI playbooks covering repeatable use cases, domain context, and execution patterns. The playbooks are intended to help enterprises scale AI adoption from experimentation to enterprise-wide impact.

## AI First Value Framework

Infosys’ AI-first value framework is designed to help enterprises scale AI using the Infosys Topaz™ generative and agentic AI suite. The company estimates an incremental AI-first services opportunity of USD 300–400 billion by 2030, based on market estimates.

The framework defines six interconnected value pools:

1. **AI Strategy & Engineering:** Design and implement AI strategies, platforms, and architectures, including AI agents, proprietary platforms, third-party tools, and purpose-built infrastructure.
2. **Data for AI:** Prepare structured and unstructured enterprise data for AI model readiness through AI-ready data platforms and AI-grade data engineering, including fingerprinting and synthetic data services.
3. **Process AI:** Redesign end-to-end business workflows by integrating AI agents and human expertise to improve efficiency, experience, and business outcomes.
4. **Agentic Legacy Modernization:** Use AI agents to reverse-engineer existing estates, understand their intent, and progressively modernize them while reducing disruption and technical debt.
5. **Physical AI:** Embed AI into physical devices and intelligent products using sensor data, digital twins, robotics, autonomous systems, and edge intelligence.
6. **AI Trust:** Apply responsible, secure, and ethical AI practices through risk assessments, policy design, security testing, governance, and assurance.

Infosys is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys.

## Platforms and IP

Infosys is using Infosys Topaz Fabric™ and partnerships with AI disruptors to deliver AI-augmented and AI-first services.

**Infosys Topaz Fabric™** is an enterprise-grade AI services layer within Infosys Topaz. It operationalizes AI by embedding enterprise context across data, applications, processes, and systems. It integrates models, data pipelines, agents, and platforms across technology environments and supports the reimagination of business processes while building on existing investments.

**Infosys Topaz AI Next** is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across people, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve product innovation with Infosys’ global scale and is intended to help large and mid-sized enterprises move toward AI-driven operations and business functions.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is designed for clients that have already made AI investments and need a composable, modular agentic AI platform. Infosys Topaz AI Next addresses clients seeking an end-to-end, vertically integrated AI platform for reimagining core business processes.

The platforms institutionalize enterprise knowledge and process semantics, support AI deployment from experimentation to large-scale production, and embed Responsible AI, security, governance, and assurance.
