For Foreign Institutional Investors (FII) and Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI), tax will be deducted under Section 393 of the IT Act 2025 at 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or at the rate provided under the relevant Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), read with the Multilateral Instrument (MLI), whichever is more beneficial, subject to submission of the required documents.

The required documents must be uploaded on or before June 11, 2026. Shareholders can check their tax credit in Form 168, formerly Form 26AS, through their income-tax e-filing account.

Dividends that remain unclaimed for seven years from the date of transfer to the Company's Unpaid Dividend Account will be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). Shares for which dividends remain unclaimed for seven consecutive years will also be transferred to the IEPF under Section 124 of the Act and applicable IEPF rules.

In compliance with Section 108 of the Act, Regulation 44 of the LODR Regulations, and the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, members may vote electronically through the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL). Members who vote through remote e-voting before the AGM may attend the AGM but cannot vote again on the same resolutions. Hemanth, Holla & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries, has been appointed as Scrutinizer for the e-voting process.

Members holding shares in physical or dematerialized form as of the cut-off date, June 16, 2026, may vote electronically. The remote e-voting period runs from Thursday, June 18, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. IST to Monday, June 22, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. IST. Voting rights are proportionate to each member's share of the Company's paid-up equity share capital as of June 16, 2026. A person who is not a member on the cut-off date may treat the Notice as information only.

Voting will also be available during the AGM. Members attending through video conference who have not voted through remote e-voting and are otherwise eligible may vote during the AGM.

A person holding shares in physical mode, or a person who acquires shares and becomes a member after the Notice is sent but holds shares on June 16, 2026, may obtain an e-voting login ID and password by contacting NSDL. Members already registered for remote e-voting may use their existing credentials.

The Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, Notice of the 45th AGM, and e-voting instructions are being sent electronically to members whose email addresses are registered with the Company or their depository participant. Members without registered email addresses will receive a letter containing the web link and access path for the Integrated Annual Report.

For physical shareholders, dividends are paid exclusively through electronic mode to shareholders who have completed the mandatory KYC requirements effective April 1, 2024. Required KYC details include:

- PAN
- Postal address with PIN and mobile number
- Bank account details
- Specimen signature

| Type of holder | Investor service or requirement | Form or process |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Register or update PAN, email address, bank details, and other KYC details for securities held in physical mode | Form ISR-1 |
| Physical | Update the signature of the securities holder | Form ISR-2 |
| Physical | Submit a nomination under Rule 19(1) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 | Form SH-13 |
| Physical | Declaration to opt out of nomination | Form ISR-3 |
| Physical | Cancel a nomination or change the nominee | Form SH-14, along with ISR-3 where applicable |
| Physical | Request a duplicate certificate or other service requests for shares, debentures, or bonds held in physical mode | Form ISR-4 |
| Demat | Register or update email address and bank account details | Contact the respective depository participant and follow its process |

Members holding shares in physical mode may submit a nomination using Form SH-13. Members holding shares in dematerialized mode may contact their respective depository participants to update nomination details.

After completing scrutiny of votes cast during the AGM and through remote e-voting, the Scrutinizer will submit the report to the Chairman or an authorized person within 48 hours of the conclusion of the AGM. The voting results and Scrutinizer's report will be communicated to the stock exchanges, NSDL, and the RTA, and displayed on the Company's website.

As the AGM will be held through video conference, the route map, proxy form, and attendance slip are not attached to the Notice.
