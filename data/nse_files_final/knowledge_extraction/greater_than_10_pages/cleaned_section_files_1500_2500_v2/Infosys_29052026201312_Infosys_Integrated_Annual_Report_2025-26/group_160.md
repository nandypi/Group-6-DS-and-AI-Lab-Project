## Item 6: Approval of the request for re-classification of certain members of the 'Promoter and Promoter Group' of Company to 'Public' category

The following ordinary resolution is proposed under Regulation 31A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, subject to applicable approvals:

> **RESOLVED THAT** consent of the members of the Company be and is hereby accorded for the re-classification of the following applicants from the 'Promoter and Promoter Group' category to the 'Public' category.

| Sl. No. | Name of the entity | Category |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Shreyas Shibulal | Promoter |
| 2 | Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal | Promoter Group |

The Board of Directors approved the request at its meeting held on April 30, 2026. No-objection letters were received from BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited on May 11, 2026.

The directors and Company Secretary are authorized to make filings, represent the Company before the stock exchanges and relevant authorities, obtain required approvals, and take other necessary steps to implement the resolution.

## Notes

1. The AGM is being held through video conferencing in accordance with the applicable Ministry of Corporate Affairs and SEBI circulars. The AGM date is June 23, 2026. Members participating through video conferencing will be counted for quorum purposes. Proxy appointments will not be available because the AGM is being held through video conferencing.

2. Corporate members authorizing representatives to participate and vote must send a certified copy of the Board resolution or authorization letter to the Scrutinizer by email.

3. The registers of directors and Key Managerial Personnel and their shareholding, and contracts or arrangements in which directors are interested, will be available electronically for inspection during the AGM. Documents referred to in the Notice will be available for electronic inspection from the date of circulation of the Notice until June 23, 2026.

4. The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026. Subject to shareholder approval at the AGM, the dividend will be paid on June 25, 2026.

5. Dividend paid or distributed by the Company is taxable in the hands of members under the Income-tax Act, 2025. The Company will deduct tax at source when paying the final dividend.

### Resident shareholders

| Member category | Tax deducted at source |
|---|---:|
| Members having a valid PAN | 10% or the rate notified by the Government of India |
| Members not having a PAN or having an invalid PAN | 20% or the rate notified by the Government of India |

Under Section 262 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, PAN holders eligible to obtain Aadhaar must link PAN with Aadhaar. If PAN is not linked with Aadhaar, it may be deemed invalid or inoperative, and tax may be deducted at the higher rate under Section 397, currently stated as 20%.

No tax will be deducted on dividends payable to resident individual shareholders if their total dividend for tax year 2026–27 does not exceed ₹10,000, or where they provide Form 121, subject to the conditions of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Shareholders may also submit prescribed documents to claim lower or nil withholding. PAN is mandatory when providing Form 121 or other such documents.

### Non-resident shareholders

Tax will generally be withheld at 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or at the rate notified by the Government of India, under Section 393 and other applicable provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Non-resident shareholders may elect to claim benefits under the applicable Double Tax Avoidance Agreement, read with the Multilateral Instrument, where those provisions are more beneficial. They must provide:

- A copy of the Indian PAN card, duly attested. If PAN is unavailable, information required under sub-rule 2 of rule 217 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026.
- A duly attested Tax Residency Certificate for tax year 2026–27 issued by the tax authorities of the country of tax residence.
- Form 41 for claiming tax treaty benefits.
- A self-declaration confirming that they have no permanent establishment in India, in accordance with the applicable tax treaty and the Income-tax Act, 2025.
- A self-declaration confirming beneficial ownership of the equity shares.
- A self-declaration confirming fulfillment of all applicable tax treaty conditions.
- Any other documents prescribed under the Income-tax Act, 2025, or a duly attested certificate for lower withholding of taxes.
