## Governance, leadership and oversight

## 7. Statement by director responsible for the business responsibility report, highlighting ESG-related challenges, targets and achievements

Infosys’ ESG Vision 2030 guides its governance, environmental stewardship, workplace inclusion and diversity, and use of technology to create long-term stakeholder value.

Fiscal 2026 priorities included:

- Accelerating climate commitments.
- Enabling large-scale skilling.
- Embedding responsible practices across operations and the value chain.
- Enhancing ESG frameworks and disclosures through governance mechanisms, performance metrics and stakeholder engagement.

The company identified challenges relating to climate transition, talent transformation, rapid technological change, increasing regulatory requirements, stakeholder expectations regarding transparency and accountability, and a complex macroeconomic environment.

Infosys states that information on ESG-related challenges, targets, performance and achievements is provided in its ESG Report and in the “Approaching Value Creation” and “Delivering Value” chapters of the Integrated Annual Report. It expects regulatory landscapes, stakeholder expectations and climate-related risks to continue evolving.
