## Information Other than the Financial Statements and Auditor's Report Thereon

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible for the other information included in the Management Discussion and Analysis, Board's Report and its annexures, Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, Corporate Governance, and Shareholder's Information. This other information excludes the Consolidated Financial Statements, Standalone Financial Statements, and the auditor's report thereon.

The auditor's opinion on the Standalone Financial Statements does not cover the other information. The auditor's responsibility was to read the other information and consider whether it was materially inconsistent with the Standalone Financial Statements, audit knowledge, or otherwise materially misstated. The auditor stated that there was nothing to report regarding material misstatements in the other information.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Standalone Financial Statements

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible under Section 134(5) of the Act for preparing Standalone Financial Statements that give a true and fair view of the Company's financial position, financial performance including other comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows, in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in India and Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.

These responsibilities include:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records to safeguard assets and prevent and detect fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls over the accuracy and completeness of accounting records and the preparation and presentation of the Standalone Financial Statements.
- Assessing the Company's ability to continue as a going concern and making relevant disclosures.
- Using the going concern basis of accounting unless the Board intends to liquidate the Company, cease operations, or has no realistic alternative.
- Overseeing the Company's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Standalone Financial Statements

The auditor's objectives are to obtain reasonable assurance that the Standalone Financial Statements are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing the auditor's opinion. Reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but is not a guarantee that an audit will always detect a material misstatement.

The audit responsibilities included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement, designing responsive audit procedures, and obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal financial controls. Under Section 143(3)(i) of the Act, the auditor also reports on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of the Company's internal financial controls with reference to the Standalone Financial Statements.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies, the reasonableness of accounting estimates, and related disclosures.
- Evaluating management's use of the going concern basis and determining whether material uncertainty exists regarding events or conditions that could cast significant doubt on the Company's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the Standalone Financial Statements.

Materiality is the magnitude of misstatements that, individually or in aggregate, could reasonably influence the economic decisions of users of the Standalone Financial Statements. The auditor considers quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating results, and assessing identified misstatements.

The auditor communicates with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, and significant deficiencies in internal financial controls. The auditor also provides an independence statement and communicates relevant relationships, matters, and safeguards.

The auditor determines the matters of most significance in the current-period audit as key audit matters and describes them in the auditor's report unless disclosure is precluded by law or regulation or the adverse consequences of disclosure would outweigh the public interest benefits.

## Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements

### Section 143(3) of the Act

Based on the audit, the auditor reported that:

- All information and explanations necessary for the audit were sought and obtained.
- Proper books of account required by law were maintained, based on examination of those books.
- The Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit and Loss including Other Comprehensive Income, Statement of Changes in Equity, and Statement of Cash Flows agreed with the books of account.
- The Standalone Financial Statements complied with the Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.
- Based on written representations received from the directors and recorded by the Board, none of the directors was disqualified as of March 31, 2026 under Section 164(2) of the Act.
- The auditor's separate report in Annexure A expressed an unmodified opinion on the adequacy and operating effectiveness of the Company's internal financial controls with reference to the Standalone Financial Statements.
- Directors' remuneration paid during the year complied with Section 197 of the Act.

With respect to Rule 11 of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014, as amended, the auditor reported that:

- The Company disclosed the impact of pending litigation on its financial position in the Standalone Financial Statements. Refer to Note 2.23.
- The Company made provisions required under applicable law or accounting standards for material foreseeable losses. Refer to Note 2.16.
- The Company did not have any long-term derivative contracts.
- There was no delay in transferring amounts required to be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund.
- Management represented that, to the best of its knowledge and belief, the Company had not advanced, loaned, or invested funds from borrowed funds, share premium, or other sources through intermediaries with an understanding that the intermediaries would provide funds, investments, guarantees, or securities to ultimate beneficiaries identified by or on behalf of the Company.
- Management represented that the Company had not received funds from funding parties with an understanding that the Company would provide funds, investments, guarantees, or securities to ultimate beneficiaries identified by or on behalf of the funding parties.
- Based on audit procedures considered reasonable and appropriate, nothing came to the auditor's attention indicating that these management representations contained a material misstatement.

As stated in Note 2.12.3 to the Standalone Financial Statements:

- The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared, and paid during the year complied with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.
- The interim dividend declared and paid during the year and up to the date of the auditor's report complied with Section 123 of the Act.
- The Board proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed amount complied with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

Based on examination including test checks, the Company used accounting software systems for maintaining its books for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The systems had an audit trail/edit-log facility that operated throughout the year for all relevant recorded transactions. The auditor did not identify any instance of tampering with the audit trail, and the audit trail was preserved in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

### Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020

As required by the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020, issued by the Central Government under Section 143(11) of the Act, the auditor provided in Annexure B a statement on the matters specified in paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Order.
