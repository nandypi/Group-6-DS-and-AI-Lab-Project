1 crore = 10 million.

The figures are extracted from the audited standalone and consolidated financial statements of the Company under the Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS). Equity shares have a par value of ` 5 per share.

- Includes profit on sale of property, plant and equipment amounting to ` 165 crore on both standalone and consolidated bases for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- Includes interest income (pre-tax) of ` 381 crore and ` 327 crore for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively, and reversal of tax provisions amounting to ` 774 crore and ` 101 crore for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively, on standalone and consolidated bases. These amounts resulted from orders received under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, for certain assessment years.
- The Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025 increased gratuity liability, a defined benefit plan arising from past service cost relating to plan amendment, and compensated absences by ` 1,146 crore on a standalone basis and ` 1,289 crore on a consolidated basis for the year ended March 31, 2026. This also resulted in lower tax of ` 282 crore on a standalone basis and ` 318 crore on a consolidated basis.
- Excluding the effects of the Income-tax orders received under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and the Labour Codes provisions notified by the Government of India:
  - Basic EPS increased year over year by 15.9% on a standalone basis and 12.1% on a consolidated basis for the year ended March 31, 2026.
  - Diluted EPS increased year over year by 16.0% on a standalone basis and 12.2% on a consolidated basis for the year ended March 31, 2026.
