## Note:

- The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends distributed outside India are subject to applicable Indian withholding taxes and foreign-exchange laws.
- The payout ratio is computed as a percentage of free cash flow prepared under IFRS.
- Buyback transaction costs of ₹58 crore are excluded.
- The Board recommended the final dividend at its meeting held on April 23, 2026. Payment is subject to shareholder approval at the AGM on June 23, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026, and the proposed payment date is June 25, 2026.
- Infosys' capital allocation policy is to pay approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through semiannual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and approvals. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure under IFRS.
- Including buybacks, the Company returned 82.1% of cumulative free cash flow for the years ended March 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026.

## 2. Business description

## Strategy

Infosys' clients and prospective clients face business opportunities arising from advances in software, computing technology, and artificial intelligence. Infosys' strategy is to help clients navigate their AI journeys, unlock business value, and reimagine offerings, processes, and systems.

The Company helps clients ideate, plan, and scale their AI capabilities by building data foundations and cloud capabilities. It also promotes Responsible AI practices addressing privacy, ethics, and controls.

## Organization

Infosys' go-to-market business units and solutions are detailed in the Operating Context section of this Integrated Annual Report.

## Infrastructure

During fiscal 2026, Infosys added 1.87 million square feet of physical infrastructure space. As of March 31, 2026, total available space was 58.20 million square feet, with a presence in 59 countries across 290 locations.

## Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)

Infosys' M&A approach is aimed at strengthening capabilities, deepening industry expertise, and expanding its geographical footprint.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership or voting interests in:

1. MRE Consulting Ltd., an energy and business consulting services company headquartered in Texas, U.S., acquired on April 30, 2025.
2. The Missing Link Group, a cybersecurity services provider headquartered in Australia, acquired on April 30, 2025.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group entered into definitive agreements to:

1. Form a joint venture with Telstra, an Australian telecommunications and technology company. As part of the transaction, Infosys will acquire a 75% stake in the Versent Group, a Telstra Group subsidiary providing cloud and digital transformation services.
2. Acquire Stratus Global LLC, a U.S.-based insurance consulting and technology services company focused on Guidewire services. The acquisition had been completed as of the date of the report.
3. Acquire Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc., together with its subsidiaries, including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC, a U.S.-based healthcare digital transformation and consulting company.

The Versent Group comprises Telstra Purple Pty Ltd and the integrated operations of Versent, Epicon, Telstra Purple Digital, and associated Cloud Access products. Telstra Purple Digital is a carve-out from Telstra's Enterprise Tech Services/Telstra Purple portfolio and excludes Alliance Automation Pty Ltd, Aqura Technologies Pty Ltd, and Telstra Broadcast Services Pty Limited.

## 3. Human resources management

Infosys' people strategy focuses on attracting and developing talent, building internal leadership pipelines, and providing an employee experience supported by meritocracy, continuous learning, diversity, equity and inclusion, flexible work models, and technology-led enablement.

The Company operates structured Resolution Hubs for employee grievance redressal, guided by confidentiality, non-retaliation, natural justice, and timely resolution. Internal Committees for the prevention and redressal of sexual harassment support the Company's statutory grievance mechanisms.

Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report. No complaint cases were pending for more than 90 days.
