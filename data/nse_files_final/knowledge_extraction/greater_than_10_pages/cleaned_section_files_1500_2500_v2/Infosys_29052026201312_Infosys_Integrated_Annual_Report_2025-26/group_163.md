# Section 6 c) Parameters for Vesting

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relative Total Shareholder Return | Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR) against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators. The Administrator will identify the comparators from a basket of 10 industry peers, including Indian and global companies: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. Relative TSR will also be measured against broader domestic and global market indices comprising Nifty 50, S&P 500, and an MSCI index to be determined by the Administrator. | Long-term Relative TSR for rolling three years against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from the same basket of 10 industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. The Company will implement a phased transition to a three-year rolling relative TSR parameter: one-year TSR in Year 1, two-year TSR in Year 2, and a full three-year TSR measure from Year 3 onwards.<br><br>Annual TSR will also include: (A) relative TSR against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators from the same 10-peer basket; and (B) relative TSR against broader domestic and global indices comprising MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50, as determined by the Administrator. | The change is intended to align management compensation with the long-term interests of shareholders through a three-year rolling Relative TSR parameter. There are no changes to the basket of existing industry peers. |
| Operating performance metrics | Operating lead performance metrics used to derive shareholder value: total revenue growth, digital revenue growth, and operating margin. Annual performance targets will be determined by the Administrator. Each performance parameter will be distinct for calculating the number of shares to vest. For Relative TSR, 100% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest at or above the 75th percentile; vesting will occur proportionately on a linear scale between 60% and 100% for performance between the 50th and 75th percentiles; and no RSUs will vest below the 50th percentile. | Annual operating performance metrics: total revenue growth and operating margin. Performance targets will be determined by the Administrator. For Relative TSR, 100% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest at or above the 75th percentile; vesting will occur proportionately on a linear scale between 60% and 100% for performance between the 50th and 75th percentiles; and no RSUs will vest below the 50th percentile.<br><br>For operating performance metrics: 75% of the RSUs due for vesting will vest at the minimum threshold; no RSUs will vest below the threshold; 100% will vest at target; and 125% will vest at stretch target. Performance between threshold and target results in vesting on a linear scale between 75% and 100%, while performance between target and stretch target results in vesting on a linear scale between 100% and 125%. | The operating metrics were rationalized to focus on revenue and margin and to improve consistency and comparability over time. Sub-segment metrics such as digital revenue growth may become transient and less comparable over the long term of 10 years. The revised provisions also aim to increase transparency regarding the payout philosophy. |
| Operating performance vesting cap | — | Individual performance outcomes may exceed 100%, but aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter will not exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter. | — |

# Section 6(e)

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Exercise Period for the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(II) above, as relevant, shall be the following:

- In the event of termination of employment or resignation of the Participant, vested Restricted Stock Units held by the Participant on the date of termination or resignation, provided the termination is not for misconduct, must be exercised within 90 calendar days of the date of vesting.

The change was made for clarity and consistency in the operation of the Plan’s provisions relating to the exercise period for vested RSUs. The revised language clarifies that the exercise period commences from the date on which the relevant RSUs vest.
