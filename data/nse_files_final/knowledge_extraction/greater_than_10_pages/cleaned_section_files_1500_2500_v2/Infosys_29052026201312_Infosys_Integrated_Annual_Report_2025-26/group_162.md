## Explanatory Statement

## Background and context for item nos. 4 and 5

The proposal covers amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 (the “Amended 2019 Plan”) and the grant of stock incentives to eligible employees of Infosys and its subsidiaries.

Infosys has operated employee stock ownership programs since 1994. The 2015 Incentive Compensation Plan was approved by shareholders through a postal ballot dated March 31, 2016. Its vesting criteria are largely time-based.

The existing Expanded Stock Ownership Program, 2019 (the “2019 Plan”), was approved at the Annual General Meeting held on June 22, 2019, for a pool of approximately 5,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 1.15% of the Company’s equity shares outstanding at that time.

As of the date of the Notice:

- 3,26,63,903 equity shares remained available for grant under the existing plan. This represented the unutilized balance from the original shareholder-approved pool of 5,00,00,000 shares and approximately 0.8% of the Company’s outstanding equity share capital.
- 4,44,19,917 RSUs were available for grant under all ESOP plans, representing approximately 1.1% of the outstanding equity share capital. This included vested but unexercised RSUs and RSUs that had not yet vested.
- The 2019 Plan was due to expire on June 21, 2026.

The Board proposed amendments to provide:

- Continuity of the performance-based stock incentive program across an expanded employee base.
- Ability to attract, retain, and incentivize key talent.
- Continued alignment between employee interests and long-term shareholder value creation.

## The summary of the proposed amendments is as under:

## 1. Extension of the 2019 Plan tenure:

The 2019 Plan would be extended for a further period of seven (7) years from the date of shareholder approval. Grants under the 2019 Plan would be made over seven (7) years, and the plan would remain valid for ten (10) years from the date shareholder approval is received.

## 2. Strengthening alignment with Long-Term Shareholder Value creation and rationalization of Operational Metrics based on the inputs received from investors:

- A three-year rolling Relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) would be introduced as a vesting parameter.
- Operating metrics would be rationalized to maintain focus on revenue and margin, supporting consistency and comparability over time. Sub-segment metrics, such as digital revenue growth, may become less comparable as technology and business models evolve.

## 3. Share pool and dilution

The proposed extension would not increase the originally approved share pool of 5,00,00,000 equity shares. The Company would continue to operate within the existing approved pool, with 3,26,63,903 shares available for grant under the amended plan. The proposal would not result in incremental dilution of the equity share capital compared with the earlier approval.

## 4. Regulatory alignment

Other amendments would provide clarity and consistency and align the 2019 Plan with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
