## Lake rejuvenation

Extending water stewardship beyond campus boundaries, Infosys continues to implement lake rejuvenation projects in partnership with local stakeholders. These efforts increased cumulative storage capacity from 6 bn liters to 10.3 bn liters, adding 4.3 bn liters of freshwater capacity.

## Waste management

Infosys’ waste management strategy is based on reducing, reusing, refurbishing, repurposing, and recycling, with progress toward zero waste to landfill. The company uses waste segregation, onsite processing, and material recovery across its campuses.

Organic waste is treated onsite through biogas plants, composting, and recycling. These processes reduce offsite disposal and associated emissions and replace part of the cooking gas requirements at campuses.

E-waste includes IT equipment, mobile phones, printers, cartridges, refrigerators, microwaves, air conditioners, lithium-ion batteries, and cables. Infosys extends asset life through hardware upgrades, component replacements, re-imaging, reuse, redeployment, and refurbishment.

The company’s waste management practices are aligned with the TRUE Zero Waste certification framework. Eleven Infosys India campuses have achieved TRUE Zero Waste certification.

## Biodiversity

Infosys is strengthening biodiversity stewardship across campuses through native plantation, habitat enrichment, and knowledge sharing through the annual BioConserve event.

In FY26, Infosys launched the BioConserve platform, a digital open repository for resources, best practices, and research on biodiversity conservation.

## ASSURE

Launched in partnership with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements (IIHS) in April 2024, Accelerating Sustainable Super Efficient Real Estate (ASSURE) is a multi-stakeholder platform focused on replicating climate-resilient, energy-efficient buildings across India, with a target of 100 million sq. ft. by 2030.

During the year, more than 15 million sq. ft. of commercial buildings across India were onboarded as lighthouse projects. More than 1,250 students and practitioners were upskilled on high-performance buildings through webinars, in-person workshops, and site visits.

ASSURE brings together policymakers, developers, experts, and industry leaders to advance performance measurement, skills, policy, and financing mechanisms.

## Embedding circularity and innovation in design

Infosys integrates Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) into early-stage decision-making for material selection and design optimization.

Steel and cement account for nearly 70% of embodied carbon in office buildings. Infosys has prioritized secondary steel produced from recycled scrap, with an emission intensity of 1–1.5 tCO2e/t compared with 2.5–3.5 tCO2e/t for primary steel. Cement use is optimized through blending with industrial by-products such as fly ash and ground granulated blast furnace slag.

Infosys is advancing thermal management through radiant heating and free cooling under favorable climatic conditions. Air-conditioning equipment procurement has been standardized around refrigerants with very low global warming potential.

The company has eliminated plastic- and thermocol-based packaging for IT equipment and introduced sustainable packaging criteria into procurement. Suppliers provide paper-based, recycled, and biodegradable fibre alternatives, including materials derived from agricultural waste.

## Resource circularity in operations

Infosys’ circular economy initiatives focus on responsible IT asset utilization across the asset lifecycle. The company uses reuse, redeployment, refurbishment, and responsible end-of-life management to reduce e-waste and demand for new resources.

IT assets are repurposed and reused across functions and delivery centers to improve utilization and avoid premature disposal.

## Data center efficiency

InfosysIT has made data center modernization a key part of its sustainability and responsible growth strategy. Its data center ecosystem uses density-optimized hyperscale platforms, cloud-scale agility, and high-density server virtualization to reduce infrastructure sprawl and improve compute utilization.

InfosysIT has rationalized and consolidated data centers and server rooms across development centers, reducing redundancy and optimizing space usage.

The company has invested in Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) capabilities to provide integrated, real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure. These tools support data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.

## Infrastructure as code

Infrastructure as code supports continuous deployment, integration, and touchless lifecycle management of infrastructure components. It addresses demand at scale and improves deployment speed and consistency across teams. Infosys has developed playbooks to automate platform-related processes.

## AIOps-powered digital operations

InfosysIT uses an AIOps platform with intelligent noise suppression and context-driven notifications. The platform provides unified observability across the infrastructure landscape, visibility into capacity usage and cost efficiency, and processes high-volume data from diverse IT sources to support proactive operations.

## Public cloud adoption

Infosys has largely transitioned its internal IT applications to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premises data centers. Employees use cloud-based collaboration and secure access solutions for hybrid work.

The IT Service Management (ITSM) landscape uses a standardized, AI-enabled service desk on a SaaS-hosted platform. This has supported standardized processes, greater automation, and AI-assisted agents across support services.
