## Philosophy

Infosys is committed to a positive and equal-opportunity workplace free of discrimination and harassment. The Company’s “Experience by Design” approach focuses on consistent policies, processes, programs, and systems that place employees at the center.

Infosys considers its people a core strength and has implemented initiatives focused on meaningful work, high-performing teams, continuous learning, employee development, leadership development, workforce productivity, employee engagement, recognition and rewards, and diversity, equity, and inclusion.

Infosys has adopted a hybrid work model to balance flexibility with business needs. Technology and automation are used to support workforce productivity and operational excellence.

## Resolution hubs

Infosys maintains an open-door policy and provides employees with forums to raise workplace concerns. Its grievance resolution mechanism is based on natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Investigations provide all involved parties with an opportunity to present facts and material evidence.

## Corner Stones

The Infosys Human Rights Statement, Global Policy on Anti-harassment / Discrimination, and Code of Conduct & Ethics form the cornerstones of this philosophy.

## Culture

Infosys promotes a speak-up culture in which employees are encouraged to report concerns or suspected violations of the Code, Company policies and procedures, or applicable laws.

## Resolution Hubs at Infosys include

## HEAR (Hearing Employees And Resolving)

- Formal forum for workplace grievances unresolved at the Unit HR or manager levels.
- Multiple layers of grievance redressal, including an appeal mechanism.
- Neutral panel investigations to support objective outcomes.
- Review of anonymous concerns in collaboration with the Whistleblower Office.

The HR team implemented enablement and preventive initiatives to strengthen awareness, enhance employee responsibility, and support a more engaged and empathetic work culture.

## HEAR (Hearing Employees and Resolving)

HEAR is an independent employee grievance resolution hub under the Human Resources Department. It reviews grievances relating to workplace matters that may impede a positive work environment.

The HEAR Office follows a decentralized model with an institutionalized presence across businesses, units, geographies, affiliates, and subsidiaries. It responds to grievances through objective reviews mediated by neutral panels or through compliance audits assessing adherence to relevant policies.

The HEAR Office also advises other departments on policy and process compliance and provides upward feedback to senior management on employee sentiment. Its stated mission is to serve as a fair, trusted, and effective independent resolution mechanism.

## The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI)

The Anti Sexual Harassment Initiative (ASHI) is an enterprise-wide framework for preventing, prohibiting, and redressing sexual harassment at the workplace. It applies a gender-neutral, employee-centric, and globally consistent approach, with procedural fairness, confidentiality, non-retaliation, and adherence to natural justice.

## ASHI (Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative )

- Aims to prevent, prohibit, and redress sexual harassment-related concerns.
- The Grievance Redressal Body (GRB) is the custodian of the initiative.
- Uses an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) framework for handling and resolving incidents.
- Has a gender-neutral scope covering sexual harassment at the workplace.

In accordance with the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, Internal Committees have been established across development centers falling under regulatory jurisdiction. These committees are responsible for redressing sexual harassment concerns raised by women employees.

ASHI includes:

- Multiple safe reporting channels.
- Continuous awareness, education, and enablement.
- Monitoring and analytics.

The GRB comprises external experts, senior internal leaders, and the Investigative Council. It oversees governance, policy interpretation, and implementation relating to the prevention of sexual harassment at the workplace.

Employees can report concerns through the ASHI web application, the InfyMe app, or GRB@infosys.com. Cases follow a formal process aligned with statutory requirements and Company policies.

## Behavior Matters

The Behavior Matters campaign promotes workplace behavior that supports respect, collaboration, productivity, employee well-being, and an inclusive work environment.

## Extending the commitment to contract staff

Infosys extends its commitment to a safe and inclusive workplace to third-party personnel operating on its campuses. Refresher training sessions are delivered in nine Indian languages. Emergency and safety cards with essential contact information are provided to employees and contracted staff.

## Whistleblower Policy

Infosys’ Whistleblower Policy is aligned with the regulatory requirements of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX), 2002, and the Companies Act, 2013, including the requirement for listed companies to establish a vigil mechanism for reporting genuine concerns.

The mechanism primarily covers complaints regarding probable discrepancies in accounting, internal accounting controls, or auditing and permits anonymous reporting. The Office of Integrity and Compliance administers the policy and forum. Complaints concerning deviations in workplace policies or processes involving employees are reviewed in coordination with the Employee Relations Department.

## Human rights

As a signatory to the United Nations Global Compact, Infosys supports human rights in accordance with the United Nations Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and the International Labor Organization’s Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work. Infosys is also a signatory to the UN Women’s Empowerment Principles.

The Infosys Code of Conduct and Ethics includes the principle of “Respecting Each Other.” The Human Rights Statement provides a framework for treating employees with respect and dignity and for applying common human-rights principles to business practices.

[Further details are available in the Infosys Code of Conduct](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-Governance/documents/codeofconduct.pdf).

## Recruitment

As of March 31, 2026, the Group employed 3,28,594 employees, including 3,10,887 professionals involved in service delivery to clients, including trainees.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys:

- Received 58,84,480 employment applications.
- Interviewed 4,50,901 applicants.
- Extended offers of employment to 87,286 applicants.

These recruitment statistics exclude subsidiaries.

Through the RestartWithInfy initiative, Infosys hired 947 women into the Infosys ecosystem in fiscal 2026.

## Education, training and assessment (ETA)

Infosys’ Foundation Training Program prepares newly onboarded entry-level hires for corporate roles. The curriculum covers more than 40 technology streams and includes generative AI and prompt engineering courses. In fiscal 2026, 10,766 fresh graduates completed training at the Global Education Center campus in Mysuru and were released to delivery units.

The Continuous Education Program provides online self-learning, instructor-led virtual training, and in-person classroom training to reskill and upskill employees in digital technologies, methodologies, and generative AI. Bridge programs provide training and internship opportunities for employees transitioning to new career fields.

In fiscal 2026, 81.8% of the workforce received training, with average annual employee learning hours of 113. Cumulatively, 84% of the workforce was AI-aware.

Lex, Infosys’ in-house learning platform, offered more than 40,000 curated courses, including more than 30,000 courses procured from vendor partners. On average, 37,000 employees used Lex on weekdays and approximately 8,000 used it on weekends, with average learning time of 39 minutes.

Infosys Wingspan is a configurable talent-transformation platform used by global organizations for talent-transformation programs.

Infosys Springboard offers more than 20,000 courses. Approximately 11 million learners worldwide actively consume content on the platform.
