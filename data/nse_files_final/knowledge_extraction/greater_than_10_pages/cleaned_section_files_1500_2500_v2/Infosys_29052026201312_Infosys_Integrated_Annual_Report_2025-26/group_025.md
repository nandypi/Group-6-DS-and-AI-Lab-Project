## Annexure 4: Independent Auditor's certificate on corporate governance

## CERTIFICATE ON CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants and Statutory Auditors of Infosys Limited, examined the Company’s compliance with corporate governance conditions for the year ended March 31, 2026. The examination covered regulations 17 to 27, clauses (b) to (i) of regulation 46(2), and paragraphs C and D of Schedule V of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

### Managements' Responsibility

Management was responsible for complying with the corporate governance conditions, including designing, implementing, and maintaining internal controls and procedures to ensure compliance with the Listing Regulations.

### Auditor's Responsibility

The auditors’ responsibility was limited to examining the procedures adopted and their implementation for ensuring compliance with corporate governance conditions. The work was neither an audit nor an expression of opinion on the Company’s financial statements.

The auditors examined the Company’s books of account and other relevant records and documents to provide reasonable assurance regarding compliance with corporate governance requirements. The examination was conducted in accordance with the ICAI Guidance Note on Certification of Corporate Governance, applicable requirements of Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the ICAI Guidance Note on Reports or Certificates for Special Purposes.

The auditors complied with applicable requirements of Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1, *Quality Control for Firms that Perform Audits and Reviews of Historical Financial Information, and Other Assurance and Related Services Engagements*, and with the ICAI Code of Ethics.
