## 5. Cybersecurity and data privacy framework

Yes. Infosys has a cybersecurity framework, SEED, aligned to NIST's Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), supported by supplementary policies, processes, procedures, and standards for enterprise-level information security objectives.

Infosys also has a Data Privacy Policy published on the Company's intranet. The policy applies across Infosys operations, including activities involving service providers. Privacy notices are provided at the point of data collection for internal and external data subjects.

## 6. Corrective actions relating to consumer, cybersecurity, data privacy, and product safety issues

Infosys has reviewed its cybersecurity posture, technology, and threat landscape to further strengthen its cyber defense capabilities. The Company continues to review and strengthen cybersecurity processes and controls across its network in line with industry best practices.

### Consumer satisfaction surveys

Yes. Infosys conducts surveys to gauge customer satisfaction for its major services. In addition to regular client interactions, the Company conducts an annual Client Value Survey and periodic engagement surveys.

The surveys use a structured questionnaire, and feedback is collected through a web survey hosted by an independent organization. Survey results help Infosys understand client expectations and needs and serve as an input for investment decisions.

## CEO and CFO certification

Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, and Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer of Infosys Limited, certified the following for the year ended March 31, 2026:

1. They reviewed the Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit and Loss, Statement of Changes in Equity, Statement of Cash Flows, significant accounting policies, explanatory information, and the Board's report.
2. The statements do not contain any materially untrue statement or omit any material fact necessary to ensure that the statements are not misleading.
3. The financial statements and other financial information present, in all material respects, a true and fair view of the Company's affairs, financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows, and comply with applicable accounting standards, laws, and regulations.
4. No transactions were entered into during the year that were fraudulent, illegal, or in violation of the Company's Code of Conduct and Ethics, except as disclosed to the auditors and the Audit Committee.
5. Management is responsible for establishing and maintaining disclosure controls and internal controls over financial reporting. Management certified that it:
   - Designed disclosure controls and procedures to ensure that material information, including information relating to consolidated subsidiaries, was made known to management during preparation of the report.
   - Designed internal control over financial reporting to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and preparation of financial statements under Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS).
   - Evaluated the effectiveness of disclosure controls and procedures.
   - Disclosed any changes in internal control over financial reporting that materially affected, or were reasonably likely to materially affect, those controls.
6. Based on the evaluation of internal control over financial reporting, management disclosed to the auditors and the Audit Committee, where applicable:
   - Any deficiencies in the design or operation of internal controls that could adversely affect financial reporting, and confirmed that there were no material weaknesses, including related corrective actions.
   - Any significant changes in internal controls during the year.
   - All significant changes in accounting policies, which were disclosed in the notes to the financial statements.
   - Any significant fraud involving management or employees with a significant role in internal control over financial reporting.
7. No personnel were denied access to the Audit Committee regarding alleged misconduct, and whistleblowers were protected from unfair termination and other unfair or prejudicial employment practices.
8. All Board members and senior management personnel affirmed compliance with the Code of Conduct and Ethics for the year.

The certification was dated April 23, 2026, in Bengaluru.
