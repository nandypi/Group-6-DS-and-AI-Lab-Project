## Fairness and excellence

## Board member evaluation

The Board monitors and reviews the Board evaluation framework and works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee to establish evaluation criteria for the Chairman, the Board, Board committees, and executive, non-executive, and independent directors through peer evaluation, excluding the director being evaluated.

Independent directors are evaluated based on factors including:

- Ability to contribute to and monitor corporate governance practices.
- Ability to introduce international best practices to address business challenges and risks.
- Active participation in long-term strategic planning.
- Commitment to directors' obligations and fiduciary responsibilities, including participation in Board and committee meetings.
- Participation in a formal annual Board review to improve the effectiveness of the Board, its committees, and individual directors.

The Board engaged Egon Zehnder, a leadership advisory firm on board matters, to conduct the Board evaluation for fiscal 2026. The evaluation covered Board dynamics, softer aspects, committee effectiveness, and information flow to the Board or its committees. The methodology included questionnaires and one-on-one discussions. Succession planning and committee composition were also considered.

The evaluation process was completed during fiscal 2026. Recommendations from the previous year's evaluation were discussed with the Board, individual feedback was provided, and progress on prior recommendations and current-year recommendations was reviewed.

The evaluation was also based on confirmation from the independent directors that they met the independence criteria under the Companies Act, the Listing Regulations, and the NYSE listing manual.

## Board and executive leadership compensation

### Executive leadership compensation

Executive compensation programs link a significant portion of total rewards to long-term corporate performance goals. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommends to the Board the remuneration of directors, KMP, and senior management, as required under the Listing Regulations.

### Non-executive and non-independent chairman's compensation

Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman, voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company.

### Independent directors' compensation

Compensation payable to independent directors is limited to a fixed amount per year determined and approved by the Board. The total does not exceed 1% of net profit for the year, calculated under the Companies Act, 2013.

The Board considers global board compensation benchmarking, individual participation in Board and committee meetings, committee membership or chairmanship, time spent on other duties, roles and functions prescribed under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Listing Regulations, and other relevant factors.

Shareholders at the 34th AGM held on June 22, 2015, approved payment of up to 1% of the Company's annual net profit, calculated under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013, to some or all non-executive directors as determined by the Board.

The amount payable to independent directors for the year ended March 31, 2026 was ₹19.91 crore. Independent directors were also reimbursed for expenses incurred in performing their official duties. None of the non-executive directors received remuneration amounting to 50% of the total remuneration paid to non-executive directors during the year ended March 31, 2026.

## Corporate governance report

## Indemnification agreements

The Company entered into agreements to indemnify its directors and officers for claims brought against them to the fullest extent permitted under applicable law. The agreements cover certain expenses, judgments, fines, and settlement amounts incurred in actions or proceedings, including actions by or on behalf of the Company, arising from their services as directors or officers. They also cover expenses related to public relations consultation, if required.

## Agreements binding listed entities

Pursuant to Regulation 30A of the Listing Regulations, no agreement was entered into or executed during fiscal 2026 by the shareholders, promoters, promoter group entities, related parties, directors, key managerial personnel, or employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.
