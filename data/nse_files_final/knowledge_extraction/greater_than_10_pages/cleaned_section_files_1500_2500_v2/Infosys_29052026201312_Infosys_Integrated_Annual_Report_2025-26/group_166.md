## Additional information on directors recommended for appointment / reappointment as required under Regulation 36 of the LODR Regulations and applicable secretarial standards

### Nandan M. Nilekani — Chairman

Nandan M. Nilekani is the Co-Founder and Chairman of Infosys Technologies Limited. He was the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), in the rank of a Cabinet Minister, from 2009 to 2014. He is also the Co-Founder and Chairman of EkStep Foundation, a not-for-profit organization focused on technology-based learning, literacy, and numeracy. In January 2023, he was appointed co-chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion, and Development.

Nandan received a bachelor's degree from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bombay.

**Age:** 70 years

**Nature of expertise in specific functional areas:** Information Technology, Leadership, Strategy, Board service and governance, Financial, Diversity, Global business, Sales and marketing, Cybersecurity, Mergers and Acquisitions, Risk management, and Sustainability and ESG.
