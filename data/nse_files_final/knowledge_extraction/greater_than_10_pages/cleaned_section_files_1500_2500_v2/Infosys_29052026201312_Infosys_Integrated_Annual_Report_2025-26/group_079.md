## viii.

There were no transactions relating to previously unrecorded income that had been surrendered or disclosed as income during the year in tax assessments under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## ix.

- The Company had not taken any loans or other borrowings from any lender. Reporting under clause 3(ix)(a) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- The Company had not been declared a wilful defaulter by any bank, financial institution, government, or government authority.
- The Company had not taken any term loan during the year, and there were no outstanding term loans at the beginning of the year. Reporting under clause 3(ix)(c) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- Based on an overall examination of the financial statements, short-term funds had not, prima facie, been used for long-term purposes during the year.
- The Company had not taken funds from any entity or person to meet the obligations of its subsidiaries.
- The Company had not raised any loans during the year. Reporting under clause 3(ix)(f) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

## x.

- The Company had not raised money through an initial public offer, further public offer, or debt instruments during the year. Reporting under clause 3(x)(a) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- The Company had not made any preferential allotment or private placement of shares or fully, partly, or optionally convertible debentures during the year. Reporting under clause 3(x)(b) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

## xi.

- No fraud by the Company, and no material fraud on the Company, had been noticed or reported during the year.
- No report under Section 143(12) of the Companies Act had been filed in Form ADT-4 with the Central Government during the year or up to the date of the report.
- Whistleblower complaints received by the Company during the year and up to the date of the report were considered in determining the nature, timing, and extent of audit procedures.

## xii.

The Company was not a Nidhi Company. Reporting under clause (xii) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

## xiii.

The Company was in compliance with Sections 177 and 188 of the Companies Act, 2013, regarding applicable related-party transactions. Details of related-party transactions were disclosed in the Standalone Financial Statements as required by applicable accounting standards.

## xiv.

- The Company had an adequate internal audit system commensurate with the size and nature of its business.
- Internal audit reports issued to the Company during the year and up to the date of the report were considered in determining the nature, timing, and extent of audit procedures.

## xv.

The Company had not entered into any non-cash transactions with its Directors or persons connected with its Directors during the year. Provisions of Section 192 of the Companies Act, 2013 were therefore not applicable.

## xvi.

- The Company was not required to be registered under Section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. Reporting under clauses 3(xvi)(a), (b), and (c) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- There was no core investment company within the Group, as defined in the Core Investment Companies (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016. Reporting under clause 3(xvi)(d) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

## xvii.

The Company had not incurred cash losses during the financial year covered by the audit or during the immediately preceding financial year.

## xviii.

There had been no resignation of the Company’s statutory auditors during the year.

## xix.

Based on the financial ratios, ageing and expected dates of realisation of financial assets and payment of financial liabilities, other information accompanying the financial statements, the Board of Directors’ and Management’s plans, and examination of evidence supporting the assumptions, nothing had come to the auditors’ attention indicating that any material uncertainty existed as at the date of the audit report regarding the Company’s ability to meet liabilities existing at the balance sheet date as they fell due within one year.

This was not an assurance about the Company’s future viability. The reporting was based on facts available up to the date of the audit report and did not provide a guarantee or assurance that all liabilities falling due within one year from the balance sheet date would be discharged when due.

## xx.

- There were no unspent amounts relating to Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) other than ongoing projects that required transfer to a Fund specified in Schedule VII to the Companies Act, 2013. Reporting under clause 3(xx)(a) of the Order was therefore not applicable for the year.
- For ongoing projects, the Company had transferred unspent CSR amounts as at the end of the previous financial year to a Special Account within 30 days from the end of that financial year, in compliance with Section 135(6) of the Companies Act, 2013.
- For ongoing projects, the Company had not transferred the unspent CSR amount as at the balance sheet date relating to amounts required to be spent during the year to a Special Account as at the date of the report, because the applicable 30-day transfer period from the end of the financial year had not elapsed.
