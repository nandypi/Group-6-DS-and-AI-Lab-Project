## Management's discussion and analysis

## Overview

Infosys is a provider of AI-first business consulting and technology services. It has more than four decades of experience managing the systems and operations of global enterprises. Its strategy is to help clients ideate, plan, and execute their transition to an AI-first operating model.

Infosys’s stated vision is to deliver business solutions using technology and its core values are Client Value, Leadership by Example, Integrity and Transparency, Fairness, and Excellence (C-LIFE).

The company’s strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization, remain relevant to clients, create employee growth opportunities, generate profitable returns for investors, and contribute to the communities in which it operates. Business risks and challenges are discussed in the Risk management report of the Integrated Annual Report.

## I. Industry structure and developments

Fiscal 2026 saw lower interest rates and stable economic sentiment, while policy changes and geopolitical uncertainties weighed on the technology services industry.

The year was characterized by subdued growth, with some economic green shoots supporting technology-led growth. AI-driven technology transformation is changing discretionary spending patterns and increasing pressure on traditional IT services.

Global GDP growth remained flat at 3.3% in CY 2025. During the same period, IT services contracted by 10 basis points and grew at 4.6%. Infosys expects client spending and investment to continue shifting toward AI in the near term.

Businesses used enterprise AI to reimagine cost structures, improve resilience and agility, personalize customer and employee experiences, and launch new products and services.

For more information, refer to the Our business context section of the Integrated Annual Report.

## II. Opportunities and threats

## Our strategy

Infosys’s clients and prospective clients are responding to advances in software and computing technology, especially AI. They are reimagining core offerings, processes, and systems and positioning themselves as leaders in their industries.

Infosys’s strategy is to act as a navigator for clients on their AI journeys and help them unlock business value. It supports clients in ideating, planning, and scaling their AI initiatives through data foundations and cloud capabilities. Infosys also describes Responsible AI practices focused on privacy, ethics, and controls.

## Our strengths

Infosys states that its more than four decades of experience managing global enterprise systems, AI-first strategy, and continued investment in AI capabilities support its ability to help clients navigate AI transformation.

## Our competition

Infosys operates in an intensely competitive environment that includes traditional services providers, rapid technology change, and specialized players serving niche markets.

The company has invested in proprietary intellectual property in software platforms and products, including:

- Infosys Cobalt: services, solutions, and platforms intended to accelerate enterprise cloud journeys.
- Infosys Topaz: AI-first services, solutions, and platforms using generative and agentic AI technology.
- Infosys Aster: an AI-amplified marketing suite.

## III. Financial condition

Refer to the Standalone and Consolidated financial statements in this Integrated Annual Report for detailed schedules and notes.

## 1. Equity share capital

Infosys has one class of equity shares with a par value of ₹5 each.

During the year, share capital movements resulted from:

- Buyback of 10,00,00,000 shares, resulting in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs.
- Shares issued on the exercise of stock options.

## 2. Other equity

Retained earnings changed due to profit earned during the year, payment of dividends, and the buyback of equity shares.

The company funded the buyback from free reserves, including securities premium, in accordance with Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013. As at March 31, 2026, it created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, through an appropriation from the general reserve under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The increase in securities premium was mainly due to the exercise of stock options. The Group made an irrevocable election to present subsequent changes in the fair value of certain instruments in other comprehensive income.

The Special Economic Zone Re-Investment Reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income-Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under Section 10AA(2) of the Income-Tax Act, 1961.

## 3. Property, plant and equipment

Additions to gross block were mainly related to computer equipment and infrastructure.

## 4. Goodwill and other intangible assets

On a consolidated basis, the carrying value of goodwill was ₹12,117 crore as at March 31, 2026, compared with ₹10,106 crore as at March 31, 2025.

The increase was primarily attributable to:

- Goodwill of ₹444 crore recognized on the acquisition of MRE Consulting Ltd. and The Missing Link Security Pty. Ltd., The Missing Link Security Limited, and The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd., collectively referred to as “The Missing Link”.
- A translation difference of ₹1,567 crore arising from foreign currency fluctuations.

The consolidated carrying value of intangible assets was ₹2,825 crore as at March 31, 2026, compared with ₹2,766 crore as at March 31, 2025. During the year ended March 31, 2026, intangible assets of ₹297 crore, mainly customer-related intangibles, were added through acquisitions.

## 5. Financial assets

## A. Investments

On a standalone basis, Infosys made additional investments in subsidiaries during the year for acquisitions, operations, and expansion.

Refer to Annexure 1 to the Board's report for the statement under Section 129(3) of the Companies Act, 2013, summarizing the financial performance of subsidiaries.

Infosys invests in the startup ecosystem through minority equity positions in startup companies and/or venture capital funds. These investments provide access to innovations that may be combined with its services and solutions for clients.

Investments comprise:

- Mutual fund units
- Tax-free bonds
- Non-convertible debentures
- Certificates of deposit
- Commercial paper
- Quoted bonds and securities issued by government and quasi-government organizations

Certificates of deposit and commercial paper are marketable securities issued by banks, non-bank financial companies, and eligible financial institutions for specified periods. These instruments carry high credit ratings from domestic credit rating agencies.

Government securities are liquid and marketable instruments issued across different tenures and backed by the Government. Non-convertible debentures comprise debt instruments issued by government-aided institutions and financial institutions with high credit ratings.

Most investments are fair valued using Level 1 or Level 2 inputs. Infosys assesses counterparty risk using criteria including Tier-I capital, capital adequacy ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and the deposit base of banks and financial institutions. These risks are monitored regularly as part of the risk management program.

## B. Trade receivables

Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) in USD terms was 67 days for the year ended March 31, 2026, compared with 69 days in the previous year.

## C. Cash and cash equivalents

Cash and cash equivalents comprise deposits with banks that have high credit ratings from international and domestic credit rating agencies. The deposits can be withdrawn at any time without prior notice or penalty on principal. Ratings are monitored periodically.

## D. Loans

Loans are provided to subsidiaries based on business requirements. Infosys had no loans outstanding as at March 31, 2026.

## E. Other financial assets

Deposits with corporations represent restricted deposits used to settle employee-related obligations arising in the normal course of business.

Unbilled revenues are classified as financial assets when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

Foreign currency forward and options contracts are entered into to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates on foreign currency exposures. The counterparty for these contracts is generally a bank.

## 6. Other assets

Unbilled revenues are classified as non-financial assets when the right to consideration depends on the completion of contractual milestones.

Withholding taxes and other amounts represent credits that can be used against local taxes payable in various countries.

Deferred contract costs mainly comprise the costs of obtaining and fulfilling contracts, recorded in accordance with Ind AS 115, Revenue from Contracts with Customers.

## 7. Deferred tax assets / liabilities

Deferred tax assets primarily arise from temporary differences relating to property, plant and equipment, lease liabilities, allowances for trade receivables, credits related to branch profits, and compensated absences.

These assets are partially offset by deferred tax liabilities relating to the Special Economic Zone Re-investment Reserve, branch profit tax, and intangible assets from business combinations.

A deferred income tax asset is recognized to the extent that it is probable that future taxable profits will be available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized.
