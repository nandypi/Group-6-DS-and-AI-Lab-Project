## VIII. Other details

## 1. Quality

The Quality function at Infosys has three strategic imperatives:

- Differentiate Infosys' services through superior performance and quality.
- Optimize client projects and internal functions for greater efficiency and agility.
- De-risk operations through delivery excellence, compliance, and sustainability.

To support different levels of client AI maturity, the Quality team has defined an adaptable AI-first software engineering framework covering AI-augmented and agentic AI engineering. The framework integrates AI-infused processes with Infosys and third-party platforms, tools, and accelerators. For early AI adopters, agentic AI includes tailorable blueprints, pre-built agents with human-in-the-loop orchestration, agent governance, and responsible AI guardrails.

Infosys is evolving its ways of working across Agile, DevSecOps, Site Reliability Engineering, and its product-led, platform-driven operating model. Quality consulting supports AI-infused ways of working and culture, AI engineering practices, and AI governance.

The Quality function has deployed frameworks, process levers, tools, and platforms across service lines to improve productivity and quality. Its automation maturity model supports projects in progressing from point tools toward cognitive and autonomous operations using AI and generative AI.

Quality uses structured audits and assessment frameworks to de-risk operations, with coverage expanded across a broader range of services, centers, and subsidiaries.

Infosys complies with international management-system standards and models, including:

- ISO 9001
- ISO 27001
- ISO 14001
- ISO 45001
- ISO 50001
- ISO 22301
- ISO 20000
- ISO 27701
- AS 9100
- ISO 13485

Infosys has onboarded AI practices from CMMI.AI and was among the first organizations to complete a pilot assessment by the CMMI Institute. Infosys also achieved reasonable assurance on ISO 14068-1, *Climate change management — Transition to net zero — Part 1: Carbon neutrality*. Infosys China and Infosys Brazil received certification under ISO 27701, the Privacy Information Management System standard.

## 2. Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (ICETS)

ICETS focuses on emerging technologies and enterprise-scale AI transformation, including research, experimentation, platforms, governance, and deployment.

## A structured innovation journey: Next, Near, and Now

The ICETS innovation framework consists of:

- **Next:** Applied Research Centers
- **Near:** Living Labs
- **Now:** Platforms and ecosystems, including Infosys Topaz Fabric

## Next - Applied Research Centers (ARC)

Applied Research Centers research, create, and apply emerging technologies, including advanced AI, cybersecurity, robotics, autonomous machines, quantum computing, and next-generation interaction models. Their work combines medium- and long-term research with near-term client applications and focuses on building in-house expertise and intellectual property.

## Advanced AI

ICETS is addressing enterprise AI execution challenges through:

- AI agent orchestration frameworks
- Automated model lifecycle transitions from large language models (LLMs) to small language models (SLMs)
- Agent certification and governance models
- Runtime observability, auditability, and control

The AI systems being developed are intended to plan and execute multi-step processes, collaborate with humans and other agents, and adapt to changing contexts.

## New interaction model

ICETS is researching generative-AI interfaces using expressive avatars and digital humans. Capabilities include:

- Realistic speech, lip-sync, and facial expressions
- Emotion-aware, personalized, and context-driven engagement

## Cybersecurity

ICETS cybersecurity research includes:

- AI-driven threat detection and response
- Privacy-preserving techniques, including federated learning and secure inference
- Multimodal threat analytics and real-time security operations

## Autonomous Machines

Research areas include:

- Intelligent systems operating in dynamic, real-world environments
- Sensor fusion, simulation, and edge intelligence
- Scalable deployment across logistics, mobility, and infrastructure

## Humanoids

Research areas include:

- Human-like robots with advanced perception and dexterity
- Collaboration between humans and machines in complex tasks
- Safety, productivity, and real-world adaptability

## Quantum

Research areas include:

- Hybrid quantum-classical computing
- Optimization and scientific-computing use cases
- Post-quantum cryptography and enterprise readiness

## Near - Living Labs

Living Labs support co-creation, rapid prototyping, and real-world validation so clients can contextualize, experiment with, iterate on, and de-risk innovations in controlled environments.

## Infosys Living Labs

Infosys Living Labs are present in all major geographies, and the network is expanding. They have hosted hundreds of client visits.

The Living Labs showcase Infosys Topaz, Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Aster, brand partnerships, hyperscaler and other alliances, the Infosys Innovation Network of startups, academic collaborations, CSR initiatives, and Infosys service lines and business verticals. The showcases also cover themes such as responsible AI, accessibility, cybersecurity, future of retail, payments, banking, and manufacturing.

## Client Living Labs

Client Living Labs are innovation-as-a-service engagements addressing client- and industry-specific challenges through emerging technologies, design, innovation-management expertise, and rapid experimentation.

Client Living Labs include 40 active engagements centered on:

- **IncubateNext:** Rapid experimentation through internal experts and external partnerships, applying emerging technologies and design to real-world use cases across domains and geographies.
- **ImagineNext:** Exploration of future possibilities through trend sensing and innovation insights to identify new business opportunities.
- **SenseNext:** Identification of early market and technology signals and translation of those insights into actionable strategies.

## Now - Platforms and Ecosystems: Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys Topaz Fabric is a composable and interoperable platform of AI agents, tools, services, and models. It supports enterprise-scale AI adoption through reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities.

The platform helps organizations optimize existing IT processes, automate or eliminate manual tasks, and build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

## Consulting

Infosys consulting brings together strategic intelligence, AI-powered insights, and structured frameworks for research, product discovery, large-deal solutions, value management, process re-engineering, roadmaps, and initiative prioritization.

## Engineering

Infosys engineering covers greenfield engineering, application modernization, platform and cloud migration, data and quality engineering, and process re-engineering. AI and intelligent automation are applied across build, migrate, and run activities.

## Operations

Infosys operations covers IT operations, business operations, integrated BizTech operations, network operations, and cybersecurity security operations centers. Capabilities include AI-driven monitoring, automation, real-time intelligence, self-healing, and predictive insights.

## Driving measurable business impact

ICETS' stated focus areas include accelerating enterprise-wide AI adoption, enabling faster experimentation and deployment, strengthening governance, improving cost efficiency and operational predictability, and building capabilities across industries.

## Looking ahead

ICETS is focused on research, experimentation, tools, platforms, governance, and expertise for applying emerging technologies and AI in enterprise environments. Its stated priorities include execution excellence, ecosystem collaboration, scalability, and responsible innovation.
