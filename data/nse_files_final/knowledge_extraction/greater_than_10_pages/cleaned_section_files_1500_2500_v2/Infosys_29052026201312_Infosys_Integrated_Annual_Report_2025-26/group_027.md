## 4. Executive summary along with web-link(s) of Impact Assessment of CSR Projects carried out in pursuance of subrule (3) of rule 8:

For fiscal 2026, the Company engaged an external agency to support an independent review and impact assessment of its CSR projects.

### Methodology:

The impact assessment used quantitative and qualitative research tools, as relevant, for primary data collection. The assessment team conducted virtual and on-field interactions with project beneficiaries and other relevant stakeholders. The findings were consolidated into a report for Management's consideration.

The study was guided by the Inclusiveness, Relevance, Effectiveness, Convergence and Sustainability (IRECS) Framework. The framework provided feedback on implementation efficacy and efficiency, including achievement of desired project outputs relative to inputs.

### Summary:

During the year, impact assessments were conducted for 12 eligible CSR projects across various states in India. The projects aligned with the ESG framework and supported 13 UN Sustainable Development Goals across education, healthcare, women empowerment, sustainability, and other themes.

## Theme 1: Education and skill development

**Impact:** Digital education and improved access to online learning content positively affected students' academic performance and reduced dropout rates.

|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sree Guruvayurappan Bhajan Samaj (SGBS) Unnati Foundation - UNXT Youth Training | Karnataka | Infosys Foundation |
| 2 | Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI) - Oriental Studies Research and Preservation | Maharashtra | Infosys Foundation |
| 3 | eVidyaloka Trust - Rural digital and STEM education programme | Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Uttar Pradesh | Infosys Foundation |

**Impact:** Improved cookstove projects reported reductions in waste generation, cooking time, indoor air pollution, and associated health hazards. Women were empowered through engagement in social activities within their communities.

## 5. CSR obligation and expenditure

- **(a) Average net profit of the Company as per sub-section (5) of Section 135:** ₹28,848.03 crore
- **(b) Two percent of average net profit of the Company as per sub-section (5) of Section 135:** ₹576.96 crore
- **(c) Surplus arising out of CSR projects, programmes, or activities of previous financial years:** ₹0.40 crore*
- **(d) Amount required to be set off for the financial year:** Nil
- **(e) Total CSR obligation for the financial year [(b) + (c) - (d)]:** ₹577.36 crore

- **(g) Amount spent on administrative overheads:** ₹9.25 crore
- **(h) Amount spent on impact assessment:** ₹1.69 crore
- **(i) Total amount spent for the financial year [(f) + (g) + (h)]:** ₹558.44 crore
