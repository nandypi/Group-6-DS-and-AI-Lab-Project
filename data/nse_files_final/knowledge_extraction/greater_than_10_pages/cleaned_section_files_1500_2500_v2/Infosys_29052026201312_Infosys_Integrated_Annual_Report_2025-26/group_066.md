## 5. Describe the internal mechanisms in place to redress grievances related to human rights issues.

Infosys provides employees and contract staff with access to a grievance-resolution mechanism known as Resolution hubs. Employees and contract staff can raise workplace concerns, including human-rights issues, through this mechanism.

Resolution hubs follow the principles of natural justice, confidentiality, sensitivity, non-retaliation, and fairness. Concerns are investigated through a process that gives all involved parties an opportunity to present facts and relevant evidence, followed by timely action and closure.

## 8. Mechanism to prevent adverse consequences to the complainant in discrimination and harassment cases

Infosys’ non-retaliation policy is part of its Code of Conduct. The company states that complainants will be protected from retaliation for good-faith reports of integrity concerns. This protection also extends to individuals who assist with or cooperate in investigations or report an integrity concern or question.
