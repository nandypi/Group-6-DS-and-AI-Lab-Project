## Cybersecurity

Infosys operates with employees in a hybrid model and monitors global cybersecurity events to maintain its cybersecurity posture and support compliance.

Infosys is certified against the Information Security Management System (ISMS) Standard ISO 27001:2022. It has also received independent attestations for SSAE 18 and ISAE 3402 SOC 1 and SOC 2.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys strengthened its cybersecurity capabilities through technology, tools, and processes. Its initiatives included:

- Continuous cybersecurity workforce training and reskilling.
- An inclusive security culture with shared ownership.
- Enhanced capabilities in AI/ML security, cyber resiliency, and secure supply chain ecosystems.
- Dedicated cybersecurity courses and resource kits for developers.

## Internal financial control and its adequacy

The Board has adopted policies and procedures covering:

- Orderly and efficient conduct of business.
- Adherence to the Company's policies.
- Safeguarding of assets.
- Prevention and detection of fraud.
- Error-reporting mechanisms.
- Accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Timely preparation of reliable financial disclosures.

Further details are provided in the “Internal control systems and their adequacy” section of the Management's Discussion and Analysis.

## Listing on stock exchanges

Infosys shares are listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares (ADSs) are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).

## Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF)

During fiscal 2026:

- The Company transferred unclaimed and unencashed dividends of `6,12,63,126` to the IEPF.
- The Company transferred 7,23,720 corresponding shares on which dividends had remained unclaimed for seven consecutive years.
- The Company paid `3,39,64,248` as dividends in respect of shares held by the IEPF.

Details of benefits arising from shares transferred to the IEPF, year-wise unclaimed or unencashed dividend amounts, and shares liable to be transferred are provided in the Corporate Governance Report and on the [Infosys IEPF page](https://www.infosys.com/investors/shareholder-services/unclaimed-dividend-shares.html).

## Directors' responsibility statement

The financial statements are prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), under the historical cost convention and on an accrual basis, except for:

- Certain financial instruments measured at fair value.
- Defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

The financial statements are also prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act and guidelines issued by SEBI. Ind AS are prescribed under Section 133 of the Act, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and subsequent amendment rules.

Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except when a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing standard requires a change in policy.

## Board's report

The directors confirm that:

- Applicable accounting standards were followed in preparing the annual accounts for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, with no material departures.
- Accounting policies were selected and applied consistently, with reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates to present a true and fair view of the Company's affairs and profit for the period.
- Proper and sufficient care was taken to maintain adequate accounting records, safeguard assets, and prevent and detect fraud and other irregularities.
- The annual accounts were prepared on a going-concern basis.
- Adequate internal financial controls were established and operated effectively.
- Systems were established to ensure compliance with applicable laws, and those systems were adequate and operated effectively.

LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), as statutory auditor. The Board approved the announcement of this proposed appointment at its meeting held on April 23, 2026.

The proposed appointment is to be recommended to shareholders at the 46th AGM in 2027 for an initial term of five consecutive years, until the conclusion of the 51st AGM in 2032. The appointment is subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements.

## 6. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Infosys undertakes CSR initiatives primarily through the Infosys Foundation. Its focus areas include:

- Education and skill development to support sustained livelihoods.
- Healthcare, including preventive healthcare.
- Environmental sustainability and ecological balance.
- Gender equality and women's empowerment.
- National heritage, art, and culture.
- Rural development.
- Disaster relief and rehabilitation.

The Company also undertakes CSR initiatives outside India, including in the United States, Australia, and parts of Europe. Initiatives in the United States are carried out through Infosys Foundation USA. These initiatives are additional to the statutory requirement.

The annual report on CSR activities is included as Annexure 6 to the Board's report.

## Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)

Infosys launched its ESG Vision 2030 in October 2020.

Environmental priorities include:

- Using technology to address climate change.
- Water management.
- Waste management.

Social priorities include:

- Digital skilling.
- Diversity and inclusion.
- Employee wellness and experience.
- Technology for social benefit.
- Supporting the communities in which Infosys operates.

The Company also focuses on stakeholder interests, core values, and corporate governance.

The ESG Committee was constituted on April 14, 2021, to oversee organization-wide ESG initiatives, priorities, and ESG practices. The Committee reports to the Board and meets quarterly to review progress against the ESG Vision 2030 ambitions.
