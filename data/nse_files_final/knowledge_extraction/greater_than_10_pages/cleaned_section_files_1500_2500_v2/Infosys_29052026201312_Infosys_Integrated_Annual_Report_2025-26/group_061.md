## Leadership Indicators

1. Has the entity conducted Life Cycle Perspective / Assessments (LCA) for any of its products (for manufacturing industry) or for its services (for service industry)? If yes, provide details in the following format?

Life cycle assessment is in progress. Infosys is an IT consulting and software services organization. Its life cycle practices include green procurement considerations in technical specifications for purchases and end-of-life management to maximize recycling.

Infosys has mandated Life Cycle Assessment for new physical infrastructure to support informed decisions about environmentally friendly products in the construction and operation of its buildings.

2. If there are any significant social or environmental concerns and / or risks arising from production or disposal of your products / services, as identified in the Life Cycle Perspective / Assessments (LCA) or through any other means, briefly describe the same along-with action taken to mitigate the same

Infosys reported no significant social or environmental concerns or risks arising from the production and disposal of its products and services. Its environmental footprint is discussed in Principle 6 of the BRSR report.
