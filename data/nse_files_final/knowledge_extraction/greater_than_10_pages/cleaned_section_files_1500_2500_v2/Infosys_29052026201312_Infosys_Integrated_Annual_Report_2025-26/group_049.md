## Outstanding ADSs / GDRs / warrants or any convertible instruments

Infosys ADSs, evidenced by ADRs, trade in the United States on the NYSE under the ticker symbol `INFY`. The currency of trade is USD, and each ADS represents one equity share. ADRs evidencing the ADSs began trading on the NYSE on December 12, 2012.

As of March 31, 2026, there were 1,34,734 ADR record holders representing 30,50,54,064 ADSs. The Company had no outstanding GDRs, warrants, or convertible instruments as of that date.

## Communication to the shareholders

Infosys makes the following investor and shareholder information available through its website:

- Quarterly reports, additional information, and official news releases.
- Standalone and consolidated quarterly and annual financial statements, including segmental information.
- Earnings calls, analyst and investor call transcripts, AGM webcasts, AGM presentations, transcripts, and video archives.
- The annual Form 20-F filed with the SEC.
- Press releases, stock-exchange disclosures, and presentations made to investors and analysts.
- Statutory SEC filings available through the SEC website.

Quarterly and annual results are generally published in *Business Standard*, an English-language national daily, and *Prajavani*, a regional daily circulating in Karnataka.

## Registered office and global locations

Infosys' operations were spread across 290 locations in 59 countries as of the reporting period. The Company had no manufacturing plants; its operations consisted of development centers and offices in India and overseas.

## Subsidiaries

As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 30 direct subsidiaries and 84 step-down subsidiaries. The Company did not have any material subsidiary.
