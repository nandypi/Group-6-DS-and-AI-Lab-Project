## Board as a trustee

The Board recognizes its primary role as trustee of shareholder capital and seeks to uphold corporate governance standards based on excellence and integrity.

## Corporate governance guidelines

Infosys’ corporate governance guidelines provide a framework for the Board and Management to pursue the Company’s objectives for the benefit of stakeholders. The guidelines are framed with reference to the Company’s Memorandum & Articles of Association, Board committee charters, and applicable laws, regulations, and guidelines in India, the US, and other jurisdictions.

## Role of the Board of Directors

The primary role of the Board is trusteeship: protecting and enhancing shareholder value. The Board is responsible for:

- Exercising appropriate control to ensure that the Company is managed efficiently and fulfills stakeholder and societal expectations.
- Monitoring the effectiveness of the Company’s governance practices and making changes as necessary.
- Providing strategic guidance and monitoring Management.
- Exercising independent judgment on corporate affairs.
- Assigning sufficient non-executive directors to matters involving potential conflicts of interest.
- Reviewing and guiding corporate strategy, major action plans, risk policy, annual budgets, and business plans.
- Setting performance objectives, monitoring implementation and corporate performance, and overseeing major capital expenditures, acquisitions, and divestments.

## Independent directors

Infosys follows the definitions of an independent director under the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the New York Stock Exchange listed company manual, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, and applicable US securities laws.

Based on disclosures received from the independent directors and the Board’s assessment, the independent directors fulfill the applicable independence requirements and are independent of Management.

## Board composition

Infosys recognizes the importance of Board diversity across thought, perspective, regional and industry experience, cultural and geographical background, age, ethnicity, race, gender, knowledge, and skills. Relevant expertise includes financial management, global business, leadership, technology, mergers and acquisitions, Board service, strategy, sales and marketing, environmental, social and governance (ESG), risk, and cybersecurity.

As of April 23, 2026, the Board comprised ten members: a non-executive and non-independent Chairman, a Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director (CEO & MD), and eight independent directors.

Director profiles, including nationality, age, appointment and reappointment dates, Board tenure, term-ending date, shareholding, Board memberships in Indian companies, committee details under Regulation 26 of the Listing Regulations, and core expertise and competency, are provided in the Infosys Board of Directors section of the Integrated Annual Report.

There are no inter se relationships among the Board members. The Company does not have any pecuniary relationship with its non-executive directors. During the year, the Company did not provide loans or advances to firms or companies in which directors are interested.

The Board has six committees:

- Audit Committee
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee
- Stakeholders Relationship Committee
- Risk Management Committee
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee
- ESG Committee

All committees comprise only independent directors, with one director serving as chairperson. The Company also has a Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee under the Risk Management Committee, comprising only independent directors.

## Board meetings

### Scheduling and selection of agenda items for Board meetings

Tentative dates for Board meetings for the next fiscal year are decided in advance and published in the Integrated Annual Report. The Chairman and the Company Secretary, in consultation with the CEO & MD and lead independent director, propose the agenda for each meeting. The agenda and explanatory notes are circulated to directors in advance. Each Board member may suggest additional agenda items.

The Board meets at least once a quarter to review quarterly results and other agenda items. Additional meetings are held when necessary. Independent directors are expected to attend at least four quarterly Board meetings and the Annual General Meeting (AGM). Video and teleconferencing facilities are provided to enable participation by directors based in different parts of the world.

Board members are expected to prepare for, attend, and participate in Board and applicable committee meetings and to ensure that other commitments do not materially interfere with their responsibilities to the Company.

### Meeting of independent directors

Independent directors are required under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, and the related rules to hold at least one meeting each financial year without the attendance of non-independent directors and Management members.

During the year, the independent directors met four times without executive directors and Management. They discussed matters including:

- Company performance and risks
- Flow of information to the Board
- Competition and strategy
- Leadership strengths and weaknesses
- Governance and compliance
- Board movements and succession planning
- Human resources matters
- Performance of executive Board members, committees, and the Chairman

## Responsible leadership

The Board and Management work together to establish standards of responsible leadership.

## Responsibilities of the Board leadership

The Board oversees Management’s functions and protects the long-term interests of stakeholders. The responsibilities and authority of the Chairman, the CEO & MD, and the lead independent director are described below.

## Chairman

Infosys separates the roles of Chairman and CEO & MD. The Chairman leads the Board, promotes Board integrity, and supports a culture in which the Board works for the long-term benefit of the Company and its stakeholders. The Chairman presides over Board and shareholder meetings.

The Chairman facilitates communication among directors and oversees governance matters, including the organization, composition, and effectiveness of the Board and its committees and the performance of individual directors.

The Chairman works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee on Board and committee composition, director induction, succession planning, Board evaluation, and feedback to individual directors.

## CEO & MD

The CEO & MD is responsible for executing corporate strategy in consultation with the Board, managing the Company, achieving annual and long-term business targets, and overseeing brand equity, planning, and external contacts.

The CEO & MD monitors competitive conditions and industry developments and standards, identifies opportunities for expansion and acquisition, and builds customer and market relationships. The CEO & MD also acts as a link between the Board and Management and leads and evaluates other executive leaders.

## Lead Independent Director

The lead independent director was appointed to provide independent leadership to the Board. The general authority and responsibilities of the lead independent director are determined by the independent directors, with additional duties assigned by the Board.

The lead independent director provides leadership to the independent directors, liaises on their behalf, and supports the Board’s effectiveness and the organization’s governance.

## Key Board qualifications, expertise and attributes

The following qualifications, skills, and attributes are considered when nominating candidates to the Board.

| Qualification or attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Financial | Leadership of a financial firm or management of an enterprise finance function, resulting in proficiency in complex financial management, capital allocation, and financial reporting, or experience actively supervising a principal financial officer, principal accounting officer, controller, public accountant, auditor, or person performing similar functions. |
| Diversity of perspectives | Representation of gender, ethnic, geographic, cultural, or other perspectives that broaden the Board’s understanding of customers, partners, employees, governments, and other stakeholders worldwide. |
| Global business | Experience driving business success in markets worldwide, with an understanding of diverse business environments, economic conditions, cultures, regulatory frameworks, and global market opportunities. |
| Leadership | Extensive leadership experience at a significant enterprise, including practical understanding of organizations, processes, strategic planning, and risk management, along with strengths in talent development, succession planning, change, and long-term growth. |
| Information Technology | Significant technology background, including knowledge of anticipating technological trends, generating disruptive innovation, and extending or creating new business models. |
| Mergers and acquisitions | Experience leading growth through acquisitions and other business combinations, assessing “build or buy” decisions, analyzing strategic and cultural fit, valuing transactions, and evaluating operational integration plans. |
| Board service and governance | Public company Board service providing insight into Board and Management accountability, protection of shareholder interests, and appropriate governance practices. |
| Sales and marketing | Experience developing strategies to grow sales and market share, build brand awareness and equity, and enhance enterprise reputation. |
| Sustainability and ESG | Experience leading organizational sustainability and ESG initiatives and integrating them into Company strategy. |
| Risk management | Experience identifying and evaluating significant risks to the Company’s business strategy and assessing Management’s mitigation of strategic, legal, compliance, and operational risks. |
| Cybersecurity | Experience assessing and managing cybersecurity risks and implementing cybersecurity policies, procedures, and strategies. |

The details of each director’s core expertise and competency are provided in the Infosys Board of Directors section of the Integrated Annual Report.
