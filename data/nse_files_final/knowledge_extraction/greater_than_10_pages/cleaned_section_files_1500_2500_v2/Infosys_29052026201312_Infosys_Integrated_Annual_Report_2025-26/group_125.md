## 2. Notes to the Consolidated financial statements

## 2.1 Business combinations

## Accounting policy

Business combinations are accounted for using the acquisition method under Ind AS 103, *Business Combinations*.

The purchase price is measured at the fair value of assets transferred, equity instruments issued, and liabilities incurred or assumed at the date control is transferred to the Group. It also includes the fair value of contingent consideration. Identifiable assets acquired, liabilities assumed, and contingent liabilities are initially measured at fair value on the acquisition date. Contingent consideration is remeasured at fair value at each reporting date, with changes recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

Non-controlling interests are initially measured either at fair value or at the proportionate share of the acquiree’s identifiable net assets. The measurement basis is selected separately for each acquisition. After acquisition, the carrying amount of non-controlling interests includes the initial amount recognized and their share of subsequent changes in subsidiary equity.

Business combinations between entities under common control are accounted for at the carrying value of assets acquired and liabilities assumed in the Group’s consolidated financial statements.

Options issued by the Group over non-controlling interests in subsidiaries are accounted for as financial liabilities. They are initially recognized at the estimated present value of the gross obligation and subsequently measured at fair value. If an option expires unexercised, the liability is derecognized.

## Acquisition during the year ended March 31, 2026

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership interests or voting interests in the following entities on April 30, 2025:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — An energy and business consulting services company headquartered in Texas, USA. The acquisition is expected to add capabilities in trading and risk management, particularly in the energy sector.
2. **The Missing Link** — Comprising The Missing Link Security Pty. Ltd., The Missing Link Security Limited, and The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd. The cybersecurity service provider is headquartered in Australia. The acquisition is expected to strengthen the Group’s cybersecurity capabilities and Australian market presence.

The purchase price was allocated to assets acquired and liabilities assumed based on their fair values at the acquisition date.

(1) Includes cash and cash equivalents acquired of ₹102 crore.

The excess of purchase consideration over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating goodwill were the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill amounting to ₹79 crore is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of ₹813 crore comprised upfront cash consideration of ₹743 crore and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of ₹70 crore at the acquisition date. The key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were probabilities assigned to the achievement of financial targets and discount rates ranging from 2% to 3%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026 was approximately ₹88 crore.

Retention bonuses and management incentives are payable to acquiree employees over two to three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. These amounts are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was ₹194 crore at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts had been substantially collected.

Transaction costs, including finder’s fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of ₹34 crore related to these acquisitions were included in administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended March 31, 2026.
