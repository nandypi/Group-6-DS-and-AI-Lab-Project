## From possibilities to enterprise AI impact

Infosys describes a shift from enterprise AI experimentation and pilots toward measurable outcomes involving revenue, costs, customer experience, operational responsiveness, and enterprise-wide adoption.

The Infosys AI First Value Framework consists of interconnected value pools powered by Infosys Topaz™, its generative and agentic AI suite, and Infosys Topaz Fabric™, its agentic services suite. The framework focuses on applying AI at scale across data, business processes, legacy modernization, physical devices, sensors, robotics, autonomous systems, digital twins, edge intelligence, and responsible and secure AI governance.

Infosys states that it is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys, with more than 4,600 AI projects underway. It has also developed more than 30 new service offerings across six value pools.

## Liberty Global

### From AI experiments to enterprise impact: How Liberty Global is improving customer experience with Infosys.

### Supporting 1000+ Platform Deliveries

Liberty Global, a European connectivity and entertainment provider, partnered with Infosys to operate and evolve connectivity and entertainment platforms serving tens of millions of customers across Europe.

Over the previous year, Infosys supported more than 1,000 platform deliveries. AI-enabled solutions included:

- **Super Search**, serving approximately 8 million TV customers and using large language models to support conversational content discovery across linear TV, on-demand, and streaming services.
- **Agent Assist** and **Customer Assist**, deployed across multiple markets to support self-service customer journeys, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce pressure on customer-care teams.

The initiatives resulted in 50% fewer outages year over year and 60% fewer customers impacted.

The Infosys–Liberty Global partnership spans more than two decades, including more than a decade as a formal strategic partnership.

## Hertz

## AI First Approach

Hertz used complex COBOL systems on a legacy platform and worked with Infosys on an AI-first modernization approach.

Infosys analyzed nearly three million lines of legacy COBOL code and used multimodal large language models to develop a cloud modernization blueprint. The modernization involved AI-powered reverse engineering, extraction of core business logic, and redevelopment as domain-centric Java microservices using Infosys iLEAD and GitHub Copilot. The technology stack included OpenAI, Claude Sonnet, and AWS Bedrock, supported by AI-enabled testing, reusable components, and enterprise governance.

The resulting microservices-based digital core is intended to improve platform agility, scalability, and system resilience, support high transaction volumes, and enable faster responses to changing business and customer requirements on AWS Cloud.

The engagement is expected to deliver 60% faster modernization timelines, increased reuse of cross-channel business capabilities, reduced hosting costs, and a scalable foundation for further modernization and automation across additional business domains.
