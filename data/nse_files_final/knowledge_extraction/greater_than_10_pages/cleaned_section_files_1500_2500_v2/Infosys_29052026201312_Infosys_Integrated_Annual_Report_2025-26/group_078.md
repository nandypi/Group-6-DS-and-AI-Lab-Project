## Annexure 'A' to the Independent Auditor's Report

*(Referred to in paragraph 1(f) under 'Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements' section of our report to the Members of Infosys Limited of even date)*

## Report on the Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements under Clause (i) of sub-section 3 of Section 143 of the Companies Act, 2013 (the 'Act')

We have audited the internal financial controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements of INFOSYS LIMITED (the 'Company') as of March 31, 2026, in conjunction with our audit of the Standalone Financial Statements of the Company for the year ended on that date.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for Internal Financial Controls

The Company's Management and Board of Directors are responsible for establishing and maintaining internal financial controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements based on the internal control over financial reporting criteria established by the Company, considering the essential components of internal control stated in the Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (the 'ICAI').

These responsibilities include the design, implementation, and maintenance of adequate internal financial controls that operate effectively to ensure:

- The orderly and efficient conduct of business.
- Adherence to company policies.
- Safeguarding of assets.
- Prevention and detection of frauds and errors.
- Accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Timely preparation of reliable financial information, as required under the Act.

## Auditor's Responsibility

The auditor’s responsibility is to express an opinion on the Company's internal financial controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements based on the audit.

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting issued by the ICAI and the Standards on Auditing prescribed under Section 143(10) of the Act, to the extent applicable. These standards require compliance with ethical requirements and planning and performance of the audit to obtain reasonable assurance about whether adequate internal financial controls were established, maintained, and operated effectively in all material respects.

The audit included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the Company's internal financial controls.
- Assessing the risk that a material weakness exists.
- Testing and evaluating the design and operating effectiveness of internal controls based on the assessed risk.
- Performing procedures selected based on the auditor's judgment, including the risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error.

The auditor concluded that the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the audit opinion.

## Meaning of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Internal financial control with reference to Standalone Financial Statements is a process designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding:

1. The reliability of financial reporting.
2. The preparation of financial statements for external purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
3. The maintenance of records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and dispositions of company assets.
4. The recording of transactions necessary to permit preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
5. The authorization of receipts and expenditures by management and directors.
6. The prevention or timely detection of unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposition of company assets that could materially affect the financial statements.

## Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements

Because of the inherent limitations of internal financial controls over financial reporting, including the possibility of collusion or improper management override of controls, material misstatements due to error or fraud may occur and not be detected.

Evaluations of internal financial controls for future periods are subject to the risk that controls may become inadequate because of changes in conditions, or that the degree of compliance with policies and procedures may deteriorate.

## Opinion

In the auditor’s opinion, based on the information, explanations, and audit evidence obtained, Infosys Limited had adequate internal financial controls with reference to Standalone Financial Statements in all material respects as of March 31, 2026. These controls were operating effectively based on the criteria established by the Company, considering the essential components of internal control stated in the ICAI Guidance Note.

## Annexure 'B' to the Independent Auditor's Report

*(Referred to in paragraph 2 under 'Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements' section of our report to the Members of Infosys Limited of even date)*

Based on the information and explanations provided by the Company and the books of account and records examined in the normal course of audit:

### Property, Plant and Equipment, Right-of-Use Assets, and Intangible Assets

- The Company maintained proper records showing full particulars, including quantitative details and location, of property, plant and equipment, and relevant details of right-of-use assets.
- The Company maintained proper records showing full particulars of intangible assets.
- The Company had a program to physically verify property, plant and equipment and right-of-use assets at least once every three years. Assets due for verification during the year were physically verified by management, and no material discrepancies were reported.
- Based on property tax receipts, lease agreements, registered sale deeds, transfer deeds, and conveyance deeds examined, the titles of self-constructed buildings and other immovable properties disclosed under Property, Plant and Equipment were held in the Company's name as of the balance sheet date, except for properties where the Company is the lessee under duly executed lease agreements.
- The Company did not revalue any property, plant and equipment, including right-of-use assets, or intangible assets during the year.
- No proceedings were initiated or pending against the Company as of March 31, 2026, for holding benami property under the Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 1988, as amended in 2016, and related rules.

### Inventory and Working Capital

- The Company did not have any inventory; reporting under clause 3(ii)(a) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- The Company was not sanctioned working capital limits exceeding `5 crore in aggregate at any time during the year from banks or financial institutions against the security of current assets. Reporting under clause 3(ii)(b) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

### Investments, Loans, Guarantees, and Securities

- The Company did not provide loans or advances in the nature of loans, guarantees, or securities to any other entity during the year; reporting under clause 3(iii)(a) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- In the auditor’s opinion, the investments made and the terms and conditions of loans granted during the year were, prima facie, not prejudicial to the Company's interests.
- For loans granted by the Company, repayment schedules for principal and payment of interest were stipulated. Repayments of principal and receipts of interest were generally regular.
- No overdue amount remained outstanding for loans granted by the Company as of the balance sheet date.
- No loan that fell due during the year was renewed or extended, and no fresh loan was granted to settle overdue amounts of existing loans to the same parties.
- The Company did not grant loans or advances in the nature of loans that were repayable on demand or without specified repayment terms or periods. Reporting under clause 3(iii)(f) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
- The Company did not make investments in firms or limited liability partnerships during the year.
- The Company did not provide guarantees or securities or grant advances in the nature of loans, secured or unsecured, to companies, firms, limited liability partnerships, or other parties.

### Companies Act Compliance

The Company complied with Sections 185 and 186 of the Companies Act, 2013, in respect of loans granted, investments made, and guarantees and securities provided, as applicable.

### Deposits

The Company did not accept deposits or amounts deemed to be deposits. Reporting under clause 3(v) of the Order was therefore not applicable.

### Cost Records

The Central Government had not specified maintenance of cost records under Section 148(1) of the Companies Act, 2013, for the business activities carried out by the Company. Reporting under clause 3(vi) of the Order was therefore not applicable.
