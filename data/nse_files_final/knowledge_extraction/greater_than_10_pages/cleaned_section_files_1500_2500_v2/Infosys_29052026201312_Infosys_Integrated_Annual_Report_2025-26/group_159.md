Item no. 5 - Approval of the proposed amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 ('the Amended 2019 Plan') and grant of stock incentives to the eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries under the Amended 2019 Plan

To consider and, if thought fit, to pass the following resolution as a Special Resolution:

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to the applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021 ('SEBI Regulations'), as amended from time to time, and other applicable rules, regulations, circulars and guidelines, and subject to required approvals, permissions, sanctions, conditions, and modifications, shareholder approval is accorded to the proposed amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019. The details of the amendments are provided in the explanatory statement to the Notice.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board, including authorized Company officials, may introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives under the Amended 2019 Plan to eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries, whether working in India or outside India, subsidiary directors, and other persons eligible under applicable laws. Eligible persons exclude promoters and members of the promoter group, independent directors, and directors who, directly or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company's outstanding equity shares. Incentives may be granted at prices, in tranches, and on terms and conditions determined by the Board under the Amended 2019 Plan.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the following amendments and modifications to the existing plan are approved:

- **A)** Section 13 will be amended to extend the period during which awards may be granted to eligible employees by seven years from the date of shareholder approval. Consequently, the Amended 2019 Plan will remain in effect for a further period of 10 years from that approval date, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
- **B)** Section 6(c), relating to vesting based on performance parameters, will be amended.
- **C)** Section 3 will be amended to reflect the unutilized share pool available for grant.
- **D)** Editorial and clarificatory changes will be made, including changes required to align the plan with the amended SEBI Regulations.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the maximum number of stock incentives granted to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries, in aggregate, under the Amended 2019 Plan will be **3,26,63,903 equity shares**. This represents the unutilized balance from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**. The exercise price and vesting criteria will be determined by the Board and/or the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, referred to as the Administrator. Administration relating to secondary acquisition of the Company's equity shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust may be delegated to that Trust from time to time in accordance with the Amended 2019 Plan.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** in the event of corporate actions such as rights issues, bonus issues, mergers, restructuring, sale of a division, or similar events, the Board and/or the Administrator may take actions permitted under applicable laws to ensure fair and equitable benefits under the Amended 2019 Plan for eligible employees.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board is authorized to allot the Company's equity shares required for the Amended 2019 Plan.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board and/or the Administrator is authorized to take the necessary steps to list shares allotted under the Amended 2019 Plan on the stock exchanges in accordance with applicable laws and stock-exchange requirements.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board and/or the Administrator is authorized, on behalf of the Company, to take all actions and execute all documents necessary or desirable to implement the Amended 2019 Plan and these resolutions, including resolving related issues, questions, difficulties, or doubts.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust and its trustees are authorized to take steps required in relation to the Amended 2019 Plan or its implementation.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board and/or the Administrator may delegate any powers conferred by these resolutions to a committee of directors, with authority for further delegation to Company executives or officers and for execution of necessary documents.
