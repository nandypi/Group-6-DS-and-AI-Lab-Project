## 2.21.2 Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund, which is a defined benefit plan. Both eligible employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered employees' salaries.

The Company contributes part of its contribution to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust. The trust invests in designated instruments permitted under Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. Infosys is obligated to cover any shortfall between the trust's investment returns and the notified interest rate.

## 2.21.3 Superannuation

Certain Infosys employees participate in a defined contribution plan. The Company has no further obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## 2.21.4 Compensated absences

Infosys has a policy covering accumulating and non-accumulating compensated absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined through actuarial valuation by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The valuation is based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the balance sheet date.

Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
