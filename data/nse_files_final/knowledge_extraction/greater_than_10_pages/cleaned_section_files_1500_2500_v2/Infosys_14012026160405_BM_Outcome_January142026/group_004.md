## Deloitte Haskins &amp; Sells LLP

The auditor’s responsibilities include:

- Concluding on the appropriateness of the Board of Directors’ use of the going-concern basis of accounting.
- Determining, based on audit evidence obtained, whether a material uncertainty exists regarding events or conditions that may cast significant doubt on the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Drawing attention in the auditor’s report to related disclosures when a material uncertainty exists, or modifying the audit opinion if those disclosures are inadequate.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures in the Statement, including whether it represents the underlying transactions and events fairly.
- Performing procedures in accordance with the circular issued by SEBI under Regulation 33(8) of the LODR Regulations, to the extent applicable.
- Obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group.
- Directing, supervising, and performing the audit of the financial information of entities included in the Statement for which the firm acts as independent auditor.

Materiality is the magnitude of misstatements in the Statement that, individually or in aggregate, makes it probable that the economic decisions of a reasonably knowledgeable user may be influenced. The auditor considers quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when:

1. Planning the scope of audit work.
2. Evaluating the results of audit work.
3. Evaluating the effect of identified misstatements in the Statement.

The auditor communicates with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, and any significant deficiencies in internal control identified during the audit.

The auditor also provides those charged with governance with a statement confirming compliance with relevant ethical requirements regarding independence and communicates relationships and other matters that may reasonably be considered to bear on independence, including related safeguards where applicable.
