## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Metro Bank to transform the bank’s finance operations using a suite of Workday solutions. The collaboration is intended to unify core finance operations, provide self-service tools, and simplify daily operations.
- Infosys unveiled an AI-first global capability center (GCC) model to accelerate the establishment and transformation of GCCs into AI-powered innovation and growth hubs. Infosys is collaborating with Lufthansa Systems to establish a dedicated GCC focused on aviation safety, operational efficiency, and customer experience.
- Infosys announced a collaboration with NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA) to deliver a workforce management solution for the NHS in England and Wales. The solution is intended to replace the existing ESR system and support workforce attraction, retention, and employee support.
- Infosys collaborated with Telenor Shared Services to modernize HR operations through an Oracle Fusion Cloud Human Capital Management (HCM) solution. The implementation is intended to streamline processes and provide a unified view of critical data.
- Infosys launched Infosys Topaz Fabric, a purpose-built agentic services suite and multi-layer AI fabric that unifies infrastructure, models, data, applications, and workflows into a composable, agent-ready ecosystem. Infosys is collaborating with Nu Skin to use Topaz Fabric for Agent Assist capabilities supporting application and infrastructure operations, automation, resilience, and user experience.
- Infosys launched the Infosys Customer Experience Suite for Salesforce to support enterprises’ agentic transformation and digital workforce scaling. VTT used Agentforce, with Infosys and Fluido, to automate lead grooming, contextual emails, and meeting setup. VTT subsequently collaborated with Infosys and Fluido to develop two additional Agentforce agents for organizational support.
- Infosys collaborated with Barry Callebaut on a multi-year, AI-powered digital transformation under the BC Next Level strategic investment program. The program is focused on creating an agile, technology-enabled enterprise, improving customer experience and operational efficiency, and advancing innovation.
- Infosys collaborated with Fresenius on ELEVATE, a business transformation initiative to unify and modernize global business processes and IT systems through SAP S/4HANA. Infosys was selected as the systems integration partner after a competitive selection process.

## About Infosys

Infosys is a global digital services and consulting company. It has more than 330,000 employees and serves clients in 63 countries. The company has more than four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises and provides services involving cloud, AI, digital transformation, and digital skills transfer. Infosys states that it is committed to governance, environmental sustainability, and workplace inclusion. Infosys shares are listed on the NSE, BSE, and NYSE under the ticker INFY.
