## Dividend

The final dividend on shares is recorded as a liability when approved by shareholders. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability when declared by the Company’s Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid or distributed after deducting applicable taxes. Remittances of dividends outside India are governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and are subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a defined benefit retirement plan, the Gratuity Plan, covering eligible employees mainly of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure.

The Company contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. Trustees administer the contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group also operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Defined benefit plan liabilities are determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest-rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in the balance sheet. Re-measurement gains and losses on the net defined benefit liability or asset are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The portion of actual returns on plan assets exceeding the returns calculated using the discount rate is recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution plan. The Group has no further obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Provident fund

Eligible Infosys employees receive benefits from a provident fund classified as a defined benefit plan. Both employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary.

The Company contributes part of these amounts to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Provident Fund Trust. The trust invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund. The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the trust’s investment returns and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries participate in defined contribution provident funds. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no obligations beyond their monthly contributions.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated-absence policy covers accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed for unused accumulated entitlements. Expenses for non-accumulating absences are recognized when the absences occur.

## Other income, net

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, and exchange gains or losses on forward and option contracts and on the translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities.

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## Functional currency and presentation currency

The functional currency of Infosys, its Indian subsidiaries, and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. Foreign subsidiaries use their respective local currencies as their functional currencies.

The financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars, rounded to the nearest million, to facilitate comparison of Infosys’ performance and financial position with similar companies domiciled in other geographic locations.

## Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency using exchange rates in effect at the balance sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income within exchange gains or losses on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in other comprehensive income as qualifying cash-flow hedges.

Foreign-currency-denominated non-monetary assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated using the exchange rate at the date fair value is determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated using the exchange rate at the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized on settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Revenue, expense, and cash-flow items denominated in foreign currencies are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate at the transaction date.

For translation of foreign subsidiaries’ financial statements into the presentation currency, assets and liabilities are translated using the exchange rate at the balance sheet date. Revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated using the average exchange rate for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves within other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is disposed of in full, the relevant translation amount is transferred to net profit in the statement of comprehensive income. Changes in the parent’s ownership that do not result in loss of control are recorded through equity.

Other comprehensive income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair value adjustments arising from the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated using the exchange rate at the balance sheet date.
