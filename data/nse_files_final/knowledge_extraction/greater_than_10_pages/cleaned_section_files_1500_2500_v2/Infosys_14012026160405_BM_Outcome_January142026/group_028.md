## CSR Capital Assets

Following the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Amendment Rules, 2021, the Company was required to transfer CSR capital assets installed before January 2021. The Company incorporated Infosys Green Forum (IGF), a subsidiary under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, for this purpose.

During the year ended March 31, 2022, the Company completed the transfer of assets after obtaining required regulatory approvals, as applicable. During fiscal 2024, IGF’s application to regularize its provisional registration was rejected, and its registration was cancelled by order of the Income Tax Commissioner (Exemption) dated March 26, 2024. IGF has filed an appeal before the Income Tax Tribunal.

## Capital Expenditure Commitments

The Group had contractual commitments for capital expenditure, primarily comprising infrastructure facilities and computer equipment, of $127 million as at December 31, 2025, compared with $109 million as at March 31, 2025.
