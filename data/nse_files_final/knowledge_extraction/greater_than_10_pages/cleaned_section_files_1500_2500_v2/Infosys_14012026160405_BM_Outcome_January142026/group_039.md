# Report on the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the three months and nine months ended December 31, 2025. The statements include:

- Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet as at December 31, 2025
- Condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the three months and nine months ended December 31, 2025
- Condensed Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity
- Condensed Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows for the nine months ended December 31, 2025
- Notes to the financial statements, including material accounting policies and explanatory information

In the auditors’ opinion, the interim condensed consolidated financial statements give a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated financial position as at December 31, 2025, its consolidated profit and total comprehensive income for the three months and nine months ended on that date, and its consolidated changes in equity and cash flows for the nine months ended on that date.

The statements were prepared in conformity with International Accounting Standard 34, “Interim Financial Reporting” (IAS 34), issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The auditors stated that they were independent of the Group in accordance with the ICAI Code of Ethics and had fulfilled their other ethical responsibilities.

The auditors stated that the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for their opinion.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The Company’s Board of Directors is responsible for the preparation and presentation of interim condensed consolidated financial statements that give a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated financial position, performance, total comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows in accordance with IAS 34.

The respective boards of directors of the entities included in the Group are responsible for:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records
- Safeguarding Group assets
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls
- Preparing financial statements that are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error

The respective boards are responsible for assessing the ability of the entities to continue as going concerns and for disclosing matters related to any intention to liquidate or cease operations, or where there is no realistic alternative to doing so.

The respective boards are also responsible for overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.

## Auditor’s Responsibilities for the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The auditors’ objective was to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the interim condensed consolidated financial statements as a whole were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an audit report containing their opinion.

As part of the audit, the auditors:

- Identified and assessed risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error.
- Designed and performed audit procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtained sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtained an understanding of relevant internal financial controls to design appropriate audit procedures.
- Evaluated the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates.
- Assessed management’s use of the going-concern basis of accounting.
- Considered whether events or conditions created a material uncertainty that could cast significant doubt on the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluated the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures in the interim condensed consolidated financial statements.
- Obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group.

The auditors noted that the risk of failing to detect a material misstatement resulting from fraud is higher than for an error because fraud may involve collusion, forgery, intentional omissions, misrepresentations, or management override of internal controls.

The auditors’ conclusions were based on audit evidence obtained up to the date of the auditors’ report. Future events or conditions may cause the Group to cease to continue as a going concern.

## Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP

The auditors communicated with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, and any significant deficiencies in internal financial controls identified during the audit.

They also communicated compliance with relevant ethical requirements relating to independence and applicable safeguards.

The report was dated January 14, 2026, in Bengaluru.
