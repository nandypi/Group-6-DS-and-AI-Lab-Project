## Independent Auditor's Report on the Audit of Quarterly and Nine Months Ended Standalone Financial Results

### To the Board of Directors of Infosys Limited

### Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited Infosys Limited's standalone financial results for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025. The results were submitted pursuant to Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

In the auditor's opinion, the Statement:

- Was presented in accordance with the requirements of Regulation 33 of the SEBI Listing Regulations.
- Gave a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles of Ind AS 34, “Interim Financial Reporting,” and other generally accepted accounting principles in India, of the net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information.

### Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. Deloitte stated that it was independent of the Company in accordance with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India Code of Ethics and had fulfilled its other ethical responsibilities. The auditor considered the audit evidence obtained to be sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for the opinion.

### Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for the Statement

The Board of Directors was responsible for the preparation and presentation of the standalone financial results in accordance with Ind AS 34, the Companies Act, 2013, and related regulations. These responsibilities included:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records.
- Safeguarding the Company's assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining effective internal financial controls.
- Preparing financial results that are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error.

The Board of Directors was also responsible for assessing the Company's ability to continue as a going concern, making related disclosures where applicable, and overseeing the financial reporting process.

### Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit

The auditor's objective was to obtain reasonable assurance that the Statement was free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an audit report containing an opinion.

The audit responsibilities included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement.
- Designing and performing audit procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal controls.
- Evaluating the accounting policies and estimates used by the Board of Directors.
- Evaluating the disclosures required under Regulation 33 of the SEBI Listing Regulations.
- Assessing the appropriateness of using the going-concern basis of accounting and whether any material uncertainty existed.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures in the Statement.
