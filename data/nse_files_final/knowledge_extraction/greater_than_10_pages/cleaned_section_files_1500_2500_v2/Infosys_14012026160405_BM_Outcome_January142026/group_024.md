## 2.3.5 Impairment

The Group recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenue that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

Trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component are measured at lifetime ECL. Other financial assets are measured at 12-month ECL unless credit risk has increased significantly since initial recognition, in which case lifetime ECL is used.

The Group determines credit loss allowances using historical loss experience adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions, including conditions affecting the industries in which the Group operates and the countries where it conducts business.

The ECL, or reversal of ECL, required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.
