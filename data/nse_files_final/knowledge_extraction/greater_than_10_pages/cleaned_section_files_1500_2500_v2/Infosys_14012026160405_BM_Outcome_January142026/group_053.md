## (1) Cash settled stock compensation expense included in the above

The fair value of the awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The model inputs include the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on historical volatility in the observed market prices of the Company's publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is modeled using historical movements in the market prices of their publicly traded equity shares over an equivalent period. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.

The fair value of each equity-settled award is estimated on the grant date using the following assumptions:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Weighted average share price (₹) / ($ ADS) | 1,505 | 16.57 | 1,554 | 17.93 | 1,437 | 18.42 |
| Exercise price (₹) / ($ ADS) | 5.00 | 0.10 | 1,554 | 17.93 | 5.00 | 0.07 |
| Expected volatility (%) | 23–26 | 25–26 | 25–28 | 26–30 | 21–26 | 23–28 |
| Expected life of the option (years) | 1–4 | 1–4 | 3–7 | 3–7 | 1–4 | 1–4 |
| Expected dividends (%) | 2–3 | 2–3 | 2–3 | 2–3 | 2–3 | 2–3 |
| Risk-free interest rate (%) | 6 | 4 | 6 | 4 | 7 | 4–5 |
| Weighted average fair value as on grant date (₹) / ($ ADS) | 1,354 | 15.16 | 390 | 4.09 | 1,319 | 16.94 |

The expected life of the RSU/ESOP is estimated based on the vesting term and contractual term of the RSU/ESOP, as well as the expected exercise behavior of the employee who receives the RSU/ESOP.
