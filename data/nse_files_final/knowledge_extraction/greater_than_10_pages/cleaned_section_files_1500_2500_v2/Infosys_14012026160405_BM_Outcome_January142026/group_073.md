The Company has only one class of shares, referred to as equity shares, with a par value of ₹ 5/- per share. Each holder of equity shares is entitled to one vote per share. Equity shares represented by American Depository Shares (ADS) carry similar voting and dividend rights as other equity shares. Each ADS represents one underlying equity share.

In the event of liquidation, holders of equity shares are entitled to receive the remaining assets of the Company in proportion to their shareholdings after distribution of all preferential amounts. No such preferential amounts currently exist.

Options issued under the Company's share option plans do not carry voting, dividend, or liquidation rights.

For details of shares reserved for issue under the employee stock option plan, refer to the note below.

## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals, if any.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

In line with the capital allocation policy, the Board, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹ 5/- each for an amount of ₹ 18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting by postal ballot, with the results declared on November 6, 2025. The buyback comprised the purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company's total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, at a price of ₹ 1,800 per equity share.

The buyback was offered to all eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who became equity shareholders by cancelling ADSs and withdrawing the underlying equity shares, as of the record date of November 14, 2025. It was conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route.

The tender period commenced on November 20, 2025 and closed on November 26, 2025. The Company concluded the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025. All 10,00,00,000 equity shares were bought back and extinguished. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹ 18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs.

The Company funded the buyback from free reserves, including securities premium, in accordance with Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013. In accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹ 50 crore, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, through an appropriation from the general reserve during the quarter ended December 31, 2025.

The Company's objective in managing capital is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. To maintain or achieve this structure, the Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares.

As of December 31, 2025, the Company had only one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## 2.11.2 DIVIDEND

Final dividends on shares are recorded as a liability on the date of shareholder approval, while interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the Company's Board of Directors. Income tax consequences of dividends on financial instruments classified as equity are recognized according to where the entity originally recognized the transactions or events that generated distributable profits.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid or distributed after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## 2.11.3 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP):

## Accounting Policy

The Company recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of the awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, as if the award comprised multiple awards, with a corresponding increase in the share options outstanding account.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan):

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan may not exceed 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the 2019 Plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through the secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on achievement of defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The performance parameters are based on a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company's operating performance metrics.

Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares to vest based on performance. These instruments generally vest between a minimum of one year and a maximum of three years from the grant date.
