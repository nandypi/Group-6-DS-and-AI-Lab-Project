## Trade Receivables and Contract Balances

The timing of revenue recognition, billings, and cash collections results in receivables, unbilled revenue, and unearned revenue on the Group's Consolidated Balance Sheet. Amounts are billed as work progresses under agreed contractual terms, either at periodic intervals such as monthly or quarterly, or upon achievement of contractual milestones.

The Group's receivables are unconditional rights to consideration. Unbilled revenue in excess of billings from time-and-material contracts and fixed-price maintenance contracts is classified as a financial asset when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

Invoicing for other fixed-price contracts is based on contractual milestones. Because the timing of revenue recognition differs from the timing of invoicing, unbilled revenue for these contracts is classified as a non-financial contract asset when the right to consideration depends on completion of contractual milestones.

Invoicing in excess of earnings is classified as unearned revenue. Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are presented net of impairment in the Consolidated Balance Sheet.

## 2.17 OTHER INCOME, NET

## Accounting policy

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, and exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts and on the translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities. Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## Functional currency

The functional currency of Infosys, its Indian subsidiaries, and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. The functional currencies of foreign subsidiaries are their respective local currencies. The financial statements are presented in Indian rupees, rounded to crore; one crore equals ten million.

## Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the Balance Sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss and reported within exchange gains or losses on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in Other Comprehensive Income as qualifying cash-flow hedges.

Non-monetary assets and liabilities denominated in a foreign currency and measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate prevailing when fair value was determined. Non-monetary assets and liabilities measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate prevailing on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized upon settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items denominated in foreign currencies are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate in effect on the transaction date.

Financial statements of foreign subsidiaries are translated into the presentation currency using the exchange rate at the Balance Sheet date for assets and liabilities and the average exchange rate for revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves under other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is disposed of in full, the relevant currency translation amount is transferred to net profit in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss. Changes in the parent's ownership that do not result in loss of control are recorded through equity.

Other Comprehensive Income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair-value adjustments arising on the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated at the exchange rate in effect at the Balance Sheet date.

## Government grant

The Group recognizes government grants only when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received. Grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss on a systematic and rational basis over the asset's useful life. Grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in net profit over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.

## 2.18 EXPENSES

## Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a defined benefit retirement plan, the Gratuity Plan, covering eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to the Infosys BPM Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively. Trustees administer contributions to the trusts, which are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic payouts after retirement or a lump-sum payment under the rules of each fund, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Liabilities for these defined benefit plans are determined by actuarial valuation performed by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to actuarial risks including longevity risk, interest-rate risk, and market risk.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its Balance Sheet. Re-measurement gains and losses on the net defined benefit liability or asset are recognized in Other Comprehensive Income and are not reclassified to profit or loss in subsequent periods. The actual return on plan assets in excess of the yield calculated using the discount rate applied to the defined benefit obligation is recognized in Other Comprehensive Income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.
