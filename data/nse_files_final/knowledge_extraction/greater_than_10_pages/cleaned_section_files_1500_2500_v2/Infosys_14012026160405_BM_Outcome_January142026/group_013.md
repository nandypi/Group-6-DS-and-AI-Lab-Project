## Infosys Finacle Awards

Infosys Finacle and its customers received four awards at the Global Banking and Finance® Awards 2025:

- Innovation Award for Excellence in Margin Finance Innovation in India with HDFC Bank.
- Most Innovative Payments Channel Modernization in Colombia with Bancolombia.
- Technology Award for Best Core Banking Transformation with Real-Time Eventing with Emirates NBD Bank.
- Award for Best Customer Journey Initiative in Australia with Australian Military Bank.

Infosys Finacle was recognized as The World’s Best Software Provider for Virtual Accounts 2025 and The World’s Best Software Provider for Liquidity Management 2025 by the Euromoney Transaction Banking Awards.

## About Infosys

Infosys is a global digital services and consulting company. More than 330,000 employees serve clients in 63 countries. The company has over four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises, with capabilities spanning cloud, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, digital skills, and innovation.

Infosys states that it is focused on governance, environmental sustainability, and an inclusive workplace.
