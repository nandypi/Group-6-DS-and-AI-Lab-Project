## NOTE:

1. On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Labour Codes, which consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during employment and post-employment. The Labour Codes introduce changes including a uniform definition of wages and enhanced leave-related benefits. The adjustments represent a $143 million increase in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and an increase in leave liability, recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.

## Report on the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

## Opinion

The auditor audited the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the period ended December 31, 2025.

In the auditor's opinion, the statements give a true and fair view of the Group's consolidated state of affairs as at December 31, 2025; its consolidated profit and total comprehensive income for the three and nine months ended on that date; and its consolidated changes in equity and cash flows for the nine months ended on that date.

The financial statements were prepared in conformity with International Accounting Standard 34, “Interim Financial Reporting” (IAS 34), as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The auditor stated that it was independent of the Group in accordance with the ICAI Code of Ethics and that sufficient and appropriate audit evidence was obtained to provide a basis for the audit opinion.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The Company's Board of Directors is responsible for preparing and presenting interim condensed consolidated financial statements that give a true and fair view of the Group's consolidated financial position, performance, total comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows in accordance with IAS 34.

The Boards of Directors of the entities included in the Group are responsible for maintaining adequate accounting records, safeguarding Group assets, preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities, selecting and applying accounting policies, making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates, and maintaining effective internal financial controls relevant to the preparation and presentation of the financial statements.

The Boards of Directors are also responsible for assessing the entities' ability to continue as a going concern, making relevant disclosures, using the going-concern basis of accounting where appropriate, and overseeing the Group's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The auditor's objectives are to obtain reasonable assurance that the interim condensed consolidated financial statements are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing the auditor's opinion.

The audit responsibilities included assessing risks of material misstatement, obtaining an understanding of relevant internal financial controls, evaluating accounting policies and management estimates, evaluating the use of the going-concern basis of accounting, assessing the overall presentation and disclosures, and obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group.
