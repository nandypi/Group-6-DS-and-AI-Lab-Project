The audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025, were taken on record by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on January 14, 2026. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP expressed an unmodified audit opinion. The statements were prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind-AS) under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015 and subsequent amendment rules.

## b) Buyback of equity shares

Shareholders approved the Board-recommended buyback through e-voting by postal ballot, with results declared on November 6, 2025.

The offer comprised the purchase of 100,000,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the total paid-up equity share capital as of June 2025 on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800 per equity share. The offer was made to all eligible equity shareholders, including those who became equity shareholders as of the record date.

The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The Company completed the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025. A total of 100,000,000 equity shares were bought back and extinguished. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs.

The Company funded the buyback from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013. In accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, through an appropriation from the general reserve during the quarter ended December 31, 2025.

## c) Impact of Labour Codes

On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Code on Social Security, 2020; and an Occupational Code whose full name is incomplete in the source.

The changes resulted in an increase of ₹1,289 crore in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and an increase in leave liability. As the impact of the new legislation was considered non-recurring, the Group presented the incremental amount as “Impact of Labour Codes” under “Exceptional item” in the Condensed Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the quarter and nine months ended December 31, 2025.

The Group continues to monitor developments relating to the Labour Codes and will evaluate any impact on the measurement of employee-benefit liabilities.

## d) Update on stock grants

On January 14, 2026, the Board, based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved annual time-based stock incentives in the form of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) for Salil Parekh, CEO and MD.

The RSUs had a market value of ₹3 crore as of the grant date and were approved under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan, in accordance with the terms of his employment agreement. The RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement.

The RSUs will be granted with effect from February 1, 2026. The number of RSUs will be calculated based on the market price at the close of trading on the day immediately preceding the grant date. The exercise price will equal the par value of the share.

## Notes on segment information

## Business segments

Based on the “management approach” required by Ind-AS 108, Operating Segments, the Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates the Group’s performance and allocates resources based on various performance indicators by business segment. Accordingly, information is presented by business segment. The accounting principles used to prepare the financial statements are applied consistently in recording revenue and expenditure for individual segments.

## Segmental capital employed

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Management believes that providing segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities is currently impracticable because meaningful segregation of the available data would be onerous.
