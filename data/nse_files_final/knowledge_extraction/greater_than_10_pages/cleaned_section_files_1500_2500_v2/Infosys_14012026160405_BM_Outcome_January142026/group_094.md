## Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP):

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, with a corresponding increase to the share options outstanding account.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan) :

On June 22, 2019, pursuant to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan is 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Performance parameters include a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader-market domestic and global indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics. Each performance parameter is separately used to calculate the quantity of shares that vests.

Awards generally vest between 1 and 3 years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan) :

On March 31, 2016, pursuant to shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan is 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as at March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were subsequently adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Awards generally vest over 4 years. Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over 4 years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs is equal to the par value of the shares, while the exercise price of stock options is the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 89,84,436 shares as at December 31, 2025, compared with 96,55,927 shares as at March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities at each date.

## Under the 2015 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following fiscal 2026 grants, which were made effective May 2, 2025:

- 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹2 crore, linked to environment, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, with a fair value of ₹5 crore, linked to the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Although the annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as at December 31, 2025, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense under Ind AS 102, Share-based Payment, because the service commencement date preceded the grant date. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

Model inputs include the share price on the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on the historical volatility of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparable companies is modeled using historical movements in their publicly traded equity shares over an equivalent period. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
