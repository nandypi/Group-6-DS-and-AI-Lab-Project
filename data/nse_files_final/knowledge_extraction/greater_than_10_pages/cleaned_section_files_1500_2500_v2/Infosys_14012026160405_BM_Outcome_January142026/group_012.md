# Press Release

- Reported operating margin: 18.4%.
- Adjusted operating margin: 21.2%.
- Reported YTD EPS increase in rupee terms: 6.6%.
- Adjusted YTD EPS increase in rupee terms: 11.5%.
- Large-deal total contract value: $4.8 billion, of which 57% was net new.
- Q3 sequential revenue growth: 0.6%.
- Adjusted operating margin expansion: 0.2%.
- Adjusted free cash generation: $965 million.
- Infosys completed a buyback of ₹18,000 crore and paid an interim dividend to shareholders.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Metro Bank to transform the bank’s finance operations using a suite of Workday solutions.

- Infosys introduced an AI-first global capability center (GCC) model to accelerate the establishment and transformation of GCCs into AI-powered innovation and growth hubs. Infosys is collaborating with Lufthansa Systems on a dedicated GCC.

- Infosys is collaborating with NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA) to deliver a workforce management solution for the NHS in England and Wales.

- Infosys collaborated with Telenor Shared Services to modernize its HR operations through an Oracle Fusion Cloud Human Capital Management (HCM) solution.

- Infosys launched Infosys Topaz Fabric, a purpose-built agentic services suite and multi-layer AI fabric that unifies infrastructure, models, data, applications, and workflows into a composable, agent-ready ecosystem. Infosys is collaborating with Nu Skin to use Topaz Fabric for Agent Assist features supporting application and infrastructure operations.

- Infosys launched the Infosys Customer Experience Suite for Salesforce to help enterprises with agentic transformation and scaling their digital workforce. Infosys and Fluido collaborated with VTT on Salesforce Agentforce implementation, including a live SDR Agentforce agent, with plans to develop two additional Agentforce agents.

- Infosys collaborated with Barry Callebaut on a multi-year, AI-powered digital transformation intended to create an agile, technology-enabled enterprise and improve customer experience, operational efficiency, and innovation.

- Infosys collaborated with Fresenius on ELEVATE, a business transformation initiative to unify and modernize global business processes and IT systems through SAP S/4HANA. Infosys was selected as the systems integration partner for the program.
