## Accounting policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the period is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. Highly liquid investments readily convertible to known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited provides consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services to support clients’ digital transformation. Infosys, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts, is referred to as the “Group.”

The Company is incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares, representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on January 14, 2026.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared in compliance with Indian Accounting Standard (Ind AS) 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, under the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

The statements were prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). They do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the Company’s Annual Report for the year ended March 31, 2025.

Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard requires a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information used in preparing the interim condensed consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Consequently, quarter figures may not always add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.
