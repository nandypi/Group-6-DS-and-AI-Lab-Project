## 2.15 Segment reporting

IFRS 8 Operating Segments establishes standards for reporting information about operating segments, products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group’s operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to help clients enhance business performance. The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates performance and allocates resources using performance indicators by business segment. Segment revenue and expenditure are prepared using the accounting principles described in the accounting policies.

The Group’s business segments are:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment combines the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because they have similar economic characteristics. All other segments include businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are allocated to segments based on items individually identifiable with each segment. Revenue for All other segments includes revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other Public Services enterprises.

Allocated expenses include expenses incurred in offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated segment effort. Depreciation and amortization and certain other expenses are not specifically allocated because the underlying assets are used interchangeably between segments. These expenses are separately disclosed as unallocated expenses and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Meaningful segregation of total assets and liabilities is not currently practicable.

Business segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized.

Disclosure of revenue by geographic locations is given in note 2.16, Revenue from operations.
