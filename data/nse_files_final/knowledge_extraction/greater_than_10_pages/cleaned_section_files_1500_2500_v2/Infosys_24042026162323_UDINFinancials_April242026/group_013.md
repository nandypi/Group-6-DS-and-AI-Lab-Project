## Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP

### Report on the Internal Financial Controls with reference to Consolidated Financial Statements

In conjunction with the audit of the consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its Indian subsidiary companies for the year ended March 31, 2026, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the internal financial controls with reference to consolidated financial statements as of that date.

### Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for Internal Financial Controls

Management and the respective Boards of Directors were responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining internal financial controls based on the criteria established by the respective companies, considering the essential components of internal controls stated in the Guidance Note issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

These responsibilities included:

- Maintaining reliable accounting records.
- Safeguarding assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and errors.
- Ensuring the accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Preparing reliable financial information on a timely basis.
- Ensuring adherence to company policies.

### Auditor's Responsibility

Deloitte conducted the audit in accordance with the ICAI Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting and the Standards on Auditing prescribed under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013.

The audit included:

- Understanding the internal financial controls relevant to the audit.
- Assessing the risk of material weakness.
- Testing and evaluating the design and operating effectiveness of controls.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.

### Meaning of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Consolidated Financial Statements

Internal financial controls with reference to consolidated financial statements are processes designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding:

- The reliability of financial reporting.
- The preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
- The maintenance of records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and asset dispositions.
- The authorization of receipts and expenditures by management and directors.
- The prevention or timely detection of unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposition of assets that could materially affect the financial statements.

### Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Consolidated Financial Statements

Because of the inherent limitations of internal financial controls, misstatements due to error or fraud may occur and not be detected. Evaluations of internal financial controls are also subject to the risk that controls or procedures may deteriorate over time.

### Opinion

In Deloitte’s opinion, Infosys Limited and its Indian subsidiary companies had, in all material respects, an adequate system of internal financial controls with reference to consolidated financial statements as of March 31, 2026. The controls were operating effectively based on the criteria stated in the ICAI Guidance Note on Audit of Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting.

## Report on the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

### Opinion

Deloitte audited the interim condensed consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries, collectively referred to as the Group. These statements comprised:

- The condensed consolidated balance sheet as at March 31, 2026.
- The condensed consolidated statement of profit and loss, including other comprehensive income, for the relevant three-month and year-end periods.
- The condensed consolidated statement of cash flows for the year ended March 31, 2026.
- Notes to the financial statements, including material accounting policies and explanatory information.

In Deloitte’s opinion, the interim condensed consolidated financial statements gave a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated financial position, consolidated financial performance, consolidated other comprehensive income, and consolidated cash flows in conformity with Indian Accounting Standard 34, “Interim Financial Reporting” (Ind AS 34), the Companies Act, 2013, and generally accepted accounting principles in India.

### Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. Deloitte stated that it was independent of the Group in accordance with the ICAI Code of Ethics and had fulfilled its other ethical responsibilities.

Deloitte stated that it believed the audit evidence obtained was sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for its opinion.

### Statements

The respective Boards of Directors of the entities included in the Group were responsible for:

- Preparing interim condensed consolidated financial statements that give a true and fair view.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records.
- Safeguarding Group assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls.
- Assessing the entities’ ability to continue as a going concern.
- Disclosing relevant going-concern matters.
- Using the going-concern basis of accounting unless liquidation or cessation of operations was intended or there was no realistic alternative.
- Overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.

### Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

The auditor’s objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance that the interim condensed consolidated financial statements were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor’s report containing an opinion.

The audit responsibilities included:

- Identifying and assessing the risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error.
- Designing and performing audit procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Understanding internal financial controls relevant to the audit for designing appropriate procedures.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates and related disclosures.
- Assessing management’s use of the going-concern basis of accounting and whether a material uncertainty existed regarding the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the interim condensed consolidated financial statements.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group.
- Directing, supervising, and performing the audit of the financial statements of entities included in the consolidated financial statements.
