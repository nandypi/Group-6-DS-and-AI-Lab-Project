## Report of auditors on financial statements for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 with UDIN

### Standalone Financial Results

#### Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the standalone financial results of Infosys Limited for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, submitted under Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

In the auditors’ opinion, the standalone financial results:

- Give a true and fair view, in conformity with the recognition and measurement principles under the Companies Act, 2013, relevant rules, Ind AS 34, and other generally accepted accounting principles in India, of the standalone net profit, other comprehensive income, and other financial information for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.

#### Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. The auditors stated that they were independent of the Company under the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India’s Code of Ethics and had fulfilled their other ethical responsibilities. They considered the audit evidence obtained to be sufficient and appropriate as a basis for their opinion.

#### Management’s and Board of Directors’ Responsibilities for the Statement

The standalone financial results were the responsibility of the Company’s Board of Directors and were approved by them for issuance. They were compiled from the related audited interim condensed standalone financial statements for the three months and year ended March 31, 2026.

The Board’s responsibilities included:

- Preparing and presenting the financial results in accordance with Ind AS 34, the Companies Act, 2013, relevant rules, and Regulation 33 of the SEBI Listing Regulations.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records to safeguard the Company’s assets and prevent and detect fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies and making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls to ensure the accuracy and completeness of accounting records relevant to the preparation of the financial results.
- Assessing the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern, disclosing applicable going-concern matters, and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless the Board intends to liquidate the Company, cease operations, or has no realistic alternative.

#### Auditor’s Responsibilities for the Audit of the Standalone Financial Results

The auditors’ objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance that the standalone financial results as a whole were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor’s report containing their opinion.

The audit procedures included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error and designing procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtaining an understanding of internal control relevant to the audit.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by the Board.
- Evaluating the adequacy and reasonableness of disclosures made by the Board.
- Concluding on the appropriateness of the going-concern basis of accounting and whether any material uncertainty existed that could cast significant doubt on the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and underlying transactions and events represented in the financial results.
- Obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial results.

Materiality was assessed based on the magnitude of misstatements that, individually or in aggregate, could influence the economic decisions of a reasonably knowledgeable user. The auditors considered quantitative and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating its results, and assessing identified misstatements.

The auditors also stated that they communicated significant audit findings and any significant deficiencies in internal control to those charged with governance, confirmed compliance with relevant ethical requirements regarding independence, and communicated relationships or other matters relevant to their independence.

### Consolidated Financial Results

#### Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026. The Company and its subsidiaries are collectively referred to as the Group.

In the auditors’ opinion, the consolidated financial results:

- Include the financial results of the subsidiaries listed in the report’s annexure.
- Give a true and fair view, in conformity with Ind AS 34, the Companies Act, 2013, relevant rules, and other generally accepted accounting principles in India, of the consolidated net profit, consolidated other comprehensive income, and other financial information of the Group for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
