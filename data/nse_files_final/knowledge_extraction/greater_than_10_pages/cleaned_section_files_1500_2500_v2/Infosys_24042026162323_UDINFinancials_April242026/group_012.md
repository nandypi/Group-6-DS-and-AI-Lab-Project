## Deloitte Haskins &amp; Sells LLP

Management represented that neither the Company nor its Indian subsidiary companies whose financial statements were audited under the Companies Act had received funds from any person or entity, including foreign entities, with the understanding that they would directly or indirectly lend or invest in identified ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees, securities, or similar support on their behalf.

Based on the audit procedures performed, the auditors did not identify anything causing them to believe that these management representations contained any material misstatement.

As stated in Note 2.12 to the Consolidated Financial Statements:

- The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared and paid during the year, complied with Section 123 of the Companies Act, as applicable.
- The interim dividend declared and paid during the year and up to the date of the auditor's report complied with Section 123 of the Companies Act.
- The Board of Directors proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to shareholder approval at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed amount complied with Section 123 of the Companies Act, as applicable.

Based on examination procedures, including test checks, the Company and its Indian subsidiaries used accounting software with audit-trail or edit-log functionality for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The audit-trail feature operated throughout the year for relevant transactions, and the auditors did not identify any instance of tampering. The Parent Company and the relevant Indian subsidiaries preserved the audit trail in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

The financial statements of five subsidiaries that were not material to the Group's Consolidated Financial Statements had not been audited under the Companies Act as of the date of the auditor's report. Accordingly, the auditors were unable to comment on the reporting requirement under Rule 11(g) of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014 for these subsidiaries.

With respect to matters specified in paragraphs 3(xxi) and 4 of the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020 (CARO), the auditors reported that, for companies whose audits under Section 143 of the Companies Act had been completed, they had not reported any qualifications or adverse remarks.
