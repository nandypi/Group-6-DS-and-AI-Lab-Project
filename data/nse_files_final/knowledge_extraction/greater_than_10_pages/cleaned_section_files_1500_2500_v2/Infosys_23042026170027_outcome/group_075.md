## 2.15 Segment reporting

IFRS 8 Operating Segments establishes standards for reporting information about operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers. The Group’s operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to enable clients to enhance business performance.

The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates the Group’s performance and allocates resources based on performance indicators by business segment. Information is therefore presented by business segment. The accounting principles used to prepare the financial statements are consistently applied to record revenue and expenditure in individual segments.

The Group’s business segments primarily comprise:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment aggregates the Financial Services operating segment and Finacle operating segment because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments include operating segments relating to businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are categorized based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Revenue for “all other segments” represents revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other enterprises in Public Services.

Allocated segment expenses include expenses incurred for services provided by the Group’s offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized based on the associated efforts of each segment. Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocable because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are separately disclosed as “unallocated” and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not identified by reportable segment because they are used interchangeably. Management believes that providing segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities is not currently practicable because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for which revenue is otherwise recognized. Revenue by geographic location is disclosed in note 2.16, Revenue from operations.
