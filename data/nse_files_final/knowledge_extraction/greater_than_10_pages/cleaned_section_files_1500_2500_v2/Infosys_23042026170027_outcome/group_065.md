# 2.10 Business combinations

## Accounting policy

Business combinations have been accounted for using the acquisition method under the provisions of IFRS 3 (Revised), Business Combinations.

The purchase price in an acquisition is measured at the fair value of the assets transferred, equity instruments issued, and liabilities incurred or assumed at the date of acquisition, which is the date on which control is transferred to the Group. The purchase price also includes the fair value of any contingent consideration.

Identifiable assets acquired and liabilities and contingent liabilities assumed in a business combination are initially measured at fair value on the date of acquisition. Contingent consideration is remeasured at fair value at each reporting date, with changes recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.

The interest of non-controlling shareholders is initially measured either at fair value or at the non-controlling interests' proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets. The choice of measurement basis is made on an acquisition-by-acquisition basis.

Business combinations between entities under common control are outside the scope of IFRS 3 (Revised), Business Combinations, and are accounted for at the carrying value of assets acquired and liabilities assumed.

Payments related to options issued by the Group over non-controlling interests in its subsidiaries are accounted for as financial liabilities and initially recognized at the estimated present value of gross obligations. These options are subsequently measured at fair value. If an option expires unexercised, the liability is derecognized.

## Acquisition during the year ended March 31, 2026

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership interests or voting interests in the following companies:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — Acquired on April 30, 2025. The company is headquartered in Texas, U.S., and provides energy and business consulting services. The acquisition is expected to bring newer capabilities in trading and risk management, particularly in the energy sector.

2. **The Missing Link** — Comprising The Missing Link Security Pty. Ltd., The Missing Link Security Limited, and The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd. The companies were acquired on April 30, 2025 and are headquartered in Australia. The acquisition is expected to strengthen the Group's cybersecurity capabilities and its presence in the Australian market.

(1) Includes cash and cash equivalents acquired of ₹102 crore.

The excess of purchase consideration paid over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill were the value of the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualified as an intangible asset.

Goodwill amounting to ₹79 crore is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of ₹813 crore included upfront cash consideration of ₹743 crore and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of ₹70 crore at the acquisition date.

The key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration at the acquisition date were probabilities assigned to the achievement of financial targets and discount rates ranging from 2% to 3%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026 was approximately ₹88 crore.

The acquisitions also included retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over two to three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets. These amounts are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Statement of Comprehensive Income over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was ₹194 crore at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts had been substantially collected.

Transaction costs incurred in connection with the business combinations, including finder's fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of ₹34 crore related to the acquisitions were included under administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the year ended March 31, 2026.
