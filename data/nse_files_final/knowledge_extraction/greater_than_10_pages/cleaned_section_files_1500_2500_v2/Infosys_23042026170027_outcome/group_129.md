## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. Highly liquid investments readily convertible to known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited provides AI-first business consulting and technology services. Its capabilities include enabling organizations to unlock AI value at scale, managing the systems and operations of global enterprises, supporting business transformation through an AI-first value framework and domain expertise, and coordinating innovation from its AI-native partner ecosystem.

Infosys is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares representing equity shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).
