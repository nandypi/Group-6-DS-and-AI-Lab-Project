## Outcome of Board meeting

The Board meeting held on April 22–23, 2026 approved or considered the following matters.

## Financial Results

The Board approved:

1. Audited consolidated financial results of the Company and its subsidiaries under Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
2. Audited standalone financial results of the Company under Ind AS for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
3. Audited financial statements of the Company and its subsidiaries under Ind AS and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026.
4. Audited financial statements of the Company and its subsidiaries under Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026.
5. Audited financial statements of the Company under Ind AS for the year ended March 31, 2026.

## Dividend

The Board recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

- Record date: June 10, 2026
- Dividend payment date: June 25, 2026

## Annual General Meeting

The 45th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the Members of the Company will be held on Tuesday, June 23, 2026.

## Stock grants

### 8. Grants to CEO & MD

Based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following annual grants to Salil Parekh, CEO and MD, under his employment agreement approved by shareholders:

- Annual performance-based stock incentives, in the form of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs), covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹34.75 crore as of the grant date under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan. The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of performance targets determined by the Board.
- Annual performance-based ESG stock incentives, in the form of RSUs, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹2 crore as of the grant date under the 2015 Plan. The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- Annual performance-based TSR stock incentives, in the form of RSUs, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹5 crore as of the grant date under the 2015 Plan. The RSUs will vest on or after March 31, 2027, subject to the Company’s performance on cumulative relative total shareholder return (TSR) over the two-year cumulative period, as determined by the Board.
- 2019 Annual Performance Equity Grant, in the form of RSUs, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹10 crore as of the grant date under the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program-2019. The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of the performance criteria specified in the 2019 Plan.

The above RSUs will be granted effective May 2, 2026. The number of RSUs will be calculated using the market price at the close of trading on the day immediately preceding the grant date.

### Grants to other employees

The Board approved the following grants to eligible employees:

- 27,193 RSUs under the 2015 Plan.
- Performance-based stock incentives in the form of Performance Stock Units (PSUs) under the 2019 Plan, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹1.90 crore as of the grant date. The number of PSUs will be calculated using the market price at the close of trading on the day immediately preceding the grant date.

Grants under the 2015 Plan will vest equally over two to three years. Grants under the 2019 Plan will vest over two years, subject to achievement of the performance parameters defined in the 2019 Plan. The RSUs and PSUs will be granted effective May 2, 2026, with an exercise price equal to the par value of the shares.

### 9. Proposed change of Auditors on account of mandatory rotation requirement in India

Under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the applicable rules, Infosys Limited is required to rotate its current statutory auditors upon completion of the maximum permitted term.

The Audit Committee proposed the appointment of BSR & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), as the Company’s statutory auditors. The Board approved the announcement of its intention to recommend BSR & Co. LLP to shareholders at the 46th AGM in 2027.

The proposed appointment would be for a first term of five consecutive years, until the conclusion of the 51st AGM in 2032. The first year of audit by BSR & Co. LLP would be the financial year ending March 31, 2028, including the audit of the quarterly financial statements for that year.

The proposed appointment is subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations. Additional information under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations is enclosed as Annexure I.

### 10. Proposed change in the Company’s certifying accountant for filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

Infosys is registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (U.S. SEC) and is required to appoint a certifying accountant to audit its financial statements.

The Audit Committee and the Board approved the announcement of the Company’s intention to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as its independent registered public accounting firm. The proposed appointment is expected to be effective for the year ending March 31, 2028.

KPMG would audit the Company’s annual financial statements included in the Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the U.S. SEC for the year ending March 31, 2028.

The proposed appointment is subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.

## Amendment to the Infosys Expanded stock ownership program - 2019 ('the 2019 Plan')

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved an amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program-2019 to extend the grant period by seven years from the date of shareholder approval. This would extend the Plan’s validity to a total period of ten years from shareholder approval and allow stock incentives to be granted to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiary companies.

The amendment is subject to shareholder approval.

## Postal ballot notice

The Board approved the Postal Ballot Notice seeking shareholder approval for:

- Appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200) as an Independent Director for a three-year term from April 22, 2026 to April 21, 2029.
- Re-appointment of Helene Auriol Potier (DIN: 10166891) as an Independent Director for a second term of five years from May 26, 2026 to May 25, 2031.

The Postal Ballot Notice will be sent to shareholders in due course.

## Incorporation of a subsidiary

The Board approved the incorporation of a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of the Company in Japan. Additional information required under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 will be disclosed in due course.
