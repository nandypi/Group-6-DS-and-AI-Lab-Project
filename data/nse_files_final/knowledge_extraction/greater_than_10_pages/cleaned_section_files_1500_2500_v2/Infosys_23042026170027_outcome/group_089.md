## Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP

- No report under section 143(12) of the Companies Act was filed in Form ADT-4 with the Central Government during the year or up to the date of the audit report.
- The auditors considered whistle-blower complaints received by the company during the year and up to the date of the report when determining the nature, timing, and extent of audit procedures.
- The company is not a Nidhi Company; reporting under clause 3(xii) of the applicable order was therefore not applicable.
- The auditors stated that the company complied with sections 177 and 188 of the Companies Act, 2013, for applicable related-party transactions. Details were disclosed in the standalone financial statements as required by applicable accounting standards.
- The company had an adequate internal audit system commensurate with the size and nature of its business. The auditors considered internal audit reports issued during the year and up to the date of the report.
- The company did not enter into non-cash transactions with directors or persons connected with directors during the year. Section 192 of the Companies Act, 2013, was therefore not applicable.
- The company was not required to register under section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. Reporting under clauses 3(xvi)(a), (b), and (c) was not applicable.
- The auditors stated that there was no core investment company within the group as defined in the Core Investment Companies (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016. Reporting under clause 3(xvi)(d) was therefore not applicable.
- The company did not incur cash losses during the financial year covered by the audit or during the immediately preceding financial year.
- There was no resignation of the statutory auditors during the year.
- Based on financial ratios, ageing and expected realisation of financial assets, payment dates for financial liabilities, accompanying information, management plans, and supporting evidence, the auditors reported that nothing had come to their attention indicating material uncertainty about the company’s ability to meet liabilities existing at the balance-sheet date as they fell due within one year. The auditors stated that this was not an assurance of future viability or a guarantee that all liabilities falling due within one year would be discharged.

## Corporate Social Responsibility

- There were no unspent CSR amounts, other than amounts relating to ongoing projects, requiring transfer to a fund specified in Schedule VII to the Companies Act, 2013.
- For ongoing projects, the company transferred unspent CSR amounts as at the end of the previous financial year to a special account within 30 days from the end of that financial year, in compliance with section 135(6) of the Companies Act, 2013.
- For ongoing projects, the company had not transferred to a special account the unspent CSR amount as at the balance-sheet date relating to amounts required to be spent during the year, because the applicable 30-day transfer period had not elapsed by the date of the auditors’ report.
