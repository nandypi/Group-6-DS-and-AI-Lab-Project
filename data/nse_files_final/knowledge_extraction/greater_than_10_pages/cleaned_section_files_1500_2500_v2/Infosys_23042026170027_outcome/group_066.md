## in-tech Holding GmbH

On July 17, 2024, Infosys Germany GmbH, a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of Infosys Limited, acquired 100% of the voting interests in in-tech Holding GmbH, an engineering R&D services provider headquartered in Germany. The acquisition was intended to strengthen Infosys’ engineering R&D capabilities for global clients’ digital engineering requirements.

The total purchase consideration of EUR 356 million (₹3,228 crore) comprised cash consideration paid to the selling shareholders at the acquisition date.

The bonuses and incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Statement of Comprehensive Income over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was ₹139 crore at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, these amounts had been fully collected.
