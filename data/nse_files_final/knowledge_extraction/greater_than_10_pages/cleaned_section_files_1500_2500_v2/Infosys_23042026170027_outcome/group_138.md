## Fair value hierarchy

- **Level 1:** Quoted prices, unadjusted, in active markets for identical assets or liabilities.
- **Level 2:** Inputs other than quoted prices included in Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly as prices or indirectly as derived prices.
- **Level 3:** Inputs for assets or liabilities that are not based on observable market data.

## 2.11 EQUITY

### Ordinary Shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity share capital. Incremental costs directly attributable to the issuance of new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of tax effects.

### Capital redemption reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

### Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the accumulated earnings of the Company.

### Securities premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as securities premium. Amounts have been utilized for bonus issues and share buybacks from the share premium account.

### Share options outstanding account

The share options outstanding account records the fair value of equity-settled share-based payment transactions with employees. Amounts recorded in this account are transferred to securities premium upon exercise of stock options and to general reserve for stock options not exercised by employees.

### Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of the eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be utilized for acquiring new plant and machinery for the Company’s business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

### Other components of equity

Other components of equity include:

- Remeasurement of net defined benefit liability or asset.
- Equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income.
- Changes in the fair value of investments.
- Changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes.

### Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the cash flow hedge reserve is transferred to the condensed standalone Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecast transaction occurs.
