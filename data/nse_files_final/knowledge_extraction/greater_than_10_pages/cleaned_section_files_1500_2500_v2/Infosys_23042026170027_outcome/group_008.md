## Infosys Subsidiaries, Acquisitions, Incorporations, and Liquidations

100. **Danske IT and Support Services India Private Limited** — Acquired by Infosys Limited on September 1, 2023; renamed **Idunn Information Technology Private Limited** effective April 1, 2024.

101. **InSemi Technology Services Pvt. Ltd.** — Acquired by Infosys Limited on May 10, 2024.

102. **Elbrus Labs Private Limited** — Wholly owned subsidiary of InSemi Technology Services Pvt. Ltd.; acquired by Infosys Limited on May 10, 2024.

103. **Infosys Services (Thailand) Limited** — Wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited; incorporated on July 26, 2024.

104. **Infy tech SAS** — Wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Limited; incorporated on July 3, 2024.

105. **in-tech Holding GmbH** — Acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Limited, on July 17, 2024; merged into in-tech GmbH effective January 1, 2025.

106. **in-tech GmbH** — Subsidiary of in-tech Holding GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.

107. **in-tech Automotive Engineering SL** — Subsidiary of in-tech GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.

108. **ProIT** — Subsidiary of in-tech GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.

109. **in-tech Automotive Engineering de R.L. de C.V.** — Subsidiary of in-tech GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024; liquidated effective May 7, 2025.

110. **drivetech Fahrversuch GmbH** — Subsidiary of in-tech GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.

111. **Friedrich Wagner Holding Inc** — Subsidiary of in-tech GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024; under liquidation.

112. **in-tech Automotive Engineering LLC** — Subsidiary of Friedrich Wagner Holding Inc; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024; liquidated effective November 30, 2024.

113. **in-tech Services LLC** — Subsidiary of Friedrich Wagner Holding Inc; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024; liquidated effective November 30, 2024.

114. **Friedrich & Wagner Asia Pacific GmbH** — Subsidiary of in-tech GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024; merged into in-tech GmbH effective January 1, 2025.

115. **in-tech engineering s.r.o.** — Subsidiary of Friedrich & Wagner Asia Pacific GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.

116. **in-tech engineering GmbH** — Subsidiary of Friedrich & Wagner Asia Pacific GmbH; acquired by Infosys Germany GmbH on July 17, 2024.
