## 2.21.2 Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund, which is a defined benefit plan. Both eligible employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of the covered employee's salary.

The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust. The trust invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on the trust's investments and the notified interest rate.

## 2.21.3 Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys participate in a defined contribution plan. The Company has no further obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## 2.21.4 Compensated absences

Infosys has a policy for accumulating and non-accumulating compensated absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The calculation is based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the Balance Sheet date.

Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.

(1) For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, because the market for high-quality corporate bonds is not developed, the yield on government bonds is considered the discount rate. For most overseas defined benefit plans, because the market for high-quality corporate bonds is not developed, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used.

(2) The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined by the Company, considering factors such as past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management's estimate of future salary increases.

(3) The attrition rate is based on management's estimate from the Company's past long-term employee turnover trend. Tenure reflects the past long-term trend in employees' average remaining service life and the average estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, assumptions regarding future mortality experience are based on published statistics from the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas defined benefit plans, assumptions regarding future mortality experience are based on the latest statistics on life expectancy, plan experience, and other relevant data.

The Company assesses these assumptions with its projected long-term plans for growth and prevalent industry standards.

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Trustees administer contributions made to the trust.

Plan assets of overseas defined benefit plans are primarily invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined based on investment criteria prescribed under applicable pension fund regulations and by insurer managers. The insurers' investments are diversified and provide guaranteed-interest-rate arrangements.
