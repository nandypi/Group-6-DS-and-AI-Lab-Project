## 1. Notes

a) The audited interim condensed standalone financial statements for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026 were taken on record by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on April 23, 2026. The statutory auditors, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, expressed an unmodified audit opinion.

The information presented above is extracted from the audited interim condensed standalone financial statements, which were prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind-AS), as prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and relevant amendment rules thereafter.

## b) Proposed change of Auditors on account of mandatory rotation requirement in India

Under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the rules made thereunder, Infosys Limited is required to rotate its current statutory auditors upon completion of the maximum permitted term.

On April 23, 2026, the Audit Committee proposed recommending the appointment of BSR & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), as the Company’s statutory auditors. The Board of Directors approved the announcement of the Company’s intention to recommend the appointment.

The proposed appointment will be recommended to shareholders at the 46th Annual General Meeting in 2027 for an initial term of five consecutive years, until the conclusion of the 51st Annual General Meeting in 2032. BSR & Co.’s first year of audit is expected to be the financial year ending March 31, 2028, including the audit of the quarterly financial statements for that year.

The proposed appointment is subject to all applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.

## c) Proposed change in the Company's certifying accountant for filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ('U.S. SEC')

Infosys is registered with the U.S. SEC and is required to appoint a certifying accountant to audit its financial statements. The Audit Committee and the Board of Directors approved the announcement of the Company’s intention to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as its independent registered public accounting firm.

The proposed appointment is expected to be effective for the year ending March 31, 2028. KPMG would audit the annual financial statements included in Infosys’s Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the U.S. SEC for that year.

The proposed appointment is subject to all applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.

## d) Appointment of Independent Director

Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the appointment of Diane Enberg Jurgens (DIN: 11585200) as an Additional and Independent Director, effective April 22, 2026, for a period of three years, subject to shareholder approval.

## e) Impact of Labour Codes

On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified provisions of the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Code on Social Security, 2020; and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (collectively, the “Labour Codes”). These codes consolidate 29 existing labour laws into a unified framework governing employee benefits during and after employment.

The Labour Codes introduced changes including a uniform definition of wages and enhanced leave-related benefits. Infosys assessed the financial implications and recorded an increase of ₹1,146 crore in gratuity liability arising from past service cost and in leave liability.

Because the impact of the new legislation was considered non-recurring, Infosys presented the incremental amount as “Impact of Labour Codes” under “Exceptional Item” in the Standalone Statement of Profit and Loss for the quarter ended December 31, 2025, and the year ended March 31, 2026.

The Company continues to monitor developments relating to the Labour Codes and will evaluate any impact on the measurement of employee benefit liabilities.
