## 2.22 EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

## Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a gratuity defined benefit retirement plan covering eligible employees, mainly of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. The trusts are administered by trustees, and contributions are invested in schemes with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Required contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies with employee age.

Defined benefit liabilities are determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest rate, and market risks.

The net obligation of a defined benefit plan is recognized in the Balance Sheet as an asset or liability. Remeasurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not reclassified to profit or loss. Actual returns on plan assets exceeding the returns calculated using the applicable discount rate are also recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

## Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. Employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. A portion is contributed to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law; the remaining portion is contributed to a government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the trust’s investment returns and the notified interest rate.

In Indian subsidiaries, eligible employees participate in defined contribution provident funds. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in defined contribution superannuation plans. The Group has no obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed for unused entitlements accumulated at the reporting date. Expense for non-accumulating absences is recognized when the absences occur.
