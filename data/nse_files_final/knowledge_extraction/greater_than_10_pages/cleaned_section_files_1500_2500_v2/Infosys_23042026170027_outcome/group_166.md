## Capital Redemption Reserve

In accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve or retained earnings.

## Securities premium

Amounts received in excess of the par value of equity shares are classified as securities premium. The securities premium account has been used for bonus issues and share buybacks.

## Share options outstanding account

The share options outstanding account records the fair value of equity-settled share-based payment transactions with employees. Amounts are transferred to securities premium when stock options are exercised and to general reserve when stock options are not exercised by employees.

## Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used to acquire new plant and machinery for the Company’s business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include:

- Currency translation
- Remeasurement of net defined benefit liability or asset
- Equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income
- Changes in the fair value of investments
- Changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes

## Currency translation reserve

Exchange differences arising from the translation of financial statements of foreign subsidiaries whose functional currency is not the Indian rupee are recognized in other comprehensive income and presented within equity.

## Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedging reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the reserve is transferred to the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecasted transaction occurs.

The Company has one class of shares, consisting of equity shares with a par value of ₹5 each. Each equity share carries one vote. Equity shares represented by American Depositary Shares (ADS) carry the same voting and dividend rights as other equity shares. Each ADS represents one underlying equity share.

In the event of liquidation, equity shareholders are entitled to receive the remaining assets of the Company in proportion to their shareholding after distribution of all preferential amounts. No such preferential amounts currently exist, other than amounts held by irrevocable controlled trusts. For these trusts, the corpus would be settled in favor of the beneficiaries.

Holders of options issued under the Company’s share option plans have no voting, dividend, or liquidation rights.

Details of shares reserved for issue under the Company’s employee stock option plan are provided in the related note.

## Buyback

During the five years immediately preceding March 31, 2026, the Company purchased and extinguished 21,62,33,685 fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each from the stock exchange.

## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

At its meeting on September 11, 2025, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each for ₹18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting by postal ballot, with results declared on November 6, 2025. The buyback comprised 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025 on a standalone basis, at a price of ₹1,800 per equity share.

The offer was made to eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who became equity shareholders by cancelling ADSs and withdrawing the underlying equity shares, as of the record date of November 14, 2025. The buyback was conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route.

The tender period ran from November 20, 2025 to November 26, 2025. The Company completed the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025, after buying back and extinguishing 10,00,00,000 equity shares. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs.

The Company funded the buyback from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013. As of March 31, 2026, it had created a capital redemption reserve of ₹50 crore, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, appropriated from general reserve in accordance with Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The Company’s capital management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. It may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares to maintain or achieve this structure.

As of March 31, 2026, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.
