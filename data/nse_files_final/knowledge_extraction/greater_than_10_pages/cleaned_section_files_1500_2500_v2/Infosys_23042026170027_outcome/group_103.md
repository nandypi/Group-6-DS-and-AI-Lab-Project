## Fair value hierarchy

- **Level 1:** Quoted prices, unadjusted, in active markets for identical assets or liabilities.
- **Level 2:** Inputs other than quoted prices included in Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly or indirectly.
- **Level 3:** Inputs for assets or liabilities that are not based on observable market data and are therefore unobservable.

During the year ended March 31, 2025:

- Government securities of ₹36 crore and non-convertible debentures of ₹261 crore were transferred from Level 2 to Level 1 because they were valued using quoted prices.
- Tax-free bonds of ₹554 crore were transferred from Level 1 to Level 2 because they were valued using market-observable inputs.

The majority of the Company’s investments were fair valued using Level 1 or Level 2 inputs. These investments primarily included mutual fund units, target maturity fund units, tax-free bonds, certificates of deposit, commercial papers, treasury bills, government securities, non-convertible debentures, and quoted bonds issued by government and quasi-government organisations.

The Company considered counterparty risk using criteria including Tier I capital, capital adequacy ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and deposit base of banks and financial institutions. These risks were monitored under the Company’s risk management programme.
