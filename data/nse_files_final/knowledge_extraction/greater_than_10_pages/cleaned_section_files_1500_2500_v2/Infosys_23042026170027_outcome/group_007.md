## Deloitte Haskins &amp; Sells LLP

The auditor's responsibilities include:

- Assessing whether the Group can continue as a going concern. If a material uncertainty exists, the auditor is required to draw attention to the related disclosures or modify the opinion if the disclosures are inadequate. Future events or conditions may cause the Group to cease to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures in the Statement, including whether it represents the underlying transactions and events fairly.
- Performing procedures in accordance with the circular issued by SEBI under Regulation 33(8) of the LODR Regulations, to the extent applicable.
- Obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group to express an opinion on the Statement. The auditors are responsible for the direction, supervision, and performance of the audit of financial information of entities included in the Statement for which they are the independent auditors.

Materiality is the magnitude of misstatements in the Statement, individually or in aggregate, that could influence the economic decisions of a reasonably knowledgeable user. The auditors consider quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when:

1. Planning the scope of audit work and evaluating its results.
2. Evaluating the effect of identified misstatements in the Statement.

The auditors communicate with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, and any significant deficiencies in internal control identified during the audit.

The auditors also provide those charged with governance with a statement confirming compliance with relevant ethical requirements regarding independence and communicate relationships or other matters that may reasonably be considered to bear on their independence, together with related safeguards where applicable.
