## Condensed Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows

### Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately. Highly liquid investments readily convertible to known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

## Overview and Notes to the Interim Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (`the Company` or `Infosys`) provides AI-first business consulting and technology services. The Group includes Infosys, its subsidiaries, and controlled trusts.

Infosys is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary listings are on BSE Ltd. and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares representing equity shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Board of Directors on April 23, 2026.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared in compliance with IAS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

The statements use the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of defined benefit obligations less the fair value of plan assets.

The interim statements do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2025.

Accounting policies were applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard was initially adopted or a revision to an existing standard required a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information used in preparing the audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from source data and rounded to the nearest digits. Consequently, quarterly figures may not add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.

## 1.3 Basis of consolidation

Infosys consolidates entities that it owns or controls. The interim condensed consolidated financial statements comprise the financial statements of the Company, its controlled trusts, and its subsidiaries.

Control exists when the parent has power over an entity, is exposed or has rights to variable returns from its involvement with the entity, and can affect those returns through its power. Power is demonstrated through existing rights that provide the ability to direct the activities that significantly affect an entity’s returns.

Subsidiaries are consolidated from the date control commences until the date control ceases. Group company financial statements are consolidated on a line-by-line basis. Intra-group balances and transactions, including unrealized gains or losses, are eliminated on consolidation.

The Group applies uniform accounting policies. Non-controlling interests representing the portion of net profit or loss and net assets of subsidiaries not directly or indirectly owned or controlled by the Company are excluded.
