## Liquidity risk

Liquidity risk is the risk that the Group will not be able to settle or meet its obligations on time.

The Group’s principal sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents, investments, and cash flow generated from operations. The Group has no outstanding borrowings and believes that working capital is sufficient to meet its current requirements.

As at March 31, 2025, the Group had working capital of ₹51,167 crore, including cash and cash equivalents of ₹22,201 crore and current investments of ₹12,950 crore. As at March 31, 2025, the Group had working capital of ₹54,249 crore, including cash and cash equivalents of ₹24,455 crore and current investments of ₹12,482 crore.

As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, outstanding employee benefit obligations were ₹3,641 crore and ₹3,007 crore, respectively, and were substantially funded.
