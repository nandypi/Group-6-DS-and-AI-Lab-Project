## Sensitivity of significant assumptions used for valuation of defined benefit obligation

| Impact from | Gratuity: 1% point increase/decrease | Pension: 0.5% point increase/decrease |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount rate | ₹205 crore | ₹70 crore |
| Weighted average rate of increase in compensation levels | ₹220 crore | ₹12 crore |

Sensitivity to significant actuarial assumptions is computed by varying one actuarial assumption used for the valuation of the defined benefit obligation while keeping all other actuarial assumptions constant. In practice, this is not probable, and changes in some assumptions may be correlated.

The Group expects to contribute ₹540 crore to gratuity and ₹66 crore to pension during fiscal 2027.

## 2.20.2 Provident fund

Infosys has an obligation to fund any shortfall in the yield of the trust’s investments relative to the administered interest rates on an annual basis. These administered rates are determined annually, predominantly considering social and economic factors. The actuary has provided a valuation for provident fund liabilities based on guidance issued by the Actuarial Society of India.

### Actuarial assumptions

The assumptions used in determining the present value obligation of the defined benefit plan under the Deterministic Approach are as follows:

| Particulars | As at March 31, 2026 | As at March 31, 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Government of India (GOI) bond yield (1) | 6.50% | 6.50% |
| Expected rate of return on plan assets | 8.25% | 8.00% |
| Remaining term to maturity of portfolio | 6 years | 6 years |
| Expected guaranteed interest rate | 8.25% | 8.25% |

(1) In India, the market for high-quality corporate bonds is not developed; therefore, the yield of government bonds is considered the discount rate. The tenure considers the past long-term trend of employees’ average remaining service life, which reflects the average estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

The asset allocation for plan assets is determined based on the investment criteria prescribed under the relevant regulations.

The actuarial valuation of provident fund liability exposes the Group to interest rate risk. The defined benefit obligation uses a discount rate based on government bonds. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation will tend to increase.

As at March 31, 2026, the defined benefit obligation would be affected by approximately ₹76 crore and ₹151 crore on account of a 0.25% increase/decrease in the expected rate of return on plan assets.

The Group contributed ₹391 crore and ₹351 crore to the provident fund during the three months ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively. The Group contributed ₹1,515 crore and ₹1,323 crore during the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively. These amounts were recognized in net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income under employee benefit expense.

The provident plans apply only to employees drawing a salary in Indian rupees.
