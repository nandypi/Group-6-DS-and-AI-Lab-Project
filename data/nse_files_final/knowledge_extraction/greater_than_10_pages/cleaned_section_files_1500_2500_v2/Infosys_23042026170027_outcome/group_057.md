## Credit risk

Credit risk refers to the risk of default by a counterparty resulting in a financial loss. At the reporting date, maximum exposure to credit risk was primarily from:

| Particulars | March 31, 2026 (₹ crore) | March 31, 2025 (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trade receivables | 35,234 | 31,158 |
| Unbilled revenue | 17,221 | 15,083 |

Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are typically unsecured and are derived from revenue earned from customers primarily located in the United States of America and Europe.

The Group manages credit risk through credit approvals, credit limits, and continuous monitoring of customer creditworthiness. It uses the expected credit loss model to assess required allowances and a provision matrix to calculate expected credit loss allowances for trade receivables and unbilled revenue. The matrix considers credit reports and other available credit information.

The Group’s exposure to credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of customers and concentration among its largest customers. Customer exposure is diversified, and no single customer contributed more than 10% of outstanding trade receivables and unbilled revenue.

## Credit exposure

The Group’s credit period generally ranges from 30–75 days.

| Particulars | As at March 31, 2026 (₹ crore) | As at March 31, 2025 (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trade receivables | 35,234 | 31,158 |
| Unbilled revenue | 17,221 | 15,083 |

Days sales outstanding was 67 days as of March 31, 2026 and 69 days as of March 31, 2025.

Credit risk on cash and cash equivalents is limited because the Group generally invests in deposits with banks that have high ratings assigned by international and domestic credit-rating agencies. Ratings are monitored periodically, and the Group considers the latest available credit ratings as at the date of approval of its consolidated financial statements.

The Group’s investments primarily include liquid mutual fund units, quoted debt securities, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, and quoted bonds issued by government and quasi-government organizations.

The Group assesses counterparty risk using multiple criteria, including Tier I capital, capital adequacy ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and deposit base of banks and financial institutions. These risks are monitored regularly under the Group’s risk-management program.
