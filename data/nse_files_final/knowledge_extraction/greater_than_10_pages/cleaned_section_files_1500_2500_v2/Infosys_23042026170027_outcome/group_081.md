(1) For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, because the market for high-quality corporate bonds is not developed, the yield on government bonds is considered the discount rate. For most overseas defined benefit plans, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used.

(2) The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined by the Company, considering the Company’s past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management’s estimate of future salary increases.

(3) The attrition rate is based on management’s estimate using the Company’s past long-term employee turnover trend. Tenure considers the past long-term trend in employees’ average remaining service life, reflecting the average estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, assumptions regarding future mortality experience are based on published statistics from the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas defined benefit plans, assumptions regarding future mortality experience consider the latest life expectancy statistics, plan experience, and other relevant data.

The Group assesses these assumptions against its projected long-term growth plans and prevailing industry standards.

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to the Infosys BPM Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively. Trustees administer the contributions to the trusts as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025. Gratuity contributions are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

Plan assets of overseas defined benefit plans are primarily invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined using investment criteria prescribed under regulations applicable to pension funds and insurer managers. The insurers’ investments are diversified and provide guaranteed interest-rate arrangements.

These defined benefit plans expose the Group to the following actuarial risks:

- **Interest rate risk:** The present value of defined benefit plan liabilities is generally calculated using a discount rate based on government bond yields. In certain overseas jurisdictions, the government bond yield is adjusted for a corporate spread. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation will tend to increase.
- **Life expectancy and investment risk:** The pension fund offers a choice between a lifelong pension and a cash lump sum at retirement. The fund has defined rates for converting the lump sum to a pension. Risk arises if members live longer than implied by those conversion rates or if pension assets do not achieve the investment return implied by the rates.
- **Asset volatility:** A portion of the pension fund is held in equities, which are expected to outperform corporate bonds over the long term but create short-term volatility and risk. The insurer’s pension fund board is responsible for the investment strategy. Equity allocation is based on the fund’s long-term investment horizon and objective of providing a reasonable long-term return on members’ account balances.
