## f) Update on orders received from the Indian Income tax department

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment year 2013–14 and assessment years 2017–18 to 2021–22.

The orders confirmed the Company's position regarding the tax treatment of certain matters. As a result:

- Pre-tax interest income of ₹381 crore was recognized in other income.
- Income-tax provisions aggregating ₹869 crore were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹86 crore following resolution of the disputes.

## g) Other income

Other income included interest on income-tax refunds as follows:

- ₹381 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2026.
- ₹327 crore for the quarter ended March 31, 2025.
- Approximately ₹381 crore for the year ended March 31, 2025.
- Approximately ₹340 crore for the year ended March 31, 2024.
- Less than ₹1 crore for the quarter ended December 31, 2025.

## h) Update on stock grants

### i) Grants to CEO & MD

On April 23, 2026, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following annual grants to Salil Parekh, CEO and MD, under the employment agreement approved by shareholders:

- Annual performance-based stock incentives, in the form of Restricted Stock Units (RSUs), covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹34.75 crore under the Infosys 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (2015 Plan). The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of performance targets determined by the Board.
- Annual performance-based ESG stock incentives, in the form of RSUs, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹2 crore under the 2015 Plan. The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of specified environmental, social and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- Annual performance-based TSR stock incentives, in the form of RSUs, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹5 crore under the 2015 Plan. The RSUs will vest on or after March 31, 2027, subject to the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the two-year cumulative period, as determined by the Board.
- The 2019 Annual Performance Equity Grant, in the form of RSUs, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹10 crore under the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program-2019 (2019 Plan). The RSUs will vest 12 months from the grant date, subject to achievement of performance criteria specified in the 2019 Plan.

The RSUs will be granted with effect from May 2, 2026. The number of RSUs will be calculated using the market price at the close of trading on the date immediately preceding the grant date.

### ii) Grants to other employees

On April 23, 2026, the Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved:

- 27,193 RSUs under the 2015 Plan for eligible employees.
- Performance-based stock incentives in the form of Performance Stock Units (PSUs) under the 2019 Plan for eligible employees, covering Company equity shares with a market value of ₹1.90 crore as at the grant date. The number of PSUs will be calculated using the market price at the close of trading on the date immediately preceding the grant date.

The grants under the 2015 Plan will vest equally over two to three years. Grants under the 2019 Plan will vest over two years, subject to achievement of performance parameters defined in the 2019 Plan. The RSUs and PSUs will be granted with effect from May 2, 2026, with an exercise price equal to the par value of the share.

## 2. Information on dividends for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026

For financial year 2026, the Board recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share with a par value of ₹5 each. The payment is subject to shareholder approval at the Company's Annual General Meeting (AGM) on June 23, 2026. The record date for the final dividend is June 10, 2026, and the dividend will be paid on June 25, 2026.

For financial year 2025, the Company declared a final dividend of ₹22 per equity share with a par value of ₹5 each.

The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹23 per equity share at its meeting held on October 16, 2025. The record date was October 27, 2025, and the dividend was paid on November 7, 2025. The interim dividend declared in the previous year was ₹21 per equity share.
