## Financial risk factors

The Group’s activities expose it to market, credit, and liquidity risks. Its primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. The Group uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange exposures.

Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of each customer and the concentration of risk among the top few customers.

## Market risk

The Group operates internationally, with business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies.

The Group holds foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of exchange-rate changes. Fluctuations in the Indian rupee against foreign currencies can adversely affect the Group’s operating results.

The counterparty for these contracts is generally a bank. These instruments are valued using quoted prices for similar assets and liabilities in active markets or inputs that are directly or indirectly observable in the marketplace.

During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Group designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions.

The related hedge transactions for the cash flow hedge balance as at March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss within three months.

The Group determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at hedge inception and through periodic prospective assessments.

If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal but the risk management objective remains unchanged and hedge accounting continues to qualify, the hedge relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.
