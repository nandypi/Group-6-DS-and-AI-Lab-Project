## 2.23 SEGMENT REPORTING

Ind AS 108, Operating segments, establishes standards for reporting information about operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers.

The Group’s operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to enable clients to enhance business performance. The Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates the Group’s performance and allocates resources based on various performance indicators by business segment.

The Group’s business segments primarily comprise enterprises in:

- Financial Services and Insurance
- Manufacturing
- Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Hi-Tech
- Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment aggregates the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments represent the operating segments of businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and identified enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are categorized based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Revenue for “all other segments” represents revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other enterprises in Public Services.

Allocated segment expenses include expenses incurred for services provided by the Group’s offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated segment effort.

Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocable to individual segments because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are disclosed separately as “unallocated” and adjusted against total Group income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. The Management considers it impracticable to provide segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for whom revenue is otherwise recognized. Disclosure of revenue by geographic location is provided in Note 2.16, Revenue from operations.
