## Proposed Acquisition

1. On August 13, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 75% of the equity share capital in Telstra Purple Pty Ltd, including some of its subsidiaries, together known as Versent Group. Versent Group is an Australian digital transformation solutions provider. The consideration, including earn-outs and deferred consideration, amounts to up to AUD 233 million, approximately ₹1,335 crore, excluding retention bonuses and management incentives. The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing adjustments.

2. On March 25, 2026, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the equity share capital of Optimum Achieve Holdings Inc. and its subsidiaries, including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC. Optimum Achieve Holdings Inc. is a healthcare digital transformation and consulting firm headquartered in the USA. The consideration, including earn-outs, amounts to up to $465 million, approximately ₹4,410 crore, excluding management incentives and retention bonuses, subject to customary closing adjustments.

## Update on acquisition completed after the end of the reporting period

On March 25, 2026, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the partnership interests of Stratus Global LLC, an insurance technology partner serving property and casualty insurers and managing general agents (MGAs), headquartered in the USA. The consideration, including earn-outs, amounts to up to $95 million, approximately ₹901 crore, excluding management incentives and retention bonuses, subject to customary closing adjustments.

In April 2026, as of the date the financial statements were authorized for issuance, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC completed the acquisition of Stratus Global LLC. Because of the recent timing of the acquisition and the pending valuation of identifiable net assets acquired and liabilities assumed, the initial accounting for the business combination was incomplete when the financial statements were authorized for issuance. Accordingly, all required business-combination disclosures had not been made.

## 2.11 Employees' Stock Option Plans (ESOP)

## Accounting Policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized as an expense in net profit in the interim consolidated statement of comprehensive income on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, as if the award comprised multiple awards, with a corresponding increase to share premium.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan):

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan shall not exceed 50,000,000 equity shares. To implement the 2019 Plan, up to 45,000,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The parameters may include relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, broader domestic and global market indices, and company operating-performance metrics. Each parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest. These instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan):

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan shall not exceed 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. These instruments generally vest over four years. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options is the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 8,650,911 shares as of March 31, 2026 and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025 under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each date.

## Under the 2015 plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following fiscal 2026 grants, effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement upon achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement upon achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, an annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs vest based on the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employee agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ₹3 crore. The grant vests over time in three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the grant date. Accordingly, an annual time-based grant of 18,132 RSUs was made effective February 1, 2026 for fiscal 2026.

Although the annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of March 31, 2026, the Company recorded employment stock-compensation expense under IFRS 2, *Share-based Payment*, because the service commencement date preceded the grant date. For this purpose, the grant date under IFRS 2 was July 1, 2022.
