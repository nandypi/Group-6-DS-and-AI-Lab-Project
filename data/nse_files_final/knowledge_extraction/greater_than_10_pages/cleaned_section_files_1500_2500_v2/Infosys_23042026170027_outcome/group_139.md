## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

At its meeting on September 11, 2025, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each for ₹18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot. Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting, with results declared on November 6, 2025.

The buyback comprised 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis. The buyback price was ₹1,800 per equity share.

The offer was made to eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who became eligible as of the Record Date by cancelling American Depository Shares and withdrawing the underlying equity shares. The shares were offered on a proportionate basis through the tender-offer route.

The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The Company completed the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025, and all 10,00,00,000 shares were bought back and extinguished. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs.

The Company funded the buyback from free reserves, including the securities premium, in accordance with Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013. Under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore as of March 31, 2026, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back. The reserve was created through an appropriation from the general reserve.

The Company’s capital management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. To maintain or achieve this structure, the Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares.

As of March 31, 2026, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## 2.11.2 DIVIDEND

Final dividends on shares are recorded as liabilities on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as liabilities on the date of declaration by the Company’s Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Remittances of dividends outside India are governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and are subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## 2.11.3 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

## Accounting Policy

The Company recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan)

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan is 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. These parameters include relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader market domestic and global indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest. The instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan)

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan is 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust for the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were subsequently adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 86,50,911 shares as of March 31, 2026, and 96,55,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities at each reporting date.
