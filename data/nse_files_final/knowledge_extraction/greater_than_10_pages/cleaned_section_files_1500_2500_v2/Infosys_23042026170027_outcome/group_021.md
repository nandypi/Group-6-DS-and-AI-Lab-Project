## Reconciliation of Reported IFRS financial measures to Adjusted non-IFRS financial measures for year ended (in ₹ crore)

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Adjusted non-IFRS | 37,543 | 21.0 | 34,424 | 21.1 |

## NOTES:

1. The adjusted non-IFRS measures exclude the effects of provisions related to the Labour Codes notified by the Government of India on November 21, 2025. These provisions increased gratuity liability, arising from past service cost relating to a plan amendment, and leave liability by ₹1,289 crore. The amount was recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income and resulted in a lower tax expense of ₹318 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026.
2. Excluding the effects of Income Tax orders received under sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and the Labour Codes provisions, EPS increased by 12.1% for the year ended March 31, 2026 and by 13.9% for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, in rupee terms year over year.
3. Free cash flow included cash payments related to the Labour Codes of ₹452 crore for the quarter and ₹902 crore for the year ended March 31, 2026.
4. Infosys uses non-IFRS financial performance measures to supplement financial information reported on an IFRS basis. These measures should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for the relevant IFRS measures.

## Press Release

Infosys reported FY26 revenue of $20,158 million, representing constant-currency growth of 3.1%. Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.3%, adjusted operating margin was 21.0%, EPS growth was 11.0% in rupee terms, and free cash flow was $3,733 million. Total contract value of large deal wins was $14.9 billion, with net new business representing 55% of the total.

Q4 revenue was $5,040 million, with constant-currency growth of 4.1% year over year and a decline of 1.3% quarter over quarter. Q4 operating margin was 20.9%.

## Guidance for FY27:

- Revenue growth of 1.5%–3.5% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%–22%.

## Key highlights:

### For the quarter ended March 31, 2026

- Revenue in constant-currency terms grew 4.1% year over year and declined 1.3% quarter over quarter.
- Reported IFRS revenue was $5,040 million, up 6.6% year over year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.9%.
- Basic EPS was $0.23, up 15.7% year over year and 25.3% quarter over quarter.
- Free cash flow was $833 million, representing 90.6% of net profit.

### For the year ended March 31, 2026

- Revenue in constant-currency terms grew 3.1% year over year.
- Reported IFRS revenue was $20,158 million, up 4.6% year over year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.3%; adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.
- Basic EPS was $0.81, up 5.6% year over year.
- Free cash flow was $3,733 million, representing 112.6% of net profit.

Infosys stated that savings from Project Maximus enabled investment in talent, AI, and sales and marketing.

The Board proposed a final dividend of ₹25 per share. Together with the interim dividend and recently concluded buyback, shareholder returns for FY26 exceeded ₹37,500 crore.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys collaborated with Citizens to accelerate AI-driven transformation across banking operations, product development, and customer experience, using Infosys Topaz Fabric.
- Infosys collaborated with ExxonMobil on the development and deployment of high-efficiency cooling systems for AI and high-performance computing workloads.
- Infosys collaborated with Crocs on IT and business process transformation using AI-powered innovation and advanced automation.
- Infosys entered into a strategic collaboration with Incora to improve the speed, accuracy, and resilience of global supply chain operations using artificial intelligence.
- Infosys and the University of Nottingham extended their strategic collaboration to strengthen the digital infrastructure of the University's Student Management System, including performance and security compliance.
