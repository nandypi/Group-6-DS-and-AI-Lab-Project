## 2.18 OTHER INCOME, NET

## 2.18.1 Other income

## Accounting Policy

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, and exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts and the translation of foreign currency assets and liabilities. Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## 2.18.2 Foreign currency

## Functional currency

The functional currency of the Company is the Indian rupee. The financial statements are presented in Indian rupees, rounded off to crore. One crore equals ten million.

## Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the Balance Sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the condensed standalone Statement of Profit and Loss within exchange gains/(losses) on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in Other Comprehensive Income as qualifying cash flow hedges.

Non-monetary foreign-currency assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate on the date when fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized on settlement of foreign currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Foreign-currency-denominated revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate on the transaction date.

Other Comprehensive Income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments and measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

## Government grant

Government grants are recognized only when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Government grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss on a systematic and rational basis over the useful life of the asset. Government grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in net profit over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.

## 2.19 EXPENSES

## 2.19.1 Gratuity and Pension

The Company provides gratuity, a defined benefit retirement plan, to eligible Indian employees of Infosys. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure. Gratuity liabilities are contributed to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Trustees administer contributions to the Trust, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Company operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic payouts after retirement and/or lump-sum payments under the rules of each fund, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to the employee's age.

Liabilities for these defined benefit plans are determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Company to actuarial risks, including longevity risk, interest rate risk, and market risk.

The net obligation of a defined benefit plan is recognized in the Balance Sheet as an asset or liability. Re-measurement gains and losses on the net defined benefit liability/(asset) are recognized in Other Comprehensive Income and are not reclassified to profit or loss in subsequent periods. The actual return on plan assets in excess of the yield calculated using the discount rate applied to the defined benefit obligation is recognized in Other Comprehensive Income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss.

## 2.19.2 Provident fund

Eligible employees receive benefits from a provident fund, which is a defined benefit plan. Both eligible employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary.

The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust. The Trust invests in designated instruments as permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund. The annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the Trust is administered by the Government of India. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on the Trust's investments and the notified interest rate.

## 2.19.3 Superannuation

Certain employees participate in a defined contribution plan. The Company has no obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund whose corpus is invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## 2.19.4 Compensated absences

The Company has a policy covering accumulating and non-accumulating compensated absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the Balance Sheet date. Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
