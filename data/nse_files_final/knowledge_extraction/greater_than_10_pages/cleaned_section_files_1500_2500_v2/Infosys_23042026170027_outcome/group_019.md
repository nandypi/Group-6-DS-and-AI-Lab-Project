## Guidance for FY27:

- Revenue growth of 1.5%-3.5% in constant currency
- Operating margin of 20%-22%

## Key highlights:

## For the quarter ended March 31, 2026

- Revenues in constant currency terms grew by 4.1% year over year and declined by 1.3% quarter over quarter.
- Reported revenues were ₹46,402 crore, up 13.4% year over year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 21.0%.
- Basic EPS was ₹21.01, up 23.8% year over year and 29.9% quarter over quarter.
- Free cash flow was ₹7,711 crore; free cash flow conversion was 90.6% of net profit.

## For the year ended March 31, 2026

- Revenues in constant currency terms grew by 3.1% year over year.
- Reported revenues were ₹178,650 crore, up 9.6% year over year.
- Reported IFRS operating margin was 20.3%; adjusted operating margin was 21.0%.
- Basic EPS was ₹71.58, up 11.0% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹33,097 crore; free cash flow conversion was 112.3% of net profit.

The CFO stated that savings from Project Maximus supported investment in talent, AI, and sales and marketing. The Board proposed a final dividend of ₹25 per share. Including the interim dividend and recently concluded buyback, the total amount returned to shareholders for FY26 was expected to exceed ₹37,500 crore.

- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Anthropic to support automated workflows, software delivery, and agentic AI solutions across complex, regulated industries.
- Infosys and Intel expanded their strategic collaboration to help enterprises move AI initiatives from pilots to production at scale across data centers, cloud, and edge environments.
- Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Cursor to help enterprises build and scale AI-powered digital solutions. The collaboration included enabling more than 100,000 Infosys software engineers with agentic coding platforms.
- Infosys and Cognition announced a strategic collaboration focused on agentic and autonomous engineering capabilities for global enterprises, combining Infosys Topaz Fabric with Cognition’s Devin.
