## 1. Notes

### a) Audited financial statements and audit opinion

The audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, were taken on record by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on April 23, 2026. The statements were prepared in accordance with Ind-AS under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and subsequent relevant amendments.

### b) Proposed change of auditors on account of mandatory rotation requirement in India

Under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, Infosys Limited is required to rotate its current statutory auditors after completion of the maximum permitted term.

On April 23, 2026, the Audit Committee proposed recommending BSR &amp; Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), as the company’s statutory auditors. The Board approved the announcement of this intention at its meeting held on the same date.

The proposed appointment is to be recommended to shareholders at the 46th Annual General Meeting in 2027 for a first term of five consecutive years, ending with the conclusion of the 51st AGM in 2032. The first year of audit by BSR &amp; Co. LLP is expected to be the financial year ending March 31, 2028, including the audit of that year’s quarterly financial statements.

The proposed appointment is subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.

### c) Proposed change in the company’s certifying accountant for filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

Infosys Limited is registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and is required to appoint a certifying accountant to audit its financial statements.

The Audit Committee and Board of Directors approved the announcement of the company’s intention to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as its independent registered public accounting firm. The proposed appointment is expected to be effective for the year ending March 31, 2028. KPMG would audit the annual financial statements included in Infosys’s Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ending March 31, 2028.

The proposed appointment is subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.
