## Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements

1. As required by Section 143(3) of the Companies Act, based on the audit, the auditor reported that:

   - All information and explanations necessary for the audit had been obtained.
   - Proper books of account and relevant records had been maintained by the Group.
   - The Consolidated Balance Sheet, Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss including Other Comprehensive Income, Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity, and Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows agreed with the relevant books of account.
   - The consolidated financial statements complied with the Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.
   - Based on written representations from directors and reports from statutory auditors of Indian subsidiaries, none of the directors of Group companies incorporated in India was disqualified under Section 164(2) as of March 31, 2026.
   - The auditor’s separate report on internal financial controls with reference to the consolidated financial statements expressed an unmodified opinion on their adequacy and operating effectiveness for the Company and its Indian subsidiaries.
   - Directors’ remuneration paid during the year was in accordance with Section 197 of the Act.

2. With respect to Rule 11 of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014:

   - The consolidated financial statements disclosed the impact of pending litigation on the Group’s consolidated financial position. Refer to Note 2.24.
   - The Group made provisions for material foreseeable losses as required under applicable law or accounting standards. Refer to Note 2.16.
   - The Group did not have any long-term derivative contracts.
   - There was no delay in transferring amounts required to be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund by the Company and its Indian subsidiaries.
   - Management represented that no funds had been advanced, loaned, or invested by the Company or its Indian subsidiaries to intermediaries with the understanding that they would fund ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees or securities on their behalf.
   - Management also represented that no funds had been received from funding parties with the understanding that the Company or its Indian subsidiaries would fund ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees or securities on their behalf.
   - Based on the audit procedures performed, nothing came to the auditor’s attention indicating that these representations contained any material misstatement.
   - The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared, and paid during the year complied with Section 123 of the Act.
   - The interim dividend declared and paid during the year and up to the date of the auditor’s report complied with Section 123 of the Act.
   - The Board proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed amount complied with Section 123 of the Act.

3. Based on test checks, the Company and its Indian subsidiaries used accounting software with audit-trail or edit-log functionality for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. Except for the instances referred to in the report:

   - The audit-trail feature operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions recorded in the systems.
   - No instance of audit-trail tampering came to the auditor’s attention.
   - The audit trail had been preserved by the Parent Company and the relevant Indian subsidiaries in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

The financial statements of five subsidiaries that were not material to the Group’s consolidated financial statements had not been audited under the provisions of the Act as of the date of the auditor’s report. Accordingly, the auditor was unable to comment on the reporting requirement under Rule 11(g) for those five subsidiaries.

4. With respect to paragraphs 3(xxi) and 4 of the Companies (Auditor’s Report) Order, 2020 (CARO), based on available information and the auditors’ reports for the year ended March 31, 2026, no qualifications or adverse remarks had been reported for companies whose audits under Section 143 had been completed.
