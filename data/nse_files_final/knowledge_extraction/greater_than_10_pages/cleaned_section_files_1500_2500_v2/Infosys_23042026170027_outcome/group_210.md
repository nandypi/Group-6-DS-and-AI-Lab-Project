## Infosys Completes Acquisition of Leading Insurance Consulting Technology Company, Stratus

Infosys completed the acquisition of Stratus on April 23, 2026, following its announcement of the acquisition on March 25, 2026.

Stratus is a technology solutions provider for the property and casualty (P&C) insurance industry. Headquartered in the United States, it has more than 450 experts and a delivery footprint across the United States, Canada, and India.

Stratus is a Guidewire Software partner providing end-to-end Guidewire InsuranceSuite capabilities, including:

- PolicyCenter
- ClaimCenter
- BillingCenter
- Integrations
- Upgrades
- Cloud migrations
- Application managed services
- Data and analytics
- Core modernization

The acquisition combines Stratus’ Guidewire and P&C insurance consulting capabilities with Infosys’ global reach, Infosys Topaz AI offerings, and Infosys Cobalt cloud offerings. The combined capabilities are intended to support insurers with core modernization, cloud adoption, data-driven transformation, and customer-experience initiatives.

## About Stratus

Stratus Technologies provides transformation solutions for P&C insurers across the United States, Canada, and India. Its capabilities include Guidewire Cloud migrations, data and analytics, managed services, and talent innovation. Its delivery frameworks cover PolicyCenter, CloudReady, BillingCenter, ClaimCenter, and Guidewire Cloud.

## About Infosys

Infosys provides business consulting and technology services. More than 325,000 people work across 63 countries. The company has more than four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises.
