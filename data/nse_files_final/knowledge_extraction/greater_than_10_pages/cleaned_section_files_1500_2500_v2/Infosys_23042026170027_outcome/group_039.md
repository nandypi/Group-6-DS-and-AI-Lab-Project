## Under the 2019 plan:

The Board, on April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved performance-based grants of RSUs amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants to other KMPs under the 2015 Plan:

- 237,370 time-based ESOPs. These ESOPs vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price is the market price on the grant date.
- 82,400 time-based RSUs. These RSUs vest over four years.

## Under the 2019 plan:

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of 60,600 RSUs to other KMPs under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs vest over three years based on achievement of specified performance targets.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for total shareholder return (TSR)-based options.

The valuation inputs include the share price on the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility for the Company's publicly traded equity shares and comparative companies is based on historical market-price movements over a period equivalent to the expected term of the options. For TSR-based options, the correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the relevant indices as a whole or between entities in the peer group.

## 2.12 Income Taxes

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income, except for amounts relating to items recognized directly in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements, except for temporary differences arising from the initial recognition of goodwill or an asset or liability in a transaction that is not a business combination and affects neither accounting nor taxable profit or loss at the time of the transaction.

Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced to the extent that it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized in income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment.

A deferred tax asset is recognized to the extent that future taxable profit is probable and available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to set off the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

Interim income tax provision is based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions earned on the exercise of employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.

Income tax expense for the three months ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 includes reversals, net of provisions, of $94 million and $14 million, respectively. Income tax expense for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 includes reversals, net of provisions, of $93 million and provisions, net of reversals, of $16 million, respectively. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily relate to adjudication of disputed matters, filing of tax returns, and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the quarter and year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received orders under Sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment year 2013-14 and assessment years 2017-18 to 2021-22. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. As a result:

- Pre-tax interest income of $41 million was recognized.
- Income tax provisions aggregating $93 million were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by $9 million following resolution of the disputes.

During the three months ended March 31, 2025, the Company received orders under Section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment years 2016-17 and 2019-20. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. As a result:

- Pre-tax interest income of $38 million was recognized.
- Income tax provisions aggregating $21 million were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by $125 million following resolution of the disputes.

Deferred income tax for the three months and years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially relates to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

The Company's Advanced Pricing Arrangement (APA) with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) for US branch income tax expired in March 2021. The Company has applied for renewal of the APA. US taxable income is currently based on the Company's best estimate determined using the expected value method.

As at March 31, 2026, claims against the Group not acknowledged as debts from the Income Tax Authorities amounted to $207 million (₹1,964 crore). As at March 31, 2025, such claims amounted to $226 million (₹1,933 crore).

The claims against the Group primarily represent demands arising on completion of assessment proceedings under the Income Tax Act, 1961. They relate to issues including disallowance of expenditure on software treated as capital in nature and payments to associated enterprises treated as liable for withholding taxes. These matters are pending before various Income Tax Authorities. Management and its tax advisors expect that the Company's position will likely be upheld on ultimate resolution and that the matters will not have a material adverse effect on the Group's financial position and results of operations.
