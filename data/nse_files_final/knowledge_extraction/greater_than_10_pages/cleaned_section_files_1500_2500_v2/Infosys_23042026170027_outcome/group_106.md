## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include remeasurement of the net defined benefit liability or asset, equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income, changes in the fair value of investments, and changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes.

## Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in the derivative’s fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the cash flow hedge reserve is transferred to the Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecasted transaction occurs.

In the event of liquidation, equity shareholders are entitled to receive the remaining assets of the Company in proportion to their shareholding after distribution of all preferential amounts. No preferential amounts currently exist.

Options issued under the Company’s share option plans do not carry voting, dividend, or liquidation rights. Details of shares reserved for issue under the employee stock option plan are provided in the related note.

## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

At its meeting on September 11, 2025, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each for ₹18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval through postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting by postal ballot, with results declared on November 6, 2025. The buyback comprised 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800 per equity share.

The offer was made to eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who became equity shareholders by cancelling ADSs and withdrawing the underlying equity shares, as of the November 14, 2025 record date. The buyback was conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025.

The Company completed the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025. All 10,00,00,000 equity shares were bought back and extinguished, resulting in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs. The buyback was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Company created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore as of March 31, 2026, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, through an appropriation from the general reserve.

The Company’s capital management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. It may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares. As of March 31, 2026, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## 2.12.3 DIVIDEND

Final dividends on shares are recorded as a liability on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the Company’s Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid or distributed after deducting applicable taxes. Remittances of dividends outside India are governed by Indian foreign exchange law and are subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.
