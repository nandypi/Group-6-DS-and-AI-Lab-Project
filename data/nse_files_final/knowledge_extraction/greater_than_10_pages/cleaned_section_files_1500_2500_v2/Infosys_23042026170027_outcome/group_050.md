## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately. The Group considers highly liquid investments readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.

## Overview and Notes to the Interim Consolidated Financial Statements

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (“the Company” or “Infosys”) provides AI-first business consulting and technology services. Infosys has over four decades of experience managing the systems and workings of global enterprises and uses an AI-first value framework, domain expertise, and an AI-native partner ecosystem to support business transformation.

Infosys, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts, is referred to as the “Group.”

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary equity listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares, representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s interim consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on April 23, 2026.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim consolidated financial statements were prepared in compliance with IAS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

The statements use the historical cost convention and accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of defined benefit obligations less the fair value of plan assets.

Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard was initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard required a change in accounting policy.

The material accounting policy information used in preparing the audited interim consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are sourced and rounded to the nearest digits. Consequently, quarter figures may not always add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.
