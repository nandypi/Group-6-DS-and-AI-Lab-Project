# Infosys Limited Q2 FY26 Financial Results Press Conference Call

October 16, 2025

## Corporate Participants

- Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director
- Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer
- Rishi Basu, Associate Vice President and Global Head of Corporate Communications

## Opening Remarks

### Salil Parekh

Infosys had a strong performance in Q2 FY26:

- Revenue grew 2.2% sequentially and 2.9% year on year in constant-currency terms.
- Operating margin was 21%.
- Large deals totaled $3.1 billion, of which 67% was net new work.
- Infosys added 8,000 employees during the quarter.
- A $1.6 billion mega deal was announced after the quarter ended but before the results announcement.

Client interactions showed a strong focus on deploying AI across the enterprise for both growth and cost-efficiency programs. Infosys is continuing to scale its team of forward-deployed engineers.

Following the Q2 performance, Infosys revised its full-year revenue growth guidance to 2%-3% in constant-currency terms, compared with the previous range of 1%-3%. Full-year operating margin guidance remained unchanged at 20%-22%.

## Business Environment and Guidance

### Industry Performance

Management reported good performance in Manufacturing and Financial Services but continued to see constraints in Retail. Infosys has a good Retail pipeline and will assess how it develops in coming quarters.

The second half of the financial year is typically slower than the first half. Infosys maintained this seasonal pattern in its guidance. Management said the environment remained uncertain:

- The lower end of the guidance range assumes a worsening environment.
- The upper end assumes stability in the environment.

Despite the uncertainty, Q2 performance gave management greater confidence to raise the lower end of the guidance range from 1% to 2%.

## H-1B Visas and U.S. Delivery

Management said that employees requiring Infosys sponsorship for immigration represent a minority of the company's U.S. workforce. The majority of U.S.-based employees do not require Infosys immigration support.

Infosys has built U.S. centers and hubs focused on digital, innovation, technology, and AI. The company also has relationships with universities and a training facility in the United States.

Management said Infosys had reached out to each client to ensure that delivery continuity remained on track. The company did not provide specific figures for the number or percentage of employees deployed on H-1B visas.

## Versent Group Acquisition and M&A

The Versent Group acquisition had not contributed to the quarter because it was still undergoing regulatory approvals and related processes. Management expected the transaction to close in the coming months but did not provide an expected revenue contribution at that stage.

Jayesh Sanghrajka said Infosys had previously disclosed Versent's prior-year numbers in stock-exchange filings, from which an estimate could be made.

Infosys is evaluating additional acquisition opportunities and has a pipeline of potential transactions, although management did not provide details on timing, areas, or geographies.

## Enterprise AI

Infosys is scaling its AI activities and has a large team of forward-deployed engineers. Its enterprise AI projects cover:

- Growth initiatives in sales and marketing
- Cost-focused process optimization
- Customer service
- Code development

Management said a large number of clients consider Infosys their AI partner of choice. Infosys also has partnerships with several large technology companies and sees significant opportunity in enterprise AI, supported by its experience working within enterprise environments.
