## Salil Parekh

The announced mega deal is with the U.K. NHS. It involves a complete transformation program supported by multiple new technologies, including AI. Infosys is working with multiple partners to deliver the program.

Infosys's nearshore strategy is part of its geographic localization approach across Europe, Australia, and the U.S. The company hires local employees and builds nearshore centers in the U.S., Canada, Mexico, Latin America, Europe, and other locations. The nearshore model has scaled significantly and is expected to expand further.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Infosys does not separately disclose how many freshers are hired into higher-skill versus regular-skill roles. Each fresher receives training based on the required skills and the relevant project requirements.

## Rishi Basu

The next question is from Rukmini Rao of *Fortune India*.

## Rukmini Rao

Questions were raised about Board-level planning under uncertain conditions, additions to contingency plans, the disclosed U.S. Department of Justice investigation concerning H-1B visas, Infosys's internal investigation, and liability arrangements in partnerships with hyperscalers for AI technologies.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys's Board has experience evaluating global situations and uncertainties. The Board considers different scenarios and their broader implications rather than focusing only on quarterly business conditions.

The risk committee regularly reviews and evaluates these scenarios.

Legal questions concerning liabilities in new AI partnerships are not yet fully clarified. Infosys's approach is to accept responsibility for matters within its control. The allocation of responsibility beyond that is addressed through discussions with clients and partners.

Infosys had no comment on the investigation at that stage.

## Rishi Basu

The next question is from Sanjana of *The Hindu BusinessLine*.

## Sanjana B

Questions were raised about the types of inorganic opportunities Infosys is examining, the contribution of acquisitions to growth, public-sector opportunities across geographies, the recent buyback announcement, the determination of its size and price, and the outlook for the second half of FY26.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys has historically focused on the public sector and is increasing its focus across markets. The company has public-sector business in Australia and a smaller business in the U.S., and the U.K. NHS program expands its presence in the U.K. Infosys also has experience in India with the Income Tax Department, GSTN, and related public-sector work, which it can leverage globally.

Infosys's primary focus is organic growth with clients. Acquisitions are not the main driver of growth but are considered when they add capabilities in an industry, service line, skill area, or geography. Areas mentioned include energy and consulting, cybersecurity, and digital, AI, and cloud.

## Sanjana B

A follow-up question was asked about the buyback.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys's capital return policy guides its capital allocation decisions. The company has a policy of returning 85% of free cash flow over a five-year period through regular dividends and other forms of capital return.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Infosys is currently in the second year of the five-year capital return period. The company generated strong cash flow in the previous year, partly due to tax refunds, creating scope for additional capital returns to shareholders.

The announced buyback is for INR 18,000 crores at INR 1,800 per share. Execution was subject to shareholder approval, and the postal ballot had been issued.

## Ritu Singh

A question was raised about whether it remains viable to send employees to the U.S. on H-1B visas, whether Infosys would participate in the next cycle, and whether it would reduce the minority portion of its workforce currently using these visas.

## Salil Parekh

The majority of Infosys employees in the U.S. do not require Infosys immigration support. Future decisions will be guided by client requirements, business scaling, local hiring, and the company's localization strategy. Infosys has been working with clients to maintain service delivery and business continuity.

A follow-up question was raised about whether the cost of sending employees to the U.S. on H-1B visas remains viable.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys referred to its earlier comments on H-1B visas and did not provide additional details.

## Rukmini Rao

Questions were raised about whether the Egypt subsidiary supports expansion into North Africa and whether higher subcontracting costs were related to specialized AI skills.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

The Egypt subsidiary was established to meet the requirements of a specific project for a client based in Egypt. Infosys needed a local entity to send employees to the project location.

Subcontracting costs vary depending on demand, available talent, project requirements, and location. The subcontracted skills may include newer capabilities such as AI as well as regular skills required for specific projects.
