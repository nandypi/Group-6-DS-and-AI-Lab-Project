## Satish H. C.

Infosys described itself as an enterprise AI services and solutions provider. Eight industry analyst firms ranked Infosys as a global leader in 20 separate AI rankings during the previous 12 months. The company was delivering more than 2,500 generative AI and AI projects and more than 200 agentic AI projects for clients.

## The first one is making Infosys AI-first:

Infosys began its AI-first journey in 2023. About 90% of employees are AI aware and equipped to collaborate with and use AI tools responsibly in their daily work.

## The next tier is the AI builders

10% of Infosys’s top technology talent pool is engaged in innovative AI projects and solution building. AI masters and forward-deployed engineers work on complex industry challenges for clients.

## On the process front

Infosys is using AI to change how it works. AI code assistants are accelerating the development life cycle, and developers have produced more than 25 million lines of code using generative AI.

Infosys has deployed AI agents across internal operations. Its multi-agent invoice automation solution unlocked $50 million in incremental cash flow and improved free cash flow conversion.

AI has also been deployed to accelerate compliance processes. Certain activities have achieved gains of more than 20x, while overall end-to-end process productivity has improved by 40% to 50%.

## Now coming to our industry-leading AI offerings

Infosys has applied its AI capabilities to internal operations and delivers these capabilities to clients through Infosys Topaz, a suite of generative and agentic AI-powered services and solutions.

The company delivers value through two strategic frameworks:

- **services.ai:** Builds a foundation for business services by accelerating IT capabilities and operations for Infosys and its clients. Its integrated services stack combines contextualized AI services and agents with human agents to improve the velocity, productivity, and quality of IT services and operations.
- **client.ai:** Focuses on business transformation and enterprise-wide outcomes such as revenue growth, efficiency, and productivity improvements. Infosys has 22 industry blueprints and more than 400 agents tailored to specific industry verticals. It also uses “power vibing” to build proofs of value and iterate business-solution prototypes.

## In terms of delivering enterprise value to surmount pilot paralysis

Infosys identified the extraction of value from enterprise AI investments and pilots as a major priority for global enterprises. It described five delivery levers.

With Infosys forward-deployed engineers, specialized engineering talent is embedded within client businesses to deliver AI at enterprise scale. For a global logistics leader, forward-deployed engineers co-created a solution using real-time data streams and AI to predict shipment life cycles across regions and operating companies. The platform:

- Delivers 400 million messages daily with sub-minute latency.
- Operates continuously, 24/7.
- Generated $1.5 million in immediate benefits.
- Produces $8 million in annual savings.
- Reduced customer service call volumes by 12%.

The Infosys Topaz data workbench provides solutions for data preparation, engineering, and governance. For a leading industrial manufacturer, Infosys built a unified data fabric powered by more than 100 domain-driven, multimodal data products focused on equipment operations. The fabric covers more than 10 petabytes of structured and unstructured data and powers more than 30 AI companions across business functions, driving a reported improvement of more than 90% in precision, performance, and productivity.

Infosys provides small language models for context engineering in agentic AI solutions. It has built four small language models for banking, IT operations, cybersecurity, and enterprises generally. The models are also offered as services for businesses developing custom AI models. Clients using Infosys Finacle can use these models to create contextual banking experiences and innovations.

Through the Infosys Responsible AI Office, the company supports responsible AI implementation. Infosys was among the first companies certified to ISO 42001:2023 for management systems implementing responsible AI projects. Its Responsible AI toolkit provides technical guardrails and defenses to address AI-related risks.

Infosys’s Polydelivery AI model uses a hybrid and flexible approach intended to reduce dependence on individual AI providers and avoid vendor lock-in. Using this model, Infosys helped a European bank establish an AI innovation lab. The bank has deployed 13 AI and agentic AI solutions, with additional solutions in development.

Infosys has established strategic alliances with Nvidia, Microsoft, AWS, Intel, Meta, Google Cloud, and other companies. Infosys has deployed GitHub Copilot at scale, with more than 22,000 developers onboard. In collaboration with Google Cloud, it has developed more than 200 enterprise-grade AI agents. These partnerships and open-source solutions support flexible, vendor-agnostic AI ecosystems.

Through the Infosys Innovation Network, the company engages with AI start-ups in AI, cybersecurity, data management, and other emerging areas. Academic collaborations include institutions such as Cambridge, Columbia, Cornell, Stanford HAI, and MIT.

Infosys has partnered with OWASP on large language model security, advised policymakers, and collaborated with regulators on emerging AI standards.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys is partnering with companies building AI capabilities across multiple layers, including chips, infrastructure, models, and deployment.

Enterprise modernization is a significant part of Infosys’s business. AI tools are expected to improve the duration and return on investment of enterprise and legacy modernization projects. Once these tools become sufficiently mature and stable, Infosys sees modernization as a growth opportunity.

Infosys can support enterprise AI companies that focus on areas such as sales and marketing growth, revenue enhancement, process cost reduction, customer service, and modernization. Infosys contributes knowledge of clients’ large and complex technology landscapes and helps deploy AI into those environments.
