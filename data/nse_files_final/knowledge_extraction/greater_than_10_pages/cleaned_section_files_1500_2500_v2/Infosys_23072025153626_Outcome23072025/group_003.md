## Auditor's Responsibilities for audit of the Consolidated Financial Results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025

The auditors' objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance that the consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025, as a whole were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor's report containing their opinion. Misstatements may arise from fraud or error and are considered material if they could reasonably be expected to influence users' economic decisions based on the consolidated financial results.

As part of an audit conducted in accordance with the applicable Standards on Auditing, the auditors:

- Identified and assessed the risks of material misstatement due to fraud or error, designed responsive audit procedures, and obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence.
- Obtained an understanding of internal control relevant to the audit to design appropriate audit procedures, without expressing an opinion on the effectiveness of those controls.
- Evaluated the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by the Board of Directors.
- Concluded on the appropriateness of the Board of Directors' use of the going-concern basis of accounting and assessed whether material uncertainty existed regarding the Group's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluated the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures in the consolidated financial results.
- Performed procedures in accordance with the SEBI circular under Regulation 33(8) of the LODR Regulations, to the extent applicable.
- Obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group and assumed responsibility for the direction, supervision, and performance of the audit work relating to those entities.

The auditors considered quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating audit results, and assessing identified misstatements. They communicated with those charged with governance regarding significant deficiencies in internal control identified during the audit, relationships and other matters relevant to auditor independence, and applicable safeguards.
