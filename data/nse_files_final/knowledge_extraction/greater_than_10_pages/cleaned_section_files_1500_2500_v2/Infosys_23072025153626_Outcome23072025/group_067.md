## Trade receivables and Contract Balances

The timing of revenue recognition, billings, and cash collections results in trade receivables, unbilled revenue, and unearned revenue on the Company's Balance Sheet. Amounts are billed as work progresses under agreed contractual terms, either periodically, such as monthly or quarterly, or upon achievement of contractual milestones.

Trade receivables are rights to consideration that are unconditional. Unbilled revenues from time-and-material contracts and fixed-price maintenance contracts are classified as financial assets when the right to consideration is unconditional and payment is due only after the passage of time.

For other fixed-price contracts, invoicing is based on contractual milestones. Because revenue recognition may occur at a different time from invoicing, unbilled revenues are classified as nonfinancial contract assets when the right to consideration depends on completion of contractual milestones.

Invoicing in excess of earnings is classified as unearned revenue. Trade receivables and unbilled revenues are presented net of impairment in the Balance Sheet.

## 2.18 OTHER INCOME, NET

### 2.18.1 Other income

#### Accounting Policy

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts, and exchange gains or losses on the translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities.

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

### 2.18.2 Foreign currency

#### Functional currency

The functional currency of the Company is the Indian rupee. These financial statements are presented in Indian rupees, rounded to crore. One crore equals ten million.

#### Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the Balance Sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the condensed standalone Statement of Profit and Loss within exchange gains/(losses) on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in Other Comprehensive Income as qualifying cash flow hedges.

Non-monetary assets and liabilities denominated in a foreign currency and measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate prevailing when fair value was determined. Non-monetary assets and liabilities measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate prevailing on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized on settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Revenue, expense, and cash-flow items denominated in foreign currencies are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate in effect on the transaction date.

Other Comprehensive Income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

#### Government grant

Government grants are recognized only when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss on a systematic and rational basis over the asset's useful life. Grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in net profit over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.

## 2.19 EXPENSES

### 2.19.1 Gratuity and Pension

The Company provides gratuity through a defined benefit retirement plan covering eligible Indian employees of Infosys. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination of employment, based on salary and tenure.

The Company contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Trustees administer contributions to the Trust, which are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

The Company operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Liabilities for these plans are determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Company to actuarial risks, including longevity risk, interest-rate risk, and market risk.

The net obligation of a defined benefit plan is recognized in the Balance Sheet as an asset or liability. Re-measurement gains and losses on the net defined benefit liability/(asset) are recognized in Other Comprehensive Income and are not reclassified to profit or loss in subsequent periods. The portion of actual returns on plan assets exceeding the yields calculated using the discount rate applied to the defined benefit obligation is recognized in Other Comprehensive Income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss.

### 2.19.2 Provident fund

Eligible employees receive benefits from a provident fund, which is a defined benefit plan. Employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary.

A portion of the Company's contribution is made to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the Trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the Trust's investment return and the notified interest rate.

### 2.19.3 Superannuation

Certain employees participate in a defined contribution superannuation plan. The Company has no obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

### 2.19.4 Compensated absences

The Company's compensated-absences policy covers accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed because of unused entitlement accumulated at the Balance Sheet date.

Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
