## Under the 2015 plan

The Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following fiscal 2026 grants on April 17, 2025. The grants were effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of performance targets in line with the employment agreement.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. The RSUs will vest based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance, as determined by the Board.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as at June 30, 2025. Because the service commencement date precedes the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*. The grant date for this purpose was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 plan

The Board, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved a ₹10 crore performance-based RSU grant for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan on April 17, 2025. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

Model inputs include the share price on the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility is based on historical movements in the market prices of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares and, for comparative companies, their publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
