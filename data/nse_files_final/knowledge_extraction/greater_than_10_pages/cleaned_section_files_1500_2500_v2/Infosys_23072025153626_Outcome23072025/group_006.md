### Acquisitions

Infosys acquired a services company headquartered in the USA for consideration including earnouts amounting to up to $36 million (₹306 crore).

Infosys acquired The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd. (collectively known as “The Missing Link”), a cybersecurity service provider headquartered in Australia, for consideration including earnouts amounting to up to AUD 93 million (₹505 crore).

### U.S. Department of Justice Investigation

The U.S. Department of Justice (“DoJ”) is conducting an investigation regarding how Infosys classified certain H-1B visa-recipient employees working for one of its clients in immigration documents filed with certain U.S. government authorities. Infosys is engaged in discussions with the DoJ and has commenced its own inquiry. At this stage, Infosys is unable to predict the outcome, including whether it could have a material adverse effect on the company’s business and results of operations.
