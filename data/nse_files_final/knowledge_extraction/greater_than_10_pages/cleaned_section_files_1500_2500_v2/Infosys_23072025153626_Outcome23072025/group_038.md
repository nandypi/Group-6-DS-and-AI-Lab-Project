## 2.3.3 Derecognition of financial instruments

The Group derecognizes a financial asset when the contractual rights to its cash flows expire or when it transfers the financial asset and the transfer qualifies for derecognition under IFRS 9.

A financial liability, or part of a financial liability, is derecognized from the Group's Balance Sheet when the contractual obligation is discharged, cancelled, or expires.

## 2.3.4 Fair value of financial instruments

The Group determines the fair value of financial instruments using methods and assumptions based on market conditions and risks existing at each reporting date. Methods include discounted cash flow analysis, quoted market prices, option pricing models, market multiples, and dealer quotes.

Fair-value measurements are generally approximations and may not reflect amounts ultimately realized. For financial assets and liabilities maturing within one year from the Balance Sheet date and not carried at fair value, carrying amounts approximate fair value because of their short maturity.

## 2.3.5 Impairment

The Group recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenue that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

- For trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component, loss allowances equal lifetime ECL.
- For all other financial assets, ECL equals 12-month ECL unless credit risk has increased significantly since initial recognition, in which case lifetime ECL is recognized.

The Group determines credit-loss allowances using historical loss experience adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions, including conditions affecting the industries it serves and the countries in which it operates.

The ECL, or reversal, required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.
