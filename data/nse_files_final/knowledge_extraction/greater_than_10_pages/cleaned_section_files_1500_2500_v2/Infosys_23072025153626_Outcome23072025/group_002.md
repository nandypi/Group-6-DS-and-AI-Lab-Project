## Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited Infosys Limited’s consolidated financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2025, including the Company and its subsidiaries.

The auditor stated that the consolidated financial results:

- Include the financial results of the subsidiaries listed in the report’s annexure.
- Are presented in accordance with Regulation 33 of the Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015, as amended.
- Give a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated net profit, consolidated other comprehensive income, and other financial information for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
- Conform to the recognition and measurement principles of Indian Accounting Standard 34, “Interim Financial Reporting,” prescribed under section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, together with relevant rules and generally accepted accounting principles in India.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP stated that it was independent of the Group under the Code of Ethics issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and had fulfilled its other ethical responsibilities. The auditor considered the audit evidence obtained to be sufficient and appropriate as a basis for its opinion.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for the Statement

The consolidated financial results were the responsibility of Infosys Limited’s Board of Directors and were approved by them for issuance. The results were compiled from the audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

The respective boards of the companies in the Group were responsible for:

- Preparing and presenting the consolidated financial results in accordance with Ind AS 34, applicable rules, generally accepted accounting principles in India, and Regulation 33 of the Listing Regulations.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records, safeguarding Group assets, and preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies and making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining effective internal financial controls supporting accurate and complete accounting records and financial reporting free from material misstatement.
- Assessing each entity’s ability to continue as a going concern, making related disclosures where applicable, and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless liquidation or cessation of operations was intended or unavoidable.
- Overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.
