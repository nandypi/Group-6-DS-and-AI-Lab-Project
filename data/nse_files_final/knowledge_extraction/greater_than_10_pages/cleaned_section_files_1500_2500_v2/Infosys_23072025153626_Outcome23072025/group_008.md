The audited interim condensed standalone financial statements for the quarter ended June 30, 2025, were taken on record by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on July 23, 2025. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP expressed an unmodified audit opinion. The statements were prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and subsequent relevant amendment rules.

The U.S. Department of Justice is investigating how Infosys classified certain H-1B visa-recipient employees working for one of its clients in immigration documents filed with certain U.S. government authorities. Infosys is discussing the matter with the DOJ and has commenced its own inquiry. At this stage, Infosys is unable to predict the outcome, including whether it could have a material adverse effect on its business and results of operations.

## 2.InformationondividendsforthequarterendedJune30,2025

For financial year 2025, the Board recommended a final dividend of ₹22 per equity share of ₹5 par value. The dividend was approved by shareholders at the Annual General Meeting held on June 25, 2025, and paid on June 30, 2025.

## 3.SegmentReporting

Infosys publishes standalone financial statements along with consolidated financial statements. In accordance with Ind AS 108, Operating Segments, segment information is disclosed in the audited interim consolidated financial statements. Accordingly, segment information is provided in the audited consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

## Press Release

For the quarter ended June 30, 2025, Infosys reported:

- Revenue of $4,941 million, representing year-on-year growth of 3.8% and sequential growth of 2.6% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20.8%.
- Free cash flow of $884 million, equal to 109.3% of net profit.
- Large deal total contract value of $3.8 billion, with 55% net new.
- Return on equity of 30.4%, an improvement of 140 basis points.

### Guidance for FY26:

- Revenue growth of 1%–3% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%–22%.

### Key highlights:

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 3.8% year over year and 2.6% sequentially.
- Reported revenues were ₹42,279 crore, representing year-on-year growth of 7.5%.
- Operating margin was 20.8%, declining 0.3% year over year and 0.2% sequentially.
- Basic EPS was ₹16.70, increasing 8.6% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹7,533 crore, declining 17.7% year over year; free cash flow conversion was 108.8% of net profit.
