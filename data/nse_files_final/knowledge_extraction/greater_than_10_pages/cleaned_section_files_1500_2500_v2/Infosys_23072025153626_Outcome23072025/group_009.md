## Client wins &amp; Testimonials

1. Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Select Portfolio Servicing, Inc. (SPS), covering hybrid cloud solutions, application portfolio, IT operations, IaaS, SaaS, security operations, and quality assurance through a fully managed services offering. SPS will leverage Infosys Topaz for AI adoption across business, technology, and enterprise functions.

2. Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with AIB to accelerate digital transformation initiatives, including modernization of technology and data capabilities.

3. Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with E.ON to enable AI-powered digital workplace transformation across Europe.

4. Infosys expanded its strategic collaboration with DNB Bank ASA to accelerate digital transformation, modernize IT infrastructure, and leverage AI and machine learning.

5. Infosys announced a strategic collaboration with Yorkshire Building Society to accelerate digital transformation, including investments in faster payments and enhanced security, in support of its 2030 ambitions.

6. Infosys and Spark New Zealand announced a strategic agreement to transform Spark's technology delivery model through digital innovation. The collaboration builds on a relationship of more than 16 years and includes access to Infosys' global scale, AI, and innovation investments.

7. Infosys collaborated with Perfection Fresh Australia to implement Microsoft Sustainability Manager. The rollout expanded from 4 sites to all 17 locations, providing real-time visibility of produce across locations, improving operational efficiency and audit transparency, and reducing wastage.

8. Infosys Finacle announced a strategic collaboration with Bank of Sydney to support digital transformation using the Infosys Finacle Digital Banking Suite.

9. Infosys BPM launched AI agents for invoice processing within its Infosys Accounts Payable on Cloud solution. Americana Restaurants reported faster invoice processing, improved accuracy and efficiency, and reduced manual dependencies through the AI-powered solution.

10. Infosys announced a three-year strategic collaboration with the Lawn Tennis Association (LTA) to deliver AI-powered innovations, including match insights and immersive fan experiences for the HSBC Championships.

11. Infosys and Economist Impact announced the launch of The Sustainability Atlas, which uses Infosys' generative AI capabilities to bring together sustainability data and insights for policymakers and business leaders.
