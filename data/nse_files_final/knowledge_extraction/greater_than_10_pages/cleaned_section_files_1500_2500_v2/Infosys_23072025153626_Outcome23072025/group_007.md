## Notes on segment information

### Business segments

Based on the “management approach” required by Ind-AS 108, Operating Segments, the Chief Operating Decision Maker evaluates the Group’s performance and allocates resources based on an analysis of performance indicators by business segment. Information is presented by these business segments. The accounting principles used to prepare the financial statements are consistently applied to record revenue and expenditure in individual segments.

### Segmental capital employed

Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not identified with individual reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Management believes that providing segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities is currently not practicable because meaningful segregation of the available data would be onerous.
