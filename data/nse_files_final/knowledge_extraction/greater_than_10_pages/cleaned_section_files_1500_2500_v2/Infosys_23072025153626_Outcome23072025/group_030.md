# 2.19 Break-up of expenses and other income, net

## 2.19.1 Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a defined benefit retirement plan, the Gratuity Plan, for eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. Trustees administer the contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group also operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Required contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies according to employee age.

Liabilities under the defined benefit plans are determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net defined benefit obligation as an asset or liability in the Balance Sheet. Re-measurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profits in the interim condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.

## 2.19.2 Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution plan. The Group has no further obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## 2.19.3 Provident fund

Eligible Infosys employees receive benefits from a provident fund classified as a defined benefit plan. Employees and the company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. A portion is contributed to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust is administered by the Government of India. The company must make good any shortfall between the return on the trust’s investments and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries participate in defined contribution provident fund plans. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no further obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## 2.19.4 Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy covers both accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed for unused entitlement accumulated at that date.

Expenses for non-accumulating compensated absences are recognized in the period in which the absences occur.

## 2.19.5 Other income, net

Other income primarily comprises:

- Interest income
- Dividend income
- Gains or losses on investments
- Exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts
- Exchange gains or losses on translation of foreign currency assets and liabilities

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## 2.19.6 Foreign Currency

### Functional currency and presentation currency

The functional currency of Infosys, its Indian subsidiaries, and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. Foreign subsidiaries use their respective local currencies as their functional currencies.

The financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars, rounded to the nearest million, to facilitate comparison with similar companies domiciled in other geographic locations.

### Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the Balance Sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income and reported within exchange gains or losses on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except amounts deferred in other comprehensive income as qualifying cash flow hedges.

Non-monetary foreign-currency assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate on the date fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized on settlement are included in net profit for the period in which settlement occurs. Foreign-currency-denominated revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate on the transaction date.

For translating foreign subsidiaries’ financial statements into the presentation currency, assets and liabilities are translated at the Balance Sheet date exchange rate, while revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated using the average exchange rate for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves within other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is disposed of in full, the relevant currency translation reserve is transferred to net profit in the Statement of Comprehensive Income. Changes in the parent’s ownership that do not result in loss of control are recorded through equity.

Other comprehensive income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair value adjustments arising from the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated at the exchange rate in effect at the Balance Sheet date.

## 2.19.7 Government grants

The Group recognizes government grants when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the statement of comprehensive income systematically over the useful life of the asset. Grants related to revenue are recognized systematically over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.

## 2.19.8 Operating Profits

Operating profit is computed using revenue, net of cost of sales, selling and marketing expenses, and administrative expenses.
