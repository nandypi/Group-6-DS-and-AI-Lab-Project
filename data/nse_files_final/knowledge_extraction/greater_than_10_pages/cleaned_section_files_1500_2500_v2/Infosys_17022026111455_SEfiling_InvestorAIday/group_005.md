## Agentic Legacy Modernization and Process AI at Posti

### Business context

Posti’s legacy IT estate was associated with:

- High run costs
- Operational risk
- Slow change cycles

Posti identified a critical need to execute a strategic pivot toward end-to-end logistics and e-commerce services.

### AI First Solutions

Infosys described an AI-first operating model across Run and Transform, including:

- Run-to-Grow transformation, reallocating spend from maintenance to growth capabilities
- An AI orchestrator layer leveraging GitHub, AWS Bedrock, LangChain, and Copilot

Posti SVP and CIO Petteri Naulapaa described the partnership with Infosys as supporting Posti’s evolution toward leadership in logistics and e-commerce and becoming a digital frontrunner in the Nordics.

**50%+** of software code developed by agents

**35%** improvement in productivity

**70%** improvement in mean time to recovery
