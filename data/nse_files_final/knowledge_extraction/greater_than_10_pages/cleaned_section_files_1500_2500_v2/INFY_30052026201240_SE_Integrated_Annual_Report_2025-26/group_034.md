## VIII. Other details

## 1.  Quality

The Quality function at Infosys, aligned with the organization’s vision and strategy of “Navigate the Next,” has three strategic imperatives:

- Differentiate Infosys’ services through superior performance and quality.
- Optimize client projects and internal functions for greater efficiency and agility.
- De-risk operations through delivery excellence, compliance, and sustainability.

To standardize AI adoption across services while accounting for different client maturity levels, the Quality team has defined an adaptable AI-first software engineering framework covering AI-augmented and agentic AI engineering.

AI-augmented engineering integrates AI-infused processes with Infosys and third-party AI platforms, tools, and accelerators. For early AI adopters, agentic AI supports redesigned ways of working through configurable blueprints, pre-built agents orchestrated with humans in the loop, agent governance, and responsible-AI guardrails.

Infosys continues to develop ways of working for an AI-first environment, including Agile, DevSecOps, Site Reliability Engineering, and product-led, platform-driven operating models. Quality consulting helps clients adopt AI-infused ways of working and cultures, implement AI engineering practices, and establish AI governance.

Quality has deployed frameworks, process levers, tools, and platforms across service lines to improve productivity and quality. Its automation maturity model helps projects progress from point tools toward cognitive and autonomous operations using AI and generative AI.

Through structured audits and assessment frameworks, Quality focuses on de-risking operations and has expanded coverage across a broader range of services, centers, and subsidiaries.

Infosys complies with international management system standards and models, including:

- ISO 9001
- ISO 27001
- ISO 14001
- ISO 45001
- ISO 50001
- ISO 22301
- ISO 20000
- ISO 27701
- AS 9100
- ISO 13485

Infosys has onboarded AI practices from CMMI.AI and was among the first organizations to complete a pilot assessment by the CMMI Institute. Infosys also achieved reasonable assurance under ISO 14068-1, “Climate change management — Transition to net zero — Part 1: Carbon neutrality.” Infosys China and Infosys Brazil received certification under ISO 27701 for Privacy Information Management Systems.

## 2.  Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (ICETS)

ICETS supports enterprises with emerging technologies through a structured, outcome-driven approach focused on scalable execution, intelligence, autonomy, ethics, and governance.

## A structured innovation journey: Next, Near, and Now

The ICETS innovation framework balances long-term research and vision with near-term experimentation and immediate business impact.

## Next - Applied Research Centers (ARC)

Applied Research Centers research, create, and apply emerging technologies, including advanced AI, cybersecurity, robotics, autonomous machines, quantum computing, and next-generation interaction models. Their work combines medium- and long-term research with near-term applications for clients and focuses on building in-house expertise and intellectual property.

## Advanced AI

Advanced AI research is increasingly focused on how AI systems reason, act, and operate autonomously in enterprise environments. AI agents are being embedded into workflows to:

- Plan and execute multi-step processes.
- Collaborate with humans and other agents.
- Adapt dynamically to changing contexts.

This creates challenges related to coordination, governance, and cost predictability. ICETS Applied Research Centers are addressing these challenges through:

- AI agent orchestration frameworks.
- Automated model lifecycle transitions from large language models (LLMs) to small language models (SLMs).
- Agent certification and governance models.
- Runtime observability, auditability, and control.

## New interaction model

Generative AI is enabling expressive avatars and digital humans for more natural interactions in customer experience, education, and accessibility. Relevant capabilities include:

- Realistic speech, lip-sync, and facial expressions.
- Emotion-aware, personalized, and context-driven engagement.

## Cybersecurity

ICETS cybersecurity work includes:

- AI-driven threat detection and response.
- Privacy-preserving techniques, including federated learning and secure inference.
- Multimodal threat analytics and real-time security operations.

## Autonomous Machines

Autonomous-machine capabilities include:

- Intelligent systems operating in dynamic, real-world environments.
- Sensor fusion, simulation, and edge intelligence.
- Scalable deployment across logistics, mobility, and infrastructure.

## Humanoids

Humanoid research includes:

- Human-like robots with advanced perception and dexterity.
- Human-machine collaboration in complex tasks.
- Safety, productivity, and real-world adaptability.

## Quantum

Quantum-related capabilities include:

- Hybrid quantum-classical computing approaches.
- Optimization and scientific-computing use cases.
- Post-quantum cryptography and enterprise readiness.

## Near - Living Labs

Living Labs support co-creation, rapid prototyping, and real-world validation. They enable clients to contextualize, experiment with, iterate on, and de-risk innovations in controlled environments.

## Infosys Living Labs

Infosys Living Labs are present across all major geographies and have hosted hundreds of client visits. They showcase capabilities across:

- Infosys Topaz, Infosys Cobalt, and Infosys Aster.
- Brand partnerships, hyperscaler and other alliances, and the Infosys Innovation Network of startups.
- Academic collaborations and CSR initiatives.
- Service lines and business verticals.
- Responsible AI, accessibility, cybersecurity, Future of Retail, payments, banking, and manufacturing.

The Living Labs also provide environments for Infosys innovation teams to work with individual clients on specific challenges and opportunities.

## Client Living Labs

Client Living Labs are innovation-as-a-service engagements driven by client needs and experience. They address client- and industry-specific challenges using emerging technologies, design, innovation-management expertise, and rapid experimentation.

The Client Living Labs network includes 40 active engagements centered on:

- **IncubateNext:** Rapid experimentation through internal experts and external partnerships, applying emerging technologies and design to real-world use cases across domains and geographies.
- **ImagineNext:** Exploration of future possibilities through trend sensing and innovation insights, including identification of new business opportunities.
- **SenseNext:** Identification of early market and technology signals and translation of those insights into actionable strategies.

## Now - Platforms and Ecosystems: Infosys Topaz Fabric

Infosys Topaz Fabric provides reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities to operationalize AI at scale. Its composable and interoperable stack includes AI agents, tools, services, and models.

The platform helps organizations optimize existing IT processes, eliminate or automate manual tasks, and build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach. It is intended to accelerate value realization across the enterprise.

## Consulting

ICETS consulting combines strategic intelligence, AI-powered insights, and structured frameworks. It supports advisory needs from research and product discovery to large-deal solutions, value management, and process re-engineering, helping enterprises design roadmaps, prioritize initiatives, and realize measurable business outcomes.

## Engineering

ICETS engineering covers greenfield engineering, application modernization, platform and cloud migration, data and quality engineering, and process re-engineering. It combines modern architectures, intelligent automation, and AI-driven execution across build, migrate, and run phases.

## Operations

ICETS operations supports IT, business, and network environments through AI-driven monitoring, automation, and real-time intelligence. Areas include IT Operations, Business Operations, Integrated BizTech Operations, Network Operations, and Cybersecurity Security Operations Centers. Self-healing capabilities and predictive insights are used to improve service reliability and issue resolution.

## Driving measurable business impact

ICETS focuses on accelerating enterprise-wide AI adoption, enabling faster experimentation and deployment, strengthening trusted governance, improving cost efficiency and operational predictability, and building future-ready capabilities across industries.

## Looking ahead

ICETS remains focused on research, experimentation, tools, platforms, and expertise to support enterprises and Infosys in applying emerging technologies to real-world use cases. Its stated focus includes execution excellence, ecosystem collaboration, scalability, governance, and responsible innovation.
