## Risk management report

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Contractual liabilities | Risk of clients demanding more favorable terms, including onerous liability clauses, and the company’s inability to adhere to contractual obligations may lead to litigation, fines, and adverse impacts on reputation, revenue, and profitability. Impacted capitals: Social & Relationship and Financial. | Client engagement on contractual terms through a dedicated in-house team; a contract legal playbook and risk framework to identify high-risk clauses and plan controls; a multi-layered contract approval process; dedicated teams to adhere to, monitor, and audit contractual obligations; and Board-level monitoring, reporting, and governance of contractual risks. |
| Complex and evolving regulatory environment | Failure to comply with the complex regulatory landscape, including immigration, wages, tax, and sanctions, could result in investigations, regulatory inquiries, litigation, fines, and negative client sentiment. Evolving regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and public disclosure requirements add uncertainty to compliance policies. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, Social & Relationship, and Natural. | A dedicated in-house compliance team working with external partners; a compliance framework, policies, controls, and programs to identify regulatory changes, assess their impact, and implement actions; regular engagement with regulatory authorities and compliance experts; contract awareness programs and training for employees and vendors; periodic compliance certification with defined accountability; and monitoring, reporting, governance, and Board oversight. |

## Cybersecurity risk management

Cyber risks are managed through multi-layered controls using a defence-in-depth approach, covering cybersecurity strategy and preventive, detective, and corrective policies, processes, and controls.

The cybersecurity strategy focuses on four areas:

- Transparency and experience
- Continual improvement and compliance
- Cyber resilience
- Building and maintaining a positive cybersecurity culture within the organization

The enterprise Information Security Council (ISC), a high-level working group, governs and oversees the Information Security Management System (ISMS) at Infosys. The ISC establishes, directs, monitors, and executes the information security program, with representation from various departments and business units. It reports key risks to executive leadership through the Operational Risk Council.

## INDEPENDENT PRACTITIONER'S REASONABLE ASSURANCE REPORT ON IDENTIFIED SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION IN INFOSYS LIMITED'S BUSINESS RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

## To the Board of Directors of INFOSYS LIMITED

1. We undertook a reasonable assurance engagement for INFOSYS LIMITED (the “Company”) under an engagement letter dated January 16, 2026, covering the identified sustainability information, or BRSR Core indicators, specified in the Appendix I to the report.

   The sustainability information is included in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (the “BRSR” or the “Report”), which forms part of the Integrated Annual Report (the “IAR”) for the year ended March 31, 2026. The engagement was conducted by a multidisciplinary team including assurance practitioners, environmental engineers, and specialists.

## 2. Identified Sustainability Information

The reasonable assurance scope consists of the BRSR Core indicators listed in Appendix I to the report. The BRSR reporting boundary is disclosed in Question 13 of Section A: General Disclosure of the BRSR, including applicable exceptions disclosed in notes under the respective questions.

The engagement covered information for the year ended March 31, 2026 only, unless otherwise stated. No procedures were performed on other elements of the BRSR, and no opinion is expressed on those elements.

## 3. Criteria

The criteria used by the Company to prepare the identified sustainability information were:

- Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
- Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting Requirements for listed entities under Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 (the “SEBI Master Circular”).
