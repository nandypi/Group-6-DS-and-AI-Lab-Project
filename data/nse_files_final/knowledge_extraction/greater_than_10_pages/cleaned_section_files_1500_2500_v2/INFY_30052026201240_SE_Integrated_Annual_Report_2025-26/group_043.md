## Fairness and excellence

The Board works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee to establish evaluation criteria for the Chairman, the Board, Board committees, and executive, non-executive, and independent directors through peer evaluation, excluding the director being evaluated.

Independent directors are evaluated on:

- Contribution to and monitoring of corporate governance practices.
- Contribution of international best practices to address business challenges and risks.
- Active participation in long-term strategic planning.
- Commitment to directors’ obligations and fiduciary responsibilities, including participation in Board and committee meetings.
- Participation in a formal annual Board review to improve the effectiveness of the Board, its committees, and individual directors.

## Board member evaluation

The Board engaged Egon Zehnder, a leadership advisory firm, to conduct the Board evaluation for fiscal 2026. The evaluation focused on Board dynamics, softer aspects, committee effectiveness, and information flow to the Board or its committees. The methodology included questionnaires and one-on-one discussions. Succession planning and committee composition were also considered.

The Board evaluation process was completed during fiscal 2026. Recommendations from the previous year's evaluation were discussed with the Board, individual feedback was provided, and progress on prior recommendations and current-year recommendations was discussed.

The evaluation process was based on affirmations from the independent directors that they met the independence criteria under the Companies Act, the Listing Regulations, and the NYSE listing manual.

## Board and executive leadership compensation

### Executive leadership compensation

Executive compensation programs reward performance, with a significant portion of executives’ total rewards tied to long-term corporate performance goals to align with shareholders’ interests.

As required under the Listing Regulations, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommends to the Board the remuneration payable to directors, key managerial personnel (KMP), and senior management. The Company’s Nomination and Remuneration Policy is available on its website.

### Non-executive and non-independent chairman's compensation

Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman, voluntarily chose not to receive remuneration for services rendered to the Company.

### Independent directors' compensation

Compensation payable to independent directors is limited to a fixed amount per year determined and approved by the Board. The aggregate amount does not exceed 1% of net profit for the year, calculated under the Companies Act, 2013. The Board reviews independent directors’ performance annually.

The Board considers global Board compensation benchmarks, individual participation in Board and committee meetings, committee responsibilities, time spent on other duties, and roles prescribed under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, the Listing Regulations, and other relevant factors.

Shareholders at the 34th AGM held on June 22, 2015, approved an amount not exceeding 1% of the Company’s annual net profit, calculated under Section 198 of the Companies Act, 2013, to be paid and distributed among some or all non-executive directors as determined by the Board.

## Corporate governance report

## Indemnification agreements

The Company entered into agreements to indemnify its directors and officers for claims brought against them to the fullest extent permitted under applicable law. The agreements cover certain expenses, judgments, fines, settlement amounts, and public relations consultation expenses incurred in actions or proceedings arising from their services as directors or officers.

## Agreements binding listed entities

Pursuant to Regulation 30A of the Listing Regulations, no agreement was entered into or executed during fiscal 2026 by the shareholders, promoters, promoter group entities, related parties, directors, key managerial personnel, or employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.
