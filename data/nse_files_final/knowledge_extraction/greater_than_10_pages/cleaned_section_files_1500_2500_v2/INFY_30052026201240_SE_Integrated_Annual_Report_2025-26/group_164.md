## Background and context for item no. 6

Regulation 31A of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, provides a mechanism for reclassification of members from the “Promoters and Promoter Group” category to the “Public” category, subject to specified conditions.

The Company received a joint request letter dated April 30, 2026, from Mr. Shreyas Shibulal and Ms. Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal (collectively, the “Applicants”) seeking reclassification from the “Promoter and Promoter Group” category to the “Public” category under Regulation 31A of the LODR Regulations.

The Applicants’ shareholdings as of March 31, 2026, were:

| Sl. No. | Name of outgoing promoter/promoter group member(s) | No. of shares held | % of holding |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Shreyas Shibulal | 1,79,37,000 | 0.44 |
| 2 | Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal | 48,85,500 | 0.12 |
| Total |  | 2,28,22,500 | 0.56 |

The Applicants stated that their classification as “Promoter and Promoter Group” was solely due to their relationship with Mr. S. D. Shibulal: Mr. Shreyas Shibulal is his son and Ms. Bhairavi Madhusudhan Shibulal is his daughter-in-law. Mr. S. D. Shibulal was a co-founder and promoter of the Company but has ceased to be involved with the Company since 2014.

The Applicants stated that neither had worked with the Company or been involved in its business, management, or decision-making. They had never held a key managerial personnel or director position, had no right to appoint a director, and had no ability to control the Company’s management or policy decisions. They also confirmed that they received information about the Company only as ordinary public shareholders.

The Applicants undertook that, together with persons related to them, they:

- Do not hold more than 10% of the Company’s total voting rights.
- Do not exercise direct or indirect control over the Company’s affairs.
- Do not have special rights in relation to the Company through formal or informal arrangements, including shareholder agreements.
- Are not represented on the Board, including through nominee directorship.
- Do not act as key managerial personnel of the Company.
- Are not wilful defaulters under Reserve Bank of India guidelines.
- Are not fugitive economic offenders.

The Applicants confirmed that there was no pending regulatory action against them. They further confirmed that they would:

- Continue to comply, from the date of reclassification, with the conditions relating to voting rights, control, and special rights under Regulation 31A(3) of the LODR Regulations.
- Comply with the conditions relating to Board representation and key managerial personnel for at least three years from the date of reclassification.

At its meeting held on April 30, 2026, the Board noted that:

- The Applicants were engaged in their respective independent professions or businesses and were not connected, directly or indirectly, with the Company’s operations or decision-making.
- The Applicants did not exercise influence over the Company’s decisions.
- The Applicants had no representation on the Board and were not acting as key managerial personnel.
- The Applicants held less than 1% of the Company’s paid-up equity share capital, and the Applicants together with persons related to them held less than 10%.

The Board approved the Applicants’ reclassification from the “Promoter and Promoter Group” category to the “Public” category, subject to stock-exchange approval, shareholder approval, and compliance with other applicable requirements.

The Company filed applications with BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited on May 1, 2026. It received no-objection letters dated May 11, 2026, from:

- BSE: Letter no. LIST/COMP/HG/059/2026-27
- NSE: Letter no. NSE/LIST/COMP/INFY/587/2026-2027

## Further, the Board confirms the following:

- The Company complies with the minimum public shareholding requirement under Regulation 38 of the LODR Regulations.
- Trading in the Company’s equity shares was not suspended by any stock exchange on which the Company is listed.
- The Company had no outstanding dues to SEBI, the stock exchanges, or the depositories.

The Board recommended that the proposed resolution under Item no. 6 be passed as an ordinary resolution. Under Regulation 31A(3)(a)(vi) of the LODR Regulations, the Applicants and persons related to them, as defined under Regulation 31A, would not be permitted to vote on the resolution.

No directors, key managerial personnel, or their relatives were interested or concerned in the resolution.

## Additional information on directors recommended for appointment / reappointment as required under Regulation 36 of the LODR Regulations and applicable secretarial standards

### Nandan M. Nilekani — Chairman

Nandan M. Nilekani is the Co-Founder and Chairman of Infosys Technologies Limited. He was the Founding Chairman of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), in the rank of a Cabinet Minister, from 2009 to 2014. He is also co-founder and Chairman of EkStep Foundation, a not-for-profit organisation focused on a technology-based platform for literacy and numeracy.

In January 2023, he was appointed co-chair of the G20 Task Force on Digital Public Infrastructure for Economic Transformation, Financial Inclusion, and Development.

Nandan M. Nilekani received a bachelor’s degree from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bombay. He is the author or co-author of three books: *Imagining India*, *Rebooting India: Realizing a Billion Aspirations*, and *The Art of Bitfulness: Keeping Calm in the Digital World*, released in January 2022.

- **Age:** 70 years
- **Areas of expertise:** Information Technology, leadership, strategy, board service and governance, financial matters, diversity, global business, sales and marketing, cybersecurity, mergers and acquisitions, risk management, and sustainability and ESG
