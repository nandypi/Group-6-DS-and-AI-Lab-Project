## Energy-efficient IT infrastructure

InfosysIT has adopted measures to improve the energy efficiency and environmental performance of its internal IT infrastructure.

- **Energy-efficient network infrastructure:** Network equipment with power-saving features is used to improve energy efficiency.
- **Public cloud adoption:** Internal IT applications have largely transitioned to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premises data centers. Cloud-based collaboration and secure-access solutions support hybrid work.
- **IT service management:** The IT service management landscape has been standardized through an AI-enabled service desk hosted on a SaaS platform. The platform supports process standardization, automation, and improved user experience. AI-assisted agents support employees with daily tasks and productivity.
- **Data center modernization:** Data center modernization is a key part of the sustainability and responsible-growth strategy. Density-optimized hyperscale platforms support cloud-scale agility and high-density server virtualization, reducing infrastructure sprawl and improving compute-resource utilization. Investments in Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) provide integrated, real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure for data-driven optimization and consolidation planning. Observability and governance are embedded into the operating model to make sustainability outcomes measurable and scalable.
- **Enterprise storage:** Internal IT provides storage services to delivery units using multi-protocol storage technology, including flash storage, fabric pool, and storage grid solutions. Storage grid technology helps reduce the physical data-center footprint, power consumption, and cooling requirements.
- **Hybrid cloud storage:** Hybrid cloud storage is used for certain workloads to provide flexibility and scalability. Built-in data protection and seamless scaling reduce the need for buffer investments and lower on-premises data-center space, power, and cooling requirements.
- **Cloud-native development environment:** The internal IT team uses an open-source-based cloud-native development platform built on Hyper Converged Infrastructure (HCI) and compute resources. Resource consolidation through HCI reduces the physical data-center footprint and hardware requirements.
