## Microsoft

### Microsoft cut root cause analysis turnaround by 40% through Infosys' AI-led mission-critical support

#### Faster resolutions with agentic AI

Microsoft's Support for Mission Critical (SfMC) provides high-reliability support for enterprise customers, focusing on minimal downtime and rapid incident resolution. Delays or inefficiencies in critical support can pose risks to customers' critical infrastructure availability, operations, and trust.

Microsoft partnered with Infosys to integrate AI capabilities into SfMC's support architecture. Infosys deployed the Routing and Entitlement Validation Agent to triage and route critical cases to technically aligned engineers, and used the Azure Resiliency Correlation Engine to accelerate root cause analysis through automated recommendations. The Notes Observation and Validation Agent conducts continuous quality audits across global operations.

The transformation delivered the following results:

- Critical incident response times improved by 33%, reducing engagement time from 15 minutes to under 10 minutes.
- Root cause analysis preparation time decreased by 40%, reducing turnaround from five days to one day.
- Proactive AI-driven insights improved the resiliency of customers' critical workloads.
- More than 90% of critical cases received top boxed ratings.
- Low case-handling scores were virtually eliminated.

## ABN AMRO

### ABN AMRO collaborates with Infosys to modernize its enterprise content operations

#### Unlocking €64 Million in Annual Efficiency Savings

ABN AMRO is a Netherlands-based bank serving around 5 million retail customers and 365,000 business clients. Its enterprise content management ecosystem contains billions of documents, including scanned inputs, emails, and archived records.

While partial automation supported simple cases, complex document retrieval could require several minutes per file. Documents had to be downloaded from the ECM archive, making compliance enforcement difficult. Increasing regulatory pressure and data volumes further constrained scalability.

ABN AMRO partnered with Infosys to modernize its enterprise content operations. The engagement began with an assessment of the ECM landscape and a proof of concept using the Infosys Document Intelligence platform. ABN AMRO and Infosys then implemented a generative AI-powered ECM AI layer using retrieval-augmented generation, integrated with Microsoft Azure AI and data services.

The solution enabled automated classification, contextual information extraction, and faster retrieval of complex documents without requiring downloads. Delivered on a scalable, cloud-native platform with agile, cross-functional teams, it helped ABN AMRO unlock an estimated €64 million in annual efficiency savings. The transformation also improved employee productivity, reduced manual effort, strengthened compliance, and created a foundation for expanding intelligent document processing across additional workflows.

## An introduction to the report

Infosys adopted the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) principles to disclose performance on non-financial aspects of the business 18 years ago and became the first IT company to publish sustainability performance in accordance with the GRI G4 comprehensive criteria in 2014.

The Infosys Integrated Annual Report provides quantitative and qualitative disclosures on financial performance, environmental sustainability, social responsibility, and stakeholder relationships. It also describes the company's strategy, leadership commitment, and culture.

The Infosys Integrated Annual Report 2025-26 was prepared in accordance with the International Integrated Reporting Framework developed by the International Integrated Reporting Council (IIRC), the GRI Standards, and the International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS). It also includes the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR), prepared in accordance with guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), and maps Infosys' contribution to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through the Infosys ESG Report.

The financial and statutory data disclosed in the statutory sections meet the requirements of the Companies Act, 2013, including the rules made under it, and applicable SEBI regulations.

## About Infosys

Infosys is a global provider of AI-first business consulting and technology services. More than 325,000 employees work across 59 countries. The company provides enterprise transformation services using its AI-first value framework, domain expertise, and AI-native partner ecosystem.

Established in 1981 with initial capital of US$250, Infosys states that it has grown to a market capitalization of approximately US$54.8 billion. The company pioneered the Global Delivery Model and became the first Indian IT company to be listed on NASDAQ.

Since refreshing its ESG Vision 2030 in 2025, Infosys states that it has advanced its climate-positive goals, expanded skilling and community initiatives, and strengthened its inclusive workforce. The company also describes its governance and ethics programs as extending across its value chain.
