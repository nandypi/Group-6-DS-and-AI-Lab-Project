## Explanatory Statement

## Background and context for item nos. 4 and 5

The proposal seeks shareholder approval for amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program, 2019 (the “Amended 2019 Plan”) and for grants of stock incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.

Infosys began employee stock ownership programs in 1994. The 2015 Incentive Compensation Plan was approved by shareholders through a postal ballot dated March 31, 2016. Its vesting criteria are largely time-based.

The existing Expanded Stock Ownership Program, 2019 (the “2019 Plan”) was approved at the Annual General Meeting held on June 22, 2019, for seven years and is due to expire on June 21, 2026. The plan provided for approximately 1.15% of the Company’s then-outstanding equity shares, equivalent to approximately 5,00,00,000 shares.

As of the date of the Notice:

- 3,26,63,903 shares remained available for grant under the 2019 Plan, representing the unutilized balance of the original shareholder-approved pool and approximately 0.8% of the Company’s outstanding equity share capital.
- 4,44,19,917 RSUs were available for grant under all ESOP plans, representing approximately 1.1% of the Company’s outstanding equity share capital. This figure included vested but unexercised RSUs and RSUs that had not yet vested.

The proposed amendments are intended to:

- Continue the performance-based stock incentive program across an expanded employee base.
- Support the attraction, retention, and incentivization of key talent.
- Align employee interests with long-term shareholder value creation.

## The summary of the proposed amendments is as under:

## 1. Extension of the 2019 Plan tenure:

The 2019 Plan would be extended for a further period of seven (7) years from the date of shareholder approval. Grants under the 2019 Plan would be made over seven (7) years, and the plan would remain valid for ten (10) years from the date shareholder approval is received.

## 2. Strengthening alignment with Long-Term Shareholder Value creation and rationalization of Operational Metrics based on the inputs received from investors:

- A three-year rolling Relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) metric would be introduced as a vesting parameter.
- Operating metrics would be rationalized to maintain focus on revenue and margin, with consistency and comparability over time. Sub-segment metrics such as digital revenue growth may become transient and less comparable as technology and business models evolve.

## 3. Share pool and equity dilution

The proposed extension would not increase the original shareholder-approved pool of 5,00,00,000 equity shares. The Company would continue to operate within the existing approved pool, with no further dilution of equity share capital compared with the earlier approval.

Under the proposed amended plan, the share reserve would be 3,26,63,903 shares, representing the balance of the unutilized original pool and approximately 0.8% of the Company’s equity capital as of May 28, 2026.

## 4. Regulatory amendments

Other amendments would provide clarity and consistency and align the 2019 Plan with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021.
