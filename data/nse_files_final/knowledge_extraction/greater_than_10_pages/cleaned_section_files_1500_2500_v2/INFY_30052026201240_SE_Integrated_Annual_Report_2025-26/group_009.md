## Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (iCETS)

iCETS is Infosys' emerging technology incubation unit. It delivers next-generation platforms and innovation-as-a-service powered by New Emerging and eXploratory Technologies (NEXT).

As generative AI moves from experimentation to enterprise deployment, iCETS enables enterprises to apply emerging technologies through a structured, outcome-driven approach.

### Now

The “Now” framework focuses on enterprise-scale value through reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities that operationalize AI at scale. Infosys Topaz Fabric provides composable and interoperable AI agents, tools, services, and models. It is designed to help organizations optimize existing IT processes, automate or eliminate manual tasks, and build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

### Near

Infosys Living Labs are global innovation hubs where clients, partners, startups, and academia co-create solutions using emerging technologies. They support contextualization, experimentation, iteration, and real-world validation in controlled environments.

Client Living Labs deliver innovation-as-a-service through three flagship programs:

- **IncubateNext:** Scalable, real-world innovation.
- **ImagineNext:** Future opportunity discovery.
- **SenseNext:** Converting early market and technology signals into action.

## Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI)

The Infosys Knowledge Institute is the business research and thought leadership arm of Infosys. It combines the expertise of Infosys and client collaborations to produce content and insights on the business impact of technology for enterprises.

IKI also develops proprietary data and insights through primary research, including large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis. IKI has collaborated with over 1,000 clients and created over 2,000 assets since inception.

## Advancing climate positivity

In FY26, Infosys continued its transition from carbon neutrality toward climate positivity by combining efficiency, clean energy, carbon sequestration, and nature-based solutions. The initiatives focus on reducing emissions and restoring ecosystems across operations, value chains, and communities.

## 1. Energy efficiency and lowcarbon approach

Infosys embeds energy efficiency across the life cycle of its buildings. Its campuses use radiant cooling, radiant heating, climate-adaptive cooling, intelligent controls, and climate-responsive design to reduce operational energy demand.

A total of 84% of Infosys' office space is certified to the highest green-building standards.

## 2. Renewable energy

Infosys has installed 62.42 MWp of solar photovoltaic capacity through rooftop and ground-mounted systems. It also procures renewable power through third-party power purchase agreements and green tariff mechanisms, adapting its sourcing strategy to regulatory requirements across geographies.

## 3. Decarbonizing the supply chain

Infosys works with its supplier ecosystem to decarbonize the value chain. It has prioritized high-emission IT hardware suppliers through onsite assessments and senior-level discussions focused on decarbonization roadmaps.

The company supports suppliers in measuring Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions, establishing baselines, and adopting verified Environmental Product Declarations. Ongoing support and performance tracking are used to embed climate action into supplier operations.

## 4. Carbon offsets and regeneration

Infosys addresses unavoidable emissions through community-focused carbon offset programs, including clean cookstove initiatives in Rajasthan and biogas projects in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

The company has planted approximately 14 million saplings across multiple states through its corporate agroforestry program. The plantations are intended to improve carbon sequestration, soil and water resilience, and rural livelihoods.

These initiatives have benefited over 300,000 rural families and created more than 4,800 local jobs.

## Water stewardship

Infosys identifies water stewardship as a core part of its environmental strategy. As a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate, it focuses on reducing consumption, increasing reuse, and improving water availability in operating communities.

All wastewater across Infosys campuses is recycled. The company also uses large-scale rainwater harvesting and demand-side efficiency measures.

The Bangalore campus is certified as water positive. In continuation of its efforts to achieve water-positive or water-neutral status based on NITI AAYOG guidelines, Infosys achieved Scope 1 certification at nine campuses in India during the year.
