# Item no. 5 - Approval of the proposed amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 ('the Amended 2019 Plan') and grant of stock incentives to the eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries under the Amended 2019 Plan

To consider and, if thought fit, to pass the following resolution as a Special Resolution:

Shareholder approval was proposed for amendments to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019, as applicable to eligible employees of the Company's subsidiaries, and for the grant of share-based incentives under the Amended 2019 Plan.

The Board would be authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives in one or more tranches to:

- Permanent employees of the Company's subsidiaries, whether working in India or outside India.
- Directors of the Company's subsidiaries.
- Other persons who may become eligible under applicable laws.

Promoters, members of the promoter group, independent directors, and directors who, individually or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company's outstanding equity shares would be excluded.

The proposed amendments and modifications to the existing plan are:

- Amend Section 13 to extend the period during which awards may be granted by seven years from the date of shareholder approval.
- Continue the Amended 2019 Plan for a further period of 10 years from the date of shareholder approval.
- Amend Section 6(c) relating to vesting based on performance parameters.
- Amend Section 3 to reflect the unutilized share pool available for grant.
- Make editorial and clarificatory changes and align the plan with the amended SEBI Regulations.

The maximum number of stock incentives granted to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries, in aggregate, under the Amended 2019 Plan would be **3,26,63,903 equity shares**. This represents the unutilized balance from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**.

The exercise price and vesting criteria would be determined by the Board and/or the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Administration relating to the secondary acquisition of the Company's equity shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust may be delegated to the Trust from time to time in accordance with the Amended 2019 Plan.

The Board and/or the Administrator would be authorized to make adjustments to ensure fair and equitable benefits for eligible employees in connection with rights issues, bonus issues, mergers, restructuring, sales of divisions, or similar corporate actions, subject to applicable laws.

The Board would be authorized to:

- Allot equity shares required for the Amended 2019 Plan.
- Take steps to list shares allotted under the plan on the stock exchanges.
- Resolve issues, questions, difficulties, or doubts arising in connection with implementation.
- Delegate these powers to committees of directors and further to executives or officers.
- Execute documents and take other steps required to implement the resolutions.

The Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust and its trustees would be authorized to take steps required in relation to the Amended 2019 Plan and its implementation.
