## Information Other than the Financial Statements and Auditor's Report Thereon

The other information comprises the Management Discussion and Analysis, Board's Report and its annexures, Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report, Corporate Governance, and Shareholder's Information. It excludes the Consolidated Financial Statements, Standalone Financial Statements, and the auditor's report.

The auditor's opinion on the Standalone Financial Statements does not cover the other information. The auditor's responsibility was to read the other information and consider whether it was materially inconsistent with the Standalone Financial Statements, audit knowledge, or otherwise materially misstated. The auditor had nothing to report.

## Responsibilities of Management and Board of Directors for the Standalone Financial Statements

The Board of Directors is responsible under Section 134(5) of the Companies Act, 2013, for preparing standalone financial statements that give a true and fair view of the Company's financial position, financial performance including other comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows, in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in India and Ind AS specified under Section 133.

These responsibilities include:

- Maintaining adequate accounting records to safeguard assets and prevent and detect fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining effective internal financial controls to ensure accurate and complete accounting records and financial statement presentation free from material misstatement due to fraud or error.
- Assessing the Company's ability to continue as a going concern, making related disclosures where applicable, and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless liquidation or cessation of operations is intended or there is no realistic alternative.
- Overseeing the Company's financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for the Audit of the Standalone Financial Statements

The auditors' objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance that the Standalone Financial Statements were free from material misstatement due to fraud or error and to issue an auditor's report containing their opinion.

As part of the audit, the auditors exercised professional judgment and professional scepticism and:

- Identified and assessed risks of material misstatement, including the higher risk of fraud involving collusion, forgery, intentional omissions, misrepresentations, or management override of internal controls.
- Obtained an understanding of relevant internal financial controls and, under Section 143(3)(i) of the Act, reported on their adequacy and operating effectiveness.
- Evaluated the appropriateness of accounting policies, the reasonableness of accounting estimates, and related disclosures.
- Evaluated the appropriateness of management's use of the going-concern basis and whether events or conditions indicated a material uncertainty that could cast significant doubt on the Company's ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluated the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the Standalone Financial Statements.

Materiality was assessed using quantitative and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating audit results, and assessing identified misstatements.

The auditors communicated with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, and any significant deficiencies in internal financial controls. They also communicated compliance with relevant ethical and independence requirements. Matters of most significance in the current-period audit were identified as key audit matters, subject to legal and regulatory disclosure restrictions.

## Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements

1. As required by Section 143(3) of the Act, based on the audit, the auditors reported that:

   - The information and explanations necessary for the audit were sought and obtained.
   - Proper books of account required by law were maintained, based on examination of those books.
   - The Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit and Loss including Other Comprehensive Income, Statement of Changes in Equity, and Statement of Cash Flows agreed with the books of account.
   - The Standalone Financial Statements complied with the Ind AS specified under Section 133 of the Act.
   - Based on written representations received from the directors and recorded by the Board as of March 31, 2026, none of the directors was disqualified as of that date under Section 164(2) of the Act.
   - The Company's internal financial controls with reference to the Standalone Financial Statements were adequate and operating effectively. The separate report in `Annexure A` expressed an unmodified opinion.
   - Directors' remuneration paid during the year complied with Section 197 of the Act.
   - The Company disclosed the impact of pending litigation on its financial position in the Standalone Financial Statements. Refer Note 2.23.
   - The Company made provisions required under applicable law or accounting standards for material foreseeable losses. Refer Note 2.16. The Company did not have any long-term derivative contracts.
   - There was no delay in transferring amounts required to be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund.

   - Management represented that, to the best of its knowledge and belief:
     - No funds were advanced, loaned, or invested by the Company, including from borrowed funds or share premium, in any person or entity, including foreign entities, with an understanding that the intermediary would directly or indirectly lend or invest in identified ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees, securities, or similar support on their behalf.
     - No funds were received by the Company from any person or entity, including foreign entities, with an understanding that the Company would directly or indirectly lend or invest in identified ultimate beneficiaries or provide guarantees, securities, or similar support on their behalf.
     - Based on audit procedures considered reasonable and appropriate, nothing came to the auditors' attention indicating that these representations contained a material misstatement.

   - As stated in Note 2.12.3:
     - The final dividend proposed in the previous year and declared and paid during the year complied with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.
     - The interim dividend declared and paid during the year and up to the date of the report complied with Section 123 of the Act.
     - The Board proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to member approval at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed amount complied with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

   - The Company used accounting software with an audit-trail or edit-log facility for maintaining its books for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The facility operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions, no instance of tampering came to the auditors' attention, and the audit trail was preserved in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

2. As required by the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020, issued under Section 143(11) of the Act, `Annexure B` contains the statement on matters specified in paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Order.
