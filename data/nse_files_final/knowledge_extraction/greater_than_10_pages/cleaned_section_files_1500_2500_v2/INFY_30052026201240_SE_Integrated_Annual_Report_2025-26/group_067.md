## 10. Briefly describe the waste management practices adopted in your establishments. Describe the strategy adopted by your company to reduce usage of hazardous and toxic chemicals in your products and processes and the practices adopted to manage such wastes.

Infosys' waste management strategy is based on the 5R principle: **Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Refurbish/Repurpose, and Recycle**. Through reduction and recovery practices, 99% of disposed waste is kept out of landfills.

Infosys is working toward its 2030 goal of zero waste to landfills by adopting TRUE Zero Waste Certification at its India campuses. As of fiscal 2026, 46% of its owned campuses in India had achieved TRUE Zero Waste Certification from Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI).

As an IT company, Infosys generates e-waste and follows a refurbishment-first approach where feasible. Materials that cannot be refurbished are handled through authorized recycling vendors. Authorized Pollution Control Board recyclers and refurbishers are periodically evaluated for compliance with applicable legal requirements and the extent of resource circularity in their processes.
