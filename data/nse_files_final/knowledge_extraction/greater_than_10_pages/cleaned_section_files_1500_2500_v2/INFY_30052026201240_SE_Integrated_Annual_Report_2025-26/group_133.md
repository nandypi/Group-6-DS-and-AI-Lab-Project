## Financial risk management

## Financial risk factors

The Group’s activities expose it to market risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk. Its primary market risk is foreign exchange risk, which it manages partly through derivative financial instruments. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of customers and the concentration of risk among the top few customers.

## Market risk

The Group operates internationally, with a substantial portion of its business transacted in multiple currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies. The Group uses foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate these exposures.

Changes in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and foreign currencies may adversely affect the Group’s operating results.

During the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, the Group designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions for the cash flow hedge balance as of March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss within three months.

The Group determines the existence of an economic relationship between the hedging instrument and the hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at the inception of the hedge relationship and through periodic prospective effectiveness assessments. These assessments ensure that an economic relationship exists between the hedged item and hedging instrument and that the hedging instrument is expected to offset changes in the cash flows of hedged items.

If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal but the risk-management objective remains unchanged and the hedge continues to qualify for hedge accounting, the hedge relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.
