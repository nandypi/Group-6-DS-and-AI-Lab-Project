## Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report

Sustainability and responsible business are integrated into Infosys' decisions, operations, and governance. Infosys states that its purpose is to amplify human potential and create opportunities for people, businesses, and communities through technology and solutions.

Infosys adopted the GRI Framework in 2008 and was among the first companies to publish a Business Responsibility Report in fiscal 2013. The BRSR follows the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct principles and addresses the ESG-related requirements under Regulation 34(2)(f) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.
