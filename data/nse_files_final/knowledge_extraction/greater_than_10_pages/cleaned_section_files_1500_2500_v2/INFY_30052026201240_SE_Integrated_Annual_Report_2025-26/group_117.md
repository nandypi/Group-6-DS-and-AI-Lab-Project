## Annexure 'A' To The Independent Auditor's Report

(Referred to in paragraph 1(f) under the “Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements” section of the report to the Members of Infosys Limited.)

## Report on the Internal Financial Controls with reference to Consolidated Financial Statements under Clause (i) of sub-section 3 of Section 143 of the Companies Act, 2013 (the 'Act')

The auditors audited the internal financial controls with reference to the consolidated financial statements of Infosys Limited and its subsidiary companies incorporated in India as of March 31, 2026, in conjunction with the audit of the consolidated financial statements for the year ended on that date.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for Internal Financial Controls

Management and the Boards of Directors of Infosys Limited and its Indian subsidiary companies are responsible for establishing and maintaining adequate internal financial controls based on the criteria established by the respective companies, considering the essential components described in the Guidance Note issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

These responsibilities include ensuring orderly and efficient business conduct, adherence to company policies, safeguarding assets, preventing and detecting fraud and errors, maintaining accurate and complete accounting records, and preparing reliable financial information on a timely basis.

## Auditor's Responsibility

The auditors' responsibility was to express an opinion on the internal financial controls with reference to the consolidated financial statements. The audit was conducted in accordance with the ICAI Guidance Note and applicable Standards on Auditing prescribed under Section 143(10) of the Act.

The audit procedures included:

- Obtaining an understanding of the internal financial controls.
- Assessing the risk of material weakness.
- Testing and evaluating the design and operating effectiveness of controls based on the assessed risk.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.

## Meaning of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Consolidated Financial Statements

Internal financial controls over consolidated financial reporting are processes designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

They include controls that:

1. Maintain records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and asset dispositions.
2. Ensure transactions are recorded as necessary to prepare financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
3. Ensure receipts and expenditures are made only in accordance with management and director authorisations.
4. Prevent or timely detect unauthorised acquisition, use, or disposition of assets that could materially affect the financial statements.

## Inherent Limitations of Internal Financial Controls with reference to Consolidated Financial Statements

Because of inherent limitations, including possible collusion or management override of controls, material misstatements due to error or fraud may occur and remain undetected. Evaluations of controls for future periods are also subject to the risk that controls may become inadequate because of changing conditions or deteriorating compliance.

## Opinion

In the auditors' opinion, Infosys Limited and its Indian subsidiary companies had, in all material respects, an adequate internal financial controls system with reference to the consolidated financial statements as of March 31, 2026. The controls were operating effectively based on the criteria established by the respective companies, considering the essential components described in the ICAI Guidance Note.
