## Letter to the Shareholder

### Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

### Dear shareholder,

Last year marked an inflection point as AI began evolving from experimentation to enterprise-scale adoption. Infosys shifted its portfolio, invested in talent, and scaled partnerships to help clients deploy AI at speed and scale. AI-led programs were deployed across 90% of its top 200 clients.

Infosys’s AI strategy is built on six pillars of its AI-First value framework:

- Strategy and engineering
- Data
- Process
- Legacy modernization
- Physical AI
- Trust

Together, these areas address a US$300 billion opportunity, based on market estimates. They support growth programs, legacy-system modernization, data and process readiness, and responsible AI deployment.

For Q3, Infosys reported that revenue from these six areas represented 5.5% of total revenue and was growing faster than the company. Infosys has partnerships with AI-native companies and employees trained on their foundation models. The company is reskilling more than 325,000 employees on AI.

Client AI transformation programs include:

- Coding and code modernization
- Building agents across business functions

Infosys Topaz is the company’s AI suite for enterprise growth and productivity. Topaz Fabric provides a composable stack of services and agents that enables clients to use different foundation models in a token-efficient manner, with the stated objective of maximizing value while minimizing cost and risk.

Cloud and data expertise remain foundational to Infosys’s AI-powered transformation capabilities. The company’s investments in Cobalt and its Data and Analytics portfolio are intended to support its participation in the expanding market opportunity.

Infosys reported the following performance and capital-allocation figures:

- Revenue of more than US$20 billion
- Adjusted operating margin of 21%
- Free cash flow of US$3.7 billion
- More than US$4 billion returned to shareholders
  - Dividends: US$2.1 billion
  - Share buybacks: US$2 billion
- Total dividend announced: ₹48 per share
- Large deals during the year: US$14.9 billion
- College graduates recruited: more than 20,000

Infosys launched a focused growth program targeting large global companies where it has a smaller share of client spending. The company stated that the program had already produced results and was developing into a key growth driver.

Infosys Foundation impacted more than seven million people in India. Springboard provided free digital learning programs to 10 million people worldwide. The company reported a workforce of more than 325,000 employees.

Bengaluru, May 23, 2026

Salil Parekh  
Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director
