## 10b. What are the processes used to identify work-related hazards and assess risks on a routine and non-routine basis by the entity?

Infosys conducts hazard identification and risk assessments for existing, new, and modified activities, processes, and services, including in response to regulatory updates.

Routine assessments cover:

- Periodic workplace and ergonomic evaluations.
- Utility operations.
- Employee commute risks.

Non-routine activities, including construction, infrastructure development, preventive maintenance, and facility modifications, undergo specific risk assessments before and during execution.

Incidents and near misses are analyzed quarterly to identify trends and emerging risks. Hazards are prioritized and mitigated using the hierarchy of controls. Residual risks are reassessed, and the risk control matrix is updated as required. Employees and stakeholders participate in the risk assessment process, and learnings from past projects and current operations are incorporated on an ongoing basis.

## 10c. Whether you have processes for workers to report the work-related hazards and to remove themselves from such risks. (Y / N)

Yes. Infosys maintains an incident reporting and management process covering accidents, near misses, unsafe acts, and unsafe conditions. Employees can report health and safety concerns through the Infosys Helpdesk (IHD) or designated email addresses. Contract staff without system access report concerns to their supervisors, who escalate them to the local HSE team.

All incidents undergo root cause analysis. Corrective and preventive actions are implemented to prevent recurrence, injuries, and losses. Awareness of incident reporting is supported through induction programs, online Lex training modules, targeted campaigns, and mailers. Incident learnings and corrective actions are shared across locations.

## 10d. Do the employees/ worker of the entity have access to non-occupational medical and healthcare services?

Yes. Infosys operates Occupational Health Centers (OHCs), including physiotherapy facilities, across its campuses in India. Competent medical professionals from accredited partner hospitals manage the OHCs and physiotherapy centers in accordance with applicable statutory and other requirements.

Periodic health risk assessments monitor health trends and support specialist consultations and targeted interventions. Employee well-being initiatives include workshops, awareness campaigns, health check-ups, online chat sessions, counseling services such as Samaritans, and targeted wellness programs. OHC visit information is recorded digitally in accordance with applicable regional data protection and privacy regulations.

Hospital tie-ups provide employees and their dependents with access to health screenings and treatment for non-occupational illnesses. Employees, including contractual staff, receive insurance coverage. Contractual employees are also covered by targeted programs, including mandatory health screenings and interventions based on identified risks, hazards, and emerging health trends.

## 12. Describe the measures taken by the entity to ensure a safe and healthy work place.

Infosys’s occupational health and safety policies, processes, and practices address the physical, emotional, and social well-being of employees. Its Health and Safety Management System is certified to ISO 45001:2018 for all Indian locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries.

Measures include:

1. **Incident management:** Incidents, near misses, potential hazards, and accidents are reported through internal applications or email. Causes are analyzed and corrective actions are taken to prevent recurrence and related injuries or losses.
2. **OH&S Committee:** Employees and other than permanent employees participate in the Infosys OH&S Committee. Employee representation in safety committees is 100%.
3. **Training and awareness:** Programs include awareness sessions, mock drills, classroom sessions, and periodic demonstrations. HSEMS training is part of employee induction, and an HSE awareness module is available on Lex. Contract staff receive job-specific and general induction training, followed by refresher training.
4. **Safety interventions:** Controls include work permits, training, Lock Out Tag Out (LOTO), safety inspections, audits, operational controls, and monitoring. Policies address women’s safety, lone working, transport, travel, construction, and other activities.
5. **Medical services:** OHCs have been established on campuses in India. Most OHCs operate around the clock based on business requirements. Ambulance services or ambulance arrangements are available at all India-based locations.
6. **Health Risk Assessment (HRA):** HRA is conducted annually using inputs from OHCs. Relevant risks include ergonomics, Musculoskeletal Disorders (MSDs), emotional well-being, workplace operations, utility operations, and commuting.
7. **Ergonomics programs:** Programs include on-site physiotherapy centers in India, interventions by ergonomic experts, ergonomic infrastructure, and sessions on back care, Repetitive Stress Injuries, and seating posture for employees globally.
8. **Physical and emotional well-being:** Nutritionists at locations in India provide counseling and guidance. Gyms are also available for physical and mental fitness.
9. **Programs for mothers:** Pregna Care is a healthcare program for expectant mothers in India.
10. **Working environment:** Infosys monitors indoor air quality, lighting levels, and noise levels and promotes the use of green-seal chemicals.
