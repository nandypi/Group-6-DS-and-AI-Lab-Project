In 2020, Infosys became carbon neutral, 30 years ahead of the timeline set by the Paris Agreement. In October 2020, the company launched its ESG Vision and ambitions for 2030. In 2025, Infosys renewed its ESG Vision 2030 and committed to further progress on its ESG ambitions.

[ESG Vision 2030 refresh](https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/esg-vision-2030/docs/infosys-esg-vision-2030-refresh.pdf)

### 6. Performance against commitments, goals, and targets

Details of performance against ESG goals are available in the chapters “Approaching value creation” and “Delivering value” in the Integrated Annual Report and in the ESG Report 2026. Infosys reports that it is on track to meet the ESG goals and targets outlined in its ESG Vision 2030.
