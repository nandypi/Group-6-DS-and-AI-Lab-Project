- The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared and paid by the Company during the year, was in accordance with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.
- The interim dividend declared and paid by the Company during the year and up to the date of the report complied with Section 123 of the Act.
- The Board of Directors proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by the members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed amount complied with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.
- Based on examination, including test checks, the Company and its subsidiaries incorporated in India used accounting software systems for the financial year ended March 31, 2026 that included audit-trail (edit-log) functionality. The audit trail operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions recorded in the systems. No instance of tampering was identified during the audit, and the Parent Company and the relevant Indian subsidiaries preserved the audit trail in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

The financial statements of five subsidiaries that were not material to the Group’s Consolidated Financial Statements had not been audited under the provisions of the Act as of the report date. Accordingly, the auditors were unable to comment on the reporting requirement under Rule 11(g) of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014 for those subsidiaries.

With respect to paragraphs 3(xxi) and 4 of the Companies (Auditor’s Report) Order, 2020 (CARO), based on the information and explanations provided and the auditors’ reports included in the Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended March 31, 2026, no qualifications or adverse remarks were reported for companies whose audits under Section 143 of the Act had been completed.
