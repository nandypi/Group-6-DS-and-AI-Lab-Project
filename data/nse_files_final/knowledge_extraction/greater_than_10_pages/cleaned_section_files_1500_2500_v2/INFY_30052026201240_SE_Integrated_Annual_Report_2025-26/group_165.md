## Instructions for participation through VC

The 45th AGM will be conducted through video conference in compliance with applicable legal provisions and circulars. Members may participate using a desktop, laptop, smartphone, or tablet. A stable broadband, Wi-Fi, or LAN connection is recommended.

Institutional and corporate shareholders must upload a Board Resolution or Authorization Letter authorizing their representatives to attend the AGM through video conference.

Members may ask questions through the designated question facility during the AGM and may also be enabled to ask questions through video conference.

Members who have not voted on the resolutions through remote e-voting and are otherwise eligible may vote through the e-voting system during the AGM.

## General guidelines for VC participation

- The video conference facility will open 60 minutes before the scheduled start of the AGM.
- Participation through video conference will be available on a first-come-first-served basis.
- The Company may limit the number of members asking questions depending on the time available at the AGM.
- Participants using mobile devices, tablets, or mobile hotspots may experience audio or video loss because of network fluctuations.

## Instructions for e-voting

The NSDL e-voting process consists of two steps:

1. Access the NSDL e-voting system.
2. Log in using the applicable demat account or depository participant credentials and cast the vote during the remote e-voting period or, where eligible, during the virtual meeting.

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their NSDL or CDSL demat accounts, depository participants, or the applicable e-voting service. Shareholders are advised to keep their mobile number and email ID updated in their demat accounts.

Shareholders using NSDL may access e-voting through the NSDL e-Services platform, OTP-based login, or the NSDL e-voting website. Shareholders using CDSL Easi or Easiest may access e-voting through their existing credentials or through the CDSL e-voting facility.

Shareholders who cannot retrieve their user ID or password may use the “Forgot User ID” and “Forgot Password” options on the relevant e-voting website.
