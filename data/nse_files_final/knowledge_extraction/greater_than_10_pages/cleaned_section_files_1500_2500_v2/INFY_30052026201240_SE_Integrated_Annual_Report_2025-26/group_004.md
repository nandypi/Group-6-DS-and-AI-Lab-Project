## Chairman's message Performance overview

### The age of orchestration

Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman

Infosys states that generative AI is reshaping software development and enterprise technology work. The company plans to adopt coding tools, improve productivity, and continue work across the software development lifecycle, including testing, validation, architecture, cybersecurity, data protection, and governance.

Infosys identifies continuous learning and talent transformation as core capabilities. It plans to prepare its talent for the AI era and redistribute employees released through productivity improvements toward new accounts and offerings.

The AI transition is increasing customer demand for legacy modernization, elimination of data silos, remediation of AI-identified cyber vulnerabilities, and reduction of technical debt. The message also identifies opportunities in building software, integrating AI models and agents with deterministic enterprise systems, and modernizing enterprise processes and operating models.

The message describes the deployment gap as the difficulty enterprises face in moving from AI pilots to scaled implementation because of legacy architecture, organizational inertia, and changing jurisdictional rules. It states that addressing this gap may require redesigning business processes, customer journeys, and operating models rather than adding AI to existing structures.

Technical debt is described as a strategic liability because older systems become more expensive to maintain and constrain the agility required for AI adoption. The message identifies organizational readiness, data integrity, governance, and execution pace as factors affecting enterprise AI outcomes.

The message states that enterprise AI modernization is particularly difficult in brownfield environments because each company has different legacy systems, data, and undocumented dependencies. It describes trusted client relationships, execution experience, and the ability to combine existing enterprise systems with new AI capabilities as relevant to supporting clients.

Supply-chain changes and AI regulation across multiple jurisdictions are identified as ongoing factors affecting enterprise technology work. The message also cites a World Economic Forum projection that by 2030, 92 million jobs will be displaced and 170 million created, for a net gain of 78 million jobs.
