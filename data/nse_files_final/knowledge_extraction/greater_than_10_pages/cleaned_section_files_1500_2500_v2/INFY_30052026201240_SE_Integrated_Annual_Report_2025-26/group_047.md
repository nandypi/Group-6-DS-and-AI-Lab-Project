## Outstanding ADSs / GDRs / warrants or any convertible instruments

Infosys ADSs, evidenced by ADRs, trade in the US on the NYSE under the ticker symbol `INFY`. The trading currency is USD, and each ADS represents one equity share. ADRs evidencing ADSs began trading on the NYSE on December 12, 2012.

As of March 31, 2026, there were 1,34,734 record holders of ADRs evidencing 30,50,54,064 ADSs. Infosys had no outstanding GDRs, warrants, or convertible instruments as of that date.

## Communication to the shareholders

Infosys makes the following information available to shareholders through its investor-relations website:

- Quarterly and annual standalone and consolidated financial statements, including segmental information.
- Quarterly reports containing selected financial data extracted from audited consolidated financial statements under IFRS and Ind AS.
- Official news releases, press releases, stock-exchange disclosures, and investor and analyst presentations.
- Earnings calls, analyst and investor call transcripts, AGM presentations, AGM transcripts, and AGM video archives.
- Annual Form 20-F filings submitted to the US Securities and Exchange Commission.

## Registered office and global locations

Infosys’s registered office is at Electronics City, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560100, Karnataka, India.

As of the reporting period, Infosys operated across 290 locations in 59 countries. The Company had no manufacturing plants and operated development centers and offices in India and overseas.

## Subsidiaries

As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 30 direct subsidiaries and 84 step-down subsidiaries. The Company did not have any material subsidiary.
