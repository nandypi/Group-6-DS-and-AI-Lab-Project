## Note

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends distributed outside India are subject to applicable Indian foreign-exchange laws and withholding tax.

- The payout ratio is computed as a percentage of free cash flow prepared under IFRS.
- The buyback amount excludes transaction costs of ₹58 crore.
- The final dividend was recommended by the Board of Directors on April 23, 2026. Payment is subject to shareholder approval at the AGM scheduled for June 23, 2026. The record date is June 10, 2026, and payment is scheduled for June 25, 2026.
- Infosys' capital allocation policy is to pay approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semiannual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and approvals. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure under IFRS. Including buybacks, the Company returned 82.1% of cumulative free cash flow for the years ended March 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026.

## 2. Business description

### Strategy

Infosys helps clients address business opportunities arising from advances in software and computing technology, particularly AI. Its strategy is to help clients ideate, plan, and scale their AI initiatives by building data foundations and cloud capabilities. The Company also promotes safeguards relating to privacy, ethics, and controls through Responsible AI practices.

### Organization

Infosys' go-to-market business units and solutions are detailed in the Operating Context section of this Integrated Annual Report.

### Infrastructure

During fiscal 2026, Infosys increased its physical infrastructure space by a net 1.87 million sq. ft. Total available space was 58.20 million sq. ft. as of March 31, 2026. The Company had a presence in 59 countries across 290 locations.

### Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)

Infosys' M&A approach is aimed at strengthening capabilities, deepening industry expertise, and expanding its geographical footprint.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% of the partnership interests or voting interests in:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — an energy and business consulting services company headquartered in Texas, U.S.; acquired on April 30, 2025.
2. **The Missing Link Group** — a cybersecurity services provider headquartered in Australia; acquired on April 30, 2025.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group entered into definitive agreements to:

1. Form a joint venture with Telstra and acquire a 75% stake in the Versent Group, a Telstra Group subsidiary providing cloud and digital transformation services.
2. Acquire Stratus Global LLC, a U.S.-headquartered insurance consulting and technology services company focused on Guidewire services. The acquisition was completed as of the date of the report.
3. Acquire Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries, including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC, a U.S.-headquartered healthcare digital transformation and consulting company.

The Versent Group comprises Telstra Purple Pty Ltd and the integrated operations of Versent, Epicon, Telstra Purple Digital, and associated Cloud Access products. Telstra Purple Digital is a carve-out from Telstra's Enterprise Tech Services/Telstra Purple portfolio and excludes Alliance Automation Pty Ltd, Aqura Technologies Pty Ltd, and Telstra Broadcast Services Pty Limited.

## 3. Human resources management

Infosys' people strategy focuses on attracting and developing talent, building internal leadership pipelines, employee experience, continuous learning, diversity, equity and inclusion, flexible work models, and technology-enabled employee programs.

The Company has a structured employee grievance-redressal framework through Resolution Hubs. The framework is guided by natural justice, confidentiality, non-retaliation, and timely resolution. Internal Committees for the prevention and redressal of sexual harassment support the Company's statutory compliance framework.

Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report. No complaint cases were pending for more than 90 days.

Other disclosures and information are set out in the Management's Discussion and Analysis section.
