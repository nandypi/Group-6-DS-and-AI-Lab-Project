## Board as a trustee

The Board recognizes its primary role as trustee of shareholder capital and seeks to uphold corporate governance standards.

## Corporate governance guidelines

Infosys’ corporate governance guidelines are established by the Board and provide a framework for directors and Management to pursue the Company’s objectives for stakeholders. They are framed in conjunction with the Company’s Memorandum & Articles of Association, Board committee charters, and applicable laws, regulations, and guidelines in India, the US, and other jurisdictions.

## Role of the Board of Directors

The primary role of the Board is trusteeship: protecting and enhancing shareholder value. The Board is responsible for:

- Exercising appropriate control to ensure that the Company is managed efficiently and fulfills stakeholder and societal expectations.
- Monitoring the effectiveness of governance practices and making changes as necessary.
- Providing strategic guidance and effectively monitoring Management.
- Exercising independent judgment on corporate affairs.
- Assigning a sufficient number of non-executive directors to matters involving potential conflicts of interest.
- Reviewing and guiding corporate strategy, major action plans, risk policy, annual budgets, and business plans.
- Setting performance objectives, monitoring implementation and corporate performance, and overseeing major capital expenditures, acquisitions, and divestments.

## Independent directors

Infosys follows the definitions and requirements for independent directors under the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the New York Stock Exchange listed company manual, the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, and applicable US securities laws.

Based on disclosures received from all independent directors and the Board’s assessment, the independent directors fulfill the applicable independence conditions and are independent of Management.

## Board composition

As on April 23, 2026, the Board comprised ten members:

- One non-executive and non-independent Chairman
- One Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director (CEO & MD)
- Eight independent directors

The Board profiles in the Integrated Annual Report include nationality, age, appointment and reappointment dates, Board tenure, term-ending dates, shareholding, Board memberships in Indian companies, committee details under Regulation 26 of the Listing Regulations, and each director’s core expertise and competencies.

There are no inter se relationships among the Board members. The Company has no pecuniary relationship with any non-executive director. During the year, the Company did not provide loans or advances to firms or companies in which directors are interested.

The Company has six Board committees:

- Audit Committee
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee
- Stakeholders Relationship Committee
- Risk Management Committee
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee
- ESG Committee

All committees comprise only independent directors, with one independent director serving as chairperson. The Company also has a Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee under the Risk Management Committee, comprising only independent directors.

## Board meetings

### Scheduling and selection of agenda items for Board meetings

Tentative dates for Board meetings in the next fiscal year are decided in advance and published in the Integrated Annual Report. The Chairman and Company Secretary, in consultation with the CEO & MD and lead independent director, propose the agenda for each meeting. The agenda and explanatory notes are circulated to directors in advance. Board members may suggest additional agenda items.

The Board meets at least once each quarter to review quarterly results and other agenda items. Additional meetings are held when necessary. Independent directors are expected to attend at least four quarterly Board meetings and the Annual General Meeting (AGM). Video and teleconferencing facilities are provided to support participation by directors located in different parts of the world.

Board members are expected to prepare for, attend, and participate in Board and applicable committee meetings, while ensuring that other commitments do not materially interfere with their responsibilities to the Company.

## Meeting of independent directors

The independent directors are required under Schedule IV of the Companies Act, 2013, and related rules to hold at least one meeting each financial year without non-independent directors or Management present.

During the year, the independent directors met four times without executive directors and Management. They discussed, among other matters:

- Company performance and risks
- Flow of information to the Board
- Competition and strategy
- Leadership strengths and weaknesses
- Governance and compliance
- Board movements and succession planning
- Human resources matters
- Performance of executive Board members, committees, and the Chairman

## Responsible leadership

The Board and Management work together on responsible leadership and corporate governance.

## Responsibilities of the Board leadership

The Board oversees Management’s functions and protects the long-term interests of stakeholders. The responsibilities and authority of the Chairman, CEO & MD, and lead independent director are described below.

## Chairman

Infosys has separated the roles of Chairman and CEO & MD. The Chairman leads the Board, presides over Board and shareholder meetings, and oversees governance matters concerning the organization, composition, and effectiveness of the Board and its committees, as well as the performance of individual directors.

The Chairman facilitates communication among directors and works with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee on:

- Board and committee composition
- Induction of directors
- Director succession planning
- Board evaluation
- Feedback and advice to individual directors

## CEO & MD

The CEO & MD is responsible for executing corporate strategy in consultation with the Board and for managing the Company. Responsibilities include:

- Achieving annual and long-term business targets
- Planning and building external contacts
- Monitoring the external and internal competitive landscape
- Monitoring new industry developments and standards
- Identifying opportunities for expansion and acquisition
- Building relationships with customers and markets
- Leading and evaluating other executive leaders
- Acting as the link between the Board and Management

## Lead Independent Director

The lead independent director provides leadership to the independent directors, liaises on their behalf, and supports the Board’s effectiveness and high-quality governance. The lead independent director’s authority and responsibilities are determined by the group of independent directors, with additional duties assigned by the Board as required.

## Key Board qualifications, expertise and attributes

The following qualifications, skills, and attributes are considered when nominating candidates to the Board.

| Qualification or attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Financial | Leadership of a financial firm or management of an enterprise’s finance function, resulting in proficiency in complex financial management, capital allocation, and financial reporting, or experience supervising a principal financial officer, principal accounting officer, controller, public accountant, auditor, or person performing similar functions. |
| Diversity of perspectives | Representation of gender, ethnic, geographic, cultural, or other perspectives that broaden the Board’s understanding of customers, partners, employees, governments, and other stakeholders worldwide. |
| Global business | Experience driving business success in markets around the world, including an understanding of diverse business environments, economic conditions, cultures, regulatory frameworks, and global market opportunities. |
| Leadership | Extensive leadership experience at a significant enterprise, including practical understanding of organizations, processes, strategic planning, and risk management, along with demonstrated strengths in developing talent, planning succession, driving change, and supporting long-term growth. |
| Information Technology | Significant technology background, including knowledge of anticipating technological trends, generating disruptive innovation, and extending or creating new business models. |
| Mergers and acquisitions | Experience leading growth through acquisitions and other business combinations, assessing “build or buy” decisions, analyzing strategic and cultural fit, valuing transactions, and evaluating operational integration plans. |
| Board service and governance | Service on a public company board, providing insight into Board and Management accountability, shareholder interests, and governance practices. |
| Sales and marketing | Experience developing strategies to grow sales and market share, build brand awareness and equity, and enhance enterprise reputation. |
| Sustainability and Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) | Experience leading organizational sustainability and ESG visions and integrating them into Company strategy. |
| Risk management | Experience identifying and evaluating significant risks to business strategy and assessing Management actions to mitigate strategic, legal, compliance, and operational risks. |
| Cybersecurity | Experience assessing and managing cybersecurity risks and implementing cybersecurity policies, procedures, and strategies. |

The core expertise and competencies of each director are provided in the Infosys Board of Directors section of the Integrated Annual Report.
