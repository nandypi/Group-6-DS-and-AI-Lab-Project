## a. Post-sales client support

The Company provides fixed-period post-sales support for fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts. Related support costs are accrued when the associated revenue is recognized and included in the Statement of Profit and Loss. The costs are estimated using historical experience and reviewed periodically for material changes in assumptions and likelihood of occurrence.

## b. Onerous contracts

Provisions for onerous contracts are recognized when the expected benefits from a contract are lower than the unavoidable costs of meeting future obligations. Estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable, based on estimated efforts or costs to complete the contract.

The provision is measured at the present value of the lower of the expected cost of terminating the contract and the expected net cost of continuing with the contract. Any impairment loss on assets associated with the contract is recognized before establishing the provision.

## 2.17 Income taxes

### Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss, except when it relates to items recognized directly in equity, in which case it is recognized in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized as income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment.

A deferred tax asset is recognized when it is probable that future taxable profit will be available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, are offset when the Company has a legally enforceable right to set off the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

Interim-period income tax provisions are based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions on exercise of employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.

The applicable Indian corporate statutory tax rate was 25.17% for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025.

Income tax expense for the year ended March 31, 2026 includes a reversal, net of provisions, of ₹779 crore. Income tax expense for the year ended March 31, 2025 includes provisions, net of reversals, of ₹97 crore. These prior-period provisions and reversals primarily relate to adjudication of disputed matters following the filing of tax returns and completion of assessments across various jurisdictions.

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Company received orders under sections 250 and 254 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment year 2013–14 and assessment years 2017–18 to 2021–22. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently:

- Pretax interest income of ₹381 crore was recognized.
- Income tax provisions aggregating ₹869 crore were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹86 crore following resolution of the disputes.

During the quarter ended March 31, 2025, the Company received orders under section 250 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, from the Income Tax Authorities in India for assessment years 2016–17 and 2019–20. The orders confirmed the Company's position on the tax treatment of certain matters. Consequently:

- Pretax interest income of ₹327 crore was recognized.
- Income tax provisions aggregating ₹183 crore were reversed, with a corresponding credit to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- Contingent liabilities were reduced by ₹1,068 crore following resolution of the disputes.

Foreign tax expense primarily relates to income taxes payable overseas, principally in the United States.

In India, the Company benefited in prior years from income tax incentives for software and services exports from units registered under the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005. Eligible units that began providing services on or after April 1, 2005, were eligible for:

- A deduction of 100% of profits or gains from export of services for the first five years from the financial year in which the unit commenced providing services.
- A deduction of 50% for the subsequent five years.
- A deduction of up to 50% for a further five years, subject to creation of a Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve and use of that reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes, in accordance with the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Refer to Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve under Note 2.12 Equity.

Deferred income tax for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially relates to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

Infosys is subject to a 15% Branch Profit Tax (BPT) in the United States when the U.S. branch's net profit during the year exceeds the increase in the branch's net assets during the year, as computed under the Internal Revenue Code. As at March 31, 2026:

- The U.S. branch's net assets were approximately ₹7,736 crore.
- The Company had a deferred tax liability for branch profit tax of ₹207 crore, net of credits, because it estimates that these branch profits are expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches because the Company controls the timing of reversal and expects that the differences will probably not reverse in the foreseeable future.
