## Our Strategy Approaching value creation

Infosys’ strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities in which it operates.

Infosys’ strategy is to help clients ideate, plan, and scale their AI future by building strong data foundations and robust cloud capabilities. The company also emphasizes safeguards for privacy, ethics, and controls through Responsible AI practices.

In 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy:

1. Scale Agile Digital
2. Energize the Core
3. Reskill our People
4. Expand Localization

The company describes the current AI transition as transformational. It involves a shift:

- From application-centric technology stacks to platforms incorporating intelligent agents, orchestration layers, and adaptive learning components.
- From linear, role-based workflows to AI-driven operating loops that continuously sense, decide, and act.
- From an emphasis on code creation toward orchestration, system supervision, domain interpretation, and outcome validation.

These changes require new roles, large-scale reskilling, and a recalibration of workforce composition.

## Infosys AI vision

Infosys aspires to be a leading partner in unlocking AI value and delivering business outcomes in revenue growth, cost optimization, and innovation.

The company has a two-pronged AI strategy:

- **AI-first services:** Capture new demand and drive growth.
- **AI-augmented services:** Reinvent existing services to win higher wallet share.

This strategy is supported by Infosys’ platforms and intellectual property, ecosystem partnerships, talent base, and brand.

AI-first services use AI as the core execution layer to enable new business models, products, and revenue streams for clients. AI-augmented services integrate AI into established systems and workflows to improve efficiency, personalization, and outcomes while preserving and extending the value of existing investments.

Infosys has developed more than 25 industry-focused AI playbooks covering repeatable use cases, domain context, and execution patterns. These playbooks are intended to help enterprises scale AI adoption from experimentation to enterprise-wide impact.

## AI First Value Framework

Infosys’ AI-first value framework is designed to help global enterprises unlock AI value at scale using its generative and agentic AI suite, Infosys Topaz™. Infosys cites market estimates for an incremental AI-first services opportunity of USD 300–400 billion by 2030.

The framework defines six interconnected value pools:

1. **AI Strategy & Engineering:** Designing and implementing AI strategies, platforms, and architectures. This includes orchestrating AI agents, proprietary platforms, and third-party tools on purpose-built infrastructure.
2. **Data for AI:** Preparing structured and unstructured enterprise data for AI model readiness through AI-ready data platforms and AI-grade data engineering, including fingerprinting and synthetic data services.
3. **Process AI:** Transforming core business processes by integrating AI agents and human expertise, redesigning end-to-end workflows, and enabling domain-aware agents to work alongside people.
4. **Agentic Legacy Modernization:** Using AI agents to reverse-engineer existing estates, understand their intent, and progressively modernize them without disruption.
5. **Physical AI:** Designing intelligent products and embedding AI into physical devices that capture sensor data, interpret signals, and take real-time action. Relevant capabilities include digital twins, robotics, autonomous systems, and edge intelligence.
6. **AI Trust:** Applying responsible, secure, and ethical AI practices throughout the AI lifecycle, including risk assessments, policy design, security testing, and governance.

Infosys states that it is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys.

## Platforms and IP

Infosys is leveraging Infosys Topaz Fabric™, a purpose-built, composable, and open agentic services suite, together with collaborations with AI disruptors, to deliver AI-augmented and AI-first services.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is an enterprise-grade AI services layer within Infosys Topaz. It embeds enterprise context across data, applications, processes, and systems, and integrates models, data pipelines, agents, and platforms across diverse technology environments. It is designed to help organizations reimagine business processes while building on existing investments and maintaining strategic autonomy.

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across humans, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve product innovation with Infosys’ global scale to transform enterprise operations and core business functions.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is designed for clients that have already made AI investments and need a composable and modular agentic AI platform. Infosys Topaz AI Next is designed for clients seeking an end-to-end, vertically integrated AI platform to reimagine core business processes.

The platforms institutionalize enterprise knowledge and process semantics and are designed to support AI deployment from experimentation through large-scale production. Responsible AI, security, governance, and assurance are embedded by design.
