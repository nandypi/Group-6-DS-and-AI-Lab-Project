## Our business context

Technology continues to reshape businesses across industries worldwide. AI is increasingly being applied to domain-specific capabilities and industry-focused use cases. Clients are addressing the need to reinvent processes and systems in the AI era, including through emerging technologies, new ways of working, AI ecosystems, business processes, and operational practices.

Infosys supports clients in transforming their businesses through technology, from building a digital core to reimagining business operations. The stated objectives include improving agility, efficiency, and resilience; accelerating growth initiatives; reconfiguring cost structures; improving resilience and agility; delivering personalized customer and employee experiences; and launching new products and services.

Responsible business practices, including the adoption of ESG principles, continue to gain momentum.

The traditional services market remains competitive, driven by rapid change and new entrants in specialized technology niches. Infosys identifies its industry expertise, end-to-end service capabilities, scalable digital solutions, established platforms, quality and process excellence, distributed agile global delivery model, experienced leadership team, skilled workforce, and execution track record as differentiators.

## Infosys services brands

Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Topaz, and Infosys Aster represent focused, domain-led offerings that bring together the company's capabilities and expertise for enterprise transformation needs across cloud, AI, and marketing.

- Infosys Cobalt is a set of services, solutions, and platforms for enterprises to accelerate their cloud journey.
- Infosys Topaz is an AI-first set of services, solutions, and platforms using generative and agentic AI technologies.
- Infosys Aster is an AI-amplified marketing suite focused on brand experiences, marketing efficiency, and business growth.
