For Foreign Institutional Investors (FII) and Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI), tax will be deducted under Section 393 of the IT Act 2025 at 20%, plus applicable surcharge and cess, or at the rate provided in the relevant DTAA, read with the MLI, whichever is more beneficial, subject to submission of the applicable documents.

The required documents must be uploaded on the shareholder portal by June 11, 2026. Shareholders can check their tax credit in Form 168, formerly Form 26AS, through their income-tax e-filing account.

Dividends that remain unclaimed for seven years from the date of transfer to the Company’s Unpaid Dividend Account will be transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). Shares on which dividends remain unclaimed for seven consecutive years will also be transferred to the IEPF in accordance with Section 124 of the Act and applicable IEPF rules.

In compliance with Section 108 of the Act, Regulation 44 of the LODR Regulations, and SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/242 dated December 9, 2020, members may vote electronically through the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) e-voting facility. Members who cast their votes through remote e-voting before the AGM may participate in the AGM but may not vote again on the same resolutions.

Hemanth, Holla & Co., Practicing Company Secretaries, represented by Hemanth Holla (Membership No. FCS 6374; CP No. 6519), has been appointed as Scrutinizer for the e-voting process.

Members holding shares in physical or dematerialized form as of the cut-off date, June 16, 2026, may vote electronically. Remote e-voting will begin on Thursday, June 18, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. IST and end on Monday, June 22, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. IST. The NSDL e-voting module will be disabled thereafter.

Voting rights will be proportionate to each member’s share of the Company’s paid-up equity share capital as of June 16, 2026. Persons who are not members as of the cut-off date may treat the Notice as being provided for information only.

Voting will also be available during the AGM. Members attending through video conferencing who have not voted remotely and are otherwise eligible may vote through the e-voting system during the AGM.

Persons holding shares in physical mode, or persons who acquire shares and become members after the Notice is sent but hold shares as of June 16, 2026, may obtain login credentials by requesting them from NSDL. Members already registered for remote e-voting may use their existing user ID and password.

The Integrated Annual Report for 2025-26, the Notice of the 45th AGM, and e-voting instructions will be sent electronically to members whose email addresses are registered with the Company or their depository participant(s). Members without registered email addresses will receive a letter containing a web link and access path to the Integrated Annual Report.

Members holding shares in dematerialized form who have not registered their email addresses should update them with their depository participant. Members holding physical shares should update their email addresses and bank details with the Company’s registrar and transfer agent, KFin Technologies Limited.

An Explanatory Statement under Section 102 of the Act for the business under item numbers 4 to 6, together with additional information under Regulation 36 of the LODR Regulations concerning directors seeking appointment or reappointment at the AGM, forms part of the Notice.

Physical shareholders must furnish the following KYC details:

- PAN
- Contact details, including postal address with PIN and mobile number
- Bank-account details
- Specimen signature

Effective April 1, 2024, dividends will be paid exclusively through electronic mode to physical shareholders who have completed the mandatory KYC requirements.

| Type of holder | Investor service or requirement | Form or process |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Register or update PAN, email address, bank details, and other KYC details for securities held in physical mode | Form ISR-1 |
| Physical | Update the signature of the securities holder | Form ISR-2 |
| Physical | Submit a nomination under Rule 19(1) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 | Form SH-13 |
| Physical | Declaration to opt out of nomination | Form ISR-3 |
| Physical | Cancel a nomination or change the nominee | Form SH-14, along with ISR-3 where applicable |
| Physical | Request a duplicate certificate or other service requests for shares, debentures, or bonds held in physical mode | Form ISR-4 |
| Demat | Register or update email address and bank-account details | Contact the depository participant and follow its prescribed process |

Under Section 72 of the Act, members holding shares in physical mode may submit a nomination using Form SH-13. Members holding shares in dematerialized mode may contact their depository participants to update nomination details.

The Scrutinizer will submit the report to the Chairman or another person authorized by the Chairman within 48 hours of the conclusion of the AGM. The voting results and Scrutinizer’s report will be communicated to the stock exchanges, NSDL, and the registrar and transfer agent, and displayed on the Company’s website.

As the AGM will be held through video conferencing, the Notice does not include a route map, proxy form, or attendance slip.
