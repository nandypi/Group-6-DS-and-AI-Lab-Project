## Outcome of the Board meeting

The Board meeting held on October 15-16, 2025 approved the following:

### Financial Results

1. Audited consolidated financial results of the Company and its subsidiaries under Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025.
2. Audited standalone financial results of the Company under Ind AS for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025.
3. Audited financial statements of the Company and its subsidiaries under Ind AS and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2025.

### Dividend

4. An interim dividend of ₹23 per equity share was declared.

- Record date: October 27, 2025
- Payout date: November 7, 2025

### Stock grants

5. Based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved:

- Grant of 109,893 Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan to eligible employees.
- Grant of Performance-Based Stock Incentives (PSUs) under the Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 to eligible employees. The grant covers equity shares having a market value of ₹44.20 lakh as of the grant date. The number of PSUs will be calculated using the market price at the close of trading on November 1, 2025.

The grants under the 2015 Plan will vest equally over three to four years. Grants under the 2019 Plan will vest over three years, subject to achievement of the performance parameters defined in the 2019 Plan. The RSUs and PSUs will be granted with effect from November 1, 2025, at an exercise price equal to the par value of the share.

### Incorporation of a subsidiary

6. The Board approved the incorporation of a step-down wholly owned subsidiary in Egypt, as a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte Ltd.

### Transfer of shareholding in a subsidiary

7. As part of an internal reorganization, the Board approved the transfer of 90% of the shareholding held by Infosys Limited in Infosys Consulting S.R.L., Argentina, to Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited.
