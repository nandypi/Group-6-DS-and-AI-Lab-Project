## Accounting Policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the period is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Cash flows from operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately. The Group considers highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (“the Company” or “Infosys”) provides consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services to enable clients to execute digital transformation strategies.

Infosys’s stated strategic objective is to build a sustainable organization that remains relevant to clients’ agendas, creates growth opportunities for employees, and generates profitable returns for investors. Its stated strategy is to help clients ideate, plan, and execute their journey to a digital future.

Infosys, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts, is referred to as the “Group.” The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary listings are on BSE Ltd. and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares, representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on October 16, 2025.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements were prepared in compliance with IAS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

They were prepared under the historical cost convention and on the accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities/(assets) recognized at the present value of defined benefit obligations less the fair value of plan assets.

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2025.

Accounting policies were applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard was initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard required a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information used in preparing the audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. Accordingly, quarterly figures may not always add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.

## 1.3 Basis of consolidation

Infosys consolidates entities it owns or controls. The interim condensed consolidated financial statements comprise the financial statements of the Company, its controlled trusts, and its subsidiaries.

Control exists when the parent has power over an entity, is exposed or has rights to variable returns from its involvement with the entity, and can affect those returns through its power over the entity. Power is demonstrated through existing rights that provide the ability to direct the relevant activities that significantly affect the entity’s returns. Subsidiaries are consolidated from the date control commences until the date control ceases.

Group company financial statements are consolidated on a line-by-line basis. Intra-group balances and transactions, including unrealized gains or losses, are eliminated on consolidation. The financial statements are prepared using uniform accounting policies across the Group.

Non-controlling interests represent the portion of the net profit or loss and net assets of subsidiaries that are not directly or indirectly owned or controlled by the Company.
