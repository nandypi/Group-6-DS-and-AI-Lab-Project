## 2.3.5 Impairment

The Group recognizes loss allowances using the expected credit loss (ECL) model for financial assets and unbilled revenue that are not fair valued through profit or loss.

Loss allowances for trade receivables and unbilled revenues with no significant financing component are measured at an amount equal to lifetime ECL. For all other financial assets, expected credit losses are measured at 12-month ECL unless there has been a significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition; in that case, lifetime ECL is used.

The Group determines allowances for credit losses based on historical loss experience, adjusted for current and estimated future economic conditions. It considers current and anticipated future economic conditions in the industries with which it deals and the countries where it operates.

The ECL, or reversal, required to adjust the loss allowance at the reporting date is recognized as an impairment loss or gain in the condensed consolidated statement of comprehensive income.
