## 5.Segment Reporting

The Company publishes standalone financial statements along with consolidated financial statements. In accordance with Ind AS 108, *Operating Segments*, segment information is disclosed in the audited interim consolidated financial statements. The segment information is included in the audited consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2025.

## IFRS -INR IFRS -INR Press Release

Quarterly revenue crossed the $5 billion mark, with second consecutive-quarter strong performance. Revenue grew 2.2% sequentially in Q2 and 3.3% in H1 in constant currency. Large-deal TCV was $3.1 billion, free cash flow was $1.1 billion, FY26 revenue guidance was revised to 2%-3%, and margin guidance was retained at 20%-22%.

Infosys delivered Q2 revenues of $5,076 million, representing year-over-year growth of 2.9% and sequential growth of 2.2% in constant currency. Operating margin was 21.0%. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion, equal to 131.1% of net profit. Total contract value of large deal wins was $3.1 billion, of which 67% was net new. Employee headcount increased by 8,203.

H1 revenues grew 3.3% year over year in constant currency. H1 operating margin was 20.9%.

Salil Parekh, CEO and Managing Director, stated that strong deal wins, including 67% net-new business in Q2, reflected client demand for AI-related value delivery. He also stated that Infosys’ investments over the prior three years in an AI-first culture had supported employee reskilling for a human-plus-AI workplace, and that Infosys Topaz was being used in transformation programs.

## Guidance for FY26:

- Revenue growth of 2%-3% in constant currency
- Operating margin of 20%-22%

## Key highlights:

### For the quarter ended September 30, 2025

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 2.9% year over year and 2.2% quarter over quarter.
- Reported revenues were ₹44,490 crore, up 8.6% year over year.
- Operating margin was 21.0%, down 0.1% year over year and up 0.2% quarter over quarter.
- Basic EPS was ₹17.76, up 13.1% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹9,677 crore, up 38.0% year over year; free-cash-flow conversion was 131.2% of net profit.

### For the six months ended September 30, 2025

- Revenues in constant-currency terms grew 3.3% year over year.
- Reported revenues were ₹86,769 crore, up 8.1% year over year.
- Operating margin was 20.9%, down 0.2% year over year.
- Basic EPS was ₹34.47, up 10.9% year over year.
- Free cash flow was ₹17,210 crore, up 6.5% year over year; free-cash-flow conversion was 120.4% of net profit.

Infosys reported strong Q2 performance, including growth, resilient margins, cash generation, and 13.1% year-over-year EPS growth in rupee terms. During the quarter, it announced a ₹18,000 crore share buyback and an interim dividend of ₹23 per share, representing a 9.5% increase over the prior fiscal year.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys transformed ABN AMRO’s lending process through an nCino platform implementation that consolidated multiple legacy systems into a unified platform. The stated objectives included improving operational efficiency, collateral-management capabilities, and customer transparency and speed.

- Infosys collaborated with Mastercard to provide financial institutions enhanced access to Mastercard Move’s money-movement capabilities and scale cross-border payments. The collaboration was described as supporting faster, more secure, and reliable cross-border payments, while improving control over risk, operations, costs, and liquidity.

- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Sunrise to accelerate IT transformation, focusing on data security, operational agility, and future AI integration. The work includes consolidating Sunrise’s technology landscape and developing more agile, predictive, and scalable platforms.

- Infosys announced a joint venture with Telstra in Australia by acquiring 75% of the shareholding in Versent Group, a wholly owned subsidiary of Telstra Group. The stated objective was to advance AI-enabled cloud and digital solutions for enterprises and accelerate Versent Group’s growth across the region.

- Infosys collaborated with RWE AG on automated digital workplace transformation and operational efficiency, including the use of modern technologies aligned with sustainability and efficiency goals.

- Infosys collaborated with HanesBrands Inc. on hyper-productivity and AI-driven efficiency across its digital, business-applications, and data landscape.

- Infosys collaborated with AGCO to deliver IT and HR operations transformation through an efficient, future-ready operating framework for growth.
