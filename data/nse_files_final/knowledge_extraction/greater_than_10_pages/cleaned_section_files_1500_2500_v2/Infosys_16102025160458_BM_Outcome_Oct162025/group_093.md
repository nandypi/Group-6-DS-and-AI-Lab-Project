## Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of the eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve is to be used for acquiring new plant and machinery for the Company's business under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Other components of equity

Other components of equity include:

- Currency translation
- Remeasurement of net defined benefit liability or asset
- Equity instruments fair valued through other comprehensive income
- Changes in the fair value of investments
- Changes in the fair value of derivatives designated as cash flow hedges, net of taxes

## Currency translation reserve

Exchange differences arising from translating the financial statements of foreign subsidiaries whose functional currency is other than the Indian rupee are recognized in other comprehensive income and presented within equity.

## Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the cash flow hedge reserve is transferred to the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss when the related forecast transaction occurs.

No preferential amounts currently exist, other than amounts held by irrevocable controlled trusts. For such trusts, the corpus would be settled in favor of the beneficiaries.

## Capital allocation policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, based on the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Update on buyback announced in September 2025

At its meeting on September 11, 2025, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each from eligible equity shareholders, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

If approved, the buyback would comprise the purchase of 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company's total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis. The buyback price would be ₹1,800 per equity share, for a total amount of ₹18,000 crore.

The buyback is proposed for all eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who become eligible as of the record date by cancelling ADSs and withdrawing the underlying equity shares. The buyback would be conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. The record date would be determined by the Board or Buyback Committee.

The Company sent notice to shareholders as of September 26, 2025 seeking approval through postal ballot. Voting was expected to end on November 4, 2025.

The Company's capital management objective is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. To maintain or achieve this structure, the Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares.

As of September 30, 2025, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. There were no externally imposed capital requirements.

## Dividend

Final dividends are recorded as liabilities on the date of shareholder approval. Interim dividends are recorded as liabilities on the date of declaration by the Company's Board of Directors.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.
