## About Infosys

Infosys provides digital services and consulting. More than 320,000 employees support clients in 59 countries. The company has over four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises, with capabilities in cloud, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, digital skills development, and continuous learning.

Infosys states that it is committed to governance, environmental sustainability, and an inclusive workplace.
