AGCO Corporation stated that its collaboration with Infosys is intended to create a responsive, streamlined, and innovative operational ecosystem across IT and other functions, allowing teams to focus on strategic initiatives centered on farmers.

Uniting Financial Services (UFS), Australia, subscribed to Infosys Finacle’s Digital Banking SaaS suite on the AWS cloud. The go-live was completed in less than five months. UFS stated that the platform was intended to modernize its core banking and digital capabilities, improve the client experience, and support its future operational needs.

Infosys collaborated with Glion Arena Kobe as its official digital innovation and GX partner. The collaboration is intended to support smart and sustainable entertainment experiences through data aggregation, cloud technologies, new revenue opportunities, and seamless experiences for fans and visitors.

## Recognitions &amp; Awards

- Infosys received the Platinum Award for Excellence and the Best Investor Relations Team award at The Asset Corporate Sustainability Leadership Awards 2025.
- Infosys was recognized as one of the World’s Best Companies 2025 by TIME and Statista for employee satisfaction, revenue growth, and sustainability transparency.
- Infosys was recognized among the World’s Most Trustworthy Companies 2025 by Newsweek and Statista.
- Infosys was recognized as one of the Best Companies for Women in India in the Hall of Fame for the seventh consecutive year and among the 2025 Avtar &amp; Seramount Best Companies for Women in India in the IT sector.
- Infosys received the Exemplars of Inclusion recognition in the Most Inclusive Companies Index 2025 by Avtar &amp; Seramount.
- Infosys was ranked among the top 10 in the Best Companies for ESG in India category launched by Avtar &amp; Seramount in 2025.
- The Infosys Foundation and Infosys ESG Annual Report FY24-25 received Gold Stevie® Awards for Best Annual Report—Nonprofit Organizations and Other Publication—Company.
- The Infosys Integrated Report received a Silver Stevie® Award for Best Annual Report—Publicly Held Corporations at the 22nd Annual International Business Awards®.
- Infosys BPM received the 9th Edition Women Empowerment Summit and GIWL Award for Best Organization for Women Empowerment.
