# The estimated useful life is around 1 year to 7 years

The excess of the purchase consideration paid over the fair value of assets acquired has been attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill are the value of the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill amounting to $9 million is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of $96 million includes upfront cash consideration of $88 million and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $8 million as of the acquisition date.

At the acquisition date, the key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were the probabilities assigned to achieving financial targets and discount rates ranging from 2%–3%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of September 30, 2025 was approximately $9 million.

The acquisitions also include retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over two to three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Retention bonuses and management incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Statement of Comprehensive Income over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was $23 million as of the acquisition date. As of September 30, 2025, the amounts had been substantially collected.

Transaction costs incurred in connection with a business combination, including finder's fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of $4 million related to the acquisition were included in administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

## Proposed Acquisitions

On August 13, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 75% of the equity share capital of Telstra Purple Pty Ltd, including certain subsidiaries collectively known as Versent Group. Versent Group is described as an Australian digital transformation solutions provider.

The consideration, including earn-outs and deferred consideration, is up to AUD 233 million, approximately $152 million, excluding retention bonuses and management incentives. The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing adjustments.

## 2.11 Employees' Stock Option Plans (ESOP)

## Accounting Policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value of awards is recognized as an expense in net profit in the interim condensed consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, with a corresponding increase to share premium.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan)

On June 22, 2019, pursuant to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan shall not exceed 50,000,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 45,000,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on achievement of defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The performance parameters may include:

- Relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers.
- Broader market domestic and global indices.
- Operating performance metrics of the Company.

Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest based on performance. The instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan):

On March 31, 2016, pursuant to shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan shall not exceed 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were subsequently adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

The instruments generally vest over four years. Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares, while the exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 9,091,403 shares as of September 30, 2025 and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025 under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each date.

## Under the 2015 plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants became effective on May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs, described as the annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs vest based on achievement of specified performance targets in accordance with the employment agreement.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, described as the annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs vest based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, described as the annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs vest based on the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the vesting period, as determined by the Board.

Although the annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of September 30, 2025, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with IFRS 2, *Share-based Payment*, because the service commencement date preceded the grant date. For this purpose, the grant date under IFRS 2 was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the six months ended September 30, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved time-based grants of 237,370 ESOPs to other KMP under the 2015 Plan. These stock options vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price equals the market price on the grant date.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The model inputs include the share price on the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on historical volatility in the observed market prices of the Company's publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term of the options. Expected volatility for comparative companies is modeled using historical movements in the market prices of their publicly traded equity shares over an equivalent period. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
