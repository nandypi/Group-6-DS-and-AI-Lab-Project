## 2.14 PROVISIONS

## Accounting policy

A provision is recognized when, as a result of a past event, the Group has a present legal or constructive obligation that is reasonably estimable and an outflow of economic benefits is probable. Provisions are measured by discounting expected future cash flows at a pre-tax rate reflecting current market assessments of the time value of money and risks specific to the liability. A reimbursement asset is recognized only when receipt of the reimbursement is virtually certain if the Group settles the obligation.

## a. Post sales client support

The Group provides fixed-period post-sales support to clients under fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts. Related costs are accrued when the associated revenue is recorded and included in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss. Costs are estimated using historical experience and reviewed periodically for material changes in assumptions and likelihood of occurrence.

## b. Onerous contracts

Provisions for onerous contracts are recognized when the expected benefits from a contract are lower than the unavoidable costs of meeting future obligations. Estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable, based on estimated efforts or costs to complete the contract. The provision is measured at the present value of the lower of the expected cost of terminating the contract and the expected net cost of continuing the contract. Any impairment loss on associated assets is recognized before establishing the provision.

## 2.15 INCOME TAXES

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss unless it relates to items recognized directly in equity, in which case it is recognized in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date. Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts, subject to applicable exceptions.

Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when realization of the related tax benefit is no longer probable. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date that are expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized in income or expense in the period of enactment or substantive enactment.

A deferred tax asset is recognized when future taxable profit is probable against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when those earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to offset the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously. Interim income tax provision is based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions earned on the exercise of employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.
