## 2.19 Break-up of expenses and other income, net

## Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides gratuity, a defined benefit retirement plan, to eligible employees, mainly of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees upon retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. The trustees administer contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Required contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies by employee age.

Liabilities under defined benefit plans are determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest-rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its Balance Sheet. Re-measurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income.

## Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. Both employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. A portion is contributed to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted under Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to a government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the investment return of the trust and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries participate in defined contribution provident funds. Both employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no obligations beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in defined contribution superannuation plans. The Group has no obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated-absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating leave. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed due to unused entitlement accumulated at that date.

Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.

## Other income, net

Other income primarily comprises:

- Interest income
- Dividend income
- Gains or losses on investments
- Exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts
- Exchange gains or losses on translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## Foreign currency

### Functional currency

The functional currency of Infosys, its Indian subsidiaries, and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. Foreign subsidiaries use their respective local currencies as functional currencies. The financial statements are presented in Indian rupees, rounded to crore; one crore equals ten million.

### Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates prevailing at the Balance Sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income within exchange gains/(losses) on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in other comprehensive income as qualifying cash-flow hedges.

Non-monetary foreign-currency assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate prevailing when fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate prevailing on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized upon settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period of settlement. Foreign-currency revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate on the transaction date.

For translation of foreign subsidiaries’ financial statements into the presentation currency:

- Assets and liabilities are translated using the exchange rate at the Balance Sheet date.
- Revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated using the average exchange rate for the relevant periods.
- Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves under other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is disposed of in full, the relevant amount is transferred to net profit in the statement of comprehensive income. Changes in the parent’s ownership that do not result in loss of control are recorded through equity.

Other Comprehensive Income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair value adjustments arising from the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated at the exchange rate prevailing at the Balance Sheet date.

## Government grants

The Group recognizes government grants when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the statement of comprehensive income on a systematic and rational basis over the asset’s useful life. Grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in the statement of comprehensive income over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs.

## Operating Profits

Operating profit is computed as revenue less cost of sales, selling and marketing expenses, and administrative expenses.
