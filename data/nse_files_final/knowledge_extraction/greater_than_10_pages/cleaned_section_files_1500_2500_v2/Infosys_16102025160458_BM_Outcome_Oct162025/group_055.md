The fair value of the awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time- and non-market-performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The inputs to the models include the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility for the Company's equity shares is based on historical volatility of observed market prices over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is modeled using historical movements in their publicly traded equity shares over an equivalent period. Correlation coefficients are calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.

## 2.12 Income Taxes

## Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the interim condensed Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income, except for amounts relating to items recognized directly in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts, except where the deferred tax arises from the initial recognition of goodwill or an asset or liability in a transaction that is not a business combination and affects neither accounting nor taxable profit or loss at the time of the transaction. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced when it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and laws enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized as income or expense in the period that includes the enactment or substantive enactment date. Deferred tax assets are recognized to the extent that future taxable profit is probable and deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized.

Deferred taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when those earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Group offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to offset the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously. Interim income tax provisions are based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions on the exercise of employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.

These matters are pending before various income tax authorities. Management and its tax advisors expect the Group's position to be upheld on ultimate resolution and do not expect a material adverse effect on the Group's financial position and results of operations.

## 2.13 Earnings per equity share

## Accounting Policy

Basic earnings per equity share is computed by dividing net profit attributable to equity holders of the Group by the weighted average number of equity shares outstanding during the period.

Diluted earnings per equity share is computed using the weighted average number of equity shares used for basic earnings per equity share, adjusted for the weighted average number of shares that could have been issued upon conversion of all dilutive potential equity shares. Dilutive potential equity shares are adjusted for proceeds receivable had the shares been issued at fair value, based on the average market value of outstanding equity shares.

Dilutive potential equity shares are deemed converted at the beginning of the period unless issued later and are determined independently for each period presented.

The number of equity shares and potentially dilutive equity shares is adjusted retrospectively for share splits and bonus share issues, including changes effected before approval of the financial statements by the Board of Directors.
