## b) Update on employee stock grants

The Board, on October 16, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved:

- Grant of performance-based stock incentives (PSUs) to eligible employees under the Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (2019 Plan), covering the Company’s equity shares having a market value of ₹44.20 lakh as of the grant date.
- The number of PSUs will be calculated based on the market price at the close of trading on November 1, 2025.
- Grants made under the 2015 Plan will vest equally over three to four years.
- Grants made under the 2019 Plan will vest over three years, subject to the par value of the share.

## c) Proposed Buyback

The Board, at its meeting on September 11, 2025, approved a proposal to buy back 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each. The proposed buyback represents approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis.

The proposed buyback price is ₹1,800 per equity share. It is proposed to be made from all eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who become equity shareholders by cancelling American Depository Shares and withdrawing the underlying equity shares, as of the record date. The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route.

The Company sent a notice to shareholders on September 26, 2025, seeking approval through postal ballot. Voting was expected to end on November 4, 2025.

## 2. Information on dividends for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2025

The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹23 per equity share. The record date for payment is October 27, 2025, and the interim dividend will be paid on November 7, 2025. The interim dividend declared in the previous year was ₹21 per equity share.
