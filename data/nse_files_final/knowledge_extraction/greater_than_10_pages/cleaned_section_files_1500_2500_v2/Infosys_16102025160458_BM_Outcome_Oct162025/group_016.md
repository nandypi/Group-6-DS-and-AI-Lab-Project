## NOTES

1. The information is extracted from the audited condensed consolidated Balance Sheet and Statement of Comprehensive Income for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, taken on record at the Board meeting held on October 16, 2025.
2. A Fact Sheet providing the operating metrics of the Company can be downloaded from www.infosys.com.
3. Other income is net of finance cost.
4. Quarter and six-month figures are sourced and rounded to the nearest digits; therefore, quarterly figures may not always add up to the reported six-month figures.

## Guidance for FY26

- Revenue growth of 2%-3% in constant currency.
- Operating margin of 20%-22%.

## Key highlights

### Quarter ended September 30, 2025

- Revenues were $5,076 million, representing reported growth of 3.7% year over year.
- Revenue growth was 2.9% year over year and 2.2% sequentially in constant currency.
- Operating margin was 21.0%, down 0.1 percentage points year over year and up 0.2 percentage points sequentially.
- Basic EPS was $0.20, up 7.9% year over year.
- Free cash flow was $1,101 million, up 31.2% year over year, with free cash flow conversion of 131.1% of net profit.
- Large-deal total contract value was $3.1 billion, with 67% net new.
- Employee headcount increased by 8,203.

### Six months ended September 30, 2025

- Revenues were $10,018 million, representing reported growth of 4.3% year over year.
- Revenue growth was 3.3% year over year in constant currency.
- Operating margin was 20.9%, down 0.2 percentage points year over year.
- Basic EPS was $0.40, up 6.9% year over year.
- Free cash flow was $1,985 million, up 2.7% year over year, with free cash flow conversion of 120.4% of net profit.

The company reported 13.1% year-over-year EPS growth in rupee terms for Q2. During the quarter, Infosys announced a share buyback of ₹18,000 crores and an interim dividend of ₹23 per share, an increase of 9.5% over the previous fiscal year.

## Client Wins & Testimonials

- Infosys implemented the nCino platform for ABN AMRO, consolidating multiple legacy systems into a unified lending platform.
- Infosys collaborated with Mastercard to provide financial institutions enhanced access to Mastercard Move and scale cross-border payments.
- Infosys extended its strategic collaboration with Sunrise to support IT transformation, data security, operational agility, and future AI integration.
- Infosys announced a joint venture with Telstra in Australia by acquiring 75% of Versent Group, a wholly owned subsidiary of Telstra Group. The transaction is intended to expand AI-enabled cloud and digital solutions for enterprises.
- Infosys collaborated with RWE AG on automated digital workplace transformation and operational efficiency, using modern technologies aligned with sustainability and efficiency goals.
- Infosys collaborated with HanesBrands Inc. on productivity and AI-driven efficiency across digital, business applications, and data operations.
- Infosys collaborated with AGCO on IT and HR operations transformation and the development of a streamlined operational framework.
- Uniting Financial Services in Australia subscribed to Infosys Finacle’s Digital Banking SaaS suite on AWS cloud. The implementation was completed in less than five months.
- Infosys collaborated with Glion Arena Kobe as its official digital innovation and GX partner to support data aggregation, cloud technologies, new revenue opportunities, and sustainable entertainment experiences.
