## Accounting policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the period is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows are presented separately. Highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash are considered cash equivalents.

## 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited, together with its subsidiaries and controlled trusts referred to as the “Group,” provides consulting, technology, outsourcing, and next-generation digital services to support clients’ digital transformation.

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India. Its primary listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares representing equity shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s interim condensed consolidated financial statements were approved for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on October 16, 2025.

## 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The interim condensed consolidated financial statements are prepared in compliance with Indian Accounting Standard (Ind AS) 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, under the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

The statements are prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, guidelines issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India, and applicable Ind AS rules and amendments. They do not include all information required for a complete set of financial statements and should be read with the consolidated financial statements and related notes in the Company’s Annual Report for the year ended March 31, 2025.

Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except where a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing standard requires a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information is discussed in the respective notes.

Quarter and year-to-date figures are taken from the source and rounded to the nearest digits. As a result, quarter figures may not always add up to the year-to-date figures reported in the statement.
