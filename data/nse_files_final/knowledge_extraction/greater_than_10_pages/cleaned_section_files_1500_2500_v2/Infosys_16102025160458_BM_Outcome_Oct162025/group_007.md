## b) Update on employee stock grants

On October 16, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the grant of performance-based stock incentives (PSUs) to eligible employees under the Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (2019 Plan). The grants cover the Company’s equity shares having a market value of ₹44.20 lakh as of the grant date. The number of PSUs will be calculated using the market price at the close of trading on November 1, 2025.

Grants made under the 2015 Plan vest equally over three to four years. Grants made under the 2019 Plan vest over three years, subject to the Company achieving the performance parameters defined in the 2019 Plan. The RSUs and PSUs will be granted effective November 1, 2025, at an exercise price equal to the par value of the share.

## c) Proposed acquisition

On August 13, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 75% of the equity share capital of Telstra Purple Pty for consideration of up to AUD 233 million, approximately ₹1,335 crore, excluding retention bonuses and management incentives. The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing adjustments.

## d) Proposed Buyback

The Company proposed to buy back equity shares comprising approximately 2.41% of its total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, at a price of ₹1,800 per equity share. The buyback will be conducted on a proportionate basis through the “Tender offer” route, based on the equity shares held by shareholders as of the record date to be determined by the Board or Buyback Committee.

The Company sent a notice to shareholders as of September 26, 2025, seeking approval through postal ballot. Voting on the postal ballot was expected to end on November 4, 2025.

## 2. Information on dividends for the quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2025

The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹23 per equity share. The record date for payment is October 27, 2025, and the interim dividend will be paid on November 7, 2025. The interim dividend declared in the previous year was ₹21 per equity share.
