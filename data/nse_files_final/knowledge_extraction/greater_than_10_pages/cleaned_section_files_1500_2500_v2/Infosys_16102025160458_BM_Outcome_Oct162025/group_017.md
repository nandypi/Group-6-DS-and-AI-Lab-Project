### About Infosys

Infosys is a global digital services and consulting company. More than 320,000 employees work across 59 countries. The company has more than four decades of experience managing systems and operations for global enterprises and provides services powered by cloud and AI.

The company describes its capabilities as including an AI-first core, agile digital services at scale, continuous improvement, and transfer of digital skills, expertise, and ideas through its innovation ecosystem. It also states that it is committed to governance, environmental sustainability, diversity, and inclusion.
