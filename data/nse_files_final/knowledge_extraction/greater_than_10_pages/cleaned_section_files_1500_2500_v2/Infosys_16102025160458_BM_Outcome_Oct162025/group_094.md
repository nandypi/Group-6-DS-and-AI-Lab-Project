## Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense relating to share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair values of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value of awards is recognized as an expense in the statement of profit and loss on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, as if the award comprised multiple awards, with a corresponding increase to the share options outstanding account.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan):

On June 22, 2019, pursuant to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan shall not exceed 5,00,00,000 equity shares. To implement the 2019 Plan, up to 4,50,00,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan shall vest based on achievement of defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The performance parameters comprise a combination of relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers, broader domestic and global market indices, and the Company’s operating performance metrics. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. These instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan):

On March 31, 2016, pursuant to shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan shall not exceed 2,40,38,883 equity shares, including 1,12,23,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue. Instruments generally vest over four years.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs is equal to the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options is the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 90,91,403 shares as of September 30, 2025, and 96,55,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of each date.

## Under the 2015 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants were made effective May 2, 2025:

- 2,30,621 performance-based RSUs, described as an annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore. These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement based on achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, described as an annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore. These RSUs will vest based on achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, described as an annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore. These RSUs will vest based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Although the annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of September 30, 2025, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense under Ind AS 102, *Share-based Payment*, because the service commencement date preceded the grant date. The grant date for this purpose under Ind AS 102 was July 1, 2022.

## Under the 2019 Plan:

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs will vest based on achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Under the 2015 plan:

During the six months ended September 30, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved time-based grants of 237,370 ESOPs to other KMP under the 2015 Plan. These stock options will vest over four years and will be exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price will be the market price on the grant date.

The fair value of awards is estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time-based and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model is used for TSR-based options.

The model inputs include the share price on the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility during the expected term is based on the historical volatility of the observed market prices of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. Expected volatility for comparative companies is modeled using historical movements in the market prices of their publicly traded equity shares over a period equivalent to the expected term. The correlation coefficient is calculated between each peer entity and the indices as a whole, or between each entity in the peer group.
