## Changes in Subsidiaries

During the six months ended September 30, 2025, the following changes occurred in the subsidiaries:

- Infosys Energy Consulting Services LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, was incorporated on April 16, 2025.
- Infosys Saudi Arabia LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, was incorporated on April 21, 2025.
- Infosys Australia Technology Service Pty Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Limited, was incorporated on April 23, 2025.
- On April 30, 2025, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, acquired 98.21% of the partnership interests in MRE Consulting Ltd and its subsidiary, MRE Technology Services, LLC. The remaining 1.79% was acquired by Infosys Energy Consulting Services LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Nova Holdings LLC.
- On April 30, 2025, Infosys Australia Technology Service Pty Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Limited, acquired 100% of the voting interests in The Missing Link Automation Pty Ltd, The Missing Link Network Integration Pty Ltd, and The Missing Link Security Pty Ltd, along with their subsidiary, The Missing Link Security Ltd.
- in-tech Automotive Engineering de R.L. de C.V., a wholly owned subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, was liquidated effective May 7, 2025.
- On May 13, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte Ltd diluted its 2% stake in HIPUS Co., Ltd. to Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd.
- Infosys BPM Canada Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys BPM UK Limited, was incorporated on July 28, 2025.
- Infosys Germany GmbH, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte Ltd, merged into Infosys Germany SE, formerly known as Blitz 24-893 SE, effective September 24, 2025.

## 2.15 Segment reporting

IFRS 8 Operating Segments establishes standards for reporting information about operating segments and related disclosures about products and services, geographic areas, and major customers. The Group's operations predominantly relate to providing end-to-end business solutions to enable clients to enhance business performance.

The Chief Operating Decision Maker (CODM) evaluates the Group's performance and allocates resources based on various performance indicators by business segment. Accordingly, information is presented by business segment. The accounting principles used to prepare the financial statements are consistently applied to record revenue and expenditure in individual segments and are set out in the accounting policies.

The Group's business segments primarily comprise:

- Enterprises in Financial Services and Insurance
- Enterprises in Manufacturing
- Enterprises in Retail, Consumer Packaged Goods and Logistics
- Enterprises in Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services
- Enterprises in Communication, Telecom OEM and Media
- Enterprises in Hi-Tech
- Enterprises in Life Sciences and Healthcare
- All other segments

The Financial Services reportable segment aggregates the Financial Services and Finacle operating segments because of the similarity of their economic characteristics. All other segments represent operating segments of businesses in India, Japan, China, Infosys Public Services, and other enterprises in Public Services.

Revenue and identifiable operating expenses are categorized based on items individually identifiable to each segment. Revenue for “all other segments” represents revenue generated by Infosys Public Services, customers located in India, Japan, and China, and other enterprises in Public Services.

Allocated segment expenses include expenses incurred for services provided by the Group's offshore software development centers and on-site expenses, categorized according to the associated efforts of each segment. Certain expenses, including depreciation and amortization, are not specifically allocable to segments because the underlying assets are used interchangeably. These expenses are disclosed separately as “unallocated” and adjusted against the Group's total income.

Assets and liabilities used in the Group's business are not identified with reportable segments because they are used interchangeably between segments. Management considers it impracticable to provide segment disclosures for total assets and liabilities because meaningful segregation of the available data is onerous.

Business-segment revenue information is collated based on individual customers invoiced or for whom revenue is otherwise recognized.

Disclosure of revenue by geographic location is given in note 2.16, Revenue from operations.
