## Instructions for e-voting

The process to vote electronically on the NSDL e-Voting system consists of two steps.

## Step 1: Access to NSDL e-Voting system

## A) Login method for e-Voting for Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode

Under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/242 dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and depository participants. Shareholders are advised to update their mobile number and email ID in their demat accounts to access the e-Voting facility.

### Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode with NSDL

1. For OTP-based login, visit [NSDL e-Services](https://eservices.nsdl.com/SecureWeb/evoting/evotinglogin.jsp). Enter the 8-digit DP ID, 8-digit Client ID, PAN, and verification code, and generate an OTP. Enter the OTP received on the registered email ID or mobile number and log in. After authentication, shareholders will be redirected to the NSDL Depository site. Select the company name or e-Voting service provider, NSDL, to access the NSDL e-Voting website and cast the vote during the remote e-Voting period.

2. Existing IDeAS users can visit the [NSDL e-Services website](https://eservices.nsdl.com) on a computer or mobile device. Under the IDeAS section, select the “Beneficial Owner” icon under “Login” and enter the existing User ID and password. After authentication, select “Access to e-Voting” under Value Added Services, then select the company name or NSDL as the e-Voting service provider to access the e-Voting website.

3. Shareholders not registered for IDeAS can register through the [NSDL e-Services website](https://eservices.nsdl.com) by selecting “Register Online for IDeAS Portal” or using the [direct IDeAS registration page](https://eservices.nsdl.com/SecureWeb/IdeasDirectReg.jsp).

4. Shareholders can visit the [NSDL e-Voting website](https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/). Select “Login” under “Shareholder/Member” and enter the User ID, password or OTP, and verification code. The User ID is the sixteen-digit NSDL demat account number. After authentication, select the company name or NSDL as the e-Voting service provider to cast the vote during the remote e-Voting period.

5. Shareholders can download the NSDL Speede mobile app by scanning the QR code mentioned in the notice.

### Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode with CDSL

1. Users registered for CDSL Easi or Easiest can log in with their existing user ID and password. An option will be provided to access the e-Voting page without further authentication. Users should visit the [CDSL website](https://www.cdslindia.com), select the login icon and the “New System Myeasi” tab, and use their existing myEasi username and password.

2. After successful login, Easi or Easiest users can view the e-Voting option for eligible companies where e-Voting is in progress. Selecting the e-Voting option opens the e-Voting service provider’s page for casting the vote. Links to the systems of all e-Voting service providers may also be provided.

3. Shareholders not registered for Easi or Easiest can register through the CDSL website by selecting “Login,” then “New System Myeasi,” and the registration option.

4. Alternatively, shareholders can access the e-Voting page through the CDSL website by providing their demat account number and PAN. The system will authenticate the shareholder by sending an OTP to the registered mobile number and email address recorded in the demat account. After authentication, shareholders can view the e-Voting option for companies where voting is in progress and access the systems of e-Voting service providers.

### Login through depository participants

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode can use their demat account login credentials through a depository participant registered with NSDL or CDSL for e-Voting. After logging in, select the e-Voting option. Following authentication, shareholders will be redirected to the NSDL or CDSL Depository site, where they can select the company name or e-Voting service provider, NSDL, and access the NSDL e-Voting website.

**Important note:** Members unable to retrieve their User ID or password should use the “Forget User ID” and “Forget Password” options available on the relevant website.
