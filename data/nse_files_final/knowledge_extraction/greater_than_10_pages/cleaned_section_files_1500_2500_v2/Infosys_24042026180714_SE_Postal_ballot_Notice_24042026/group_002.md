## Item no. 2: Re-appointment of Helene Auriol Potier (DIN: 10166891), as an Independent Director for a second term of five years effective from May 26, 2026 to May 25, 2031

To consider and, if thought fit, pass the following resolution as a Special Resolution:

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to Sections 149, 150, 152, Schedule IV, and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the rules framed thereunder, applicable provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and the Articles of Association of the Company, and based on the approval and recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and the Board of Directors, Helene Auriol Potier (DIN: 10166891), who holds office as an Independent Director up to May 25, 2026, be and is hereby re-appointed as an Independent Director, not liable to retire by rotation, for a second term of five years commencing on May 26, 2026 and ending on May 25, 2031.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board be and is hereby authorized to delegate all or any of its powers to any committee of directors, with power to further delegate to any other officer or authorized representative of the Company, to take all acts, deeds, and steps necessary to give effect to this resolution.

## Notes

1. The explanatory statement pursuant to Section 102 read with Section 110 of the Companies Act, 2013, together with details under Regulation 36(3) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, is annexed to the notice.

2. The Postal Ballot Notice was sent to members whose names appeared on the register of members or the list of beneficial owners received from NSDL and CDSL, and whose email addresses were registered with the Company or their Depository Participant, as of April 17, 2026, the Cut-off Date.

   Voting on the proposed resolution is restricted to electronic voting. Physical copies of the notice, postal ballot form, and prepaid business reply envelope were not being sent. Member assent or dissent could be communicated only through the e-voting system.

3. Once a member casts a vote, it cannot subsequently be changed. Voting rights are proportional to the member’s shares in the paid-up equity share capital of the Company as of April 17, 2026, the Cut-off Date.

4. For registered holders of Infosys American Depositary Shares, April 17, 2026 was established as the ADS postal ballot cut-off date for determining eligibility to receive postal ballot materials and voting instructions. The Depositary was expected to use the notice to prepare voting instruction forms for registered holders of American Depositary Receipts evidencing ADSs. Beneficial holders of ADSs holding through the Depository Trust Company in street name were to receive the notice through the relevant mailing agents.

5. If passed by the requisite majority, the resolution would be deemed to have been passed on May 24, 2026, the last date specified for voting. Resolutions passed through postal ballot are deemed to have been passed as if they were passed at a general meeting of the members.

6. The Scrutinizer was to submit the report to the Chairman or a person authorized by the Chairman after completion of the e-voting scrutiny. The voting result was to be announced no later than two working days after the conclusion of e-voting and communicated to the stock exchanges and the Registrar and Transfer Agent.

7. Material documents referred to in the explanatory statement were available for electronic inspection on working days from dispatch of the notice until May 24, 2026, the last date for receipt of e-voting votes.

8. Members holding shares in electronic form who had not registered their email addresses were requested to register them with their Depository Participants. Members holding shares in physical form were requested to update their email addresses with the Company’s Registrar and Transfer Agent, KFin Technologies Limited.

   The following shareholder service forms and processes were specified for physical holdings:

| Type of holder | Investor service | Form or process |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Register or update PAN, email address, bank details, and other KYC details for securities held in physical mode | Form ISR-1 |
| Physical | Update the signature of the securities holder | Form ISR-2 |
| Physical | Nomination under Rule 19 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debenture) Rules, 2014 | Form SH-13 |
| Physical | Declaration to opt out of nomination | Form ISR-3 |
| Physical | Cancellation of nomination by the holder or change of nominee | Form SH-14, along with ISR-3 where applicable |
| Physical | Issue of duplicate certificate and other service requests for shares, debentures, bonds, and other securities held in physical form | Form ISR-4 |
| Demat | Register email address and bank account details in the demat account | Contact the relevant Depository Participant |

9. SEBI mandated the submission of PAN, KYC details, and nomination details by holders of physical securities, as well as linking PAN with Aadhaar, under circulars dated March 16, 2023, and November 17, 2023.

   Holders of physical securities were requested to submit these details to KFin Technologies Limited. Members holding shares in electronic form were requested to submit their PAN to their Depository Participant.

10. The Postal Ballot Notice was also placed on the websites of Infosys and NSDL.
