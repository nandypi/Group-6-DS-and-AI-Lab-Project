## Helpdesk for Individual Shareholders holding securities in demat mode for any technical issues related to login through Depository i.e. NSDL and CDSL.

Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may contact the relevant depository helpdesk for technical login issues.

## B) Login method for shareholders other than Individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode and shareholders holding securities in physical mode.

### How to Log-in to NSDL e-Voting website?

1. Visit the NSDL e-Voting website at <https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/>.
2. Select **Login** under the **Shareholder/Member** section.
3. Enter the User ID, password or OTP, and verification code. Shareholders registered for NSDL IDeAS may log in through <https://eservices.nsdl.com/>, select **e-Voting**, and proceed to cast their vote.
4. User IDs are assigned as follows:

| Manner of holding shares | User ID |
|---|---|
| Demat account with NSDL | 8-character DP ID followed by the 8-digit Client ID. |
| Demat account with CDSL | 16-digit Beneficiary ID. |
| Physical form | EVEN number followed by the registered folio number. |

5. Existing e-Voting users may use their current password.
6. First-time users must retrieve the initial password communicated by NSDL and change it when prompted.
7. If the email ID is registered with the depository or company, the initial password is provided by email in a PDF attachment. The PDF password is the 8-digit NSDL Client ID, the last 8 digits of the CDSL Client ID, or the folio number for physical shares.
8. If the email ID is not registered, follow the process described under [Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories](#process-for-those-shareholders-whose-email-ids-are-not-registered-with-the-depositories-for-procuring-user-id-and-password-and-registration-of-e-mail-ids-for-e-voting-for-the-resolutions-set-out-in-this-notice).
9. Shareholders who cannot retrieve or have not received the initial password may use the **Forgot User Details/Password?** option for demat holdings or **Physical User Reset Password?** for physical holdings. NSDL also provides OTP-based login.
10. Accept the Terms and Conditions and select **Login**.

## Step 2: Cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-Voting system.

1. After logging in, view the companies and voting cycles associated with the shareholder’s holdings.
2. Select Infosys Limited’s relevant **EVEN** during the remote e-Voting period.
3. Select assent or dissent, verify or modify the number of shares for which the vote is being cast, and select **Submit** followed by **Confirm**.
4. A successful vote generates a confirmation message. Shareholders may print the confirmation page.
5. Once a vote on a resolution is confirmed, it cannot be modified.

## General Guidelines for shareholders

1. Institutional shareholders must provide a scanned PDF or JPG copy of the applicable Board Resolution, Power of Attorney, or Authority Letter, together with the attested specimen signature of the authorized signatory. The documents may be submitted to the Scrutinizer or uploaded through the **Upload Board Resolution / Authority Letter** option in the e-Voting section.
2. Shareholders should keep their passwords confidential. Login is disabled after five unsuccessful password attempts and must then be reset through the applicable password-reset option.
3. Shareholders may refer to the NSDL shareholder FAQs and e-Voting user manual for additional procedural information.

## Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories for procuring user id and password and registration of e mail ids for e-voting for the resolutions set out in this notice:

1. For physical holdings, shareholders must provide the folio number, shareholder name, a scanned copy of the front and back of the share certificate, self-attested PAN, and self-attested Aadhaar.
2. For demat holdings, shareholders must provide the DPID-CLID or 16-digit Beneficiary ID, name, client master or Consolidated Account Statement, self-attested PAN, and self-attested Aadhaar.
3. Shareholders may alternatively request NSDL to provide the User ID and password by submitting the required documents.
4. Under the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through accounts maintained with depositories and Depository Participants. Their mobile number and email ID must be correctly updated in the demat account.
