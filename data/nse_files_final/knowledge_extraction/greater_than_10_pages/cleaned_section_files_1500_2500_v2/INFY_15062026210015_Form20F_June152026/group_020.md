## Enterprise Cloud Application Solutions ('ECAS')

Infosys ECAS Practice has been a strategic Salesforce partner for over two decades, delivering end-to-end advisory, implementation, integration, and managed services across the Salesforce ecosystem. Its services cover Agentforce Sales, Agentforce Service, Marketing Cloud Next, Commerce Cloud, Experience Cloud, Salesforce Platform, Data 360, Agentforce AI, Industry Clouds, AppExchange capabilities, and AI-led capabilities.

As a Salesforce Summit Partner, Infosys has invested in global Centers of Excellence, industry accelerators, and AI-first delivery models. Its partner ecosystem includes Conga, CloudSense, Copado, ServiceMax, and Certinia, supporting capabilities in quote-to-cash, service transformation, DevOps, and enterprise finance, health, and life sciences use cases.

Infosys has vertical expertise across Life Sciences, Healthcare, Communications, Financial Services, Automotive, Manufacturing, Consumer Goods, Retail, Public Sector, and Education. Its Salesforce-native solutions, Customer Experience (CX) AI Suite, and Salesforce AI accelerators support intelligent automation, predictive insights, and agentic workflows across customer journeys.

The acquisitions of Fluido and Simplus, Salesforce consulting companies with operations in the Nordics, EMEA, and the Americas, strengthened Infosys' global Salesforce footprint. They expanded Infosys' advisory, implementation, training, and change-led adoption capabilities for cloud-first and experience-led transformations.

## Digital Design and Experience Capabilities

Infosys' Digital Experience offering covers consulting and advisory, digital transformation, implementation and integration, management and operations, and business services using digital experience platforms (DXPs).

Key offerings include:

- **User Experience (UX) services:** Reimagining data, content, platforms, brands, and experiences around the human interactions clients seek to influence, including the use of generative AI capabilities.
- **Studio-as-a-Service:** Digital studios that combine design thinking, agile sprints, rapid prototyping, digital experience engineering, collaboration across globally connected studios, digital skills, and AI accelerators.
- **Digital Marketing services:** Personalized experiences and content using data-driven insights to strengthen customer relationships, brand experience, always-on marketing, and marketing-spend optimization.
- **Content Management and Marketing Operations:** Website management; content strategy, planning, and operations; digital asset management; multichannel campaign management across email, search, display, mobile, social media, and mobile applications; SEO/SEM; social media and promotion management; creative services; and e-commerce operations, including marketplace campaigns, catalog management, and loyalty management.
- **Digital Commerce services:** Creative commerce, omnichannel commerce, cloud-enabled commerce, and conversational commerce that extends traditional commerce and self-service capabilities.
- **Digital Interactions services:** Cross-channel experiences that connect physical and digital environments, including hyperpersonalized immersive experiences, digital platforms, and digital enterprise capabilities.

Infosys' in-house creative digital innovation agency, WongDoody, provides creative consulting, experience design, immersive experiences, and marketing capabilities. It has more than 350 global awards, over 20 design studios, and more than 15,000 designers, strategists, researchers, and marketers.

Infosys acquired Blue Acorn iCi, a specialty Adobe and digital marketing company in the United States, to support its digital experience services strategy.
