In addition, Infosys has established a Joint Value Office within its GCC model to support enterprise-wide alignment, measurable impact, and sustained transformation. The centralized function co-owns and manages an integrated roadmap with client stakeholders, prioritizes high-value use cases across lines of business, tracks benefit realization through defined KPIs, supports adoption through structured change management, and assigns accountability across business and technology teams.

## Services Brands:

Infosys’s services brands—Infosys Cobalt, Infosys Topaz, and Infosys Aster—are focused, domain-led offerings that combine the Company’s capabilities and expertise to address enterprise transformation needs across cloud, AI, and marketing.

## Infosys Aster

Infosys Aster is an AI-amplified marketing suite designed to support brand experiences, marketing efficiency, and business growth. It provides an integrated, real-time view across customers, brands, channels, and trends. The suite uses generative and agentic AI to support personalized experiences, real-time customer engagement, and autonomous marketing journeys.

Aster combines first-, second-, and third-party data in a governed, AI-ready ecosystem that supports real-time insights, predictive intelligence, and audience segmentation for B2B and B2C brands. The source states that brands using Aster have reported increases in repeat buyers and sales, as well as improvements in the cost of marketing operations.

Key capabilities include:

- **Engaging brand experiences:** Uses human experience design, real-time Unreal Engine 3D, AR/VR/XR, digital-twin CGI modeling, AI-powered customer and marketing data platforms, and ecosystem capabilities to support immersive experiences and omnichannel engagement.
- **Enhanced marketing efficiency:** Combines generative-AI-amplified content and creative services, recommendations from the MarTech stack, and marketing insights to support faster, cost-effective campaigns. Marketing shared services centralize and automate content and design operations. Infosys Topaz-powered AI analyzes performance data and adapts channel strategies in real time.
- **Accelerated effectiveness for business growth:** Creates shared digital foundations for sales and marketing. AI-driven analytics and customer insights support customer-lifetime-value improvement through personalized outreach, customer conversations, and lead generation.
- **AI-enabled growth:** Uses predictive marketing analytics to anticipate customer needs, behaviors, and preferences. Customer intelligence, real-time insights, and ecosystem partners support campaign design, messaging, and demand-aligned inventory decisions. Agentic AI can deploy autonomous agents that plan, execute, and refine experiences across the customer journey.

Infosys Aster is built on Infosys’s Cloud, Data & AI ecosystems, including Infosys Cobalt and Infosys Topaz. It is supported by products including Adobe, Sitecore, Magnolia, Optimizely, Newgen, and SAP Commerce, as well as AI assets and tools. The suite covers creative services, content supply chain, MarTech, e-commerce, performance marketing, and marketing operations.
