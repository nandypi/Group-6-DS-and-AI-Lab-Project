## What does it mean to act with integrity and transparency?

Acting with integrity and transparency means being ethical, sincere, and open in all transactions. Personal accountability helps demonstrate to clients and employees that they can rely on Infosys. Employees and leaders are expected to keep commitments, act consistently with stated values, and speak up when they are uncomfortable or uncertain, particularly regarding actions, conditions, or behaviors that contradict Infosys' values and culture.

## What does it mean to create client value?

Client value means bringing ideas and recommendations that are in the client's best interests and fulfilling professional responsibilities in a way that supports long-term partnerships.

This means employees should:

- Consider the customer's perspective and view the business through the client's eyes.
- Work consistently to improve customer satisfaction, including by soliciting honest feedback through regular surveys.
- Develop a memorable customer experience and support customer retention and referrals.

## What does it mean to lead by example?

Infosys seeks to inspire and mentor employees to achieve exceptional results.

This means employees should:

- Act with fairness and honesty in all dealings and remain objective and transaction-oriented.
- Ensure that supervisors and direct reports understand and follow the Code, company policies, and applicable laws.
- Know what resources are available to provide help.
- Support employees who, in good faith, ask questions, raise concerns, or cooperate with investigations.
- Raise integrity concerns immediately. Violations of the Code, company policies, or applicable laws frequently become more serious over time.

## What does it mean to be fair?

Fairness in the workplace means respecting the rights of everyone who works with Infosys.

This means employees should:

- Treat employees fairly while considering differing skills, abilities, and circumstances.
- As managers, communicate expectations and evaluation criteria.
- Give employees an equal opportunity to be heard, whether they are sharing ideas or raising grievances.
- Discourage politicking and encourage team members to communicate openly to resolve issues.
- Give generous credit and recognize employees for their ideas and contributions.

## What does it mean to be excellent?

Excellence is the pursuit of the highest standards and a commitment to continuous learning and upskilling to remain relevant in a rapidly evolving technological environment.

## THE CODE IS MORE THAN JUST WORDS ON A PAGE—IT'S A WAY OF LIFE FOR INFOSYS

The Code of Conduct expresses Infosys' commitment to conducting business ethically. It explains expectations for acting with integrity and transparency and for following Infosys' culture and values.

Members of the Infosys organization are expected to follow both the letter and the intent and spirit of the Code. This means they should:

- Understand the areas covered by the Code, company policies and procedures, and laws applicable to their work.
- Follow the legal requirements of all locations where Infosys does business.
- Conduct themselves consistently with the Code, company policies and procedures, and applicable laws.
- Speak up if they have concerns or suspect violations of the Code, company policies and procedures, or laws.
- Certify when requested that they have reviewed, understood, and agreed to follow the Code.
- Understand that following the Code is a mandatory part of their job.

The Code cannot address every situation. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and ask questions when they need guidance or clarification. Resources include managers, the Office of Integrity and Compliance, Human Resources, the Legal Department, the Helpline, and other resources listed at the end of the Code. Employees should also be aware of company policies and procedures applicable to their work; the Policy Portal serves as a repository for these policies.

## WHAT ARE MY RESPONSIBILITIES?

## I Follow the Code

The Code applies equally to all Infosys directors, officers, and employees globally across its subsidiaries. It also applies to partners, suppliers, agents, and others acting on the Company's behalf. Employees are expected to know and follow the Code as a guideline for decision-making paired with integrity.

## I Lead by Example

Regardless of role, everyone is expected to lead on ethical matters and be accountable for their actions. Employees are expected to act with responsibility and integrity in accordance with Infosys' C-LIFE values.
