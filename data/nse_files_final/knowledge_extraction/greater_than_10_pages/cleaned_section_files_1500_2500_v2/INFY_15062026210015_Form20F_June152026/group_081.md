During fiscal 2026, 2025, and 2024, the Group designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions included in the cash flow hedge reserve as of March 31, 2026 are expected to occur and be reclassified to the statement of comprehensive income within three months.

The Group determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at inception and through periodic prospective assessments.

If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal but the risk management objective remains unchanged and the hedge continues to qualify for hedge accounting, the relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in profit or loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.

## Credit risk

Credit risk refers to the risk of counterparty default resulting in a financial loss. Maximum exposure at the reporting date was primarily from trade receivables of $3,715 million as of March 31, 2026 and $3,645 million as of March 31, 2025, and unbilled revenue of $1,816 million and $1,764 million, respectively.

Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are typically unsecured and are derived primarily from customers in the United States of America and Europe. The Group manages credit risk through credit approvals, credit limits, and continuous monitoring of customer creditworthiness. It uses the expected credit loss model and a provision matrix to calculate allowances for trade receivables and unbilled revenue, considering credit reports and other available credit information.

Credit risk is influenced mainly by individual customer characteristics and concentration among the largest customers. Customer exposure is diversified, and no single customer contributed more than 10% of outstanding trade receivables and unbilled revenue.

Days Sales Outstanding was 67 days as of March 31, 2026 and 69 days as of March 31, 2025.

Credit risk on cash and cash equivalents is limited because the Group generally invests in deposits with banks that have high ratings from international and domestic credit rating agencies. Ratings are monitored periodically, using the latest available ratings as of the date of approval of the Consolidated Financial Statements.

The Group’s investments primarily include mutual fund units, quoted debt securities, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, and quoted bonds issued by government and quasi-government organizations. The Group assesses counterparty risk using criteria including Tier I capital, capital adequacy ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and deposit base. These risks are monitored regularly under the Group’s risk management program.
