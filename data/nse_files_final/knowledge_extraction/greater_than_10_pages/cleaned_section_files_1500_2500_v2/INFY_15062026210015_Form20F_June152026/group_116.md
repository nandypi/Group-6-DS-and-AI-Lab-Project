## Compliance with Regulations

## Information Requests

Each ADS holder and beneficial owner shall provide information that Infosys or the depositary may request pursuant to applicable law, including India and United States law, Infosys’ memorandum and articles of association, applicable board resolutions, the requirements of relevant markets or exchanges, and the requirements of any electronic book-entry system through which ADSs or ADRs may be transferred.

Requested information may relate to:

- The capacity in which the holder owns or owned ADRs.
- The identity of other persons interested in the ADRs.
- The nature of those interests.
- Other applicable matters.

## Disclosure of Interests

Each ADS holder shall comply with Infosys’ memorandum and articles of association and applicable India law regarding notification of interests in the equity shares underlying the ADSs. This includes disclosure of certain acquisitions and dispositions of ADSs or underlying equity shares within the prescribed period.

ADS holders must also respond to requests made under India law, the rules and requirements of the New York Stock Exchange and other applicable stock exchanges, and Infosys’ memorandum and articles of association. Requests may seek information about the capacity in which an ADS holder owns ADSs, the identity of other persons interested in the ADSs, the nature of those interests, and other relevant matters.

## Fees and Expenses

ADS holders must pay service fees to the depositary bank, as well as applicable taxes and governmental charges. They may also be responsible for fees, expenses, taxes, and other governmental charges payable on the deposited securities represented by their ADSs.

The depositary service fees, taxes, and governmental charges are described under “Item 12. Description of Securities Other Than Equity Securities—Fees and charges payable by holders of our ADSs” in Infosys’ Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

## Payment of Taxes

ADS holders are responsible for taxes and other governmental charges payable, or that become payable, on their ADSs or the deposited securities represented by their ADSs.

The depositary may:

- Refuse to register or transfer ADSs.
- Refuse to permit withdrawal of deposited securities.
- Apply payments owed to an ADS holder toward unpaid taxes or charges.
- Sell deposited securities represented by the ADSs to pay taxes or charges.

If deposited securities are sold, the depositary may reduce the number of ADSs accordingly and pay the ADS holder any remaining net proceeds or deliver any remaining property after payment of the taxes or charges. The ADS holder remains liable for any deficiency.

ADS holders agree to indemnify and hold harmless Infosys, the depositary, the custodian, and their respective agents, directors, employees, and affiliates against claims relating to taxes, including applicable interest and penalties, arising from a tax refund, reduced withholding rate, or other tax benefit obtained for the ADS holder. These obligations survive transfers or surrenders of ADRs, withdrawals of deposited securities, and termination of the deposit agreement.

## Reclassifications, Recapitalizations and Mergers

## If we:

- Reclassify, split up, or consolidate any deposited securities.

## Then:

- Each ADS will automatically represent its equal share of the new deposited securities.

The source text also lists the following actions without specifying the corresponding treatment:

- Distribution of securities on equity shares that are not distributed to ADS holders.
- Recapitalization.
- Reorganization.
- Merger.
- Liquidation.
- Sale of all or substantially all assets.
- Similar actions.

## Amendment and Termination

## How may the deposit agreement be amended?

Infosys may agree with the depositary to amend the deposit agreement and the form of ADR without ADS holder consent.

If an amendment adds or increases fees or charges, other than specified taxes, governmental charges, and certain depositary expenses, or prejudices a substantial existing right of ADS holders, it will not become effective for outstanding ADSs until 30 days after the depositary notifies ADS holders.

By continuing to hold ADSs after an amendment becomes effective, ADS holders are considered to agree to the amendment and to be bound by the amended ADRs and deposit agreement.

If new laws require the deposit agreement to be amended for compliance, Infosys and the depositary may amend it in accordance with those laws. Such an amendment may become effective before notice is given to ADS holders.

## How may the deposit agreement be terminated?

The depositary will terminate the deposit agreement at Infosys’ request after giving ADS holders at least 90 days’ notice.

The depositary may also terminate the deposit agreement if:

- It has notified Infosys that it wishes to resign, or Infosys has removed it; and
- Infosys has not appointed a new depositary within 60 days.

In either case, the depositary must notify ADS holders at least 30 days before termination.

After termination, the depositary and its agents will only:

- Collect distributions on deposited securities.
- Sell rights and other property.
- Deliver equity shares and other deposited securities upon cancellation of ADSs after payment of applicable fees, charges, taxes, and governmental charges.

Six months or more after termination, the depositary may sell remaining deposited securities by public or private sale. It will hold the sale proceeds and other cash under the deposit agreement for the pro rata benefit of ADS holders who have not surrendered their ADSs. The depositary will not invest the money and has no liability for interest.

After the sale, the depositary’s only obligations will be to account for the money and other cash. Following termination, Infosys will be discharged from all obligations under the deposit agreement except its obligations to the depositary.

## Books of Depositary

The depositary will maintain ADS holder records at its depositary office. ADS holders may inspect these records during regular business hours solely to communicate with other holders about business matters relating to Infosys, the ADRs, and the deposit agreement.

The depositary will maintain facilities in Manhattan, New York, to record and process the issuance, cancellation, combination, split-up, and transfer of ADRs. These facilities may be closed when the depositary considers closure necessary or advisable in connection with its duties, or at Infosys’ reasonable written request.

The depositary may distribute some or all cash, shares, or other securities it receives. It may deliver new ADSs or require holders to surrender outstanding ADRs in exchange for new ADRs identifying new deposited securities.

If received shares or securities cannot be lawfully distributed, the depositary may sell them by public or private sale and distribute the net proceeds.

## Limitations on Obligations and Liability

The deposit agreement limits the obligations and liability of Infosys, the depositary, and the custodian. They are only obligated to take actions specifically set forth in the deposit agreement, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

They are generally not liable when performance is prevented, prohibited, delayed, or subject to civil or criminal penalties or restraints because of:

- Present or future laws or regulations of the United States, any U.S. state, India, or another country.
- Requirements of governmental or regulatory authorities or stock exchanges.
- Infosys’ memorandum and articles of association.
- Provisions governing deposited securities.
- Events beyond their control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, nationalization, expropriation, currency restrictions, work stoppages, strikes, civil unrest, revolutions, rebellions, explosions, and computer failure.

They are also generally not liable for:

- Exercising or failing to exercise discretion permitted under the deposit agreement, Infosys’ memorandum and articles of association, or provisions governing deposited securities.
- Actions or inactions taken in good-faith reliance on advice or information from legal counsel, accountants, persons presenting equity shares for deposit, ADS holders, beneficial owners, authorized representatives, or other persons believed competent to provide such advice or information.
- An ADS holder’s or beneficial owner’s inability to benefit from a distribution, offering, right, or other benefit made available to holders of deposited securities but not to ADS holders under the deposit agreement.
- Special, consequential, indirect, or punitive damages for breach of the deposit agreement or otherwise.
- Reliance in good faith on documents believed to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper party.
