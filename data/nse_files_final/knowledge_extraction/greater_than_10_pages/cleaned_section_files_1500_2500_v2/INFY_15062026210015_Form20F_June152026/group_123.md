## 2. Prohibition on communicating or procuring UPSI

An Insider shall not:

- Communicate, provide, or allow access to any UPSI relating to the Company or its Securities to any person, including other Insiders, except as permitted by this Code or the SEBI Regulations.
- Procure from or cause the communication by an Insider of UPSI relating to the Company or its Securities.

These restrictions do not apply when UPSI is communicated, provided, made accessible, or procured:

- In furtherance of legitimate purposes, performance of duties, or discharge of legal obligations, subject to appropriate notice and the execution of confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements.
- When the Board directs or causes public disclosure of UPSI in the best interest of the Company.
- Within a group of persons identified and secluded from the rest of the Company by the Compliance Officer through a “Chinese wall” or information barrier for a particular purpose or specified period. Such persons must be subject to additional confidentiality obligations, information barriers preventing exchanges of UPSI outside the Chinese wall, and an undertaking to abstain from or forego trading during the seclusion period, until the UPSI becomes generally available information, or until it otherwise ceases to constitute UPSI.

The Company will determine the applicable Chinese wall procedures and the process for permitting a designated person to cross the wall from time to time.

## 3. Prohibition on Insider Trading

### 1. Trading while in possession of UPSI

An Insider shall not, directly or indirectly:

- Trade in Securities of the Company that are listed or proposed to be listed while in possession of UPSI.
- Trade in Securities of the Company except when the Trading Window is open and the Insider is not in possession of UPSI.
- Provide advice or tips to any third party on trading in the Company’s Securities while in possession of UPSI.

### 2. Trading in securities of other companies

No Designated Person possessing unpublished price-sensitive information about another public company, obtained during employment with the Company, shall:

- Trade in the Securities of that other public company.
- Tip or disclose material non-public information concerning that company to anyone.
- Give trading advice of any kind concerning that other public company.

### 3. Derivative transactions

No Designated Person shall take positions in derivative transactions involving the Securities of the Company.

### 4. Exceptions

The restrictions in paragraph 3(1) may not apply to:

- An off-market inter-se transfer between Insiders who possessed the same UPSI without breaching this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
- A transaction carried out through the block deal window mechanism between persons who possessed UPSI without breaching this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
- A transaction carried out pursuant to a statutory or regulatory obligation.
- A transaction undertaken pursuant to the exercise of stock options where the exercise price was predetermined in accordance with applicable regulations.
- Trades pursuant to a Trading Plan established in accordance with this Code and the SEBI Regulations.

These exceptions reflect the statutory exceptions in Regulation 4(1) of the SEBI Regulations. They do not preclude prior approval or other requirements relating to trading in the Company’s Securities under this Code.

## 4. Trading Window

1. The Compliance Officer shall notify a Trading Window during which Designated Persons may trade in the Company’s Securities after obtaining pre-clearance in accordance with this Code.

2. Designated Persons and their Immediate Relatives shall not trade in the Company’s Securities when the Trading Window is closed.

3. The Trading Window shall generally be closed for all Designated Persons from the sixteenth day before the last day of any financial period for which results are required to be announced by the Company until 48 hours after disclosure of the financial results.

4. The Trading Window shall also be closed for a Designated Person or class of Designated Persons when the Compliance Officer determines that they can reasonably be expected to possess UPSI. The closure shall continue for the period determined by the Compliance Officer and required by the SEBI Regulations. The relevant Designated Person or class of Designated Persons shall be notified of such special blackout periods.

5. The Trading Window may be reopened no earlier than 48 hours after the relevant UPSI becomes generally available information or is no longer classified as UPSI.

6. The Trading Window restriction shall not apply to:

   - An off-market inter-se transfer between Insiders who possessed the same UPSI without violating this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
   - A transaction carried out through the block deal window mechanism between persons who possessed UPSI without violating this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
   - A bona fide transaction carried out pursuant to a statutory or regulatory obligation.
   - A transaction undertaken pursuant to the exercise of stock options where the exercise price was predetermined in compliance with applicable regulations.
   - Trades executed under a Trading Plan established in accordance with this Code.
   - A pledge of shares for a bona fide purpose, such as raising funds, subject to pre-clearance by the Compliance Officer.
   - Transactions undertaken in accordance with SEBI regulations, including acquisition through conversion of warrants or debentures, subscription to a rights issue, further public issue, preferential allotment, or tendering of shares in a buyback offer, open offer, or delisting offer, and other mechanisms specified by SEBI.

## 5. Pre-clearance of Trading

1. All Designated Persons intending to trade in Securities of the Company, either in their own name or through their Immediate Relatives, must obtain pre-clearance when the estimated value of the Securities to be traded, whether in one transaction or a series of transactions during a calendar quarter, exceeds Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh). This includes buying, selling, gifting, transferring, pledging, or un-pledging Securities.

   The application must be submitted to the Compliance Officer in the format set out in Annexure 1. It must state the estimated number of units to be traded and other details specified in the form. The applicant must also declare, in accordance with Annexure 2, that they are not in possession of UPSI.

2. The Compliance Officer shall not approve a proposed trade if the Designated Person is in possession of UPSI, even when the Trading Window is open.

3. After being satisfied that the application and undertaking are true and accurate, the Compliance Officer may approve the trade subject to execution within seven trading days after the approval date.

4. The Board of Directors shall be the approving authority for the Compliance Officer’s pre-clearance application.

5. Within two days of executing the trade, the Designated Person shall submit the trade details to the Compliance Officer in accordance with Annexure 3. If the transaction is not undertaken, a report to that effect must be filed in the same form.

6. If a pre-cleared trade is not executed within seven trading days after approval, the Designated Person must obtain pre-clearance again.

7. Pre-clearance is not required for a trade executed under an approved Trading Plan or pursuant to transmission of Securities.

8. A Designated Person who trades without complying with the pre-clearance procedure, or who gives false undertakings or makes misrepresentations in the undertakings submitted during the pre-clearance process, shall be subject to the penalties specified in this Code.

## 6. Additional Trading Restrictions on Designated Persons

1. No Designated Person shall enter into derivative transactions involving the Securities of the Company.

2. A Designated Person who trades in Securities of the Company shall not enter into a Contra Trade during the six months following the prior transaction.

   If a Contra Trade is executed, inadvertently or otherwise, in violation of this restriction, the profits from the trade shall be liable to be disgorged for remittance to SEBI for credit to the Investor Protection and Education Fund administered by SEBI.

3. The Contra Trade restriction shall not apply to the exercise or sale of Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) Securities, provided that the Designated Person does not possess UPSI, the sale is executed when the Trading Window is open, and pre-clearance is obtained.

4. The Contra Trade restriction shall not apply to transmission of Securities.

## 7. Trading Plan

1. A Designated Person may formulate a Trading Plan that complies with the SEBI Regulations and submit it to the Compliance Officer for approval and public disclosure.

   The Trading Plan may be executed only after it has been approved by the Compliance Officer and disclosed to the stock exchanges on which the Company’s Securities are listed.

2. A Designated Person may delegate discretionary authority to their broker. However, after the Trading Plan commences, the Designated Person shall not consult with the broker regarding the execution of transactions or disclose information concerning the Company that might influence execution of transactions under the Trading Plan.
