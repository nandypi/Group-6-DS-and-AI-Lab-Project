## 2.15 Equity

### Ordinary Shares

Ordinary shares are classified as equity. Incremental costs directly attributable to issuing new ordinary shares, share options, and buybacks are recognized as a deduction from equity, net of tax effects.

### Treasury Shares

When an entity within the Group purchases the company’s ordinary shares, the consideration paid, including directly attributable incremental costs, is presented as a deduction from total equity until the shares are cancelled, sold, or reissued. When treasury shares are subsequently sold or reissued, the amount received is recognized as an increase in equity, and the resulting surplus or deficit is transferred to or from share premium.

### Share premium

Amounts received in excess of par value are classified as share premium. Share-based compensation recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income is also credited to share premium. Amounts have been utilized for bonus issues and share buybacks from the share premium account.

### Retained earnings

Retained earnings represent the accumulated earnings of the Group.

### Other Reserves

The Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve was created from the profit of an eligible SEZ unit under Section 10AA(1)(ii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The reserve must be utilized by the Company to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under Section 10AA(2) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

### Capital Redemption Reserve

Under Section 69 of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, the Company creates a capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value of shares bought back, appropriated from general reserves or retained earnings.

### Cash flow hedge reserve

When a derivative is designated as a cash flow hedging instrument, the effective portion of changes in its fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve. The cumulative gain or loss previously recognized in the reserve is transferred to net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income when the related forecast transaction occurs.

### Other components of equity

Other components of equity include currency translation; remeasurement of the net defined benefit liability or asset; fair value changes of equity instruments measured through other comprehensive income; and changes in the fair value of investments, net of taxes.

### 2.15.1 Voting

Each holder of equity shares is entitled to one vote per share. Equity shares represented by American Depositary Shares (ADS) carry the same voting and dividend rights as other equity shares. Each ADS represents one underlying equity share.

### 2.15.2 Liquidation

In the event of liquidation, shareholders are entitled to receive the remaining assets of the Company after distribution of all preferential amounts. No such preferential amounts currently exist, other than amounts held by irrevocable controlled trusts. Distributions are proportional to the number of equity shares held. For irrevocable controlled trusts, the corpus is settled in favor of the beneficiaries.

### 2.15.3 Share options

Holders of options issued under the Company’s share option plans have no voting, dividend, or liquidation rights.

### 2.15.4 Share capital and share premium

The Company has one class of equity shares with a par value of ₹5 each. Controlled trusts held 8,650,911 shares as of March 31, 2026, and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025.

## Capital Allocation Policy

Effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue its policy of returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and required approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Buyback completed in December 2025

In line with the capital allocation policy, the Board approved a proposal at its September 11, 2025 meeting to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each for ₹18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot. Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting, with results declared on November 6, 2025.

The offer comprised the purchase of 100,000,000 equity shares, representing approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800 per equity share. The buyback was offered to eligible equity shareholders as of November 14, 2025, the record date, on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. The tender period ran from November 20 through November 26, 2025.

The Company concluded the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025. All 100,000,000 equity shares were bought back and extinguished. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs, approximately $2,000 million. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

As of March 31, 2026, the Company had created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore, approximately $6 million, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, appropriated from the general reserve under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Buyback completed in February 2023

In line with the capital allocation policy, the Board approved a proposal at its October 13, 2022 meeting to buy back equity shares through the open-market route on Indian stock exchanges for up to ₹9,300 crore, excluding buyback tax, at a price not exceeding ₹1,850 per share, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting, with results declared on December 3, 2022. The buyback was offered to all equity shareholders other than the Promoters, Promoter Group, and persons in control of the Company. The buyback commenced on December 7, 2022, and was completed on February 13, 2023.

During the buyback period, the Company purchased and extinguished 60,426,348 equity shares at a volume-weighted average buyback price of ₹1,539.06 per equity share. The shares represented 1.44% of the pre-buyback paid-up equity share capital.

The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹9,300 crore, excluding transaction costs and buyback tax, equivalent to $1,130 million. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

As of March 31, 2023, the Company had created a Capital Redemption Reserve of $3 million, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back, appropriated from general reserves and retained earnings under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013.

The Company’s objective in managing capital is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and maintain an optimal capital structure. To maintain or achieve this structure, the Company may adjust dividend payments, return capital to shareholders, issue new shares, or buy back issued shares. As of March 31, 2026, the Company had one class of equity shares and no debt. Consequently, there were no externally imposed capital requirements.
