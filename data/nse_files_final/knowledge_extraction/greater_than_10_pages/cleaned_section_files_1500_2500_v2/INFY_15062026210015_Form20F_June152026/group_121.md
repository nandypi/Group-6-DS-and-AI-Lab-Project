## Infosys Limited Code of Conduct for Prohibition of Insider Trading

## I. BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVE

Infosys Limited’s equity shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE), and are subject to rules and regulations issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). The Company’s American Depositary Shares (ADSs) are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE). Infosys is a reporting company subject to applicable US securities laws, including the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and regulations of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). ADSs representing the Company’s equity shares are also considered securities or shares of the Company.

The Board adopted this code to regulate, monitor, and report trading by Designated Persons and their Immediate Relatives, as defined in the code. The code is intended to comply with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, as amended, and applicable US securities laws.

The code applies to Designated Persons and the Immediate Relatives of Designated Persons.

The SEBI Regulations prohibit an Insider from trading in the securities of a company listed on a stock exchange while in possession of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI).

## II. CODE OF CONDUCT

## 1. Definitions

1. **Act** means the SEBI Act, 1992 (15 of 1992).

2. **Audit Committee** means the committee of the Board constituted under Section 177 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Regulation 18 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI LODR).

3. **Board** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

4. **Compliance Officer** means the Company Secretary or another senior officer designated by the Company who reports to the Board, or the head of the organization where there is no Board. The Compliance Officer must be financially literate and capable of understanding legal and regulatory compliance requirements under the SEBI Regulations.

   The Compliance Officer is responsible for compliance with policies and procedures, maintenance of records, monitoring adherence to the preservation of UPSI, monitoring trades, and implementing the codes specified under the SEBI Regulations under the overall supervision of the Board.

5. **Connected Person** means:

   i. A Director of the Company.

   ii. A Key Managerial Personnel of the Company.

   iii. An Officer of the Company.

   iv. Any person who is or has been in a contractual, fiduciary, or employment relationship with the Company at any time during the six-month period before determining whether that person was, as a result of the relationship, directly or indirectly allowed access to UPSI or could reasonably be expected to be allowed access to UPSI.

   v. Any person who is or has been in frequent communication with an Officer of the Company at any time during the six-month period before determining whether that person was, as a result of the communication, directly or indirectly allowed access to UPSI or could reasonably be expected to be allowed access to UPSI.

   vi. An employee of the Company who has access to UPSI or could reasonably be expected to have access to UPSI.

   vii. Any person who has a professional or business relationship with the Company, whether temporary or permanent, where that relationship directly or indirectly allows access to UPSI or could reasonably be expected to allow access to UPSI.

   The following persons are deemed to be Connected Persons unless the contrary is established:

   1. A relative of a person specified in clauses (i) to (vii) above.
   2. A holding company, associate company, or subsidiary company.
   3. An intermediary specified in Section 12 of the SEBI Act, or an employee or director of such intermediary.
   4. An investment company, trustee company, or asset management company, or an employee or director of such entity.
   5. An official of a stock exchange, clearing house, or clearing corporation.
   6. A member of the board of trustees of a mutual fund, a member of the board of directors of the asset management company of a mutual fund, or an employee of either.
   7. A member of the board of directors or an employee of a public financial institution as defined in Section 2(72) of the Companies Act, 2013.
   8. An official or employee of a self-regulatory organization recognized or authorized by SEBI.
   9. A banker of the Company.
   10. A concern, firm, trust, Hindu undivided family, company, or association of persons in which a Director of the Company, the Director’s Relative, or the Company’s banker has more than 10% holding or interest.
   11. A firm, or its partner or employee, in which a Connected Person specified in clauses (i) to (vii) above is also a partner.
   12. A person sharing a household or residence with a Connected Person specified in clauses (i) to (vii) above.

6. **Contra trade** means a trade or transaction involving the buying or selling of the Company’s securities followed, within six months, by an opposite transaction involving the selling or buying of those securities.

7. **Designated Person** includes:

   i. Promoters of the Company.

   ii. Directors and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company and its material subsidiaries.

   iii. Employees in Finance and Accounts, Corporate Planning, Legal, Enterprise Risk Management, Corporate Strategy, Investor Relations, Information Security, Data Privacy, Mergers & Acquisitions, Corporate Secretarial, Marketing, Compensation and Benefits, and any other departments of the Company and its material subsidiaries, where designated based on their functional role or access to UPSI.

   iv. The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and employees up to two levels below the CEO of the Company and its material subsidiaries.

   v. Executive Secretaries of Directors, Key Managerial Personnel, and Executive Officers of the Company, and other support staff, including IT and secretarial staff, who are likely to have access to UPSI.

   vi. Any other person designated based on a functional role that provides access to UPSI.

8. **Director** has the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013.

9. **Generally available information** means information accessible to the public on a non-discriminatory basis. It does not include unverified events or information reported in print or electronic media. Information published on the websites of stock exchanges where the Company’s securities are listed, or disseminated through a Company press release, would ordinarily be considered generally available.

10. **Immediate Relative** means the spouse of a person and includes the person’s or spouse’s parent, sibling, or child if financially dependent on that person or if they consult that person when making decisions relating to trading in securities.

11. **Insider** means any person who is:

   i. A Connected Person; or

   ii. In possession of or having access to UPSI.

12. **Key Managerial Personnel** has the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013.

13. **Leak of UPSI** refers to an act or circumstance through which UPSI is made available or becomes available, by any means or mode, to any person, association, body, firm, agency, society, entity, or group before the information becomes generally available. It also includes any purported attempt to do so.

   This includes instances where UPSI is shared with any person, association, body, firm, agency, society, entity, or group except in compliance with applicable law.

14. **Legitimate purpose** includes sharing UPSI in the ordinary course of business by an Insider with partners, collaborators, lenders, customers, suppliers, merchant bankers, legal advisers, auditors, insolvency professionals, or other advisers or consultants, provided that the sharing is not carried out to evade or circumvent the prohibitions of the SEBI Regulations. Annexure 8 provides an illustrative list of legitimate purposes.

15. **Material financial relationship** means a relationship in which one person receives any kind of payment, such as a loan or gift, from a Designated Person during the immediately preceding 12 months, equivalent to at least 25% of the annual income of that Designated Person. Relationships involving payments based on arm’s-length transactions are excluded.

16. **Officer** has the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013.

17. **Promoter and Promoter Group**

   **Promoter** has the meaning assigned under the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, or any modification thereof.

   **Promoter Group** has the meaning assigned under the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, or any modification thereof.

18. **Relatives** means:

   i. The spouse of the person.

   ii. The parent of the person and of the person’s spouse.

   iii. The sibling of the person and of the person’s spouse.

   iv. The child of the person and of the person’s spouse.

   v. The spouse of a person referred to in clauses (iii) and (iv) above.

19. **Securities** has the meaning assigned under the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, or any modification thereof, and includes ADSs.

20. **Trading** or **Trade** includes subscribing, redeeming, switching, buying, selling, dealing in, or agreeing to subscribe, redeem, switch, buy, sell, pledge, revoke or invoke a pledge, or otherwise deal in the Company’s securities, either directly or through portfolio management services.

21. **Trading day** means a day on which recognized stock exchanges are open for trading.
