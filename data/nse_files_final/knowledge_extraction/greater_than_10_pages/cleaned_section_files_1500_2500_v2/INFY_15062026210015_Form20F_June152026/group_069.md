## Governance

Infosys’s cybersecurity program is managed by the Information Security Group (ISG), which uses a multi-layered defense-in-depth approach supported by cybersecurity policies, processes, and preventive, detective, and corrective controls.

A full-time Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) has overall responsibility for the cybersecurity program. The CISO and ISG identify, detect, assess, and mitigate risks and threats relating to information technology systems, networks, and data. They also establish and implement cybersecurity policies, processes, and practices; monitor the cyber threat landscape, security incidents, risks, cybersecurity metrics, and technology implementations; and support strategic improvements.

The ISG includes the Cyber Defense Centre, Technical Operations, Business Security, and Risk Management teams.

Infosys has established a Board-level Risk Management Committee (RMC), composed exclusively of independent directors. The RMC oversees, evaluates, and approves the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework and related practices, including the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of strategic, operational, and external-environment risks.

The Board has constituted a Cybersecurity Risk Subcommittee under the RMC. The Subcommittee includes four independent directors and assesses cybersecurity-related risks and the company’s preparedness to mitigate and respond to those risks. It receives periodic updates from management and recommends its findings, if any, to the RMC. The Board receives periodic updates from the RMC on cybersecurity and other information security matters.

The Information Security Council (ISC) governs and oversees Infosys’s Information Security Management System. It establishes, directs, and monitors the information security governance framework and reports to the Operational Risk Council and the Legal & Compliance Risk Council. The ISC meets periodically to discuss cybersecurity and other information security matters and to monitor adherence to the information security program.

Infosys operates with a hybrid employee model and monitors global cybersecurity events to maintain its cybersecurity posture and compliance. Infosys is certified against the Information Security Management System Standard ISO 27001:2022. It has also received independent-firm attestations for SSAE 18 and ISAE 3402 SOC 1 and SOC 2.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys strengthened its cybersecurity posture through technology, tools, and process improvements. Initiatives included continuous cybersecurity workforce training and reskilling, development of an inclusive security culture, and strengthened capabilities in AI/ML security, cyber resiliency, and secure supply-chain ecosystems. Infosys also provided the developer community with dedicated courses and resource kits to support cybersecurity processes, technologies, and overall security posture.

## Report of management

Management is responsible for preparing Infosys Limited’s consolidated financial statements and related information included in the Annual Report. Management believes that the consolidated financial statements fairly reflect the form and substance of transactions and reasonably present the financial condition and results of operations of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries in conformity with International Financial Reporting Standards as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

The consolidated financial statements include amounts based on estimates and judgments that management believes are reasonable under the circumstances.

Infosys maintains internal procedures and controls intended to provide reasonable assurance, at appropriate cost, that:

- Transactions are executed in accordance with company authorization.
- Transactions are properly recorded and reported in the consolidated financial statements.
- Assets are adequately safeguarded.

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the company’s consolidated financial statements for the years ended March 31, 2026, March 31, 2025, and March 31, 2024, in accordance with PCAOB standards.

The Board of Directors has appointed an Audit Committee composed of outside directors. The Committee meets with management, internal auditors, and independent auditors to review internal accounting controls and accounting, auditing, and financial reporting matters.

## Opinion on the Financial Statements

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the consolidated balance sheets of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries as of March 31, 2026 and 2025; the related consolidated statements of comprehensive income, changes in equity, and cash flows for each of the three years in the period ended March 31, 2026; and the related notes.

In the auditor’s opinion, the consolidated financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the Group’s financial position as of March 31, 2026 and 2025, and its results of operations and cash flows for each of the three years in the period ended March 31, 2026, in conformity with the IFRS Accounting Standards issued by the International Accounting Standards Board.

The auditors also audited the Group’s internal control over financial reporting as of March 31, 2026, based on the Internal Control—Integrated Framework (2013) issued by the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission. Their report dated June 15, 2026 expressed an unqualified opinion on the Group’s internal control over financial reporting.

## Basis for Opinion

The consolidated financial statements are the responsibility of Group management. The auditors’ responsibility is to express an opinion based on their audits. The auditors are registered with the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board and are independent of the Group under applicable U.S. federal securities laws and Securities and Exchange Commission and PCAOB rules and regulations.

The audits were conducted in accordance with PCAOB standards. The procedures included assessing the risks of material misstatement due to error or fraud; examining, on a test basis, evidence regarding amounts and disclosures; evaluating accounting principles and significant management estimates; and evaluating the overall presentation of the consolidated financial statements. The auditors stated that the audits provided a reasonable basis for their opinion.
