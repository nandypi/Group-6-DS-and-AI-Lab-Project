## Capital allocation policy and related payouts

As per the present capital allocation policy, effective fiscal 2025, the Company expects to continue returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semiannual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

The Board, at its meeting held on April 23, 2026, recommended a final dividend of ₹25 per equity share, approximately $0.26 per equity share, for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. Payment of the dividend is subject to shareholder approval at the Company’s AGM on June 23, 2026. If approved, the dividend would result in a net cash outflow of approximately $1,067 million, excluding dividends on treasury shares.

> Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS.

Dividend and buyback amounts include applicable taxes.

In line with the capital allocation policy, the Board approved a proposal at its September 11, 2025 meeting to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each, for an amount of ₹18,000 crore, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the proposal through e-voting by postal ballot, with results declared on November 6, 2025. The buyback comprised 100,000,000 equity shares, approximately 2.41% of the Company’s total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800 per equity share.

The buyback was offered to all eligible equity shareholders, including shareholders who became equity shareholders by cancelling American Depositary Shares and withdrawing the underlying equity shares, as of the November 14, 2025 record date. It was conducted on a proportionate basis through the tender-offer route.

The tender period ran from November 20 through November 26, 2025. The Company concluded the buyback procedures on December 4, 2025. All 100,000,000 equity shares were bought back and extinguished. The buyback resulted in a cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, excluding transaction costs, and was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures about Market Risk

## General

Market risk relates to market-sensitive financial instruments, including foreign-currency receivables and payables. The value of these instruments may change due to movements in interest rates, foreign-currency exchange rates, commodity prices, equity prices, and other market factors.

The Company’s exposure to market risk depends on its revenue-generating activities and any future foreign-currency borrowing activities. The objective of market-risk management is to avoid excessive exposure of earnings and equity to loss. Most market-risk exposure arises from foreign-currency revenues, receivables, and payables.

The Company uses Alternative 1 under Item 11 of Form 20-F for quantitative market-risk disclosures. The required information is included in the market-risk components below or incorporated by reference to Note 2.3 under Item 18 of the Annual Report.
