(1) Includes cash and cash equivalents acquired of $5 million.

The excess of the purchase consideration paid over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill were the value of the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill is not tax-deductible.

The purchase consideration of $24 million included cash of $20 million and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of $4 million at the acquisition date.

At the acquisition date, the key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were the probabilities assigned to achievement of financial targets and a discount rate of 5.9%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026, was $4 million.

The acquisition also included retention bonuses and management incentives payable to employees of the acquiree over three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Bonuses and incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income over the period of service.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was $4 million at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts had been fully collected.

Transaction costs incurred by the Group in connection with a business combination, including finder’s fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of less than $1 million related to the acquisition were included under administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Comprehensive Income for the year ended March 31, 2025.

## in-tech Holding GmbH

On July 17, 2024, Infosys Germany GmbH, a wholly owned step-down subsidiary of Infosys Limited, acquired 100% of the voting interests in in-tech Holding GmbH, a provider of engineering R&D services headquartered in Germany. The acquisition was expected to strengthen Infosys’ engineering R&D capabilities and support its global clients’ digital engineering activities.
