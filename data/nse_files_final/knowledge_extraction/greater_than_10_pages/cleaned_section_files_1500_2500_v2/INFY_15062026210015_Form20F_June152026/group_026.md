## Infosys Wingspan

Infosys Wingspan is an AI-first, mobile-first talent transformation platform that supports enterprise learning, continuous reskilling, and workforce readiness. It is used in enterprise-wide people and transformation initiatives, including Training as a Service (TaaS), digital and generative AI skilling programs, and structured onboarding and transition journeys.

The platform supports guided learning pathways, role-based journeys, and blended learning models combining self-paced digital content, instructor-led programs, and experiential learning. It also provides social and collaborative learning, decentralized content creation, and analytics-driven insights.

Its scalable architecture and mobile accessibility support consistent learning experiences across geographies. Outcome-oriented measurement frameworks help organizations assess learning effectiveness and align capability development with business priorities.

## Infosys Panaya

Panaya is a SaaS-based company certified by SAP, Oracle, and Salesforce.com. It offers an integrated platform for smart testing and change intelligence for ERP, CRM, and cloud business applications.

Panaya provides AI-powered test automation, test management, and impact analysis. Its platform supports collaboration between business and IT users, real-time project visibility, faster releases, and continuous delivery of software.

Founded in 2006, Panaya serves Fortune 500 companies in enterprise business application testing and change management.

## Infosys Stater

Infosys Stater operates across the mortgage and consumer loan value chain, with capabilities in digital origination, servicing, and collections. Its digital transformation accelerators include dynamic workflow, API layers, robotic process automation (RPA), and analytics.

## SALES AND MARKETING OVERVIEW

Infosys has organized its sales and marketing functions into teams across 59 countries. Its geographic footprint and industry expertise support global delivery tailored to local client requirements.

Infosys's sales and marketing activities promote enterprise transformation through its AI-first value framework, domain expertise, partner ecosystem, and Infosys Topaz generative and agentic AI suite.

## COMPETITION

Infosys operates in an intensely competitive environment characterized by competition in traditional services, rapid technological change, and specialized providers serving niche areas. Cloud, AI, and platform architectures have lowered barriers to entry, enabling niche providers to offer software-as-a-service platforms, industry-specific products, configurable AI models, agents, and plug-ins embedded in enterprise workflows.

Large technology platforms continue to expand their service layers, while clients selectively insource capabilities as AI improves productivity and reduces dependence on external labor-based models.

Infosys has invested in proprietary intellectual property across software platforms and products. Its investments include:

- Infosys Cobalt, which accelerates enterprise cloud transformation.
- Infosys Topaz, an AI-first portfolio of services, solutions, and platforms.
- Infosys Aster, an AI-amplified marketing suite.

These offerings are designed to integrate with client ecosystems, partner technologies, and emerging AI capabilities while incorporating enterprise context, governance, and security.

## HUMAN CAPITAL

As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 328,594 employees, including 310,887 professionals involved in service delivery to clients and trainees.

Infosys recruits fresh graduates from universities, colleges, and institutes globally, as well as developers, architects, technical leaders, and project managers in digital, cloud, and transformation areas. It recruits students from campuses in India and countries including the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Singapore, Japan, Germany, Canada, Mexico, Mauritius, and China.

Its selection process evaluates mathematical and logical aptitude, coding ability, and interview performance. The process is assessed and refined using factors including performance tracking of past recruits.

Infosys's InfyTQ platform provides training and assessments, including Infosys Certification opportunities. HackWithInfy is an online coding contest used in its recruitment efforts.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys received 5,884,480 employment applications, interviewed 450,901 applicants, and extended employment offers to 87,286 applicants. These statistics exclude subsidiaries.
