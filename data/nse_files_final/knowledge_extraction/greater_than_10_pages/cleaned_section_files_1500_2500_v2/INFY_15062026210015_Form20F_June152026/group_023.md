## Business Process Management

Infosys BPM provides end-to-end Business Process Management (BPM) services. Its consultative approach uses customer insights, domain expertise, empathetic intelligence, generative AI, and agentic AI to support business-process transformation and innovation. Infosys BPM also uses AI to support employee skill enhancement, reskilling, complex problem-solving, judgment-based decision-making, and domain expertise.

Infosys BPM offers services in:

- Finance & Accounting (F&A)
- Sourcing & Procurement (S&P)
- Sales & Fulfilment (S&F)
- Customer Service (CS)
- Human Resource Outsourcing (HRO)
- Legal Process Management (LPM)
- Digital Interactive Services (DIS)
- Digital Transformation Services (DTS)
- Analytics (AT)
- Generative AI
- Business Transformation Services
- Annotation Services for AI and machine learning
- Learning Services
- Master Data Management
- Geospatial Data Services
- Business Process as a Service (BPaaS)
- Global Capability Centers (GCC)
- Financial Crime Compliance (FCC)
- Trust & Safety (T&S)
- Travel, Transport & Hospitality (TTH)
- Industry solutions

Industry coverage includes banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI), manufacturing, HiTech, retail, services, utilities, resources and energy (SURE), and telecommunications.

Platform-based digital capabilities include:

- McCamish NGIN, a policy administration and insurance platform
- Stater, a utilities platform for the mortgage industry
- A sourcing and procurement joint venture in Japan with Hitachi, Panasonic, Mitsubishi, and Pasona
- APOC (Account Payables on Cloud), an enterprise capabilities platform

## Digital Workplace Services

Infosys Digital Workplace Services (DWS) provides AI-first, human-centric, and sustainable workplace solutions focused on work, workforce, and workplace transformation. Its offerings use intelligent automation, adaptive learning platforms, and connected work environments to support employee experience, operational resilience, agility, collaboration, hybrid work, and productivity.

DWS offerings include:

1. **AI-first Workplace Strategy and Advisory Services:** Develops AI-led workplace strategies, AI-enabled work models, ROI-driven business cases, and experience transformation using Experience Management Offices (XMOs) and Experience Level Agreements (XLAs).
2. **Next-generation AI service desk:** Uses agentic virtual assistants to personalize, automate, and autonomously resolve IT and HR interactions at scale.
3. **DaaS and Cloud Endpoint Management:** Provides secure, cloud-native device and application management, including virtual desktops.
4. **Digital Collaboration:** Provides AI-enhanced collaboration that supports team connectivity, routine-task automation, and information flow.
5. **Unified Communication:** Provides an AI-driven Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) platform for messaging, calling, file sharing, and collaboration.
6. **Employee Experience:** Designs adaptive, human-centric, AI-led experiences that integrate people, processes, and technology.
7. **Connected and Sustainable Workspaces:** Supports sustainable workplaces through device circularity, smart buildings, and carbon-footprint intelligence.
8. **Workplace Security:** Provides a cyber-resilient ecosystem with agentic AI for real-time threat detection and response.
9. **Experience Management Office (XMO):** Supports a shift from service-level agreement (SLA)-based delivery to experience-led outcomes through XLA governance.

Infosys has established dedicated Future of Work Zones in Bangalore and Düsseldorf, where clients can experience AI and emerging workplace technologies.

## Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions ('iCETS')

iCETS is Infosys’ emerging-technology incubation partner. It delivers next-generation platforms and Innovation-as-a-Service using New Emerging and eXploratory Technologies (NEXT).

The iCETS innovation framework is organized into three stages:

- **Now — Platforms and Ecosystems:** Uses reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities to operationalize AI at scale. Infosys Topaz Fabric is a composable and interoperable collection of AI agents, tools, services, and models. It supports optimization, automation, and modernization across existing IT processes and investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.
  
  Topaz Fabric supports three interconnected domains:
  
  - **Consulting:** Provides advisory services combining strategic intelligence, AI insights, structured frameworks, priorities, roadmaps, and measurable outcomes.
  - **Engineering:** Uses modern architectures and AI-driven automation for software delivery, modernization, cloud migration, and data engineering.
  - **Operations:** Supports resilient, outcome-driven operations through AI-powered monitoring, automation, self-healing, and predictive insights.

- **Near — Scaling Innovation Through Living Labs:** Uses co-creation, rapid prototyping, and real-world validation to help clients contextualize, experiment with, iterate on, and de-risk innovations in controlled environments. Infosys Living Labs are global innovation hubs where clients, partners, startups, and academia co-create solutions using emerging technologies.
  
  Client Living Labs deliver Innovation-as-a-Service through three flagship programs:
  
  - **IncubateNext:** Scalable, real-world innovation
  - **ImagineNext:** Future opportunity discovery
  - **SenseNext:** Conversion of early market and technology signals into action

- **Next — Applied Research Centers (ARC):** Conducts innovation across AI, cybersecurity, robotics, autonomous systems, quantum computing, and next-generation interaction models. Focus areas include Advanced AI, New Interaction Models, Cybersecurity, Autonomous Machines, Humanoids, and Quantum.

## AI-First GCC

The AI-First GCC Model is an offering for setting up and transforming Global Capability Centers (GCCs) into AI-powered hubs for innovation and growth. It provides an end-to-end path covering GCC setup, talent strategies, operational readiness, and AI-led transformation using production-grade agents and a unified fabric.

The model is designed to position GCCs as modernization hubs for transformation across business operations, software engineering, and technology operations. It uses Infosys Topaz Fabric to build and scale production-grade AI agents and run applied and agentic AI at enterprise scale. The Infosys Topaz suite supports AI-first services and solutions throughout the GCC lifecycle.

Key capabilities include:

- **End-to-end New GCC Setup:** GCC lifecycle management from strategy development and site selection through entity setup, recruitment, and operational launch.
- **GCC Scale-up and Transformation:** Embeds enterprise AI capabilities across GCC operations to support cost efficiency, faster time to market, and new business opportunities.
- **GCC Carve-outs:** Explores the takeover of GCCs and the carve-out of specialized services such as testing and research and development, as well as partial or full divestment of existing GCCs.
