| Total cost of sales | 14,079 | 13,405 | 674 |

Cost of sales increased as a percentage of revenues by 0.3% in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025. This was mainly due to an increase in cost of efforts, comprising employee and technical subcontractor costs, as a percentage of revenues, partially offset by a decrease in third-party items bought for service delivery to clients.

Cost of efforts as a percentage of revenues increased mainly due to compensation increases, the impact of the Labour Codes, reduced utilization, and greater use of subcontractors, partially offset by an improved offshore mix.

On November 21, 2025, the Government of India notified Labour Codes. Their implementation increased gratuity liability, defined benefit plan past-service cost relating to plan amendments, and compensated absences by $134 million, equivalent to 0.6% of revenues and 1% of total cost of sales, recognized in fiscal 2026.

Third-party items bought for service delivery to clients include software and hardware integral to overall client service delivery.
