## 2.14 Employee benefits

## Gratuity and Pensions

The Group provides a gratuity defined benefit retirement plan covering eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment to vested employees at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. Trustees administer the contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. These plans are managed by third-party fund managers and provide periodic retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments, including death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies with employee age.

An external actuary determines liabilities for these defined benefit plans at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The plans expose the Group to longevity, interest rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its balance sheet. Remeasurement gains and losses are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. Returns on plan assets in excess of the yields calculated using the applicable discount rate are also recognized in other comprehensive income. The effects of plan amendments are recognized in net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income.

## Provident fund

Eligible Infosys employees receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. The employee and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary.

The Company contributes part of the amount to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining amount is contributed to the government-administered pension fund. The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the trust’s investment returns and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries receive benefits from provident funds treated as defined contribution plans. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in defined contribution superannuation plans. The Group has no obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating entitlements. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed because of unused accumulated entitlement. Expenses for non-accumulating absences are recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
