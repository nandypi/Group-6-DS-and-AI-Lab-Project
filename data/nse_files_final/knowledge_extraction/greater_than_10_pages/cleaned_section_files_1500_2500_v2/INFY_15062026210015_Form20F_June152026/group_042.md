## Term of Office

Nandan M. Nilekani, Non-executive and Non-independent Chairman, retires by rotation at the 2026 Annual General Meeting pursuant to applicable provisions of the Indian Companies Act, 2013. He is eligible for re-appointment, has sought re-appointment, and the Board has recommended his re-appointment for shareholder approval.

The Indian Companies Act, 2013 precludes independent directors from retiring by rotation. Independent directors may hold office for a term of up to five consecutive years on the board and are eligible for re-appointment upon passage of a special resolution by the Company.

## Employment and Indemnification agreements

Under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, shareholders must approve the salary, bonus, benefits, and stock incentives of all executive directors.

Infosys has entered into agreements with Salil Parekh, its Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director. Details of these contracts are provided in the section titled “Material Contracts” in Item 10 of the Annual Report on Form 20-F.

Infosys has also entered into agreements to indemnify its directors and officers for claims brought against them to the fullest extent permitted under applicable law. These agreements cover certain expenses, judgments, fines, and settlement amounts incurred in actions or proceedings, including actions by or on behalf of Infosys Limited, arising from the individuals’ services as directors or officers. They also cover expenses related to public relations consultation when required.

Other than the appointment and indemnification agreements described above, Infosys has not entered into agreements with non-executive directors.
