## Dividends

The final dividend on shares is recorded as a liability on the date of approval by shareholders. Interim dividends are recorded as a liability on the date of declaration by the company's Board of Directors. Income tax consequences of dividends on financial instruments classified as equity are recognized according to where the entity originally recognized the transactions or events that generated distributable profits.

The Company declares and pays dividends in Indian rupees. Dividends are paid or distributed after deducting applicable taxes. Remittance of dividends outside India is governed by Indian foreign-exchange law and is subject to withholding tax at applicable rates.

## 2.16 Other income, net

## Accounting policy

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, and exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts and on the translation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities. Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## Functional currency and presentation currency

The functional currency of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. The functional currencies of foreign subsidiaries are their respective local currencies. The financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars, rounded to the nearest million.

## Transactions and translations

Foreign-currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the balance-sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income within exchange gains or losses on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in other comprehensive income as qualifying cash-flow hedges.

Non-monetary foreign-currency assets and liabilities measured at fair value are translated at the exchange rate prevailing when fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated at the exchange rate prevailing on the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized upon settlement of foreign-currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which the transaction is settled. Revenue, expense, and cash-flow items denominated in foreign currencies are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate in effect on the transaction date.

For translation of foreign subsidiaries' financial statements into the presentation currency, assets and liabilities are translated using the exchange rate at the balance-sheet date, while revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated using the average exchange rate for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves under other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is disposed of in full, the relevant currency translation amount is transferred to net profit in the statement of comprehensive income. When a change in the parent's ownership does not result in loss of control of a subsidiary, the change is recorded through equity.

Other comprehensive income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair-value adjustments arising on the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated at the exchange rate in effect at the balance-sheet date.

## Government grants

The Group recognizes government grants only when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received. Government grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the statement of comprehensive income on a systematic and rational basis over the useful life of the asset. Government grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in the statement of comprehensive income over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs.

## 2.17 Employees' Stock Option Plans (ESOP)

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense for share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value of each separately vesting portion is recognized as an expense in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period, as if the award were, in substance, multiple awards, with a corresponding increase in share premium.
