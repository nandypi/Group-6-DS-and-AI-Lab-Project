## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan)

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan shall not exceed 50,000,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 45,000,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on the achievement of annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The parameters include:

- Relative Total Shareholder Return (TSR) against selected industry peers.
- Broader domestic and global market indices.
- Operating performance metrics of the Company.

Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest. Awards generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan)

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan shall not exceed 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. The plan numbers were subsequently adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares. The exercise price of stock options, or ESOPs, is the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 8,650,911 shares as of March 31, 2026, and 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, under the 2015 Plan. Of these, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities at each year-end.

## Notes on grants to KMP

## CEO & MD

### Under the 2015 Plan

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants for fiscal 2026. The grants were made effective May 2, 2025:

- 230,621 performance-based RSUs, described as an annual performance equity grant, with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore (approximately $4.07 million). These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement upon achievement of specified performance targets.
- 13,273 performance-based RSUs, described as an annual performance equity ESG grant, with a fair value of ₹2 crore (approximately $0.23 million). These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement upon achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 33,183 performance-based RSUs, described as an annual performance equity TSR grant, with a fair value of ₹5 crore (approximately $0.59 million). These RSUs vest in line with the employment agreement based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance over the relevant years, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employee agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ₹3 crore (approximately $0.33 million). The grant vests over time in three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the respective grant date. Accordingly, an annual time-based grant of 18,132 RSUs was made effective February 1, 2026, for fiscal 2026.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027, had not been granted as of March 31, 2026. Since the service commencement date preceded the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with IFRS 2, *Share-based Payment*. For this purpose, the grant date under IFRS 2 was July 1, 2022.

### Under the 2019 Plan

On April 17, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based RSU grants amounting to ₹10 crore (approximately $1.17 million) for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest upon achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 66,366 performance-based RSUs were granted effective May 2, 2025.

## Other KMP

### Under the 2015 Plan

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved the following grants to other KMPs under the 2015 Plan:

- 231,270 time-based ESOPs, vesting over four years and exercisable within the period approved by the Committee. The exercise price is the market price on the grant date.
- 82,400 time-based RSUs, vesting over four years.

### Under the 2019 Plan

During the year ended March 31, 2026, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of 58,150 RSUs to other KMPs under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs vest over three years upon achievement of specified performance targets.
