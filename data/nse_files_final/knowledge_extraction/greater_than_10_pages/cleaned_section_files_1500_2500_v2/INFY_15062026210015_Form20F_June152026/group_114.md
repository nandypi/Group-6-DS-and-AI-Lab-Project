## Holding the ADSs

## How will you hold your ADSs?

ADSs may be held either directly or indirectly:

1. Directly, through an American Depositary Receipt (ADR), which is a certificate evidencing a specific number of ADSs and registered in the holder’s name, or through the Direct Registration System (DRS). A holder who holds ADSs directly is an ADS holder.
2. Indirectly, through a broker or other financial institution.

ADSs will be issued through DRS unless a holder specifically requests certificated ADRs. Holders who own ADSs indirectly must rely on their broker’s or financial institution’s procedures to assert the rights of ADS holders and should consult those institutions regarding the applicable procedures.

## Dividends and Other Distributions

## How will you receive dividends and other distributions on the shares?

The depositary has agreed to pay ADS holders the cash dividends and other distributions it or the custodian receives on equity shares or other deposited securities, after deducting its fees and expenses. Distributions will be made in proportion to the number of equity shares represented by the ADSs as of the record date established by the depositary, which will be as close as practicable to the record date for the equity shares.

- **Cash.** The depositary will convert, or cause to be converted, cash dividends, other cash distributions, and net proceeds from the sale of equity shares, rights, securities, or other entitlements into U.S. dollars when conversion and transfer to the United States are reasonably possible. It will promptly distribute the amount received.

  If conversion or transfer is not possible or lawful, or if required government approval or licensing cannot be obtained at a reasonable cost within a reasonable period, the depositary may distribute foreign currency only to ADS holders for whom distribution is possible. Foreign currency that cannot be converted will be held for the accounts of unpaid ADS holders. The depositary will not invest these funds and will not be liable for interest on them.

- **Deductions and rounding.** Before making a distribution, the depositary will deduct applicable taxes, governmental charges, and its fees and expenses. It will distribute only whole U.S. dollars and cents and will round fractional cents down to the nearest whole cent.

- **Shares.** For equity shares distributed as a dividend or free distribution, the depositary may either:

  1. distribute additional ADSs representing those equity shares; or
  2. arrange for existing ADSs as of the applicable record date to represent rights and interests in the additional equity shares.

  These actions are subject to applicable law and to the deduction of the depositary’s fees, charges, and expenses and applicable taxes and governmental charges. The depositary will distribute only whole ADSs. It may sell equity shares that would require delivery of a fractional ADS and distribute the net proceeds as it does with cash. It may also sell part of the distributed equity shares to pay related fees, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges.

- **Elective distributions in cash or shares.** If holders of equity shares are offered a choice between cash and shares, the depositary may determine, after consultation with the company, whether the election will be made available to ADS holders. The company must timely instruct the depositary and provide satisfactory evidence that the distribution is lawful.

  The depositary may determine that the elective distribution is not legal or reasonably practicable for ADS holders. In that case, it will distribute either cash or additional ADSs based on the determination made for equity shares for which no election was made. The depositary is not required to provide a method for ADS holders to receive the elective dividend in shares rather than ADSs. ADS holders may not receive the opportunity to participate on the same terms and conditions as equity shareholders.

- **Rights to purchase additional shares.** If holders of equity shares are offered rights to subscribe for additional shares, the depositary will consult with the company after receiving timely notice. The company must determine whether making the rights available to ADS holders is lawful and reasonably practicable, instruct the depositary to do so, and provide satisfactory evidence of legality.

  If the depositary determines that the rights cannot be made available but may lawfully and reasonably be sold, it will endeavor to sell them and distribute the net proceeds as it does with cash. Rights that are neither distributed nor sold will be allowed to lapse, and ADS holders will receive no value for them.

  If rights are made available, the depositary will establish procedures for their distribution and exercise upon payment of applicable fees, charges, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges. The depositary is not required to provide a method for ADS holders to exercise rights to subscribe for equity shares rather than ADSs.

  U.S. securities laws may restrict the transfer and cancellation of ADSs representing shares purchased through the exercise of rights. ADS holders may not be able to trade those ADSs freely in the United States. In such cases, the depositary may deliver restricted depositary shares with the same terms as the ADSs described in this section, subject to the restrictions required by law.

  ADS holders may not receive the opportunity to exercise rights on the same terms and conditions as equity shareholders or may be unable to exercise those rights.

- **Other distributions.** Subject to timely notice from the company, and provided the depositary determines that the distribution is lawful, reasonably practicable, feasible, and consistent with the deposit agreement, it will distribute other property received on deposited securities through a method it reasonably considers equitable and practicable. ADS holders must pay applicable fees, charges, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges.

  If these conditions are not met, the depositary may sell the distributed property and distribute the net proceeds as it does with cash. If it cannot sell the property, it may dispose of it in a reasonably practicable manner or return it to the company. In either case, ADS holders may have no rights to or arising from that property.
