# OUR SOLUTIONS AND CAPABILITIES

Infosys provides business and technology solutions and capabilities across consulting and enterprise services.

## (1) Consulting

Infosys Consulting provides AI-first, outcome-driven consulting services that connect strategy, technology, and execution. Its capabilities include:

- Defining enterprise AI value theses and strategic priorities.
- Designing human-AI operating models.
- Orchestrating execution across platforms and ecosystems.
- Supporting continuous value realization with trust, governance, and assurance.
- Using reusable domain assets and an AI-augmented workforce.
- Supporting clients across more than 50 countries in North America, EMEA, and APAC.

## (2) Enterprise Service Capabilities

## SAP

The Infosys SAP Practice provides end-to-end consulting, implementation, and managed services for core modernization and AI-first operating models. Its capabilities include:

- SAP S/4HANA Cloud transformations using cloud-first and AI-first approaches.
- AI agents built on SAP Joule and the SAP Generative AI Hub.
- Integration with AI-native providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and GitHub.
- SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC), and AI-enabled enterprise processes.
- Real-time insights, data-driven decision-making, and autonomous actions across the value chain.
- RISE with SAP and GROW with SAP offerings.
- Transformation governance, process excellence, and user adoption using SAP Signavio, LeanIX, and WalkMe.
- Support for maintaining a clean core and improving cost, performance, operational resilience, and agility.

Infosys is a RISE with SAP Validated Partner.

## Oracle

The Infosys Oracle Practice has nearly three decades of partnership with Oracle and supports enterprise transformation across:

- Oracle ERP, SCM, CX, and HCM platforms.
- Industry solutions.
- Infrastructure and technology modernization.
- Oracle Cloud and industry transformation.
- Client-specific AI capabilities and intelligent agents within Oracle applications and industry clouds.
- Implementation, operation, and optimization of Oracle programs across applications, infrastructure, and technology estates.

## Enterprise Application Integration ('EAIS')

The EAIS Practice provides consulting, implementation, integration, and support services for AI-led enterprise transformation. Its offerings include:

- Cloud-First Integration.
- Cloud-Native Development.
- Agentic Process Automation.
- AI development platforms.
- Digital Supply Chain solutions.
- Agentic process automation for digital experiences and operational processes.
- AI-enabled software development.
- Enterprise packages for resilient supply chains.

### Enterprise Integration and Orchestration for AI

This practice provides integration solutions for MCP-driven value networks, including:

- Agentic API management.
- Open APIs.
- MCP gateways.
- Integration platform as a service (iPaaS) solutions.
- Real-time data-streaming architectures using platforms such as Confluent.
- Event-driven, data-in-motion ecosystems.
- Digital backbone modernization and legacy landscape simplification.

### Agentic Process Automation

The practice supports process discovery, mining, optimization, and automation across business processes. Platforms include:

- Pega
- Appian
- Camunda
- Unqork
- Celonis
- ARIS
- UiPath
- Blue Prism
- Power Apps
- Related process automation technologies

### AI-Led Application Development

This competency builds and modernizes business platforms and intelligent applications using AI-driven development models and platforms such as Devin, Anthropic, and Infosys Topaz. It also designs, builds, and operates cloud-native applications using microservices and containerized architectures.

### Digital Supply Chain

The Digital Supply Chain practice provides digital solutions across the value chain, with focus areas including:

- Planning
- Fulfillment
- Spend management
- Asset lifecycle management
- Order management systems
- Warehouse management systems
- Procurement
- Enterprise asset management

## Microsoft Business Applications Services Practice

The Microsoft Business Applications Practice supports digital and AI-enabled transformations across customer experience (CX), employee experience (EX), and operational excellence (OX). Its platforms include:

- D365 ERP and CRM Suite
- Business Central
- Power Platform
- Copilot Studio
- Azure AI Foundry
- Microsoft Fabric

Capabilities include AI-enabled CRM, ERP, and Power Platform journeys; custom copilots and AI agents; industry-specific AI playbooks; and agent-operated platforms built on Dataverse and modern data stores. These platforms support business-process automation under human direction.

## Core Capabilities across Microsoft Business Applications

- **Consulting-led, industry-aligned transformations:** AI and digital transformation advisory, industry-specific consulting, business process redesign, simplification, harmonization, standardization, and persona-driven experience design.

- **Agentic AI solutions:** Business-centric agentic AI solutions using Copilot Studio and AI Builder, including joint use-case creation, platform governance, reusable assets, and architectural practices for enterprise adoption.

- **ERP (Finance & Supply Chain) transformations:** End-to-end ERP modernization across finance, supply chain, manufacturing, warehouse operations, project operations, and asset management using D365 Finance, Supply Chain, and Cloud for Sustainability, supported by Power Automate.

- **Redefining Customer CX Journeys:** End-to-end CX transformation across sales, customer service, field service, marketing, and contact centers, supported by customer insights, unified user experience design, application modernization, rapid prototyping, and process digitalization using Power Platform.
