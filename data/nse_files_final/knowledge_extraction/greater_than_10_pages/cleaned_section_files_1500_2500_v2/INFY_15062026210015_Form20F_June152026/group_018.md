## OUR STRATEGY

Infosys’ strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities in which it operates.

Clients are seeking to reimagine core offerings, processes, and systems in response to advances in software and computing technology, especially AI. Infosys’ strategy is to help clients navigate their AI journeys and unlock business value by helping them ideate, plan, and scale their AI futures through strong data foundations and robust cloud capabilities. Infosys also emphasizes safeguards for privacy, ethics, and controls through Responsible AI practices.

In 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy to strengthen client relevance and drive value creation:

- Scale Agile Digital
- Energize the Core
- Reskill our People
- Expand Localization

The current AI transition is described as transformational rather than incremental. Unlike prior waves centered on deterministic software and application-led modernization, AI requires foundational redesign of enterprise systems, architectures, and operating models. AI-native systems are built on probabilistic inference, continuous learning, and agent-based execution.

The impact of AI spans multiple dimensions:

- **Technology:** Enterprises are shifting from application-centric stacks to platforms incorporating intelligent agents, orchestration layers, and adaptive learning components.
- **Business:** Operating models are evolving from linear, role-based execution toward AI-driven operating loops that continuously sense, decide, and act.
- **Talent and organization:** Work is shifting from code creation toward orchestration, system supervision, domain interpretation, and outcome validation. This requires new roles, large-scale reskilling, and changes in workforce composition.

## Infosys AI vision:

Infosys aspires to be a leading partner for unlocking AI value and delivering business outcomes in revenue growth, cost optimization, and innovation.

Infosys’ two-pronged AI strategy consists of:

- **AI-first services:** Capture new demand and drive growth.
- **AI-augmented services:** Reinvent existing services to increase wallet share.

These services are supported by platforms, intellectual property, ecosystem partnerships, and talent.

AI-first services are designed with AI as the core execution layer, enabling new business models, products, and revenue streams by embedding intelligence into enterprise operations and decision-making.

AI-augmented services integrate AI into established systems and workflows to improve efficiency, personalization, and outcomes while preserving and extending the value of existing investments.

Infosys has developed more than 25 industry-focused AI playbooks covering repeatable use cases, domain context, and execution patterns. These playbooks are intended to help enterprises scale AI adoption and move from experimentation to enterprise-wide impact across growth, efficiency, and innovation.

## AI First Value Framework

Infosys unveiled its AI-first value framework to help global enterprises unlock AI value at scale using Infosys Topaz, its generative and agentic AI suite. Infosys estimates that this creates an incremental AI-first services opportunity of USD 300–400 billion by 2030, based on market estimates.

The framework is intended to help clients move beyond experimentation and responsibly scale AI within enterprise systems. It defines six interconnected value pools for coordinating data, technology, and operations:

- **AI Strategy & Engineering:** Design and implement AI strategies, platforms, and architectures. This includes orchestrating AI agents, proprietary platforms, and third-party tools on purpose-built infrastructure to establish an enterprise-wide AI operating model.
- **Data for AI:** Prepare structured and unstructured enterprise data for AI model readiness through AI-ready data platforms and AI-grade data engineering, including fingerprinting and synthetic data services.
- **Process AI:** Transform core business processes by integrating AI agents and human expertise and redesigning end-to-end workflows.
- **Agentic Legacy Modernization:** Use AI agents to reverse-engineer existing systems, understand their intent, and progressively modernize them while reducing technical debt.
- **Physical AI:** Design intelligent products and embed AI into physical devices that capture sensor data, interpret signals, and take real-time action using digital twins, robotics, autonomous systems, and edge intelligence.
- **AI Trust:** Apply responsible, secure, and ethical AI practices throughout the AI lifecycle, including risk assessments, policy design, security testing, and governance.

Infosys is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys.

## Platforms and IP:

Infosys is leveraging its composable and open agentic services suite, Infosys Topaz Fabric, and collaborations with AI disruptors to deliver AI-augmented and AI-first services.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is an enterprise-grade AI services layer within Infosys Topaz. It embeds enterprise context across data, applications, processes, and systems and integrates models, data pipelines, agents, and platforms across diverse technology environments. It is designed to help organizations reimagine business processes while building on existing investments and retaining strategic autonomy.

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across humans, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve’s product innovation with Infosys’ global scale to transform enterprise operations and core business functions.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is designed for clients that have already made AI investments and want a composable, modular agentic AI platform. Infosys Topaz AI Next addresses clients seeking an end-to-end, vertically integrated AI platform to reimagine core business processes.

Both platforms institutionalize enterprise knowledge and process semantics and support the AI lifecycle from experimentation to large-scale production deployment. Responsible AI, security, governance, and assurance are embedded by design.
