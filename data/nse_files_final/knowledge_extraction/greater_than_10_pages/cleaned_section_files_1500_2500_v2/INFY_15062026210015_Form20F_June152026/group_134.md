## Retaliation is against our values

Infosys encourages employees to report violations of its values and principles to the Helpline. The company does not tolerate threats, retribution, or retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report or assists with or cooperates in an investigation.

## Form of Acknowledgment of Receipt of Code of Conduct and Ethics

Employees acknowledge that they have received and read Infosys’ Code of Conduct and Ethics, understand the standards and policies it contains, and agree to follow the company’s values and comply with the Code.

Employees may consult their manager, the Office of Integrity & Compliance, Human Resources, or the Legal Department regarding the Code, company policies, or legal and regulatory requirements applicable to their work. Reports and questions to these sources are maintained in confidence.

## CERTIFICATION OF CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER PURSUANT TO SECTION 302 OF THE SARBANES-OXLEY ACT OF 2002

Salil Parekh certified that:

1. He reviewed Infosys Limited’s Annual Report on Form 20-F.
2. Based on his knowledge, the report does not contain an untrue statement of a material fact or omit a material fact necessary to make the statements, in light of the circumstances under which they were made, not misleading for the period covered.
3. Based on his knowledge, the financial statements and other financial information included in the report fairly present, in all material respects, Infosys’ financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows for the periods presented.
4. The company’s certifying officers are responsible for establishing and maintaining disclosure controls and procedures and internal control over financial reporting. Their responsibilities include:
   - Designing disclosure controls and procedures to ensure that material information relating to the company and its consolidated subsidiaries is made known to them during preparation of the report.
   - Designing internal control over financial reporting to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and preparation of financial statements for external purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
   - Evaluating the effectiveness of disclosure controls and procedures as of the end of the period covered by the report and presenting their conclusions.
   - Disclosing changes in internal control over financial reporting during the reporting period that materially affected, or were reasonably likely to materially affect, those controls.
5. Based on their most recent evaluation of internal control over financial reporting, the certifying officers disclosed to the company’s auditors and audit committee:
   - All significant deficiencies and material weaknesses in the design or operation of internal control over financial reporting that were reasonably likely to adversely affect the company’s ability to record, process, summarize, and report financial information.
   - Any fraud, whether or not material, involving management or other employees with a significant role in internal control over financial reporting.

*/s/ Salil Parekh*  
Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director
