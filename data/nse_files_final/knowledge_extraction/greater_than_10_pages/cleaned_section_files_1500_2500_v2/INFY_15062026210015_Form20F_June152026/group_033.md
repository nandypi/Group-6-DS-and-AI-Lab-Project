Depreciation of the Indian rupee against the U.S. dollar or other currencies affects margins positively, while appreciation of the Indian rupee affects margins negatively. Exchange rates between the Indian rupee, other currencies, and the U.S. dollar have fluctuated substantially in recent years and may continue to do so. The impact of future fluctuations on operating margins cannot be predicted.

For further discussion of foreign exchange exposure, see Item 3, “Risk Factors—Risks Related to Our Cost Structure—Currency fluctuations and changes in interest rates may affect the results of our operations and yield on cash balances,” in the Annual Report on Form 20-F.
