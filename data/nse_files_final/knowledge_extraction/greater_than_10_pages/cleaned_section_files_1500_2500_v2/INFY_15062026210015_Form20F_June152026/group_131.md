## Conflict of Interest

### What does conflict of interest mean?

A conflict of interest occurs when the interests or benefits of a person conflict with those of Infosys. Employees and directors must avoid actual or potential conflicts that could affect, or appear to affect, their judgment or responsibilities toward the Company, shareholders, or customers.

Conflicts may also arise when employees or their family members receive improper personal benefits or preferential treatment because of their position or a family member’s position at Infosys.

### QUESTION

I work in a country where the laws are different from those in the country where I am based. Does the Code cover both locations?

### RESPONSE

Infosys expects employees to comply with the laws, policies, and procedures applicable in every location where they work. The Code is intended to apply worldwide, but legal requirements differ between countries. If the Code, Company policies, local laws, or customs appear to conflict, employees should consult the designated Company resources.

### When could I be faced with a 'conflict of interest' issue?

#### OUTSIDE EMPLOYMENT OR ENGAGEMENT

Outside employment or engagement is a conflict of interest when it supports a competitor or involves simultaneous employment with another company or business entity. This includes providing services as an employee, agent, or contractor to a customer, supplier, or other entity with a business relationship with Infosys, or accepting an engagement that adversely affects responsibilities at Infosys.

#### RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS

Employees should avoid conducting Company business with a Family Member or Relative, or with a business in which a Family Member or Relative has a significant role. Family Members or Relatives include spouses, siblings, children, parents, grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews, cousins, step-relations, and in-laws.

Material transactions, particularly those involving Company directors or executive officers, must be reviewed and approved in writing in advance by the Audit Committee. Material related-party transactions must be disclosed to regulators where legally required, conducted at arm’s length, and carried out without preferential treatment.

#### WORKING WITH FAMILY

Employees should not directly work for, supervise, or make employment decisions about a Family Member or Relative. This includes positions or assignments in the same department and roles involving financial or other dependence or influence, such as auditing, control, or supervisor-subordinate relationships.

The Human Resources Department determines whether an acknowledged relationship is covered by the policy.

#### RELATIONSHIPS AT WORK

Personal or romantic relationships with a competitor, supplier, or another Company employee may affect an employee’s ability to exercise good judgment. Relationships between employees in a manager-employee reporting structure may create team-management challenges and reduce morale. Such relationships must be disclosed immediately to the manager, who may take corrective action.

#### OUTSIDE DIRECTORSHIP

It is a conflict of interest for employees or directors to serve as a director, partner, sole proprietor, or equivalent position in a company, firm, or business entity that competes with Infosys or has a similar line of business.

With prior approval from the Office of Integrity and Compliance, employees may serve on the boards or governing bodies of two other business entities, provided those entities do not compete with Infosys.

#### OUTSIDE INVESTMENTS

Employees should not hold a financial interest, including through a relative, in an organization if that interest would create or appear to create a conflict of interest with Infosys. Particular care is required for financial interests in competitors, suppliers, customers, distributors, and strategic partners.

#### LOANS

Loans and guarantees provided by Infosys to employees may constitute improper personal benefits depending on the facts and circumstances. Loans by the Company to, or guarantees by the Company of obligations of, any director or executive officer or their family members are prohibited by law.

## Conflict of Interest

### When faced with such issues, ask yourself:

- Would this create or appear to create an unfair incentive for me or my family?
- Am I putting Infosys at risk of violating laws or agreements with customers?
- Would this look bad if it were discussed on social media?
- Would this distract me from doing my job?

### What should I do if I have any potential/ actual conflict of interest?

All actual or potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed through the Smart Awareness Quiz (SAQ) platform under “Conflict of Interest” or through the Office of Integrity and Compliance. Changes to existing disclosures must also be updated.

Because possible conflicts are wide-ranging, not every situation can be listed. Proposed transactions or situations that raise questions or doubts should be referred to the Office of Integrity and Compliance.

## Political Activities

Infosys’ funds or assets must not be used for political campaign contributions or political activities without the Board’s prior written approval. Employees must not seek reimbursement for political contributions, use Infosys resources for personal political activities, or imply that they represent Infosys’ position regarding a candidate, political cause, or government decision.

Infosys may communicate its position on important issues to elected representatives and government officials.

## Lobbying

Lobbying includes activities intended to influence laws, regulations, public policies, or government decision-making. Direct or indirect interactions with government officials, including preparing policy positions or engaging through industry associations or third parties, may constitute lobbying.

Employees must not claim to represent Infosys in such engagements unless specifically designated to do so. Lobbying laws and reporting requirements vary across countries, and employees must understand and comply with applicable requirements. Such meetings must be conducted with integrity and transparency.

## Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing

Money laundering involves directly or indirectly participating in processes connected with the proceeds of crime, including concealing, possessing, acquiring, or using such proceeds, or presenting them as untainted property.

Terrorism financing occurs when money or other resources are made available to commit terrorist acts or support terrorist organizations. Suspected activity should be reported through the designated helpline.

## Immigration

Employees traveling internationally to support business requirements must comply with applicable immigration laws and Company policies.

## C. PROTECTING COMPANY ASSETS

## Company Confidential Information

Confidential information is a valuable Company asset. Directors, employees, and agents must protect all non-public information, including personal information obtained in the course of business.

Confidential information must be used only for Company business purposes. Personal information must be processed on the Company’s instructions, in accordance with Company policies, procedures, guidelines, and applicable privacy laws and regulations. Employees working remotely must prevent unauthorized access and use secure networks.

Upon joining Infosys, employees sign a Confidentiality and Nondisclosure Agreement setting out their confidentiality obligations. Employees must preserve the confidentiality of non-public information obtained through their work for Infosys.

## Company Confidential Information

Unless Infosys provides specific consent, preferably in writing, or there is a legal or professional right or duty to disclose, employees must not disclose confidential Company information. Confidential or proprietary information concerning clients, Infosys, or other parties obtained through employment or affiliation with Infosys must not be used for personal advantage or for the benefit of third parties.

The Information Security Policy establishes requirements for protecting Infosys and third-party confidential information and maintaining security, availability, integrity, and confidentiality. The Acceptable Usage Policy defines permitted uses of Infosys IT resources and cybersecurity responsibilities, including preventing inappropriate or unauthorized access and other cybersecurity breaches.

The Infosys Privacy Policy and Binding Corporate Rules Policy govern the processing of personal information belonging to employees, clients, agents, consultants, suppliers, and contractors. They address compliance with applicable privacy laws and regulations, including cross-border processing of personal data between group entities. Unless authorized and contractually agreed, Infosys protects such information, implements appropriate technical and organizational measures, and requires employees to follow mandated processes for its responsible use, disclosure, storage, retention, and other processing.

Actual or suspected personal-data incidents must be reported to the Data Privacy Office through the Infosys Data Privacy Portal. Actual or suspected unauthorized access to or use of systems or information, including incidents in remote settings, must be reported to the Information Security Group.
