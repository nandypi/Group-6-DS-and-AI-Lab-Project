## Annexure 2

## UNDERTAKING TO BE ACCOMPANIED WITH THE APPLICATION FOR PRECLEARANCE

To: The Compliance Officer, Infosys Limited (“Company”)

I, ____________________, being a designated person of the Company under its code for trading in securities, residing at ____________________, desire to trade in the Company’s shares as stated in my pre-clearance application dated ____________________.

I declare that:

- I am not in possession of any unpublished price sensitive information (“UPSI”) at the time of signing this undertaking.
- If I receive or obtain access to UPSI after signing this undertaking but before executing the approved transaction, I will inform the Compliance Officer and refrain completely from trading in the Company’s securities until the information becomes public.
- I have not contravened the Company’s trading code as notified from time to time.
- I have made full and true disclosure in this matter.

If the transaction violates the trading code or applicable laws, I will:

- Unconditionally release, hold harmless, and indemnify the Company and its directors and officers for losses, damages, fines, and expenses suffered in relation to the transaction.
- Compensate them for expenses incurred in investigation, defence, crisis management, or public-relations activities related to the transaction.
- Authorise the Company to recover the profits arising from the transaction and remit them to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (“SEBI”) for credit to the SEBI Investor Protection and Education Fund.

I undertake to submit the required report within two days of executing the transaction, or a “Nil” report if the transaction is not undertaken.

If approval is granted, I will execute the trade within seven days of receiving approval. If the trade is not executed within that period, I will seek fresh pre-clearance.

Signature: ____________________  
Name: ____________________  
Date: ____________________
