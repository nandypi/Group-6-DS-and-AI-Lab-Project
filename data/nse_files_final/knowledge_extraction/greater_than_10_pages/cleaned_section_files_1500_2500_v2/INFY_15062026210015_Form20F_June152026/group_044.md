## Nomination and Remuneration Committee

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee comprises four independent directors, each determined by the Board to be independent under applicable NYSE rules:

- D. Sundaram, Chairperson
- Michael Gibbs
- Govind Iyer
- Nitin Paranjpe

The Committee's responsibilities include:

- Assisting the Board with remuneration matters concerning the Company's directors, Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), and senior management.
- Evaluating and approving remuneration plans, policies, programs, and succession plans for directors, KMP, and senior management.
- Identifying candidates for these positions and recommending appointments and removals to the Board.
- Formulating criteria for director qualifications, positive attributes, independence, and performance evaluation.
- Administering equity-based plans and schemes approved by shareholders.
- Overseeing the nomination process for KMP and senior management.
- Identifying, screening, and reviewing candidates for director, KMP, and senior management positions.
- Recommending director appointments and removals for approval at the annual meeting of shareholders.
- Evaluating the Board's performance and reviewing implementation and compliance with the evaluation.
- Supporting leadership development.
- Developing and maintaining corporate governance policies.
- Establishing a Board diversity policy.

The Committee oversees the recruitment of new Board members and the Company's processes for recruiting, motivating, and retaining senior management, as well as its overall approach to human resources management.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## Risk Management Committee

The Risk Management Committee comprises seven independent directors, each determined by the Board to be independent under applicable NYSE rules:

- D. Sundaram, Chairperson
- Michael Gibbs
- Bobby Parikh
- Chitra Nayak
- Govind Iyer
- Helene Auriol Potier
- Nitin Paranjpe

The Committee's responsibilities include:

- Assisting the Board in overseeing the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of strategic, operational, and external-environment risks.
- Monitoring and approving the enterprise risk management framework and related practices.
- Assessing risks to the execution of business strategy and reviewing relevant leading indicators.
- Reviewing risk management processes and practices to support an appropriate balance between risk and reward in ongoing and new business activities.
- Evaluating significant risk exposures and management actions to mitigate them.
- Evaluating cybersecurity risks and ensuring procedures are in place to mitigate them.
- Coordinating with the Audit Committee where risk and audit activities overlap.
- Periodically reviewing the adequacy of the Risk Management Committee Charter and recommending changes to the Board.
- Obtaining internal information and advice from internal or external legal, accounting, or other advisors.
- Appointing, removing, and approving the remuneration terms of the Chief Risk Officer.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

The Risk Management Committee Charter is available on the [Company's website](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/risk-management-committee-charter.pdf).

## Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee

The Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee comprises five independent directors:

- Michael Gibbs, Chairperson and cybersecurity expert
- D. Sundaram
- Govind Iyer
- Chitra Nayak
- Helene Auriol Potier (1)

The Sub-committee assesses cybersecurity-related risks and the Company's preparedness to mitigate and respond to those risks. It meets periodically and reports its findings, if any, to the Risk Management Committee.

The Sub-committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

(1) Helene Auriol Potier was appointed as a member of the Cyber Security Risk Sub-committee effective August 1, 2025.

## Stakeholders Relationship Committee

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee comprises four independent directors:

- Michael Gibbs, Chairperson
- D. Sundaram
- Bobby Parikh
- Chitra Nayak

The Board appointed A.G.S. Manikantha, Company Secretary, as Compliance Officer under the Listing Regulations and as Nodal Officer for compliance with the Investor Education Protection Fund (IEPF) Rules.

The Committee's responsibilities include:

- Considering and resolving security holders' concerns or complaints.
- Monitoring and reviewing investor service standards.
- Understanding shareholders' views through direct interaction, analyst briefings, or shareholder surveys.
- Overseeing shareholder engagement and communications.
- Ensuring that shareholder views and concerns are reported to the Board and addressed appropriately.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter is available on the [Company's website](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/stakeholders-relationship-committee.pdf).

## Corporate Social Responsibility Committee ('CSR Committee')

The CSR Committee comprises three independent directors:

- Govind Iyer, Chairperson
- Chitra Nayak
- Michael Gibbs

The Committee's responsibilities include:

- Identifying areas for CSR activities.
- Recommending expenditure for identified CSR activities.
- Evaluating CSR proposals received from the Company.
- Implementing and monitoring the CSR Policy.
- Formulating and recommending the annual CSR action plan to the Board.
- Reviewing the Company's CSR initiatives and programs.
- Coordinating with Infosys Foundation and agencies such as registered trusts, societies, or Section 8 companies to implement CSR programs and initiatives.

The Committee oversees CSR activities, programs, and initiatives undertaken by the Company, including activities of Infosys Foundation, Infosys Foundation USA, and other initiatives in Australia and parts of Europe. The Foundation reports progress on deployed initiatives to the Committee and supports periodic disclosures.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## Environment, Social and Governance Committee ('ESG Committee')

The ESG Committee comprises three independent directors:

- Chitra Nayak, Chairperson
- Govind Iyer
- Helene Auriol Potier

The Committee's responsibilities include:

- Guiding the creation and periodic refresh of the Company's ESG Vision and Ambitions.
- Reviewing the operation and work of the ESG Council.
- Ensuring implementation of measures to advance the Company's ESG objectives.
- Reviewing periodic ESG disclosures and communications, including annual report sections, ESG reports, and Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) filings.
- Obtaining advice and assistance from internal or external experts and advisors.
- Reporting periodically to the Board on matters reviewed and decisions or recommendations made.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2026. Its charter is available on the [Company's website](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporategovernance/documents/environment-social-governance-committee-charter.pdf).
