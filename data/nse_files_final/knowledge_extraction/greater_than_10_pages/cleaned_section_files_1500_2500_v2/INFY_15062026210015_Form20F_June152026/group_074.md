## Infosys Limited and subsidiaries Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows Accounting policy

Cash flows are reported using the indirect method. Profit for the year is adjusted for non-cash transactions, deferrals or accruals of past or future operating cash receipts or payments, and income or expenses associated with investing or financing cash flows. Operating, investing, and financing activities are presented separately. The Group considers highly liquid investments readily convertible to known amounts of cash to be cash equivalents.

## Overview and Notes to the Consolidated Financial Statements

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Company overview

Infosys Limited (the “Company” or “Infosys”) provides AI-first business consulting and technology services. The Group comprises Infosys, its subsidiaries, and controlled trusts.

The Company is a public limited company incorporated and domiciled in India, with its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Its primary listings are on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited in India. Its American Depositary Shares, representing equity shares, are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

The Group’s consolidated financial statements were authorized for issue by the Company’s Board of Directors on June 15, 2026.

### 1.2 Basis of preparation of financial statements

The consolidated financial statements are prepared in compliance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB). They use the historical cost convention and accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of defined benefit obligations less the fair value of plan assets.

Accounting policies are applied consistently, except when a newly issued accounting standard is initially adopted or a revision to an existing accounting standard requires a change in accounting policy. Material accounting policy information is discussed in the respective notes.

### 1.3 Basis of consolidation

Infosys consolidates entities it owns or controls. The consolidated financial statements comprise the financial statements of the Company, its controlled trusts, and its subsidiaries.

Control exists when the parent has power over an entity, is exposed or has rights to variable returns from its involvement with the entity, and can affect those returns through its power. Power is demonstrated through existing rights that provide the ability to direct relevant activities that significantly affect the entity’s returns. Subsidiaries are consolidated from the date control commences until the date control ceases.

Group companies’ financial statements are consolidated on a line-by-line basis. Intra-group balances and transactions, including unrealized gains or losses, are eliminated on consolidation. The financial statements are prepared using uniform accounting policies across the Group. Non-controlling interests represent the portion of net profit or loss and net assets of subsidiaries not directly or indirectly owned or controlled by the Company and are excluded from the Company’s ownership.

Refer to Note 2.20 for the list of the Company’s subsidiaries and controlled trusts.
