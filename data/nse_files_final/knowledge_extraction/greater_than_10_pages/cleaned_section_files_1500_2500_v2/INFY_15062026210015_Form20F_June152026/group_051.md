## Bonus Shares

In addition to permitting dividends to be paid out of current or retained earnings, the Indian Companies Act, 2013 permits a company to distribute amounts transferred from its free reserves to shareholders in the form of bonus shares. Bonus shares may also be issued by capitalizing the securities premium account or the capital redemption reserve account. Reserves created through asset revaluation cannot be capitalized for this purpose.

Bonus shares are distributed to shareholders in the proportion recommended by the Board. Shareholders holding shares on a fixed record date are entitled to receive the bonus shares. Once the Board announces its decision to recommend a bonus issue, the decision cannot be withdrawn.

Any bonus issue is subject to applicable SEBI guidelines. These guidelines require that the company:

- Be authorized by its articles of association to issue bonus shares.
- Obtain stock exchange approval for listing and trading of all securities issued before the bonus issue, excluding employee stock options and convertible securities.
- Reserve equity shares of the same class for holders of outstanding compulsorily convertible debt instruments, if any, in proportion to their convertible portion.
- Fund the bonus issue from free reserves, the securities premium account, or the capital redemption reserve account, based on genuine profits or share premium collected in cash.
- Make any existing partly paid shares fully paid-up before issuing bonus shares.
- Not have defaulted on interest or principal payments for fixed deposits, or on interest payments or redemption principal for existing debentures.
- Have sufficient reason to believe that it has not defaulted on employee statutory dues, including provident fund contributions, gratuity, and bonus payments.

A company cannot declare bonus shares in lieu of dividends. Bonus shares may be allotted only in dematerialized form.

The bonus issue must be implemented within 15 days of Board approval when shareholder approval is not required. If shareholder approval is required for capitalization of profits or reserves, the bonus issue must be implemented within two months of Board approval. A bonus issue cannot be withdrawn after the decision to issue it has been announced.
