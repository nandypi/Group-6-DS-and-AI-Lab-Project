## Basis for Opinion

Management is responsible for maintaining effective internal control over financial reporting and assessing its effectiveness. The auditor’s responsibility was to express an opinion based on the audit.

The audit was conducted in accordance with Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (PCAOB) standards. The procedures included understanding internal controls, assessing the risk of material weakness, testing and evaluating control design and operating effectiveness, and performing other procedures considered necessary. The auditor stated that the audit provided a reasonable basis for its opinion.

## Definition and Limitations of Internal Control over Financial Reporting

Internal control over financial reporting is a process designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles. It includes policies and procedures that:

1. Maintain records that accurately and fairly reflect transactions and dispositions of assets.
2. Provide reasonable assurance that transactions are recorded as necessary to prepare financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, and that receipts and expenditures are made only with management or director authorization.
3. Provide reasonable assurance regarding the prevention or timely detection of unauthorized acquisition, use, or disposition of assets that could materially affect the financial statements.

Because of its inherent limitations, internal control over financial reporting may not prevent or detect misstatements. Evaluations of effectiveness for future periods are subject to the risk that controls may become inadequate due to changes in conditions or that compliance with policies and procedures may deteriorate.

## CHANGES IN INTERNAL CONTROL OVER FINANCIAL REPORTING

## Item 16E. Purchases of Equity Securities by the Issuer and Affiliated Purchasers

## Buyback completed in December 2025

At its September 11, 2025 meeting, the Board approved a proposal to buy back fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each for ₹18,000 crore, approximately $2,000 million, subject to shareholder approval by postal ballot.

Shareholders approved the buyback through e-voting, with results declared on November 6, 2025. The offer covered 100,000,000 equity shares, approximately 2.41% of the total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025 on a standalone basis, at ₹1,800, or $20, per equity share.

The offer was made to eligible equity shareholders as of the November 14, 2025 record date on a proportionate basis through the tender offer route. The tender period ran from November 20 to November 26, 2025. The company completed the buyback on December 4, 2025, buying back and extinguishing 100,000,000 equity shares.

The buyback resulted in cash outflow of ₹18,000 crore, approximately $2,000 million, excluding transaction costs. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.

As of March 31, 2026, the company had created a Capital Redemption Reserve of ₹50 crore, approximately $6 million, equal to the nominal value of the shares bought back. The reserve was appropriated from the general reserve under Section 69 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Buyback completed in February 2023

Shareholders approved a proposal through a special resolution passed by postal ballot on December 2, 2022, with results declared on December 3, 2022. The proposal authorized an open-market buyback through Indian stock exchanges of fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each, for an aggregate amount of up to ₹9,300 crore, excluding buyback tax, at a price not exceeding ₹1,850 per equity share.

The buyback was offered to all equity shareholders other than the promoters, promoter group, and persons in control of the company. It commenced on December 7, 2022, and was completed on February 13, 2023.

During the buyback period, the company purchased and extinguished 60,426,348 equity shares at a volume-weighted average buyback price of ₹1,539.06 per equity share. The shares represented 1.44% of pre-buyback paid-up equity share capital.

The buyback resulted in cash outflow of ₹9,300 crore, excluding transaction costs and buyback tax, or $1,130 million. It was funded from free reserves, including securities premium, under Section 68 of the Companies Act, 2013.
