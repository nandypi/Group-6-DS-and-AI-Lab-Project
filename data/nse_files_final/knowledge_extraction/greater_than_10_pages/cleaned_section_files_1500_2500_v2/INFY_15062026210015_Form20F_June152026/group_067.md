## Item 16F. Change in Registrant's Certifying Accountant

### Proposed change of Auditors on account of mandatory rotation requirement in India

Under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the related rules, Infosys Limited must rotate its statutory auditors after completion of the maximum permitted term.

On April 23, 2026, the Audit Committee proposed recommending the appointment of BSR & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), as the Company's statutory auditors. The Board of Directors approved the announcement of this intention at its meeting held on April 23, 2026.

The Board will recommend the appointment to shareholders at the Company's 46th Annual General Meeting in 2027. The proposed appointment would be for a first term of five consecutive years, ending at the conclusion of the 51st Annual General Meeting in 2032.

BSR & Co.'s first audit would cover the financial statements for the year ending March 31, 2028, including the quarterly financial statements for that year. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP will remain the Company's auditors for the financial statements for the year ending March 31, 2027.

The proposed appointment of BSR & Co. is subject to all applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.

### Proposed change in the Company's certifying accountant for filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ('U.S. SEC')

Infosys is registered with the U.S. SEC and is required to appoint a certifying accountant to audit its financial statements.

The Audit Committee and Board of Directors approved the announcement of the Company's intention to appoint KPMG Assurance and Consulting Services LLP as its independent registered public accounting firm. The proposed appointment is expected to be effective for the year ending March 31, 2028.

KPMG would audit the annual financial statements included in the Company's Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the U.S. SEC for the year ending March 31, 2028. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP will remain the Company's certifying accountant for the financial statements included in the Form 20-F for the year ending March 31, 2027.

The proposed appointment of KPMG is subject to all applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements under relevant laws and regulations.

## Item 16G. Corporate Governance

Section 303A of the Listed Company Manual of the NYSE permits a foreign private issuer to follow home-country corporate governance practices instead of NYSE requirements, provided that it:

1. Has an Audit Committee satisfying the requirements of Rule 10A-3 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
2. Discloses significant differences between its corporate governance practices and those followed by domestic companies under NYSE listing standards in its annual reports filed with the SEC on Form 20-F.
3. Promptly notifies the NYSE of non-compliance with Section 303A of the NYSE Listed Company Manual.
4. Complies with the NYSE's annual and interim certification requirements.

Infosys stated that although Indian home-country corporate governance standards may differ from NYSE listing standards, its corporate governance policies and practices generally comply with the NYSE listing standards applicable to domestic companies.

Key differences between the applicable Indian listing regulations and NYSE listing requirements include:

1. Under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended (SEBI LODR), a company with a non-executive Chairman, such as Infosys, must have at least one-third of its Board comprising independent directors. NYSE listing requirements specify that a majority of the Board must be independent. As of March 31, 2026, Infosys' Board had nine members: one executive director, one non-executive and non-independent Chairman, and seven independent directors.
2. SEBI LODR requires two-thirds of Audit Committee members to be independent directors, while the NYSE Listed Company Manual requires all Audit Committee members to be independent. Infosys' Audit Committee consists only of independent directors.
3. SEBI LODR requires at least one woman director. It also requires the boards of the top 500 listed entities to include at least one independent woman director, and, effective April 1, 2020, requires the boards of the top 1,000 listed companies to include at least one independent woman director. Infosys' Board had two independent women directors as of March 31, 2026.
4. The criteria for determining director independence differ between NYSE listing standards and SEBI LODR. Infosys follows the criteria prescribed under both jurisdictions.
