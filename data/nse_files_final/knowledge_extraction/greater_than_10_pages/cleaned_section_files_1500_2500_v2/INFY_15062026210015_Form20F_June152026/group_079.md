## Fair value hierarchy

- **Level 1:** Quoted prices, unadjusted, in active markets for identical assets or liabilities.
- **Level 2:** Inputs other than quoted prices included in Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly as prices or indirectly as derived prices.
- **Level 3:** Inputs for assets or liabilities that are not based on observable market data.

During fiscal 2025, quoted debt securities of $35 million were transferred from Level 2 to Level 1 because they were valued based on quoted prices. Quoted debt securities of $65 million were transferred from Level 1 to Level 2 because they were valued based on market-observable inputs.

The majority of the Group’s investments are fair valued using Level 1 or Level 2 inputs. These investments primarily include mutual fund units, target maturity fund units, quoted debt securities, certificates of deposit, commercial paper, and quoted bonds issued by government and quasi-government organizations.

The Group assesses counterparty risk using multiple criteria, including Tier I Capital, Capital Adequacy Ratio, credit rating, profitability, non-performing asset levels, and the deposit base of banks and financial institutions. These risks are monitored regularly under the Group’s risk management program.
