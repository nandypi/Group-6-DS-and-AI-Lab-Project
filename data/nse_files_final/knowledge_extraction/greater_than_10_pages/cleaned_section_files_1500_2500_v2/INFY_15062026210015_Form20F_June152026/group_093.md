For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, the yield on government bonds is used as the discount rate because the market for high-quality corporate bonds is not developed. For most overseas defined benefit plans, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used.

The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined using the Company’s past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management’s estimate of future salary increases. The attrition rate is based on management’s estimate derived from the Company’s long-term employee turnover trends. Employee tenure reflects the long-term trend in employees’ average remaining service life and the estimated term of the post-employment benefit obligation.

For the domestic defined benefit plan in India, assumptions regarding future mortality experience follow published statistics from the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas defined benefit plans, mortality assumptions are based on the latest life-expectancy statistics, plan experience, and other relevant data. The Group assesses these assumptions against its projected long-term plans for growth and prevailing industry standards.

## Plan Funding and Investments

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve make contributions to the Infosys BPM Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively.

Trustees administer the contributions. Gratuity contributions are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India, as permitted by Indian law.

Assets of overseas defined benefit plans are primarily invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined according to investment criteria prescribed under the relevant regulations applicable to pension funds and insurer managers. The insurers’ investments are diversified and provide guaranteed-interest-rate arrangements.

## Actuarial Risks

These defined benefit plans expose the Group to the following actuarial risks:

| Risk | Description |
|---|---|
| Interest rate risk | The present value of the defined benefit plan liability is generally calculated using a discount rate based on government bond yields and, in certain overseas jurisdictions, government bond yields adjusted for a corporate spread. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation generally increases. |
| Life expectancy and investment risk | The pension fund offers a choice between a lifelong pension and a cash lump sum upon retirement. The fund has defined rates for converting the lump sum to a pension. Risk arises if members live longer than implied by these conversion rates or if pension assets do not achieve the investment return implied by the rates. |
| Asset volatility | A proportion of the pension fund is held in equities, which are expected to outperform corporate bonds over the long term but create short-term volatility and risk. The pension fund board of the insurer is responsible for the investment strategy and equity allocation.
