## 2019 Plan

The Board approved the 2019 Plan on May 15, 2019, subject to shareholder approval. The plan provides performance-based stock incentives to eligible employees of Infosys and its subsidiaries.

Shareholders approved the plan at the June 22, 2019 AGM. The maximum number of shares under the plan is 50,000,000, of which up to 45,000,000 may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

RSUs vest based on annual performance parameters determined by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. These parameters may include relative Total Shareholder Return against selected industry peers, broader domestic and global market indices, and Infosys operating performance metrics. Awards generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

The Board approved amendments relating to the duration of the plan, vesting parameters, and certain administrative matters. The amendments are subject to shareholder approval. Details are available in the Explanatory Statement to the Notice of the 45th Annual General Meeting.

Infosys ADSs are listed on the NYSE. Each ADS represents one equity share with a par value of ₹5 per share. As of May 20, 2026, ADSs were held by 121,599 holders of record in the United States.

Infosys equity shares may be held by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs). To the best of Infosys’ knowledge, it is not owned or controlled directly or indirectly by any government, corporation, or other natural or legal person. Infosys is not aware of any arrangement that could subsequently result in a change in control.
