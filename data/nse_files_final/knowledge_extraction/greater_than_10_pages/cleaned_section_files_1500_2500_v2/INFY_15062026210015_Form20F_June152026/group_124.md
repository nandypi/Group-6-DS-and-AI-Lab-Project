The approved Trading Plan is irrevocable. The Designated Person must implement it and may not deviate from it or trade in Securities outside its scope, as provided under SEBI regulations.

Implementation must not begin if the Designated Person possessed UPSI when the plan was formulated and that information has not become generally available by the planned commencement date. Implementation is deferred until the UPSI becomes generally available.

## 8. Penalty for Insider Trading

- A Designated Person, officer, or employee who violates this Code may face disciplinary action, including wage freeze, suspension, recovery, ineligibility for future participation in the Company's stock option plans, or termination.
- Stock exchanges or other appropriate regulatory authorities must be informed of violations in the form and manner specified by SEBI.
- Amounts collected as penalties under this Code must be credited to the Investor Protection and Education Fund administered by SEBI.

## 9. Disclosure requirements

### i. Initial Disclosure

- Every person appointed as a Key Managerial Personnel or Director, or who becomes a Promoter or member of the promoter group, must disclose their own and their Immediate Relatives' holdings of Company Securities as of the appointment or promotion date within seven days, using Form A in Annexure 4.
- Every Designated Person must disclose the following information for:
  - Immediate Relatives
  - Persons with whom the Designated Person shares a material financial relationship
  - Phone and mobile numbers used by the Designated Person

The disclosures include the Permanent Account Number or another identifier authorized by law, names of educational institutions attended, and names of past employers, where applicable.

### ii. Continual Disclosure

- Every Designated Person and member of the Company's Promoter Group, including their Immediate Relatives, must disclose Securities acquired or disposed of within two trading days when the aggregate traded value in one transaction or a series of transactions during a calendar quarter exceeds INR 10,00,000 (ten lakh rupees), or another threshold prescribed by SEBI regulations or applicable law. The disclosure must use Form B in Annexure 5.
- Every Designated Person must disclose the names and Permanent Account Numbers or other legally authorized identifiers of their Immediate Relatives and persons with whom they share a material financial relationship. Phone and mobile numbers used by them must also be disclosed annually and whenever the information changes.
- Off-market trades conducted under paragraph 4(6)(i) of this Code must be reported to the Company within two working days.

### iii. Disclosures by other Connected Persons

The Compliance Officer may require any other Connected Person to disclose holdings and trading in Company Securities using Form C in Annexure 6, at a frequency determined by the Compliance Officer.

## 10. Mechanism for prevention of Insider Trading

1. The CEO, Managing Director, or analogous person, in consultation with the Compliance Officer, Chief Legal Officer, Chief Compliance Officer, and other relevant senior-management members, must establish and maintain adequate and effective internal controls for compliance with SEBI regulations. The controls must be periodically reviewed for effectiveness.
2. The Board must ensure that the CEO, Managing Director, and other relevant senior-management members comply with these internal-control requirements.
3. The Audit Committee must review compliance with SEBI regulations at least once in each financial year and verify that internal-control systems are adequate and operating effectively.

## 11. Miscellaneous

- The Board may amend, modify, and interpret this Code. Amendments become effective on the date notified by the Board.
- The Compliance Officer must provide the Board's Audit Committee with a quarterly update on compliance, violations, and other matters directed by the Audit Committee.
- The Compliance Officer must maintain:
  - An updated list of Designated Persons
  - Records of disclosures, pre-clearance applications, and undertakings for eight years
  - Other information required under SEBI regulations
- The Board must ensure that a structured digital database is maintained for every person possessing UPSI. The database must record:
  - The nature of the UPSI
  - The names of persons who shared the information
  - The names and Permanent Account Numbers, or other legally authorized identifiers where a Permanent Account Number is unavailable, of persons with whom the information is shared under SEBI regulations

The database must be maintained internally with adequate controls and checks, including time stamping and audit trails to prevent tampering.

- The Company may engage a market intermediary or another person required to handle UPSI who has formulated a code of conduct under the PIT Regulations. If such person observes a violation of SEBI regulations, they must promptly inform the Board.
- The Company has adopted the amended Infosys Code on Fair Disclosures and Investor Relations to regulate fair disclosure of UPSI and comply with SEC regulations.
- Suspected UPSI leaks or violations of this Code may be reported under the whistleblower policy.
- Retaliation against employees who report suspected violations of insider-trading laws under the informant mechanism is prohibited. Protected conduct includes reports made in accordance with the Regulations, and protection applies against discharge, termination, demotion, suspension, threats, harassment, or discrimination.
- Intermediaries or fiduciaries engaged by the Company must maintain adequate and effective internal controls to comply with SEBI regulations and prevent insider trading.
- Connected Persons are responsible for complying with this Code.
- The policy and procedure for inquiries into actual or suspected UPSI leaks is enclosed as Annexure 7 and forms an integral part of this Code.

The Company must maintain a process for determining how and when persons are brought inside on sensitive transactions. Individuals receiving inside information must be informed of their related duties, responsibilities, and liability for misuse or unwarranted use of the information.

## 12. Limitation, Review and Amendment

If this Code conflicts with SEBI regulations or another legal requirement, the applicable law prevails. Subsequent amendments or modifications to applicable law automatically apply to this Code.

The Board may review and amend this Code from time to time.
