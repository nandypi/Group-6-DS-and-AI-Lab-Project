Inderpreet Sawhney is responsible for Legal and Data Protection at Infosys. She is the Chairperson of Infosys Foundation USA and a trustee of Infosys Foundation India. She serves on the Global Board of Directors of the Association of Corporate Counsel and previously served on the Board of Directors of Hillenbrand Inc. from 2021 to 2026. She was Co-Chair of the World Economic Forum Global Future Council for Good Governance from 2023 to 2024.

Shaji Mathew is the Chief Human Resource Officer at Infosys. He is responsible for the HR roadmap, strategy, and operational priorities aligned with the organizational mandate of Infosys and its group companies. Shaji has over 33 years of experience at Infosys.

Before becoming Chief Human Resource Officer, Shaji was Infosys’ Global Head of Delivery for Financial Services, Insurance, Healthcare, and Life Sciences. He has held leadership roles in delivery, client relationships, commercial management, solutioning, and people management across the United States, Europe, and Asia Pacific. He has also led efforts to create a more inclusive and diverse workforce.

Shaji is a trustee of Infosys Foundation, focusing on corporate social responsibility. He serves on the board of the International Institute of Information Technology, Bangalore, and is a member of the Confederation of Indian Industry National Committee on Leadership and HR. He is a rank holder from the National Institute of Technology, Calicut, and has completed leadership programs at Stanford University Graduate School of Business and Harvard Business School.

Jayesh Sanghrajka is the Executive Vice President and Group CFO of Infosys Ltd. He has over three decades of experience in global finance and strategic leadership. He oversees the global finance organization, including financial planning and analysis, investor relations, treasury, tax, risk management, facilities, and infrastructure.

Jayesh is responsible for supporting growth at good margins, financial and operational excellence, and leadership development. He has led the finance organization’s digital transformation and cross-functional initiatives focused on identifying revenue growth opportunities and optimizing costs.

Jayesh is responsible for mergers and acquisitions, helping create and sustain Infosys’s long-term competitive advantage. He also champions the company’s ESG agenda, including sustainable growth, compliance with international standards, and stakeholder transparency.

Before joining Infosys, Jayesh worked at Tishman Speyer, Rediff.com, Mu Sigma, and KPMG, where he contributed to digital and financial transformations. He holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce from Mumbai University, is a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and has a degree in cost accounting. He has attended executive global leadership development programs at Stanford University and Harvard University.
