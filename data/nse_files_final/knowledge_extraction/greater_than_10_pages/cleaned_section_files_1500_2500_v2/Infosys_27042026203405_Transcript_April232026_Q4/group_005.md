## Salil Parekh

Q4 is seasonally weaker than the first half of the year, consistent with Infosys’ normal pattern in which the second half is somewhat lower than the first half.

Infosys is hiring people with different skill sets and compensation levels, including candidates more attuned to AI. The company is also building a forward-deployed engineer team to work directly with clients on business and technology applications of AI.

More than 90% of Infosys employees are being skilled on different AI platforms.

## Rishi Basu

The next question is from Jas Bardia of *Mint*.

## Jas Bardia

Brokerages have estimated that the three acquisitions announced during the previous fiscal year could contribute approximately 200–225 basis points of growth in FY27. The question concerns the growth trajectory, including the effects of AI-led deflation and clients reducing technology spending.

Additional questions addressed Board oversight of AI use for clients and internally, and the types of acquisitions Infosys may pursue.

## Salil Parekh

The revenue contribution of an acquisition depends on its closing date. One acquisition that has already closed has been included. The company does not provide guidance assuming acquisitions will close, but will include the contribution once an acquisition closes. On a full-year basis, the estimated contribution of approximately 200–225 basis points is broadly correct.

Infosys expects growth in FY27 both excluding and including acquisitions. Growth is expected from AI services work and large deals, although there is some compression that the company is managing.

The Board is actively involved in Infosys’ AI work. The company provides detailed internal updates to the Board, which discusses the company’s AI activities. Nandan Nilekani is also actively involved in shaping the company’s AI vision and has contributed ideas related to Topaz Fabric.

Infosys expects to continue making acquisitions and has a good pipeline. Potential areas include healthcare, where the company believes it can expand in a strong market.

## Jas Bardia

The question concerned the evolution of contracts over the previous three to four years, including movement from fixed-price arrangements toward outcome-based contracts. The question also addressed the means by which the Board oversees AI work.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys continues to use the types of contracts it used in the past. Because AI is transformative, the company is discussing whether some engagements can be structured on an outcome-based basis. Delivery and sales leadership have developed models and templates for these arrangements, and active discussions are underway. Most contracts continue to follow the previous contracting approach.

## Infosys Limited Q4 FY26 Earnings Conference Call

April 23, 2026

## Sandeep Mahindroo

Infosys delivered FY26 financial results. Future outlook statements should be read together with the risks faced by the company, as described in its SEC filings.

## Salil Parekh

Infosys reported strong performance in financial year 2026:

- Full-year revenue growth was 3.1% in constant currency terms.
- Q4 revenue growth was 4.1% year-on-year in constant currency terms.
- Full-year growth was strong in Financial Services, Communications, Manufacturing, and Europe.
- FY26 large deals totaled $14.9 billion, up 28% from the prior year.
- Q4 large deals totaled $3.2 billion.

Infosys identified a large addressable market for AI services across six areas:

1. AI strategy and engineering
2. Data
3. Process
4. Legacy modernization
5. Physical AI
6. Trust

Infosys uses its Topaz Fabric AI platform and Cobalt cloud platform to support clients across these areas.

## Some examples of the work we are doing,

## Infosys

- For Ralph Lauren, Infosys helped build a conversational and personalized AI tool that converted customer interest into a shopping experience. The work resulted in a 12% increase in revenue and a 50% increase in customer engagement.
- For Hertz, Infosys used AI foundation models to migrate 3 million lines of COBOL code to a modern microservices environment. The cost was 60% lower and the timeline was 60% quicker than the non-AI approach.
- For BP, Infosys deployed 50 AI-agent initiatives across trading, supply chain, sustainability, and core operations. The initiatives addressed software development, knowledge automation, legacy modernization, and digital decision support. Reported outcomes included 95% payment accuracy, 50% faster contract validation, and an 18% improvement in IT operations efficiency.

Infosys has strategic collaborations with emerging foundation-model companies including Anthropic and OpenAI to support software development, legacy modernization, and agent building. The company also has strategic AI collaborations with Google Gemini, NVIDIA, Microsoft, AWS, Google Cloud, and Intel, among others.

More than 30,000 Infosys developers have been deployed on GitHub Copilot.

For financial year 2027, Infosys expects large opportunities in AI services, continued competitive intensity, and an impact from AI productivity. The company provided the following guidance:

- Revenue growth: 1.5%–3.5% year-on-year in constant currency terms
- Operating margin: 20%–22%
- Expected growth acceleration: Financial Services and Energy, Utilities and Resources Services
- Seasonality: H1 expected to be stronger than H2, consistent with normal seasonality
