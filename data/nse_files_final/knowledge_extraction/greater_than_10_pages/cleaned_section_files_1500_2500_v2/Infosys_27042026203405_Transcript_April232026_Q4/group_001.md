# Infosys Limited Q4 FY26 Media Conference Call

**Date:** April 23, 2026

**Participants:**

- Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director
- Jayesh Sanghrajka, Chief Financial Officer
- Rishi Basu, Associate Vice President and Global Head—Corporate Communications

## FY26 and Q4 FY26 performance

Infosys reported full-year FY26 constant-currency revenue growth of 3.1% and Q4 year-on-year constant-currency growth of 4.1%.

Strong growth was reported in Financial Services, Communications, and Manufacturing by industry, and in Europe by geography.

Large-deal total contract value was $14.9 billion for FY26 and $3.2 billion for Q4 FY26. FY26 large deals were 28% higher than in the previous year. Net-new large deals represented 55% of the full-year large-deal total.

## AI services and platforms

Infosys described a large addressable market for AI services across six areas:

- AI strategy engineering
- Data
- Process
- Legacy modernization
- Physical AI
- Trust

The company highlighted its Topaz Fabric platform for AI and Cobalt platform for cloud as operational capabilities being used with clients across these areas.

AI services revenue was 5.5% of revenue in Q3 FY26. Management stated that the percentage was higher in Q4 and that AI services revenue was growing strongly, but did not disclose the Q4 figure.

Management referenced an externally studied AI services addressable market of approximately $300 billion to $400 billion for 2030 and stated that Infosys was targeting a significant market share.

## FY27 outlook and guidance

Infosys identified continued opportunities in AI services, alongside competitive intensity and an expected AI productivity impact. Management stated that these factors, together with growth in selected industries and the contribution from an acquisition, informed the FY27 guidance.

FY27 guidance:

- Revenue growth: 1.5% to 3.5% year-on-year in constant currency
- Operating margin: 20% to 22%

Management expects growth to accelerate in Financial Services and in the Energy, Utilities, Resources and Services vertical.

## Acquisitions and joint venture

Infosys discussed three transactions announced during the previous year:

- **Stratus:** The acquisition involving an insurance company had closed and was included in FY27 guidance. Its contribution represented approximately 25 basis points, or 0.25 percentage points, of the guidance.
- **Optimum:** The acquisition had not closed and was not included in FY27 guidance because certain regulatory approvals were pending.
- **Australian joint venture:** The joint venture with an Australian client was pending regulatory approvals and was not included in FY27 guidance.

## Revenue growth considerations

FY26's 3.1% growth included the impact of:

- 1% lower third-party-related revenue
- 70 basis points of lower on-site mix

Management stated that Financial Services growth was expected to accelerate in FY27. Growth was also expected to increase in Energy, Utilities, and Resources.

The company continued to observe AI-related compression and competitive intensity, but management stated that the situation had not changed unusually between the previous quarter and Q4.

## Operating-margin performance and drivers

Full-year FY26 operating margin was 21%. Q4 operating margin was 20.9%.

The quarter-on-quarter margin movement included:

- 50 basis points of acquisition-related amortization
- A 30-basis-point headwind from comparing against a one-off benefit in Q3
- A 20-basis-point impact from compensation-related matters
- A 40-basis-point benefit from currency
- A 30-basis-point contribution from Maximus performance

## Fresher hiring

Infosys had announced a plan to hire 20,000 freshers in FY26 and hired more than 20,000 freshers during the year.

The company expected to hire at least 20,000 freshers in FY27.
