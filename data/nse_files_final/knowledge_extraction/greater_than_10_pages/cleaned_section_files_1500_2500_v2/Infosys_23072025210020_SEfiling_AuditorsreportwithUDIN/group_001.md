## RESULTS

## Opinion

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP audited the accompanying statement of consolidated financial results of Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.

In the auditor’s opinion, the statement:

- Includes the financial results of the subsidiaries listed in the annexure.
- Is presented in accordance with the requirements of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, including Regulation 33.
- Gives a true and fair view of the Group’s consolidated net profit, consolidated other comprehensive income, and other financial information for the quarter ended June 30, 2025.
- Conforms to the recognition and measurement principles of Ind AS 34, *Interim Financial Reporting*, prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, together with applicable rules and generally accepted accounting principles in India.

## Basis for Opinion

The audit was conducted in accordance with the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013. The auditors stated that they were independent of the Group in accordance with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India’s Code of Ethics and applicable ethical requirements, and that sufficient and appropriate audit evidence was obtained as a basis for the audit opinion.

## Management's and Board of Directors' Responsibilities for the Statement

The consolidated financial results were compiled from the related audited interim condensed consolidated financial statements for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

The respective boards of directors of the companies included in the Group were responsible for:

- Preparing and presenting financial results that give a true and fair view in accordance with Ind AS 34 and applicable Indian accounting principles.
- Maintaining adequate accounting records and safeguarding the Group’s assets.
- Preventing and detecting fraud and other irregularities.
- Selecting and applying appropriate accounting policies.
- Making reasonable and prudent judgments and estimates.
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining adequate internal financial controls operating effectively to ensure the accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Assessing the ability of the respective entities to continue as going concerns.
- Disclosing, where applicable, matters related to going concern and using the going-concern basis of accounting unless liquidation or cessation of operations was intended or unavoidable.
- Overseeing the Group’s financial reporting process.

## Auditor's Responsibilities for Audit of the Consolidated Financial Results for the Quarter Ended June 30, 2025

The auditors’ objectives were to obtain reasonable assurance that the consolidated financial results were free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor’s report containing their opinion. The report states that reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but does not guarantee that an audit will detect every material misstatement.

The auditors’ responsibilities included:

- Identifying and assessing risks of material misstatement and designing audit procedures responsive to those risks.
- Obtaining sufficient and appropriate audit evidence.
- Considering that the risk of failing to detect fraud-related misstatement is higher because fraud may involve collusion, forgery, intentional omissions, misrepresentations, or management override of internal controls.
- Obtaining an understanding of relevant internal controls to design appropriate audit procedures, without expressing an opinion on the effectiveness of those controls.
- Evaluating the appropriateness of accounting policies and the reasonableness of accounting estimates made by the Board of Directors.
- Evaluating the appropriateness and reasonableness of disclosures.
- Assessing the appropriateness of the going-concern basis of accounting and whether events or conditions created a material uncertainty about the Group’s ability to continue as a going concern.
- Evaluating the overall presentation, structure, content, and disclosures of the consolidated financial results.
- Performing procedures required under the SEBI circular issued under Regulation 33(8) of the Listing Regulations, where applicable.
- Obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence regarding the financial information of entities within the Group and taking responsibility for the direction, supervision, and performance of audits of entities included in the statement where the reporting auditors were the independent auditors.

The auditors considered quantitative materiality and qualitative factors when planning the audit, evaluating audit results, and assessing the effect of identified misstatements. They also communicated with those charged with governance regarding the planned scope and timing of the audit, significant audit findings, internal control deficiencies, auditor independence, and related safeguards.
