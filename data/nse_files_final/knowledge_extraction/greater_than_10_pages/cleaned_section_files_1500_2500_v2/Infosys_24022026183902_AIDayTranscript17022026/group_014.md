Infosys described several initiatives intended to strengthen its association with enterprise AI:

- It launched Infosys Topaz as an AI services brand.
- It conducted enterprise AI conversations and Enterprise AI World Tour events globally. In the last 12 months, 700 clients interacted with and learned about Infosys’s enterprise AI approach.
- It expanded the Infosys Knowledge Institute to conduct research on the direction of AI, including the Enterprise AI Radar and AI indexes across different verticals.

Infosys stated that it conducts quarterly research to track its brand association with enterprise AI in a rapidly changing market. It reported being at the top of the industry rankings over the last year.

The company also tracks client voice as a measure of success and stated that word-of-mouth recognition has been consistently rising. It attributed a year-on-year increase of over 100% in global media share of volume to its focused thought-leadership efforts.

Infosys stated that industry analysts have consistently ranked it highly for leadership across digital and AI ratings. The company linked its enterprise AI efforts to staying ahead of the market on revenue and achieving market share gains.

Going forward, Infosys intends to fulfill its “navigate your next” brand promise as clients transition through another major technology shift, with unlocking AI value as the central focus of its brand efforts.
