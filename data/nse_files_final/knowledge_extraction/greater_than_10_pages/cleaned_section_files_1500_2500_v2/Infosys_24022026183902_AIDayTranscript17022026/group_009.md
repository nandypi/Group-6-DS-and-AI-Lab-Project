## Dennis Gada

## Infosys

Financial services is at the forefront of adopting and scaling AI. Banks, asset and wealth managers, custodians, and card providers are using AI to bend the cost and growth curves while managing risk and compliance.

AI adoption in financial services is increasing spending on both AI-first services and AI-augmented services. Key constraints include regulation, data privacy, change management, and adoption. Even if AI technology stopped evolving, financial services firms would still have substantial work to deploy and benefit from existing capabilities.

Infosys is supporting AI use cases across financial services sub-verticals, including:

- Fraud prevention in payments and consumer banking.
- Customer experience through contact centers and UI/UX.
- Data insights and productivity tools for commercial banking relationship managers, asset and wealth management advisers, and financial analysts.
- Agentic commerce and payments, which are still at an early stage.

These activities are supported by AI-based software engineering, process orchestration, and data transformation.

### Citizens Bank

Citizens Bank is a top-15 bank in the United States that has grown organically and through acquisitions. It launched the “Reimagine the Bank” program to use AI to expand its services and improve efficiency. Infosys was selected as the strategic partner for the initiative, and an AI innovation hub dedicated to Citizens Bank was opened in Bangalore.

Infosys previously helped Citizens Bank move 100% of its operations to the cloud. Using the Infosys Topaz Fabric suite of agents, Infosys is helping the bank build its own Agentic AI and GenAI platform for use cases across the bank.

Reported outcomes and targets include:

- A 44% reduction in calls to the contact center generated from the mobile app.
- A $450 million cost run-rate reduction target under the “Reimagine the Bank” program.

### Other Financial Services Client Engagements

Financial services is Infosys’s largest industry segment. Infosys works with global and regional banks, card providers, and asset and wealth management firms. Fifteen of its top 25 financial services clients have selected Infosys as a strategic partner for AI services.

For its top three card-provider clients, Infosys is using AI to modernize core card platforms containing approximately 40 million lines of code developed over several decades. The modernization work has reduced the time required by 50% and generated efficiency benefits. Following the success of one program, the client requested two or three additional modernization programs.

For a large global wealth management firm, Infosys is helping build an Agentic AI platform and supporting initiatives that provide financial advisers with better data insights and higher productivity.

Financial services organizations have complex legacy environments and significant regulatory oversight. Infosys cites its industry expertise, AI capabilities, and long-term client relationships—including relationships of more than a decade—as relevant to supporting AI adoption and operating-model changes.

## Ashiss Kumar Dash

## Infosys?

The energy, utilities, resources and services segment is both a major consumer of AI and an enabler of AI infrastructure.

Electric utilities influence where data centers can be built and how quickly they can grow. Data centers are projected to consume approximately 10% to 12% of global electricity by 2030, nearly four times the current level.

Oil and gas support the global energy supply. Natural gas and CNG are becoming transient fuels for data centers, supporting grid reliability and load balancing when wind and solar generation is unavailable.

Resources companies provide materials required to scale AI infrastructure, including copper, lithium, nickel, cobalt, rare earth materials, and aluminum. Services businesses are significant AI consumers for inference because of the B2C nature of their operations.

The segment is experiencing increased demand and high digital intensity. AI is being used in industrial applications including:

- Subsurface computing.
- Digital and remote mining.
- Grid reliability and load prediction.

These industries are asset-heavy and ERP-heavy, with decades of business rules, data, compliance requirements, and regulations embedded in ERP systems. Infosys is applying AI to unlock value from ERP data and create an orchestration layer for improved human experience. AI-driven operating-cost savings can support additional discretionary and transformational projects.

Infosys identifies three capabilities supporting its work in the segment:

- Understanding of the client’s context.
- Domain consulting expertise.
- Enterprise-scale AI engineering.

Infosys is the AI partner for 15 of its top 25 clients in this segment.

### Energy, Utilities and Resources AI Use Cases

- Infosys created digital twins for a large oil and gas major using asset telemetry to improve asset intelligence and automation and reduce downtime.
- For a large electricity provider, Infosys performed AI-grade data engineering to predict grid loads and identify areas of grid congestion requiring investment to support data-center electricity demand.
- Infosys is helping more than 20 clients reimagine business processes, including finance and accounting and customer service.

The stated enterprise outcomes include revenue growth, cost reduction, margin expansion, customer satisfaction, and innovation.
