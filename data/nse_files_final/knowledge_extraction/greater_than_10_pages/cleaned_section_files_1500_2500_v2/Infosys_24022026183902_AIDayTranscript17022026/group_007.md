# Topaz Fabric, AI Next, and Enterprise AI Capabilities

Infosys describes four enterprise-AI “runways,” including the development of enterprise context. Its enterprise context maps how systems work, what the infrastructure looks like, where applications run, where data resides, and how these components connect.

Infosys is also building industry context models for retail, banking, and consumer packaged goods (CPG). These models are delivered through graph technology and are used to build enterprise twins. The company is developing tooling for multispeed IT governance, AI-tool onboarding, guardrails, and trusted delivery of AI solutions through Infosys Topaz.

## AI Next and Topaz Fabric

Infosys uses AI Next when customers want an end-to-end, vertically integrated AI and agentic platform. It uses Topaz Fabric when customers want a composable, modular platform that works with their existing AI investments.

Topaz Fabric provides the following capabilities:

- It works above customers’ existing model layers, platforms, and enterprise systems without replacing them.
- It can integrate with any model or framework.
- It provides close to 600 purpose-built agents for AI-first services, AI-augmented services, and industry-specific workflows.
- It provides out-of-the-box integrations with coding tools, AI models, business platforms such as SAP, Oracle, and Salesforce, data platforms such as Snowflake and Databricks, and enterprise platforms such as ServiceNow.
- It includes pre-packaged ontologies and models that incorporate enterprise context and hybrid intelligence.
- As the platform is deployed, it learns from data and processes, creating a feedback loop in which the context improves over time.

Infosys combines deterministic rules with AI models and its own small language models to improve predictability, cost, and time to market. The company states that this work is supported by research and patents filed over the years.

## AI-Native Partnerships and Research

Infosys works with AI-native partners in three ways:

1. It leverages partner platforms for enterprise-relevant tasks. Its partnership with Cognition uses Devin for brownfield engineering.
2. It builds embedded agents that operate within partner tools, including Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and ServiceNow.
3. It integrates with partner tooling to support the end-to-end value chain.

Infosys conducts joint research with Cambridge, Columbia, and Cornell on agentic technology, scaling, and trust. It also conducts research through its internal research centers.

## Logistics Customer-Care Automation

For a logistics client, Infosys used AI Next to uncover existing customer-specific knowledge and extracted approximately 8,000 rules.

Workflow automation increased from approximately 0%–10% at the start of the project to approximately 70%. Turnaround time decreased from 24 hours to approximately 30 minutes.

To address the client’s stack-sovereignty requirements, Infosys used Mistral and Pixtral models. The solution supports orders or bookings from 116 countries and 15 languages.

As the solution scaled, Infosys introduced optimizations to significantly reduce the customer’s cost. The solution had been live for one year at the time of the discussion.
