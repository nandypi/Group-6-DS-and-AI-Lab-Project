## Nandan M. Nilekani

### Fiscal 2026 performance and capital allocation

Infosys reported fiscal 2026 revenue of US$20.2 billion, representing 3.1% growth in constant currency. Adjusted operating margin was 21%. The company generated US$3.7 billion in free cash flow, equal to 112.6% of net profit and marking the second consecutive year of free cash flow above 100% of net profit.

Large deal TCV for fiscal 2026 was US$14.9 billion, of which 55% was net new.

For fiscal 2026, the Board recommended a final dividend of ₹25, in addition to the interim dividend of ₹23 already paid. Total dividend was ₹48, representing 11.6% year-on-year growth. Total payout for FY 2025-26, including the recently completed buyback, was 113.9% of free cash flow. Infosys had cumulatively returned 82.1% of free cash flow over two years under its Capital Allocation Policy.

### Board and governance matters

Nitin Paranjpe, an Independent Director, was appointed Vice Chairman of the Company effective April 30, 2026.

After fiscal 2026, the Board appointed Diane Jurgens as an Independent Director effective April 22, 2026, for a three-year term ending April 21, 2029.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommended the reappointment of Helene Potier for a second five-year term. The Board approved her reappointment as an Independent Director from May 26, 2026, to May 25, 2031.

The appointments of Diane Jurgens and Helene Portier’s reappointment as an Independent Director for a second term were approved by shareholders through a postal ballot.

### Enterprise AI and technology strategy

Infosys stated that the AI transition is increasing the importance of enterprise context, compatibility with existing investments, rigorous testing, resilient architecture, foundational cybersecurity, and organization-specific data governance.

The company identified the AI deployment gap among large enterprise clients as an area of work. It also stated that AI is making legacy modernization more urgent, with clients seeking to retire technical debt accumulated over decades. The company expects a preference to build rather than buy software and identified the integration of AI systems, models, and agents with mission-critical enterprise platforms and traditional transaction systems as a source of future opportunities.

Infosys was collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys. The company described Infosys Topaz as supporting AI adoption and Infosys Cobalt as providing cloud capabilities for scaling AI. Infosys also unveiled an AI-first value framework for global enterprises and identified an AI-first services opportunity of US$300 billion to US$400 billion by 2030.

### Talent and partnerships

Infosys stated that continuous learning and talent transformation remain core priorities. During fiscal 2026, the company recruited more than 20,000 college graduates and ended the year with a workforce of more than 325,000 employees.

Infosys described its AI partnership ecosystem as spanning compute and cloud platforms, model developers, data and analytics providers, applications, and security technologies.

During the year, Infosys was recognized as a global top employer in 20 countries for human resources practices and was named one of India's Best Employers among Nation Builders 2025 by Great Place to Work.

### Acquisitions and strategic transactions

During fiscal 2026, Infosys completed the acquisitions of:

- MRE Consulting Limited, a United States-based energy and business consulting firm.
- The Missing Link Group, an Australia-based cybersecurity service provider.

Infosys also entered into definitive agreements on three strategic fronts:

- Forming a joint venture with Telstra and acquiring a 75% stake in the Versent Group, an Australian digital transformation solutions provider and wholly owned subsidiary of Telstra Group Limited, which delivers cloud and digital transformation services.
- Acquiring Stratus Global LLC, a United States-based insurance, consulting, and technology services company focused on Guidewire services.
- Acquiring Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc., together with subsidiaries including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC, a United States-based healthcare digital transformation and consulting company.

The acquisitions of Stratus Global LLC and Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc. were completed.

### ESG and social impact

Infosys stated that its ESG Vision for 2030 covers climate action, inclusive and equitable practices, and stakeholder trust through ethical governance.

The company achieved carbon neutrality for the seventh consecutive year across its global operations through reduced consumption, renewable sourcing, and carbon offsets. Renewables accounted for 81.8% of Infosys's India operations. Its campuses recycled 100% of wastewater through rainwater harvesting and demand-side efficiency measures. Eleven campuses achieved true zero-waste certification.

Infosys continued its social impact programs through Infosys Foundation, Infosys Science Foundation, Infosys Prize, and Infosys Foundation USA, covering education, skilling and livelihood training, healthcare, women's empowerment, science and research, and environmental sustainability.

During the year, Infosys Foundation undertook 200 projects in India that impacted seven million lives. Infosys Springboard digital-skilling initiatives reached 15 million people.
