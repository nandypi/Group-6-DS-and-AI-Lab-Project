## Om Prakash Kejriwal

Om Prakash Kejriwal, an equity shareholder from Kolkata, said that virtual AGMs had enabled him to attend his fifth AGM and requested that Infosys continue offering virtual participation so investors from different locations could join and express their views.

He noted that Infosys had increased its dividend from ₹43 to ₹48 per share on a face value of ₹5.

The shareholder said that Infosys’s share price was declining and was approximately ₹1,030, which he described as a 12-month low. He attributed the decline to insufficient investment in future growth and foreign investors selling the shares. He compared India’s capitalisation with Google’s stated market capitalisation of US$4.5 trillion and cited Taiwan and South Korea as examples of countries whose capital markets had grown through investment.

He urged Infosys and the Government of India to invest in future growth and skill development.

The shareholder criticised the buyback, stating that Infosys had spent ₹1,800 crore on it. He said that he had sent three emails suggesting changes to the buyback and claimed that the company’s capital could have been reduced by up to 80% if his suggestions had been followed. He also stated that long-term investors, including promoters, did not benefit from the buyback and that he had not participated through family members’ accounts because he believed participation could damage the balance sheet.

He referred to N. R. Narayana Murthy’s contribution to Aadhaar, which he said was widely used in direct benefit transfer schemes, and to Salil Parekh’s contribution to new software for the income tax department.
