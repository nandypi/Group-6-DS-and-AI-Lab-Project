## Salil Parekh

Salil Parekh, CEO and Managing Director, addressed shareholders on the financial year 2026 performance and the company’s outlook.

### Financial year 2026 performance

Infosys reported:

- Revenue of more than US$20 billion.
- Constant-currency growth of 3.1%.
- Operating margin of 21%.
- Strong free cash flow.
- More than 325,000 employees worldwide.
- Recruitment of 20,000 college graduates across India and other parts of the world.
- 41 clients with revenue of more than US$100 million at the close of the financial year.

Management stated that several areas of financial performance showed positive movement from the previous year. Cash flow also improved after adjusting for tax refunds received in the previous financial year.

### Capital returns and balance sheet

Infosys returned more than ₹55,000 crore to shareholders over the previous two years through dividends and a completed buyback.

The company’s capital return policy is structured over a five-year period, during which Infosys aims to return 85% of free cash flow.

Management stated that the balance sheet remained debt-free and highly liquid, with substantial cash and cash investments. The company continued to maintain this liquidity while returning capital to shareholders and pursuing acquisitions.

### AI transformation strategy and market approach

Infosys launched its AI approach in the market after working on AI internally for several years. The company developed Topaz and Topaz Fabric to provide AI capabilities to clients in partnership with AI companies.

Management identified six broad areas in which clients are using AI to improve growth, service quality, efficiency, and costs:

1. AI engineering and strategy, including the development of agents across platforms and tools.
2. Data, including the use of enterprise data in AI systems.
3. Process transformation, including process efficiency and the use of agents.
4. Technology modernization using agents.
5. Physical AI in manufacturing and other areas, including medical and automotive applications.
6. AI trust, including security and responsible AI.

Infosys estimated the addressable market across these six areas at approximately US$300 billion from the current period through 2030.

The company stated that it was working with clients across all six areas on large-scale programs rather than proof-of-concept projects.

### Enterprise AI capabilities

Infosys supports clients moving from their current technology environments toward AI-driven operations. Management identified the following capabilities as important to this work:

- Understanding of large enterprise environments, including technology, business, architecture, and data landscapes.
- Engineering talent and training in AI.
- Employees trained and certified on foundation models, coding tools, and AI compute platforms.
- Topaz Fabric, the company’s platform and intellectual property, which can work with different AI foundation models.
- A partner ecosystem involving AI foundation-model companies and compute providers.
- The ability to select relevant foundation models according to the client’s industry and requirements.

In Q3 of the previous year, Infosys had reported that approximately 5.5% of revenue came from AI services, equivalent to approximately US$1 billion on an annualized basis. Management reconfirmed this figure and stated that AI services revenue was growing at a strong pace.

Infosys stated that it had received leadership recognition in 16 areas across the six AI service categories from the analyst community.

### Cloud and data foundations

Management identified cloud and data as two foundational elements of enterprise AI.

Infosys has developed Cobalt over several years and uses its cloud capabilities, partnerships, and cloud-compute expertise to support AI implementation and migration. The company stated that many foundation models operate across multiple cloud platforms.

For enterprise data, Infosys works with structured and unstructured data and helps make that data available to AI models. Management stated that using a company’s own data and knowledge can support differentiation, while data security and trust remain important considerations.

Enterprises are also bringing customer and supplier data into their AI environments in addition to their internal data.

### Internal efficiency and delivery capabilities

Infosys has focused on internal efficiency and maintaining operating performance. Management attributed efficiency improvements to:

- Automation.
- The employee pyramid.
- Productivity initiatives.
- Use of AI in internal functions such as finance and marketing.
- Use of AI in coding.
- Portfolio improvements.
- Reductions in indirect costs.

Management also emphasized Infosys’s delivery organization, employee training, and ability to deliver multiservice offerings and complex programs. The company is increasing its focus on AI-related delivery capabilities.

### Acquisitions

Infosys completed several acquisitions during the financial year and completed or announced additional acquisitions shortly after the financial year and before the annual general meeting.

Management identified acquisitions in:

- Healthcare.
- Insurance.

The stated objective was to pursue opportunities in areas where Infosys could scale the acquired capabilities and integrate them into the broader business.

### CSR and community initiatives

Infosys described the following community and CSR activities:

- Approximately 200 projects in India.
- Contributions affecting approximately 7 million people in India.
- Infosys Springboard, a free online training capability that has benefited more than 10 million people worldwide.
- Sustainable solutions to improve water capacity in lakes in different parts of India.

### One Infosys operating model

Infosys described its internal operating approach as “One Infosys,” covering service lines, industries, and geographies. The objective is to make the company’s best capabilities available to every client and to support collaboration across internal teams.
