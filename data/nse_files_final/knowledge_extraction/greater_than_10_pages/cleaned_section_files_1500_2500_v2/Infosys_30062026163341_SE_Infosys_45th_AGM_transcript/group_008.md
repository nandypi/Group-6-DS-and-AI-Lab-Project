## Jaydip Bakshi

Jaydip Bakshi asked about Infosys' future AI growth strategy, its vision for building a global organization in a competitive market, and revenue-growth prospects in the coming years. He also asked about the attrition rate, talent development, and the company's approach to cybersecurity.

## Atanu Saha

Atanu Saha asked about Infosys' future plans for artificial intelligence. He cited the following figures from the AGM presentation or annual report:

- Collaboration with 90% of the top 200 clients on their AI journey.
- More than 4,600 AI projects underway.
- Employee-related figure of 84%.
- Employee satisfaction score of 79%.

He asked how long shareholders might have to wait for profitability from the substantial operating costs associated with AI. He also raised concerns about slowing or declining revenue growth and declining operating profit, referring to information on page 52.

## Santosh Chopra

Santosh Chopra described AI as both an opportunity and a potential threat for IT companies. He cited Infosys' experience with Y2K, the dot-com crash, and the emergence of cloud technology.

He suggested that Infosys should:

- Put people first in its AI strategy.
- Reskill employees rapidly.
- Develop AI solutions in Hindi for rural farmers and small shopkeepers in India.

## Vasudha Dakwe

Vasudha Dakwe asked why Infosys' attrition rate was high and what steps the company was taking to reduce it. She also asked why the company's employee-related rate or figure was not lower than that of other companies despite its overall performance.

## Kaushik Shahukar

Kaushik Shahukar asked what percentage of Infosys' current revenue was linked to AI-enabled businesses, services, and solutions. He also asked about management's target for AI-related business over the next two, three, and five years.

He suggested that Infosys expand AI lead-generation services and subscription-based digital platforms for small and medium enterprises globally. He said this could create recurring revenue, deepen client relationships, and reduce dependence on traditional project-based revenue.

## Yusuf Yunus

Yusuf Yunus asked why Infosys' share price had declined by ₹30 that day.
