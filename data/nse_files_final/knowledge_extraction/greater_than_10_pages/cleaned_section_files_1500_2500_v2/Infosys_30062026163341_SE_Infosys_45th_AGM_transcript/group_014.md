The company’s priorities for reaching $30 billion in revenue include capturing growth opportunities from increasing AI adoption, improving productivity, leveraging AI partnerships, and reskilling employees to support clients’ AI journeys.

Infosys identified six AI service areas where it expects significant impact, including modernization of clients’ technology estates. Clients are increasingly moving from AI pilots and small projects toward enterprise-wide AI deployments. Management also noted increased IT spending on AI services and modernization despite macroeconomic headwinds.

Infosys has AI partnerships and collaborates with foundation-model, compute, and tools companies. It has invested in Infosys Topaz Fabric and in continuous employee learning and skilling.

Last year, Infosys recruited 20,000 new college graduates and planned a similar increase in the current financial year. Management expects human employees and AI agents to work together, with overall work expanding as AI adoption develops across clients and industries. Employees are being developed as AI-aware employees, AI builders, and AI masters, with domain expertise used across different types of work.

Employee attrition was 12.6% last year, compared with 14.1% in the previous year and 12.6% in the year before that. Infosys’s employee value proposition includes career opportunities, rewards, recognition, learning and development, wellness, upskilling, reskilling, career-growth paths, and a flexible work environment. Its total-reward approach is benchmarked against the competitive industry.

Infosys reported approximately $15 billion in large deals last year. Of these deals, 55% were net new, representing a 24% increase from the previous year. The company also has medium-sized and small deals across its client portfolio. Management noted significant activity in AI-first global capability centers (GCCs).

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Management stated that financial guidance reduces information asymmetry between management and stakeholders, provides an anchor for market expectations, and communicates current plans and likely performance. Guidance was described as a global best practice adopted by the company for the benefit of shareholders.

Infosys stated that it is investing to remain competitive in AI-led transformation, including through Infosys Topaz Fabric, partnerships across the AI ecosystem, and continuous workforce learning and skilling.

Management did not comment on share-price movements. The recently concluded tender-offer buyback of ₹18,000 crore was stated to have resulted in EPS accretion for shareholders, increased return on equity, and returned cash in accordance with the company’s capital-allocation policy.

## A.G.S. Manikantha

The Board designated A.G.S. Manikantha as the nodal officer for the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF). Shareholders whose shares have been transferred to IEPF can follow the detailed claim process provided on the company’s website.

Infosys periodically informs shareholders about encashing unclaimed dividends and updating bank-account details with the registrar and transfer agent or depository participants. The company publishes information about unclaimed dividends and corresponding shares on its website.

Infosys also receives updated bank details from the depository for eligible shareholders so that dividends can be processed directly without a specific shareholder request.

## Nandan M. Nilekani

E-voting on the NSDL platform remained available for 30 minutes after the announcement. The Board appointed B. Hemanth, Practicing Company Secretary, as scrutinizer to supervise the voting process and authorized the Company Secretary to declare the voting results and publish them on the company’s website.

The resolutions set forth in the notice were to be deemed passed on the date of the meeting, subject to receipt of the requisite number of votes. A total of 326 members participated in Infosys Limited’s 45th Annual General Meeting.
