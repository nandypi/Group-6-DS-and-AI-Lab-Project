## A.G.S. Manikantha

The moderator invited shareholder Santosh Kumar Saraf to ask his question.

## Santosh Kumar Saraf

Santosh Kumar Saraf of Kolkata asked:

> Infosys successfully closed a US$20 million revenue milestone. What are the top three strategic priorities that will help the Company reach US$30 million in revenue while maintaining an industry-leading margin and creating superior shareholder retention?

## A.G.S. Manikantha

The moderator invited Bharat Shah and Smita Shah to speak.

## Smita Shah

Smita Shah:

- Asked the Chairman to explain Infosys’s future plans for the next five years.
- Asked about the impact of AI on the Company.
- Asked which new technologies Infosys intends to pursue.
- Praised the leadership of Chairman Nandan Nilekani, Managing Director Salil Parekh, the Board, employees, Company Secretary A.G.S. Manikantha, and the Secretarial team.
- Commented positively on the comprehensive presentation of the balance sheet and the investor services provided to shareholders.
- Expressed strong support for all the AGM resolutions.
- Expressed the hope that dividends would increase year after year.

## Bharat Shah

Bharat Shah:

- Praised Infosys’s shareholder returns and dividends.
- Requested consideration of a bonus issue in the next two or three years.
- Commented positively on the Company’s CSR activities.
- Praised the Company Secretary and Secretarial team for resolving shareholder queries, facilitating participation through video conferencing, and maintaining contact with shareholders.
- Expressed full support for all the AGM resolutions.
