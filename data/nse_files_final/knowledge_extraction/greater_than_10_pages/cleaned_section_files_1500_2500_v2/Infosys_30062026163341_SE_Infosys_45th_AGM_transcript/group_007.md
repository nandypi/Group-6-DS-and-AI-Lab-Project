## Reddeppa Gundluru

The shareholder asked Infosys management about:

- Key growth priorities for the next two to three years, particularly in artificial intelligence and generative AI.
- Plans to improve revenue growth and deal conversion in the current global economic environment.
- Opportunities arising from increased digital transformation spending by global enterprises.
- The outlook for employee retention, talent development, and skill development in emerging technologies.
- Potential strategic acquisitions to strengthen capabilities in AI, cybersecurity, and cloud services.

## Bharat Raj

The shareholder thanked the company for its dividend payout and asked whether Infosys would consider issuing a bonus.

He also asked about the company’s plans regarding “ASF,” referring to a recent 10% decline in its price, and how Infosys would respond to global changes involving AI and ChatGPT.

He requested that the company arrange a shareholder visit to the Mangalore campus, noting that physical AGMs were no longer being held.

## P. Jaychand

P. Jaychand, a joint holder with P. Shyam Sundari, asked management to reduce expenditure and improve cost effectiveness.

The shareholder requested consideration of:

- A bonus issue.
- A rights issue if a bonus issue was not considered.
- Measures addressing the decline in the share price.
- Future plans to enhance the business.
- Measures to withstand tougher competition.

The shareholder also asked whether Infosys would consider conducting a physical AGM in the future.

## Abhishek

The shareholder asked about:

- New plans, innovations, projects, and geographic areas of focus.
- The current status and future expansion of the Infosys campus at Pocharam in Hyderabad.
- Whether the Pocharam campus was fully operational.
- The number of employees deployed at the campus.

The shareholder also requested that the company consider job opportunities for eligible relatives of shareholders and expressed hope for a hybrid AGM the following year.
