## A.G.S. Manikantha

The statutory auditors, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, and the Secretarial Auditor, Makarand M. Joshi and Company, expressed unqualified opinions in their respective audit reports for FY 2025–26. There were no qualifications, observations, or adverse comments on the financial statements that had a material bearing on the functioning of the Company.

The statutory auditors’ reports on the standalone and consolidated financial statements are available on pages 226 and 307, respectively, of the Integrated Annual Report. The Secretarial Auditor’s report is enclosed as Annexure 5 to the Board’s report on page 91 of the Integrated Annual Report.

## Nandan M. Nilekani

The resolutions set forth in the Notice were tabled for consideration:

1. **Adoption of the financial statements:** Adoption of the Company’s financial statements, including the consolidated financial statements, for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, together with the reports of the Board of Directors and auditors.

2. **Declaration of dividend:** Declaration of a final dividend of ₹25 per share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. An interim dividend of ₹23 per equity share had already been paid for the same financial year.

3. **Appointment of Nandan M. Nilekani as a director liable to retire by rotation.**

4. **Amendment of the Infosys Expanded Stock Option Ownership Program 2019:** Approval of the amended 2019 Plan and the grant of stock incentives to eligible employees of the Company.

5. **Amendment of the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019:** Approval of the amended 2019 Plan and the grant of stock incentives to eligible employees of the Company’s subsidiaries.

6. **Reclassification of promoter and promoter-group members:** Approval of the request to reclassify certain members of the promoter and promoter group of the Company to the public category. In accordance with SEBI regulations, the applicants and persons related to them were not permitted to vote on this resolution.

Members were informed that electronic voting was available for all resolutions through NSDL and that voting by show of hands would not take place.

## A.G.S. Manikantha

Shareholders participating in the question-and-answer session were asked to state their name and location and were given two minutes for questions. The Board would respond to the questions at the end of the session.

## Dharav Vipul Jamadar, Gujarat

The shareholder asked about the following matters:

- Whether the IT industry had moved beyond its earlier period of double-digit growth and whether there were signs of an industry recovery.
- The outlook for Infosys’ margins and ROCE, including why currency depreciation had not translated into margin expansion and higher ROCE.
- Which geographies and industries were expected to be more stable and grow robustly, and which might face the greatest disruption from technology and geopolitical changes.
- The effect of macroeconomic conditions, including El Niño, potentially higher interest rates, inflationary pressure, volatile energy prices, and currency depreciation, on public and private IT-services capital expenditure.
- Which parts of the deal-wins matrix were expected to grow most and which might face a drag during the financial year.
- How Infosys planned to fund expansion into AI, data centers, quantum computing, and other areas requiring substantial capital.
- Whether future growth would be funded through higher leverage and debt, and how this would affect the capital structure, dividend distribution policy, and ROCE.
- Whether Infosys should invest in global AI companies or pursue mergers and acquisitions to accelerate its AI capabilities.
- How Infosys could benefit from India securing trade-related advantages with developed nations, given its presence in developing and developed markets.
- What additional government relaxations or regulations would be requested for the IT-services industry.

## Vipul Kumar Shah

The shareholder asked about:

- The potential renewal or extension of CEO Salil Parekh’s tenure and whether the Board was considering an extension or another candidate.
- Whether Infosys should reconsider providing quarterly guidance because minor quarterly misses or revenue shifts between contracts can create stock-price volatility.
- Revenue from AI-related offerings during the last financial year, expected growth from those offerings, the margin difference between AI-related and normal services, and the contribution of AI-related services to total revenue.
- Nandan M. Nilekani’s Analyst Day comment that opportunities in the AI world are endless and that execution is the main risk, including how Infosys was progressing in that direction.
