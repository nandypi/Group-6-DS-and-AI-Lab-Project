## Compensation

Infosys’ executive compensation programs tie a significant portion of executive total rewards to long-term corporate performance goals. The Overview of Executive Leadership Compensation is filed as an exhibit to the Annual Report on Form 20-F.

The 2015 Plan and 2019 Plan were previously filed as exhibits to the Annual Report on Form 20-F.

Under NYSE listing regulations effective April 1, 2019, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee recommends remuneration for senior management to the Board.

## Independent Directors' Compensation

Compensation payable to independent directors is limited to a fixed annual amount determined and approved by the Board. The total does not exceed 1% of net profits for the year, calculated under the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

The Board reviews independent directors’ performance annually. Factors considered in determining compensation include global board compensation benchmarking, participation in Board and Committee meetings, committee membership or chairmanship, time spent on other duties, roles and functions under Schedule IV of the Indian Companies Act, 2013, Listing Regulations, and other factors determined by the Board. Independent directors are also reimbursed for expenses incurred in performing their official duties.
