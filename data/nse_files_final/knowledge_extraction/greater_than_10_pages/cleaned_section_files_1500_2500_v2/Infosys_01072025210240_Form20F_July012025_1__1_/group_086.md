## Notes on grants to KMP

## CEO & MD

### Under the 2015 Plan

The Board, on April 18, 2024, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved the following grants for fiscal 2025. The grants became effective May 2, 2024:

- 245,679 performance-based RSUs (annual performance equity grant) with a fair value of ₹34.75 crore (approximately $4.17 million). These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement upon achievement of specified performance targets.
- 14,140 performance-based RSUs (annual performance equity ESG grant) with a fair value of ₹2 crore (approximately $0.24 million). These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement upon achievement of environmental, social, and governance milestones determined by the Board.
- 35,349 performance-based RSUs (annual performance equity TSR grant) with a fair value of ₹5 crore (approximately $0.60 million). These RSUs will vest based on the Company's cumulative relative TSR performance over the applicable years, as determined by the Board.

Under the shareholder-approved employment agreement, the CEO is eligible to receive an annual RSU grant with a fair value of ₹3 crore (approximately $0.35 million). The grant vests over three equal annual installments upon completion of each year of service from the grant date. Accordingly, 16,204 annual time-based RSUs became effective February 1, 2025, for fiscal 2025.

The annual time-based grants and annual performance equity TSR grant for the remaining employment term ending March 31, 2027 had not been granted as of March 31, 2025. Because the service commencement date preceded the grant date, the Company recorded employment stock compensation expense in accordance with IFRS 2, *Share-based Payment*. For this purpose, the grant date under IFRS 2 was July 1, 2022.

### Under the 2019 Plan

On April 18, 2024, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of RSUs amounting to ₹10 crore (approximately $1.20 million) for fiscal 2025 under the 2019 Plan. The RSUs vest upon achievement of specified performance targets. Accordingly, 70,699 performance-based RSUs became effective May 2, 2024.

## Other KMP

### Under the 2015 Plan

During the year ended March 31, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved 69,470 time-based RSUs for other KMP under the 2015 Plan. The time-based RSUs vest over four years.

### Under the 2019 Plan

During the year ended March 31, 2025, based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board approved performance-based grants of 47,000 RSUs for other KMP under the 2019 Plan. These RSUs vest over three years upon achievement of specified performance targets.
