## A RESPECTING EACH OTHER

At Infosys, the work environment is intended to be free of discrimination and harassment. Infosys is an equal opportunity employer, and employment decisions are based on merit and business needs.

Infosys is committed to fair employment practices that provide equal opportunities to all employees. The company does not discriminate against or permit harassment based on race, color, religion, disability, gender, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, age, genetic information, military status, or any other legally protected status.

Infosys’s Human Rights Statement further describes its position on these matters. Employees in the U.S. may also refer to the company’s U.S. equal opportunity statement.

Infosys states that diversity provides a competitive advantage. Decisions regarding hiring, promotion, termination, transfer, leave of absence, and compensation should be based only on relevant business factors.

Employees must not verbally or physically mistreat others or engage in offensive behavior, and should not tolerate such conduct. The company’s policies on prevention and redressal of harassment, including location-specific procedures on the company intranet, provide further guidance. Employees may report concerns through the relevant company reporting channels.
