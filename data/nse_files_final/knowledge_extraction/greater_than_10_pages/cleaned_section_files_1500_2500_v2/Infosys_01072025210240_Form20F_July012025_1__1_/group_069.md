## Fair value hierarchy

Level 1 comprises quoted prices, unadjusted, in active markets for identical assets or liabilities.

Level 2 comprises inputs other than quoted prices included within Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly as prices or indirectly as derived from prices.

Level 3 comprises inputs for assets or liabilities that are not based on observable market data.

## Financial risk management

## Financial risk factors

The Group’s activities expose it to market risk, credit risk, and liquidity risk. The primary market risk is foreign exchange risk. The Group uses derivative financial instruments to mitigate foreign exchange-related exposures. Credit risk is influenced mainly by the individual characteristics of each customer and the concentration of risk among the largest customers.

## Market risk

The Group operates internationally, with a major portion of its business transacted in several currencies. It is exposed to foreign exchange risk through sales and services in the United States and elsewhere, purchases from overseas suppliers in various foreign currencies, and intercompany transactions in foreign currencies.

The Group uses foreign exchange forward and option contracts to mitigate the risk of changes in exchange rates on foreign currency exposures. Changes in the exchange rate between the Indian rupee and foreign currencies may affect the Group’s operating results.
