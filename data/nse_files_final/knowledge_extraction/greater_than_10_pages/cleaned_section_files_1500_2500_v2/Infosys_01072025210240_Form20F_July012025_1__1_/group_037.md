## Nomination and Remuneration Committee

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee comprised four independent directors as of March 31, 2025, each determined by the Board to be independent under applicable NYSE rules:

- D. Sundaram, Chairperson
- Michael Gibbs
- Govind Iyer
- Nitin Paranjpe

The Committee's responsibilities include:

1. Assisting the Board with remuneration matters relating to directors, Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), and senior management.
2. Evaluating and approving remuneration plans, policies, programs, and succession plans for directors, KMP, and senior management.
3. Identifying candidates for KMP and senior management positions and recommending their appointment or removal.
4. Formulating criteria for director qualifications, positive attributes, independence, and performance evaluation.
5. Administering equity-based plans and schemes approved by shareholders.
6. Overseeing the nomination process for KMP and senior management and identifying, screening, and reviewing qualified candidates.
7. Recommending the appointment and removal of directors for approval at the annual meeting of shareholders.
8. Evaluating Board performance and reviewing implementation and compliance.
9. Supporting leadership development.
10. Developing and maintaining corporate governance policies.
11. Devising a Board diversity policy.

The Committee oversees the recruitment of new Board members, the recruitment, motivation, and retention of senior management, and the Company's overall approach to human resources management.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2025.

- [Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/nomination-remunerationcommittee-charter.pdf)
- [Nomination and Remuneration Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/nomination-remunerationpolicy.pdf)

## Risk Management Committee

The Risk Management Committee comprised seven independent directors as of March 31, 2025, each determined by the Board to be independent under applicable NYSE rules:

- D. Sundaram, Chairperson
- Michael Gibbs
- Bobby Parikh
- Chitra Nayak
- Govind Iyer
- Helene Auriol Potier
- Nitin Paranjpe

The Committee's responsibilities include:

1. Assisting the Board in overseeing the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of strategic, operational, and external-environment risks.
2. Monitoring and approving the enterprise risk management framework and related practices.
3. Assessing risks to the execution of business strategy and reviewing relevant leading indicators.
4. Reviewing risk management processes and practices and the balance between risk and reward in ongoing and new business activities.
5. Evaluating significant risk exposures and management's mitigation actions.
6. Evaluating cybersecurity risks and ensuring that appropriate mitigation procedures are in place.
7. Coordinating with the Audit Committee where responsibilities overlap.
8. Periodically reviewing the adequacy of the Committee Charter and recommending changes to the Board.
9. Accessing internal information and obtaining advice from internal or external legal, accounting, or other advisors.
10. Appointing, removing, and approving the remuneration terms of the Chief Risk Officer.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2025.

[Risk Management Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/risk-management-committee-charter.pdf)

## Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee

As of March 31, 2025, the Cybersecurity Risk Sub-committee comprised four independent directors:

- Michael Gibbs, Chairperson and cybersecurity expert
- D. Sundaram
- Govind Iyer
- Chitra Nayak

The Sub-committee assesses cybersecurity-related risks and the Company's preparedness to mitigate and respond to those risks. It meets periodically and reports its findings, if any, to the Risk Management Committee.

The Sub-committee met four times during fiscal 2025.

## Stakeholders Relationship Committee

The Stakeholders Relationship Committee comprised four independent directors as of March 31, 2025:

- Michael Gibbs, Chairperson
- D. Sundaram
- Bobby Parikh
- Chitra Nayak

The Board appointed A.G.S. Manikantha, Company Secretary, as the Compliance Officer required under the Listing Regulations and as the Nodal Officer for compliance with the Investor Education Protection Fund (IEPF) Rules.

The Committee's responsibilities include:

1. Considering and resolving security-holder concerns and complaints.
2. Monitoring and reviewing investor service standards.
3. Understanding shareholder views through direct interaction, analyst briefings, or shareholder surveys.
4. Overseeing shareholder engagement and communication and ensuring that shareholder views and concerns are presented to the Board and addressed appropriately.

The Committee assists the Board and the Company in overseeing stakeholder interests, including those of shareholders, debenture holders, and other security holders.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2025.

[Stakeholders Relationship Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/stakeholders-relationship-committee.pdf)

## Corporate Social Responsibility Committee ('CSR Committee')

The CSR Committee comprised three independent directors as of March 31, 2025:

- Govind Iyer, Chairperson
- Chitra Nayak
- Michael Gibbs

The Committee identifies CSR activity areas, programs, and execution initiatives in accordance with defined guidelines. It oversees the activities and functioning of the Infosys Foundation, Infosys Foundation USA, and other Company initiatives, including initiatives in Australia and parts of Europe. The Foundation reports progress on deployed initiatives to the Committee and supports periodic disclosures.

The Committee met four times during fiscal 2025.

- [CSR Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/corporate-social-responsibilitycommittee-charter.pdf)
- [CSR Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/corporate-social-responsibilitypolicy.pdf)

## Environment, Social and Governance Committee ('ESG Committee')

The ESG Committee comprised three independent directors as of March 31, 2025:

- Chitra Nayak, Chairperson
- Govind Iyer
- Helene Auriol Potier

The Committee's responsibilities include:

1. Guiding the creation of the Company's ESG vision and ambitions and reviewing progress against ESG goals.
2. Reviewing the ESG Operations Council and its work, including forming and delegating authority to subcommittees when appropriate.
3. Ensuring that the Company takes appropriate measures to implement actions supporting its ESG vision and ambitions.
4. Reviewing statutory sustainability-reporting requirements, including Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting, and guiding leadership on global ESG assessments.
5. Obtaining advice from internal or external experts and advisors.
6. Periodically reviewing the adequacy of the ESG Committee Charter and recommending changes to the Board.

The Committee guides the Company's ESG activities, which began in 2011. It met four times during fiscal 2025.

[ESG Committee Charter](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/environment-social-governancecommittee-charter.pdf)

## RETURN TO OFFICE AND HYBRID MODEL OF WORK

Infosys uses a hybrid work model focused on flexibility, employee safety and wellbeing, and client commitments. The model includes office-based work and remote work, with offices designed to support collaboration, innovation, ideation, self-learning, and employee development.
