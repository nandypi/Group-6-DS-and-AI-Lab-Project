## 2.14.2 Superannuation

The Group contributed $61 million, $62 million, and $60 million to the superannuation plan during fiscal 2025, 2024, and 2023, respectively. These contributions were recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income under employee benefit expense.

Superannuation contributions were apportioned between cost of sales, selling and marketing expenses, and administrative expenses based on direct employee cost:

| Expense category | 2025 (Dollars in millions) | 2024 (Dollars in millions) | 2023 (Dollars in millions) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cost of sales | 55 | 56 | 55 |
| Selling and marketing expenses | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Administrative expenses | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Total | 61 | 62 | 60 |

## 2.14.3 Provident fund

Infosys has an obligation to fund any shortfall between the yield on the trust’s investments and the administered interest rates on an annual basis. These administered rates are determined annually, predominantly considering social and economic factors. An actuary valued provident fund liabilities based on guidance issued by the Actuarial Society of India.

The asset allocation for plan assets is determined based on investment criteria prescribed under the relevant regulations.

The actuarial valuation of provident fund liabilities exposes the Group to interest rate risk. The defined benefit obligation uses a discount rate based on government bonds; if bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation tends to increase.

As of March 31, 2025, the defined benefit obligation would be affected by approximately $15 million by a 0.25% increase or decrease in the expected rate of return on plan assets.

The Group contributed $156 million, $152 million, and $148 million to the provident fund during fiscal 2025, 2024, and 2023, respectively. These contributions were recognized in net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income under employee benefit expense.

Provident fund contributions were apportioned between cost of sales, selling and marketing expenses, and administrative expenses based on direct employee cost:

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cost of sales | 141 | 137 | 134 |
| Selling and marketing expenses | 10 | 10 | 9 |
| Administrative expenses | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| Total | 156 | 152 | 148 |

The provident plans apply only to employees drawing a salary in Indian rupees.
