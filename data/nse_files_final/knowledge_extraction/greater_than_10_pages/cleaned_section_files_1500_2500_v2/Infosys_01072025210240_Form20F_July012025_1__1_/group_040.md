## AWARDS:

- Infosys was recognized among LinkedIn's Top Companies 2025 in India, the United States and Canada.
- Infosys was recognized as one of the World's Most Ethical Companies in 2025 by Ethisphere for the fifth consecutive year.
- Infosys received Global Top Employer 2025 certification in 20 countries across Asia Pacific and Europe and was recognized as a top-five global employer across four regions.

  - **America:** United States, Canada and Mexico
  - **Asia Pacific:** India, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Japan and China
  - **Europe:** United Kingdom, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland, Sweden and Romania
  - **Middle East:** United Arab Emirates, Qatar and Saudi Arabia

- Infosys was recognized as the largest IT employer in Karnataka at the Invest Karnataka 2025 Summit.
- Brand Finance ranked Infosys among the top three IT services brands globally for the fourth consecutive year in its Global 500 2025 report. The report also recognized Infosys for the fastest five-year compound annual growth rate in brand value in the IT services industry.
- At The Asset ESG Corporate Awards 2024, Infosys received the Platinum Award for Excellence, Best Investor Relations Team, Best Initiative in Environmental Responsibility and Best Initiative in Diversity and Inclusion.
- Infosys was recognized among India's Best Workplaces for Women 2024 for the fourth consecutive year.
- Infosys was recognized among India's Best Workplaces in Diversity, Equity, Inclusion & Belonging 2024.
- Infosys was recognized as one of the Best Companies for Women in 2024 by Avtar and Seramount for the sixth consecutive time and was included in the Hall of Fame for appearing in the list for more than five years.
- Infosys was recognized as Champion of Inclusion in the Most Inclusive Companies Index 2024 by Avtar and Seramount for the fifth year.
- Infosys received the Sustainability Champion of the Year Award at the FICCI Young Leaders Awards 2024.
- Infosys was recognized as one of the Most Honored companies in the 2024 All-Asia Executive Team Rankings by Institutional Investor. The rankings were based on the opinions of 5,894 investment professionals.
- Infosys received the Diversity and Inclusion Employer of the Year award at the 2024 Digital Revolution Awards.
- Infosys' Investor Relations function was recognized as one of the top two IR functions among Indian companies in an annual FinanceAsia survey.
- Infosys was placed in the Leadership category in the Indian Corporate Governance Scorecard Assessment by Institutional Investor Advisory Services for the eighth consecutive year.
- Infosys received the following wellness and employee-development recognitions:

  - NHRD Welcon 2024 for Holistic Wellness
  - Winners of Winners of Welcon 2024 for Holistic Wellness at The People Matters Total Rewards & Wellbeing Conference 2024
  - Association for Talent Development Best of the Best 2025, received for the twelfth time
  - Gold Award for Best Advance in Learning Management Technology and Bronze Award for Best Corporate Learning University at the Brandon Hall Technology Excellence Awards 2024
  - Inclusion of Infosys and three managers in the 100 Great People Manager Studies 2024 by Great Manager Institute in association with Economic Times
  - Company with Great Managers Award 2024 by People Business for the fifth consecutive year
  - Great Manager Awards 2024 for three Infosys managers
