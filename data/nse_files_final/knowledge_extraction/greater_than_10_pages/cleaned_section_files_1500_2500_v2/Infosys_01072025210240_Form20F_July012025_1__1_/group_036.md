## Employment and Indemnification agreements

Under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, shareholders must approve the salary, bonus, benefits, and stock incentives of executive directors.

Infosys has entered into agreements with Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director. Details of his contracts are included in the section titled “Material Contracts” in Item 10 of the Annual Report on Form 20-F.

Infosys has also entered into agreements indemnifying directors and officers, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, for certain expenses, judgments, fines, and settlement amounts incurred in connection with actions or proceedings arising from their services as directors or officers. Covered expenses include public relations consultation, if required.

Other than the appointment and indemnification agreements, Infosys has not entered into agreements with non-executive directors.

## Board Leadership Structure

Nandan M. Nilekani has served as Non-Executive, Non-Independent Chairman of the Board since August 24, 2017. Salil Parekh has served as Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director since January 2, 2018. The Board appointed D. Sundaram as Lead Independent Director effective March 23, 2023. D. Sundaram also chairs the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and Risk Management Committee.

The roles of Chairman and CEO & MD are separated.

## Chairman

The Chairman leads the Board, presides over Board and shareholder meetings, and promotes Board integrity and effective communication among directors.

The Chairman oversees governance matters, including the organization, composition, and effectiveness of the Board and its committees, as well as individual director performance. In cooperation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Chairman participates in Board and committee composition, director induction and succession planning, Board evaluation, and feedback to individual directors.

## CEO & MD

The CEO & MD is responsible for executing corporate strategy in consultation with the Board, managing the Company, achieving annual and long-term business targets, and leading the Company’s overall direction.

The CEO & MD monitors the competitive landscape and industry developments and standards, identifies opportunities for expansion and acquisition, and develops relationships with customers and markets. The CEO & MD acts as a link between the Board and management and leads and evaluates other executive leaders.

## Lead Independent Director

The Lead Independent Director provides independent leadership to the Board and leads and liaises on behalf of the independent directors.

The Lead Independent Director supports Board effectiveness, high-quality governance, and the effective functioning of the Board. The general authority and responsibilities of the role are determined by the independent directors, with additional duties assigned by the Board.

## Board's Role in Risk Oversight

The Board has overall responsibility for risk management oversight. The Risk Management Committee, comprising independent directors, assists the Board with the identification, evaluation, and mitigation of strategic, operational, and external-environment risks.

The Risk Management Committee monitors and approves the enterprise risk management framework and related practices and reviews and approves risk disclosure statements in public documents and disclosures.

The Board receives periodic presentations on cybersecurity and other information security matters. The Audit Committee and Risk Management Committee receive regular management updates on cybersecurity. The Company’s cybersecurity policy and risk management framework are presented annually to both committees. Management meets periodically to discuss cybersecurity and information security matters and oversee compliance with the information security program.

## Board Member Evaluation

The Board monitors and reviews the Board evaluation framework. In consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, it establishes evaluation criteria for the Chairman, the Board, Board committees, and executive, non-executive, and independent directors through peer evaluation, excluding the director being evaluated.

Independent directors are evaluated on governance, control, and guidance, including:

- Contribution to and monitoring of corporate governance practices.
- Contribution of international best practices to address business challenges and risks.
- Participation in long-term strategic planning.
- Commitment to director obligations and fiduciary responsibilities, including participation in Board and committee meetings.

A formal annual review is conducted to improve the effectiveness of the Board, its committees, and individual directors.

For fiscal 2025, the Board engaged Egon Zehnder, a leadership advisory firm specializing in board matters, to conduct the Board evaluation. The process considered Board dynamics, softer aspects, committee effectiveness, information flow to the Board and its committees, succession planning, and committee composition. It used questionnaires and one-on-one discussions. Recommendations and progress on the prior year’s recommendations were discussed with the Board, and individual feedback was provided. The evaluation was completed during fiscal 2025.

The evaluation process was based on affirmations from independent directors that they met the independence criteria under the Companies Act, 2013, the Listing Regulations, and the NYSE listing manual.

## Succession Planning

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee works with the Board on leadership succession planning for Board appointments and senior management positions.

Infosys seeks to maintain an appropriate balance of skills and experience while introducing new perspectives and preserving experience and continuity. The Company also promotes senior management internally to support future leadership development.

## Board Committees

As of March 31, 2025, the Board had six committees:

- Audit Committee
- Nomination and Remuneration Committee
- Stakeholders Relationship Committee
- Risk Management Committee
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee
- Environment, Social and Governance Committee

All committees comprised only independent directors, with one director serving as chairperson. The Board also created a Cybersecurity Risk Sub-Committee of the Risk Management Committee.

During the year, all committee recommendations were approved by the Board.

The Board, in consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, assigns committee members and determines their terms of service. The Chairman, in consultation with the Company Secretary and relevant committee chairperson, determines meeting frequency. Committees generally meet four times a year.

The quorum for committee meetings is the higher of two members or one-third of the committee’s total membership.

## Audit Committee

As of March 31, 2025, the Audit Committee comprised three independent directors. The Board determined that each satisfied applicable NYSE independence requirements and Rule 10A-3 under the Exchange Act:

- Bobby Parikh, Chairperson and Financial Expert
- D. Sundaram, Financial Expert
- Michael Gibbs

Nitin Paranjpe was appointed as an Audit Committee member effective April 16, 2025. The Company Secretary serves as secretary to the Audit Committee.

The Audit Committee assists the Board in overseeing:

1. The accuracy, integrity, and transparency of the Company’s financial statements and timely disclosures.
2. Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
3. The professional qualifications and independence of the independent auditor.
4. The performance of the independent and internal auditors.
5. Acquisitions and investments made by the Company.

The Audit Committee met seven times during fiscal 2025.

The Audit Committee approved, and the Board adopted, the Related Party Transaction Policy, Code on Fair Disclosure and Investor Relations, and Insider Trading Policy.

Infosys is listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited in India and on the NYSE in the United States. Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations in India and the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission’s Blue-Ribbon Committee requirements mandate an appropriate Audit Committee charter for listed companies.

See Item 18 for the Audit Committee report.
