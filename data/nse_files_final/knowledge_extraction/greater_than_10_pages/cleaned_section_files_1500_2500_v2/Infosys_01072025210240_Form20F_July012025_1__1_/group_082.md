The weighted-average assumptions used to determine net periodic benefit cost for fiscal 2025, 2024, and 2023 are set out below.

| Assumption | Gratuity 2025 | Gratuity 2024 | Gratuity 2023 | Pension 2025 | Pension 2024 | Pension 2023 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Discount rate | 7.0% | 7.1% | 6.5% | 1.5%-3.4% | 1.8%-3.8% | 0.4%-1.7% |
| Weighted average rate of increase in compensation levels | 6.0% | 6.0% | 6.0% | 1.0%-3.0% | 1.0%-3.0% | 1.0%-3.0% |

For domestic defined benefit plans in India, the market for high-quality corporate bonds is not developed, so the yield on government bonds is used as the discount rate. For most overseas defined benefit plans, the government bond rate adjusted for corporate spreads is used.

The average rate of increase in compensation levels is determined using the Company’s past compensation revision trends, inflation in the respective markets, and management’s estimate of future salary increases. The attrition rate is based on management’s estimate of the Company’s long-term employee turnover trend. Employee tenure reflects the long-term trend in employees’ average remaining service life.

For domestic defined benefit plans in India, assumptions regarding future mortality experience are based on statistics published by the Life Insurance Corporation of India. For overseas defined benefit plans, mortality assumptions consider the latest life-expectancy statistics, plan experience, and other relevant data.

The Group assesses these assumptions against its projected long-term growth plans and prevalent industry standards.

The Company contributes all ascertained gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to the Infosys BPM Employees’ Gratuity Fund Trust and EdgeVerve Systems Limited Employees Gratuity Fund Trust, respectively. Trustees administered the trust contributions as of March 31, 2025, and March 31, 2024. Gratuity contributions are invested in a scheme with the Life Insurance Corporation of India as permitted by Indian law.

Plan assets for overseas defined benefit plans are primarily invested in insurer-managed funds. Asset allocation is determined based on investment criteria prescribed under the applicable pension-fund regulations and by insurer managers. The insurers’ investments are diversified and provide guaranteed-interest-rate arrangements.

These defined benefit plans expose the Group to the following actuarial risks:

| Risk | Description |
|---|---|
| Interest rate risk | The present value of the defined benefit plan liability is generally calculated using a discount rate based on government bond yields. In certain overseas jurisdictions, the rate is based on government bond yields adjusted for a corporate spread. If bond yields fall, the defined benefit obligation generally increases. |
| Life expectancy and investment risk | The pension fund offers a choice between a lifelong pension and a cash lump sum at retirement. Defined conversion rates are used to convert the lump sum to a pension, creating a risk that members live longer than implied by those rates or that pension assets do not achieve the investment return implied by the rates. |
| Asset volatility | A proportion of the pension fund is invested in equities. Equity investments are expected to outperform corporate bonds over the long term but create short-term volatility and risk. The pension fund board of the insurer is responsible for the investment strategy and equity allocation. |

### Sensitivity of significant assumptions used for valuation of defined benefit obligation

| Assumption | Gratuity: 1% point increase/decrease | Pension: 0.5% point increase/decrease |
|---|---:|---:|
| Discount rate | 16 | 6 |
| Weighted average rate of increase in compensation levels | 16 | 1 |

Sensitivity to significant actuarial assumptions is computed by varying one actuarial assumption used to value the defined benefit obligation while keeping all other actuarial assumptions constant. In practice, this is not probable, and changes in some assumptions may be correlated.

The Group expects to contribute $43 million to gratuity and $5 million to pension during fiscal 2026.
