## Code of Conduct for Prohibition of Insider Trading

## I. BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVE

Infosys Limited (the “Company”) is a public company whose equity shares are listed on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and BSE Limited (BSE), and is subject to rules and regulations issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

The Company’s American Depositary Shares (ADSs) are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE). The Company is a reporting company subject to the Securities Act of 1933, the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and applicable regulations of the United States Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). ADSs representing the Company’s equity shares are also considered securities or shares of the Company.

The Board adopted this code of conduct to regulate, monitor, and report trading by Designated Persons and their Immediate Relatives, as defined in the code, in compliance with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, as amended, and applicable US securities laws.

The code applies to Designated Persons and the Immediate Relatives of Designated Persons.

The SEBI Regulations prohibit an Insider from trading in the securities of a company listed on a stock exchange while in possession of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI).

## II. CODE OF CONDUCT

## 1. Definitions

1. **Act** means the SEBI Act, 1992 (15 of 1992).

2. **Audit Committee** means the committee of the Board constituted under Section 177 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Regulation 18 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (SEBI LODR).

3. **Board** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

4. **Compliance Officer** means the Company Secretary of the Company or another senior officer designated by and reporting to the Board. The Compliance Officer must be financially literate and capable of understanding legal and regulatory compliance requirements under the SEBI Regulations. The Compliance Officer is responsible for compliance with policies and procedures, maintaining records, monitoring adherence to rules for preserving UPSI, monitoring trades, and implementing the codes specified under the SEBI Regulations under the overall supervision of the Board.

5. **Connected Person** means:

   - A Director of the Company.
   - A Key Managerial Personnel of the Company.
   - An Officer of the Company.
   - Any person who is or has been in a contractual, fiduciary, or employment relationship at any time during the six-month period before determining whether that person was, directly or indirectly, allowed access to UPSI or was reasonably expected to be allowed access to UPSI as a result of that relationship.
   - Any person who is or has been in frequent communication with an Officer of the Company at any time during the six-month period before determining whether that person was, directly or indirectly, allowed access to UPSI or was reasonably expected to be allowed access to UPSI as a result of that communication.
   - An employee of the Company who has access to UPSI or is reasonably expected to have access to UPSI.
   - Any person who has a professional or business relationship with the Company, whether temporary or permanent, where that relationship directly or indirectly allows access to UPSI or is reasonably expected to allow access to UPSI.

The following persons are deemed to be Connected Persons unless the contrary is established:

1. A relative of Connected Persons specified above.
2. A holding company, associate company, or subsidiary company.
3. An intermediary specified in Section 12 of the Act, or an employee or director of such intermediary.
4. An investment company, trustee company, or asset management company, or an employee or director of such entity.
5. An official of a stock exchange, clearing house, or clearing corporation.
6. A member of the board of trustees of a mutual fund, a member of the board of directors of the asset management company of a mutual fund, or an employee of either.
7. A member of the board of directors or an employee of a public financial institution as defined in Section 2(72) of the Companies Act, 2013.
8. An official or employee of a self-regulatory organization recognized or authorized by SEBI.
9. A banker of the Company.
10. A concern, firm, trust, Hindu undivided family, company, or association of persons in which a Director of the Company, the Director’s Relative, or the Company’s banker has more than 10% holding or interest.
11. A firm, its partner, or its employee in which a Connected Person specified above is also a partner.
12. A person sharing a household or residence with a Connected Person specified above.

6. **Contra trade** means a trade or transaction involving buying or selling the Company’s securities followed within six months by an opposite transaction involving a sale or purchase.

7. **Designated Person** includes:

   - Promoters of the Company.
   - Directors and Key Managerial Personnel of the Company and its material subsidiaries.
   - Employees in Finance and Accounts, Corporate Planning, Legal, Enterprise Risk Management, Corporate Strategy, Investor Relations, Information Security, Data Privacy, Mergers & Acquisitions, Corporate Secretarial, Marketing, Compensation and Benefits, and any other department of the Company and its material subsidiaries, based on their functional role or access to UPSI, as designated from time to time.
   - The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and employees up to two levels below the CEO of the Company and its material subsidiaries.
   - Executive Secretaries of Directors, Key Managerial Personnel, and Executive Officers of the Company, and other support staff, including IT or secretarial staff, who are likely to have access to UPSI.
   - Any other person designated based on a functional role that provides access to UPSI.

8. **Director** has the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013.

9. **Generally available information** means information accessible to the public on a nondiscriminatory basis. It does not include unverified events or information reported in print or electronic media. Information published on the website of a stock exchange where the Company’s securities are listed or issued through a Company press release would ordinarily be considered generally available.

10. **Immediate Relative** means a person’s spouse and includes the person’s parent, sibling, or child, or the spouse’s parent, sibling, or child, if financially dependent on the person or if they consult the person when making decisions relating to trading in securities.

11. **Insider** means any person who is:

   - A Connected Person; or
   - In possession of or having access to UPSI.

12. **Key Managerial Personnel** has the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013.

13. **Leak of UPSI** means any act or circumstance through which UPSI is made available or becomes available, by any means or mode, to any person, association, body, firm, agency, society, entity, or group thereof, whether registered or otherwise, before the information becomes generally available. It includes any purported attempt to make such information available.

This includes instances where UPSI has been shared with any person, association, body, firm, agency, society, entity, or group thereof except in compliance with applicable law.

14. **Legitimate purpose** includes sharing UPSI in the ordinary course of business by an Insider with partners, collaborators, lenders, customers, suppliers, merchant bankers, legal advisors, auditors, insolvency professionals, or other advisors or consultants, provided that the sharing is not carried out to evade or circumvent the prohibitions of the applicable regulations. Annexure 8 provides an illustrative list of legitimate purposes.

15. **Material financial relationship** means a relationship in which one person receives any kind of payment, such as a loan or gift, from a Designated Person during the immediately preceding 12 months equivalent to at least 25% of the Designated Person’s annual income. Relationships involving payments based on arm’s-length transactions are excluded.

16. **Officer** has the meaning assigned under the Companies Act, 2013.

17. **Promoter and Promoter Group** have the meanings assigned under the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, or any modification thereof.

18. **Relatives** means:

   - The spouse of the person.
   - The parent of the person or the person’s spouse.
   - The sibling of the person or the person’s spouse.
   - The child of the person or the person’s spouse.
   - The spouse of a person referred to in the preceding two items.

19. **Securities** have the meaning assigned under the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, or any modification thereof, and include ADSs.

20. **Trading** or **Trade** includes subscribing, redeeming, switching, buying, selling, dealing, agreeing to subscribe, redeem, switch, buy, sell, pledge, revoke or invoke a pledge, or deal in the Company’s securities, either directly or through portfolio management services. “Trade” is construed accordingly.

21. **Trading day** means a day on which recognized stock exchanges are open for trading.

22. **Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI)** means any information relating directly or indirectly to a company or its securities that is not generally available information and that, upon becoming generally available, is likely to materially affect the price of the securities.
