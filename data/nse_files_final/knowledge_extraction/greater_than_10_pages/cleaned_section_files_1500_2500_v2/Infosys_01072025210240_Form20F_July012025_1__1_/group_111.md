## Compliance with Regulations

## Information Requests

Each ADS holder and beneficial owner shall provide information requested by Infosys or the depositary pursuant to applicable law, including India and United States law, the memorandum and articles of association, Board resolutions, applicable market or exchange requirements, and electronic book-entry system requirements.

Requested information may include:

- The capacity in which the person owns or owned ADRs.
- The identity of any other persons who are or were interested in the ADRs.
- The nature of those interests.
- Any other applicable matters.

## Disclosure of Interests

Each ADS holder shall comply with the memorandum and articles of association and India law regarding notification to Infosys of interests in the equity shares underlying the holder’s ADSs. This includes disclosure of certain acquisitions and dispositions of ADSs or underlying equity shares within the prescribed period.

ADS holders must also comply with requests made under India law, the rules and requirements of the New York Stock Exchange and other applicable stock exchanges, and the memorandum and articles of association. Such requests may seek information about:

- The capacity in which an ADS holder owns ADSs.
- The identity of any other person interested in the ADSs.
- The nature of that interest.
- Other relevant matters, whether or not the person remains an ADS holder when the request is made.

## Fees and Expenses

ADS holders must pay service fees to the depositary bank and applicable taxes and governmental charges, in addition to fees, expenses, taxes, and governmental charges payable on the deposited securities represented by their ADSs.

The applicable service fees, taxes, and governmental charges are described under “Item 12. Description of Securities Other Than Equity Securities — Fees and charges payable by holders of our ADSs” in Infosys’ Annual Report on Form 20-F filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

## Payment of Taxes

ADS holders are responsible for taxes and other governmental charges payable on their ADSs or the deposited securities represented by their ADSs.

The depositary may refuse to register or transfer ADSs, or permit withdrawal of deposited securities, until applicable taxes or charges are paid. It may apply payments owed to the holder or sell deposited securities to pay taxes. If the sale proceeds are insufficient, the holder remains liable for the deficiency.

If deposited securities are sold, the depositary may reduce the number of ADSs to reflect the sale and pay the holder any net proceeds or deliver any remaining property after paying the taxes.

ADS holders agree to indemnify and hold harmless Infosys, the depositary, the custodian, and their respective agents, directors, employees, and affiliates against claims relating to taxes, including applicable interest and penalties, arising from any tax refund, reduced withholding rate, or other tax benefit obtained for the holder. These obligations survive the transfer or surrender of ADRs, withdrawal of deposited securities, and termination of the deposit agreement.

## Reclassifications, Recapitalizations and Mergers

If Infosys:

- Reclassifies, splits, or consolidates any deposited securities;
- Distributes securities on the equity shares that are not distributed to ADS holders;
- Recapitalizes, reorganizes, merges, liquidates, sells all or substantially all of its assets; or
- Takes any similar action,

each ADS will automatically represent its proportionate share of the new deposited securities.

The depositary may distribute some or all of the cash, shares, or other securities it receives. It may also deliver new ADSs or require holders to surrender outstanding ADRs in exchange for new ADRs identifying the new deposited securities.

If the securities received cannot lawfully be distributed, the depositary may sell them in a public or private sale and distribute the net proceeds.

## Amendment and Termination

### How may the deposit agreement be amended?

Infosys and the depositary may agree to amend the deposit agreement and the form of ADR without ADS holder consent.

An amendment that adds or increases fees or charges, other than specified taxes, governmental charges, and certain depositary expenses, or that prejudices a substantial existing right of ADS holders, will not become effective for outstanding ADSs until 30 days after the depositary notifies ADS holders.

By continuing to hold ADSs after an amendment becomes effective, holders are considered to agree to the amendment and to be bound by the amended ADRs and deposit agreement.

If new laws require amendment of the deposit agreement for compliance, Infosys and the depositary may amend it in accordance with those laws. Such an amendment may become effective before notice is given to ADS holders.

### How may the deposit agreement be terminated?

The depositary will terminate the deposit agreement if Infosys requests termination and will provide at least 90 days’ prior notice to ADS holders.

The depositary may also terminate the agreement if it has notified Infosys of its intention to resign, or if Infosys has removed the depositary, and a new depositary is not appointed within 60 days. In either case, the depositary must provide at least 30 days’ notice before termination.

After termination, the depositary and its agents will:

- Collect distributions on deposited securities.
- Sell rights and other property.
- Deliver equity shares and other deposited securities upon cancellation of ADSs after payment of applicable fees, charges, taxes, and governmental charges.

Six months or more after termination, the depositary may sell remaining deposited securities by public or private sale. It will hold the sale proceeds and other cash under the deposit agreement for the pro rata benefit of ADS holders who have not surrendered their ADSs. The depositary will not invest the money and has no liability for interest.

After the sale, the depositary’s obligations are limited to accounting for the money and other cash. Infosys will be discharged from its obligations under the deposit agreement, except for its obligations to the depositary under that agreement.

## Books of Depositary

The depositary will maintain ADS holder records at its depositary office. ADS holders may inspect those records during regular business hours solely to communicate with other holders about business matters relating to Infosys, the ADRs, and the deposit agreement.

The depositary will maintain facilities in the Borough of Manhattan, The City of New York, to record and process the issuance, cancellation, combination, split-up, and transfer of ADRs.

The facilities may be closed when the depositary considers closure necessary or advisable in performing its duties under the deposit agreement, or at Infosys’ reasonable written request.

## Limitations on Obligations and Liability

The deposit agreement limits the obligations and liabilities of Infosys, the depositary, and the custodian. They:

- Are obligated only to take actions specifically set forth in the deposit agreement, absent gross negligence or willful misconduct.
- Are not liable when prevented, forbidden, delayed, or subjected to civil or criminal penalties or restraints while performing obligations because of applicable laws, regulations, governmental or regulatory authority, stock exchange requirements, the memorandum and articles of association, provisions governing deposited securities, acts of God, war, terrorism, nationalization, expropriation, currency restrictions, work stoppages, strikes, civil unrest, revolutions, rebellions, explosions, computer failure, or other circumstances beyond their control.
- Are not liable for exercising or failing to exercise discretion provided under the deposit agreement, the memorandum and articles of association, or provisions governing deposited securities.
- Are not liable for actions or inactions taken in reliance, in good faith, on advice or information from legal counsel, accountants, persons presenting equity shares for deposit, ADS holders, beneficial owners, authorized representatives, or other persons believed competent to provide such advice or information.
- Are not liable for an ADS holder’s or beneficial owner’s inability to benefit from a distribution, offering, right, or other benefit made available to holders of deposited securities but not made available to ADS holders under the deposit agreement.
- Are not liable for special, consequential, indirect, or punitive damages arising from a breach of the deposit agreement or otherwise.
- May rely on documents believed in good faith to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper party.

The depositary and its agents also disclaim liability for:

- Failure to carry out voting instructions, the manner in which a vote is cast, the effect of a vote, failure to determine whether a distribution or action is lawful or reasonably practicable, or failure to exercise voting rights.
- Failure or delay in providing notice from Infosys, the content of information submitted by Infosys for distribution, or inaccuracies in translations.
- Investment risks associated with acquiring an interest in deposited securities or the creditworthiness of a third party.
- Tax consequences arising from ownership of ADSs, equity shares, or deposited securities.
- Acts or omissions of a successor depositary.

Each party to the deposit agreement, including ADS holders, beneficial owners, and holders of interests in ADRs, irrevocably waives, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, any right to a jury trial in lawsuits or proceedings against Infosys or the depositary relating to Infosys’ shares, the ADSs, or the deposit agreement.

Infosys and the depositary agree to indemnify each other under certain circumstances.
