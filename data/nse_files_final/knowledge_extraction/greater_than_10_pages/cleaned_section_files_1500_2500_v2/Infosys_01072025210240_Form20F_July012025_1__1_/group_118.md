## 8. Penalty for Insider Trading

1. Designated persons, officers, and employees who violate the Code may be subject to disciplinary action, including wage freeze, suspension, recovery, ineligibility for future participation in the Company's stock option plans, or termination.

2. The stock exchanges or other appropriate regulatory authorities shall be informed of violations in the form and manner specified by SEBI.

3. Amounts collected as penalties under the Code shall be credited to the Investor Protection and Education Fund administered by SEBI.

## 9. Disclosure requirements

### i. Initial Disclosure

- Every person appointed as a Key Managerial Personnel or Director, or who becomes a Promoter or member of the promoter group, must disclose their own and their Immediate Relatives' holdings of Company securities as of the appointment or promotion date within seven days, using Form A in Annexure 4.
- Every Designated Person must disclose the following information for:
  - Immediate Relatives;
  - Persons with whom the Designated Person shares a material financial relationship; and
  - Phone and mobile numbers used by the Designated Person:
    - Permanent Account Number or another legally authorized identifier;
    - Names of educational institutions from which they graduated; and
    - Names of past employers.

### ii. Continual Disclosure

- Every Designated Person and member of the promoter group, including their Immediate Relatives, must disclose securities acquired or disposed of within two trading days if the traded value, in one transaction or a series of transactions during a calendar quarter, exceeds INR 10,00,000 (ten lakh rupees) or another threshold prescribed under SEBI Regulations or applicable law. Disclosures must use Form B in Annexure 5.
- Every Designated Person must disclose, annually and whenever the information changes, the names and Permanent Account Numbers or other legally authorized identifiers of:
  - Immediate Relatives;
  - Persons with whom the Designated Person shares a material financial relationship; and
  - Phone and mobile numbers used by the Designated Person.
- Off-market trades conducted under paragraph 4(6)(i) of the Code must be reported by Insiders to the Company within two working days.

### iii. Disclosures by other Connected Persons

The Compliance Officer may require any other Connected Person to disclose holdings and trading in Company securities using Form C in Annexure 6, at a frequency determined by the Compliance Officer.

## 10. Mechanism for prevention of Insider Trading

1. The CEO, Managing Director, or analogous officer, in consultation with the Compliance Officer, Chief Legal Officer, Chief Compliance Officer, and other relevant senior-management members, shall establish and maintain adequate and effective internal controls for compliance with SEBI Regulations. The controls shall be periodically reviewed for effectiveness.

2. The Board shall ensure that the CEO, Managing Director, and other relevant senior-management members have taken the required steps.

3. The Audit Committee shall review compliance with SEBI Regulations at least once in each financial year and verify that the internal-control systems are adequate and operating effectively.

## 11. Miscellaneous

- The Board may amend, modify, and interpret the Code and determine its effective date.
- The Compliance Officer shall provide the Audit Committee with quarterly updates on compliance, violations, and other matters directed by the Audit Committee.
- The Compliance Officer shall maintain:
  - An updated list of Designated Persons;
  - Records of disclosures, pre-clearance applications, and undertakings for eight years; and
  - Other information required under SEBI Regulations.
- The Board shall ensure that a structured digital database is maintained for every person possessing UPSI. The database must include:
  - The nature of the UPSI;
  - The names of persons who shared the information; and
  - The names and Permanent Account Numbers or other legally authorized identifiers of persons with whom the information was shared under SEBI Regulations.
  
  The database must be maintained internally with controls and checks, including time stamping and audit trails, to prevent tampering.
- The Company may engage market intermediaries or other persons required to handle UPSI who have formulated a code of conduct consistent with the PIT Regulations. Such persons must promptly inform the Board of any observed violation of SEBI Regulations.
- The Company has adopted the amended Infosys Code on Fair Disclosures and Investor Relations to regulate fair disclosure of UPSI and comply with SEC Regulations.
- Suspected leaks of UPSI or violations of the Code may be reported under the whistleblower policy.

## How to Report:

Concerns may be reported to a manager, Human Resources manager, the helpline, the Chief Legal Officer, or the Audit Committee of the Company's Board through the reporting channels specified in the Code.

Retaliation against an employee who reports suspected violations in accordance with the informant mechanism under the Regulations is prohibited. Protection applies against discharge, termination, demotion, suspension, threats, harassment, or discrimination, whether direct or indirect.

Intermediaries or fiduciaries engaged by the Company shall maintain adequate and effective internal controls to comply with SEBI Regulations and prevent insider trading.

Connected Persons are responsible for complying with the Code.

The policy and procedure for inquiries into suspected leaks of UPSI or actual leaks of UPSI is set out in Annexure 7 and forms an integral part of the Code.

The Company shall maintain a process for determining how and when persons are brought “inside” on sensitive transactions. Individuals receiving inside information must be made aware of their related duties, responsibilities, and liability for misuse or unwarranted use of that information.

## 12. Limitation, Review and Amendment

If the Code conflicts with SEBI Regulations or any other legal requirement (“Applicable Law”), the Applicable Law shall prevail. Subsequent amendments or modifications to Applicable Law shall automatically apply to the Code.

The Board may review and amend the Code from time to time.
