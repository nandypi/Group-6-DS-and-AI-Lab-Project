## UNDERTAKING TO BE ACCOMPANIED WITH THE APPLICATION FOR PRECLEARANCE

A designated person of Infosys Limited applying for pre-clearance to trade in the company’s securities must declare that they are not in possession of unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) when signing the undertaking.

If the person receives or gains access to UPSI after signing but before executing the approved transaction, they must inform the Compliance Officer and refrain from trading until the information becomes public.

The applicant must also declare that they have not contravened the company’s insider-trading code. If the transaction violates the code or applicable laws, the applicant agrees to:

- Release, hold harmless, and indemnify Infosys Limited, its directors, and officers for related losses, damages, fines, and expenses.
- Compensate those indemnified persons for investigation, defense, crisis-management, and public-relations expenses related to the transaction.
- Authorize Infosys to recover profits arising from the transaction and remit them to SEBI for credit to the Investor Protection and Education Fund.

The applicant must submit the required report within two days of executing the transaction, or submit a “Nil” report if the transaction is not undertaken.

An approved trade must be executed within seven days of receiving approval. Otherwise, the applicant must obtain fresh pre-clearance.

## DISCLOSURE OF TRANSACTIONS

Disclosure must be submitted within two trading days of trading in Infosys securities.

The person must indicate either that no securities were bought, sold, or subscribed, or provide details of the transaction:

| Name of holder | No. of securities traded | Bought / sold / subscribed | DP ID / Client ID / Folio No. | Price (Rs.) |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|

The person must declare that the information is correct and that the transaction did not contravene the company’s code or applicable laws and regulations.

## FORM A

**SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 — Regulation 7(1)(b) read with Regulation 6(2)**

**Disclosure on becoming a director, KMP, promoter, or member of the promoter group**

### Securities Held on Appointment or Becoming a Promoter

The disclosure covers securities held by a key managerial personnel (KMP), director, promoter, member of the promoter group, immediate relative of such persons, and other persons specified in Regulation 6(2).

| Name, PAN, CIN/DIN, address, contact | Category of person | Date of appointment as director/KMP or date of becoming promoter/member of promoter group | Securities held at appointment or on becoming promoter/member of promoter group | % of shareholding |
|---|---|---|---|---:|

**Note:** “Securities” has the meaning assigned under Regulation 2(1)(i) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

### Derivative Open Interest

The disclosure must include derivative open interest held on appointment as KMP or director, or on becoming a promoter or member of the promoter group, by the persons covered under Regulation 6(2).

| Future contract specifications | Number of units (contracts × lot size) | Notional value in rupee terms | Option contract specifications | Number of units (contracts × lot size) | Notional value in rupee terms |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| Contract specifications | Number of units (contracts × lot size) | Notional value in rupee terms | Contract specifications | Number of units (contracts × lot size) | Notional value in rupee terms |

**Note:** For options, notional value is calculated based on the premium plus the strike price of the options.
