During fiscal 2025, 2024, and 2023, the Group designated certain foreign exchange forward and option contracts as cash flow hedges to mitigate foreign exchange exposure on highly probable forecast cash transactions. The related hedge transactions for the cash flow hedging reserve balance as of March 31, 2025, are expected to occur and be reclassified to the statement of comprehensive income within three months.

The Group determines whether an economic relationship exists between the hedging instrument and hedged item based on the currency, amount, and timing of forecast cash flows. Hedge effectiveness is determined at hedge inception and through periodic prospective assessments, including whether the hedging instrument is expected to offset changes in the cash flows of hedged items.

If the hedge ratio is no longer optimal but the risk-management objective remains unchanged and the hedge continues to qualify for hedge accounting, the hedge relationship is rebalanced by adjusting the volume of the hedging instrument or hedged item. Any hedge ineffectiveness is calculated and recognized in profit or loss when the hedge relationship is rebalanced.

## Credit risk

Credit risk is the risk of counterparty default resulting in a financial loss. The Group’s maximum exposure to credit risk at the reporting date primarily comprised:

| Exposure | March 31, 2025 (Dollars in millions) | March 31, 2024 (Dollars in millions) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Trade receivables | 3,645 | 3,620 |
| Unbilled revenue | 1,764 | 1,744 |

Trade receivables and unbilled revenue are typically unsecured and arise primarily from customers located in the United States of America and Europe. The Group manages credit risk through credit approvals, credit limits, and continuous monitoring of customer creditworthiness. It uses the expected credit loss model and a provision matrix for trade receivables and unbilled revenues, incorporating available credit reports and other relevant credit information.

Credit-risk exposure is influenced mainly by individual customer characteristics and concentration among the largest customers. Customer exposure is diversified, and no single customer contributed more than 10% of outstanding trade receivables and unbilled revenues.
