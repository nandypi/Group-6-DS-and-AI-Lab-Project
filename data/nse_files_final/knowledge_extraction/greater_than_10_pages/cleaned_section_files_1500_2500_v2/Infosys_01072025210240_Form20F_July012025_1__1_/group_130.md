## POLICY FOR DIVIDEND DISTRIBUTION OF INFOSYS LIMITED

This Dividend Distribution Policy applies to dividends distributed by Infosys Limited and was formulated under the Companies Act, 2013, and the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended.

## Definitions

Terms used in the Policy have the meanings assigned under the Companies Act, related rules, and the Listing Regulations.

## Scope

The Policy sets out the circumstances, parameters, and factors considered by the Board of Directors when determining dividend distributions to shareholders.

### a. The circumstances under which the shareholders may or may not expect dividend

The Board determines dividends for a particular period after considering the company’s financial performance, advice from executive management, and the other parameters described in the Policy, subject to applicable statutory requirements concerning dividends and retained earnings.

### b. The financial parameters / internal factors that shall be considered while declaring dividend

The Board may consider the following financial parameters and internal factors:

- Capital allocation plans, including:
  - Expected cash requirements for working capital and capital expenditure on technology and infrastructure.
  - Investments required to execute the company’s strategy.
  - Funds required for acquisitions approved by the Board.
  - Share buyback plans.
- Minimum cash required for contingencies or unforeseen events.
- Funds required to service outstanding loans.
- Liquidity and return ratios.
- Other significant developments requiring cash investments.

### c. External factors that shall be considered for declaration of dividend

The Board may consider the following external factors:

- Significant changes in the macroeconomic environment affecting India, the geographies in which the company operates, the company’s business, or its clients.
- Political, tax, and regulatory changes in the geographies in which the company operates.
- Significant changes in the business or technological environment requiring material investment to change the company’s business model.
- Changes in the competitive environment requiring significant investment.

## d. Policy as to how the retained earnings shall be utilized.

Consolidated profits may be retained in the business and used for purposes determined by the Board, including the purposes described under clause (b), or distributed to shareholders.

## e. Provisions in regard to various classes of shares.

The Policy applies to all classes of shares. Infosys Limited currently has one class of shares: equity shares.

If the company declares dividends using parameters in addition to those in clauses (a) to (e), or changes any additional parameters or the Policy, it must disclose the changes and the rationale in its annual report and on its website.

## Disclosure of the policy

The Policy will be uploaded on the company’s website. The company also announces its capital allocation policy from time to time.

## Limitation and Amendment

If the Policy conflicts with the Companies Act, the Listing Regulations, or other applicable statutory enactments or rules, those laws and regulations prevail.

Subsequent amendments or modifications to the applicable laws and regulations automatically apply to the Policy. The Board may review and amend the Policy from time to time.

## COMPENSATION RECOVERY POLICY

Infosys Limited’s Board of Directors has adopted the Compensation Recovery Policy, also referred to as the clawback policy. The Policy supports the company’s pay-for-performance approach and provides for the reasonably prompt recovery of certain compensation received by Covered Executives following an Accounting Restatement.

Application of the Policy to Covered Executives is generally non-discretionary, except to the limited extent specified in the Policy, and applies regardless of whether a Covered Executive was at fault.

The Policy is intended to comply with Section 10D of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, Exchange Act Rule 10D-1, and the listing standards of the New York Stock Exchange.
