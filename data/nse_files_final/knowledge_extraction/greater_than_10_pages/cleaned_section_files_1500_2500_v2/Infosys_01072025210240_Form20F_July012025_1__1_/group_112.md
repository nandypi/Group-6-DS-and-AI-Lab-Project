## Requirements for Depositary Actions

Before the depositary will issue, deliver, or register a transfer of an ADS; split, subdivide, or combine ADSs; make a distribution on an ADS; or permit withdrawal of equity shares, the depositary or Infosys may require:

- Payment of stock transfer or other taxes, governmental charges, third-party transfer or registration fees, and applicable depositary fees, expenses, and charges.
- Satisfactory proof of the identity and genuineness of any signature or other matters contemplated in the deposit agreement.
- Compliance with:
  - The provisions of Infosys’s memorandum and articles of association, and resolutions and regulations of the Board adopted under them.
  - Laws and governmental regulations relating to the execution and delivery of ADRs or ADSs, or the withdrawal or delivery of deposited securities.
  - Reasonable regulations and procedures established by the depositary from time to time, consistent with the deposit agreement and applicable laws, including presentation of transfer documents.

The depositary may refuse to issue and deliver ADSs or register transfers of ADSs generally when the depositary’s register or Infosys’s transfer books are closed. It may also do so at any time if the depositary or Infosys determines that refusal is necessary or advisable.

## Your Right to Receive the Shares Underlying Your ADSs

ADS holders have the right to cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying equity shares at any time, except:

- During temporary delays caused by:
  1. Closure of the depositary’s or Infosys’s transfer books.
  2. Blocking of equity-share transfers to permit voting at a shareholders’ meeting.
  3. Payment of a dividend on the equity shares.
- When the ADS holder owes fees, taxes, or similar charges.
- When withdrawals must be prohibited to comply with laws or governmental regulations applicable to ADSs or the withdrawal of equity shares or other deposited securities.
- In other circumstances specifically contemplated by Section I.A.(l) of the General Instructions to Form F-6, as amended from time to time.
- For any other reason if the depositary or Infosys determines in good faith that prohibiting withdrawals is necessary or advisable.

The depositary may not knowingly accept for deposit under the deposit agreement any equity shares or other deposited securities required to be registered under the Securities Act unless a registration statement covering those securities is effective.

The right of withdrawal may not be limited by any other provision of the deposit agreement.

## Direct Registration System

The deposit agreement provides that the DRS and Profile Modification System, or Profile, apply to uncertificated ADSs upon their acceptance into DRS by DTC.

DRS is administered by DTC and allows the depositary to register ownership of uncertificated ADSs. Ownership is evidenced by periodic statements issued by the depositary to the entitled ADS holders.

Profile is a required feature of DRS. It allows a DTC participant claiming to act on behalf of an ADS holder to direct the depositary to register a transfer of those ADSs to DTC or its nominee and deliver them to the DTC account of that participant without the depositary first receiving authorization from the ADS holder.

# OVERVIEW OF EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP COMPENSATION

## 1. Executive Leadership Compensation at Infosys

Infosys executive leadership compensation follows a Total Rewards approach comprising three primary components: Base/Fixed Pay, Performance Bonus, and Stock Incentives. A significant portion of rewards is provided through stock incentives.

Executive Leadership, also called Executive Officers, includes the Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer, and Chief Human Resources Officer.

## 2. Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee of the Board, referred to as the NRC, acts on behalf of the Board on matters relating to compensation for Directors and Executive Officers. It recommends changes to the compensation structure within the limits approved by shareholders, where applicable.

Shareholders approve the overall compensation structure for all whole-time directors.

## 3. Key Aspects of Executive Compensation Policy

### a. Key Objectives:

Infosys’s executive pay programs aim to:

- Attract and retain executive talent.
- Create a performance-oriented culture by rewarding performance against financial, strategic, and operational goals.
- Emphasize sustainable, long-term shareholder value creation through performance-oriented stock-based incentives.

### b. Primary Pay Components of the Executive Officers' Compensation

The executive pay program is based on three primary components: Base/Fixed Pay, Performance Bonus, and Stock Incentives. Together, these components constitute the Executive’s Total Rewards.

- **Base/Fixed Pay:** Fixed cash compensation that is guaranteed and paid periodically.
- **Performance Bonus:** Cash compensation linked to performance against defined Key Performance Indicators. Infosys operates a non-contractual, discretionary Performance Bonus Plan, subject to eligibility.
- **Stock Incentives:** Stock- or equity-based incentives form a substantial part of Executive Total Rewards and are determined by the desired pay-mix standards for the relevant level. Stock incentives may be granted as part of Total Rewards or in addition to Total Rewards, as decided by the CEO and NRC from time to time.

Stock incentives are governed by the 2015 Incentive Compensation Plan, the Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019, or other plans approved by shareholders from time to time. Equity grants generally are granted or vested based on Company and/or individual performance parameters, although some grants may be purely time-based.

The NRC may approve special or one-time stock-based incentives, including time-based retention grants or grants whose award or vesting is performance-based and, where appropriate, linked to extraordinary performance goals. One-time equity grants may also be issued to new Executive hires to meet specific compensation expectations and may include special vesting conditions.

### c. Executive Compensation Fitment and Benchmarking

Executive compensation at Infosys generally targets the market median of total compensation among a selected peer group, adjusted as needed for internal equity, consistency, and overall affordability.

For Executives, stock incentives and performance bonuses together constitute approximately 60% to 90% of total rewards.

### d. Compensation Practices

Current compensation practices for Executive Officers include:

- Using appropriate peer groups to establish total-rewards benchmarks for particular Executive roles.
- Aligning executive pay with shareholder value through performance-based stock incentives.
- Retaining Executives through multi-year vesting of stock incentives.
