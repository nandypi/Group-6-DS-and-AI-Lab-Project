## PRESS RELEASE Infosys Annual Report on Form 20-F for fiscal 2025 Available Online for ADS holders

Bengaluru, July 1, 2025 — Infosys announced that it filed its Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended March 31, 2025 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

The financial statements included in the Form 20-F were prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB). The Indian Integrated Annual Report is filed with the Indian stock exchanges within the statutory time limit before the Annual General Meeting.

Infosys makes its Annual Reports on Form 20-F available on its website to American Depositary Share (ADS) holders in lieu of physical distribution. Physical or email copies are available at no cost to ADS holders upon request.

## About Infosys

Infosys provides digital services and consulting to clients in more than 59 countries. The company describes its capabilities as including enterprise systems management, digital transformation, an AI-powered core, agile digital delivery, and continuous development and transfer of digital skills, expertise, and ideas through its innovation ecosystem.

- Auditor: Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP
- Auditor ID: 01180
- Auditor location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

### Securities registered pursuant to Section 12(b)

| Title of each class | Trading symbol | Exchange |
|---|---|---|

Securities registered pursuant to Section 12(g): None.

The number of outstanding shares as of the close of the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025 was 4,153,263,455 equity shares.

## Currency of presentation and certain defined terms

Infosys's consolidated financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars and prepared in accordance with IFRS as issued by the IASB.

- “U.S.” or “United States” means the United States of America, its territories, and its possessions.
- “India” means the Republic of India.
- “$,” “dollars,” and “U.S. dollars” mean the legal currency of the United States.
- “₹,” “Rupees,” and “Indian rupees” mean the legal currency of India.
- References to a particular fiscal year mean the fiscal year ended March 31 of that year.
- “We,” “us,” “our,” “Infosys,” “Company,” and “Group” mean Infosys Limited and its consolidated subsidiaries unless the context indicates otherwise.
- “IT services” excludes business process management services and the products and platforms business.

Unless otherwise stated, translations from Indian rupees to U.S. dollars are based on the fixing rate in Mumbai on business days for cable transfers in Indian rupees, as published by the Foreign Exchange Dealers’ Association of India (FEDAI).

On March 31, 2025, the exchange rate was ₹85.48 per $1.00. No representation is made that Indian rupee amounts could be converted into U.S. dollars at this or any other rate. Differences between table totals and the sums of listed amounts may result from rounding.
