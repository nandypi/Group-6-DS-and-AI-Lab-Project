## RECORDS, DISCLOSURES AND AUDITS

## Maintaining Accurate Records

The integrity of financial transactions and records is critical to the operation of the business. As a listed company, Infosys is required to maintain accurate financial reporting and appropriate internal controls and procedures.

Employees involved in financial reporting or accounting are expected to understand and follow relevant accounting and financial policies, controls, and procedures. Senior officers are expected to ensure that internal controls and procedures in their business areas are in place, understood, and followed.

All employees must ensure that business records and reports are honestly prepared, accurate, complete, and reliable. Records must also comply with the Document Retention and Archival Policy and other applicable policies based on location.

## Ensuring Accurate Public Disclosures

Infosys is committed to providing full, fair, accurate, timely, and clear disclosures in reports and documents filed with regulators and in other public communications. This requires compliance with disclosure controls and procedures and internal control over financial reporting.

## Auditors

Outside auditors must review company records fairly and accurately. Employees must cooperate with auditors in good faith and in accordance with law and must not mislead them about financial records, processes, controls, procedures, or other matters under review.

## Interacting with Regulators

Infosys must fully and truthfully cooperate with examinations or requests for information from regulators or law enforcement agencies. Contacts with law enforcement agencies or regulators must be coordinated through the Legal Department.

## Records on Legal Hold

In circumstances such as litigation or government investigations, the Legal Department may place a legal hold on records. A legal hold prevents the destruction of documents that may be required for an investigation. Employees must comply with Legal Department instructions while a legal hold is in effect.

Unless released in writing by the Legal Department, a legal hold remains effective.

## Record Retention

Company records must be maintained in accordance with the Document Retention and Archival Policy.

## ADMINISTERING OUR CODE

The Office of Integrity and Compliance is responsible for administering the Code and reports to the General Counsel and the Audit Committee of the Company.

## Acknowledgement

Employees and the Board of Directors are required to acknowledge that they have read and understood the Code. Independent directors may also be required to acknowledge acceptance of the Code for Independent Directors. Failure to read the Code, sign an acknowledgement, or certify online does not exempt anyone from the obligation to comply with it.

## Investigations

Infosys has a process to review and investigate potential legal or Code violations. Investigations are conducted confidentially and respectfully and fairly.

If an allegation is substantiated, the appropriate management team reviews the findings and determines the final outcome. Individuals who report potential violations in good faith, and those assisting in related investigations or processes, are supported by the Company. Retaliation may be reported to the Helpline.

Employees who are the subject of an external investigation must immediately report it to their manager unless prohibited by law.

## Waivers

Any waiver of the Code requires prior written approval from the Office of Integrity and Compliance or, in certain circumstances, the Board of Directors or a committee thereof. Waivers will be promptly disclosed as required by applicable law.

## Amendments/Modifications to Our Code

The Board is responsible for approving and issuing the Code. The Code was first effective on April 10, 2003, with revisions through July 12, 2019, October 13, 2021, October 13, 2022, and July 18, 2024.

The Code is reviewed annually to determine whether revisions are required because of changes in law or regulations, or changes in the business or business environment. The Board of Directors must approve changes to the Code.

## Disciplinary Actions

Violations of the Code, Company policies, or applicable laws may result in corrective or disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Infosys seeks consistency and fairness in discipline, subject to local law.

Disciplinary actions may include:

- Verbal or written warning
- Suspension with or without pay
- Loss or reduction of bonus or stock options
- Termination for serious offenses or repeated misconduct

The nature, severity, and frequency of the violation determine the disciplinary action. Violations of laws or regulations may expose individuals and the Company to substantial civil damages and criminal penalties.

Corrective action may be taken if an individual:

- Violates the Code, Company policies and procedures, or applicable laws
- Directs others to violate the Code, Company policies and procedures, or applicable laws
- Is aware of a violation or potential violation and fails to report it
- Fails to effectively monitor the actions of people they manage
- Does not cooperate in a Company audit or investigation
- Fails to participate in required training
- Retaliates against someone for reporting a concern in good faith or participating in an investigation
- Discloses information learned during an internal investigation

Concerns may be reported to a manager, Human Resources, a People & Culture Specialist, Accounting and Auditing, or the Helpline. Reportable concerns include:

- Inaccuracy of financial records
- Accounting and auditing irregularities
- Bribery, corruption, or illegal payments
- Criminal conduct and violations of law
- Discrimination and harassment
