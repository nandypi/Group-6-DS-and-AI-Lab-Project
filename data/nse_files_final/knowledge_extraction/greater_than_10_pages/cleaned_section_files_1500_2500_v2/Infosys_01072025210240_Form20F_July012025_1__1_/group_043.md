## Capital allocation policy

The capital allocation policy effective from fiscal 2020 was to pay approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and required approvals.

Effective from fiscal 2025, Infosys expects to continue returning approximately 85% of cumulative free cash flow over a five-year period through semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and required approvals. Under this policy, the company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure, as reported in the consolidated statement of cash flows prepared under IFRS. Dividends and buybacks include applicable taxes.

## Dividends

Under Indian law, dividends are paid upon recommendation by the Board and approval by a majority of shareholders. Shareholders may decrease, but not increase, the dividend amount recommended by the Board. Dividends may be paid from current-year profits after providing depreciation, undistributed profits from previous fiscal years, or both.

Holders of ADSs are entitled to receive dividends payable on the equity shares represented by those ADSs. Cash dividends are paid to the depositary in Indian rupees and are generally converted into U.S. dollars and distributed to ADS holders after deducting depositary fees, taxes, and expenses.

Although Infosys has no current intention to discontinue dividend payments, future dividends may not be declared or paid, and the amount may be decreased.

## Dividend Distribution Policy

Under Regulation 43A of the SEBI LODR, the top 500 listed companies must formulate a dividend distribution policy. Infosys adopted a policy describing the parameters and circumstances considered by the Board when determining dividend distributions and whether to retain profits.

The dividend policy is filed as an exhibit to this Annual Report on Form 20-F. Refer to Note 2.15, “Equity,” in Item 18 for details of buybacks completed and dividends recognized during the three years ended March 31, 2025.

## Item 9. The Offer and Listing

## PRICE HISTORY

Infosys equity shares are traded in India on the BSE and NSE. Infosys ADSs trade on the NYSE under the ticker symbol `INFY`, with each ADS representing one equity share.

Infosys ADSs began trading on NASDAQ on March 11, 1999. Following the voluntary delisting from the NASDAQ Global Select Market on December 11, 2012, the ADSs began trading on the NYSE on December 12, 2012. Infosys listed its ADSs on the Euronext London and Paris markets on February 20, 2013.

Effective July 5, 2018, Infosys voluntarily delisted its ADSs from the Euronext Paris and Euronext London exchanges because the average daily trading volume was low relative to the related administrative expenses. Infosys ADSs continue to be listed on the NYSE under the symbol `INFY`.

Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas serves as depositary for the ADSs traded under the Deposit Agreement dated March 10, 1999, as amended and restated on June 28, 2017.
