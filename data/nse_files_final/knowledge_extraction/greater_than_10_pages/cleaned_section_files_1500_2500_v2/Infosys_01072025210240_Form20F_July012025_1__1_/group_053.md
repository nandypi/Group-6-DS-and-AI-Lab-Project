The Finance Act, 2018 amended the Income Tax Act to provide that long-term capital gains exceeding ₹100,000 from the sale of equity shares in a company, a unit of an equity-oriented fund, or a unit of a business trust are taxable at 10%, subject to Securities Transaction Tax (STT) being paid at the relevant purchase and sale transactions. The gains do not receive the benefit of indexation. The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 increased the threshold from ₹100,000 to ₹125,000.

| Transfer takes place | Tax rate |
|---|---:|
| Before July 23, 2024 | 10% |
| On or after July 23, 2024 | 12.5% |

For investments made on or before January 31, 2018, the cost of acquisition is deemed to be the higher of:

1. The actual cost of acquisition; and
2. The lower of:
   - The fair market value (FMV) of the investment as of January 31, 2018; and
   - The full value of consideration received or accruing from the transfer of the capital asset, namely, the sale price.

For assets listed as of January 31, 2018, the FMV is the highest price quoted on a recognized stock exchange on that date. If the asset was not traded on that exchange on January 31, 2018, the highest price on the immediately preceding trading day is used.

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 amended the tax rates for short-term capital gains on the sale of equity shares in a company, a unit of an equity-oriented fund, or a unit of a business trust, subject to STT being paid at the time of sale.

| Transfer takes place | Tax rate |
|---|---:|
| Before July 23, 2024 | 15% |
| On or after July 23, 2024 | 20% |

Applicable surcharge rates are as follows:

| Total taxable income range | Domestic company | Foreign company |
|---|---:|---:|
| More than ₹10,000,000 but not more than ₹100,000,000 | 7% | 2% |
| More than ₹100,000,000 | 12% | 5% |

Taxes and applicable surcharge are increased by a health and education cess of 4%.

## Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

STT is levied on the purchase and sale of equity shares and derivatives on a recognized stock exchange.

| Type of transaction | STT rate | Levied on |
|---|---:|---|
| Sale of equity shares, delivery-based | 0.1% of the transaction value of securities | Buyer and seller |
| Sale of equity shares, non-delivery-based | 0.025% of the transaction value of securities | Seller |
| Sale of options in securities | 0.0625% of the option premium | Seller |
| Sale of options in securities where the option is exercised | 0.125% of the intrinsic value, defined as settlement price minus strike price | Buyer |
| Sale of futures in securities | 0.0125% of the transaction value | Seller |

Taxes on capital gains arising from these transactions may be offset by applicable credit mechanisms under double tax avoidance agreements.

Capital gains tax is computed by applying the applicable tax rate to the difference between the sale price and purchase price of ADSs or equity shares.

Under the Scheme, the purchase price of equity shares in an Indian listed company received in exchange for ADSs is the market price of the underlying shares on the date the Depositary gives notice to the custodian of delivery of the equity shares in exchange for the corresponding ADSs, or the “stepped-up” basis purchase price. The market price is the prevailing price of the equity shares on the BSE or NSE, as applicable.

The Income Tax Act does not contain a corresponding provision for the stepped-up basis of equity shares. However, to the best of the company’s knowledge, the Indian tax department has not denied this benefit. If the tax department denies the benefit, the original purchase price of the ADSs would be used to compute capital gains tax.

Under the Scheme, a non-resident holder’s holding period for determining the applicable Indian capital gains tax rate on equity shares received in exchange for ADSs begins on the date the Depositary gives the custodian notice of redemption. The Scheme does not address the holding-period commencement date for resident employees, so the applicable date for resident employees is unclear.

## Withholding Tax on Capital Gains

Any taxable gain realized by a non-resident on the sale of ADSs or equity shares is subject to withholding at source by the buyer.

Under Section 196C of the Income Tax Act, when interest, dividends, or long-term capital gains from the transfer of bonds or global depository receipts referred to in Section 115AC are payable to a non-resident, the payer must deduct tax at the time of credit or payment, whichever is earlier. The rate is 10%, subject to any concessional rate available under the relevant tax treaty read with the applicable Multilateral Instrument (MLI).

For long-term capital gains from the transfer of such bonds or global depository receipts on or after July 23, 2024, tax is deducted at 12.5%, subject to any concessional rate available under the relevant tax treaty read with the applicable MLI. A non-resident shareholder must provide required tax forms, including a tax residency certificate, to claim the treaty concession.

Under Section 196D(2) of the Income Tax Act, no withholding tax is required on capital gains earned by Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), as defined under Section 115AD, from the transfer of securities covered by that section.
