## Term of Office

The Indian Companies Act, 2013, precludes independent directors from retiring by rotation. Independent directors hold office for a term of up to five consecutive years on the board of directors and are eligible for re-appointment upon the Company passing a special resolution.

Salil Parekh, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director, retired by rotation at the 2025 Annual General Meeting under the applicable provisions of the Indian Companies Act, 2013. He sought re-appointment, and the Board recommended his re-appointment for shareholder approval. Salil Parekh’s re-appointment was approved by the shareholders at the Annual General Meeting on June 25, 2025, through March 31, 2027.
