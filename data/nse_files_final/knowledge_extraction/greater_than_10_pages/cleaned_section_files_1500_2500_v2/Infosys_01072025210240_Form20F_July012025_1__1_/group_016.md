## OUR STRATEGY

Infosys’s strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities where it operates.

Infosys aims to help clients ideate, plan, and execute their transition to an AI-enabled future by building strong data foundations and robust cloud capabilities. The strategy also includes safeguards for privacy, ethics, controls, and Responsible AI practices.

In 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy:

1. Scale Agile Digital
2. Energize the Core
3. Reskill our People
4. Expand Localization

Infosys reports that most employees worldwide have been enabled to work in a hybrid model, supporting operational stability, delivery flexibility, and business continuity.

### 1. Scale Agile Digital

Infosys describes its digital transformation as increasingly enhanced by AI and reports that it is progressing toward becoming an AI-first organization. It was rated a “leader” in 58 analyst ratings across digital service offerings in fiscal 2025.

During the fiscal year, Infosys:

- Launched Infosys Aster™, a set of AI-amplified marketing services, solutions, and platforms.
- Completed the acquisition of in-tech, an engineering R&D services provider.
- Completed the acquisition of InSemi, a semiconductor design and embedded services provider.
- Strengthened its insight and data analytics services through Infosys AI solutions and Infosys Topaz®.
- Expanded capabilities in agile software development, 5G, autonomous technology, product engineering, the Internet of Things, and blockchain.
- Invested in partnerships with cloud hyperscalers including AWS, GCP, and Microsoft Azure, as well as Software-as-a-Service providers.
- Expanded automation and AI services through alliances with Robotic Process Automation providers, AI infrastructure companies such as Nvidia, and niche AI companies.
- Built four small language models for banking, IT operations, cybersecurity, and enterprise AI implementation and scaling.
- Obtained ISO 42001:2023 certification for its Artificial Intelligence Management System framework.
- Continued to expand software testing and cybersecurity services through Cyber Next.
- Trained more than 9,700 employees in niche digital skills through academia partnerships with Purdue, Trinity, RISD, and Cornell.

### 2. Energize the Core

Infosys uses automation and AI to help clients modernize legacy technology and process landscapes. In fiscal 2025, it won total contract value of more than $11.6 billion in large deals.

Investments in internal systems, internal process reimagination, and software development automation were intended to increase agility, improve productivity, and enhance competitiveness. Infosys supports client growth areas including AI, cloud, and digital, as well as efficiency areas including automation, cost reduction, and consolidation.

Infosys also expanded its capabilities for Global Capability Center scope across consulting, sales, build, and delivery functions.

### 3. Reskill our People

Continuous learning and reskilling are part of Infosys’s operating model. Its reskilling program is intended to increase fulfillment of demand for digital skills in client projects and expand the expertise of its global workforce in next-generation technologies and methodologies.

Infosys scaled its global digital reskilling program to include generative AI courses. More than 270,000 employees are described as AI-aware.

Lex, Infosys’s in-house learning platform, offers more than 36,000 courses and includes mobile learning, telemetry, gamification, and certification features. More than 307,000 active employees use Lex.

### 4. Expand Localization

Infosys has invested in expanding its local workforce in the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, Japan, China, Canada, and Australia. It has established innovation hubs, near-shore centers, and digital design studios across these geographies.

Infosys has also expanded university and community college partnerships in these regions to support internships, recruitment, training, and joint research.

### Priorities

Infosys prioritizes:

- Scaling cloud capabilities, including cloud advisory, data on cloud, cloud security, SaaS, Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS), Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS), and private cloud.
- Expanding capabilities in AI, product engineering, cybersecurity, and human experience.
- Strengthening its employee value proposition for newer contexts of work and workplace.
- Operating in a cost-effective and agile manner, including increasing automation in service delivery.
- Delivering on ESG commitments while helping clients achieve sustainability goals.

## OUR SOLUTIONS AND CAPABILITIES

Infosys provides business and technology solutions and capabilities across consulting and other service areas.

### (1) Consulting

Infosys Consulting helps organizations address complex challenges through strategic insight, industry knowledge, technology capabilities, and transformation execution. It operates across more than 50 countries in North America, EMEA, and APAC.

Its work includes legacy-system modernization, cloud and data strategies, and the integration of generative AI into core operations. Its methods and technologies include design thinking, data-driven methodologies, AI, automation, open-source software, and innovation from the start-up ecosystem.

### Industry expertise

Infosys Consulting serves a broad range of industry sectors, combining global scale with local market knowledge.

### Consulting capabilities

Infosys Consulting’s multidisciplinary teams provide:

- **Enterprise AI solutions:** Next-generation technologies for business challenges, smart automation, efficiency, and growth.
- **Digital platforms:** Design, implementation, and management of platforms including SAP, Oracle, and Salesforce.
- **Technology transformation:** Strategic visioning, enterprise architecture, cloud enablement, cybersecurity, M&A integration, and modern operating models.
- **Workforce transformation:** Learning, communication, organizational design, and other measures supporting adoption and long-term value.
