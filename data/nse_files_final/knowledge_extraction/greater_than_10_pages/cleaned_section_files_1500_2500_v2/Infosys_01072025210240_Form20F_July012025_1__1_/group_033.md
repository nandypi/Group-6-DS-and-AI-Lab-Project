# Leadership and Board Profiles

## Helene Auriol Potier

Helene Auriol Potier is an Independent Director of Infosys and a member of the Environment, Social and Governance Committee and the Risk Management Committee.

She began her telecommunications career in New York in 1986. In 1990, she joined Nortel Networks Corporation, where she spent 15 years in senior leadership roles, including Vice President, Sales, Mobile Division Worldwide, and Vice President, Services & Operations, EMEA. In 2005, she joined Dell Inc. as CEO, Africa, Mediterranean and CEE. She joined Microsoft Corporation in 2008 and held senior roles for 10 years, including CEO, Microsoft Singapore, and Managing Director, Artificial Intelligence, Europe. From November 2018 to December 2020, she was Executive Vice President, International Business, at Orange.

She served as an independent director on the boards of Safan S.A. until May 2025, Mimecast Limited until May 2022, Ispen S.A. until May 2018, and Faiveley Transport S.A. until November 2016. She also served as ESG co-chair and board member of Institut Français des Administrateurs (IFA).

She currently serves as an independent non-executive director on the boards of Accor S.A., Randstad N.V., and Oddo BHF S.C.A. She chairs the Randstad board technology committee, the Accor board ESG committee, and the Oddo BHF board compensation committee. She is also a senior advisor at a global private equity firm.

Helene holds a Master of Science in Engineering from Telecom Paris and an Executive MBA from INSEAD.

## Nitin Paranjpe

Nitin Paranjpe is an Independent Director of Infosys and a member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Audit Committee.

He is currently the Non-Executive Chairman of Hindustan Unilever Limited and a member of the Supervisory Board of Heineken N.V. Previously, he was a member of the Unilever Leadership Executive, where he served as Chief People and Transformation Officer and Chief Operating Officer.

Nitin joined Hindustan Unilever Limited in 1987 and held roles in marketing and sales. In April 2008, he became Chief Executive Officer, India, and Executive Vice President for Unilever, South Asia. He is also a member of the Chinmaya Mission Advisory Council and the WeSchool Innovation Advisory Board.

Nitin holds a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering and an MBA in Marketing from Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management, Mumbai.

## Inderpreet Sawhney

Inderpreet Sawhney is the Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer of Infosys Limited. She leads the company’s legal and compliance function, including legal and regulatory matters, the compliance and ethics program, and privacy and data protection.

She provides counsel to the Infosys Board of Directors on critical matters and serves as a director on the boards of EdgeVerve Systems Limited, Infosys BPM Limited, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, and Infosys Consulting Holding AG. She is also a board member of Hillenbrand Inc., a New York Stock Exchange-listed company.

Previously, Inderpreet served as General Counsel of another large IT services company and as Managing Partner of a mid-sized law firm in Silicon Valley, where she led complex international transactions.

She is a trustee of the Infosys Foundation, India, and Chairperson of the Board of Trustees of Infosys Foundation USA. She is a member of the World Economic Forum’s Global Future Council on Good Governance and served as its co-chair for the 2023–2024 term.

Inderpreet holds a B.A. (Hons.) and L.L.B. degree from Delhi University and an L.L.M. from Queen’s University, Kingston, Canada.

## Shaji Mathew

Shaji Mathew is the Chief Human Resource Officer of Infosys. He is responsible for the HR roadmap, strategy, and operational priorities for Infosys and its group companies.

Shaji has more than three decades of experience at Infosys. Before his current role, he was Infosys’ Global Head of Delivery for Financial Services, Insurance, Healthcare, and Life Sciences. He has held leadership roles in delivery, client relationships, commercials, solutioning, and people management across the United States, Europe, and Asia Pacific.

He has been involved in the company’s efforts to create a more inclusive and diverse workforce. He is also a trustee of Infosys Foundation, focusing on corporate social responsibility.

Shaji serves on the board of the International Institute of Information Technology, Bangalore, and is a member of the Confederation of Indian Industry National Committee on Leadership and HR.

He is a rank holder from the National Institute of Technology, Calicut. He completed a Global Leadership Program at Stanford University Graduate School of Business and a leadership program at Harvard Business School.

## Jayesh Sanghrajka

Jayesh Sanghrajka is the Executive Vice President and Group CFO of Infosys Ltd. He leads the company’s global finance organization, including financial planning and analysis, investor relations, treasury, tax, and risk management.

His responsibilities include supporting growth at good margins, financial and operational excellence, finance transformation, identifying revenue growth opportunities, and driving cost optimization. He is also responsible for mergers and acquisitions and supports the company’s long-term competitive advantage.

Jayesh champions Infosys’ Environmental, Social, and Governance agenda, including sustainable growth, compliance with international standards, and stakeholder transparency.

Before joining Infosys, he worked at Tishman-Speyer, Rediff.com, Mu Sigma, and KPMG, where he contributed to digital and financial transformation initiatives.

Jayesh holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce from Mumbai University, is a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and has a degree in cost accounting. He has attended executive global leadership development programs at Stanford University and Harvard University.
