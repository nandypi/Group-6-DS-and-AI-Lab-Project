## Form B

**SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015**  
Regulation 7(2) read with Regulation 6(2)—continual disclosure.

### Securities Transactions

Details of changes in securities holdings by a promoter, member of the promoter group, designated person, director of a listed company, immediate relatives of such persons, and other persons identified under Regulation 6(2).

- Name of the company
- ISIN of the company
- Name and identification details of the insider
- Category of person: promoter, member of promoter group, designated person, or director
- Type of security
- Securities held before acquisition or disposal
- Securities acquired or disposed
- Securities held after acquisition or disposal
- Date of allotment advice, acquisition, or disposal
- Date of intimation to the company
- Mode of acquisition or disposal: on-market, public, rights, preferential offer, off-market, or inter se transfer
- Exchange on which the trade was executed

**Note:** “Securities” has the meaning defined under Regulation 2(1)(i) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

### Trading in Derivatives

Details of derivatives trading in the company’s securities by a promoter, member of the promoter group, designated person, director of a listed company, immediate relatives of such persons, and other persons identified under Regulation 6(2).

- Contract specifications
- Type of contract
- Derivatives type: futures, options, or other specified type
- Number of units or contracts multiplied by lot size
- Notional value
- Buy or sell transaction
- Exchange on which the trade was executed

**Note:** For options, notional value is calculated based on the premium plus the strike price of the options.

## FORM C (Indicative format)

**SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015**  
Regulation 7(3)—transactions by other connected persons as identified by the company.

### Securities Transactions by Other Connected Persons

The disclosure format covers:

- Identification details of the other connected person
- Connection with the company
- Type of security, including shares, warrants, or convertible debentures
- Securities held or disposed
- Rights, number, and percentage of shareholding

**Note:** “Securities” has the meaning defined under Regulation 2(1)(i) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

### Details of Trading in Derivatives by Other Connected Persons

The disclosure format covers:

- Contract specifications
- Type of contract
- Derivatives type, including futures or options
- Number of units or contracts multiplied by lot size
- Buy or sell transaction
- Notional value
- Exchange on which the trade was executed

**Note:** For options, notional value is calculated based on the premium plus the strike price of the options.
