## VALUES IN ACTION

Acting with integrity and transparency means being ethical, sincere, and open in all transactions. Employees and leaders are expected to take personal accountability, keep commitments, and speak up when actions, conditions, or behaviors conflict with Infosys values and culture.

## What does it mean to act with integrity and transparency?

Employees are expected to act ethically, communicate openly, and raise concerns when they are uncomfortable or uncertain about conduct that may conflict with the company’s values, policies, or laws.

## What does it mean to create client value?

Creating client value means bringing forward ideas and recommendations that are in the client’s best interests and fulfilling professional responsibilities in a way that supports long-term partnerships.

This includes:

- Consistently working to improve customer satisfaction.
- Soliciting honest customer feedback through regular surveys.
- Developing a memorable customer experience.
- Making additional efforts to support customer retention and referrals.

## VALUES IN ACTION

Infosys states that employees should act on the conviction that ordinary people can be inspired and mentored to achieve extraordinary things.

## What does it mean to lead by example?

Employees and leaders should:

- Act with fairness and honesty in all business activities.
- Ensure that people they supervise and report to understand and follow the Code, company policies, and applicable laws.
- Know what resources are available to provide guidance.
- Support employees who, in good faith, ask questions, raise concerns, or cooperate with investigations.
- Raise integrity concerns immediately.

## What does it mean to be fair?

Fairness in the workplace means respecting the rights of everyone who works with Infosys.

This includes:

- Treating employees fairly while considering differing skills, abilities, and circumstances.
- Making expectations and evaluation criteria clear.
- Giving employees an equal opportunity to share ideas and raise grievances.
- Discouraging politicking and encouraging open communication.
- Giving employees credit for their ideas and contributions.

## What does it mean to be excellent?

Excellence includes:

- Taking responsibility for actions.
- Focusing attention on the present moment.
- Speaking with purpose and ensuring that intentions and words are sincere.
- Learning from mistakes and using feedback to grow.
- Matching behavior with personal and company values.
- Doing things differently when appropriate.
- Maintaining balance when making choices about time and energy.
- Being responsible for thoughts, feelings, words, choices, and results.

The Code of Conduct expresses Infosys’ commitment to conducting business ethically and in accordance with its culture and values. Employees are expected to follow both the letter and the intent of the Code.

Employees should:

- Understand the areas covered by the Code, company policies and procedures, and laws applicable to their work.
- Follow the legal requirements of all locations where Infosys does business.
- Conduct themselves consistently with the Code, company policies, procedures, and applicable laws.
- Speak up if they have concerns or suspect violations.
- Certify, when requested, that they have reviewed, understood, and agreed to follow the Code.
- Understand that following the Code is a mandatory part of their job.

## THE CODE IS MORE THAN JUST WORDS ON A PAGE

The Code cannot address every situation. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and ask questions when they need guidance or clarification.

Resources available to employees include:

- Managers
- The Office of Integrity and Compliance
- Human Resources
- The Legal Department
- The Helpline
- Other resources listed in the Code

Employees should also be aware of all company policies and procedures applicable to their work. The Policy Portal serves as a repository for company policies.

## I am the Example for my Team

Managers are often the first people contacted about concerns in the work environment. Their responsibilities include:

- Acting as role models of ethical behavior.
- Encouraging team members to raise issues and speak up.
- Communicating a positive commitment to ethics and compliance.
- Promoting company values, the Code of Conduct, and compliance with policies and laws.
- Supporting ethics and compliance awareness and training programs.
- Maintaining open avenues for communication.
- Listening and responding fairly to employee concerns.
- Finding satisfactory and complete resolutions to ethical issues.
- Escalating concerns when additional assistance is needed.

## I Follow the Code

The Code applies equally to all Infosys directors, officers, and employees globally, including employees of its subsidiaries. It also applies to partners, suppliers, agents, and others acting on behalf of Infosys.

Employees are expected to know and follow the Code as a guideline for decision-making based on integrity.

## I Lead by Example

Every employee is expected to lead when ethics are involved and to be accountable for their actions in accordance with Infosys’ C-LIFE values.

Infosys’ non-retaliation policy is described as an embodiment of its values and a cornerstone of the Code. Employees are encouraged to report violations of Infosys values and principles to the Helpline.

Infosys states that it will protect individuals from retaliation when they make good-faith reports of integrity concerns. Retaliation by a manager, coworker, or other person is not tolerated against anyone who makes such a report. This protection also extends to individuals who assist with or cooperate in an investigation or report concerning an integrity issue.
