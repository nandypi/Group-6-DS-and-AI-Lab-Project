## Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund, which is a defined benefit plan. Both eligible employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered employees’ salaries. The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees’ Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to the government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to cover any shortfall between the trust’s investment returns and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries receive benefits from a defined contribution provident fund. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered employees’ salaries. Contributions are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no further obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution plan. The Group has no further obligation beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating absences is determined by an external actuary at each balance sheet date using the projected unit credit method. The calculation reflects the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the balance sheet date. Expense for non-accumulating absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
