## 2. Prohibition on communicating or procuring UPSI

An Insider shall not:

- Communicate, provide, or allow access to any UPSI relating to the Company or its Securities to any person, including other Insiders, except as permitted by this Code or the SEBI Regulations.
- Procure from or cause the communication by an Insider of UPSI relating to the Company or its Securities.

These restrictions do not apply when UPSI is communicated, provided, made accessible, or procured:

- In furtherance of legitimate purposes, performance of duties, or discharge of legal obligations, subject to appropriate notice and the execution of confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements.
- When the Board directs or causes public disclosure of UPSI in the best interest of the Company.
- Within a group of persons identified and secluded behind a “Chinese wall” or information barrier by the Compliance Officer for a particular purpose or specified period in furtherance of legitimate purposes, performance of duties, or discharge of legal obligations.

Persons within a “Chinese wall” may be subject to additional confidentiality obligations, information barriers preventing exchanges of UPSI outside the wall, and an undertaking to abstain from or forego trading during the period of seclusion or until the UPSI becomes generally available information or ceases to exist as UPSI. The Company determines the applicable Chinese wall procedures and processes for permitting a designated person to cross the wall.

## 3. Prohibition on Insider Trading

1. An Insider shall not, directly or indirectly:

   - Trade in Securities of the Company that are listed or proposed to be listed while in possession of UPSI.
   - Trade in Securities of the Company except when the Trading Window is open and the Insider is not in possession of UPSI.
   - Provide advice or tips to any third party regarding trading in the Company’s Securities while in possession of UPSI.

2. A Designated Person possessing unpublished price sensitive information about another public company gained during employment with the Company shall not:

   - Trade in the Securities of that other public company.
   - Tip or disclose the material non-public information concerning that company to anyone.
   - Give trading advice concerning that other public company.

3. No Designated Person shall take positions in derivative transactions in the Securities of the Company at any time.

4. The restriction in paragraph 3(1) may not apply to:

   - **(a)** An off-market inter-se transfer between Insiders possessing the same UPSI without breaching this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
   - **(b)** A transaction carried out through the block deal window mechanism between persons possessing UPSI without breaching this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
   - **(c)** A transaction carried out pursuant to a statutory or regulatory obligation.
   - **(d)** A transaction undertaken pursuant to the exercise of stock options where the exercise price is pre-determined in accordance with applicable regulations.
   - **(e)** Trades pursuant to a Trading Plan established in accordance with this Code and the SEBI Regulations.

The exceptions in paragraph 3(5) reflect the statutory exceptions in Regulation 4(1) of the SEBI Regulations. They do not preclude prior approval or other requirements applicable to trading in the Company’s Securities under this Code.

## 4. Trading Window

1. The Compliance Officer shall notify a Trading Window during which Designated Persons may trade in the Company’s Securities after securing pre-clearance in accordance with this Code.
2. Designated Persons and their Immediate Relatives shall not trade in the Company’s Securities when the Trading Window is closed.
3. The Trading Window shall generally be closed for all Designated Persons from the sixteenth day before the last day of any financial period for which the Company is required to announce results until 48 hours after disclosure of those financial results.
4. The Trading Window shall also be closed for a Designated Person or class of Designated Persons when the Compliance Officer determines that they can reasonably be expected to possess UPSI, for periods determined by the Compliance Officer. The relevant Designated Person or class of Designated Persons shall receive notification of such special blackout periods.
5. The Trading Window may be reopened no earlier than 48 hours after the UPSI becomes generally available information or is no longer classified as UPSI.
6. Trading Window restrictions shall not apply to:

   - Off-market inter-se transfers between Insiders possessing the same UPSI without violating this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
   - Transactions carried out through the block deal window mechanism between persons possessing UPSI without violating this Code, where both parties made a conscious and informed trading decision.
   - Transactions carried out pursuant to a statutory or regulatory obligation to undertake a bona fide transaction.
   - Transactions undertaken pursuant to the exercise of stock options where the exercise price was pre-determined in compliance with applicable regulations.
   - Trades executed under a Trading Plan established in accordance with this Code.
   - The pledge of shares for a bona fide purpose, such as raising funds, subject to pre-clearance by the Compliance Officer.
   - Transactions undertaken in accordance with SEBI regulations, including acquisition through conversion of warrants or debentures, subscription to a rights issue, further public issue, preferential allotment, or tendering of shares in a buy-back offer, open offer, or delisting offer, and transactions undertaken through any other mechanism specified by SEBI.

## 5. Pre-clearance of Trading

6. Designated Persons who intend to trade in Securities of the Company, either in their own name or through their Immediate Relatives, including to buy, sell, gift, transfer, pledge, or unpledge Securities, must obtain pre-clearance when the Trading Window is open and the value of the Securities to be traded, whether in one transaction or a series of transactions during a calendar quarter, exceeds Rs. 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh). The application shall be made to the Compliance Officer in the format in Annexure 1, stating the estimated number of units and other specified details, together with a declaration in the format in Annexure 2 that the applicant is not in possession of UPSI.

7. The Compliance Officer shall not approve a proposed trade if the Designated Person is determined to be in possession of UPSI, even when the Trading Window is open.

8. After being satisfied that the application and undertaking are true and accurate, the Compliance Officer may approve the trade subject to execution within seven trading days following the date of approval.

9. The Board of Directors of the Company shall be the approving authority for the Compliance Officer’s pre-clearance application.

10. Within two days of executing the trade, the Designated Person shall submit the trade details to the Compliance Officer in accordance with Annexure 3. If the transaction is not undertaken, a report to that effect shall be filed in the same form.

11. If a pre-cleared trade is not executed within seven trading days after approval, the Designated Person must obtain pre-clearance again.

12. Pre-clearance is not required for trades executed under an approved Trading Plan or pursuant to transmission of Securities.

13. A Designated Person who trades in Securities without complying with the pre-clearance procedure, or who gives false undertakings or makes misrepresentations in the undertakings submitted during the pre-clearance procedure, shall be subject to the penalties specified in this Code.

## 6. Additional Trading restrictions on Designated Persons

1. No Designated Person shall enter into derivative transactions in respect of Securities of the Company.

2. A Designated Person who trades in Securities of the Company shall not enter into a Contra Trade during the six months following the prior transaction. If a Contra Trade is executed, inadvertently or otherwise, in violation of this restriction, the profits from that trade shall be liable to be disgorged for remittance to SEBI for credit to the Investor Protection and Education Fund administered by SEBI.

3. The Contra Trade restriction shall not apply to the exercise or sale of employee stock option plan (ESOP) Securities, provided that the Designated Person does not possess UPSI, the sale is executed when the Trading Window is open, and pre-clearance has been obtained.

4. The Contra Trade restriction shall also not apply to transmission of Securities.

## 7. Trading Plan

1. A Designated Person may formulate a Trading Plan that complies with the SEBI Regulations and present it to the Compliance Officer for approval and public disclosure. The Trading Plan may be executed only after approval by the Compliance Officer and disclosure to the stock exchanges on which the Company’s Securities are listed.

2. A Designated Person may delegate discretionary authority to their broker. However, after the Trading Plan commences, the Designated Person shall not consult with the broker regarding execution of transactions or disclose to the broker information concerning the Company that might influence execution of transactions under the Trading Plan.

Once approved, the Trading Plan shall be irrevocable. The Designated Person must implement the plan and may not deviate from it or execute trades in the Company’s Securities outside its scope, except as otherwise provided under the SEBI Regulations.

Implementation of the Trading Plan shall not commence if, when the plan was formulated, the Designated Person possessed UPSI that had not become generally available information when implementation was due to begin. Commencement shall be deferred until the UPSI becomes generally available information.
