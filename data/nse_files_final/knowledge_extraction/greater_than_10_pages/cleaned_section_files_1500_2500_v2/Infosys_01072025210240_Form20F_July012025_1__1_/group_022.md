## HUMAN CAPITAL

Infosys focuses its human resources management on hiring and retaining talent, supporting employee skill development and career growth, and maintaining a collaborative, transparent, and participative organizational culture.

As of March 31, 2025, Infosys employed 323,578 employees, including 306,599 professionals involved in service delivery to clients, including trainees.

Infosys recruits fresh graduates globally, including from universities, colleges, and institutes in India and other countries. It recruits developers, architects, technical leaders, and project managers in digital, cloud, and transformation areas. Its recruitment and training initiatives include the InfyTQ learning and assessment platform, Infosys Certification, and HackWithInfy, an online coding contest.

Infosys recruits students from campuses outside India, including in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Singapore, Japan, Germany, Canada, Mexico, Mauritius, and China. Its selection process evaluates mathematical and logical aptitude, coding ability, and interview performance. Interviews are conducted virtually across the globe, and the in-house applicant tracking system is used for hiring in India and China.

During fiscal 2025, Infosys received 4,457,748 employment applications, interviewed 430,080 applicants, and extended offers of employment to 83,207 applicants. These statistics exclude subsidiaries.

## INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Infosys protects its intellectual property through patent, copyright, trademark, and design laws; trade secrets; confidentiality procedures; and contractual provisions.

As of March 31, 2025, Infosys had 823 pending or registered patents in India, the European Union, the United States, and other key jurisdictions. Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries also had more than 870 trademarks pending or registered across classes covering various goods and services in 52 countries.

Employees, independent contractors, and, where possible, vendors are required to enter into confidentiality agreements. These agreements generally require confidential or proprietary information developed by Infosys or on its behalf, and information disclosed to third parties during the course of business, to be kept confidential. Clients usually own the intellectual property in software developed for them.

Infosys has registered the key brand name “INFOSYS” as a trademark in India and the United States and takes steps to protect its trade names, trademarks, service marks, and domain names from infringement.

## EFFECT OF GOVERNMENT REGULATION ON OUR BUSINESS

Indian government regulation affects Infosys’ business in several ways. Infosys benefits from certain Government of India tax incentives, including incentives related to the export of services from special economic zones (SEZs). These incentives have resulted in relatively lower Indian tax liabilities for its operations.

Infosys has also benefited from the liberalization and deregulation of the Indian economy since 1991. Certain Indian laws and regulations remain applicable to its business, including requirements in some cases to obtain approval from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and/or the Ministry of Finance to acquire companies incorporated outside India. Subject to exceptions, approval from relevant Indian government authorities may also be required to raise capital outside India. The conversion of Infosys equity shares into American Depositary Shares (ADSs) is governed by RBI guidelines.

Under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, a company meeting specified financial thresholds must constitute a Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Committee of the board and spend at least 2% of its average net profit for the immediately preceding three financial years on CSR. The thresholds include net worth of ₹500 crore or more, approximately $60 million; turnover of ₹1,000 crore or more, approximately $120 million; or net profit of ₹5 crore or more, approximately $1 million, during the immediately preceding financial year. The CSR Committee must consist of at least three directors, including at least one independent director, and the company must have a board-approved CSR policy.

Infosys contributed approximately $69 million toward corporate social responsibility activities during fiscal 2025.

The ability of Infosys’ technology professionals to work in the United States, Europe, and other countries depends on obtaining the necessary visas and work permits, as described in Item 3—Risk Factors.

## LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

### McCamish Cybersecurity incident

From March 6, 2024, through July 25, 2024, six actions were filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia against McCamish in connection with the cybersecurity incident initially disclosed on November 3, 2023.

The six actions were consolidated, and a consolidated class action complaint was filed on November 7, 2024, purportedly on behalf of all persons residing in the United States whose personally identifiable information was compromised in the incident, including persons who received an incident notice.

On December 20, 2024, the court granted the parties’ joint motion to stay proceedings while they pursued mediation. On March 13, 2025, McCamish and the plaintiffs participated in mediation, resulting in an in-principle agreement covering the proposed settlement of the class action lawsuits against McCamish and seven class action lawsuits arising from the incident filed against McCamish’s customers.

On May 9, 2025, McCamish and the plaintiffs entered into a definitive settlement agreement, and the plaintiffs moved for preliminary approval. Under the settlement terms, McCamish agreed to pay $17.5 million into a fund to settle these matters. The settlement remained subject to preliminary and final court approval. If approved, it would resolve the allegations made in the class action lawsuits without an admission of liability.

Apart from proceedings and claims arising from the McCamish cybersecurity incident, the Group is subject to legal proceedings and claims arising in the ordinary course of business. Management reasonably expects that these ordinary-course legal actions, when ultimately concluded and determined, will not have a material adverse effect on the Group’s results of operations or financial condition.

## D. PROPERTY, PLANT AND EQUIPMENT

Infosys’ corporate headquarters campus is located at Electronics City, Bengaluru, India. Infosys Electronics City consists of approximately 4.7 million square feet of land and 6.5 million square feet of operational facilities. The campus includes an Education, Training and Assessment unit, a Management Development Center, and conference facilities.

Infosys also leases approximately 380,000 square feet of facilities in Electronics City, accommodating approximately 4,700 employees.

Capital expenditure on property, plant, and equipment was $263 million in fiscal 2025, $266 million in fiscal 2024, and $319 million in fiscal 2023. All capital expenditures were financed from cash generated from operations. As of March 31, 2025, Infosys had contractual capital expenditure commitments of $109 million.

Infosys’ software development facilities include networked workstations, servers, data communication links, and videoconferencing infrastructure.

Infosys had a presence in 292 locations across 59 countries. Expansion plans were being undertaken to support expected future growth.
