## 2.16 Other income, net

## Accounting policy

Other income primarily comprises interest income, dividend income, gains or losses on investments, exchange gains or losses on forward and options contracts, and exchange gains or losses on the translation of foreign currency assets and liabilities.

Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to receive payment is established.

## Functional currency and presentation currency

The functional currency of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries and controlled trusts is the Indian rupee. The functional currencies of foreign subsidiaries are their respective local currencies. The financial statements are presented in U.S. dollars, rounded to the nearest million.

## Transactions and translations

Foreign currency-denominated monetary assets and liabilities are translated into the relevant functional currency at exchange rates in effect at the balance sheet date. Resulting gains or losses are recognized in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income within exchange gains/(losses) on translation of assets and liabilities, net, except when deferred in other comprehensive income as qualifying cash flow hedges.

Non-monetary assets and liabilities denominated in a foreign currency and measured at fair value are translated using the exchange rate at the date on which fair value was determined. Those measured at historical cost are translated using the exchange rate at the transaction date. Related revenue and expenses are recognized using the same exchange rate.

Transaction gains or losses realized upon settlement of foreign currency transactions are included in net profit for the period in which the transaction is settled. Revenue, expense, and cash-flow items denominated in foreign currencies are translated into the relevant functional currencies using the exchange rate on the transaction date.

For translation of foreign subsidiaries' financial statements into the presentation currency, assets and liabilities are translated using the exchange rate at the balance sheet date, while revenue, expenses, and cash-flow items are translated using the average exchange rate for the respective periods. Resulting gains or losses are included in currency translation reserves under other components of equity.

When a subsidiary is disposed of in full, the relevant currency translation amount is transferred to net profit in the statement of comprehensive income. A change in the parent's ownership that does not result in loss of control is recorded through equity.

Other comprehensive income, net of taxes, includes translation differences on non-monetary financial assets measured at fair value at the reporting date, including equities classified as financial instruments measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (FVOCI).

Goodwill and fair value adjustments arising on the acquisition of a foreign entity are treated as assets and liabilities of that foreign entity and translated at the exchange rate in effect at the balance sheet date.

## Government grants

The Group recognizes government grants only when there is reasonable assurance that the attached conditions will be complied with and that the grants will be received.

Government grants related to assets are treated as deferred income and recognized in net profit in the statement of comprehensive income on a systematic and rational basis over the useful life of the asset. Government grants related to revenue are recognized systematically in the statement of comprehensive income over the periods necessary to match them with the related costs they are intended to compensate.

## 2.17 Employees' Stock Option Plans (ESOP)

## Accounting policy

The Group recognizes compensation expense relating to share-based payments in net profit based on the estimated fair value of awards on the grant date. The estimated fair value of awards is recognized as an expense in net profit in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income on a straight-line basis over the requisite service period for each separately vesting portion of the award, as if the award comprised multiple awards, with a corresponding increase to share premium.

## Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program 2019 (the 2019 Plan):

On June 22, 2019, following shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2019 Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2019 Plan may not exceed 50,000,000 equity shares. To implement the plan, up to 45,000,000 equity shares may be issued through secondary acquisition of shares by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

Restricted stock units (RSUs) granted under the 2019 Plan vest based on achievement of defined annual performance parameters determined by the administrator, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Performance parameters are based on a combination of relative total shareholder return (TSR) against selected industry peers, selected broader domestic and global market indices, and company operating performance metrics. Each parameter is distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. These instruments generally vest between one and three years from the grant date.

## 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan (the 2015 Plan):

On March 31, 2016, following shareholder approval through postal ballot, the Board was authorized to introduce, offer, issue, and allot share-based incentives to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries under the 2015 Stock Incentive Compensation Plan.

The maximum number of shares under the 2015 Plan may not exceed 24,038,883 equity shares, including 11,223,576 equity shares held by the trust toward the 2011 Plan as of March 31, 2016. These instruments generally vest over four years. The plan numbers were further adjusted for the September 2018 bonus issue.

Equity-settled and cash-settled RSUs and stock options generally vest over four years and are exercisable within the period approved by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NARC). The exercise price of RSUs equals the par value of the shares, while the exercise price of stock options equals the market price on the grant date.

The controlled trust held 9,655,927 shares as of March 31, 2025, and 10,916,829 shares as of March 31, 2024, under the 2015 Plan. Of these shares, 200,000 equity shares were earmarked for employee welfare activities as of both March 31, 2025, and March 31, 2024.
