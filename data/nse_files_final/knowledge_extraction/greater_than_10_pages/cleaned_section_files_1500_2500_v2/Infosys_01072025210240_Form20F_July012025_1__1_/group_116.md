The following matters shall ordinarily be disclosed, including but not limited to:

1. Financial results and guidance.
2. Dividends.
3. Changes in capital structure.
4. Mergers, de-mergers, acquisitions, delisting, disposals, and expansion of business.
5. Awards or terminations of orders or contracts outside the normal course of business, and other similar transactions.
6. Changes in key managerial personnel (KMP), as defined under the Companies Act, 2013, except changes resulting from superannuation or the end of a term.
7. Resignation of a statutory auditor or secretarial auditor.
8. Changes in ratings other than ESG ratings.
9. Proposed fundraising.
10. Agreements, by whatever name called, that affect the management or control of the company.
11. Fraud or defaults by the company, its promoter, director, key managerial personnel, or subsidiary, whether in India or abroad.
12. Arrest of key managerial personnel, promoters, or directors, whether in India or abroad.
13. Resolution plans, restructuring, or one-time settlements relating to loans or borrowings from banks or financial institutions.
14. Admission of a winding-up petition filed by any party or creditor.
15. Admission by the Tribunal of an application filed by the corporate applicant or financial creditors to initiate corporate insolvency resolution proceedings against the company as a corporate debtor.
16. Approval or rejection of a resolution plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.
17. Initiation of a forensic audit, by the company or another entity, to detect misstatements in financial statements, misappropriation, siphoning, or diversion of funds, and receipt of the final forensic audit report.
18. Actions initiated or orders passed in India or abroad by a regulatory, statutory, enforcement, or judicial body against the listed entity or its directors, key managerial personnel, senior management, promoter, or subsidiary in relation to the listed entity.
19. Outcomes of litigation or disputes that may affect the listed entity.
20. Guarantees or indemnities, or becoming a surety for a third party outside the normal course of business.
21. Granting, withdrawal, surrender, cancellation, or suspension of key licenses or regulatory approvals.
22. Other matters specified under SEBI regulations or decided by the company from time to time.

**Explanation 1 — Fraud and default**

- “Fraud” has the meaning assigned in Regulation 2(1)(c) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003.
- “Default” has the meaning assigned in Clause 6 of paragraph A of Part A of Schedule III of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

**Explanation 2 — Materiality**

For identifying the events listed in this clause as unpublished price-sensitive information, the materiality guidelines in paragraph A of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations, as specified by the Board from time to time, and the materiality requirements in paragraph B of Part A of Schedule III of the Listing Regulations shall apply.

Information is “non-public” or “unpublished” until it has been widely disseminated to the public, such as through a filing with the NSE, BSE, NYSE, or SEC, a press conference, or a public release, or until it is accessible to the public on a non-discriminatory basis.

Words and expressions used but not defined in this code, and defined in the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, the Depositories Act, 1996, or the Companies Act, 2013, and the rules and regulations made under those laws, have the meanings assigned to them in those laws.
