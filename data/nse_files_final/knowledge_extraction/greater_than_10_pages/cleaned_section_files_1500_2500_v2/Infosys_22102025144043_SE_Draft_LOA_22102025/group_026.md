## Clubbing of Entitlement

**22.24** To ensure that the same Eligible Shareholder(s) with multiple demat accounts or folios do not receive a higher entitlement under the Small Shareholder category, the Company will club together Equity Shares held by shareholders with a common PAN when determining the category—Small Shareholder or General—and entitlement under the Buyback.

For joint shareholding, the Company will club Equity Shares where the sequence of PANs of the joint shareholders is identical. If PANs for all joint shareholders are unavailable, the Company will compare the sequence of the joint holders’ names and club Equity Shares where the names are identical.

For physical shareholders, where the sequence of PANs is identical and PANs for all joint shareholders are unavailable, the Registrar to the Buyback will compare the sequence of the joint holders’ names. Equity Shares will be clubbed where the sequence of PANs and the names of the joint shareholders are identical.

Institutional investors, including mutual funds, pension funds or trusts, and insurance companies, with a common PAN will not be clubbed when determining the category or entitlement. They will be considered separately where the Equity Shares are held for different schemes and under different demat account nomenclature, based on information prepared by the Registrar and Transfer Agent from shareholder records received from the Depositories.

Equity Shares held under the categories of “clearing members,” “corporate body margin account,” or “corporate body—broker,” as shown in the beneficial position data on the Record Date, will not be clubbed where they have a common PAN and are assumed to be held on behalf of clients.

## Miscellaneous

**22.25** For avoidance of doubt:

**22.25.1** Eligible Shareholders are advised to tender Equity Shares from the demat accounts or folios in which they held the Equity Shares on the Record Date.

**22.25.2** Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback from each Eligible Shareholder will not exceed the number of Equity Shares tendered by that Eligible Shareholder.

**22.25.3** Equity Shares accepted under the Buyback from each Eligible Shareholder will not exceed the number of Equity Shares held by that Eligible Shareholder on the Record Date.

**22.25.4** Equity Shares tendered above the number held by an Eligible Shareholder on the Record Date will not be considered for acceptance.

**22.25.5** In case of practical issues arising from rounding or otherwise, the Board or the Buyback Committee authorized by the Board may decide the final allocation, including any excess or shortage of Equity Shares after allocation under the process described in this Section 22.

## Participation by ADS Holders

**22.26** ADS holders will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. As stated in the Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025, ADS holders wishing to participate had to withdraw the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs before the Record Date and become holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date.

To do so, ADS holders needed to:

1. Apply for and obtain a PAN from the Indian Income Tax Department.
2. Establish an account with a bank, broker, or other nominee in India to receive Equity Shares in electronic or dematerialized form.
3. Establish an Indian demat account (Subtype DR) and a brokerage account before the Record Date.
4. Submit the desired number of ADSs to Deutsche Bank Trust Company Americas, as ADR depositary, for cancellation with proper cancellation instructions sufficiently in advance of the Record Date.
5. Withdraw the underlying Equity Shares so that they held Equity Shares of the Company on the Record Date.

As holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date, they could tender any or all of those withdrawn Equity Shares in accordance with the Buyback terms when the Tendering Period commenced.

**22.27** ADS holders who cancelled ADSs and withdrew the underlying Equity Shares, becoming holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date, would receive an entitlement based on the number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date.

**22.28** Equity Shares trade on the NSE and BSE and cannot be traded on the NYSE. There is no guarantee that Equity Shares withdrawn following ADS cancellation would be accepted in whole or in part in the Buyback.

The Company had received an intimation from SEBI in 2017 that the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014, issued by the Government of India, was in force. Since the scheme had not been amended with respect to conversion or reconversion of depository receipts, that advice continued to apply. Conversion of ADSs into Equity Shares and reconversion of Equity Shares into ADSs would be subject to the Depository Receipt Scheme, 2014 and the terms of the ADSs.

**22.29** If an ADS holder withdrew the Equity Shares underlying ADSs after the ADS postal ballot cut-off date and before the last date of the Tendering Period, the holder could re-deposit any Equity Shares that were not tendered or were tendered but not accepted in the Buyback to receive ADSs representing those Equity Shares. The holder had to deliver the Equity Shares to the Depositary’s Custodian, together with ADS issuance instructions, on or before 30 days after the expiration of the Tendering Period. The Depositary agreed to waive the ADS issuance fee for ADSs issued against such re-deposited Equity Shares during that 30-day period.

**22.30** Withdrawn Equity Shares for which re-deposit did not occur, or instructions were not given, within the 30-day period, as well as Equity Shares withdrawn before the ADS postal ballot cut-off date or after the last day of the Tendering Period, could be re-deposited only if doing so would not cause the specified maximum number of outstanding ADSs to be exceeded. The Company could not assure ADS holders that such Equity Shares would be eligible for re-deposit. In these circumstances, the ADS issuance fee would not be waived.

## 23. PROCEDURE FOR TENDER / OFFER AND SETTLEMENT

**23.1** The Buyback is open to all Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in physical or dematerialized form on the Record Date. It also includes ADS holders who submitted the desired number of ADSs to the Depositary for cancellation and withdrew the underlying Equity Shares from the American Depositary Receipt program no later than the Record Date.

The Company will effect the Buyback through a Tender Offer on a proportionate basis. The Letter of Offer and Tender Form will be sent electronically to Eligible Shareholders who have registered their email addresses with the Depositories or the Company. They will be sent by speed post or courier to Equity Shareholders in the United States who have not registered their email addresses. In case of non-receipt, the procedure in Paragraph 23.11 applies. The Company will provide a physical copy of the Letter of Offer upon request.

**23.2** The Company will not accept Equity Shares that:

- are subject to a court or competent authority restraint order restricting transfer or sale;
- have disputed or unclear title;
- relate to lost share certificates where duplicate certificates have not been issued because the request remains under process or for another legal reason;
- are locked-in or non-transferable during the applicable lock-in period or until they become transferable, in accordance with Regulation 24(v) of the Buyback Regulations.

**23.3** The Company will accept Equity Shares validly tendered by Eligible Shareholders based on their Buyback Entitlement. Additional Equity Shares tendered by Eligible Shareholders, if any, will be accepted in accordance with Paragraph 22.21.

**23.4** Eligible Shareholders must tender Equity Shares from the same demat account in which they held the Equity Shares on the Record Date. If they held Equity Shares in multiple demat accounts, they must submit separate applications from each demat account.

If the demat account in which the Equity Shares were held on the Record Date has changed, the Eligible Shareholder must provide sufficient proof to the Registrar. Tendered Equity Shares may be accepted subject to verification and validation by the Registrar. If sufficient proof is not received, the Board or the Buyback Committee authorized by the Board may decide the final allocation.
