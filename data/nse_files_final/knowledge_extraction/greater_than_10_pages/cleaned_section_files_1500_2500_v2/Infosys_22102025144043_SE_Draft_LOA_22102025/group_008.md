## Disclaimer for Persons in countries other than India

Except for the filing of the Letter of Offer with the SEC, the Letter of Offer has not been filed, registered, or approved in any jurisdiction outside India. Shareholders outside India must comply with applicable local legal requirements. The Buyback is not directed to any jurisdiction or person where participation would violate applicable laws or require additional registrations or approvals.

The Letter of Offer has been prepared for compliance with the Buyback Regulations, and its disclosures may differ from those required in jurisdictions outside India. The Company and the Manager to the Buyback are not required to update the information after the date of the Letter of Offer except as required by law. Shareholders tendering Equity Shares represent that they are authorised under applicable local laws to participate in the Buyback.

## 4. TEXT OF THE RESOLUTION PASSED AT THE BOARD MEETING

The Buyback through the tender offer route was authorised and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on September 11, 2025. The resolution provided:

In accordance with Article 14 of the Articles of Association of the Company, Sections 68, 69, and 70 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015, and the SEBI Buy-back of Securities Regulations, 2018, and subject to shareholder approval by special resolution and other required approvals, including exemptive relief from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the Board approved the buyback of fully paid-up equity shares with a face value of ₹5 each.

The Equity Shares would be bought back from shareholders as of a Record Date to be determined by the Board, on a proportionate basis, at a price of ₹1,800 per Equity Share. The total Buyback Offer Size would be ₹18,000 crore, representing 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate of total paid-up share capital and free reserves based on the Company’s latest audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.

The Buyback Offer Size excludes transaction costs, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory and intermediary fees, public announcement publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, securities transaction tax, goods and services tax, stamp duty, and other incidental expenses.

The Buyback Period would begin on the date of declaration of the postal-ballot results for the special resolution and continue until the last date on which payment for the Equity Shares bought back by the Company is made.

The Board or Buyback Committee could, until one working day before the Record Date, increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the Buyback Offer Size remained unchanged.

All equity shareholders as of the Record Date would be eligible to participate, including holders of American Depositary Shares (ADSs) who cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date. ADS holders could re-deposit any withdrawn Equity Shares not tendered, or tendered but not accepted, in the Buyback for the creation of ADSs.

To the extent legally permissible, the Company would implement the Buyback using SEBI’s mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchanges and approach BSE and/or NSE to facilitate the Buyback. Subject to the decision of the Board or Buyback Committee, either BSE or NSE would act as the designated stock exchange.

The Buyback would be funded from the Company’s free reserves and securities premium account, or another source permitted under the Buyback Regulations or the Companies Act, 2013. It would be undertaken through the tender offer route on the Indian stock exchanges on terms and conditions determined by the Board.

The approved Buyback Price and Buyback Offer Size would result in the proposed repurchase of 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up Equity Shares, representing 2.41% of the fully paid-up share capital.

The amount required for the Buyback was intended to be funded from the Company’s current surplus, cash balances, and/or cash generated from internal accruals.

The Company could buy back Equity Shares from all existing shareholders on a proportionate basis. At least 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, or the number of Equity Shares to which Small Shareholders were entitled based on their shareholding, whichever was higher, would be reserved for Small Shareholders. Small Shareholders were shareholders whose shares or other specified securities had a market value of not more than ₹2,00,000 based on the closing price on the recognised stock exchange with the highest trading volume as of the Record Date. If shares tendered under the reservation were fewer than the reserved quantity, the shortfall would be adjusted in the general category.

The Buyback of Equity Shares from non-resident members, including Foreign Corporate Bodies, Foreign Institutional Investors, Foreign Portfolio Investors, foreign-national shareholders, and ADS holders with underlying Equity Shares, would be subject to the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, applicable rules and regulations, the Income Tax Act, 1961, the Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014, and required approvals, including approvals from the Reserve Bank of India where applicable. Such approvals would be obtained by the relevant non-resident shareholders.

A Buyback Committee comprising the Chief Financial Officer, the Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer, and the Company Secretary was constituted. The Buyback Committee was authorised to take actions necessary for implementing the Buyback, including:

1. Seeking all regulatory approvals, if any.
2. Initiating actions for the preparation and amendment of the notice to the general meeting or postal-ballot notice, as applicable, and issuing the public announcement, Letter of Offer, and other related documents.
3. Taking necessary actions concerning the opening, operation, and closure of required accounts, including an escrow account with a bank; issuing a bank guarantee; obtaining banking credit facilities, including non-fund-based and/or fund-based limits; depositing acceptable securities with appropriate margin with the merchant bankers; and entering into related agreements.
