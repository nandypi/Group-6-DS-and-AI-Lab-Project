## FORM OF TRANSFER DEED

### Form No. SH-4 - Securities Transfer Form

Pursuant to section 56 of the Companies Act, 2013 and sub-rule (1) of rule 11 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014.

The form records the transfer of securities from the transferor(s) to the transferee(s), subject to the conditions on which the securities are held.

**Company:** INFOSYS LIMITED  
**CIN:** L85110KA1981PLC013115  
**Stock exchanges:** BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited

The form includes fields for the number of securities transferred, consideration received, distinctive numbers, certificate numbers, and transfer details.

### Declaration

The transferee must indicate whether:

- Government approval under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 is not required before the transfer of shares; or
- Government approval is required, has been obtained, and is enclosed with the form.

### Enclosures

1. Certificate of shares, debentures, or other securities.
2. Letter of allotment, if no certificate has been issued.
3. Copy of the PAN card of all transferees for listed companies.
4. Other documents, if applicable.
