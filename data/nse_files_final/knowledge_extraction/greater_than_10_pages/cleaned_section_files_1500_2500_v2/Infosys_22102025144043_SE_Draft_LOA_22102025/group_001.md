## Update on proposal for Buyback of Equity Shares of the Company

**October 22, 2025**

Infosys Limited reported an update on its proposal to buy back equity shares. The Board had approved a proposed buyback of:

- Up to 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares.
- Face value: ₹5 per equity share.
- Buyback price: ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash.
- Aggregate buyback size: up to ₹18,000 crore.
- Maximum shares as a percentage of existing total paid-up equity share capital, on a standalone basis: 2.41%.
- Route: tender offer.
- Basis: proportionate basis, subject to the small shareholder reservation.
- Record date: to be announced later.

A draft version of the Letter of Offer was being filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for U.S. regulatory purposes. The final Letter of Offer was to be filed under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, and the Companies Act, 2013, each as amended.

The draft Letter of Offer was provided for informational purposes only. It did not constitute an invitation or offer to acquire or purchase securities, or an invitation or offer to participate in the proposed buyback. Any shareholder decision to participate, if and when the buyback was launched, was to be based on the final Letter of Offer, other definitive documents issued by the Company, and applicable laws and regulations.

## Additional information pursuant to U.S. Law

As of October 22, 2025, the proposed buyback had not been approved by the Company’s shareholders and had not commenced.

If approved by shareholders, any offer to purchase or solicitation of an offer to sell securities in the United States would be made pursuant to a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO, including the Letter of Offer and other documents filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Security holders were advised to review those documents, including any amendments and other buyback-related SEC filings, before making a decision regarding the buyback because they would contain the offer’s terms and conditions.

## LETTER OF OFFER

The Letter of Offer was intended for registered equity shareholders and beneficial owners of fully paid-up equity shares of Infosys as of the Record Date. The Record Date was specified as `[●]` in the draft.

The proposed offer covered up to 10,00,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each, representing 2.41% of the total number of equity shares in the Company’s paid-up equity share capital on a standalone basis. The shares were proposed to be bought back from eligible shareholders on a proportionate basis, subject to the small shareholder reservation, through the tender-offer route at ₹1,800 per equity share for aggregate consideration of up to ₹18,000 crore.

1. The buyback was to be undertaken in accordance with Article 14 of the Company’s Articles of Association; Sections 68, 69, and 70 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013; relevant rules; applicable LODR Regulations; the Buyback Regulations; SEBI circulars; and other applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

2. The buyback was subject to necessary approvals, permissions, consents, exemptions, sanctions, conditions, and modifications from statutory, regulatory, or governmental authorities, including SEBI and the BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited.

3. The Buyback Offer Size represented:

   - 24.31% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis.
   - 21.68% of the corresponding aggregate on a consolidated basis.

   The Buyback Offer Size was stated to be within the statutory limit of 25% of the aggregate fully paid-up capital and free reserves under Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations.

4. The Letter of Offer was to be sent electronically to equity shareholders as of the Record Date. A physical copy was to be provided if requested by an eligible shareholder, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

5. The Form of Acceptance-cum-Acknowledgement, or Tender Form, and the share transfer form, Form SH-4, were to be enclosed with the Letter of Offer. The procedure for acceptance was described in the section titled “Procedure for Tender/Offer and Settlement” on page 52 of the draft Letter of Offer.

6. The draft Letter of Offer also referred shareholders to the section titled “Process and Methodology for the Buyback” on page 48 for the acceptance procedure and to “Procedure for Tender/Offer and Settlement – Method of Settlement” on page 57 for payment of cash consideration.

7. Shareholders were advised to read the Letter of Offer, including the sections titled “Details of the Statutory Approvals” and “Note on Taxation,” before tendering their equity shares. These sections were referenced on pages 46 and 59, respectively.
