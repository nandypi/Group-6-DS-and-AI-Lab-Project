## 11. MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF THE LIKELY IMPACT OF THE BUYBACK ON THE COMPANY

### 11.1 Impact on Profitability and Earnings

The Company believes that the Buyback is not likely to materially affect profitability or earnings, except through a reduction in funds available for investment and the resulting investment income. Assuming full acceptance of the Buyback, the Company may deploy up to ₹18,000 Crore, excluding Transaction Costs, which would reduce the funds available for investments and affect investment income.

### 11.2 Promoter and Public Shareholding

Under the Tender Offer route and the SEBI Buyback Regulations, the Promoter and Promoter Group may participate in the Buyback. The Promoter and Promoter Group stated in letters dated September 14, 2025, September 16, 2025, September 17, 2025, September 18, 2025, and September 19, 2025, that they did not intend to participate.

Assuming full acceptance from all other Eligible Shareholders up to their Buyback Entitlement, the aggregate shareholding of the Promoters and members of the Promoter Group after completion of the Buyback would increase to 13.37% of the post-Buyback total paid-up Equity Share capital, from 13.05% of the pre-Buyback total paid-up Equity Share capital as of the date of the Public Announcement. Public shareholding would change to 86.63% of the post-Buyback total paid-up Equity Share capital, from 86.95% of the pre-Buyback total paid-up Equity Share capital.

### 11.3 Change in Control and Management

The Buyback is not expected to result in a change in control or otherwise affect the existing management structure of the Company.

### 11.4 Change in Shareholdings and Foreign Investment Limits

Following the Buyback, the shareholding of Non-Resident Shareholders, Indian financial institutions, banks, mutual funds, public shareholders, and other bodies corporate will change based on the number of Equity Shares bought back from each category. FIIs and FPIs are advised to ensure that their investment in the Company remains within the limits prescribed under applicable laws after completion of the Buyback.

### 11.5 Growth Opportunities

The Company believes that the Buyback will not affect its growth opportunities.

### 11.6 Return on Equity and Earnings per Share

The Buyback is expected to improve return on equity through the distribution of cash and improve earnings per share over the long term by reducing the equity base.

### 11.7 Debt Position

As of the date of the Letter of Offer, the Company has no outstanding debt from lenders. Accordingly, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone or consolidated financial statements as of June 30, 2025, the ratio of the aggregate secured and unsecured debt owed by the Company is not, and will not be, more than twice its paid-up share capital and free reserves.

### 11.8 Restriction on Further Capital Raising

The Company will not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period, except in discharge of subsisting obligations.

### 11.9 Restriction on Issue of Equity Shares and Specified Securities

Subject to applicable law, the Company will not issue Equity Shares or other specified securities, including through a bonus issue, until expiry of the Buyback Period. Exceptions include the discharge of subsisting obligations through conversion of warrants, stock option schemes, sweat equity, or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares. Relevant details and the potential impact of these subsisting obligations have been disclosed in the Public Announcement.

### 11.10 Defaults in Payments

The Company has had no subsisting defaults during the last three years in:

- Repayment of deposits or payment of interest on deposits.
- Redemption of debentures or payment of interest on debentures.
- Payment of dividends due to shareholders.
- Repayment of term loans or payment of interest to any financial institution or banking company.

### 11.11 Delisting

The Company is not undertaking the Buyback to delist its shares from Indian stock exchanges.
