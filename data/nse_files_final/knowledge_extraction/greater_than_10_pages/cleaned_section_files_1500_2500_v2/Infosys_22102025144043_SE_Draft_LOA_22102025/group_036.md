## Management's Responsibility

Management is responsible for preparing the Statement in accordance with Section 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations, and other applicable requirements. This includes computing the permissible capital payment, maintaining supporting records, establishing relevant internal controls, selecting an appropriate basis of preparation, and making reasonable estimates.

## Auditor's Responsibility:

Pursuant to the Buyback Regulations, the auditors’ responsibility was to provide reasonable assurance that:

1. They had inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
2. The permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback, as stated in Annexure A, had been properly determined using those financial statements and in accordance with Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, Regulation 4(i), and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations.
3. The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, had formed the solvency opinion required under Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations on reasonable grounds. The auditors reported that the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from the Board resolution dated September 11, 2025, or during the one-year period immediately following the shareholders’ resolution on the proposed buyback.

The interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements had been audited, and an unmodified audit opinion was issued on July 23, 2025. The audit was conducted in accordance with Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013, applicable authoritative pronouncements of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and relevant auditing standards.

The examination of the Statement was conducted in accordance with the ICAI Guidance Note on Audit Reports and Certificates for Special Purposes (Revised 2016), applicable auditing standards, the ICAI Code of Ethics, and applicable quality-control requirements under SQC 1.

## Opinion

Based on the inquiries and examination performed, the auditors reported that:

1. They had inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to its audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025. These financial statements were approved by the Board of Directors on July 23, 2025.
2. The permissible capital payment toward the proposed buyback, as computed in Annexure A, had been properly determined in accordance with Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, Regulation 4(i), and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations. The share capital and free reserve amounts were extracted from the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
3. The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, had formed its solvency opinion under Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations on reasonable grounds. The auditors reported that the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from the Board resolution dated September 11, 2025, or during the one-year period immediately following the shareholders’ resolution on the proposed buyback.

## Restriction on Use

The report was issued at the Company’s request for use in connection with the proposed buyback, including the explanatory statement, public announcement, other buyback documents, regulatory filings, submissions to depositories, and submissions to the merchant bankers. It was not intended for any other purpose or person without the auditors’ prior written consent.
