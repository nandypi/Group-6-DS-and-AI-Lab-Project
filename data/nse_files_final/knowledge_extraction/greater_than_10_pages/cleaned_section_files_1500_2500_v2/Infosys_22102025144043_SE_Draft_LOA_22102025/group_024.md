## 20. DETAILS OF STATUTORY APPROVALS

- The Board approved the buyback proposal at its meeting held on September 11, 2025. Shareholders approved the buyback through a special resolution by postal ballot and remote e-voting; the results were announced on [●], 2025.
- The buyback is subject to approvals required under the Companies Act, 2013, the Buyback Regulations, and other applicable laws and regulations.
- Each eligible shareholder is responsible for obtaining any statutory consents and approvals required to tender shares, including approvals from the RBI and/or SEBI where applicable. Copies of such approvals must be provided to the Registrar to the Buyback.
- The buyback from non-resident shareholders is subject to approvals from relevant authorities, including the RBI, where required. Non-resident shareholders must submit the required approvals with the Tender Form. The Company may make payment where prior RBI approval is not required and may reject shares where required approvals have not been submitted.
- Non-resident shareholders, excluding overseas corporate bodies, who are permitted to participate under the general permission framework of India’s consolidated Foreign Direct Investment policy and applicable FEMA regulations are not required to obtain RBI approval.
- By participating in the buyback, each eligible shareholder, including each non-resident shareholder, undertakes to complete applicable regulatory and statutory filings, including Form FC-TRS where required. Shareholders authorise the Company to undertake necessary filings on their behalf where required and agree to provide the necessary assistance.
- Because the Company has securities registered under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and U.S. residents hold ADSs and equity shares, the Company obtained exemptive relief from the SEC for certain tender-offer procedures affected by conflicting Indian and U.S. regulatory requirements.
- As of the date of the Letter of Offer, no other statutory or regulatory approvals are required to implement the buyback apart from those described above. If further approvals become applicable, the buyback will be subject to them. Any resulting changes to the proposed timetable will be communicated to the Indian stock exchanges.
- The buyback from eligible shareholders resident outside India, including foreign corporate bodies, foreign portfolio investors, non-resident Indians, and persons of foreign nationality, is subject to FEMA, applicable rules and regulations, the Income Tax Act, 1961, and required approvals from relevant authorities.
- Reporting requirements under the RBI, FEMA, and other applicable rules for remittance of funds must be completed by the eligible shareholder and/or the shareholder’s broker through which the bid is placed.
- If the completed Tender Form and other documents are not received but the equity shares are credited to the Clearing Corporations’ accounts and a valid bid is placed in the exchange bidding system, the bid will be deemed accepted.
- The Company has no outstanding debt from lenders and therefore does not require prior lender consent for breach of any lender covenant.

## 21. DETAILS OF THE REGISTRAR TO THE BUYBACK AND COLLECTION CENTRES

Eligible shareholders wishing to tender shares may send or hand-deliver the Tender Form, the transaction registration slip generated by the exchange bidding system, and relevant documents to the Registrar to the Buyback so that they are received by the Buyback Window Closing Date, [●], by 5:00 p.m. IST. The envelope should be marked “Infosys Limited - Buyback 2025”.

### KFin Technologies Limited

KFin Technologies Limited is the Registrar to the Buyback for Infosys Limited. The Registrar’s SEBI registration number is `INR000000221` and its Corporate Identity Number is `L72400MH2017PLC444072`.

For eligible shareholders holding equity shares in dematerialised form, the Tender Form and transaction registration slip are not required to be submitted to the Company, the Manager to the Buyback, or the Registrar. After confirmation of the lien marked in the shareholder’s demat account to the Clearing Corporation and placement of a valid bid in the exchange bidding system, the bid will be deemed accepted.

Eligible shareholders should not send the Tender Form, transaction registration slip, or other relevant documents to the Company or the Manager to the Buyback. Incomplete documents may be rejected.

## 22. PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY FOR THE BUYBACK

- The Company proposes to buy back 10,00,00,000 equity shares, representing 2.41% of its fully paid-up equity share capital on a standalone basis, through a proportionate tender offer.
- The total buyback consideration is ₹18,000 crore, at a price of ₹1,800 per equity share, payable in cash.
- The buyback is pursuant to Article 14 of the Company’s Articles of Association, Sections 68, 69 and 70 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the relevant rules including the Share Capital Rules, and the Buyback Regulations.
- The buyback size represents 24.31% of the aggregate of the Company’s total paid-up share capital and free reserves on a standalone basis and 21.68% on a consolidated basis, based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025.
- Shareholders approved the buyback through a special resolution by postal ballot; the results were announced on [●]. The buyback remains subject to approvals, permissions, and sanctions required from statutory, regulatory, or governmental authorities, including the RBI, SEBI, and/or the Indian stock exchanges.
- The buyback is open to all eligible shareholders and beneficial owners holding equity shares in physical or electronic form as of the Record Date. This includes ADS holders who submitted their desired number of ADSs to the Depositary and withdrew the underlying equity shares so that they held equity shares as of the Record Date.
- The Company has expressed no opinion on whether eligible shareholders should participate in the buyback.
- The Promoters and Promoter Group held 54,20,29,249 equity shares as of the date of the Public Announcement and the Letter of Offer, representing 13.05% of the existing equity share capital.
- Under the tender-offer route and the SEBI Buyback Regulations, the Promoter and Promoter Group may participate in the buyback. They expressed their intention not to participate through letters dated September 14, 16, 17, 18, and 19, 2025.
- Assuming 100% participation and full acceptance from all other eligible shareholders up to their buyback entitlement, the Promoters and members of the Promoter Group’s shareholding would increase from 13.05% of the pre-buyback paid-up equity share capital to 13.37% of the post-buyback paid-up equity share capital.
