## 17.1.3 Overview of the Company

Infosys is a global provider of digital services and consulting, delivering AI, cloud, and other digital solutions for specific industries in more than 59 countries. The Company's solutions are classified as Digital and Core.

## Digital

- Experience
- Insight

## Core

- Application management services
- Proprietary application development services
- Independent validation solutions
- Product engineering and management
- Infrastructure management services
- Traditional enterprise application implementation
- Support and integration services
- Business Process Management

## 17.2 Growth of Business

### 17.2.1 Standalone Financial Performance

For the financial years ended March 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, the Company recorded the following standalone results:

| Financial year ended March 31 | Revenue from operations (₹ crore) | Profit after tax (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2025 | 136,592 | 25,568 |
| 2024 | 128,933 | 27,234 |
| 2023 | 124,014 | 23,268 |

For the year ended March 31, 2025, standalone revenue grew 5.94% year on year, while standalone profit after tax declined 6.12%.

### 17.2.2 Standalone Results for the Six Months Ended September 30, 2025

For the six months ended September 30, 2025, the Company recorded standalone revenue from operations of ₹72,182 crore and standalone profit after tax of ₹13,874 crore. Compared with the same period in the previous year, standalone revenue grew 6.87% and standalone profit after tax grew 10.28%.

### 17.2.3 Consolidated Financial Performance

For the financial years ended March 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, the Company recorded the following consolidated results:

| Financial year ended March 31 | Consolidated revenue (₹ crore) | Consolidated profit after tax (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2025 | 162,990 | 26,750 |
| 2024 | 153,670 | 26,248 |
| 2023 | 146,767 | 24,108 |

For the year ended March 31, 2025, consolidated revenue grew 6.06% year on year and consolidated profit after tax grew 1.91%.

### 17.2.4 Consolidated Results for the Six Months Ended September 30, 2025

For the six months ended September 30, 2025, the Company recorded consolidated revenue from operations of ₹86,769 crore and consolidated profit after tax of ₹14,300 crore. Compared with the same period in the previous year, consolidated revenue grew 8.06% and consolidated profit after tax grew 10.94%.
