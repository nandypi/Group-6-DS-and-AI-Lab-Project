## Notes

- In February 1993, the Company made a public issue of 13,76,000 Equity Shares of ₹10 each at a premium of ₹85 per share. The issue was fully subscribed, and allotment was made on April 29, 1993.
- During fiscal 1995, the Company placed 5,50,000 Equity Shares at ₹450 per share on a preferential basis with FIIs, FIs, mutual funds, and institutional funds.
- During fiscal 1999, 10,35,000 Equity Shares with a par value of ₹10 each were issued under the ADS program at US$68 per Equity Share. The ADSs were listed on NASDAQ and are currently listed on the NYSE following the Company's voluntary delisting from the NASDAQ Global Select Market on December 11, 2012.
- On November 29, 1999, the Company announced a stock split, subdividing every Equity Share with a par value of ₹10 into two Equity Shares with a par value of ₹5 each. The split took effect in February 2000.
- Board meeting dates referenced in the source: April 28, 1994; April 8, 1997; December 23, 1998; November 29, 1999; April 13, 2004; April 14, 2006; October 10, 2014; April 24, 2015; August 19, 2017; July 13, 2018; January 11, 2019; April 14, 2021; and October 13, 2022.
- For ESOP allotment, the average issue price represents the average exercise price of options exercised during the period, adjusted for bonus issues and splits wherever applicable.
