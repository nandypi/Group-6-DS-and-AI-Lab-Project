## Legend Required by U.S. Law

The buyback referenced in this document had not yet commenced. This draft of the Letter of Offer was provided for informational purposes only and was neither an offer to purchase nor a solicitation of an offer to sell the Company's securities.

Any offer to purchase or solicitation of an offer to sell was to be made through a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO, including the Letter of Offer and other documents relating to the tender offer, to be filed by the Company with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

Security holders were advised to read the SEC filings, any amendments, and other documents relating to the buyback in their entirety before making any decision, because those documents contained important information, including the offer's terms and conditions. When available, copies were to be obtainable free of charge from the SEC's website or the Company's Investor Relations department.

Neither the SEC nor any state securities commission had approved or disapproved of the transaction, passed on its merits or fairness, or passed on the adequacy or accuracy of the information contained in the document.
