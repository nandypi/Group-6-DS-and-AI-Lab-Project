## 23.11 In case of non-receipt of the Letter of Offer and the Tender Form:

### 23.11.1 In case the Eligible Shareholder holds Equity Shares in dematerialised form:

Eligible Shareholders who receive the Letter of Offer electronically may request a physical copy from the Company or the Registrar by providing their name, address, number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date, Client ID number, DP name or ID, and beneficiary account number.

An Eligible Shareholder may participate in the Buyback by downloading the Tender Form from the websites of the Company, NSE, BSE, the Registrar, or the Manager to the Buyback. Alternatively, the shareholder may submit a signed plain-paper application stating:

- Name and address of the Eligible Shareholder(s)
- Number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date
- Client ID number
- DP name and DP ID
- Beneficiary account number
- Number of Equity Shares tendered for the Buyback

For the Buyback tendering procedure, shareholders should follow Paragraph 23.25.

### 23.11.2 In case the Eligible Shareholder holds Equity Shares in physical form:

An Eligible Shareholder may participate in the Buyback by downloading the Tender Form from the websites of the Company, NSE, BSE, the Registrar, or the Manager to the Buyback. Alternatively, the shareholder may submit a signed plain-paper application stating:

- Name and address of the Eligible Shareholder(s)
- Folio number
- Number of Equity Shares held
- Equity Share certificate number
- Number of Equity Shares tendered for the Buyback
- Distinctive numbers of the shares

The application must enclose the original Equity Share certificate(s), a copy of the Eligible Shareholder’s PAN card(s), and an executed share transfer form in favour of the Company. The transfer form, Form SH-4, may be downloaded from the websites of the Company, NSE, BSE, the Registrar, or the Manager to the Buyback.

The Tender Form, Transaction Registration Slip (TRS), and requisite documents must reach the Registrar by the Buyback Window Closing Date, specified as `[●]`, by 5 p.m. Tender Forms may be rejected if the shareholder signatures differ from the specimen signatures recorded with the Registrar or are not in the same order, even if attested.

For the Buyback tendering procedure, shareholders should follow Paragraph 23.26.

- **23.12** The Company shall accept Equity Shares validly tendered by Eligible Shareholders based on their shareholding on the Record Date and their Buyback Entitlement. Shareholders using the plain-paper option are advised to confirm their Buyback Entitlement with the Registrar before participating.
- **23.13** Eligible Shareholders intending to participate must approach their respective Stock Brokers. For physical shares, they must also provide the complete set of documents required for verification. The Stock Broker must enter the bid on the electronic platform provided by the Indian Stock Exchanges before the Buyback Window Closing Date.
- **23.14** Participation in the Buyback is at the discretion of each Eligible Shareholder. The Company does not accept responsibility for an Eligible Shareholder’s decision to participate or not participate, or for the loss of Equity Share certificates and other documents during transit.
- **23.15** Kotak Securities Limited has been appointed as the Company’s Broker to facilitate tendering through the stock exchange mechanism and to handle purchases and settlements relating to the Buyback.
- **23.16** The Company shall request BSE and NSE to provide a separate acquisition window, called the **Acquisition Window**, for placing sell orders by Eligible Shareholders. `[●]` has been appointed as the Designated Stock Exchange. The Indian Stock Exchanges will specify the details of the Acquisition Window from time to time.
- **23.17** Eligible Shareholders, through their respective Stock Brokers, are responsible for placing orders in the Acquisition Window.
- **23.18** Orders may be entered for Equity Shares held in dematerialised or physical form.
- **23.19** During the Tendering Period, Eligible Shareholders must place orders to sell their Equity Shares through their respective Stock Brokers during normal secondary-market trading hours.
- **23.20** If an Eligible Shareholder’s Stock Broker is not registered with BSE or NSE as a trading member or stock broker, the shareholder may approach another BSE/NSE-registered stock broker and register using the quick unique client code (UCC) facility, subject to applicable know-your-customer requirements. If registration through another broker is not possible, the shareholder may approach the Company’s Broker to place bids, subject to completion of its know-your-customer requirements.
- **23.21** The Buyback Regulations permit multiple bids and modification of bids. Modification, cancellation, and multiple bids are allowed only during the Tendering Period. Multiple bids by one Eligible Shareholder shall be clubbed and treated as one bid for acceptance purposes.
- **23.22** The cumulative quantity tendered shall be published on the BSE and NSE websites throughout the Tendering Period and updated at specified intervals.
- **23.23** Documents sent by Eligible Shareholders are sent at their own risk.
- **23.24** Non-Resident Shareholders and/or the Stock Brokers through whom they place orders or bids are responsible for reporting requirements under FEMA and other applicable RBI rules, regulations, and guidelines relating to remittance of funds.

## 23.25 Procedure to be followed by Eligible Shareholders holding Equity Shares in dematerialised form:

### 23.25.1

Eligible Shareholders wishing to tender Equity Shares in electronic form must do so through their respective Stock Brokers by providing details of the Equity Shares they intend to tender.

### 23.25.2

Stock Brokers must place an order or bid on behalf of Eligible Shareholders through the BSE/NSE Acquisition Window.

### 23.25.3

A lien shall be marked in the Eligible Shareholder’s demat account for the shares tendered in the Tender Offer. Depositories shall provide details of shares marked as lien to the Clearing Corporations.

### 23.25.4

If the shareholder’s demat account is held with one depository while the clearing member pool and Clearing Corporation account are held with another, the shares shall be blocked in the shareholder’s demat account at the source depository during the Tendering Period.

Inter-depository tender offer (IDT) instructions shall be initiated by the Eligible Shareholder at the source depository to the clearing member or Clearing Corporation account at the target depository. The source depository shall block the shareholder’s securities by transferring them from free balance to blocked balance and send an IDT message to the target depository to confirm creation of the lien. The target depository shall provide details of the blocked shares to the Clearing Corporation.

### 23.25.5

For custodian participant orders involving dematerialised Equity Shares, early pay-in is mandatory before the custodian confirms the order. The custodian must confirm or reject orders by the time specified by the Indian Stock Exchanges on the last day of the Tendering Period, being the Buyback Window Closing Date. Unconfirmed orders shall be deemed rejected.

For confirmed custodian participant orders, any modification by the Stock Broker revokes the custodian’s confirmation. The revised order must be sent to the custodian for confirmation again.

### 23.25.6

After placing a bid, the Stock Broker shall provide the Eligible Shareholder with a TRS generated by the exchange bidding system. The TRS contains details including the Bid ID number, Application number, DP ID, Client ID, and number of Equity Shares tendered.

If the completed Tender Form and other documents are not received but a lien has been marked on the Equity Shares and a valid bid exists in the exchange bidding system, the Eligible Shareholder’s bid shall be deemed accepted.
