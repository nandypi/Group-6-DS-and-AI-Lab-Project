## 6.13 Intention of Promoter and Promoter Group to Participate in Buyback

### 6.13.1

Under the tender offer route, the Promoter and Promoter Group had the option to participate in the Buyback. The Promoter and Promoter Group expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback through letters dated September 14, 2025, September 16, 2025, September 17, 2025, September 18, 2025, and September 19, 2025.

### 6.13.2

The date, price of acquisition, and other details of the Equity Shares held by the promoter and promoter group intending to tender their shares: Not applicable.

### 6.14

Depending on the response to the Buyback, the voting rights of the Promoters and Promoter Group, which constituted 13.05% as of the date of the Public Announcement, may change.

After completion of the Buyback, the public shareholding of the Company shall not fall below the minimum level required under Regulation 38 of the LODR Regulations. Any change in the voting rights of the Promoters and Promoter Group after conclusion of the Buyback will not result in a change in control over the Company.

## 7. AUTHORITY FOR THE BUYBACK

### 7.1

The Buyback is being undertaken in accordance with Article 14 of the Articles of Association of the Company; Sections 68, 69, 70, and other applicable provisions of the Act; the relevant rules framed under the Act, including the Share Capital Rules and the Management Rules, to the extent applicable; and the Buyback Regulations.

### 7.2

Because the Company's class of securities is registered under Section 12 of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and U.S. residents hold securities through ADSs and Equity Shares, the Company was required to obtain exemptive relief from the SEC on certain aspects of the tender offer procedures. This was due to conflicting regulatory requirements under Indian and U.S. laws for tender offer buybacks. The relief was obtained after the Board approved the Buyback.

### 7.3

The Buyback is also subject to approvals, permissions, and sanctions required under applicable laws from statutory, regulatory, or governmental authorities, including the RBI, SEBI, and the Indian Stock Exchanges.

### 7.4

At its meeting dated September 11, 2025, the Board approved the Buyback and sought shareholder approval through a special resolution under a Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025. Shareholders approved the Buyback by special resolution through the postal ballot concluded on [●].

## 8. NECESSITY / OBJECTIVE OF THE BUYBACK

### 8.1

The Company is undertaking the Buyback after considering its medium-term strategic and operational cash needs and the return of surplus funds to members in line with its capital allocation policy.

The financial parameters and internal factors considered include:

- Expected cash requirements for working capital, investments in AI and other areas, and capital expenditure in technology and infrastructure.
- Investments required to execute the Company's strategy.
- Funds required for acquisitions that the Board may approve.
- Minimum cash required for contingencies or unforeseen events.
- Other significant developments requiring cash investments.

### 8.2

The Company stated that its expected cash generation and strong balance sheet position allow it to reward members while retaining sufficient capital for business requirements. Since 2017, the Company has undertaken multiple buybacks, together with regular and special dividends, to distribute surplus cash to shareholders.

### 8.3

As of June 30, 2025, the Company had consolidated cash and investments of ₹45,204 crore. This comprised cash and cash equivalents, current and non-current investments excluding investments in equity and preference shares, and others.

### 8.4

The Company stated that the Buyback would:

- Return surplus cash to members in line with the stated Capital Allocation Policy.
- Improve return on equity through cash distribution and improve earnings per share over the long term by reducing the equity base.
- Give members as of the Record Date the option to sell their Equity Shares for cash or retain them and obtain a resultant increase in their percentage shareholding after the Buyback, without additional investment.
- Reserve for Small Shareholders the higher of:
  - The number of shares to which they are entitled based on their shareholding; or
  - 15% of the number of shares to be bought back, as required under Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations.

### 8.5

Under the stated Capital Allocation Policy, effective from financial year 2025, the Company expects to continue returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals.

Under this policy, the Company expects to progressively increase its annual Dividend Per Share, excluding any special dividend. The Company stated that the Buyback is expected to create long-term value for shareholders through a reduction in the equity base.

## 9. MAXIMUM NUMBER OF EQUITY SHARES THAT THE COMPANY PROPOSES TO BUYBACK

### 9.1

The indicative number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back at the Buyback Price was 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares. This represented approximately 2.41% of the Company's total paid-up equity share capital as of June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis.

## 10. MAXIMUM AMOUNT REQUIRED UNDER THE BUYBACK

### 10.1

The funds required for the Buyback were expected to aggregate to ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand Crore only). This represented 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves of the Company on a standalone and consolidated basis, respectively.

The amount was less than 25% of the aggregate total paid-up share capital and free reserves based on the Company's latest audited interim condensed financial statements as of June 30, 2025, on both a standalone and consolidated basis.

### 10.2

The Buyback Offer Size did not include expenses or transaction costs incurred or to be incurred for the Buyback, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory fees, intermediary fees, public announcement publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, applicable taxes such as securities transaction tax, goods and services tax, and stamp duty, as well as other incidental and related expenses.

### 10.3

The proposed Buyback was to be funded from the Company's free reserves and securities premium account, or another source permitted by the Buyback Regulations or the Act. Borrowed funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, would not be used for the Buyback.
