## 17. BRIEF INFORMATION OF THE COMPANY

## 17.1 History and Overview of the Company

- **17.1.1** Infosys provides digital services and consulting.

- **17.1.2** The Company was incorporated on July 2, 1981, in Maharashtra, India, as Infosys Consultants Private Limited under the Indian Companies Act, 1956. Its name changed to Infosys Technologies Private Limited in April 1992, Infosys Technologies Limited in June 1992 when it became a public limited company, and Infosys Limited in June 2011 following approval by the Board, shareholders, and Indian regulatory authorities.

  The Company made an initial public offering of equity shares in India in February 1993, with the shares listed on Indian stock exchanges in June 1993. It completed its initial public offering of ADSs in the United States in 1999, followed by sponsored secondary ADS offerings in 2003, 2005, and 2006. The 2005 and 2006 offerings also included public offerings without listing in Japan.

  Infosys became an original component member of *The Global Dow* in 2008. Following its voluntary delisting from the NASDAQ Global Select Market on December 11, 2012, its ADSs began trading on the NYSE on December 12, 2012, under the ticker symbol INFY. The Company also listed its ADSs on Euronext Paris and Euronext London on February 20, 2013. Infosys voluntarily delisted its ADSs from those markets on July 5, 2018, citing low average daily trading volume relative to the related administrative expenses. Infosys ADSs continue to be listed on the NYSE under the symbol INFY.
