## Board-Approved Acquisitions

At its meeting on March 25, 2026, the Infosys Board approved:

1. The acquisition of Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries, including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC.
2. The acquisition of Stratus Global LLC (Stratus), USA.

The Board meeting commenced at 8:35 p.m. IST and concluded at 9:17 p.m. IST.

## Infosys to Acquire Leading Healthcare Digital Transformation Company, Optimum Healthcare IT

Infosys announced a definitive agreement to acquire Optimum Achieve Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries, including Optimum Healthcare IT, LLC. The transaction is intended to expand Infosys’ healthcare capabilities and support AI-powered cloud and digital transformation for healthcare providers.

Optimum Healthcare IT provides healthcare provider-domain expertise and a delivery model focused on large-scale clinical and operational transformation. The acquisition is expected to expand Infosys’ presence in the healthcare provider segment by adding clients and relationships, technology capabilities, and access to new buying centers.

Optimum Healthcare IT’s capabilities include:

- Health record advisory, implementation, and support
- Enterprise Application Services
- Digital Transformation
- Workforce Management
- Optimum CareerPath®, a skill development program

The combined offerings are expected to provide healthcare providers with access to Infosys Topaz AI offerings, Infosys Cobalt cloud offerings, cloud engineering, infrastructure services, cybersecurity, application transformation, and Optimum Healthcare IT’s provider-focused capabilities.

Optimum Healthcare IT has more than 1,600 healthcare experts. It is an Elite ServiceNow partner and received the 2026 ServiceNow Partner of the Year Award. It is also a Premier AWS partner, Workday Services partner, and Microsoft Azure partner.

The transaction is expected to close during the first quarter of FY 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and completion of closing conditions. Antitrust approvals under United States competition laws and any other required regulatory approvals or waivers are required.

Investment bank Harris Williams advised Optimum Healthcare IT on the transaction.

## About Optimum Healthcare IT

Optimum Healthcare IT is a healthcare IT digital transformation and consulting firm based in Jacksonville Beach, Florida. It serves healthcare organizations through enterprise application services, digital transformation, and workforce management.

## About Infosys

Infosys provides digital services and consulting, including cloud, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, infrastructure services, application transformation, and digital transformation. The company has more than 330,000 employees and serves clients in 63 countries.
