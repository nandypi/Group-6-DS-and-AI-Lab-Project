## Management's Responsibility

Management was responsible for preparing the Statement in accordance with Section 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations, and other applicable requirements. This included computing the permissible capital payment, maintaining supporting records, establishing relevant internal controls, selecting an appropriate basis of preparation, and making reasonable estimates.

## Auditor's Responsibility:

Pursuant to the Buyback Regulations, the auditors were responsible for providing reasonable assurance that:

1. They had inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to its audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
2. The permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback, as stated in Annexure A, had been properly determined using those financial statements and in accordance with Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, Regulation 4(i), and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations.
3. The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, had formed the required opinion under Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations on reasonable grounds. Based on the Company’s state of affairs, the Board had determined that the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from the Board resolution dated September 11, 2025, or within one year immediately following the shareholders’ resolution on the proposed buyback.

The interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements had been audited by Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, which issued an unmodified audit opinion dated July 23, 2025. The audit was conducted under the Standards on Auditing specified under Section 143(10) of the Companies Act, 2013, and applicable authoritative pronouncements of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

The auditors examined the Statement in accordance with the ICAI Guidance Note on Audit Reports and Certificates for Special Purposes (Revised 2016), applicable auditing standards, and the ICAI Code of Ethics. They also complied with the applicable requirements of Standard on Quality Control (SQC) 1.

## Opinion

Based on their inquiries and examination, the auditors reported that:

1. They had inquired into the state of affairs of the Company in relation to its audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025. Those financial statements were approved by the Board of Directors on July 23, 2025.
2. The permissible capital payment for the proposed buyback, as computed in Annexure A, had been properly determined under Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, Regulation 4(i), and Regulation 5(i)(b) of the Buyback Regulations. The share capital and free reserves were extracted from the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.
3. The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, had formed the required opinion under Clause (x) of Schedule I to the Buyback Regulations on reasonable grounds and had determined that the Company would not be rendered insolvent within one year from the Board resolution dated September 11, 2025, or within one year immediately following the shareholders’ resolution on the proposed buyback.

## Restriction on Use

The report was issued at the Company’s request for use in connection with the proposed buyback, including the explanatory statement for the special resolution, public announcement, other buyback documents sent to shareholders or filed with regulatory authorities, and submissions to the Merchant Bankers. It was not intended for any other purpose or person without the auditors’ prior written consent.

## Annexure A - Statement of Permissible Capital Payment

Computation of the permissible capital payment, including premium, towards the buyback of equity shares under Sections 68(2)(b) and 68(2)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018. The computation was based on audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at and for the three months ended June 30, 2025.

| Particulars | Amount (Rs. in crore) — Standalone | Amount (Rs. in crore) — Consolidated |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-up equity capital as at June 30, 2025 (A) | 2,077 | 2,074 |
| Retained earnings* | 70,295 | 78,177 |
| Securities premium | 1,258 | 1,295 |
| General reserve | 412 | 1,465 |
| Total free reserves (B) | 71,965 | 80,937 |
| Total paid-up equity capital and free reserves (A+B) | 74,042 | 83,011 |
