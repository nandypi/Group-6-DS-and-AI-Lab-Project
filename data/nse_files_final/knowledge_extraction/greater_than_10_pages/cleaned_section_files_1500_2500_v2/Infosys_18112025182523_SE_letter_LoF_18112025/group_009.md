## RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Board hereby confirms that:

1. All the Equity Shares proposed for buyback are fully paid-up.
2. Subject to applicable law, the Company shall not issue any shares or other specified securities, including by way of bonus issue, until the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations through stock option schemes or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares. Relevant details and potential impacts of such obligations shall be disclosed in the public announcement.
3. Subject to applicable law, the Company shall not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations.
4. In accordance with Section 68(8) of the Act, the Company shall not make any further issue of the same kind of shares or other securities, including allotment of new shares under Section 62(1)(a) of the Act, within six months after completion of the Buyback, except by way of bonus shares or equity shares issued to discharge subsisting obligations such as conversion of warrants, stock option schemes, sweat equity, or conversion of preference shares or debentures into Equity Shares.
5. The Company shall not buy back locked-in Equity Shares or non-transferable Equity Shares until the lock-in ends or the Equity Shares become transferable.
6. The Company shall not buy back its Equity Shares through negotiated deals, whether on or off the stock exchanges, spot transactions, or private arrangements.
7. There are no subsisting defaults in repayment of deposits or interest, redemption of debentures or interest, redemption of preference shares, payment of dividends, or repayment of term loans or related interest payable to any financial institution or banking company.
8. The Company has complied with Sections 92, 123, 127, and 129 of the Act.
9. Funds borrowed from banks and financial institutions will not be used for the Buyback.
10. The aggregate Buyback amount of ₹18,000 crore (Rupees Eighteen Thousand crore only) does not exceed 25% of the total paid-up capital and free reserves of the Company, based on the audited interim condensed standalone and consolidated financial statements as at June 30, 2025.
11. The proposed buyback of 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares (Ten crore) does not exceed 25% of the total number of Equity Shares in the paid-up equity share capital as at June 30, 2025.
12. Subject to applicable laws, the Company shall not make another buyback offer within one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period.
13. The Buyback does not contravene Regulation 4(vii) of the Buyback Regulations because the Company has not made a buyback offer within one year from the expiry of the buyback period of the preceding offer.
14. As at the relevant date, no scheme of amalgamation, compromise, or arrangement under the Act is pending.
15. The Company shall earmark and arrange adequate sources of funds for the Buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.
16. The Company shall not directly or indirectly purchase its own Equity Shares or other specified securities:
   - Through any subsidiary company, including its own subsidiary companies; or
   - Through any investment company or group of investment companies.
17. Consideration for Equity Shares bought back by the Company shall be paid only in cash.
18. The Buyback will not result in delisting of the Equity Shares from BSE Limited or National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
19. As at the relevant date, the ratio of the aggregate secured and unsecured debt owed by the Company is not, and shall not be, more than twice the paid-up share capital and free reserves, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone or consolidated financial statements as at June 30, 2025.
20. The Buyback offer shall not be withdrawn once the public announcement is made.
21. The Company shall transfer from its free reserves, securities premium account, or other sources permitted by law an amount equal to the nominal value of the Equity Shares purchased through the Buyback to the capital redemption reserve account. Details of this transfer shall be disclosed in subsequent audited financial statements.
22. The Company shall reduce its share capital after the Buyback. Equity Shares bought back shall be extinguished and physically destroyed, if applicable, in the manner and within the timelines prescribed under the Buyback Regulations and the Act.
23. The Company has no outstanding debt from lenders and therefore is not required to obtain prior lender consent for breach of any covenant with such lenders.

In accordance with the Buyback Regulations, if the Company fails to fulfil its obligations, the escrow account shall be forfeited in full or in part and distributed pro rata among security-holders who accepted the offer. Any balance shall be used for investor protection in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.

## 5. DETAILS OF THE PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT

5.1 In accordance with Regulation 7(i) of the Buyback Regulations, the Company made the Public Announcement dated November 7, 2025, which was published on November 10, 2025.

5.2 The Public Announcement was issued within two Working Days of the declaration of the postal ballot results on November 6, 2025, approving the Buyback by special resolution.

5.3 The Company may publish further notices or corrigenda in the newspapers listed below.

## 6. DETAILS OF THE BUYBACK

6.1 The Buyback was authorized by a Board resolution dated September 11, 2025. The Board sought shareholder approval through a special resolution and postal ballot notice dated September 25, 2025. The postal ballot results were announced on November 6, 2025, and the shareholders approved the Buyback by special resolution.
