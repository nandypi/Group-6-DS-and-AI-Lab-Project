## INSTRUCTIONS

1. The buyback will open on November 20, 2025, and close on November 26, 2025.

2. This Tender Form must be read along with the Letter of Offer and is subject to its terms and conditions.

3. Eligible shareholders wishing to tender equity shares must deliver the following documents to the Registrar, as mentioned in the Letter of Offer, by 5:00 PM IST on November 26, 2025:

   - Duly signed Tender Form. For jointly held shares, all equity shareholders must sign in the same order in which they hold the equity shares.
   - Original share certificates.
   - Valid share transfer form(s) (SH-4), duly filled and signed by all registered shareholders in the same order and according to the specimen signatures registered with the Company or Registrar, and witnessed at the appropriate place authorising transfer in favour of the Company.
   - Self-attested PAN Card copies of all eligible shareholders where the equity shares are jointly held.

4. Eligible shareholders must provide all other relevant documents, including:

   - A duly attested Power of Attorney registered with the Registrar if someone other than the eligible shareholder has signed the Tender Form.
   - Necessary corporate authorisations, such as board resolutions, in the case of companies.
   - If the shareholder’s address has changed from the address recorded in the Company’s Register of Members, a self-attested address proof consisting of any one of the following:
     - Valid Aadhaar Card
     - Voter Identity Card
     - Passport

5. Non-resident shareholders must, where applicable, enclose permission received from the RBI to acquire the equity shares held in the Company.

   If the equity shares are held on a repatriation basis, the non-resident shareholder must obtain and enclose a letter from its authorised dealer or bank confirming that payment for acquiring the equity shares was made from the appropriate account specified by the RBI in its approval.

   If the non-resident shareholder cannot provide this certificate, the shares will be deemed to have been acquired on a non-repatriation basis. The shareholder must then submit a consent letter addressed to the Company allowing payment on a non-repatriation basis for valid shares accepted under the buyback.

   If applicable documents are not enclosed with the Tender Form, the equity shares tendered may be rejected.

6. If a registered entity has merged with another entity and the merger has been approved and become effective, but the successor company has not yet been registered as the shareholder, the entity must file the following documents with the Tender Form:

   - Approval from the appropriate authority for the merger
   - Scheme of merger
   - Requisite form filed with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs intimating the merger

7. For the Clearing Corporation to make a payout exceeding Rs. 50,00,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Crores only), the eligible shareholder must provide a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) number.

   An eligible shareholder tendering shares valued at more than Rs. 50,00,00,000 must provide the LEI number and supporting documents, such as the LEI registration certificate, to the Registrar by email at `infosys.buyback2025@kfintech.com` by 5:00 PM IST on the buyback window closing date.

8. If an eligible shareholder does not receive the Letter of Offer, the shareholder may participate by submitting a written application on plain paper, signed by the eligible shareholder or all eligible shareholders in the case of jointly held shares. The application must state:

   - Name and address of the eligible shareholder(s)
   - Number of equity shares held on the Record Date
   - Client ID number
   - DP name/ID
   - Beneficiary account number
   - Number of equity shares tendered for the buyback

9. Eligible shareholders may tender equity shares wholly or partly up to their entitlement, or in excess of their entitlement, but not exceeding their holding on the Record Date.

10. Documents sent by the stock broker, custodian, or eligible shareholder will be sent at their own risk. Stock brokers, custodians, and eligible shareholders should adequately safeguard their interests in this regard.

11. For the procedure to be followed when tendering shares in the buyback, refer to Section 23 of the Letter of Offer.

12. All required documents must be enclosed with a valid Tender Form. Otherwise, the equity shares tendered may be rejected. Grounds for rejection include:

   - Share certificates of a company other than Infosys Limited are enclosed.
   - Transmission of the equity shares is incomplete and the shares are not in the name of the eligible shareholder.
   - The Registrar does not receive the physical equity share certificate.
   - Signatures on the Tender Form and Form SH-4 do not match the specimen signature recorded with the Company or Registrar.
   - The shareholder was not an eligible shareholder of the Company on the Record Date.
   - There is a name mismatch on the share certificate.
   - Self-attested PAN Cards of the shareholder and all joint holders are not submitted.
   - The eligible shareholder has submitted a duplicate bid or order.
   - A court or other competent authority has issued a restraint order relating to transfer, disposal, or sale; loss of share certificates has been notified to the Company; title to the equity shares is disputed or unclear; or another restraint subsists.
   - Required documents are not received by the Registrar by 5:00 PM IST on November 26, 2025.

13. The Company will not accept equity shares offered in the buyback that are subject to a court restraint order relating to transfer or sale.

14. By agreeing to participate in the buyback, each eligible shareholder, including each non-resident shareholder, undertakes to complete all regulatory and statutory filings and compliances required under applicable law.

   Each eligible shareholder authorises the Company to make, sign, execute, deliver, acknowledge, and perform applications for regulatory reporting, including Form FC-TRS if required, and undertakes to assist the Company with such regulatory reporting where requested.

15. Non-resident shareholders must obtain and submit all necessary approvals, to the extent required, from the relevant authorities, including RBI approvals under the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, as amended, and the rules and regulations made under it.

   Non-resident shareholders must also comply with applicable FEMA reporting requirements and other rules, regulations, and guidelines relating to remittance of funds outside India.
