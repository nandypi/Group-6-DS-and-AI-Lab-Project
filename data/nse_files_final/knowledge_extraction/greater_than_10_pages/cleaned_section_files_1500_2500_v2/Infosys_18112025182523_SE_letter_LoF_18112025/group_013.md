## 11. MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF THE LIKELY IMPACT OF THE BUYBACK ON THE COMPANY

- The Company does not expect the Buyback to materially affect profitability or earnings, except through reduced investment income from funds deployed in the Buyback. Assuming full acceptance, the Buyback would use up to ₹18,000 crore, excluding Transaction Costs.
- The Promoter and Promoter Group have expressed their intention not to participate in the Buyback in letters dated September 14, 2025, September 16, 2025, September 17, 2025, September 18, 2025, and September 19, 2025. Assuming full acceptance from all other Eligible Shareholders up to their Buyback Entitlement, Promoter and Promoter Group shareholding would increase from 13.05% of pre-Buyback paid-up equity share capital to 13.37% of post-Buyback paid-up equity share capital. Public shareholding would change from 86.95% to 86.63%.
- The Buyback is not expected to result in a change in control or affect the existing management structure.
- Shareholdings of Non-Resident Shareholders, Indian financial institutions, banks, mutual funds, the public, and other bodies corporate will change based on the number of Equity Shares bought back from them. FIIs/FPIs are advised to ensure that their investment remains within applicable legal limits after completion of the Buyback.
- The Company does not expect the Buyback to affect its growth opportunities.
- The Buyback is expected to improve return on equity through cash distribution and improve earnings per share through a reduction in the equity base over the long term.
- As of the date of the Letter of Offer, the Company has no outstanding debt from lenders. The ratio of aggregate secured and unsecured debt to paid-up share capital and free reserves is not and is not expected to exceed 2:1, based on the lower of the audited interim condensed standalone or consolidated financial statements as of June 30, 2025.
- The Company will not raise further capital for one year from the expiry of the Buyback Period, except to discharge subsisting obligations.
- Subject to applicable law, the Company will not issue Equity Shares or other specified securities, including by way of bonus issue, until expiry of the Buyback Period, except for conversion of stock option schemes into Equity Shares to discharge subsisting obligations. As of the date of the Public Announcement, 5,24,185 RSUs were vested and could be converted into Equity Shares during the Buyback Period.
- During the last three years, there were no defaults in repayment of deposits or related interest, redemption of debentures or related interest, payment of dividends, or repayment of term loans or related interest to financial institutions or banking companies.
- The Buyback is not being undertaken to delist the Company’s shares from Indian stock exchanges.

## 12. BUYBACK PRICE AND BASIS OF DETERMINING THE PRICE OF THE BUYBACK

- The Equity Shares are proposed to be bought back at ₹1,800 per Equity Share (“Buyback Price”).
- The Buyback Price was determined after considering volume-weighted average market prices on BSE and NSE, the price-earnings ratio, the impact on other financial parameters, and the potential impact on earnings per Equity Share.
- Under Regulation 5(via) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board/Buyback Committee may increase the Buyback Price and decrease the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, provided that the Buyback Offer Size does not change, until one working day before the Record Date.

The Buyback Price represents:

- A premium of 15.60% over the BSE volume-weighted average market price and 16.99% over the NSE volume-weighted average market price during the three-month period ending September 8, 2025.
- A premium of 20.78% over the BSE volume-weighted average market price and 20.67% over the NSE volume-weighted average market price during the two-week period ending September 8, 2025.
- A premium of 25.64% over the BSE closing price and 25.62% over the NSE closing price as of September 8, 2025.

## 13. SOURCES OF FUNDS FOR THE BUYBACK

- Assuming full acceptance, the Company would use ₹18,000 crore for the Buyback, excluding Transaction Costs.
- The Buyback will be funded from the Company’s free reserves, including securities premium, or another source permitted under the Buyback Regulations or the Act.
- The Buyback is not expected to materially affect earnings, except through the temporary reduction in treasury income that could otherwise have been earned on the funds deployed until distribution to shareholders under the Capital Allocation Policy.
- The Company will transfer from free reserves an amount equal to the nominal value of the Equity Shares bought back to the Capital Redemption Reserve Account. Details of the transfer will be disclosed in subsequent audited financial statements.
- Borrowed funds from banks and financial institutions, if any, will not be used for the Buyback.
