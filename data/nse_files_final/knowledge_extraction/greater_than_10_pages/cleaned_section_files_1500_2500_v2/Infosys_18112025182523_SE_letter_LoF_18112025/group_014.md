## 14. DETAILS OF THE ESCROW ACCOUNT AND THE AMOUNT TO BE DEPOSITED THEREIN

- 14.1 In accordance with Regulation 9(xi) of the Buyback Regulations, an Escrow Agreement was entered into among the Company, the Manager, and the Escrow Agent.
- 14.2 In accordance with the Buyback Regulations and pursuant to the Escrow Agreement, the Company opened an Escrow Account in the name and style “Infosys Ltd Buyback 2025 - Escrow Account,” bearing account number 6052691234 with the Escrow Agent, whose registered office is in Mumbai.
- 14.3 In accordance with Regulation 9(xi) of the Buyback Regulations, the Company deposited ₹1,815 crore in the Escrow Account. This represented 25% of the amount up to ₹100 crore and 10% of the amount thereafter, based on the Buyback Offer Size. Under the Escrow Agreement, the Manager to the Buyback was empowered to operate the Escrow Account.
- 14.4 In accordance with Regulation 10 of the Buyback Regulations, immediately after closure of the Tendering Period, the Company shall deposit the consideration payable to Eligible Shareholders in a special escrow account with the Escrow Agent. This consideration will comprise up to 90% of the amount lying in the Escrow Account and the balance amount payable to Eligible Shareholders, constituting the entire consideration payable for the Buyback under the Buyback Regulations.

## 15. FIRM FINANCING ARRANGEMENTS

- 15.1 The Company identified and earmarked funds to fulfil its obligations under the Buyback. These earmarked funds, together with funds provided for escrow arrangements, exceeded the Buyback Offer Size.
- 15.2 Based on the resolution of the Buyback Committee dated November 7, 2025, and other relevant facts and documents, Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Statutory Auditors of the Company, certified by a letter dated November 7, 2025, that the Company had made firm financing arrangements to fulfil its obligations under the Buyback.
- 15.3 Based on this certificate, the Manager to the Buyback confirmed that firm arrangements were in place and that the Company had the ability to implement the Buyback in accordance with the Buyback Regulations.
