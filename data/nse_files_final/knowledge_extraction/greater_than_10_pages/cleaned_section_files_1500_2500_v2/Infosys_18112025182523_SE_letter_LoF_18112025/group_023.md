## Record Date and Ratio of Buyback as per the Buyback Entitlement in each Category

- **22.6** The Company fixed November 14, 2025, as the Record Date for determining the Buyback Entitlement and the names of Equity Shareholders eligible to participate in the Buyback.
- **22.7** The Equity Shares to be bought back were divided into two categories:
  - **22.7.1** Reserved category for Small Shareholders (“Reserved Category”).
  - **22.7.2** General category for all other Eligible Shareholders (“General Category”).
- **22.8** A “Small Shareholder” was an Eligible Shareholder holding Equity Shares with a market value of not more than ₹2,00,000, based on the closing price on the BSE or NSE having the highest trading volume on the Record Date. The closing price on NSE, the stock exchange with the highest trading volume, was ₹1,502.80 per Equity Share. Accordingly, Eligible Shareholders holding not more than 133 Equity Shares on the Record Date were classified as Small Shareholders.
- **22.9** There were 25,85,684 Small Shareholders holding an aggregate of 8,25,43,948 Equity Shares on the Record Date. This represented 1.99% of the Company’s outstanding Equity Shares and 82.54% of the maximum number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back.
- **22.10** Under Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, the reservation for Small Shareholders was the higher of:
  - **22.10.1** Fifteen percent of the 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, equal to 1,50,00,000 Equity Shares.
  - **22.10.2** The number of Equity Shares entitled based on Small Shareholders’ shareholding on the Record Date: `(8,25,43,948 / 361,23,72,100) × 10,00,00,000`, equal to 22,85,034 Equity Shares.
- **22.11** The Promoters and Promoter Group had declared that they would not participate in the Buyback. Their Equity Shares were therefore excluded when computing the entitlement ratio.
- **22.12** Based on this analysis and Regulation 6 of the Buyback Regulations, 1,50,00,000 Equity Shares were reserved for Small Shareholders (“Reserved Portion”). The General Category for all other Eligible Shareholders therefore consisted of 8,50,00,000 Equity Shares (“General Portion”).
- **22.13** The indicative Buyback Entitlement ratios were:

| Category of Eligible Shareholder | Ratio of Buyback (i.e. Buyback Entitlement) |
|---|---|
| Reserved category for Small Shareholders | 2 Equity Shares for every 11 Equity Shares held on the Record Date |
| General category for all other Eligible Shareholders | 17 Equity Shares for every 706 Equity Shares held on the Record Date |

The ratios were approximate and indicative. Computations using these ratios could differ slightly from the actual entitlement due to rounding. The actual Buyback Entitlement factor was 18.17213782892960% for Small Shareholders in the Reserved Category and 2.40804924035293% for other shareholders in the General Category. Entitlements calculated using the actual factors could also differ from the entitlement printed in the Tender Form because of rounding.

## Fractional Entitlements

- **22.14** If the Buyback Entitlement calculated by applying the above ratios to the Equity Shares held on the Record Date was not a whole number, the fractional entitlement was ignored for calculating the Equity Shares eligible to be tendered in the Buyback.
- **22.15** Because fractional entitlements were ignored, Small Shareholders holding five or fewer Equity Shares on the Record Date received a Tender Form with zero entitlement. They could tender Additional Equity Shares and would receive preference for acceptance of one Equity Share if they tendered Additional Equity Shares. The Company would make best efforts, subject to the Buyback Regulations, to accept such tendered Equity Shares to the extent possible and permissible.

## Basis of Acceptance of Equity Shares validly tendered in the Reserved Category for Small Shareholders

- **22.16** Subject to the Letter of Offer, the Company would accept Equity Shares tendered by Small Shareholders in the Reserved Category in the following order:
  - **22.16.1** Accept 100% of the Equity Shares validly tendered, up to the shareholder’s Buyback Entitlement or the number of Equity Shares tendered, whichever was lower.
  - **22.16.2** If Equity Shares remained available in the Reserved Category, give preference to Small Shareholders with zero entitlement because of fractional entitlement who had tendered Additional Equity Shares. One Equity Share from the Additional Equity Shares tendered by each such shareholder would be accepted.
  - **22.16.3** If Equity Shares still remained available, accept Additional Equity Shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement in proportion to the Additional Equity Shares tendered, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations. For this calculation, the Additional Equity Shares of a Small Shareholder from whom one Equity Share was accepted under paragraph 22.16.2 would be reduced by one.

## Adjustment for fractional results in case of proportionate Acceptance, as described above

- **22.17** If the proportionate calculation for Additional Equity Shares to be accepted resulted in a fraction of 0.50 or more, the fraction would be rounded up to the next whole number.
- **22.18** If the fraction was less than 0.50, it would be ignored.

## Basis of Acceptance of Equity Shares validly tendered in the General Category

- **22.19** Subject to the Letter of Offer, the Company would accept Equity Shares tendered by other Eligible Shareholders in the General Category in the following order:
  - **22.19.1** Accept 100% of the Equity Shares validly tendered, up to the shareholder’s Buyback Entitlement or the number of Equity Shares tendered, whichever was lower.
  - **22.19.2** If Equity Shares remained available in the General Category, accept Additional Equity Shares tendered above the Buyback Entitlement in proportion to the Additional Equity Shares tendered, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations. Acceptance per shareholder would equal the shareholder’s validly tendered Additional Equity Shares divided by the total validly tendered Additional Equity Shares in the General Category, multiplied by the total pending number of Equity Shares to be accepted in the General Category.

## Adjustment for fractional results in case of proportionate acceptance as described above

- **22.20** If the proportionate calculation for Additional Equity Shares to be accepted resulted in a fraction of 0.50 or more, the fraction would be rounded up to the next whole number. If the fraction was less than 0.50, it would be ignored.

## Basis of Acceptance of Equity Shares between categories

- **22.21** If Equity Shares remained available for buyback in one category (“Partially Filled Category”) after applying the acceptance methodology for both categories, and validly tendered Equity Shares remained unaccepted in the other category (“Over Tendered Category”), Additional Equity Shares from the Over Tendered Category would be accepted proportionately. Acceptance per Eligible Shareholder would equal the shareholder’s additional outstanding Equity Shares validly tendered in the Over Tendered Category divided by the total additional outstanding Equity Shares validly tendered in that category, multiplied by the total pending number of Equity Shares to be bought back in the Partially Filled Category.
- **22.22** If the Partially Filled Category was the General Category and the Over Tendered Category was the Reserved Category, the Additional Equity Shares tendered by a Small Shareholder from whom one Equity Share was accepted under paragraph 22.21 would be reduced by one Equity Share for this calculation.
- **22.23** For proportionate acceptance under paragraph 22.21:
  - **22.23.1** If the fractional Acceptance was 0.50 or more, the fraction would be rounded up to the next whole number.
  - **22.23.2** If the fractional Acceptance was less than 0.50, the fraction would be ignored.
