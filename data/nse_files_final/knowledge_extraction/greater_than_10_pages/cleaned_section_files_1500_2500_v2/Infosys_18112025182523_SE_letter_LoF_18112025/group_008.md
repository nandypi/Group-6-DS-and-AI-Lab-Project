The Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief Legal Officer and Chief Compliance Officer, and Company Secretary were authorized to represent the Company before the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, SEBI, the SEC, the stock exchanges on which the Company’s equity shares are listed, the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), and other agencies connected with the Buyback. They were also authorized to sign and submit forms, letters, documents, and other papers required for implementation of the Buyback.

These officers were authorized severally to sign and file forms, pay fees, and undertake other acts and deeds necessary or incidental to implementation of the Buyback.

The Buyback Committee was authorized to implement the Buyback, including appointing merchant bankers, brokers, lawyers, depository participants, escrow agents, bankers, advisors, registrars, scrutinizers, consultants, representatives, intermediaries, agencies, printers, and advertising agencies. Its authority also covered incidental documentation, applications for regulatory approvals, preparation and issuance of buyback documents, opening of accounts, public announcements, extinguishment of share certificates, and filing the Certificate of Extinguishment and other undertakings, agreements, papers, documents, and correspondence with:

- SEBI;
- the Reserve Bank of India;
- the Government of India;
- the SEC;
- BSE Limited (BSE);
- National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE);
- the NYSE;
- the Registrar of Companies;
- depositories; and
- other relevant authorities.

Subject to applicable law and approval of the Buyback by the members, the Buyback Committee could alter or modify the Buyback terms and conditions, delegate or revoke delegated authority, issue directions, and settle questions, difficulties, or doubts relating to the Buyback without further Board approval.

The quorum for a meeting of the Buyback Committee was any two members. The Committee could regulate its proceedings, meet as often as required, or act through circular resolutions. It was required to report the status and progress of Buyback-related actions periodically to the Board or its committee.

Nothing in the resolutions created a right for any member to offer shares or an obligation for the Company or the Board to buy back shares. The resolutions also did not impair the Company’s or the Board’s power to terminate any Buyback process where permitted by law.

## Board Solvency Confirmation

The Board confirmed that it had made a full inquiry into the Company’s affairs and prospects and formed the following opinions:

1. Immediately following the Board meeting held on September 11, 2025 and the date of the members’ resolution approving the Buyback, there would be no grounds on which the Company could be found unable to pay its debts.

2. Having regard to the Company’s prospects for the year following the September 11, 2025 Board meeting and the year following the members’ resolution, the Board’s intended management of the business, and the financial resources expected to be available, the Company would be able to meet its liabilities as they fell due and would not become insolvent within one year from either date.

3. In forming these opinions, the Board considered the Company’s liabilities, including prospective and contingent liabilities, as if the Company were being wound up under the Companies Act and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as amended.
