# Buyback

| Particulars | Details |
|---|---|
| Number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back pursuant to the Buyback as a percentage of the existing paid-up equity share capital of the Company | Buyback of up to 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares, representing up to 2.41% of the total number of Equity Shares in the existing total paid-up Equity Share capital of the Company on a standalone basis. |
| Buyback Offer Price | ₹1,800 per Equity Share. |
| Applicable regulations of SEBI and provisions of the Act, in accordance with which the Buyback is made | The Buyback is being undertaken in terms of Chapter III of the Buyback Regulations through the tender offer route and in accordance with other applicable provisions of the Buyback Regulations, SEBI Circulars, LODR Regulations, and Sections 68, 69, 70 and other applicable provisions, if any, of the Act and relevant rules framed thereunder, including the Share Capital Rules and the Management Rules, to the extent applicable. |
| Methodology for the Buyback | Equity Shares shall be bought back on a proportionate basis from all Eligible Shareholders, including holders of ADSs who cancel their ADSs and withdraw the underlying Equity Shares before the Record Date so that they become equity shareholders of the Company as of the Record Date. The Buyback will be conducted through the Tender Offer route, facilitated through the stock exchange mechanism specified by SEBI. The Company will request BSE and NSE to provide the acquisition window for tendering Equity Shares. |
| Buyback Offer Size and its percentage with respect to the total paid-up equity share capital and free reserves and percentage with respect to net worth of the Company | Aggregate consideration of up to ₹18,000 crore, representing up to 24.31% and 21.68% of the aggregate fully paid-up Equity Share capital and free reserves based on the latest audited interim condensed financial statements as at June 30, 2025, on a standalone basis and consolidated basis, respectively. This is within the prescribed limit of 25% under the Act and Regulation 4(i) of the Buyback Regulations. The Buyback Offer Size excludes expenses and transaction costs, including brokerage, filing fees, advisory and intermediary fees, public announcement and publication expenses, printing and dispatch expenses, applicable taxes, stamp duty, and other incidental and related expenses. |
| Other Information | Under applicable Indian laws, holders of ADSs will not be eligible to tender ADSs in the Buyback. To participate, ADS holders must withdraw the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs before the Record Date and become holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date. The Postal Ballot Notice dated September 25, 2025 stated that ADS holders had approximately 49 calendar days from the date of the notice to take the required steps. Additional details are provided in paragraphs 22.26–22.30 of this Letter of Offer, “Participation by ADS Holders.” |

- 6.2 The Equity Shares of the Company are listed on NSE and BSE. The ADSs of the Company are listed on the New York Stock Exchange.
- 6.3 The Buyback from Eligible Shareholders who are Non-Resident Shareholders is subject to approvals, if any, required from concerned authorities, including approvals from the RBI under FEMA. Such approvals are required to be obtained by the relevant Non-Resident Shareholders.
- 6.4 The Buyback includes a reservation for Small Shareholders equal to 15% of the number of Equity Shares proposed to be bought back, or their entitlement, whichever is higher.
