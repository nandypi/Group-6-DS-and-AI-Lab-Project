## 28. DETAILS OF THE COMPLIANCE OFFICER

The Compliance Officer is Mr. A.G.S. Manikantha, Company Secretary and Compliance Officer. Eligible Shareholders may contact the Compliance Officer for clarification or to address grievances between 10:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. on working days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

## 29. DETAILS OF THE REMEDIES AVAILABLE TO THE ELIGIBLE SHAREHOLDERS

### 29.1

For grievances relating to the Buyback, including non-receipt of Buyback consideration, Equity Share certificates, or demat credit, an Eligible Shareholder may approach the Compliance Officer, the Manager to the Buyback, or the Registrar to the Buyback.

### 29.2

If the Company defaults in complying with the requirements of Sections 68, 69, or 70 of the Companies Act, 2013, or applicable rules made under those sections, the Company or any officer in default may be subject to imprisonment, a fine, or both, in accordance with the Act.

### 29.3

The concerned Registrar of Companies is the Registrar of Companies, Karnataka at Bengaluru.

## 30. DETAILS OF INVESTOR SERVICE CENTRE

KFin Technologies Limited is appointed as the Investor Service Centre and Registrar to the Buyback. Eligible Shareholders may contact the Investor Service Centre for buyback-related queries on working days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays, between 10:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.

## 31. DETAILS OF THE MANAGER TO THE BUYBACK

Kotak Mahindra Capital Company Limited is the Manager to the Buyback.

## 32. DECLARATION BY THE DIRECTORS REGARDING AUTHENTICITY OF THE INFORMATION IN THE OFFER DOCUMENT

As per Regulation 24(i)(a) of the Buyback Regulations, the Board of Directors accepts full responsibility for the information contained in the Letter of Offer and confirms that it contains true, factual, and material information and does not contain misleading information.

The Letter of Offer is issued under the authority of the Board and pursuant to the resolution passed by the Buyback Committee on November 18, 2025.

## ANNEXURE I

## SUMMARY TERM SHEET

The Company is offering to buy back 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares of face value ₹5 each at ₹1,800 per Equity Share through the Tender Offer route, in accordance with the Buyback Regulations and applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013. This summary highlights selected material information; shareholders are directed to the full Letter of Offer for complete details.

### Who is offering to purchase the Equity Shares?

The Company is offering to purchase 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares. See Sections 6 and 9.

### What will the purchase price for Equity Shares be and what will be the form of payment?

The Company will pay ₹1,800 per Equity Share purchased in the Buyback. Payment will be made in cash promptly after the expiration of the Offer and no later than 5 working days from the closure of the Offer Period. See Sections 1 and 23.

### What is the recent market price of the Equity Shares?

On November 10, 2025, the date of publication of the Public Announcement, the closing price was ₹1,514.60 on the BSE and ₹1,513.50 on the NSE.

On September 8, 2025, the date of intimation to the Indian Stock Exchanges of the Board Meeting to consider the Buyback, the closing price was ₹1,432.65 on the BSE and ₹1,432.90 on the NSE. See Sections 6 and 12.

### Has the Company or its Board of Directors adopted a position on the Buyback?

The Board of Directors approved the Buyback. The Company, its Board of Directors, and the Managers to the Buyback do not make any recommendation to shareholders regarding whether to tender their Equity Shares.

### How many Equity Shares will the Company purchase in the Buyback?

The Company will purchase up to 10,00,00,000 (Ten crore) Equity Shares, representing 2.41% of its total paid-up equity share capital.

The shares will be purchased from Eligible Shareholders on a proportionate basis, subject to the reservation for Small Shareholders. If more than 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares are tendered, the Company will first purchase shares tendered by Small Shareholders on a proportionate basis in the Reserved Category, followed by purchases from other Shareholders on a proportionate basis in the General Category. Any additional Equity Shares will be accepted proportionately as described in Section 23.

### What happens if more than 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares are tendered?

The Company will not accept more than 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares. Excess tenders will be dealt with through the proportionate acceptance process for the Reserved Category and General Category described in Section 23.

### How will the Company pay for the Equity Shares?

If the maximum 10,00,00,000 Equity Shares are tendered at ₹1,800 per Equity Share, the aggregate consideration will be ₹18,000 crore.

The Company expects to fund the Buyback from cash on hand generated through internal accruals and/or by liquidating financial instruments held by the Company.

### When can an Eligible Shareholder tender his Equity Shares?

Eligible Shareholders may tender their Equity Shares during the Tendering Period from November 20, 2025 to November 26, 2025.

### How long does an Eligible Shareholder have to tender his Equity Shares; can the Offer be extended, amended or terminated?

Eligible Shareholders may tender their Equity Shares from November 20, 2025 to November 26, 2025. The Offer Period cannot be extended, amended, or terminated.

### How will an Eligible Shareholder be notified if the Company extends the Offer Period or amends the terms of the Buyback?

The Offer Period cannot be extended, and the Company will not amend the terms of the Buyback after the date of the Letter of Offer.

### How can an ADS holder participate in the Buyback?

ADS holders needed to withdraw the Equity Shares underlying their ADSs before the Record Date of November 14, 2025 and become holders of Equity Shares on the Record Date.

The Postal Ballot Notice stated that ADS holders had approximately 49 calendar days from the date of that notice to complete the steps required to hold Equity Shares on the Record Date. Further details are provided in paragraph 22.26–22.30, “Participation by ADS Holders,” of the Letter of Offer.
