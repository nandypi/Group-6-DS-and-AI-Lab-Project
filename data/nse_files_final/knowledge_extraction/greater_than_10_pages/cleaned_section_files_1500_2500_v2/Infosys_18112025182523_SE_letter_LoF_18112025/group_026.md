## 23.11.1 In case the Eligible Shareholder holds Equity Shares in dematerialised form :

Eligible Shareholders who received the Letter of Offer electronically may request a physical copy from the Company or Registrar by providing their name, address, number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date, client ID number, DP name/ID, and beneficiary account number.

Eligible Shareholders may participate in the Buyback by downloading the Tender Form from the Company, NSE, BSE, Registrar, or Manager websites, or by submitting a signed plain-paper application. The application must state the Eligible Shareholder’s name and address, number of Equity Shares held on the Record Date, client ID number, DP name and ID, beneficiary account number, and number of Equity Shares tendered.

The tendering procedure is specified in Paragraph 23.25.

## 23.11.2 In case the Eligible Shareholder holds Equity Shares in physical form :

Eligible Shareholders may participate in the Buyback by downloading the Tender Form or by submitting a signed plain-paper application. The application must state the Eligible Shareholder’s name and address, folio number, number of Equity Shares held, Equity Share certificate number, number of Equity Shares tendered, and the distinctive numbers of those shares.

The application must include the original Equity Share certificate(s), a copy of the Eligible Shareholder’s PAN card(s), and an executed share transfer form in favour of the Company. The transfer form is Form SH-4.

The Tender Form, TRS, and required documents must reach the Registrar to the Buyback by the Buyback Window Closing Date, November 26, 2025, by 5:00 p.m. Applications may be rejected if the signatures differ from the specimen signatures recorded with the Registrar or are not in the same order, even if attested.

The tendering procedure is specified in Paragraph 23.26.

### 23.12

The Company shall accept Equity Shares validly tendered by Eligible Shareholders based on their shareholding on the Record Date and their Buyback Entitlement. Shareholders using the plain-paper option are advised to confirm their Buyback Entitlement with the Registrar before participating.

### 23.13

Eligible Shareholders participating in the Buyback must approach their respective Stock Broker(s). For physical shares, they must provide the complete set of documents required for verification. The Stock Broker must enter the bid on the electronic platform provided by the Indian Stock Exchanges before the Buyback Window Closing Date; otherwise, the bid may be rejected.

### 23.14

Acceptance of the Buyback is at the discretion of each Eligible Shareholder. The Company is not responsible for an Eligible Shareholder’s decision to participate or not participate, or for loss of Equity Share certificates and other documents during transit.

### 23.15

Infosys appointed Kotak Securities Limited as the registered broker to facilitate the tendering of Equity Shares through the stock exchange mechanism and to handle purchases and settlements for the Buyback.

Kotak Securities Limited’s SEBI registration number is **INZ000200137**.

### 23.16

The Company shall request BSE and NSE to provide a separate acquisition window, called the **Acquisition Window**, for Eligible Shareholders wishing to tender their Equity Shares. BSE was appointed as the **Designated Stock Exchange**. The Indian Stock Exchanges will specify the details of the Acquisition Window from time to time.

### 23.17

Eligible Shareholders must place orders in the Acquisition Window through their respective Stock Broker(s).

### 23.18

Eligible Shareholders may enter orders for Equity Shares held in dematerialised or physical form.

### 23.19

During the Tendering Period, Eligible Shareholders must place orders to sell their Equity Shares through their respective Stock Broker(s) during normal secondary-market trading hours.

### 23.20

If an Eligible Shareholder’s Stock Broker is not registered with BSE or NSE as a trading member or stock broker, the shareholder may approach a BSE- or NSE-registered stock broker and register using the quick unique client code (UCC) facility, subject to providing the details required under applicable law.

If the shareholder cannot register through the quick UCC facility, they may approach the Company’s Broker to place bids, subject to completion of the Company’s Broker’s Know Your Customer requirements.

### 23.21

The Buyback Regulations permit Eligible Shareholders to place multiple bids and modify bids. Modification, cancellation, and multiple bids are permitted only during the Buyback Tendering Period.

Multiple bids by a single Eligible Shareholder will be clubbed and treated as one bid for acceptance purposes.

### 23.22

The cumulative quantity tendered will be available on the Indian Stock Exchanges’ websites at [www.bseindia.com](https://www.bseindia.com) and [www.nseindia.com](https://www.nseindia.com) throughout the Tendering Period and will be updated at specific intervals.

### 23.23

Eligible Shareholders send all documents at their own risk and are advised to safeguard their interests accordingly.

### 23.24

Non-Resident Shareholders and/or the Stock Brokers through whom they place orders or bids must comply with reporting requirements under FEMA and applicable RBI rules, regulations, and guidelines relating to remittance of funds.
