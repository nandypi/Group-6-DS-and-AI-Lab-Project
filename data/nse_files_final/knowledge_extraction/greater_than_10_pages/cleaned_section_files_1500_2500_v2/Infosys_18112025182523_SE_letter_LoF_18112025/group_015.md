## 17. BRIEF INFORMATION OF THE COMPANY

## 17.1 History and Overview of the Company

Infosys is a global provider of next-generation digital services and consulting.

The Company was incorporated on July 2, 1981, in Maharashtra, India, as `Infosys Consultants Private Limited`, a private limited company under the Indian Companies Act, 1956. Its name changed to `Infosys Technologies Private Limited` in April 1992 and to `Infosys Technologies Limited` in June 1992, when it became a public limited company. In June 2011, the Company changed its name to `Infosys Limited`.

The Company’s registered office is at Electronics City, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560 100, India. Infosys completed its initial public offering of equity shares in India in February 1993, with the shares listed on Indian stock exchanges in June 1993. It completed its initial public offering of American Depositary Shares (ADSs) in the United States in 1999 and sponsored secondary offerings of ADSs in the United States in 2003, 2005, and 2006. The 2005 and 2006 offerings also included public offerings without listing in Japan.

Infosys voluntarily delisted from the NASDAQ Global Select Market on December 11, 2012. Its ADSs began trading on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) on December 12, 2012, under the ticker symbol `INFY`. Infosys listed its ADSs on Euronext Paris and Euronext London on February 20, 2013, and voluntarily delisted them from both markets on July 5, 2018, citing low average daily trading volume relative to administrative expenses. Infosys ADSs continue to be listed on the NYSE under the symbol `INFY`.
