     - 33,183 performance-based RSUs under the 2015 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on the Company’s cumulative relative TSR performance as determined by the Board.

     - 66,366 performance-based RSUs for fiscal 2026 under the 2019 Plan, effective May 2, 2025, vesting based on performance criteria set out in the 2019 Plan.

These RSUs will vest in line with the employment agreement. The RSUs were issued at par value.

In accordance with the Listing Regulations, no employee, including key managerial personnel, senior management, directors, or promoters of a listed entity, may enter into an agreement with a shareholder or another third party regarding compensation or profit-sharing in connection with dealings in the Company’s securities without prior Board approval and shareholder approval by ordinary resolution. No such instances were reported during fiscal 2026.
