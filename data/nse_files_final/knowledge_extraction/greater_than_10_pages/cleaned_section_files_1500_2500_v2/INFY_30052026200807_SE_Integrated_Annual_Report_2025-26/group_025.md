## 4. Executive summary along with web-link(s) of Impact Assessment of CSR Projects carried out in pursuance of subrule (3) of rule 8

For fiscal 2026, the Company engaged an external agency to support an independent review and impact assessment of its CSR projects.

### Methodology

The assessment used quantitative and qualitative research tools, as relevant, for primary data collection. The assessment team conducted virtual and on-field interactions with project beneficiaries and other stakeholders. The findings were consolidated into a report for Management’s consideration.

The study was guided by the Inclusiveness, Relevance, Effectiveness, Convergence and Sustainability (IRECS) Framework. The framework was used to assess implementation efficacy and efficiency in achieving desired project outputs relative to inputs.

### Summary

During the year, impact assessments were conducted for 12 eligible CSR projects across various states in India. The projects aligned with the ESG framework and supported 13 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals, covering education, healthcare, women empowerment, sustainability, and other themes.

### Theme 1: Education and skill development

**Impact:** Digital education and improved access to online learning content had a positive impact on students’ academic performance and contributed to reduced dropout rates.

|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sree Guruvayurappan Bhajan Samaj (SGBS) Unnati Foundation – UNXT Youth Training | Karnataka | Infosys Foundation |
| 2 | Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI) – Oriental Studies Research and Preservation | Maharashtra | Infosys Foundation |
| 3 | eVidyaloka Trust – Rural digital and STEM education programme | Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Uttar Pradesh | Infosys Foundation |

### Theme 2: Environment sustainability and ecological balance

**Impact:** Improved cookstove projects reported reductions in waste generation, cooking time, indoor air pollution, and associated health hazards. Women were empowered through engagement in social activities within their communities.
