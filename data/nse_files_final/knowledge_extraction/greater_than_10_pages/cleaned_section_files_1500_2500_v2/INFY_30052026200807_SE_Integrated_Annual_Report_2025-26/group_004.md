## Chairman's message Performance overview

### The age of orchestration

Nandan M. Nilekani, Chairman, discusses the implications of artificial intelligence and generative AI for Infosys and its enterprise clients.

Infosys intends to adopt coding tools to improve productivity while continuing to focus on software development lifecycle activities such as testing, validation, architecture design, cybersecurity, data protection, and governance. The company plans to prepare and retrain its talent for the AI era and redeploy productivity gains toward new accounts and offerings.

The AI transition is increasing customer demand for legacy modernization, elimination of data silos, remediation of AI-identified cybersecurity vulnerabilities, and reduction of accumulated technical debt. The chairman also identifies opportunities to combine AI models and agents with existing deterministic enterprise systems.

Supply chains continue to change, while AI regulation is being developed across multiple jurisdictions. Enterprises commonly face deployment challenges caused by legacy architecture, organizational inertia, and evolving regulatory requirements. Scaling AI may require redesigning business processes, customer journeys, and operating models rather than adding AI to existing structures.

Technology debt is described as a strategic liability because older systems become more expensive to maintain and can constrain the agility required for AI adoption. Successful enterprise AI adoption depends on organizational readiness, data integrity, governance, and execution pace, as well as the specific legacy systems, data, and undocumented dependencies of each company.

The chairman emphasizes the role of trusted partners with long-term client relationships, execution capabilities, and the ability to combine established systems with new AI tools while preserving client autonomy, control over context, and focus on business outcomes.

The World Economic Forum is cited as projecting that by 2030, 92 million jobs will be displaced and 170 million created, representing a net gain of 78 million.
