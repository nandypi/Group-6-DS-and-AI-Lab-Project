## 2. Details of the scope and coverage of any human rights due diligence conducted.

Infosys conducts human rights due diligence across its organization and supply chain, considering local laws and international standards. The process includes periodic internal audits covering labor rights, workplace safety, and non-discrimination. Findings are incorporated into company policies and practices to prevent, mitigate, and address adverse impacts.

Infosys engages employees through the Pulse periodic survey and suppliers through the VenSat survey to gather feedback, including on human rights concerns. The Company provides training and awareness programs to employees and partners, addresses identified issues through grievance mechanisms, and monitors and reports progress.

The premises and offices of the entity are accessible to differently-abled visitors, as required under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, India.
