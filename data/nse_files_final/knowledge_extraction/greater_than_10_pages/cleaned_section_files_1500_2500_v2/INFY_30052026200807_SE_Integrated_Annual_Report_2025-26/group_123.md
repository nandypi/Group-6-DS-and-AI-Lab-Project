## 2. Notes to the Consolidated financial statements

## 2.1 Business combinations

## Accounting policy

Business combinations have been accounted for using the acquisition method under the provisions of Ind AS 103, Business Combinations.

The purchase price in an acquisition is measured at the fair value of the assets transferred, equity instruments issued, and liabilities incurred or assumed at the date control is transferred to the Group. The purchase price also includes the fair value of any contingent consideration.

Identifiable assets acquired and liabilities and contingent liabilities assumed in a business combination are initially measured at fair value on the acquisition date. Contingent consideration is remeasured at fair value at each reporting date, with changes recognized in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

Non-controlling interests are initially measured either at fair value or at the non-controlling interests' proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets. The measurement basis is selected separately for each acquisition. After acquisition, the carrying amount of non-controlling interests includes the initial amount recognized and the non-controlling interests' share of subsequent changes in the equity of subsidiaries.

Business combinations between entities under common control are accounted for at the carrying value of the assets acquired and liabilities assumed in the Group's consolidated financial statements.

Payments related to options issued by the Group over non-controlling interests in subsidiaries are accounted for as financial liabilities. They are initially recognized at the estimated present value of gross obligations and subsequently measured at fair value to reflect the amount payable when the option becomes exercisable. If the option expires unexercised, the liability is derecognized.

## Acquisition during the year ended March 31, 2026

During the year ended March 31, 2026, the Group completed two business combinations by acquiring 100% partnership interests or voting interests in:

1. **MRE Consulting Ltd.** — Acquired on April 30, 2025. The energy and business consulting services company is headquartered in Texas, USA. The acquisition is expected to add capabilities in trading and risk management, particularly in the energy sector.
2. **The Missing Link** — The Missing Link Security Pty. Ltd., The Missing Link Security Limited, and The Missing Link Automation Pty. Ltd., collectively known as “The Missing Link,” were acquired on April 30, 2025. The cybersecurity service provider is headquartered in Australia. The acquisition is expected to strengthen the Group's cybersecurity capabilities and its presence in the Australian market.

The purchase price was allocated to assets acquired and liabilities assumed based on fair values at the acquisition date:

| Component | Acquiree's carrying amount | Fair value adjustments | Purchase price allocated (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net assets (1) | 118 | - | 118 |
| Intangible assets: Customer related | - | 222 | 222 |
| Intangible assets: Vendor relationship | - | 55 | 55 |
| Intangible assets: Brand | - | 20 | 20 |
| Deferred tax liabilities on intangible assets | - | (46) | (46) |
| Total | 118 | 251 | 369 |
| Goodwill | - | - | 444 |
| Total purchase price | - | - | 813 |

(1) Includes cash and cash equivalents acquired of ₹102 crore.

The excess of the purchase consideration over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill were the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill amounting to ₹79 crore is expected to be deductible for tax purposes.

The total purchase consideration of ₹813 crore includes upfront cash consideration of ₹743 crore and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of ₹70 crore at the acquisition date.

At the acquisition date, the key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were the probabilities assigned to achievement of financial targets and discount rates ranging from 2% to 3%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026 was approximately ₹88 crore.

The acquisitions include retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over two to three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Retention bonuses and management incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was ₹194 crore at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts were substantially collected.

Transaction costs, including finder's fees, legal fees, due diligence fees, and other professional and consulting fees, are expensed as incurred. Transaction costs of ₹34 crore related to the acquisitions were included under administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended March 31, 2026.

## Acquisition during the year ended March 31, 2025

## InSemi

On May 10, 2024, Infosys Ltd acquired 100% voting interests in InSemi Technology Services Private Limited, a semiconductor design services company headquartered in India. The acquisition is expected to strengthen the Group's expertise in the semiconductor ecosystem and engineering R&D services.

The purchase price was allocated to assets acquired and liabilities assumed based on fair values at the acquisition date:

| Component | Acquiree's carrying amount | Fair value adjustments | Purchase price allocated (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net assets (1) | 40 | - | 40 |
| Intangible assets: Customer-related | - | 60 | 60 |
| Intangible assets: Brand | - | 13 | 13 |
| Deferred tax liabilities on intangible assets | - | (18) | (18) |
| Total | - | - | 95 |
| Goodwill | - | - | 103 |
| Total purchase price | - | - | 198 |

(1) Includes cash and cash equivalents acquired of ₹41 crore.

The excess of the purchase consideration over the fair value of assets acquired was attributed to goodwill. The primary items generating this goodwill were the acquired assembled workforce and estimated synergies, neither of which qualifies as an intangible asset.

Goodwill is not tax-deductible.

The purchase consideration of ₹198 crore includes cash of ₹168 crore and contingent consideration with an estimated fair value of ₹30 crore at the acquisition date.

At the acquisition date, the key inputs used to determine the fair value of contingent consideration were the probabilities assigned to achievement of financial targets and a discount rate of 5.9%. The undiscounted value of contingent consideration as of March 31, 2026 was approximately ₹20 crore.

The acquisition includes retention bonuses and management incentives payable to acquiree employees over three years, subject to continuous employment with the Group and achievement of financial targets for the respective years. Bonuses and incentives are recognized in employee benefit expenses in the Statement of Profit and Loss over the service period.

The fair value of trade receivables acquired was ₹32 crore at the acquisition date. As of March 31, 2026, the amounts were fully collected.

Transaction costs of ₹2 crore related to the acquisition were included under administrative expenses in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss for the year ended March 31, 2025.
