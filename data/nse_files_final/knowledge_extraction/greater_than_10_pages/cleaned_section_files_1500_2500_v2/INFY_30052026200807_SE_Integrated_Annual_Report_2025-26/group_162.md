The Board of Directors approved amendments to the existing plan at its meeting held on April 23, 2026. The amendments are intended to extend the performance-based stock incentive plan to an expanded employee base, align employee interests with shareholder value creation, reinforce long-term performance orientation, and align operating performance metrics with relevant financial metrics.

The Amended 2019 Plan is available for shareholder review at [expanded stock ownership program 2019](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/Documents/expanded-stock-ownership-program2019.pdf).

The resolutions under Item 4 and Item 5 seek shareholder approval to amend the existing plan and authorize the Board to grant RSUs and create, issue, offer, and provide equity shares, American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), or cash to eligible employees of the Company or its subsidiaries.

Pursuant to Regulations 6 and 14 of the SEBI Regulations and Section 67(3)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 16(2) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, the key details of the Amended 2019 Plan are set out below.

All capitalized terms not defined herein have the meaning assigned to them in the 2019 Plan.

## 1.  Brief description of the Amended 2019 Plan

The purpose of the Amended 2019 Plan is to:

- Encourage employees to align individual performance with the Company’s shareholder value creation objectives.
- Attract, retain, and motivate talented and identified employees.
- Reward employee performance with ownership.

The Amended 2019 Plan grants Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) to eligible employees. Subject to applicable law and the applicable conditions, eligible employees may receive equity shares, ADRs, or cash upon exercise of the RSUs.

The plan is administered by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), referred to as the Administrator. For secondary acquisition and related administrative matters, administration may also be delegated to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. The Administrator’s decisions, determinations, and interpretations are final and binding on eligible employees and participants.

Each RSU grant is evidenced by an award agreement specifying the applicable terms and conditions.

The performance-linked RSUs are intended to align employee rewards with shareholder interests through a focus on the Company’s sustainable, long-term performance and value creation.

## 2. Total number of shares or benefits to be granted

The total number of equity shares and ADRs that may be provided upon exercise of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan to eligible employees of the Company and its subsidiaries shall not cumulatively exceed **3,26,63,903 equity shares**.

This represents the balance of the unutilized equity shares from the original shareholder-approved pool of **5,00,00,000 equity shares**, or approximately **0.8% of issued capital** as of the date of the notice.

## 3. Identification of classes of employees entitled to participate and be beneficiaries in the 2019 Plan

Employees, as defined under the SEBI Regulations, of the Company and its subsidiary companies, whether working in India or abroad, may participate in the Amended 2019 Plan subject to eligibility criteria specified under the SEBI Regulations or determined by the Administrator.

Under the SEBI Regulations and the Companies Act, 2013, the following are not eligible to participate:

- Promoters or members of the promoter group.
- Independent directors.
- Directors who, themselves or through a relative or body corporate, directly or indirectly hold more than 10% of the Company’s outstanding equity shares.

The appraisal process described in paragraph 8 determines the grant of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan.

## 4. Requirements of vesting and period of vesting

RSUs granted under the Amended 2019 Plan vest based on three performance parameters. The NRC determines the relative weightage of each parameter, subject to the following conditions:

- Each parameter must have a weightage of at least 25%.
- No single parameter may have a weightage exceeding 50%.

The performance parameters are:

1. **Long-term Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR):** Rolling three-year TSR measured against a peer comparator group of at least eight companies. The Administrator will identify the comparator group from a basket of ten industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL.

   If a peer company is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company in the comparator group.

   The Company will transition in phases to a three-year rolling Relative TSR measure:

   - One-year TSR in Year 1.
   - Two-year TSR in Year 2.
   - Full three-year TSR from Year 3 onward.

2. **Annual TSR:**

   - **Relative TSR against industry peers:** Relative TSR measured against a peer comparator group of at least eight companies selected by the Administrator from the same basket of ten industry peers.
   - **Relative TSR against broader market indices:** Relative TSR measured against domestic and global indices comprising MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50, as determined by the Administrator.

   If a peer company is delisted during the performance measurement period, the NRC may include an additional peer company in the comparator group.

3. **Annual operating performance metrics:** Company performance measured using:

   - Total revenue growth.
   - Operating margin.

The Administrator determines the performance targets for these metrics. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the number of shares that vest.

### Relative TSR vesting outcomes

| Company performance ranking | RSUs vesting |
|---|---:|
| 75th percentile and above | 100% of RSUs due for vesting |
| 50th to below 75th percentile | Proportionate vesting on a linear scale between 60% and 100%; the balance lapses |
| Below 50th percentile | 0%; all RSUs due for vesting lapse |

### Operating performance vesting outcomes

| Performance achievement | RSUs vesting |
|---|---:|
| Below minimum threshold | 0%; all RSUs due for vesting lapse |
| Minimum threshold | 75% of RSUs due for vesting |
| Greater than threshold and below target | Proportionate vesting on a linear scale between 75% and 100%; the balance lapses |
| Target | 100% of RSUs due for vesting |
| Greater than target and below stretch target | Proportionate vesting on a linear scale between 100% and 125% |
| Stretch target | 125% of RSUs due for vesting |

Although individual performance outcomes may exceed 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter shall not exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter.

Any increased dilution of equity share capital or cost of secondary acquisition of equity shares is expected to be offset by growth in shareholder value and increased shareholder returns.

All RSUs vest according to the schedule determined by the Administrator from the grant date. RSUs that lapse or are forfeited may be used for future grants.

In subsequent annual reports, the Company will disclose:

- The performance parameters and their weightages.
- Target achievement for each performance parameter.
- Performance achieved during the year and the basis for RSU vesting.
- Number of employees covered.
- Number of RSUs granted.
- Number of employees whose RSUs vested.
- Number of RSUs due for vesting under the vesting schedule.
- Number of RSUs vested after meeting the vesting conditions.

The Company has a Compensation Recovery Policy supporting its pay-for-performance philosophy. The policy is available at [Compensation Recovery Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporategovernance/documents/compensation-recovery-policy.pdf).

## 5. Maximum period within which the RSUs shall vest

RSUs shall vest between a minimum of **1 year** and a maximum of **3 years** from the grant date, as specified in the award agreement. No vesting tranche may exceed **50% of the total grants** made under the plan.

When determining an eligible employee’s vesting schedule, the Administrator considers factors including alignment with accelerated shareholder value creation, retention of key talent, future potential, and tenure.

RSUs vest based on achievement of the performance criteria described in paragraph 4.
