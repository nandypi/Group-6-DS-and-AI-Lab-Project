## 10b. What are the processes used to identify work-related hazards and assess risks on a routine and non-routine basis by the entity?

Infosys identifies and assesses work-related hazards across routine and non-routine activities. Assessments are conducted proactively for existing, new, and modified activities, processes, and services, and in response to regulatory updates.

Routine risk assessments include:

- Periodic workplace and ergonomic evaluations.
- Monitoring utility operations.
- Reviewing employee commute risks.

Non-routine activities, including construction, infrastructure development, preventive maintenance, and facility modifications, undergo specific risk assessments before and during execution.

Incidents and near misses are analyzed quarterly to identify trends and emerging risks. Hazards are prioritized and mitigated using the hierarchy of controls. Residual risks are reassessed, and the risk control matrix is updated as required. Employees and stakeholders participate in the risk assessment process, and learnings from past projects and current operations are incorporated continuously.

## 10c. Whether you have processes for workers to report the work-related hazards and to remove themselves from such risks. (Y / N)

Yes. Infosys maintains an incident reporting and management process covering accidents, near misses, unsafe acts, and unsafe conditions. Incidents are reported, investigated, and addressed through corrective and preventive actions.

Employees can report health and safety concerns through the Infosys Helpdesk (IHD) or designated email addresses. Contract staff without system access report concerns to their supervisors, who escalate them to the local HSE team.

All incidents undergo root cause analysis. Awareness of incident reporting is supported through induction programs, online training modules on Lex, targeted awareness campaigns, and mailers. Learnings and corrective actions are shared across locations to support consistent application and prevent recurrence.

## 10d. Do the employees/ worker of the entity have access to non-occupational medical and healthcare services?

Yes. Infosys operates Occupational Health Centers (OHCs), including physiotherapy facilities, across its campuses in India. Competent medical professionals from accredited partner hospitals manage the OHCs and physiotherapy centers in accordance with applicable statutory and other requirements.

Periodic health risk assessments monitor health trends and support timely specialist consultations and targeted interventions. Employee well-being initiatives include workshops, awareness campaigns, regular health check-ups, online chat sessions, counseling services such as Samaritans, and targeted wellness programs.

OHC visit details are recorded digitally in accordance with applicable regional data protection and privacy regulations.

Infosys also has hospital tie-ups that provide employees and their dependents with access to health screenings and treatments for non-occupational illnesses. Employees, including contractual staff, receive insurance coverage. Contractual employees are covered by targeted programs, including mandatory health screenings and interventions based on identified risks, hazards, and emerging health trends.

## 12. Describe the measures taken by the entity to ensure a safe and healthy work place.

Infosys's occupational health and safety policies, processes, and practices address the physical, emotional, and social well-being of employees. Its Health and Safety Management System is certified to ISO 45001:2018 for all Indian locations and Australia offices, including Infosys Limited and its subsidiaries.

Measures include:

1. **Incident management:** Near misses, potential hazards, and accidents are reported through internal applications or email. Causes are identified and analyzed, and corrective actions are taken to prevent recurrence and injuries or losses.
2. **OH&S Committee:** Employees and non-permanent employees participate in the Infosys OH&S Committee. Employee representation on safety committees is 100%. The committee supports employee-management consultation on occupational health and safety.
3. **Training and awareness:** Programs include awareness sessions, mock drills, classroom sessions, and periodic demonstrations. HSEMS training is part of employee induction, and an HSE awareness module is available on Lex. Contract staff receive job-specific and general induction training, followed by refresher training.
4. **Safety interventions:** Safety systems include work permits, training, Lock Out Tag Out (LOTO), safety inspections, audits, operational controls, and monitoring. Policies address women's safety, lone working, transport, travel, and construction.
5. **Medical services:** Infosys operates OHCs on its campuses in India. Most OHCs operate around the clock based on business requirements. Ambulance services are available, or arrangements are made for ambulances, at all India-based locations.
6. **Health Risk Assessment (HRA):** HRA is conducted annually using inputs from OHCs. Prevalent risks for an IT/ITES company include ergonomics, musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs), emotional well-being, workplace operations, utility operations, and commuting. Initiatives, interventions, virtual engagement sessions, and process controls address these risks.
7. **Ergonomics programs:** Programs include on-site physiotherapy centers in India, ergonomic expert interventions, ergonomic infrastructure, and periodic sessions on back care, repetitive stress injuries, and seating posture for employees globally.
8. **Physical and emotional well-being:** Nutritionists at locations in India provide counseling and guidance. Infosys also operates gyms focused on physical and mental fitness.
9. **Programs for mothers:** Pregna Care is a healthcare program for expectant mothers in India.
10. **Working environment:** Infosys monitors indoor air quality, lighting levels, and noise levels and promotes the use of green seal chemicals.
