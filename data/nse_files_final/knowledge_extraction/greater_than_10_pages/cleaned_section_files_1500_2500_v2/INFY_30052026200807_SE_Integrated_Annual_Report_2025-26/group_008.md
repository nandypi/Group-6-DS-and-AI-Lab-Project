Infosys obtains financial capital through funds generated from business operations and financing activities. Its performance has resulted in high growth, strong cash generation, sustained profitability, and a debt-free, liquid balance sheet.

Over ₹37,500 crore was returned to shareholders for fiscal 2026 through the Company's capital allocation activities.

## Managing financial capital

Infosys has a high cash-generating business and access to capital markets globally. Its strong credit rating allows it to raise debt at competitive rates in the future, if needed. Its primary sources of funds are cash from operations and income from short- and long-term investments.

The primary sources of liquidity are cash and cash equivalents and cash flow generated from operations. Infosys remains debt-free and maintains adequate cash to meet operational and strategic requirements, unforeseen events, and return objectives.

Consolidated cash and investments include:

- Deposits in banks
- Investments in debt mutual funds
- Commercial Paper (CP)
- Quoted bonds and securities issued by government and quasigovernment organizations
- Non-convertible debentures
- Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
- Instruments issued by eligible financial institutions with high credit ratings, guided by the Company's treasury policy

Infosys also invests in the startup ecosystem to access innovation that, together with its services and solutions, delivers benefits to clients. These investments most often comprise minority equity positions in startup organizations and/or venture capital funds.

## Shareholder value creation

Infosys seeks to meet investor expectations through responsible business decisions, governance, integrity, and transparency. The Company aims to deliver revenue growth and operating margins through financial management and corporate governance, supporting share value appreciation and sustained value creation.

The Company maintains regular investor communication through quarterly post-result calls, analyst meets, the Annual General Meeting, and one-to-one and group interactions.

## Distribution of value created through Capital Allocation Policy

Effective from financial year 2025, Infosys expects to continue returning approximately 85% of free cash flow cumulatively over a five-year period through a combination of semi-annual dividends, share buybacks, and/or special dividends, subject to applicable laws and requisite approvals. The Company expects to progressively increase its annual dividend per share, excluding any special dividend.

Infosys returned approximately 82.1% of free cash flow for fiscals 2025 and 2026 through dividends and buybacks, in line with its Capital Allocation Policy.

## Delivering value through business strategy

Infosys' market-oriented four-pronged strategy supports investment in its global digital footprint. The strategy is intended to strengthen its position as a digital transformation partner, increase its potential to attract larger Total Contract Value (TCV) deals and clients, and support growth and profitability.

Infosys' Employee Value Proposition (EVP) focuses on meaningful work, continuous growth, protected well-being, and a sense of belonging. The Company follows an “Experience by Design” approach to employee policies, processes, programs, and systems.

## Employee Value Proposition

The EVP comprises the experiences, rewards, benefits, and fulfillment employees receive in return for their commitment and skills.

## Employee health and wellness

Infosys is committed to a safe, healthy, and incident-free workplace for employees, contractors, and other stakeholders. Its integrated Occupational Health and Safety Management System is aligned with ISO 45001 and includes workplace hazard identification, risk mitigation controls, audits, safety initiatives, employee engagement programs, and emergency preparedness drills.

The Company's HALE (Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment) framework integrates well-being into its culture and operating model. It covers Physical, Emotional, Social, and Digital Well-being, with Safety as a core component.

## Nurturing workplace inclusivity

Infosys describes inclusion as part of its culture and leadership, learning, and growth practices.

As part of its ESG Vision 2030, Infosys aims to achieve 45% female representation in its workforce by 2030. Women represented 39.5% of the total workforce in fiscal 2026.

## Infosys Pulse

Infosys Pulse is an employee feedback platform that captures insights on workplace experience. It supports leadership alignment with employee sentiment and data-driven organizational actions.

## InStep

InStep is Infosys' global internship program. It has:

- More than 220 partner institutions in over 50 countries
- More than 3,500 alumni members and interns from over 50 nationalities

The program supports academic partnerships, patents, publications, and localization efforts. The launch of InStep Japan expanded the program's global talent initiatives.

## Flexibility by design and distributed workforce

Infosys has established innovation hubs, nearshore centers, and digital design studios across geographies. Its hybrid work model supports working from home and offices located close to employees' homes. Over the last few years, the Company established offices in Ahmedabad, Guwahati, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam, Noida, Hubballi, Indore, Navi Mumbai, Nagpur, and Coimbatore in India.

The distributed-hybrid model supports self-development, collaboration, and business outcomes. The majority of the workforce operates in a hybrid manner. Infosys monitors work-hour patterns to identify excessive or unsustainable workloads and supports managers in addressing workload imbalances, staffing challenges, and employee support needs.

## Leveraging AI to improve employee experience

Infosys is integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) across the talent lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, continuous learning, and offboarding.

InfyMe is a mobile-first, self-service platform integrating more than 200 service touchpoints into one interface. The platform supports employee operations, connection, and collaboration, particularly in the hybrid work model.

## Our NAVI-powered interventions

- **Data Assist:** A natural-language AI assistant for queries related to leave, attendance, allocation, assets, confirmation, and master data.
- **Policy Advisor:** An AI assistant that interprets policy documents and personalizes responses to employee queries.

## Moments That Matter (MTM)

Moments That Matter is a celebration platform integrated into events throughout the employee lifecycle. Smart alerts inform employees about upcoming moments, and AI can generate cards and messages for colleagues and teams.

AI-driven Pulse analysis supports continuous listening by identifying employee sentiment and enabling early action on well-being and engagement.

In learning and development, Infosys is deploying generative AI to create personalized learning paths, simulate real-world scenarios, and assist with content creation. AI companions on its Lex learning platform, including Zoiee and SynthAIz, provide intelligent tutoring, summarization, and contextual learning support.
