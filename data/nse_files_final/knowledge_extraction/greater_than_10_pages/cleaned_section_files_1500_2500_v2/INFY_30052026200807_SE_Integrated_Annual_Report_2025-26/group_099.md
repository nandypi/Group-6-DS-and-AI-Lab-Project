## a. Post-sales client support

The Company provides clients with fixed-period post-sales support on fixed-price, fixed-timeframe contracts. Related costs are accrued when the related revenue is recognized and included in the Statement of Profit and Loss. Costs are estimated using historical experience, with assumptions and the likelihood of occurrence reviewed periodically for material changes.

## b. Onerous contracts

Provisions for onerous contracts are recognized when the expected benefits from a contract are lower than the unavoidable costs of meeting future obligations. Estimated losses on incomplete contracts are recorded when the losses become probable, based on estimated efforts or costs to complete the contract.

The provision is measured at the present value of the lower of:

- the expected cost of terminating the contract; and
- the expected net cost of continuing with the contract.

Before establishing a provision, the Company recognizes any impairment loss on assets associated with the contract.

## 2.17 Income taxes

### Accounting policy

Income tax expense comprises current and deferred income tax. It is recognized in net profit in the Statement of Profit and Loss, except when it relates to items recognized directly in equity, in which case it is recognized in equity or other comprehensive income.

Current income tax for current and prior periods is recognized at the amount expected to be paid to or recovered from tax authorities, using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are recognized for temporary differences between the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements. Deferred tax assets are reviewed at each reporting date and reduced to the extent that it is no longer probable that the related tax benefit will be realized.

Deferred income tax assets and liabilities are measured using tax rates and tax laws enacted or substantively enacted by the Balance Sheet date and expected to apply when the temporary differences are recovered or settled. Changes in tax rates are recognized as income or expense in the period that includes the enactment or substantive enactment date.

A deferred income tax asset is recognized when it is probable that future taxable profit will be available against which deductible temporary differences and tax losses can be utilized. Deferred income taxes are not provided on undistributed earnings of subsidiaries and branches when the earnings are not expected to be distributed in the foreseeable future.

The Company offsets current tax assets and liabilities, and deferred tax assets and liabilities, when it has a legally enforceable right to offset the recognized amounts and intends either to settle on a net basis or to realize the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.

Interim income tax provisions are based on the best estimate of the annual average tax rate expected to apply for the full financial year. Tax benefits from deductions earned on the exercise of employee share options in excess of compensation charged to income are credited to equity.

### Indian SEZ tax incentives

In prior years, the Company benefited from Indian income tax incentives for software and service exports from units registered under the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005.

SEZ units that began providing services on or after April 1, 2005, were eligible for:

- a 100% deduction of profits or gains from export services for the first five years from the financial year in which service provision commenced;
- a 50% deduction for the following five years; and
- up to a 50% deduction for an additional five years, subject to creating a Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve and using the reserve to acquire new plant and machinery for business purposes under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Refer to “Special Economic Zone Re-investment reserve” under Note 2.12, Equity.

Deferred income tax for the years ended March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025 substantially related to the origination and reversal of temporary differences.

### U.S. branch profit tax

Infosys is subject to a 15% Branch Profit Tax in the United States to the extent that its U.S. branch's net profit during the year exceeds the increase in the U.S. branch's net assets during the year, calculated under the Internal Revenue Code.

As at March 31, 2026:

- the U.S. branch's net assets were approximately ₹7,736 crore; and
- the Company had a deferred tax liability for branch profit tax of ₹207 crore, net of credits, because it estimated that these branch profits would be distributed in the foreseeable future.

Deferred income tax liabilities were not recognized on temporary differences associated with investments in subsidiaries and branches amounting to ₹19,270 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹16,593 crore as at March 31, 2025. The Company can control the timing of reversal of these temporary differences and considers it probable that they will not reverse in the foreseeable future. The Company mainly intends to repatriate earnings from subsidiaries and branches to the extent that they can be distributed in a tax-free manner.

Deferred income tax assets were not recognized on accumulated losses of ₹1,310 crore as at March 31, 2026 and ₹1,466 crore as at March 31, 2025, because it was probable that future taxable profit would not be available against which the unused tax losses could be utilized in the foreseeable future. Most accumulated losses as at March 31, 2026 are expected to expire between financial years 2028 and 2033.
