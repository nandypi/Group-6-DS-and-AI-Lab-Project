## Our Strategy Approaching value creation

Infosys’ strategic objective is to build a sustainable and resilient organization that remains relevant to clients, creates growth opportunities for employees, generates profitable returns for investors, and contributes to the communities in which it operates.

Clients and prospective clients are pursuing transformative opportunities enabled by software, computing technology, and AI. Infosys aims to help clients reimagine offerings, processes, and systems; build strong data foundations and cloud capabilities; and scale their AI adoption. The company also emphasizes privacy, ethics, controls, and Responsible AI practices.

In 2018, Infosys adopted a four-pronged strategy:

1. Scale Agile Digital
2. Energize the Core
3. Reskill our People
4. Expand Localization

The AI transition is described as transformational rather than incremental. Enterprise technology is shifting from application-centric stacks toward platforms incorporating intelligent agents, orchestration layers, and adaptive learning components. Operating models are evolving from linear, role-based execution toward AI-driven loops that continuously sense, decide, and act. Workforce priorities are shifting toward orchestration, system supervision, domain interpretation, and outcome validation, requiring new roles, large-scale reskilling, and changes in workforce composition.

## Infosys AI vision

Infosys aspires to be a leading partner to “unlock AI value” and deliver business outcomes in revenue growth, cost optimization, and innovation.

Its two-pronged AI strategy consists of:

- **AI-first services:** Services designed with AI as the core execution layer, enabling new business models, products, and revenue streams.
- **AI-augmented services:** Existing services enhanced by integrating AI into established systems and workflows to improve efficiency, personalization, and outcomes while extending the value of existing investments.

These services are supported by Infosys’ platforms and intellectual property, ecosystem partnerships, talent base, and brand. Infosys has developed over 25 industry-focused AI playbooks covering repeatable use cases, domain context, and execution patterns.

## AI First Value Framework

Infosys unveiled its AI-first value framework to help global enterprises scale AI adoption through its generative and agentic AI suite, Infosys Topaz™. The company stated that this positions it to address an incremental AI-first services opportunity estimated at USD 300–400 billion by 2030.

The framework defines six interconnected value pools:

1. **AI Strategy & Engineering:** Designing and implementing AI strategies, platforms, and architectures; orchestrating AI agents, proprietary platforms, and third-party tools on purpose-built infrastructure.
2. **Data for AI:** Preparing structured and unstructured enterprise data for AI model readiness through AI-ready data platforms and AI-grade data engineering, including fingerprinting and synthetic data services.
3. **Process AI:** Transforming end-to-end business processes by integrating AI agents and human expertise to improve efficiency, experience, and business outcomes.
4. **Agentic Legacy Modernization:** Using AI agents to reverse-engineer existing estates, understand their intent, and progressively modernize them while reducing technical debt.
5. **Physical AI:** Designing intelligent products and embedding AI into physical devices that capture sensor data, interpret signals, and take real-time action using digital twins, robotics, autonomous systems, and edge intelligence.
6. **AI Trust:** Applying responsible, secure, and ethical AI practices throughout the AI lifecycle, including risk assessments, policy design, security testing, and governance.

Infosys stated that it was collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys.

## Platforms and IP

Infosys is using its purpose-built, composable, and open agentic services suite, Infosys Topaz Fabric™, together with partnerships with AI disruptors, to deliver AI-augmented and AI-first services.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is an enterprise-grade AI services layer within Infosys Topaz. It embeds enterprise context across data, applications, processes, and systems, and integrates models, data pipelines, agents, and platforms across diverse technology environments. It is designed to help organizations reimagine business processes while building on existing investments and maintaining strategic autonomy.

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multi-agent enterprise workflows across humans, AI agents, and enterprise systems. It combines EdgeVerve product innovation with Infosys’ global scale and is intended to help large and mid-sized enterprises move from traditional operations to digital-native, AI-driven enterprises.

Infosys Topaz Fabric is designed for clients that have already made AI investments and need a composable and modular agentic AI platform. Infosys Topaz AI Next addresses clients seeking an end-to-end, vertically integrated AI platform for reimagining core business processes.

The platforms institutionalize enterprise knowledge and process semantics so that AI solutions remain relevant, scalable, and linked to business outcomes. They support the AI lifecycle from experimentation through large-scale production deployment. Responsible AI, security, governance, and assurance are embedded by design.

The six AI-first value pools and related capabilities are:

| AI Strategy & Engineering | Data for AI | Process AI | Agentic Legacy Modernization | Physical AI | AI Trust |

Infosys stated that its strategic investments position it to advise and support clients through structural changes in technology, particularly as enterprises scale AI adoption and require contextual grounding.
