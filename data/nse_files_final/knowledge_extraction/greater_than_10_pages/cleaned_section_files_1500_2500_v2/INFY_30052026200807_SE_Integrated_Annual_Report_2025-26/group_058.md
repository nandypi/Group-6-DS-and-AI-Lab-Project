## 2. Processes to avoid or manage conflicts of interest involving members of the Board

Yes. The Company has established processes to avoid and manage conflicts of interest involving Board members:

1. **Declaration of interest:** Board members must disclose financial interests, affiliations, or relationships that could influence decision-making at appointment and periodically thereafter, in compliance with statutory requirements.
2. **Code of Conduct and Ethics:** The Company’s formal code defines conflicts of interest and provides guidelines for identifying and managing them.
3. **Recusal procedures:** A Board member with a potential conflict must recuse themselves from discussions and voting on the relevant agenda item.
4. **Approval requirements:** Transactions involving entities in which Directors have an interest require prior Board approval. Transactions exceeding the prescribed threshold also require shareholder approval.

### 2a. Procedures for sustainable sourcing

Yes. Infosys has adopted a Responsible Supply Chain & Supplier Diversity Policy and a Green Procurement Policy. Suppliers must sign the Supplier Code of Conduct, and periodic ESG assessments are conducted internally and through a third party.

### 2b. Percentage of inputs sourced sustainably

All procurements follow the principles of sustainable sourcing. Suppliers are required to accept the Supplier Code of Conduct, which is based on the UNGC Principles.

### 4. Applicability of Extended Producer Responsibility

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is not applicable to the entity’s activities. Infosys stated that it does not manufacture products and is an IT services company.
