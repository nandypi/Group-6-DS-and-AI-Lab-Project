# Proposed Amendments to the 2019 Plan

## Section 6 c) Parameters for Vesting

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relative Total Shareholder Return | Relative Total Shareholder Return (Relative TSR) against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from a basket of 10 Indian and global industry peers: Accenture, DXC Technologies, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Atos, and HCL. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. Relative TSR was also measured against broader domestic and global market indices comprising the Nifty 50, S&P 500, and an MSCI index determined by the Administrator. | Long-term Relative TSR for rolling three-year TSR against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators, identified by the Administrator from the same basket of 10 industry peers. If any peer is delisted during the performance measurement period, the Administrator may include an additional peer company. The Company will implement a phased transition to a three-year rolling Relative TSR parameter: one-year TSR in Year 1, two-year TSR in Year 2, and a full three-year TSR measure from Year 3 onwards. Annual TSR will also comprise: (A) Relative TSR against a peer comparator group of not less than eight comparators from the same 10-peer basket; and (B) Relative TSR against broader domestic and global indices comprising MSCI, S&P 500, and Nifty 50, as determined by the Administrator. | The amendment is intended to strengthen alignment with long-term shareholder value creation and rationalize operational metrics. It responds to shareholder feedback regarding alignment of management compensation with long-term shareholder interests. There are no changes to the existing industry peer basket. |

## Operating Performance Metrics

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating performance metrics | Operating lead performance metrics used to derive shareholder value: total revenue growth, digital revenue growth, and operating margin. Annual performance targets were to be determined by the Administrator. Each performance parameter was distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. For Relative TSR performance, 100% of the RSUs due for vesting would vest at or above the 75th percentile; vesting would be proportionate on a linear scale between 60% and 100% from the 50th to the 75th percentile; and no RSUs would vest below the 50th percentile. | Annual operating performance metrics used to derive shareholder value: total revenue growth and operating margin. Performance targets are to be determined by the Administrator. Each performance parameter is distinct for calculating the quantity of shares to vest. For Relative TSR performance, 100% of the RSUs due for vesting would vest at or above the 75th percentile; vesting would be proportionate on a linear scale between 60% and 100% from the 50th to the 75th percentile; and no RSUs would vest below the 50th percentile. For operating performance metrics: at minimum or threshold performance, 75% of the RSUs due for vesting would vest; below threshold, no RSUs would vest; at target performance, 100% would vest; at stretch target performance, 125% would vest; between threshold and target, vesting would be proportionate on a linear scale between 75% and 100%; and between target and stretch target, vesting would be proportionate on a linear scale between 100% and 125%. | The amendment rationalizes operating metrics around revenue and margin to support consistency and comparability over time. Digital revenue growth may become transient and less comparable over the long-term 10-year period because of changes in technology and business models. The amendment also seeks to increase transparency regarding the payout philosophy. |

Under the proposed amended 2019 Plan, although individual performance outcomes may result in achievement levels above 100%, aggregate vesting under the operating performance parameter shall not exceed 100% of the shares due for vesting under that parameter.

## Section 6(e)

| Particulars | 2019 Plan | Proposed amended 2019 Plan | Rationale / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exercise period following termination or resignation | For the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(iv), where employment terminates or the participant resigns, vested Restricted Stock Units held by the participant as of the termination or resignation date, provided the termination is not for misconduct, must be exercised within 90 calendar days of the termination or resignation date. | For the circumstances enumerated in Section 6(c)(II), where employment terminates or the participant resigns, vested Restricted Stock Units held by the participant, provided the termination is not for misconduct, must be exercised within 90 calendar days of the date of vesting. | The change clarifies and ensures consistency in the operation of the Plan’s provisions concerning the exercise period for vested RSUs. The revised language specifies that the exercise period begins on the date on which the relevant RSUs vest. |
