### Notes

1. Turnover, profit or loss before taxation, provision for taxation, and profit or loss after taxation are converted at monthly average exchange rates.
2. Investments exclude investments in subsidiaries.
3. Reserves and surplus includes other comprehensive income and securities premium.
4. Proposed dividend from subsidiaries is nil except for Infosys BPM Limited and EdgeVerve Systems Limited, which proposed final dividends of ₹89,000 per equity share (₹10,000 par value) and ₹5 per equity share (₹10 par value), respectively, subject to shareholder approval at the respective ensuing annual general meetings.
5. Infosys Limited SPC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, was incorporated effective December 12, 2024, and had not commenced operations.
6. Infosys Saudi Arabia LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, was incorporated effective April 21, 2025, and had not commenced operations.
7. Infosys BPM Canada Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys BPM UK Ltd, was incorporated effective July 28, 2025, and had not commenced operations.
8. Infosys Enterprise Business Services Pty Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., was incorporated effective March 19, 2026, and had not commenced operations.
9. In-tech Automotive Engineering de R.L. de C.V. was liquidated effective May 7, 2025.
10. Infosys Germany GmbH, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., acquired 100% of the voting interests in in-tech Holding GmbH and its subsidiaries on July 17, 2024. The acquired entities included in-tech GmbH, in-tech Automotive Engineering SL, ProIT, in-tech Automotive Engineering de R.L. de C.V., drivetech Fahrversuch GmbH, Friedrich Wagner Holding Inc. and its subsidiaries, Friedrich & Wagner Asia Pacific GmbH and its subsidiaries, in-tech Group Ltd and its subsidiary in-tech Group India Private Limited, and In-tech Automotive Engineering Shenyang Co., Ltd and its subsidiary In-tech Automotive Engineering Beijing Co., Ltd. Subsequently, on September 1, 2024, in-tech Group India Private Limited became a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited. Infosys Germany GmbH merged into Infosys Germany SE, formerly known as Blitz 24-893 SE, effective September 24, 2025.
11. On October 17, 2024, Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd. acquired 100% of the voting interests in Infosys Germany SE, formerly known as Blitz 24-893 SE.
12. Infosys Limited acquired 100% of the voting interests in InSemi Technology Services Private Limited and its subsidiary Elbrus Labs Private Limited on May 10, 2024.
13. Entities incorporated during the period included Infosys Limited SPC on July 26, 2024; Infosys Saudi Arabia LLC on April 21, 2025; Infosys BPM Canada Inc. on July 28, 2025; and other entities incorporated on December 12, 2024, March 20, 2025, April 16, 2025, and April 23, 2025.
14. Infosys Consulting S.R.L. (Argentina) became a majority-owned and controlled subsidiary of Infosys Nova Holdings LLC effective January 28, 2026.
15. InSemi Technology Services Private Limited's wholly owned subsidiary is identified in the source notes.
16. On April 30, 2025, Infosys Nova Holdings LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited, acquired 98.21% of the voting interests in MRE Consulting Ltd and its subsidiary MRE Technology Services, LLC. The remaining 1.79% was acquired by Infosys Energy Consulting Services LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Nova Holdings LLC.
17. On April 30, 2025, Infosys Australia Technology Services Pty Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd., acquired 100% of the voting interests in The Missing Link Automation Pty Ltd, The Missing Link Network Integration Pty Ltd, and The Missing Link Security Pty Ltd, along with their subsidiary The Missing Link Security Ltd.
18. On May 13, 2025, Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd. diluted its 2% stake in HIPUS Co., Ltd. to Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd.
19. In-tech Engineering Services S.R.L., a wholly owned subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, merged into ProIT, a wholly owned subsidiary of in-tech GmbH, effective November 30, 2025.
20. Brilliant Basics Holdings Limited and GuideVision UK Ltd are identified as being under liquidation. Brilliant Basics Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Brilliant Basics Holdings Limited.
21. MRE Technology Services, LLC was acquired through MRE Consulting Ltd. The acquisition took place on April 30, 2025.
22. Infosys Germany SE, Friedrich Wagner Holding, Inc., and related in-tech entities are identified in the source notes as part of the July 17, 2024 acquisition and subsequent corporate restructuring.
