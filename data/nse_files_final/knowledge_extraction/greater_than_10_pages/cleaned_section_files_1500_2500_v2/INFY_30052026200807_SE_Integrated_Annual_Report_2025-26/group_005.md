## Letter to the Shareholder

## Salil Parekh

Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director

## Dear shareholder,

Last year marked an inflection point as AI began evolving from experimentation to enterprise-scale adoption. Infosys shifted its portfolio, invested in talent, and scaled partnerships to support clients' AI adoption. AI-led programs were deployed across 90% of Infosys' top 200 clients and were scaling across industries.

Infosys' AI strategy is built on six pillars:

- Strategy and engineering
- Data
- Process
- Legacy modernization
- Physical AI
- Trust

Together, these capabilities address a US$300 billion opportunity, based on market estimates. They support growth programs, legacy-system modernization, data and process readiness, and responsible AI deployment.

Revenue from these six areas was 5.5% of total revenue in Q3 and was growing faster than the company. Infosys developed partnerships with AI-native companies and trained employees on their foundation models. The company was reskilling more than 3,25,000 employees on AI.

Client AI transformation programs included coding and code modernization, as well as building agents across business functions.

Infosys Topaz, its AI suite, supports enterprise growth and productivity. Topaz Fabric provides a composable stack of services and agents that allows clients to use different foundation models in a token-efficient manner, with the stated aim of maximizing value while minimizing cost and risk.

Cloud and data expertise remain foundational to AI-powered transformation. Infosys' investments in Cobalt and its Data and Analytics portfolio support its participation in this market.

Infosys reported the following financial and shareholder-return figures:

- Revenue: over US$20 billion
- Adjusted operating margin: 21%
- Free cash flow: US$3.7 billion
- Total shareholder returns through dividends and buybacks: over US$4 billion
  - Dividends: US$2.1 billion
  - Share buybacks: US$2 billion
- Total dividend announced: ₹48 per share

Infosys reported large deals worth US$14.9 billion during the year. The company also launched a focused growth program targeting large global companies where it has a smaller share of client spending.

Infosys recruited over 20,000 college graduates during the year.

The Infosys Foundation's programs in skilling and livelihoods, healthcare, and sustainability impacted over seven million people in India. Springboard provided free digital learning programs to 10 million people worldwide.

Bengaluru, May 23, 2026

Salil Parekh  
Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director
