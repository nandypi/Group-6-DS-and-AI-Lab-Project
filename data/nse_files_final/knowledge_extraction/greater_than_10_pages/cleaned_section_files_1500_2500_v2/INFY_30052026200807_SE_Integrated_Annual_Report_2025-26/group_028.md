## Energy-efficient IT infrastructure

Infosys has adopted measures to make its network and computer workloads more energy-efficient and environmentally friendly, including:

- **Energy-efficient network infrastructure:** Network equipment with power-saving features is used to improve energy efficiency.
- **Public cloud adoption:** Internal IT applications have largely transitioned to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premise data centers. Cloud-based collaboration and secure access solutions support hybrid work.
- **IT service management:** The IT service management landscape has adopted a standardized, AI-enabled service desk on a SaaS-hosted platform. This has standardized processes, increased automation, improved user experience, and introduced AI-assisted agents to support employees with daily tasks.
- **Data center modernization:** Data center modernization is a key part of the sustainability and responsible growth strategy. Density-optimized hyperscale platforms support cloud-scale agility and high-density server virtualization, reducing infrastructure sprawl and improving compute-resource utilization. Investments in Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) provide real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure, supporting data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.
- **Enterprise storage:** Internal IT provides storage services to delivery units using multi-protocol storage technology, including flash storage, fabric pool, and storage grid solutions. Storage grid technology helps reduce data center physical footprint, power consumption, and cooling requirements. Hybrid cloud storage is used for selected workloads and provides built-in data protection and seamless scaling, reducing the need for buffer investments and additional on-premise data center capacity.
- **Cloud-native development environment:** The internal IT team uses an open-source-based cloud-native development platform built on Hyper Converged Infrastructure (HCI) and compute resources. Consolidating resources through HCI reduces the physical data center footprint and hardware requirements.
