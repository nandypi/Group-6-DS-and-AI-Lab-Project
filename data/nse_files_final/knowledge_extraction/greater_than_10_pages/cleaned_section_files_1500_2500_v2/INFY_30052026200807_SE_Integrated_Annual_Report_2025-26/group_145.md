## 2.22 Employee benefits

## Gratuity and pension

The Group provides a gratuity defined benefit retirement plan covering eligible employees, primarily of Infosys and its Indian subsidiaries. The plan provides a lump-sum payment at retirement, death, incapacitation, or termination, based on salary and tenure.

Infosys contributes gratuity liabilities to the Infosys Limited Employees' Gratuity Fund Trust. Infosys BPM and EdgeVerve contribute to their respective gratuity fund trusts. The trustees administer the contributions, which are invested in a Life Insurance Corporation of India scheme as permitted by Indian law.

The Group operates defined benefit pension plans in certain overseas jurisdictions in accordance with local laws. Third-party fund managers manage these plans. Benefits include periodic post-retirement payouts and/or lump-sum payments, as well as death and disability benefits. Contributions are based on a percentage of salary that varies with employee age.

External actuaries determine liabilities for defined benefit plans at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method. These plans expose the Group to actuarial risks, including longevity, interest-rate, and market risks.

The Group recognizes the net obligation of a defined benefit plan as an asset or liability in its Balance Sheet. Remeasurement gains and losses on the net defined benefit liability/(asset) are recognized in other comprehensive income and are not subsequently reclassified to profit or loss. The actual return on plan assets exceeding the return calculated using the discount rate is also recognized in other comprehensive income. The effect of plan amendments is recognized in net profit in the Consolidated Statement of Profit and Loss.

## Provident fund

Eligible employees of Infosys receive benefits from a provident fund that is treated as a defined benefit plan. Employees and the Company make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. The Company contributes a portion to the Infosys Limited Employees' Provident Fund Trust, which invests in designated instruments permitted by Indian law. The remaining portion is contributed to a government-administered pension fund.

The Government of India administers the annual interest rate payable to beneficiaries by the trust. The Company is obligated to make good any shortfall between the return on the trust’s investments and the notified interest rate.

Eligible employees of Indian subsidiaries receive benefits from provident funds treated as defined contribution plans. Employees and the respective companies make monthly contributions equal to a specified percentage of covered salary. Amounts collected are deposited in a government-administered provident fund, and the companies have no further obligation beyond their monthly contributions.

## Superannuation

Certain employees of Infosys, Infosys BPM, and EdgeVerve participate in a defined contribution plan. The Group has no further obligations beyond its monthly contributions, which are periodically contributed to a trust fund whose corpus is invested with the Life Insurance Corporation of India.

## Compensated absences

The Group’s compensated absence policy includes accumulating and non-accumulating absences. The expected cost of accumulating compensated absences is determined by an external actuary at each Balance Sheet date using the projected unit credit method, based on the additional amount expected to be paid or availed as a result of unused entitlement accumulated at the Balance Sheet date. Expense for non-accumulating compensated absences is recognized in the period in which the absences occur.
