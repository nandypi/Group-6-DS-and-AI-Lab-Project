## From possibilities to enterprise AI impact

Infosys describes an enterprise AI approach focused on measurable outcomes in revenue, cost, customer experience, decision-making, workflow redesign, legacy modernization, security, ethics, and regulatory alignment.

The Infosys AI First Value Framework comprises six interconnected value pools and is powered by Infosys Topaz™, its generative and agentic AI suite, and Infosys Topaz Fabric™, its agentic services suite. The approach includes preparing structured and unstructured data for AI, redesigning processes around domain-aware agents, and progressively modernizing legacy systems through AI-enabled reverse engineering.

Infosys states that it is collaborating with 90% of its top 200 clients on their AI journeys, with more than 4,600 AI projects underway. It has also developed more than 30 new service offerings across the six value pools.

## Liberty Global

### From AI experiments to enterprise impact: How Liberty Global is improving customer experience with Infosys.

### Supporting 1000+ Platform Deliveries

Liberty Global, a European connectivity and entertainment provider, partners with Infosys to operate and evolve connectivity and entertainment platforms serving tens of millions of customers across Europe.

Over the past year, Infosys supported more than 1,000 platform deliveries for Liberty Global. AI-enabled solutions included:

- **Super Search**, serving approximately 8 million television customers and using large language models to support conversational content discovery across linear television, on-demand, and streaming services.
- **Agent Assist and Customer Assist**, deployed across multiple markets to enable more self-service customer journeys, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce pressure on customer-care teams.

The initiatives resulted in 50% fewer outages year over year and 60% fewer customers impacted.

The Infosys–Liberty Global partnership spans more than two decades, including more than a decade as a formal strategic partnership.

## Hertz

### Hertz modernized critical workflows 60% faster with AI-first code engineering.

### AI First Approach

Hertz relied on complex COBOL systems running on a legacy platform. Infosys analyzed nearly three million lines of legacy COBOL code and used multimodal large language models to create a cloud modernization blueprint.

Infosys applied AI-powered reverse engineering to extract core business logic and reimagine it as domain-centric Java microservices using Infosys iLEAD and GitHub Copilot. The modernization also used AI-enabled testing, reusable components, enterprise governance, and Infosys’ mainframe-to-cloud expertise. The AI stack included OpenAI, Claude Sonnet, and AWS Bedrock.

The resulting microservices-based digital core is intended to improve platform agility, scalability, and system resilience while supporting high transaction volumes on AWS Cloud. Reported outcomes include:

- 60% faster modernization timelines
- Increased reuse of cross-channel business capabilities
- Reduced hosting costs
- A scalable foundation for further modernization and automation across business domains

## Mondelēz

### Mondelēz International establishes an end-to-end traceability reporting capability with EdgeVerve AI Next.

### Enterprise Wide Traceability at Scale

Mondelēz International operates a global snack supply chain involving thousands of suppliers, hundreds of manufacturing plants, and a global distribution network. Fragmented legacy systems and siloed data created challenges for food-safety and regulatory compliance, including traceability gaps across suppliers, plants, and distribution centers.

Infosys worked with Mondelēz teams across quality, manufacturing, distribution, customer service, and IT to map vulnerabilities across production batch and quality management, bills of material, raw-material conversion, finished-goods receipt, palletizing and labeling, shipping, and downstream data management.

Infosys deployed EdgeVerve AI Next Product Traceability, a cloud-based platform that integrates near-real-time and batch data through AI Next Fabric. The platform includes:

- A graph data store providing visibility from product origin to delivery
- A centralized traceability portal supporting top-down and bottom-up tracking
- A regulatory control tower for incident response
- Prebuilt reports for compliance with the FDA Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA)
- One-touch batch and handling-unit traceability

The platform has been implemented in North America, with the European rollout planned to begin in the second half of 2026. It connects more than 50 suppliers, captures over 0.5 million events per day, integrates more than 220 plants and distribution centers, and maps more than 150 raw-material-to-finished-goods processes.
