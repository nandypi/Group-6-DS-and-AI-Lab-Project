## Notice of the 45 th Annual General Meeting

May 29, 2026

Notice is hereby given that the 45 th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the members of Infosys Limited will be held on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. IST through video conference and other audio-visual means.

## Ordinary business

## Item no. 1 - Adoption of financial statements

To consider and adopt the audited financial statements, including the consolidated financial statements, of the Company for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, together with the reports of the Board of Directors and auditors.

## Item no. 2 - Declaration of dividend

To declare a final dividend of ₹25/- per equity share for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

## Item no. 3 - Appointment of Nandan M. Nilekani as a director, liable to retire by rotation

Nandan M. Nilekani (DIN: 00041245), who is liable to retire by rotation and is eligible for reappointment, seeks reappointment as a director.

He was initially appointed on August 24, 2017, and was last appointed on June 26, 2024. Based on his performance evaluation and the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board recommends his reappointment.

The proposed ordinary resolution is:

> RESOLVED THAT pursuant to Section 152 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the approval of the members of the Company be and is hereby accorded to reappoint Nandan M. Nilekani (DIN: 00041245) as a director liable to retire by rotation.

## Special business

## Item no. 4 - Approval of the proposed amendment to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 ('Amended 2019 Plan') and grant of stock incentives to the eligible employees of the Company under the Amended 2019 Plan

The Company proposes to amend the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Program - 2019 and introduce, offer, issue, and provide share-based incentives under the amended plan, subject to applicable laws and required approvals.

The Board would be authorized to grant stock incentives to eligible persons, including:

- Permanent employees of the Company working in India or outside India.
- Directors of the Company.
- Other persons who may be eligible under applicable laws.

The following persons would be excluded:

- Promoters and members of the promoter group.
- Independent directors.
- Directors who, either directly or through a relative or body corporate, hold more than 10% of the Company’s outstanding equity shares.

Stock incentives may be granted at prices, in tranches, and on terms and conditions determined by the Board in accordance with the Amended 2019 Plan.

The proposed amendments and modifications to the existing plan are:

- **A)** Amend Section 13 to extend the period during which awards may be granted to eligible employees by a further seven years from the date of shareholder approval. The Amended 2019 Plan would consequently remain in effect for a further period of 10 years from the date of shareholder approval.
- **B)** Amend Section 6(c) relating to vesting based on performance parameters.
- **C)** Amend Section 3 to reflect the unutilized share pool available for grant.
- **D)** Make editorial and clarificatory changes and align the plan with the amended SEBI Regulations.
