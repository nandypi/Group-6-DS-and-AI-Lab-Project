## Procedure for postal ballot

The postal ballot was carried out under Sections 108 and 110 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, the rules framed thereunder, and applicable circulars issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

## Details of special resolution proposed to be transacted through postal ballot

None of the businesses proposed to be transacted at the ensuing AGM requires passing a special resolution through postal ballot.

## Legal compliance

Disclosures relating to legal compliance, including non-compliance, regulatory orders, certifications, and complaints, are provided in the corporate governance report.

## Details of non-compliance

No penalty was imposed by any stock exchange, SEBI, or the SEC. There was no instance of non-compliance with legal requirements or capital-market matters during the last three years.

## Regulatory orders

There were no material regulatory orders pertaining to the Company for fiscal 2026.

## CEO and CFO certification

The CEO and CFO certification required under the Listing Regulations is provided in this Integrated Annual Report.

## Code of Conduct and Ethics

Infosys has adopted a Code of Conduct and Ethics under the Listing Regulations and the Companies Act, 2013. The Code applies to the members of the Board, executive officers, and all employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.

All members of the Board, executive officers, and senior management affirmed compliance with the Code as of March 31, 2026. The Code is available at the [Code of Conduct and Ethics](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/codeofconduct.pdf).

## Establishment of vigil / whistleblower mechanism

The Company has established a mechanism for directors and employees to report concerns about unethical behavior, actual or suspected fraud, and violations of the Code. The mechanism includes safeguards against victimization and provides direct access to the chairperson of the Audit Committee in exceptional cases.

During the year, no person was denied access to the Audit Committee. The Whistleblower Policy is available at the [Whistleblower Policy](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/whistleblower-policy.pdf).

## Complaints pertaining to sexual harassment

Details of sexual-harassment complaints filed, disposed of, and pending during the financial year are provided in the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report of this Integrated Annual Report.

## Prevention of insider trading

The Company amended its Code of Conduct for Prohibition of Insider Trading on April 23, 2026, and its Code on Fair Disclosures and Investor Relations on April 17, 2025.

The policies and procedures regulate, monitor, and report trading by Designated Persons and their immediate relatives. They also address matters under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, including inquiries into leaks or suspected leaks of unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI). The Code is available at the [Code of Conduct for Prohibition of Insider Trading](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/code-conduct-prohibition-insider-trading.pdf).

## Compliance with discretionary requirements

The Company implemented the following nonmandatory governance requirements:

- Separate positions for the Chairman and CEO & MD, with reimbursement of expenses incurred in the performance of official duties.
- A non-executive chairperson who is not related to the CEO & MD.
- A separate office for the Chairman within the Company premises.
- Unmodified audit opinions and reporting.
- Internal auditor reporting directly to the Audit Committee.

## Other disclosures and affirmations

- The Company complied with the corporate governance requirements under Regulations 17 to 27 and the website disclosure requirements under Regulation 46(2) of the Listing Regulations.
- The securities of the Company were not suspended from trading at any time during fiscal 2026.
