## Lake rejuvenation

Extending water stewardship beyond campus boundaries, Infosys continues to implement lake rejuvenation projects in partnership with local stakeholders. Cumulative storage capacity has increased from 6 billion liters to 10.3 billion liters, adding 4.3 billion liters of freshwater capacity.

## Waste management

Infosys’ waste management strategy is based on reducing, reusing, refurbishing, repurposing, and recycling, with an ambition of zero waste to landfill. The company is strengthening waste segregation, onsite processing, and material recovery across its campuses.

Organic waste is treated onsite through biogas plants, composting, and recycling. These measures reduce offsite disposal and associated emissions and replace part of the cooking gas requirements at campuses.

E-waste includes IT equipment, mobile phones, printers, cartridges, refrigerators, microwaves, air conditioners, lithium-ion batteries, and cables. Infosys extends asset life through hardware upgrades, component replacements, and re-imaging.

Eleven campuses across Infosys India have achieved TRUE Zero Waste certification.

## Biodiversity

Infosys is strengthening biodiversity stewardship across campuses through native plantation, habitat enrichment, and knowledge sharing through the annual BioConserve event.

In FY26, Infosys launched the BioConserve platform, a digital open repository for resources, best practices, and research on biodiversity conservation.

## ASSURE

Launched in partnership with the Indian Institute for Human Settlements (IIHS) in April 2024, Accelerating Sustainable Super Efficient Real Estate (ASSURE) is a multi-stakeholder platform supporting the replication of climate-resilient, energy-efficient buildings across India. Its target is 100 million sq. ft. by 2030.

During the year, more than 15 million sq. ft. of commercial buildings across India were onboarded as lighthouse projects. More than 1,250 students and practitioners were upskilled on high-performance buildings through webinars, in-person workshops, and site visits.

The initiative brings together policymakers, developers, experts, and industry leaders to advance performance measurement, skills, policy, and financing mechanisms.

### Advancing the frontiers of energy performance

Infosys uses progressive design strategies, enhanced building envelopes, intelligent HVAC systems, renewable energy, high-performance materials with recycled content, passive design principles, and real-time monitoring to reduce energy intensity and support occupant well-being.

Campus design principles include health and wellness, social connection, sustainability, inclusivity, and technology enablement. Environmental measures include efficient interior solutions, sustainable materials, enhanced lighting and air quality, and intelligent digital systems.

Eighty-four percent of Infosys’ office spaces are certified as green buildings.

### Embedding circularity and innovation in design

Infosys integrates Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) into early-stage decision-making for material selection and design optimization.

Steel and cement account for nearly 70% of embodied carbon in office buildings. Infosys has prioritized secondary steel produced from recycled scrap, with an emission intensity of 1–1.5 tCO2e/t compared with 2.5–3.5 tCO2e/t for primary steel. Cement use is optimized through blending with industrial by-products such as fly ash and ground granulated blast furnace slag.

Infosys is advancing thermal management through radiant heating and free cooling under favorable climatic conditions. Air-conditioning equipment is procured using refrigerants with very low global warming potential.

The company has eliminated plastic- and thermocol-based packaging for IT equipment and introduced sustainable packaging criteria into procurement. Suppliers provide paper-based, recycled, and biodegradable fiber alternatives, including materials derived from agricultural waste.

### Resource circularity in operations

Infosys’ Circular Economy initiatives focus on responsible use of IT assets across their lifecycle. The company extends asset life through reuse, redeployment, and refurbishment, reducing e-waste generation and demand for new resources.

IT assets are repurposed and reused across functions and delivery centers. Assets reaching the end of their usable life are managed through responsible end-of-life processes.

## Green IT

InfosysIT has embedded sustainable practices across the life cycles of service design, operations, and disposal of IT assets.

### Data center efficiency

InfosysIT has made data center modernization a key part of its sustainability and responsible growth strategy. Density-optimized hyperscale platforms support cloud-scale agility and high-density server virtualization, reducing infrastructure sprawl and increasing compute-resource utilization.

InfosysIT has rationalized and consolidated data centers and server rooms across development centers, reducing redundancy and optimizing space usage.

Investments in Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) provide integrated, real-time visibility across IT and facilities infrastructure. These capabilities support data-driven decisions, proactive optimization, and consolidation planning.

### Infrastructure as code

Infrastructure as code supports continuous deployment, integration, and touchless lifecycle management of infrastructure components. InfosysIT has developed playbooks to automate platform-related processes and improve deployment speed and consistency.

### AIOps-powered digital operations

InfosysIT uses an AIOps platform with intelligent noise suppression and context-driven notifications. The platform provides unified observability across the infrastructure landscape, visibility into capacity usage and cost efficiency, and proactive operational support using data from diverse IT sources.

### Public cloud adoption

Infosys has largely transitioned its internal IT applications to the public cloud, enabling scalable, on-demand infrastructure and reducing reliance on on-premises data centers. Employees use cloud-based collaboration and secure-access solutions to support hybrid work.

The IT Service Management (ITSM) landscape uses a standardized, AI-enabled service desk on a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform. This has supported process standardization, automation, and AI-assisted agents across support services.
