## 6. Exercise price, purchase price or pricing formula

The exercise price for the RSUs will be equal to the par value of the shares.

## 7. Exercise period and process of exercise

The exercise period for RSUs shall be up to 90 days, except in cases of death or permanent incapacitation of the employee, where RSUs may be exercised within 365 days.

The Administrator may decide the exercise period and process of exercise, which will be specified in the respective eligible employee’s award agreement. RSUs will be deemed exercised when the Company or its appointed agent receives written or electronic notice of exercise from the person entitled to exercise them.

Subject to the award agreement, the person exercising the RSUs must make full payment for the equity shares or ADRs, together with the applicable tax amount, unless the person is eligible for and elects cashless exercise. Where decided by the Administrator, income tax may instead be deducted from the employee’s salary, in which case payment for the equity shares or ADRs need not include the tax amount.

RSUs will lapse if the employee fails to exercise them within the applicable exercise period.

## 8. The appraisal process for determining the eligibility of employees for the Amended 2019 Plan

Eligibility for RSU grants under the Amended 2019 Plan will be determined by the Administrator based on the employee’s organizational level, performance, future potential, and other criteria determined from time to time. Eligible employees will be selected based on these criteria and recommendations from Company management.

## 9. Maximum number of shares, to be issued per employee and in aggregate

- No eligible employee may be issued more than 50,00,000 equity shares of the Company, subject to adjustment for changes in the Company’s capital structure.
- Grants in any one year covering equity shares or ADRs equal to or exceeding 1% of the Company’s issued capital at the time of grant require shareholder approval by special resolution at a general meeting.
- The total number of equity shares or ADRs to be provided to employees of the Company and its subsidiaries upon exercise of RSUs under the Amended 2019 Plan may not cumulatively exceed 3,26,63,903 equity shares, subject to adjustment for changes in the Company’s capital structure.

## 10. Maximum quantum of benefits to be provided per employee under the scheme(s)

Please see paragraph 9 above.

## 11. Whether the scheme(s) is to be implemented and administered directly by the Company or through a trust

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee will administer the Amended 2019 Plan. For secondary acquisition and related administrative matters, administration may also be delegated to the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust.

## 12. Whether the scheme(s) involves fresh issue of shares by the Company or secondary acquisition by the trust or both

Equity shares may be acquired through both fresh issue and secondary acquisition by the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust, subject to regulatory approvals and the original shareholder approval at the Company’s 38th Annual General Meeting dated June 22, 2019.

## 13. The amount of provision of money (by way of grant and / or loan) to be provided for implementation of the scheme(s) by the Company to the trust - and if a loan, its tenure, utilization, repayment terms, etc.

From time to time, the Company may provide money by way of a grant or loan, or provide a guarantee or security in connection with a loan, to fund the Infosys Expanded Stock Ownership Trust. The funds may be used to purchase the Company’s equity shares from the secondary market through a recognized stock exchange for the Amended 2019 Plan. The amount provided may not exceed statutory limits.

If the Company provides a loan, the exercise price received from eligible employees upon exercise of RSUs will be used to repay the loan. Dividends received on equity shares purchased under the Amended 2019 Plan may be used as determined by the Administrator, including for secondary acquisition of shares or repayment of any loan provided by the Company. The tenure and other loan terms will be mutually agreed at the time the loan is granted.

## 15. A statement to the effect that the Company shall conform to the accounting policies specified in Regulation 15

The Company will comply with the applicable accounting policies prescribed under the SEBI Regulations, or any other policies prescribed under law for accounting for stock options, including related disclosure requirements.

## 16. The method which the Company shall use to value its options:

The Company will use the Fair Value Method to value RSUs granted for calculating employee compensation cost.

## 17. Lock-in

Shares issued upon exercise of RSUs will not be subject to a lock-in period.

## Validity period of the Amended 2019 Plan

Grants under the Amended 2019 Plan will be made over a further period of seven (7) years from the date of approval of the Amended Plan by the Company’s shareholders. The Amended 2019 Plan will remain in effect for a further period of ten (10) years from that approval date. Shareholders may extend this validity period if required.

Because the Amended 2019 Plan provides for the issue of equity shares and ADRs to persons other than the Company’s existing shareholders, shareholder consent is being sought under Section 62 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and Regulation 6 of the SEBI Regulations.

Under the SEBI Regulations, a separate resolution is required for grants of RSUs to employees of subsidiaries. Accordingly, resolutions in Item Nos. 4 and 5 are being placed separately for shareholder approval.

Promoters, promoter group members, and their relatives are not concerned or interested in these resolutions, except to the extent of their shareholding as shareholders. Directors, key managerial personnel, and their relatives may be deemed concerned or interested to the extent of RSUs granted to them and their shareholding as shareholders.

The Board of Directors recommends that shareholders pass the proposed resolutions in Item Nos. 4 and 5 as special resolutions.
