## Cybersecurity

Infosys operates with its employees in a hybrid model and monitors global cybersecurity events to maintain its cybersecurity posture and compliance.

Infosys is certified against the Information Security Management System (ISMS) Standard ISO 27001:2022. It has also been attested for SSAE 18 and ISAE 3402 SOC 1 and SOC 2 by an independent audit firm.

During fiscal 2026, Infosys strengthened its cybersecurity capabilities through technology, tools, and processes. Its initiatives included:

- Continuous cybersecurity workforce training and reskilling.
- Security culture and shared ownership.
- AI/ML security.
- Cyber resiliency.
- Secure supply chain ecosystems.
- Developer courses and resource kits to strengthen cybersecurity processes and technologies.

## Internal financial control and its adequacy

The Board has adopted policies and procedures covering:

- Orderly and efficient conduct of business.
- Adherence to Company policies.
- Safeguarding of assets.
- Prevention and detection of fraud.
- Error-reporting mechanisms.
- Accuracy and completeness of accounting records.
- Timely preparation of reliable financial disclosures.

Further details are provided in the “Internal control systems and their adequacy” section of the Management’s Discussion and Analysis.

## Secretarial standards

The Company complies with all applicable secretarial standards issued by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India.

## Listing on stock exchanges

The Company’s shares are listed on BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Its American Depositary Shares (ADSs) are listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).

## Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF)

During the year, the Company:

- Transferred unclaimed and unencashed dividends of `6,12,63,126` to the IEPF.
- Transferred 7,23,720 corresponding shares on which dividends had remained unclaimed for seven consecutive years.
- Paid `3,39,64,248` towards dividends relating to shares held by the IEPF.

Details of benefits arising from shares transferred to the IEPF, unclaimed or unencashed dividends in unpaid dividend accounts, and shares liable for transfer are provided in the Corporate Governance Report.

## Directors' responsibility statement

The financial statements are prepared in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), the provisions of the Companies Act, and guidelines issued by SEBI.

The accounts are prepared under the historical cost convention on an accrual basis, except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value and defined benefit liabilities or assets recognized at the present value of the defined benefit obligation less the fair value of plan assets.

Ind AS is prescribed under Section 133 of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 3 of the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, and subsequent amendment rules. Accounting policies have been applied consistently, except when a newly issued or revised accounting standard requires a change.

## Board's report

The directors confirm that:

- Applicable accounting standards were followed in preparing the annual accounts for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, with no material departures.

## 5. Audit reports and auditors

### Auditors

#### Statutory auditor

Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 117366 W/W-100018), was appointed as statutory auditor for a second term of five consecutive years from the conclusion of the 41st AGM held on June 25, 2022, until the conclusion of the 46th AGM to be held in 2027.

Under the mandatory auditor rotation requirements of Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Audit Committee proposed recommending BSR & Co. LLP, Chartered Accountants (Firm Registration No. 101248W/W-100022), as statutory auditor.

The Board approved the announcement of this intention at its meeting held on April 23, 2026. The proposed appointment is to be recommended to shareholders at the 46th AGM in 2027 for a first term of five consecutive years, until the conclusion of the 51st AGM in 2032. The appointment remains subject to applicable regulatory requirements, including auditor independence requirements.

#### Secretarial auditor

Makarand M. Joshi & Co., Company Secretaries (Firm Registration No. P2009MH007000), was appointed as secretarial auditor for five consecutive years from financial year 2025-26 through financial year 2029-30 at the 44th AGM held on June 25, 2025.

#### Cost records and cost audit

Maintenance of cost records and the requirement for cost audit under Section 148(1) of the Companies Act are not applicable to the business activities carried out by the Company.

#### Reporting of frauds by auditors

During fiscal 2026, neither the statutory auditor nor the secretarial auditor reported any instance of fraud committed in the Company by its officers or employees.

## 6. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Infosys undertakes CSR initiatives primarily through the Infosys Foundation. Its focus areas include:

- Education and skill development to enable sustained livelihoods.
- Healthcare, including preventive health.
- Environmental sustainability and ecological balance.
- Gender equality and women’s empowerment.
- National heritage, art, and culture.
- Rural development.
- Disaster relief and rehabilitation.

Infosys also undertakes CSR initiatives outside India in the United States, Australia, and various parts of Europe. Initiatives in the United States are carried out through Infosys Foundation USA. These initiatives are in addition to the statutory requirement.

The annual report on CSR activities is included as Annexure 6 to the Board’s report.
