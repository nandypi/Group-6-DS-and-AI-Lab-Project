## B) Login method for e-voting and voting during the meeting for shareholders other than individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode and shareholders holding securities in physical mode

### How to Log-in to NSDL e-voting website?

1. Visit the NSDL e-voting website at [www.evoting.nsdl.com](https://www.evoting.nsdl.com/).
2. Select **Login** under **Shareholder/Member**.
3. Enter the User ID, Password or OTP, and verification code. Shareholders registered for NSDL e-services through IDeAS may log in at [https://eservices.nsdl.com/](https://eservices.nsdl.com/), select e-voting, and proceed to cast their vote.

User ID formats are as follows:

| Manner of holding shares | User ID |
|---|---|
| Demat account with NSDL | 8-character DP ID followed by the 8-digit Client ID. |
| Demat account with CDSL | 16-digit Beneficiary ID. |
| Physical mode | EVEN Number followed by the Folio Number registered with the Company. |

Shareholders who are already registered for e-voting may use their existing password. First-time users must retrieve the initial password communicated to them and change it when prompted.

If the email ID is registered with the depository or the Company, the initial password is sent by email as a PDF attachment. The PDF password is:

- The 8-digit Client ID for an NSDL account.
- The last 8 digits of the Client ID for a CDSL account.
- The Folio Number for physical shares.

If the email ID is not registered, shareholders should follow the process described under [Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories](#process-for-those-shareholders-whose-email-ids-are-not-registered-with-the-depositories-for-procuring-user-id-and-password-and-registration-of-e-mail-ids-for-e-voting-for-the-resolutions-set-out-in-this-notice).

If the password cannot be retrieved or has been forgotten:

- Demat shareholders may select **Forgot User Details / Password?**
- Physical shareholders may select **Physical User Reset Password?**
- Shareholders may use OTP-based login.
- After entering the password, select **Agree with Terms and Conditions**, then select **Login**.

### Step 2: Cast your vote electronically on NSDL e-voting system

1. After logging in, view the companies and their **EVENs** for which voting is available.
2. Select the Company’s EVEN during the remote e-voting period.
3. Select assent or dissent and verify or modify the number of shares for which the vote is being cast.
4. Select **Submit**, then **Confirm** when prompted.
5. A successful vote confirmation message will be displayed.
6. A printout of the votes cast may be taken from the confirmation page.
7. Once a vote on a resolution is confirmed, it cannot be modified.

## General guidelines for shareholders

1. Institutional shareholders must provide a scanned copy in PDF or JPG format of the relevant Board Resolution, authority letter, or other authorization document, together with the attested specimen signature of the authorized signatory or signatories. These documents may also be uploaded through the **Upload Board Resolution / Authority Letter** option under the e-voting tab.
2. Shareholders should keep their passwords confidential. Login access is disabled after five unsuccessful password attempts. Passwords can then be reset using the applicable password-recovery option.
3. Shareholders may refer to the shareholder FAQs and e-voting user manual available in the download section of the NSDL e-voting website.

## Process for those shareholders whose email ids are not registered with the depositories for procuring user id and password and registration of e mail ids for e-voting for the resolutions set out in this notice:

1. For physical shares, provide the Folio Number, shareholder name, a scanned copy of the share certificate showing the front and back, a self-attested scanned copy of the PAN card, and a self-attested scanned copy of the Aadhaar card.
2. For demat shares, provide the 16-digit DPID-CLID or Beneficiary ID, name, client master or Consolidated Account Statement, a self-attested scanned copy of the PAN card, and a self-attested scanned copy of the Aadhaar card.
3. Shareholders may alternatively request their user ID and password from NSDL by providing the required documents.
4. Under the SEBI circular dated December 9, 2020, individual shareholders holding securities in demat mode may vote through their demat accounts maintained with depositories and depository participants. Their mobile number and email ID must be correctly updated in the demat account to access the e-voting facility.
