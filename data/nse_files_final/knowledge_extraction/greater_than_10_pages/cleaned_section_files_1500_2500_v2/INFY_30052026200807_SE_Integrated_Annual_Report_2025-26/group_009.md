Infosys’ intellectual capital is developed through collaboration with experts, partners, academia, and other stakeholders. The company develops products and services, strengthens Tech for Good solutions, and incubates new businesses and startup-led offerings.

Its intellectual-capital portfolio includes iCETS, Living Labs, the Infosys Innovation Network, and the Infosys Business Incubator. The Infosys Prize and Aarohan Social Innovation Awards provide platforms for innovators and social entrepreneurs.

## Infosys Center for Emerging Technology Solutions (iCETS)

iCETS is Infosys’ emerging-technology incubation unit. It delivers next-generation platforms and innovation-as-a-service powered by New Emerging and eXploratory Technologies (NEXT).

As generative AI scales from experimentation to enterprise deployment, iCETS enables enterprises to apply emerging technologies through a structured, outcome-driven approach.

Its innovation framework is organized into three stages: Now, Near, and Next.

## Now

Infosys uses reusable platforms, accelerators, and integration capabilities to operationalize AI at enterprise scale. The core of the Now strategy is Infosys Topaz Fabric, which provides composable and interoperable AI agents, tools, services, and models.

The platform is intended to help organizations optimize existing IT processes, automate or eliminate manual tasks, and build on existing investments through a platform-of-platforms approach.

## Near

### Living Labs

Infosys Living Labs are global innovation hubs where clients, partners, startups, and academia co-create solutions using emerging technologies. They support contextualization, experimentation, iteration, and real-world validation in controlled environments.

Client Living Labs deliver innovation-as-a-service through three flagship programs:

- IncubateNext: scalable, real-world innovation
- ImagineNext: future opportunity discovery
- SenseNext: converting early market and technology signals into action

## Nurturing innovation

Be The Navigator (BTN) provides a structured framework for ideation, prototyping, and incubation. A total of 12,435 BTN ideas were submitted, 2,429 implementations were completed, and 1,101 were in progress.

## Infosys Innovation Network (IIN)

The Infosys Innovation Network is a partnership involving selected startups, universities, hyperscalers, and Infosys. It focuses on emerging technology areas including AI, generative AI, data, fintech, cloud, cybersecurity, and InsurTech.

IIN includes more than 430 startups, which have generated more than 1,300 client impressions.

## Infosys Business Incubator (IBI)

The Infosys Business Incubator develops and scales entrepreneurship capabilities within Infosys through next-generation micro-platform and micro-service-line businesses. It curates new business cohorts annually.

IBI has engaged with more than 1,000 entrepreneurs. Five ventures were selected for incubation from cohort 1 and three from cohort 2.

The Be A Maker program supports the development and showcasing of ideas in a phygital format. More than 200 ideas have been generated and five phygital showcases have been created for clients.

## Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI)

The Infosys Knowledge Institute is the business research and thought-leadership arm of Infosys. It works with Infosys experts and clients to produce research and insights on the business impact of technology.

IKI conducts primary research, including large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis. Its publications include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology-trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals.

Since inception, IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets.

## Product innovation

Infosys Topaz AI Next is a unified AI platform designed to orchestrate complex, multiagent enterprise workflows across people, AI agents, and enterprise systems. ## IP, patents and trademarks

Infosys develops platforms, products, and tools that form part of its intellectual-property portfolio. These assets are available through the Infosys Marketplace and are used for market differentiation and productivity enhancement.

The portfolio includes 973 patents granted or pending. More than 900 trademarks are registered or pending across 51 countries.

Infosys is regarded by Indian authorities as a “Well Known” trademark.

## Advancing climate positivity

In FY26, Infosys continued its transition from carbon neutrality to climate positivity by integrating energy efficiency, clean energy, carbon sequestration, and regenerative nature-based solutions.

## 1. Energy efficiency and lowcarbon approach

Infosys incorporates energy efficiency across the life cycle of its buildings. Its campuses use radiant cooling, radiant heating, climate-adaptive cooling, intelligent controls, and climate-responsive design to reduce operational energy demand.

A total of 84% of Infosys’ office space is certified to the highest green-building standards.

## 2. Renewable energy

Infosys has installed 62.42 MWp of solar photovoltaic capacity through rooftop and ground-mounted systems.

The company also procures renewable power through third-party power-purchase agreements and green-tariff mechanisms, adapting its sourcing strategy to regulatory conditions across geographies.

## 3. Decarbonizing the supply chain

Infosys works with its supplier ecosystem to decarbonize the value chain, with a focus on high-emission IT hardware suppliers. Activities include onsite assessments, senior-level discussions, support for Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions measurement, establishment of emissions baselines, and adoption of verified Environmental Product Declarations.

## 4. Carbon offsets and regeneration

Infosys addresses unavoidable emissions through community-focused carbon-offset programs, including clean cookstove initiatives in Rajasthan and biogas projects in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

The company has planted approximately 14 million saplings across multiple states through its agroforestry program. These plantations support carbon sequestration, soil and water resilience, and rural livelihoods.

The initiatives have benefited more than 300,000 rural families and created more than 4,800 local jobs.

## Water stewardship

Infosys focuses on reducing freshwater withdrawal, increasing water reuse, and improving water availability in communities where it operates. The company is a signatory to the UN CEO Water Mandate.

All wastewater across Infosys campuses is recycled. The company also uses rainwater harvesting and demand-side efficiency measures to reduce reliance on external water sources.

The Bangalore campus is certified as water positive. During the year, nine Infosys campuses in India achieved Scope 1 certification related to water-positive or water-neutral status under NITI Aayog guidelines.
