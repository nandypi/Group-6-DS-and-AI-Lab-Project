## 3.  Branding

Brand Infosys is described as a key intangible asset and was ranked among the world's Top 100 brands in FY26. The brand positions Infosys as an enterprise AI partner, supported by its experience managing global enterprise systems and its capabilities in AI, cloud, digital technologies, continuous learning, and innovation.

Infosys' global marketing reach includes digital-first campaigns and strategic digital innovation partnerships with the Australian Open, Roland-Garros, ATP, The International Tennis Hall of Fame, Madison Square Garden properties, and the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship. Strategic partnerships also include Dow Jones, The Economist Group, Financial Times, and Handelsblatt Media Group. Infosys organizes Confluence, a client event series across the US, Europe, and APAC, along with business and industry events and CXO roundtables.

## 5.  Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI)

The Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI) focuses on:

- Succession planning
- Leadership bench strength
- Development of title holders
- Strengthening inclusivity in leadership

ILI delivered the AI for Business Growth program in partnership with Kellogg. More than 1,500 senior leaders across Infosys completed the program over the last two years. A senior-leader AI immersion program provided hands-on experience with AI tools, including Infosys Topaz Fabric. Executive leadership programs were also delivered in partnership with Harvard Business School and MIT through weeklong residential cohorts at the Mysuru campus.

ILI strengthened enterprise-wide succession planning through a structured, data-led approach, including regular reviews, leadership profiles, personalized development plans, executive education, and external coaching. The Constellation program was refreshed to align with enterprise priorities. During the year, leaders completed a 10-month program with Oxford Saïd Business School and executed 12 cross-functional projects involving strategically critical enterprise matters.

Development programs for title holders included:

- The 10-month Enterprise Leadership Program for AVPs
- The Executive Communication program for VPs, in partnership with The Economist
- The InFusion program for new title holders

Infosys leaders collectively invested more than 10,000 leadership learning days annually during each of the last two years. ILI recorded a Net Promoter Score of more than 96% for the third consecutive year and was recognized as the top unit on Internal Partner Satisfaction for three consecutive years.

The #IamtheFuture program continued to develop the leadership pipeline through multi-year, role-specific development journeys. It covered more than 700 leaders and was redesigned to differentiate development experiences across career stages, with emphasis on application, simulations, and contextual learning.

ILI was recognized as the Best Business Enabling Function (small) for 2025-26 at the Infosys Awards for Excellence.

## 6.  Infosys Knowledge Institute

The Infosys Knowledge Institute (IKI) is the business research and thought leadership arm of Infosys. It uses the expertise of Infosys professionals and client collaborations to develop content and insights on the business impact of technology. IKI also conducts primary research, including large-scale surveys and quantitative analysis.

IKI's publications include Radar maturity reports, the annual Tech Navigator report, the TechCompass technology trends series, industry-focused market outlooks, and executive journals. Since inception, IKI has collaborated with more than 1,000 clients and created more than 2,000 assets.

## 7. ESG vision and ambitions

Infosys launched its ESG Vision 2030 in October 2020. In fiscal 2025, at the midpoint of its 2030 goals, the company launched the ESG Vision 2030 Refresh to address changing stakeholder priorities.

Infosys remained carbon neutral across its global operations for the seventh consecutive year. Its refreshed 2030 ambitions include:

- Becoming climate positive by implementing initiatives across Scope 1, 2, and 3
- Reducing its own carbon footprint and sequestering more carbon from the atmosphere than its emissions
- Engaging clients on climate action through Infosys solutions
- Maintaining 100% recycling of wastewater
- Sequestering more water than it consumes year-on-year
- Achieving zero waste to landfill
- Digitally skilling more than 18 million people
- Enabling employment opportunities for more than 500,000 people
- Expanding technology-for-good initiatives, CSR efforts, employee volunteering, healthcare initiatives, and women empowerment programs
- Supporting employee experience and recognition as an employer in key operating regions

Infosys also stated that it would continue focusing on an empowered, diverse, and inclusive board, leading data privacy standards, and information security practices.

## Corporate governance report

Corporate governance guides how a company is directed and how it manages relationships with stakeholders. Infosys states that its governance framework is intended to support trust, transparency, accountability, long-term capital, economic growth, and financial stability.

## Our corporate governance philosophy

Infosys' corporate governance reflects its culture, policies, and stakeholder relationships. The Company states that its affairs are managed fairly and transparently through ethical business decisions, adherence to values, and attention to stakeholder expectations.

The governance framework is based on the C-LIFE values:

- Client value
- Leadership by example
- Integrity and transparency
- Fairness
- Excellence

It is also guided by the principles of the OECD (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development).

The framework is reinforced through the Company's Code of Conduct and Ethics, corporate governance guidelines, and committee charters. Board and management processes, audits, and internal control systems reflect the governance framework. The report provides an overview of how Infosys' governance adheres to its seven-pillar governance framework.
