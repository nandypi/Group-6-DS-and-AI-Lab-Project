## Our Purpose

To amplify human potential and create the next opportunity for people, businesses and communities.

## Our Values

Infosys’ Code of Conduct is founded on the C-LIFE values:

- **Client value:** To surpass client expectations consistently.
- **Leadership by example:** To set standards in business and transactions and be an exemplar for the industry and the Company.
- **Integrity and transparency:** To be ethical, sincere, and open in all transactions.
- **Fairness:** To be objective and transaction-oriented, thereby earning trust and respect.
- **Excellence:** To strive relentlessly, constantly improve, and develop the Company’s teams, services, and products.
