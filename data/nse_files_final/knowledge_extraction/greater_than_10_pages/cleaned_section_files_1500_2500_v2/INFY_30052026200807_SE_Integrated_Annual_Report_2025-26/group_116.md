The final dividend proposed in the previous year, declared and paid by the Company during the year, was in accordance with Section 123 of the Companies Act, as applicable.

The interim dividend declared and paid by the Company during the year and until the date of the report complied with Section 123 of the Act.

The Board of Directors proposed a final dividend for the year, subject to approval by the members at the ensuing Annual General Meeting. The proposed dividend complies with Section 123 of the Act, as applicable.

Based on audit examination, including test checks, the Company and its subsidiaries incorporated in India used accounting software for the financial year ended March 31, 2026 that included an audit-trail or edit-log facility. The facility operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions recorded in the systems. The auditors did not identify any instance of tampering with the audit trail. The Parent Company and the relevant Indian subsidiaries preserved the audit trail in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements.

The financial statements of five subsidiaries that were not material to the Group's Consolidated Financial Statements had not been audited under the Act as of the report date. Accordingly, the auditors were unable to comment on the reporting requirement under Rule 11(g) of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014 for those subsidiaries.

With respect to paragraphs 3(xxi) and 4 of the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020 (CARO), the auditors reported that, for companies whose audits under Section 143 of the Act had been completed, no qualifications or adverse remarks had been reported based on the available auditor's reports.
