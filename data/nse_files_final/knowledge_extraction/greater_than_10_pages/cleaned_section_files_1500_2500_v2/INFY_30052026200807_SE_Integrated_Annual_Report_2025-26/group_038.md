## Governance of Board committees

The Board, in consultation with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, is responsible for assigning and determining the terms of service for committee members.

The Chairman of the Board, in consultation with the Company Secretary and the respective committee chairperson, determines the frequency of committee meetings. Generally, committees meet four times a year. Committee recommendations are submitted to the Board for approval. During the year, all committee recommendations were approved by the Board.

The quorum for meetings is the higher of two members or one-third of the total number of committee members.

## Objectives of the Committee

The primary objectives of the Committee are to assist the Board with oversight of:

1. The accuracy, integrity, and transparency of the Company's financial statements, including adequate and timely disclosures.
2. Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.
3. The professional qualifications and independence of the Company's independent auditors.
4. The performance of the Company's independent auditors and internal auditors.
5. Acquisitions and investments made by the Company.

## Audit Committee Charter ('the Charter')

Infosys is listed on BSE Limited, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited, and the New York Stock Exchange in the United States.

Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations in India and the Blue Ribbon Committee established by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission mandate that listed companies adopt an appropriate charter. The Audit Committee is guided by the Charter adopted by the Board.

The Charter is available at [https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/audit-committee-charter.pdf](https://www.infosys.com/investors/corporate-governance/documents/audit-committee-charter.pdf).

## Process adopted by the Committee to fulfill its objectives

The Committee:

- Ensures an effective and independent internal audit function that provides assurance regarding the adequacy and effectiveness of internal controls.
- Reviews processes intended to safeguard Company assets, use Company resources effectively and efficiently, and record transactions accurately and promptly.
- Meets the independent auditors at the end of each quarter and financial year to discuss key observations relating to the relevant financial statements.
- Provides an independent communication channel for the Chief Compliance Officer, internal auditors, and independent auditors.
- Invites Management members and, at its discretion, external legal, financial, and technical experts to provide clarification, advice, and guidance.
- Provides periodic feedback and reports to the Board.
- Periodically reviews its Charter, structure, processes, and membership, and recommends changes to the Board for approval.
- Meets at least four times a year, with no more than 120 days between two meetings.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises four members and fulfills the requirements of:

- The Audit Committee Charter
- Sections 149 and 177 of the Companies Act, 2013
- Regulation 18 of the Listing Regulations
- NYSE guidelines, as applicable

The Committee relies on the financial expertise of Management, the internal auditors, and the independent auditors to carry out its responsibilities efficiently and transparently.

Management is responsible for internal control over financial reporting and the financial reporting process. The independent auditors are responsible for independently auditing the Company's financial statements in accordance with Generally Accepted Auditing Principles and issuing an audit report.
