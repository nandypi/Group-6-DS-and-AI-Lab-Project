## Risk management report

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Contractual liabilities | Risk of clients demanding more favorable terms, including onerous liability clauses, and the risk of inability to adhere to contractual obligations. These could lead to litigation, fines, and adverse impacts on reputation, revenue, and profitability. Impacted capitals: Social & Relationship and Financial. | Client engagement on contractual terms through a dedicated in-house team; a contract legal playbook and risk framework to identify high-risk clauses and plan controls; a multi-layered contract approval process; dedicated teams to adhere to, monitor, and audit contractual obligations; and Board-level monitoring, reporting, and governance of contractual risks. |
| Complex and evolving regulatory environment | Failure to comply with the complex regulatory landscape, including immigration, wages, tax, and sanctions, could result in investigations, regulatory inquiries, litigation, fines, and negative client sentiment. Emerging risks include evolving regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and public disclosure requirements, which add uncertainty to compliance policies. Impacted capitals: Financial, Human, Intellectual, Social & Relationship, and Natural. | A dedicated in-house compliance team working with external partners; a compliance framework, policies, controls, and programs to identify regulatory changes, assess their impact, and implement actions; regular engagement with regulatory authorities and compliance experts; contract-awareness programs and training for employees and vendors; periodic compliance certification with defined accountability; and monitoring, reporting, governance, and Board oversight. |

## Cybersecurity risk management

Cyber risks are managed through multi-layered controls using a defense-in-depth approach based on cybersecurity strategy, policies, processes, and preventive, detective, and corrective controls.

The cybersecurity strategy focuses on four areas:

- Transparency and experience
- Continual improvement and compliance
- Cyber resilience
- Building and maintaining a positive cybersecurity culture within the organization

The enterprise Information Security Council (ISC), a high-level working group, governs and oversees Infosys’ Information Security Management System (ISMS). The ISC establishes, directs, monitors, and executes the information security program, with representation from various departments and business units. It reports key risks to executive leadership through the Operational Risk Council.

## INDEPENDENT PRACTITIONER'S REASONABLE ASSURANCE REPORT ON IDENTIFIED SUSTAINABILITY INFORMATION IN INFOSYS LIMITED'S BUSINESS RESPONSIBILITY AND SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

## To the Board of Directors of INFOSYS LIMITED

The reasonable assurance engagement was undertaken for Infosys Limited under an engagement letter dated January 16, 2026, covering the identified sustainability information, or BRSR Core indicators, in the Company’s Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) included in the Integrated Annual Report for the year ended March 31, 2026. The engagement was conducted by a multidisciplinary team including assurance practitioners, environmental engineers, and specialists.

## 2. Identified Sustainability Information

The scope of reasonable assurance consists of the BRSR Core indicators listed in Appendix I to the report. The BRSR reporting boundary is disclosed in Question 13 of Section A: General Disclosure of the BRSR, including applicable exceptions disclosed in notes under the respective questions.

The engagement covered information for the year ended March 31, 2026, unless otherwise stated. No procedures were performed on other elements of the BRSR, and no opinion was expressed on those elements.

## 3. Criteria

The criteria used by the Company to prepare the identified sustainability information were:

- Regulation 34(2)(f) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015, as amended.
- Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting Requirements for listed entities under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026.
