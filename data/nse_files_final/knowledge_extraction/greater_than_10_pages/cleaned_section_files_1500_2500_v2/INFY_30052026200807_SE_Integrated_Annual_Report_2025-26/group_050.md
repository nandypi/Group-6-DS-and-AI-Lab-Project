## Salient features of our Enterprise Risk Management program

Our enterprise risk management program uses methods to identify risks, evaluate potential impact, and promote risk awareness across the organization.

## Highlights of fiscal 2026

During fiscal 2026, risk management initiatives focused on service delivery, transparent stakeholder communication, social responsibility, employee safety and health, and strengthening risk culture. The Risk Office assessed, monitored, and reported on risks related to geopolitical scenarios, economic uncertainty, inflation, artificial intelligence, technology disruption and innovation, talent availability, cybersecurity, data protection and privacy, ESG, contractual liabilities, and complex and evolving regulation.

The key and emerging risks and the Company's approach to mitigating them are described below.

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Geopolitical, macroeconomic or health events | Revenues are concentrated in a few geographies and client industries, and a large part of revenue depends on a limited number of clients. Adverse geopolitical, economic, or health events may cause currency volatility and reduced spending on technology products and services, affecting demand, growth, and profitability. **Emerging risk aspect:** Dynamic geopolitical, economic, and health events, including government policy changes such as tariffs and trade restrictions, can make impacts difficult to predict and assess. **Impacted capitals:** Financial, Social & Relationship, and Human. | Broad-based growth to reduce concentration in any single region, client, or industry; monitoring of geopolitical and macroeconomic scenarios; additional controls to build operational agility; currency hedging practices; business continuity protocols and resilience measures in affected regions; hybrid working model; diversified supply chain; acquisitions to expand into newer areas or regions. **Opportunity:** Clients are seeking cost takeout projects and vendor consolidation. |

## Risk management report

| Key / Emerging risks | Impact on Company | Mitigation / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Artificial Intelligence (AI) risks | Failure to transform existing services using AI or scale new AI-first service offerings could affect win rates, market share, revenue, and profits. **Impacted capitals:** Financial and Intellectual. | Differentiation of services through innovation and industry solutions leveraging AI; continued investment to identify, incubate, develop, and launch AI-first offerings; proactive measures to identify and mitigate operational issues; AI talent transformation strategy; development of deep AI engineering expertise. |
| Global Capability Centers (GCCs) risks | Customers may insource or establish or expand GCCs for services provided by Infosys, reducing addressable market share and affecting growth and profitability. **Impacted capitals:** Financial and Intellectual. | Collaboration and co-creation for GCC requirements; scaling a GCC practice for assisted GCC build, build-operate-transfer, captive carveouts, and other engagement models; an AI-first model to transform GCCs into innovation hubs. |
| Technology disruption and innovation | **Emerging risk:** Inadequate frameworks or approaches to identify, invest in, incubate, and operationalize services aligned with changes in AI, client preferences, and market expectations could disrupt the value proposition and reduce relevance to customers, affecting revenue and profitability. The speed and nature of technological change make trends difficult to predict. **Impacted capitals:** Financial, Human, and Intellectual. | Executive-led innovation council; innovation showcases with clients, industry forums, alliances, partners, and academia; research and development investments; reskilling in newer technologies and methodologies; large-deal investments to drive innovation, productivity, and industry-domain expertise; alliances or partnerships with emerging technology firms. **Opportunity:** Identify, develop, and deploy offerings leveraging next-generation technologies. |
| Talent supply constraints | As of March 31, 2026, Infosys had 3,28,594 employees across the globe. The Company's success depends on skilled technology, AI, and management talent. Difficulties attracting, hiring, engaging, motivating, reskilling, and retaining talent; managing leadership succession; reshaping the AI-driven talent model; protecting human rights; or evolving the hybrid work model could affect reputation, project staffing, execution of large and complex programs, and cost structures. **Impacted capitals:** Financial, Human, Intellectual, and Manufactured. | Recruitment and employee onboarding improvements; employee engagement and support initiatives; retention and recognition efforts; career and leadership development programs; a hybrid operating model balancing client requirements, employee preferences, legal requirements, and information security risks; redesigned career architecture; a future-ready workforce supported by global learning infrastructure. |
| Cybersecurity | Cyber threats evolve rapidly, and failure to adapt threat detection and prevention measures could result in attacks that breach information networks. Failure to protect sensitive and confidential stakeholder information in accordance with applicable laws and contractual obligations could adversely affect operations and client satisfaction and result in significant regulatory penalties. **Impacted capitals:** Financial, Human, Intellectual, and Manufactured. | Robust cybersecurity strategy, framework, processes, policies, and controls; multilayered governance with executive and Board oversight; continued investment in technologies addressing evolving cyber threats; collaboration with cyber intelligence and forensic consultants; periodic tabletop exercises; an updated cybersecurity crisis plan; cybersecurity awareness programs and training with consequence management; security-by-design training; encryption, data backup, and recovery mechanisms; a Cyber Risk Assessment Framework aligned with ISO 31000, ISO 27001, and ISO 27005. **Opportunity:** Cybersecurity services for customers. |
| Data protection and privacy | Failure to protect personal and sensitive stakeholder information in accordance with applicable laws could affect operations or result in significant regulatory penalties. **Impacted capitals:** Financial, Human, and Intellectual. | Robust data privacy framework, policies, processes, and controls; employee training on privacy by design and data protection and privacy awareness, including geographical nuances; multilayered governance with executive and Board oversight; continuous monitoring of the regulatory landscape. |
| Cost inflation / Inability to improve margin | Failure to operate with sustainable cost levers could adversely affect operations and long-term profitability. **Impacted capital:** Financial. | Effective operations with sustainable cost optimization and margin improvement levers; automation and a planned capital expenditure program focused on technology adoption and efficiency. |
| Environment, Social, Governance (ESG) ambition | Reputation, access to capital, and longer-term financial stability could be at risk if stated goals under the ESG 2030 Vision are not met. Climate change risks are increasingly manifested as strategic, physical, transitional, market, and compliance risks; inadequate management could affect reputation and profitability. **Emerging risk aspect:** ESG expectations may continue to change due to evolving stakeholder expectations and regulatory requirements. | ESG 2030 ambitions and execution roadmap with dedicated owners; Board-level governance and oversight through a dedicated ESG Committee of the Board; periodic risk assessments; alignment of ESG ambitions with strategy; regular regulatory monitoring. **Opportunity:** Climate change-related solutions and services for customers. For more details, visit [Infosys ESG Vision 2030](https://www.infosys.com/about/esg/esg-vision-2030.html).
