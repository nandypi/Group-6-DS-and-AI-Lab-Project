### 4. Resource-efficiency and emissions-impact initiatives

| S. No. | Initiative undertaken | Details of the initiative | Outcome of the initiative |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Energy efficiency improvement in buildings | Radiant heating uses existing radiant panels to heat surfaces that radiate heat into the room, reducing the need for conventional high-intensity heating. Free cooling uses naturally cool water generated by cooling towers in favorable climates. The cooled water is circulated through a plate heat exchanger and supplied to a dedicated free-cooling coil in air-handling units, reducing mechanical cooling. | Designed for one office location, the system has the potential to reduce energy consumption by approximately 70% compared with traditional resistance heating. The strategy contributed to an approximately 15% reduction in the building’s annual HVAC load and is expected to reduce energy consumption and Scope 2 emissions. |
| 2 | Supply-chain decarbonization initiatives | Climate action and sustainable packaging requirements were integrated into IT hardware procurement. Suppliers were required to disclose verified Product Carbon Footprints, climate commitments, and detailed packaging-material information along with technical specifications. | In fiscal 2026 laptop procurement, the lowest-scoring supplier was excluded and purchase volumes were allocated proportionately to higher-performing suppliers. This resulted in avoided emissions of 8,955 tCO₂e against a standard product specification. |
| 3 | Supply-chain decarbonization initiatives | Green material standards were institutionalized to support the Net Zero journey. The specifications address embodied carbon, energy and water efficiency, responsible material sourcing, elimination of hazardous substances, circular-economy practices, and indoor environmental quality. | Standardized sustainable specifications were established for steel, concrete, glazing, furniture, HVAC systems, electrical equipment, IT hardware, landscaping elements, and other materials. |
| 4 | Smart water metering | Meters were installed in water pipes at distribution and consumption points to record flow rates in real time. | Smart water metering systems installed at most campuses helped identify undetected leaks, reduce unaccounted water, identify savings opportunities, and provide insights for new designs. |
