## Leadership indicators

1. **Stakeholder consultation and Board engagement:** Infosys identified material issues through a data-driven and consultative exercise. Topics were shortlisted and prioritized based on their impact on stakeholders and the business. Periodic meetings were held to update Board sub-committees.

2. **Use of stakeholder consultation:** Yes. Material topics were shortlisted and prioritized based on their impact on stakeholders and the business. The material topics and linked ambitions are described in the Infosys ESG Vision 2030 and annual ESG Reports.

3. **Engagement with vulnerable or marginalized stakeholder groups:** Infosys Foundation supports underprivileged sections of society, creates opportunities, and works toward a more equitable society. Infosys contributes to the Foundation through its CSR initiatives. The Foundation engages with communities, particularly vulnerable and marginalized groups, across several focus areas. The percentage of beneficiaries of CSR projects is referenced under Principle 8, Q.6 (Leadership Indicators).

## 2. Details of minimum wages paid to employees and workers

Infosys operates in 59 countries, with employees hired across geographies. Legal minimum wages vary by country and, in some countries, by state. They may depend on parameters including tenure, role, location, and citizenship status. Infosys has defined processes to ensure employees are paid according to applicable local regulations and laws.
