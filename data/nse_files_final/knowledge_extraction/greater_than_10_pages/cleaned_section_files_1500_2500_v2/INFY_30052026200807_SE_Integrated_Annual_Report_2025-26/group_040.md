## Corporate Social Responsibility Committee

1. Govind Iyer, Chairperson
2. Chitra Nayak
3. Michael Gibbs

## Our CSR philosophy

The Company’s Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) activities encompass community development, institution-building, and sustainability-related initiatives, in addition to philanthropy.

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

The primary objective of the Committee is to assist the Board in fulfilling its corporate social responsibility. The Committee is responsible for:

1. Identifying areas of CSR activity.
2. Recommending expenditure for identified CSR activities.
3. Evaluating CSR proposals received from the Company.
4. Implementing and monitoring the CSR Policy.
5. Formulating a CSR annual action plan and recommending it to the Board.
6. Reviewing the Company’s CSR initiatives and programs.
7. Coordinating with Infosys Foundation and agencies such as registered trusts, societies, or Section 8 companies to implement programs and initiatives under the CSR Policy.
8. Reviewing impact assessment reports issued by independent agencies for the Company’s projects.
9. Reviewing CSR reporting and disclosures required under applicable statutes.
10. Reviewing certificates issued for utilization of CSR funds earmarked for specific themes or projects.

## Committee governance

The Committee comprises three independent directors and fulfills the requirements of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the CSR Committee Charter.

The CSR Committee identifies CSR activities, programs, and initiatives under defined guidelines and oversees the activities and functioning of Infosys Foundation, Infosys Foundation USA, and other initiatives undertaken by the Company, including in Australia and various parts of Europe. The Foundations report to the Committee on the progress of deployed initiatives and assist in making periodic disclosures.

The CSR Committee met four times during fiscal 2026.

## CSR report

The CSR report for the year ended March 31, 2026, as required under the Companies Act, 2013, is attached as Annexure 6 to the Board’s report.

The Committee periodically reviewed and approved the budget and disbursement of funds. The Committee ensures that at least 2% of the average net profits made during the three immediately preceding financial years is allocated for CSR activities in India.

During fiscal 2026:

- The Company spent ₹558.04 crore on various CSR projects.
- An unspent balance of ₹19.00 crore related to ongoing projects will be transferred to the unspent CSR account and spent in accordance with the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014, and amendments thereunder.
- The Company spent an additional ₹9.21 crore on ongoing projects from fiscals 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- Aggregate CSR expenditure outside India was ₹43.03 crore. Spending in the US, Australia, and various parts of Europe was over and above the statutory requirement in India.

## ESG Committee

The ESG Committee was constituted with effect from April 14, 2021. It comprises only independent directors.

As of March 31, 2026, the ESG Committee comprised:

1. Chitra Nayak, Chairperson
2. Govind Iyer
3. Helene Auriol Potier

## Objectives and responsibilities of the Committee

1. Guide the creation and periodic refresh of the Company’s ESG Vision and Ambitions and incorporate updates to the ESG vision and goals.
2. Review the operation and working of the ESG Council. The Committee may form sub-committees and delegate authority as appropriate.
3. Ensure that the Company implements appropriate measures to advance its ESG objectives and provide access to internal information necessary to fulfill its duties.
4. Oversee periodic ESG disclosures and communications, including annual report sections, ESG reports, and Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) filings.
5. Obtain advice and assistance from internal or external experts and advisors.
6. Report periodically to the Board on matters reviewed and decisions or recommendations made.
