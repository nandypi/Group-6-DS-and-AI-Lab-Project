## Annexure 2 - Particulars of contracts / arrangements made with related parties

*Pursuant to Clause (h) of sub-section (3) of Section 134 of the Companies Act, 2013, and Rule 8(2) of the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014 - AOC-2*

This Form pertains to the disclosure of particulars of contracts, arrangements, or transactions entered into by the Company with related parties referred to in sub-section (1) of Section 188 of the Companies Act, 2013, including certain arm’s-length transactions under the third proviso thereto.

## Details of contracts or arrangements or transactions not at arm's length basis

There were no contracts, arrangements, or transactions entered into during the year ended March 31, 2026, that were not at arm’s length.

## Details of material contracts or arrangement or transactions at arm's length basis

There were no material contracts, arrangements, or transactions entered into during the year ended March 31, 2026.

There were no material contracts, arrangements, or transactions requiring disclosure under the Companies Act, 2013. However, the Company obtained shareholder approval for material related-party transactions in accordance with Regulation 23 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Details are available in the [2025 AGM notice](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/documents/agm-notice2025.pdf).

## Annexure 3 - Particulars of employees

Infosys is a provider of AI-first business consulting and technology services, serving organizations across 59 countries. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee reviews the compensation of the CEO and other Senior Management Personnel to align short-term and long-term business objectives and link compensation with achievement of goals.

Director, key managerial personnel (KMP), and other employee remuneration details comply with Rule 5 of Chapter XIII of the Companies (Appointment and Remuneration of Managerial Personnel) Rules, 2014. Tables 3(a) and 3(b) include the perquisite value of stock incentives when exercised, but not their value at grant.

The change in remuneration in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025 was primarily due to changes in the perquisite value of stock incentives granted in previous years and exercised during the year, as well as compensation revisions. The change in perquisite value also reflects the number of units exercised and changes in the share price.

### Notes

- The remuneration details in the table pertain to directors and KMP as required under the Companies Act, 2013. The table also includes the percentage increase in remuneration and the ratio of remuneration to MRE, excluding the perquisite value of stock incentives exercised during the year.
- The details are presented on an accrual basis.
- The percentage increase in remuneration is provided for directors and KMP with remuneration for the full current and previous fiscal years. The ratio of remuneration to MRE is provided for directors and KMP with remuneration for the full current fiscal year.
- The change in remuneration of independent directors is primarily attributable to foreign exchange fluctuations and changes in the composition of Board committees.
