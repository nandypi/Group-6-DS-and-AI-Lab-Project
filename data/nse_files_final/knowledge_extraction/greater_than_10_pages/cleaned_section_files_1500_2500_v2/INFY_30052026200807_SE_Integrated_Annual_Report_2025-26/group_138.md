As at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, 3,87,949 and 2,88,384 cash-settled options were outstanding, respectively. The carrying value of the liability towards cash-settled share-based payments was ₹15 crore and ₹18 crore as at March 31, 2026 and March 31, 2025, respectively.

The fair value of the awards was estimated using the Black-Scholes model for time- and non-market performance-based options. The Monte Carlo simulation model was used for TSR-based options.

The valuation models used the share price at the grant date, exercise price, expected volatility, expected dividends, expected option term, and risk-free interest rate. Expected volatility was based on historical movements in the market prices of the Company’s publicly traded equity shares and, for comparative companies, their publicly traded equity shares over periods equivalent to the expected option terms. Correlation coefficients were calculated between each peer entity and the relevant indices or between entities in the peer group.

The fair value of each equity-settled award was estimated on the grant date using the following assumptions:
