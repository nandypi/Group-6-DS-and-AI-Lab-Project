## Vipulkumar Anopchand Shah

The shareholder asked about:

- Revenue from AI projects during the financial year.
- Whether AI project margins are higher or lower than margins in other business segments.
- Disclosure of total TCV in addition to large-deal TCV.
- Contingency plans if the rupee appreciates against the US dollar.
- Providing a hybrid AGM format, allowing both physical and virtual participation.
- Extending quarterly earnings conference calls by 15–20 minutes to give individual shareholders more opportunities to ask questions.

## Om Prakash Kejriwal

The shareholder acknowledged:

- A dividend of ₹43 per share on a face value of ₹5 despite a difficult business environment.
- Finalization of the accounts on 17 April 2025, within 17 days.
- The company’s 128 subsidiaries and associates operating in different countries.
- The joint venture with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Japan.

He asked what type of work would be undertaken through the Mitsubishi Heavy Industries joint venture. He also referred to consolidated EPS of ₹64.50 compared with ₹63.39 previously, both on a face value of ₹5, and asked about future expectations and the possibility of a special dividend.

He suggested using CSR funds for:

- Drinking water facilities near company offices and business locations.
- Support for cancer patients.

He also requested continued virtual AGMs, development of an internally built platform for virtual meetings, and opportunities for shareholders to visit the Bengaluru campus.

## Atanu Saha

The shareholder noted that he had voted on all resolutions on 17 June and expressed interest in the company’s results and dividend decisions.

He referred to the Integrated Annual Report, including:

- The leadership team on page 26.
- The value creation model on page 42, including capital utilization and inputs and outputs.
- The company’s alignment with the 17 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
- The Capital Allocation Policy on page 61.

He asked about developing information technology centers in eastern India, including Kolkata and Bhubaneswar, the possibility of a historical museum in Pune, and the number of MSMEs working with Infosys and their total transaction value.

## Hiranand Kotwani

The shareholder asked how Infosys manages its whistleblower policy, including the handling of complaints and suggestions.

He also asked about:

- Listing fees paid in India and overseas for fiscal 2025.
- Employee health management across different geographies.
- Training programs at Infosys.

## Santosh Kumar Saraf

The shareholder thanked Infosys employees and the Company Secretary.

He asked about:

- The company’s presence on stock exchange platforms.
- New technologies developed to support company growth.
- Steps taken to protect profitability and future growth amid geopolitical uncertainty.

He also requested continued opportunities for shareholders to participate in virtual meetings.
