## A.G.S. Manikantha

Shareholders can request a physical copy of the annual report by emailing their DPID and Client ID details to investors@infosys.com. The interactive annual report is available on the Infosys website.

## Nandan Nilekani

### CSR

Infosys's CSR focus areas include women's empowerment, education, skilling, and health. The company has projects across India, including water-rejuvenation projects addressing groundwater and cleaner water sources. Suggestions concerning drinking water near offices and cancer treatment will be considered by the CSR Committee.

### Whistleblower Mechanism

Infosys has a whistleblower mechanism for reporting concerns about unethical behavior, actual or suspected fraud, and violations of the Company's Code of Conduct and Ethics. The company provides an open-door policy, multiple reporting forums, and a grievance-resolution mechanism. Concerns are handled objectively with timely action and closure.

### AI Adoption and Employees

AI adoption is expected to improve productivity and automate tasks. Employees released from automated projects may be redeployed to other client projects. Infosys stated that more than 275,000 employees had been trained in AI at various levels of proficiency. The company also works with partners to continuously upskill and reskill employees.

Questions on AI, dividends, bonuses, and buybacks had been addressed in the previous section.

## Salil Parekh

### Growth and Margin Improvement

Infosys stated that its positioning in digital transformation and cost takeout supported industry-leading growth over a five-year period. Its digital leadership ranking increased from 25 in financial year 2018 to 58 in financial year 2025.

The company linked its cost-takeout capabilities to strong large-deal wins. It also launched a project across different streams to improve margins and change the margin trajectory. This was one of several factors contributing to a 50-basis-point margin improvement in financial year 2025. Infosys stated that its aspiration is to increase margins over the medium to long term.

### Geopolitical Environment and Public-Sector Programs

Infosys identified technology as a key medium- to long-term driver and cited capabilities in digital, cloud, data, cybersecurity, and AI. The company stated that generative AI is already part of discussions for almost all client projects and that it is pursuing an AI-first approach internally.

Infosys is working on several large public-sector programs, including work related to income tax and GST, as well as other government departments.

### Employee Health and Wellness

Employee wellbeing is supported through the Health Assessment and Lifestyle Enrichment program, known as HALE. The program covers physical wellbeing, social wellbeing, emotional wellbeing, and overall safety. Infosys stated that employee wellbeing scores had reached high levels recently and that it had received health and wellness recognition, including the Wellcon award.

### Technology Platforms and Growth Engines

Infosys identified AI, cloud, and digital enterprise services as growth engines. It also cited cost efficiency, automation, and AI-driven productivity as sources of new opportunities.

The company launched Aster, an AI-powered marketing platform, and an AI foundry with 200 AI agents. Infosys Cobalt provides scalable, secure, and resilient cloud infrastructure to host and manage AI models. The company stated that Cobalt, Topaz, Aster, and its cost- and efficiency-related capabilities support future growth.

### Recruitment

Infosys does not have a shareholder quota for recruitment. It follows a merit-based talent-acquisition process intended to meet client needs and support delivery of complex programs.

### Attrition

Infosys stated that its attrition rate of 14.1% was within an acceptable range and at or below the lower end of the industry range. Initiatives addressing attrition include increased promotions, compensation reviews, skill incentives, employee-engagement actions, career mobility, and wellness measures.

### Revenue by Geography and North American Demand

Management referred to an earlier response concerning Infosys's activities and growth in India and outside India.

In North America, changes in the economic outlook had affected discretionary spending in industries including retail, consumer products, and manufacturing. Clients involved in supply-chain logistics were also reassessing conditions, and some clients were recalibrating plans in response to the changing economic environment.

Infosys is working with clients on cost and efficiency solutions and on support related to the changing environment. The company cited its AI, digital, cost, and efficiency capabilities and stated that it expects to continue gaining market share as economic conditions evolve.

### CSR Activities Across India

Infosys stated that its CSR activities and beneficiaries are located across India. The Infosys Foundation report, page 72, describes work across sectors and multiple states. Pages 202 and 203 of the annual report describe work across multiple states, including aspirational districts.

### Sexual-Harassment Complaints and Workplace Governance

Infosys's Anti-Sexual Harassment Initiative, or ASHI, supports a harassment-free workplace. The company has constituted internal committees at all delivery centers within the applicable regulatory jurisdictions to address sexual-harassment matters.

A grievance redressal body, or GRB, supports implementation of the company's policy on prevention of sexual harassment at the workplace. Infosys stated that it maintains zero-tolerance and non-retaliation policies and conducts ongoing awareness and education sessions.

### Shareholder Visits

Infosys stated that shareholders would be informed in advance if programs for campus visits are organized in the future.
