## A.G.S. Manikantha

The moderator invited shareholder Bharat Raj to ask his question.

## Bharat Raj

Bharat Raj:

- Asked about Infosys’s plans for artificial intelligence, including whether increased use of AI could reduce employment.
- Requested consideration of bonus shares.
- Praised the company’s performance, dividend payments, and CSR program.
- Requested a hybrid AGM format, combining physical attendance in Bengaluru with video conferencing for shareholders unable to attend in person.
- Requested opportunities for shareholders to visit the Bengaluru and Mangaluru campuses and meet company representatives.

## A.G.S. Manikantha

The moderator invited shareholder Ramesh Shanker Golla to ask his question.

## Ramesh Shanker Golla

Ramesh Shanker Golla:

- Asked about the company’s expansion plans.
- Asked about the risks associated with artificial intelligence.
- Requested consideration of bonus shares.
- Asked why EPS was declining.
- Requested hybrid-mode AGMs, with both physical and video-conference participation.
- Requested opportunities to visit the Mysuru and Bengaluru campuses and meet company representatives.
- Stated that he had voted in favour of all resolutions.
- Thanked the company for its dividend payments and CSR programs.

## A.G.S. Manikantha

The moderator invited the final shareholder speaker, Reddeppa Gundluru.

## Reddeppa Gundluru

Reddeppa Gundluru, attending from Hyderabad, commented on Infosys’s financial performance for 2025, operational performance, consistent financial growth, dividends, and wealth creation.

He also commented on:

- The detailed annual report and presentation of the Board of Directors.
- Corporate governance, transparency, and ethical conduct by the Company Secretary and CFO teams.
- Nandan Nilekani’s support for Indian IITs.
- The continuation of the company’s CSR legacy.
- Women empowerment and the statement that Infosys was among the top 22 countries’ employers in this area.
- Infosys’s debt-free position and good return on equity.
