## Jayesh Sanghrajka

### Cash and Cash Equivalents

The yield on cash and cash equivalents was approximately 7% in FY 2025.

### Large Deals

Infosys discloses all deals with a total contract value greater than $50 million. It won 96 large deals worth $11.6 billion in FY 2025.

### AI Revenue Disclosure

Infosys is not currently publishing AI revenue or its share of total company revenue.

### HIPUS and Mitsubishi

HIPUS, in which Infosys owns a majority stake, was established in FY 2019 as a joint venture to provide procurement process, sourcing, and category expertise through digital platforms for Japanese corporations.

Mitsubishi, a long-standing HIPUS customer, acquired a 2% stake in HIPUS from Infosys. The transaction expands the collaboration between the companies.

### Capital Allocation

The Board considers dividend, bonus, buyback, stock split, and other capital allocation options, along with the advantages and disadvantages of each. Infosys had not announced a share buyback or stock split at the time of the AGM.

### Singapore Investment

Singapore is a strategic market for Infosys. The company has a joint venture there with Temasek and provides e-commerce applications, IT consulting, and other services to clients across multiple industry verticals. The investment was made in connection with these activities.

### EPS and FY 2025-26 Outlook

Standalone EPS does not fully reflect Group operations and includes intercompany transactions. Consolidated EPS had benefited in FY 2024 from a one-time tax-related benefit and interest income. Adjusting for this one-time benefit, normalized EPS for FY 2025 grew 8.3% in INR terms.

For FY 2025-26:

- Revenue growth guidance is 0–3%.
- Operating margin guidance is 20–22%.
- Financial services growth accelerated to double digits year over year in Q4 FY 2025, and the company expected the sector to perform well in the near term.
- Manufacturing grew at a double-digit rate in FY 2025, but near-term growth was expected to be more subdued because of global uncertainties, particularly in European manufacturing.
- Changes in other expenses were described as not material.

## A.G.S. Manikantha

### Prevention of Transfer to IEPF

Infosys takes the following steps to help shareholders claim unclaimed dividends and prevent the transfer of shares or dividends to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF):

- Sends periodic communications requesting shareholders to encash unclaimed dividends and update bank account details with the registrar and depository.
- Publishes information on unclaimed dividends and corresponding shares on the company website.
- Sends letters to shareholders whose dividends remain unclaimed because valid bank details are unavailable.
- Runs a special automated remittance cycle so shareholders can receive dividends in updated bank accounts before transfer to IEPF.
- Sends reminder letters and publishes newspaper advertisements for holders of unclaimed dividends as required by applicable laws.

### KFintech Shareholder Grievances

Infosys follows up with KFintech on outstanding grievances and provides guidance to help resolve them within the required timelines. Information on KFintech support channels is published on the company and stock exchange websites and in the annual report.

### Investor Grievances

Investor grievances and requests include non-receipt of dividends, IEPF-related matters, and KYC discrepancies. Infosys addresses these complaints within the timelines prescribed by SEBI regulations.

### Listing Fees

Infosys is listed on the NYSE, NSE, and BSE. Listing fees are determined according to applicable listing norms, including thresholds based on the number of shareholders and market capitalization.

### AGM Format

The MCA and SEBI permit companies to hold AGMs through virtual or hybrid modes. Shareholders have requested both fully virtual and hybrid meetings. The Board will communicate the format of future AGMs.

## Nandan Nilekani

The e-voting platform remained available for 30 minutes after the voting announcement. The Board appointed B. Hemanth, a practicing Company Secretary, as scrutinizer for the e-voting process and authorized Manikantha, the Company Secretary, to declare the voting results and publish them on the company website.

The resolutions in the AGM notice were to be deemed passed on the meeting date, subject to receipt of the required number of votes.
