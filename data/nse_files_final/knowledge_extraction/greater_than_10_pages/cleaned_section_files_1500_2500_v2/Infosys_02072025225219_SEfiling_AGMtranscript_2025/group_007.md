## A.G.S. Manikantha

The next shareholder, Sunil Kumar Modak, was invited to ask his question.

## Sunil Kumar Modak

Sunil Kumar Modak joined the virtual meeting from Kolkata. He referred to employee strength of 3,23,598 as a significant company asset and questioned why the country’s contribution to revenue creation was only 3.1%.

He noted that Infosys had 292 offices in 59 countries and questioned the revenue contribution from North America and Europe. He identified strategy, strength, and competition as factors in company growth, and referred to the company’s performance in financial year 2024–25.

He stated that he had voted in support of all resolutions.

## N. Prakash Chand Galada

N. Prakash Chand Galada made the following requests and observations:

- Consider holding physical or hybrid meetings so shareholders can participate more effectively.
- Provide senior-citizen investors with year-round telephone support through the company’s registrar and transfer agent, KFIN, including a toll-free number.
- Make the company’s 10-year performance easier to trace in the annual report.
- Consider issuing a bonus, noting that the last bonus issue was in 2019.
- Continue improving the dividend.
- Consider a buyback from shareholders rather than through the open market.

## Yusuf Yunus Rangwala

Yusuf Yunus Rangwala referred to an interim dividend of ₹21 and a final dividend of ₹22, together totalling ₹43.

He requested that the company consider splitting its ₹5 shares into ₹1 shares. He also asked why the company had not declared a bonus issue for several years and requested consideration of a future bonus issue.

He requested that the company resume in-person shareholder gatherings and consider holding future annual general meetings in a physical or hybrid format.

## Abhishek J.

Abhishek J. asked for the company’s current employee count. He also requested that Infosys consider providing employment-related consideration to shareholders’ children who meet the company’s recruitment criteria.

He requested that future annual general meetings be conducted in hybrid format because some shareholders face digital-access challenges with virtual meetings. He stated that hybrid meetings would allow shareholders to participate either physically or virtually and provide suggestions to management.

## Ashish Shankar Bansal

Ashish Shankar Bansal reported that testing during the AGM had prevented him from hearing the Chairman’s speech. He requested that technical testing not occur during the AGM and that the speaker number be displayed on screen so shareholders can identify the current speaker.

## Reddeppa Gundluru

Reddeppa Gundluru confirmed that he could be heard and asked whether he was clearly visible.
