## Salil Parekh - CEO &amp; MD's address

Salil Parekh discussed Infosys’ performance during the last financial year under the theme of leading in AI and cloud.

The business grew by 4.2% year-on-year. Large deals totaled US$11.6 billion, and the number of clients generating more than US$100 million increased to 39. Operating margin and cash generation were described as strong, with free cash flow of US$4.1 billion.

In rupee terms:

- Total revenue grew by 6.1%.
- Operating profit grew by 8.4%.
- Operating cash flow increased by more than 41% to a record level.
- Net profit increased by 1.8%.
- Basic EPS increased by 1.8%.
- Free cash flow increased by more than 44.8%.

The Board recommended a dividend of ₹43 per share. Over the previous five years, Infosys returned more than ₹98,000 crore to shareholders in cash.

The balance sheet was described as fully debt-free and liquid. The Company continued to invest cash in building assets for long-term growth.

### Artificial intelligence

Infosys was working on 400 generative AI projects for clients and had generated 10 million lines of code using generative AI tools. The Company had developed 200 AI agents, including industry-specific and horizontal or functional agents, and was deploying them across 30 agent-based AI engagements.

Around 20,000 employees were using GitHub Copilot for coding. Infosys stated that it had been recognized as a market leader in nine generative AI studies by market analysts.

Reported client applications and outcomes included:

- A large bank used generative AI for financial processes such as know-your-customer activities, with a reported 50% reduction in processing time.
- A large audit firm used AI agents for preliminary audit work.
- A large automotive company used AI agents in customer service.
- A large telecommunications company used generative AI to support revenue growth.
- A technology company reported a 24% improvement in customer satisfaction in certain customer-service areas.

### Cloud and data capabilities

Infosys described cloud as core infrastructure for AI. Its Cobalt offering includes cloud capabilities supporting turnkey network transformation, migration, modernization, and cost reduction. Infosys was also building AI agents on major public cloud platforms, where its libraries included both Infosys-developed and client-specific agents.

Data readiness was identified as another requirement for successful AI adoption. Infosys was developing industry data offerings for retail, consumer products, insurance, industrial, automotive, aerospace, and utilities. Its data work included:

- Preparing enterprise data architectures for AI.
- Using data for business transformation.
- Supporting an AI economy based on data.
- Modernizing data capabilities from legacy systems to cloud-based architectures.
- Working with structured and unstructured data systems.

### Efficiency and delivery capabilities

Client demand was focused on cost efficiency. Infosys identified automation, operational efficiency, lean delivery, and AI-supported productivity improvement as key capabilities.

Its delivery organization supported large and complex programs involving multiple services, including cloud, cybersecurity, and infrastructure. The Company’s employee training capability remained a foundation of its delivery model, with new employees joining from colleges undergoing training during their first several weeks.

Infosys continued to build on its digital capabilities as a foundation for cloud, AI, and data services. The Company also described a “One Infosys” approach, in which capabilities across geographies, industries, and service lines are combined to support clients.

### Acquisitions

During the year, Infosys completed small acquisitions in:

- Energy consulting in the United States.
- Cybersecurity in Australia.

These followed earlier acquisitions in engineering services and Salesforce services in Europe and the United States, as well as investments in joint ventures.

### Community and sustainability initiatives

During the last financial year, Infosys reported:

- Completion of 200 community projects.
- Positive impact on more than 1 crore people in India.
- More than 9 million people receiving free technology learning worldwide through Springboard.
- Enhancement of more than 4 billion liters of water capacity through the sustainable restoration of 11 lakes.
- More than 28 lakh people benefiting from healthcare initiatives.

## A.G.S. Manikantha

The statutory auditors, Deloitte, Haskins and Sells LLP, and the secretarial auditor, Makarand M. Joshi and Company, issued unqualified opinions in their respective audit reports for financial year 2024–25.

There were no qualifications, observations, or adverse comments on the financial statements or on matters having a material bearing on the functioning of the Company.

The statutory auditors’ reports on the standalone and consolidated financial statements are available on pages 210 and 293, respectively, of the Integrated Annual Report. The secretarial auditors’ report is included as Annexure 5 to the Board’s Report on page 85 of the Integrated Annual Report 2024–25.
