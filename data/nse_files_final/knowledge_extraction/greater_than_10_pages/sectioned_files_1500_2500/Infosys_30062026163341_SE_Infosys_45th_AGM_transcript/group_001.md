## Welcome address - Nandan M. Nilekani
I welcome the members to the 45th Annual General Meeting. Hope all of you are safe and in good health. This meeting is being held through video conference in accordance with the circulars issued  by  the  Ministry  of  Corporate  Affairs  and SEBI.  Members  participating  through  video conference are being considered for the purpose of quorum, as per the circular issued by the MCA and Section 103 of the Companies Act, 2013. Before  we  start  the  main  proceedings  of  the  meeting,  I  request  my  colleagues  on  the  video conference to introduce themselves. Nitin.
