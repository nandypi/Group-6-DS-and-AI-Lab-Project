## Additional information pursuant to U.S. Law
The Buyback for the outstanding equity shares of the Company referenced herein has not yet commenced. The communication is provided for informational purposes only and is neither an offer to purchase nor a solicitation of an offer to sell any securities of the Company pursuant to Company's Buyback or otherwise. Any offers to purchase or solicitations of offers to sell will be made pursuant to a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO (including the letter of offer and other documents relating to the tender offer) which will be filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ('SEC') by the Company.
